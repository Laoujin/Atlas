> **TL;DR** Cáceres has two confirmed events in fall 2026: **Potencial Digital** (Sep 24–25, free, institutional congress) and **Extremadura Digital Day** (Oct 3, €25–40, community multi-track). For a visitor, EDD is the richer peer-community experience; Potencial Digital is the bigger headline congress with startup pitches and Hack The Box. Outside those, the scene runs on annual hackathons and AEXTIC thematic sessions. [[1]](https://potencialdigital.es/) [[3]](https://www.eventbrite.es/e/registro-extremadura-digital-day-2026-1985466953104)

## Confirmed Events — Fall 2026

### Potencial Digital 2026
**Sep 24–25 · Palacio de Congresos de Cáceres · Free**

The 3rd Extremaduran Congress on Digital Transformation, AI, Cybersecurity and Robotics [[1]](https://potencialdigital.es/). Organized by FEVAL, CDTIC Extremadura, CEMD and Extremadura Avante; headline sponsors include Telefónica, EY, Oracle, IBM, Google Cloud, Accenture and SAP.

Four tracks: Digital Transformation, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Robotics. Side programme: startup pitch competition (seed/growth stage, cash prizes + sponsor services), Hack The Box ethical hacking challenges, kids' robotics workshops ("Misión Tecno-Reto 360" — drone and LEGO robot building). Invited region for 2026: Andalucía.

Scale: the inaugural edition (Sep 2024) drew 6,000+ visitors including 1,262 students and 1,123 tech workers, with 61 speakers and 50+ sponsors; 14% of attending job-seekers were hired on the spot [[2]](https://www.juntaex.es/w/congreso-digital).

### Extremadura Digital Day 2026 (EDD26)
**Oct 3 · Complejo Cultural San Francisco, Cáceres · €25–40**

[Extremadura Digital Day](https://extremaduradigitalday.com/) is Extremadura's largest community-run tech event [[3]](https://www.eventbrite.es/e/registro-extremadura-digital-day-2026-1985466953104). Multi-track programme curated via open CFP: software dev & architecture, IoT & hardware, UX & accessibility, AI & data science, business transformation. Talks are 15–30 min in Spanish or English; speakers get dinner, meals, and travel/accommodation covered.

EDD25 (Sep 27 2025, same venue) drew 400+ attendees — 80% professionals, 20% students [[4]](https://www.papercall.io/extremadura-digital-day-2026). CFP for EDD26 closed May 16; speaker notifications expected June 2026.

Tickets: Early Bird €30 (active now), General €40. Student and visitor discount codes released Sep 1 [[3]](https://www.eventbrite.es/e/registro-extremadura-digital-day-2026-1985466953104).

## Recurring / Periodic Events

### Mobbeel Innovation Challenge
**Annual · Oct · Garaje 2.0, Cáceres · Free**

No-code hackathon by [Mobbeel](https://www.mobbeel.com/innovation-challenge/) in partnership with FUNDECYT-PCTEX and EDIH [[5]](https://www.mobbeel.com/en/blog/mobbeel-innovation-challenge-2025-2/). 2nd edition (Oct 23 2025): 100+ participants, two challenge tracks — Multimodal Biometry and GenAI/LLMs — prizes up to €500 per team. No 3rd edition date announced; annual cadence expected.

### JITEL — Jornadas de Ingeniería Telemática
**Academic · rotating host (Cáceres hosted Nov 2025)**

Spain's academic telematics engineering conference, run by SCITEL. XVII edition (Nov 12–14 2025) was hosted by Universidad de Extremadura in Cáceres: 79 accepted papers across AI-for-networks, comms security, next-gen networks, multimedia, and educational tech [[6]](https://publicauex.wordpress.com/2026/02/05/actas-de-las-xvii-jornadas-de-ingenieria-telematica-jitel-2025-caceres-espana-12-14-de-noviembre-de-2025/). Host rotates between Spanish universities; no 2026 Cáceres edition announced.

### ExtreTech (AEXTIC)
**Rolling thematic sessions · province-wide**

[AEXTIC](https://www.aextic.com/) (Extremadura ICT Business Association) runs short ExtreTech sessions targeting SMEs and public administrations across Cáceres province — topics cycle through cybersecurity, AI, digital invoicing and sector verticals (tourism, culture) [[7]](https://www.aextic.com/extretech-turismo-y-cultura-tecnologia-que-impulsa-la-eficiencia-y-el-crecimiento-empresarial/). Funded by the Cáceres Provincial Council. No fixed calendar; check their events page per cycle.

### Open Data Cáceres Hackathon
**Periodic · Escuela Politécnica de Cáceres**

At least four editions (2015–2018) challenged teams to build web/mobile apps on the city's open-data portal [[8]](https://hackathonspain.com/calendario/iv-hackathon-open-data-caceres/). No edition confirmed since 2018.
