## Headliner Details

### Cybersecurity

**[RSAC 2026](https://conferenceinc.net/post/rsac/) (Mar 23–26, Moscone)** — The world's largest cybersecurity event, 2026 theme "The Power of Community" [[1]](https://conferenceinc.net/post/rsac/). Free expo pass available with sponsor promo codes; full conference pass $2k+. Runs in tandem with satellite events across SoMa all week.

**[BSidesSF 2026](https://bsidessf.org/) (Mar 21–22, City View at Metreon)** — Volunteer-run community security conf immediately preceding RSAC, drawing practitioners who skip the vendor floor [[2]](https://bsidessf.org/). ~600 talk submissions vetted down to a tight program. ~$110 at the door.

**[OWASP Global AppSec USA](https://owasp.glueup.com/event/owasp-global-appsec-usa-2026-167174/) (Nov 2–6, Hyatt Regency SF Embarcadero)** — Four days of application security training (Nov 2–4) followed by a two-day conference (Nov 5–6) [[14]](https://owasp.glueup.com/event/owasp-global-appsec-usa-2026-167174/).

### AI & Machine Learning

**[AI Dev 26 × SF](https://ai-dev.deeplearning.ai/) (Apr 28–29, Pier 48)** — Andrew Ng's DeepLearning.AI developer conf with five tracks: agentic AI, context engineering, multimodal apps, AI governance, coding agents [[3]](https://ai-dev.deeplearning.ai/). Intentionally hands-on over keynote-heavy; 3,000+ developers.

**[AI Engineer World's Fair](https://www.ai.engineer/worldsfair/2026) (Jun 29–Jul 2, Moscone West)** — Billed as the largest technical AI conference globally: 10 parallel engineering miniconferences (Coding Agents, Robotics, LLM RecSys, Inference, etc.) plus two leadership tracks, 400+ sessions, 100+ expo partners [[8]](https://www.ai.engineer/worldsfair/2026). Group discounts start at 5 tickets.

**[The AI Conference](https://aiconference.com/) (Sep 29–Oct 1, Pier 48)** — Vendor-neutral, founded by ex-O'Reilly AI Conference chairs. Day Zero (Sep 29) is capped at 300 for workshops + AI Hack Day; main conference is Sept 30–Oct 1 with 5,500+ attendees [[11]](https://aiconference.com/). Five tracks: AI Frontiers, AI Builders, The AI Stack, Applied AI, AI Strategy.

### Developer & Platform

**[Microsoft Build](https://www.sfbayareatimes.com/posts/microsoft-build-2026-san-francisco) (Jun 2–3, Fort Mason Center)** — First SF edition in ~10 years; ~2,500 developers in-person at Fort Mason; keynotes free via livestream at build.microsoft.com [[7]](https://www.sfbayareatimes.com/posts/microsoft-build-2026-san-francisco). In-person ticket $1,099. Focus on Azure AI, GitHub Copilot, and developer tooling.

**[TechCrunch Disrupt](https://techcrunch.com/events/techcrunch-disrupt/) (Oct 13–15, Moscone West)** — Startup pitch competition (Startup Battlefield, $100k prize), 10k+ founders and VCs, Expo floor [[13]](https://techcrunch.com/events/techcrunch-disrupt/). Broad badge tiers from $75 (Expo+) to full Founder/Investor passes.

### Data & Cloud

**[Snowflake Summit](https://www.snowflake.com/en/summit/) (Jun 1–4, Moscone Center)** — Snowflake's flagship annual conf, themed "Making AI Real for Business." 500+ sessions across 12 tracks, hands-on instructor-led labs, expo hall with 190+ partners [[6]](https://www.snowflake.com/en/summit/). At-the-door ticket $2,495; group rates from $1,495 (30+).

### Startups & Venture

**[SF Tech Week](https://www.tech-week.com/) (Oct 5–11, citywide)** — Hundreds of independently hosted events presented by a16z: hackathons, fireside chats, demo days, community meetups across SF [[12]](https://www.tech-week.com/). Each event managed separately; browse and apply via the Luma calendar. Most events are free or low-cost.

**[Step SF](https://stepsf.com/) (Aug 26–27, The Midway)** — Two-day AI startup festival: day one citywide kick-off events, day two main conference at The Midway with four stages, 50+ speakers, founders/investors/operators [[9]](https://stepsf.com/). Tickets $75–$525.

### Fintech & DevOps

**[Stripe Sessions](https://register.stripesessions.com/2026) (Apr 29–30, Moscone West)** — Stripe's annual developer/business conf covering payments, AI-native commerce, and crypto commerce models; 5k–10k attendees, workrooms, certification exams [[4]](https://register.stripesessions.com/2026).

**[SREday SF](https://sreday.com/2026-san-francisco-q2/) (Apr 17, Harness HQ)** — Community-run single-day SRE and DevOps conf; no remote option; talks on incident response, observability, OpenTelemetry [[5]](https://sreday.com/2026-san-francisco-q2/).
