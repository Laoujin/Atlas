The two weekends to book — and the year that frames them.

Only two windows actually pull a tech-curious traveller to Modena: the spring burst around Motor Valley Fest (May 28–31)[1] and the autumn arc from DevFest Modena → Smart Life Festival (Oct 3–18)[5][8]. Everything else on this calendar is a meetup-evening backup — pleasant, but not worth re-anchoring a weekend around.

Three things to know before you book

01 · Pair with the dinner
Motor Valley Fest is Thu–Sun; Friday's tech day pairs naturally with a Saturday-night Michelin seating. The October arc gives more weekend choices — DevFest (Sat) or Smart Life opening (Thu Oct 15) both work as anchors[8].
02 · Language
Most events run in Italian, slides often English. Smart Life Festival is largely Italian-language. DevFest historically has a mix.
03 · Side-trip
Reggio Emilia is 25 minutes by train — worth noting if a sister meetup or a restaurant pulls you there[21].

Michelin 2 and 3-star restaurants in Modena
Modena has exactly one 2★-or-3★ Michelin restaurant — Osteria Francescana (3★). The 2-star tier is empty; nearest 2★ is San Domenico in Imola, ~1h drive.
