The shape

"The dinner is the constraint, not the choice."

Modena has exactly one 2★-or-above restaurant — Osteria Francescana, three stars[1] — and the 2★ tier is empty across the city and province in the 2026 Guida[2]. Tables open ~1 month ahead. Pick the Saturday first, then plan around it.
