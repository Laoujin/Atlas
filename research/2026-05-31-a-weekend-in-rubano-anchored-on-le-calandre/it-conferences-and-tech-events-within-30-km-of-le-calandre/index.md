> **TL;DR** — The 30 km circle around Le Calandre covers Padova city, Piazzola sul Brenta and Vicenza. Inside it, six dated IT/innovation events run through year-end 2026: **EG-ICE** (Jul 1-3, academic) [[1]](https://www.dicea.unipd.it/eg-ice-2026-padua), **AzureOnBeer** (Sep 11, Vicenza, community) [[2]](https://www.azurelab.blog/2026/05/25/azureonbeer-la-seconda-edizione-si-terra-l11-settembre-2026/), **VenetoNight** (Sep 25, public science) [[7]](https://www.unipd.it/universa/notte-ricercatori-venetonight), **IMPACT** (Oct 14-16, trade fair) [[4]](https://www.imp-act.it/), **DIGITALmeet** (Oct 19-24, festival) [[6]](https://digitalmeet.it/), plus recurring **AWS / Azure / WordPress** meetups. Only DIGITALmeet's final day is a Saturday — the natural pairing with a Saturday dinner at Le Calandre [[6]](https://digitalmeet.it/).

## What's inside the radius

Le Calandre sits in Rubano, 10 km west of Padova. A 30 km road radius captures:

- **Padova** city (~10 km) — university, Fiera, downtown venues
- **Piazzola sul Brenta** (~13 km) — THRON HQ, the recurring AWS meetup venue [[8]](https://www.meetup.com/it-it/aws-user-group-padova/)
- **Vicenza** (~28 km by road, 24 km straight-line) — Azure community hub [[13]](https://www.percorsokm.it/it-it/percorso-da-Vicenza-a-Rubano)

Verona (~80 km), Mestre and Treviso fall **outside** the circle — UseCaseConf and angularday Verona, the only other Veneto dev-conferences in 2026, are therefore out of scope [[10]](https://dev.events/EU/IT).

## The events, briefly

**EG-ICE 2026** [[1]](https://www.dicea.unipd.it/eg-ice-2026-padua) — European Group for Intelligent Computing in Engineering, 3-day academic workshop on AI/ML applied to civil and built-environment engineering. Hosted by Università di Padova's DICEA department. Engineering and CS audience.

**AzureOnBeer 2026** [[2]](https://www.azurelab.blog/2026/05/25/azureonbeer-la-seconda-edizione-si-terra-l11-settembre-2026/) — Second edition. Friday afternoon at Lucky Brews craft brewery in Vicenza, half-day of Microsoft Azure talks on AI, security, infra and modern work, followed by a Master-Brewer-led tasting [[3]](https://azureonbeer.azmv.it/). Run by the 267-member [Azure Meetup Veneto](https://www.meetup.com/azure-meetup-veneto/) community [[9]](https://www.meetup.com/azure-meetup-veneto/). The closest dedicated tech-conference to Le Calandre in 2026.

**VenetoNight** [[7]](https://www.unipd.it/universa/notte-ricercatori-venetonight) — The European Researchers' Night, free, public. Across Padova, Venice, Verona on the last Friday of September. Padova programming covers AI, quantum, physics, life sciences. Spillover events ("Aspettando la Notte") run earlier the same week.

**IMPACT 2026** [[4]](https://www.imp-act.it/) — 3-day B2B trade fair at PadovaFiere with 100+ exhibitors, 30+ conferences, AI-powered B2B matching, co-located with Green Logistics Expo [[5]](https://padovahall.com/event/impact/). Free registration. Business-services and innovation focus, not deep-tech.

**DIGITALmeet 2026** [[6]](https://digitalmeet.it/) — 14th edition of Italy's largest digital-literacy festival, opening from the University of Padova, then 150+ distributed events across Italy. AI and entrepreneurship themes. **Runs through Saturday 24 October**, the only event here that aligns with a Saturday-evening Le Calandre booking.

## Recurring meetups (no fixed 2026 cadence)

Three active developer communities are based inside the radius:

- **AWS User Group Padova** [[8]](https://www.meetup.com/it-it/aws-user-group-padova/) — 489 members, meets ~3-4× a year at THRON S.p.A. in Piazzola sul Brenta (~13 km). Last event 21 May 2026; no next date posted at time of writing.
- **Azure Meetup Veneto** [[9]](https://www.meetup.com/azure-meetup-veneto/) — 267 members, Viest Hotel Vicenza for in-person. Hosts the AzureOnBeer event above.
- **Padova WordPress Meetup** — 559 members; the largest tech-community group in Padova by raw membership [meetup.com/find/it--pd--padova/](https://www.meetup.com/find/it--pd--padova/).

Worth checking these group pages a week or two before the trip — calendars are short-horizon.

## What's *not* happening in 2026

- **SMAU Padova** — the Padova stop is **not** on SMAU's 2026 roadshow (only Paris, Stockholm, NYC, Marche, Milano) [[11]](https://www.smau.it/). Historically a March fixture; absent this year.
- **Codemotion** — Italian editions are in Rome (Mar) and Milan (Oct 28-29), not Padova/Vicenza [[10]](https://dev.events/EU/IT).
- **Galileo Festival** [[12]](https://www.galileofestival.it/galileo-festival-2026-a-padova-i-grandi-esperti-di-space-economy-ai-life-sciences-energia-e-ambiente/), **Talent Week** [[15]](https://www.talentweek.it/), **Digital Artifex** [[14]](https://www.padovaoggi.it/attualita/padova-terza-edizione-digital-artifex-19-marzo-2026.html) — all already happened in spring 2026; relevant only for a 2027 trip.

## Practical pick for a Le Calandre weekend

If timing the trip is open, **Saturday 24 October 2026** is the standout: DIGITALmeet's closing day in Padova (10 km, transit easy), with a Saturday-evening Le Calandre booking as the cap. Second-best is **Friday 11 September**: AzureOnBeer in Vicenza ends mid-evening, leaves Saturday free for Padova sightseeing before the dinner. Any other weekend before December has no in-radius tech anchor — pair the dinner with the city's permanent attractions instead.
