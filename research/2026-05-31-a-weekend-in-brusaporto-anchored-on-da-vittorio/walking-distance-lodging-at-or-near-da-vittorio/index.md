> **Decision.** Book one of the **10 rooms at [La Dimora](https://davittorio.com/en/hotel-en/la-dimora.html)** — the on-site Relais & Châteaux hotel inside Da Vittorio's 10-hectare Cantalupa estate [[1]](https://davittorio.com/en/hotel-en/la-dimora.html) [[2]](https://www.davittorio.com/en/location-en/cantalupa.html) [[4]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/hotel/da-vittorio/). It is literally the only address you can fall out of a three-star tasting menu [[5]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/lombardia/brusaporto/restaurant/da-vittorio) and into a bed without crossing a road. From €650 [[1]](https://davittorio.com/en/hotel-en/la-dimora.html) and books months out — TripAdvisor ranks it #1 of 2 hotels in Brusaporto at 4.7/5 [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g966832-d668060-Reviews-Da_Vittorio-Brusaporto_Province_of_Bergamo_Lombardy.html). If those ten rooms are gone, the *only* genuinely walkable fallback is **[Joia Hotel & Luxury Apartments](https://www.joiahotel.it/)** — Brusaporto's only other hotel — at about 1.4 km / 19 min on village roads from €86–€220 [[10]](https://www.italia.it/en/lombardia/brusaporto/where-to-sleep/joia-hotel-and-luxury-apartments) [[19]](https://www.kayak.com/Brusaporto-Hotels-Joia-Hotel-Luxury-Apartments.374062.ksp) [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g966832-d1007707-Reviews-Joia_Hotel_Luxury_Apartments-Brusaporto_Province_of_Bergamo_Lombardy.html). Everything else needs a taxi.

## Context

Da Vittorio sits at Via Cantalupa 17, on top of the wooded Cantalupa hill above Brusaporto village, on a 10-hectare estate with vineyard, two natural lakes, an 800-metre walking circuit and a tennis court [[2]](https://www.davittorio.com/en/location-en/cantalupa.html). The Cerea family runs both the restaurant and the hotel as a single property [[16]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/restaurant/da-vittorio/) [[4]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/hotel/da-vittorio/). Brusaporto itself is a hill village 10 minutes by car from Bergamo [[2]](https://www.davittorio.com/en/location-en/cantalupa.html); TripAdvisor lists exactly **two** hotels in the municipality — Da Vittorio's own La Dimora and the apartment-style Joia Hotel [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g966832-d668060-Reviews-Da_Vittorio-Brusaporto_Province_of_Bergamo_Lombardy.html) [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g966832-d1007707-Reviews-Joia_Hotel_Luxury_Apartments-Brusaporto_Province_of_Bergamo_Lombardy.html). The next-nearest branded hotel is **3.7 mi / ~6 km** away in Grassobbio or Orio al Serio, well outside any reasonable post-dinner walk in tasting-menu attire [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g966832-d668060-Da_Vittorio-Brusaporto_Province_of_Bergamo_Lombardy.html).

## Reasoning per tier

**Tier 1 is the entire point of the trip.** Ten rooms, one estate, one kitchen — sleeping under the same roof as the three-Michelin-star tasting menu [[5]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/lombardia/brusaporto/restaurant/da-vittorio) removes every transport question and folds breakfast in the picture-window dining room into the price [[1]](https://davittorio.com/en/hotel-en/la-dimora.html). Reviewers consistently call out the welcome aperitif, Cerea-family presence in the dining room and a breakfast spread "among the best" they've eaten [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g966832-d668060-Reviews-Da_Vittorio-Brusaporto_Province_of_Bergamo_Lombardy.html) [[16]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/restaurant/da-vittorio/). Cited downsides are small: one 2019 reviewer flagged event-noise from rooms above the restaurant; à-la-carte breakfast extras (eggs) cost on top of the included buffet [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g966832-d668060-Reviews-Da_Vittorio-Brusaporto_Province_of_Bergamo_Lombardy.html). Book three to six months out — Saturday-night Michelin pilgrims drain the calendar fast.

**Tier 2 — Joia — is the *only* legitimate "walked it" backup**, and only just. Italia.it (the Italian tourism authority) explicitly quotes the 19-minute walk to Da Vittorio [[10]](https://www.italia.it/en/lombardia/brusaporto/where-to-sleep/joia-hotel-and-luxury-apartments); multiple guest reviews caution that the surrounding area is "very deserted" with "no shops or restaurants nearby" → you need a car for everything except the dinner walk [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g966832-d1007707-Reviews-Joia_Hotel_Luxury_Apartments-Brusaporto_Province_of_Bergamo_Lombardy.html). Trade-off is real: at ~$150 it's a third of La Dimora's price [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g966832-d1007707-Reviews-Joia_Hotel_Luxury_Apartments-Brusaporto_Province_of_Bergamo_Lombardy.html), and you can book a one-bed apartment with kitchenette and pool access, but a 19-min walk back uphill at 23:00 in evening wear is the kind of "walkable" that looks better on a map than at the end of a wine pairing. If it's raining, call a taxi.

**Tier 3 — skip walking entirely and pick a base that earns its keep on the rest of the weekend.** **Hotel Excelsior San Marco** anchors you on Bergamo Bassa's main square with two-minute access to the Città Alta funicular [[13]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/it/hotelexcelsiorsanmarcobergamo.en-gb.html) — best balance of dinner-taxi practicality (10–15 min) and walk-everywhere upper-town tourism. **GombitHotel** is the Città Alta design boutique inside the UNESCO Venetian walls [[14]](https://www.gombithotel.com/en/) — pick this if Saturday morning is for Città Alta and dinner is genuinely the only Brusaporto event. **B&B Corte Seguini** in Bagnatica is a quiet 1600s village stay one cab-ride south at half-luxury price [[15]](https://www.corteseguini.it/en/). The TripAdvisor "10 closest hotels" cohort — Winter Garden, NH Orio al Serio, Radisson Blu ChorusLife — is all 6–8 km out and chosen for airport proximity, not dinner [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g966832-d668060-Da_Vittorio-Brusaporto_Province_of_Bergamo_Lombardy.html); they only earn their keep if you're flying in Sunday morning.

## Practical

- **La Dimora is ten rooms.** That number isn't a typo and it doesn't grow on demand. Saturday-night dinner-and-stay should be booked 3–6 months ahead, especially in May–October — check the restaurant calendar [[5]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/lombardia/brusaporto/restaurant/da-vittorio) before locking the room.
- **Helicopter is an actual option.** Da Vittorio partners with Air-Dynamic for private heli arrivals directly onto the estate [[17]](https://davittorio.com/contatti.html) — silly if you're flying into Orio al Serio (10 min by taxi), interesting if your start point is a Milan rooftop.
- **Joia walk practicalities.** Wear flat shoes, arrange a return cab as plan B, and confirm Joia reception staffs the night desk before assuming a 1 a.m. walk-up is fine — TripAdvisor warns about 28 steps from the underground car park to room level [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g966832-d1007707-Reviews-Joia_Hotel_Luxury_Apartments-Brusaporto_Province_of_Bergamo_Lombardy.html).
- **Airport transfer.** Orio al Serio (BGY) is ~10 km from Brusaporto; taxi €20–€35 [[18]](https://www.bergamo-airport.com/taxi.html). Pre-book a transfer if you land Saturday afternoon — Saturday dinner reservations don't wait for the queue at the rank.
- **No real B&Bs inside Brusaporto.** The municipality has Da Vittorio, Joia, and a pilgrim-route donation B&B ("A casa di Lalla") at Via Monte Alben 11 with bunk beds and an 18:00–08:00 check-in window aimed at walkers, not late-dinner guests [[20]](https://www.laviadellesorelle.it/en/resource/accomodation/brusaporto-a-casa-di-lalla/) — flagged here only so future searches don't waste time on it.
- **Day-trips and onward planning** for the rest of the weekend (Bergamo Città Alta, Lake Iseo, Franciacorta) live in the sibling research alongside this one in the parent trip plan.
