"IT" inside this ring means industrial IT — robotics, mechatronics, Industry 4.0 — clustered at Kilometro Rosso[5] and the University of Bergamo[3]. Milan is ~45 km from Bergamo and outside the ring[1].

The cluster sits on the A4

Brusaporto is 8 km east of Bergamo center[2]; the Kilometro Rosso innovation district (80 resident partners, 2,500 staff) sits 15 km west at the Stezzano motorway exit[5]. The UniBg engineering campus in Dalmine[4] anchors the southern flank; the Sant'Agostino humanities campus[3] anchors the city center.

Milan's tech scene sits roughly 50 km west — outside the ring, but ≤1 h via A4 if you decouple from the Saturday-dinner anchor.

Saturday 20:00+ seating at Da Vittorio = the calendar's gravitational center. Every event below is sorted by whether you can be at the tasting menu that evening.

The three still-bookable anchors

THU JUN 18 · 8:45–17:30

Forum Robotica Innovativa

Kilometro Rosso GATE 4 · Via Stezzano 87 · 15 km west
Pair with: a Saturday Jun 20 seating. The Thursday talk closes by dinner-time; you have two full days to settle in at La Dimora before the tasting menu [7][8].
SAT SEP 19
SEP 13–17 · WEEK-LONG

APMS 2026 — IFIP WG 5.7

University of Bergamo · city center · 10 km west
Pair with: closing-day Saturday booking — Sep 19 follows the conference dinner-Friday. Springer AICT proceedings; paper submissions closed Apr 10 but attendee registration remains open [10].

OCT 1–11 · 11 DAYS

BergamoScienza — Convergenze

Bergamo città + province · multi-site, walkable from Città Alta
Pair with: either Saturday Oct 3 or Oct 10. Weekend programming leans workshop-heavy; big-data + AI threads are explicitly in the 2026 program [11].
