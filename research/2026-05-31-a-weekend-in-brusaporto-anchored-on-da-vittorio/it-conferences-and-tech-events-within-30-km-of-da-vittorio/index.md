> **TL;DR.** "IT" inside this radius means **industrial tech** — robotics, mechatronics, Industry 4.0. The cluster sits at the [Kilometro Rosso](https://www.kilometrorosso.com/en/innovation-district-en/) innovation district (~15 km from Brusaporto) and the [University of Bergamo](https://en.unibg.it/events) Sant'Agostino + Dalmine campuses. **Milan is outside the radius** (~45 km from Bergamo [[1]](https://www.distance.to/Milan/Bergamo)). For a 2026 Saturday-dinner anchor, the catches you can still hit are **Forum Robotica Innovativa (Jun 18)**, the **CREO Hackathon (Jun 17–19, UniBg-only)**, the **APMS Conference (Sep 13–17)** and **BergamoScienza "Convergenze" (Oct 1–11)**. Pure dev/software conferences (Codemotion, PyCon Italia) don't run in this radius.

## Geography sanity check

## Practical pairing notes

- **June weekend:** Thu Jun 18 is a heavy day — the [Forum Robotica Innovativa](https://www.eventbrite.it/e/biglietti-forum-robotica-innovativa-1984777795816) runs 8:45–17:30 at Kilometro Rosso, finishing in time for a 20:00+ Da Vittorio Saturday seating two days later [[7]](https://www.eventbrite.it/e/biglietti-forum-robotica-innovativa-1984777795816). The [CREO Hackathon](https://creo.unibg.it/en/opportunita/creo-hackathon-2026-smart-spaces-smarter-decisions/) (Jun 17–19) overlaps but is **closed to non-UniBg participants** — relevant only if you're connected to the university [[9]](https://creo.unibg.it/en/opportunita/creo-hackathon-2026-smart-spaces-smarter-decisions/).
- **September:** The [APMS Conference](https://www.apms-conference.org/apms-2026/) (Sep 13–17) is the only week-long international IT-adjacent conference in the radius for 2026; a Saturday-night Da Vittorio booking aligns with the closing day [[10]](https://www.apms-conference.org/apms-2026/). Paper submission closed Apr 10 2026, but attendee registration remains open via the conference site [[10]](https://www.apms-conference.org/apms-2026/).
- **October:** [BergamoScienza](https://www.bergamoscienza.it/en/festival-corrente/24th-edition-bergamoscienza-2026) "Convergenze" runs daytime + evening across the city Oct 1–11; weekend visitors get the workshop-heavy programming on Saturdays/Sundays. Big-data and AI threads are part of the 2026 program [[11]](https://www.bergamoscienza.it/en/festival-corrente/24th-edition-bergamoscienza-2026).
- **Recurring calendars to watch for ad-hoc lectures:** the [UniBg events page](https://en.unibg.it/events) and the [Confindustria Bergamo Innovation events feed](https://www.confindustriabergamo.it/aree-di-interesse/innovazione-tecnologica-e-organizzativa/eventi-e-webinar) surface short-form talks and member webinars on AI, Industry 4.0 and digital transformation that don't make the festival calendars [[3]](https://en.unibg.it/events) [[18]](https://www.confindustriabergamo.it/aree-di-interesse/innovazione-tecnologica-e-organizzativa/eventi-e-webinar).

## What this radius does *not* offer

- **Mainstream dev conferences.** Codemotion 2026 runs in [Rome and Madrid](https://events.codemotion.com/), not Lombardy. PyCon Italia is in Bologna. Italian Tech Week is in Turin. None of these are within 30 km of Brusaporto.
- **Milan tech scene.** Digital Design Days, AI Week, Politecnico events — Milan is ~45 km out [[1]](https://www.distance.to/Milan/Bergamo). The Brebemi A35 / A4 keep the drive ≤1 h but breaks the "anchor at Da Vittorio" frame.
- **Grassroots developer meetups.** The Bergamo scene runs predominantly through institutional venues (Confindustria, UniBg, Kilometro Rosso) and the local CCIAA training arm ([Bergamo Sviluppo](https://bergamosviluppo.it/sito/lista-eventi.html)) rather than community-organised meetups [[6]](https://bergamosviluppo.it/sito/lista-eventi.html).
- **Pure-software vendor events.** AWS Summit, Google I/O Extended, Microsoft Build watch-parties — these route through Milan when they happen at all.

The pattern: **Bergamo's "IT" is industrial IT**, sponsored by the manufacturing cluster on the A4 between Stezzano and Dalmine [[5]](https://www.kilometrorosso.com/en/innovation-district-en/). If your weekend at Da Vittorio falls on one of the Kilometro Rosso forum dates, the venue is 15 minutes away. Otherwise the calendar is sparse outside the Bergamo Next Level / APMS / BergamoScienza tentpoles.
