Maison Colette Guesthouse

Five deluxe rooms above the kitchen.
0 m · on-site €305 / room 5 rooms ★★ Michelin

A renovated former physician's residence on the small square next to Tongerlo church — two Michelin stars downstairs [3], five rooms upstairs, breakfast in your room. Each suite carries a double bed, desk, coffee machine, minibar, safe, robes and slippers [1].

Book this first The 5 rooms are only sold alongside a dinner reservation — and you're booking the Saturday dinner anyway. Wine pairings without a drive home, the table secured, the only on-square lodging at De Trannoyplein 17.

Check-in 16:00–18:00 · Check-out 10:30 · Salon Anna breakfast if >1 room booked [1].

Tongerlo Abbey · Gastenkwartier

Abdijstraat 40 · Bisschopshuis & Prelatuur
~12 min · 0.94 km not public 5 single + 4 double

5 single rooms in Bisschopshuis (en-suite, WiFi) plus 4 doubles in the Prelatuur; meals taken communally in the refectory [10].

Not for a Michelin weekend. Multi-night minimum, advance booking only, no walk-ins, "participation in liturgical prayer times greatly valued" [10].

B&B · Manor house

B&B Villa Zonneweelde

Langstraat 70, Westerlo-Tongerlo · 1947 manor + garden
~14 min · 1.01 km on request not public

An authentic 1947 manor house with a sunny green garden, advertised by Toerisme Westerlo as "within walking distance of Tongerlo Abbey" [5]. The only true "walking back from dinner in heels" option besides the abbey.

Top fallback. Pricing not published — phone +32 474 32 49 93 or email info@villazonneweelde.be [14].

B&B Carpe Diem

Kaaibeeksedijk 6, Westerlo · 2 rooms · Auping beds
~21 min · 1.61 km €122–€188 2 rooms

Two named rooms — Gravin de Merode and Prins de Merode — with smart TV, AC, terrace, homemade breakfast [6]. Tripadvisor 5/5, Travelers' Choice, #1 of 2 in Westerlo [7].

Strongest reviewed B&B in range. Bookable direct or via Booking.com [16].

B&B Wolf & Belle

de Merodedreef 55, Westerlo · Beeltjens woods edge
~26 min · 1.94 km on request 1 room

A characterful single-suite B&B with garden view, walk-in shower and infrared cabin; Booking.com guest scores of 9.1–9.3 [13].

26-minute walk is the long edge of "walkable" — fine in May, factor weather [17].
5 Only Hotel in Range
★★★★ Hotel · 4 generations

Hotel Geerts

Grote Markt 50, 2260 Westerlo · own restaurant, orangerie, garden
~26 min · 1.97 km on request 18 rooms 4-star

Four-generation family hotel on Westerlo's main square — on-site restaurant, orangerie event space, garden [11]. The only proper hotel inside true walk-back range; 18 rooms means availability is rarely the constraint [12].

Grote Markt convenience. Morning-coffee infrastructure on the doorstep. 26 minutes is the long edge of walkable [18].
