> **Decision.** Book one of Maison Colette's own 5 deluxe rooms — they sit literally above the restaurant, are €305/room incl. breakfast, and are only sold *with* a dinner reservation [[1]](https://maison-colette.be/en/overnight-stay). If they're full, the closest real walks are [Tongerlo Abbey's gastenkwartier](https://www.tongerlo.org/tongerlo/gastenkwartier/) (0.94 km, ~12 min — but minimum multi-night and quasi-monastic) [[10]](https://www.tongerlo.org/tongerlo/gastenkwartier/)[[15]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/4.9169387,51.1034583;4.9069945,51.1054086?overview=false) and [B&B Villa Zonneweelde](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/bb-villa-zonneweelde) (1.01 km, ~14 min) [[5]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/bb-villa-zonneweelde)[[14]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/4.9169387,51.1034583;4.9135046,51.1119881?overview=false). Everything else is a 20–26 min walk into Westerlo centre — fine in dry weather, less fun in March rain.

## Anchor point

Maison Colette is at **De Trannoyplein 17, 2260 Westerlo** — the Tongerlo deelgemeente of Westerlo, on the small square next to the Tongerlo church, ~879 m from Tongerlo Abbey [[2]](https://maison-colette.be/en/contact-us). Two Michelin stars in the current guide; the property is a renovated former physician's residence with five upstairs guestrooms [[3]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/antwerpen/tongerlo_1063046/restaurant/maison-colette).

Distances below are **OSRM foot-routing** from De Trannoyplein 17 — i.e. the actual walking route a phone would give you, not straight-line.

## Why the on-site guesthouse is the right answer

Three reasons compound:

1. **You've already met the booking precondition.** The 5 rooms can only be reserved alongside a dinner reservation [[1]](https://maison-colette.be/en/overnight-stay) — and you're booking the Saturday-evening dinner anyway. Nobody else gets the rooms without making the same commitment, so availability is essentially gated to people in your situation.
2. **Wine pairings.** A two-star dinner with the pairing is genuinely impaired if you're driving — or expensive if you're calling a taxi back to Westerlo centre at 23:30 (post-midnight, in Tongerlo, no Uber).
3. **The product itself.** Each room has a double bed, desk, coffee machine, minibar, safe, robes, slippers, and breakfast served *in the room* — or in Salon Anna if you've booked more than one room [[1]](https://maison-colette.be/en/overnight-stay). For €305 incl. breakfast it's priced like a city-centre boutique hotel, not a Michelin add-on.

## When the guesthouse won't work

Five rooms book out far in advance. Fallbacks, in order:

- **Villa Zonneweelde** — 14 min walk, the only true "walking back from dinner in heels" option besides the abbey [[5]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/bb-villa-zonneweelde)[[14]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/4.9169387,51.1034583;4.9135046,51.1119881?overview=false). Pricing isn't published — phone +32 474 32 49 93 or email info@villazonneweelde.be.
- **B&B Carpe Diem** — 21 min walk, only 2 rooms but consistent 5★ reviews [[6]](https://www.bedandbreakfastcarpediem.be/)[[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g230027-d12149679-Reviews-B_B_Carpe_Diem-Westerlo_Antwerp_Province.html)[[16]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/4.9169387,51.1034583;4.9241598,51.0905750?overview=false). Bookable on Booking.com or directly.
- **Hotel Geerts** — the only hotel in true walk-back range (1.97 km, 26 min) [[11]](https://www.hotelgeerts.be/en/)[[12]](https://hotel-geerts.2flanders.com/en/)[[18]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/4.9169387,51.1034583;4.9187583,51.0873886?overview=false). 18 rooms, so availability is much less of a problem; it's on Westerlo's main square so you get morning-coffee infrastructure on the doorstep. 26 minutes is the long edge of "walkable" — fine in May, factor weather.

## What to skip

- **Tongerlo Abbey gastenkwartier** is technically closest at 0.94 km, but it isn't a hotel: it's a working Norbertine monastery whose guest quarters are for "reflection, faith deepening or rest", requires multi-night bookings, expects you in the refectory and at liturgy, and won't take walk-ins [[10]](https://www.tongerlo.org/tongerlo/gastenkwartier/)[[15]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/4.9169387,51.1034583;4.9069945,51.1054086?overview=false). Not the vibe for a Michelin weekend.
- **B&B Butch** (5.94 km) and **B&B Crea Els** (4.0 km, Oevel) are listed by Toerisme Westerlo [[4]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/overnachten) but neither is walkable from De Trannoyplein [[19]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/4.9169387,51.1034583;4.8476112,51.1200781?overview=false)[[20]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/4.9169387,51.1034583;4.9082005,51.1359899?overview=false). Cheaper (€95–€100) and pleasant if you have a car, but they fail the brief.
- **Hotel Vivaldi** — listed by Toerisme Westerlo [[4]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/overnachten) but described elsewhere as ~5.5 km from Tongerlo Abbey, a 10-minute drive — out of scope.
