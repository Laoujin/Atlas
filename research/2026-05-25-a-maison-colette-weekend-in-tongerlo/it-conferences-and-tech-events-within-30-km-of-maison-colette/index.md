> **TL;DR.** Strictly inside the 30 km bubble, the action is academic / meetup-scale: KU Leuven hosts the [Leuven IP & AI Conference](https://www.law.kuleuven.be/citip/en/citip-conferences/ip-and-ai/leuven-ip-and-ai-conference-2026) (May 18-19) and [Large-Scale AI Risks](https://www.kuleuven.be/ethics-kuleuven/chair-ai/conference-ai-risks) (June 23-24); the recurring meetups worth pinning are [Data Science Leuven](https://www.meetup.com/data-science-leuven/), [Data Mesh Belgium](https://www.meetup.com/data-mesh-belgium/), and [Ixor Tech Talks Mechelen](https://www.meetup.com/ixor-tech-talks-mechelen/) [[12]](https://www.law.kuleuven.be/citip/en/citip-conferences/ip-and-ai/leuven-ip-and-ai-conference-2026)[[13]](https://www.kuleuven.be/ethics-kuleuven/chair-ai/conference-ai-risks)[[14]](https://www.meetup.com/data-science-leuven/)[[16]](https://www.meetup.com/ixor-tech-talks-mechelen/). The headliner Antwerp conferences — [Techorama](https://techorama.be/), [DDD Europe](https://2026.dddeurope.com/), [BC TechDays](https://www.bctechdays.com/event), [Devoxx](https://devoxx.be/), [SmashingConf](https://smashingconf.com/antwerp-2026/), [AutomationSTAR](https://automation.eurostarsoftwaretesting.com/) — sit just outside (~35-47 km by road) but are reachable in well under an hour from Tongerlo [[2]](https://www.travelmath.com/distance/from/Westerlo,+Belgium/to/Antwerp,+Belgium)[[19]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Westerlo/Antwerp).

## The 30 km radius, honestly

Maison Colette is at De Trannoyplein 17, Tongerlo (Westerlo), in Antwerp Province [[1]](https://maison-colette.be/en/contact-us)[[18]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/antwerpen/tongerlo_1063046/restaurant/maison-colette). A 30 km straight-line circle around Tongerlo captures Geel, Herentals, Aarschot, Lier, Turnhout, Mechelen (edge), and Leuven (edge) — but **not** Antwerp city: Westerlo→Antwerp is ~38 km straight-line, ~47 km / 45 min by car [[2]](https://www.travelmath.com/distance/from/Westerlo,+Belgium/to/Antwerp,+Belgium)[[19]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Westerlo/Antwerp). Since the major Flemish IT conferences cluster in Antwerp, the table below splits "strictly inside 30 km" from "Antwerp (~35-47 km)" so you can make the call.

## Around the trip date (May 2026)

If the Saturday-evening dinner is in the May 23 or May 30 weekend, the conference calendar in the neighbourhood looks like this:

- **Tue May 12 – Wed May 13:** [Techorama 2026](https://techorama.be/) at Kinepolis Antwerp — strongest "tack it onto the trip" candidate for .NET / cloud / data folks; ~45 min from Tongerlo [[3]](https://techorama.be/).
- **Mon May 18 – Tue May 19:** [Leuven IP & AI Conference](https://www.law.kuleuven.be/citip/en/citip-conferences/ip-and-ai/leuven-ip-and-ai-conference-2026) at KU Leuven — only fits the trip if you arrive early; closest big-name venue inside 30 km [[12]](https://www.law.kuleuven.be/citip/en/citip-conferences/ip-and-ai/leuven-ip-and-ai-conference-2026).
- **Wed May 20:** [Data Science Leuven](https://www.meetup.com/data-science-leuven/) monthly meetup at STUK Leuven [[14]](https://www.meetup.com/data-science-leuven/) — evening event, low commitment.
- **Thu May 28 – Fri May 29:** Online certification masterclasses (The Principal Dev, Clean Architecture) listed on the Antwerp IT calendar — remote-only so geography doesn't apply [[17]](https://dev.events/EU/BE/Antwerp/it).

Nothing major hits the strict Saturday May 23 / May 30 windows themselves — the Belgian conference rhythm is Tuesday-through-Friday, with the next packed cluster being **June 8-12** (DDD Europe + Data Mesh Live + BC TechDays all in Antwerp) [[6]](https://www.bctechdays.com/event)[[8]](https://2026.dddeurope.com/)[[9]](https://2026.datameshlive.com/). If extending the trip is on the table, that week stacks three world-class conferences inside a 45-min drive of the restaurant.

## Getting from Tongerlo to the Antwerp venues

Westerlo→Antwerp is ~47 km / ~45 min by car; ~1h15 by bus + train via Heist-op-den-Berg if you'd rather not park [[2]](https://www.travelmath.com/distance/from/Westerlo,+Belgium/to/Antwerp,+Belgium)[[19]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Westerlo/Antwerp). For Antwerp-Central venues (DDD Europe, SmashingConf, AutomationSTAR), train is the better call. For Kinepolis Antwerp-North (Techorama, Devoxx, BC TechDays), driving wins — the venue is on the ring road [[5]](https://devoxx.be/)[[3]](https://techorama.be/).
