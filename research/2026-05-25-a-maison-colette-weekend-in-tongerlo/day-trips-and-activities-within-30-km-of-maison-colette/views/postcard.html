Greetings from
LIER
22 km

Everything in a 15-min walking loop. UNESCO begijnhof, the Zimmertoren clock, a spiced Liers Vlaaike on the way out.

1st pick · Culture Zimmertoren + Begijnhof (1258, UNESCO 1998) + Liers Vlaaike [40] [41] [42]

AVERBODE
8 km

593 ha of heath, dunes and Nardus grassland — restoration brought the nightjar back. Red loop 11.2 km / 170 min.

1st pick · Outdoor Averbode Bos en Heide · Trailhead Abdijstraat 1, Averbode [23] [24] [25]

Greetings from
HET MOMENT
8 km

The only place to taste the abbey's own draught — micro-brewed inside the café. Bottles are contract-brewed elsewhere.

Het Moment at Abdij Averbode (Norbertine, 1134, 55 canons) [72] [71] [26]

Greetings from
BOBBEJAAN-
LAND
10 km

Fury — 43 m tall, 106.6 km/h, triple-launch, riders vote forwards or backwards. Typhoon's 97° drop.

1st pick · Family Bobbejaanland · €28.90 with code OPEN26 (reg. €49.90) [58] [59] [60]

Greetings from
WESTMALLE
28 km

Abbey gate stays shut except one open day a year — but the café opposite pours Extra, Dubbel, Tripel + abbey cheese until midnight.

1st pick · Beer Café Trappisten · daily 10:00–24:00 · seats 1,200, big terrace [75] [74]

Greetings from
DIEST
20 km

Groot Begijnhof founded 1253 — among the largest in the world. The 1845 citadel is one of few in Europe still intact.

Begijnhof Diest (UNESCO) + Sint-Sulpitiuskerk (Orange-Nassau tomb) [45] [44] [46]

Greetings from
LOMMEL
SAHARA
30 km

Sand dunes inside Bosland. The red loop (11.7 km) is the one voted Belgium's most beautiful hike.

Lommel Sahara · Blue 3.8 km · Orange 5.7 · Red 11.7 [62]
NoteSwimming forbidden in the lakes

Greetings from
KASTEEL
DE MERODE
2 km

Moated, still lived in by Prince Simon. Most days you walk the perimeter; book July's Kasteelfeesten to actually go inside.

Kasteel Prins de Merode · 12 ha English park + 60 ha French park across the Nete [10] [11]
Open Kasteelfeesten 1st w/e July; Kerstmagie December [13] [14]

Greetings from
DE BEELTJENS
1 km

Westerlo's backyard — 127 ha of star-shaped 18th-c. avenues. Asberg dune (24 m) is the highest point in town.

De Beeltjens · Gravinnekespad sensory trail for ages 3–10 [29]

Greetings from
GEEL
10 km

Sint-Dimpna church and the medieval-rooted family-foster-care tradition. ~500 psychiatric residents still placed with townspeople today.

Gasthuismuseum + Sint-Dimpnakerk [54] [53]

Take home from
VLAAIKE
22 km

300-year-old spiced pastry — candy syrup + four spices. EU-recognised regional product since 2013.

Liers Vlaaike — buy on the way back from the Zimmertoren [42]

Detail from
DA VINCI
in church

Multispectral analysis pins the sfumato and St. John's face on Leonardo himself; Giampietrino, Solario and d'Oggiono identified as workshop collaborators.

Tongerlo canvas, 8 × 4 m, painted 1507–08 in Milan; bought 1545 by Abbot Streyters for 450 Rhenish florins [3]

Tear-off Day Plan — finish by 17:00 sharp

№ 01

Cultural

Tongerlo at 08:00 → drive to Lier for noon → walking loop + Vlaaike → back by 17:00.

№ 02

Outdoor

Averbode red loop (11.2 km) → lunch at Het Moment → De Beeltjens stroll back.

№ 03

Family

Bobbejaanland 10:00–17:00, full day. Wet-weather fallback: Plopsa Hasselt or Sunparks Aquafun.

№ 04

Beer-themed

Het Moment for the abbey-only draught → Café Trappisten Westmalle for Extra / Dubbel / Tripel + cheese.

№ 05

Begijnhof tour

Diest morning (UNESCO + Sulpitius + citadel) → Tongerlo abbey late afternoon → dinner.
