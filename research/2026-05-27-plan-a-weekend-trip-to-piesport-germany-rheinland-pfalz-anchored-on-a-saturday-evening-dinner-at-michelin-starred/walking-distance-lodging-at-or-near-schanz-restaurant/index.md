> **Decision.** **Book schanz.hotel itself** — the 12-room hotel is directly above the restaurant at Bahnhofstraße 8a [[2]](https://www.schanz-restaurant.de/en/hotel/about-the-hotel), and breakfast runs until noon so a late tasting-menu evening doesn't cost you the morning [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g319769-d3531788-Reviews-Schanz_Hotel-Piesport_Rhineland_Palatinate.html). If it's full, fall back to a Moselstraße address (Alt Reinsport, Moselloreley, Moselblick) — all on the same Niederemmel bank as Schanz, 5–10 min flat walk down Bahnhofstraße to the river [[9]](https://www.moselblick-piesport.de/anreise.php).

## Why "walking distance" splits in two

The restaurant is at **Bahnhofstraße 8a in the Niederemmel district** of Piesport [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/rheinland-pfalz/piesport/restaurant/schanz-restaurant). Niederemmel (formed from Müstert, Emmel and Reinsport) sits on the Hunsrück-side bank; Piesport-proper sits opposite on the Eifel-side bank; a single Mosel bridge connects them [[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piesport). So every lodging option falls into one of two buckets:

- **Same bank (Niederemmel):** ~300–600 m, flat, ≤8 min walk → six options.
- **Across the bridge (Piesport-proper):** ~1.2–1.5 km, real walk → two options.

## Property notes

### [schanz.hotel](https://www.schanz-restaurant.de/en/hotel/about-the-hotel) — the obvious answer
Twelve designer rooms directly above the restaurant, themed by tibo architects after culinary concepts, with walk-in showers and a sun terrace with illuminated water basin [[2]](https://www.schanz-restaurant.de/en/hotel/about-the-hotel). TripAdvisor 4.2/5 across 55 reviews, ranked #1 of 3 small hotels in Piesport; rooms from ~€175/night including breakfast, which runs until 12:00 [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g319769-d3531788-Reviews-Schanz_Hotel-Piesport_Rhineland_Palatinate.html). The restaurant — three Michelin stars, modern French [[4]](https://en.visitmosel.de/wine-cuisine/gastro/restaurant-schanz) — is "directly downstairs" with minimal noise [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g319769-d3531788-Reviews-Schanz_Hotel-Piesport_Rhineland_Palatinate.html). ⚠ Only 12 rooms — book the same time you secure the dinner reservation.

### [Alt Reinsport](https://www.booking.com/hotel/de/alt-reinsport.html) — budget fallback
Pension-Café on Moselstraße 4, family-run by Josef Klein. Singles from €72, doubles from €104, apartments from €65; breakfast on request, free parking and WiFi [[7]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/de/alt-reinsport.html). Three TripAdvisor reviews, all 5/5 — small sample but consistent praise for the breakfast [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotels-g319769-Piesport_Rhineland_Palatinate-Hotels.html). ~5 min flat walk down Bahnhofstraße, turn left onto Moselstraße.

### [Hotel-Weingut Moselloreley](https://moselloreley.de/) — Mosel + Riesling
Moselstraße 16, working winery with restaurant and hotel rooms from €89 [[10]](https://moselloreley.de/kontakt/kontakt-oeffnungszeiten). TripAdvisor 4.5/5 across 39 reviews [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotels-g319769-Piesport_Rhineland_Palatinate-Hotels.html). ~7 min walk; good pick if you want a Riesling tasting on the same trip.

### [Hotel Moselblick](https://www.moselblick-piesport.de/) — riverside, cycle-path
Moselstraße 20, directly on the Mosel cycle path with its own jetty [[8]](https://www.yelp.com/biz/hotel-moselblick-piesport). Their own directions explicitly read "follow Bahnhofstraße through Piesport, turn left onto Moselstraße at the river, ~100 m" — so from Schanz it's the same Bahnhofstraße end-to-end [[9]](https://www.moselblick-piesport.de/anreise.php). TripAdvisor 4.2/5 across 44 reviews [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotels-g319769-Piesport_Rhineland_Palatinate-Hotels.html).

### [Lehnert-Veit](https://lehnert-veit.de/) & [Lehnert-Später](https://www.lehnert-spaeter.de/) — winery guesthouses
Both are old Piesport Riesling families with guestrooms over the winery: Lehnert-Veit at In der Dur 6-10 [[11]](https://www.yelp.com/biz/weingut-lehnert-veit-piesport), Lehnert-Später at Brückenstraße 21 near the bridge [[14]](https://www.lehnert-spaeter.de/). Both ~5 min from Schanz on the Niederemmel side. Pick these if the wine matters more than hotel polish — Lehnert-Veit's TripAdvisor is 3.6/5 across 17 reviews [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotels-g319769-Piesport_Rhineland_Palatinate-Hotels.html), but you're sleeping above a Goldtröpfchen vineyard.

### Across-the-bridge options
**[Hotel-Café Alt-Piesport](https://www.hotel-alt-piesport.de/)** (Ausoniusufer 1) — riverside terrace with Mosel views [[12]](https://www.outdooractive.com/en/accommodation/moseltal/hotel-cafe-alt-piesport/1674583/), but mid-tier TripAdvisor (3.0/5, 8 reviews) [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotels-g319769-Piesport_Rhineland_Palatinate-Hotels.html). **Weingutshotel Piesporter Goldtröpfchen** (Am Domhof 5) — the Hain family's winemaker B&B, 4.5/5 across 62 reviews [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotels-g319769-Piesport_Rhineland_Palatinate-Hotels.html), [[13]](https://triptap.com/places/de/rheinland-pfalz/piesport/weingutshotel-piesporter-goldtropfchen-t04940a2). Walkable after dinner but the bridge crossing is exposed in winter or rain — and you'll add 15+ min versus a Niederemmel-side stay.

## Practical notes

- The entire village is compact — Piesport-proper and Niederemmel together fit inside a ~1 km Mosel bend [[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piesport). Every option above is "walkable" from Schanz; the real choice is bank, not distance.
- Schanz serves Wednesday–Saturday, lunch and dinner [[4]](https://en.visitmosel.de/wine-cuisine/gastro/restaurant-schanz) — match lodging dates accordingly.
- Distances are address-based estimates; verify the actual route in Google Maps before relying on them.
