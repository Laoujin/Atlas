## In town: the 3-hour classic

**Sacro Monte di Orta** is the easiest UNESCO site you'll ever bag — 20 chapels on the life of Saint Francis arranged along a wooded hilltop ~600 m from Villa Crespi [[10]](https://www.illagomaggiore.com/itineraries/sacro-monte-di-orta-the-sacred-mountain-and-san-giulio-island/). Free admission; open 09:30-18:00 weekdays, 09:30-18:30 weekends/holidays from 29 Mar to 24 Oct 2026 [[1]](https://www.sacrimonti.org/en/sacro-monte-di-orta). Allow 1.5-2 hours; wear real shoes [[7]](https://www.explorelakemaggiore.com/discover/must-see-attractions/sacro-monte-dorta/). Descend the far side to find yourself ~15 minutes from Villa Crespi's door [[10]](https://www.illagomaggiore.com/itineraries/sacro-monte-di-orta-the-sacred-mountain-and-san-giulio-island/).

**Piazza Motta** has hosted a Wednesday market continuously since 1228 [[9]](https://discovernorthernitaly.com/orta-san-giulio/). On its left side sits the [Palazzotto della Comunità](https://www.followingtherivera.com/things-to-do-in-orta-san-giulio/), a 1582 frescoed loggia where the General Council of the autonomous Riviera d'Orta republic met from 1345 to 1753 [[5]](https://www.followingtherivera.com/things-to-do-in-orta-san-giulio/). The square is also the trailhead for Villa Crespi's signed lake walks [[6]](https://villacrespi.it/en/five-ways-to-discover-lake-orta/).

**Isola San Giulio** — the boat. Two services from Piazza Motta:

| Operator                                   | Frequency      | Price (return) | Notes                                  |
|--------------------------------------------|----------------|----------------|----------------------------------------|
| [Navigazione Lago d'Orta][b1] (motorboats) | Every 15 min   | €5.00          | From 09:00 daily, 5-min crossing       |
| Public ferry                               | Every 30-45 min| €3.80          | Cheaper, less frequent                 |

[b1]: https://www.navigazioneorta.it/en/public-boat-service.html

Sources: motorboat consortium [[2]](https://www.navigazioneorta.it/en/public-boat-service.html); ferry comparison [[3]](https://insieme-piemonte.com/en/isola-san-giulio-lake-orta/).

On the island, the [Basilica di San Giulio](https://discovernorthernitaly.com/isola-san-giulio/) is a 12th-century Romanesque church built atop 5th-century foundations, with 14th-16th-century apostle frescoes, baroque additions by Carlo Borsetti and Francesco del Cairo, a black-marble pulpit on four pillars, and the saint's relics in the crypt [[4]](https://discovernorthernitaly.com/isola-san-giulio/) [[5]](https://www.followingtherivera.com/things-to-do-in-orta-san-giulio/). A 500-metre cobbled loop — the **Way of Silence / Meditation** — circles the island past the cloistered [Mater Ecclesiae](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/579390) Benedictine abbey (founded 1973, nuns weave liturgical paraments), with reflection placards in four languages [[8]](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/579390) [[11]](https://travels-with-my-camera.blog/2017/09/22/lake-ortas-walk-of-silence-meditation/).

Isola Bella: Palazzo Borromeo opens 12 Mar - 8 Nov, €25 adult + €0.50 landing fee [[13]](https://terreborromeo.it/en/isola-bella/hours-prices). Isola Madre's 8-hectare gardens are among Italy's oldest, with the Piazzale dei Pappagalli where parrots, golden pheasants and peacocks roam free [[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giardini_Botanici_dell%27Isola_Madre). A combined Bella + Madre ticket runs ~€36.50; the Navigazione Laghi day pass ~€16.90 [[21]](https://borromean-islands.com/). Isola dei Pescatori is a 100×350 m fishermen's hamlet — narrow lanes, balconied houses, seafood trattorias — the sensible lunch stop [[16]](https://www.islands.com/1823719/tiny-fishermans-island-isola-dei-pescatori-italys-lake-maggiore-romantic-seafood-restaurants-secluded-stays/).

Back on shore, [Stresa's Corso Umberto I lungolago](https://paliparan.com/2024/12/18/stresa-borromean-islands/) is a Belle Époque promenade culminating at the [Grand Hotel des Iles Borromées](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g187847-d202819-Reviews-Grand_Hotel_des_Iles_Borromees_Spa-Stresa_Lake_Maggiore_Piedmont.html), open since 1863 with the chandeliers preserved [[19]](https://paliparan.com/2024/12/18/stresa-borromean-islands/) [[20]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g187847-d202819-Reviews-Grand_Hotel_des_Iles_Borromees_Spa-Stresa_Lake_Maggiore_Piedmont.html).

## Mottarone: the cable car is still closed

The Stresa-Alpino-Mottarone funivia from Piazzale Lido in Carciano has been closed since the 23 May 2021 disaster, when investigators established that operators had been routinely blocking emergency brakes to avoid downtime [[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stresa%E2%80%93Alpino%E2%80%93Mottarone_Cable_Car) [[25]](https://ambrosioecommodo.it/en/approfondimenti/tragedia-della-funivia-stresa-mottarone-nuovo-caso-in-cui-il-risarcimento-deve-essere-esemplare/). The Stresa tourism office still publishes "THE CABLECAR IS CLOSED" with no 2026 reopening announced [[24]](https://www.stresaturismo.it/en/excursions/the-stresa-alpino-mottarone-cablecar-the-cablecar-is-closed/). Two driving alternatives:

| Route from Villa Crespi   | Cost                | Notes                                        |
|---------------------------|---------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Via Armeno (SP41 "Due Riviere") | Free          | ~30-min winding drive from Orta — best for Lake Orta-side guests [[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mottarone) [[37]](https://www.visitlakeorta.com/attrazioni/mottarone/) |
| Via Stresa-Gignese "Borromea" toll | €10 car / €7 motorbike | Private road from Alpino side [[35]](https://www.autoclassmagazine.com/web/mottarone/) |

The summit (1,492 m per Parco del Mottarone) delivers a 360° panorama spanning the Ligurian Apennines, Maritime Alps, Monte Rosa, Monviso and the Swiss high peaks [[31]](https://terreborromeo.it/en/parco-del-mottarone) [[32]](https://www.illagomaggiore.com/en_US/26064,Poi.html), with seven lakes visible — Orta, Maggiore, Mergozzo, Comabbio, Monate, Varese and Biandronno [[30]](https://www.milansperanza.it/en/attractions/monte-mottarone-and-7-lakes/) [[36]](https://villacrespi.blastness.info/en/magazine/territory/monte-mottarone-mountain-overlooking-the-lakes/). Stops at the top:

- **[Alpyland](https://www.alpyland.com/en/)** — twin-seat alpine bobsled coaster: €6.50 single full ride / €5.50 reduced, multi-ride packs to 10 trips; 10:00-17:00 weekdays, 10:00-18:00 weekends; min age 4, min height 100 cm [[26]](https://www.alpyland.com/en/opening-hours-and-rates/) [[27]](https://www.alpyland.com/en/).
- **[Giardino Botanico Alpinia](https://giardinobotanicoalpinia.altervista.org/)** at the Alpino mid-station (~800 m) — open daily 09:30-18:00 weather permitting, €5 / €4.50 reduced, specializing in alpine flora [[28]](https://giardinobotanicoalpinia.altervista.org/orari-tariffe/) [[29]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giardino_Botanico_Alpinia).
- **Rifugio Genziana** for a Piedmontese-rustic lunch at altitude [[31]](https://terreborromeo.it/en/parco-del-mottarone).
- **[Mottarone Trail Park](https://www.trailforks.com/region/mottarone-mtb-area/)** — ~10 maintained MTB singletracks, easy-to-black, on-site rental at Bar Stazione near Alpino [[33]](https://www.trailforks.com/region/mottarone-mtb-area/).

**Villa Taranto** in Pallanza is ~22 km / 23 minutes from Villa Crespi [[48]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Orta-San-Giulio/Verbania-Pallanza). The 2026 season opens earlier than usual on 6 March and runs through 1 November, with full daily 09:00-18:30 from 19 March [[39]](https://www.illagomaggiore.com/news/villa-taranto-2026-season/). Tickets: €15 adult, €10 youth (12-26), €2.50 child (6-11) [[38]](https://www.grandigiardini.it/lang_EN/15-visit-Giardini-Botanici-di-Villa-Taranto-event-tickets). Late May falls between the famous Bulb Festival (tulips, 22 Mar - 20 Apr) and the Dahlia Display (Jul - late Oct) [[40]](https://www.villataranto.it/en/flowering-calendar/), so the headline payoff in late May is the **Rhododendron Wood** — ~800 specimens covering the sloping garden [[41]](https://www.villataranto.it/en/gardens/) — plus azaleas, camellias, magnolias, exotics and emerging water lilies [[42]](https://italien.expert/en/giardino-botanico-villa-taranto-verbania-lago-maggiore/).

**Parco Pallavicino** — 18 ha mixing historic gardens (roses, azaleas, rhododendrons) with a wildlife area of deer, llamas, alpacas, peacocks, kangaroos [[45]](https://www.lovingstresa.com/en/parco-pallavicino). Now part of the Borromean experience pass — Isola Bella + Pallavicino €37 adult; a three-site pass (any 3 of Isola Bella / Isola Madre / Pallavicino / Rocca di Angera) is €55 adult, valid two consecutive days [[44]](https://navigazioneisoleborromee.com/en/lake-maggiore/borromean-islands/individual-admission-fees-borromean-islands/). ⚠ Closed Fri 22 May 2026 for the Giro [[43]](https://terreborromeo.it/en/parco-pallavicino/timetables-and-fares).

**Villa San Remigio** is not visitable — the gardens are under renovation in anticipation of a future reopening, and the main villa was acquired by the Italian State in the 1960s and remains inaccessible, used for bureaucratic offices [[46]](https://viviverbania.it/en/places/villa-san-remigio-18291) [[47]](https://www.villasanremigio.it/en/storia-villa-san-remigio-lago-maggiore).

## Outdoor & water

### Hiking around Lake Orta

The **Anello Azzurro (Blue Ring)** circumnavigates Lake Orta over 43.6 km in three moderate stages — Orta San Giulio→Pella (15.7 km), Pella→Omegna (11.8 km), Omegna→Orta San Giulio (16.1 km) — ~1,150 m cumulative climb [[49]](https://www.komoot.com/collection/1610082/trekking-around-italys-lake-orta-the-blue-ring-anello-azzurro). Waymarked with blue-ring "Anello Azzurro" signs sharing CAI red/white in places; mixed trail, asphalt and dirt [[50]](https://www.illagomaggiore.com/en_US/53648,Itineraries.html).

The half-day classic is the climb from **Pella through Boleto to the [Sanctuary of Madonna del Sasso](https://www.distrettolaghi.it/en/experiences/art-culture-faith/faith/sanctuary-of-madonna-del-sasso)** — a 1748 Baroque Greek-cross church with Peracino frescoes perched on a granite cliff 338 m above the west shore [[51]](https://www.piemonte.uno/en/piedmont/verbano-cusio-ossola/sanctuary-madonna-del-sasso) [[52]](https://www.der-ortasee-ruft.com/en/637650/hike-pella---boleto---madonna-del-sasso) [[67]](https://www.distrettolaghi.it/en/experiences/art-culture-faith/faith/sanctuary-of-madonna-del-sasso). The forecourt — locally the *balcony of Cusio* — surveys nearly the whole lake plus Mottarone, the Alps and the Novara plain [[68]](https://www.visitlakeorta.com/attrazioni/madonna-del-sasso/).

## Offbeat: design pilgrimage, niche museums, wine

### Omegna — Italian homeware was invented here

Omegna at the north tip of Lake Orta is the design-pilgrimage anchor. The **[Alessi factory outlet](https://www.visitomegna.it/en/poi/outlets/alessi-outlet/)** on via privata Alessi 6 in Crusinallo is open daily 10:00-18:00, stocking the full catalogue alongside discounted seconds and end-of-range pieces [[64]](https://www.visitomegna.it/en/poi/outlets/alessi-outlet/). The **Museo Alessi** sits across the driveway — guided visit by appointment, book at least two weeks ahead [[65]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g3347016-d10684211-Reviews-Alessi-Crusinallo_Province_of_Verbano_Cusio_Ossola_Piedmont.html). Five minutes away, the **[Forum Omegna](https://www.distrettolaghi.it/en/experiences/art-culture-faith/museums/fondazione-museo-arti-e-industria-forum)** design museum occupies the reconverted Cobianchi steelworks (redesigned by Alessandro Mendini) and since 1998 has held a permanent collection of household design from Alessi, Lagostina, Girmi, Calderoni, Piazza and others [[66]](https://www.distrettolaghi.it/en/experiences/art-culture-faith/museums/fondazione-museo-arti-e-industria-forum).

This is the **Moka birthplace** — Alfonso Bialetti opened his Omegna aluminium workshop in 1919 and launched the Moka Express in 1933; the *omino con i baffi* logo arrived in 1953 [[77]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_Bialetti). Lagostina's 1955 'Thermoplan' double-bottom (steel + aluminium) is another local milestone [[78]](https://www.wetheitalians.com/news/italian-handcrafts-homeware-omegna).

### Wine: Alto Piemonte Nebbiolo at the doorstep

The **[Strada del Vino delle Colline Novaresi](https://www.cantine.wine/en/strada/colline-novaresi)** spans 13 municipalities including Boca, Ghemme, Sizzano and Fara Novarese [[74]](https://www.cantine.wine/en/strada/colline-novaresi). Villa Crespi pegs the Ghemme/Gattinara cellars at ~30 minutes' drive and runs its own wine-tour package with private transfer [[75]](https://villacrespi.it/en/pacchetti_vc/lovers-of-good-food/). **Cantine Aperte Alto Piemonte 2026** opens Gattinara, Ghemme, Boca, Bramaterra and Colline Novaresi cellars on **30-31 May 2026** [[73]](https://www.altopiemontemag.it/cantine-aperte-alto-piemonte-2026-gattinara-e-ghemme/) — if your weekend overlaps, it's the easy win.

### Local food specialties to ask for

- **Tapulone** (Borgomanero) — slow-cooked stew of minced donkey meat with lard, onion, garlic, red wine, cabbage, bay, cloves and rosemary, served with polenta. The culinary symbol of Borgomanero [[76]](https://villacrespi.blastness.info/en/magazine/local-products/tapulone/).
- **Miacce** — paper-thin waffle-like discs made since the 15th century in long-handled irons over fire (batter of milk, flour, eggs); round in Piedmont, often paired with soft cheese or jam [[79]](https://www.facarospauls.com/apps/italian-food-decoder/28031/miaccia).
- **Gorgonzola DOP** — legally producible only in a defined set of provinces including Novara and Verbano-Cusio-Ossola, putting the cheese's heartland directly around Lake Orta [[80]](https://en.gorgonzola.com/production-area/).
