TL;DR Four conferences land in Madrid this week (June 3–6): South Summit (Europe's largest AI forum, 20,000+ attendees), the free AWS Summit at IFEMA, the free CollabDays Madrid for Microsoft 365 practitioners, and Commit Conf, the flagship open developer conference with 1,000+ attendees.[19][7][3] Later in the year: CONVEX Summit (June 17–18, .NET/AI/Architecture, €99) and Tech Show Madrid (Nov 4–5, 27,000 visitors) are the standout picks.[5][11]

This Week — June 3–6, 2026

Jun 3–5
South Summit Madrid
La Nave, C/ Cifuentes 5  ·  20,000+ attendees[8]
€299–€1,399 Now

Europe's largest AI & startup forum (15th edition, theme "AI Convergence"). 600+ speakers, 30 unicorns, 2,100+ investors ($580B+ under management), 7,500+ startups from 134 countries.[19] Unique Defence & AI track with NATO's DIANA accelerator. Four ticket tiers: Ecosystem €299 → Investor €1,399.[9]

Jun 4
AWS Summit Madrid
IFEMA Madrid, Av. del Partenón 5[7]
Free Tomorrow

Free one-day event with 90+ sessions on AI, agentic systems, serverless, cloud migration, and AWS European Sovereign Cloud. Keynote 10:00–11:30 (Tanuja Randery, VP/MD EMEA). Includes AWS Village, Training & Certification zone, developer community zone, and networking reception 17:30–18:30.[7]

Jun 4
CollabDays Madrid
Microsoft Ibérica, Pozuelo de Alarcón[22]
Free Tomorrow

Spain's largest Microsoft 365 & Modern Work conference, heir to SharePoint Saturday Madrid (10+ year history). Full day on Teams, Viva, Power Platform, Copilot, SharePoint, Exchange, Security, and Microsoft Graph. Organized by Microsoft MVPs.[6]

Jun 5–6
Commit Conf
ETSI Informáticos, UPM Campus Sur (Vallecas)[4]
Tickets on Koliseo This week

Madrid's flagship independent developer conference: 70 talks, 1,000+ attendees, backed by 40+ Spanish tech communities. Covers all of tech — web, mobile, cloud, data. One ticket covers both days (meals included). Diversity tickets available. Metro: L1 Sierra de Guadalupe; Cercanías C2/C7.[3]
