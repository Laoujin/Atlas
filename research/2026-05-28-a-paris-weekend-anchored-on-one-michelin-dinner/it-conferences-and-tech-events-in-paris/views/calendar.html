Headliner · Scale
June 17–20, 2026

VivaTech

Paris Expo Porte de Versailles · M12
10th anniversary edition. Founders, corporates, investors, public sector — all on one floor. Closer to a trade-show-meets-keynote-festival than a developer conference. Best paired with a Michelin dinner because the show ends early enough most evenings [1][2][25].
{ Devoxx }
Headliner · Depth
April 22–24, 2026

Devoxx France

Palais des Congrès, Porte Maillot · M1 / RER C
14th edition · 240 talks · ~4,600 attendees · French and English. The seriously-technical alternative to VivaTech — Java leans heaviest but every stack shows up. Hotels near Porte Maillot fill fast [3][4].
