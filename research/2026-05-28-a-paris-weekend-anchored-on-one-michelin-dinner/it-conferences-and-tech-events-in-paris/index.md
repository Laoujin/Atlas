## This weekend (May 28–31, 2026)

No headline conference falls on this weekend. The fillers are community-grade, not destination-grade:

- **[Station F](https://stationf.co/events)** runs its rolling event calendar across the campus's Open Platform, Google Office, Junior Stage and Central Room — evenings on May 27-28 and Jun 1-2 are visible on the public schedule [[26]](https://stationf.co/events).
- **[AI Tinkerers Paris](https://paris.aitinkerers.org/)** ran *Conversational DevOps & AI Infrastructure* on May 21 — next sessions land roughly monthly, demo-only format, free, technical screening [[21]](https://paris.aitinkerers.org/).

If the weekend is flexible, the obvious pivot is **+3 weeks to VivaTech** (Jun 17-20) [[1]](https://vivatech.com/).

## The two headliners

- **[VivaTech](https://vivatech.com/) — Jun 17–20** at [Paris Expo Porte de Versailles](https://www.eubiz.org/events-2026/vivatech-paris-2026.html). 10th anniversary edition [[25]](https://www.jds.fr/paris/foires-et-salons/foires/vivatech-1345494_A). Founders, corporates, investors, public sector all on one floor — closer to a trade-show-meets-keynote-festival than a developer conference. The reason to go is the floor and the after-hours; not the talks. Best paired with a Michelin dinner because the show ends early enough most evenings [[1]](https://vivatech.com/).
- **[Devoxx France](https://www.devoxx.fr/en/) — Apr 22–24** at Palais des Congrès Porte Maillot. 14th edition, 240 talks, ~4,600 attendees, French and English [[3]](https://www.devoxx.fr/en/) [[4]](https://numerique360.banquedesterritoires.fr/evenements/nationaux/devoxx-france-2026/). The seriously-technical alternative to VivaTech — Java leans heaviest but every stack shows up. Hotels near Porte Maillot fill fast.

## Specialist deep-dives (the rest of the year)

Single-day or two-day events, smaller venues, sharper focus — these are where you go to learn, not to network.

- **[dotAI](https://www.dotai.io/) (Sep 17) + [dotJS](https://www.dotjs.io/) (Sep 18)** — back-to-back single-track conferences at the [Folies Bergère](https://www.dotjs.io/) theatre. Curated speaker line-up, no parallel tracks, expensive but legendary among European developers [[11]](https://www.dotai.io/) [[12]](https://www.dotjs.io/) [[13]](https://www.createwith.com/event/paris-dotai-artificial-intelligence-conference-in-europe-sep-2026).
- **[Big Data & AI Paris](https://www.bigdataparis.com/en-gb.html) (Sep 15–16)** — enterprise-data show at Porte de Versailles. 2026 edition repositions on large-org decision-makers; ~15,000 visitors, 180 exhibitors [[14]](https://www.bigdataparis.com/en-gb.html) [[15]](https://10times.com/big-data-paris-france). Pairs naturally with the dotAI/dotJS week immediately after.
- **[Paris Blockchain Week](https://www.parisblockchainweek.com/) (Apr 15–16)** — at [Carrousel du Louvre](https://www.parisblockchainweek.com/), opening VIP dinner at Château de Versailles on Apr 14 [[9]](https://www.parisblockchainweek.com/) [[10]](https://chainwire.org/2026/03/03/paris-blockchain-week-2026-returns-to-bridge-institutions-and-digital-assets/). 2026 positioning is institutions × digital assets, not retail crypto.
- **[Tech Show Paris](https://www.tradefairdates.com/TECH-SHOW-M9860/Paris.html) (Nov 18–19)** — Closer Still's bundle of five expos under one badge: Cloud Expo Europe, Cloud & Cyber Security Expo, Data & AI Leaders Summit, Data Centre World, DevOps Live [[18]](https://www.showsbee.com/fairs/86252-Tech-Show-Paris-2026.html). Vendor-heavy floor, decision-maker audience [[17]](https://www.tradefairdates.com/TECH-SHOW-M9860/Paris.html).
- **[apidays Paris](https://www.apidays.global/events/paris) (Dec 1–3)** — 2026 theme is the *sovereign autonomous API stack* — on-prem agentic AI, EU data residency, operational continuity. Same week as the Hacking APIs sister conference [[16]](https://www.apidays.global/events/paris).
- **[React Paris](https://react.paris/) (Mar 26–27)** — 3rd edition, hybrid (Pullman Montparnasse + online), ~40 speakers, React Server Components / AI / Remix / Typescript [[7]](https://react.paris/) [[8]](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/bejs/1821207).
- **[AndroidMakers](https://androidmakers.droidcon.com/) (Apr 9–10)** — community-built, droidcon family, held at Beffroi de Montrouge [[5]](https://androidmakers.droidcon.com/) [[6]](https://conferenceindex.org/event/android-makers-by-droidcon-2026-april-paris-fr).
- **[GOSIM Paris](https://paris2026.gosim.org/) (May 4–6)** — Station F, open-source × agentic-AI, workshops + hackathons + demos [[22]](https://paris2026.gosim.org/).
- **[Paris Web](https://appel.paris-web.fr/) (Sep 24–26)** — long-running French web/a11y/quality conference at INSEP; two conference days plus workshops [[19]](https://10times.com/paris-web-montrouge) [[20]](https://appel.paris-web.fr/).
- **[ai-PULSE](https://www.ai-pulse.eu/) (Oct 13)** — one-day AI conference at Station F focused on research + product demos [[23]](https://www.ai-pulse.eu/).
- **[leHACK](https://lehack.org/) (Jun 26–28)** — Cité des Sciences. France's main offensive-security conference (continuation of Hack in Paris), 24-hour CTF, listed by France's national cybersecurity agency ANSSI [[27]](https://lehack.org/) [[28]](https://cyber.gouv.fr/agenda-de-lanssi/le-hack-2026/).
