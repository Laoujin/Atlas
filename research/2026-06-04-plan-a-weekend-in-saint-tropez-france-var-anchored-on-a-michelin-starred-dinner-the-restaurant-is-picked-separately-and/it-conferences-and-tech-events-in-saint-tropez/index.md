> **TL;DR** Saint-Tropez has no IT conferences and no conference infrastructure — it is a 6,000-person resort village. The nearest tech events are in Cannes (~1.5 hrs by road) and Sophia Antipolis (~2 hrs). The closest organized tech community is [French Tech Toulon Var](https://www.frenchtechtoulon.fr/) [[6]](https://www.frenchtechtoulon.fr/agenda), ~70 km / ~1 hr away in Toulon.

Road distances go around the Gulf of Saint-Tropez; direct distances are roughly half. The A8 autoroute connects Cannes/Sophia Antipolis; Toulon is reached via the A57.

## French Tech Toulon Var — the Var department's tech ecosystem

The closest organized tech community to Saint-Tropez is [French Tech Toulon Var](https://www.frenchtechtoulon.fr/) [[6]](https://www.frenchtechtoulon.fr/agenda), which renewed its national French Tech label in May 2026 [[7]](https://metropoletpm.fr/actualites/french-tech-toulon-var-un-label-renouvele-une-ambition-affirmee). Its three strategic pillars are embedded & maritime AI, defense innovation, and technological sovereignty. It runs monthly meetups in Toulon open to founders, managers, and engineers.

The **Salon VAR TECH** [[8]](https://www.echosciences-paca.fr/evenements/salon-var-tech-2) in La Farlède (Var department, ~55 km from Saint-Tropez) is the most geographically accessible public tech event. It is free, family-friendly, and covers robotics, cybersecurity, drone piloting, and digital education. The 2026 date has not been announced; the 2024 edition ran in October.

## Why Saint-Tropez has no tech events

Saint-Tropez (pop. ~6,000) has no convention center, no large indoor venues, and an economy entirely oriented toward luxury tourism and hospitality. The Côte d'Azur's tech infrastructure is concentrated in:

- **Sophia Antipolis** (near Valbonne/Antibes) — Europe's largest tech park, home to ETSI and dozens of multinationals
- **Cannes** — Palais des Festivals hosts large congresses (Datacloud, MIPIM, Cannes Lions)
- **Nice / Côte d'Azur** — university tech spinoffs and [French Tech Côte d'Azur](https://www.investincotedazur.com/en/evenements/team-nice-cote-dazur-mobile-world-congress-2026/)
- **Toulon** — French Tech Toulon Var, defense/maritime tech focus

All four hubs are accessible as day trips from Saint-Tropez, though road times via the coast can be long in summer tourist season — Cannes in particular requires going around the Gulf (~100 km despite appearing close on a map).
