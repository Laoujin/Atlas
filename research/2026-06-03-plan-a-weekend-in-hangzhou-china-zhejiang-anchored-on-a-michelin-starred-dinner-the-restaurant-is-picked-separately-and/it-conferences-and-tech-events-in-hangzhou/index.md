## Yunqi Conference (云栖大会) / Apsara Conference

Alibaba Cloud's flagship annual gathering — the defining tech event in Hangzhou and one of China's largest cloud/AI conferences, founded in 2009. [[3]](https://yunqi.aliyun.com/about) [[18]](https://www.chinabankingnews.com/wiki/yunqi-conference/)

| Attribute      | Details                                                                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **When**       | Sep 1, 2026 confirmed; past editions ran 3 days (2024: Sep 19–21 [[6]](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2024-09-21/VHJhbnNjcmlwdDgwOTM4/index.html); 2025: Sep 24–26) |
| **Venue**      | No. 369 Heshan St, Xihu District (Yunqi Town International Convention Center)  |
| **Scale**      | ~60,000 attendees, 238 exhibitors, 40,000 sqm expo, 158 product launches [[2]](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/apsara-conference) |
| **2026 theme** | "Computing, for the value that cannot be computed"                              |
| **Topics**     | Cloud computing, LLMs (Qwen), AI agents, enterprise digital transformation      |
| **Tickets**    | ⚠ Already sold out for 2026; monitor apsaraticket@service.alibaba.com           |

The 2025 edition pivoted from LLM showcases toward practical AI agent deployments, with companies demonstrating production applications on Alibaba's open-source Qwen models. Alibaba CEO announced ¥380B (~$53B) in AI/cloud infrastructure investment. [[1]](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3327089/chinas-full-stack-ai-ambitions-show-alibabas-apsara-conference) [[4]](https://www.alizila.com/exploring-future-ai-5-takeaways-alibaba-cloud-apsara-conference/)

## September Cluster: GDTE + ISSE (Sep 23–27, 2026)

Two large expos co-located at the Hangzhou Grand Convention and Exhibition Center — often the same week as or immediately after Yunqi.

**[Global Digital Trade Expo (GDTE)](https://www.gdte.org.cn/En/)** — China's national digital commerce expo, co-hosted by Zhejiang Province and the Ministry of Commerce. [[7]](https://www.gdte.org.cn/En/)

| Attribute   | Details                                                              |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Topics**  | E-commerce, smart logistics, digital medicine, AI, smart living      |
| **Scale**   | 10,000+ buyers; ¥162B in trade agreements (4th edition, 2025)       |
| **Register**| gdte.org.cn / reg.gdteonline.com                                     |

**[International Smart Space Exhibition (ISSE)](https://international-smart-space-exhibition.hk.messefrankfurt.com/hangzhou/en.html)** — Messe Frankfurt's smart-city and intelligent-building show, 2nd edition co-located with GDTE. [[8]](https://international-smart-space-exhibition.hk.messefrankfurt.com/hangzhou/en.html)

| Attribute   | Details                                                              |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Topics**  | Smart cities, IoT, energy-efficient buildings, AI urban governance   |
| **Organizer**| Messe Frankfurt (HK) Ltd + Hangzhou Expo Group                     |

## World Internet Conference Wuzhen Summit *(day trip)*

Held in Wuzhen — a canal town 74 km from Hangzhou (~1 hour by car or bus). [[11]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Hangzhou/Wuzhen) China's top internet governance forum, annually in early November, organized by the Chinese government. [[12]](https://www.wicinternet.org/) [[13]](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2025-10-17/2025-World-Internet-Conference-Wuzhen-Summit-to-be-held-in-November-1HxXcbtnwdy/p.html)

| Attribute   | Details                                                               |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **When**    | Early November (2025: Nov 6–9; 2026 date unannounced)                |
| **Topics**  | Internet governance, AI policy, digital economy, cybersecurity        |
| **Scale**   | Representatives from 50+ countries; senior officials and CEOs        |
| **Format**  | Invitation-heavy; press and observer credentials available            |

The 2025 summit featured Hangzhou's "Six Little Dragons" startups and announced China's national AI advisory body. [[14]](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/wuzhen-summit-china-creates-ai-093000258.html)

## Hangzhou's Tech Ecosystem

Hangzhou is China's self-styled "City of the Internet," home to Alibaba Group HQ and a dense cluster of AI companies including DeepSeek, NetEase, and DJI software teams. [[17]](https://www.ehangzhou.gov.cn/2026-04/10/c_297382.htm) The **ModelScope Developer Center** (Xihu District, opened late 2025, 10,000+ sqm) hosts regular hackathons and technical seminars — continuous programming for developers year-round, not just major conferences. [[17]](https://www.ehangzhou.gov.cn/2026-04/10/c_297382.htm) ModelScope itself hosts 170,000+ open-source AI models serving 25M+ developers globally.

Recurring community groups on Meetup.com:

- [Hangzhou Apache Spark Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/hangzhou-apache-spark-meetup/) — 1,077 members [[19]](https://www.meetup.com/hangzhou-apache-spark-meetup/)
- [Hangzhou Big Data Developers](https://www.meetup.com/hangzhou-big-data-developers/) — 97 members
- [Unity Hangzhou](https://www.meetup.com/unity-hangzhou/) — 242 members
