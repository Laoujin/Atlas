## Upcoming events — details

### [AI Conf 2026](https://www.aiconf.it/) — Jun 24 [[10]](https://www.aiconf.it/)

Italy's longest-running native AI conference (7th edition). Three tracks: Data Science, AI Technologies, Use Cases — plus an Executive track (Business Pass only) on strategy, governance, and ROI. Speakers include reps from AWS, NVIDIA, Google Cloud, Generali, Sky Italia, and Lamborghini. Sessions recorded and available via Improove Play. **Note: sessions mostly in Italian.**

- Venue: Talent Garden Calabiana, via Arcivescovo Calabiana 6
- Tickets: €99 Conference Pass / €169 Business Pass

---

### [ECIS 2026](https://ecis2026.it/) — Jun 15–17 [[9]](https://ecis2026.it/)

34th European Conference on Information Systems, hosted by Bocconi University and University of Milan. Theme: *"Reimagining Digital Technology for Business, Management, and Society."* Academic focus with tracks on digital transformation, AI in organisations, and sustainable IT. Pre-conference doctoral consortium and workshops run June 12–14.

- Venue: Bocconi University
- Audience: researchers and IS academics; less practitioner-focused than industry conferences

---

### [ServerlessDays Milano](https://www.serverlessdays.io/milano) — Oct 13 [[11]](https://www.serverlessdays.io/milano)

Part of the global non-profit ServerlessDays series. Described as "local, non-profit, accessible and inclusive." Community-driven with regional speakers; free or low-cost by design. Venue TBC — check the official site closer to the date.

---

### [Codemotion Milan 2026](https://conferences.codemotion.com/milan/) — Oct 28–29 [[12]](https://conferences.codemotion.com/milan/) [[13]](https://sessionize.com/codemotion-milan-26/)

The broadest developer conference on the Milan calendar. Explicitly targets mid-to-advanced practitioners — introductory content is filtered out at CFP stage. Four main topic areas: AI/ML, Software Engineering & Architecture, Cloud & DevOps, Cybersecurity. Confirmed speakers from Atlassian, Uber, Nationale-Nederlanden; keynote includes the creators of CSS and JSON. Special CTO Connect track for engineering leaders.

- Venue: Superstudio Più, via Tortona 27
- Tickets: €179 (early) → €499 (door); student discount available
- Two full days; no single-day tickets

---

### [Cloud Day 2026](https://www.cloudday.it/) — Nov 18 [[14]](https://www.cloudday.it/)

8th edition. Vendor-neutral, practitioner-focused cloud conference: 3 tracks, 24 talks, targeting architects, CTOs, and engineering decision-makers. Topics: cloud sovereignty, AI on cloud (cost/scalability/security), real production case studies. Explicitly no "generic demos" or marketing content. Sponsors: beSharp, Reply (Storm Reply), Condense. **Sessions mostly in Italian.**

- Venue: Talent Garden Calabiana, via Arcivescovo Calabiana 6
- Tickets: €49 Super Early Bird (until Sep 12) → €99 regular

## May 2026 cluster — recap

Five major events in 10 days made May the highest-density week of the year:

**[AI Week](https://www.aiweek.it/en/)** [[2]](https://www.aiweek.it/en/) [[3]](https://www.i-scoop.eu/ai-week-milan-2026/) — May 19–20, Fiera Milano Rho. Europe's self-styled largest AI event: 700+ speakers, 17 stages, 25,000+ attendees, 250 exhibitors. Hackathon (AI Agent Olympics), startup pitches. Business Pass was €159 early / €399 final.

**[ETHMilan 2026](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/04/04/ethmilan-returns-for-its-fourth-edition/)** [[5]](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/04/04/ethmilan-returns-for-its-fourth-edition/) — May 21–22, Museo Nazionale della Scienza e della Tecnologia. Italy's largest Web3 conference (4th edition): 2,000+ attendees, 100+ speakers, tracks on DeFi, ZK Proofs, Stablecoins, Agentic Payments, Real World Assets. Students admitted free.

**[Google Cloud AI Live Milano](https://datwave.ai/event/enterprise-intelligence-agentic-ai-cloud-ai-live-milano-2026/)** [[6]](https://datwave.ai/event/enterprise-intelligence-agentic-ai-cloud-ai-live-milano-2026/) — May 21, CityOval. Free, full-day event on agentic AI, MarTech, and cloud architecture. On-demand recordings now available.

**[ItaliaSec 2026](https://italy.cyberseries.io/)** [[7]](https://italy.cyberseries.io/) — May 26–27, Milan Marriott. 10th anniversary; intimate (~150 attendees), exclusive CISO-track event. Speakers from Ferrari, L'Oreal, Barilla, Philip Morris. Sessions in Italian. Executive-level pricing.

**[AWS Summit Milano 2026](https://aws.amazon.com/it/events/summits/milano/)** [[8]](https://aws.amazon.com/it/events/summits/milano/) — May 28, Fiera Milano Rho. Free one-day event; 100+ technical sessions on GenAI, serverless, security, and data analytics. Keynote by Giulia Gasparini (AWS Italy Country Leader). On-demand available.
