> **Decision.** Spend Saturday in Nice itself (Cours Saleya market → Vieux Nice → Castle Hill sunset → JAN at 19:30), give Sunday to **one** day trip — pair Èze Village in the morning with Monaco in the afternoon on bus #602, or take the TER 5 minutes east to Villefranche-sur-Mer + Cap Ferrat for the swim and Villa Ephrussi. If you have a third day, swap in Antibes (Picasso) + Cannes (Lérins ferry). Skip Saint-Paul-de-Vence and the Esterel unless you have a car. ⚠ Saturday is **June 6 = Monaco Grand Prix weekend** (qualifying Saturday, race Sunday June 7) [[76]](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2026/monaco) — every bus and train through Monaco is jammed, all Monaco hotels are full, and bus #600/#602 routings are often diverted. Skip Monaco entirely or do it from a different weekend.

## Why this layout works

Nice has been a UNESCO World Heritage site since 27 July 2021 under the name "Nice, Winter Resort Town of the Riviera" [[1]](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1635/) [[3]](https://www.monaco-tribune.com/en/2021/07/city-of-nice-joins-unesco-world-heritage-sites/) — the 522-hectare zone covers exactly the walkable strip a weekender wants (Promenade des Anglais, Old Town, Port Lympia), so a no-car itinerary works fine. The Promenade des Anglais runs ~7 km from the airport to Quai des États-Unis and was begun in 1820 by Nice's wintering English community [[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promenade_des_Anglais). The TER coastal train runs every ~15 min until 22:00 and reaches every other Riviera town for €2–€8 [[67]](https://www.bestofniceblog.com/transport-in-nice/train/) [[68]](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/en/practical-information/getting-around/regional-trains/), so day trips don't need a car. Lonely Planet's standard play is to fly in Thursday and use Friday–Sunday [[12]](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/ultimate-weekend-in-nice).

**The Èze + Monaco classic**, slightly more detail: bus #82 *or* #602 from Vauban station, alternating roughly hourly — they share the Vauban–Èze Village–Monaco corridor, while **bus #600 goes to seaside Èze-sur-Mer, not the hilltop**, so avoid it for Èze [[13]](https://www.bestofniceblog.com/transport-in-nice/buses-in-nice/bus-from-nice-to-eze/). Èze in the morning beats the cruise-bus crush, then Monaco in the afternoon as the cruise passengers re-board [[14]](https://www.frenchrivieratraveller.com/Nice/day-trip-eze-monaco.html). On Sunday June 7 specifically: **don't try Monaco** — it's the GP race itself.

**Saint-Paul-de-Vence note:** the old #400 direct bus is dead. The current route is TER to Cagnes-sur-Mer (15 min, €3.80) then Zou bus #655 to the Saint-Paul Village stop (30 min, €2.10), total ~€5.90 each way [[20]](https://niceandbeyond.com/get-from-nice-to-saint-paul-de-vence/). Alternative: tram T2 → Parc Phoenix → bus #9 → Vence → bus #655 (~1 h) [[37]](https://www.frenchrivieratraveller.com/Transport/getting-to-saint-paul.html). The Fondation Maeght stop is *before* the village — flag the driver, and check the summer **Maeght-Matisse shuttle** (1 Jul–30 Sept only, Tue–Sat, 5×/day) linking the Foundation, Saint-Paul and Matisse's Rosary Chapel in Vence [[21]](https://www.bestofniceblog.com/day-trips-from-nice/day-trip-to-vence-and-saint-paul-de-vence/) — it's not running yet in early June.

## Eating Niçoise (and where, beyond JAN)

### What to order

Niçoise cuisine has its own short, defensible vocabulary [[40]](https://fromparistoprovence.com/cuisine-nicoise/):

- **Socca** — wood-fired chickpea galette; pure, hot, cheap.
- **Pissaladière** — caramelised onion + anchovy + Niçois-olive tart on thin crust. **No cheese.**
- **Pan-bagnat** — round bread soaked with the *salade niçoise* fixings; takeaway, single portion.
- **Salade niçoise (the real one)** — raw vegetables + hard-boiled egg + tuna/anchovy. **No potato. No green beans.** Jacques Médecin codified this in 1972 [[41]](https://www.pardonyourfrench.com/authentic-nicoise-salad/); anything else is a tourist remix.
- **Petits farcis niçois** — small stuffed vegetables (courgette, tomato, onion, bell pepper).
- **Daube niçoise** — slow-braised beef stew with red wine and orange peel.
- **Tourte de blettes** — chard tart with raisins and pine nuts. Sweet or savoury — the official Nice tourism board flags it as the test case for Niçoise vegetables-as-dessert weirdness [[48]](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/en/explore/art-of-living/gastronomy-and-local-produce/nicoise-recipes/tourte-de-blettes/).

## Beaches and outdoor

### Walks and hikes worth a half-day

- **Sentier littoral du Cap Ferrat** — 6–7 km loop, half-day, rocky and exposed in places despite "easy" billing [[52]](https://franceadventurer.com/hiking-sentier-du-littoral-cap-ferrat/); Paloma sits at the most picturesque eastern end [[53]](https://www.characrosstheworld.com/sentier-littoral-cap-ferrat/).
- **Cap d'Ail → Monaco coastal path** — ~3.6 km, ~1.5 h end-to-end from Marquet Beach via Plage de la Mala to Monte Carlo [[64]](https://villa-valbonne.nl/en/valbonne-cote-dazur/walking-trail-cap-dail-to-monaco-about-1-5-hours-with-restaurants/) (Sunday June 7: this delivers you straight into the GP — don't).
- **Mont Boron–Mont Alban** — ~2 h 50 min loop above Nice with panoramas from Cap Ferrat to the Esterel [[65]](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/france/alpes-maritimes/mont-boron-mont-alban).
- **Mercantour day hike** — 90 min to 2 h from Nice by car; Vallée des Merveilles holds 40,000 Bronze-Age rock carvings but the protected zone is **only accessible with an accredited guide** [[57]](https://tourismattractions.net/france/hiking-trails-in-the-mercantour-from-nice-2026) [[58]](https://www.explore-share.com/trip/valley-of-marvels-hiking-day-tour-mercantour-national-park/).
- **Esterel massif** — volcanic red cliffs between Saint-Raphaël and Cannes, Mont Vinaigre 618 m [[59]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massif_de_l%27Esterel); best done as the 30 km Corniche d'Or drive [[60]](https://frenchmoments.eu/esterel-corniche-road/) or by boat from Saint-Raphaël/Agay for the hidden calanques [[61]](https://exploretheriviera.com/nice-esterel-massif/). Too far for a Nice-based weekend without a car.
- **Marseille Calanques** — skip from Nice. Many hours west [[66]](https://travelinginreallife.com/best-day-trip-from-marseille-calanques/).

**Lérins Islands ferry from Cannes**: every ~30 min from Quai Laubeuf, 15-min crossing, Sainte-Marguerite round-trip from €16 / Saint-Honorat €20.50 online [[19]](https://en.cannes-france.com/discover-visit/lerins-islands/lerins-island-guide/) [[62]](https://en.cannes-france.com/discover-visit/lerins-islands/book-a-boat-trip/).

- **#82 / #602** from Vauban to **Èze Village** (~30 min, hourly alternating); **#600 ≠ Èze Village** (it serves Èze-sur-Mer at sea level) [[13]](https://www.bestofniceblog.com/transport-in-nice/buses-in-nice/bus-from-nice-to-eze/).
- **#600** Nice → Villefranche → Beaulieu → Èze-sur-Mer → Cap-d'Ail → Monaco → Roquebrune → Menton, every 15 min weekdays, ~1 h end-to-end [[72]](https://www.monaco-tribune.com/2026/02/ligne-de-bus-600-nice-monaco-menton-plans-horaires-tarifs/).

**Bike share:** Vélo Bleu is dead; ~2,000 free-floating bikes from **Pony** (blue, e-bike) and **Lime** (green) [[82]](https://www.frenchrivieratraveller.com/Nice/Transport/Velo-Bleu.html). Useful for the Promenade.

### Where to stay

- **Vieux Nice (Old Town):** from ~$160/night [[80]](https://goaskalocal.com/blog/where-to-stay-in-nice). Walk to JAN; bring earplugs.
- **Carré d'Or** (Jean Médecin / Bd Victor Hugo / Bd Gambetta / Promenade rectangle): the boutique-hotel band, central, quieter than the Old Town [[80]](https://goaskalocal.com/blog/where-to-stay-in-nice).
- **Promenade des Anglais:** ~17% below city average ($143) — the older grande-dame stock plus business hotels [[80]](https://goaskalocal.com/blog/where-to-stay-in-nice).

## Weather, crowds, calendar

- **June** delivers 24–27 °C days, 17 °C nights, 23 °C sea, ~12 h sunshine; rain ~20–30 mm across 4 days [[63]](https://provencelovers.fr/en/cote-azur-june-weather-temperatures/) [[75]](https://gotripzi.com/destinations/nice-fr/weather/june). Markedly less crowded than July–August peak.
- **2026 event calendar** (affects prices and crowds):
  - Nice Carnival — 11 Feb–28 Feb 2026 [[79]](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/en/news/the-programme-for-the-2026-nice-carnival-long-live-the-queen-has-been-unveiled/)
  - Cannes Film Festival — 12–23 May 2026 [[77]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Cannes_Film_Festival)
  - **Monaco Grand Prix — 5–7 June 2026** [[76]](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2026/monaco) ⚠
  - Jazz à Juan (Juan-les-Pins) — 9–19 July 2026 [[78]](https://www.rivieraedition.com/en/jazz-a-juan-2026-dates-and-line-up-announced-featuring-tom-jones-seal-and-more/)
  - Nice Jazz Festival — 23–26 July 2026 [[78]](https://www.rivieraedition.com/en/jazz-a-juan-2026-dates-and-line-up-announced-featuring-tom-jones-seal-and-more/)
- **Safety:** the city is safe; the real risk is pickpockets working the Promenade, Old Town markets, and trams — often groups using a ticket-machine "help" gambit [[81]](https://www.bestofniceblog.com/practical-information/crime-and-scams-in-nice/).

## What to skip

- **MAMAC** — closed through ~2028 [[28]](https://www.mamac-nice.org/en/).
- **Calanques National Park** — too far west to fold into a Nice weekend [[66]](https://travelinginreallife.com/best-day-trip-from-marseille-calanques/).
- **Monaco on June 7, 2026** — GP raceday [[76]](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2026/monaco).
- **"Salade niçoise" anywhere with potato or green beans** — that's a Parisian remix [[41]](https://www.pardonyourfrench.com/authentic-nicoise-salad/).
- **Bus #600 to "Èze"** — wrong Èze [[13]](https://www.bestofniceblog.com/transport-in-nice/buses-in-nice/bus-from-nice-to-eze/).
