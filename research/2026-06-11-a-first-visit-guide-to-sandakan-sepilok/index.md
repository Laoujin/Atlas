> **TL;DR:** Sandakan & Sepilok is a **wildlife basecamp with a war-port soul** — the reason to come is the **Sepilok trio** (orangutan rehabilitation centre, the adjoining Bornean Sun Bear centre, and the Rainforest Discovery Centre canopy walk), all sharing one gate at Batu 14 [[1]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/sepilok-orangutan-rehabilitation-centre/)[[2]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/bornean-sun-bear-conservation-centre/)[[3]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/rainforest-discovery-centre-rdc/), and the **Kinabatangan river overnight** — dawn and night cruises for pygmy elephants, proboscis monkeys and hornbills that simply can't be done as a day-trip [[4]](https://girlonazebra.com/kinabatangan-river-cruise/). Round it out with the poignant **Sandakan Memorial Park / Death March** history [[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandakan_Memorial_Park) and, if your dates suit, an overnight on **Selingan Turtle Island** [[7]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/selingan-turtle-island/). Come **March–September** (driest; seas and rivers workable) and avoid the NE monsoon **Nov–Jan**, which can cancel island boats [[12]](https://sabahguide.com/places/districts/sandakan/best-time-to-visit/).

## Best season & practical flags

**When.** East-coast Sabah is at its best **March–September** — the sweet spot is **April–June** (drier, fewer crowds, lower lodge surcharges), while **August–September** adds peak **turtle nesting** at Selingan [[7]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/selingan-turtle-island/)[[12]](https://sabahguide.com/places/districts/sandakan/best-time-to-visit/). The NE monsoon (**Nov–Jan**) brings rough seas that can scrub the Turtle Island and island crossings and a wetter jungle [[12]](https://sabahguide.com/places/districts/sandakan/best-time-to-visit/). Note **June and September** carry Kinabatangan peak surcharges. Sabah-wide **Kaamatan** (Harvest Festival, **30–31 May 2026**) is worth knowing about, but its grand finale is near Kota Kinabalu, not Sandakan [[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaamatan).

**Money & logistics.** Prices across these guides are in ringgit (RM) at **€1 ≈ RM 4.70** (June 2026) [[24]](https://www.exchange-rates.org/converter/myr-eur); budget roughly **€60–110/day** before the big-ticket items — the Kinabatangan and Selingan **overnight packages** and the Sepilok entry fees are where the money goes [[5]](https://girlonazebra.com/where-to-stay-kinabatangan-river/). Get a SIM at the airport. **Grab is thin out in Sepilok** — use the local bus #14 or a pre-arranged lodge transfer for the 25 km from town [[14]](https://sabahtourism.com/article/how-to-get-from-sandakan-airport-to-sepilok/). Carry a **reusable bag**: Sabah Parks enforce a single-use-plastic ban on the islands [[7]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/selingan-turtle-island/).

## A suggested ~3-night plan

**Day 1 — arrive from Kota Kinabalu, town heritage & sunset.** Take the **45-minute flight BKI→Sandakan**, not the 6-hour mountain bus [[13]](https://www.airasia.com/flights/from-kota-kinabalu-bki-to-sandakan-sdk/), then transfer toward Sepilok or into town [[14]](https://sabahtourism.com/article/how-to-get-from-sandakan-airport-to-sepilok/). Walk the **Sandakan Heritage Trail** — the 100 Steps, St Michael's, and **Agnes Keith House** with its "Land Below the Wind" story [[10]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/sandakan-heritage-trail/)[[11]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/agnes-keith-house/) — and time **colonial high tea or sunset at the English Tea House** [[17]](https://www.englishteahouse.org/), or the harbour view from the hilltop **Puu Jih Shih Temple** [[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puu_Jih_Shih_Temple). Dinner: charcoal seafood out in the **Sim Sim** stilt village [[19]](https://harbourmallsandakan.com/2025/01/08/the-sea-garden-sim-sim/), with the accidental **UFO tart** for dessert [[18]](https://danielfooddiary.com/2025/11/24/sabahsandakanufotart/).

**Day 2 — the Sepilok wildlife day.** Base out at Sepilok and do the three Batu-14 centres as one block: the **orangutan** feeding platform [[1]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/sepilok-orangutan-rehabilitation-centre/), the **Bornean Sun Bear** centre next door [[2]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/bornean-sun-bear-conservation-centre/), and the **Rainforest Discovery Centre** canopy walk and birding [[3]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/rainforest-discovery-centre-rdc/). Spend the afternoon with the cliff-top **Labuk Bay proboscis monkeys** [[8]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/labuk-bay-proboscis-monkey-sanctuary/) or back in town at **Sandakan Memorial Park** for the Death March history [[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandakan_Memorial_Park).

**Day 3 — Kinabatangan river overnight (the headline).** Transfer to a **Sukau/Bilit river lodge** for afternoon, dawn and night cruises — Borneo's best-odds wildlife watching [[4]](https://girlonazebra.com/kinabatangan-river-cruise/)[[5]](https://girlonazebra.com/where-to-stay-kinabatangan-river/)[[22]](https://www.borneonaturelodge.com.my/). En route, the **Gomantong Caves** bat-and-swiftlet spectacle is a worthwhile (if pungent) stop [[6]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/gomantong-cave/). This is an **overnight, not a day-trip** — that's the whole point.

**Day 4 — turtles or onward.** If the season fits and you booked ahead, swap a night for an **overnight on Selingan Turtle Island** (~50 permits/night) [[7]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/selingan-turtle-island/); otherwise fly **onward SDK→KL via KK** for the next leg [[15]](https://www.airpaz.com/en/flight-tickets/city-city/sandakan-my-kuala-lumpur-my). Offbeat alternative: a **Pulau Berhala** day-trip for its leper-colony-and-POW-escape history [[21]](https://1stopborneoguide.com/berhala-island/).

**Where to sleep it all from.** Match the base to the night: a **Sepilok jungle lodge** like Sepilok Nature Resort for the wildlife days [[23]](https://dive-malaysia.com/sepilok-nature-resort/), a **Kinabatangan river lodge** for the overnight [[22]](https://www.borneonaturelodge.com.my/), and only a night or two of **Sandakan town heritage** — the in-town stock is thin on character beyond the English Tea House grounds [[17]](https://www.englishteahouse.org/).

**Cautions & footer.** The two signature experiences — **Kinabatangan and Selingan — are overnights**, and Selingan and the river lodges sell out, so **book weeks ahead** [[4]](https://girlonazebra.com/kinabatangan-river-cruise/)[[5]](https://girlonazebra.com/where-to-stay-kinabatangan-river/)[[7]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/selingan-turtle-island/). Time boats for **Mar–Sep**; the NE monsoon (Nov–Jan) can cancel crossings [[12]](https://sabahguide.com/places/districts/sandakan/best-time-to-visit/). On responsible travel: Sepilok and Labuk Bay are **managed feeding sites**, and the most ethical river operators watch from the boat rather than baiting — choose accordingly [[1]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/sepilok-orangutan-rehabilitation-centre/)[[8]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/labuk-bay-proboscis-monkey-sanctuary/).
