> **TL;DR** — Base in **Sepilok** (the wildlife cluster) and dip into **Sandakan town** (20-25 km / ~30 min) for heritage. The four unmissables: **Sepilok Orangutan Rehabilitation Centre** [[16]](https://ecotourmalaysia.com/best-of-sepilok-orangutan-centre/), the **Bornean Sun Bear Centre** next door [[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bornean_Sun_Bear_Conservation_Centre), the **Sandakan Memorial Park** (Death March) [[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandakan_Memorial_Park), and an overnight **Kinabatangan** river safari [[31]](https://girlonazebra.com/kinabatangan-river-cruise/). Best window: **late March-May or July-September** (drier east coast); **avoid Nov-Dec/Jan** NE-monsoon rains [[35]](https://sabahguide.com/places/districts/sandakan/best-time-to-visit/)[[36]](https://www.clladventureborneo.com/best-time-to-travel-to-sabah/). **Book ahead now** for Selingan turtle island, Kinabatangan lodges and Lankayan — they sell out [[26]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/selingan-turtle-island/)[[27]](https://thefamilyfreestylers.com/turtle-island-borneo-the-ultimate-guide-to-visiting-palau-selingan/).

## When to go — the weather window

The east coast (Sandakan, Sepilok, Kinabatangan) is wettest under the **Northeast monsoon, Oct-Feb** [[36]](https://www.clladventureborneo.com/best-time-to-travel-to-sabah/). Drier, calmer months are **March-September** [[35]](https://sabahguide.com/places/districts/sandakan/best-time-to-visit/)[[37]](https://www.responsibletravel.com/holidays/sabah/travel-guide/best-time-to-visit-sabah). Trade-offs for an undecided date:

| Window | Pros | ⚠ Traps |
|---|---|---|
| **Late Mar-May** | Drier, fewer crowds, lower lodge rates; good all-round | Heat & humidity building; pre-peak [[35]](https://sabahguide.com/places/districts/sandakan/best-time-to-visit/) |
| **Jul-Sep** | Peak turtle nesting (Jul-Oct), calm seas for islands/diving | Malaysian school holidays → rates up 30-50%, crowds; Kinabatangan peak surcharge applies Jun & Sep 2026 [[26]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/selingan-turtle-island/)[[32]](https://www.amazingborneo.com/package/sandakan-gomantong-cave-kinabatangan-river-cruise) |
| **Nov-Jan** | Cheapest, quietest | Heaviest rain, rough sea crossings, some island resorts close [[36]](https://www.clladventureborneo.com/best-time-to-travel-to-sabah/) |

Sweet spot for a first visit wanting both wildlife and calm seas: **late August-September**, or **April-May** if you'd rather dodge the school-holiday crowds.

## Sandakan town — the Heritage Trail

A self-guided **1.5-3 h walk** linking restored colonial buildings and old places of worship [[1]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/sandakan-heritage-trail/)[[2]](https://milas.travel/sandakan-city-heritage-trail-self-guided-walking-tour/). Do it **early morning or after 3pm** — midday is brutal [[2]](https://milas.travel/sandakan-city-heritage-trail-self-guided-walking-tour/). Rough order: Jamik Mosque → William Pryer monument → **100 Steps** → Agnes Keith House → Goddess of Mercy Temple → St Michael's → Sam Sing Kung [[2]](https://milas.travel/sandakan-city-heritage-trail-self-guided-walking-tour/).

- **[Agnes Keith House](https://museum.sabah.gov.my/exhibition/72)** — the headline stop. "Newlands", former home of American author **Agnes Newton Keith**, who wrote *Land Below the Wind* (1939) here; the timber house was rebuilt post-war (1946/47) and opened as a museum 26 Apr 2004 [[6]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/agnes-keith-house/)[[44]](https://museum.sabah.gov.my/exhibition/72). Open daily **9am-5pm**, non-Malaysian entry **RM15 (~€3.20)** [[7]](https://hellosabah.com/agnes-keith-house-a-colonial-era-treasure-in-sandakan-you-cant-miss/). Pair it with the colonial-style **[English Tea House](https://www.englishteahouse.org/sandakan_agnes_keith.html)** next door — scones and a croquet lawn over the bay [[8]](https://www.englishteahouse.org/sandakan_agnes_keith.html).
- **The 100 Steps** — the staircase climbing from the lower town to the colonial hilltop, ending in a town-and-bay view [[45]](https://www.clladventureborneo.com/sandakan-heritage-trail/).
- **St Michael's & All Angels Church** — Gothic, and one of *very few stone buildings in all of Sabah*; construction begun **1893** [[3]](https://www.malaysia-traveller.com/sandakan-heritage-trail.html).
- **Sam Sing Kung (Three Saints) Temple** — built **1887**, Sandakan's third-oldest temple (the Goddess of Mercy Temple, 1868, is the oldest) [[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Sing_Kung_Temple). Its bronze bell was donated by Fung Ming Shan, Sandakan's first Kapitan Cina, the same year [[5]](https://www.asiatimes.com.my/2025/06/12/sandakans-138-year-old-sam-sing-kung-temple-steeped-in-heritage-and-memory/).

### Sandakan Memorial Park — the Death March

The single most affecting sight. Built on the grounds of the WWII Japanese POW camp: **2,434 Allied prisoners died** and only **six Australians survived** the marches and captivity [[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandakan_Memorial_Park). More than 1,000 men were force-marched **260 km to Ranau**, about half dying en route [[11]](https://www.burwood.nsw.gov.au/Our-Council/Our-Burwood/Burwood-Annual-Memorial-Services/The-Sandakan-Death-Marches/The-history-of-the-Sandakan-Death-Marches). **[Sandakan Memorial Park](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/sandakan-memorial-park/)** is free, open daily, with a Commemorative Pavilion (opened 1999) and bilingual exhibition; the annual memorial service is **15 August** [[10]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/sandakan-memorial-park/).

### Puu Jih Shih Temple — the harbour-view sunset

Hilltop Buddhist temple at Tanah Merah, ~4 km west of the centre and the town's largest Chinese temple (built 1987), with a seven-tier pagoda [[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puu_Jih_Shih_Temple). Go **late afternoon** for commanding views over Sandakan Bay and the Sulu Sea — one of the best sunset spots in town [[13]](https://www.mysabah.com/puu-jih/). Needs a Grab/taxi.

### Waterfront, Central Market & Sim Sim

Lower-key and local. **Sandakan Central Market** and the stilt-village **Buli Sim Sim** are where to eat grilled seafood cheaply [[15]](https://www.klook.com/en-IN/destination/c485-sandakan/). Sim Sim — plank walkways over the water — marks the **site of the original 1879 town** and has floating seafood restaurants [[14]](https://sandakan.rainforestlodge.com/pages/buli-sim-sim-water-village). Offbeat; skip if time-poor.

## Sepilok — the wildlife cluster

Three sights within walking distance of each other, 23-25 km from Sandakan [[20]](https://www.rainforestdiscoverycentre.org/canopy-walkway). This is where to base yourself.

- **[Sepilok Orangutan Rehabilitation Centre](https://www.orangutan-appeal.org.uk/about-us/sepilok-orangutan-rehabilitation-centre)** — *the* Borneo icon. Feeding-platform sessions at **10:00 and 15:00**; non-Malaysian entry **RM30 (~€6.40)** plus a **RM10 camera fee**; one ticket covers both feeds [[16]](https://ecotourmalaysia.com/best-of-sepilok-orangutan-centre/). ⚠ Orangutans are wild and free-ranging — sightings are **never guaranteed**, least likely during fruiting season or rain, so the morning feed and the boardwalks improve your odds [[17]](https://www.orangutan-appeal.org.uk/about-us/sepilok-orangutan-rehabilitation-centre).
- **[Bornean Sun Bear Conservation Centre (BSBCC)](https://www.bsbcc.org.my/visit-us)** — the **world's only** sun-bear sanctuary, right beside the orangutan centre in the Kabili-Sepilok reserve [[18]](https://www.bsbcc.org.my/visit-us)[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bornean_Sun_Bear_Conservation_Centre). Combine it with the orangutan visit on one ticket-run; book online to skip queues [[42]](https://us.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/sepilok/bornean-sun-bear-conservation-centre-28741080/).
- **[Rainforest Discovery Centre (RDC)](https://www.rainforestdiscoverycentre.org/canopy-walkway)** — a **620 m canopy skywalk** (longest in Sabah) up to 27 m high [[20]](https://www.rainforestdiscoverycentre.org/canopy-walkway). Open **8am-5pm**, non-resident adult **RM30**; **night walks RM50 (~€10.60)**, bookable ahead — the best low-effort jungle/birding add-on [[21]](https://girlonazebra.com/rainforest-discovery-center-sepilok/).
- **[Labuk Bay Proboscis Monkey Sanctuary](https://www.proboscis.cc/visit)** — short drive from Sepilok; near-guaranteed close views of the big-nosed proboscis monkeys at feeds: **Platform A 9:30 & 14:30, Platform B 11:30 & 16:30** [[22]](https://www.proboscis.cc/visit). Adult **RM60 (~€12.80)** + RM10 photo fee [[23]](https://www.proboscis.cc/buytickets). Touristy and the monkeys are habituated, but reliable.

## Wilderness icons — day-trip / overnight

### Kinabatangan River — the landscape must-do

Borneo's wildlife river: do it as an **overnight lodge stay**, not a rushed day-trip, to catch the dusk and dawn cruises and night walks where the wildlife is [[31]](https://girlonazebra.com/kinabatangan-river-cruise/). Lodges run **2-night packages with ~4 cruises** plus night/dawn safaris — e.g. Nature Lodge Kinabatangan, and the upmarket **Sukau Rainforest Lodge** [[43]](https://www.mamholidaysltd.com/tours/top-kinabatangan-wildlife-river-cruise-tour-from-sandakan). ⚠ Book lodges well ahead; a **peak surcharge applies in June and September 2026** [[32]](https://www.amazingborneo.com/package/sandakan-gomantong-cave-kinabatangan-river-cruise). Best wildlife corridor along the lower river is around **Sukau/Bilit**.

### Gomantong Caves

Pair with the Kinabatangan en route. Sabah's **largest edible-bird's-nest cave** — WWF-rated "best managed" — with the cathedral-like Simud Hitam chamber (ceilings to 90 m) [[29]](https://www.etawau.com/Geography/Sabah/5_TawauDivision/LahadDatu/Gomantong_Caves.htm). ~1.5 h drive from Sandakan; at dusk **~2 million bats** pour out [[30]](https://sandakan.rainforestlodge.com/pages/gomantong-cave). ⚠ Not for the squeamish — guano, cockroaches, smell. Nest-harvesting seasons: Feb-Apr and Jul-Sep [[29]](https://www.etawau.com/Geography/Sabah/5_TawauDivision/LahadDatu/Gomantong_Caves.htm).

### Selingan — Turtle Islands Park

Bucket-list. **Turtle Islands Park** is 40 km north of Sandakan (Selingan, Gulisan, Bakungan Kechil); green and hawksbill turtles nest here [[24]](https://www.sabahparks.org.my/turtle-islands-park)[[41]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulau_Selingan). Turtles come ashore **nightly, year-round**, so an **overnight stay** all-but-guarantees seeing a nesting female, the egg-collection and a hatchling release [[25]](https://borneoadventure.com/destinations/selingan-island/). ⚠ Capacity is **~50 visitors per night** and it sells out — even off-peak April tours fill; book as far ahead as you can via the Sabah Parks concessionaire (**[Selingan/Crystal Quest](https://www.selinganturtleisland.com/)**) [[26]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/selingan-turtle-island/)[[27]](https://thefamilyfreestylers.com/turtle-island-borneo-the-ultimate-guide-to-visiting-palau-selingan/)[[28]](https://www.selinganturtleisland.com/). Peak nesting Jul-Oct, but that's also when it's hardest to get a bed [[26]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/selingan-turtle-island/).

### Lankayan Island — diving side-note (flagged)

For divers only. **[Lankayan](https://www.sipadan.com/Lankayan.php)** is a luxury one-resort private island ~90 min by boat, with beach and over-water chalets and house-reef diving [[33]](https://www.sipadan.com/Lankayan.php). ⚠ Boat transfers run **daily only in peak (Jul-Sep)**, otherwise 3×/week, so it dictates your itinerary; multi-day dive packages, premium pricing [[34]](https://www.borneodream.com/product/lankayan-island-package/). Not a casual beach day — commit several nights or skip.

## Getting there & around

Fly into Sandakan (SDK): ~**2h50 from Kuala Lumpur** or ~**45 min from Kota Kinabalu** (Firefly) [[38]](https://girlonazebra.com/kota-kinabalu-to-sandakan/). Sepilok is ~25 km from town — local bus **RM4 (~45 min)** or Grab/taxi **~RM50 (~€10.60)** [[39]](https://asocialnomad.com/malaysia/sandakan-to-sepilok/)[[40]](https://sabahtourism.com/article/how-to-get-from-sandakan-airport-to-sepilok/). With a comfortable budget, base in a character Sepilok lodge near the orangutan centre and Grab into town for heritage and sunset at Puu Jih Shih.
