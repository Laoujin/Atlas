> **TL;DR.** Fly KK→Sandakan (~45 min, ~RM110/€24) — skip the 6-9 h winding coach unless you want the scenery [[3]](https://girlonazebra.com/kota-kinabalu-to-sandakan/)[[5]](https://www.airpaz.com/en/airlines/AK-AirAsia/city-city/kota-kinabalu-my-sandakan-my). **Base in Sepilok, not Sandakan town** — the wildlife trio (orangutans, sun bears, RDC) is walkable from the lodges, and you only need town for a half-day heritage walk [[15]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/sepilok-orangutan-rehabilitation-centre/). Get around by Grab; no car hire needed [[9]](https://asocialnomad.com/malaysia/sandakan-to-sepilok/). The real day-trip is **Labuk Bay proboscis monkeys**; ⚠ **Kinabatangan and Turtle Island are overnights, not day-trips** — don't try to compress them [[18]](https://www.amazingborneo.com/package/2d1n-borneo-natural-sukau-bilit-resort-kinabatangan-river-cruises-budget)[[13]](https://www.amazingborneo.com/package/2d1n-sandakan-selingan-turtle-island-escape).

June 2026: €1 ≈ RM 4.70. Prices are indicative; book flights early for the best fares.

## Getting in: Kota Kinabalu → Sandakan

| Mode | Time | Approx fare | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flight** ([AirAsia](https://www.airasia.com/flights/from-kota-kinabalu-bki-to-sandakan-sdk/) / [AirBorneo/MASwings](https://www.maswings.com.my/)) | ~45-50 min | RM110-150 / €24-32; sale fares from ~RM70 / €15 | Easiest, several daily; ⚠ book ahead [[6]](https://www.airasia.com/flights/from-kota-kinabalu-bki-to-sandakan-sdk/)[[5]](https://www.airpaz.com/en/airlines/AK-AirAsia/city-city/kota-kinabalu-my-sandakan-my)[[3]](https://girlonazebra.com/kota-kinabalu-to-sandakan/) |
| **Coach** (Sida / Tung Ma / Amin Jaya) | 5h30-7h30 scheduled, ~9-10 h door-to-door | RM43-50 / €9-11 | Departs **Inanam terminal** (north KK); ~14/day; ⚠ very winding road, motion-sickness risk; book on Easybook [[1]](https://www.checkmybus.co.uk/kota-kinabalu/sandakan)[[2]](https://www.easybook.com/bus/booking/kotakinabalu-to-sandakan)[[3]](https://girlonazebra.com/kota-kinabalu-to-sandakan/) |
| **Self-drive** | ~6 h | car hire + fuel | Pan-Borneo road; only if you want stops [[4]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Kota-Kinabalu/Sandakan) |

**Verdict:** the flight wins on every axis except cost-by-a-few-euros. Take it.

## Getting out: onward from Sandakan

[Sandakan Airport (SDK)](https://sabahtourism.com/article/how-to-get-from-sandakan-airport-to-sepilok/) is small and domestic, ~13-15 km from town and ~16 km / 13-15 min from Sepilok — handy if you base in Sepilok [[26]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sandakan-Airport-SDK/Sepilok)[[25]](https://sabahtourism.com/article/how-to-get-from-sandakan-airport-to-sepilok/).

| Destination | Routing | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kuching** (Sarawak) | No direct; connect via **KK** or **KUL** | half-day with layover | SDK→KUL from ~US$31, then KUL→Kuching; or SDK→KK→Kuching [[27]](https://www.airpaz.com/en/flight-tickets/city-city/sandakan-my-kuala-lumpur-my) |
| **Kuala Lumpur** | Direct AirAsia | ~2h45 | Frequent; the main hub out of Sabah's east coast [[27]](https://www.airpaz.com/en/flight-tickets/city-city/sandakan-my-kuala-lumpur-my) |
| **Kota Kinabalu** | Direct AirAsia / MASwings | ~45 min | Reverse of the inbound hop [[6]](https://www.airasia.com/flights/from-kota-kinabalu-bki-to-sandakan-sdk/)[[7]](https://www.maswings.com.my/) |
| **Semporna / Tawau** (divers → Sipadan) | Overland coach | ~5-6 h direct (or break at Tawau) | Private car ~4h15; minivan Semporna↔Tawau ~1.5 h [[19]](https://malaysiauncovered.com/sandakan-to-semporna/)[[20]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sandakan/Semporna) |

## Getting around locally

**Base in Sepilok (Batu 14), not Sandakan town.** The orangutan centre, sun bears and RDC are clustered there and walkable between lodges; town is a 25-26 km / ~25 min hop away and only needed for the heritage half-day [[8]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sandakan/Sepilok)[[15]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/sepilok-orangutan-rehabilitation-centre/).

| Hop | Mode | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sandakan town ↔ Sepilok | Local **bus #14** | RM4 / €0.85 | ~45 min, ~4 departures/day — infrequent [[9]](https://asocialnomad.com/malaysia/sandakan-to-sepilok/) |
| Sandakan town ↔ Sepilok | **Grab** | ~RM25 / €5 | Easiest; ~50% cheaper than street taxi [[9]](https://asocialnomad.com/malaysia/sandakan-to-sepilok/) |
| SDK airport → Sepilok | Taxi | ~RM40 / €8.50 | ~15 min [[24]](https://www.travelfish.org/transport/malaysia/sabah/sabah/sandakan/all)[[26]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sandakan-Airport-SDK/Sepilok) |
| SDK airport → town | Taxi | ~RM30 / €6.40 | Fixed-ish coupon fares [[24]](https://www.travelfish.org/transport/malaysia/sabah/sabah/sandakan/all) |

**Car hire?** Not needed. Grab covers town, Sepilok and the airport; day-trips and overnights are best done by tour transfer. Self-drive only adds value for Labuk Bay + Gomantong on your own schedule [[9]](https://asocialnomad.com/malaysia/sandakan-to-sepilok/)[[10]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sandakan/Labuk-Bay-Proboscis-Monkey-Sanctuary).

### How to sequence it

- **Day 1 (Sepilok base):** orangutan AM feeding → sun bears → RDC canopy → afternoon orangutan feeding. All on foot [[15]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/sepilok-orangutan-rehabilitation-centre/)[[17]](https://www.bsbcc.org.my/visit-us)[[16]](https://www.rainforestdiscoverycentre.org/).
- **Day 2:** Labuk Bay (half-day, time it for a feeding) [[11]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/labuk-bay-proboscis-monkey-sanctuary/).
- **Add an overnight:** Kinabatangan 2D1N for wildlife, or Selingan for turtles — pick one, both if time allows [[18]](https://www.amazingborneo.com/package/2d1n-borneo-natural-sukau-bilit-resort-kinabatangan-river-cruises-budget)[[13]](https://www.amazingborneo.com/package/2d1n-sandakan-selingan-turtle-island-escape).
- **Half-day filler:** Sandakan heritage trail before flying onward [[21]](https://sabahtourism.com/destination/sandakan-heritage-trail/).
