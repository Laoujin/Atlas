> **TL;DR** Larrabetzu is a village of ~1,900 people with no conference infrastructure — don't go there for a tech event. The nearest tech hub is [Zamudio/Derio Technology Park](https://parke.eus/en/campus/zamudio-derio/) [[1]](https://parke.eus/en/campus/zamudio-derio/) (5 km away, Spain's first tech park, founded 1985). Bilbao (~20 km) hosts the region's main events: [BilboStack](https://bilbostack.com/en/) (developers, January), [Euskal Encounter](https://ee26.euskalencounter.org/es/index.html) (LAN/gaming, July), and [DOK Summit](https://doksummit.com/) (AI/data, November). If you're combining an Azurmendi dinner with a conference, your most realistic targets are **Euskal Encounter (Jul 23–26)** or **DOK Summit (Nov 23–24)**.

---

## Larrabetzu: no tech events, close to everything

Larrabetzu sits in a rural valley in Bizkaia, 20 km northeast of Bilbao. It has no event venues, no convention spaces, and no record of hosting tech conferences [[2]](https://www.bizkaia.eus/Ogasuna/Gestion_Fondos/ProyectosDestacados/zamudio.asp?Idioma=IN&Tem_Codigo=10510). What it does have is proximity: Zamudio Technology Park — Spain's first, founded in 1985, home to hundreds of R&D companies — is a 10-minute drive away [[1]](https://parke.eus/en/campus/zamudio-derio/).

## The two upcoming events worth planning around

### Euskal Encounter 34 — Jul 23–26, 2026

One of Europe's oldest LAN parties [[7]](https://ee26.euskalencounter.org/es/index.html), running since 1994 [[10]](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euskal_Encounter). The 2026 edition at BEC Barakaldo has 5,024 participant workstation spots and an **Opengune** — a free-access public zone with workshops, 3D printing, gaming arenas, and company stands. If you're visiting Larrabetzu for Azurmendi and want to pair it with a tech-culture event (not a formal conference), this is the best logistical fit: the BEC is 25 km from Larrabetzu, and the event runs all day for four days [[7]](https://ee26.euskalencounter.org/es/index.html).

### DOK Summit — Nov 23–24, 2026

DOK positions itself as Euskadi's digital-transformation summit, integrating AI, cybersecurity, DevOps, and the data economy into a two-day professional programme [[8]](https://doksummit.com/dok-2026-bilbao-conectar-innovacion-tecnologia-personas/). The 2024 edition drew 1,100+ professionals and industry leaders. It also absorbed LibreCon (open-source track), so both open and proprietary stacks are represented [[8]](https://doksummit.com/dok-2026-bilbao-conectar-innovacion-tecnologia-personas/). Good fit if your Larrabetzu weekend is in late November.

## Zamudio Technology Park: the proximate tech hub

[Zamudio/Derio Technology Park](https://parke.eus/en/campus/zamudio-derio/) [[1]](https://parke.eus/en/campus/zamudio-derio/) is 5 km from Larrabetzu and houses hundreds of R&D and tech companies. The park runs its own events — including an auditorium (225-seat capacity) used for smaller conferences and workshops [[1]](https://parke.eus/en/campus/zamudio-derio/). There are no major annual public tech conferences anchored there, but it is the physical home of Basque tech industry clusters and Basque Research & Technology Alliance (BRTA) member centres [[11]](https://brta.euskadi.eus/en/home/).

## Ongoing tech community (Bilbao)

If you're in the area regularly, the Bilbao tech meetup scene includes [[12]](https://www.meetup.com/gdg-bilbao/):

- **GDG Bilbao** — Google Developer Group, events on Android, cloud, AI
- **Bilbao Tech** — AI, ML, and data science meetups
- **Bilbao Python Meetup** — monthly sessions
- **TheThingsNetwork Bilbao** — IoT and LoRa

All activity is in Bilbao city; none in Larrabetzu [[13]](https://dev.events/meetups/EU/ES/Bilbao/tech).

## Bottom line

Larrabetzu is not a tech-event destination. Plan any conference attendance around Bilbao, then drive the 20 km back for dinner. The most natural calendar combos for a Larrabetzu weekend are:

- **July**: Euskal Encounter (Jul 23–26) + Azurmendi dinner in the same trip [[7]](https://ee26.euskalencounter.org/es/index.html)
- **November**: DOK Summit (Nov 23–24) + Azurmendi dinner the same or prior evening [[8]](https://doksummit.com/dok-2026-bilbao-conectar-innovacion-tecnologia-personas/)
- **January** (future planning): BilboStack — the Spanish developer conference benchmark, consistently rated among Spain's best networking events [[3]](https://bilbostack.com/en/) [[14]](https://www.euskaldunabilbao.com/en/comenzamos-2026-impulsando-el-conocimiento-y-el-debate-profesional-en-euskalduna-bilbao/)
