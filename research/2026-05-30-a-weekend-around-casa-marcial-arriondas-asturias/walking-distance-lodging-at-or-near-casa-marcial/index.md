> **TL;DR / Decision.** There is no proper "next door" hotel — [Casa Marcial](https://casamarcial.es/en/) sits in the hamlet of La Salgar with one country lane in and out [[8]](https://www.oadguides.com/restaurant/casa-marcial)[[14]](https://mapcarta.com/N6190480691). The only sub-2 km bed is [El Interior de Gaia](https://www.elinteriordegaia.com/en/) — ~1.8 km on a rural road, $86-$119/night, 5 rooms [[2]](https://www.tripadvisor.com.sg/HotelsNear-g608995-d1001616-Casa_Marcial-Arriondas_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html)[[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g608995-d16663074-Reviews-El_Interior_De_Gaia-Arriondas_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html). For most diners the right play is to stay in Arriondas town (~3 km away) and take a taxi the restaurant arranges on request [[1]](https://casamarcial.es/en/reservas/)[[13]](https://arriondasentaxi.com/). For the full Manzano-family bundle, book the restaurant's official partner [Narbasu](https://www.narbasu.com/en/), a 14th-century palace 20 minutes by car [[1]](https://casamarcial.es/en/reservas/)[[5]](https://www.narbasu.com/en/).

## Why "walking distance" barely exists here

Casa Marcial is at Calle La Salgar, s/n, 33549 Arriondas, Parres [[8]](https://www.oadguides.com/restaurant/casa-marcial) — a "bucolic hamlet" of a handful of houses [[16]](https://www.verpueblos.com/asturias/asturias/la+salgar/), 3.5 km north of the Arriondas railway station along a single lane [[14]](https://mapcarta.com/N6190480691). The AS-260 (Fito road) is the only paved route in and out, about a kilometre south of the hamlet [[16]](https://www.verpueblos.com/asturias/asturias/la+salgar/). There are no streetlights past the restaurant gate, no sidewalk, and no second hotel cluster within easy walking distance.

The restaurant itself answers this by directing every guest to its sister hotel **Narbasu**, 20 minutes away by car [[1]](https://casamarcial.es/en/reservas/) — Casa Marcial does not operate on-site rooms [[7]](https://casamarcial.es/en/casa/).

Located in Montealea hamlet at Barrio La Quintana 12, it is a country house with 5 ecosystem-themed double rooms, host Antonio, and breakfast included [[4]](https://www.elinteriordegaia.com/en/)[[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g608995-d16663074-Reviews-El_Interior_De_Gaia-Arriondas_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html). ⚠ The 1.8 km route is along unlit country roads between hamlets — fine in daylight after lunch, awkward in dinner clothes after a tasting menu. Plan a return lift back if you book the dinner sitting.

## Stay-in-Arriondas + taxi (the pragmatic play)

Arriondas town sits ~3 km south of La Salgar [[8]](https://www.oadguides.com/restaurant/casa-marcial)[[14]](https://mapcarta.com/N6190480691). The local move is to walk the town centre at sunset, then taxi to dinner — the restaurant arranges one on request and [Arriondas en Taxi](https://arriondasentaxi.com/) runs 24h locally [[13]](https://arriondasentaxi.com/)[[1]](https://casamarcial.es/en/reservas/).

For most diners the right combo is **Hotel Casona del Sella or Hotel Ribera del Chicu + a short taxi**: 5-12 minutes each way, ~3 km, and the restaurant books the return at the end of service [[1]](https://casamarcial.es/en/reservas/). Walking the 3 km on the AS-260 at night is technically possible but unappealing — narrow shoulders, no light.

Narbasu is the Manzano family's other property — same chef family, food described as "reminiscent of the early menus of Casa Marcial" [[5]](https://www.narbasu.com/en/) — and the only lodging Casa Marcial itself recommends [[1]](https://casamarcial.es/en/reservas/)[[7]](https://casamarcial.es/en/casa/). Pick it if you'd rather wake up in a palace at the foot of the Picos than save the 20-minute drive.

## Decision tree

- **Want to walk to dinner at all costs →** El Interior de Gaia. 1.8 km country lane. Daylight arrival, lift home after dark.
- **Want a real town with shops, the train station, and other restaurants →** Hotel Casona del Sella or Hotel Ribera del Chicu in Arriondas, taxi 3 km each way.
- **Want the full Manzano-family experience and don't mind the drive →** Narbasu.
- **Want spa-day wellness around the meal →** Puebloastur Eco-Resort.

There is no "stay next door to Casa Marcial" option. La Salgar simply doesn't have one — that isolation is part of what the restaurant sells [[7]](https://casamarcial.es/en/casa/).
