> **TL;DR — none.** Casa Marcial sits in La Salgar, ~3 km from Arriondas in the Parres council [[1]](https://casamarcial.es/en/casa/) — rural eastern Asturias. Within a 30 km radius (Parres, Cangas de Onís, Onís, Amieva, Piloña, Ribadesella, Caravia, Colunga) no IT conference, developer meetup, or tech-industry event runs in 2026 [[3]](https://dev.events/EU/ES/Oviedo)[[4]](https://techconf.es/). The nearest tech scene is in Oviedo (~62 km W) [[5]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/arriondas_les_arriondes-33540-asturias-principality_of_asturias-spain-to-oviedo-33001-asturias-principality_of_asturias-spain) and Gijón (~64 km NW) [[6]](https://www.rome2rio.com/es/s/Arriondas/Gij%C3%B3n). To combine the weekend with a tech event, you must leave the radius.

## Why the radius is empty

Eastern Asturias around Arriondas (postcode 33549, Parres council [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/principado-de-asturias/arriondas/restaurant/casa-marcial)) is Picos de Europa foothills, the Sella river, and the cider valley — a rural-tourism triangle of Cangas de Onís, Llanes and Ribadesella [[18]](https://turismoribadesella.com/cangas-de-onis-llanes-y-ribadesella/). The towns inside the 30 km circle are small (Cangas de Onís ≈ 6.5k pop., Ribadesella ≈ 5.7k). No university campus, no tech park, no incubator. Searching the area returns IT-service shops — sales, repair, web design — not event venues [[7]](https://www.facebook.com/cangasinformatica/?locale=es_ES).

## Wider Spain — for calibration

The community-curated lists confirm the gap is regional, not just hyper-local:

- **Chimichurri Code 2026 calendar:** zero conferences in Asturias; the northernmost listed is [NSSpain](https://nsspain.com/) in Logroño (~250 km from Arriondas) in September [[4]](https://techconf.es/)[[17]](https://nsspain.com/).
- **dev.events:** 29 in-person Spain conferences in 2026, none in Asturias [[3]](https://dev.events/EU/ES/Oviedo).
- **[npatarino/tech-conferences-spain](https://github.com/npatarino/tech-conferences-spain) ⭐ 81:** community list, no Asturias entries [[16]](https://github.com/npatarino/tech-conferences-spain).

The Spanish tech-conference centre of gravity is Madrid (Commit Conf, TarugoConf, South Summit), Barcelona (DevBcn, PyConEs, DevOpsDays, Software Crafters), Málaga/Torremolinos (J On The Beach, Lambda World, Wey Wey Web, OpenSouthCode), and Valladolid (LechazoConf) [[4]](https://techconf.es/). None within plausible day-trip range of Arriondas.

## Recommendation

If the trip is anchored on Casa Marcial and the question is "can I add an IT event?", the honest answer is **no, not inside 30 km, and probably not inside 60 km on a typical weekend** — the Oviedo/Gijón options are mostly weekday faculty events or monthly meetups, not a Saturday programme that would justify the drive after a tasting-menu dinner. Treat the weekend as gastronomy + Picos de Europa, and if you want to bolt on tech, extend by a workday in Oviedo or Gijón and time it against the CyberCamp UniOvi or Semana de la Transferencia calendar.
