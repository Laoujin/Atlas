Booking logisticsThree rules before you book

  • Casa Marcial dinner first, lodging second. The dining-room calendar is the constraint; book months ahead and let the date drive the hotel. [2]
  • Pre-book the return taxi. La Salgar has no rank — get the driver's number on the way in or have the restaurant call one. [1][6]
  • Sunday extension? Narbasu runs Thu–Sun only — a Sat-night dinner with Sun-morning checkout aligns, but a Sun-night stay does not. [4]

The backup pickIf everything above is sold out

Hotel El Quintanal — a renovated 2008 Asturian manor in Bode, 2 km from Arriondas, 11 rooms (two Junior Suites with hydromassage). [17]

Less heritage character than the seven above — it reads more "country B&B" than "boutique" — but a workable fallback when the rest of the catalog has closed its books for the night.

One diner used Ribadesella (coastal resort, ~30 min drive) as the base; works but adds a longer taxi than any pick here. [16]
