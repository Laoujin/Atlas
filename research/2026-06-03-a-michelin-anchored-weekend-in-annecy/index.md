Three research angles converge on a single practical constraint: **pick the restaurant first, then build the weekend around its closed days**.

Every starred restaurant in the region carries a closure pattern that shapes the itinerary. [Le Clos des Sens](https://closdessens.com/) (the only three-star) is closed Sunday and Monday — which locks the logical slot to Saturday evening and leaves Sunday free for an active outing. [Maison Benoît Vidal](https://www.maison-benoit-vidal.com/)'s €74 entry menu is lunch-only, Wednesday–Saturday — meaning a Friday arrival to catch a Saturday lunch at the cheapest Michelin price in the region, then a different dinner or none, is a valid budget-optimising frame. [[1]](https://closdessens.com/la-table/) [[2]](https://www.maison-benoit-vidal.com/) Confirm opening days at [Jean Sulpice](https://www.perebise.com/en/restaurant-jean-sulpice/) and [Yoann Conte](https://yoann-conte.com/en/) directly before committing travel dates to either.

**Geography is the unexpected optimizer.** Two of the five starred restaurants sit inside destinations the activities research already recommends as day-trips. Jean Sulpice [[3]](https://www.perebise.com/en/restaurant-jean-sulpice/) is in Talloires — the activities child's headline lakeside village, 14 km from the Old Town, reachable by boat. [L'Auberge de Montmin](https://aubergedemontmin.com/en/) sits at 1,147 m on Col de la Forclaz [[4]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/auvergne-rhone-alpes/talloires-montmin/restaurant/l-auberge-de-montmin) — the same ridge from which tandem paragliding launches (€90–€180) [[5]](https://en.k2parapente.com/paragliding-tandem-flights-annecy/). Routing a Talloires village afternoon into a Jean Sulpice dinner, or a morning paraglide into a Montmin dinner, costs zero extra driving. The five-restaurant spread is not just a price spread — it is a geographic one, and three of the options (Clos des Sens at 3 km, Benoît Vidal at 5 km, Yoann Conte at 7 km) cluster close enough to Annecy to walk or taxi.

**The price cliff is steeper than the star count implies.** The €74 Benoît Vidal weekday lunch and the €420 Yoann Conte full tasting menu [[6]](https://yoann-conte.com/en/) both carry two Michelin stars — a 6× spread. The cheapest path to a three-star meal is the Clos des Sens Prémices weekday lunch at €168. [[7]](https://closdessens.com/la-table/) Since the activities layer (Old Town canals, Plage d'Albigny, the Sunday market, Roc de Chère) is largely free or costs under €20, the marginal spend of the trip concentrates almost entirely in one line item: dinner.

**Timing pressure from the third angle.** The IT conferences research was included because the current date is June 3, 2026, and [MIFA](https://www.annecyfestival.com/en) (the world's largest animation industry market) opens June 21 — eighteen days from now. [[8]](https://www.prensario.net/Annecy-International-Animation-Film-Festival-and-Mifa-confirm-2026-dates-51505.note.aspx) Hotels that are bookable today become near-impossible to find at normal prices by mid-June. A weekend trip planned for after June 14 should treat accommodation as the hardest constraint, ahead of the restaurant reservation. The activities child flags this too: the Animation Festival is a public event open alongside MIFA, so the week of June 21–27 transforms the city entirely — it is a draw or a deterrent depending on the traveller, not a neutral backdrop.

**What remains unanswered.** The restaurant research does not uniformly surface booking platforms. Clos des Sens lists ZenChef [[9]](https://bookings.zenchef.com/results?rid=357602); Yoann Conte points to its own booking page [[10]](https://yoann-conte.com/en/reserver/); Jean Sulpice and Auberge de Montmin are silent on whether TheFork or direct-phone is the working path. Confirm the reservation channel before setting travel dates — some of these kitchens are booked 2–3 months out, and the closed-day grid means a wrong assumption about availability can invalidate the whole weekend structure.
