> **Decision.** On a 3–4 day visit, take **zero** ferry day trips. The one boat worth a day is a **local Naxos day cruise** (Agia Anna 09:30→17:30, Rina cave + Schinoussa/Iraklia, from €93.60) [[1]](https://zastours.com/day-cruise-to-rina-and-schinoussa-on-a-traditional-boat) — you get the Small Cyclades water without the ferry-timetable tax. Everything else burns 4–6 hours in ports and boats to buy 3 hours ashore. If you insist on one island, make it **Paros** — 10+ crossings/day, 20–45 min, from €5.70, so a missed boat isn't a lost night [[2]](https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/ferry-routes/direct/naxos-paros).

## Traps

- **Skopelitis as a day trip.** It leaves Naxos at 14:00–14:30 and only returns the following midday — Iraklia, Schinoussa and Donousa are overnight destinations, not day trips [[6]](https://www.ferries.gr/en/ferry-companies/small-cyclades-lines/route/amorgos-donoussa-koufonissi-schinoussa-iraklia/).
- **Santorini.** ~€120 pp return for 4–6 hours in the day-tripper crush, on the vessel class the meltemi cancels first [[12]](https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/ferry-routes/direct/naxos-santorini) [[5]](https://teleporio.com/blog/meltemi-wind-greece-ferry-guide/).
- **Delos+Mykonos.** ⚠ The Naxos SeaBus is a transfer, not a tour — no guide, Delos entry paid on arrival, ~10 hours for ~3 hours of ruins [[9]](https://www.travelersuniverse.com/delos-and-mykonos-one-day-cruise-from-naxos/).
- **Mid-July to August**, meltemi hits 7–8 Beaufort and high-speed catamarans cancel at short notice — never put a day trip on the day before your flight [[5]](https://teleporio.com/blog/meltemi-wind-greece-ferry-guide/).
