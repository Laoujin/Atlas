> **Decision.** Fly or take the **high-speed** ferry from Piraeus (3h15–3h25 vs 5h+ on Blue Star) [[1]](https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/ferry-routes/direct/piraeus-naxos), and **rent a car for 2 of your 3–4 days** — the mountain villages and the south/east coast are the half of Naxos the bus network does not usefully reach [[7]](https://greekislandbuses.com/guides/exploring-naxos-by-bus)[[8]](https://www.greeka.com/cyclades/naxos/car-bus/). Beach days need no car: Line 1 runs the west-coast strip every 30 min in season [[8]](https://www.greeka.com/cyclades/naxos/car-bus/).

Book July–August crossings **2–3 months ahead**; high-speed seats, cabins and car spots sell out, and 15 August is a national travel peak [[3]](https://santorinidave.com/greek-ferries). Blue Star is the weather-robust choice — catamarans cancel first in wind [[3]](https://santorinidave.com/greek-ferries). Turboprops mean small holds: ~8 kg cabin at 55×40×20 cm, checked 20–23 kg only on higher fares, and hand luggage is often gate-checked into the hold on ATRs [[6]](https://baggagepolicies.com/sky-express-baggage-policy/).

⚠ A conventional afternoon Piraeus sailing lands after dark [[1]](https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/ferry-routes/direct/piraeus-naxos) → rental desks and the KTEL office are shut, so day 1 is written off. The port is in Chora itself and walkable to town [[1]](https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/ferry-routes/direct/piraeus-naxos); the airport is 3 km out with no bus, ~€15–18 by taxi [[7]](https://greekislandbuses.com/guides/exploring-naxos-by-bus)[[9]](https://cyclades.travel/naxos/getting-around-naxos/).

## Getting around

| Mode | 2026 cost | Reaches |
|------------|--------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Car        | £50–70/day peak Jul–Aug, £26 avg summer [[9]](https://cyclades.travel/naxos/getting-around-naxos/) | everything paved, incl. mountain villages |
| ATV / quad | €30 (150cc) – €50 (300cc)/day [[10]](https://explorenaxosparos.com/renting-an-atv-in-naxos/) | dirt tracks, but most contracts void insurance off-tarmac [[10]](https://explorenaxosparos.com/renting-an-atv-in-naxos/) |
| KTEL bus   | €1.80–6.20 [[8]](https://www.greeka.com/cyclades/naxos/car-bus/) | 14 lines; west-coast beaches every 30 min, Apollonas 2×/day (75 min) [[7]](https://greekislandbuses.com/guides/exploring-naxos-by-bus) |
| Taxi       | €3.50 base + ~€1/km [[9]](https://cyclades.travel/naxos/getting-around-naxos/) | airport runs, late returns |

EU, UK, US, Canadian and Australian licences are accepted without an IDP; a category-B car licence covers ATVs and quads [[11]](https://mymoto.gr/license-requirements/). Main and mountain roads are paved, steep and winding — 40–50 km/h through villages; park only in Chora's municipal lots (€40 fine otherwise) [[9]](https://cyclades.travel/naxos/getting-around-naxos/). Buy bus tickets before boarding — drivers sell none [[7]](https://greekislandbuses.com/guides/exploring-naxos-by-bus).

**Bus-only trip loses:** the Tragaea/Apeiranthos loop on your own schedule, remote east-coast coves, and the far south [[7]](https://greekislandbuses.com/guides/exploring-naxos-by-bus)[[8]](https://www.greeka.com/cyclades/naxos/car-bus/).
