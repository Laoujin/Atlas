> **Decision.** Base in **Chora (Naxos Town)** for a 3–4 night first visit: the ferry port is in town [[1]](https://santorinidave.com/best-places-naxos), every KTEL bus line radiates from there [[2]](https://www.thecommonwanderer.com/blog/best-naxos-hotels-where-to-stay), Agios Georgios beach is a ~5-minute walk [[2]](https://www.thecommonwanderer.com/blog/best-naxos-hotels-where-to-stay), and it's the cheapest area on the island [[2]](https://www.thecommonwanderer.com/blog/best-naxos-hotels-where-to-stay). **Unless** you want a pure swim-and-sleep trip → **Agios Prokopios / Agia Anna**; or the best sand plus quiet and you have a car → **Plaka**. Skip the mountains and the far south as a base on this trip length.

## Split the stay?

No. Over 3–4 nights a second check-in costs you a half-day, and the payoff is small: the whole beach strip is 15–25 minutes and €1.80–2.00 from Chora by a bus that runs roughly every 30 minutes [[3]](https://naxosbuses.com/)[[4]](https://naxosinsider.com/naxos-bus-timetable/). Base once in Chora and day-trip. Split only at 5+ nights, and then Chora → Plaka, not Chora → mountains.
