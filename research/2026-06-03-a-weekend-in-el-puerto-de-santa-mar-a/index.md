The dining anchor is settled: [Aponiente](https://www.aponiente.com/en/) (★★★) is the only 2+-star Michelin restaurant in El Puerto de Santa María [[1]](https://bonviveur.com/es/noticias/restaurantes-con-3-estrellas-michelin), and its 2026 format is a departure from any typical tasting-menu experience — guests physically walk the salt marshes of Cádiz Bay before sitting down in the tide mill, duration tied to weather and tides [[2]](https://www.mateoandco.es/2026/03/11/aponiente-2026-angel-leon-se-sumerge-en-la-marisma-y-transforma-la-experiencia-gastronomica/). That outdoor element changes weekend logistics: a car or confirmed ride-share isn't optional, and the booking confirmation should specify the start window, not just the date.

Three tiers of the same ocean philosophy exist within 35 km — a decision tree not visible from either child in isolation. [Aponiente](https://www.aponiente.com/en/) (€400, in-town) is the apex; [Alevante](https://alevanteangelleon.com/eng/) (★★, ~€175, 35 min at Novo Sancti Petri) carries Ángel León's concept at roughly 45% of the price [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g658916-d12322999-Reviews-Restaurante_Alevante-Novo_Sancti_Petri_Chiclana_de_la_Frontera_Costa_de_la_Luz_A.html); [La Taberna del Chef del Mar](https://latabernadelchefdelmar.com/en/) (€5–25 tapas, old-town) fills the backup slot if Aponiente is fully booked [[4]](https://latabernadelchefdelmar.com/en/). The fourth entry: [LÚ Cocina y Alma](https://lucocinayalma.com/) (★★, €180–210, Jerez, 25 min) was launched by a chef who trained under Ángel León — even the outside option traces the same lineage [[5]](https://theluxuryvillacollection.com/michelin-star-restaurants-cadiz/).

Jerez surfaces independently in both children. The activities child recommends it as the horse-and-sherry day-trip [[6]](https://shawnhennessey.substack.com/p/a-day-trip-to-jerez-de-la-frontera); the Michelin child places LÚ there. These collapse into one trip: a Sunday in Jerez (Real Escuela → bodegas → LÚ dinner) is more efficient than treating them as separate entries. Sanlúcar de Barrameda (~30 km north) closes the Sherry Triangle — manzanilla at source and Doñana views — and is comfortably day-trip scale.

Spring is structurally the right window. The almadraba red-tuna season peaks April–June [[7]](https://triton.rest/donde-comer-en-el-puerto-de-santa-maria-sabores-del-mar-y-el-mejor-atun-rojo/), Aponiente opens in March and runs seasonally [[8]](https://www.laviezine.com/1564218/my-19th-visit-to-aponiente-the-2026-season-feels-like-another-major-step-forward/), and the Feria de Primavera runs 29 April–4 May 2026 [[9]](https://campinglarosaleda.com/feria-de-el-puerto-de-santa-maria/). All three converge — booking a spring weekend catches the best of every layer simultaneously.

The IT conferences child is out of scope for leisure planning. The only genuinely local event — the Gestión de Territorios Inteligentes jornada on June 11, 2026 at Palacio Juan de Araníbar [[10]](https://rtv.aytochipiona.com/2026/06/01/el-puerto-de-santa-maria-sede-de-una-jornada-de-gestion-de-territorios-inteligentes/) — targets municipal professionals and institutional representatives, not tourists.

The open question this expedition leaves: the 2026 Aponiente marsh walk is ~3 hours and explicitly weather/tide-dependent [[2]](https://www.mateoandco.es/2026/03/11/aponiente-2026-angel-leon-se-sumerge-en-la-marisma-y-transforma-la-experiencia-gastronomica/) — what is the contingency if conditions aren't right on the booking night, and does the restaurant rebook or refund?
