> **TL;DR** El Puerto de Santa María has no dedicated IT conference of its own, but it is home to [CEEI Bahía de Cádiz](https://ceeicadiz.com/) — an EU Business Innovation Centre that runs local innovation jornadas — and in June 2026 it hosts a Smart Territory tech event. For proper tech conferences, Seville (80 km, ~1 hr) is the nearest hub: CTx Tech Experience and 5G Forum both ran there in 2026. [[2]](https://ceeicadiz.com/) [[6]](https://ctx-tech.com/es) [[8]](https://www.5gforum.es/en/)

## In El Puerto de Santa María

### Gestión de Territorios Inteligentes — June 11, 2026

The most tech-forward event ever hosted in El Puerto: a full-day conference at [Palacio Juan de Araníbar](https://rtv.aytochipiona.com/2026/06/01/el-puerto-de-santa-maria-sede-de-una-jornada-de-gestion-de-territorios-inteligentes/) on smart city strategy, data governance, and municipal digitisation. [[1]](https://rtv.aytochipiona.com/2026/06/01/el-puerto-de-santa-maria-sede-de-una-jornada-de-gestion-de-territorios-inteligentes/) Organised by APYTERMI (Asociación de Profesionales para los Retos del Tercer Milenio) and Fundación Territorio Inteligente, with backing from the local council. At the event, El Puerto receives Spain's inaugural **Distintivo Territorio Inteligente** — recognising achievements in sustainability, quality certifications, collaborative governance, and deployed tech tools. [[1]](https://rtv.aytochipiona.com/2026/06/01/el-puerto-de-santa-maria-sede-de-una-jornada-de-gestion-de-territorios-inteligentes/)

Audience: municipal technicians, institutional representatives, and companies working on public-sector digital transformation.

### CEEI Bahía de Cádiz — ongoing jornadas

[CEEI Bahía de Cádiz](https://ceeicadiz.com/) is the city's anchor tech institution — an EU Business Innovation Centre (EU|BIC) founded 1997 with campuses in El Puerto, Cádiz, San Fernando, and Rota. [[2]](https://ceeicadiz.com/) It runs ad-hoc jornadas at its El Puerto campus (C/ Delta 1, Polígono Las Salinas) on topics ranging from industrial law to AI in aquaculture. [[4]](https://elpuertoalminuto.es/art/14494/el-ceei-bahia-de-cadiz-acoge-una-jornada-sobre-la-nueva-ley-de-espacios-productivos-y-los-incentivos-incea) The June 11, 2026 programme includes an **Introduction to AI Tools in Aquaculture** slot (3–4 pm). [[2]](https://ceeicadiz.com/) Annual highlight: the **Premio Emprendedor Local** award ceremony judging startups on innovation, tech level, job creation, and sustainability. [[3]](https://ceeicadiz.com/entelequia-y-people-and-more-s-l-reconocidos-con-el-premio-emprendedor-local-2025-en-el-puerto-de-santa-maria/)

Event announcements: follow [@ceeibahiadecadiz](https://www.instagram.com/ceeibahiadecadiz/) or sign up at ceei@ceeicadiz.com.

### Municipal Entrepreneurship Training — year-round

The council's Employment department runs a free recurring programme [[11]](https://www.elpuertodesantamaria.es/areas-municipales/fomento-y-empleo/emprendimiento/formacion-para-emprender/programa-formativo-emprendedores-2025) at the Municipal Business Centre (C/ Delta 1). Modules include digital marketing, social media strategy, and business launch practice. Sessions run Tues/Thurs 17:00–20:00. Not a conference — more a hands-on course for aspiring founders.

## Bay of Cádiz region

### Cádiz WordPress Meetup — Cádiz & Jerez

Active community (180 members, ⭐ 4.9) with regular meetups in Cádiz city and Jerez (~20 km from El Puerto). [[9]](https://www.meetup.com/cadiz-wordpress/) Topics span cybersecurity, development, and networking socials ("Cañas & NetWordPress"). Best local touchpoint for developers living in the Bay area.

### CADIZ.RED — October, Jerez de la Frontera

Annual entrepreneurship and collaboration summit at the [Universidad de Cádiz](https://www.corporaciontecnologica.com/eventos/evento-cadiz-red/) Jerez campus (Avda. de la Universidad s/n). [[10]](https://www.corporaciontecnologica.com/eventos/evento-cadiz-red/) First edition (2023) drew 300+ registrants from public bodies, businesses, and startups across the province. Focus is entrepreneurship and regional economic development, not deep tech — but good for networking with the local ecosystem. Funded by Diputación Provincial de Cádiz.

## Seville — 80 km, ~1 hour

The nearest city with a real tech conference circuit. Both major 2026 events are at [Sevilla TechPark](https://sevillatechpark.es/en/).

| Event                      | Dates           | Focus                                             | Scale                      |
|----------------------------|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------|----------------------------|
| [CTx Tech Experience][CTx] | Mar 19–20, 2026 | AI, quantum computing, cybersecurity, startups    | 400 speakers, 1,000+ startups [[6]](https://ctx-tech.com/es) [[7]](https://sevillatechpark.es/en/evento/ctx-tech-experience-2026/) |
| [5G Forum][5gf]            | May 12–15, 2026 | 5G/6G, IoT, edge computing, AI, smart cities      | 85 speakers, 80+ orgs, hybrid [[8]](https://www.5gforum.es/en/) |

[CTx]: https://ctx-tech.com/es
[5gf]: https://www.5gforum.es/en/

**CTx Tech Experience** (inaugural 2026 edition) is the region's broadest tech gathering — startups, corporates, VCs, and public sector. Microsoft, Salesforce, and Uber were among participants. [[6]](https://ctx-tech.com/es)

**5G Forum** (9th edition) is more specialised: telecom operators (Orange, Telefónica, Vodafone, Digi), plus public sector and enterprise, dissecting 5G deployment, 6G roadmaps, IoT, and satellite connectivity. Virtual days included. [[8]](https://www.5gforum.es/en/)

Travel: El Puerto → Seville by car is ~80 km on the A-4; by train (Renfe MD) roughly 1 h 15 min from El Puerto de Santa María station.

## Bottom line

El Puerto is not on any tech-conference circuit. The local tech infrastructure is anchored by CEEI Bahía de Cádiz's campus and the Smart Territory initiative; meaningful IT events happen in Seville (day-trip viable) or Jerez/Cádiz. If you are visiting El Puerto and want to combine a tech event with the trip, the June 11, 2026 Smart Territory jornada at Palacio Juan de Araníbar is the only on-site option right now.
