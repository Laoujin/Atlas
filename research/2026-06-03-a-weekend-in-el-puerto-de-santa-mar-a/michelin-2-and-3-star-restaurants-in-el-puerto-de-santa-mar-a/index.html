Decision: There is exactly one restaurant rated 2+ stars in El Puerto de Santa María itself: Aponiente ★★★, Ángel León's radical 2026 immersive salt-marsh tasting experience (€400/person, Wed–Sat, from March). The two nearest 2-star options require a short drive: LÚ Cocina y Alma (Jerez, ~25 min, €180–210) and Alevante (Chiclana, ~35 min, ~€175). The former 1-star Tohqa in El Puerto closed October 2025 — it is no longer operating.[8]
In El Puerto de Santa María

Aponiente

★★★ + Green Star
Chef Ángel León ("Chef del Mar") · Contemporary oceanic · 19th-c tide mill + salt marshes[9]
2026 menu Menú AQUA — ~27 courses, immersive marshland journey[2]
Menu price €400 per person (food only, VAT incl.)[2]
Pairings NoLo €180 · Albaira €210 · Diatomeas €330[2]
Season Opens Mar 11 2026 · Wed–Sat[3]
Duration ~3 hours · weather & tide-dependent[2]
TripAdvisor 4.6/5 · 874 reviews[3]
Address C/ Francisco Cossi Ochoa, s/n, El Puerto de Santa María
Booking reservas@aponiente.com · +34 956 851 870
What's new in 2026: Aponiente has abandoned its traditional dining-room format. Guests now physically walk the salt marshes of Cádiz Bay — eating where ingredients are harvested, with a traditional harvester present — before concluding in the historic tide mill.[2] Highlights: halófitas & sea urchin in the estero, moray eel, roe flan finale. One of 16 restaurants in Spain to hold three Michelin stars.[1] Book well in advance — seats fill months out.
