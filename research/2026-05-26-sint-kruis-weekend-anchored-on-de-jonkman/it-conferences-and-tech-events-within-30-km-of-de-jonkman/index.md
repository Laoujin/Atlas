> **Decision.** Weekend-only window: the only Saturday-aligned IT thing in the 30 km bubble is **CoderDojo Brugge** (kids 7–18, monthly Sat mornings) [[1]](https://www.brugge.be/activiteiten/coderdojo-brugge-952026). For an adult tech anchor, plan a **week** around a conference, not a weekend — best pick is **EUSIPCO 2026** at Concertgebouw Brugge, 31 Aug–4 Sep, ending the Friday before a Sat De Jonkman dinner [[2]](https://eusipco2026.org/). Everything else — Building Bruges, Howest Tech&Meet, Cyber 3 Lab seminars — is Tuesday/Wednesday evenings at Howest Campus Brugge Station, 6 km from De Jonkman [[3]](https://sites.google.com/howest.be/howest-techmeet) [[4]](https://buildingbruges.be/).

## Geography

De Jonkman sits at Maalsesteenweg 438, Sint-Kruis 8310 — eastern edge of Brugge city [[5]](https://dejonkman.be/en/contact). A 30 km radius covers the whole of Brugge plus Knokke-Heist, Zeebrugge, Blankenberge, Damme, Torhout and just reaches Oostende's InnovOcean Campus. **Out of range:** Kortrijk (~50 km, where Howest's other campus and ABISS Summit live), Ghent (~50 km), Brussels (~95 km).

## Recurring / community meetups

Walk-up tech community in Brugge runs on weekday evenings out of two regular hosts.

### [Building Bruges][b1] — software-dev community

- Founded ~2019 as a developer community for Bruges and surroundings; 575 members on Meetup, 4.3★ from 88 reviews [[11]][b2].
- Wednesday-evening cadence (~quarterly), starts 19:45, typically at [Bits of Love][b3] (Koning Albert I-laan 96a, ~7 km from De Jonkman) or Howest Bldg A [[11]][b2].
- Recent talks: "Things I wish I knew when I started programming" (Brent Roose, 18 Mar 2026), "Refactoring Workshop" (Peter Morlion, 3 Dec 2025), "Software architecture spices" (3 Sep 2025), "15 years of #devops" (Kris Buytaert, 19 Feb 2025) [[11]][b2].
- Free; registration via Meetup.

### [Howest Tech&Meet][h1] — applied-CS lecture series

- Tuesday evenings, 19:00, Howest Campus Brugge Station (Rijselstraat 5, Sint-Michiels — 6 km from De Jonkman). Free, registration required [[3]][h1].
- 2025 programme ran ~weekly Mar–May covering Generative AI, Quantum-Safe Cryptography (IBM), TryHackMe, Holistic Cybersecurity, CyFun Framework (CCB), AI Hacking (Inti De Ceukelaire), Cyber Deception (Accenture), NATO Cyber Defence, Event Sourcing & CQRS, Infrastructure as Code (FlowFactor) [[3]][h1].
- Heavy cybersecurity tilt — same room and team feeds **[Cyber 3 Lab][h2]** events (Maritime Cybersecurity, OT/ICS, CRA in de Praktijk, Web3/metaverse lab visits, Digital Twins masterclass, Web Dev with React masterclass) [[12]][h3].
- Contact: cyber3lab@howest.be, 050/68.26.87 [[12]][h3].

[h2]: https://www.cyber3lab.be/en

## The flagship: EUSIPCO 2026

If you want to anchor a tech-trip on Bruges, this is the week.

- **34th European Signal Processing Conference**, organised by EURASIP. 31 Aug → 4 Sep 2026 at [Concertgebouw Brugge][e1] ('t Zand 34, ~5 km from De Jonkman) [[2]][e1] [[14]][e2].
- Programme: tutorials (Mon), keynotes, oral + poster sessions, satellite workshops, industry expo. ~600 expected attendees [[2]][e1] [[14]][e2].
- Topic scope is broader than "DSP" — heavy ML/AI, biomedical signals, communications, image/video, audio, sensor arrays, security. Co-located with KU Leuven STADIUS hosting [[15]][e3].
- Conference ends Friday afternoon — Saturday De Jonkman dinner pairs naturally as the trip's closing night (Wed–Sat or Mon–Sat stay).

[e3]: https://www.esat.kuleuven.be/stadius/?p=2882
