The trip's only fixed point is dinner: [De Jonkman](https://dejonkman.be/en/) serves Saturday 19:00–20:30 [[1]](https://dejonkman.be/en/contact), Filip Claeys' 2-Michelin-star kitchen (Gault&Millau 18/20) in the former hunting lodge of Male Castle [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/sint-kruis/restaurant/de-jonkman). A tasting-menu evening realistically lands you back at the door around 22:30. The closed days (Sun/Mon/Tue) make Saturday the natural anchor [[1]](https://dejonkman.be/en/contact). Everything else flexes around that.

**Lodging splits into two coherent shapes, not a continuum.** If the goal is zero taxi friction, only three properties are inside an honest walk: [B&B Domo Togan](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/domo-togan.html) at 41 m — three rooms, cash only, book first — [Hof Ter Beuke](https://hofterbeuke.be/) at ~10 min (in-suite spa), and [Hotel Lodewijk Van Male](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/lodewijk-van-male.html) at ~13 min (request a renovated room; TripAdvisor 3.4/5 is bimodal) [[3]](walking-distance-lodging-at-or-near-de-jonkman/). Past ~25 min you are cabbing back. If the goal is instead a heritage-room weekend in old Bruges, every shortlist option is a ~10-min, 4–6 km ride: budget €35–€50 round trip with the post-22:00 night surcharge on the return leg [[4]](https://www.brugge.be/mobiliteit/taxi/standplaatstaxis-en-tarieven), and pre-book — Saturday-night taxi supply thins after 22:00 exactly when a 2★ menu finishes [[5]](special-character-lodging-within-taxi-range-of-de-jonkman/). The two shapes converge on one property: [Huis @ Sint Kruis](https://www.bruges-belgium-hotels.com/en/property/huis-sint-kruis.html), a 2-bed self-catering villa 5 min on foot from the restaurant — the only character-led option also inside the walking circle [[6]](https://www.bruges-belgium-hotels.com/en/property/huis-sint-kruis.html).

**The activity afternoon should be one thing, not three.** Inside the 30 km road radius, the [UNESCO-listed Bruges historic centre](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/996/) absorbs a full day on its own — Belfry, Burg, Groeningemuseum, Sint-Janshospitaal, Onze-Lieve-Vrouwekerk with Michelangelo's *Madonna of Bruges* (1504, donated 1514) [[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Our_Lady,_Bruges). The cleanest "second move" is the flat [Damse Vaart bike path](https://scheldedelta.eu/en/damse-vaart) or the [Lamme Goedzak paddle-steamer](https://www.visitdamme.be/lammegoedzak) to Damme (€12 one-way / €18 return, daily 4 Apr–4 Oct) [[8]](https://www.visitdamme.be/lammegoedzak). The coastal trio — Knokke-Heist (nature, [Het Zwin](https://www.zwin.be/en/exhibition) with its 1.7 km cabin trail), Blankenberge (family beach), Oostende (art + [Atlantikwall Raversyde](https://www.raversyde.be/en/atlantikwall-raversyde/atlantikwall), 28 km) — should be picked-one, not toured [[9]](day-trips-and-activities-within-30-km-of-de-jonkman/#north-sea-coast--pick-one-not-all-three). Polder villages (Lissewege "Het Witte Dorp", Oostkerke, Hoeke windmill) work best as a single bike loop. Re-checking the lodging sub-topics for villages worth adding to the activities map: neither shortlist surfaced anywhere new — character options are all old-Bruges townhouses, walking-circle options are all on Maalsesteenweg — so the activities radius is complete as drawn.

**Tech overlap is thin.** Most of the Bruges 30-km tech scene is Tue–Wed evenings at Howest Brugge Station and Bits of Love [[10]](it-conferences-and-tech-events-within-30-km-of-de-jonkman/); the one conference that genuinely justifies anchoring on a Saturday De Jonkman dinner is **[EUSIPCO 2026](https://eusipco2026.org/)** at Concertgebouw Brugge, 31 Aug–4 Sep — the Mon–Fri programme ends the day before the natural Saturday slot [[11]](https://eusipco2026.org/). The IT-events sub-topic emitted a ledger validation error (duplicate URL across two Cyber 3 Lab rows) — the calendar still resolves, but treat individual Howest dates as recheckable before booking around them.

**Booking order matters.** Confirm De Jonkman first (Tue–Sat lunch/dinner, the table books independently from any room) [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/sint-kruis/restaurant/de-jonkman); then the room — Domo Togan's three beds two doors down are the single most contended asset in this entire plan. The only question that should override the walking-distance default is whether Saturday morning should start in medieval Bruges, or in a quiet residential street outside it.
