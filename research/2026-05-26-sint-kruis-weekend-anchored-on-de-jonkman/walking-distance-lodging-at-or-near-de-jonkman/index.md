Anything past ~25 min stops being a walk and becomes a taxi.

## The anchor

De Jonkman sits at Maalsesteenweg 438, the old Bruges-Maldegem road just east of the Kruispoort ring. It's chef Filip Claeys' 2-star (Gault&Millau 18/20) seafood-led kitchen in the former hunting lodge of Male Castle [[1]](https://www.visitbruges.be/en/eat-and-sleep/food-bars-cafes/de-jonkman) [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/sint-kruis/restaurant/de-jonkman). The restaurant has its own car park, so lodging-with-parking isn't a hard requirement, but walking back after a tasting menu and pairing is the point of the exercise.

Walking distances below are great-circle along streets, measured with the OSRM foot profile on OpenStreetMap data; minutes assume a 5 km/h pedestrian pace [[3]](https://project-osrm.org/).

## Picks in detail

### B&B Domo Togan — the obvious pick
Maalsesteenweg 434, literally two parcels west of De Jonkman. Three non-smoking rooms (double / single / twin-double), free on-site parking, bicycle rental, English-speaking owners; cash-only [[6]](https://en.planetofhotels.com/belgium/bruges/bb-domo-togan). TripAdvisor 4.8/5, ranked #1 of 2 B&Bs in Sint-Kruis, with hostess Veerle singled out for breakfast and local recommendations [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1813818-d3262124-Reviews-Domo_Togan_Bed_Breakfast-Sint_Kruis_West_Flanders_Province.html). Booking aggregate 9.3 [[4]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/domo-togan.html). At three rooms, book early — there is no Plan B at this distance.

### Hof Ter Beuke — the wellness upgrade
Beukenlaan 22, ~10 min walk via Vossensteert. 4-star B&B with five named suites (Habanero, Curcuma, Saffraan, Kalisse, Wasabi), in-room spa bath, sauna, and a quiet garden — explicitly pitched as a "green oasis of quiet" [[7]](https://hofterbeuke.be/). Aggregate listings put it at 6 rooms with a spa lounge, from ~$207/night [[8]](https://www.bruges-belgium-hotels.com/en/property/hof-ter-beuke.html). Closest thing to a small destination-hotel within walking range; pair with the dinner for a full reset weekend.

### Hotel Lodewijk Van Male — for groups, with caveats
Maalse Steenweg 488, ~13 min walk east — same road, slightly past De Jonkman. 20 rooms in a country house with pond and gardens, free parking, free WiFi, breakfast strongly praised [[9]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/lodewijk-van-male.html). The catch: TripAdvisor sits at 3.4/5 because room quality is bimodal — renovated rooms get strong reviews, older rooms get stained-carpet / musty complaints; €135+ rates don't feel like value if you draw an un-renovated room [[10]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1813818-d1794515-Reviews-Lodewijk_Van_Male-Sint_Kruis_West_Flanders_Province.html). **How to apply:** if you book, message the hotel before arrival and explicitly ask for a renovated room on the second floor.

### Guesthouse next Do — whole-house option
Schaakstraat 51, ~26 min walk via Pijpeweg / Pannebekestraat. Two-bedroom holiday home with kitchen, terrace, ensuite + walk-in rain shower; 9.6/10 with strong facilities (9.9) and cleanliness (9.9) marks across 116 reviews [[11]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/guesthouse-next-do.html). It's a self-catering rental, not a B&B — no breakfast service. Practical for two couples or a family who want a base for a longer Bruges stay; for a one-night dinner trip, the rental friction outweighs the savings.

### Wellness Sweet Bonihu — private jacuzzi alternative
Fortuinstraat 79, ~26 min walk west. Two-room wellness B&B; "The Loft" suite has a private jacuzzi and infrared sauna in-room, "Midsomer" is ground-floor with terrace and welcomes dogs [[13]](https://www.wellnesssweetbonihu.com/en). Vegetarian breakfast served in-room. Distinct from Hof Ter Beuke in that the wellness is *private to your suite*, not shared — useful if a soak after the tasting menu is the point. Trade-off: it's the same 26-min walk as next Do, so practically a cab-back situation after dinner.

### B&B La Corneille — the "too far" entry
Sint-Godelievedreef 32, **4.3 km** — about a 52-min walk. Excellent property (9.3/10, 339 reviews, hosts Lawrence and Steve frequently named, cash only) [[12]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/b-amp-b-la-corneille.en-gb.html), but list-honesty matters: a one-hour walk after a 2-star tasting menu is not on. Listed so you can rule it out, or pick it on the understanding that a Bolt back from De Jonkman is part of the plan.

## Practical notes

- **Cash:** Domo Togan and La Corneille are cash-only [[6]](https://en.planetofhotels.com/belgium/bruges/bb-domo-togan) [[12]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/b-amp-b-la-corneille.en-gb.html). Bring euros if you book either.
- **Driving from town:** All six are 5–10 min by car from the historic centre via the R30 ring → Kruispoort → Maalsesteenweg.
- **Bus fallback:** De Lijn stop *Sint-Kruis Vijversdreef* is a 1-min walk from De Jonkman, with buses to Bruges Station [[1]](https://www.visitbruges.be/en/eat-and-sleep/food-bars-cafes/de-jonkman).
- **Reservation timing:** De Jonkman serves Tue–Sat lunch (noon–14:00) and dinner (19:00–21:00) [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/sint-kruis/restaurant/de-jonkman) — confirm the room before you confirm the table, the three-room B&B fills first.
