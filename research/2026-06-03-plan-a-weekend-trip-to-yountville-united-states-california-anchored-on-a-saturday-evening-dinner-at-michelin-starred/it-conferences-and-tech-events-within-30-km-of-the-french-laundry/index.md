> **TL;DR** — The 30 km radius is Napa Valley wine country, not Silicon Valley. Pure IT conferences are nearly absent; what exists are executive-tier or niche-technical gatherings at resort venues. Three upcoming 2026 events qualify: PV ModuleTech USA (Jun 16-17, cleantech), Operating Partners Forum Napa (Jun 23-25, PE/AI), and Summit Napa Valley (Oct 12-14, business leadership/AI). All are 10-16 km from The French Laundry. The French Laundry books weeks out — lock a reservation first, then build the itinerary around it.

### PV ModuleTech USA 2026 [[2]](https://www.pvtechconferences.com/pv-moduletech-usa/)

**June 16-17 · [Napa Valley Marriott Hotel & Spa](https://www.pvtechconferences.com/pv-moduletech-usa/venue/), 3425 Solano Ave, Napa · ~10 km from TFL** [[3]](https://www.pvtechconferences.com/pv-moduletech-usa/venue/)

Cleantech rather than pure IT. This fifth annual gathering maps U.S. solar PV module supply chains through 2027 — new suppliers from Southeast Asia, India, and Europe, pricing, traceability, and emerging manufacturing tech. Audience: solar developers, asset owners, investors, manufacturers, policymakers. Intimate scale (~100 attendees). Open registration via the conference website.

→ Combine with a Saturday dinner at TFL on Jun 13 or Jun 20.

### Operating Partners Forum Napa 2026 [[4]](https://live.peievents.com/operating-partners-forum-napa)

**June 23-25 · [Silverado Resort](https://www.alhi.com/listing/silverado-resort/619/), 1600 Atlas Peak Rd, Napa · ~11 km from TFL**

Private equity operating partners focused on AI and digital value creation — the closest thing to a pure tech-strategy conference within the radius. Dedicated tracks: AI Operating Partner Perspectives, digital transformation spotlights, GTM/finance/ops think tanks. Cites a 135+ respondent Technology Value Creation Survey. 200+ OPs attended in 2025, 300+ PE firms in the community. Paid access; oriented toward PE professionals.

→ Saturday dinner at TFL on Jun 20 or Jun 27 slots into this window.

### Summit Napa Valley 2026 [[1]](https://summit.co/event/napa26)

**October 12-14 · [The Meritage Resort and Spa](https://www.meritageresort.com/gather/business-meetings/), 875 Bordeaux Way, Napa · ~16 km from TFL** [[8]](https://www.meritageresort.com/gather/business-meetings/)

Summit's flagship wine-country retreat for ~500 CEOs, founders, and investors. Six programming pillars including AI landscape understanding, emerging tech operating models, and human performance — alongside curated dinners, music, and culinary experiences [[9]](https://www.napavalley.com/event-planning/corporate-events/). Broad-interest rather than narrow-IT. Access is invitation-only / Summit membership.

→ Most naturally paired with a Saturday dinner at TFL on Oct 10 or Oct 17.

## Practical Note

The French Laundry books out weeks in advance; tech events in this radius are sparse and none are a calendar anchor in the way that RSA or re:Invent would be. The better planning order: secure a TFL reservation first (check [exploretock.com](https://www.exploretock.com/thefrenchlaundry) ~8 weeks out), then match against any conference attendance. For a pure tech-conference trip, San Francisco is a 90-min drive and hosts dozens of events monthly — the Napa pairing works best as a dinner excursion from SF rather than a conference-first destination [[9]](https://www.napavalley.com/event-planning/corporate-events/).
