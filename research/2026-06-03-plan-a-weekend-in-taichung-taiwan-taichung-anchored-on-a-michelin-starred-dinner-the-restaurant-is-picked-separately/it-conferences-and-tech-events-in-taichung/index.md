> **TL;DR** Taichung's dev scene is lean but active. [GDG Taichung](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-taichung/) anchors it with free monthly **Build with AI** workshops and an annual **DevFest** (~250 attendees, November, Feng Chia University) [[1]](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-taichung/)[[2]](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-taichung-presents-devfest-taichung-2025/). Enterprise / industry events are growing fast around the **TICEC** convention center that opened March 2026 [[6]](https://focustaiwan.tw/ad/20260331000001). National marquee events — COMPUTEX, COSCUP — stay in Taipei (50 min by HSR).

## GDG Taichung — the anchor developer community

[GDG Taichung](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-taichung/) (Google Developer Group) has 969 members and is Taichung's most consistent developer community [[1]](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-taichung/). Since early 2026 they run a monthly **Build with AI** series at Feng Chia University's AI Foundry lab (資電大樓 B1, B29) — covering Gemini, Vertex AI, Firebase, and ADK [[3]](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-taichung-presents-si-yue-build-with-ai-taichung-2026/). Sessions are free and technical: April 2026 featured "Agentic Fine-Tuning on TPU" and Google's MedGemma 1.5 multimodal medical AI [[3]](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-taichung-presents-si-yue-build-with-ai-taichung-2026/). **Next event: June 6, 2026** — frontend toolchain anatomy (tree-shaking, Vite plugins, SSR) [[1]](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-taichung/). Organizers: Ahdaa Yeh and ATai Lin, partnered with FCU's AI Research Center (FCUAI).

**Primary venue:** 逢甲大學 資電大樓 B1, B29 AI Foundry — 407台中市西屯區文華路100號

## DevFest Taichung — annual flagship

[DevFest](https://gdg.tw/annual_activity/devfest/) is GDG's worldwide annual conference series; the Taichung edition runs each **November** [[14]](https://gdg.tw/annual_activity/devfest/). DevFest Taichung 2025 (November 8, Feng Chia University 人言大樓) drew 236 RSVPs, free admission [[2]](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-taichung-presents-devfest-taichung-2025/). Topics: AI × Cloud × Security × Scalability — ADK/A2A/MCP multi-agent architecture, LangGraph agent deployment, Gemini CLI, Flutter + Firebase, client-side AI [[2]](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-taichung-presents-devfest-taichung-2025/). DevFest Taichung 2026 is not yet announced; expect October–November based on the annual pattern [[14]](https://gdg.tw/annual_activity/devfest/).

## TICEC & trade shows

The **Taichung International Convention and Exhibition Center** ([TICEC](https://english.taichung.gov.tw/10273/10554/1278975/3243377)) opened March 23, 2026 — Taiwan's newest and largest convention venue outside Taipei [[6]](https://focustaiwan.tw/ad/20260331000001). It spans 132,000 m², holds 2,360 exhibition booths, 5,000 conference seats, and up to 12,600 attendees [[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taichung_International_Convention_and_Exhibition_Center). In its opening year, 50+ exhibitions and 300+ conferences are already booked across smart technology, green energy, and precision machinery [[6]](https://focustaiwan.tw/ad/20260331000001). Location: Shuinan Economic and Trade Park, Xitun District (near FCU).

**TMTS 2026** — [Taiwan International Machine Tool Show](https://www.tmts.tw/en) (March 25–28, TICEC) was TICEC's first major tech event: 400 companies, 1,800 booths, theme "AI-Powered Sustainable Manufacturing" [[8]](https://www.tmts.tw/en)[[9]](https://www.showsbee.com/fairs/TMTS.html). TMTS is biennial — next edition 2028.

## Adjacent programs

[Taiwan AI Academy Taichung](https://aiacademy.tw/tech-tc/) (台中技術領袖班) runs a multi-cohort **Technical Leaders** professional program at Tunghai and Feng Chia campuses [[13]](https://aiacademy.tw/tech-tc/). It's a multi-week upskilling course (not a single-day conference) backed by Academia Sinica. Contact: tc@aiacademy.tw, (04) 2301-5116.
