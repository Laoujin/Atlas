> **TL;DR** There is no IT conference scene *in* Dénia proper. The town's tech footprint is a monthly WordPress meetup (631 members, 61 events held [[1]](https://wpdenia.com)[[2]](https://www.meetup.com/es-es/wordpress-marina-alta/)) and a freshly-launched annual student hackathon [[3]](https://deniadigital.es/art/22586/el-i-hackathon-intercentros-marina-alta-reune-en-denia-a-alumnado-y-empresas-en-torno-a-retos-reales). For conferences, look 90 km up the coast: Valencia hosts the [Valencia Digital Summit](https://vds.tech) (12,000+ attendees) and [PGConf.EU](https://2026.pgconf.eu) on the same October 20-23 window — both a day-trip or overnight from Dénia.

## In Dénia: Events

### [WordPress Dénia Meetup](https://wpdenia.com)

The only recurring professional tech community in Dénia. [[1]](https://wpdenia.com)[[2]](https://www.meetup.com/es-es/wordpress-marina-alta/)

| Attribute    | Detail                                                    |
|:-------------|:----------------------------------------------------------|
| Members      | 631, rated 4.8 ★ (123 reviews)                          |
| Events held  | 61 past events                                            |
| Schedule     | 3rd Thursday of every month, 19:00                        |
| Venue        | [Knowhere Dénia](https://www.knowheredenia.com/coworking-denia/), Calle Ramón y Cajal 30 |
| Topics       | WordPress dev, design, e-commerce, remote work, community |
| Sponsors     | WooCommerce, Jetpack, WordPress.com, Hostinger            |
| Contact      | meetup@wpdenia.com                                        |

⚠ No upcoming events were listed as of June 2026 — the group operates on a loose monthly rhythm rather than fixed future bookings. Check [meetup.com/es-es/wordpress-marina-alta](https://www.meetup.com/es-es/wordpress-marina-alta/) or [wpdenia.com](https://wpdenia.com) before planning.

### I Hackathon Intercentros Marina Alta (inaugural, April 2026)

Dénia hosted its first inter-school tech competition on **21 April 2026** at [IES Maria Ibars](https://tvdenia.com/la-marina-alta-pone-en-marcha-su-i-hackathon-intercentros-el-proximo-21-de-abril/). [[3]](https://deniadigital.es/art/22586/el-i-hackathon-intercentros-marina-alta-reune-en-denia-a-alumnado-y-empresas-en-torno-a-retos-reales)[[4]](https://tvdenia.com/la-marina-alta-pone-en-marcha-su-i-hackathon-intercentros-el-proximo-21-de-abril/)[[5]](https://en.lamarinaalta.com/como-piensan-los-profesionales-del-futuro-la-marina-alta-celebra-su-primer-hackathon-educativo/)

- **Format:** 36 vocational-training (FP) students from three schools — IES Maria Ibars (Dénia), IES La Mar (Xàbia), CIPFP Dénia — split into cross-school teams.
- **Challenges set by:** Helados Palacio, Hotel Daniya, BMW Benigar, Baleària (ferry company posed the final-round challenge). [[4]](https://tvdenia.com/la-marina-alta-pone-en-marcha-su-i-hackathon-intercentros-el-proximo-21-de-abril/)
- **Winning team:** students from all three schools, recognised for creativity and problem-solving; prize: Baleària boat trip. [[16]](https://canfali.com/i-hackathon-intercentros-el-talento-joven-de-la-marina-alta-se-pone-a-prueba/)
- **Next edition:** organizers are "already preparing future convocations with new centers" — expected to repeat annually in spring. [[5]](https://en.lamarinaalta.com/como-piensan-los-profesionales-del-futuro-la-marina-alta-celebra-su-primer-hackathon-educativo/)

This is an **educational** hackathon, not a professional conference. Visiting techies can't participate, but it signals a growing local tech-education culture.

## In Dénia: Community Infrastructure

Three coworking spaces provide the backbone for Dénia's distributed tech community:

| Space | Address | Notable for |
|:------|:--------|:------------|
| [Knowhere Dénia](https://www.knowheredenia.com/coworking-denia/) [[9]](https://www.knowheredenia.com/coworking-denia/) | C/ Ramón y Cajal 30 (near castle) | WordPress meetup host; **Makerspace** with 3D printing, Arduino, robotics courses |
| [Micelium](https://micelium.es/en/) [[10]](https://micelium.es/en/) | City centre | Telegram community of devs, SEO/SEM, creatives; training room (15 pax) |
| [CO-CO Coliving](https://coliving-costablanca.com/) [[11]](https://coliving-costablanca.com/) | Between mountain and sea, Dénia | Coliving + coworking launched late 2023; workations, retreats, digital nomads |

[CoworkZINQ](https://www.coworkzinq.com/) is a fourth option focused on minimalist shared desks with 1 Gb fibre. None of the spaces publish a public tech-event calendar, but Knowhere's Makerspace runs ad-hoc courses and robotics workshops worth enquiring about directly.

## Day-Trip Range: Valencia (90 km, ~1 h by car)

The nearest major tech conference city. Two events coincide on **20–23 October 2026**, making a combined Valencia trip particularly efficient:

### [Valencia Digital Summit (VDS) 2026](https://vds.tech) — 21-22 Oct

Europe's leading startup-to-corporate networking event, 9th edition. [[6]](https://vds.tech)[[14]](https://eurotravelo.com/explore/vds-valencia-spain/)

| Detail | Info |
|:-------|:-----|
| Venue  | City of Arts and Sciences, Valencia |
| Scale  | 12,000+ attendees, 120+ countries, 3,000 startups, 800+ investors (€300B AUM) |
| Stages | 7 high-production stages, 600+ speakers |
| Topics | AI, HealthTech, GreenTech, Cybersecurity, venture |
| Passes | Ecosystem (~€83 early), Business, Investor (~€845) |

### [PGConf.EU 2026](https://2026.pgconf.eu) — 20-23 Oct

16th annual European PostgreSQL conference. [[7]](https://2026.pgconf.eu)

| Detail | Info |
|:-------|:-----|
| Venue  | Palacio de Congresos, Valencia |
| Format | 3-day / 4-track conference + Community Day (Oct 23) |
| Sponsors | Google Cloud, Microsoft, AWS, Aiven, EDB, CYBERTEC, pganalyze |
| CFP closed | 1 June 2026 |

### WordCamp Valencia (annual, next edition TBD)

WordCamp Valencia 2025 (Nov 8-9) drew 300+ attendees under the "WordPress Tech Congress" banner, covering AI, SEO, e-commerce, and web dev. [[13]](https://central.wordcamp.org/news/2026/02/wordpress-tech-congress-wordcamp-valencia-2025-tradition-and-innovation/) Three Valencia organizers now lead WordCamp Europe 2026 (Kraków, 4-6 June). No 2026 Valencia date is announced yet; the event has run annually since 2021.

### SeleniumConf & AppiumConf Valencia 2026

Already concluded (May 6-8, 2026) at Veles e Vents. [[12]](https://seleniumconf.com/) Filed here as context for the regional conference density — Valencia is an active host city.

## Day-Trip Range: Alicante (80 km, ~1 h by car)

### [Alicante Tech Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/alicantetech/)

The most active ongoing tech community within reach. [[8]](https://www.meetup.com/alicantetech/)

- **3,331 members**, 4.7 ★ (344 reviews), 303 past events since 2013
- Regular formats: After Work Drinks (AWD, monthly), AI Co-Lab (AI coworking sessions), Friday Network Brunch (12 people, €5)
- Topics: AI, cloud, Linux, open source, web dev, DevOps, blockchain, 3D printing

→ The most practical option if you want a casual tech gathering without a conference ticket.

**Alicante Nomad Summit 2026** — also in the pipeline for 2026, with hands-on sessions and skill-sharing for digital nomads and remote workers; dates not yet confirmed.

## Timing

If your Dénia weekend is **flexible**, October is the richest window: VDS + PGConf.EU land on adjacent days in Valencia, making a single overnight extension cover both. For a standalone Dénia visit any other month, the WordPress meetup (3rd Thursday) is the only predictable local event to anchor on — worth emailing wpdenia.com ahead to confirm it is running that month.
