The weekend has one structural dependency: the Michelin dinner. [Quique Dacosta](https://quiquedacosta.es/en/home/) (3★, Dénia) holds only 30 seats per service and sells out weeks ahead [[1]](https://en.quiquedacosta.es/ver/92/-frequently-asked-questions.html); [BonAmb](https://bonamb.com/en/) (2★, Jávea) runs dinner only Thu–Sat [[2]](https://bonamb.com/en/reservations/). Confirm the reservation before planning anything else — it gates the entire weekend.

**The restaurant choice doubles as a day-trip decision.** Quique Dacosta sits on Las Marinas beach 3 km from Dénia's centre, so the afternoon before dinner is naturally a snorkel at nearby Les Rotes or Punta Negra cove [[3]](https://en.denia.com/playa-de-la-punta-negra/). BonAmb is a restored Valencian farmhouse in Jávea [[4]](https://www.spainlifeexclusive.com/michelin-restaurant-alicante-bonamb/), 16 km along the coast — heading there for dinner means the same day can include the dramatic Cap de Sant Antoni clifftop mirador (150 m+ cliffs, the Montgó headland's far edge [[5]](https://www.javeaonline24.com/info/cap-de-sant-antoni)) or Cap de la Nau's boat-only sea cave [[6]](https://airial.travel/attractions/spain/cap-de-la-nau/cap-de-la-nau-x%C3%A0bia-gJ7VxEzJ).

**Gastronomy runs deeper than the Michelin table.** Dénia is a [UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy since 2015](https://denia.net/en/discover/enogastronomy/creative-city-of-gastronomy/), anchored on the *gamba roja* — the deep-sea red prawn netted at ~600 m, landing at €150–170/kg [[7]](https://en.denia.com/la-gamba-roja-de-denia/). The same ingredient appears at the Mercat Municipal on market morning (Mon–Sat, 08:00–15:00 [[8]](https://en.denia.com/mercado-de-denia/)), at casual rice houses on Calle Loreto, and as the stock base of *arròs a banda* [[9]](https://en.denia.com/donde-comer-paella-denia/) — an arc that runs from street-level to 3-star. Plan a market lunch on the day before the big dinner, not after.

**Outdoor timing has its own constraints.** The Cova Tallada sea cave — Dénia's most distinctive excursion — requires a free permit continuously from 15 June through 15 October [[10]](https://covatallada.org/en/reservations/); arriving by kayak with an authorised operator bypasses this entirely [[11]](https://marinalia.es/en/adventure-sports/water-sports/513-kayak-rental-available-from-las-rotas-to-visit-the-cova-tallada.html). The Montgó summit (753 m, 3–5 h return) demands an early start to dodge midday heat in summer [[12]](https://en.denia.com/rutas-montgo/). Both are half-day commitments that work best on the day *without* the fine-dining reservation — keep the dinner evening light.

**The IT conference thread** was researched as part of this expedition but has no practical bearing on a leisure weekend: Dénia has only a monthly WordPress meetup [[13]](https://wpdenia.com) and a student hackathon [[14]](https://deniadigital.es/art/22586/el-i-hackathon-intercentros-marina-alta-reune-en-denia-a-alumnado-y-empresas-en-torno-a-retos-reales). Anyone combining a tech event with the trip would need to extend to Valencia (90 km north) in the October window for VDS or PGConf.EU [[15]](https://vds.tech).

The one question this expedition cannot answer: whether you can actually book Quique Dacosta — reservations open seasonally, fill within days, and the window is unpredictable [[1]](https://en.quiquedacosta.es/ver/92/-frequently-asked-questions.html). Book via [quiquedacosta.es](https://quiquedacosta.es/en/booking/) or call +34 965 784 179 (11 AM–2 PM); if it's sold out, BonAmb on a Thursday or Friday evening is the natural pivot, and the Jávea day pairing more than compensates.
