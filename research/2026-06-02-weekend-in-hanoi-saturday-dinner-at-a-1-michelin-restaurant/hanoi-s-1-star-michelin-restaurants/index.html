Decision

Gia if you want Hanoi's finest modern-Vietnamese tasting experience — 12 courses, 100% local ingredients, ~$130/person before tax.[4] Hibana by Koki for intimate Japanese teppanyaki theatre inside the Capella luxury hotel, ~$60–110/person.[5] Tầm Vị for the most affordable Michelin-starred meal in Hanoi — honest Northern Vietnamese home cooking at ~$10–20/person.[9] All three are open Saturday evenings.

⚠ Based on the 2025 MICHELIN Guide Vietnam.[11] The 2026 ceremony takes place 4 June 2026 in Hanoi and may update this list — it marks the guide's 4th edition in Vietnam and the global 100th anniversary of the Michelin Star.[12]

Booking Tips
  • Gia: Reserve at gia-hanoi.com/reservation up to 60 days out. A 1,000,000 VND/diner card hold is taken at booking. For groups of 7+, email reservations@gia-hanoi.com directly. Budget ~3 hours for dinner.
  • Hibana by Koki: Call Capella Hanoi (+84 24 3987 8888) or book via the hotel website. With 14 seats and strong demand, aim for 2–4 weeks ahead minimum. Last dinner order is 20:30 — book the first seating if you want the full experience without time pressure.
  • Tầm Vị: Call (+84) 96 632 31 31 or DM @nhahang.tamvi on Instagram. Walk-ins accepted but Saturday evenings fill up. Arrive at opening (17:00) to beat the crowd and get more attentive service.
