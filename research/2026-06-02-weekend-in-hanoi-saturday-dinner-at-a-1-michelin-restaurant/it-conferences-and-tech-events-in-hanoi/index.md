### GITEX Vietnam 2026 — details

Debut edition of the global GITEX brand in Vietnam [[4]](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/gitex-vietnam-2026-tickets-1672669211639). Organised by Dubai World Trade Centre (DWTC) and KAOUN International, with the Vietnam National Innovation Center as partner. Five sub-tracks: AI Everything Vietnam, GITEX Cyber Valley Vietnam, Global Data Centres Vietnam, North Star Vietnam (startups), and GITEX Quantum Expo Vietnam. Projects 20,000+ attendees, 850+ enterprises/startups, 200+ speakers, 150+ investors from 75 countries.

### KCD & OpenInfra Days Vietnam 2026 — details

Annual event co-locating Kubernetes Community Day (KCD) and OpenInfra Days [[1]](https://2026.vietopeninfra.org). Four conference tracks: AI/ML Technology Innovation, Cloud-Native Practices (Kubernetes, OpenStack, observability), Computing Infrastructure (scheduling, storage), Security & Trusted Computing. The 2025 edition drew 1,000+ professionals [[14]](https://community.cncf.io/events/details/cncf-cloud-native-hanoi-presents-openinfra-amp-cloud-native-day-vietnam-2025/). CFP via sessionize.com; tickets via ticketbox.vn.

## Key venues

- **Vietnam National Innovation Center (NIC)** — Tòa nhà Lô D25, Ngõ 7 Tôn Thất Thuyết, Cầu Giấy [[15]](https://www.govserv.org/VN/Hanoi/110083374159476/NIC---Vietnam-National-Innovation-Center). Major tech expos hub; houses startups and investment funds year-round.
- **Sheraton Hanoi Hotel** — 11 Xuân Diệu, Tây Hồ. Hosts KCD & OpenInfra Days; close to West Lake.
- **Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST)** — Hai Bà Trưng. Hosts free public tech forums like AI Day.
