Pick by trade-off:

Splurge / book-the-experience: stay in the restaurant — Maison Lameloise (16-room 4★ Relais & Châteaux, €280+/night[2], ranked #1 of 6 hotels in Chagny[4]).

Best value, 2-min walk: Hôtel de la Poste at 17 Rue de la Poste — 3★, ~$93–$112/night, breakfast and reviews both excellent[7][8].

Boutique character, same 2-min walk: Hôtel de la Ferté — 12-room 15th-century building with vaulted wine cellar[11]. Ranks #6 of 6 locally though[10] — accept the dated décor or skip it.

All distances measured from the restaurant at 36 Place d'Armes[1]. Chagny is small; everything in the table below is reachable on foot after dinner in normal weather. The €335 four-course Dégustation tasting menu[19] ends late — short walks home matter.

The properties

Maison Lameloise

36 Place d'Armes • 4★ Relais & Châteaux • 16 rooms • from €280[1][2][3]
  • Former 15th-century coaching inn; six room categories — Standard, Comfort, Junior Suite, Deluxe terrace (3 SW-facing with private terraces), Small Deluxe (Pierre-Yves Rochon design), Grand Deluxe[1].
  • Ranked #1 of 6 in Chagny, 4.7/5 across 294 reviews — Travelers' Choice[4].
  • You can book the room and table together through the official site[5]. ⚠ Restaurant closed Tue/Wed and 18 Aug → 2 Sep 2026[5].
  • Minor gripes from reviewers: small TVs, "gloomy" rear terrace, €10 supplement for an extra egg at breakfast[4].

Hôtel de la Poste

17 Rue de la Poste • 3★, 11 rooms • $93–$112 • 2-min walk[9][7]
  • Strongest value pick in town — 8.3/10 on Booking (242 reviews), with cleanliness 8.9, location 9.0, staff 9.4, value 8.8[8].
  • Free secure parking with locked gate at night; quality breakfast (fresh bread, croissants, OJ); pet-friendly[7].
  • Trade-off: rooms run small, no on-site restaurant — fine if Lameloise IS your restaurant[7].

Hôtel de la Ferté

11 Bd de la Liberté • 3★, 12 rooms • $98–$118 • 100 m / 2-min walk[11][20]
  • Boutique character in a 15th-century stone building; individual A/C, Nespresso, bio toiletries; vaulted Burgundy wine cellar[11].
  • Garden with fruit trees + herbs, free enclosed parking, pet-friendly[10].
  • ⚠ Ranks last (#6 of 6) on TripAdvisor at 3.6/5 — flags are steep stairs, dated décor, limited English, mixed responsiveness to issues[10].

Auberge de la Musardière

30 Route de Chalon • 3★ Logis, 12 rooms • $105–$158 • ~12-min walk[12]
  • Best ranked option after Lameloise (#3 of 6, 4.2/5 TripAdvisor; 8.2/10 across 537 Booking reviews)[12][13].
  • Acclaimed on-site gastronomic restaurant — reviewers call it "worthy of a Michelin"[13]. Useful for the Friday or Sunday night around the Lameloise anchor.
  • ⚠ Trade-offs: 0.9 km along Route de Chalon is the longest walk in this list (still doable post-dinner), and reviewers flag mid-renovation rooms + à-la-carte prices that climb fast[12].

Camping du Pâquier Fané

Rue du Pâquier Fané • Municipal campsite • 700 m / 9-min walk[14]
  • 4.3/5 across 168 reviews; reviewers explicitly mention using it as a Lameloise base[14].
  • For the contrast: a 3★ Michelin dinner followed by a 9-minute walk back to your tent. Pool, spotless facilities[14].

Le Gîte du Prieuré

6 Ruelle du Gourdier • Whole-house gîte, sleeps up to 15[16]
  • Group/family option in central Chagny: renovated former factory in Burgundy stone, 5 bedrooms, indoor pool, equipped kitchen, garden, petanque[16].
  • Whole-house rental, no breakfast service — useful only if you're a group of 6+.

What to skip — out of walking range

  • Hôtel-Restaurant Le Chagny — sits on the RN6 trunk road well outside the centre (positioned 5 km from Santenay, 17 km from Beaune)[17]. Drive-to property, not a walking option to Place d'Armes.
  • Chez Sylvie et Jean-Yves — chambres d'hôtes at 16 Chemin du Champ de Tir on the outskirts[18]. Nice setting (garden, seasonal pool, bike rental) but a longer walk after dinner; works better if you're driving.
⚠ Booking logistics worth knowing:
  • Maison Lameloise restaurant is closed Tuesday + Wednesday year-round and shut 18 Aug → 2 Sep 2026[5] — confirm your Saturday date avoids both.
  • The Dégustation menu runs €335 / person before wine[19]; doubling that with the Lameloise room (€280+) clears €950 for the night. The Hôtel de la Poste swap saves ~€200 with a 2-minute walk penalty[7].
  • Only 16 + 11 + 12 + 12 = 51 hotel rooms exist within easy walking distance of Lameloise. Saturday-night dinner crowds book early — lock lodging when you lock the table.
