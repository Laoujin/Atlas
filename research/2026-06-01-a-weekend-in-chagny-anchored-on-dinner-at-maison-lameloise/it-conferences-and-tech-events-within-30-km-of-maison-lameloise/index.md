> **Decision.** The radius is mostly Burgundy vineyard and small cities, so the IT calendar is sparse. Anchor a tech detour on **[Forum InCyber des Territoires](https://europe.forum-incyber.com/creusot/)** in Le Creusot, **Thu–Fri 11–12 June 2026** [[6]](https://franceurbaine.org/initiatives-locales/la-communaute-urbaine-creusot-montceau-co-organise-le-forum-incyber-des-territoires/) — the one regional event of national weight. Everything else in-radius is a half-day local salon ([CGLOBAL'IT](https://www.cglobal.fr/salon-cglobalit-jeudi-26-03-2026-colisee-de-chalon-sur-saone/) and [CAP 26](https://www.c2ip.com/c2ip-salon-cap-26-chalon-sur-saone-juin-2026) in Chalon-sur-Saône) [[8]](https://www.cglobal.fr/salon-cglobalit-jeudi-26-03-2026-colisee-de-chalon-sur-saone/)[[9]](https://www.c2ip.com/c2ip-salon-cap-26-chalon-sur-saone-juin-2026). All weekday-only — to pair tech with the Saturday dinner you must extend the weekend into Thu–Sat. If your dates miss the cluster, the radius offers permanent innovation venues but no Saturday meetup scene.

## Geometry

[Maison Lameloise](https://www.lameloise.fr/en/) sits on Place d'Armes in Chagny, Saône-et-Loire [[1]](https://www.lameloise.fr/en/). The 30 km circle from Chagny captures the three cities that matter for tech: Beaune (~15 km / 20 min) [[2]](https://www.burgundy-tourism.com/staying-here/hotels/HOTBOU071146263/detail), Chalon-sur-Saône (17 km) [[3]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/gb/france/good-to-know-lameloise) and — at the edge — Le Creusot (28 km straight, 35 km by road, ~44 min) [[4]](https://ladistance.fr/Chagny/Le-Creusot). Dijon (~45 km) and Cluny (~50 km) sit outside the line.

**The standout.** Forum InCyber des Territoires is the local arm of the major French cybersecurity forum, organised with the urban community of Creusot-Montceau in the new **[Hub & Go technopole](https://www.creusot-infos.com/news/vie-locale/le-creusot/le-creusot-entrez-dans-hub-go-le-site-technopolitain.html)** — 4,000 m² with FabLab, 3D additive-manufacturing platform, ISEREM engineering school and the ImVia imaging/vision research lab [[14]](https://www.creusot-infos.com/news/vie-locale/le-creusot/le-creusot-entrez-dans-hub-go-le-site-technopolitain.html). The 2025 first edition pulled Framatome, Safran Aircraft Engines, ArcelorMittal, Alstom, Baker Hughes and 8 higher-ed institutions of Campus Sud Bourgogne — a serious industrial-cyber audience, not a vendor expo [[7]](https://parolesdelus.com/actualites/1er-forum-incyber-des-territoires-le-creusot-a-lavant-garde/).

**The local salon.** [CGLOBAL'IT][i4] is biennial — March 2026 has passed and the next edition is expected in 2028 [[8]][i4]. It is a half-day vendor floor (Lenovo, Microsoft, Fortinet, Synology, SentinelOne, Hikvision, Eaton, 3CX, Alcatel-Lucent Enterprise) with two cyber conference slots — useful if your trip dates match, less so as a destination event.

**The SME track.** [CAP 26][i5] is the CPME 71 chamber salon for Saône-et-Loire SMEs; not a tech conference per se, but C2iP and other regional IT/security partners exhibit, with an explicit pitch on "structuring and securing the digital environment" [[9]][i5]. Free, walk-in.

## Permanent innovation venues (good for off-event visits)

If no event aligns, the area's tech identity lives in three campus/lab venues you can tour or contact:

- **[L'Usinerie][i9]** (Chalon-sur-Saône, 17 km) — the BFC-region Industry 4.0 reference platform, in a restored 19th-century steam mill; partners include Arts et Métiers, Cnam Bourgogne-Franche-Comté and UIMM 71 [[12]][i9].
- **[Nicéphore Cité](https://www.nicephorecite.com/)** (Chalon, 17 km) — digital pole for image, sound, virtual reality; runs Innovation Meetups, Entrepreneurship Masterclasses and Connected Agriculture cycles [[15]](https://www.nicephorecite.com/).
- **[Hub & Go](https://www.creusot-infos.com/news/vie-locale/le-creusot/le-creusot-entrez-dans-hub-go-le-site-technopolitain.html)** (Le Creusot, ~28 km) — venue of the InCyber forum; the only metal-additive-manufacturing platform in the radius [[14]](https://www.creusot-infos.com/news/vie-locale/le-creusot/le-creusot-entrez-dans-hub-go-le-site-technopolitain.html).
- **[Institut Arts et Métiers Chalon-sur-Saône](https://www.legrandchalon.fr/competences/enseignement-superieur-vie-etudiante/institut-arts-metiers-de-chalon-sur-saone/)** (17 km) — hosts the Institut Image research lab on multisensory immersion / AR-VR [[20]](https://www.legrandchalon.fr/competences/enseignement-superieur-vie-etudiante/institut-arts-metiers-de-chalon-sur-saone/).

## What this means for a Saturday-dinner trip

- **No Saturday-only event** falls in the radius in 2026. Tech salons here run Thursday–Friday; that's the BFC pattern.
- **Best pairing window: 11–13 June 2026** — Forum InCyber Thu–Fri at Le Creusot (35 km / 44 min from Chagny by car), then Saturday dinner at Maison Lameloise on the 13th. The InCyber 2025 edition was Thu–Fri 22–23 May — the 2026 weekday-then-weekend pattern is consistent [[7]](https://parolesdelus.com/actualites/1er-forum-incyber-des-territoires-le-creusot-a-lavant-garde/).
- **Alternative pairings.** Thu 4 Jun (CAP 26 at Chalon) → Sat 6 Jun dinner is a lighter day-trip option. The 12 → 13 Dec axis pairs DevFest Dijon (Friday) with a Saturday dinner if you accept the 45 km drive each way.
- **Meetup floor: thin.** Recurring developer meetups on meetup.com show no active tech group in Chalon-sur-Saône, Beaune or Chagny — the dev community concentrates in Dijon (Developers Group Dijon runs DevFest) [[16]][o1]. Pre-arrange a visit to L'Usinerie or Nicéphore Cité if you want a tech touchpoint outside a formal event.
- **Skip:** [Bourgogne BTP Expo](https://beaunecongres.com/fr/agenda/) at the Palais des Congrès de Beaune on 25–26 June 2026 — listed in some "industry" round-ups, but it is construction/transport, not IT [[21]](https://beaunecongres.com/fr/agenda/).
