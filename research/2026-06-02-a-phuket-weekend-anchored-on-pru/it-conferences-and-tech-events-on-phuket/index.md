> **TL;DR** Phuket is not a tech-conference city — Bangkok owns Thailand's event calendar. The island's one standout recurring tech summit (AI Marketing & SEO, each April) already ran in 2026; the next edition is April 22–24, 2027 [[1]](https://phuketsummit.com/). For the remainder of 2026, the [Global Wellness Summit](https://www.globalwellnesssummit.com/2026-global-wellness-summit/) (Nov 10–13, Angsana Laguna) is the only upcoming Phuket event with meaningful tech/innovation content [[3]](https://www.globalwellnesssummit.com/2026-global-wellness-summit/). The living tech scene on the island is its digital-nomad community, anchored around coworking spaces in Rawai and Patong.

## Phuket AI Marketing SEO Summit

The island's only recurring practitioner tech event [[2]](https://www.phuket.net/events/phuket-summit-ai-marketing-workshop-led-summit/). Annual format: three-day workshop (not lecture series) covering AI agent systems, SEO automation, agentic workflows, and generative-engine optimisation. Deliberately capped at ~190 seats for high-trust, resort-setting networking [[1]](https://phuketsummit.com/).

**2026 edition (past):** April 3–5, Centara Grand, Karon Beach. Ticket price USD $350 [[2]](https://www.phuket.net/events/phuket-summit-ai-marketing-workshop-led-summit/). Speakers included Amit Deshpande (Gemini Enterprise, Google), Cindy Krum ([MobileMoxie](https://www.mobilemoxie.com/)), Craig Campbell, San Eng (WorkOptional.ai) [[4]](https://www.admarkevents.com/phuket-ai-marketing-seo-summit-2026).

**2027 edition (upcoming):** April 22–24, same resort. Super Early Bird $300; VIP $900 [[1]](https://phuketsummit.com/). Audience skews founders/business owners (70%), investors (20%), agency professionals (10%) [[1]](https://phuketsummit.com/).

## Global Wellness Summit 2026

The 20th anniversary edition lands in Phuket — the first time in Southeast Asia [[5]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/global-wellness-summit-to-hold-20th-anniversary-event-in-phuket-thailand-302706342.html). Theme: "The Science, Art and Soul of Wellness." The summit convenes C-suite wellness executives, longevity physicians, and technology innovators; track content includes wellness-tech startups, AI in health, and longevity science [[3]](https://www.globalwellnesssummit.com/2026-global-wellness-summit/).

Attendance profile: resort/spa CEOs, healthtech VCs, wellness-tourism policymakers. Less developer-oriented than Techsauce, but relevant to anyone building in the health-tech or wellness-app space [[3]](https://www.globalwellnesssummit.com/2026-global-wellness-summit/).

## Business Networking (November 2026)

November is Phuket's busiest month for professional gatherings [[7]](https://denzphuket.com/networking-events-and-business-summits-in-phuket-2026-complete-guide/):

- **EABC Phuket Rendezvous** — European Chamber of Commerce Thailand, cocktail dinner at Four Points by Sheraton Patong, bridging EU business with regional stakeholders [[7]](https://denzphuket.com/networking-events-and-business-summits-in-phuket-2026-complete-guide/).
- **All Hands Phuket Real Estate Forum** (Phuket Property Association / C9 Hotelworks) — invitation-only, C-level property/hospitality analytics [[7]](https://denzphuket.com/networking-events-and-business-summits-in-phuket-2026-complete-guide/).
- **Franco-Thai Chamber Speed Networking** — periodic; 15+ structured 10-min meetings, 1,500–3,000 THB [[7]](https://denzphuket.com/networking-events-and-business-summits-in-phuket-2026-complete-guide/).

## Digital Nomad & Coworking Scene

Phuket's real tech community is informal and nomad-driven rather than conference-driven. Main hubs [[8]](https://www.facebook.com/groups/phuketnomads/) [[9]](https://www.facebook.com/groups/digitalnomadsrawai/):

| Space | Area | Vibe |
|---|---|---|
| [Denz Coworking Café](https://denzphuket.com/) | Patong Hill | Weekly skill-shares, mastermind groups, networking mixers |
| [The Project Phuket](https://projectphuket.com/) | Rawai | Outdoor/green, digital nomad community hub |
| [Let's Work](https://www.letswork.co.th/) | Rawai beachfront | Sea view, productive environment |
| HUB53 | Phuket Town | 24/7, ergonomic, high-speed internet |

Facebook communities: [Phuket Digital Nomads](https://www.facebook.com/groups/phuketnomads/) (~active, tech/remote-work discussions) and [Digital Nomads Rawai Phuket](https://www.facebook.com/groups/digitalnomadsrawai/) (south island focus). Ad-hoc meetups surface via these groups and coworking space notice boards [[8]](https://www.facebook.com/groups/phuketnomads/) [[9]](https://www.facebook.com/groups/digitalnomadsrawai/).

## Bangkok as Day-Trip Alternative

A 90-min flight (or a direct bus transfer) puts you in range of Thailand's real tech event cluster [[11]](https://asterlion.com/9-startup-tech-events-thailand/). **Techsauce Global Summit** (Aug 27–29, 2026, QSNCC) is the flagship: Thailand's answer to SXSW, 300+ speakers, 400+ sessions spanning AI, fintech, and deep tech, 4,000+ attendees [[6]](https://fintechnews.sg/fintech-events-singapore/event/techsauce-global-summit-2026/). [Money20/20 Asia](https://asia.money2020.com/) (fintech, Bangkok) and [DigiTech ASEAN](https://digitechasean.com/) (B2B tech expo, IMPACT Centre, Bangkok) round out the tier-1 options [[11]](https://asterlion.com/9-startup-tech-events-thailand/).

## Dev.events Verdict

[dev.events](https://dev.events/AS/TH/Phuket/tech) lists zero in-person tech conferences in Phuket as of June 2026, compared to 143 across Asia broadly [[13]](https://dev.events/AS/TH/Phuket/tech). Academic conference aggregators (allconferencealert, conferenceindex) list occasional small multidisciplinary IT symposia in Phuket, but these are academic paper-presentation events, not practitioner conferences [[12]](https://conferenceindex.org/conferences/information-technology/phuket-th).

**Bottom line:** Plan a Phuket trip around the April AI/SEO summit or November Global Wellness Summit; otherwise, schedule Bangkok days around Techsauce if tech events are the primary draw.
