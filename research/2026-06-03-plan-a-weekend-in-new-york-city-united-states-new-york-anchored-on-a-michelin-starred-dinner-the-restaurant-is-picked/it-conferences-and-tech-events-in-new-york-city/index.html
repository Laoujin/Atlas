TL;DR — June is NYC’s peak tech month: NYC Tech Week (Jun 1–7, 1000+ events, mostly free)[1] and AWS Summit NYC (Jun 17, free, 200+ sessions at Javits)[7] anchor the same fortnight alongside DASH by Datadog (Jun 9–10).[5] For paid deep-dives: DevOpsCon + MLCon (Sep 28–Oct 2, Brooklyn, from $989)[9] and FinovateFall (Sep 9–11, Times Square) for fintech.[8]

Anchor Events

NYC Tech WeekMOSTLY FREE

Jun 1–7 · Citywide — Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens

1000+ events hosted by a16z, OpenAI, NVIDIA, Stripe, AWS, Atlassian, and hundreds of indie organizers. Tracks: AI, fintech, climate, crypto, founders, investors, hackathons, students. ~60–70% of events are free; major sponsor sessions may require tickets.[1]

AWS Summit New YorkFREE

Jun 17 · Javits Convention Center

One-day event with 200+ sessions on agentic AI, data, security, and cloud. Keynote by Dr. Swami Sivasubramanian (VP Agentic AI, AWS) and Lauren Woods (CIO, Southwest Airlines). Free registration includes expo, sessions, and 5–6 PM networking reception. Pre-day badge pickup Jun 16.[7]

DASH by DatadogPAID

Jun 9–10 · North Javits Center

Datadog’s 9th annual observability + AI conference: 4000+ attendees, 100+ sessions, 20+ workshops. Focus: AI monitoring, cloud security, reliability engineering. Customer-led sessions from fintech, retail, and media companies.[6]

DevOpsCon + MLCon Clusterfrom $989

Sep 28–Oct 2 · Brooklyn Marriott at the Bridge

4 conferences in one week: DevOpsCon, MLCon, JavaScriptCon, APICon. DevOpsCon covers Kubernetes, CI/CD, DevSecOps, and Platform Engineering across 6 tracks. MLCon add-on: $250. Early-bird 2-day pass: $989 (deadline Jun 11).[9]

FinovateFallPAID

Sep 9–11 · Marriott Marquis Times Square

Fintech’s flagship demo event: 60+ live product demos (no slides or canned video), 120+ speakers, 2000+ senior decision-makers. Topics: agentic commerce, payments, stablecoins, fraud/AI, open banking. 70% of attendees are C-suite or SVP+.[8]

Legalweek New YorkPAID

Mar 9–12 (past for 2026) · Javits Convention Center

Leading legal-tech event: 6000+ attendees, 400+ speakers, 100+ sessions on AI, automation, eDiscovery, data governance, and litigation strategy. Moved from NY Hilton Midtown to Javits for 2026 after 39 years — unified expo hall, larger session rooms.[2]
