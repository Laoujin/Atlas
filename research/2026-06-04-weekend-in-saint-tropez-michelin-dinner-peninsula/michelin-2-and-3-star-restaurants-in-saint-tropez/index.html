Decision: Only two restaurants on the Saint-Tropez peninsula hold 2+ Michelin stars in the 2026 Guide.[7] Book La Vague d'Or (3★, in Saint-Tropez) for a once-in-a-generation meal — France's youngest-ever triple-starred chef, plan 6 months out, budget €450–700+/person with wine.[1] Choose La Voile (2★, Ramatuelle, 10 km south) for lighter Mediterranean cooking at €245 tasting menu, open daily, sea-view terrace — still exceptional but ~€200 cheaper per head.[2]

La Vague d'Or

⭐⭐⭐
3 stars — Exceptional cuisine, worth a special journey
Chef
Arnaud Donckele — trained under Michel Guérard and Alain Ducasse; youngest chef in France to earn three Michelin stars; Best Chef in the World 2018; 19/20 Gault & Millau[1]
Cuisine
Mediterranean Provençal — local fish treated with meticulous precision; sauces are the chef’s defining signature (“intensely flavorful, refined, and central to each dish’s identity”); seasonal garden produce[1][3]
Signature dishes
  • Spider crab in citrus bath[1]
  • Turbot in Camargue sea-salt crust[1]
  • Saint-Pierre with courgettes & caviar[1]
  • Turbot with seaweed & lime; rabbit with absinthe & country bacon[3]
Menus & Price
“Run Away Together” and “Epicurean Adventure” tasting menus from €445/person; wine pairing +€250; full menu with cheese board ~€510[4][5]
Season & Hours
7 May – 10 Oct 2026  ·  Dinner only, 7:30–9:30 PM  ·  Mon, Tue, Fri, Sat, Sun — closed Wed & Thu[4]
Capacity
40 indoor + 40 terrace seats, overlooking the Golfe de Saint-Tropez[4]
Location
Plage de la Bouillabaisse, Cheval Blanc St-Tropez, 83990 Saint-Tropez
Accolades
3★ Michelin 2026[12]; 5 toques Gault & Millau; 4.8/5 on TripAdvisor (490 reviews) — #1 restaurant in Saint-Tropez[6]
Reservation
Up to 6-month waitlist during peak season — book the moment availability opens.[6]

La Voile

⭐⭐
2 stars — Excellent cooking, worth a detour

Éric Canino — trained under Michel Guérard at Eugénie-les-Bains; second star awarded January 2020[8][9]
Cuisine
Light Mediterranean — fish & seafood with aromatic herbs, citrus and olive oil; notable all-vegetable tasting menu (starter to dessert); “cuisine as light as a gentle breeze”[10]
Signature dishes
  • Rouget poché à l’eau de mer (sea bream poached in seawater)[8]
  • Full all-vegetable tasting menu (complete alternative, starter to dessert)[10]
