★ Anchor pick

For a software-flavoured trip, plan around Hello Stavanger — Wed–Thu, Oct 21–22 at Tou Scene.

It's Stavanger's only home-grown software conference, walkable to the city centre [1]. Pick ONS instead if you're into AI/digital in energy — by far the largest event of the year, with a real Digital Transformation track [2][3]. ERF, Energy Norway and Nordic Edge Expo all wrapped before late May.

Pair a conference with a weekend

All 3 sit mid-week (Mon–Thu or Wed–Thu)
Option A · the pick
Hello Stavanger long weekend
Tue Oct 20 → Sun Oct 25

Fly in Tue Oct 20, conference Wed–Thu, stay through the weekend. Best fit for a "tech + culture" trip — Tou Scene is in walking distance of restaurants & waterfront [14].

Option B
ONS bookend
Fri Aug 21 → Sun Aug 23 (before) · or Fri Aug 28 → Sun Aug 30 (after)

Stay on the shoulders of the event, attend Mon–Wed onward. Avoid arriving mid-event — rooms inside the city are saturated by 72k visitors.

Option C
HealthTech morning
Thu Sep 10 → Sun Sep 13

Book a normal Thu–Sun, attend the half-day Sep 10, dinner that evening, weekend in town. Lightest commitment.
