III
Feast
Either eve · The anchor

RE-NAA, sixteen courses

22 seats around the open kitchen. ~NOK 4,500 per guest. Books out far in advance — declare any allergies at booking, not on arrival.

  • Format~16-course chef-at-table [5]
  • PriceNOK 4,500 / guest [3]
  • RuleHike the OTHER day — never both

three things to mind before you go —

  1. RE-NAA cannot do vegan, milk-protein-free, egg-free or citrus-free menus. Declare at booking, not on arrival. [3]
  2. Vinmonopolet (state monopoly for >4.7% ABV) shuts Sat ~15:00 and all day Sunday — buy wine Friday or order at the table. [11]
  3. Don't anchor on ONS (Aug 24–27): 72k visitors saturate hotels city-wide — the parent's "lodging is a non-issue" framing breaks for that one week. [10]
