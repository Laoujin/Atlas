Two forces structure the weekend and they cannot share an evening. The [Arena di Verona Opera Festival](https://www.arena.it/en/arena-verona-opera-festival/) (103rd edition, 12 June – 12 September, curtains at 21:15–21:30) [[1]](https://www.arena.it/en/arena-verona-opera-festival/events/) and the Michelin tasting menu both start after 19:30 and run 3–4 hours. A well-built itinerary assigns one evening to each and keeps the daytime programme independent — city sights and wine on one thread, an opera or Michelin dinner anchoring each night.

**Restaurant choice constrains which evenings are available.** [Casa Perbellini 12 Apostoli](https://www.casaperbellini.com/en/) (⭐⭐⭐, the only 3-star in Verona) closes Saturday dinner, Sunday, and Monday [[2]](https://www.casaperbellini.com/en/tasting-experiences/). For visitors on a standard Friday-to-Sunday city break, the lunch service — same tasting menus, same prices (€240–280 pp) — is the practical entry point, or arrival must shift to Tuesday–Friday. [Famiglia Rana](https://www.ristorantefamigliarana.it/en/homepage) (⭐⭐, Oppeano, 20 min south by taxi) runs dinner Wednesday–Saturday [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1549067-d13549425-Reviews-Ristorante_Famiglia_Rana-Vallese_di_Oppeano_Province_of_Verona_Veneto.html) and is therefore the more forgiving pairing for a weekend centred on a Friday or Saturday opera. Its Friday–Sunday lunch (from €70 for 4 courses) is the highest-rated value proposition in the province [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1549067-d13549425-Reviews-Ristorante_Famiglia_Rana-Vallese_di_Oppeano_Province_of_Verona_Veneto.html).

**A wine thread runs through all three sub-topics.** Valpolicella and Soave vineyards sit 20–45 minutes from the city [[5]](https://www.getyourguide.com/verona-l389/from-verona-valpolicella-and-amarone-wine-tasting-tour-t93759/) [[6]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Verona-Italy/Soave), and both Michelin kitchens are anchored to the same terroir: Famiglia Rana's 1,800-label cellar goes deep into Veneto and natural-wine producers [[7]](https://www.ristorantiweb.com/featured/famiglia-rana-una-cucina-di-cuore-e-tecnologia/); Casa Perbellini pairs heritage Italian cuisine in rooms above Roman cellars. A morning in the vineyards before the tasting menu is not redundant — it sharpens the vocabulary for the sommelier conversation. One consistent warning across both restaurants: bottle markups are steep, flagged independently by reviewers at each table [[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g187871-d7349300-Reviews-Casa_Perbellini_12_Apostoli-Verona_Province_of_Verona_Veneto.html) [[9]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1549067-d13549425-Reviews-Ristorante_Famiglia_Rana-Vallese_di_Oppeano_Province_of_Verona_Veneto.html). The curated wine-pairing add-on (€120–180 at Casa Perbellini [[10]](https://www.casaperbellini.com/en/tasting-experiences/), €80–130 at Famiglia Rana [[11]](https://www.enoplane.com/ristorante-famiglia-rana-oppeano-menu-the-doors/)) generally outperforms à la carte ordering from a marked-up list.

**For a developer adding a conference to the trip:** [GrUSP](https://www.grusp.org/en/)'s [reactjsday](https://www.reactjsday.it/) (Thu 19 Nov) and [angularday](https://www.angularday.it/) (Fri 20 Nov) run on consecutive days at the same Hotel San Marco venue [[12]](https://www.grusp.org/en/conferences/). Casa Perbellini's dinner service runs Tuesday–Friday — a post-conference table on either conference night is feasible. The combination eliminates any need for a separate leisure visit: the Arena, Piazza delle Erbe, Castel San Pietro, and the Amarone wine bars are all walkable, and Famiglia Rana stays open through November. The Arena opera season closes after September, which removes that scheduling constraint for November trips entirely.

**Can you do both restaurants in one weekend?** Logistically yes: Famiglia Rana dinner Friday, Casa Perbellini lunch Saturday flows cleanly. Whether two tasting menus within 18 hours is an itinerary or a dare is the question no research can answer.
