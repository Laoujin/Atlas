> **TL;DR** Córdoba is not a major IT conference destination — Spain's big events concentrate in Barcelona and Madrid. Its scene is UCO-anchored: [SalmorejoTech](https://salmorejo.tech/2026/) (annual free full-day conference, ~400 attendees, typically first or second Friday of May) is the clear flagship [[1]](https://salmorejo.tech/2026/); [GDG Córdoba](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-cordoba/) runs meetups 4–6× per academic year [[6]](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-cordoba/). **To catch an event, target May.**

## SalmorejoTech — Annual Flagship Conference

[SalmorejoTech](https://salmorejo.tech/2026/) is Córdoba's main IT conference, run by the [Aula de Software Libre](https://www.uco.es/aulasoftwarelibre/) at the Universidad de Córdoba. The VII edition ran on **8 May 2026**, at the UCO Rectorate, drawing 400+ attendees. [[2]](https://www.lanocion.es/cordoba/20260508/la-vii-edicion-de-salmorejotech-situa-a-cordoba-46564.html) It has run every year since its first edition in 2019. [[5]](https://foss.events/2025/05-09-salmorejo-tech.html)

| Detail        | Value                                                          |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cadence       | Annual · typically first or second Friday of May               |
| Venue         | Rectorado, Universidad de Córdoba                              |
| Attendance    | 400+ (2026 VII edition)                                        |
| Price         | Free                                                           |
| Tracks        | Omeya (backend / systems) · Mudéjar (frontend / design)        |
| Topics (2026) | Linux, AI optimisation, React, cybersecurity, event-driven architecture, community building |
| Contact       | aulasoftwarelibre@uco.es                                       |

The programme is student-designed and speakers are mainly UCO alumni now working across Spain's tech industry. The 2026 line-up included Juan Luis Cano Rodríguez on Linux packaging, talks on agentic AI, API security, and tech entrepreneurship from Andalusia. [[3]](https://cordopolis.eldiario.es/mas-noticias/vii-salmorejotech-situa-cordoba-foco-innovacion-tecnologica_1_13207243.html) The event is organised by Professor Antonio Arauzo and a student committee; the opening drew UCO's vice-rector for Digital Transformation and SUSE's Linux Business Unit manager. [[4]](https://www.eventbrite.es/e/entradas-salmorejotech-2026-1986054851524) [[15]](https://uco.edu.es/servicios/actualidad/sociedad/item/159846-el-aula-de-software-libre-de-la-universidad-de-cordoba-organiza-el-congreso-tecnologico-salmorejotech)

## GDG Córdoba — Regular Meetups

[GDG Córdoba](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-cordoba/) is the city's Google Developer Group, 108 members strong, meeting at the Aula de Software Libre on the UCO Rabanales Campus. [[6]](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-cordoba/) [[7]](https://www.meetup.com/gdg-cordoba-meetup-group/) Events run roughly every 6–8 weeks during the academic year (October–June); summer is quiet.

**2025/26 recent sessions:**

- *Orquestando agentes: Frameworks Prácticos para la Ingeniería de Software Agéntica* — Feb 2026
- *Ralph Loop: Cuando la IA necesita tatuarse la palabra "programar"* — Apr 2026
- *Google I/O Watch Party* — May 2026
- *Google Summer of Code 2026 preparation* — Nov 2025 (Aula de Software Libre, UCO) [[8]](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-cordoba-presents-si-te-gusta-programar-preparate-para-un-verano-2026-pagado-por-google/)

All events are free. Check [gdg.community.dev/gdg-cordoba](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-cordoba/) for the next session.

## Python Córdoba

[Python Córdoba](https://www.meetup.com/python-cordoba/) organises talks, workshops, and seminars in collaboration with [Python España](https://pretix.eu/python-spain/), covering Python, Django, data science, and ML. [[9]](https://www.meetup.com/python-cordoba/) No events were scheduled as of June 2026, but the group is active in prior academic-year cycles.

## The Hub: Aula de Software Libre (UCO)

The [Aula de Software Libre](https://www.uco.es/aulasoftwarelibre/) is the organisational home for nearly all of Córdoba's tech community activity, founded in 2006. [[10]](https://www.uco.es/aulasoftwarelibre/) It runs:

- **Jornadas Técnicas sobre Software y Conocimiento Libre** — annual technical workshop days (February–May), free and open to the public.
- **Linux Install Parties** — hands-on recurring events.
- It is the registered venue for GDG Córdoba and produces SalmorejoTech each year.

Location: Aulario Averroes, Campus Rabanales, UCO (N-IV km 396, Córdoba).

## SISTEDES — Occasional National Conference

[SISTEDES](https://www.uco.es/sistedes2025/) — Spain's national software engineering multi-conference (JISBD + JCIS + PROLE) — was hosted at UCO Rabanales on **9–11 September 2025**, covering software architecture, AI for SE, data science, quantum computing, and model-driven engineering. [[11]](https://www.uco.es/sistedes2025/index.php) The conference rotates cities annually; the 2026 edition moved to Alicante. [[12]](https://sistedes.lucentia.es/) Worth monitoring for future Córdoba returns.

## Rabanales 21 / Córdoba TechPark

[Rabanales 21](https://ptcordoba.es/) is the science and technology park adjacent to the UCO campus: 110 companies, 550 employees, €60M revenue (2025). [[13]](https://www.cordobabn.com/articulo/cordoba/empresas-parque-cientifico-tecnologico-rabanales-21-facturan-60-millones-euros/20260107125158244781.html) [[14]](https://ptcordoba.es/) It hosts irregular industry encounters (energy management, BIM/construction tech, dual-use technologies) for park tenants and partners — not publicly open events, but a signal of the commercial tech density around UCO.

## When to Visit for Tech

| Period       | What's on                                                            |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **May**      | SalmorejoTech (flagship, free, ~400 pax) — best time for tech visit  |
| Feb–Apr      | UCO Jornadas Técnicas; GDG Córdoba meetups most active               |
| Oct–Nov      | GDG Córdoba restarts after summer; SISTEDES could return to Córdoba  |
| Dec          | GDG Córdoba Code Challenge (Xmas Edition, Dec 2025)                  |
| Jun–Sep      | Summer lull — GDG and Python Córdoba typically quiet                 |

**Bottom line:** Córdoba's tech calendar is thin but not empty. SalmorejoTech in May is worth building a visit around if you're a developer — it's free, well-attended for a city this size, and pairs cleanly with Córdoba's other attractions. Outside May, GDG meetups offer lighter touchpoints.
