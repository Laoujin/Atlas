Bottom line: Córdoba has exactly one restaurant rated above 1-star — Noor (★★★) by Paco Morales. There are no 2-star Michelin restaurants in Córdoba city or province as of the 2026 guide. [1] [2] For a fine-dining dinner anchored on prestige, Noor is the only choice; book 2–3 months ahead, Thu–Sat only.

3-Star: Noor

Noor  ★★★ 3 Michelin Stars

The first restaurant in Córdoba's history to reach three Michelin stars,[3] awarded at the November 2023 ceremony.[4] Chef: Paco Morales (head), Paola Gualandi (second-in-command).[5]

Concept: each season traces one century of Al-Andalus history through contemporary technique. Ingredients unavailable in that era are excluded — no potatoes or tomatoes before the 15th century, for example. [6] Season 10 (Tiempo de Evolución, opens Sep 17 2025) covers the 19th century — progress, industry, the threshold of modernity.[7]

2-Star: none in Córdoba

The 2026 Michelin Guide lists no 2-star restaurants in Córdoba city or province.[2] [1] Córdoba's hierarchy jumps directly from Noor's three stars to the 1-star tier. The nearest 2-star options are in Seville and Málaga.
