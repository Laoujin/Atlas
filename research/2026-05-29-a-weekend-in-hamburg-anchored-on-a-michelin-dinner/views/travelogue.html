02

Why this weekend, not another

three constraints, one window
Michelin window

4 rooms, 1 closure

Hamburg has exactly four 2★/3★ rooms in the active guide[1]. Haerlin is dark 19 Jul–16 Aug & 18–26 Oct 2026[2]. The Table sells its 20-seat counter ~3 months out, same day[3]. Don't book bianc — closed 31 Dec 2025[4].

Tech anchor

Jun 26–27 stacks three

ITCS Fri free with a 16:00 after-party[5], Product at Heart Fri at Kampnagel[6], CollabDays Sat free[7]. Saturday afternoon ends in time for evening dinner.

Harbour geometry

Three rooms, one walk

Three of four ★ rooms sit on the HafenCity / Binnenalster axis — walkable from the Elbphilharmonie Plaza and the Speicherstadt UNESCO ring[14]. Lakeside is the outlier on the Außenalster[16] — pair it with the 7.4 km Sunday loop[17].

The pick — Saturday, smart casual, lake-side window

the sharpest recommendation
SCOUT SAT★ 27 · 06
SAT 27 JUN · 19:30 · BINNENALSTER

Restaurant Haerlin

Fairmont Hotel Vier Jahreszeiten · Neuer Jungfernstieg

Three stars, classic-leaning, lake-side window, smart casual — and the only one of the four 3★ rooms that's both *bookable* <10 weeks out AND comfortably outside the summer closure[2].

"Haerlin / Lakeside / 100·200 weekend" if you booked late. The Table's counter has already sold out for Saturday — Haerlin still answers the phone.[3]

→ book 6–8 weeks ahead. Dress: jacket, no tie.

The 48-hour spread

an hour-by-hour read
Freitag 26.06.26
  • 10:00 Arrive Hbf Drop bag at HafenCity hotel. Coffee on Stadthausbrücke.
  • 11:30 ITCS Hamburg Free, festival format. Hop in mid-morning.[5]
  • 14:00 Product at Heart @ Kampnagel If product is your thing. Otherwise stay at ITCS.[6]
  • 16:00 ITCS after-party Hamburg conference scene's loosest social.[5]
  • 20:00 Casual harbour dinner Save the appetite. Save the wallet.
  • 22:00 Wasserlichtspiele Free water-light show in Planten un Blomen, ~30 min.[20]
Samstag ★ 27.06.26
  • 09:30 Speicherstadt + Elbphilharmonie Plaza UNESCO warehouse ring, then the free 8th-floor plaza view.[14]
  • 11:00 CollabDays (optional) Free MS 365 community day. Half-day works.[7]
  • 14:00 Rathaus + Binnenalster stroll Late-May / June: outdoor cafés on the Jungfernstieg.
  • 17:00 Hotel — change for dinner Jacket, no tie. No sneakers.
  • 19:30 ★★★ Haerlin · Fairmont Tasting menu, lake-window. 3–3.5 hours.[12]
  • 23:30 Nightcap on Neuer Jungfernstieg Or roll to St. Pauli if you've got the energy.
Sonntag 28.06.26
  • 09:00 Coffee at Landungsbrücken HVV day ticket: €8.30. Buy on the app.
  • 10:00 HADAG ferry line 62 Hamburg's official "ride this, not the paid cruise" pick. Free with day ticket.[21]
  • 11:30 Fischbrötchen @ Brücke 10 Bismarck or Matjes. Eat at the rail.[22]
  • 13:00 Coffee + train Hbf is 8 min from Landungsbrücken by S-Bahn.
  • 14:00 ICE home Berlin 1h45, Köln 4h, Amsterdam 5h15.

07

Two stamped warnings

don't plan around these

Deichtorhallen

Contemporary art hall is closed until 5 Jun 2026. Late-May trips can't see it.[18]

Hafengeburtstag

The 837th harbour birthday already ran 8–10 May 2026. Substitute the free 10pm Wasserlichtspiele.[19]

If a vegetarian is at the table

the calculus inverts
Open question · pin to the suitcase

Move the anchor to 100/200.

100/200's Die Saison rotation runs fully vegetarian[23] and re-centres the weekend on Rothenburgsort and the gallery-level Glorie carte. Demote the Alster walk in favour of the Övelgönne–Teufelsbrück Elbe-beach stroll[24].
