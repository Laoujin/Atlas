Three angles converge on a single date math: **when does your weekend actually land?**

The Michelin layer narrows it. Hamburg has exactly four restaurants at 2★ or above in the active 2025 guide [[1]](https://marketing.hamburg.de/en/media-service/tourism-culture/culinary-in-hamburg/hamburgs-stars) — and one of them, Haerlin, is dark 19 Jul–16 Aug 2026 and 18–26 Oct 2026 [[2]](https://hvj.de/en/restaurant-haerlin.html). The Table Kevin Fehling opens its 20-seat counter ~3 months ahead and sells out the same day [[3]](https://www.accountingfortasteblog.com/blog/the-table-kevin-fehling-review), so a weekend chosen <10 weeks before today is effectively a Haerlin / Lakeside / 100·200 weekend. The fifth 2★, **bianc**, closed permanently on 31 Dec 2025 — older overviews still list it; don't book it [[4]](https://www.genussguide-hamburg.com/top-listen/sternerestaurants-hamburg/).

The tech-event layer adds two anchor weekends in 2026. **Jun 26–27** stacks ITCS Hamburg (Fri, free, festival format with a 16:00 after-party) [[5]](https://it-cs.io/en/events/hamburg/), Product at Heart (Fri, Kampnagel) [[6]](https://productatheart.com/conference), and CollabDays Hamburg (Sat, free) [[7]](https://www.collabdays.org/2026-hamburg/) back-to-back — Saturday afternoon ends in time for an evening dinner. **Sep 16–19** is Reeperbahn Festival across ~70 St. Pauli venues with a music-tech B2B conference attached [[8]](https://www.reeperbahnfestival.com/en) — broader, louder, and the only weekend where the nightlife layer (Molotow, Mojo, Große Freiheit 36 [[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molotow_Club)) becomes the whole point. DevFest Hamburg (Oct 16–17) is the pure-developer alternative [[10]](https://hamburg.devfest.de/).

The geometry is friendlier than it looks. **Three of the four Michelin rooms sit on or next to the HafenCity / Binnenalster axis**: The Table on Shanghaiallee in HafenCity [[11]](https://the-table-hamburg.de/), Haerlin in the Fairmont on Neuer Jungfernstieg [[12]](https://restaurant-haerlin.de/en/home.html), 100/200 in a 1926 warehouse in Rothenburgsort [[13]](https://www.100200.kitchen/en/) — all walkable from the Elbphilharmonie Plaza and the Speicherstadt UNESCO ring [[14]](https://www.hamburg.com/visitors/sights/architecture/speicherstadt-19324). The same HafenCity neighborhood holds KINOPOLIS, code.talks' Nov 4–5 venue [[15]](https://codetalks.com/). Lakeside is the outlier — 7th-floor Außenalster panorama [[16]](https://www.thefontenay.com/en/restaurants-bar/lakeside-restaurant/), which is the same lake as the 7.4 km Outer Alster Sunday-morning loop [[17]](https://www.hamburg.com/visitors/hamburg-by/foot/11877548/outer-alster-route/). Picking Lakeside puts your dinner and your Sunday walk on one body of water.

Two more watch-outs before you commit: the Deichtorhallen contemporary hall is closed until 5 Jun 2026 [[18]](https://www.deichtorhallen.de/en/besuch), and the 837th Hafengeburtstag harbour birthday already ran 8–10 May 2026 [[19]](https://www.hafen-hamburg.de/en/events/port-anniversary/) — late-May trips get Planten un Blomen's free 10pm water-light show instead [[20]](https://www.wasserlichtspiele-hamburg.de/).

**Sharpest recommendation.** Target **Jun 26–27**. Anchor Saturday on **Haerlin** (Binnenalster window, smart casual, easier to book than The Table, comfortably outside the summer closure [[2]](https://hvj.de/en/restaurant-haerlin.html)). Use Friday for ITCS or Product at Heart. Sunday morning: HADAG ferry line 62 from Landungsbrücken on an HVV day ticket — Hamburg's official "ride this, not the paid cruises" pick [[21]](https://hadag.de/en/routes/62/) — ending at Brücke 10 for fischbrötchen [[22]](https://hamburgerbruecke10.de/) before the train home.

**Open question.** If a vegetarian is at the table, the calculus inverts: 100/200's "Die Saison" rotation runs fully vegetarian [[23]](https://www.kochfreunde.com/en/2024/restaurant-100-200-the-vegetarian-menu/) and reframes the whole weekend around Rothenburgsort and the gallery-level Glorie carte, with the Alster walk demoted in favour of the Övelgönne–Teufelsbrück Elbe-beach stroll [[24]](https://www.hamburg-travel.com/see-explore/maritime-hamburg/himmelsleiter-oevelgoenne/).
