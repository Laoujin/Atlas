## Marquee picks, by what you'd want from a Hamburg weekend

### Best weekend-trip overlap → ITCS + CollabDays (Jun 26–27)
The cleanest "tech weekend in Hamburg" answer of 2026. **ITCS Hamburg** opens Fri Jun 26 at Messe Hamburg from 10:00 — a hybrid IT job fair + tech conference + festival with two stages, 30+ talks, 80+ exhibitors and an after-party from 16:00 with DJ, drinks, table football; admission is free [[1]](https://it-cs.io/en/events/hamburg/). The next morning, **CollabDays Hamburg** (Sat Jun 27) is a community-run Microsoft 365 / SharePoint / Power Platform / AI day with international speakers — also free [[2]](https://www.collabdays.org/2026-hamburg/). Two adjacent ecosystems back-to-back; Saturday evening stays open for the Michelin dinner. Note that **Product at Heart** (single-track product-management conference, AI theme) also runs Fri Jun 26 at Kampnagel [[10]](https://productatheart.com/conference) — if product is your tribe, that's the Friday swap.

### Best straight-up developer conference → code.talks (Nov 4–5)
Hamburg's flagship dev event. 100+ curated sessions across 13 tracks (GenAI & LLMs, Architecture, Cloud, Security, Frontend, Product, Data, Culture, Craft, DevEx, Science, Impact, Geek-Out) at the **KINOPOLIS HafenCity cinema** — talks on cinema screens is the format people remember; 3,500+ devs and CTOs expected [[4]](https://codetalks.com/). Tickets: Regular €729, Team €659/seat (4+), Executive €1,199; all-in (meals, popcorn, after-party) [[4]](https://codetalks.com/). Falls Wed–Thu, so a "weekend" version means extending into Sat.

### Best for cloud-native / Kubernetes → ContainerDays (Sep 2–4)
Europe's largest and longest-running container/Kubernetes conference, held at **Schuppen 52** — an Imperial-era warehouse on the water with Hamburg-skyline views [[12]](https://www.containerdays.io/containerdays-hamburg-2026/). 2026 program leans hard into Kubernetes internals, eBPF, network policy, runtime security, multi-cluster architectures, platform engineering economics, and an expanded EU regulatory track [[12]](https://www.containerdays.io/containerdays-hamburg-2026/). Wed–Fri timing → arrive Tue, conference through Fri, full weekend in Hamburg after.

### Best for HPC / AI / quantum research → ISC High Performance (Jun 22–26)
Europe's leading HPC, AI & Quantum conference + exhibition, at CCH. 3,500+ scientists, researchers and engineers, alongside 200 technology providers; opening keynote by Prof. Martin Schulz on "HPC: A Heterogeneous Future" [[9]](https://isc-hpc.com/). Heavy week — tutorials Mon, conference + expo Tue–Thu, workshops Fri. Not a casual weekend visit but the Sat after empties out nicely for a calm dinner.

### Best music + tech crossover → Reeperbahn Festival (Sep 16–19)
A four-day music festival across ~70 St. Pauli venues paired with a serious B2B conference for the music + digital industries — MusicTech, EventTech, ticketing, digital audio [[14]](https://www.reeperbahnfestival.com/en). 2026 adds a first-ever start-up contest with prizes >€15,000 for music-industry tools (mentoring, 6 months workspace, 3 conference tickets, pitch slot) [[20]](https://www.reeperbahnfestival.com/call-start-ups). Wed–Sat means the festival straddles a full weekend — the strongest tech+culture combo of the year for a Hamburg weekend trip. Note: **NEXT Conference**, historically Hamburg's parallel digital-business event during Reeperbahn week, is not running in 2026 due to a strategic realignment [[26]](https://nextconf.eu/) — so don't plan around it.

### Best for cybersecurity → Elbsides (Jun 4–5)
Small, community-driven cybersecurity conference at Hotel Hafen Hamburg's Elbkuppel with harbor views; workshop day Thu Jun 4, conference Fri Jun 5 [[7]](https://www.elbsides.eu/2026/). 2026 topics emphasized include quantum threats ("harvest now, decrypt later") and German NIS-2 directive implementation (active national law since Dec 2025) [[7]](https://www.elbsides.eu/2026/). The Fri date lets the weekend stay free.
