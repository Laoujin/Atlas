Decision: Chengdu has two Michelin 2-star restaurants and no 3-stars as of the 2026 edition.[1] Go to Yu Zhi Lan for the quintessential Chengdu experience — elevated Sichuan cuisine in an intimate, chef-owned 18-seat house, 2-star since the guide launched in 2022.[1] Go to Xin Rong Ji for luxury Taizhou seafood with Sichuan touches in a sleek high-rise setting — if Sichuan spice isn't your focus.[2]

The Two Restaurants

★★

Yu Zhi Lan 玉芝兰

Michelin 2-Star every edition since 2022 · Modern Sichuan
Chef
Lan Guijun — owner-chef, local legend; also handcrafts the pottery decorating the dining room[4]
Cuisine
Modern Sichuan fine dining with restraint — fermented black beans, aged vinegars, Sichuan peppercorns at measured heat; technique over intensity[3]
Setting
Unmarked old house, 18 seats across 3 rooms; rustic decor with chef's ceramic art; deliberately low-key — no signage outside[5]
Signature dishes
Strange Flavour Noodles · Fish Maw Mushroom Soup · Morchella two ways · Sea Cucumber Hot-Sour Noodles · Lobster Dumplings[5]
Menu
~20-course degustation (cold bites + hot dishes); market-dependent; dietary restrictions accommodated at booking; printed English menu provided[6]
Price/person
~600–2,380 RMB (set menu tier; ~1,300 RMB typical report)[6]
Address
No. 1, 24 Changfa Street, Qingyang District, Chengdu[4]
Phone
+86 28 6249 1966
Booking
Call directly or via hotel concierge; 50% non-refundable deposit required; book 1–2 weeks ahead minimum[6]
Other accolades
World's 50 Best Discovery listing[7] · Featured on Anthony Bourdain's Chengdu episode (2016)[4]
Best for: experiencing Sichuan's highest culinary tradition in a chef-owned, singular setting.
★★

Xin Rong Ji 新荣记

Michelin 2-Star since 2024 · Taizhou (Zhejiang seafood)

Taizhou coastal seafood tradition (Zhejiang province), jazzed up with Sichuan local touches — a deliberate counterpoint to the city's signature spice culture[8]

5th floor of the Chinese Financial Center; lavish but understated interior with views of Chengdu's Twin Towers[2]

Sea anemone glass noodles with house-made pickles · Spicy braised pork intestine with Qiantong dried tofu · Mapo tofu with cod milt[2]
