April is the highest-density window: three overlapping events in 13 days — a 12,000-person AI-media conference (Apr 14–16), the 8-year-old C3 Security summit (Apr 17–18), and an IEEE academic track (Apr 24–26). July brings two large-scale electronics expos at Century City — B2B semiconductors then B2C gadgets. All other months are quiet or carry only community forums.
