Three Qing-era lanes — Wide for tea, Narrow for boutiques. Go before 11 AM or face a human sea.[14]

Heming Teahouse — People's Park local Tea from 16 CNY · 1 hr

Chengdu's communal living room. Gaiwan tea, mahjong tables, ear-cleaning (~100 RMB). Optional — skip if you want more museum time.[15]

13:00
Lunch + transit to Wuhou optional Metro or DiDi

Keep it light — dan dan noodles (12–15 RMB) or wontons if you pass a street stall. Save appetite for dinner.

14:00
Wuhou Shrine essential CNY 50 · opens 9:00–18:00 · 1.5 hrs

Three Kingdoms temple to strategist Zhuge Liang. Flows directly into Jinli street next door — plan to exit there.[5]

17:00
Jinli Ancient Street essential Free · best after 7:30 PM

Lantern-lit food-and-craft street. Red lanterns light ~7:30 PM in summer. Snacks are tourist-priced — walk to Kuixinglou St for cheaper eats.[13]

20:00
★★ Michelin Dinner anchor

Yu Zhi Lan — 2 km from Jinli, Changfa St, Qingyang. ~20-course degustation, 600–2,380 RMB.[2] Or Xin Rong Ji — Wuhou District financial center, taxi from anywhere.[3]

Quick Logistics Reference
Payment

Link Visa/Mastercard to Alipay AND WeChat Pay. Set up early — 24–72 h verification window since late 2025.[7] Carry some cash too.

Getting around

Metro QR via Alipay. DiDi (ride-share) runs as an English mini-program inside WeChat — no separate app needed.

Airports

Shuangliu (CTU) is ~16 km; Tianfu (TFU) is ~50–60 km — express metro 33 min to South Railway Station (hourly, 6 AM–11 PM).

Best time to visit

April and October (15–22 °C, active pandas). Avoid Golden Week Oct 1–7 — in 2026, Mid-Autumn Sep 25–27 precedes it, doubling crowds.

Where to stay

Chunxi Road / Taikoo Li: central, Metro Lines 2 & 3, walkable to food and nightlife.

Visa

240-hour (10-day) visa-free transit covers 54 countries incl. US/UK/CA via Tianfu Airport. Must hold onward ticket to a third country.
