Oslo tech weeks,
and what they do to hotel night one.

Sixteen confirmed dev events fill 2026's Oslo calendar — but only three blocks materially compress downtown room inventory. The grid below colors them in. Mid-September burns hot. Mid-to-late October burns hotter, twice. Everything else is leisure-weekend friendly.

If you don't have a ticket — avoid

Sep 14 – 18
Oct 13 – 23
Oct 26 – 27

A May 2026 weekend lands clear of every hot week on this page — easy inventory window.

Sep 14 – 18 · Mon → Fri

NDC Oslo hot

5-day event at Oslo Spektrum, between Jernbanetorget and Grønland. 154 speakers, 14 workshops. Every Sentrum / Grünerløkka hotel takes a price hit. Book 4–6 weeks out [1].

Oct 13 – 23 · 11-day block

NIC → Oslo Innovation Week hot

Back-to-back. NIC at Oslo Spektrum (13-15) [4], then OIW pulls 16,000 attendees into 50+ city-center venues (19-23) [12]. The 11-day span effectively eats October's middle.

Oct 26 – 27 · Mon → Tue

Y Oslo warm

Scandinavia's biggest UX conference at Chateau Neuf in Majorstuen — hits Frogner / Majorstuen rather than Sentrum, but 2,500+ attendees still bend the market [11].
