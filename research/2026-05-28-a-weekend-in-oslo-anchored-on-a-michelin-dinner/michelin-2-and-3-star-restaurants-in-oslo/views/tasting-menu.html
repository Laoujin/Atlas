The Two Houses & Their Tasting Menus

A diptych for the weekend diner
Late May 2026 · prix-fixe only · dinner service Tue – Sat

Oslo offers two tables in scope: one wears three stars, the other two. Both pour Nordic; both bear the Green Star. They diverge on price, on portion of the evening, and on the patience required to win the seat. Anchor the trip on Maaemo if the 1st-of-the-month lottery and the ~NOK 4,500 cover are welcome [7][9]. Otherwise Kontrast at NOK 2,300 is a confident Plan A [16].

— Service Premier —

MAAEMO

Bjørvika, by the Opera House
★ ★ ★ ☘ Green Star — three stars held since 2022 [1]
— the room, Dronning Eufemias gate 23 —
Chef-Patron Esben Holmboe Bang — Danish-Norwegian; canonical New Nordic [4]
  1. I.
    L'Adresse
    Dronning Eufemias gate 23, 0194 Oslo[8] Waterfront, opposite the Opera House; minimalist, industrial setting close to the old dock[17]
  2. II.
    Le Format
    Set tasting, 20+ courses[9] No à la carte. The menu is the evening — allow three to four hours.
  3. III.
    Le Couvert · Price-per-Cover
    NOK 4 500 ≈ USD 450 per guest[9] Wine pairings included only at certain tiers — confirm at booking.
  4. IV.
    Provenance
    Every ingredient Norwegian, seasonal, organic / biodynamic / wild — mapped to its source for the guest[10]. Holds the Michelin Green Star for sustainable gastronomy[2].
  5. V.
    Les Heures
    Tuesday – Saturday, 18:00 – 24:00 [18] Dinner only. Closed Sunday and Monday.
  6. VI.
    La Réservation · Lead Time
    Booked through Tock[6]. Tables released the 1st of each month, 12:00 CET, for dates ~two months out[7]. Set the alarm. For a late-July weekend, the drop is 1 June at noon. Have a fallback weekend ready.
  7. VII.
    Historique
    Opened Dec 2010; first Nordic restaurant ever to win 2 stars on debut (Mar 2012); 3rd star Feb 2016; closed Grønland original 2019; relocated Bjørvika 2021; reclaimed all three Sep 2021[3].
— Service Second —

KONTRAST

Vulkan, on the Akerselva
★ ★ ☘ Green Star — two stars since 2024 (upgraded from one) [1]
— signature plating; simplicity over complexity —
Chef-Patron Mikael Svensson — Swedish, from Skåne; co-founded b.culture in 2020 [13]
  1. I.
    L'Adresse
    Maridalsveien 15, 0175 Oslo[12] Ex-industrial Vulkan development beside the Akerselva river — stark interiors, an industrial edge[14]
  2. II.
    Le Format
    Big Kontrasts tasting menu[16] Contemporary Nordic; simple presentation masking complex technique[14].
  3. III.
    Le Couvert · Price-per-Cover
    NOK 2 300 ≈ USD 230 per guest[16] Roughly half of Maaemo's cover; leaves room in the budget for the pairing.
  4. IV.
    La Cave · Wine Pairing
    Standard NOK 1 700 · Upgraded NOK 2 450 · Alcohol-free NOK 945[16] Three tiers; the alcohol-free tier is fully developed, not an afterthought.
  5. V.
    Provenance
    ~98 % of produce sourced locally[11]; b.culture ferments the kitchen's byproducts into new ingredients[13]. Sustainability is operational, not marketing.
  6. VI.
    Les Heures
    Tuesday – Saturday, dinner service[14] Same Tue–Sat window as Maaemo — either pairs with a Fri or Sat anchor.
  7. VII.
    La Réservation · Lead Time
    Direct on the restaurant's own platform, kontrast.superbexperience.com[12]. No monthly drop; bookable continuously. Materially easier than Maaemo; weekends visible weeks ahead.
  8. VIII.
    Historique
    Opened 2013; first star 2016; promoted to two stars 2024, alongside the Green Star the same year[15][11].

  1. For the three-star landmark, willing to plan around the seat: Maaemo. Treat the reservation as the trip's first move; every other choice cascades.
  2. For a two-star Nordic at roughly half-cover, with a forgiving booking: Kontrast. Substantially easier to slot into a weekend already on rails[16].
  3. The risk-managed plan: apply for Maaemo on the 1st, but also hold a Kontrast slot for the same weekend that you can cancel free if Maaemo confirms. Both serve Tue–Sat dinner only — a Friday or Saturday anchor fits either[18][14].
