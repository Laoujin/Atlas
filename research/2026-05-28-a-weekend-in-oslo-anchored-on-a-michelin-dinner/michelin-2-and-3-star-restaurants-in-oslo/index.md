> **Decision.** Oslo has exactly two restaurants in scope: [Maaemo][m-site] (3★) and [Kontrast][k-site] (2★). Both are Nordic, both hold a Michelin Green Star. **Anchor the weekend on Maaemo** if you can win the 1st-of-the-month reservation lottery two months out [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g190479-d2447055-t6331138-Can_you_only_book_via_the_website_90_days_before.html) and the ~NOK 4,500 / 20+ course commitment is desirable [[9]](https://www.top25restaurants.com/europe/norway/restaurant/maaemo-oslo/). Otherwise **Kontrast** at NOK 2,300 is a strong Plan A in its own right, with materially easier availability [[16]](https://restaurantsforkings.com/blog/oslo-best-restaurants-proposal-2026.html).

## [Maaemo](https://maaemo.no/) — three stars, Bjørvika

Norway's only 3-star restaurant [[2]](https://www.visitoslo.com/en/product/?tlp=3057333), and the first restaurant in the Nordics ever to reach 2 stars on its first rating (March 2012) [[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maaemo). The third star came in February 2016; the team closed the original Grønland location in December 2019 to relocate to a much larger Bjørvika site near the Opera House, lost all three stars in the 2020 guide, and reclaimed all three in September 2021 [[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maaemo). Chef-owner [Esben Holmboe Bang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esben_Holmboe_Bang) is Danish-Norwegian and one of the canonical New Nordic figures [[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esben_Holmboe_Bang).

The format is a set 20+ course tasting menu, ~NOK 4,500, dinner only Tue–Sat 18:00–24:00 [[9]](https://www.top25restaurants.com/europe/norway/restaurant/maaemo-oslo/) [[18]](https://restaurantguru.com/Maaemo-Oslo). Every ingredient is Norwegian, seasonal, organic / biodynamic / wild — explicitly mapped to its source for the guest [[10]](https://www.cnn.com/travel/article/norway-esben-holmboe-bang-maaemo-oslo/index.html). The room is described by [The World's 50 Best Discovery][wb-m] as "strikingly minimalist, industrial setting close to Oslo's old dock" [[17]][wb-m]. Expect a 3–4 hour evening.

[wb-m]: https://www.theworlds50best.com/discovery/Establishments/Norway/Oslo/Maaemo.html

⚠ **Booking is the bottleneck.** Maaemo releases tables via [Tock](https://www.exploretock.com/maaemo) [[6]](https://www.exploretock.com/maaemo) on the **1st of each month at 12:00 CET, opening dates ~2 months out** [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ_Answers-g190479-d2447055-t6331138-Can_you_only_book_via_the_website_90_days_before.html). A weekend in, e.g., late July → set an alarm for 1 June 12:00 CET. Have a backup weekend in case the slot you want is gone within minutes.

## [Kontrast](https://restaurant-kontrast.no/) — two stars, Vulkan

Opened 2013, first star 2016, promoted to two stars in 2024 alongside a Michelin Green Star the same year [[15]](https://eatweekguide.com/kontrast-oslo/) [[11]](https://guide.michelin.com/ie/en/article/sustainable-gastronomy/kontrast-oslo-s-new-two-michelin-star-restaurant). Chef-owner [Mikael Svensson](https://www.image.ie/living/chef-mikael-svensson-of-the-two-michelin-starred-kontrast-in-oslo-shares-his-life-in-food-982381) is Swedish (Skåne); in 2020 he co-founded [b.culture](https://www.image.ie/living/chef-mikael-svensson-of-the-two-michelin-starred-kontrast-in-oslo-shares-his-life-in-food-982381) to upcycle Kontrast's byproducts via fermentation — the sustainability story is operational, not marketing veneer [[13]](https://www.image.ie/living/chef-mikael-svensson-of-the-two-michelin-starred-kontrast-in-oslo-shares-his-life-in-food-982381). About **98% of the kitchen's produce is sourced locally** [[11]](https://guide.michelin.com/ie/en/article/sustainable-gastronomy/kontrast-oslo-s-new-two-michelin-star-restaurant).

The room sits in the Vulkan ex-industrial development next to the Akerselva river — stark, minimalist, an "industrial edge"; the food's signature is "simple presentation [masking] the complex nature of what's served" [[14]](https://www.theworlds50best.com/discovery/Establishments/Norway/Oslo/Kontrast.html). Big Kontrasts tasting NOK 2,300; wine pairing NOK 1,700 (standard), NOK 2,450 (upgraded), or NOK 945 alcohol-free [[16]](https://restaurantsforkings.com/blog/oslo-best-restaurants-proposal-2026.html). Booking is direct on Kontrast's own platform — no monthly drop, much more forgiving [[12]](https://restaurant-kontrast.no/).

## How to choose

- **Want the 3-star landmark dinner, willing to plan the trip around the reservation:** Maaemo. Treat the booking as the first thing you lock in; everything else flexes around it.
- **Want a 2-star Nordic tasting at roughly half the price, with a relaxed booking process:** Kontrast. Substantially easier to fit into a weekend that's already on rails.
- **Risk-managed plan:** apply for Maaemo on the 1st of the month, but also hold a Kontrast slot for the same weekend you can cancel free if Maaemo comes through. Both restaurants are dinner only and run Tue–Sat, so a Friday or Saturday anchor works for either [[18]](https://restaurantguru.com/Maaemo-Oslo) [[14]](https://www.theworlds50best.com/discovery/Establishments/Norway/Oslo/Kontrast.html).
