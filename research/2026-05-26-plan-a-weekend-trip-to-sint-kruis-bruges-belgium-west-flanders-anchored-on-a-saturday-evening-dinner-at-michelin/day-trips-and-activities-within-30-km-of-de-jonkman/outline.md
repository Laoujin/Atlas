Bruges historic centre: must-see sights, museums, and architectural highlights within walking distance of De Jonkman (Sint-Kruis, ~2 km from Markt).
Damme and the Bruges canal: cycling/walking route from Bruges, town highlights (Damme bookshop, ramparts, Onze-Lieve-Vrouwekerk, Tijl Uilenspiegel).
Belgian North Sea coast within 30 km: Knokke-Heist (Het Zwin nature park, beach art), Blankenberge (pier, beach), Oostende (Beaufort sculpture trail, James Ensor) — what's worth a half-day.
Polders, lesser-known villages, and cross-border options: Lissewege (white village), Oostkerke, Hoeke, Sluis (NL) for lunch/shopping, polders by bike.
Outdoor recreation near Bruges: cycling knooppunten network, walking trails, nature reserves (Tillegembos, Beisbroek, Bulskampveld), castles (Loppem, Tillegem, Male).
Family-friendly and rainy-day activities within 30 km: Choco-Story, Historium, Boudewijn Seapark, Sea Life Blankenberge, Mu.ZEE, Atlantikwall Raversyde.
