TL;DR — pick by interest:
  • One headline day: Windsor Castle + Long Walk + Eton Walkway. Castle is 10 min from Bray, £32 advance[1], closed Tue/Wed[2].
  • Best riverside walk: Thames Path Henley → Marlow, 8.7 miles past Hambleden Lock and Temple Island[44].
  • One stately home: Cliveden — 10 miles, NT estate, Cliveden Reach is the prettiest river view in southern England[25].
  • With kids: Legoland (5 km, 30th-birthday year with Minifigure Speedway)[59] or Bekonscot model village.
  • Time it with a fixture: Royal Ascot (16–20 Jun)[74], Henley Royal Regatta (30 Jun–5 Jul)[77], Cartier Queen's Cup polo (14 Jun)[80].
  • Skip: Henley's River & Rowing Museum (closed Sep 2025)[16]; Whipsnade Zoo (61 km, outside radius)[73]; Newbury Racecourse (56 km)[88].

Thames riverside towns

Henley-on-Thames

18 km · 16 min[14]
"Town of Festivals" with 300+ historic buildings, St Mary's, the Town Hall and Henley Bridge[15]. The Hambleden Mill circular (6.1 mi) takes in Temple Island and Fawley Court — said to be the Toad Hall inspiration[17]. ⚠ The River & Rowing Museum closed permanently 21 Sep 2025[16].

Marlow

11 km · 15–20 min[18]
"England's riverside gastronomic hub", anchored by Tom Kerridge's Hand & Flowers — the UK's only two-Michelin-star pub[19]. Centrepieces: William Tierney Clark's 1832 suspension bridge and All Saints by the river[20], with the 400-year-old Compleat Angler at the bridgehead[21].

Cookham

6 km · 10 min[23]
Compact village trip built around the Stanley Spencer Gallery — daily 10:30–16:30 from 4 April 2026, £9 entry, free "Revealing Genius" workbook and Bloomberg Connects audio guide[22].

Maidenhead

~3 km · 5 min
Boulter's Lock and Ray Mill Island, rebuilt 1912 after the Thames Conservancy bought the island in 1909 — Edwardian boating landmark and a kayaking site with a summer canoe flume[24]. Launchpad for paddleboarding[53].

Eton

Across Windsor footbridge
A mile-long independents-led High Street[26] paired with the 2-mile Eton Walkway through 18 bronze-marker points[27].

Sonning

~16 km
The Mill at Sonning — dinner-theatre on a Thames island; 2026 season includes Anything Goes and Shear Madness[28]. The 600-year-old Bull Inn is the Thames Path stop[29].

Wallingford

Outer edge of 30 km
Worth the boundary trip for William the Conqueror's 1067 castle ruins, a 19-arch medieval Thames bridge, Wallingford Museum and Agatha Christie heritage trails[30].

Cycling

NCN4 runs east–west through the Royal Borough — Egham, Windsor, Eton, Bray, Maidenhead, Cookham, towards Henley[51]. Inside Windsor Great Park (free, 7 am–dusk), cycles are allowed everywhere except the Long Walk, Deer Park and Valley Gardens; groups capped at 6 riders, max 2 abreast[52].

What to skip

  • Henley River & Rowing Museum — closed permanently 21 Sep 2025[16].
  • Eton College Heritage Tours — cancelled all 2026 for building works; only the free Sunday museums remain[5].
  • Mapledurham House — currently closed to the general public[41].
  • Whipsnade Zoo (38 mi from Windsor)[73] and Newbury Racecourse (35 mi)[88] — both outside the 30 km radius.
  • "Maidenhead Carnival" as a single-day event doesn't exist in 2026 — it's a year-round distributed festival programme instead[86].
