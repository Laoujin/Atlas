> **TL;DR** — Within 30 km of Bray, the tech-event scene is concentrated in Reading (~20 km west). Free monthly developer meetups cover most of the calendar — **Reading Geek Night** (2nd Tue) [[2]](https://rdggeeknight.wordpress.com/), **Reading Rust Workshop** (2nd Wed) [[11]](https://rustworkshop.co/meetup/), **.NET Thames Valley** (bimonthly) [[5]](https://www.meetup.com/dot-net-thames-valley/) — plus a hybrid **ISC2 Thames Valley** cybersecurity series running 7 dates Apr-Dec 2026 [[7]](https://isc2tvch.co.uk/events.html). For multi-day conferences in radius there are exactly two: **DigiMarCon UK** at Heathrow T5 (3-4 Sep 2026) [[12]](https://digimarconuk.co.uk/) and the **ECMWF Annual Seminar** in Reading on ML for Earth-system assimilation (14-18 Sep 2026) [[13]](https://research.reading.ac.uk/met-darc/news-and-events/conferences/). Anything bigger means a 36-40 km trip to central London.

## Geography

Bray sits ~3 km south of Maidenhead, in the middle of the Thames Valley tech corridor. Microsoft's UK headquarters at Thames Valley Park is ~5 km away [[20]](https://www.microsoft.com/en-gb/about/offices/reading/), but the corporate campus doesn't run a public events programme — community life happens in Reading town centre, ~20 km west, and at Henley Business School's Greenlands campus, ~14 km north-west. Heathrow T5 (~15 km east) is also in radius; central London (Paddington) is ~36 km and falls outside.

Reading Geek Night is the longest-running and broadest of these — it's run by the BCS Berkshire branch (2,500+ members) and welcomes non-members [[8]](https://www.bcs.org/membership-and-registrations/member-communities/berkshire-branch/). Talks are 20-25 min plus Q&A, then networking downstairs at the bar [[2]](https://rdggeeknight.wordpress.com/). The published 2026 calendar has 12 dates locked in (13 Jan STEAM social, 10 Feb, 10 Mar, 14 Apr, 12 May, 9 Jun, 14 Jul, 11 Aug, 8 Sep, 13 Oct, 10 Nov, 8 Dec break) [[1]](https://rdggeeknight.wordpress.com/event-calendar/). The Rust workshop's next confirmed date is Wed 11 Feb 2026 at 6pm [[4]](https://www.meetup.com/reading-rust-workshop/events/csvcvtyjcdbpb/).

ISC2 Thames Valley is the only series that publishes a full 2026 talks schedule in advance — 7 hybrid sessions from May to December at Thirsty Bear or CGI's Reading office, covering AWS hybrid networking, IAM with Microsoft Entra, ISO 27001/42001 integration, and "How compliance gets companies hacked" [[7]](https://isc2tvch.co.uk/events.html). Free, open to non-members.

The Thames Valley AI HUB (TVAI) is the academic-linked AI track — University of Reading-supported, with recent events including an NVIDIA AI Day on the Whiteknights campus and panels on AI in childhood and recruitment [[6]](https://www.meetup.com/thames-valley-ai-hub/). The annual TVAI Summit (launched September 2025) is the closest thing to a regional AI conference in radius.

ECMWF's seminar is academic and free for ECMWF Member-State affiliates — narrow scope (data assimilation in numerical weather prediction) but real ML content [[13]](https://research.reading.ac.uk/met-darc/news-and-events/conferences/).

## Henley Business School (AI executive education)

[Henley's Greenlands campus](https://www.henley.ac.uk/) sits on the Thames at ~14 km from Bray. It runs **AI Strategy and Implementation** as an in-person executive day on 3 June 2026 at Greenlands [[14]](https://www.henley.ac.uk/study/corporate-development/henley-ai-strategy-and-implementation), and a free online "Meet the AI Programme Directors" webinar on 12 February 2026 [[15]](https://www.henley.ac.uk/events/meet-henleys-ai-programme-directors). These are paid programmes (Strategy & Implementation is corporate-development priced), not community meetups — useful only if you're after MBA-flavoured AI exec content rather than developer track.

## Maker / repair scene

Reading Hackspace (rLab) runs Wednesday-evening open nights at Weldale Street [[9]](https://www.meetup.com/reading-hackspace/) and hosts the Reading Repair Cafe — confirmed dates include 19 April 2026 (12:30-4:30pm) at 10 Weldale St [[19]](https://allevents.in/reading/feburary-repair-cafe-rlab/200029377605228). The "STEAM Meetup Groups" social on 13 January 2026 was a Reading Geek Night joint event that brought together rLab, the rust workshop, and other groups under one roof [[18]](https://rdggeeknight.wordpress.com/2025/12/09/reading-steam-meetup-groups-new-year-social-tuesday-13th-january-2026/) — worth tracking for next year's edition.

These don't make the radius but are the next-nearest tech events:

If you're on a Bray weekend, the realistic plan is: a Tuesday-evening pint at Reading Geek Night at Zero Degrees, or a Wednesday at the Biscuit Factory for Rust. For a multi-day conference, only DigiMarCon at Heathrow T5 in early September is genuinely in radius — the rest of the tech-conference calendar lives in central London.
