Bray-centred 30 km radius

A year of meetups,
two conferences,
one booking trap.

Within 30 km of Bray the tech-event scene is concentrated in Reading (~20 km west). Six free meetup series carry the calendar; the only multi-day conferences are DigiMarCon UK at Heathrow T5 (3–4 Sep) and the ECMWF Annual Seminar in Reading (14–18 Sep). Anything bigger is a 36–40 km trip to central London.

§ 02  ·  The two big weeks

September is the only multi-day month

both inside 30 km · both hybrid · zero overlap
Conf · 2-day
Thu 3 – Fri 4 Sep 2026

DigiMarCon UK

Heathrow T5 venue ~15 km Hybrid Marketing/ad-tech

The only multi-day commercial tech conference physically inside the Bray radius — the usual global DigiMarCon menu of social, SEO, content and ad-tech tracks [12]. Thu–Fri pairs cleanly with a Friday-night three-star dinner.

digimarconuk.co.uk 15 km E of Bray

ECMWF Annual Seminar

Reading ~20 km 5 days ML / weather

"Advancing the assimilation of Earth-system observations with new methodology and machine learning" — academic, free for Member-State affiliates, narrow scope but real ML content [13].

research.reading.ac.uk 20 km W of Bray

Henley AI Strategy & Implementation

Greenlands campus ~14 km 1 day MBA-flavoured

In-person executive day at Henley Business School's Greenlands estate on the Thames [14]. Corporate-development priced — useful if you want AI exec content rather than developer track. A free "Meet the AI Programme Directors" webinar runs 12 Feb [15].

henley.ac.uk 14 km NW of Bray

The Tuesday–Wednesday trap a weekend in Bray + a meetup in Reading is structurally impossible

  • Tue 2nd Reading Geek Night runs 2nd Tue from 7pm at Zero Degrees — same night both three-stars are closed [2]
  • Wed 2nd Rust Workshop at Biscuit Factory · plus rLab's weekly open night · Windsor Castle is also shut Tue–Wed [11]
  • Conflict Fat Duck and Waterside Inn both close Mon–Tue. A Bray weekend + a meetup needs an extra weekday tacked on. parent expedition
