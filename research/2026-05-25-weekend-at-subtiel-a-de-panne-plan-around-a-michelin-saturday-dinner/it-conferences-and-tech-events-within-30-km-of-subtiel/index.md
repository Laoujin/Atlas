> **TL;DR** — Subtiel is at Belgium's western corner, so 30 km is sparse for IT conferences. The single in-radius event overlapping your trip is the **[Blue Innovation Village at Ostend at Anchor](https://www.bluecluster.be/calendar/blue-innovation-village-at-ostend-at-anchor-2026), 28-31 May 2026** in Oostende [[8]](https://www.bluecluster.be/calendar/blue-innovation-village-at-ostend-at-anchor-2026) — borderline ~30 km, blue-economy / marine-tech focus, free. The two recurring anchors — **Belgian Offshore Days** (Oostende, March) and **Dunkerque Digital Day** (May) — are already past for 2026; next editions land in 2027. For a generalist dev/IT conference, the nearest is **Devoxx Belgium** in Antwerp, October 2026 [[18]](https://devoxx.be/) — ~145 km, outside scope.

## Geographic scope (Subtiel as origin)

Subtiel is at Westhoeklaan 31, 8660 De Panne — a one-star Michelin restaurant just outside the village centre [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/west-vlaanderen/de-panne/restaurant/subtiel). De Panne is the terminus of the Belgian Coast Tram (67 km to Knokke-Heist) [[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coast_Tram). A 30 km radius reaches into both Flanders and France:

## Year-round ecosystem (who runs things in the radius)

When there's no scheduled conference, this is where local-tech activity happens — useful to know if you want to drop in on a coworking space or check for a one-off meetup.

### Oostende (BE) — blue-economy / maritime tech

- **[Bluebridge / Ostend Science Park](https://ostendsciencepark.be/what-we-do-2/)** — UGent-campus incubator for aquaculture, blue energy, marine biotech, coastal protection, blue tourism; 150-seat meeting + 200-cap event space [[13]](https://ostendsciencepark.be/what-we-do-2/) [[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenbridge_science_park).
- **[The Beacon](https://www.thebeacon.eu/)** — Antwerp-HQ digital-innovation hub that runs *Smart Port Week* visits into Oostende's Maritime/Hydraulics Laboratory (Towing Tank), Skills Lab (data, AR/VR/XR, offshore), and live autonomous-vessel demos [[12]](https://www.thebeacon.eu/innovation/projects/smart-port-week/).
- **[Blue Cluster](https://www.bluecluster.be/)** — orchestrates the calendar above; the place to subscribe for surprise sessions.
- **[POM West-Vlaanderen](https://www.pomwvl.be/)** — provincial development agency; co-organises Belgian Offshore Days [[19]](https://www.belgianoffshoredays.be/en/).

### Dunkerque (FR) — French Tech Côte d'Opale

- **[La French Tech Littoral Hauts-de-France](https://la-frenchtech-littoral-hdf.fr/)** — labelled since 2019; members get priority invites to AI / FIML / afterwork sessions across Dunkerque-Calais-Boulogne-Montreuil; membership runs Sept 2025 → Dec 2026 [[7]](https://la-frenchtech-littoral-hdf.fr/).
- **[Spark Lab](https://www.coworking-france.com/espace-coworking/spark-lab/)** — Dunkerque's first FabLab (since Jan 2018); workshops, digital fabrication, community events [[15]](https://www.coworking-france.com/espace-coworking/spark-lab/).
- **Communauté Urbaine de Dunkerque + Club Tactic Littoral** — co-organisers of Dunkerque Digital Day [[4]](https://www.hautsdefrance-id.fr/agenda/dunkerque-digital-day/).

### Westhoek (Veurne / Ieper / Diksmuide / Poperinge)

No tech-conference programme runs here. Voka West-Vlaanderen's 2026 agenda for the Westhoek is land-use, recruitment and SME growth — not events [[16]](https://www.voka.be/west-vlaanderen/nieuws/voka-nieuwjaarsboodschap-2026-regio-westhoek).
