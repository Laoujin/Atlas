The Skip List · sounds close, isn't

Wrong side of the reserve

Ara Dune Hotel

Reads "near the Westhoek" but sits on the far side of the nature reserve at Duinhoekstraat 164 — 3.8 km from the reserve boundary. You'd need the car or the coastal tram, defeating the point. [20]

Wrong end of town

Hotel Plopsa

De Pannelaan 68 — Adinkerke, next to the theme park, 3+ km south. Wrong end of town entirely for a fine-dining anchor. [8]

Not a hostellerie

Subtiel on site

No rooms above the restaurant. Subtiel explicitly directs overnight guests to Visit De Panne for lodging — book the bed separately. [17]

Practical notes for a Saturday dinner

Book direct

Rates shown are OTA snapshots (Kayak / Booking). Direct rates on the hotel sites are often equal or lower and avoid OTA cancellation rules — relevant when a Michelin booking might shift dates.

Parking

Subtiel is on a residential street with no dedicated guest lot. Hotel Donny offers free guest parking on-site, which keeps the car-arrival path simple. [5]

Tram fallback

The Kusttram (line KT) runs along the coast through De Panne every 10–20 min daytime, every 30 min in the evening; "De Panne Esplanade" stop is ~800 m from Subtiel. [21]

The Dumontwijk

De Panne's listed heritage quarter — Art Nouveau, Art Deco and Cottage-style villas a few blocks east of Subtiel. Three of the picks (Parkhotel, Bon Papa, Esprit de Mer) sit inside it. [16]
