> **Decision** — Closest by a wide margin is [Hotel Donny](https://www.hoteldonny.com/en/) on Donnylaan, a ~5-min walk and the only 4-star with full spa within shouting distance of the restaurant [[5]](https://www.hoteldonny.com/en/) [[19]](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?format=json&q=Donnylaan+17,+De+Panne,+Belgium). For heritage atmosphere, pick [Parkhotel](https://www.parkhoteldepanne.be/en) or [B&B Villa Bon Papa](https://www.villabonpapa.be/en) in the listed Dumontwijk (~12-min walk) [[7]](https://www.parkhoteldepanne.be/en) [[15]](https://www.villabonpapa.be/en). For toes-in-the-sand, [Hotel Villa Select](https://hotelvillaselect.be/en/) is 33 ft from the beach but a ~19-min walk back after dinner [[9]](https://hotelvillaselect.be/en/). Subtiel itself has no rooms — the restaurant directs all overnight guests to Visit De Panne [[17]](https://www.subtiel-dining.be/en/spending-the-night).

## What "walking distance" means here

[Subtiel](https://www.subtiel-dining.be/en) (1 Michelin star, 2026 guide) is at Westhoeklaan 31, 8660 De Panne — a converted villa "just outside the town centre" rather than on the seafront [[1]](https://www.subtiel-dining.be/en) [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/west-vlaanderen/de-panne/restaurant/subtiel) [[3]](https://www.gaultmillau.be/en/restaurants/subtiel-de-panne). OpenStreetMap geocodes the door to 51.0935 N, 2.5831 E [[4]](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?format=json&q=Westhoeklaan+31,+De+Panne,+Belgium). The straight-line and walking-time figures below are computed from that fix at 5 km/h; actual paths can be ~10–25 % longer than the crow-flies line.

## The picks

### If you want the shortest walk back

[Hotel Donny](https://www.hoteldonny.com/en/) — Donnylaan 17, 4 stars, 45 rooms, indoor heated pool, sauna, gym, free parking and EV chargers [[5]](https://www.hoteldonny.com/en/) [[6]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/donny.en-gb.html). Donnylaan branches off the same residential block as Westhoeklaan; you can walk to Subtiel in well under ten minutes. Recent Kayak bookings from ~$201/night [[6]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/donny.en-gb.html). This is the obvious default unless something on the list below speaks louder to you.

### If you want Dumontwijk charm

The Dumontwijk is De Panne's listed heritage quarter — Art Nouveau, Art Deco and Cottage-style villas a few blocks east of Subtiel. The official tourist B&B index lists all three of the options below [[16]](https://www.depanne.be/en/unique-bbs).

- **[Parkhotel De Panne](https://www.parkhoteldepanne.be/en)** — Albert Dumontlaan 30, ~12-min walk. 39–44 modern rooms wrapped around a 2000 m² garden, 200 m from the beach [[7]](https://www.parkhoteldepanne.be/en) [[18]](https://www.depanne.be/en/node/3402). Hotel comforts in a heritage setting.
- **[B&B Villa Bon Papa](https://www.villabonpapa.be/en)** — A. Dumontlaan 5, ~13-min walk. A 1924 villa on the listed townscape, three rooms, 9.6/10 guest score [[15]](https://www.villabonpapa.be/en). Pick this over the hotel if you want intimacy over amenities.
- **[B&B Esprit de Mer](https://espritdemer.be/)** — Visserslaan 10, ~12-min walk. Former doctor's house, three rooms, ranked #1 of De Panne's B&Bs on Tripadvisor (4.9/5 from 47 reviews) [[13]](https://espritdemer.be/). Books out early on summer Saturdays.

### If you want the beach at your feet

- **[Hotel Villa Select](https://hotelvillaselect.be/en/)** — Walckierstraat 7, ~17-min walk. Quite literally on the dyke; sea-view rooms, indoor pool, sauna, Turkish bath, in-house French restaurant for breakfast and dinner [[9]](https://hotelvillaselect.be/en/). The trade-off: the walk back from Subtiel is the longest of the recommendable set.
- **[Hotel Iris](https://www.hotel-iris.be/en)** — Duinkerkelaan 41, ~12-min walk. 4-star, 24 rooms, 30 m to the sand, seasonal outdoor pool, balconies with garden view [[12]](https://www.hotel-iris.be/en). The best beach-plus-short-walk compromise on the list.

### If budget matters more than stars

- **[Hotel Sfinx](https://hotel-sfinx.belgiancoast.net/en/)** — Sloepenlaan 10, ~15-min walk. 2 stars, 15 rooms, simple but clean, near the Markt; KAYAK lists rooms from ~$103/night [[10]](https://hotel-sfinx.belgiancoast.net/en/).
- **[B&B 't Zeetje](https://tzeetje-guesthouse.com/)** — Kasteelstraat 35, ~10-min walk. Centre-of-town guesthouse, private check-in, bike parking, homemade breakfast [[14]](https://tzeetje-guesthouse.com/).

## Skip list

- **[Ara Dune Hotel](https://www.ara-hotel.be/)** — Duinhoekstraat 164. Reads "near the Westhoek" but is on the *far* side of the nature reserve, 3.8 km from the reserve boundary [[20]](https://www.ara-hotel.be/). Not walking distance to Subtiel; you'd need the car or coastal tram.
- **[Hotel Cajou](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/cajoueuropedepanne.html)** ⚠ — Nieuwpoortlaan 42. Borderline at ~19 min; doable in summer twilight, less appealing in a January gale.
- **[Hotel Plopsa](https://www.depanne.be/en/hotels)** — De Pannelaan 68. Adinkerke, next to the theme park, 3+ km south [[8]](https://www.depanne.be/en/hotels). Wrong end of town for a fine-dining anchor.

## Practical notes for a Saturday dinner

- **No hostellerie on site.** Subtiel is dining only — book lodging separately [[17]](https://www.subtiel-dining.be/en/spending-the-night).
- **Tram fallback.** The Kusttram (line KT) runs along the coast through De Panne every 10–20 min daytime, every 30 min in the evening; the "De Panne Esplanade" stop is ~800 m from Subtiel [[21]](https://www.delijn.be/en/lijnen/5500/). Useful if you stay further east toward Sint-Idesbald or Koksijde.
- **Parking.** Subtiel is on a residential street with no dedicated guest lot. Hotel Donny offers free guest parking on-site, which keeps the car-arrival path simple [[5]](https://www.hoteldonny.com/en/).
- **Book direct.** Prices shown above are OTA snapshots (KAYAK / Booking); direct rates on the hotel sites are often equal or lower and avoid OTA cancellation rules — relevant when a Michelin booking might shift dates.
