## The shape of the weekend

The anchor is fixed: dinner Saturday at [Subtiel](https://www.subtiel-dining.be/en), Westhoeklaan 31, a converted villa on the Westhoek edge of De Panne and a one-star in the 2026 Michelin guide [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/west-vlaanderen/de-panne/restaurant/subtiel). Subtiel runs no rooms and explicitly routes diners to Visit De Panne instead of curating a hotel list [[2]](https://www.subtiel-dining.be/en/spending-the-night) — so the no-car-after-dinner constraint forces a real decision rather than a package booking.

## What the two lodging angles converge on

Both lodging sub-topics independently land in the **Dumontwijk** — De Panne's listed Art Nouveau / Art Deco quarter one block east of the restaurant [[3]](https://top.vlaanderen/en/historische-gebouwen/dumontwijk-de-panne/). [Parkhotel De Panne](https://www.parkhoteldepanne.be/en) (39 rooms wrapped around a 2000 m² garden, ~10-min walk) and [B&B Villa Bon Papa](https://www.villabonpapa.be/en) (1924 listed villa, 9.6 on Booking, ~13-min walk) appear on both shortlists [[4]](https://www.parkhoteldepanne.be/en) [[5]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/b-amp-b-villa-bon-papa.html). [Hotel Donny](https://www.hoteldonny.com/en/) wins on raw distance — ~5–7 min on foot from Donnylaan 17 — but trades heritage character for 4-star spa amenities [[6]](https://www.hoteldonny.com/en/). The split between angles is clean: walking-distance ranks by minutes-on-foot; the special-character angle accepts a 7-min taxi to Veurne in exchange for a 1725 farmhouse B&B ([Auberge De Klasse](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641834-d1550488-Reviews-Auberge_De_Klasse-Veurne_West_Flanders_Province.html), Tripadvisor's #1 of 11 Veurne B&Bs) [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641834-d1550488-Reviews-Auberge_De_Klasse-Veurne_West_Flanders_Province.html). The taxi run is €25–35 each way [[8]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/De-Panne/Veurne) — the price of trading the Belgian coast for a Flemish market town.

## Where the activity radius bends the lodging choice

The villages surfaced by lodging (Veurne, Oostduinkerke) reappear in the day-trips angle as half-day destinations in their own right. Veurne's Grote Markt and the Scheldt-Gothic Sint-Walburga church reward a morning even if you're not sleeping there [[9]](https://www.veurne.be/en/sint-walburga-church); Oostduinkerke is where the UNESCO horseback shrimp fishers ride — ~40 free 45-min demos Apr–Oct, with confirmed 2026 dates that include the **27–28 June Garnaalfeesten** weekend [[10]](https://www.navigomuseum.be/en/when-do-horseback-fishers-go-out-sea) [[11]](https://oostduinkerke.com/en/event/680/shrimp-festival-and-shrimp-parade-in-oostduinkerke.html). The radius itself is honest about what it excludes: Ypres at 37 km / 39 min is **just outside** [[12]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ieper/De-Panne), so the natural WWI day stays north of the Salient — Nieuwpoort's [King Albert I Westfront](https://www.visit-nieuwpoort.be/en/westfront-nieuwpoort-0) (€9.50) [[13]](https://www.visit-nieuwpoort.be/en/westfront-nieuwpoort-0) and Diksmuide's [IJzertoren](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/museum-aan-de-ijzer-ijzertoren-diksmuide-1506/tickets-prices) (€11.50) [[14]](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/museum-aan-de-ijzer-ijzertoren-diksmuide-1506/tickets-prices) carry the front-line story without leaving the radius.

## The tech-event angle, honestly

A general-purpose dev conference inside 30 km does not exist for 2026. The only overlap event is **[Blue Innovation Village @ Ostend at Anchor, 28–31 May 2026](https://www.bluecluster.be/calendar/blue-innovation-village-at-ostend-at-anchor-2026)** — free, blue-economy, and at the very edge of the radius at ~30 km [[15]](https://www.bluecluster.be/calendar/blue-innovation-village-at-ostend-at-anchor-2026). [Devoxx Belgium](https://devoxx.be/) (5–9 Oct 2026, Antwerp) is the regional dev anchor but sits ~145 km out [[16]](https://devoxx.be/). Treat tech as opportunistic, not a planning input.

## Open question the children did not close

None of the four sub-topics verified the **Subtiel reservation mechanics** that the parent brief named as a constraint feeding every angle — tasting-menu vs à-la-carte pricing for 2026, wine-pairing cost, meal length (which sets the Saturday time anchor), dress code, and how far ahead a Michelin one-star villa in a 30-seat seaside village books. The lodging children confirmed that Subtiel has no rooms and that the table does **not** book with a room [[2]](https://www.subtiel-dining.be/en/spending-the-night), which is the load-bearing fact, but the rest still needs a phone call to **+32 58 41 53 70** or the [restaurant's reservation page](https://www.subtiel-dining.be/en) before any of the lodging picks below get booked.
