> **TL;DR**
> - **One hike from the village**: pick a [Genießerpfad](https://www.baiersbronn.de/natur-aktivitaeten/wandern/geniesserpfade) — Sankenbachsteig is the classic (12.7 km, ~430 m climb, manually-sluiced 40 m waterfall) [[3]](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/e748097189/baiersbronner-sankenbachsteig-schwarzwaelder-geniesserpfad).
> - **One half-day on the [B500 Schwarzwaldhochstraße](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwarzwaldhochstra%C3%9Fe)**: Schliffkopf summit → Lotharpfad storm boardwalk → Mummelsee → Ruhestein national-park centre, all within 25 km [[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwarzwaldhochstra%C3%9Fe)[[17]](https://www.blackforest-highlights.com/poi/detail/nationalparkzentrum-ruhestein-fa286a02f5).
> - **One half-day for [Freudenstadt](https://www.freudenstadt.de/City-history-of-Freudenstadt)** (7 km — Germany's largest market square) [[22]](https://www.freudenstadt.de/City-history-of-Freudenstadt), then loop home via the [Allerheiligen waterfalls and ruined monastery](https://www.renchtal-tourismus.de/en/poi/5-all-saints-waterfalls/) (~25 km, 90 m of cascades below a Gothic ruin) [[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Saints'_Abbey_(Baden-W%C3%BCrttemberg))[[36]](https://www.renchtal-tourismus.de/en/poi/5-all-saints-waterfalls/).
> - **Beer-and-cloister day**: [Alpirsbach](https://www.kloster-alpirsbach.de/en/visitor-information) (17 km) for the Benedictine monastery and the [Klosterbräu](https://alpirsbacher.de/besuchen/museumsfuehrungen/) brewery tour — combi-ticket bundles both [[43]](https://www.kloster-alpirsbach.de/en/visitor-information)[[47]](https://alpirsbacher.de/besuchen/museumsfuehrungen/).
> - **The stretch trip**: [Bad Wildbad](https://www.bad-wildbad.de/de/kurpark/) is 38 km out — past the 30 km line — but earns it for the [Baumwipfelpfad](https://treetop-walks.com/schwarzwald/en/) + [Wildline](https://wildline.de/en/) + [Palais Thermal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palais_Thermal) trio reached by the 1908 [Sommerbergbahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sommerbergbahn) funicular [[52]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Baiersbronn/Bad-Wildbad)[[32]](https://www.schwarzwaldportal.com/en/warum-der-sommerberg-in-bad-wildbad-zum-beliebtesten-ausflugsziel-wurde.html).

Distances are by road from Baiersbronn village. Anything labelled "stretch" is past the strict 30 km radius but routinely treated as a feasible day-trip.

## 1. Hike from the village — the Genießerpfade

Baiersbronn is the only Black Forest municipality with **four certified Genießerpfade** ("pleasure trails"), each 11–14 km, embedded in a 550 km marked network the village markets as the "[Baiersbronner Wanderhimmel](https://www.baiersbronn.de/natur-aktivitaeten/wandern)" [[1]](https://www.baiersbronn.de/natur-aktivitaeten/wandern/geniesserpfade)[[2]](https://www.baiersbronn.de/natur-aktivitaeten/wandern). Add the **Murgleiter** — Deutsches-Wanderinstitut-certified premium long-distance trail, 110 km in 5 stages, all rated difficult [[6]](https://www.schwarzwald-tourismus.info/erleben/wandern/fernwandern/murgleiter).

| Trail            | Length    | Climb  | Difficulty | Signature feature                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------- | --------- | ------ | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Sankenbachsteig  | 12.7 km   | ~430 m | Demanding  | Sankenbachsee + manually-sluiced 40 m waterfall + Glasmännlehütte at 777 m. Starts from Sesselbahn car park through Bannwald primordial forest [[3]](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/e748097189/baiersbronner-sankenbachsteig-schwarzwaelder-geniesserpfad) [[5]](https://www.schwarzwald-informationen.de/baiersbronner-wanderhimmel/baiersbronner-geniesserpfade.html) |
| Panoramasteig    | 14.3 km   | ~430 m | Moderate   | "Sun terrace of the Murgtal", Mähderbrunnen wine-fountain (bottles cool in the spring), Sackmann's Panorama-Hütte [[4]](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/e748175458/baiersbronner-panoramasteig-wanderhuetten-in-baiersbronn)                                                                                                                                                  |
| Tonbachsteig     | 14 km     | ~482 m | Moderate   | 70 % inside the Black Forest National Park; Oberer Zinken & Huzenbacher Seeblick viewpoints; Kleemisse high moor; Traube Tonbach log-cabin stop [[7]](https://www.blackforest-highlights.com/poi/detail/baiersbronner-tonbachsteig-schwarzwaelder-geniesserpfad-75fb7fcd6e)                                                                                  |
| Satteleisteig    | ~11.4 km  | —      | Moderate   | Rinkenberg loop above Tonbachtal — best panoramas of Baiersbronn village itself [[1]](https://www.baiersbronn.de/natur-aktivitaeten/wandern/geniesserpfade)                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Bannwald-Tour    | 13.4 km   | 624 m  | Hard       | One of nine themed Himmelswege; high-moor crossing to Seibelseckle with Vosges + Rhine-plain views [[8]](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/germany/baden-wurttemberg/baiersbronner-himmelsweg-bannwald-tour)                                                                                                                                                       |
| Murgleiter st. 5 | 21.7 km   | —      | Difficult  | Baiersbronn → Schliffkopf, the long-distance trail's most scenic stage [[6]](https://www.schwarzwald-tourismus.info/erleben/wandern/fernwandern/murgleiter)                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

The **Sankenbachsteig** is the iconic pick — you release the wooden sluice yourself and then watch the 40 m drop [[3]](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/e748097189/baiersbronner-sankenbachsteig-schwarzwaelder-geniesserpfad). The **Panoramasteig** is the easier "wine and views" option — moderate climb, a wine fountain where Oberkirch bottles cool in the spring water, and Sackmann's hut for a meal [[4]](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/e748175458/baiersbronner-panoramasteig-wanderhuetten-in-baiersbronn).

Slow afternoon? The **Buhlbachsee**, a tiny glacial pond hidden in the Obertal forest, is reachable on gravel-and-narrow paths from Obertal or the B500 [[10]](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/187889); the same valley hosts the [Forellenhof Buhlbach](https://www.schwarzwald-tourismus.info/gastronomie/baiersbronn-buhlbach-forellenhof-9c52f58574) trout farm/restaurant raising ~65,000 fish a year on the edge of the national park [[78]](https://www.schwarzwald-tourismus.info/gastronomie/baiersbronn-buhlbach-forellenhof-9c52f58574). Mountain-bikers get a parallel **Bikehimmel** — 400 km of network, eleven tours from 13 to 81 km, with the 35.3 km T6 Hirschkopf-Trailtour as flagship [[9]](https://www.baiersbronn.de/natur-aktivitaeten/biken).

## 2. The B500 Schwarzwaldhochstraße — a half-day chain

The [Schwarzwaldhochstraße](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwarzwaldhochstra%C3%9Fe) is Germany's oldest themed scenic drive, completed in 1952, running ~60 km from Baden-Baden to Freudenstadt at 800–1,000 m elevation [[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwarzwaldhochstra%C3%9Fe). Within 30 km north of Baiersbronn it threads four can't-skip stops, all walk-up from B500 lay-bys.

| Stop             | km from village | Why stop                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Schliffkopf      | ~12             | 1,054 m summit; 3.5 km / 1.5 h Grinden-heath circuit; 360° panorama to Vosges; capercaillie & adders [[15]](https://www.nationalpark-schwarzwald.de/erleben/unterwegs-im-park/zu-fuss-unterwegs/schliffkopfrundweg) [[16]](https://www.wunderschoener-schwarzwald.de/aussichtsplattform-schliffkopf/) |
| Lotharpfad       | ~14             | 900 m boardwalk loop (opened 2003) through the 10 ha scar of Cyclone Lothar (26 Dec 1999, 200 km/h winds); free entry [[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lothar_Path)                                                                              |
| Ruhestein centre | ~17             | Black Forest National Park HQ; ~2.5 h one-way interactive exhibition, cinema, 36 m cantilevered Bridge of Wilderness; opened 2021; free entry [[17]](https://www.blackforest-highlights.com/poi/detail/nationalparkzentrum-ruhestein-fa286a02f5) [[18]](https://www.sbp.de/en/project/national-park-center-ruhestein/) |
| Wildsee          | ~17             | Short ranger-led hike from Ruhestein into the bann-forest untouched since 1911 [[19]](https://www.baden-baden.com/en/media/tours/wilderness-trail) [[20]](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/germany/baden-wurttemberg/von-ruhestein-zum-wildsee)            |
| Mummelsee        | ~22             | Largest of seven Black Forest cirque lakes — 3.7 ha, 18 m deep at 1,036 m; lakeside Berghotel + boat hire + 800 m loop; Mörike water-nymph legend [[12]](https://www.mummelsee.de/en_US/erlebnis/mummelsee)                                                |
| Hornisgrinde     | ~24             | 1,164 m, highest peak in the Northern Black Forest; 23 m Hornisgrinde Tower (1910); 6,000-year-old, 5 m-thick raised bog; easiest ascent from Mummelsee car park [[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hornisgrinde)                                                |

The **Lotharpfad** is the under-rated one — a free, 900 m boardwalk through a quarter-century-old windthrow that's been left to recolonise on its own, with sightlines to Strasbourg and the Vosges from the platform [[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lothar_Path). The **Ruhestein** visitor centre is the only purpose-built indoor stop on this stretch — useful as a rainy-day pivot [[17]](https://www.blackforest-highlights.com/poi/detail/nationalparkzentrum-ruhestein-fa286a02f5).

## 3. Allerheiligen — ruins and the Black Forest's biggest waterfall

About **25 km** northwest of the village. Drop off the Schwarzwaldhochstraße at the Ruhestein pass into the Lierbach valley toward Oppenau [[40]](https://pages.destination.one/de/baiersbronn/wlan/detail/POI/p_100152428/allerheiligen-wasserflle).

- **[Kloster Allerheiligen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Saints'_Abbey_(Baden-W%C3%BCrttemberg))** — Premonstratensian monastery at 620 m. Foundation charter issued by Duchess Uta of Schauenburg in 1196 after a wooden chapel was raised on the remote spot ~1192; dissolved Nov 1802; lightning strike on the tower in 1804 caused the final fire that left the Gothic ruins seen today [[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Saints'_Abbey_(Baden-W%C3%BCrttemberg))[[35]](https://www.spottinghistory.com/view/4876/all-saints-abbey-ruins/). A small site museum and café operate next to the ruins.
- **[Allerheiligen-Wasserfälle](https://www.renchtal-tourismus.de/en/poi/5-all-saints-waterfalls/)** — seven cascades dropping nearly 90 m, reckoned the Black Forest's largest natural waterfall; the gorge path (numerous stairways, several bridges) was opened in 1840 and broadened in 1842 [[36]](https://www.renchtal-tourismus.de/en/poi/5-all-saints-waterfalls/)[[37]](https://www.schwarzwald-informationen.de/allerheiligen-wasserfaelle.html). Free parking at both the lower entrance and upper ruins.
- **Hike loop**: standard Komoot circuit from Oppenau is **13.2 km / 370 m / ~3 h 53 min**, moderate, starting at the Sohlberg parking [[38]](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/473870). Ridge variant (15.6 km, 681 m climb) bags the **Sohlberg-Blick** viewpoint at 1,002 m between Hermann-Beier-Hütte and Melkereikopf en route to Schliffkopf [[39]](https://www.bergfex.de/sommer/baden-wuerttemberg/touren/wanderung/3443101,oppenau--melkereikopf--schliffkopf--allerheiligen-wasserfaelle/).
- **Lunch**: [Restaurant Kloster Allerheiligen](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1063569-d7747913-Reviews-Restaurant_Kloster_Allerheiligen-Oppenau_Baden_Wurttemberg.html), adjacent to the ruins, is in the Slow Food dining guide — Mangalitza pork and Demeter-farm produce [[41]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1063569-d7747913-Reviews-Restaurant_Kloster_Allerheiligen-Oppenau_Baden_Wurttemberg.html).
- **Extra viewpoints**: the 28 m [Buchkopfturm](https://www.schwarzwald-tourismus.info/attraktionen/buchkopfturm-oppenau-98f32f61f7) (opened Apr 2015, ~900 m elevation) and the 1890 Moosturm on the Mooskopf round out the Oppenau lookouts [[42]](https://www.schwarzwald-tourismus.info/attraktionen/buchkopfturm-oppenau-98f32f61f7).

## 4. Freudenstadt — the planned-Renaissance town next door

**~7 km** from Baiersbronn. Founded **1599** by Duke Frederick I of Württemberg; designed by Heinrich Schickhardt for ~11,000 Protestant exiles from Inner Austria; laid out as a Renaissance grid based on a mill board-game pattern [[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freudenstadt)[[22]](https://www.freudenstadt.de/City-history-of-Freudenstadt).

- **[Marktplatz](https://www.tourism-bw.com/attractions/freudenstadt-marktplatz-85843346f3)** — **219 × 216 m** (4.74 ha), Germany's largest market square. Divided into Oberer Marktplatz, Unterer Marktplatz and Postplatz by streets; arcaded buildings on all sides; 50 water fountains in summer [[22]](https://www.freudenstadt.de/City-history-of-Freudenstadt)[[23]](https://www.tourism-bw.com/attractions/freudenstadt-marktplatz-85843346f3).
- **[Stadtkirche](https://www.waymarking.com/waymarks/WMM769_Evangelische_Stadtkirche_Freudenstadt_Germany_BW)** (1601–1608) — unusual **L-shaped plan**: pews segregated by sex along each wing while keeping the whole congregation facing the preacher at the vertex [[24]](https://www.waymarking.com/waymarks/WMM769_Evangelische_Stadtkirche_Freudenstadt_Germany_BW).
- **[Panorama-Bad](https://panorama-bad.de/)** — 25 m sports pool, year-round heated outdoor pool, 110 m "Black Hole" slide, 1,500 m² vitality area with salt/fire/ice grottos, 1,300 m² five-sauna landscape including MeerKlimaSauna. 350,000+ visitors/year; entry from €5.90 / 105 min; Tue–Sun [[25]](https://panorama-bad.de/)[[26]](https://www.schwarzwald-tourismus.info/attraktionen/freudenstadt-panorama-bad-mit-freibad-10dbf1af4b).
- **Friedrichsturm on the Kienberg** — 25 m red-sandstone observation tower built 1899 for the town's 300th anniversary. ⚠ Closed 1 Nov – 30 Mar [[27]](https://www.freudenstadt.de/de/Unsere-Stadt/Aktuelles/Neues-aus-der-Stadt/Nachricht?view=publish&item=article&id=5421). Easy circular walk from the Marktplatz: **~1.3 km / ~70 m climb**, with café, rose path and sculptures [[28]](https://www.komoot.com/tour/1485063125).
- **[Murgtal Dampfzug](https://www.schwarzwald-tourismus.info/veranstaltungen/murgtal-dampfzugfahrten-0405e4e09e)** — historic steam-train trips on the 1901-opened Murgtalbahn from Karlsruhe through Rastatt, Gernsbach and Klosterreichenbach to Baiersbronn, with catering and free bike transport. 2026 bookings opened 31 Mar 2026 [[30]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murgtalbahn)[[31]](https://www.schwarzwald-tourismus.info/veranstaltungen/murgtal-dampfzugfahrten-0405e4e09e).
- **Sankenbach waterfall** — the same 40 m sluice-released drop on the Sankenbachsteig is reachable on its own as a shorter Baiersbronn-side family walk [[29]](https://www.alltrails.com/poi/germany/baden-wurttemberg/sankenbachwasserfall).
- **Winter**: the 30 × 45 m Baiersbronn **ice rink** is described as unique within a 60 km radius, with skate hire at the ticket office [[33]](https://www.baiersbronn.de/natur-aktivitaeten/wintersport/eislaufen).

## 5. Alpirsbach — Benedictine monastery + working brewery

**~17 km** south of Baiersbronn down the Kinzig valley. The two attractions share a townscape and a combi-ticket [[47]](https://alpirsbacher.de/besuchen/museumsfuehrungen/).

### [Kloster Alpirsbach](https://www.kloster-alpirsbach.de/en/visitor-information)

Founded **1095** when three noblemen donated ~50 km² to the Benedictines; the Romanesque church of St Nicholas (built 1125–1133, consecrated **1128**) follows the austere Hirsau Reform style [[44]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpirsbach_Abbey). The headline draw is the **"Monks and Scholars"** permanent exhibit in the former abbot's quarters: renovators cracked open hollow vault spaces in the east cloister wing and found a 15th–16th-century cache of clothing, shoes, schoolboy homework, drawings and report cards now on display [[45]](https://www.kloster-alpirsbach.de/en/visitor-experience/the-monastery-museum).

- **Hours**: Apr 1 – Nov 1, Wed–Sat 10:30–16:00, Sun/holidays 11:00–16:30; off-season Sat/Sun/holidays 11:00–15:00 [[43]](https://www.kloster-alpirsbach.de/en/visitor-information).
- **Admission**: €8 standard or €10 with guided tour (German, English, Dutch, Spanish year-round) [[43]](https://www.kloster-alpirsbach.de/en/visitor-information).
- **[Kreuzgangkonzerte](https://www.kreuzgangkonzerte.de/)** — running in the Gothic cloister courtyard since **1952**. 2026 summer season: four Saturday concerts at 20:00 on 27 Jun, 11 Jul, 25 Jul, 1 Aug [[46]](https://www.kreuzgangkonzerte.de/).

### [Alpirsbacher Klosterbräu Brauwelt](https://alpirsbacher.de/unsere-brauwelt/)

Next door to the monastery. The brewery runs **daily 90-minute public tours at 12:00 and 14:30** through the historic Sudhaus, with one freshly tapped beer included [[47]](https://alpirsbacher.de/besuchen/museumsfuehrungen/). The Brauwelt complex extends well beyond the brewhouse: wedding chapel in the former brewhouse, beer cellar, monastery distillery, on-site beer-praline confectionery, glass-blower, and the Brauladen retail shop [[48]](https://alpirsbacher.de/unsere-brauwelt/). The combi-ticket from **€28.40** bundles monastery + brewery tour with two tastings, a schnapps, bratwursts, pretzel and a gift [[47]](https://alpirsbacher.de/besuchen/museumsfuehrungen/).

## 6. Bad Wildbad — the "stretch" trip

⚠ **Distance check**: Bad Wildbad is **~38.5 km / 39 min** by car from Baiersbronn — *just past* the strict 30 km radius [[52]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Baiersbronn/Bad-Wildbad). Bad Herrenalb (the other spa-town candidate) is **47.5 km / 56 min** — beyond comfortable day-trip range without committing most of a day to driving [[64]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Baiersbronn/Bad-Herrenalb)[[65]](https://www.mygermancity.com/bad-herrenalb). Bad Wildbad still earns the extra eight kilometres because three of its marquee attractions cluster on one Sommerberg plateau [[32]](https://www.schwarzwaldportal.com/en/warum-der-sommerberg-in-bad-wildbad-zum-beliebtesten-ausflugsziel-wurde.html).

- **[Sommerbergbahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sommerbergbahn) funicular** — opened **1908**, 756 m long, climbs 324 m at up to **53 % gradient** in 3.5 minutes; the natural entry point for everything else on this trip [[53]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sommerbergbahn).
- **[Baumwipfelpfad Schwarzwald](https://treetop-walks.com/schwarzwald/en/)** — **1,250 m** barrier-free treetop walkway up to **20 m** above beech, fir and spruce; culminates in a **40 m** observation tower with a **55 m** tunnel slide inside [[54]](https://treetop-walks.com/schwarzwald/en/)[[55]](https://www.schwarzwaldportal.com/en/ausflugsziel-baumwipfelpfad-schwarzwald.html).
- **[Wildline](https://wildline.de/en/) suspension bridge** — opened July **2018**, **380 m** long, ~60 m above the Enz valley, suspended in an arched form on two steel cables → the only one of its kind in Europe. Daily 9:00–19:30 (last entry 19:00), Heermannsweg 100 [[56]](https://www.schwarzwald.de/en/points-of-interest/wildline-suspension-rope-bridge)[[57]](https://wildline.de/en/).
- **[Palais Thermal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palais_Thermal)** — Graf-Eberhard-Bad building from **1847** by Nikolaus Friedrich von Thouret; bathing tradition documented since **1521**; restored 1995. **12 thermal pools 32–38 °C across 2,000 m² over four levels**, including Moorish halls tiled with **1.4 million mosaics** [[58]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palais_Thermal)[[60]](https://thermelust.com/places/palais-thermal-bad-wildbad/). ⚠ Upper floors and saunas are **textile-free** (nude) → Tuesdays are the designated textile day if that's not your thing [[59]](https://www.moknis.com/en/palais-thermal-and-textile-free-what-does-that-mean/).
- **[Enztalradweg](https://www.enztalradweg.de/orte-freizeit/bad-wildbad)** — signposted **100+ km** cycle path, mostly downhill, from the Enz source near Enzklösterle through Bad Wildbad and Pforzheim to the Neckar at Walheim [[61]](https://www.komoot.com/collection/1816217/auf-dem-enztal-radweg-vom-schwarzwald-an-den-neckar)[[62]](https://www.enztalradweg.de/orte-freizeit/bad-wildbad).
- **[Kurpark + Enzauenpark](https://www.bad-wildbad.de/de/kurpark/)** — 38 acres along the Enz, legacy of the **1992** State Garden Show, with water playgrounds and the Römerauen game park [[63]](https://www.bad-wildbad.de/de/kurpark/).

## 7. Family + rainy-day extras

Anchors that work with kids in tow or when the weather closes in.

- **[Naturbad Mitteltal](https://www.naturbad-mitteltal.de/)** — Baiersbronn-Mitteltal's biologically-treated open-air pool (not heated). 25 m basin, diving rock, toddler pool with slide, massage jets and a playground. **2026 season opens 14 May**. **€5 adult / €3 child (6–14) / free under 6**. Weekday 12:00–20:00, weekends/holidays 10:00–20:00 [[66]](https://www.naturbad-mitteltal.de/)[[67]](https://www.naturbad-mitteltal.de/oeffnungszeiten/).
- **Free deer enclosures (Wildgehege)** — Baiersbronn keeps **five free wildlife enclosures** across its districts: Sankenbachtal, Mitteltal (Eulengrund + Ellbachtal), Tonbach, Klosterreichenbach (Ailwald) and Hinterlangenbach [[69]](https://www.schwarzwald-informationen.de/zoologische-gaerten-tierparks-schwarzwald.html). [Wildgehege Ellbachtal](https://www.nationalparkregion-schwarzwald.de/poi/wildgehege-ellbachtal-mitteltal) in Mitteltal has a playground next to the red-deer paddock [[68]](https://www.nationalparkregion-schwarzwald.de/poi/wildgehege-ellbachtal-mitteltal).
- **[Alternativer Wolf- und Bärenpark Schwarzwald](https://www.baer.de/projekte/alternativer-wolf-und-baerenpark-schwarzwald/oeffnungszeiten-preise)** at Bad Rippoldsau-Schapbach (~25 km / 25 min) — 10 rescued brown bears, 3 wolves and lynx over 10 ha of forest and meadow with a natural playground and explorer trail. **Daily 10:00–18:00 Mar–Oct**, **€14 adult / €12 reduced / €35 family** [[70]](https://www.baer.de/projekte/alternativer-wolf-und-baerenpark-schwarzwald/oeffnungszeiten-preise)[[71]](https://www.schwarzwald-tourismus.info/attraktionen/alternativer-wolf-und-baerenpark-schwarzwald-f55d920fad).
- **Minigolf** — four village courses spread across Baiersbronn, Obertal, Klosterreichenbach (a "natural-obstacle" course) and Schönmünzach [[72]](https://www.baiersbronn.de/natur-aktivitaeten/aktiv/minigolf).
