Decision: Stay at Iniala Beach House if being steps from Aulis is the priority — the restaurant is in the hotel gardens and packages can bundle dinner with accommodation.[1] For the same Natai Beach location without the premium, Aleenta (nearest neighbour, ~10 min walk) or Santhiya (~5 min drive) both offer private-pool beachfront villas at 2–3× lower rates. Six Senses Yao Noi is spectacular but requires a 45-min speedboat + 30-min drive to reach Aulis — worth it only if you're building a multi-day island itinerary around the dinner.

Island alternative — Phang Nga Bay

If you want to pair Aulis dinner with Phang Nga Bay's iconic limestone karst scenery, the island stay requires planning a separate boat + road transfer back to Natai Beach.

Six Senses Yao Noi

Koh Yao Noi island · Phang Nga Bay · 56 pool villas · MICHELIN Guide
Limestone karst views MICHELIN recommended Hilltop infinity pool

from $623 / night [16]

56 elevated private pool villas spread through lush tropical vegetation with panoramic Phang Nga Bay views.[14] Three restaurants, hilltop infinity pool, large Six Senses Spa. MICHELIN Guide recommended hotel.[15] Transfer: 45-min speedboat from Phuket (THB 2,000/person each way)[17] + ~30 min drive to Iniala. Best booked as the island leg of a 2–3 night itinerary: arrive island, spend a night, take the boat back to the mainland for Aulis dinner, then stay at Iniala or Aleenta that night.

Logistics

  • Airport: All Natai Beach properties are 15–25 min north of Phuket International Airport — no need for Phuket town accommodation.[1]
  • Aulis reservations: Book via aulisphuket.com or info@aulisphuket.com; the 15-seat chef's table fills quickly.[2]
  • Getting between hotels: Natai Beach has no walkable commercial strip; hotels provide tuk-tuk/driver transfers. Iniala guests not dining at Aulis that evening can request driver service to nearby properties.
  • Season: Andaman coast high season runs Nov–Apr; Jun–Oct is monsoon with reduced crowds and resort discounts. Aulis operates year-round as a permanent 1-star in the MICHELIN Guide Thailand 2026.[18]
