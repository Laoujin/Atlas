ZOOM · WEEK 22 · 25 – 31 MAY

What's actually on, Fri 29 – Sun 31

The three top lanes are empty all week. re:publica wrapped Wed 20 May[2]; Tech Open Air opens Tue 2 Jun[5]. The community lane carries the weekend — and a Thursday arrival catches the one-day applied-ML conference before everything pivots to design for the next 96 hours.

primary anchor · fri–sun

Berlin Design Week

28–31 MAY · 90+ locations · 15,000+ visitors

Theme DESIGN REAL, 100+ participants — includes a digital-design track. The Berlin Format conference runs 28 May; the Design Pool fair runs 30–31 May at PLATTE.BERLIN, covering both Saturday and Sunday of the weekend.[6]

thu 28 may · one day

Applied AI Conf

28 MAY · community-driven

Real-world applied-ML focus. Arrive Thursday morning to catch it before the weekend starts.[7]
