> **TL;DR.** Berlin's tech calendar is anchored by four flagships: **IFA** (4–8 Sep, ~220k visitors, consumer electronics) [[1]](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/event/ifa-2026-innovation-all), **re:publica** (18–20 May, 1,000+ speakers, digital society) [[2]](https://www.re-publica.com/en), **WeAreDevelopers World Congress** (8–10 Jul, 15k+ devs) [[3]](https://www.wearedevelopers.com/world-congress), and **Bosch ConnectedWorld** (10–11 Jun, AI/IoT/mobility) [[4]](https://bosch-connected-world.com/). For the **29–31 May 2026** weekend the marquee IT shows are quiet — re:publica wrapped 20 May [[2]](https://www.re-publica.com/en) and [Tech Open Air](https://event.toa.media/) opens 2 Jun [[5]](https://event.toa.media/). What's actually on those dates: **Berlin Design Week's** closing days [[6]](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/event/berlin-design-week-2026) plus the one-day **Applied AI Conference** on 28 May [[7]](https://www.techinberlin.com/news/top-tech-and-startup-events-in-berlin-to-attend-in-2026).

## On the ground 29–31 May 2026

Quiet weekend for the tier-1 IT shows — re:publica wrapped on 20 May [[2]](https://www.re-publica.com/en); [Tech Open Air](https://event.toa.media/) opens 2 Jun [[5]](https://event.toa.media/). What's actually on:

- [**Berlin Design Week**](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/event/berlin-design-week-2026) (28–31 May, overlapping the whole weekend) — theme *DESIGN REAL*, 100+ participants across 90+ locations, 15k+ visitors; includes digital-design tracks. The Berlin Format conference runs 28 May; the Design Pool fair runs 30–31 May at PLATTE.BERLIN [[6]](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/event/berlin-design-week-2026).
- [**Applied AI Conference Berlin**](https://www.techinberlin.com/news/top-tech-and-startup-events-in-berlin-to-attend-in-2026) (Thu 28 May, one day) — community-driven, real-world applied ML; arrive a day early to catch it [[7]](https://www.techinberlin.com/news/top-tech-and-startup-events-in-berlin-to-attend-in-2026).
- [**Deep Tech Momentum**](https://www.techinberlin.com/news/top-tech-and-startup-events-in-berlin-to-attend-in-2026) (20–21 May, week prior) — robotics, AI, energy, space, advanced materials; missed for this weekend but worth a note [[7]](https://www.techinberlin.com/news/top-tech-and-startup-events-in-berlin-to-attend-in-2026).

If anchoring the trip on tech rather than dinner, shifting one week (5–7 Jun) catches **TOA** [[5]](https://event.toa.media/); two weeks (12–14 Jun) catches **Bosch CW + Berlin Buzzwords** [[4]](https://bosch-connected-world.com/) [[11]](https://2026.berlinbuzzwords.de/).

## Recurring meetups & community scenes

- [**Tech in Berlin Meetup**](https://www.meetup.com/tech-in-berlin/) — 10k+ members; cross-disciplinary AI / Web3 / data / product events, near-weekly cadence [[19]](https://www.meetup.com/tech-in-berlin/).
- [**Disruption Network Lab**](https://www.disruptionlab.org/) — interdisciplinary conferences + community meetups on tech-and-society / whistleblowing; next conference *Exposing Crimes is Not a Crime* runs 19–22 Mar 2026 [[20]](https://www.disruptionlab.org/).
- [Berlin AI Developers Group](https://www.meetup.com/berlin-ai-tech-talk/) — drop-in if a session lands on your dates.
