The three angles snap together around one constraint: **the restaurant pick decides which neighbourhood Saturday lives in**, and the 29–31 May 2026 window (Fri–Sun) narrows the field harder than the Michelin list suggests.

**The Saturday shortlist is brutal.** Rutz — Berlin's only 3★ — runs Mon–Fri dinner only [[1]](https://rutz-restaurant.de/en/). Facil is closed weekends [[2]](https://www.facil.de/en/). Tim Raue is closed Sun–Mon [[3]](https://tim-raue.com/en/contact/). Of five 2★/3★ houses, only **Horváth (Kreuzberg), Coda (Neukölln), and Tim Raue (Sat lunch or dinner)** are even bookable on Sat 30 May. Sunday at this tier is zero. If the anchor *must* be Saturday dinner, that's the menu. Arriving Thursday or Friday — and putting the dinner on Friday — re-opens Rutz and Facil.

**Neighbourhood-geometry then dictates the rest of the day.** The Mitte landmark loop (Brandenburg Gate → Reichstag → Museum Island) fits a Saturday morning on foot [[4]](https://goaskalocal.com/blog/3-day-berlin-itinerary) regardless of pick. The afternoon and after-dinner segments fork:

- **Horváth on Paul-Lincke-Ufer** pulls the day into Kreuzberg — afternoon at East Side Gallery, walk the canal to dinner [[5]](https://restaurant-horvath.de/en/), then Tresor or A-Trane jazz a short ride away [[6]](https://www.nightflow.com/tresor-berlin/) [[7]](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/blog/11-jazz-bars-berlin).
- **Coda in Neukölln** sits 15 minutes from Klunkerkranich rooftop [[8]](https://www.therooftopguide.com/rooftop-bars-in-berlin/klunkerkranich-rooftop.html); pair it with Tempelhofer Feld in the afternoon and you have a tight Neukölln day [[9]](https://www.tempelhoferfeld.de/en/discoveries-experiences/sports/).
- **Tim Raue's Friday "Early Kolibri" 17:00 seating** is the loophole if you want a marquee-name dinner without surrendering Saturday [[10]](https://tim-raue.com/en/menu/).

**Two booking windows close earlier than the trip itself.** The Reichstag dome's 3-month registration opens roughly end of February for end-of-May visits, and peak 10:00–14:00 slots evaporate within a week [[11]](https://reichstagbuilding.com/how-to-visit-reichstag-dome-free-tickets-registration-guide-2026/). Rutz asks 6–8 weeks lead, well past that for peak weekends [[1]](https://rutz-restaurant.de/en/). Do both same-day, in February, or lose the option.

**2026 closures bite the activity plan, not the dinner plan.** The Pergamon is fully shut until partial reopening 4 June 2027 (Ishtar Gate not back until the 2030s) [[12]](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/05/04/germany-berlin-pergamon-museum-reopening-2027-altar/9b428284-47c2-11f1-a119-857cd2bf4fd4_story.html); the Asisi Panorama across the canal is the substitute [[13]](https://www.smb.museum/en/museums-institutions/pergamonmuseum-das-panorama/plan-your-visit/prices-tickets/). Watergate and Wilde Renate are gone — older nightlife guides will mislead you [[14]](https://mixmag.net/read/berlin-nightclub-watergate-close-permanently-22-years-news). The Stadtbahn closure starts 14 June, after this weekend [[15]](https://www.vbb.de/news/stadtbahn2026/).

**The tech-event overlay is mostly empty.** 29–31 May falls between re:publica (wrapped 20 May) [[16]](https://www.re-publica.com/en) and Tech Open Air (opens 2 Jun) [[17]](https://event.toa.media/). What's actually on: **Berlin Design Week's closing days** (Design Pool fair 30–31 May at PLATTE.BERLIN) [[18]](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/event/berlin-design-week-2026), and the Applied AI Conference one day earlier on Thu 28 May if you arrive a day early [[19]](https://www.techinberlin.com/news/top-tech-and-startup-events-in-berlin-to-attend-in-2026). Sliding the trip one week catches TOA [[17]](https://event.toa.media/); two weeks catches Bosch ConnectedWorld and Berlin Buzzwords [[20]](https://bosch-connected-world.com/) [[21]](https://2026.berlinbuzzwords.de/).

**Gap this run didn't cover:** the brief capped day-trips at ~30 km road distance, which puts Potsdam (Sanssouci) right at the edge and not researched — the obvious follow-up if the Saturday afternoon needs filling beyond the Mitte loop.
