> **Decision** — Restaurant Ralf Berendsen (two Michelin stars, also listed on World's 50 Best Discovery) is inside [Domaine La Butte aux Bois](https://labutteauxbois.be/) at Paalsteenlaan 90, Neerharen [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/be-limburg/neerharen/restaurant/ralf-berendsen) [[8]](https://labutteauxbois.be/) [[14]](https://www.theworlds50best.com/discovery/Establishments/Belgium/Lanaken/Ralf-Berendsen.html). The only sane "walking distance" choice is **stay in the hotel itself** — zero metres to your two-Michelin-star dinner, Relais & Châteaux / Small Luxury Hotels property [[3]](https://slh.com/hotels/la-butte-aux-bois) [[7]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/destinations/europe/belgium/) [[17]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/mice-tourism/la-butte-aux-bois), with the in-house Shiseido Spa Aquamarijn to recover in [[8]](https://labutteauxbois.be/). If La Butte's ~$240–$400/night Saturday rate [[5]](https://www.kayak.com/Lanaken-Hotels-Domaine-La-Butte-aux-Bois.201884.ksp) is out of scope, **Hotel Admiraal** at Staatsbaan 81 (50.9110°N, 5.6812°E) [[11]](https://nl.near-place.com/admiraal-hotel-staatsbaan-81-neerharen/en) is a 1.1 km walk (~14 min) along the N78 [[12]](https://router.project-osrm.org/route/v1/foot/5.6812467,50.9109774;5.668067,50.914917?overview=false) at roughly €85–90 [[13]](https://www.kayak.com/Lanaken-Hotels-Hotel-Admiraal.252987.ksp). Nothing else in Neerharen meets a fair definition of "walking distance" — the village is small and the restaurant sits at the entrance to Hoge Kempen National Park [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/hotels-stays/lanaken/domaine-la-butte-aux-bois-9801).

## Why nothing else qualifies

Restaurant Ralf Berendsen sits at the western end of Paalsteenlaan, against the wooded edge of Hoge Kempen National Park [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/hotels-stays/lanaken/domaine-la-butte-aux-bois-9801). Paalsteenlaan is a short side street off the N78 (called Steenweg / Staatsbaan locally) [[15]](https://lanaken.fandom.com/nl/wiki/Paalsteenlaan) — west of Paalsteenlaan is forest, east is residential Neerharen with no hotels until you reach the N78 itself. The nearest public-transport stop, Neerharen Dobbelsteynstraat, is ~400 m from La Butte [[16]](https://www.belvicci.com/hotel/belgium/neerharen/la-butte-aux-bois), but no other accredited lodging clusters around it.

The next-closest accommodation that appears in B&B aggregators is **[Mimi's natuurhuisje](https://www.mimisnatuurhuisje.be/)** (Heidestraat 101 E73 — a 2-person nature cottage, 9.6/10 on bedandbreakfast.eu) [[18]](https://www.mimisnatuurhuisje.be/). Crow-flies it's ~660 m from La Butte, but on foot the OSRM route is **3.7 km / ≈45 min** because Heidestraat sits in a holiday bungalow park inside Hoge Kempen with no direct path to Paalsteenlaan. For "walking after a 2★ dinner in dress shoes" this fails the test — treat it as a short-drive option, not walking distance.

## Practical notes for booking

- **Saturday-night Ralf Berendsen + La Butte room** is the booking the hotel is built around — the gastronomic-stay packages on labutteauxbois.be bundle the dinner with the room and spa access [[8]](https://labutteauxbois.be/). If you want the dinner reservation guaranteed, book the package rather than treating room and table as separate inventories.
- **The 1.1 km walk** from Hotel Admiraal traces a sidewalk along Staatsbaan, then turns west onto Paalsteenlaan — fine in daylight or after dark with a phone torch, but the N78 is a 70 km/h regional road [[15]](https://lanaken.fandom.com/nl/wiki/Paalsteenlaan), so dress-shoe walkers in rain may prefer a 5 min taxi.
- **Beyond walking distance** but still ≤ 10 min by car: central Lanaken (4 km south) and Maastricht NL (5 km north-east) have far more lodging choice — only relevant if neither option above fits.
