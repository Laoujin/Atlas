Two keys. One walks you home.

A 2-Michelin-star Saturday dinner at Restaurant Ralf Berendsen[1] means lodging has to be solved before anything else. In Neerharen the answer collapses to a binary: stay inside the restaurant's hotel, or take the only honest 14-minute walk on offer. The reception desk has exactly two sleeves.

Mimi's natuurhuisje

A two-person nature cottage at Heidestraat 101 E73 with an outdoor bath, free bikes, and a 9.6/10 on bedandbreakfast.eu[18]. Crow-flies it sits ~660 m from La Butte. On foot the OSRM route is 3.7 km / ~45 min because Heidestraat is inside a bungalow park in Hoge Kempen with no direct path to Paalsteenlaan.

Treat as a short-drive option, not walking distance. For dress shoes after a two-star dinner, the route fails the test.

CONCIERGE — BEFORE YOU PHONE THE HOTEL

Three small things that change the booking math.

1 · labutteauxbois.be[8] sells gastronomic-stay packages that bundle the Ralf Berendsen dinner with the room. The table and the room are separate inventories otherwise — packages are the only clean way to guarantee both, especially on a Saturday.

2 · Ralf Berendsen runs Tuesday through Saturday, 18:30–21:00; Sunday and Monday are dark[1]. A Sunday- or Monday-arrival weekend is not a Ralf Berendsen weekend. Pivot the calendar before you pivot the property.

3 · Beyond walking distance but inside ~10 minutes by car: central Lanaken (4 km south) and Maastricht NL (5 km north-east) have far more lodging choice. Only relevant if neither sleeve above fits — see the character-lodging folder in the wallet below.

WHY THE SHELF IS THIS SHORT

Neerharen is a village, not a hotel district.

Restaurant Ralf Berendsen sits at the western end of Paalsteenlaan, against the wooded edge of Hoge Kempen National Park[2]. West of Paalsteenlaan is forest. East is residential Neerharen with no hotels until the N78 itself[15]. The reception desk has the two sleeves it has — and Mimi's, returned. Nothing else clears the bar for "walking distance after a two-star dinner."
