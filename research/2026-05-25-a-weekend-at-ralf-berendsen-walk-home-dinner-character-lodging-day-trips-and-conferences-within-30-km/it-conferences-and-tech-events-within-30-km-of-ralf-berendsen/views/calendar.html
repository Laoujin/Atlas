▲ THE SATURDAY-DINNER ALIGNMENT

Three Friday closes that hand off cleanly to a Sat-night reservation.

Ralf Berendsen serves Tue – Sat 18:30 – 21:00 (dark Sun/Mon)[1]. A Friday-finishing conference is the rare shape that lets you trade the badge for cutlery without an extra travel day.

  • ✦ Best Fri 13 Mar 2026 BSides Limburg Conference day ends late afternoon. Corda Campus Hasselt → Lanaken = ~25 min drive on E314/N78[26]. Saturday morning free for Hoge Kempen.
  • Fri 26 Jun 2026 IEEE WCCI close-out Closing sessions at MECC Maastricht; 7 km / 15 min taxi to the restaurant.[3]
  • Fri 23 Oct 2026 BNAIC / BeNeLearn close Maastricht University, 7 km. Coincides with the busiest October week — BSBF arrives the following Monday.

Sundays and Mondays are out: Ralf Berendsen is closed both days[1], so a conference that ends Sun (or starts Mon) wastes the dinner anchor. Tue-, Wed- or Thu-evening conference dinners are possible but break the natural weekend shape.
