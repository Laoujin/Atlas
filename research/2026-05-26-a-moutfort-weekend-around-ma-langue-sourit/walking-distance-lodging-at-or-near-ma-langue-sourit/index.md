> **Decision.** There is **no hotel within walking distance** of Ma Langue Sourit [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotels-g1049422-Moutfort-Hotels.html). The restaurant sits at the Éitermillen mill, 1.4 km from Moutfort village along a country lane [[2]](https://www.bergfex.com/sommer/luxemburg-region/touren/wanderung/4214228,eitermillen--moutfort--ma-langue-sourit/). Pick by trade-off: **walk + DIY** → Airbnb in Moutfort village (≈17–20 min walk, from ~$80/night) [[7]](https://www.airbnb.com/moutfort-contern-luxembourg/stays); **walk + train access** → Airbnb in Oetrange (≈25–30 min walk, near CFL line 30) [[8]](https://www.airbnb.com/oetrange-contern-luxembourg/stays) [[9]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Oetrange/Luxembourg-Station); **drive/taxi + hotel comforts** → Mercure Kikuoka in Canach, 4.3 km away, 4★ spa-and-golf, $179–$311/night [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1096258-d250745-Reviews-Mercure_Kikuoka_Golf_Club-Canach.html) [[6]](https://all.accor.com/hotel/2898/index.en.shtml). Late return is solved either way — MLS staff call you a taxi if buses/trains are done [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1049422-d1060870-Reviews-Ma_Langue_Sourit-Moutfort.html).

## Why "walking distance" is harder than it looks

MLS's postal address is **1 rue de Remich, L-5331 Moutfort** [[1]](https://mls.lu/en/contact-us.html), but the building actually sits at **Éitermillen** — a single-mill hamlet that the Michelin Guide files under *Oetrange* rather than Moutfort [[13]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/luxembourg/oetrange/restaurant/ma-langue-sourit). The mill is **1.4 km from Moutfort village centre** along the CR132 country road [[2]](https://www.bergfex.com/sommer/luxemburg-region/touren/wanderung/4214228,eitermillen--moutfort--ma-langue-sourit/), in what one reviewer accurately calls *"a tranquil area near the small village of Moutfort"* [[14]](https://www.wbpstars.com/restaurant/ma-langue-sourit/). That isolation is why Moutfort itself has **zero hotels**, B&Bs, or guesthouses on Booking/Tripadvisor inventory [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotels-g1049422-Moutfort-Hotels.html) [[12]](https://www.booking.com/city/lu/moutfort.en-gb.html) — and why "Moutfort" results on Booking.com all redirect to **Luxembourg City** properties 10–15 km away [[12]](https://www.booking.com/city/lu/moutfort.en-gb.html).

## The realistic Saturday-dinner playbook

**If "walking distance" is non-negotiable** → book one of the handful of [Moutfort-village Airbnbs](https://www.airbnb.com/moutfort-contern-luxembourg/stays) early (inventory is thin) [[7]](https://www.airbnb.com/moutfort-contern-luxembourg/stays). The 1.4 km walk is along the CR132 — a rural road with mild elevation gain (≈14 m) [[2]](https://www.bergfex.com/sommer/luxemburg-region/touren/wanderung/4214228,eitermillen--moutfort--ma-langue-sourit/). It's pleasant in the late-spring evening light you'll have in late May (sunset ~21:30), but completely unlit on the return after midnight. Bring a phone torch or reflective layer; no sidewalk for stretches.

**If you want hotel amenities** → [Mercure Kikuoka](https://all.accor.com/hotel/2898/index.en.shtml) at Scheierhaff, Canach is the only sensible hotel pick: 4★, 4.3/5 over 1,122 reviews [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1096258-d250745-Reviews-Mercure_Kikuoka_Golf_Club-Canach.html), a real spa, on-site golf, ample parking [[6]](https://all.accor.com/hotel/2898/index.en.shtml). It explicitly highlights MLS as its showcase dining neighbour [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1096258-d250745-Reviews-Mercure_Kikuoka_Golf_Club-Canach.html). The 4.3 km hop is a 5–8 min taxi; book a return cab when leaving the hotel since there's no concierge shuttle [[15]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Luxembourg/Mercure-Luxembourg-Kikuoka-Golf-Spa-Canach).

**If you want a city weekend with MLS bolted on** → stay in Luxembourg City (Sofitel, Le Royal, Place d'Armes, etc.) [[12]](https://www.booking.com/city/lu/moutfort.en-gb.html). All sit 10–12 km from MLS; taxi runs ~€25–€35 each way. MLS reception will call you a return taxi if your evening runs past the last CFL train [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1049422-d1060870-Reviews-Ma_Langue_Sourit-Moutfort.html).

## Practical notes

- **Parking.** MLS has on-site validated parking [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1049422-d1060870-Reviews-Ma_Langue_Sourit-Moutfort.html) — most diners arrive by car, which is the default the area is designed for.
- **Public transit.** Luxembourg's nationwide free buses + trains reach Moutfort, but the **last bus from the village is around 22:00–22:30**; a tasting-menu Saturday dinner will run past that. The CFL station at **Oetrange** (≈2.3 km from MLS) is on line 30 [[9]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Oetrange/Luxembourg-Station) [[10]](https://www.cfl.lu/en-GB/network/station/Gare-de-Oetrange) but the last trains also stop well before midnight. Plan a taxi back regardless.
- **Taxi back.** This is the standard MLS pattern — staff actively call cabs for late-departing guests [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1049422-d1060870-Reviews-Ma_Langue_Sourit-Moutfort.html). Budget €10–15 to Moutfort/Oetrange, €15–20 to Canach (Mercure), €30–40 to Luxembourg City.
- **Booking lead time.** Moutfort/Oetrange Airbnb inventory is small (single-digit listings) [[7]](https://www.airbnb.com/moutfort-contern-luxembourg/stays) [[8]](https://www.airbnb.com/oetrange-contern-luxembourg/stays); book the apartment **before** confirming the MLS reservation, not after.
