The Brief

Geography, not gastronomy, is the load-bearing fact.

The postal address reads 1 rue de Remich, Moutfort, but the dining room is at the Éitermillen mill — a country lane outside the village, climbing 141 m in the dark [3]. And the village itself, per every major hotel inventory, contains no hotels, B&Bs, or guesthouses [4].

The "walking distance after dinner" constraint, then, forces a binary: an apartment rental in Moutfort or Oetrange [5], or accept a taxi. There is no third option. Once the taxi is accepted, the 30 km circle opens — and three coherent weekends fall out, each anchored on the same Saturday-evening table.

Verdict
Book the table first. Then choose one of the three archetypes — they don't mix.
Open Tue–Sat, closed Sun–Mon [1]. On-site parking, validated; staff call taxis for late departures [11]. Reservations: +352 26 35 20 31.
The Three Archetypes

Three weekends, one table.

Each archetype solves the dinner mechanics differently — through the city, through the spa, or through the Moselle. Pick one before you book a bed.
Bock Casemates · Luxembourg City
I Dinner-as-Event

The UNESCO Old Town overnight.

Sleep among 17th-century ramparts. Cab to dinner.

Base in Luxembourg City's Old Town. By day, the Chemin de la Corniche — Batty Weber's "most beautiful balcony of Europe" [19] — then the Bock Casemates, 23 km of tunnels carved into the promontory [18]. Taxi at 19:00 (€30–40), back near midnight.

II Dinner-as-Spa-Spine

The thermal park weekend.

Luxembourg's only natural-thermal spa. Twelve minutes by cab to MLS.

Mondorf Parc Hôtel, 4★ superior with 108 rooms set in a 45-ha park [13], opens onto a 36 °C mineral pool, 11 saunas and three hammams [14]. The single cleanest overlap of lodging and day-trip — your "activity" and your bed share an address. Saturday afternoon at the thermal pool, robe to room, dressed for dinner, 12 minutes by taxi to the mill.

Domaine la Forêt · Remich
III Dinner-as-Moselle-Tour

The vineyard ribbon.

Sleep above Remich. Cruise the Moselle to Schengen. Taxi to the mill.

Domaine la Forêt — 16 rooms, 700 m² spa, a MICHELIN Guide restaurant, set between vineyards and woodland above Remich [15]. Or Hotel de l'Ecluse, Stadtbredimus, every room balconied onto river or vines [16]. The Saturday-afternoon spine is the MS Princesse Marie-Astrid Remich-Schengen [21] — wine villages, no hurry. Then north 19 km to dinner.
