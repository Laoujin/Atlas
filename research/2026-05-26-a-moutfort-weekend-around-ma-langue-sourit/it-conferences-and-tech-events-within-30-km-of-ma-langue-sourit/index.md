> **Decision.** Anchor a tech-trip weekend on **[Voxxed Days Luxembourg](https://luxembourg.voxxeddays.com/en/), 18–19 June 2026** at Casino 2000 in Mondorf-les-Bains — it is the only major IT conference of 2026 that sits inside Moutfort village's 10 km circle [[8]](https://luxembourg.voxxeddays.com/en/). For a longer stay, [**Nexus Luxembourg**, 10–11 June at LuxExpo The Box](https://nexusluxembourg.com/) (~12 km away) is the flagship AI/tech summit [[2]](https://nexusluxembourg.com/), and [**hack.lu**, 20–23 October](https://hack.lu/) at Parc Hotel Alvisse is the 20th-edition anchor for the cybersecurity crowd [[4]](https://hack.lu/).

[Ma Langue Sourit](https://mls.lu/en/) sits at 1 rue de Remich, Moutfort, ~10 km southeast of Luxembourg City and adjacent to Findel airport [[1]](https://mls.lu/en/contact-us.html). A 30 km radius from there covers **all** of Luxembourg's IT-event venues — LuxExpo The Box (Kirchberg), Chamber of Commerce (Kirchberg), Parc Hotel Alvisse (north city), Crystal Park (Cloche d'Or), Belval/Esch-sur-Alzette, and Mondorf-les-Bains. It does **not** quite reach Trier (~35 km), Arlon, Metz or Saarbrücken, so this list is essentially Luxembourg-domestic.

### Pick one, by goal

- **Strategic / business networking** → Nexus Luxembourg (10–11 Jun). PM Frieden, Klaba (OVHcloud), Paytm CEO confirmed; ⚠ ticketed [[2]](https://nexusluxembourg.com/).
- **Hands-on developer day-job stuff** → Voxxed Days (18–19 Jun). 5 parallel rooms, catering included, organised by [YAJUG](https://yajug.lu/) — the local JVM community [[8]](https://luxembourg.voxxeddays.com/en/)[[16]](https://yajug.lu/).

- **Network/cloud sovereignty, public-sector ICT** → Luxembourg Internet Days (17–18 Nov). Free, but registration mandatory [[3]](https://luxembourg-internet-days.com/).

## Cross-border edge: what falls just outside 30 km

Moutfort is ~10 km from Luxembourg City centre, but the 30 km circle ends roughly at the German and French borders. **Trier (~35 km)** is the nearest German tech-event hub but sits just outside the radius — its main 2026 activity is academic ([CiE 2026, 27–31 Jul](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-cie-2026) and [MCU 2026, 27–29 Jul](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-mcu-2026) at Universität Trier) [[26]](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-cie-2026). **Arlon (BE)** sits right on the radius edge. If you're willing to push past 30 km, [IT.CON Saarbrücken](https://www.instagram.com/itcon.saarland/) (~70 km, 29 Sept 2026) is the closest cross-border IT/cybersecurity option — but it needs a separate trip from any Moutfort dinner reservation [[27]](https://www.instagram.com/itcon.saarland/).

## Practical tips for pairing a conference with dinner at Ma Langue Sourit

- The restaurant is open **Tuesday–Saturday** and is closed Sun/Mon [[1]](https://mls.lu/en/contact-us.html). All the conferences above run on weekdays or weekday-into-Saturday — there is no Sunday-only conflict.
- **Voxxed Days (Thu–Fri 18–19 Jun)** is the cleanest pairing: a Saturday-night dinner at Ma Langue Sourit closes the trip without conflicting with the conference.
- **Nexus (Wed–Thu 10–11 Jun)** likewise leaves the Friday/Saturday open. Same for **Luxembourg Internet Days (Tue–Wed 17–18 Nov)**.
- **hack.lu (Tue–Fri 20–23 Oct)** runs through Friday — book Saturday-night dinner in the same week, then fly out Sunday.
- Venues used are all <25-minute drive from Moutfort: LuxExpo (12 km), Casino 2000 (9 km), Parc Hotel Alvisse (12 km), Crystal Park (10 km), Belval (25 km).
