Decision: Megève has exactly one restaurant above 1★: Flocons de Sel (3★, Emmanuel Renaut, ~€310/person) — no 2-star establishment exists in the village.[1] The 3-star table reopens 5 June 2026 after its summer break[2]; book immediately — it fills weeks out. If Flocons de Sel is full, Vous (1★, Julien Gatillon, €140–190) is the intimate year-round alternative. La Table de l'Alpaga (1★) is winter-only and closed until December.[7]

Flocons de Sel Opens 5 Jun

Chef: Emmanuel Renaut (Meilleur Ouvrier de France)
Address: 1775 route du Leutaz, 74120 Megève
Tel: +33 4 50 21 49 99
Service: Dinner Mon, Thu–Sun · Lunch Fri–Sun · Closed Tue–Wed
Season: 5 Jun – 1 Nov · 6 Dec onwards[2]
Price: ~€310/person, wine pairing €115–160[4]
Format: 5- or 8-course tasting menu (à la carte also available)

The summit of Savoie gastronomy. Renaut's dishes speak of the alpine landscape — lake fish (pike, char, whitefish), wild mushrooms, mountain herbs and berries — rendered with quiet precision and no ostentation.[1] Critics single out the Arctic char, rock lobster with caviar under frozen crustacean extract, and the smoked chocolate dessert.[3] The cellar runs to ~1,200 labels with deep Savoie coverage and a legendary pre-1900 Chartreuse collection.[5]

Vous Open year-round

Chef: Julien Gatillon & Jean Pastre
Address: 36 Rue Saint-François de Sales, 74120 Megève
Tel: +33 6 59 55 95 83
Service: Wed–Sun lunch & dinner (off-season Thu–Sun) · single sitting
Price: €140–190/person[9] · Gault Millau 14/20
Seats: 14 at open counter

Awarded its first star in the 2025 Guide.[10] An intimate counter experience in the village centre: guests watch the full brigade work through a 5-course menu. Standouts include cauliflower with iodized sauce and pike roe, and wagyu chuck in Mondeuse jus.[9] The ~350-label wine cellar and an option to reserve the entire counter for a private event make it a flexible choice. Best option if Flocons de Sel is booked out — and significantly easier to get a table.

Baule puts vegetables at the centre — Savoie roots, herbs, and tubers — with fish and meat as supporting acts. Housed in Hôtel L'Alpaga (Beaumier group).[6] Closed June through November; revisit this option for a winter weekend plan.

Closed: La Dame de Pic – Le 1920 (Four Seasons)

Anne-Sophie Pic's 1-star restaurant[12] at the Four Seasons Hotel Megève closed permanently in 2024–25[11] and has been replaced by a brasserie. It no longer appears in the 2025–26 Michelin Guide.

Booking note

Flocons de Sel is the only 3-star table in the Mont Blanc massif area; demand is high from day one of each season. Reserve online via TheFork or by phone (+33 4 50 21 49 99) as early as possible — ideally 4–6 weeks ahead for the summer season. Groups of 7+ must email contact@floconsdesel.com.[5] Vous books three months out by phone (+33 6 59 55 95 83) or online form; with only 14 seats expect limited same-week availability.[8]
