> **Decision.** Don't plan the weekend around an IT conference within 30 km of Fontjoncouse [[1]](https://www.audetourisme.com/en/fiche/fontjoncouse/auberge-du-vieux-puits_TFORESLAR011FS000AQ/) — there are essentially none. The radius captures only Narbonne (23 km straight-line) [[2]](https://www.streetplace.org/distance/3017840-2990919/Fontjoncouse/Narbonne/), whose tech footprint is one Fablab + a small public coding school at [IN'ESS](https://www.narbonne.fr/in-ess) [[5]](https://www.narbonne.fr/in-ess) — useful for a weekday daytime visit, not a Saturday/Sunday conference. The nearest *actual* tech events sit 40–60 km out: an [IUT Béziers digital-sovereignty talk on 2 April 2026](https://iut-beziers.edu.umontpellier.fr/en/2026/03/19/conference-sur-la-souverainete-numerique/) [[17]](https://iut-beziers.edu.umontpellier.fr/en/2026/03/19/conference-sur-la-souverainete-numerique/), and the [Train French Tech Perpignan→Madrid on 1–4 June 2026](https://frenchtech-perpignan.fr/train-french-tech-2026/) [[15]](https://frenchtech-perpignan.fr/train-french-tech-2026/).

## Inside the 30 km circle

Everything below sits in Narbonne. There are no IT or tech events in Sigean, Lézignan-Corbières, Port-la-Nouvelle, Tuchan, Durban-Corbières, or any other commune inside the radius.

### IN'ESS — Le Grand Narbonne (incubator + Fablab)

[IN'ESS](https://www.narbonne.fr/in-ess), 30 avenue Pompidor in Narbonne, is the only meaningful tech address inside the circle [[5]](https://www.narbonne.fr/in-ess). It's a 1000 m² business hotel that bundles a startup incubator, the Grand Narbonne Fablab, and the Occitanie École Régionale du Numérique. Not a conference venue — a year-round support facility. Public events come from its tenants, not from IN'ESS itself.

- **[Fablab du Grand Narbonne](https://www.fablabs.io/labs/fablabdugrandnarbonne)** [[9]](https://www.fablabs.io/labs/fablabdugrandnarbonne) — 70 m² (expanding to 160 m² by 5 June 2026 [[7]](https://www.legrandnarbonne.com/actualites/actualite/le-fablab-du-grand-narbonne-base-a-iness-bientot-agrandi-et-reamenage-pour-lapprentissage-des-technologies-numeriques)), Mon-Fri 14:00-18:00, €12/yr membership. Equipment: 8× 3D printers, laser cutter, CNC mill, embroidery machine, VR headsets [[6]](https://www.legrandnarbonne.com/travailler-et-entreprendre/je-veux-entreprendre-sur-le-grand-narbonne/iness/le-fablab). Served 500 people in 2024 [[7]](https://www.legrandnarbonne.com/actualites/actualite/le-fablab-du-grand-narbonne-base-a-iness-bientot-agrandi-et-reamenage-pour-lapprentissage-des-technologies-numeriques). `⚠` Closed weekends — useless for a Saturday-evening trip unless you arrive Friday afternoon.
- **[École Régionale du Numérique](https://www.legrandnarbonne.com/actualites/actualite/ecole-regionale-du-numerique-remise-des-diplomes-et-accueil-de-la-nouvelle-promotion)** — free 9-14-month state-funded training in web/mobile dev and systems/network admin. Tiny: 11 graduates in 2024-25, 13 in the 2025-26 cohort [[8]](https://www.legrandnarbonne.com/actualites/actualite/ecole-regionale-du-numerique-remise-des-diplomes-et-accueil-de-la-nouvelle-promotion). Diploma ceremonies are private events, not open conferences.
- **Manufacture du Grand Narbonne** — new co-working/innovation annex; tenants moved in 1 January 2026 [[23]](https://www.legrandnarbonne.com/actualites/actualite/appel-a-projets-devenez-les-futurs-occupants-de-la-manufacture-du-grand-narbonne). No public event programme yet.

### Narbo Via museum — FabLab digital workshops

The [Narbo Via Roman archaeology museum](https://narbovia.fr/) runs occasional digital-creation workshops with the Grand Narbonne Fablab — typically Wednesday afternoons during school terms, themed around the museum's collection [[10]](https://narbovia.fr/evenement/les-ateliers-fablab-a-narbo-via-9/) [[11]](https://narbovia.fr/evenement/les-ateliers-fablab-a-narbo-via-8/). Recent themes: virtual reconstruction of Roman mausoleums, podcast fabrication, Labopéra heroes-and-props digital creation. Aimed at the general public/families, not professionals. Worth a detour if your trip overlaps a scheduled date — check [the Narbo Via agenda](https://narbovia.fr/) before you travel.

## ~2 h drive — the closest real tech festival

If you'll stretch beyond an hour: **[Hello la Tech](https://www.lafrenchtechmed.com/event/save-the-date-hello-la-tech-revient-pour-une-5%E1%B5%89-edition/)** 5th edition, 28 May 2026, MO.CO La Panacée, Montpellier — the French Tech Méditerranée flagship, 13:00-22:30 [[18]](https://www.lafrenchtechmed.com/event/save-the-date-hello-la-tech-revient-pour-une-5%E1%B5%89-edition/). ~145 km / 1 h 45 from Fontjoncouse. The date doesn't line up with a Saturday Auberge booking in 2026 — it's a Thursday.

## Bottom line for the trip planner

If a tech event is a hard requirement: pick the weekend around an event in Béziers, Carcassonne, Perpignan, or Montpellier and accept a 45-90 minute drive to dinner at the Auberge. If the Auberge booking drives the date (typically the case — they're booked weeks out), treat the trip as pure gastronomy + Corbières wine country and skip the conference angle. The radius is too rural.
