## COMPUTEX 2026 + NVIDIA GTC Taipei — Happening Now

[COMPUTEX 2026](https://www.computextaipei.com.tw/en/index.html) runs June 2–5 across four venues in Taipei's Nangang and Xinyi districts under the theme **"AI Together"** [[1]](https://www.computextaipei.com.tw/en/index.html) [[13]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/computex-2026-opens-amid-surging-global-demand-for-ai-infrastructure-302788497.html). Three tracks dominate: AI & Computing, Robotics & Mobility, and Next-Gen Tech.

**NVIDIA GTC Taipei** ran concurrently June 1–4 at TICC [[2]](https://www.nvidia.com/en-tw/gtc/taipei/computex/): keynote June 1, workshops June 2, conference sessions June 3–4. Jensen Huang's keynote announced RTX Spark (NVIDIA × MediaTek Windows-on-Arm chip), the Vera CPU for agentic AI workloads, and cited Taiwan's GDP growing ~10% in 2026 on NVIDIA's supply chain [[3]](https://www.nvidia.com/en-tw/gtc/taipei/keynote/) [[15]](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/111907/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-keynote-live-news-blog-from-gtc-taipei-2026/index.html).

**[InnoVEX 2026](https://innovex.computex.biz/)** is the startup exhibition embedded in COMPUTEX at TaiNEX Hall 2 [[4]](https://www.computextaipei.com.tw/en/news/5745807A54416BE4/info.html):
- ~500 startups from 22 countries (11% YoY growth, new record)
- National pavilions: France, Japan, South Korea, Thailand, Australia, Israel, Canada, Italy, Czech Republic
- Pitch contest prize pool: **USD 60,000** (up to USD 30,000 per team)
- Investors: NVIDIA Inception, Garage+, StarFab, Plug and Play

## June 25–26 — DevOpsDays Taipei

[DevOpsDays Taipei 2026](https://devopsdays.org/events/2026-taipei) at Taipei New Horizon (Xinyi District) [[8]](https://devopsdays.org/events/2026-taipei). Taiwan's largest DevOps gathering: 700+ professionals, talks on automation, CI/CD, observability, SRE, security, and AI integration. Community-run, co-organized with Taiwan Agile Community, DevOps Taiwan, and HashiCorp User Group Taiwan.

## July 1 — iThome Cloud Summit

[iThome Cloud Summit 2026](https://cloudsummit.ithome.com.tw/2026/) at TaiNEX Hall 2, 7F, 9am–5pm [[9]](https://cloudsummit.ithome.com.tw/2026/). Theme: cloud as AI transformation engine (not just storage). Tracks: AI-Native Infrastructure, FinOps & Cloud Governance, Multi-Cloud & Hybrid Strategy, Green Cloud, Cloud-Native Security. Targets CIOs, CTOs, architecture leads. Free but registration screened for seniority.

## July 15–16 — AWS Summit Taipei

[AWS Summit Taipei 2026](https://aws.amazon.com/tw/events/summits/taipei/) at TICC (Xinyi District), free [[10]](https://aws.amazon.com/tw/events/summits/taipei/). 40+ sessions spanning agentic AI, serverless, cloud migrations, and industry verticals. Hands-on labs via AWS Village; no keynote speaker names announced.

## August 8–9 — COSCUP 2026

[COSCUP 2026](https://coscup.org/) at National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST, Da'an District) [[7]](https://coscup.org/2026/en/sponsorship/). The 20th edition of Taiwan's premier volunteer-run open-source conference. Free to attend. Consistently 3,000+ attendees, 20+ community tracks. [UbuCon Asia 2026](https://2026.ubucon.asia/) co-hosted this year. Strong international participation (talks in English and Mandarin).

## September 2–4 — SEMICON Taiwan 2026

[SEMICON Taiwan 2026](https://expo.semi.org/taiwan2026/Public/enter.aspx) at TaiNEX Hall 1 & 2 [[6]](https://expo.semi.org/taiwan2026/Public/enter.aspx). Asia's leading semiconductor event: 1,200 exhibitors, 4,100+ booths, 20+ international forums. Eight focus themes: advanced manufacturing, heterogeneous integration, compound semiconductors, automotive chips, smart manufacturing, sustainability, semiconductor cybersecurity, and workforce. Essential if you're anywhere near Taiwan's chip ecosystem.

## Practical Notes

- **COMPUTEX badges** grant access to TaiNEX 1&2 and TWTC simultaneously; TICC forum sessions require separate forum registration (many were fully booked before the show opened). [[1]](https://www.computextaipei.com.tw/en/index.html)
- **Getting there**: TaiNEX (Nangang) is served by MRT Blue Line (Nangang Exhibition Center station). TICC is near MRT Blue/Red Line Taipei City Hall. NTUST (COSCUP) is at MRT Daan District Office station.
- **Language**: COMPUTEX, SEMICON, AWS Summit, and GTC are English-friendly. COSCUP and DevOpsDays are bilingual. iThome Cloud Summit is primarily Mandarin.
- **CYBERSEC** returns annually in May; the 2026 edition drew 20,000 attendees and 400+ exhibiting brands. [[5]](https://10times.com/cybersec-expo)
