> **TL;DR:** Spend day one entirely on foot in the UNESCO core — Dutch Square / Stadthuys [[1]](https://www.malaysia.travel/explore/dutch-square), the A Famosa gate and St Paul's ruins on the hill [[6]](https://melaka.holidayinn.com/blog/a-famosa-st-paul-hill), then "Harmony Street" (Cheng Hoon Teng temple [[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheng_Hoon_Teng_Temple) + Kampung Kling Mosque [[47]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampung_Kling_Mosque)) and the Baba & Nyonya house [[12]](https://www.babanyonyamuseum.com/ticket/). Time it for a **Friday–Sunday** so you get the Jonker Street night market [[19]](https://www.traveloka.com/en-sg/explore/destination/jonker-street-night-market-ta/1002108), and ride the **river cruise at dusk** [[21]](https://www.getyourguide.com/malacca-city-l2562/melaka-melaka-river-cruise-e-ticket-t759882/). Save sunset for the **Straits Mosque** (Masjid Selat) [[15]](https://visitmalaysia.in/masjid-selat-melaka-the-floating-mosque-of-malaccas-shoreline/). Visit in **April–May or October** (dry shoulder season) [[27]](https://www.holidify.com/places/melaka/best-time-to-visit.html). Almost everything is cheap: the costliest single ticket here is ~€5. Rate used: **MYR 1 ≈ €0.21** (€1 ≈ MYR 4.76, 2026) [[34]](https://www.exchange-rates.org/exchange-rate-history/eur-myr-2026).

Everything below is walkable from Jonker Street / Chinatown unless tagged otherwise. **Touristy↔offbeat** and **where** tagged per sight. Prices converted at MYR→EUR 0.21 [[33]](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/eurofxref-graph-myr.en.html).

## UNESCO core — Dutch Square (Red Square)

The terracotta-red plaza is the **heart of the World Heritage zone** and the single most-photographed spot in town [[2]](https://mytrip.my/dutch-square-melaka-a-heritage-landmark-you-cant-miss/). Go early (pre-09:00) to shoot it without crowds or trishaws. *Where: UNESCO core. Touristy.*

- **Stadthuys** — the **oldest surviving Dutch building in Southeast Asia**, built ~1650 as the governor's residence; now the **History & Ethnography Museum** [[3]](https://www.melaka-tourism.com/melaka-tourism/melaka-attractions/138-the-stadhuys)[[1]](https://www.malaysia.travel/explore/dutch-square).
- **Christ Church** — built **1753**, an active Anglican church and the oldest functioning Protestant church in Malaysia, with hand-carved pews and a red Dutch facade [[1]](https://www.malaysia.travel/explore/dutch-square)[[4]](https://travelthreemuch.com/melaka-red-square-dutch-history/).
- **Clock Tower (Tan Beng Swee)** — 1886, the plaza's third photo magnet [[4]](https://travelthreemuch.com/melaka-red-square-dutch-history/).

## UNESCO core — St Paul's Hill & A Famosa

Climb the stairway behind the square (5–10 min) past **St Paul's Church** — built **1521** by the Portuguese, the **oldest church in Southeast Asia**, now a roofless shell where St Francis Xavier was once briefly entombed [[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Saint_Paul,_Malacca)[[8]](https://www.malaysiavacationguide.com/st-paul-church.html). Free, open-air, always accessible; best light + breeze late afternoon [[7]](https://www.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/melaka/church-of-saint-paul-malacca-76083/). At the foot of the hill stands **Porta de Santiago (A Famosa)** — the lone surviving gate of the 1511 Portuguese fortress, bearing the Dutch "ANNO 1670" inscription [[6]](https://melaka.holidayinn.com/blog/a-famosa-st-paul-hill). *Where: UNESCO core. Touristy, free.*

## Chinatown — "Harmony Street" (Jalan Tukang Emas)

Three faiths on one short street — a genuine UNESCO talking point. *Where: Chinatown. Touristy but living temples; free entry.*

- **Cheng Hoon Teng Temple** — **oldest functioning Chinese temple in Malaysia** (founded 1645); intricate Fujian-style carvings [[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheng_Hoon_Teng_Temple). No ticket; dress modestly (shoulders/knees), remove shoes inside [[10]](https://www.klook.com/en-US/destination/p50193929-cheng-hoon-teng-temple/).
- **Kampung Kling Mosque** — 1748 (brick rebuild 1872); a **pagoda-style minaret** and triple-tiered pyramidal roof instead of a dome — a Sumatran/Chinese/Hindu/Malay mix with English & Portuguese tiles [[47]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kampung_Kling_Mosque)[[48]](https://www.travelfish.org/sight_profile/malaysia/peninsular_malaysia/melaka/melaka/1552).
- **Sri Poyatha Moorthi Temple** — 1781, **one of the oldest Hindu temples in Malaysia**, built by the Chitty (Peranakan-Indian) community [[35]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Poyatha_Moorthi_Temple).

## Peranakan heritage — Baba & Nyonya Heritage Museum

A lavishly preserved Straits-Chinese townhouse (Nos. 48–50 Jalan Tun Tan Cheng Lock), the best window into Peranakan life; guided tour recommended, ~1–1.5 h [[13]](https://www.malaysia-traveller.com/nyonya-heritage-museum.html)[[14]](https://www.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/melaka/baba-and-nyonya-heritage-museum-15057020). Groups >10 should book ~1 month ahead [[14]](https://www.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/melaka/baba-and-nyonya-heritage-museum-15057020). Note the **RM100 surcharge for visitors in costume** [[12]](https://www.babanyonyamuseum.com/ticket/). *Where: Chinatown/Peranakan core. Touristy.*

## The river & riverfront

The **Melaka River cruise** is a 45-min, 9-km loop past shophouses, Kampung Morten and the riverside murals [[22]](https://www.mytravellane.com/a-guide-to-the-malacca-river-cruise-tickets-times-and-tips/). Boats run **09:00–23:00**, every ~30 min; **dusk/night** is the pick for lit reflections [[22]](https://www.mytravellane.com/a-guide-to-the-malacca-river-cruise-tickets-times-and-tips/)[[23]](https://ticket.melakarivercruise.my/). Foreign adult ~RM30 (**~€6.4**) [[22]](https://www.mytravellane.com/a-guide-to-the-malacca-river-cruise-tickets-times-and-tips/). Walk the riverfront for the **street-art murals** (the "Nyonya Ladies" near Jonker is the famous one) [[43]](https://solotripsandtips.com/melaka-itinerary-tourist-guide/). *Where: riverfront. Touristy.*

## Jonker Street (Chinatown) — by day and by night

By **day**, Jalan Hang Jebat is antique shops, Peranakan cafés and galleries — quieter, good for the Baba & Nyonya house and temples [[18]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g306997-d456610-Reviews-Jonker_Street-Melaka_Central_Melaka_District_Melaka_State.html). By **night**, the **Fri/Sat/Sun market** (from 18:00 to ~midnight) is the headline experience: chicken-rice balls, Nyonya laksa, cendol, satay, live music; free entry, arrive ~18:30 before food sells out [[19]](https://www.traveloka.com/en-sg/explore/destination/jonker-street-night-market-ta/1002108)[[20]](https://backpackbob.com/jonkers-street-market). ⚠ On weekdays there is **no night market** — plan your Malacca nights around Fri–Sun [[19]](https://www.traveloka.com/en-sg/explore/destination/jonker-street-night-market-ta/1002108). *Where: Chinatown. Very touristy.*

## Masjid Selat Melaka (the "Floating Mosque")

On reclaimed **Pulau Melaka** (~10-min drive/taxi from Chinatown), the mosque appears to float at high tide; **sunset over the Strait** is the signature shot, and it glows after dark [[15]](https://visitmalaysia.in/masjid-selat-melaka-the-floating-mosque-of-malaccas-shoreline/)[[16]](https://www.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/melaka-tengah/melaka-straits-mosque-15057028/). Open ~09:00–21:00 (closed around prayer times); free, donations welcome; modest dress, headscarf for women — robes usually lent at the door [[17]](https://thegeestravel.com/melaka-straits-mosque-travel-guide/). *Where: Pulau Melaka, short drive. Touristy but feels offbeat.*

## More to see (optional, all in/near the core)

- **Maritime Museum / Flor de la Mar** — full-size replica of the 1511 Portuguese galleon; 3 museums in one, 09:00–17:30 [[24]](https://www.malaysia-traveller.com/melaka-maritime-museum.html)[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_Museum_(Malaysia)). *Riverfront. Touristy.*
- **Malacca Sultanate Palace Museum** — nail-free wooden replica of Sultan Mansur Shah's 15th-c. palace; Hang Tuah/Hang Jebat legends; closed Mon [[41]](https://www.trip.com/travel-guide/attraction/melaka/muzium-istana-kesultanan-melaka-76077/). *UNESCO core.*
- **Menara Taming Sari** — revolving gyro-tower, 80 m views over the heritage zone, daily 09:00–22:00 [[39]](https://www.menaratamingsari.com/)[[40]](https://www.melakabook.my/attractions/menara-taming-sari). *UNESCO core. Touristy/kitsch.*
- **Cheng Ho Cultural Museum** — Admiral Zheng He's Ming-era links to Malacca, on his original warehouse site [[44]](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/malaysia/melaka-city/attractions/cheng-ho-cultural-museum/a/poi-sig/1041688/1324280). *Chinatown. Offbeat.*
- **St Francis Xavier Church** — twin-spired 1849 Gothic church, grander but far quieter than Christ Church [[36]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_St._Francis_Xavier_(Melaka)). *Core edge. Offbeat.*
- **Kampung Morten / Villa Sentosa** — traditional Malay stilt-village living museum (1920 house) on the river [[43]](https://solotripsandtips.com/melaka-itinerary-tourist-guide/). *10-min walk upriver. Offbeat.*

## Coast & day-trips

- **Klebang Beach + Original Coconut Shake** — ~15-min drive; the cult coconut-vanilla shake is ~RM5–8 (**~€1–1.7**); stall ~12:30–18:30 (Fri from 14:00); sand dunes nearby [[30]](https://mytrip.my/klebang-beach-klebang-sand-dunes-a-stunning-seafront-escape-in-melaka/)[[31]](https://jumppy.com.sg/malacca-klebang-coconut-shake-guide/). *Coast. Touristy with locals, offbeat for Westerners.*
- **Pulau Besar** — small island ~8 km offshore via ferry/speedboat from Jeti Anjung Umbai; quiet beaches, shrines, low-key — a half-day escape, not a polished resort [[32]](https://theoccasionaltraveller.com/pulau-besar-melaka/). *Day-trip. Offbeat.*

⚠ Malacca's beaches are muddy Strait-side and not the reason to come — treat the coast as a coconut-shake-and-sunset detour, not a beach holiday [[30]](https://mytrip.my/klebang-beach-klebang-sand-dunes-a-stunning-seafront-escape-in-melaka/).

## When to go & getting there

**Best window: April–May or October** — dry shoulder seasons, ~30 °C, fewer downpours [[27]](https://www.holidify.com/places/melaka/best-time-to-visit.html)[[28]](https://thingstodoinmelaka.com/when-to-visit/). Avoid the heavier rains of the SW monsoon (late May–Sep) and the wetter NE monsoon (Nov–Mar) [[28]](https://thingstodoinmelaka.com/when-to-visit/)[[29]](https://www.selectiveasia.com/malaysia-holidays/weather). It's hot and humid year-round — pace sightseeing for early morning and late afternoon, hide from midday heat [[27]](https://www.holidify.com/places/melaka/best-time-to-visit.html). ⚠ Crowd trap: weekends + the night market make Sat night Jonker shoulder-to-shoulder — sleep in Chinatown so you can dip in and out [[18]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g306997-d456610-Reviews-Jonker_Street-Melaka_Central_Melaka_District_Melaka_State.html).

**Getting in:** no airport of its own — fly into **Kuala Lumpur (KUL)**, then ~2–3 h by bus. StarMart Express runs **KLIA → Melaka Sentral** roughly hourly for ~RM35 (**~€7.4**) [[45]](https://www.letsventureout.com/kuala-lumpur-to-melaka-malacca/)[[46]](https://www.rome2rio.com/Bus/Kuala-Lumpur-International-Airport-2/Melaka-Central-Bus-Station). From KL city, frequent express coaches leave from TBS; Melaka Sentral is ~6 km from Chinatown (grab a taxi/Grab to Dutch Square) [[49]](https://www.klsentral.info/kl-sentral-melaka-travel/).
