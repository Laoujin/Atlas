> **TL;DR.** The 30 km radius covers exactly two towns of any size — Aire-sur-l'Adour at 13 km [[3]](https://www.planderoute.net/de-aire-sur-adour-a-eugenie-les-bains-40.htm) and Mont-de-Marsan at 26 km [[2]](https://www.distancede.com/fr/distance-entre-40000-montdemarsan-et-40320-eugenielesbains-france/HistoiredeDistance/2126341.aspx) — and IT activity is entirely Mont-de-Marsan, entirely weekday, entirely cyber-flavoured: [Mars@Hack](https://www.montdemarsan.fr/sinstaller-visiter/entreprendre-a-mont-de-marsan/marsahack/) [[7]](https://www.montdemarsan.fr/sinstaller-visiter/entreprendre-a-mont-de-marsan/marsahack/) in early March [[4]](https://www.landes.cci.fr/presse/marshack-2026-cybersecurite/) and [Rencontres de la Cybersécurité](https://www.landes.cci.fr/evenement/rencontres-de-la-cybersecurite-mont-de-marsan-28-janvier/) in late January [[9]](https://www.landes.cci.fr/evenement/rencontres-de-la-cybersecurite-mont-de-marsan-28-janvier/), both already past for 2026. No DevFest, no BarCamp, no French Tech hub inside the radius. **For a Saturday-evening dinner anchor: nothing aligns in 2026.** If matching a tech event matters, plan a weekday-anchored 2027 trip with Saturday dinner attached, or extend the radius to Pau (~60 km) for [UTNT](https://www.universite-transition-numerique.com/) [[15]](https://www.universite-transition-numerique.com/) on 7–8 Oct 2026 [[16]](https://netceed.com/nl/events/universite-de-la-transition-numerique-des-territoires-2026-pau-france/).

**Mars@Hack** is the radius's marquee event. The 7th edition was co-organized by Mont-de-Marsan Agglo, [Base Aérienne 118][ba118], the IUT Réseaux & Télécoms (UPPA), and Campus Landes [[4]][mh] [[5]](https://campuslandes.com/la-7%E1%B5%89-edition-de-marshack/); the [Ministry of the Armed Forces' ComCyber][mod] backs it [[6]](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/comcyber/evenements/marshack-2026). CTF runs 8 a.m.–6 p.m. (80 onsite + 160 online) with a 8:30–10:30 a.m. business-cyber roundtable on NIS2 [[8]][pl]. 8th edition expected March 2027.

## What's confirmed after 1 June 2026 inside the radius

Slim. The CCI and Campus Landes agendas list, for the rest of 2026:

- **5 June 2026** — *Le P'tit Déj de Conjoncture*, CCI Landes — macro briefing, not IT [[10]](https://www.landes.cci.fr/agenda-evenements/).
- **6 June 2026** — Campus Landes Mont-de-Marsan Open Day (a Saturday) — covers ESNL (École Supérieure du Numérique des Landes: web dev, cybersecurity, networks, data, UX/UI) [[11]](https://campuslandes.com/agenda/) [[12]](https://campuslandes.com/ecole-du-numerique/). Useful only if you want a campus tour, not a conference.
- **2 June 2026** — *Atelier Naviguer en ligne*, Mont-de-Marsan Archives — digital-literacy workshop, not a tech event [[13]](https://www.montdemarsan.fr/agenda/).

The CCI agenda runs sparse webinars on numérique, SEO, AI, e-invoicing and cyber awareness through the year, but these are typically 1–2 hour weekday morning sessions on Teams and don't anchor a trip [[10]](https://www.landes.cci.fr/agenda-evenements/).

## What is **not** within 30 km

- **No French Tech regional hub.** The nearest official community is [French Tech Pau Béarn][ftpb] in Pau, ~60 km south [[17]](https://www.frenchtechpaubearn.com/). It runs quarterly matinales, bi-monthly déjeuners and quarterly onboarding soirées at [Hélioparc, Pau][heli] — closest matinale: 4 June 2026, 09:00–10:30 [[18]](https://www.frenchtechpaubearn.com/les-evenements-de-la-french-tech-pau-bearn/).
- **No GDG / DevFest / BarCamp / Meetup.com tech group.** Bordeaux and Toulouse host the closest developer conferences, both 150–200 km.
- **No vendor user groups.** No AWS UG, Snowflake, Databricks, etc. within the radius.
- **No regular Saturday-format tech event.** Mars@Hack and Rencontres de la Cybersécurité are weekday; Campus Landes Open Days are Saturdays but they are recruitment events for prospective students.

[heli]: https://www.frenchtechpaubearn.com/

UTNT is the closest serious multi-day IT conference to Eugénie-les-Bains and the only one in 2026 whose timing could plausibly bracket a Saturday dinner (the Tue–Wed dates leave Sat 10 Oct or Sat 3 Oct as plausible dinner-night anchors, with a ~45-min drive from Pau).

| Goal                                | Best fit                                                                                     |

The honest read: this corner of Landes is a tech-event desert. Mars@Hack is a real, well-attended cyber CTF [[8]][pl] backed by ComCyber [[6]](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/comcyber/evenements/marshack-2026), but it lives on a fixed weekday in March. For the rest of the calendar, the local IT activity is the ESNL school running classes [[12]](https://campuslandes.com/ecole-du-numerique/) and the CCI running webinars [[10]](https://www.landes.cci.fr/agenda-evenements/) — not trip-anchors.
