> **TL;DR:** For a first Vietnam trip, make the *transport* part of the holiday. Take the **daytime Saigon→Phan Thiet train** for the dragon-fruit-and-coast run [[5]](https://blog.vexere.com/en/train-schedule-ho-chi-minh-city-to-phan-thiet-holiday-april-30-may-1-2026/), the **overnight soft-sleeper to Nha Trang** to save a hotel night and wake to Cam Ranh Bay [[4]](https://www.getyourguide.com/explorer/vietnam-ttd169193/train-journeys-in-vietnam/), a **sunrise sampan at Cai Rang floating market** in the Mekong [[22]](https://www.getyourguide.com/can-tho-l164341/can-tho-cai-rang-floating-market-sunrise-tour-t860401/), and the **Hon Thom cable car** on Phu Quoc — a 7.9 km Guinness-record ride over the sea [[16]](https://rootytrip.com/en/hon-thom-cable-car/). Fly the long Phu Quoc hop (~€23–65, 55 min) unless you actively want the boat [[12]](https://north-vietnam.com/how-to-get-from-ho-chi-minh-city-to-phu-quoc/). Prices EUR, ~27,500 VND/€, June 2026.

## Reunification Express — the southern coast by rail

The mainline south of Saigon is the country's most underrated scenic asset. Two distinct experiences:

**Saigon → Phan Thiet (for Mui Ne).** Direct daytime trains **SPT2 (dep ~06:45) / SPT4 (dep 08:00)**, ~4 h over ~190 km, hugging the coastline through green dragon-fruit fields [[5]](https://blog.vexere.com/en/train-schedule-ho-chi-minh-city-to-phan-thiet-holiday-april-30-may-1-2026/). Fares from **132,000 VND (~€5) soft seat** to **219,000 VND (~€8) 4-berth sleeper** [[5]](https://blog.vexere.com/en/train-schedule-ho-chi-minh-city-to-phan-thiet-holiday-april-30-may-1-2026/). At Phan Thiet station, Mui Né is 24 km / ~25–30 min by bus (≈6,000 VND) or taxi (~€10) [[1]](https://www.seat61.com/Vietnam.htm) [[6]](https://www.bestpricetravel.com/travel-guide/ho-chi-minh-to-mui-ne-train-2789.html).

**Saigon → Nha Trang (overnight).** Mainline trains **SE1–SE8**, ~430 km in 11–12 h overnight [[4]](https://www.getyourguide.com/explorer/vietnam-ttd169193/train-journeys-in-vietnam/). Book a **soft-sleeper** to do it as a moving hotel: ~869,000 VND lower berth (~€32), hard sleeper ~763,000 VND (~€28), soft seat ~404,000 VND (~€15) [[1]](https://www.seat61.com/Vietnam.htm). Private operators (Livitrans, Golden Trains) run 4-berth cabins on SNT services at ~$38 (~€35)/bed [[2]](https://www.bestpricetravel.com/travel-guide/vietnam-sleeper-train-2202.html). The scenic payoff is the final approach: the line glides past **Cam Ranh Bay** and palm-lined beaches into Nha Trang [[4]](https://www.getyourguide.com/explorer/vietnam-ttd169193/train-journeys-in-vietnam/) [[3]](https://www.vietnamrailwaycorp.com/blog/top-5-vietnams-scenic-train-journeys). For best light, ride in the dry season (Nov–Apr) and sit on the seaward (right when northbound) side [[3]](https://www.vietnamrailwaycorp.com/blog/top-5-vietnams-scenic-train-journeys).

**Splurge option — [The Vietage by Anantara](https://www.thevietagetrain.com/).** A single luxury carriage (12 seats, 6 booths) running Da Nang↔Quy Nhon↔Nha Trang with a 3-course meal, free-flow drinks and a head/shoulder treatment; ~$420 (~€388) one-way [[7]](https://www.thevietagetrain.com/) [[8]](https://localvietnam.com/transportation/train/best-luxury-trains-in-vietnam/). It only reaches as far south as **Nha Trang**, so it's a tack-on if you continue north — not a southern-loop ride.

## Mekong Delta — the journey *is* the destination

Water is the road here, so the boat is the point.

**Cai Rang floating market, Can Tho (do this at dawn).** The market runs ~05:00–09:00; the most photogenic window is **05:30–06:30**, before the tour boats arrive [[23]](https://workshop.thescentnote.com/can-tho-floating-markets-sunrise-itinerary-2026/). Hire a small **private sampan** (~200,000–400,000 VND, ~€7–15) rather than a shared boat for 90 minutes among the wholesale barges and their pole-hung produce signs [[23]](https://workshop.thescentnote.com/can-tho-floating-markets-sunrise-itinerary-2026/) [[22]](https://www.getyourguide.com/can-tho-l164341/can-tho-cai-rang-floating-market-sunrise-tour-t860401/). Weekdays (Mon–Wed) are quieter and more authentic [[23]](https://workshop.thescentnote.com/can-tho-floating-markets-sunrise-itinerary-2026/).

**Day trip from HCMC.** My Tho / Ben Tre full-day tours include a Mekong cruise, hand-rowed sampan through coconut-palm canals, and lunch, from ~$31 (~€29) [[18]](https://www.viator.com/tours/Ho-Chi-Minh-City/Day-Trip-to-Can-Tho-and-Cai-Rang-Floating-Market-from-Ho-Chi-Minh-City/d352-11122P7). Honest take: a day trip is a sampler — the canals are pretty but the format is touristy.

**Overnight on the river (recommended for the scenery).** Multi-day sampan/cruise routes between Cai Be and Can Tho turn the delta into a slow, offbeat journey [[19]](https://www.bestpricetravel.com/mekong-river-cruises/mekong-delta-cruises). [Victoria Mekong](https://www.victoriamekong.com/) runs refined 3D2N / 4D3N cruises (Can Tho–Tan Chau) from ~$580 (~€537) [[20]](https://www.victoriamekong.com/); private Song Xanh-style sampans cover Cai Be↔Can Tho for couples [[19]](https://www.bestpricetravel.com/mekong-river-cruises/mekong-delta-cruises).

## Coastal & sea trips

**Saigon → Vung Tau by speedboat.** The [Greenlines DP](https://greenlines-dp.com/) high-speed ferry takes ~2 h, gliding down the Saigon River past the skyline before opening onto the sea — a genuinely different view of the city; ~$13–17 (~€12–16) [[21]](https://greenlines-dp.com/). A scenic, offbeat alternative to the traffic-choked road.

**Nha Trang island-hopping.** Boat tours of Nha Trang Bay's islands (Hon Mun, Hon Tam, Mieu) for snorkelling, fishing villages and a floating bar. Group/VIP 3-island tours ~770,000 VND (~€28); Hon Mun trips 400,000–600,000 VND (~€15–22); private speedboat day ~$118 (~€109) [[26]](https://www.viator.com/tours/Nha-Trang/Private-Full-Day-Nha-Trang-Island-Hopping-Tour-with-Lunch/d4682-23224P18) [[27]](https://vinwonders.com/en/wonderpedia/news/hon-mun-island-the-complete-guide-for-a-dream-like-excursion/).

**Vinh Hy Bay coast road (offbeat gem).** Between Nha Trang and Phan Rang, route **DT/TL702** through Nui Chua National Park is rated among Vietnam's most beautiful drives — cliffs, crescent bays and cactus desert on one side, ocean on the other; ~40 km of near-empty new road [[24]](https://www.dreamytravelstory.com/en/vietnams-most-scenic-road-vinh-hy-binh-tien-pass/) [[25]](https://www.vietnamcoracle.com/vinh-hy-bay-resort/). Best by hired car or guided motorbike on a Cam Ranh→Vinh Hy→Phan Rang day [[24]](https://www.dreamytravelstory.com/en/vietnams-most-scenic-road-vinh-hy-binh-tien-pass/).

## Getting to & around Phu Quoc

**Fly it.** Direct HCMC→Phu Quoc flights (Vietnam Airlines, VietJet, Bamboo, Vietravel) take ~55 min, from ~625,000 VND (~€23) and typically €32–65 [[12]](https://north-vietnam.com/how-to-get-from-ho-chi-minh-city-to-phu-quoc/) [[13]](https://12go.asia/en/travel/ho-chi-minh/phu-quoc). For a first trip this is the obvious move.

**Or make a journey of it.** Bus to the coast then ferry: HCMC→Ha Tien (~7–8 h) or →Rach Gia (~6–7 h), then **Superdong / Phu Quoc Express** ferry — Ha Tien ~1.5 h (€8–17), Rach Gia ~2.5 h (€12–24); total 9–12 h, €17–28 [[14]](https://north-vietnam.com/boat-ferry-to-phu-quoc-island-tickets-schedule/) [[15]](https://www.phuquocislandguide.com/phu-quoc-island-ferry-schedules-fares/) [[12]](https://north-vietnam.com/how-to-get-from-ho-chi-minh-city-to-phu-quoc/). Ferries run ~07:00–14:00; book ahead and check weather — crossings cancel in rough seas [[14]](https://north-vietnam.com/boat-ferry-to-phu-quoc-island-tickets-schedule/). ⚠ There's no direct Nha Trang↔Phu Quoc ferry — connect by air.

**The headline ride — [Hon Thom cable car](https://rootytrip.com/en/hon-thom-cable-car/).** From An Thoi / Sunset Town in southern Phu Quoc, this is the **world's longest 3-wire over-sea cable car: 7,899.9 m**, a Guinness record since 2018, ~15 min each way at up to 174 m above the sea over island-dotted water [[16]](https://rootytrip.com/en/hon-thom-cable-car/). Round-trip adult ~850,000 VND (~€31), including the Kiss Bridge and a drink; combos with the mango buffet from ~1,150,000 VND (~€42) [[16]](https://rootytrip.com/en/hon-thom-cable-car/). Open daily ~09:00–17:00 (extended in peak season) [[16]](https://rootytrip.com/en/hon-thom-cable-car/). Reviewers price the park-inclusive ticket at roughly €30 [[17]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g12520037-d13563044-Reviews-Hon_Thom_Cable_Car-An_Thoi_Phu_Quoc_Island_Kien_Giang_Province.html). Even non-park-goers ride it for the view alone.

## Booking notes

- Buy train and ferry tickets via **Baolau, 12Go.Asia or Vexere** (official Vietnam Railways resale) — for sleepers, book days ahead in the Nov–Apr high season [[1]](https://www.seat61.com/Vietnam.htm) [[2]](https://www.bestpricetravel.com/travel-guide/vietnam-sleeper-train-2202.html).
- **FUTA (Phương Trang)** is the default sleeper-bus operator across the south — punctual, own terminals, not street pickups [[9]](https://daytripsvietnam.com/transport/vietnam-sleeper-buses/).
- Speedboat/ferry schedules are weather-dependent; build a buffer day before flights out [[14]](https://north-vietnam.com/boat-ferry-to-phu-quoc-island-tickets-schedule/).
