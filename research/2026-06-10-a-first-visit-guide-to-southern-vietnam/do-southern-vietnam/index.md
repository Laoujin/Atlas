> **TL;DR:** For a comfortable, semi-adventurous first trip you can do everything here without ropes or vertical risk. **Timing matters most:** your 10 June window is the southern *wet season* — Phu Quoc has rough seas and poor underwater visibility [[1]](https://vinpearl.com/en/phu-quoc-weather-in-june-how-to-plan-your-stay-accordingly), and the whole south gets short, heavy afternoon downpours [[2]](https://vinwonders.com/en/wonderpedia/news/vietnam-weather-a-detailed-guide-to-time-your-perfect-trip/). **Nha Trang is the exception** — driest coast in the country, calm 28–29 °C sea in June [[3]](https://www.tnktravel.com/blogs/vietnam-weather-in-june-your-complete-planning-guide/), so make it your snorkel/dive hub instead of Phu Quoc. Best all-weather bets: Cu Chi tunnels + a Mekong canal day, Mui Ne dunes (kite season is Nov–Apr though), and Nha Trang island-hopping/diving.

## June timing reality check (read first)

| Hub | June conditions | Do here | Skip / defer |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|----------------------------|
| HCMC       | Hot, humid, afternoon storms [[2]][w2]                          | Cu Chi, Mekong day trips, river cruise | midday outdoor slogs |
| Mekong     | Wet but lush; mornings usually clear [[2]][w2]                  | sunrise markets, kayak, cycle | —                          |
| Nha Trang  | Driest coast, calm sea, good viz [[3]][w3]                      | dive, island-hop, waterfalls  | —                          |
| Mui Ne     | Off-season for kite (peak wind Nov–Apr) [[10]][k]               | dune jeep, Ta Cu, sandboard   | kite if you need wind      |
| Phu Quoc   | Monsoon, rough seas, ferries to islets disrupted [[1]][w1]      | national-park hikes, north kayak | An Thoi snorkel/dive (poor viz) [[1]][w1] |

## Ho Chi Minh City & day-trip radius

### Cu Chi tunnels — *touristy*
Half-day, easy (optional 20–100 m crawl through widened tunnel sections), guide included. War-history site 50–70 km NW of Saigon. Group tours run ~€15–23 incl. transport and entry [[4]](https://www.machupicchu.org/cu-chi-tunnels-day-trip-from-ho-chi-minh-city-2026-vietnam-war-history-guide.htm) [[5]](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/1471-cu-chi-tunnels-experience-ho-chi-minh-city/); foreigner entrance is 125,000 VND (~€4.6) [[4]](https://www.machupicchu.org/cu-chi-tunnels-day-trip-from-ho-chi-minh-city-2026-vietnam-war-history-guide.htm). Pick **Ben Duoc** (70 km, larger, fewer crowds) over the closer, busier **Ben Dinh** [[4]](https://www.machupicchu.org/cu-chi-tunnels-day-trip-from-ho-chi-minh-city-2026-vietnam-war-history-guide.htm); a private half-day avoids the bus crush [[6]](https://www.viator.com/en-AU/tours/Ho-Chi-Minh-City/Cu-Chi-Tunnels-half-day-from-Ho-Chi-Minh-City/d352-9190P2). Pair with the War Remnants Museum back in town [[7]](https://travellinghan.com/2026/02/04/a-guide-to-visiting-the-cu-chi-tunnels-from-ho-chi-minh-city/).

### Can Gio mangrove & Monkey Island — *offbeat*
Full day (~8 h), easy, guide + boat included. UNESCO biosphere reserve SE of the city: speedboat through mangroves, 1,000+ wild macaques, crocodile area, WWII guerrilla camp [[8]](https://www.travelersuniverse.com/ho-chi-minh-city-can-gio-monkey-island-day-trip/) [[9]](https://vietfuntravel.com/tour/saigon-can-gio-1-day-tour-monkey-island). ~€35–41 incl. transport, boat, lunch, entry [[8]](https://www.travelersuniverse.com/ho-chi-minh-city-can-gio-monkey-island-day-trip/) [[10]](https://www.getyourguide.com/ho-chi-minh-city-l272/from-ho-chi-minh-city-can-gio-mangrove-monkey-island-trek-t695758/). Good rainy-day pick — it's all boat-and-forest.

### Saigon River boat trip — *touristy*
Half-day or evening, easy, no guide needed. Open-boat or speedboat cruises run ~€20–55/person; luxury speedboat day-trips to hidden southern spots ~€73 [[11]](https://www.bestpricetravel.com/travel-guide/dinner-cruise-in-ho-chi-minh-city-2891.html) [[12]](https://saigonrivertour.com/). [Les Rives](https://lesrivesexperience.com/) runs half- and full-day speedboat trips out of the city centre [[13]](https://lesrivesexperience.com/).

### Mekong Delta day trip (My Tho + Ben Tre) — *touristy*
Full day, easy, guide + boats included. Classic intro: motorboat to the river islands, hand-rowed sampan down coconut canals, coconut-candy workshop, riverside lunch [[14]](https://www.viator.com/tours/Ho-Chi-Minh-City/MEKONG-DELTA-MY-THO-and-BEN-TRE-1-DAY-SMALL-GROUP-TOUR/d352-18631P7) [[15]](https://www.getyourguide.com/ho-chi-minh-city-l272/mekong-delta-my-tho-ben-tre-full-day-trip-in-small-group-t97020/). Small-group day trips from ~€28–75 depending on group size and inclusions [[14]](https://www.viator.com/tours/Ho-Chi-Minh-City/MEKONG-DELTA-MY-THO-and-BEN-TRE-1-DAY-SMALL-GROUP-TOUR/d352-18631P7). For something quieter, choose a Ben Tre "non-touristy" operator over the My Tho conveyor belt [[16]](https://antoursvietnam.com/tours/ben-tre-mekong-delta-tour-1-day-not-touristy/).

## Mekong Delta (overnight from Saigon, or based in Can Tho)

### Kayaking & cycling the canals — *semi-offbeat*
Half- or full-day, easy–moderate, guide recommended (route-finding in the canal maze). Kayak the narrow coconut-lined channels around **Ben Tre**; cycle the flat island lanes — guided cycling days from ~€28 [[17]](https://revitrip.com/blog/mekong-delta-tour-5-best-ways-to-explore) [[18]](https://www.getyourguide.com/mekong-river-delta-l1230/bike-tours-tc7/). 2-day Ben Tre→Can Tho combos fold in homestay, market and biking [[16]](https://antoursvietnam.com/tours/ben-tre-mekong-delta-tour-1-day-not-touristy/). No technical skill required; flat terrain.

### Cai Rang floating market at sunrise (Can Tho) — *touristy but worth it*
Half-day, very easy, local boatman steers. Be on the water by 05:30–06:30 for the real wholesale bustle [[19]](https://workshop.thescentnote.com/can-tho-floating-markets-sunrise-itinerary-2026/). **Shared public boat** ~€2–4; **private 4–6-seat boat** ~€18–23 for 2–3 h [[20]](https://vinpearl.com/en/cai-rang-floating-market); guided sunrise tours also bookable [[21]](https://www.getyourguide.com/can-tho-l164341/can-tho-cai-rang-floating-market-sunrise-tour-t860401/).

### Tra Su cajuput "floating forest" (An Giang) — *offbeat*
Full day from Can Tho (far west, near Chau Doc), very easy, rowed sampan + boatman. Glide through duckweed-green flooded forest, climb the bird tower (70+ species) [[22]](https://traveltheworldpages.com/tra-su-forest/) [[23]](https://www.vietnamcoracle.com/tra-su-floating-forest/). Total ~€8 (entry + motorboat + sampan) [[22]](https://traveltheworldpages.com/tra-su-forest/). ⚠ Peak spectacle is the Sep–Nov flood season [[22]](https://traveltheworldpages.com/tra-su-forest/).

## Nha Trang (your June water-sports hub)

### Discover scuba at Hon Mun — *touristy*
Half-day, easy (intro "discover" dive, no certification), divemaster one-on-one required. Hon Mun is Vietnam's first protected marine zone, 350+ coral species [[24]](https://vinpearl.com/en/scuba-diving-nha-trang-a-worthy-experience-in-every-trip-to-islands). A beginner discover-scuba day runs ~€79 [[25]](https://authenticadventure.com.vn/tour-item/discovery-scuba-diving-on-hon-mun-island-in-nha-trang/); single guided dives for the curious start ~€20–32 [[26]](https://www.viator.com/Nha-Trang-tours/Scuba-Diving/d4682-g17-c28901). June visibility here is good unlike Phu Quoc [[3]](https://www.tnktravel.com/blogs/vietnam-weather-in-june-your-complete-planning-guide/).

### Island-hopping + snorkel — *touristy*
Full day, easy, guide + gear included. 3–4 islands, snorkel the reef, floating-bar/BBQ-lunch party boats. Budget group trips ~€14–28 [[27]](https://www.getyourguide.com/nha-trang-l1226/nha-trang-island-hopping-tour-with-snorkeling-and-lunch-t801136/) [[28]](https://www.getyourguide.com/nha-trang-l1226/nha-trang-island-hopping-tour-snorkeling-floating-party-t551330/). The party-boat versions are loud; pick a "premium"/small-boat tour if you want calm.

### Ba Ho waterfall hike — *semi-offbeat*
Half-day (~5 h), **moderate** — boulder-hopping and a rope-assisted scramble along the river, not for limited mobility [[29]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293928-d4469666-Reviews-Ba_Ho_Waterfall-Nha_Trang_Khanh_Hoa_Province.html). 25 km north of town; three stacked pools to swim. Guided half-day with pickup [[30]](https://powertraveller.com/nha-trang-ba-ho-waterfall-half-day-trip/). Guide optional but handy for the river section.

### Yang Bay waterfall — *offbeat*
Full day, moderate (steep, uneven viewpoint climb; doable for most), guide included. 80 m falls, ethnic-minority area, hot springs ~45 km inland [[31]](https://travel-buddies.com/from-nha-trang-full-day-trip-to-yang-bay-waterfall-2/).

## Mui Ne / Phan Thiet

### Sand-dunes jeep tour — *touristy*
Half-day (sunrise or sunset), easy, driver-guide. White Dunes (biggest), Red Dunes (sandboarding, small extra), Fairy Stream wade, fishing village — ~€29 per jeep (up to 5 people) [[32]](https://c2skykitecenter.com/mui-ne-sand-dune-jeep-tour/) [[33]](https://muine-explorer.com/en/mui-ne-tour/mui-ne-sunset-sand-dunes-jeep-tour.html). Sandboarding is gentle; no skill needed.

### Kitesurf intro — *touristy* (⚠ off-season in June)
Moderate, IKO-certified instructor required. [C2Sky](https://c2skykitecenter.com/) is the established school: a 2 h private **Taster €100**, 7 h / 2-day **Discovery €385**, or 1 h refresher €60 [[10]](https://c2skykitecenter.com/vietnam-kitesurf-lessons/). Mui Ne is Vietnam's kite capital but peak wind is **Nov–Apr** — June is light/unreliable, so treat this as a fair-weather-window taster, not a guaranteed session [[10]](https://c2skykitecenter.com/vietnam-kitesurf-lessons/).

### Ta Cu mountain + reclining Buddha — *semi-offbeat*
Half-day, easy by cable car (or moderate ~2 h forest climb on foot), no guide needed. 30 km SW of Phan Thiet; Vietnam's largest reclining Buddha (49 m) atop a jungle peak. Combo ticket (electric cart + cable car return) ~€9; foot entry only ~€2 [[34]](https://nashaplaneta.net/asia/vietnam/phanthiet-muine-dostoprimechatelnosti-gora-taku_en) [[35]](https://guidesandstories.com/ta-cu-mountain-travel-guide/).

## Phu Quoc (great for jungle/north in June; weak for An Thoi snorkel)

### National-park day hikes — *offbeat*
Half- or full-day, easy→challenging, most need no guide. **Ganh Dau** (flat forest track, beginner-friendly) and **Suoi Tranh** (shaded stream-and-falls walk) are easy [[36]](https://cassiacottage.com/en/exploring-phu-quoc-national-park-trails/). **Ham Ninh** is moderate; guided jungle hikes rate ~4.5/10 difficulty [[37]](https://www.vietnamparadisetravel.com/blog/phu-quoc-trekking-trails). ⚠ **Mount Chua (603 m)** is steep/strenuous — beyond the accessible brief, skip it [[37]](https://www.vietnamparadisetravel.com/blog/phu-quoc-trekking-trails). Wet-season trails are muddy; go in the morning [[38]](https://revitrip.com/blog/phu-quoc-national-park-eco-tourism-guide).

### North-island kayak + Starfish Beach (Rach Vem) — *offbeat*
Half/full-day, easy, tour/guide. Kayak the calm Cua Can river and wade the shallows at **Rach Vem** to see orange starfish at low tide [[39]](https://phuquoctrip.com/en/daily-land-tours/land-tour-2-discover-the-north-kayaking-starfish-beach-phu-quoc.html) [[40]](https://vinpearl.com/en/starfish-beach-phu-quoc-your-dream-like-vacation). North-island kayak day tours are the better June water option since they're sheltered vs. the open-sea islets [[41]](https://www.pelago.com/en/activity/pkx2phuq8-northern-phu-quoc-day-tour-starfish-beach-rach-vem-village-and-kayaking-experience-phu-quoc/).

### An Thoi 4-island snorkel + Hon Thom cable car — *touristy* (⚠ June)
Full day (~8.5 h), easy, guide included. High-speed canoe to Gam Ghi/May Rut islets to snorkel, seafood lunch, then the world's longest over-sea cable car (7.9 km) to Hon Thom [[42]](https://www.viator.com/tours/Phu-Quoc/SNORKELING-AND-SQUID-FISHING-COMBO-TOUR-IN-PHU-QUOC-ISLAND/d22452-6126P201) [[43]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionProductReview-g12520037-d28033961-Phu_Quoc_4_Islands_Snorkeling_Tour_With_Hon_Thom_Cable_Car-An_Thoi_Phu_Quoc_Isla.html). Sun World Hon Thom ticket alone is 750,000 VND (~€27); +lunch combo ~€36 [[44]](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/20750-sun-world-hon-thom-cable-car-ticket/) [[45]](https://thaiest.com/vietnam/travel/hon-thom-ticket-price). ⚠ In June rough seas reduce snorkel viz and can cancel islet runs [[1]](https://vinpearl.com/en/phu-quoc-weather-in-june-how-to-plan-your-stay-accordingly) — the cable car still operates, but don't make the snorkel the centrepiece.

### Fun diving (An Thoi, south) — *touristy*
Half/full-day, easy–moderate, certified or discover-dive option. Fun dives from ~€51; PADI Open Water course ~€350–420 over 3–4 days [[46]](https://www.viator.com/Phu-Quoc-tours/Scuba-Diving/d22452-g17-c28901) [[47]](https://divephuquoc.com/fun-diving). ⚠ June visibility is poor [[48]](https://www.padi.com/diving-in/phu-quoc/) — defer serious diving to Nha Trang this trip.

**Bottom line for June:** lead with Nha Trang for water (calm, clear) [[3]](https://www.tnktravel.com/blogs/vietnam-weather-in-june-your-complete-planning-guide/); use HCMC/Mekong for culture-and-canals (rain-tolerant, morning-clear) [[2]](https://vinwonders.com/en/wonderpedia/news/vietnam-weather-a-detailed-guide-to-time-your-perfect-trip/); on Phu Quoc favour jungle hikes and the sheltered north over open-sea snorkel [[1]](https://vinpearl.com/en/phu-quoc-weather-in-june-how-to-plan-your-stay-accordingly); save the Mui Ne kite session for a windy day, dunes any time [[10]](https://c2skykitecenter.com/vietnam-kitesurf-lessons/).
