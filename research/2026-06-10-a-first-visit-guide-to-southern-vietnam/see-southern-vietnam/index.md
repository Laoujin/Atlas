> **TL;DR:** On a first trip, the genuine must-sees are the **War Remnants Museum** + **Reunification Palace** (HCMC's war-history pair), the **Cu Chi tunnels** (go to Ben Duoc for fewer crowds), the **Cai Rang floating market** at dawn (overnight in Can Tho — a day trip won't catch it), and **Po Nagar** + an island boat day in Nha Trang. The **Cao Dai noon ceremony** at Tay Ninh is the south's most singular sight. **Phu Quoc** is for beaches, not culture. Note two things: there is **no UNESCO World Heritage Site in the deep south** [[41]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_World_Heritage_Sites_in_Vietnam) (they're all central/north), and your **2026-06-10 timing is wet season** — fine for HCMC mornings and great for Nha Trang, but bad for Phu Quoc sea trips [[30]](https://ahoyvietnam.com/best-time-to-visit-vietnam/)[[32]](https://vinpearl.com/en/phu-quoc-weather-the-best-time-to-visit).

Prices in EUR are rough conversions at ~27,500 VND/€. Almost nothing here needs advance booking; the few timed/queue-sensitive items are flagged.

## Ho Chi Minh City

**[War Remnants Museum](https://baotangchungtichchientranh.vn/en)** — the single most affecting sight in the south [[2]](https://ahoyvietnam.com/war-remnants-museum/). Confronting but essential context for the whole trip. Open daily 07:30-17:30; adults 40,000 VND (~€1.5), under-6 free [[1]](https://baotangchungtichchientranh.vn/en/visit). Ticket counter pauses around 11:00 and 16:00 — arrive mid-morning [[1]](https://baotangchungtichchientranh.vn/en/visit). Allow 2-3h. _Touristy, must-do._

**[Reunification (Independence) Palace](https://www.bestpricetravel.com/travel-guide/reunification-palace.html)** — the preserved 1960s-70s seat of South Vietnam where the war ended; 135 Nam Ky Khoi Nghia, D1 [[10]](https://viet-go.com/en/attractions/independence-palace-saigon-guide). Open ~08:00-15:30; adults 40,000 VND (~€1.5), with some combined tickets up to 80,000 VND [[11]](https://www.bestpricetravel.com/travel-guide/reunification-palace.html). Free in-lobby guides; dress modestly (no shorts/skirts) [[10]](https://viet-go.com/en/attractions/independence-palace-saigon-guide). Pair it with the museum for a half-day. _Touristy, must-do._

**[Central Post Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saigon_Central_Post_Office)** — French-colonial hall (built 1886-1891), still a working post office, free to enter, ~08:00-17:00 [[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saigon_Central_Post_Office). ⚠ The **Notre-Dame Cathedral** across the square (built 1863-1880 [[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notre-Dame_Cathedral_Basilica_of_Saigon)) is under heavy renovation, scaffolded, interior largely closed, with work expected to run to ~2027 [[22]](https://news.destination-asia.com/saigon-notre-dame-cathedral-closes-its-doors-for-renovation/) — exterior photos only for now. _Touristy, nice-to-have._

**Ben Thanh Market** — the iconic 1912 covered market; its façade and front square were refreshed in a renovation that reopened ahead of Tet 2026 [[33]](https://english.thesaigontimes.vn/ben-thanh-market-square-opens-to-visitors-after-renovation/)[[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%E1%BA%BFn_Th%C3%A0nh_Market). Good for a wander and street-food snacks; expect hard-sell and tourist pricing. _Touristy, nice-to-have._

**Skydeck view** — two options. [Bitexco Saigon Skydeck](http://www.bitexcofinancialtower.com/) (49th floor) is cheaper and central, ~200,000 VND (~€7) adult [[24]](https://ahoyvietnam.com/saigon-skydeck/)[[25]](https://www.traveloka.com/en-en/activities/vietnam/product/saigon-skydeck-at-bitexco-financial-tower-tickets-2001465639912). [Landmark 81 SkyView](https://www.klook.com/activity/27021-saigon-skyview-ticket-ho-chi-minh-city/) is higher (390m) but ~300,000 VND (~€11) and further out in Binh Thanh [[26]](https://www.klook.com/activity/27021-saigon-skyview-ticket-ho-chi-minh-city/). Many skip both in favour of a rooftop bar at sunset. _Touristy, nice-to-have._

## Day trips from HCMC

**Cu Chi tunnels** — the Viet Cong tunnel network; crawl a widened section, see booby-trap exhibits. Two sites: **Ben Dinh** (~50km, closer, more crowded, wider tourist tunnels) vs **Ben Duoc** (~70km, far fewer crowds, more authentic, 100ha vs 17ha) [[3]](https://ecosapabus.net/guides/vietnam/south-vietnam/cu-chi-tunnels-travel-guide/)[[5]](https://vietnamdiscovery.com/ho-chi-minh-city/attractions/cu-chi-tunnels/). For a first visit that wants substance over selfies, **choose Ben Duoc**. Entrance ~110,000 VND (~€4) [[3]](https://ecosapabus.net/guides/vietnam/south-vietnam/cu-chi-tunnels-travel-guide/); a guided day tour from HCMC runs 600,000-1,200,000 VND (~€22-44) [[4]](https://www.machupicchu.org/cu-chi-tunnels-day-trip-from-ho-chi-minh-city-2026-vietnam-war-history-guide.htm). _Touristy, must-do._

**Cao Dai Holy See, Tay Ninh** — the dazzling pastel cathedral of Caodaism (a 1926 Vietnamese faith fusing Buddhism, Taoism, Confucianism, Christianity and Islam) [[13]](https://www.vietnamtravelsonline.com/travel-blog/cao-dai-temple/), ~95km NW (2-2.5h). The **noon ceremony at 12:00 daily** is the draw: hundreds of white-robed adherents, observed from the upper gallery; free entry, modest dress [[14]](https://sungetawaystravel.com/cao-dai-holy-see/). ⚠ Timed: you must arrive before noon, so book a tour that explicitly targets the 12:00 service — most pair it with Cu Chi in one long day [[12]](https://www.tnktravel.com/tours/tnk-signature-tours/cao-dai-temple-cu-chi-tunnels-private-full-day-tour-from-ho-chi-minh-city). The south's most singular sight. _Mid, must-do._

**Mekong Delta** — decide between two very different trips:

| Option | Towns | Travel from HCMC | What you get |
|------------------|--------------------|------------------|-------------------------------|
| Day trip         | My Tho + Ben Tre   | ~2h each way [[44]][a] | Coconut-canal sampans, workshops; convenient but no floating market |
| Overnight        | Can Tho            | ~3-3.5h each way [[44]][a] | **Cai Rang floating market at dawn** — the real thing |

[a]: https://joyjourneys.com.vn/article/mekong-delta-things-to-do-in-one-day/

The standard one-day tour is My Tho + Ben Tre, with Ben Tre the quieter, less touristy half [[43]](https://www.viator.com/tours/Ho-Chi-Minh-City/MEKONG-DELTA-MY-THO-and-BEN-TRE-1-DAY-SMALL-GROUP-TOUR/d352-18631P7). But the postcard image — boats stacked with produce, wares hoisted on tall poles — is **Cai Rang**, the delta's biggest floating market, near Can Tho [[9]](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/points-of-interest/cai-rang-floating-market/1389935). It runs ~05:00-09:00 and peaks 05:30-06:30, so you must sleep in Can Tho and launch from Ninh Kieu wharf at dawn [[7]](https://vinpearl.com/en/cai-rang-floating-market). Shared boats ~50,000-100,000 VND (~€2-4); a private boat 500,000-600,000 VND (~€18-22) for 2-3h [[8]](https://workshop.thescentnote.com/can-tho-floating-markets-sunrise-itinerary-2026/)[[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g303942-d455150-Reviews-Cai_Rang_Floating_Market-Can_Tho_Mekong_Delta.html). _Touristy, must-do — but only the overnight version delivers it._

## Nha Trang / Mui Ne (south-central coast)

**Po Nagar Cham towers** — a 9th-century-or-older Cham Hindu temple complex on a hill above the Cai River, ~3km north of town [[16]](https://vinpearl.com/en/ponagar-cham-towers-an-outstanding-cultural-heritage-in-nha-trang)[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Po_Nagar). Open 08:00-18:00; entry ~25,000-30,000 VND (~€1) [[15]](https://seayoutravel.vn/en/po-nagar-cham/)[[16]](https://vinpearl.com/en/ponagar-cham-towers-an-outstanding-cultural-heritage-in-nha-trang). The closest thing to a "heritage" monument in the south. _Touristy, must-do._

**Island & snorkel day** — Nha Trang's bay is the reason to come. **Hon Mun** is the marine-protected snorkel/dive spot (best April-July) [[38]](https://vinwonders.com/en/wonderpedia/news/hon-mun-island-the-complete-guide-for-a-dream-like-excursion/); boat tours from ~500,000-1,500,000 VND (~€18-55) cover several islands [[39]](https://localvietnam.com/khanh-hoa/nha-trang/best-islands-in-nha-trang/). The cable car to **VinWonders** on Hon Tre is the family/theme-park option [[39]](https://localvietnam.com/khanh-hoa/nha-trang/best-islands-in-nha-trang/). _Touristy, high value._

**Mui Ne sand dunes** (Phan Thiet, ~4-5h from HCMC or en route) — White and Red sand dunes, the Fairy Stream (Suoi Tien) and a fishing village, done as a sunrise or sunset jeep tour [[27]](https://muine-explorer.com/en/tour/daily-tour-mui-ne/jeep-tour-mui-ne-vn.html). Add quad bikes/sandboarding for a few hundred thousand VND extra [[28]](https://www.getyourguide.com/fishing-village-binh-thuan-l199555/mui-ne-group-tour-sunrisesunset-quad-bike-adventure-t998086/). Worth it only if you're already on the coast; not a destination on its own for a short first trip. _Mid, nice-to-have._

## Phu Quoc (island)

Phu Quoc is a **beach-and-resort** stop, not a culture stop. Core sights:

- **Sao Beach** (Bãi Sao) — the island's signature 2.5km powder-white SE-coast beach; go before ~08:30 to beat the 10:00-14:00 crowds [[19]](https://vinpearl.com/en/the-top-11-phu-quoc-beaches-you-cannot-miss-out-on). **Khem Beach** (luxury, exclusive) and the long western **Bai Dai** are the other top picks [[19]](https://vinpearl.com/en/the-top-11-phu-quoc-beaches-you-cannot-miss-out-on)[[18]](https://stelmakh.com/blog/best-beaches-phu-quoc). An independent guide is blunt that fast development has dented several beaches — manage expectations [[20]](https://www.vietnamcoracle.com/phu-quoc-beaches-a-guide/). _Mixed touristy/offbeat._
- **[Hon Thom cable car](https://sunset-town.com/en/ve-sunworld-hon-thom/)** — the world's longest three-wire over-sea cable car (7,899.9m) to Sun World Hon Thom [[37]](https://sunset-town.com/en/ve-sunworld-hon-thom/); ~700,000 VND (~€25) all-in adult [[35]](https://hoiandaytrip.com/hon-thom-cable-car/)[[36]](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/20750-sun-world-hon-thom-cable-car-ticket/). The ride itself is the attraction. _Touristy._
- **VinWonders Phu Quoc** + Safari + **[Grand World](https://vinwonders.com/en/grandworld-phu-quoc-price-and-regulations/)** / **Sunset Town** (Kiss Bridge, evening "Kiss of the Sea" show) — Vietnam's biggest theme park (50ha) and a cluster of built attractions in the north and south of the island [[48]](https://rootytrip.com/en/vinwonders-phu-quoc/)[[49]](https://vinwonders.com/en/grandworld-phu-quoc-price-and-regulations/). _Very touristy; skip if you came for nature._

⚠ **Timing flag:** your 2026-06-10 dates sit at the start of Phu Quoc's wet/monsoon swing into July — mornings can be fine but afternoon rain and rough seas cancel snorkel/island trips; May-June is the cut-off, July is poor [[32]](https://vinpearl.com/en/phu-quoc-weather-the-best-time-to-visit)[[30]](https://ahoyvietnam.com/best-time-to-visit-vietnam/).

## Offbeat: Con Dao

The **Con Dao** archipelago (16 islands, Con Son the main one) is the south's standout offbeat option — pristine beaches like Dam Trau plus the sobering **Con Dao Prison**, the "Hell on Earth" penal complex run 1862-1975 [[46]](https://vietnam.travel/places-to-go/southern-vietnam/con-dao)[[47]](https://vinwonders.com/en/wonderpedia/news/con-dao-island-vietnam/). Reached by ~45-min VASCO/Vietnam Airlines flights from HCMC, several daily [[45]](https://www.vietnamairlines.com/us/en/plan-book/travel/travel-guide/dao-con-son). Best Nov-Apr (dry); June is shoulder. Add only if you have spare days. _Offbeat._

## On UNESCO and weather (set expectations)

**No UNESCO World Heritage Site lies in the deep south** — all nine of Vietnam's are central or north (Hoi An, My Son, Hue, Ha Long, Phong Nha, etc.) [[41]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_World_Heritage_Sites_in_Vietnam). The south's closest "UNESCO" labels are **Biosphere Reserves**, not World Heritage: **Cat Tien National Park** (UNESCO Biosphere 2001; only ever on the tentative WH list, application withdrawn 2013) [[42]](https://vinpearl.com/en/cat-tien-national-park-a-green-sanctuary-with-rare-species)[[40]](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5070/), and Phu Quoc's biosphere designation. So calibrate: in the south you're seeing **war history, Cham/colonial-era architecture, the delta and beaches** — not ancient World Heritage monuments.

**Weather for 2026-06-10:** southern Vietnam is in the wet season (heaviest rain Jun-Aug) [[29]](https://www.selectiveasia.com/vietnam-holidays/weather). In HCMC and the delta this means hot ~29°C days with short, intense afternoon downpours (~14:00-17:00) — sightsee in the mornings and it barely affects you [[30]](https://ahoyvietnam.com/best-time-to-visit-vietnam/). **Nha Trang is the exception** — its dry season runs roughly to September, so July-Sept is actually clear and ideal there [[31]](https://www.asiatouradvisor.com/get-inspired/vietnam/vietnam-weather-in-july/). Phu Quoc is the weak link for these dates [[32]](https://vinpearl.com/en/phu-quoc-weather-the-best-time-to-visit).
