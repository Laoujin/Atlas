> **TL;DR** Chengdu is a credible tech-event city — not Shanghai-scale but with genuine depth in cybersecurity (C3 Security, annual 8-year run), AI/streaming content (China Network Audio-Visual Conference, permanent host since 2016), and B2B electronics. **April** is the peak conference month; **July** brings two major electronics expos at Century City. Industry events are Chinese-language; IEEE academic conferences are English-friendly.

## Anchor Events

### C3 Security Conference (8th edition, Apr 17–18)

[C3](https://c3.asiainfo-sec.com/) is [AsiaInfo Security](https://www.asiainfo.com/en_us/content_5119.html)'s flagship annual conference, now in its 8th year in Chengdu [[5]](https://www.asiainfo.com/en_us/content_5119.html). The 2026 theme "Thriving with AI" centred on the transition from AI-as-tool to AI-as-agent; seven specialised tracks covered telecom security, financial AI, energy, manufacturing, and data governance [[5]](https://www.asiainfo.com/en_us/content_5119.html). Venue: Tianfu International Convention Center, Tianfu New District. Sponsors: Huawei, Alibaba Cloud, Baidu [[5]](https://www.asiainfo.com/en_us/content_5119.html). Primarily Chinese-language; industry practitioners rather than researchers.

### China Network Audio-Visual Conference (13th edition, Apr 14–16)

Chengdu has been the **permanent host city since 2016** [[4]](https://www.eeo.com.cn/2026/0415/838876.shtml). The 13th edition drew 12,000+ attendees from 4,000+ institutions — organised by China's National Radio and Television Administration [[3]](https://www.nrta.gov.cn/art/2026/4/15/art_112_73087.html). 2026 focus: AI content generation, virtual shooting, micro-drama economics, cross-border distribution, and AI-agent-driven production ("one-person companies") [[3]](https://www.nrta.gov.cn/art/2026/4/15/art_112_73087.html). Venue: Century City International Conference Center, Wuhou District. Relevant for streaming, video AI, or digital entertainment professionals.

### Chengdu International Industrial Expo (CDIIF, Mar 11–13)

The [CDIIF](https://www.cdiif.com/) is positioned as the "opening industrial event of the Chengdu-Chongqing economic circle" [[1]](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/03-11/10585246.shtml). Scale: 60,000 m², 800 exhibitors, 60,000 expected visitors [[2]](https://www.cdiif.com/). 2026 highlights included humanoid robots, cloud-integrated control systems, and a parallel "Eastern Data-Western Computing" summit on green-intelligent industrial transition [[1]](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/03-11/10585246.shtml). Venue: Western China International Expo City, Tianfu New District. B2B manufacturing focus; relevant for industrial IT and edge computing.

## Academic Conferences

Both IEEE events are English-friendly with proceedings published to IEEE Xplore:

- **[IEEE ICCCBDA 2026](https://icccbda.com/welcome.html)** (Apr 24–26) — 11th edition, cloud computing and big data. Co-sponsored by the Sichuan Institute of Electronics and IEEE; hosted at Tibet Hotel, Chengdu [[6]](https://icccbda.com/welcome.html). Papers indexed in EI Compendex and Scopus.
- **[IEEE ICAICI 2026](https://www.researchgate.net/post/CFP_IEEE_2026_International_Conference_on_Artificial_Intelligence_Cyber-Physical_Systems_and_Intelligent_ComputingICAICI_2026)** (Jun 12–14) — AI, cyber-physical systems, intelligent computing [[7]](https://www.researchgate.net/post/CFP_IEEE_2026_International_Conference_on_Artificial_Intelligence_Cyber-Physical_Systems_and_Intelligent_ComputingICAICI_2026). Venue TBD in Chengdu.

## Electronics Expos (July, Century City)

Two large shows in July at [Chengdu Century City New International Convention Center](https://global.cotv.tv/expo/26029), Wuhou District:

- **[China (Western) Electronic Information Expo](https://www.globalomp.com/expo/24159)** (Jul 8–10) — B2B; 14th/15th edition; 30,000 m²; exhibitors include Huawei, BOE, DJI, Intel, AMD [[8]](https://global.cotv.tv/expo/26029)[[9]](https://www.globalomp.com/expo/24159). Focus: integrated circuits, 5G/6G, AI models, low-altitude economy. ⚠ Two organiser listings show conflicting dates (Jul 8–10 vs Jul 15–17); confirm before booking.
- **[Chengdu Consumer Electronics Exhibition](https://www.globalomp.com/expo/26212)** (Jul 15–17) — B2C; 60,000 m², 80,000 visitors, 50+ concurrent forums [[10]](https://www.globalomp.com/expo/26212). Theme: "Intelligent Connected Everything: AI Empowers the Future." Focus: AI gadgets, new displays, health tech, smart home.

## Community & Recurring Forums

[CCF YOCSEF Chengdu](https://www.ccf.org.cn/YOCSEF/Branches/Chengdu/) runs monthly technical forums year-round for the local CS/engineering community [[11]](https://www.ccf.org.cn/YOCSEF/Branches/Chengdu/). The 2025–2026 programme targets AI agents, cybersecurity, low-altitude economy, and AI in education [[12]](https://yocsef.org.cn/YOCSEF/xwdt/qthd/2025-05-20/842347.shtml). Brand series: "Qingcheng Wendao" (青城问道) and "Emei Lunjian" (峨眉论剑) [[12]](https://yocsef.org.cn/YOCSEF/xwdt/qthd/2025-05-20/842347.shtml). Typically free, primarily Chinese; useful for connecting with local academia.

[Cursor](https://cursor-insider.com/meetups) runs Meetups, Hackathons, and Workshops in Chengdu as part of its China city-tour programme [[14]](https://cursor-insider.com/meetups).

## Context: Why Chengdu Punches Above Its Size

[Tianfu Long Island](https://m.sohu.com/a/1010049160_116237) digital creative park already hosts Tencent New Creative HQ, NetEase Thunder Fire Studio, DJI, and 70+ other firms with ~6,000 employees [[15]](https://m.sohu.com/a/1010049160_116237). Sichuan targets 1.7 trillion yuan in core AI industry output by 2026. This industrial base — plus Chengdu's low cost of living for talent — explains why events like C3 and the Audio-Visual Conference have made the city their permanent home rather than rotating through Beijing or Shanghai.
