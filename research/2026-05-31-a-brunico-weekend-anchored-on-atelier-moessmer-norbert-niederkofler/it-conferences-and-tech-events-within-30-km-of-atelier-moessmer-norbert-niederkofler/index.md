> **TL;DR.** Inside the 30 km radius (air-line) around [Atelier Moessmer](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/trentino-alto-adige/brunico/restaurant/atelier-moessmer-norbert-niederkofler) at Via Walther von der Vogelweide 17, Brunico [[1]](https://www.oadguides.com/restaurant/atelier-moessmer-norbert-niederkofler), there are six confirmed 2026 events. Five sit in Brixen / Bressanone (23.6 km air-line, ~35 km by road [[2]](https://www.distance.to/Brixen/Bruneck-Brunico)); one is the not-yet-dated IN MOTION Tech Festival at NOI Techpark Brunico itself [[12]](https://www.automotive-suedtirol.com/tech-festival/). The cleanest pair-with-dinner window is **23 May → 13 June**, when CSAMA, DSTNCMP and OntoBRIX cluster back-to-back in Brixen. The region's flagship — [SFSCON](https://www.sfscon.it/) — sits in Bolzano (~75 km), outside the circle [[21]](https://www.sfscon.it/).

**Distance sanity-check.** Brunico → Brixen is 23.6 km air-line and ~35 km by road [[2]](https://www.distance.to/Brixen/Bruneck-Brunico) — Brixen is *inside* a 30 km radius but *outside* a 30 km drive. If "30 km" means driving distance, drop the Brixen rows; if it means radius, they all qualify. Atelier Moessmer is in central Brunico [[1]](https://www.oadguides.com/restaurant/atelier-moessmer-norbert-niederkofler), so this difference is real — call it before you commit.

## What's actually IT vs. tech-adjacent

- **Pure IT / software-engineering:** none of the listed events is a developer conference in the JSConf / DevOpsDays sense. [SFSCON](https://www.sfscon.it/) is the one in South Tyrol that fits — and it's outside the circle [[21]](https://www.sfscon.it/).
- **Closest to IT:** [OntoBRIX](https://sites.google.com/view/spe13-ontobrix2026/) (semantic technologies, knowledge representation, CS faculty) [[8]](https://sites.google.com/view/spe13-ontobrix2026/), and [CSAMA](https://csama2026.bioconductor.eu/) (R/Bioconductor, computational biology — instructors from Harvard Medical School, EMBL, UC Louvain, Padova [[22]](https://www.huber.embl.de/group/events/csama2026.html)).
- **Tech-adjacent / industry:** [IN MOTION Tech Festival](https://www.automotive-suedtirol.com/tech-festival/) at NOI Techpark Brunico is the real local "tech expo" — AI, automation, robotics, VR, Industry 4.0 — but its 2026 date is not yet published [[12]](https://www.automotive-suedtirol.com/tech-festival/). The 2025 edition ran 10 May 2025 [[13]](https://www.altoadigeinnovazione.it/tech-festival/).
- **Domain-tech:** [Cognitive Science Arena](https://www.escop.eu/news/news/conference-news/cognitive-science-arena) (neuro / cognition) [[3]](https://www.escop.eu/news/news/conference-news/cognitive-science-arena) and [CMT@Brixen](https://cmtconference.it/) (physics) [[9]](https://cmtconference.it/) are scientific conferences with technology overlap, not IT events.
- **Tourism × tech:** [DSTNCMP](https://www.destinationcamp.com/austragungsort-2026-brixen-suedtirol) explicitly programs "technology and platforms" alongside hospitality and destinations [[5]](https://www.destinationcamp.com/2025-3) — relevant only if your interest is travel-tech, MarTech for destinations, or platform economics.

## Recurring series with no fixed 2026 date

- **[IN MOTION Tech Festival](https://www.automotive-suedtirol.com/tech-festival/)** (AES Automotive Excellence Südtirol). 2026 announced as a placeholder; 2025 ran 10 May at NOI Techpark Brunico, expect a similar May/June slot [[12]](https://www.automotive-suedtirol.com/tech-festival/) [[13]](https://www.altoadigeinnovazione.it/tech-festival/).
- **[Automotive Transformation Conference](https://www.altoadigeinnovazione.it/noi-techpark-brunico-conferenza-internazionale-automotive/)** (NOI / Interreg CE *Drive2Transform*). Ran 23–24 October 2025 at NOI Techpark Brunico; the Drive2Transform project runs through end-2026, so a 2026 successor is plausible but unannounced [[14]](https://www.altoadigeinnovazione.it/noi-techpark-brunico-conferenza-internazionale-automotive/).
- **[Tech Talks South Tyrol](https://www.meetup.com/tech-talks-south-tyrol/)** developer meetup (532 members). Alternates Brixen ↔ Bolzano; recent Brixen hosts include Progress Holding AG (May 27, 2025, 43 attendees) [[16]](https://www.meetup.com/tech-talks-south-tyrol/) and Duka (Nov 2024) [[17]](https://x.com/tech_talks_st/status/1859511228449657331). No upcoming 2026 event listed as of writing — check Meetup closer to your travel dates.
- **[TTTech Innovation Campus Brixen](https://www.tttech.com/press/innovation-campus-brixen)**. Targets 2026 opening, with a public "mailab" AI lab and machine-vision focus (TTControl, MiCROTEC). Worth a side-visit if it's open during your trip; no public conference programme yet [[15]](https://www.tttech.com/press/innovation-campus-brixen).

## Just outside the circle (for context)

- **[SFSCON 2026](https://www.sfscon.it/)** — South Tyrol's flagship Free Software conference, 13–14 Nov 2026, NOI Techpark Bolzano (~75 km from Brunico, ~1 h drive) [[21]](https://www.sfscon.it/). The closest thing to a "developer conference" in the region; falls outside any "30 km" reading.

## Picking the weekend

- **Best (May 23 → Jun 13)** — three back-to-back events (CSAMA, OntoBRIX, with DSTNCMP closing the prior weekend). Saturday Atelier Moessmer dinners pair cleanly with the CSAMA 2026 mid-week (**Sat 30 May**) or the post-CSAMA **Sat 6 Jun** (good for OntoBRIX arrivals).
- **Feb 7 (Sat)** — Cognitive Science Arena closing day, but expect cold-season alpine logistics.
- **Aug 28 (Fri evening) → 29 (Sat)** — CMT@Brixen closes Friday; Saturday Atelier Moessmer dinner is a clean cap.
- **Don't anchor on IN MOTION Tech Festival** until AES publishes the 2026 date [[12]](https://www.automotive-suedtirol.com/tech-festival/); the 2025 slot was a single Saturday (10 May).
