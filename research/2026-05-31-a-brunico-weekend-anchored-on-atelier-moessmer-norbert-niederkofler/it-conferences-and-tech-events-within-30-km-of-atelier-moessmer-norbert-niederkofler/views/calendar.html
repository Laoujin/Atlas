02Picking the Saturday

Sat 30 May · CSAMA mid-week

The best pairing

CSAMA's hands-on labs run all week; Saturday is a free evening. Take the train Brixen → Brunico (~35 min by road [2]) and let Atelier Moessmer cap the week.

Sat 6 Jun · between events

The clean cap

CSAMA finishes Fri 29 May, OntoBRIX opens Mon 8 Jun. The Saturday in between is the ideal arrival-day dinner for OntoBRIX speakers.

Sat 13 Jun · OntoBRIX wrap weekend

The unwind

OntoBRIX closes Wed 10 Jun. A Saturday dinner here lets attendees stay on for a Plan-de-Corones day before flying out — and falls inside the cluster window's last day.

The Brunico-local festival is the only event that lets the dinner happen inside Brunico itself — but AES hasn't published a 2026 date.

Until then: anchor on CSAMA week or the post-CSAMA Saturday.

04The 35 km caveat

23.6KM AIR-LINE

Brunico → Brixen is 23.6 km air-line but ~35 km by road. [2] Brixen is inside a 30 km radius but outside a 30 km drive. If "30 km" means driving distance, drop the Brixen rows. Atelier Moessmer is in central Brunico, so the difference is real — call it before you commit.
