> **Decision.** Sleep on the Troisgros estate: [Le Bois Dormant](https://www.troisgros.fr/en/le-bois-dormant/) — Relais & Châteaux, 15 rooms, from €300/night [[1]](https://fr.gaultmillau.com/en/hotels/hotel-troisgros). Same estate at half the price: [Le Pré Cheval](https://www.troisgros.fr/en/le-pre-cheval/), three gîtes run by sibling Marion (€220–320) [[2]](https://www.troisgros.fr/le-pre-cheval/les-hebergements/). Cheapest real walks: [Domaine Bérenguer](https://www.gites-de-france-loire.com/location-chambre-d-hotes-ouches-loire-42G3714.html) or [Domaine d'Aromm](https://domainedaromm.fr/), €125–165 with breakfast [[3]](https://www.gites-de-france-loire.com/location-chambre-d-hotes-ouches-loire-42G3714.html)[[4]](https://domainedaromm.fr/). Or treat dinner as a shuttled trip from another Michelin★ B&B: [Château d'Origny](https://www.chateaudorigny.com/en/) (free return ride to Troisgros) [[5]](https://www.chateaudorigny.com/en/).

## The five candidates

Distances are from each property's address to **728 Route de Villerest, Ouches** (Maison Troisgros) [[6]](https://www.troisgros.fr/en/le-bois-dormant/). All five sit inside the commune of Ouches — "walking distance" is real, but rural-road real. See *Getting home after dinner* below before you book on price alone.

## Le Bois Dormant — the obvious answer

The Troisgros family's own 5★ hotel sits on the same 17-hectare estate as the restaurant. 12 rooms in the Grande Maison, 3 in the converted *Maison des Kakis* wine press [[1]](https://fr.gaultmillau.com/en/hotels/hotel-troisgros). Rooms from €300/night without breakfast (€40 pp), with the average double-with-breakfast around €510 [[1]](https://fr.gaultmillau.com/en/hotels/hotel-troisgros)[[7]](https://www.ticati.com/hotel/maison-troisgros-88704/). #1 of 1 hotels in Ouches on TripAdvisor at 4.6/5 across 375 reviews, Travellers' Choice [[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3845710-d231071-Reviews-Troisgros-Ouches_Roanne_Loire_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html). Annual closure **Dec 22 2025 – Jan 14 2026** [[6]](https://www.troisgros.fr/en/le-bois-dormant/). A multi-hour 3★ dinner ends late and Ouches isn't lit — staying on-site removes the only logistics problem this trip has.

## Le Pré Cheval — sibling Marion's farm

Marion Troisgros — elder sister to César and Léo — runs three gîtes on the family farm next door, a few minutes' walk from the restaurant [[9]](https://www.troisgros.fr/en/le-pre-cheval/). Petit Pied-à-terre €220 (sleeps 2), Grand Pied-à-terre €320 (+2 children at €35 each), Petit Pied-à-terre familial €220 (+2 children) [[2]](https://www.troisgros.fr/le-pre-cheval/les-hebergements/). Self-service check-in from 16:30, breakfast basket from Troisgros €25 pp on reservation [[2]](https://www.troisgros.fr/le-pre-cheval/les-hebergements/). Total capacity 17 across the property — doubles as a "rent the whole farm" option for a group; closed Mondays and Tuesdays [[10]](https://www.loiretourisme.com/en/offers/le-pre-cheval-ouches-en-4992419/). Le Pré Cheval and Maison Troisgros are formally separate businesses despite the family link [[9]](https://www.troisgros.fr/en/le-pre-cheval/).

## Château d'Origny — Michelin-starred B&B, ⚠ shuttle not stroll

760 m from Troisgros on the route de Roanne side of the village. Five 40 m²+ suites in a 16th-century château, with a hammam/jacuzzi in the Colonial suite [[5]](https://www.chateaudorigny.com/en/). The complication: Château d'Origny runs its own Michelin-starred restaurant under chef Julien Laval (★ since 2023, 22-seat room) [[11]](https://www.chateaudorigny.com/en/presentation), so it competes for the dinner slot. If you're using it as base camp for Troisgros, the château offers **free return transport to La Maison Troisgros** — the only purpose-built "we'll drive you home" service among non-Troisgros properties [[5]](https://www.chateaudorigny.com/en/). TripAdvisor: 4/5 across 131 reviews, #1 of 2 B&Bs in Ouches, observed rate ~€187/night [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3845710-d2280160-Reviews-Chateau_D_origny_Les_Chambres_D_hotes-Ouches_Roanne_Loire_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html).

## Domaine d'Aromm — country B&B

420 Chemin de la Vésinière. Five rooms scaling from 2-pax classics up to a 7-pax *Grande Suite*, €125–165, breakfast included and served in a veranda over the pond [[4]](https://domainedaromm.fr/). 27 ha working equestrian estate with heated pool, two ponds, and paddocks [[13]](https://www.roannais-tourisme.com/en/chambres-hotes/domaine-daromm/). Owners pitch "less than 15 min on foot" through Roannaise nature to Troisgros — fine for the daytime check-in walk, less obviously fine at 23:00 [[4]](https://domainedaromm.fr/). The mid-week *étape* package (€155 pp dîner + room + breakfast) keeps you on-site for dinner; it's not a Troisgros night.

## Domaine Bérenguer — Gîtes de France pick

3 rooms (160×200 beds throughout), €130 double / €125 single, breakfast included [[3]](https://www.gites-de-france-loire.com/location-chambre-d-hotes-ouches-loire-42G3714.html). 1 km from Troisgros across a 2-hectare park with private pool, tennis court, and a 200-year-old sequoia. Owner Carmelle Pezzani, Gîtes de France-certified since 2021. Cleanest budget choice if you have a car for the dinner run.

## Getting home after dinner

The single non-obvious factor that decides between the top two and the rest:

- **No local taxi rank, limited Uber.** TripAdvisor guests report pre-arranging taxis for departure; the hotel itself recommends booking transport early [[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3845710-d231071-Reviews-Troisgros-Ouches_Roanne_Loire_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html).
- **Walking back is rural-road walking.** Route de Villerest and Route de Roanne have no sidewalks for the 0.7–1 km between the village and the Troisgros estate — bring a torch and a high-vis layer if you do it at night.
- **Château d'Origny's free shuttle** is the only built-in "we drive you home" service among non-Troisgros lodgings [[5]](https://www.chateaudorigny.com/en/).
- **A car works**, but a tasting menu with wine pairings is a poor pairing with a designated-driver plan.

## How to pick

- **Full experience, money no object →** Le Bois Dormant.
- **Same estate, half the price, a family or a group →** Le Pré Cheval.
- **You want a non-Troisgros bed and a guaranteed ride home →** Château d'Origny.
- **Price-sensitive with a car, willing to handle transport yourself →** Domaine d'Aromm or Domaine Bérenguer.
