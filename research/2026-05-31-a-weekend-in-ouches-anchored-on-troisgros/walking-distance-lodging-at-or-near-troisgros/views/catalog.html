After dinner

Getting home after the tasting menu

One non‑obvious factor decides between the top two and the rest. Solve it on paper before you book.

i.
No local taxi rank, limited Uber. Guests pre‑arrange taxis for departure; the hotel itself recommends booking transport early.[8]
ii.
Walking back is rural‑road walking. Route de Villerest and Route de Roanne have no sidewalks for the 0.7–1 km between the village and the Troisgros estate. Bring a torch and a hi‑vis layer if you do it at night.
iii.
Château d'Origny's free shuttle is the only built‑in "we drive you home" service among non‑Troisgros lodgings.[5]
iv.
A car works, but a tasting menu with wine pairings is a poor pairing with a designated‑driver plan.
Before you book

Things to confirm in writing

  • Book your return transport with the dinner reservation — Troisgros lists two partner taxis on their access page.
  • If aiming for Le Bois Dormant: check it isn't inside the Dec 22 – Jan 14 closure window.[6]
  • If aiming for Le Pré Cheval: it's closed Mondays & Tuesdays, and check‑in is self‑service from 16:30.[10]
  • If aiming for Château d'Origny: tell them at booking you want dinner at Troisgros — the shuttle isn't on‑demand.[5]
  • If you'd rather skip the logistics: ask the restaurant whether Le Bois Dormant has a room‑and‑table package — that's the only true bundle.
