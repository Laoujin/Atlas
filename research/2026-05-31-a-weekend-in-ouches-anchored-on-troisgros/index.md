The four sub-topics converge on a single answer: **sleep on the Troisgros estate, anchor the day on the close-in medieval pair, and treat Sunday morning as a single binary choice.** The friction points sit between the children, not inside them.

**The "walk home from a 3-star dinner" problem is real and binding.** Both lodging children land on [Le Bois Dormant](https://www.troisgros.fr/en/le-bois-dormant/), the 15-room Relais & Châteaux on the same 17-hectare estate as the restaurant, from €300/night [[1]](https://fr.gaultmillau.com/en/hotels/hotel-troisgros). The sibling option is [Le Pré Cheval](https://www.troisgros.fr/en/le-pre-cheval/), Marion Troisgros's three farm gîtes at €220–320 — same estate, half the price, self-service [[2]](https://www.troisgros.fr/le-pre-cheval/les-hebergements/). Outside the estate, Troisgros itself lists *only two* partner taxis (Dufourt 06 09 37 17 65; Christian 06 09 43 36 62) and tells guests to book the return with the reservation [[3]](https://www.troisgros.fr/en/access/) — rural roads have no sidewalks for the 0.7–1 km walk. The only non-Troisgros bed with a built-in answer is [Château d'Origny](https://www.chateaudorigny.com/en/), whose free return shuttle is the differentiator, not the suites [[4]](https://www.chateaudorigny.com/en/).

**Lodging villages double as the activity map.** Three Ouches addresses each carry a Michelin star (Troisgros, plus Château d'Origny since 2023 under chef Julien Laval [[4]](https://www.chateaudorigny.com/en/)), and the day-trip survey adds [Château de Boisy](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C3%A2teau_de_Boisy_(Pouilly-les-Nonains)) in adjacent Pouilly-les-Nonains — Jacques Cœur's 1447 manor, access by appointment only [[5]](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ch%C3%A2teau_de_Boisy_(Pouilly-les-Nonains)). [Villerest](https://www.roannais-tourisme.com/en/equipement/site-du-belvedere/) hosts both Château de Champlong and the Belvédère + Sunday boat departure [[6]](https://www.roannais-tourisme.com/en/equipement/bateau-promenade-lac-de-villerest/); Cordelle (Domaine des Grands Cèdres) sits on the line to the boat's destination, Château de la Roche on its 437 m pinnacle [[7]](https://www.loiretourisme.com/en/get-out-and-move/profiter/chateau-de-la-roche-saint-priest-la-roche-en-3351354/). Pradines (Cabanes du Grand Nord) is the one lodging-side village with no daytime hook of its own.

**Sunday closures decide the itinerary.** Côte Roannaise's two flagship cellars — [Sérol](https://www.domaine-serol.com/en/visites-et-degustations/) and [Pothiers](https://www.roannais-tourisme.com/en/degustation-produits-locaux/domaine-des-pothiers/) — are closed Sundays [[8]](https://www.domaine-serol.com/en/visites-et-degustations/)[[9]](https://www.roannais-tourisme.com/en/degustation-produits-locaux/domaine-des-pothiers/), so wine moves to Saturday morning or off the trip. Charlieu's market is Wed/Sat only [[10]](https://www.jours-de-marche.fr/42190-charlieu/). [Halles Diderot](https://www.jours-de-marche.fr/42300-roanne/) is open Sunday 8:00–noon (Mons cheese 9:00–12:00) [[11]](https://www.mons-fromages.com/en/magasin/8-roanne-halles-diderot) — the one safe Sunday market. The 09:00 Villerest → Château de la Roche cruise runs Wednesdays and Sundays only and demands a phone reservation [[6]](https://www.roannais-tourisme.com/en/equipement/bateau-promenade-lac-de-villerest/).

**The tech angle is a desert.** No full-format IT conference happens on a weekend within 30 km in 2026; the [Fête de la Science Village des Sciences in Roanne, 2-12 October 2026](https://www.fetedelascience.fr/), is the only weekend-aligned public tech event, and the next real conference is [SIDO Lyon](https://www.sido-lyon.com/en/) (~85 km, September) [[12]](https://www.fetedelascience.fr/)[[13]](https://www.sido-lyon.com/en/). If a conference is the reason to align the trip, anchor on those dates first.

**What was not researched.** The brief flagged dinner mechanics as the constraint feeding every sub-topic — tasting-menu vs à-la-carte pricing, wine-pairing cost, meal length as the Saturday time anchor, dress code, reservation lead time, and whether the table books with a room. No sub-topic was dedicated to it. Before booking the trip, confirm those directly with the restaurant (reservations: see [troisgros.fr/en/access](https://www.troisgros.fr/en/access/)); they decide whether Le Bois Dormant's room-and-table package is a real discount or just a logistical convenience [[3]](https://www.troisgros.fr/en/access/).
