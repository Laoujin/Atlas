TL;DR.

Walk back from dinner → Le Bois Dormant, the Troisgros estate's own 5★ Relais & Châteaux hotel, on the same 17-hectare grounds as the restaurant[2].

Want a 16th-century château instead → Château d'Origny, 5 suites + its own Michelin-starred restaurant, ~5 min by taxi from Troisgros — both addresses are in Ouches[5].

Want something stranger and cheaper → Les Cabanes du Grand Nord (Finnish kotas, private jacuzzi, ~25 min taxi)[13] or Domaine des Grands Cèdres (treehouses with spa, ~20 min)[15].

The catchment

Troisgros sits at 728 route de Villerest, 42155 Ouches — 10 minutes from Roanne train station, 1h30 from Lyon Saint-Exupéry airport[1]. The restaurant lists two partner taxis (Dufourt and Christian) and recommends booking by phone alongside your reservation[1]. Roanne → Ouches is 7.4 km, €17–21, ~8 min by cab[16]; everything below sits within ~25 min of the restaurant.

The picks

Le Bois Dormant (Hôtel Troisgros) on-site

Ouches · 0 km · 5★ Relais & Châteaux · from €300/night

15 rooms on the Troisgros estate itself — 12 in the Grande Maison designed by Marie-Pierre Troisgros, 3 in the converted Maison des Kakis (former wine press) that combine into an apartment[3]. Heated outdoor pool, Nordic bath, library, blown-glass-walled lounges, 17-hectare estate with meadows and forest[2]. Architecture "embraces styles and eras" around a centuries-old oak[2]. Relais & Châteaux member continuously since 1966[4]. The walk-home option after dinner — and the one place where the cooking and the bedroom share a postcode.

Château d'Origny château

Ouches · ~3 km · 5 suites · from €175/night

16th-century château built by Jean Dongui, a wealthy potter from Roanne; revived in 2007 after stints as residence, literary haunt and retirement home[5]. Five named suites overlooking the park — Colonial, Française, English, Comtesse, Marquis de Luzy; the Colonial includes bathtub, hammam and jacuzzi[6]. Holds its own Michelin star since 2023 under chef Julien Laval (menus from €89)[5], so a second gourmet night doesn't need a car. 8 minutes from Roanne; same village as Troisgros[5].

Domaine d'Aromm contemporary

Ouches · ~4 km · 5 rooms + suites · €125–165/night

Contemporary residence on 27 hectares with two ponds, meadows, a heated pool and on-site equestrian boarding — you can bring your own horse[7]. Five rooms ranging from a 2-person room to a Grande Suite that sleeps 6–7, decorated "with quality bedding and good taste"[8]. Quietest of the Ouches options, and the cheapest with real character — pick this if you want space and silence over heritage.

Château de Champlong resort

Villerest · ~10 km · 4★ · €160–245/night

16th-century château with 12 contemporary rooms (17–36 m²), each named and tonally tuned to a different spice — Saffron, Star Anise, Nutmeg, Cardamom, Paprika and on[10]. 68-hectare estate with 18-hole golf course, the David Grand Spa, gallery, and a Gault&Millau restaurant by Olivier Boizet[12]. Closed Sunday and Monday evenings[10] — fine for a Saturday-dinner trip if you also stay Friday. The pick if the trip is for two couples and one of them golfs.

Les Cabanes du Grand Nord insolite

Pradines · ~20 km · 3 Finnish kotas · from €160/night, breakfast incl.

Three Finnish kotas — Canada, Laponie, Alaska — in wooded grounds 15 min from Roanne[14]. Canada and Alaska have private outdoor jacuzzis under cover; Laponie (21 m²) offers an optional barrel sauna[13]. Breakfast basket left at the door each morning[13]. The "we just ate at a 3-star, now let's sleep in a Nordic cocoon" pick — and the cheapest character option that still feels intentional rather than makeshift.

Domaine des Grands Cèdres treehouse

Cordelle · ~15 km · 4 cabanes · €185–€297/night

Treehouses perched in the forest on the heights above Cordelle, "a few minutes" from Roanne[15]. Four named cabins: L'Échauguette (€185), Spa L'Escapade (€250), Spa L'Aventure (€260), Sauna & Spa La Belle Étoile (€297). Each has queen bed, kitchenette, bathroom, private terrace; the upper three include a private hot tub on the deck, and La Belle Étoile adds a sauna[15]. Breakfast basket included; heated pool on-site[15]. The pricier sibling to Grand Nord, with elevation.

Logistics

  • Coming in. Roanne TGV is the natural arrival point (Paris Gare de Lyon → Roanne ~3h30; Lyon Saint-Exupéry → Troisgros ~1h30 by car)[1]. A pre-booked private transfer from LYS to Troisgros runs around €315[18].
  • Local taxis. Troisgros's listed partners: Taxi Dufourt (06 09 37 17 65) and Taxi Christian (06 09 43 36 62)[1]. Book the return with dinner — the restaurant is rural and ride-hail is unreliable.
  • Roanne ↔ Ouches. 7.4 km, €17–21, ~8 min[16] — useful if you sleep in town for the cheaper hotel inventory and only taxi for dinner.
  • Driving yourself. All six properties have private parking. If you're tasting Côte Roannaise reds during the day, designate or skip the wheel.

All distances are estimates from town centres; book taxis in advance for late returns from Troisgros.
