Bruges 17 km · 21-min direct train [22]

Bruges ★ the canonical Sunday

Markt → Burg → Groeningemuseum → Begijnhof → Minnewater is one walked circle.

The Belfort climbs 366 steps for €15 [23] [24]. A canal boat ride is 30 min, €15, walk-up only [26]. End at De Halve Maan — Classic Tour €16, one Brugse Zot on the rooftop [25].

Train: 22 services/day, no transfer [22].  Park: Centrum-Station €7/24h, 1,690 spaces, free bus included.

Het Zwin 8 km · 12-min drive

Het Zwin Nature Park

Stork nests, salt marshes, 300+ recorded species.

From €13.50 adult / €6 child (under-6 free), €1.50 off online [29]. Open 10–17 daily during Flemish school holidays [30]. Stork colony in nests Feb–end of July [31].

Allow ≥ 3 hours. Salt-marsh trails can flood at spring tide — boots advised.

Sluis (NL) 9 km · 13-min drive

Sluis

The only town in the radius with shops open every Sunday.

A medieval fortress town that has drawn Belgian Sunday shoppers for over a century [32]. The only Dutch town hall with a Flemish belfort; Molen de Brak houses a brasserie that grinds its own flour [33].

Closed only Christmas Day and 1 January. ~5 million visitors/year.

Damme 15 km · paddle steamer or canal cycle

Damme

Flat poplar-lined canal, paddle steamer, book village.

The Lamme Goedzak paddle steamer runs 4 Apr – 1 Nov 2026, Bruges ↔ Damme in 35 min — €12 one-way / €18 round-trip [34]. Cycle the Damse Vaart from Knokke or Bruges [37]. Book market: second Sunday monthly [36].

Damse Vaart commissioned by Napoleon in 1810; poplars 100+ years old [35].

est. 1902
De Haan 22 km · radius edge

De Haan · Concessie

A 1995-protected Belle Époque resort with no high-rises.

White facades, red roofs, cottage style — the whole village quarter was listed as a landscape in 1995 [38]. Einstein lived in exile at Shakespearelaan 5 in 1933 [39].

Reachable along the Kusttram — world's longest metre-gauge line, 67 km coast end-to-end [45].

Lissewege 10 km · the polder hop

Lissewege

White polder village, 13th-century brick tower, c.1375 tithe barn.

Voted one of Flanders' most beautiful villages [27]. The Ter Doest tithe barn is 50.5 m long — one of the last surviving medieval tithe barns in NW Europe [28].

Pair with B&B Pronkenburg next door if Heist sells out.

Pure C ★★

Pure C — the second Michelin temptation

If Saturday at Bartholomeus inspired Sunday lunch.

Two Michelin stars on the Cadzand seafront — Sergio Herman's Zeeland-produce kitchen at the Strandhotel, with floor-to-ceiling sea views [40]. Cadzand: 11 km of beach, southernmost Dutch resort.

Same kind of pre-book required as Bartholomeus.

Around the village

If you never want to leave Heist
on the seafront

Lichttorenplein

The reconstructed 1872 lighthouse — pink-paved square, pink-tinted fountain water, tourist office next door [31a].

2 km W · dunes & tide

Baai van Heist

50 ha of beach-and-dune reserve created when Zeebrugge's breakwater accumulated sand 1977–85 — Natura 2000, walking park, birding [19a].

700 m inland

Sincfala Museum

Pannenstraat 134 — 2,000 years of Zwin region history in an 1899 neo-Gothic former fishermen's school; shrimp boat Jessica in the garden [26a].

10 min cab · Knokke

Magritte at the Casino

Salle Magritte — a 70 m × 4 m panoramic fresco finished 1953, eight panels in a curved hall. The Casino is under renovation through 2030–32, so call ahead [41].
