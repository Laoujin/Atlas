> **TL;DR** — Bartholomeus sits at Zeedijk-Heist 267 [[1]](https://www.myknokke-heist.be/en/restaurant/bartholomeus); within a 30 km arc, **Bruges (16 km)** [[2]](https://www.trippy.com/distance/Knokke-Heist-to-Bruges) is the only real tech hub. If you can only pick one upcoming event, make it **[EUSIPCO 2026](https://eusipco2026.org/)** at Concertgebouw Brugge, 31 Aug – 4 Sep — 600-attendee European signal-processing & ML conference [[4]](https://eusipco2026.org/)[[5]](https://www.visitbrugesconventionbureau.be/en/conference-calendar). For shorter-format, **Howest Brugge** runs an AI-in-healthcare keynote on 9 Jun [[8]](https://www.howest.be/en/digital-health-lab/community-en-events) and an OT/ICS security focus group + Tech BBQ on 17 & 25 Jun [[9]](https://www.cyber3lab.be/en/news/cybersecurity-ai-community-events). The big-name Belgian tech events (Connect IT, Tech Connect, Devoxx, Hack the Future) all sit **outside** the radius.

## Recurring local communities

[**Building Bruges**](https://www.meetup.com/bruges-software-development-meetup-group/) is the active developer meetup — ~575 members, 4.3★, covering DevOps, web/mobile, TDD, refactoring [[10]](https://www.meetup.com/bruges-software-development-meetup-group/). Cadence is roughly quarterly (Mar 2026: Brent Roose; Dec 2025: refactoring workshop at Howest Building A) [[11]](https://buildingbruges.be/). Venue rotates between Bits of Love and Howest. No fixed event posted as of late May 2026 — RSVP-watch the Meetup page.

Howest's [Digital Health Lab](https://www.howest.be/en/digital-health-lab/community-en-events) runs themed seasons: "Movement & AI" through Jun 2026, then "Mental wellbeing and apps" in autumn 2026, "Sleep and wearables" in 2027 [[8]](https://www.howest.be/en/digital-health-lab/community-en-events). Expect a keynote + pitch night each quarter.

## Annual events worth pencilling in for 2027

[**ESANN**](https://www.esann.org/) (European Symposium on Artificial Neural Networks, Computational Intelligence and Machine Learning) ran 22–24 Apr 2026 at [Crowne Plaza Brugge](https://www.esann.org/node/11) (Burg 10), 100+ speakers, single-track [[6]](https://www.esann.org/)[[7]](https://www.esann.org/node/11). It is annual and consistently in Bruges — if you missed the 2026 edition, ESANN 2027 will land in late April; check `esann.org` from November 2026 for the call for papers.

The Belgian tech-event centre of gravity is Brussels / Antwerp / Ghent / Kortrijk; nothing of equivalent scale to EUSIPCO or Devoxx happens on the West Flanders coast itself [[2]](https://www.trippy.com/distance/Knokke-Heist-to-Bruges)[[3]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ostend/Knokke-Heist). Ostend (29 km, on the radius edge [[3]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ostend/Knokke-Heist)) hosts no recurring IT conference series as of this scan. Knokke-Heist and Zeebrugge themselves: nothing.

## Practical note

For EUSIPCO and the BMCC events, Brugge train station is the natural arrival point — direct trains from Knokke-Heist run ~25 min and drop you a 10-min walk from Concertgebouw or a short tram to BMCC. Howest's Brugge campus is on the same line (Brugge-Station stop, ~17 km from Bartholomeus).
