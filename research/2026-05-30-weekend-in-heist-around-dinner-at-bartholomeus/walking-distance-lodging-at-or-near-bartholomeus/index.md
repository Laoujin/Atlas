> **TL;DR** — Bartholomeus is at Zeedijk-Heist 267 [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/heist/restaurant/bartholomeus). Closest seafront beds: **Hotel St. Yves** (Zeedijk-Heist 204, 3★, ~60 m west, from €100) [[17]](https://booking.westtoer.be/hotel/st-yves-hotel-knokke-heist?lang=en) and the **Brasserie Bristol residences** at Zeedijk 290-291 (~24 m east, full service-apartment range) [[3]](https://www.zeez.be/en/environment/dinner) [[4]](https://brasseriebristol.be/verblijf). For boutique B&B feel: **Aquavit** (Sint-Jorisstraat 7, ~8-10 min inland, 9.6/10) [[11]](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-knokke-heist/bb-aquavit.html). Skip Hotel Britannia — it is in Het Zoute, ~1 h walk away [[16]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/britannia.html).

## The Heist lodging map

Bartholomeus holds two Michelin stars in the 2026 guide [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/heist/restaurant/bartholomeus) and faces the beach mid-east on the Heist seafront promenade [[2]](https://www.strandverblijf.be/nl/eten-en-drinken/118/bartholomeus). Zeedijk-Heist is numbered west-to-east, so building numbers within ±150 of 267 are within a comfortable 5-min walk along the dyke. Heist is light on traditional hotels — most walking-distance inventory is service apartments, studios, or design B&Bs. Of 188 properties listed in Booking.com's Heist district, only a handful actually sit within ~600 m of Bartholomeus [[10]](https://www.booking.com/district/be/knokke-heist/heist.html).

## Beyond 10 minutes — only if the close picks are full

- [Hotel Du Soleil](https://www.hoteldusoleil.be/en) — Patriottenstraat 15, **Duinbergen**. Quiet 3★ family hotel, 50 m from the *Duinbergen* dyke with restaurant + breakfast buffet — but walking to Bartholomeus is ~20 min along the dyke [[15]](https://www.hoteldusoleil.be/en).
- **Loft Exclusif** (9.7/10, 8-min walk to Heist beach, from $328) and **Villa FER-GUY** (9.1/10, 7-min from Albertstrand) rank highest in Heist on Booking.com but sit further east, closer to Knokke centre [[10]](https://www.booking.com/district/be/knokke-heist/heist.html).
- [Hotel Britannia](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/britannia.html), Elizabetlaan 85 in **Het Zoute**, is the marquee traditional Knokke-Heist hotel but ~1 h 4 min on foot from Heist [[16]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/britannia.html). Tram/taxi only.

## Practical notes

- **Coast tram (Kusttram)** runs the full length of the Belgian coast and stops in Heist — useful if you stay in Knokke or Duinbergen and want to skip the taxi after dinner.
- **Parking**: Bristol residences charge €20/day [[5]](https://residentiebristol.be/overzicht-prijzen). Several seafront apartments offer free private parking (e.g. "Appartement zeedijk Heist met garage", 9.5/10) [[10]](https://www.booking.com/district/be/knokke-heist/heist.html). B&B B-eaufort explicitly has none [[14]](https://www.bbbelgie.be/bb-b-eaufort.html).
- **Tiny inventory**: Aquavit (2 rms), Morpheus (2 rms), St. Yves (8 rms), B-eaufort all sell out fast for Bartholomeus weekend slots — lock lodging the moment dinner is confirmed.
- **Direction matters**: Bartholomeus' counter seats 18, so most diners spill east toward Brasserie Bristol or west toward Studio Heyst on the post-dinner walk; either direction stays on the lit promenade.
