TL;DR. Spend a Hangzhou weekend with West Lake as the spine: walk the causeways at dawn, take one boat to Three Pools Mirroring the Moon, and save Leifeng Pagoda for sunset[35]. Give Lingyin Temple an early-morning slot on the other day before the 10 AM crush[34].

Around the dinner: put the Michelin meal on the evening you've done West Lake, then keep the next day light — tea country (Meijiawu), Hefang Street, or Xixi Wetland[38]. If you'd rather a big-ticket evening, Zhang Yimou's lake show at the 21:10 seating slots in nicely after an early dinner[37].

Logistics: 45 min by high-speed rail from Shanghai (passport works as ID)[24][25]; set up Alipay + WeChat Pay before you go[31]; early June is warm and showery[26].

The weekend, sequenced

Both well-regarded patterns anchor each day on one marquee sight and exploit the early-morning quiet window[33]. The version below front-loads West Lake on Day 1 so the special dinner lands that evening and Day 2 stays unhurried[38].

Day 1 — West Lake, then the dinner

  • Before 7:30 AM — Start at Broken Bridge for empty, misty photos, then walk Bai Causeway → Gushan Hill → Su Causeway[35]. The core gets busy from late morning, so the dawn start is the whole trick[36].
  • Midday — Boat to Three Pools Mirroring the Moon, the boat-only "island within a lake"[3]. CNY 55–70 including island admission, last island boat 16:00[4].
  • Late afternoon — Leifeng Pagoda at sunset (elevators inside; open to 20:00 May–Oct)[10][35].
  • Evening — The Michelin dinner (picked separately). A lakeside stroll or the free Third Park music fountain is a natural lead-in[39].

Day 2 — Lingyin early, then your pick

  • 7:00–9:00 AM — Lingyin Temple & Feilai Feng grottoes while the halls are still quiet and misty; by 10 AM they're packed[34]. Budget 2–3 hours[9]. Reserve a real-name slot online a day ahead — required since the scenic area went free[8].
  • Afternoon — Choose one: Meijiawu tea valley (pick & pan-roast your own Longjing)[16], the Hefang Street old quarter[14], or a boat through Xixi Wetland[19].
⚠ Two booking gotchas. Lingyin Temple needs a real-name online reservation at least a day ahead[8], and the Impression West Lake show sells out — book before you arrive[37].

West Lake itself

West Lake's signature views are codified as the Ten Scenes, names inscribed by the Kangxi Emperor in 1699[1]. Two causeways frame the water: Su Causeway (2.8 km, six bridges, built by the poet Su Dongpo and ranked top of the ten scenes) and Bai Causeway, whose north end holds Broken Bridge and runs ~20 min to Gushan island[2]. On a tight visit, prioritise Su Causeway plus the boat to Three Pools, padded with lakeside walking[5].

How to cover it: the classic on-foot circuit links Broken Bridge → Gushan → boat to Three Pools → Orioles Singing in the Willows → Leifeng Pagoda → Su Causeway → Yuewang Temple[7]. To circle the perimeter, the 12–15 km cycling loop takes 2–3 hours via HelloBike (works through Alipay, English interface) or the public Diing bikes (first 60 min free) — but Bai Causeway and the Hubin walking street ban cycling and Su Causeway forces dismounts on busy weekends, so bike the perimeter, walk the causeways, take one boat hop[6].

Tea country & nature

Meijiawu tea valley

half-day SW of West Lake

Quieter and broader than famous Longjing Village. Farmers let you pick tea for ¥50–100 (30–60 min), then pan-roast and taste it[16]. Picking season runs late March–early November[20].

Shili Langdang hike

3–5 hr ~10 km, easy

Jiuxi → Longjing Village → Shili Langdang ridge → Yunqi Bamboo Path: 350–470 m gain through tea gardens, lake panoramas and bamboo forest[17]. It threads the same hills as the broader West Lake circle trail[18].

Beigao Peak

quick win 314 m

Hike free via the Lingyin back-door bamboo route, or cable car (¥30 up / ¥20 down) to a viewing platform with unobstructed West Lake and city views[21].

Xixi National Wetland

half-day CNY 80 + 60 cruise

~11 km² of waterways with 200+ bird species; the boat ride is the headline. Best in spring/autumn[19].

⚠ Tea-house scam. Buying real West Lake Longjing direct from a farm is a highlight — but ignore English-speakers near the lake who invite you to a "tea ceremony"; it ends in forced bills of CNY 1,000–5,000[44].

Evenings

Impression West Lake

19:40 & 21:10 · 55 min · CNY 360–960

Zhang Yimou's outdoor spectacle on Yuehu Lake, performers seeming to walk on water from a stage set 3 cm below the surface[40]. Sells out — book ahead; the 21:10 seating pairs with an early dinner[37].

Grand Canal night cruise

~1 hr · from Wulinmen Wharf

Past lit bridges and old streets (lighting by Roger Narboni), with onboard Longjing tea; a longer 2.5-hr Canal + Qiantang River cruise leaves 18:30 daily[41].

Wushan Night Market

18:30–23:00 · 400+ stalls

Hangzhou's biggest night market near West Lake — scallion-oil pancakes, skewers, dumplings[42].

Teahouses & bars

Qinghefang / Nanshan Lu

Qingteng (Green Vine), one of the oldest teahouses, pairs a Jiangnan garden with an all-you-can-eat snack buffet[43]. Nightlife clusters on Nanshan Lu, the West Lake bar street[45].

Free spectacles: the West Lake Music Fountain (7pm & 8pm nightly) and the Qianjiang New Town light show — 700,000 LEDs across 30 towers, Tue/Fri/Sat[46].
