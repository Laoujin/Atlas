Chef & Philosophy
Fu Yueliang spent four decades reviving Southern Song-dynasty Hangzhou cooking, training under master Dong Shunxiang at the century-old Zhiweiguan. He insists on updating ingredients where tradition used inferior substitutes — grass carp replaced by sweeter marble goby for the West Lake fish, for instance. Co-founder Iris Tan (International Sommelier Association) curates wine pairings.[6]
Signature Dishes
Pagoda Dongpo Rou — a single Jinhua pork belly ribbon folded into a tower; West Lake vinegared fish — completely deboned, all 79 bones displayed in a wooden case beside the plate; Smoked tea eggs with Kaluga Queen caviar; Double-boiled fish meatball consommé with snow-like texture.[7][6]
Space
8 tables across two floors, glass-enclosed private dining rooms, whisky bar, wine cellar. Architect Louis Liou's 3-year renovation of the Botany Resource Pavilion into a Southern Song aesthetic sanctuary.[6]
Booking Tips
⚠ Next-month tables open on the 1st and sell out within minutes — set a calendar reminder. Off-peak weekday lunch is slightly easier. Call direct or use a hotel concierge. En Primeur Club members have priority access.[8]

Chef & Philosophy
Bin Yu (俞斌), winner of the inaugural MICHELIN Mentor Chef Award 2026, has run Jie Xiang Lou since 2005. He preserves the Jiangnan flavor essence while layering in modern technique and premium global ingredients — caviar and abalone sit alongside East China Sea croakers and local Zhejiang produce.[2]
Signature Dishes
Pork jowl with caviar; goose liver and smoked fish terrine; braised fresh abalone with shrimp roe; Mrs. Song's fish soup (baby yellow croakers, East China Sea); veggie beggar's purse with truffle sauce; assorted cold platter with crispy pork belly.[9]
Space
Ground-floor dining room + 13 upstairs private rooms overlooking a bamboo forest and maples; part of the Seven Villas hillside resort complex west of West Lake.[9]
Getting There
⚠ Hillside hillside resort — not walkable from the lake. Take a taxi or arrange private transport; the hotel concierge can assist.[10]

No ★★★ in Hangzhou
