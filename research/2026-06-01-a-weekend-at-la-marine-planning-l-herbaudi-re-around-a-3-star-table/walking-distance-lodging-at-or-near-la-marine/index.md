> **Decision.** Book **Maison Moizeau** — it adjoins La Marine on Rue Marie Lemonnier, is run by chef Alexandre Couillon, and has 5 rooms at €180–275 [[1]](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/dream.html). If Moizeau is sold out (likely — guests warn the rooms book a year+ in advance [[2]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g425088-d15237675-Reviews-La_Maison_Moizeau-Noirmoutier_en_l_Ile_Ile_de_Noirmoutier_Vendee_Pays_de_la_Loire.html)), walk to **Hôtel Bord à Bord** on the marina (~4 min, €80–165) [[3]](https://www.ile-noirmoutier.com/en/accommodation/our-hosting-solutions/hotels/hotel-bord-bord) or **La Tranquillité** (~12 min, 9.5/10 over 266 reviews) [[4]](https://www.chambres-hotes.fr/chambres-hotes_la-tranquillite_noirmoutier-en-l-ile_h7552795.htm). Skip Ancre Marine — its Thalgo spa is appealing but it's ~3 km out and means a taxi after dinner [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g425088-d23686307-Port_De_L_Herbaudiere-Noirmoutier_en_l_Ile_Ile_de_Noirmoutier_Vendee_Pays_de_la_Loire.html).

La Marine is at **3 Rue Marie Lemonnier, 85330 Noirmoutier-en-l'Île**, facing the L'Herbaudière fishing port [[6]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/pays-de-la-loire/l-herbaudire/restaurant/la-marine3780). The village is small — most of the lodging stock that exists at all is within a 15-minute walk of the restaurant.

## Anchor pick: [Maison Moizeau](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/dream.html)

- **7 Rue Marie Lemonnier** — literally adjoins La Marine at no. 3 [[1]](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/dream.html). One TripAdvisor reviewer puts it bluntly: *"The hotel touches the 3-star Michelin restaurant 'la marine'"* [[2]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g425088-d15237675-Reviews-La_Maison_Moizeau-Noirmoutier_en_l_Ile_Ile_de_Noirmoutier_Vendee_Pays_de_la_Loire.html).
- **5 rooms**: Lutins (ground floor, no port view) €180; Blanche/Jean €250; Madeleine/Clère (4-pax) €275; extra person €30; breakfast €25 [[1]](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/dream.html).
- **Relais & Châteaux** + Michelin Green Star [[1]](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/dream.html).
- **Seasonal:** closed December, January, and the first half of February [[7]](https://www.petitfute.co.uk/v28864-noirmoutier-en-l-ile-85330/c1166-hebergement/c151-chambre-d-hotes/1650327-la-maison-moizeau.html) — mirrors La Marine's own calendar.
- → Book as soon as your La Marine reservation is confirmed; 5 rooms × 3-star Michelin demand = months-to-years lead time.

## If Moizeau is full

**[Hôtel Bord à Bord](https://www.bordabord.fr/en/)** — 6 rue de la Linière, directly on the marina [[3]](https://www.ile-noirmoutier.com/en/accommodation/our-hosting-solutions/hotels/hotel-bord-bord). Unclassified hotel; double with harbor view + balcony €95–115, family rooms up to €165 [[3]](https://www.ile-noirmoutier.com/en/accommodation/our-hosting-solutions/hotels/hotel-bord-bord). Open **Feb 7 – Nov 30, 2026** plus a winter window Dec 19, 2026 – Jan 3, 2027 [[3]](https://www.ile-noirmoutier.com/en/accommodation/our-hosting-solutions/hotels/hotel-bord-bord). Walk-back from La Marine is ≈300 m along the quay [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g425088-d23686307-Port_De_L_Herbaudiere-Noirmoutier_en_l_Ile_Ile_de_Noirmoutier_Vendee_Pays_de_la_Loire.html). ⚠ The 3.7/5 TripAdvisor average is the lowest in this set — expect basic, not boutique [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g425088-d23686307-Port_De_L_Herbaudiere-Noirmoutier_en_l_Ile_Ile_de_Noirmoutier_Vendee_Pays_de_la_Loire.html).

**La Chambre d'Emilie** — chambre d'hôtes at the bottom of a quiet alley near the fishing/pleasure port, **from €78 for two incl. breakfast** [[8]](https://www.enpaysdelaloire.com/hebergements/chambres-d-hotes/la-chambre-d-emilie-a-l-herbaudiere-sur-l-ile-de-noirmoutier). Cheapest option that still puts you in the village; exact distance from La Marine isn't published, but the port is a few minutes' walk from anywhere in L'Herbaudière proper.

**[La Tranquillité](https://latranquillite-no.fr/)** — 80 Rue de la Tranquillité, **900 m from the port and 100 m from Linière Beach** [[9]](https://latranquillite-no.fr/). 5 themed rooms (Luzéronde, La Linière, Les Sableaux, L'Anse Rouge, Les Souzeaux), each with kitchenette, terrace, AC, free WiFi, free private parking, bikes on request [[4]](https://www.chambres-hotes.fr/chambres-hotes_la-tranquillite_noirmoutier-en-l-ile_h7552795.htm). **9.5/10 across 266 reviews** with a Certificate of Excellence — the strongest review track record in walking range [[4]](https://www.chambres-hotes.fr/chambres-hotes_la-tranquillite_noirmoutier-en-l-ile_h7552795.htm). ~12-min flat walk back from La Marine; fine after a long tasting menu.

## Skip for a walk-home dinner

**[Ancre Marine Hôtel & Spa Thalgo](https://www.ancremarine.com)** — 87a route de l'Herbaudière, **3-star, 14 rooms + studios, 160 m² Thalgo spa, heated outdoor pool, free parking, EV charging** [[10]](https://www.port-herbaudiere.fr/en/around_the_port/ancre-marine/). Genuinely lovely property (4.7/5 TripAdvisor [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g425088-d23686307-Port_De_L_Herbaudiere-Noirmoutier_en_l_Ile_Ile_de_Noirmoutier_Vendee_Pays_de_la_Loire.html)) and the only one open year-round [[10]](https://www.port-herbaudiere.fr/en/around_the_port/ancre-marine/), but it's **~3 km / 40-min walk** from La Marine [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g425088-d23686307-Port_De_L_Herbaudiere-Noirmoutier_en_l_Ile_Ile_de_Noirmoutier_Vendee_Pays_de_la_Loire.html). Reserve a taxi in advance if you go this way — taxis aren't on standby at the L'Herbaudière port at 23:00.

## Booking sequence

1. Confirm the La Marine reservation date [[6]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/pays-de-la-loire/l-herbaudire/restaurant/la-marine3780).
2. Same day: email/call Maison Moizeau (`contact@alexandrecouillon.com` / `+33 2 51 39 23 09`) [[11]](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/).
3. If Moizeau is full, hit Bord à Bord and La Tranquillité in parallel — different stock (hotel vs. B&B) and different operators.
4. Hold Ancre Marine only as a last resort, and book a taxi at booking time.
