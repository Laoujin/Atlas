La Tranquillité [9]

87a route de l'Herbaudière · 3-star · only year-round option

Genuinely lovely property — 14 rooms, 160 m² Thalgo spa, heated outdoor pool (28°C), jacuzzi, EV charging. [10] Only option open year-round. But 3 km from La Marine means a taxi at midnight — taxis are not on standby at L'Herbaudière port at 23:00. Reserve Taxi Fred (+33 2 51 39 37 31) at booking time if you choose this.
