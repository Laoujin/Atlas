The causeway at Passage du Gois emerges at 14:20 and re-floods by 17:20 — a clean three-hour walking window centred on the 15:49 low. The 19:15 dinner sits safely two hours beyond the flood. A neap coefficient of 52 means gentler tides than a spring crossing, but the road will still be underwater outside the window. Sunset at 21:59 [17] means the long tasting menu finishes in blue-hour light.

Planning Notes
Notices & Scope
⚠ Triathlon de Noirmoutier — Same Weekend
Road closures on the island during the race. Confirm the route before booking the salt-marsh saunier visit or any activity at the south end of the island. [18]
Tech conferences within 30 km: not viable. The nearest offering is a free monthly library soirée numérique — citizen digital-literacy [19] — and an October popular-science festival. [20] Real tech conferences start 80 km inland. Don't bend the weekend to chase something that isn't there.
Île d'Yeu: tempting — 30-minute crossing from Fromentine [21] — but a full day there breaks the tide-cued rhythm of Noirmoutier itself. Save it for a return trip. Use the Saturday-afternoon Gois window instead.
