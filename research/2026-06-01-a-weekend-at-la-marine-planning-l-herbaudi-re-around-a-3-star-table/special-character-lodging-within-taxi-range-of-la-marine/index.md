> **Decision.** Sleep **next door** at [Maison Moizeau](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/dream.html) — Alexandre & Céline Couillon's own 5 rooms, 30 m from the restaurant door, €180–€275 [[1]](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/dream.html). If full or you want something different: walk to [Hôtel Bord à Bord](https://www.bordabord.fr/en/) (port view, simple, ⚠ dated) or [La Chambre d'Emilie](https://www.enpaysdelaloire.com/hebergements/chambres-d-hotes/la-chambre-d-emilie-a-l-herbaudiere-sur-l-ile-de-noirmoutier) (B&B, €78) — both walkable from the restaurant. Want a proper hotel with spa/garden/character? Taxi 8–12 min to [Le Général d'Elbée](https://www.generaldelbee.fr/en/) (4★, 18C Monument Historique on the old port) or [Villa Arthus-Bertrand](https://www.villa-arthusbertrand.com/en/) (4★ mansion in the pinewood of Bois de la Chaise).

La Marine sits at 3 Rue Marie Lemonnier, L'Herbaudière, with Saturday dinner seated 7:15–8:15 pm and tasting menus €240–350 — expect to leave the table close to midnight [[5]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/pays-de-la-loire/l-herbaudire/restaurant/la-marine3780). That changes the lodging calculus: the post-dinner taxi window matters as much as the room itself.

## How "taxi range" really shakes out

Noirmoutier is small. L'Herbaudière sits on the north tip; the island town of Noirmoutier-en-l'Île is 5 km south and Bois de la Chaise is 9–10 km southeast. A taxi between L'Herbaudière and the town runs ~7–8 min and ~$17–21 one way [[6]](https://www.rome2rio.com/fr/s/L-Herbaudi%C3%A8re/Noirmoutier-en-l-%C3%8Ele). The catch: only **[Taxi Fred](https://www.ile-noirmoutier.com/en/taxis)** (+33 2 51 39 37 31) is explicitly listed as 7 days/7; the island's other five operators skew daytime/medical work [[7]](https://www.ile-noirmoutier.com/en/taxis). Book your return when you book the table, or pick a property you can walk to.

## Why Maison Moizeau wins by default

[Maison Moizeau](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/dream.html) is the lodging arm Alexandre & Céline Couillon built next to their own restaurant — same street, #7 vs #3 Rue Marie Lemonnier [[1]](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/dream.html)[[2]](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/). Five rooms: Lutins (ground floor, no harbour view, €180), Blanche and Jean (€250), and the family-sized Madeleine and Clère (€275, up to 4 guests), with breakfast €25 [[1]](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/dream.html). Décor is built around wood, metal and warm fabrics that echo the working fishing port outside — not a generic luxury kit. It's part of Relais & Châteaux through the restaurant's own membership [[3]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/restaurant/la-marine-noirmoutier/)[[20]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/hotel/la-marine-noirmoutier/). The proposition is straightforward: you finish a €240–€350 tasting menu [[5]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/pays-de-la-loire/l-herbaudire/restaurant/la-marine3780) and walk 30 m to bed — no taxi gamble, no driving impaired, no booking-window stress with Taxi Fred [[7]](https://www.ile-noirmoutier.com/en/taxis). If you can get a room, take it.

## The walkable backups in L'Herbaudière

If Moizeau's 5 rooms are gone — likely on a Saturday — stay on foot in L'Herbaudière itself.

**[Hôtel Bord à Bord](https://www.bordabord.fr/en/)** sits on the marina, perhaps 200 m from the restaurant. Doubles from €85, family rooms (2–6 people, some duplex) from €105, with the harbour view as the whole selling point [[8]](https://www.ile-noirmoutier.com/en/accommodation/our-hosting-solutions/hotels/hotel-bord-bord)[[9]](https://www.bordabord.fr/en/). TripAdvisor reviews are split — warm welcome and generous breakfast on the plus side, ⚠ worn bedding and bathrooms-needing-renovation on the minus side [[10]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g425088-d1236869-Reviews-Hotel_Bord_a_Bord-Noirmoutier_en_l_Ile_Ile_de_Noirmoutier_Vendee_Pays_de_la_Loire.html). Treat it as the practical walk-home pick, not a special-occasion stay.

**[La Chambre d'Emilie](https://www.enpaysdelaloire.com/hebergements/chambres-d-hotes/la-chambre-d-emilie-a-l-herbaudiere-sur-l-ile-de-noirmoutier)** is the local B&B option: a single 12 m² room with a 140 cm double bed and private bathroom, hosted by Geneviève Boucheron at 10 lot Boissonnelles, L'Herbaudière, from €78 incl. breakfast [[11]](https://www.enpaysdelaloire.com/hebergements/chambres-d-hotes/la-chambre-d-emilie-a-l-herbaudiere-sur-l-ile-de-noirmoutier)[[12]](https://www.clevacances.com/en/hebergement/22132). One room means one couple at a time — call early. Setting is a quiet cul-de-sac near the port, breakfast outside in good weather [[11]](https://www.enpaysdelaloire.com/hebergements/chambres-d-hotes/la-chambre-d-emilie-a-l-herbaudiere-sur-l-ile-de-noirmoutier).

## The "proper" hotels — short taxi away

If Maison Moizeau is full and you want full hotel kit (spa, pool, restaurant), accept the taxi and pick one of these. Pre-book the return at the Taxi Fred number above.

**[Le Général d'Elbée](https://www.generaldelbee.fr/en/)** — 4★, in an 18th-century Monument-Historique-listed mansion right on the old port of Noirmoutier-en-l'Île, between the medieval castle and the market. 25 individually-themed rooms across five categories up to the Elbée Suite, garden pool, NUXE spa with hammam/sauna, harbour-view bar [[15]](https://www.generaldelbee.fr/en/). The 2016 restoration by Chantal Peyrat is the design story. ⚠ Closed annually 11 November – 13 February, so a winter trip is out [[15]](https://www.generaldelbee.fr/en/). ~5 km / 7 min taxi to L'Herbaudière [[6]](https://www.rome2rio.com/fr/s/L-Herbaudi%C3%A8re/Noirmoutier-en-l-%C3%8Ele).

**[Villa Arthus-Bertrand](https://www.villa-arthusbertrand.com/en/)** — 4★, 18-room family mansion in Bois de la Chaise pinewood, heated outdoor pool, exotic garden, on-site restaurant; doubles €115–€330 low season [[13]](https://www.villa-arthusbertrand.com/en/). TripAdvisor consensus: spacious stylish rooms, lush garden, tranquil atmosphere, ⚠ no air-conditioning [[14]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g425088-d23168034-Reviews-Villa_Arthus_Bertrand-Noirmoutier_en_l_Ile_Ile_de_Noirmoutier_Vendee_Pays_de_la_Loire.html). The mansion-house feel is its character. ~9 km / 12 min taxi.

**[Les Prateaux](https://www.lesprateaux.com/en)** — 3★, [Relais du Silence](https://www.lesprateaux.com/en) member, set in a 3,700 m² park a few steps from Plage des Dames in Bois de la Chaise [[16]](https://www.lesprateaux.com/en). If "silence" is the brief, this is the pick — but ⚠ it only operates 20 March – 17 October 2026 and the restaurant is closed Wednesdays [[16]](https://www.lesprateaux.com/en).

**[Hôtel Saint-Paul](https://www.hotel-saint-paul.net/en/)** — 3★ in Bois de la Chaise, 35 rooms, indoor + outdoor heated pool, spa with hammam and balneo, on-site bistronomic restaurant L'Anse Rouge. Rates from €162 [[19]](https://www.hotel-saint-paul.net/en/). Bigger and more spa-driven than Villa Arthus-Bertrand; less mansion-feel.

## Town-centre cheap options

If your money is on the menu rather than the bed:

**[Hôtel L'Île Ô Château](https://www.ileochateau.com/)** — 2★ "charming hotel" facing Noirmoutier castle in the town centre, 23 renovated rooms, heated pool, poolside bar, bike rental, doubles from ~€78 [[17]](https://www.ileochateau.com/). ~5 km / 7 min taxi.

**[Hôtel Autre Mer](https://www.autremerhotel.fr/)** — 2★, recently renovated, 27 rooms in dune/marram-grass/wood palette, on-site bike rental; consistently positive reviews for cleanliness and the "French seaside charm" feel [[18]](https://www.autremerhotel.fr/). Same taxi distance.

## Practical notes for the Saturday-night plan

- **Book Maison Moizeau first.** When you book the table, ask the restaurant about the room directly — they're the same operation [[2]](https://www.alexandrecouillon.com/en/).
- **If walking-distance, no taxi needed.** Moizeau (30 m), Bord à Bord (~200 m), Chambre d'Emilie (~600 m) all leave you on foot at midnight.
- **If taxi-distance, pre-arrange the return.** Save Taxi Fred (+33 2 51 39 37 31) at booking time, not at the end of dinner [[7]](https://www.ile-noirmoutier.com/en/taxis).
- **Seasonality matters.** Général d'Elbée shuts 11 Nov–13 Feb [[15]](https://www.generaldelbee.fr/en/); Les Prateaux operates only 20 Mar–17 Oct [[16]](https://www.lesprateaux.com/en). La Marine itself only serves Wed–Sat dinner [[5]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/pays-de-la-loire/l-herbaudire/restaurant/la-marine3780), so a Saturday-night anchor is the natural choice.
- **La Marine confirmed 3★ in the 2026 Michelin guide.** No risk of a dropped star changing the calculus for this trip [[4]](https://vendeeinfo.fr/03/17/guide-michelin-2026-le-restaurant-la-marine-a-noirmoutier-conserve-ses-trois-etoiles/16048).
