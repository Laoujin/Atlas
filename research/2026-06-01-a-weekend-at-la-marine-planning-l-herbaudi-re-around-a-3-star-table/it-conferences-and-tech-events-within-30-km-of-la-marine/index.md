Inside the 30 km radius the offering is one free *soirée numérique* at the [Espace Grain de Sel](https://espacegraindesel.ville-noirmoutier.fr/) library on **Mon 15 Jun 2026, 18:00–19:00** [[10]](https://www.ville-noirmoutier.fr/agenda/soiree-numerique-vacances-en-ligne/), plus the annual [Festival des Sciences de Noirmoutier](https://www.lessciencesetnous.org/festival-des-sciences-de-noirmoutier/) "Prédire le futur" on **24–28 Oct 2026** [[8]](https://www.ile-noirmoutier.com/fr/agenda/du-24-au-28-octobre-2026-festival-des-sciences-12eme-edition-predire-le-futur). ## What you're really looking at

**Soirées numériques** at the Espace Grain de Sel run roughly monthly, Monday evenings, 18:00–19:00 — these are public-library digital-literacy sessions, not industry events. The 2026 line-up to date has cycled through AI history (16 Feb), AI debates (9 Mar), photo management (27 Apr), online scams (18 May), and online holidays (15 Jun) [[11]](https://www.ville-noirmoutier.fr/agenda/soiree-numerique-intelligence-artificielle-autour-de-nous/) [[12]](https://www.ville-noirmoutier.fr/agenda/soiree-numerique-histoire-de-lia/) [[13]](https://www.ville-noirmoutier.fr/agenda/soiree-numerique-les-debats-autour-de-lia/) — useful if you happen to be on the island a Monday, but it's grandma-and-laptop level, not dev-conf material.

**[Les Sciences et Nous](https://www.lessciencesetnous.org/)** is the local science-popularisation association (created 2014) that organises year-round lecture-debates and the October festival [[9]](https://www.lessciencesetnous.org/). Their tech-leaning conferences in early 2026 included Jean-Marc Menaud (Institut Mines-Télécom Nantes) on the environmental impact of digital activity (31 Jan, Salle Océane Barbâtre) [[9]](https://www.lessciencesetnous.org/) and a "L'IA dans notre quotidien" evening at Salorges with Jean Bigeon (LS2N Nantes) [[14]](https://www.lessciencesetnous.org/conference-sur-l-intelligence-artificielle-dans-notre-quotidien/) — single-speaker public lectures, ~1.5 h, free. Expect another tech-themed evening in autumn 2026 (programme not yet published).

**Rencontres de Noirmoutier** (1–5 Jun 2026, Village Vacances des 4 Vents, L'Épine) is a closed-format ~100-person fundamental-physics workshop covering dark matter, neutrinos, Standard Model, gravity, cosmology, compact objects, plus a climate-science interdisciplinary day [[5]](https://indico.ijclab.in2p3.fr/event/11873/). Tech-adjacent (instrumentation, LISA/Einstein Telescope discussions) but not IT. Walk-in attendance is not the model; if you have an academic connection, contact the organisers via Indico.

**Festival des Sciences 2026** "Prédire le futur" (24–28 Oct) is the larger public-facing event — five days at Salorges, free, with conferences, film-debates, ateliers, and round-tables on forecasting the planet, health, energy, economy, and the philosophical limits of prediction [[8]](https://www.ile-noirmoutier.com/fr/agenda/du-24-au-28-octobre-2026-festival-des-sciences-12eme-edition-predire-le-futur). The "predict the future" theme is the closest the local calendar gets to an AI/data event; speaker list is TBA on the [Les Sciences et Nous](https://www.lessciencesetnous.org/) site.

## If you'd drive 80 km (out of scope, for reference)

The actual Vendée tech ecosystem is concentrated in La Roche-sur-Yon, ~80 km south. Notable June–autumn 2026 events:

- **[Full Robotics 2026](https://www.proxinnov.com/evenement/full-robotics-2026/)** — Thu 11 Jun 2026, 08:30–16:30, Proxinnov pilot factory, 15 rue Jean Esswein, La Roche-sur-Yon. 6ᵗʰ edition. Theme: "the human at the heart of industry"; humanoid robots, demos, networking. Targets ops/innovation managers [[16]](https://www.proxinnov.com/evenement/full-robotics-2026/).
- **ADN Ouest Data & AI** — Thu 28 May 2026, 09:00–13:30, Loco Numérique, La Roche-sur-Yon. Morning on "AI agents transform the way we develop" [[17]](https://www.adnouest.org/page/agenda-des-evenements). (Just past — but the series is recurring; check the [ADN Ouest agenda](https://www.adnouest.org/page/agenda-des-evenements).)
- **PopCorn IA** — Tue 7 Jul 2026 evening, La Roche-sur-Yon. Collective evening on "human at the heart of AI" co-run by [La French Tech Vendée](https://www.vendeefrenchtech.com/), CCI Vendée, Proxinnov, Loco Numérique, Oryon, ADN Ouest [[18]](https://www.vendeefrenchtech.com/).
- Loco Numérique runs a rolling digital-events programme (conferences, fablab, startup hosting) — bookmark the [agenda](https://www.adnouest.org/page/agenda-des-evenements).

Nantes (~85 km) is the proper regional tech hub: Web2Day, FrenchTech Nantes, ADN Ouest core events, the EMC2 Forum (13 Oct 2026 at CCI Nantes-St-Nazaire) — all comfortably day-trippable from L'Herbaudière (1 h 15 by car) but well outside the 30 km perimeter.

## Bottom line

If "IT conferences within 30 km of La Marine" is meant literally, the answer is essentially *none* — the perimeter encloses a tourism island whose tech offering is library digital-literacy hours and a yearly popular-science festival. The closest real tech events require the 80 km drive inland.
