TL;DR. Book Hôtel Régis et Jacques Marcon if you can — 10 troglodyte rooms next door to the restaurant[4]. Sold out? Stay in the same hamlet at Les Yourtes de Larssialas (yurts at 1,200 m, €60–110)[7] or in-village Chambres Chatelard (suites with private hammam, €130–165)[6]. For character at a distance: Manoir du Grail in Devesset (21 km, heritage-listed manor)[10] or Domaine de Béchetoile (16th-c fortified house in a forest, 25 km)[13].

Restaurant Régis et Jacques Marcon sits at Larsiallas, 43290 Saint-Bonnet-le-Froid[1] — three Michelin stars on a wooded plateau between Haute-Loire and Ardèche. "Taxi range" here = roughly 30 minutes / 30 km, the rural distance a local cab will run for €60–95[3][18]. Selection below is filtered for character — heritage, troglodyte, yurts, restored farms — not chain comfort.

In the village (≤2 km — walk or 5-min taxi)

Hôtel Régis et Jacques Marcon

4★ Relais & Châteaux Saint-Bonnet-le-Froid · same property as restaurant

10 troglodyte rooms (9×50 m², 1×95 m²) accessed via an artist-decorated underground passage; natural climate from earth and solar; salt-treated guest-only pool; European eco-label; Auvergne/Ardèche wood throughout.[4] Panoramic terrace per room over Monts Ardéchois.

Sources: [4] [2]

Les Yourtes de Larssialas

Yurts + treehouse Larsiallas hamlet · same hill as the restaurant

3 contemporary yurts at ~1,200 m: La Chouette (2p, €60–80), Kazak'yourte (2–4p, €90–110), Champi'Yourte (2–6p, €100+). Local larch/Douglas-fir, sheep's-wool insulation, private bathroom and terrace per yurt. Treehouse cabin "L'oréade" added 2022.[7]

Source: [7]

Chambres Chatelard

B&B + hammam 16bis rue du Vivarais, in-village

4 suites pitched as "hotel quality, B&B charm, rental freedom" — each with kitchenette (cooktop, fridge, Nespresso) and private hammam access. €130 standard, €150 with breakfast+hammam, €165 with brunch+hammam.[6] Closed January; cash/check only.

Source: [6]

Hôtel Le Clos des Cimes

3★ Marcon Larsiallas · adjacent to restaurant

Sumptuous stone house with valley-view standard rooms (30 m²) and Junior suites (38 m²). Free per-night access to the Sources du Haut Plateau spa is included.[5] The fallback when the 4-star next door is booked.

Source: [5]

Auberge des Myrtilles

Restored farm 100 ch des Gorces · in-village

Country farmhouse fully restored; 5 rooms (4 doubles + 1 family, 1–5 pers), each on a red-fruit theme with private terrace. Logis Étik + Hôtel Cosy + Restaurant Terroir labels.[8] Restaurant year-round for guests, open to public Jul/Aug.

Source: [8]

Hob Fort du Pré

Hotel-spa wooded heights, in-village

29 nature-decor rooms in pine forest; heated indoor pool + sauna + hammam + jacuzzi + billiards + ping pong. Restaurant emphasises local produce. 2026 season runs daily 6 Feb–19 Dec.[9][19]

Sources: [9] [19]

Short taxi (15–25 km — €30–60, 15–25 min)

Manoir du Grail

Heritage manor Devesset · 21 km / 22 min via D 9[17]

A demeure de maître with defensive elements and mullioned windows, inscribed in the Inventaire général du patrimoine culturel since 20 January 2006.[10] Now a chambres d'hôtes with restaurant, garden, monumental fireplace, continental breakfast.[11]

Sources: [10] [11]

Hôtel Clair Matin

17th-c farm Le Chambon-sur-Lignon · ~23 km

17th-century farm run by the 4th generation of the Bard family; 25 soundproofed rooms, outdoor pool, spa with sauna and hot tub, "3 pots" Logis restaurant.[12] The largest character property in the cluster — useful for groups.

Source: [12]

Longer taxi (25–30 km — €60–95, 25–30 min)

Domaine de Béchetoile

16th-c fortified Burdignes · ~25 km, Pilat forest

16th-century fortified house built mid-forest 2 km from Burdignes, southern edge of the Parc Naturel du Pilat, 800 m altitude. 7 bedrooms across the fortified house + outbuildings, dinners served in front of a monumental fireplace. €224–280/night.[13]

Source: [13]

Au Hangar

Design barn Vernosc-lès-Annonay · ~28 km[3]

Design renovation of an old farm barn in Ardèche Verte. The "SPAcieuse" suite is the showpiece: private 4-place pro spa, sauna, king bed, kitchenette, breakfast included.[14] Massages and local Ardèche food plates on reservation.

Source: [14]

Practicalities

  • Taxi math. Local rural taxis to Saint-Bonnet-le-Froid: Tence 14.8 km / 15 min via D 18[16]; Lalouvesc 13.6 km / 15 min via D 532–D 214[15]; Saint-Agrève 21.3 km / 22 min via D 9[17]; Annonay 26.7 km / 25 min for €60–75[3]; Yssingeaux ~30 min for €70–90[18]. Book the return in advance — these are not Uber towns.
  • Why the village beats the periphery. A 5-min walk back to your room after a 4-hour tasting menu trumps a 25-min cab. Sleep in Saint-Bonnet first; reach outwards only if booked out or budget-locked.
  • What got cut. Hôtel La Découverte (3rd Marcon property) is comfortable and convenient but described as "mountain spirit" 18 m² rooms — character is thinner than its two siblings.[20] Generic chain options around Annonay/Yssingeaux exist but defeat the brief.
  • Booking lead time. The Marcon hotel sells out months ahead, particularly Friday/Saturday and around mushroom season. Reserve it together with the table.
