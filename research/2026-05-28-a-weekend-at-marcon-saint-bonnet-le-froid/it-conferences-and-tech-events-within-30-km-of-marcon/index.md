> **TL;DR — Decision:** There are **no IT conferences or tech meetups within 30 km of Marcon** scheduled for the remainder of 2026 (today: 28 May 2026). The only Ardèche AI event of the year — *Routes vers l'IA – Ardèche* at RCCM Saint-Clair, ~28 km from the restaurant — was on **28 April 2026** and is past [[2]](https://campusnumerique.auvergnerhonealpes.fr/evenement/les-routes-vers-lia-2026-ardeche/)[[3]](https://www.ene.fr/evenements/les-routes-vers-lia-saint-clair-07/). Plan the weekend as off-grid; for a real conference, drive ~50–70 km to Saint-Étienne, Le Puy-en-Velay, or Valence, or stretch to Lyon for **DevFest Lyon on 4 Dec 2026** [[16]](https://devfest.gdglyon.com/).

## Within 30 km — what's actually scheduled

The full inventory of IT-adjacent activity inside the radius for the rest of 2026:

- **Routes vers l'IA – Ardèche** — free morning conference on AI adoption for SMEs at RCCM, Saint-Clair, ~28 km away. ⚠ Already past (28 Apr 2026); the series visits each AURA department roughly once a year, so the next Ardèche edition lands around April 2027 [[2]](https://campusnumerique.auvergnerhonealpes.fr/evenement/les-routes-vers-lia-2026-ardeche/)[[3]](https://www.ene.fr/evenements/les-routes-vers-lia-saint-clair-07/).
- **Festiconf' – Annonay** (14–16 Nov 2026). ⚠ Not tech — this is a *festival des conférences gesticulées*, a French theatrical-lecture form on social/political topics. Mentioned only because the name misleads anyone searching for "conferences in Annonay" [[11]](https://annonay.centres-sociaux.fr/festiconf-une-2eme-edition-qui-tient-toutes-ses-promesses/).
- **Imp'acteurs entrepreneur workshops – Annonay / Davézieux**. Recurring 2026 series for entrepreneurs covering marketing, branding, automation, networking. No AI or advanced-tech focus, no fixed published calendar — check directly [[12]](https://www.salon-hk.com/impacteurs-ateliers-entrepreneurs/).
- **Semaine de l'Industrie en Ardèche** (mid-Nov 2026, CCI Ardèche). Coordinated open-factory visits across the department; the Annonay industrial basin participates each year. Industrial, not pure IT [[13]](https://www.ardeche.cci.fr/la-cci-ardeche/les-grands-rendez-vous-de-la-cci-ardeche/semaine-de-lindustrie).

## Permanent tech-adjacent infrastructure (inside 30 km)

Not events, but the only tech infrastructure in the radius worth knowing about:

- **Coworking municipal du Chambon-sur-Lignon** — free access, 70 Mbps, in the old mairie / Maison des Bretchs, 20 km from Marcon [[14]](https://www.lacommere43.fr/haut-lignon/item/41174-un-espace-de-coworking-municipal-operationnel-au-chambon-sur-lignon.html).
- **Annonay Rhône Agglo Coworking**, 698 rue de Vidalon, Davézieux — Mon–Fri 08:30–17:30, day rate from €5, hosts occasional France Num training and themed entrepreneurship workshops [[15]](https://www.coworking-france.com/espace-coworking/annonay-rhone-agglo-coworking/).

## Recommendation

Treat the Marcon weekend as off-grid — the plateau doesn't sustain a tech-event ecosystem. If a conference hook genuinely matters, two windows put a real event within a day's drive of Saint-Bonnet-le-Froid:

- **Late April:** *Routes vers l'IA – Ardèche* (next stop in the AURA cycle, ~28 km, free, SME-focused) plus *MiXiT* in Lyon a week or two earlier [[2]](https://campusnumerique.auvergnerhonealpes.fr/evenement/les-routes-vers-lia-2026-ardeche/)[[21]](https://mixitconf.org/fr/).
- **Early December:** *DevFest Lyon* (140 km, dev-focused, one-day) [[16]](https://devfest.gdglyon.com/).

For the rest of 2026, anchor on the food.
