SUMMIT MONT MÉZENC · 1753 m

~50 MIN SOUTH · CROIX DES BOUTIÈRES

Mont Mézenc

Highest peak of the Ardèche. On a clear day, 360°: Alps to the Puy de Dôme [4]. Two-hour up-and-back from the car park, or a 6.4 km loop from Croix de Peccata [3].

50 min 2–4 h hike +260 m

⚠ Late-spring snow can hold; clouds kill the view [65].

SUMMIT MONT GERBIER · 1551 m

~1 H SOUTH · SOURCE OF THE LOIRE

Mont Gerbier-de-Jonc

Volcanic plug where the Loire begins. Steep 1.2 km summit climb, ~1h30 round trip [1]; pair with the easy 3 km Sentier des Sources at the base.

~37 km 2–3 h +200 m steep

⚠ Trachyte stones turn slippery in rain — skip if wet [2].

LAKE LAC DE DEVESSET · 1074 m

10 MIN EAST · CLOSEST WATER

Lac de Devesset

51 ha plateau reservoir [6]. A flat 3.5 km loop on foot or by e-bike [5]. Vehicle access free outside July–August [33].

MÉMORIAL LE CHAMBON-SUR-LIGNON

18 MIN WEST · 23 ROUTE DU MAZET

Lieu de Mémoire

The plateau (pop. ~24,000) sheltered 3,000–3,500 Jews 1940–1944 under Pastor André Trocmé [18]. The only French commune collectively named Righteous Among the Nations, 1990 [19]. If history is the hook — go here.

18 min 2–3 h €5–€7

★ Through 31 May 2026: La fin de la Shoah 1944–1948 [17]. Closed Mondays.

BASILIQUE BASILIQUE SAINT-RÉGIS · LALOUVESC

14 MIN EAST · MUSÉE SAINT-RÉGIS

Basilique Saint-Régis

Built 1865–1877; houses the relics of Jesuit Jean-François Régis and (since 2018) Sainte Thérèse Couderc [22]. The Musée Saint-Régis across the square has a 19-scene Georges Serraz diorama, free, daily 30 March – 9 November [21].

PATRIMOINE YSSINGEAUX · HAUTE-LOIRE

~29 MIN NW · HÔTEL DE VILLE

Yssingeaux

The 15th-c fortified Hôtel de Ville, built by Jean de Bourbon, bishop of Le Puy [24]. Across town, the 19th-c Château de Montbarnier houses the prestigious ENSP pastry school (Thuriès & Ducasse, 1984) — visible from outside only [23].

32 km 1–2 h guided tour only

LE VILLAGE THE MARCON ECOSYSTEM

The Marcon ecosystem.

Beyond the 3★ tasting menu, the family runs six addresses in the village. You can fill an afternoon without leaving Saint-Bonnet — and the verbena liqueur is a souvenir that won't break in the suitcase.

  • Bistrot la Coulemelle 50-seat Bib Gourmand. Thu–Mon, closed Tue/Wed. ⚠ Seasonal break 17–25 June 2026 [40].
  • Cave Marcon 46 rue du Velay, open 7 days/week. Pours La Grande Verveine — distilled by Bérenger Mayoux from verbena grown in Jacques Marcon's gardens [42] [43].
  • La Chanterelle The Maisons Marcon bakery — under the same umbrella as the bistrot, Bar de Païs, hotel, gîtes, spa, cooking school [41].
  • AOPs nearby Fourme de Montbrison [50] · Picodon [52] · Lentille Verte du Puy (first European pulse AOP, 2008) [53] · Châtaigne d'Ardèche covers 118 communes incl. Saint-Agrève [54].

DRESS & APÉRITIF · DINNER AT MARCON

AEasy & weather-resilient

Drive to Lalouvesc (14 min). Coffee, Tour du Mont Chaix loop, basilica + Musée Saint-Régis, lunch on a terrace, back by 16h [57].

BBig-hitter, clear day only

Mont Mézenc summit. Leave 9h00; 2 h round trip from Croix des Boutières; picnic; back by 15h30 [4]. Check the weather the night before — clouds kill the view.

CHistory day

Le Chambon (18 min). Lieu de Mémoire (2 h), lunch in the village, short Lignon viewpoint loop, back by 16h [16]. ⚠ Closed Mondays.

Dimanche — before departure

MARKET · TRAIN · LAKE — PICK ONE

ALalouvesc market & Mass

Sunday market 7h–13h [48], basilica service if the timing fits. Easiest day.

BVelay Express from Tence

Drive 15 min, take a Sunday departure (steam or diesel) Tence ↔ Saint-Agrève or Tence ↔ Le Chambon [31]. The most distinctive Sunday-morning experience in the area.

CDevesset + Chiniac short loop

10 min to Devesset for the 3.5 km lake circuit, or 15 min to the Chante-coucou belvédère [7]. Quick, low-commitment, weather-flexible.

À noter — small print

  • The 30 km radius is generous: Peaugres, Mézenc, Gerbier, Saint-Joseph sit just outside.
  • May is statistically the wettest month — pack a shell [63]. Plan-B should not depend on a panorama.
  • Pad Google-Maps drive times 10–15 % on D-roads in summer — more after a slow tractor.
  • Reliable Sunday museums in May/June: Lieu de Mémoire & Musée Saint-Régis. Tence pharmacy & Yssingeaux Hôtel de Ville interior — July–August only.
  • Velay Express times not published online — phone +33 4 71 59 68 06 before driving to Tence.
  • If you want Bistrot Coulemelle lunch alongside Marcon dinner: note Coulemelle's Tue/Wed closure and 17–25 June 2026 break.
