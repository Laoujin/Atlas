> **Decision.** If you only do one thing → drive to [Lalouvesc](https://www.lalouvesc.fr/) (14 min east) for the Sunday market, basilica and an easy ridge walk [[57]](https://www.l-itineraire.com/de_saint-bonnet-le-froid_a_lalouvesc) [[48]](https://www.jours-de-marche.fr/43290-saint-bonnet-le-froid/). If you have a full day Saturday → loop east to [Annonay](https://www.annonay.fr/) for the [Musée des Papeteries Canson & Montgolfier](https://musee-papeteries-canson-montgolfier.fr/) and [Safari de Peaugres](https://www.safari-peaugres.com/) (40 min each way) [[20]](https://musee-papeteries-canson-montgolfier.fr/horaires-tarifs-programme/) [[30]](https://www.l-itineraire.com/de_saint-bonnet-le-froid_a_peaugres). If history is the hook → 18 min to [Le Chambon-sur-Lignon](https://memoireduchambon.com/) and the **Lieu de Mémoire** (only French commune collectively named Yad Vashem Righteous Among the Nations) [[19]](https://www.yadvashem.org/righteous/about-the-righteous/collective-rescue.html) [[27]](https://www.verymap.net/distances/2981323-3004774/saint-bonnet-le-froid/le-chambon-sur-lignon/). Sunday morning before departure → the [Velay Express](https://velay-express.fr/) heritage train from Tence (Sundays only May–Jun, both steam and diesel) [[31]](https://velay-express.fr/information/).

## Where is Marcon?

Restaurant Régis et Jacques Marcon (3 ★ Michelin) sits in Saint-Bonnet-le-Froid (1130 m, Haute-Loire) on the Haute-Loire / Ardèche border, in the Plateau Vivarais-Lignon [[62]](https://www.ot-hautlignon.com/en/discover/a-land-of-memory-and-heritage/our-villages/le-chambon-sur-lignon/). Mountain D-roads in every direction; Google-Maps drive times are generally honest but pad 10–15 % for traffic on the D9 / D532 in summer. May is statistically the wettest month here (~14 rainy days, oceanic Cfb climate) — pack a shell and warm layers, the village is at altitude [[63]](https://en.climate-data.org/europe/france/auvergne/saint-bonnet-le-froid-279714/).

## Outdoor & nature

### Mont Gerbier-de-Jonc — source of the Loire ⚠ slick in rain

A 1551 m volcanic plug, ~37 km / ~1 h south by D-roads via Saint-Agrève [[2]](https://www.lesterresdumilieu.fr/gerbier-de-jonc-sources-loire) [[59]](https://www.distance-voiture.fr/distance/mont-gerbier-de-jonc-07-126601-a). Summit climb is 1.2 km but steep — count 1h30 round trip [[1]](https://www.montagnedardeche.com/en/mont-gerbier-de-jonc/). Pair with the easy 3 km **Sentier des Sources de la Loire** at the base [[1]](https://www.montagnedardeche.com/en/mont-gerbier-de-jonc/). The stones turn slippery and dangerous in rain or storm — skip if the weather turns [[2]](https://www.lesterresdumilieu.fr/gerbier-de-jonc-sources-loire). Best late spring after snowmelt [[2]](https://www.lesterresdumilieu.fr/gerbier-de-jonc-sources-loire).

### Mont Mézenc — highest summit of the Ardèche ⚠ check for late snow

1753 m (south summit), with a 360° panorama from the Alps to the Puy de Dôme on a clear day [[4]](https://www.montagnedardeche.com/en/mont-mezenc/). Standard route: 2 h round trip from the Croix des Boutières car park [[4]](https://www.montagnedardeche.com/en/mont-mezenc/), or the slightly longer 6.4 km / +260 m loop from Croix de Peccata (~2h35, moderate) [[3]](https://www.visorando.com/en/walk-mont-mezenc/). Drive is ~50 min via Saint-Agrève and Col de la Croix de Peccata (1570 m) [[12]](http://el-grupetto.fr/etape-2-st-bonnet-le-froid-les-estables.html). The summit road is not winter-cleared and snow patches can hold into May [[65]](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/france/haute-loire/mont-mezenc) — Les Estables doubles as a small ski station [[14]](https://www.iloveski.org/en/ski-resorts/les-estables-mezencs/). Mézenc is the strongest "wow" hike at the radius edge — go on a clear day, otherwise it's just cloud.

### Lac de Devesset — closest swim & loop

51 ha reservoir at 1074 m on the Vivarais plateau, 10 min east [[6]](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lac_de_Devesset). Flat 3.5 km loop, family-friendly on foot or by bike [[5]](https://en.ardeche-hautes-vallees.fr/decouvrir/les-incontournables/lac-de-devesset/). Supervised swimming is **July–August only** (lifeguarded 13h30–18h30) — in late May the water is genuinely cold [[5]](https://en.ardeche-hautes-vallees.fr/decouvrir/les-incontournables/lac-de-devesset/). Vehicle access is free outside summer peak (paid €5 for cars on the last two weekends of June and through July/August) [[33]](https://en.ardeche-hautes-vallees.fr/equipement/baignade-au-lac-de-devesset/).

### Mont Chiniac — easy belvédère above Saint-Agrève

1116 m, accessed by the **Chante-coucou** loop: 6 km / +123 m through the Forêt de la Rèche, ending on a circular orientation table over the Velay and Boutières [[7]](https://www.ardeche-hautes-vallees.fr/patrimoine-naturel/le-mont-chiniac/) [[8]](https://www.sentiers-en-france.eu/sentiers-ardeche-07/saint-agreve/sentier-chante-coucou). The right answer when the cloud is too low for Mézenc but you still want a panorama.

### Lignon gorges & plateau hikes

The Lignon between Tence and Le Chambon offers ~240 km of marked PR trails over 24 loops, from the easy 4.9 km Pèlerins circuit to the harder PR033 Lizieux (15.5 km) and PR040 Roche druidique (12.8 km) [[10]](https://www.ot-hautlignon.com/en/discover/altitude-1000-metres/mountains-for-everyone/walks-and-hikes/). Viewpoints at Moulin de Boyer, Sauron, Beaujeu, Pont de Mars and Pont de Salettes [[9]](https://www.ot-hautlignon.com/en/discover/from-volcano-to-river/le-lignon-river-and-lakes/top-panoramas-on-the-lignon/). The **Passerelle Himalayenne** (268 m, 78 m above the Lignon) sits in the *lower* Lignon near Grazac — ~45 km from Marcon, just outside the radius, but a worthwhile detour if heading toward Le Puy [[15]](https://www.visorando.com/en/walk-la-passerelle-himalayenne-sur-les-gorges/).

### Lalouvesc — village walks at 1100 m

At the junction of the GR420 and GR430, with 22+ marked walks; the **Tour du Mont Chaix** (6 km / ~2 h) is the standard family loop with views toward the Alps [[13]](https://lalouvesc.fr/decouvrir-bouger/activites/randonnees-sympas-autour-de-lalouvesc/) [[60]](https://en.ardechegrandair.com/activites/visiter-decouvrir/5488765_lalouvesc-ville-sanctuaire-en-france/).

### Mont Pilat foothills — out of range

The Crêt de l'Oeillon (1364 m), the second-highest summit of the Pilat, sits ~50 km north — beyond easy day-trip range, mentioned only to set expectations [[11]](https://www.pilat-tourisme.fr/en/discover/mountain/cret-de-l-oeillon).

## History, memory & museums

### Le Chambon-sur-Lignon — Lieu de Mémoire

20.4 km / 18 min west by road [[27]](https://www.verymap.net/distances/2981323-3004774/saint-bonnet-le-froid/le-chambon-sur-lignon). The plateau around Le Chambon (population then ~24,000) sheltered an estimated 3,000–3,500 Jews from 1940 to 1944 under Pastor André Trocmé [[18]](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/le-chambon-sur-lignon). In 1990 Yad Vashem unveiled a plaque collectively honouring Le Chambon and the neighbouring communes as Righteous Among the Nations [[19]](https://www.yadvashem.org/righteous/about-the-righteous/collective-rescue.html) — the only French commune to hold the title collectively [[62]](https://www.ot-hautlignon.com/en/discover/a-land-of-memory-and-heritage/our-villages/le-chambon-sur-lignon/).

The **Lieu de Mémoire** (23 route du Mazet) opens daily except Monday 10h–12h30 / 14h–18h from 2 May to 30 Sep; full €5, €7 during temporary exhibitions, reduced €3 [[16]](https://memoireduchambon.com/informations-pratiques/horaires-tarifs/). For the 2026 spring window, the temporary show *La fin de la Shoah 1944–1948* runs through **31 May 2026** [[17]](https://eha-etain-meuse.over-blog.com/2026/02/haute-loire.le-lieu-de-memoire-du-chambon-sur-lignon-rouvrira-ses-portes-le-4-mars-avec-une-expo-sur-la-shoah.html) — if you are visiting before 31 May, this is the single best non-meal activity in 30 km.

### Annonay & Davézieux — Montgolfier paper mill

The Musée des Papeteries [Canson & Montgolfier](https://musee-papeteries-canson-montgolfier.fr/) sits in the **birth house** of the Montgolfier brothers, with live 17th–21st-century paper-making demonstrations [[61]](https://en.ardechegrandair.com/nos-experiences/musee-papeteries-canson-montgolfier-annonay/). Open Wed & Sun 14h30–18h year-round, daily 14h15–18h in July–August; €6.50 adult, €5 child 6–16 [[20]](https://musee-papeteries-canson-montgolfier.fr/horaires-tarifs-programme/). For a Saturday visit, note **Wed/Sun only** in May — if travelling Saturday, swap to a Sunday afternoon visit.

### Lalouvesc — basilica & Musée Saint-Régis

Basilique Saint-Régis (built 1865–1877) houses the relics of Jesuit Jean-François Régis and, since 2018, Sainte Thérèse Couderc [[22]](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilique_Saint-R%C3%A9gis_de_Lalouvesc). Across the square, the **Musée Saint-Régis** is open daily 30 March – 9 November 2026 with free admission; the centrepiece is a 19-scene Georges Serraz diorama of the saint's life [[21]](https://en.ardechegrandair.com/incontournables-annonay-lalouvesc-peaugres/138894_musee-saint-regis/).

### Yssingeaux — fortified Hôtel de Ville & ENSP

The **Hôtel de Ville** on Place du Général-de-Gaulle is a 15th-century fortified summer residence built by Jean de Bourbon, bishop of Le Puy — guided tours only [[24]](https://yssingeaux.fr/fr/decouvrir-yssingeaux/histoireetpatrimoine/flaneries-urbaines/819-hotel-de-ville). The 19th-century Château de Montbarnier now houses the **École Nationale Supérieure de la Pâtisserie** (founded 1984 by Yves Thuriès and Alain Ducasse) — the building is visible from outside, but the school doesn't run regular public tours [[23]](https://www.gralon.net/articles/enseignement-et-formation/formation/article-l-ecole-nationale-superieure-de-la-patisserie---une-ecole-prestigieuse-4546.htm).

### Retournac — Château d'Artias

A medieval ruin on a pyramidal rock with eco-museum, botanical garden and bailiff's house. Free access, ~1h30 [[25]](https://www.myhauteloire.fr/patrimoine-culturel/chateau-dartias/). Worth the detour if you're already on the Yssingeaux side; otherwise skip.

### Tence — Pénitents Blancs chapel & pharmacy museum

Both museums open July & August (Wed–Sat / Thursday respectively), shut in May — outside the season, Tence is a stop for the village fabric and the steam train, not the museums [[26]](https://www.ot-hautlignon.com/en/discover/a-land-of-memory-and-heritage/our-villages/tence/).

## Family & leisure

### Safari de Peaugres ⭐ marquee day out

36 km / 40 min east — just outside the 30 km radius but the region's single biggest family destination [[30]](https://www.l-itineraire.com/de_saint-bonnet-le-froid_a_peaugres). 80 ha, 1,200 animals, 5 km drive-through circuit (~1 h) plus a minimum 3 h walking section [[28]](https://www.zoofrance.com/safari-peaugres/). 2026 prices: €29.90 adult (11+), €25.50 child (3–10), free under 3 [[28]](https://www.zoofrance.com/safari-peaugres/). Open daily April–September 10h–18h30 (9h30–19h in July/August) [[29]](https://www.safari-peaugres.com/calendrier-horaires-ouverture/). Plan a 4–5 h on-site commitment.

### Velay Express — heritage train from Tence

The 1920s "Galoche" line, restored as a tourist railway with both steam and diesel rolling stock [[32]](https://hautpaysduvelay-tourisme.fr/incontournables/train-velay-express/). 2026 schedule: **Sundays + May public holidays from May to October**, plus Wednesdays and Thursdays from 16 July to 31 August [[31]](https://velay-express.fr/information/). Route: Raucoules → Tence → Le Chambon-sur-Lignon → Saint-Agrève; the former Dunières spur has been closed since 2015 [[64]](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velay_Express). Tickets €16.50–€22.50 adult, €13.50–€19.50 child 4–12, family-of-four packs €54–€78 [[32]](https://hautpaysduvelay-tourisme.fr/incontournables/train-velay-express/). ⚠ The official schedule page lists days but not specific May/June departure times — call **+33 4 71 59 68 06** or download the PDF before driving to Tence.

### Parcours Acrobatique des Sucs (Yssingeaux)

Four colour-graded courses (yellow from age 3, red from age 10), with two tyroliennes (ziplines) and a bungy-ejection feature [[34]](https://www.accro-sucs.fr/). Open 30 April – 20 September 2026, daily 10h–18h30, by reservation [[35]](https://www.cosycamp.com/blog/accrobranches-haute-loire/). €7 / €15 / €20 by age band [[34]](https://www.accro-sucs.fr/). 29 min drive.

### Les Marraignes — asinerie at Saint-Agrève

Free farm visits (donations welcome) and donkey-accompanied walks from €15; one packed donkey covers 3–4 adults for a full day, with 30 min of briefing before departure [[38]](https://www.ardeche-hautes-vallees.fr/commerce-service/association-les-marraignes/). School-holiday slots Tue–Thu 10h–11h30 and Fri 16h30–18h, capacity 50 — book ahead [[37]](https://lesmarraignes.fr/).

### NaturaLoisirs — e-bikes in Saint-Bonnet itself

9 rue du Velay, in the village. Giant electric and standard bike rentals, open Thu–Sun 10h–19h in spring (Wed–Sun in July–September) [[36]](https://www.naturaloisirs-sbf.fr/). Useful for a Devesset loop without driving.

## Gastronomy

### The Marcon ecosystem — in the village

Beyond the gastronomic restaurant, the [Maisons Marcon](https://www.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/) umbrella covers **Bistrot la Coulemelle** (Rue du Vivarais, 50 seats, Bib Gourmand, Thu–Mon, ⚠ seasonal break 17–25 June 2026) [[40]](https://saintbonnetlefroid.fr/restaurants/bistrot-la-coulemelle/), Bar de Païs, La Chanterelle bakery, a Relais & Châteaux hotel, gîtes, Spa du Haut Plateau and a cooking school [[41]](https://www.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/). **Cave (Vins) Marcon** at 46 rue du Velay is open 7 days/week and pours their own artisanal verbena liqueur *La Grande Verveine*, distilled by Bérenger Mayoux at Solignac-sur-Loire from verbena grown in Jacques Marcon's gardens [[42]](https://saintbonnetlefroid.fr/commerces/cave-marcon/) [[43]](https://www.lacommere43.fr/pays-de-montfaucon/item/18484-saint-bonnet-le-froid-la-cave-marcon-lance-sa-propre-liqueur-artisanale-la-grande-verveine.html). The **Foire aux Champignons** (Saint-Bonnet's annual mushroom fair) is 7–8 November 2026 — irrelevant for May, but the village's mushroom identity is year-round [[44]](https://saintbonnetlefroid.fr/foire-aux-champignons/).

### Cheese & producer AOPs

- **Fourme de Montbrison AOP** — 7 dairies (3 industrial, 4 farmhouse) across 33 Haut-Forez communes above 700 m, several open to visits year-round [[50]](https://www.produits-laitiers-aop.fr/en/products/fourme-de-montbrison/). [Fromagerie Artisanale des Hautes Chaumes](https://fromagerie-hautes-chaumes.com/) at Sauvain is the noted artisan name (founded 1999, ex-Hervé Mons MOF input) — but Sauvain is in the Loire département, ~70 km away; for a 30 km visit, use the AOP body's producer map to pick the nearest farm [[51]](https://fromagerie-hautes-chaumes.com/).
- **Picodon AOP** — goat-milk cheese from Ardèche/Drôme; the AOP producer map lists farms with direct sales [[52]](https://www.picodon-aop.fr/producteurs-picodon-aop/).
- **Lentille Verte du Puy AOP** — first European pulse to win AOP (2008), grown 600–1200 m on volcanic soils across 87 Velay communes [[53]](http://www.lalentillevertedupuy.com/en/).
- **Châtaigne d'Ardèche AOP** — covers 118 Ardèche communes including Saint-Agrève; the **Maison du Châtaignier** at Saint-Pierreville is the visitor centre [[54]](https://www.chataigne-ardeche.com/).

### Saint-Joseph wine — at the radius edge

The Saint-Joseph AOC is anchored on Tournon-sur-Rhône — about 50 km / 52 min east [[56]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Tournon-sur-Rh%C3%B4ne/Saint-Bonnet-le-Froid). The Doux river that rises in Saint-Bonnet flows down the Ardèche slopes to the **Sainte-Épine** lieu-dit, one of the appellation's premier terroirs [[55]](https://valleedurhonenord.com/en/our-appellations/saint-joseph-appellation/). Out of strict radius — drop down only if you've already done the highland options.

## Itineraries — anchored on Saturday-evening dinner at Marcon

Dinner at the gastronomic restaurant is an evening commitment with an aperitif slot before sitting — practical target: **be back in the village by 17h–17h30** to shower and dress (confirm the exact time on your booking).

### Saturday: half-day before dinner

**Option A — Easy, weather-resilient:** Lalouvesc loop. 14 min drive east [[57]](https://www.l-itineraire.com/de_saint-bonnet-le-froid_a_lalouvesc); coffee in the village, the Tour du Mont Chaix (6 km / 2 h) [[13]](https://lalouvesc.fr/decouvrir-bouger/activites/randonnees-sympas-autour-de-lalouvesc/), basilica + Musée Saint-Régis [[21]](https://en.ardechegrandair.com/incontournables-annonay-lalouvesc-peaugres/138894_musee-saint-regis/), lunch on a terrace, back by 16h.

**Option B — Big-hitter, clear day only:** Mont Mézenc summit. Leave 9h00, ~50 min drive [[12]](http://el-grupetto.fr/etape-2-st-bonnet-le-froid-les-estables.html), 2 h round-trip from Croix des Boutières [[4]](https://www.montagnedardeche.com/en/mont-mezenc/), picnic, back by 15h30. Check the Mézenc weather the night before — clouds kill the view [[65]](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/france/haute-loire/mont-mezenc).

**Option C — History day:** Le Chambon-sur-Lignon. 18 min drive [[27]](https://www.verymap.net/distances/2981323-3004774/saint-bonnet-le-froid/le-chambon-sur-lignon), Lieu de Mémoire (2 h) [[16]](https://memoireduchambon.com/informations-pratiques/horaires-tarifs/), lunch in the village, short Lignon viewpoint loop [[9]](https://www.ot-hautlignon.com/en/discover/from-volcano-to-river/le-lignon-river-and-lakes/top-panoramas-on-the-lignon/), back by 16h. ⚠ Note Lieu de Mémoire closes Mondays.

### Sunday: morning before departure

**Option A — Lalouvesc Sunday market + Mass + departure.** 7h00–13h00 market [[48]](https://www.jours-de-marche.fr/43290-saint-bonnet-le-froid/), basilica service if the timing fits. Easiest day.

**Option B — Velay Express from Tence.** Drive 15 min, take a Sunday departure (steam or diesel) Tence ↔ Saint-Agrève or Tence ↔ Le Chambon [[31]](https://velay-express.fr/information/). Call ahead for the exact 2026 departure time — schedule page lists days but not times. The most distinctive Sunday-morning experience in the area.

**Option C — Devesset + Chiniac short loop.** 10 min to Devesset for the 3.5 km lake circuit [[5]](https://en.ardeche-hautes-vallees.fr/decouvrir/les-incontournables/lac-de-devesset/), or 15 min to the Chante-coucou belvédère [[7]](https://www.ardeche-hautes-vallees.fr/patrimoine-naturel/le-mont-chiniac/). Quick, low-commitment, weather-flexible.

### Full-day Saturday or Sunday (if you have one)

**East loop:** Marcon → Annonay (Canson museum, Sun afternoon only in May [[20]](https://musee-papeteries-canson-montgolfier.fr/horaires-tarifs-programme/)) → Safari de Peaugres (4–5 h) [[28]](https://www.zoofrance.com/safari-peaugres/) → return. ~9 h door-to-door. Skip if you're doing the gastronomic dinner the same evening.

**South loop:** Marcon → Mont Gerbier (1.2 km summit + 3 km Sentier des Sources) [[1]](https://www.montagnedardeche.com/en/mont-gerbier-de-jonc/) → onward to Mont Mézenc via Les Estables / Croix des Boutières [[4]](https://www.montagnedardeche.com/en/mont-mezenc/) → return via Saint-Agrève. ~7 h.

## Caveats

- **30 km radius is generous.** Several of the strongest options (Peaugres, Mézenc, Gerbier, Saint-Joseph) sit just outside. Inside the strict radius the highlights are Lalouvesc, Le Chambon, Devesset, Tence, Saint-Agrève, the Lignon gorges, and the village of Saint-Bonnet itself.
- **May weather.** Wettest month of the year [[63]](https://en.climate-data.org/europe/france/auvergne/saint-bonnet-le-froid-279714/); summit cloud is common. Plan a Plan-B that doesn't depend on a panorama (Le Chambon, museum, Cave Marcon).
- **Drive-time padding.** Calculator drive times assume you keep moving on D-roads. Pad 10–15% in practice, more after a slow tractor.
- **Sunday closures.** Several museums (Tence pharmacy, Yssingeaux Hôtel de Ville interior) operate only July–August or by appointment — the **Lieu de Mémoire** and **Musée Saint-Régis** are the reliable open-on-Sunday museums in May/June.
- **Velay Express.** Call **+33 4 71 59 68 06** for May–June 2026 departure times — not published online.
- **Reservation timing.** If you want to combine Marcon dinner with Bistrot la Coulemelle lunch, note Coulemelle's Tue/Wed closure and 17–25 June 2026 break [[40]](https://saintbonnetlefroid.fr/restaurants/bistrot-la-coulemelle/).
