> **Decision** — Book [Hôtel Régis & Jacques Marcon][r1] (4★, on the restaurant compound, from €430) if you want to roll from tasting menu to bed [[8]](https://boutique.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/saint-bonnet-le-froid/40-chambre-suite-luxe-1-ou-2-personnes.html). Take [Le Clos des Cimes][r2] (3★, ~500 m, from €175, spa included) for a cheaper Marcon-family room with the same hospitality ecosystem [[4]](https://www.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/hotel-les-clos-des-cimes/) [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g950950-d2196801-Hotel_Regis_et_Jacques_Marcon-Saint_Bonnet_le_Froid_Haute_Loire_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html). ## The village layout

Saint-Bonnet-le-Froid is a 280-inhabitant village at 1,200 m in the Haute-Loire [[16]](https://saintbonnetlefroid.fr/). The 3-Michelin restaurant is at **18 chemin de Brard** (locality "Larsiallas"); the Marcon-family 3★ hotels are at **2 chemin du Fanget**, ~500 m away; the bistrot and bar sit on **rue du Vivarais** between them [[3]](https://www.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/venir/). Everything inside the village is genuinely walkable; rural unlit roads outside the core are not, especially after a multi-course dinner with wine pairings.

## Notes per option

**Hôtel Régis & Jacques Marcon (4★, [Relais & Châteaux](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/hotel/les-maisons-marcon/) member [[2]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/hotel/les-maisons-marcon/)).** Ten troglodyte rooms with private terraces on the same Larsiallas compound as the restaurant — same compound, not a separate building [[1]](https://www.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/hotel-regis-jacques-marcon/). 9 rooms at 50 m², one 95 m² suite; salt-treated pool reserved for guests. Tripadvisor: 4.8/5 across 125 reviews, with diners calling it "obligatory" for the 3-Michelin meal [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g950950-d2196801-Reviews-Hotel_Regis_et_Jacques_Marcon-Saint_Bonnet_le_Froid_Haute_Loire_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html). Doubles from €430 HT, breakfast €30 pp, six-month rolling reservation window opening the 1st of each month at 10:00 [[8]](https://boutique.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/saint-bonnet-le-froid/40-chambre-suite-luxe-1-ou-2-personnes.html). → **Default pick if you can get a room.**

**Le Clos des Cimes (3★, Marcon).** Stone-house property at 2 chemin du Fanget [[3]](https://www.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/venir/); two room types (30 m² valley view, 38 m² junior suite) [[4]](https://www.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/hotel-les-clos-des-cimes/). Crucially, spa access at Les Sources du Haut Plateau is included one access per person per night — same wellness ecosystem as the 4★ at a third of the price [[4]](https://www.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/hotel-les-clos-des-cimes/). 500 m walk through the village to dinner [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g950950-d2196801-Hotel_Regis_et_Jacques_Marcon-Saint_Bonnet_le_Froid_Haute_Loire_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html). From €175 per room. → **Best value among Marcon-family options.**

**La Découverte (3★, Marcon).** Same address as Clos des Cimes, smaller 18 m² rooms (double or twin, one accessible), renovated in 2015, shared lounge / fireplace / bar [[5]](https://www.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/hotel-la-decouverte/). Marketed as the partner property to Clos des Cimes [[5]](https://www.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/hotel-la-decouverte/). ⚠ Spa is "adjacent" rather than necessarily included — confirm at booking. → **Pick if Clos des Cimes is full and budget matters.**

**Chambres Chatelard (chambres d'hôtes, Clévacances label).** Four suites in a restored barn at 16bis rue du Vivarais — heart of the village, ~600 m from the restaurant [[10]](https://saintbonnetlefroid.fr/tourisme/hebergements/hebergements-locatifs/chambres-dhotes-chatelard/) [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g950950-d2196801-Hotel_Regis_et_Jacques_Marcon-Saint_Bonnet_le_Froid_Haute_Loire_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html). Hammam included; €130 room only, €150 with breakfast + hammam, €165 with brunch [[9]](https://www.chambres-chatelard.fr/). ⚠ Closed January; cash/cheque only (no cards) [[9]](https://www.chambres-chatelard.fr/). → **Best independent fallback when Marcon is sold out.**

**Les Bruyères de Gaschon (insolite).** A Finnish barrel, a gypsy caravan, and an Auvergne cabin at the hamlet of Gaschon (101 chemin du Doux), plus a gîte [[11]](https://lesbruyeresdegaschon.fr/). ⚠ Insolite-only and seasonal: hébergements insolites open early April to mid-November; gîte April through December [[11]](https://lesbruyeresdegaschon.fr/). Tripadvisor places it ~600 m (0.4 mi) from Hôtel Marcon as the crow flies; the on-foot route from chemin de Brard to chemin du Doux is longer [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g950950-d2196801-Hotel_Regis_et_Jacques_Marcon-Saint_Bonnet_le_Froid_Haute_Loire_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html). → **Skip unless you specifically want the unusual stay; not a fit for evening-dress-after-tasting-menu logistics.**

**Hob Fort du Pré.** The only fully-independent walkable hotel: 29 rooms, own restaurant, pool, spa, sauna, fitness — taken over by the Evi Hob brand in April 2022 [[13]](https://www.evihob.com/en/hobs/hob-fort-du-pre/presentation/). Standard €95–99, comfort €135–155, family €162–186; breakfast €16 [[14]](https://le-fort-du-pre-43290-2.auvergnetophotels.com/en/). ~1.0 km / 13-minute walk to Marcon per Tripadvisor [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g950950-d2196801-Hotel_Regis_et_Jacques_Marcon-Saint_Bonnet_le_Froid_Haute_Loire_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html). ⚠ March–December only (closed Tuesday eve to Sunday midday in shoulder season) [[14]](https://le-fort-du-pre-43290-2.auvergnetophotels.com/en/). → **The cheapest walkable hotel option, but it's the edge of the walking radius.**

## Booking notes

- The municipality lists only four properties under "hôtellerie" — Marcon 4★, Clos des Cimes/Découverte 3★, Hob Fort du Pré, and the Auberge des Myrtilles (2.3 km — not walking distance and excluded above) [[15]](https://saintbonnetlefroid.fr/tourisme/hebergements/hotellerie/). Everything else is chambres d'hôtes, gîtes, or insolite.
- Marcon-family hotel reservations open **on a six-month rolling window, the 1st of each month at 10:00** [[8]](https://boutique.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/saint-bonnet-le-froid/40-chambre-suite-luxe-1-ou-2-personnes.html). For a Saturday-evening dinner anchor, book the room before securing the table — the table is the bottleneck for the restaurant, but the 4★ hotel only has 10 keys.
- "Walking distance" is real here: the whole village fits inside ~1 km. The only thing to plan around is altitude (1,200 m → cool evenings even in summer) and rural lighting after the restaurant closes [[16]](https://saintbonnetlefroid.fr/).
