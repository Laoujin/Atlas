The weekend cleanly halves into two questions — *where do you sleep* and *what do you do between checkout and tasting menu* — both pinned to a single 3-Michelin reservation. Across all four sub-topics, one fact dominates: **the bottleneck is the hotel, not the table.** The 4★ Hôtel Régis & Jacques Marcon has ten keys on a six-month rolling window that opens the 1st of each month at 10h00 [[1]](https://boutique.lesmaisonsmarcon.fr/saint-bonnet-le-froid/40-chambre-suite-luxe-1-ou-2-personnes.html), so the room has to be locked in before the dinner. The dinner mechanics themselves — tasting-menu price, wine-pairing cost, exact end time, dress code, whether the booking flow bundles a room — were not verified in this run despite being framed as the trip's anchor; treat them as the open call to make.

**Walking-distance vs character.** The two lodging sub-topics overlap on the village core (Hôtel Marcon, Le Clos des Cimes, Chambres Chatelard, Hob Fort du Pré) and diverge from there. Walking radius adds Auberge des Myrtilles and Les Bruyères de Gaschon (insolite barrels — wrong logistics for evening dress after a tasting menu). Character radius adds Les Yourtes de Larssialas in the same Larsiallas hamlet at €60–110 [[2]](https://www.lesyourtesdelarssialas.fr/), plus heritage options at Devesset (Manoir du Grail, heritage-listed [[3]](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manoir_du_Grail)), Le Chambon-sur-Lignon, Burdignes (Pilat forest), and Vernosc-lès-Annonay. None of the character properties is walkable; they require a pre-booked rural taxi (~€30–95) [[4]](https://www.rome2rio.com/fr/s/Saint-Bonnet-le-Froid/Annonay).

**Lodging villages double as day-trip destinations.** Devesset is both a yurt-adjacent hamlet and a 51 ha plateau lake with a 3.5 km loop [[5]](https://en.ardeche-hautes-vallees.fr/decouvrir/les-incontournables/lac-de-devesset/); Le Chambon hosts both the 17th-c Hôtel Clair Matin and the Lieu de Mémoire, the only French commune collectively named Righteous Among the Nations [[6]](https://www.yadvashem.org/righteous/about-the-righteous/collective-rescue.html). The brief asked the activities sub-topic to reconsider every lodging village as a candidate day-trip — Saint-Agrève, Le Chambon, Devesset, Lalouvesc, Annonay all got coverage, but **Burdignes (Pilat forest edge) and Vernosc-lès-Annonay didn't get their own activity write-ups** and remain as "stay out, daytrip in" possibilities only.

**Weather and timing constraints.** May is statistically the wettest month here [[7]](https://en.climate-data.org/europe/france/auvergne/saint-bonnet-le-froid-279714/); a clear-day Mont Mézenc summit is the trip's strongest panorama, but the back-up needs to be set before arrival. The Lieu de Mémoire (daily except Monday, 10h–18h, *La fin de la Shoah* show running through 31 May [[8]](https://memoireduchambon.com/informations-pratiques/horaires-tarifs/)) and the Sunday Velay Express steam train from Tence [[9]](https://velay-express.fr/information/) are the weather-proof picks.

**No tech ecosystem.** Inside 30 km there are zero scheduled IT events for the rest of 2026 — the Ardèche AI stop at Saint-Clair (~28 km) was already on 28 April [[10]](https://campusnumerique.auvergnerhonealpes.fr/evenement/les-routes-vers-lia-2026-ardeche/); the closest dev conference is DevFest Lyon on 4 December at ~140 km [[11]](https://devfest.gdglyon.com/). Treat the weekend as off-grid by design.

**What this run did not verify** — Marcon's tasting-menu price, wine-pairing cost, dress code, exact dinner end time, and whether the table booking bundles a room. One phone call to the restaurant resolves the only constraint still open.
