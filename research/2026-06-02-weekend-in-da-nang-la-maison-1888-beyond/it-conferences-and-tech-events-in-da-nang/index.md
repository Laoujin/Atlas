## Flagship Annual Events

### DevDay Da Nang

[DevDay](https://www.devday.org/) is Da Nang's largest non-profit IT event, running since 2015 and co-organized by Axon Active and Duy Tan University (03 Quang Trung St) [[1]](https://www.devday.org/). The 2026 edition (11th year) on April 18 drew 3,000+ participants with the theme "AI × Human" — 16 workshops covering AI coding assistants, spec-driven development, testing agents, and workforce transformation [[2]](https://news.laodong.vn/giao-duc/da-nang-khai-mac-ngay-hoi-cong-nghe-thong-tin-thu-hut-hon-3000-nguoi-tham-du-1687441.ldo). Free entry; past editions archived at devday.org/2025, devday.org/2024, etc.

### Super Vietnam

[Super Vietnam](https://web3voyager.com/event/super-vietnam/) is an annual blockchain-and-AI week. The 2025 debut (Jun 4–5) included a Super Vietnam Pitchfest for Web3/AI startups, panels on digital finance, and a full Blockchain & AI Week of side events Jun 2–8 [[11]](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/tech/tech-news/global-tech-conference-super-vietnam-2025-opens-in-da-nang-4894795.html). Organized by Orochi Network and FPT Online, with explicit plans to make it annual [[19]](https://www.ainvest.com/news/super-vietnam-2025-connect-global-tech-leaders-da-nang-2506/). **2026 edition: Jul 10–11** [[10]](https://web3voyager.com/event/super-vietnam/).

## May Web3 Cluster

Three events overlap in a five-day window, effectively forming an unofficial "Web3 Week":

**[Web3 Builders' Summit (W3BS): LFBUIDL 2026](https://www.web3builderssummit.com/)** — May 24–27, Risemount Premier Resort. Builder-centric, powered by sqrDAO. 350+ builders, 50 speakers, 40 partners. Topics: stablecoins, RWA tokenization, AI-native digital assets, onchain payments. Entry free but curated — vetted to active builders and ecosystem contributors [[7]](https://www.web3builderssummit.com/).

**[DAVAS 2026](https://davas.vc/)** — May 25–27. International startup investment forum organized by the Da Nang Department of Science and Technology. 50 selected startups pitch for VC/angel funding; 50+ exhibition booths; 1:1 matchmaking. Day 1 co-hosted with W3BS [[6]](https://davas.vc/).

**[Unchained Summit Vietnam 2026](https://infosec-conferences.com/event/20260528-unchained-summit-vietnam-2026/)** — May 28–29, Furama Resort. The largest of the three: 1,000+ delegates, 70+ speakers including Nansen CEO Alex Svanevik, MEXC CEO Vugar Usi, and Tether APAC lead Quynh Le [[8]](https://infosec-conferences.com/event/20260528-unchained-summit-vietnam-2026/). Vietnam's State Securities Commission and Da Nang People's Committee participated, discussing crypto licensing and market supervision [[9]](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/05/07/da-nang-positions-itself-as-a-web3-hub-through-unchained-summit-480294/). Paid event.

## Community & Recurring Events

### GDG Cloud Da Nang

[GDG Cloud Da Nang](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-cloud-da-nang/) has 1,905 members and runs two recurring formats [[12]](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-cloud-da-nang/):

- **Cafe Code** — monthly in-person meetup (coffee-shop format), covering hands-on Cloud and AI topics
- **Build with AI** — multi-session workshop series; 2026 ran March 28 and April 4
- **DevFest Cloud Da Nang** — annual December conference at Vanda Hotel, Google Cloud + Generative AI; 2025 edition Dec 6 [[13]](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-cloud-da-nang-presents-devfest-2025-cloud-da-nang/)

### GDG Mien Trung

[GDG Mien Trung](https://gdgmientrung.com/) is a separate Google Developer Group covering Central Vietnam, also based in Da Nang [[14]](https://gdgmientrung.com/). Organizes [Google I/O Extended MienTrung](https://gdgmientrung-io25.vercel.app/) annually — the 2025 edition (Jul 12) drew 500+ developers [[15]](https://gdgmientrung-io25.vercel.app/).

## Da Nang as a Tech Hub: Context

The density of events reflects deliberate city-level investment [[18]](https://auschamvn.org/news/da-nang-city-new-hi-tech-industry-hub):

- **Da Nang Software Park 2** ICT1 building opened January 2025 [[16]](https://en.vietnamplus.vn/da-nang-opens-software-park-2-post308438.vnp)
- **Semiconductor Fab-Lab** launched July 2025 (VND 1.8 trillion; operational target Q4 2026) [[17]](https://en.vietnamplus.vn/da-nang-launches-key-projects-in-data-centre-semiconductor-and-ai-post312638.vnp)
- **Data Center** phase 2 investment planned (VND 1.2 trillion / ~$48M), operational 2026–2027 [[17]](https://en.vietnamplus.vn/da-nang-launches-key-projects-in-data-centre-semiconductor-and-ai-post312638.vnp)
- 70+ companies at Da Nang Hi-tech Park including Marvell, Synopsys, Renesas, and FPT Semiconductor — with a stated goal of becoming a global semiconductor and AI manufacturing center by 2030 [[17]](https://en.vietnamplus.vn/da-nang-launches-key-projects-in-data-centre-semiconductor-and-ai-post312638.vnp)
