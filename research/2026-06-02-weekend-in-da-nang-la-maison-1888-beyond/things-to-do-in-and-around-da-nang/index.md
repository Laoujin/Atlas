## The shape of the city

Da Nang is wedged between mountains and sea, with three natural blocks: the **downtown / Han River** strip (bridges, museums, rooftop bars), **My Khe / An Thuong** (the beach and Western Quarter), and the **Son Tra peninsula** (jungled "Monkey Mountain" headland holding the [InterContinental Sun Peninsula Resort](https://www.danang.intercontinental.com/dining/la-maison-1888/) and most of Vietnam's red-shanked douc langurs) [[44]](https://vinpearl.com/en/linh-ung-pagoda-a-famous-spiritual-site-in-da-nang-vietnam)[[47]](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/animals/article/monkey-mountain-red-shanked-doucs-vietnam). Hoi An, Marble Mountains, Ba Na Hills, My Son and Hue all sit within a 30-minute to 2.5-hour radius — Da Nang is the logistical base for all of them.

**Notes on the picks**

- **Hoi An** is the easy win — close, cheap, the lantern-lit centre is closed to traffic 6–10 PM on the **14th day of the lunar month** for the monthly Full Moon Festival (free to wander) [[21]](https://hoiandaytrip.com/hoi-an-lantern-festival/). Even on non-festival nights the lanterns are the draw, so aim for late afternoon arrival.
- **Ba Na Hills** is a built theme park, not a hike — the cable car holds the Guinness record for longest non-stop single-track at 5,801 m [[64]](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/longest-non-stop-single-track-cable-car). From 2026 the standard ticket is valid up to **3 consecutive days** with unlimited rides [[23]](https://danangfantasticity.com/en/news/from-2026-cable-car-tickets-at-sun-world-ba-na-hills-will-be-valid-for-up-to-three-consecutive-days) — useful if weather rolls in.
- **My Son** rewards an early start: gates open 6 AM, big tour buses arrive ~10, so go on the first wave [[24]](https://www.thecommonwanderer.com/blog/my-son-sanctuary-vietnam). Group day-tours from Da Nang or Hoi An run ~5–6 hours from $35 with guide and Cham dance [[25]](https://vietnammarveltravel.com/my-son-sanctuary-tour/).
- **Hai Van Pass:** Top Gear-famous 21 km coastal road [[26]](https://www.easyridersvietnam.org/hai-van-pass-easy-rider/). ⚠ Self-riding is legally fraught — only 1968 Vienna Convention IDPs are valid; US/UK/Canada/Australia IDPs are not recognised and 2025 brought visibly more police checkpoints with ~5M VND fines plus 7–15 day bike impoundment [[60]](https://rentabikevn.com/vietnam-motorbike-license-for-foreigners/)[[61]](https://theextramile.co/vietnam-driving-licenses-changes-from-01-01-2025/). Book an Easy Rider pillion (~€50–52 one-day Hoi An ↔ Hue, US$60–70 round-trip from Da Nang) [[26]](https://www.easyridersvietnam.org/hai-van-pass-easy-rider/)[[59]](https://www.getyourguide.com/da-nang-l939/hai-van-pass-motorbike-tour-easyrider-t738785/).
- **Hue** is the marginal call: 2.5+ hr each way over the pass, 1.5–2.5 hr through the 6.28 km Hai Van Tunnel (cars only, no bikes) [[27]](https://vietnamluxuryexpress.com/hue-to-da-nang/). Either sleep over or skip; the drive eats the day [[28]](https://www.getyourguide.com/da-nang-l939/da-nang-imperial-hue-and-scenic-hai-van-pass-discovery-tour-t287130/).

The Cham Sculpture Museum is the city's one essential indoor stop — it holds several Vietnamese National Treasures and is the foremost institution anywhere for understanding the Champa civilisation that ruled central Vietnam until the 15th century [[6]](https://www.novotel-danang-premier.com/destination/cham-sculpture-museum/). Pair it with My Son the same trip if the iconography interests you. The **Sun Wheel and Sun World Asia Park** were Da Nang fixtures until 3 September 2025, when they ceased operations permanently — older guidebooks and many vendor blogs still list them [[9]](https://news.tuoitre.vn/da-nang-downtown-theme-park-to-cease-operations-next-month-103250806105921039.htm)[[10]](https://hoiandaytrip.com/sun-wheel-da-nang/).

**Son Tra access (2025–2026 rules):** three loop routes — Tien Sa–Suoi Om–Ban Co, Ban Co–Bai Bac, Bai Bac–Heritage Banyan — reopened on 16 September 2025 after a three-year closure from landslide damage [[49]](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/explore-da-nangs-son-tra-peninsula-tourism-routes-reopened-with-focus-on-sustainability/). Gates: 07:30–18:30 Mar–Sep, 07:30–17:30 Oct–Feb, day-valid green card issued at the checkpoint [[50]](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/travel/places/da-nang-reopens-son-tra-peninsula-tourism-routes-after-3-year-halt-4939967.html). ⚠ **Automatic scooters are banned beyond the checkpoint** — manual bike, jeep, taxi or tour vehicle only [[51]](https://venusvietnamtravel.com/son-tra-peninsula-da-nang-vietnam-guide/).

**Wartime context for My Khe:** the long sand spit is the original "China Beach," which the US Chief of Staff designated an R&R centre for the ~75,000 GIs stationed at the Da Nang base [[45]](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/why-da-nang-beach-is-called-china-beach-E25169.html). The 1988–91 ABC drama *China Beach* set its evacuation-hospital scenes here, cementing the nickname in Western pop culture [[46]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Beach).

### [La Maison 1888](https://www.danang.intercontinental.com/dining/la-maison-1888/) — the high-end anchor

The only Michelin-starred restaurant in Central Vietnam, retained in the **2025 Michelin Guide Vietnam** (one of only nine one-star restaurants nationwide) [[31]](https://www.danang.intercontinental.com/la-maison-1888-retains-prestigious-michelin-star-reaffirming-its-position-at-the-pinnacle-of-fine-dining-in-vietnam/). The kitchen is led under the patronage of Michelin Chef **Christian Le Squer**, with an ever-evolving seasonal tasting menu including his signature **Spaghetti Debout** — meticulously assembled upright pasta with black truffle, ham and a tableside chanterelle cream [[35]](https://guide.michelin.com/vn/en/article/dining-out/the-inspectors-reveal-all-la-maison-1888-da-nang-s-first-michelin-star) — and dishes like **Croquant de Pamplemousse** [[34]](https://www.danang.intercontinental.com/dining/the-la-maison-set-menu/).

The room is the other half of the experience: a recreated French-Indochine colonial mansion designed by **Bill Bensley**, with floor-to-ceiling windows over Bai Bac, themed private dining rooms and a black-and-white yin-yang palette [[33]](https://danangfantasticity.com/en/discovery/la-maison-1888-the-only-michelin-starred-restaurant-in-da-nang).

**Booking logistics:**

- Service 18:30–21:00 daily [[32]](https://www.danang.intercontinental.com/dining/la-maison-1888/).
- Dress code: smart resort wear (no shorts; jacket not required) [[32]](https://www.danang.intercontinental.com/dining/la-maison-1888/).
- A refundable **VND 1,000,000 / guest** courtesy deposit is taken at booking [[32]](https://www.danang.intercontinental.com/dining/la-maison-1888/).
- Tasting-menu price is not published — call or email the restaurant for the current set-menu rate [[32]](https://www.danang.intercontinental.com/dining/la-maison-1888/).
- The restaurant is on the **Son Tra peninsula at the InterContinental Sun Peninsula Resort** — about 25 minutes from central Da Nang; bake transit and the funicular descent into your evening.

## Quick practical bits

- **Han Market** (06:00–19:00, Tran Phu) is fine for cheap clothing, coffee and souvenirs; haggle 20–25% off marked price [[11]](https://www.bestpricetravel.com/travel-guide/han-market.html). TripAdvisor regulars warn it can feel cramped and smelly — go for the shopping, not the atmosphere [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g298085-d5599000-Reviews-Han_Market-Da_Nang.html).
- **Pink Cathedral** (Tran Phu): visit 07:30–08:30 AM before the crowds; closed to sightseers on Sundays for mass. Free — ignore anyone selling tickets [[7]](https://exploreprivatecar.com/da-nang-cathedral-pink-church-travel-guide/)[[8]](https://hoiandaytrip.com/da-nang-cathedral-pink-church/).
- **Pink Church and Cham Museum** are 10 minutes apart on foot — pair them in one morning before lunch.
- **Bai Bac** is essentially closed to non-resort guests; if La Maison 1888 is your anchor, you have access to its cove via the InterContinental funicular [[53]](https://hoiandaytrip.com/bai-bac-beach-da-nang/).
