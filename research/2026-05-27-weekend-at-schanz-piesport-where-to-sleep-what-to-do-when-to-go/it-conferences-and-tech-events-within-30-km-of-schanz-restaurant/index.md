## The 30 km boundary, honestly

Piesport sits in the middle Moselle, between Bernkastel-Kues (17 km downriver [[4]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Piesport/Bernkastel-Kues)) and Wittlich (~15 km north). Both are inside any reasonable 30 km radius — and both are wine, tourism, and SME towns with **zero standing IT meetups or conferences** in 2026 in the public listings (Wirtschaftskreis Bernkastel-Wittlich, city event calendars, Meetup.com) [[15]](https://wirtschaftskreis.de/events) [[19]](https://www.meetup.com/cities/de/trier/).

Trier is the regional tech hub. The university's **Campus I in Tarforst** (where the major conferences happen) is on the southeast edge of the city, the closest part to Piesport; by road it's 34 km via the A1/A48 [[3]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Piesport/Trier). Wittlich-to-Trier is ~37 km [[5]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Wittlich/Trier). So Trier sits *just outside* the strict 30 km road threshold — included here because it is the only real answer to the question.

Luxembourg City (~80 km, [Nexus Luxembourg](https://nexusluxembourg.com/) AI summit, 10–11 June 2026) and Saarbrücken (~90 km) are excluded: well outside scope.

## The big one: Uni Trier's last-week-of-July CS cluster

If you want a serious IT conference in driving range of Schanz in 2026, **27–31 July is it.** Four events run together at [Universität Trier](https://www.uni-trier.de/) Campus I in Tarforst, all chaired or co-chaired by Henning Fernau (Trier) [[6]](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-cie-2026):

- **[CiE 2026](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-cie-2026)** — 21st *Computability in Europe*, theme *"Timeless Machines / Computability Across Eras."* Bridges informatics + mathematics: computability theory, complexity, formal languages, quantum computation, algorithmic learning, natural computing. Independently listed by [ILLC Amsterdam](https://www.illc.uva.nl/NewsandEvents/Events/Conferences/newsitem/15991/27---31-July-2026-Computability-in-Europe-2026-CiE-2026-Trier-D-) [[18]](https://www.illc.uva.nl/NewsandEvents/Events/Conferences/newsitem/15991/27---31-July-2026-Computability-in-Europe-2026-CiE-2026-Trier-D-). **Registration:** €200 full / €150 student until 30 June; €300 / €200 after [[6]](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-cie-2026). Includes the *Women in Computability* meeting.
- **[MCU 2026](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-mcu-2026)** — 11th *Machines, Computations and Universality*, 27–29 Jul. Turing-equivalent formalisms: cellular automata, analog computation, DNA/membrane/quantum computing. Invited: Martin Kutrib (Gießen), Olivier Bournez (Paris) [[7]](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-mcu-2026).
- **[CCA 2026](https://cca-net.de/cca2026/)** — 23rd *Computability and Complexity in Analysis*, 29–31 Jul, satellite of CiE. Computability over the reals; physics-adjacent computational questions [[8]](https://cca-net.de/cca2026/).
- **[GSW 2026](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-grammar-systems-2026)** — *Grammar Systems Workshop*, co-located. Multi-component cooperating grammars/automata for multi-agent language generation [[9]](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-grammar-systems-2026).

Note: this cluster is academic theoretical computer science, not industry/SaaS. If you want practitioner content, the cluster is the wrong fit.

→ **Practical pairing with Schanz:** CiE runs Mon–Fri. Book Schanz for **Saturday 25 July** (the day before the conference) or **Saturday 1 August** (the day after). The university opens Sunday for arrivals; Piesport is a 35-minute drive from Tarforst.

## Second pick: IRDT Conference, 10–11 September

[Institut für Recht und Digitalisierung Trier](https://irdt.uni-trier.de/) holds its annual conference **10–11 September 2026** at Uni Trier Campus II, Behringstraße 21 [[10]](https://irdt.uni-trier.de/save-the-date-irdtconference-am-10-11-09-2026/). Theme: *Politicians as Targets — Harmful Online Speech and its Impact upon Democratic Discourse*. This is digital law / online-speech regulation, not pure IT — but it's the most relevant non-academic-CS conference in the window. Registration opened 15 April 2026. Pair with Schanz on **Saturday 12 September**.
