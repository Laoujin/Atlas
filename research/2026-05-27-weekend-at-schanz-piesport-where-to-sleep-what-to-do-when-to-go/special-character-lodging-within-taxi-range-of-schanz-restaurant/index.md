> **TL;DR.** Book [**schanz. hotel.**](https://www.schanz-restaurant.de/en/) — it's the same building as the kitchen [[5]](https://en.bernkastel.de/poi/restaurant-schanz/poi.html), so the taxi problem disappears. If those twelve gourmet-themed rooms are full [[3]](https://www.schanz-restaurant.de/en/hotel/about-the-hotel), the next two character picks are [**Wein- und Tafelhaus**](https://en.visitmosel.de/wine-cuisine/a-culinary-voyage-of-discovery/restaurant-tips/wein-und-tafelhaus) in Trittenheim (7 rooms, fellow Michelin kitchen, ~8 km) [[10]](https://en.visitmosel.de/wine-cuisine/a-culinary-voyage-of-discovery/restaurant-tips/wein-und-tafelhaus) and [**Weinromantikhotel Richtershof**](https://www.weinromantikhotel.com/en/) in Mülheim (300-year winery, 16th-c. cellar, ~11 km) [[13]](https://www.weinromantikhotel.com/en/hotel). For a once-in-a-trip splurge, the riverbank castle [**Schloss Lieser**](https://www.schlosslieser.de/en/) is ~14 km [[17]](https://www.schlosslieser.de/en/).

## Why "stay on site" is the default answer

schanz. restaurant. holds three Michelin stars at Bahnhofstraße 8a, Piesport [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/rheinland-pfalz/piesport/restaurant/schanz-restaurant)[[2]](https://www.schanz-restaurant.de/en/). The hotel sits in the same family-run building and was deliberately bolted on to the kitchen — Thomas Schanz opened the restaurant in 2011 next to a hotel run by his parents [[6]](https://www.travelsforstars.com/blog/2022/12/04/schanz-piesport), and the official venue page literally pitches "leave your car after a hearty menu and wine enjoyment" [[5]](https://en.bernkastel.de/poi/restaurant-schanz/poi.html).

Rural-Mosel taxi supply after 22:00 is thin, especially Sundays. Taxi Reitz in Bernkastel-Kues (the most visible operator, 06531-6455) lists office hours Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00 and only takes off-hours rides by direct phone arrangement [[20]](https://www.taxi-reitz.de/bernkastel-kues.html). The Piesport-local backup is Fahrdienst Später (06507 802313); Taxi Keusch in Neumagen-Dhron (06507 2493) is the other nearby option [[21]](https://www.kesten-mosel.de/urlaub/verkehrsmittel/taxi/). All three want pre-booking for evening returns — do not assume a curbside hail will work.

## Notes per option

### [schanz. hotel.](https://www.schanz-restaurant.de/en/) — Piesport, 0 km
Twelve rooms designed by tibo architects around five gourmet-product concepts — colour-coded, walk-in showers, sun terrace with illuminated water basin [[3]](https://www.schanz-restaurant.de/en/hotel/about-the-hotel). Doubles €186–198 with balcony, single-use €157–166, breakfast included [[4]](https://www.schanz-restaurant.de/en/hotel/pricing). The pick if it's available — book ahead for Saturdays.

### [Weinhaus Piesporter Goldtröpfchen](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g319769-d314005-Reviews-Weinhaus_Piesporter_Goldtropfchen-Piesport_Rhineland_Palatinate.html) — Piesport, ~1 km
The Hain family's wine hotel in the village centre, named after the famous vineyard. Wood panelling, calm, panoramic Mosel views; reviewers call it "traditional and contemporary" with attentive front-of-house from Frau Hain herself [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g319769-d314005-Reviews-Weinhaus_Piesporter_Goldtropfchen-Piesport_Rhineland_Palatinate.html). Walkable from the restaurant in dry weather — no taxi needed.

### Other Piesport vintner stays — ~1–2 km
The village's bookable inventory is otherwise working wineries: [Ferienweingut Birkenhof](https://en.bernkastel.de/bernkastel-kues-holiday-region/piesport), Lehnert-Veit, Breit, Gästehaus St. Eberhard, plus the riverside Hotel-Restaurant Moselblick just outside town [[8]](https://en.bernkastel.de/bernkastel-kues-holiday-region/piesport). Character is "stay in the winemaker's house"; expect simpler furnishings than the hotels above. Taxi from these to Schanz is a sub-5-minute hop — still pre-book.

### [Weingut Koch](https://en.visitmosel.de/hosts-packages/hotel/moselurlaub-im-ferienweinhaus-koch) — Neumagen-Dhron, ~5 km
Traditional Mosel winemaker's house in Dhron, vineyard- and valley-facing apartments with full kitchens, on-site tennis and garden [[9]](https://en.visitmosel.de/hosts-packages/hotel/moselurlaub-im-ferienweinhaus-koch). Neumagen-Dhron is Germany's oldest wine town (Roman wine-ship monument); the village itself is a sight. ⚠ Local taxi (Keusch, 06507 2493) — pre-book return.

### [Wein- und Tafelhaus](https://en.visitmosel.de/wine-cuisine/a-culinary-voyage-of-discovery/restaurant-tips/wein-und-tafelhaus) — Trittenheim, ~8 km
Seven rooms, all facing the Mosel and the Grand Cru Trittenheimer Apotheke vineyard; furniture custom-built by a local carpenter [[10]](https://en.visitmosel.de/wine-cuisine/a-culinary-voyage-of-discovery/restaurant-tips/wein-und-tafelhaus). Chef Alexander Oos runs a Michelin-starred kitchen of his own [[11]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/rheinland-pfalz/trittenheim/restaurant/wein-und-tafelhaus), so you can do back-to-back fine-dining nights without changing hotels. The strongest "character" pick if Schanz's hotel is full.

### [Moselhaus by Clüsserath-Wittmann](https://moselhaus-trittenheim.de/en/) — Trittenheim, ~8 km
A 2023-build of three modern vintner-owned apartments (87–102 m²): Steinreich €160–210, Apotheke €150–190, Lagenblick €140–170, all with full kitchens, EV charging, vineyard views [[12]](https://moselhaus-trittenheim.de/en/). Best fit for a couple-plus-friends or a family — less hotel-y, more "borrow a winemaker's house." The Wittmann wine list is the bonus.

### [Weinromantikhotel Richtershof](https://www.weinromantikhotel.com/en/) — Mülheim/Mosel, ~11 km
Four-star superior Romantik Hotel inside a 300-year-old winery ensemble with a 16th-century vaulted cellar; rooms are named after vineyards, layered with Baroque, Wilhelminian and Art Nouveau detailing [[13]](https://www.weinromantikhotel.com/en/hotel). Inventory: 2 singles, 36 doubles, 5 suites [[14]](https://www.romantikhotels.com/en/hotels/weinromantikhotel-richtershof/). Best mid-luxury option that still feels rooted in Mosel wine history.

### [Schloss Lieser, Autograph Collection](https://www.schlosslieser.de/en/) — Lieser, ~14 km
Fully restored 19th-century castle on the riverbank, library, spa, in-house tasting room and vineyard, and the Puricelli fine-dining restaurant [[17]](https://www.schlosslieser.de/en/). Marriott-flagged (Autograph), so points/elite-status apply [[18]](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/hhnak-schloss-lieser-autograph-collection/overview/). The "trip-defining hotel" option — but you commit to a ~€40–55 each-way taxi on dinner night and must lock the return car in advance.

### [Hotel-Restaurant Doctor Weinstube](https://www.doctor-weinstube-bernkastel.de/) — Bernkastel-Kues, ~17 km
1668 tithe house in Bernkastel's old town, expanded into a hotel in 1974 and fully renovated in 2013; 25 doubles + 3 singles, rooms mix rustic timber with modern fittings, idyllic courtyard [[15]](https://www.doctor-weinstube-bernkastel.de/)[[16]](https://en.bernkastel.de/accommodation/hotel-restaurant-doctor-weinstube/unterkunft.html). Pick this if you also want a Saturday-morning Bernkastel-Kues walk before driving back to Piesport for dinner — and use the same logic on taxi pre-booking.

## Practical taxi playbook

- **Pre-book the return** when you book the table. State pick-up at "Restaurant Schanz, Bahnhofstraße 8a, Piesport" and the time you expect to leave (a Michelin tasting menu runs 3–4 h).
- **Numbers worth having in your phone**: Taxi Reitz Bernkastel 06531-6455 [[20]](https://www.taxi-reitz.de/bernkastel-kues.html), Fahrdienst Später Piesport 06507 802313, Taxi Keusch Neumagen-Dhron 06507 2493 [[21]](https://www.kesten-mosel.de/urlaub/verkehrsmittel/taxi/).
- **Sundays and after 22:00**: assume one operator, not three. The argument for staying at schanz. hotel. is exactly this — a 30-metre walk home.
- **No Uber/Bolt** in the Mosel villages. Don't plan around them.
