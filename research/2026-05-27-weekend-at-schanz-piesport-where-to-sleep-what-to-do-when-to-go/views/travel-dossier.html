§ Why the default holds — the taxi problem.

Rural-Mosel taxi supply after 22:00 is thin. The three visible operators all want pre-booking, and the villages have no Uber, no Bolt. Sleeping above the kitchen makes the entire question vanish — which is why both lodging sub-files reach the same recommendation from opposite directions.

Folio V · The late-July anchor
When the dates flex · § 4
iv. when

The dates, if they flex.

If "a Saturday" is open, lock onto 25 July or 1 August 2026. The week between hosts the co-located CiE / MCU / CCA / GSW theoretical-CS cluster at Uni Trier — the biggest IT-adjacent event in the region all year.

One justification for two flights.

Inside a strict 30 km radius the answer to "is there a conference?" is zero events. Trier sits at 34 km road, just over the line — and is the only honest answer. The week of 27–31 July 2026 stacks four co-located events on Uni Trier's Campus I, with Saturdays at either bookend free for the dinner.

If late July doesn't suit, the next pick is the IRDT Conference on harmful online speech on 10–11 September 2026 at Campus II, pairing with Saturday 12 September [20]. No standing tech meetups in Wittlich, Bernkastel-Kues or smaller towns [19] — the late-July week is genuinely the only show.

Folio VI · Mechanics not in the file
Memo · § 5

Memorandum — what to verify by phone.

The brief asked for the dinner mechanics — tasting-menu price, wine-pairing cost, dress code, reservation lead time, whether the table books with a room. What was actually verified is below; the rest is one call. The restaurant and hotel share a number, which is the simplest way to lock both at once.

  • Tasting-menu price. Not verified in the research. Ask on booking.
  • Wine-pairing cost. Not verified. Ask whether à-la-carte glasses are an option.
  • Dress code. Not stated by Schanz or the Michelin listing. Smart-casual at minimum given three stars.
  • Reservation lead time. Not verified. Phone the moment dates harden.
  • Room + table together. Probably yes — the operation runs as one — but confirm on the same call.
