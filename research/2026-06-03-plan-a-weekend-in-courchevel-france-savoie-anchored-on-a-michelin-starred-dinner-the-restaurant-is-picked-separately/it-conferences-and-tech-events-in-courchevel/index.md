> **TL;DR** No public IT or tech conference is based in Courchevel. The nearest equivalent is **[Les Sommets](https://www.les-sommets.fr/)** in Méribel — same Les 3 Vallées ski valley, ~30 min by road, ski-lift accessible — running **March 29–31, 2026** with AI and digital speakers [[2]](https://www.les-sommets.fr/). For private corporate tech retreats, Courchevel has capable infrastructure for up to 400 attendees [[1]](https://courchevel.com/en/espaces-groupes-et-seminaires/).

## The situation

Courchevel's official 2025–2026 winter events calendar runs ski competitions, arts festivals, and pyrotechnic events — nothing tech-facing [[9]](https://courchevel.com/en/les-evenements-de-l-hiver-a-courchevel/). Searches across French and English sources confirm no recurring public IT, tech, or digital conference is established here.

This is not unusual. Davos is the exception for alpine business summits. Most luxury ski resorts function as venues for private corporate retreats rather than public conference brands.

## Les Sommets: the only recurring business/tech event in Les 3 Vallées

**[Les Sommets](https://www.les-sommets.fr/)** — formerly **Les Sommets du Digital** — is a French business conference now based at **Méribel**, the adjacent resort in the Les 3 Vallées ski domain (connected to Courchevel by ski lifts) [[3]](https://www.meribel.net/les-sommets-repensent-entreprise-de-demain-a-meribel/).

### Event history

| Edition | Year      | Location  | Name                    |
|---------|-----------|-----------|-------------------------|
| 1st–5th | 2016–2020 | La Clusaz | Les Sommets du Digital  |
| 6th     | 2022      | Annecy    | Les Sommets             |
| 7th–10th| 2023–2026 | Méribel   | Les Sommets             |

[Source: [[4]](https://www.frenchweb.fr/comment-les-sommets-du-digital-se-reinventent-a-annecy/431398)]

The event launched as a pure digital-transformation conference covering big data, blockchain, HR tech, and e-commerce [[5]](https://www.presse-citron.net/les-sommets-du-digital-la-conference-qui-prend-de-la-hauteur/). The 2018 edition (3rd, La Clusaz) drew 330+ participants and 50+ speakers and pioneered **networking in privatised cable cars** — a signature format retained today [[5]](https://www.presse-citron.net/les-sommets-du-digital-la-conference-qui-prend-de-la-hauteur/). After five La Clusaz editions it rebranded, dropped "du Digital", expanded scope to entrepreneurship, ecology, and sport, and moved first to Annecy then to Méribel [[4]](https://www.frenchweb.fr/comment-les-sommets-du-digital-se-reinventent-a-annecy/431398).

### 2026 edition (10th)

- **Dates:** March 29–31, 2026 — Méribel [[2]](https://www.les-sommets.fr/)
- **Format:** Keynotes, workshops, outdoor activities, cable-car networking sessions
- **Audience:** ~350 executives and business leaders
- **From Courchevel 1850:** ~30 min by car (D98); direct ski access via Les 3 Vallées lifts [[3]](https://www.meribel.net/les-sommets-repensent-entreprise-de-demain-a-meribel/)

## Courchevel as a venue for private corporate tech events

No public tech conference claims Courchevel, but the infrastructure handles **private off-sites and corporate seminars** — the format many tech companies actually prefer for leadership retreats and team off-sites.

| Venue                              | Max capacity | Meeting rooms | Stars |
|------------------------------------|-------------|---------------|-------|
| Convention Center (Courchevel 1850)| 360 (auditorium) | + exhibition [[1]](https://courchevel.com/en/espaces-groupes-et-seminaires/) | — |
| Hôtel Koh-I Nor                    | 400         | 3 [[8]](https://www.seminairesbusiness.com/en/seminar-courchevel) | ★★★★★ |
| Écrin Blanc                        | 200         | 9 [[8]](https://www.seminairesbusiness.com/en/seminar-courchevel) | ★★★★  |
| Hôtel Carlina                      | 140         | – [[8]](https://www.seminairesbusiness.com/en/seminar-courchevel) | ★★★★★ |
| Alpes Hôtel du Pralong             | 70          | 2 [[7]](https://www.aleou.fr/ville/lieux-congres-seminaires/courchevel) | ★★★★★ |

All venues offer AV equipment and mountain team-building add-ons (skiing, snowshoeing, spa). For ultra-luxury retreats (product launches, board off-sites), L'Apogée Courchevel can be reserved exclusively [[1]](https://courchevel.com/en/espaces-groupes-et-seminaires/).

## Reference point: SKInnovation

The closest global model for a "ski-slope tech conference" is **[SKInnovation](https://skinnovation.at/)** in Innsbruck, Austria — a 2.5-day startup conference with ski-lift pitches, hut talks, and 1,000+ attendees from the DACH ecosystem [[6]](https://skinnovation.at/). No equivalent exists in the French Alps or in Courchevel specifically. The concept proves the format works; it simply hasn't been launched in Les 3 Vallées.

## Bottom line

| Goal | Option |

| Host a private corporate tech off-site in Courchevel | Convention Center or Koh-I Nor (up to 400) [[1]](https://courchevel.com/en/espaces-groupes-et-seminaires/)[[8]](https://www.seminairesbusiness.com/en/seminar-courchevel) |
| Find a ski + startup pitch format | SKInnovation, Innsbruck (not France) [[6]](https://skinnovation.at/) |

If basing yourself in Courchevel 1850 to attend **Les Sommets 2026**, the ski-lift connection means no rental car is strictly needed during the event — a reasonable justification for choosing Courchevel over Méribel as a base.
