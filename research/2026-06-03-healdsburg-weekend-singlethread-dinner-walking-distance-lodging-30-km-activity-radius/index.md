The entire weekend resolves to a single critical path: **secure the SingleThread table first, then build everything else around it.** The restaurant releases reservations at [9am PST on the 1st of each month for the following month](https://singlethreadfarms.com/bookings/) — prepaid, non-refundable, via OpenTable. [[1]](https://singlethreadfarms.com/bookings/) The [~$485pp Kaiseki tasting menu](https://singlethreadfarms.com/bookings/) (wine pairings $300/$500/$1500 extra) runs a minimum 2.5 hours within a roughly 4:00–8:45 pm Saturday window. [[2]](https://www.yelp.com/biz/singlethread-restaurant-and-inn-healdsburg) With four courses of logistics depending on having that table, don't plan anything else until the OpenTable confirmation email arrives.

**The one-building solve.** The five-room [SingleThread Inn](https://singlethreadfarms.com/inn/) (131 North St, directly above the kitchen) is the only accommodation that *guarantees* a dinner table — the room and the reservation are bundled. [[3]](https://singlethreadfarms.com/inn/) It books on a separate 3-month window, not the OpenTable rush, at roughly $2,000/night for a master suite. [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g32482-d11904247-Reviews-SingleThread_Farm_Restaurant_Inn-Healdsburg_Sonoma_County_California.html) If the inn is full, the next option on the same block is [Liora Estate](https://www.lioraestatehotel.com/) (27 North St, ~2-min walk, $489–$958), which rebranded from Hotel Les Mars in April 2026 with a "slow luxury" identity — vinyl record turntables per room, a sunset atmosphere ritual, and the new Maréla coastal bistro. [[5]](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/22/3279164/0/en/liora-estate-debuts-in-healdsburg-introducing-a-new-intimate-luxury-retreat-and-coastal-european-bistro.html) For guests who want the Michelin-on-Michelin pairing and are willing to take a 5-minute taxi home, [The Madrona](https://themadronahotel.com/) (1001 Westside Rd, 2 Michelin Keys, from $365) sits 0.7 mi away on an 8-acre 1881 manor estate — the only option in this set that doesn't survive on a walk home. [[6]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/hotels-stays/napa-sonoma-valleys/the-madrona-12498) Best-value walking option: [Camellia Inn](https://camelliainn.com/) (211 North St, same street, from $241–$300), a 9-room 1869 Victorian with a full breakfast and evening wine hour. [[7]](https://camelliainn.com/)

**Saturday time architecture.** Dinner consumes the evening from ~4pm onward; the morning is the primary activity slot. A [self-guided inflatable canoe trip](https://russianriveradventures.com/) from Memorial Beach (1 km from SingleThread, $80/adult, 3.5–6 hours) fits neatly — check in by 10:30am, finish early afternoon, clean up, and walk to dinner. [[8]](https://russianriveradventures.com/) If wine is the morning's purpose, hire a driver ([Private Wine Drivers](https://privatewinedrivers.com/vehicles-and-rates/), $645–$845/group for 6 hours) so no one is impaired for the pairings. [[9]](https://privatewinedrivers.com/vehicles-and-rates/) One timing watch: the [Memorial Beach swimming lagoon](https://parks.sonomacounty.ca.gov/visit/find-a-park/healdsburg-veterans-memorial-beach) doesn't form until the seasonal dam goes in (completed by July 3, 2026) — an early-June visit is prime for paddling but the famous swim hole isn't yet formed. [[10]](https://parks.sonomacounty.ca.gov/visit/find-a-park/healdsburg-veterans-memorial-beach)

**Villages within 30 km.** Both lodging sub-topics confirmed that all compelling accommodation clusters in downtown Healdsburg — no satellite village emerged as a credible lodging node. The activities research surfaced two village candidates worth a day-trip: **Geyserville** (8 mi/~10 min north), with Locals Tasting Room and [Diavola](https://www.sonoma.com/blog/geyserville-restaurants/) pizzeria, is the closest genuine alternative town and works as a lunch stop before returning for dinner. [[11]](https://www.sonoma.com/blog/town-spotlight-geyserville/) **Guerneville** (18 mi/~28 min) is the gateway to [Armstrong Redwoods State Natural Reserve](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=450) — old-growth coast redwoods with 9.2 miles of trails, $10/vehicle, cooler than the valley floor on a warm June day. [[12]](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=450)

**Tech events: confirmed null.** The IT conference sub-topic found no dedicated technology conferences within 30 km; Healdsburg is wine country with no tech campus or convention facility. The only event with any tech dimension is [WIN Expo](https://www.wineindustryexpo.com/) (AgTech/wine-industry trade show, Santa Rosa Fairgrounds, ~23 km, December 3, 2026) — viable for a winter trip that pairs the conference with a Saturday SingleThread dinner the same weekend. [[13]](https://www.wineindustryexpo.com/) For any other tech conference pairing, the better logic is to identify the San Francisco conference first and add Healdsburg as a one-night extension.

The sharpest open question this expedition leaves unresolved: **is the ~$1,500 premium for SingleThread Inn over Liora Estate worth paying purely to bypass the 9am OpenTable race?** If the Kaiseki tasting menu is the purpose of the trip, the answer may be yes — but only if the inn has availability. If it doesn't, the operational answer is to set a calendar alarm for 8:59am PST on the 1st of the target month, and stay at Liora Estate or Camellia Inn.
