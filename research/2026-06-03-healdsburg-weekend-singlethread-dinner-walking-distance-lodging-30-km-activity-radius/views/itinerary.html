Tables release at 9am PST on the 1st of each month for the following month — prepaid, non-refundable via OpenTable. Single nightly service window, roughly 4:00–8:45 pm Saturday. Morning — choose one activity
Option A — River: Check in by 10:30am at Russian River Adventures (1 km from restaurant, $80/adult). 9-mile self-guided canoe, 3.5–6 hrs. Finish by early afternoon, clean up.

Option B — Wine valley: Hire Private Wine Drivers ($645–$845/group, 6 hrs). Dry Creek or Alexander Valley. Return by 2pm.
