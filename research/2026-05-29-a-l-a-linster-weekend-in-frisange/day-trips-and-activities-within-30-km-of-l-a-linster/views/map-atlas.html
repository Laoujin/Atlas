— NAVIGATOR'S NOTICE —

Saturday afternoon · steer NNW to the capital, 15 km · the Bock Casemates and the Chemin de la Corniche before the table is laid[1].  Sunday morning · MONDORF Domaine Thermal at 10 km for the slow recovery[54], or the southern Moselle for wine villages between Schengen and Remich[89].  Families · pivot Sunday to Parc Merveilleux Bettembourg[55].  The Mullerthal trailheads and Echternach are off the chart at 45.7 km[64]. Stop trying to bundle them.

Frisange sits at the southern tip of the Grand-Duchy, four kilometres from the French line and fourteen south of the capital. The chart below carries every site within a thirty-kilometre belt — and a few, marked ⚠, that lie just over the edge.

PLATE IV

Beyond the Chart Line

Three sirens you might be tempted to chase. The chart says don't — at least, not this weekend.

Mullerthal · Echternach
Luxembourg's "Little Switzerland" — 112 km trail around Echternach. 45.7 km by road from Frisange. Treat as a separate day-trip[64] [78].
Hackenberg Maginot fort
The largest publicly accessible Maginot fort — 10 km of galleries, 19 combat blocks, 2.5–3 h tours at 12°C. Around 40+ km via Thionville. Bundle with a Thionville day, not the Linster weekend[85] [86].
Villa Borg DE · ~32 km
Only fully restored Roman villa of its kind, working baths, gardens, Roman tavern. Two kilometres over the line — close enough to bundle with Villa Nennig and Schengen as a Saarland half-day[93].

PLATE VI

The Two-Day Voyages

A pragmatic shape if the Saturday dinner is fixed at the Linster table.

The Capital · Saturday

FIXED  ·  before the table is laid
  1. Park-and-ride at Kockelscheuer; bus 18, free.
  2. Walk the Chemin de la Corniche.
  3. Bock Casemates.
  4. Place d'Armes → Notre-Dame → Pétrusse Casemates.
  5. Light lunch in Grund. ~5 km walking.
  6. Return to the hotel for a rest before dinner.

Spa Recovery · Sunday

CHOICE A  ·  slow Sunday
  1. MONDORF 3-hour pass, €43.50.
  2. Mondorf – Ellange easy loop (7.4 km) or the longer 10 km Gander loop.
  3. Rodemack ramparts, eight kilometres south.
  4. Optional dinner at Casino 2000 (adults only).

Wine on the Moselle · Sunday

CHOICE B  ·  drive the river
  1. Schengen European Museum + tri-point.
  2. Remich — Caves St Martin (reserve 48 h ahead) or Caves de Wellenstein.
  3. Optional: MS Princesse Marie-Astrid Sunday Lunch cruise.
  4. Cycle the flat Remich ↔ Schengen 20 km return on PC3.

Industrial Heritage · Sunday

CHOICE C  ·  the Terres rouges
  1. Belval blast furnaces + Massenoire.
  2. Lunch in Esch — Musée de la Résistance next.
  3. Rumelange mine 14:30 / 15:30 / 16:30.
  4. Optional: walk an Ellergronn loop.

Family Sunday · with kids

CHOICE D  ·  small legs
  1. Parc Merveilleux Bettembourg, gates 09:30.
  2. Parc Le'h Adventures in Dudelange.
  3. Quick spin to ACL Karting Mondercange — two-seaters from age 5.

Roman Saarland · Sunday

CHOICE E  ·  over the eastern line
  1. Villa Nennig mosaic (closed Mon, Dec–Feb).
  2. (Bend the rule) Villa Borg — ~32 km, just over.
  3. End at Schengen for the museum and a Crémant before the drive home.
