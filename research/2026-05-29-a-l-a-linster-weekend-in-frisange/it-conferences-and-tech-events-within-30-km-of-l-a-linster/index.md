> **Decision.** Léa Linster sits at 17 route de Luxembourg in Frisange [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/luxembourg/frisange/restaurant/lea-linster) — ~14 km from the Kirchberg conference belt [[2]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/luxembourg-l_1111-luxembourg-_-luxembourg-to-frisange-l_4661-esch_sur_alzette-_-luxembourg). The June ICT Spring and June Nexus weekends are the cleanest pairing with a Saturday dinner at Léa Linster.

### Which one to pair with the dinner?

- **Nexus, Jun 10–11 (Wed/Thu)** — biggest, flashiest, 4-track "AI/fintech/startup/national" mash-up; finishes Thursday, so a Saturday Léa Linster dinner is a relaxed cap to the week [[3]](https://nexusluxembourg.com/).
- **ICT Spring, Jun 29–30 (Mon/Tue)** — same LuxExpo venue, more enterprise-IT and fintech flavor, ~5,000 pros; the *prior* weekend (Jun 27–28) is the natural Léa Linster slot [[6]](https://startupsmagazine.co.uk/index.php/article-international-tech-conference-ict-spring-back-luxembourg-next-june-29-30).
- **hack.lu, Oct 20–23 (Tue–Fri)** — small, technical, infosec-deep; Dommeldange is the same Luxembourg-City cluster, ~18 km north. Friday wraps in time for a Saturday dinner [[9]](https://2026.hack.lu/blog/hack.lu-2026-call-for-papers/) [[10]](https://hack.lu/info/).
- **OSC Luxembourg, Oct 7 (Wed)** — one day in Belval, theme "Leading by example in Digital Sovereignty"; pair with a Saturday Oct 10 dinner [[7]](https://conference.opensource.lu/).
- **Luxembourg Internet Days, Nov 17–18 (Tue/Wed)** — Kirchberg, networking-heavy, 1,300+ ICT execs across LUNOG, healthcare IT and municipal-IT tracks [[12]](https://www.cc.lu/en/agenda/detail/luxembourg-internet-days-2026).

## Out of range (>30 km from Frisange)

Worth knowing what you're **not** getting:

- **Trier (DE)** — ~50 km east. No tier-1 IT conference inside 2026, but the Hochschule Trier hosts smaller research events.
- **Metz / Saarbrücken** — both >45 km; French-Tech-Est events happen in Thionville (~17 km, in range) but no flagship 2026 IT conference confirmed there.
- **Brussels (BE)** — FOSDEM 2026 (Jan 31 – Feb 1) is the European open-source flagship, but at ~190 km it's a trip of its own.

## How the 30 km circle maps

Frisange anchors the south-central corner of Luxembourg. The circle covers Luxembourg City and its suburbs (Kirchberg, Dommeldange, Limpertsberg), the entire southern industrial belt (Esch-sur-Alzette, Belval, Differdange, Dudelange) and reaches into French Moselle as far as Thionville [[2]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/luxembourg-l_1111-luxembourg-_-luxembourg-to-frisange-l_4661-esch_sur_alzette-_-luxembourg). It does **not** reach Trier, Saarbrücken, Metz or Arlon. Public transport across Luxembourg is free, so any Kirchberg/Belval venue is a ~20–30 min commute from Frisange by tram + bus [[10]](https://hack.lu/info/).
