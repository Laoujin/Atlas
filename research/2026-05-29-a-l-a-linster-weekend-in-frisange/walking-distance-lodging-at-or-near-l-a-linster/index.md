> **TL;DR** — There is one true walking-distance hotel in Frisange: [Hôtel de la Frontière](https://hostellerie.lu/en/hotel/hotel-restaurant-de-la-frontiere/), ~1.1 km / 13–15 min walk along the N3 from the restaurant [[1]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g206524-d4433335-Reviews-Hotel_de_la_Frontiere-Frisange.html)[[2]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g206524-d4433335-Hotel_de_la_Frontiere-Frisange.html). Book it **by phone** — its Booking.com listing is dormant [[8]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/lu/de-la-frontiere.html). Otherwise: a handful of Airbnbs in the village, or accept a 6–10 km bus/taxi to Mondorf-les-Bains [[3]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mondorf-les-Bains/Frisange).

## The geography

[Léa Linster](https://louis-linster.lu/en/) (now run by Louis Linster, 2 MICHELIN stars in 2025) sits at **17, route de Luxembourg, L-5752 Frisange**, on the N3 main road [[4]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/luxembourg/frisange/restaurant/lea-linster)[[5]](https://louis-linster.lu/fr/info.html). Frisange commune has three localities — Frisange, Aspelt, Hellange — but Aspelt and Hellange are 2.5–3 km away and not walkable for a return trip after a 7 PM tasting menu that runs to midnight [[6]](https://www.syvicol.lu/fr/annuaires/annuaire-des-communes/fichecommune/frisange)[[5]](https://louis-linster.lu/fr/info.html). The restaurant is at the **north end** of Frisange village; the only hotel sits at the **south end** by the French border, both on the same N3 axis [[1]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g206524-d4433335-Reviews-Hotel_de_la_Frontiere-Frisange.html)[[4]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/luxembourg/frisange/restaurant/lea-linster). The nearest hotel cluster — Mondorf-les-Bains — is ~8 km east [[3]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mondorf-les-Bains/Frisange).

The full hotel inventory inside Frisange = **one property** [[2]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g206524-d4433335-Hotel_de_la_Frontiere-Frisange.html). Everything else listed as "Frisange lodging" is a short-term rental.

**Note on Hôtel de la Frontière.** Reviews are consistent: clean, recently renovated, peaceful — but weak Wi-Fi, no on-site dining, no breakfast guaranteed, and the booking funnel is broken in the standard OTAs [[7]](https://hostellerie.lu/en/hotel/hotel-restaurant-de-la-frontiere/)[[8]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/lu/de-la-frontiere.html)[[9]](https://fr.trip.com/hotels/frisange-hotel-detail-10185630/hotel-de-la-frontiere/). Trip.com still shows availability and lists check-in 14:00 / check-out 12:00 [[9]](https://fr.trip.com/hotels/frisange-hotel-detail-10185630/hotel-de-la-frontiere/). The hotel's name is misleading — historically it had a French restaurant [[18]](https://en.resto.lu/restaurant/frisange/5751-frisange/14325-hotel-restaurant-de-la-frontiere/), but that service is gone, so plan dinner elsewhere on nights you aren't at Linster.

**Getting back from dinner.** Luxembourg public transport has been **nationally free since March 2020** [[16]](https://luxembourg.public.lu/en/living/mobility/public-transport.html). Bus 413 runs Mondorf ↔ Frisange in ~12 min every 20 min during the day [[3]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mondorf-les-Bains/Frisange) — but a Linster tasting menu typically ends around 22:30–23:30, after the last bus, so plan a **taxi back (~€35–45)** unless you're driving [[3]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mondorf-les-Bains/Frisange)[[5]](https://louis-linster.lu/fr/info.html).

## What you actually do

1. **First call: Hôtel de la Frontière, +352 23 61 51** (or hostellerie.lu) [[7]](https://hostellerie.lu/en/hotel/hotel-restaurant-de-la-frontiere/). Walkable, in-village, only 18 rooms → book early. Don't bother with Booking.com [[8]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/lu/de-la-frontiere.html).
2. **Backup: Airbnb in Frisange village** — filter by postal code 5751/5752 [[10]](https://www.airbnb.com/frisange-luxembourg/stays). Or the [Border Lodge][bl] across the border in Évrange if you want an apartment-style stay (~1.8 km walk including border crossing, but it's a 4.93★ property) [[11]](https://www.airbnb.com/evrange-france/stays)[[12]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/%C3%89vrange/Frisange).
3. **If both fail: [Mondorf Parc Hotel & Spa][mp]** [[13]](https://www.mondorf.lu/en/stays/mondorf-parc-hotel) + a pre-booked taxi for the return; you'll trade walking distance for a spa morning and a real hotel breakfast.

Luxembourg City (10 km north) is a separate trade-off — more nightlife and hotel choice, but you need a car or taxi back after the last train [[17]](https://www.viamichelin.com/maps/restaurants/poi/frisange-5752-3fnrqmx)[[16]](https://luxembourg.public.lu/en/living/mobility/public-transport.html). Not "walking distance" by any definition.
