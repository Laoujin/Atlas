The dinner is the constraint, and it's a sharp one. Louis Linster's two-star tasting menu at [17 route de Luxembourg, Frisange](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/luxembourg/frisange/restaurant/lea-linster) runs into the night, well past the last bus back to Mondorf [[bus]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mondorf-les-Bains/Frisange). "No driving after dinner" therefore collapses to two real choices: stay in walking distance, or pre-book a Webtaxi (€3 base + €4.30/km, +10 % after 22:00 — a 20 km ride lands near €90) [[taxi]](https://www.webtaxi.lu/en/fares.html).

**Walking distance is monogamous.** Frisange has one hotel: the 18-room [Hôtel de la Frontière](https://hostellerie.lu/en/hotel/hotel-restaurant-de-la-frontiere/), ~1.1 km south of the restaurant on the N3, bookable only by phone (+352 23 61 51) — its Booking.com listing is dormant and its in-house restaurant has closed [[book]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/lu/de-la-frontiere.html). The only other walkable options are a handful of Airbnbs in the village (a renovated 1820 farmhouse, an 18th-c. townhouse) or the [Border Lodge](https://www.airbnb.com/evrange-france/stays) apartment 1.8 km away across the French border in Évrange. That is the entire walkable inventory.

**Character lives in the ring, not the village.** Every property with a hook — thermal pools, a castle, fireplaces, an on-site Michelin star — sits 5–25 km out: [Mondorf Parc Hôtel & Spa](https://www.mondorf.lu/en/stays/mondorf-parc-hotel) (5 km, 36 °C pools since 1847), [Hôtel Château Schengen](https://www.chateauschengen.lu/) (17 km, a 1390 castle where Victor Hugo slept in 1871), [Le Place d'Armes](https://hotel-leplacedarmes.com/en) (21 km, the only Relais & Châteaux in the country, on-site 1-star), and [Victor's Schloss Berg](https://www.victors.de/en/hotels/victor-s-residenz-hotel-schloss-berg) across the Moselle bridge in Nennig (20 km, Christian Bau's three Michelin stars since 2005) [[bau]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1185063-d1751985-Reviews-Victor_s_Residenz_Hotel_Schloss_Berg-Nennig_Saarland.html). The trade-off is direct: walkable means soulless, characterful means a confirmed pickup at 22:30 from Linster's door.

**The Sunday writes itself from where you slept.** Mondorf → a 3-hour thermal pass (€43.50) and a Rodemack medieval-village stroll, 8 km over the French border [[mondorf]](https://www.mondorf.lu/en/gifts/access-rates) [[rodemack]](https://www.les-plus-beaux-villages-de-france.org/fr/nos-villages/rodemack/). Schengen or Remich → the renovated [European Museum](https://www.visitschengen.lu/visit/) (reopened June 2025), Caves St Martin's limestone galleries, and the Princesse Marie-Astrid Sunday-lunch cruise (19 Apr – 20 Sep 2026) [[boat]](https://www.entente-moselle.lu/en/bateau-princesse-marie-astrid/horaires). Luxembourg City → the Bock Casemates and the Corniche walk before the train back [[bock]](https://www.luxembourg-city.com/en/place/tour/bock-casemates). Belval and the Minett Biosphere fill an industrial-heritage day at the [blast furnace platform](https://www.visitluxembourg.com/place/blast-furnace-belval) and the [Rumelange mine](https://mnm.lu/en/). What the circle *doesn't* hold: Mullerthal and Echternach (45.7 km), Trier, Hackenberg — stop trying to bundle them [[echternach]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Frisange/Echternach).

**Add a conference, get a free flight.** Five 2026 dates inside the 30 km belt let the dinner cap a working week: [Nexus](https://nexusluxembourg.com/) (Jun 10–11), [ICT Spring](https://www.ictspring.com/) (Jun 29–30), [OSC Luxembourg](https://conference.opensource.lu/) (Oct 7, Belval), [hack.lu](https://2026.hack.lu/blog/hack.lu-2026-call-for-papers/) (Oct 20–23, Dommeldange), [Luxembourg Internet Days](https://luxembourg-internet-days.com/) (Nov 17–18). The June Nexus week is the cleanest pairing — finishes Thursday, room to recover before Saturday at Linster.

**Booking order.** Call Linster first ([+352 23 66 84 11](https://louis-linster.lu/fr/info.html)) to fix the date, then phone Hôtel de la Frontière (+352 23 61 51) — same evening if possible, because 18 rooms across a Michelin draw clear fast. If walkable is gone, your fallback is *not* to drive: it is the pre-booked Webtaxi back to Mondorf Parc or Le Place d'Armes, paid for in advance. The open question the children leave unresolved: whether the table holds a room. None of the surveyed character properties advertise a Linster package, so the room and the table are two phone calls, not one.
