> **TL;DR — How to spend a weekend.** Two to three days is the sweet spot [[66]](https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/switzerland/lausanne/enough-time-in-2659994-1). **Day 1:** Olympic Museum on the Ouchy lakefront in the morning, lunch at a lakeside café, afternoon CGN Belle Époque paddle steamer or a Lavaux vineyard walk (Saint-Saphorin → Lutry), then your Michelin dinner. **Day 2:** Plateforme 10's three museums at 10:00, fondue lunch in the old town, climb the Cathédrale belfry then the Sauvabelin Tower for sunset. Stay in **Ouchy** for lakeside calm or **Flon** for nightlife and metro access [[65]](https://seekinplaces.com/switzerland/where-to-stay-in-lausanne/). Whatever you do, collect your free **Lausanne Transport Card** at hotel check-in — it covers bus, metro and regional trains for the whole stay [[55]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/lausanne-transport-card-and-more/).

## Sights & museums

Lausanne's top sights cluster in three zones: the **lake** (Ouchy), **downtown/station** (Plateforme 10, old town), and **uphill** (Sauvabelin). The M2 metro — one of the steepest in the world, climbing an 11.6% gradient — links lake to upper city via 14 stations in about 20 minutes end-to-end [[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A9tro_Lausanne%E2%80%93Ouchy)[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lausanne_M%C3%A9tro).

## The lake: Ouchy, the promenade, and paddle steamers

Ouchy is Lausanne's lakefront — 8 minutes from Flon on the M2 [[16]](https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/experiences/ouchy-and-its-lakeside-promenade/). The tree-lined quays, first opened in 1901, run from the Haldimand tower past the Château d'Ouchy through three gardens (Denantou, Elysée, Olympic Park), and the rose garden at Place du Général Guisan holds 130+ rose species [[15]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/ouchy/). The promenade extends 9 km east to Lutry and 4 km west to Saint-Sulpice, all flat [[15]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/ouchy/).

**Belle Époque cruise.** SS *La Suisse* (1910, 78.5 m, 850 passengers, fully restored in 2009) sails three times daily from Ouchy down to St-Gingolph, returning via Lavaux with stops at Vevey, Montreux and Château de Chillon — about three hours for the full circular cruise [[12]](https://www.cgn.ch/en/la-suisse.html)[[13]](https://www.lake-geneva-switzerland.com/all-lake-geneva-ferry-boats/summer-lake-geneva-cgn-boat-excursions-and-day-cruises/). Paddle-steamer fares match the modern boats (≈CHF 33 for a 2 h cruise, CHF 64 for a Geneva-Lausanne day return), and Swiss Travel Pass / Half Fare Card are accepted [[14]](https://www.lake-geneva-switzerland.com/all-lake-geneva-ferry-boats/savings-tickets-for-cgn-lake-geneva-boat-cruises/).

**Swim.** [Bellerive-Plage](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/bellerive-swimming-pool/) opens 18 May - 1 Sep (09:30-20:00) — an Olympic pool, a 10 m diving board, three pools and a 350 m supervised lake beach [[17]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/bellerive-swimming-pool/). [Plage de Vidy](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/plage-de-vidy-vidy-beach/) is the largest beach on Lake Geneva — 500 m of sand, free, with SUP and pedalo rentals in Parc Louis Bourget [[18]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/plage-de-vidy-vidy-beach/).

**Pedalo at Ouchy harbour.** [Bateaux-Location-Ouchy](https://bateaux-location-ouchy.ch) rents 4-5-seater pedal boats (some with slides) next to the port: CHF 15 / 30 min, CHF 25 / hour, CHF 45 / 2 hours; life jackets free; unaccompanied children must be 14+ [[19]](https://bateaux-location-ouchy.ch/en/rentals/pedal-boats-rental).

## Lavaux: the UNESCO vineyard terraces

Lavaux is the half-day side trip a Lausanne weekend exists for. SBB regional trains stop at Pully, Lutry, Villette, Cully, Epesses, Rivaz and Saint-Saphorin every ~15 min; Cully is under 10 minutes from Lausanne [[29]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Lausanne/Cully). Mid-April to late October, CGN paddle steamers add a scenic alternative — Lutry ~18 min, Cully ~33 min, Rivaz-St-Saphorin ~47 min, 2-5 round trips daily [[28]](https://www.lake-geneva-switzerland.com/lavaux/lake-geneva-pleasure-cruises-from-vevey-and-lausanne/).

### Walking routes

| Route | Distance | Time | Profile |
|-------|---------|------|---------|

| St-Saphorin → Cully (half-route) | 6.5 km | ~2 h | downhill, the short version [[22]](https://swissfamilyfun.com/lavaux-vineyard-hike/) |
| Chexbres → Saint-Saphorin | ~3 km | ~1 h | mostly downhill spur [[24]](https://www.lake-geneva-switzerland.com/lavaux/hiking-in-the-vineyards-of-lavaux/) |
| Full GT trail (long-distance) | 35 km | ~8 h 30 | for hike-week visitors, not a weekend [[24]](https://www.lake-geneva-switzerland.com/lavaux/hiking-in-the-vineyards-of-lavaux/) |

### Villages worth a stop

- **Cully** — 16th-19th-c. winegrowers' houses, ideal base [[31]](https://www.decanter.com/wine-travel/from-alps-to-lake-a-wine-lovers-guide-to-lavaux-554115/)
- **Epesses** — a tiny hamlet perched above the lake [[31]](https://www.decanter.com/wine-travel/from-alps-to-lake-a-wine-lovers-guide-to-lavaux-554115/)
- **Saint-Saphorin** — between Epesses and Chardonne; vineyards planted overwhelmingly with Chasselas [[30]](https://www.wine-searcher.com/regions-saint-saphorin)
- **Rivaz** — home of Lavaux Vinorama (below)

### Tasting and tourist train

- [Lavaux Vinorama](https://lavaux-vinorama.ch) in Rivaz pours roughly **300 wines** from all the Lavaux appellations — mostly Chasselas plus the indigenous Plant Robert red — and screens an 8-language vineyard-year documentary. Open Mon-Sat 10:30-20:00, Sun to 19:00 [[25]](https://lavaux-vinorama.ch/en/wine-tour-tarifs-en/)[[26]](https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/experiences/food-wine/restaurant/lavaux-vinorama/).
- [Lavaux Express](https://www.lavauxexpress.ch) tourist train, April 1 - November 1 2026 — two 1 h 15 loops at CHF 17 (Lutry-Aran-Grandvaux; Cully-Riex-Epesses-Dezaley), plus a CHF 28 "Caveau Train" with a three-wine tasting and a CHF 25 Cully aperitif run [[27]](https://www.lavauxexpress.ch/en/courses/).

## Eat & drink (between Michelin courses)

Your Michelin dinner is the headline. These are the other meals.

### Three classic Swiss bistros, all walking distance from one another

| Place | What | Why go |
|-------|------|--------|
| **[Café Romand](https://www.cafe-romand.ch)**, Place Saint-François 2 | Moitié-moitié fondue, papet vaudois, malakoffs | Belle Époque brasserie open since 1951, awarded *Café historique de Lausanne* in 2021 [[43]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/cafe-romand/) |
| **[La Pinte Besson](https://www.pinte-besson.ch)** | Fondue with morels and cognac in a vaulted 1870 cellar bar | Founded 1780 — Lausanne's oldest pub, per Lausanne Tourisme and Time Out [[44]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/the-lausanner/articles/where-to-eat-a-fondue-in-lausanne/)[[45]](https://www.timeout.com/switzerland/restaurants-and-cafes/best-restaurant-lausanne-guide) |
| **[Café du Grütli](https://www.cafedugruetli.ch)**, Rue de la Mercerie 4 | Fondue, seasonal Vaudois dishes, a "Chasse des Grisons" game menu | Family-run by the Prutsch family, traditional Swiss cooking [[46]](https://www.cafedugruetli.ch/) |

### Brasseries, lakeside, near the cathedral

- **[Brasserie de Montbenon](https://brasserie-montbenon.ch)** — the same Vaudois register with a contemporary lake-view terrace in Montbenon park; opened 2014 [[45]](https://www.timeout.com/switzerland/restaurants-and-cafes/best-restaurant-lausanne-guide).
- **[Café du Vieil Ouchy](https://www.cafeduvieilouchy.ch)**, Place du Port 3 — the lakeside historic café (labelled *Café historique* in 2021) for fondue, Lake Geneva perch fillets, and meringues with double cream [[47]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/cafe-du-vieil-ouchy/).
- Near the **Cathédrale**: Tripadvisor's strongest names are Italian-leaning **Amici** (4.4★), Swiss **Brasserie Les 100 Suisses** (4.9★), **Creperie la Chandeleur** with a cathedral view, and old-town **Vieux-Lausanne Restaurant & Giraf Bar** (4.4★) [[52]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/RestaurantsNear-g188107-d242869-Cathedrale_de_Lausanne-Lausanne_Canton_of_Vaud.html).

### Brunch, bakery, coffee

- **[Café de Grancy](https://www.cafedegrancy.ch)** below the station — weekend brunch 10:00-15:00, Wednesday-night fondue, seasonal bistro/gastro cooking with a library and terrace [[48]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/cafe-de-grancy/).
- **Bakeries:** [Bread Store](https://www.breadstore.ch) (run by Meilleur Ouvrier de France baker Thomas Marie), Bryo "The Brioche House", El Gato (Lausanne's first gluten- and lactose-free patisserie), Maison Buet [[53]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/good-morning/).
- **Specialty coffee:** Bel-Air Coffee (Scandi feel), Ça Passe Crème, Sleepy Bear, Bold Coffee Club (Sous-Gare), Ordinary Man (roasted on-site, on Petit-Chêne), The Coffee Project [[54]](https://thelausanneguide.com/article/the-best-coffee-in-lausanne).

### Wine bars — Chasselas without leaving the city

- **[Ta Cave](https://www.ta-cave.ch)**, Rue du Simplon 35 — Switzerland's first crowdfunded wine bar (2015), 6-10 picks rotating monthly + 8 discovery wines, Meule cheese-mould bread [[49]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/ta-cave/).
- Lausanne Tourisme's curated wine-bar shortlist also names **Le 20** (Petit-Chêne 11), **Vintage** (Langallerie 7), **Vinothèque du Château d'Ouchy** (Place du Port), and **Street Cellar** in the Flon (Port-Franc 2a) [[50]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/wine-cellars-wine-bars/).

### Flon: nightlife clustered in two blocks

The Flon district packs King Size Pub, Les Jardins de PAM rooftop, Barberousse (rum bar), Cipriano Wine & Ham, Brasserie Belleville, Forbici (Italian), and the TAO lounge into one walkable nightlife strip [[51]](https://flon.ch/en/locataires/drinking-eating/1/).

## A suggested 2-day plan

**Day 1 (lakeside).**
- 10:00 — Musée Olympique [[2]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/the-olympic-museum/) (allow 2-3 h).
- 13:00 — Lunch at Café du Vieil Ouchy on Place du Port — perch fillets, meringues [[47]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/cafe-du-vieil-ouchy/).
- 15:00 — One of two: (a) SS *La Suisse* paddle steamer to Chillon and back via Lavaux [[13]](https://www.lake-geneva-switzerland.com/all-lake-geneva-ferry-boats/summer-lake-geneva-cgn-boat-excursions-and-day-cruises/); (b) train to Saint-Saphorin, walk Route 113 down to Lutry (~4 h, mostly downhill), train back [[22]](https://swissfamilyfun.com/lavaux-vineyard-hike/).
- Dinner — your Michelin restaurant.

**Day 2 (city).**
- 10:00 — Plateforme 10 combi ticket: pick MCBA + one of Photo Elysée or mudac [[3]](https://plateforme10.ch/en/tickets/).
- 12:30 — Fondue lunch in the old town: Café Romand, Pinte Besson, or Café du Grütli [[43]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/cafe-romand/)[[44]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/the-lausanner/articles/where-to-eat-a-fondue-in-lausanne/)[[46]](https://www.cafedugruetli.ch/).
- 14:30 — Cathédrale Notre-Dame (free) + climb the belfry (CHF 6) [[7]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/lausanne-cathedral/).
- 16:00 — Bus uphill to Sauvabelin (covered by your Transport Card), climb the 35 m wooden tower (free), walk the lake [[9]](https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/experiences/parc-de-sauvabelin-sauvabelin-park/)[[11]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/the-lausanner/articles/free-activities-in-lausanne/).
- Sundown — Ta Cave or Vinothèque du Château d'Ouchy for a Chasselas before train [[49]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/explore/ta-cave/)[[50]](https://www.lausanne-tourisme.ch/en/wine-cellars-wine-bars/).

If you stretch to **three days**, swap one half-day for Vevey (museums + Fork) or Chillon Castle — both <40 min by train [[33]](https://www.myswissalps.com/activity/chillon-castle/)[[35]](https://tourismattractions.net/switzerland/vevey).

## Pitfalls to plan around

- ⚠ **Museum closing days** — MCBA closes Mondays, mudac and Photo Elysée close Tuesdays, Olympic Museum closes Mondays; check the day-of [[3]](https://plateforme10.ch/en/tickets/)[[1]](https://www.olympics.com/museum/visit/practical-information/).
- ⚠ **Évian as a casual weekend hop** — the 2026 schedule cut concentrates crossings around commuter hours; if Évian is the goal, confirm CGN's live timetable first [[41]](https://www.lake-geneva-switzerland.com/lausanne/passenger-ferry-boats-from-lausanne-on-lake-geneva/)[[42]](https://www.cgn.ch/en/horaires-billets).
