> **TL;DR.** If the trip is anchored on May 29-31, **don't plan around an IT conference** — the only EPFL-hosted thing on those exact dates is a niche academic Artificial Life workshop at Centre Bernoulli [[20]](https://www.epfl.ch/campus/events/). Lausanne's headline tech events all sit Feb-April: **AMLD** (Feb 10-12, AI/ML, ~thousands of attendees from 40+ countries) [[1]](https://2026.appliedmldays.org/) [[3]](https://memento.epfl.ch/event/epfl-amld-intelligence-summit-2026-2/), **Insomni'hack** (Mar 16-20, cybersecurity + CTF, 500+ delegates) [[5]](https://insomnihack.ch/) [[6]](https://clusis.com/agenda/evenement-externe-insomnihack-lausanne-16-20-mars-2026/), and **HackSummit** (Apr 22-23, climate hardtech, 800 attendees) [[7]](https://www.hacksummit.co/). For a tech-event weekend re-roll, aim Feb (AMLD) or April (HackSummit).

## Flagship events worth planning a trip around

### [AMLD Intelligence Summit](https://2026.appliedmldays.org/) — AI / ML

Feb 10-12, 2026 at the [SwissTech Convention Center](https://www.stcc.ch/), EPFL. 10th anniversary edition [[1]](https://2026.appliedmldays.org/). Conference + exhibition Feb 10-11, hands-on workshop day Feb 12 [[2]](https://10times.com/appliedmldays). Pulls "thousands of experts and participants from over 40 countries" across industry, academia and government [[3]](https://memento.epfl.ch/event/epfl-amld-intelligence-summit-2026-2/). Format mixes keynotes, fishbowls, roundtables, startup pitches and an open stage [[1]](https://2026.appliedmldays.org/). Early-bird closed Dec 9, 2025 [[3]](https://memento.epfl.ch/event/epfl-amld-intelligence-summit-2026-2/). The biggest tech-tourism magnet on Lausanne's calendar — best single weekend to combine with a Michelin dinner.

### [Insomni'hack](https://insomnihack.ch/) — cybersecurity + CTF

Mar 16-20, 2026 at STCC, EPFL. Mon-Wed workshops, Thu-Fri talks + on-site CTF [[5]](https://insomnihack.ch/) [[6]](https://clusis.com/agenda/evenement-externe-insomnihack-lausanne-16-20-mars-2026/). Organised by Orange Cyberdefense Switzerland since 2008; ~500+ delegates [[4]](https://infosec-conferences.com/event/20260319-insomnihack-2026/). Described by its own site as "one of the largest cybersecurity events in Switzerland and neighboring Europe, as well as one of the largest on-site CTF's worldwide" [[5]](https://insomnihack.ch/). The 2026 edition closed Mar 19 — 2027 dates **Feb 1-5, 2027** at the same venue [[5]](https://insomnihack.ch/).

### [HackSummit](https://www.hacksummit.co/) — climate hardtech

Apr 22-23, 2026 at the [Beaulieu Congress Center](https://www.beaulieu-lausanne.com/), Lausanne. 5th Lausanne edition [[8]](https://www.swiss.tech/events/hacksummit-lausanne). Scale: 800 attendees, 100 speakers, 300 investors, 300 founders, 40 startup booths [[7]](https://www.hacksummit.co/). Two-track structure: Day 1 Industrial Renaissance (manufacturing, energy, materials, mining, DAC), Day 2 Food Sovereignty (alt-proteins, biotech ingredients) [[7]](https://www.hacksummit.co/) [[8]](https://www.swiss.tech/events/hacksummit-lausanne). Tickets (last-minute, all sold out at writing) ranged CHF 490 (startup) to CHF 1,520 (corporate) [[7]](https://www.hacksummit.co/). Audience-credibility note: vendor-blog framing, but the attendee numbers are corroborated by `swiss.tech` (Switzerland's federal tech-ecosystem org) [[8]](https://www.swiss.tech/events/hacksummit-lausanne).

### [Forum EPFL](https://forum-epfl.ch/2026/en/) — recruitment fair

Oct 5-9, 2026 at STCC. 44th edition. 188 companies + 133 startups + 18 NGOs/IGOs, 23,500 expected visitors [[12]](https://forum-epfl.ch/2026/en/). Marketed as "the biggest student recruitment fair in Europe" [[12]](https://forum-epfl.ch/2026/en/). Less useful for tourists, but a serious destination if recruiting EPFL graduates is on the agenda.

### [EPFL Investor Day](https://www.epfl.ch/innovation/startup/community/events/investor-day/) — deeptech investors

Nov 25, 2026 at STCC. Two-part day: invite-only AM (investors + Western Switzerland deeptech startups), public PM [[11]](https://www.epfl.ch/innovation/startup/community/events/investor-day/). Partners: UBS, Canton of Vaud, Swisscom Ventures [[11]](https://www.epfl.ch/innovation/startup/community/events/investor-day/). EPFL ranks "4 in Europe for deep tech spinout value" per the European Deep Tech Report 2025, the framing of the event [[11]](https://www.epfl.ch/innovation/startup/community/events/investor-day/).

## Around the May 29-31 weekend specifically

Slim pickings for IT/tech. What's actually on:

- **Workshop on Detection and Emergence of Complexity: Artificial Life Perspectives** — May 27-29, 9:15 AM-6:00 PM, Centre Bernoulli, EPFL [[20]](https://www.epfl.ch/campus/events/). Academic, narrow, but free and a chance to sit in on a niche EPFL research seminar.
- **PolyLAN 43** wrapped earlier in May 2026 [[16]](https://polylan.ch/); next PolyLAN won't be until ~Nov.
- **Pitch Training** (Jun 11) at EPFL Innovation Park and **Business Creation Engineering** (Jun 3-4, 17-18 + Jul 2) at Unlimitrust, Prilly — adjacent-dates startup workshops, both small-format [[21]](https://www.startupticker.ch/en/events).
- No headline conference at STCC or Beaulieu that weekend per the calendars consulted [[19]](https://www.epfl.ch/campus/events/events/public-events/) [[21]](https://www.startupticker.ch/en/events).

**Recommendation:** treat the May 29-31 trip as restaurant-and-lake-anchored. If aligning a future trip to a tech event matters, the highest-density weekends are:

| Weekend          | Why                                                              |
|------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Feb 13-15, 2027**  | Likely AMLD 11th edition (10th was Feb 10-12, 2026) [[1]][a1]    |
| **Feb 4-5, 2027**    | Insomni'hack 2027 talks (workshops Mon-Wed prior) [[5]][a5]     |
| **Late Apr 2027**    | HackSummit; pattern suggests Apr 21-22 or similar [[7]][a7]      |

## Venues you'll see repeatedly

- **[SwissTech Convention Center (STCC)](https://www.stcc.ch/)** — on the EPFL campus, the default venue for AMLD, Insomni'hack, Forum EPFL, EPFL Investor Day, and big PolyLAN editions [[23]](https://www.stcc.ch/) [[15]](https://memento.epfl.ch/event/polylan-25-the-biggest-lan-party-in-switzerland-is/).
- **[Beaulieu Congress Center](https://www.beaulieu-lausanne.com/)** — north of the city centre, used by HackSummit and one of PolyLAN's recurring locations [[7]](https://www.hacksummit.co/) [[15]](https://memento.epfl.ch/event/polylan-25-the-biggest-lan-party-in-switzerland-is/).
- **[EPFL Innovation Park](https://www.epfl-innovationpark.ch/)** — 100+ startups in residence, hosts most of the smaller pitch / training events [[26]](https://www.epfl-innovationpark.ch/).
- **[Forum Rolex](https://www.epfl.ch/campus/visitors/lausanne/rolex-learning-center/) / Rolex Learning Center** — Startup Champions Seed Night and several research showcases [[10]](https://www.venturelab.swiss/Startup-Champions-Seed-Night-celebrates-15th-edition-at-EPFL).

## Worth knowing but not Lausanne

- **Microsoft AI Tour** — the Lausanne stop ran Jun 16, 2025; the 2026 Swiss stop has moved to Zurich (Apr 29, 2026), so don't plan a 2026 Lausanne trip around it [[22]](https://www.startupticker.ch/en/events/microsoft-ai-tour-lausanne).
- **Crypto Valley Conference** is in **Zug**, not Lausanne — useful to flag if "Switzerland blockchain" search results push it. EPFL's Blockchain Student Association ran a stablecoins-focused day in Lausanne on Mar 20, 2026, but it's a student conference, not a destination event.
