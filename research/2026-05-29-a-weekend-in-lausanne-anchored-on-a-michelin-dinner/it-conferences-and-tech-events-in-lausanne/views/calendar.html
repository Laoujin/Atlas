§ 02

What's actually on, May 29-31

slim pickings · academic only · no STCC / Beaulieu headline
⊠ DEAD ZONE

FRI 29 — SUN 31 MAY 2026The IT calendar goes quiet.

No flagship conference is scheduled at SwissTech Convention Center or the Beaulieu Congress Center on these dates per the calendars consulted [19] [21]. PolyLAN 43 wrapped earlier in May [16] — the next edition is six months out.

Recommendation: treat the trip as restaurant-and-lake-anchored. If pairing a future trip to a conference matters, the right windows are the three weekends listed in § 04 below.

▾ WHAT IS ON
  • MAY 27-29 · 09:15-18:00 Workshop on Detection & Emergence of Complexity: Artificial Life Perspectives — Centre Bernoulli, EPFL. Academic, narrow, but free; a chance to sit in on a niche EPFL research seminar. [20]
  • JUN 3-4 · ADJACENT-DATES Business Creation Engineering — Unlimitrust, Prilly. Small-format startup workshop. [21]
  • JUN 11 · ADJACENT-DATES Pitch Training — EPFL Innovation Park. Single evening, small-format. [21]
