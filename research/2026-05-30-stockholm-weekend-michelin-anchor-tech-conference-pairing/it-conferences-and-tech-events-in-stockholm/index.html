Notes on the marquee events

DevSum — the one a software engineer should pick

Run by Cornerstone; positioned as the Nordic dev-flagship. Tracks span architecture, security, AI, web, cloud. 70+ sessions across two days at Kistamässan with a social event included; the 2026 keynote is by Olympic swimmer Therese Alshammar on "Winning Mindset" [1]. If you only have a weekend in Stockholm, this is the natural pair to a Tue/Wed dinner reservation.

Nordic Tech Week — the citywide format

Not one conference but a week-long umbrella. 200+ independently hosted sessions across Stockholm; anchored by an open "Tech Open" gathering in Kungsträdgården [3]. Best fit if you want optionality — drop into investor mixers, dev meetups, and demo nights without committing to one agenda. Sep 7–11, 2026.

Data Innovation Summit — the data/AI flagship

"Largest and most influential annual applied Data and AI event in the Nordics" per its own site [8]. 3,500+ attendees, 300+ speakers, 15 stages, 7 workshop rooms over three days — hybrid format with personalised tracks. Held at Kistamässan, Arne Beurlings Torg 5 [9]. Next edition: May 2027.

Stockholm Tech Show — the free walk-in expo

Branded as "the Nordic region's leading meeting place for digital transformation" — but the practical hook is that exhibition entry is free [5]. Two summits run alongside: Cyber Security (day 1) and DevOps (day 2). 250+ vendors, 5,000+ visitors. Ticket prices for the paid summits increase after 20 Dec 2025 for 2026 editions [13]. Next: May 25–26, 2027.

Techarena — startup/scaleup + investor matchmaking

The Stockholm counterpart to Helsinki's Slush. Founder/VC orientation, 2k investors with combined $2T AUM, 300+ speakers across 4 stages, 100+ side events [7]. Strawberry Arena, Solna (10 min by metro from city). 2026 already happened (Feb 11–12); 2027 dates not yet public but pre-registration is open with discounted rates [12].

Brilliant Minds — the closed leadership symposium

⚠ Not a normal conference: invite-only, founded by Daniel Ek (Spotify) and Ash Pournouri. June 7–8, 2026 in Stockholm with speakers including will.i.am, Scott Galloway, Lupita Nyong'o and former Finnish PM Sanna Marin under the "Hope in Action" theme [2]. The companion "A Taste of Brilliant Minds" programme is open to ~250 local students/young adults by application — the only path in for non-invitees.

Stockholm's tech-event geography

  • Kistamässan (Kista) — the centre of gravity for big multi-track conferences (DevSum, DIS, Stockholm Tech Show, DreamHack). 20 min north of Centralstationen on the blue line + commuter rail; not central, plan accommodation in Solna/Sundbyberg or commute from downtown.
  • Strawberry Arena (Solna) — Techarena. 10 min from T-Centralen on tunnelbana.
  • Microsoft Sweden HQ (Regeringsgatan 25) — community events (AgentCon, AI Community Days). Smack in the city centre.
  • Norrsken House (Birger Jarlsgatan 57C) — startup ecosystem hub, walkable from Östermalmstorg.
  • Grand Hôtel — Brilliant Minds' historical home, waterfront opposite the Royal Palace.

What's missing from this list

Stockholm does not have a dedicated peer to Helsinki's Slush in November — Techarena fills that startup/VC role but in February, and at smaller scale. For an autumn founder-heavy fix, the trip to Helsinki (Nov 18–19, 2026) remains the Nordic default. Stockholm's autumn density is the Oct 5–20 dev-week cluster instead.
