Friday — Arrive travel · settle · swim

Saturday — Anchor Day culture · ★ dinner
Before 09:30
Wat Suwan Khuha
Reclining Buddha sheltered inside a limestone cave — 20 THB entry. Arrive before the tour coaches hit the car park. The cool air and the quiet are worth the early start.[14]
Limestone cave temple
10:00 – 13:00
Phang Nga Town
The province's market town, 30 min from Natai. Morning coffee and local noodles, unhurried. A counterpoint to the hyper-composed evening ahead.
Afternoon
Rest & Prepare
Pool, beach walk, or a treatment if your hotel has a spa. The tasting runs 3–4 hours — you want to arrive rested and unhurried.
18:30 — Fixed
★ Aulis Phuket
15 guests. 16 courses. 95 % Thai-sourced: Katian farm herbs, day-boat Andaman seafood, 43-day aged Khon Kaen wagyu, Phang Nga clams. The meal opens in the Iniala lounge, moves to the kitchen counter, ends with petit fours at the pass.[22]

Wine pairing +THB 3,500–5,500 · Kids Club THB 1,000 · Cancellation: 5 days (≤4 guests), 10 days (≥5).[1]
Sunday — Decompress beach · memorial · fly
Morning
Khao Lak Beaches
30–45 min north of Natai. Nang Thong is the long swimming beach (~3 km). Bang Niang / Coconut Beach is quieter — pine-shaded and low-key.[15]
30-min detour
Police Boat 813 Memorial
A patrol vessel carried 2 km inland by the 2004 tsunami. Now a sober park in Bang Niang. Worth the stop before heading south.[16]
Afternoon
Phuket Airport (HKT)
Allow 30–35 min from Natai Beach. All hotels offer transfers.
Add a Day — if you can stay longer
🛶
Hong by Starlight
John Gray's Sea Canoe flagship: 10-hour expedition into the hidden limestone hongs of Phang Nga Bay. Ends after dark with bioluminescent plankton inside enclosed lagoons. ~THB 3,490–3,950 pp. Too long to pair with the 18:30 Aulis seating — schedule on Thursday (if you arrive Wednesday) or save it for a Sunday extension with a Monday departure.[13]
🌊
Khao Sok Raft House
Cheow Lan Lake, ~1 h by minivan from Khao Lak. Floating bungalows from $80/night inside 165 km² of emerald reservoir ringed by 900 m karst spires. Dawn/dusk boat safaris: gibbons, hornbills, langurs. Worth an overnight if the trip extends to three or four nights.[17]
🤿
Similan / Surin Diving
Both marine parks open mid-Oct to mid-May only.[21] 1.5–2 h speedboat from Thap Lamu pier; full-day commitment. Schedule as a dedicated pre- or post-Aulis day — not a same-day pairing. Peak dive visibility January–April. Richelieu Rock (whale sharks) and Koh Bon (mantas) require a liveaboard.
