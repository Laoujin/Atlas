> **TL;DR** Phuket itself has no standing annual IT conference — the local scene is a free weekly tech social at Mollys Tavern (Patong) and the boutique Phuket Summit (AI marketing, next edition April 2027). Every major tech conference in Thailand is in Bangkok [[11]](https://dev.events/AS/TH/Phuket/tech), a 1–1.5-hr flight away; the next Bangkok dates worth noting are IAIT2026 (Jun 17–19), Techsauce (Aug 26–28), and DigiTech ASEAN (Nov 25–27).

## In Phuket

### Phuket Tech Mixer & Social (recurring weekly)

The only standing tech community event in Phuket. Every **Friday at 7 PM** at Mollys Tavern Irish Bar, 94/1 Thawewong Rd, Patong. Free to attend — venue asks you to buy a drink. [[1]](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/phuket-tech-mixer-and-social-tech-ai-data-it-tickets-1987407724002)

- **Who shows up:** software developers, data scientists, product managers, startup founders, remote workers, cybersecurity professionals.
- **What happens:** casual networking, job leads, knowledge sharing, Discord/WhatsApp community access after RSVP.
- Organised by **Phuket Tech Social** via Eventbrite; 29+ recurring dates already listed.

### Phuket Summit — AI Marketing & SEO (annual, April)

A boutique 190-seat workshop-led summit for marketers, SEOs, and GenAI practitioners. [[2]](https://phuketsummit.com/) [[3]](https://phuketsummit.com/AI-Marketing-SEO-Summit-Phuket)

- **2026 edition:** April 3–5 — already past.
- **2027 edition:** April 22–24, Centara Grand Beach Resort, Karon Beach, Phuket.
- Tickets: $300 (early bird delegate) – $900 (VIP). Speakers from Google, LinkedIn.
- Topics: AI agent systems, autonomous marketing pipelines, GenAI for SEO, enterprise-scale systems.

### Academic micro-conferences in Phuket

Conference alert aggregators list dozens of one-day "International Conference on…" events in Phuket throughout the year. These are overwhelmingly organised by mass-submission venues (ResearchFora, SCIENCENET, ISIT) that exhibit classic predatory-conference red flags: guaranteed acceptance, generic titles, mass-email solicitation. [[13]](https://www.thesify.ai/blog/predatory-conferences-warning-signs-and-how-to-avoid-them) Not worth attending for networking or industry insight.

Bangkok is the hub. All major Thailand tech events are here.

**IAIT2026** (14th International Conference on Advances in Information Technology) is the strongest academic choice — organised by KMUTT + IEEE CIS, papers published by ACM in Scopus/ISI. Theme: *"Trustworthy AI and Cybersecurity: Foundations for a Resilient Digital Future."* Registration deadline June 5; full registration 18,500 THB / attendee-only 6,500 THB. [[4]](https://www.iait-conf.org/2026/)

**Techsauce Global Summit** is Southeast Asia's largest startup and innovation event — 400+ speakers, 130+ sessions, 30+ workshops. Good for investor/founder networking, less for deep technical content. [[5]](https://summit.techsauce.co/)

**DigiTech ASEAN Thailand & AI Connect** is an industry expo co-hosted by Thailand's Ministry of Digital Economy and Ministry of Higher Education. Enterprise-focused digital transformation and AI for business. [[6]](https://digitechasean.com/overview/)

## Local Community & Coworking

If you're based in Phuket/Phang Nga and want ongoing professional contact with tech people, the infrastructure is there even without big conferences:

- **[Phuket Digital Nomads](https://www.facebook.com/groups/phuketnomads/)** Facebook group [[17]](https://www.facebook.com/groups/phuketnomads/) — main community hub for remote workers and tech expats.
- **[Denz Coworking Café](https://denzphuket.com/)** [[18]](https://denzphuket.com/) — Patong Hill, 1 Gbps fibre, weekly skill-share evenings and social nights. Draws developers, startup founders, tech consultants.
- **[The Project Phuket](https://projectphuket.com/)** [[16]](https://projectphuket.com/) — Rawai, community-focused coworking with day passes and regular events.
- **[Let's Work](https://www.letswork.co.th/)** [[15]](https://www.letswork.co.th/) — Rawai beachfront, weekly business and social events.

These spaces run informal events year-round and are where most day-to-day tech community activity happens in the Phuket area. [[10]](https://denzphuket.com/networking-events-and-business-summits-in-phuket-2026-complete-guide/)
