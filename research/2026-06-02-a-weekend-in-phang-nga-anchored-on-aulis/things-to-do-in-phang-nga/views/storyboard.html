ACT I THE BAY Phang Nga Bay — karst seascape, 400 km²
01 EXT. KOH PHING KAN — MIDDAY (AVOID) NOV–APR

JAMES BOND ISLAND

The limestone pinnacle made famous by The Man with the Golden Gun. Walking on it is free. Every speedboat in southern Thailand converges here between 10:00–14:00 — the shot you want is before 08:30 (empty rock, soft morning light) or after 15:30 (long shadows, thinning crowds). ฿300 national park entry for Ao Phang Nga is bundled into most tours.

฿300 park entry (bundled) Tours ฿1,300–2,500 pp Full day · departs ~07:00 Midday: wall-to-wall coaches
⌗ maratnomad.com · visitor dispatch Nov 2025
02 INT. KOH PANAK — TIDE WINDOW TIDAL GATE

THE HONGS · HIDDEN LAGOONS

Collapsed-cave interior lagoons ringed by 100 m limestone walls — the bay's real showpiece. Motor boats cannot fit. Inflatable kayak entry only, through passages that flood at high tide. Koh Panak hides a network: the Ice Cream Cave (stalactite ceiling that looks mid-drip), Diamond Cave, then open-sky emerald water. Access exists only in a ~90-minute tidal window each day.

Andaman Sea Kayak · ฿1,500–2,500 pp Motor boats cannot enter Tide-gated — confirm window on day
⌗ andamanseakayak.com · field notes
03 EXT. KOH PANYEE — MIDDAY YEAR-ROUND
stilted village · 400 households

KOH PANYEE · STILTED VILLAGE

A Muslim fishing community ~200 years old, built entirely on stilts over the bay. Three floating football pitches built after 1986. Pork, alcohol and smoking are banned; dress modestly. Most full-day bay tours stop here for lunch — arrive independently from Phang Nga pier (~30 min, ~฿200) for a less hurried morning visit before the coach groups arrive.

~฿200 from Phang Nga pier · 30 min Modest dress required No alcohol · no pork · no smoking
⌗ thailandmagazine.com · Koh Panyee guide
04 EXT. PHANG NGA BAY — DUSK INTO DARK JOHN GRAY FLAGSHIP · SINCE 1989

HONG BY STARLIGHT

John Gray's 10-hour flagship — the definitive Phang Nga experience. Afternoon paddle through the hongs, sunset viewed from the escort vessel at James Bond Island, then re-entry into the lagoon after dark to float krathong lanterns in bioluminescent water. The plankton glow is real. Running since 1989; consistently top-rated across thousands of Tripadvisor reviews.

THE SINGLE BEST PHANG NGA SPLURGE. Schedule on Friday or Sunday — not the Aulis dinner day. Hotel pickup 10:20–11:15; returns ~21:30. Full 10-hour commitment.

฿3,490–3,950 pp Hotel pickup · returns ~21:30 10 hours Bioluminescence after dark
⌗ johngray-seacanoe.com · official tour page
ACT II THE JUNGLE Khao Sok National Park — inland karst, 165 km² flooded valley, wildlife
05 EXT. CHEOW LAN LAKE — DAWN NOV–MAY · FEB–MAY PEAK WILDLIFE

CHEOW LAN LAKE · RAFT HOUSE

165 km² reservoir created in 1982 when Ratchaprapha Dam flooded a limestone valley. The dam left 900 m karst spires rising straight from the water — the "Guilin of Thailand." Day trips give you the scenery but miss the window: gibbons, hornbills and langurs are crepuscular. An overnight raft-house stay (from $80/night, all-inclusive) gets you the dawn boat safari.

~1 h by minivan from Khao Lak to Ratchaprapha Pier; pick-up 08:00. Park entry ฿300. Dry season Feb–May gives the best wildlife odds — nearly 400 bird species in the park.

From $80/night all-inclusive ฿300 park entry 1 h from Khao Lak · minivan transfer Dawn wildlife safari included
⌗ passportandstamps.com · raft house field notes 2026
06 INT. NAM TALU — DRY SEASON ONLY CLOSED WET SEASON
700 m underground · waist-deep wading

NAM TALU CAVE

700 m of limestone cave reached via a 2.5 km jungle trek (2–3 h). Waist-deep wading sections. Closed in heavy rain — eight people died in a 2007 flash flood. The most adventurous of Khao Sok's three caves (Coral Cave and Pra Khie Phet are easy 30-min visits). Bundled into most overnight raft-house packages. Do not attempt in questionable weather.

Bundled in raft-house stays Flash-flood risk · dry season only 2.5 km approach · 2–3 h
⌗ cheowlanlaketravel.com · cave notes
07 EXT. SOK RIVER — DRY SEASON FEB–MAY PEAK

SOK RIVER · JUNGLE WILDLIFE

Kayak the Sok River near park HQ — monkeys, otters and snakes from the water. Dry season (Feb–May) lowers water levels and concentrates wildlife at the banks. Bonus January–March window: the Rafflesia kerrii trek — a 4 h, 5–10 km hunt for the world's largest parasitic flower (฿1,200 pp). Each bloom lasts only days; book on arrival, not in advance. Elephant sanctuary visits: Elephant Hills (no riding, 2025 Responsible Animal Welfare Award winner) or Khao Lak Elephant Sanctuary (stopped guest feeding 2025).

Rafflesia trek ฿1,200 pp · Jan–Mar only Ethical elephants available Kayak from park HQ
⌗ elephanthills.com · wildlife guide 2025
ACT III THE REEF Similan & Surin Islands — open mid-Oct to mid-May only · 500 THB entry
MID-MAY ─────── MID-OCT
Both Mu Ko Similan and Mu Ko Surin close every year for ecological recovery and monsoon safety. The 2025–26 season: Similan opened 15 Oct 2025, closes 15 May 2026. Surin: same window. A May–Oct visit rules out both headline marine parks — the most consequential seasonal constraint after Aulis.
Similan closes 15 May 2026 Surin: same window ฿500 adult / ฿250 child each park
08 EXT. RICHELIEU ROCK — UNDERWATER · 5–40m FEB–APR PEAK · LIVEABOARD ONLY

RICHELIEU ROCK · WHALE SHARK

Horseshoe-shaped pinnacle 40 km offshore in the Surin group — Thailand's #1 whale-shark dive site. Also: Thailand's manta-ray hot spot at Koh Bon and one of the region's top pinnacle dives at Koh Tachai. Peak Feb–Apr for visibility (25 m+) and whale-shark probability. January–April is the confirmed season window.

LIVEABOARD ONLY. Day trips from Thap Lamu pier reach only the inner Similan sites. Richelieu Rock, Koh Bon and Koh Tachai require a 4-day/4-night liveaboard (mid-Oct to early May). Allocate this as a separate trip segment, not same-day with Aulis.

4-day liveaboard · mid-Oct to early May 40 km offshore from Surin Whale shark: Feb–Apr peak Closed mid-May – mid-Oct ฿500 park entry
⌗ sunrise-divers.com · Richelieu Rock dive notes
09 EXT. MU KO SURIN — SHALLOW REEF OCT–MAY
Thailand's healthiest coral · beginner snorkel

SURIN ISLANDS · CORAL + MOKEN

Widely cited as Thailand's healthiest coral reefs — beginner-snorkel accessible from the beach. Day trip from Thap Lamu pier (1.5–2 h speedboat). Most Surin tours include the Moken sea-gypsy village on Ko Surin Tai, a community consolidated into Ao Bon after the 2004 tsunami. The Lobong ceremony (a sea-spirit rite, twice-yearly) is occasionally witnessed by visitors during these trips.

฿500 park entry 1.5–2 h from Thap Lamu pier Beginner snorkel from shore
⌗ thecaptainandthecook.com · Surin field guide
ACT IV THE COAST Khao Lak beaches · tsunami memorial · Takua Pa Old Town · culture
10 EXT. NANG THONG BEACH — AFTERNOON NOV–APR CALM SEAS

KHAO LAK COAST

Six distinct beaches running north from Khao Lak. Nang Thong (~3 km, calm swimming, full services) anchors the strip. Coconut Beach is national-park land: no sunbeds, no engines, no development — the quiet end. Pakarang has surf and coral patches but no swimming. Bang Niang Night Market (Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat 16:00–22:00) is the evening destination. This stretch is your operational base for all island departures.

Coconut Beach: national park land Bang Niang market: Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat 16:00–22:00 Nang Thong: 3 km · swimming Nov–Apr
⌗ similandivecenter.com · beach guide
11 EXT. BANG NIANG — MORNING YEAR-ROUND · FREE

POLICE BOAT 813 · MEMORIAL

Thai Police Boat 813, swept 2 km inland by the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami while protecting Crown Prince Bhumi Jensen (who survived). The boat still sits exactly where the wave left it, now enclosed in a free memorial park. Open 08:30–18:00 daily. The Bang Niang Night Market runs directly opposite on market evenings — pair both in a half-day.

Free entry 08:30–18:00 daily Night market opposite: market evenings
⌗ khaolakcenter.com · memorial notes
12 EXT. SRI TAKUA PA ROAD — SUNDAY EVENING SUNDAYS 15:00–20:00

TAKUA PA OLD TOWN

Originally the spice port of Takola, later a tin-dredging hub that drew Chinese, Dutch, Portuguese and British traders. When tin collapsed in the early 1900s the foreigners left; the Chinese community stayed, producing the Sino-Portuguese shophouse streetscape still intact today. The Sunday Walking Street on Sri Takua Pa Road (15:00–20:00) fills with grilled seafood, sweet roti and southern snacks. ~45 min north of Khao Lak.

Sunday Walking Street: 15:00–20:00 ~45 min from Khao Lak Free · grilled seafood · roti
⌗ windowonphuket.com · Takua Pa history
13 INT. LIMESTONE CAVE TEMPLE — MORNING YEAR-ROUND · ฿20
reclining Buddha · cave interior · macaques

WAT SUWAN KHUHA · MONKEY CAVE

A 15 m reclining golden Buddha enshrined inside a natural limestone cave, ~10 km south of Phang Nga town. Arrive before 09:30 to beat the bay-tour coaches that stop here en route. Resident macaques will take sunglasses, water bottles and anything loose from your hands — secure valuables before entry. ฿20 admission; open 08:00–17:00.

Two more culture stops nearby: Wat Tham Tapan (enter through a dragon's mouth into Buddhist hell dioramas, free, 08:30–18:00) and Sa Nang Manora Forest Park (2 km jungle trail, bat cave, swimmable waterfall pools, free).

฿20 entry · 08:00–17:00 ⚠ Macaques steal valuables Arrive before 09:30
⌗ thailandmagazine.com · Wat Suwan Khuha

Getting In

  • Phuket HKT — 70 km / ~1 h to Khao Lak (~$35–45 transfer) · rome2rio
  • Krabi KBV — 137 km / ~2 h to Khao Lak
  • No train to Phang Nga; songthaews ฿10–15, motorbike hire ฿300/day

Season Window

  • Nov–Apr — high season; calm Andaman; both parks open
  • Feb–May — best wildlife at Cheow Lan; Rafflesia (Jan–Mar)
  • Jan–Apr — peak Similan visibility; whale-shark odds highest
  • May–Oct — both Similan and Surin closed
  • Five nights in Khao Lak is the recommended sweet spot

Money & Entry

  • Markets, street food and piers: cash only
  • ATM surcharge: ฿150–220/transaction; always choose THB billing
  • Tip: ฿20–40 at mid-range; ~10% at upscale (no service charge)
  • Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) required within 72h of arrival — free, replaced TM6 form May 2025
  • 60-day visa-free for 93 countries as of mid-2026 — verify before departure
