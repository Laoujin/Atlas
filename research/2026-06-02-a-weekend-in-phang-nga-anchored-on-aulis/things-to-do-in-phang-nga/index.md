> **TL;DR.** Base in Khao Lak from **November–April** for the full menu: kayak the hidden hongs of Phang Nga Bay [[3]](https://johngray-seacanoe.com/tour/hong-by-starlight/), sleep on a floating raft house on Khao Sok's Cheow Lan Lake [[15]](https://passportandstamps.com/khao-sok-floating-bungalows/), and dive or snorkel the Similan/Surin marine parks (open mid-October to mid-May only) [[20]](https://5starmarinephuket.com/2026/01/06/similan-islands-will-close-may-15th-2026/) [[22]](https://thephuketexpress.com/2025/05/16/similan-and-surin-islands-in-phang-nga-temporarily-closed-for-ecological-recovery/). Round it out with Takua Pa's Sunday Walking Street [[42]](https://www.grandmercurekhaolakbangsak.com/beachfront-resort-in-khao-lak-grand-mercure-khao-lak-bangsak/travel-tips/takua-pa-old-town-walking-street-sunday/) and the Police Boat 813 tsunami memorial in Bang Niang [[30]](https://khaolakcenter.com/poi/813-tsunami-memorial-park-in-bang-niang/). A May–October trip loses both island parks — go shoulder season if you can.

## Where Phang Nga is, and what it isn't

Phang Nga is the mainland province directly north of Phuket. Four headline experiences cluster here: the **limestone karst seascape** of Phang Nga Bay, the **inland jungle** of Khao Sok National Park, the **diving** off the Similan and Surin Islands, and a **coastal strip** at Khao Lak. The province itself has no airport — fly into Phuket (HKT, ~1 h to Khao Lak [[72]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Phuket-Airport-HKT/Khao-Lak)) or Krabi (KBV, ~2 h [[73]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Krabi-Airport-KBV/Khao-Lak)).

## The bay: hongs, James Bond, Koh Panyee

The bay is what put Phang Nga on tourist maps. Four very different boats fight for the same water — pick the one that matches what you actually want to see.

**Khao Phing Kan (James Bond Island)** is the limestone pinnacle made famous by *The Man with the Golden Gun*. Walking on it is free, but every speedboat in southern Thailand disgorges here midday, so go early or late [[1]](https://forevervacation.com/phuket/james-bond-island). The island sits inside Ao Phang Nga National Park — 300 THB foreign-adult entry, usually bundled into the tour [[2]](https://www.phuket101.net/phang-nga-bay/) [[77]](https://simbaseatrips.com/national-park-fees-in-phuket-what-you-need-to-know/).

**Hongs** — collapsed-cave interior lagoons ringed by 100 m limestone walls — are the bay's real showpiece. The main Koh Hong lagoon is ~100 m across; Koh Panak hides a network of smaller hongs and stalactite caves like the **Ice Cream Cave** [[6]](https://www.jamesbondisland-tours.com/ice-cream-cave/) and Diamond Cave, all tide-gated [[4]](https://andamanseakayak.com/hidden-hong-lagoons-secret-caves-only-accessible-by-kayak-in-phang-nga-bay/). Only kayaks fit through the entries. [John Gray's Sea Canoe](https://johngray-seacanoe.com/tour/hong-by-starlight/) has run the flagship "Hong by Starlight" trip since 1989 [[3]](https://johngray-seacanoe.com/tour/hong-by-starlight/) — sunset paddle plus floating krathongs in the dark, consistently top-rated on Tripadvisor [[52]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionProductReview-g297930-d18925001-Hong_by_Starlight_John_Gray_s_Sea_Canoe-Patong_Kathu_Phuket.html). ⚠ Note that the Krabi-side Koh Hong (Mu Ko Hong) is a different island ~20 min from Ao Nang — confirm which you're booking [[7]](https://www.viator.com/Krabi-attractions/Hong-Islands/d348-a2306).

**Koh Panyee** is the stilted Muslim fishing village in the middle of the bay — ~200 years old, founded by Javanese families, now ~400 households, with three floating soccer pitches built after 1986 [[5]](https://thailandmagazine.com/phang-nga/see-do/koh-panyee/) [[45]](https://thephangngabay.com/koh-panyee-tours/). Pork, alcohol and smoking are banned; modest dress is expected. Lunch here is on most full-day bay itineraries.

## Khao Sok: jungle plus a flooded valley

Khao Sok National Park straddles Surat Thani and Phang Nga; the **eastern Cheow Lan Lake** sector is the postcard half and reachable in ~1 h from Khao Lak by minivan [[18]](https://www.getyourguide.com/surat-thani-city-l143468/from-khao-sok-private-longtail-boat-tour-at-cheow-lan-lake-t535852/). The 165 km² reservoir was created in 1982 behind Ratchaprapha Dam, leaving 900 m karst spires rising from emerald water [[19]](https://www.thainationalparks.com/cheow-lan-lake) — locals call it the "Guilin of Thailand."

Day trips give you the scenery but miss the wildlife window. The operator playbook is to push **overnight raft-house stays** for dawn/dusk boat safaris, which regularly turn up gibbons, hornbills, langurs and occasional wild elephants — nearly 400 bird species in the park, with dry-season Feb–May the best fauna window [[12]](https://www.elephanthills.com/khao-sok-national-park-2025-thailand/).

Stays are typically all-inclusive: pier transfer, meals, kayaks, guided cave or boat safari [[11]](https://www.seeksophie.com/magazine/a/khao-sok-things-to-do-thailand). Park entry is 300 THB adult / 150 THB child for foreigners — raised from 200/100 in May 2024 and still in force in 2026 [[10]](https://mythailandtours.com/updated-entrance-fees-at-khao-soks-cheow-lan-lake-and-ratchaprapha-dam/).

**Caves and treks.** Coral Cave and Pra Khie Phet are easy 30-min visits; **Nam Talu** is the adventurous 700 m tube reached via a 2.5 km trek with waist-deep wading — closed in heavy rain after eight people died in a 2007 flash flood [[16]](https://www.cheowlanlaketravel.com/namtalucave). The **Rafflesia kerrii trek** is a 4 h, 5–10 km hunt for the world's largest parasitic flower (฿1,200 pp); blooms last only days and only happen January–March, so the trek is bookable only on arrival [[13]](https://www.khaosok.com/national-park/rafflesia/) [[14]](https://www.khaosoktravel.com/rafflesia-tour). Sok River kayaking near the park HQ is the standout dry-season river outing — monkeys, otters and snakes from the boat [[17]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g1657011-Activities-c61-t191-Khao_Sok_National_Park_Surat_Thani_Province.html).

## Similan & Surin: the dive season

Both marine parks **close mid-May to mid-October every year** for ecological recovery and monsoon safety [[22]](https://thephuketexpress.com/2025/05/16/similan-and-surin-islands-in-phang-nga-temporarily-closed-for-ecological-recovery/) [[69]](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40066673). The 2025–26 season:

- **Mu Ko Similan** — opened 15 Oct 2025 [[21]](https://www.facebook.com/richardbarrowthailand/posts/-similan-islands-national-park-opening-seasonphang-nga-thailand%EF%B8%8F-15-october-2025/1402692664550326/), closes 15 May 2026 [[20]](https://5starmarinephuket.com/2026/01/06/similan-islands-will-close-may-15th-2026/).
- **Mu Ko Surin** — same window (16 May–14 Oct closure) [[22]](https://thephuketexpress.com/2025/05/16/similan-and-surin-islands-in-phang-nga-temporarily-closed-for-ecological-recovery/).

Foreign-adult entry is 500 THB (250 children) at either park [[23]](https://www.nationthailand.com/thai-destination/40016384) [[77]](https://simbaseatrips.com/national-park-fees-in-phuket-what-you-need-to-know/). Speedboat day trips from Thap Lamu pier take 1.5–2 h [[24]](https://www.lovephukettours.com/similan-islands-season-guide.html); peak visibility runs January–April [[24]](https://www.lovephukettours.com/similan-islands-season-guide.html).

Day trips reach the inner Similan sites; **liveaboards** (typically 4-day/4-night, mid-Oct to early May) are the only practical way to dive Koh Bon, Koh Tachai and Richelieu Rock [[25]](https://godiving.co/travel/day-trip-vs-liveaboard-similan-islands/) [[37]](https://divehappy.com/diving-similan-islands-quick-guide/). Surin tours customarily include a stop at the **Moken sea-gypsy village** on Ko Surin Tai [[28]](https://www.loveandaman.com/uncategorized/moken-village-a-hidden-gem-of-the-surin-islands/) — a community consolidated into a single Ao Bon settlement after the 2004 tsunami [[46]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moken). Their twice-yearly **Lobong Ceremony**, a sea-spirit cleansing rite, is occasionally witnessed by visitors during Surin trips [[47]](https://www.loveandaman.com/local-life/lobong-ceremony-moken-surin-islands/).

## Culture, history, southern food

Phang Nga rewards travellers willing to leave the bay tour boats. Three under-the-radar stops around Phang Nga town:

- **Wat Suwan Khuha (Monkey Cave Temple)** — 15 m reclining golden Buddha inside a limestone cave ~10 km south of town, 20 THB entry, 08:00–17:00 [[38]](https://thailandmagazine.com/phang-nga/see-do/wat-suwan-khuha/). ⚠ Macaques snatch sunglasses and water bottles — arrive before 09:30 to beat the bay-tour coaches [[39]](https://chrisandwrensworld.com/wat-suwan-khuha/).
- **Wat Tham Tapan** (Heaven and Hell Temple) — enter through a giant dragon's mouth into graphic Buddhist hell dioramas; free, 08:30–18:00, not for small children [[40]](https://thailandmagazine.com/phang-nga/see-do/wat-tham-tapan/).
- **Sa Nang Manora Forest Park** — free 2 km jungle trail with a bat cave and swimmable waterfall pools [[41]](https://thailandmagazine.com/phang-nga/see-do/sa-nang-manora-forest-park/).

**Takua Pa Old Town** is the region's heritage centrepiece. Originally the spice port of Takola, it boomed as a tin-dredging hub that pulled in Chinese, Dutch, Portuguese and British traders; when tin collapsed in the early 1900s the foreigners left but the Chinese community stayed, producing the Sino-Portuguese shophouse streetscape still standing today [[43]](https://www.windowonphuket.com/features/224/discovering-historical-takua-pa). Thailand ranked among the world's top tin exporters 1967–1984 — the district itself is named after *takua*, the Thai word for lead, even though tin was the bigger ore [[44]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphoe_Takua_Pa). The **Sunday Walking Street** on Sri Takua Pa Road runs ~15:00–20:00 with grilled seafood, sweet roti and southern snacks [[42]](https://www.grandmercurekhaolakbangsak.com/beachfront-resort-in-khao-lak-grand-mercure-khao-lak-bangsak/travel-tips/takua-pa-old-town-walking-street-sunday/). For a still more local night out, the **Long Lae Sunday Market** in Takua Thung is ~90% prepared-food stalls plus karaoke stages, 08:00–17:00, overwhelmingly Thai [[51]](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/6073250/long-lae-market).

**Southern-Muslim food register** to hunt:

- **Nam phrik takola** — Phang Nga's signature roasted shrimp-paste relish, pounded with *kung siap* (salt-cured skewered shrimp) and served with boiled and fresh vegetables [[48]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/article/features/Exploring%20Southern%20Thai%20Flavors%20-%206%20Dishes%20to%20Delight%20in%20Phuket%20and%20Phang%20Nga).
- **Khao yam** — rice salad of jasmine rice (often dyed blue with butterfly-pea flowers), toasted coconut, dried shrimp, fresh herbs, bound with pungent *budu* fermented-fish-sauce dressing [[49]](https://guide.michelin.com/th/en/article/travel/9-must-try-southern-thai-dishes-recommended-by-michelin-restaurant-chefs).
- **Roti with dal or massaman**, and **khao mok gai** (turmeric chicken biryani) — direct inheritances from South Indian Muslim traders [[50]](https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/features/the-food-of-southern-thailand-part-two-the-mainland).

## Practical: when, how, where

**When.** High season is **Nov–Apr**, with Nov–Mar the most reliable window (25–30 °C, calm seas, 60–70% humidity) [[67]](https://andamanseakayak.com/best-time-to-visit-phang-nga-weather-crowds-and-seasonal-highlights/) [[68]](https://www.aleenta.com/blog/when-best-time-visit-phang-nga/). Monsoon is May–Oct with 80–90% humidity, rougher Andaman, and **both Similan and Surin closed** [[69]](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40066673). A May–Oct trip rules out the two headline marine parks.

**Getting in.**

- **Phuket (HKT)** — dominant gateway, 70 km / ~1 h to Khao Lak by private transfer (~$35–45) [[72]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Phuket-Airport-HKT/Khao-Lak).
- **Krabi (KBV)** — alternative, 137 km / ~2 h [[73]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Krabi-Airport-KBV/Khao-Lak).
- No train serves Phang Nga town; getting around uses songthaews (10–15 THB/ride), motorbikes (~300 THB/day) and motorbike taxis [[76]](https://www.travelfish.org/transport/thailand/southern_thailand/phang_nga/phang_nga_town/all).

**Where to base.**

|------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|

| Phuket           | Short trip; bay only 25 km / ~45 min by speedboat [[2]][t7-75]                  | Misses Khao Sok and Khao Lak coast        |

| Phang Nga town   | Cheap, local; songthaews 10–15 THB, no train [[3]][t7-76]                       | Thin tourist infrastructure               |

**Entry rules, 2026.** All foreign arrivals must submit the [**Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC)**](https://tdac.immigration.go.th/) via the official portal within 72 h of arrival — free, replaced the paper TM6 in May 2025, missing it can mean boarding denial [[70]](https://tdac.immigration.go.th/) [[71]](https://www.thailawonline.com/thailand-digital-arrival-card-tdac/). The 60-day visa-free scheme still covered 93 countries as of mid-2026 (US, UK, EU, Australia, Canada included), but Thailand has announced a planned reversion to a 57-country list — verify status close to departure [[78]](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/tourism/2026/04/27/thailand-to-revert-visa-free-list-to-57-countries-scrap-60-day-scheme-for-93-nations/).

**Money.** Cards work at hotels, malls and large restaurants; markets, street food and longtail piers are cash-only. ATM withdrawals at Thai banks add **150–220 THB per transaction** — always choose **THB billing** to avoid DCC markup [[80]](https://thairanked.com/en/blogs/cash-vs-card-in-thailand/). Tipping: 20–40 THB or round-up at mid-range restaurants, ~10% at upscale spots without service charge, 50–200 THB for a Thai massage or spa [[79]](https://thaiguys.link/en/tips/tipping-guide).
