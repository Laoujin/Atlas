The **master constraint for this weekend is seasonal alignment**. Aulis shuts from 16 May to 16 November [[1]](https://www.aulisphuket.com/) — essentially the same window that closes both the Similan and Surin marine parks (mid-May to mid-October) [[2]](https://thephuketexpress.com/2025/05/16/similan-and-surin-islands-in-phang-nga-temporarily-closed-for-ecological-recovery/). Every other planning decision flows from fixing the weekend in **November through April**. The next Aulis dinner service opens 17 November 2026; until then, the restaurant is dark.

**The Natai Beach cluster is the right base.** Iniala Beach House ($575–1,256/night), [Aleenta](https://www.aleenta.com/phuket/) ($237–320), [Santhiya](https://phuketnatai.santhiya.com/main) ($129–188), and Baba Beach Club (from $125) all sit on the same strip 15–25 minutes north of Phuket Airport [[3]](https://www.designhotels.com/hotels/thailand/phang-nga/iniala-beach-house/) [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1951344-d607307-Reviews-Aleenta_Phuket_Phang_Nga_Resort_Spa-Khok_Kloi_Takua_Thung_Phang_Nga_Province.html). Iniala is the only property with Aulis in the garden and a "Stay & Dine" package on offer; Aleenta — Leading Hotels of the World, rated #1 on the beach across 1,439 reviews — delivers equivalent beachfront luxury at roughly a third of the price [[5]](https://www.lhw.com/hotel/Aleenta-Phuket-Phang-Nga-Resort-Spa-Phang-Nga-Thailand). The two properties are different kinds of decisions: Iniala is about proximity and ultra-boutique design; Aleenta is about value and scale.

**Two days, one fixed dinner, two activity windows.** The Saturday before an 18:30 Aulis seating doesn't fit a full-day bay excursion — [John Gray's Sea Canoe "Hong by Starlight"](https://johngray-seacanoe.com/tour/hong-by-starlight/) runs 10 hours and ends after dark [[6]](https://johngray-seacanoe.com/tour/hong-by-starlight/); that belongs on Friday or Sunday. Saturday morning works for lower-intensity cultural stops: Wat Suwan Khuha's reclining Buddha in a limestone cave (20 THB, before 09:30 to beat the tour coaches) [[7]](https://thailandmagazine.com/phang-nga/see-do/wat-suwan-khuha/), or Phang Nga town itself. Sunday decompression points to [Khao Lak beaches](https://similandivecenter.com/news/khao-lak-beaches/) 30–45 minutes north — Nang Thong for swimming, Coconut Beach for quiet [[8]](https://similandivecenter.com/news/khao-lak-beaches/) — with the Police Boat 813 tsunami memorial in Bang Niang worth the 30-minute detour [[9]](https://khaolakcenter.com/poi/813-tsunami-memorial-park-in-bang-niang/).

Khao Sok's Cheow Lan Lake raft houses (from $80/night, ~1 h by minivan from Khao Lak) [[10]](https://passportandstamps.com/khao-sok-floating-bungalows/) are the obvious extension if the trip stretches to three nights — but they don't fit cleanly into a 48-hour Aulis anchor unless you arrive Thursday. The Similan and Surin dive parks, when open, require a 1.5–2 h speedboat from Thap Lamu pier and occupy a full day [[11]](https://www.lovephukettours.com/similan-islands-season-guide.html): schedule them as a dedicated pre- or post-dinner day, not a same-day pairing with Aulis.

[Six Senses Yao Noi](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/hotels-stays/phang-nga/six-senses-yao-noi-328) (from $623, Koh Yao Noi, 45-min speedboat + 30-min drive to reach Aulis) [[12]](https://www.jetsetchristina.com/six-senses-yao-noi-review-thailand/) is the dramatic alternative base — but the logistics pressure on an 18:30 fixed seating make it viable only as the *first* night of the trip, moving to the mainland by early Saturday afternoon.

**IT events are not a draw for this geography.** The only standing tech event in the area is a free Friday mixer at Mollys Tavern, Patong (Phuket) [[13]](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/phuket-tech-mixer-and-social-tech-ai-data-it-tickets-1987407724002). The Phuket Summit (AI marketing, ~190 seats) doesn't recur until April 2027 [[14]](https://phuketsummit.com/AI-Marketing-SEO-Summit-Phuket); all major Thailand IT conferences — Techsauce (August), DigiTech ASEAN (November) — are in Bangkok, a 1–1.5-hr flight away. The IT angle research completed with a script-exit warning but produced valid content.

The open question worth resolving before booking: can a late-October window catch both the Similan reopening (mid-October) and Aulis's reopening (17 November 2026), or does the ~4-week gap mean you're always choosing between the two headline draws?
