**GStar Summit** is run by the New Turing Institute (formerly VietAI). The 2026 edition drew 1,000+ attendees [[13]](https://vir.com.vn/gstar-summit-2026-explores-human-centric-ai-to-boost-productivity-153812.html) with keynotes from Dr. Ed H. Chi (VP Research, Google DeepMind) and Prof. Po-Shen Loh (Carnegie Mellon) [[12]](https://summit.newturing.ai/). Co-organized with Pacific Gateway Partners.

**Test Automation Summit** theme in 2026: "Human-Centric Quality: AI-Driven, Sustainable, and Secure Testing." Ticket VND 3,000,000 (~$120). Speakers from Bosch, Parasoft, NashTech, Katalon, and Endava [[11]](https://www.testingmind.com/event/test-automation-summit-ho-chi-minh-city/).

**AI Revolution Summit** targets C-level decision-makers: 89% of 2026 attendees controlled budgets of $50K–$1M; solutions provider pass $499 [[9]](https://airevolutionsummit.com/vietnam/).

**FPT Techday** is Vietnam's largest private-sector tech forum; rotates between HCMC and Hanoi. The 2024 HCMC edition at Thiskyhall Thu Duc City drew 2,500 leaders under the theme "Future Now" [[14]](https://www.nucamp.co/blog/coding-bootcamp-viet-nam-vnm-top-10-mustattend-tech-meetups-and-conferences-in-viet-nam).

## City Context

HCMC is Vietnam's dominant tech hub: 50% of the country's startups, 40% of incubators, and 44% of venture investment in a $7.5B ecosystem [[20]](https://www.growthhq.io/our-thinking/vietnam-startup-ecosystem-2026-hcmc-hanoi-da-nang-power-big-tech-growth-in-ai-semiconductors-green-tech). Quang Trung Software City (QTSC) alone anchors 30,000 ICT workers [[20]](https://www.growthhq.io/our-thinking/vietnam-startup-ecosystem-2026-hcmc-hanoi-da-nang-power-big-tech-growth-in-ai-semiconductors-green-tech). Government Resolution 57 on science, technology, and AI is accelerating both public-sector conferences and private investment through 2027, making the event calendar denser each year.
