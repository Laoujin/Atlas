Scene 3 · Sunday

THE DAY TRIP

One essential verdict. One wet-weather fallback.

✓ Verdict
Cu Chi Tunnels — Ben Duoc
  • Ben Duoc over Ben Dinh — more authentic, far fewer bus groups
  • Upgrade: Les Rives speedboat (~USD 95 pp) — departs ahead of road convoys
  • Full day · depart 7 AM · back to HCMC by 14:00–15:00
Book speedboat 2+ days ahead. Essential on any first HCMC visit — the tunnels are unlike anything else in Southeast Asia.
CHOLON
Wet-weather alt
D5 Chinatown
  • Binh Tay Market — 1920s yellow clock tower, wholesale spices & textiles, open 8–17
  • Thien Hau Pagoda — 19th-c. Cantonese sea-goddess temple · ceramic dioramas · 6 AM–5:30 PM
  • Ha Ton Quyen 'dim sum street' — Chinese-Vietnamese dim sum cluster, lunch hours
Use when: overnight rain has made Cu Chi's outdoor sections unpleasant. A half-day — pair with an afternoon museum or Ben Thanh circuit.
