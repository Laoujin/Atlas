> **Decision.** Two touring shows do almost all the heavy lifting across the 17 cities. **[Jimmy Carr](https://www.jimmycarr.com/tour/europe/)** ("Laughs Funny") plays **ten** of them between June and December [[10]](https://www.jimmycarr.com/tour/europe/); **[Ali Wong](https://www.aliwong.com/tour)** ("Ali Wong Live") plays **eight** in a tight 17 Jun–11 Jul sprint [[49]](https://www.aliwong.com/tour). Best single trips: **Ali Wong, Amsterdam, 30 Jun** (AFAS Live, few tickets left) [[1]](https://www.ticketmaster.nl/comedy/comedy/39/events?language=en-us) or **Stockholm, 22 Jun** [[36]](https://www.livenation.se/event/ali-wong-live-stockholm-tickets-edp1651997); for a London date, **Aziz Ansari at the Royal Albert Hall, 11 Jul** [[2]](https://guides.ticketmaster.co.uk/comedy-guide/) or the **Hasan Minhaj + Ronny Chieng** double-header, 16 Oct [[2]](https://guides.ticketmaster.co.uk/comedy-guide/); and the only big stand-up name reaching CEE is **[Daniel Sloss](https://www.forumkarlin.cz/en/event/daniel-sloss-bitter/)** in Prague, 21 Sep [[40]](https://www.forumkarlin.cz/en/event/daniel-sloss-bitter/). Outside those tours plus London and Zurich, English-language comedy means **recurring expat-scene clubs** (Madrid, Lisbon, Milan, Budapest, Prague all have one, but no touring headliners) — and most marquee names you'd expect skip the continent entirely: **Mulaney, Burr, McIntyre, Ranganathan** are all US/UK-only this year [[52]](https://www.songkick.com/artists/577250-john-mulaney)[[47]](https://www.songkick.com/artists/445665-bill-burr)[[54]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macnificent_Tour)[[53]](https://www.romeshranganathan.co.uk/tour/).

**Scope.** Confirmed English-language comedy — international stand-up tours and the year-round English-language club scene — in the 17 destination cities, for shows on/after **9 Jun 2026** through Dec 2026 (reference date 2026-06-09). "Recurring" entries are clubs that run English nights every week year-round; treat them as the reliable fallback when no tour is in town. Out-of-window and not-coming acts are in the footer.

Two festival/residency clusters are worth calling out. **Amsterdam's [Boom Chicago Comedy Festival](https://boomchicago.nl/comedy-festival-2026/)** runs 25 Jun–5 Jul, concentrating most of the Boom Chicago names above plus Mike Wozniak's "The Bench" (4 Jul), Nadine Froughi and Louis Hanson into ten days [[8]](https://boomchicago.nl/comedy-festival-2026/). **Zürich's [Comedy Kiss](https://www.comedykiss.ch/zurich-shows)** is effectively a rolling autumn season of named UK/US/Irish acts (the five dates above), making Zürich the most reliable city after London for a specific headliner [[13]](https://www.comedykiss.ch/zurich-shows). London's permanent backstop is the West End: **["The Play That Goes Wrong"](https://www.mischiefcomedy.com/whats-on/the-play-that-goes-wrong/london/)** by Mischief Theatre runs open-ended at the Duchess Theatre from £25 — the one Mischief product you can actually see on the continent's edge this year [[6]](https://www.mischiefcomedy.com/whats-on/the-play-that-goes-wrong/london/).
