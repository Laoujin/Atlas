The taxi piece. Discover Soča Valley taxi will collect you, wait through the 3–4 hour meal, and drive you back to your accommodation[17] — pre-book, this is not a rank-up service.[4] Kobarid → Staro Selo is roughly €14–18.[9] The Nomago bus runs only four times a day on weekdays (€3, 5 min)[9] — useless for a 22:00 dinner finish.

Not walking distance. But the most romantic alternative if you are driving anyway.

Sequence at check-in

  1. Submit dinner and room together. Request the on-site room in the same submission as your dining reservation. The room is reviewed manually and is not auto-confirmed.[1]
  2. If the room comes back declined, book Žonir for the same date. Its host's free transfer removes the taxi problem.[8]
  3. If Žonir is also full, take a well-rated Kobarid address and pre-book the taxi both ways. Do not plan to walk home in the dark on the lime-tree avenue.[10]
