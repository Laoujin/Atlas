> **TL;DR — Within 30 km of Hiša Franko there are no traditional IT/developer conferences in 2026.** The closest tech-flavoured options are two PRC digital-skills workshops in Tolmin (AI primer 9 Jun, Google Analytics 16 Jun) [[5]](https://www.prc.si/novice-in-dogodki/dogodki)[[6]](https://www.prc.si/novice-in-dogodki/dogodki), [Coworking BAZA Tolmin](http://cw-baza.si/en/home/) as a rentable venue [[8]](http://cw-baza.si/en/home/), and [Hubs Travel's](https://www.hubs.travel/destinations/slovenian-alps-bovec) Bovec workation — winter-only (5 Jan – 15 Apr 2026), already closed by late May [[9]](https://www.hubs.travel/destinations/slovenian-alps-bovec). For a real dev/security conference, the nearest options are 60+ km away: POPRI in Nova Gorica (April, past) [[14]](https://www.mamarketing.si/organizacija-dogodka-popri2026/) or Ljubljana (~110 km) for BSides and DragonHack [[12]](https://0x7ea.bsidesljubljana.si/)[[13]](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/dragonhack-2026-tickets-1964551314867).

## What's actually on offer

### Tolmin — PRC workshops + a coworking venue

[Posoški razvojni center](https://www.prc.si/) (PRC) is the regional development agency for the Bovec–Kobarid–Tolmin municipalities and runs most of the area's digital/entrepreneurship programming [[15]](https://www.prc.si/). Their 2026 calendar has two items that touch IT, both in Tolmin:

- **9 Jun 2026 — "Umetna inteligenca za vsak dan"** — beginner AI literacy ("AI for everyday use"); not a conference, a single-session workshop [[5]](https://www.prc.si/novice-in-dogodki/dogodki).
- **16 Jun 2026 — "Upravljanje spletnih strani in osnove Google analitike"** — website management and Google Analytics basics [[6]](https://www.prc.si/novice-in-dogodki/dogodki).

PRC also runs a recurring **podjetniški zajtrk** (entrepreneur breakfast) at Kinogledališče Tolmin — finance, digital marketing, online platforms; the 8th edition fires on 3 Jun 2026 [[7]](https://www.prc.si/novice-in-dogodki/novice/4-podjetniski-zajtrk-v-tolminu-navdusil-tudi-bodoce-podjetnike-iz-programa-poni). Audience is small-business owners and PONI-programme participants, not developers.

**[Coworking BAZA](http://cw-baza.si/en/home/)** (Cankarjeva 3, Tolmin) is the only dedicated coworking space in the radius — 12 desks across 90 m², rentable whole or in parts for workshops and pop-ups, but their event slate currently reads "no upcoming events" [[8]](http://cw-baza.si/en/home/). Useful as a drop-in workspace, not as an event you can attend.

### Bovec — workations, not conferences

**[Hubs Travel's](https://www.hubs.travel/destinations/slovenian-alps-bovec) Slovenian Alps coliving** runs only in winter: **5 Jan – 15 Apr 2026**, €800/600/400 per month sliding price for a private room with shared kitchen and coworking [[9]](https://www.hubs.travel/destinations/slovenian-alps-bovec). It's a coliving residency for ~15–20 nomads (shared dinners, outdoor trips, workshops by request) — not a fixed-date conference [[10]](https://andysto.com/hubs-travel-workation-in-bovec-slovenia/). The 2026 winter cohort is already closed by the time of a May–Oct visit.

PRC also piloted a **"Digital Workation in Bovec"** in late 2024 — an experimental remote-worker residency with culinary workshops and adventure activities, limited cohort, first edition [[11]](https://www.soca-valley.com/en/soca-valley/news/2024120212170892/first-digital-workation-in-bovec/). No 2026 successor has been announced as of late May 2026.

### Cividale del Friuli — nothing tech

Within 30 km on the Italian side, [Cividale del Friuli](https://www.comune.cividale-del-friuli.ud.it/) is heritage/cultural (Mittelfest, Ville Aperte) — no developer or IT conferences indexed for 2026 in regional calendars. The nearest Italian tech scene starts in Udine (~50 km) and Trieste (~120 km).

## Practical take

If "tech event" can mean "a place to plug in and meet other remote workers," book a day at [Coworking BAZA Tolmin](http://cw-baza.si/en/home/) (15 min drive from Hiša Franko) or time the visit to overlap with a PRC June workshop. If "tech event" means a CFP-driven conference with talks, accept that the closest one requires a ≥1h drive to Nova Gorica or a 1.5–2h drive to Ljubljana, and there's nothing dated in the immediate region for the rest of 2026 as of this writing.
