The picks

Hiša Franko rooms

Staro selo 1, ~3 km from Kobarid · 14 rooms / 28 beds · 1861 building

Ana Roš's farmhouse has been "lovingly preserved and still full of soul" — no TVs, minimal electronics, each window framing meadows and mountain[1]. Bookings are released only when you also reserve dinner, and capped at one night per stay[16]. Practical truth: the soca-valley.com inventory page calls the rooms "modest" — this is not a luxury hotel, it's the chef's house above the dining room[2].

  • ✓ Zero transit after the tasting menu
  • ✓ 10 main rooms + 4-room depandance[2]
  • ⚠ Auto-confirmed? No — tick the room box, wait for the email[16]

Nebesa Chalets

Livek 39 · ~7 km · 4 chalets · 1000 m elevation

Four pared-back chalets for two on a panoramic deck above the Soča valley, with a private wellness room and wine cellar[3]. Holds a 2025 MICHELIN Key — the Guide's hotel-side equivalent of a star, awarded for "exceptional hospitality and the art of stillness"[4]. Sold as 2-night packages (€1,650 winter → €1,930 summer for two)[3].

  • ✓ Best view in the valley, full stop
  • ✓ Couples-only — adults, no children's facilities
  • ⚠ Two-night packages → can't pair with a single HF night without overlap

Hotel Dobra Vila

Bovec, 200 m from centre · ~22 km from HF · 11 rooms

A boutique hotel inside a converted 1930s telephone central office: Art Deco rooms, a library lined with eclectic books, central fireplace connecting two sitting rooms, a wine boutique, and a 1932 motorcycle parked in the front window[5]. Comfort and the breakfast buffet draw the loudest praise in recent reviews[5].

  • ✓ Stay multiple nights and explore Bovec rafting/Kanin from a real base
  • ✓ Best "design hotel" character in the valley
  • → 25–35 min taxi to HF each way

Tourist Farm Žvanč

Drežniške Ravne 33 · 7 km from Kobarid · 600 m elevation

An organic dairy farm at the foot of Mt Krn, run by the Koren family. Cows, sheep, and a local goat breed; cheese made on an open fire with tastings included[7]. €70 low / €90 high season for two — easily the best value-per-character on this list[6].

  • ✓ Real working farm, not "agriturismo-styled" decor
  • ✓ Drežnica village is itself a draw — mountain church, herders
  • ⚠ Single apartment (5+2 pax); book early

Koren Eco Chalets

Ladra, ~3 km from Kobarid · 6 wooden chalets + Lushna glamping

Wooden two-storey chalets on forest-covered terraces directly above the Soča river[8]. Strongest eco credentials of any property here: one of the first Slovenian sites awarded the EU Eco-label, Alan Rogers Award 2010, "Slovenia Green Accommodation" 2016[9]. Adjacent Lushna glamping cabins use natural spruce/larch and woodchip insulation.

  • ✓ Wake up to the Soča literally below you
  • ✓ Family-friendly; chalets sleep 4
  • → Walk to Napoleon's Bridge, taxi the 3 km to HF

Pristava Lepena

Lepena Valley, Triglav NP · 25–30 km / 40 min from Kobarid[11]

A cluster of "old Trentarian style" cottages on a plateau above the Soča, with its own stable of Lipizzaner and Andalusian horses — guests of any level can ride them into the national park[10]. Fairytale-woodland setting; the borderline case for "taxi range" on this list.

  • ✓ The only Slovenia stay where you can ride a Lipizzaner from your door
  • ⚠ 40-min taxi each way to HF — confirm a return time with the driver[15]
  • → Best if you're already pairing the trip with the upper valley (Kanin, Bovec)

Tourist Farm Kranjc

Koseč · 6 km from Kobarid · 600 m, beneath Mt Krn

Family-run farm stay sitting just below Triglav National Park, with traditional dinners from the kitchen garden and direct access to the Kozjak waterfall trail and Koseč Walk-of-Peace WWI sites[12]. Lower-key than Žvanč but a comparable budget-with-character pitch.

  • ✓ Quietest setting at 600 m above the valley floor
  • ✓ Strong base for hikers

Hiša Sonca · Hiša Kala

Kobarid town · ~3 km to HF · town-house apartments

If you want to walk to dinner in town, then taxi the last 3 km, these are the two character apartment houses in central Kobarid. Hiša Sonca has two apartments and two rooms, plus a Finnish/infrared sauna and 2-person massage tub — 100 m from the centre[13]. Hiša Kala is a renovated single-floor apartment on historic Gregorčičeva street with a private garden terrace and sole occupancy of the first floor[14].

  • ✓ Walking distance to the Kobarid Museum and Napoleon's Bridge
  • → Better as a 2-3 night base than as a 1-night HF anchor

Taxi logistics for the HF dinner

Local Soča Valley taxi services explicitly market themselves as Hiša Franko partners — they pick up from any lodging in Kobarid, Bovec, or Tolmin, wait through the 3–4 hour tasting menu, and return you afterwards[15]. Standard pickup is ~2 h 15 min before reservation time[15]. Phone +386 51 694 415 (Discover Soča Valley / VisitSoča). Prices aren't published — quoted on request based on pickup point and party size[15].

Practical pairing: book the one-night Hiša Franko room on the dinner night[16], then a second night somewhere with more character or amenity than the on-site rooms offer (Nebesa, Dobra Vila, Žvanč). The HF rooms are intentionally simple[2] — they're for not driving after wine, not for a relaxing morning.
