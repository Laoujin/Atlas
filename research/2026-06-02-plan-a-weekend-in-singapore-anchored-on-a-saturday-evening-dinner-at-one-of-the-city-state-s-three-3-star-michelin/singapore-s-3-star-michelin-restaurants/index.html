⚠ Zén is closed from June 2, 2026 — indefinite renovation, no reopen date announced.[14] The Saturday evening decision is between two:
Pick Odette (French-Asian, National Gallery, freshly renovated Dec 2025, from SGD 498++) for layered culinary storytelling with Asian soul.[8]
Pick Les Amis (classical French, Orchard, est. 1994, from SGD 565++) for Robuchon-lineage rigour and one of Asia's finest French wine cellars.[5]

Singapore holds three restaurants at the Michelin three-star level — the Guide's highest distinction, awarded to "exceptional cuisine worth a special journey."[1] All three retained their stars in the 2025 edition: Les Amis and Odette have held three stars since 2019, Zén since 2021.[2][3]
