> **Decision:** No conference-scale IT event sits inside a strict 30 km of Schauenstein. Chur (≈25 km [[1]](https://gemeinde-schweiz.ch/kanton-graubuenden/fuerstenau/)) hosts a small recurring circuit — KI-Meetup GR, KMU-Impuls AI/Innovation evenings at ibW, GRdigital networking — all weekday early-evening, free, German-language. For an actual tech-conference anchor, look to **Davos Tech Summit, 1–4 Jul 2026** (Physical AI, 31 km direct / 47 km road [[2]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Davos/F%C3%BCrstenau-Switzerland)), whose Saturday family day slots cleanly into a Sat-evening Schauenstein dinner [[10]](https://davostechsummit.com/).

## Geography

Fürstenau sits in the Domleschg valley, ~25 km southwest of Chur down the A13 [[1]](https://gemeinde-schweiz.ch/kanton-graubuenden/fuerstenau/). A strict 30 km radius captures Thusis (3 km), Sils im Domleschg, Domat/Ems, Chur, Tiefencastel and the Lenzerheide approaches. Davos is 31 km as the crow flies but 47 km by road via Tiefencastel–Wiesen [[2]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Davos/F%C3%BCrstenau-Switzerland) — technically *outside* the line, in practice the only nearby place that hosts conference-scale IT events. Vaduz, the OST Buchs campus (~35 km from Chur) and Bad Ragaz all sit further out [[16]](https://www.ost.ch/de/forschung-und-dienstleistungen/technik/systemtechnik/technologietag-2026).

## Inside the 30 km circle — Chur's recurring tech scene

Chur is the only city in the radius with an actual IT/digital programme, and it is firmly meetup-scale: 1–3 hour weekday events, mostly hosted at ibW Höhere Fachschule or FH Graubünden, organised by the cantonal digitalisation association [GRdigital](https://www.grdigital.digital/de) [[17]](https://www.grdigital.digital/de) and the Bündner Gewerbeverband. Free, German-language, ~30–80 attendees. Useful if you're already there for dinner; not a trip-anchor on its own.

Notable absences inside 30 km: no Reddit r/programming-style hackathon, no Swiss IT Forum stop, no swissICT chapter event, no dev.events listing for the Graubünden capital. The Chur IT scene is institutional (FHGR, ibW, GRdigital) rather than community-driven.

## Just outside the line — Davos as the conference magnet

Davos is 31 km direct / 47 km road [[2]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Davos/F%C3%BCrstenau-Switzerland). It technically falls outside the 30 km framing; for any weekend trip planning purpose, treat it as the only "real conference" option in striking distance.

## Gaps and stale signals

- **KI-Meetup GR Nr. 8** is the obvious next iteration after Nr. 7 (May 2026) [[3]](https://eventfrog.ch/en/p/science-and-technology/ki-meetup-graubuenden-nr-7-7447191608016009720.html); the series cadence (~3–4 months) makes a Sep–Oct 2026 date plausible but it is not yet listed on the GRdigital calendar.
- **AREON Eventhalle** opens September 2026 [[8]](https://areonchur.ch/) — the Wirtschaftsforum on 9 Oct is one of its first announced events; expect more late-2026 / 2027 congress bookings to land here that aren't yet visible.
- **OST Technologietag** (Tue 9 Jun 2026, Buchs SG) is a real engineering conference but Buchs is ~35 km from Chur and well outside the Schauenstein 30 km circle [[16]](https://www.ost.ch/de/forschung-und-dienstleistungen/technik/systemtechnik/technologietag-2026); flagged for completeness, not as a recommendation.
