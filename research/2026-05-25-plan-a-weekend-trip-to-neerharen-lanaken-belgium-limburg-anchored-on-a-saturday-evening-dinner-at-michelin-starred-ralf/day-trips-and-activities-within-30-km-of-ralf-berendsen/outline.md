Maastricht (NL, ~10 km): Vrijthof, Bonnefantenmuseum, Sint-Pietersberg caves/fort, Boekhandel Dominicanen, Stokstraat shopping. What's the one-day "best of" loop?
Hoge Kempen National Park + Maasmechelen (BE, ~5–15 km): Mechelse Heide, Connecterra gateway, Maasmechelen Village outlet, Terhills resort. Hike/bike/shop combinations.
Bokrijk open-air museum + Genk (BE, ~15–25 km): Bokrijk historic village, Cycling Through Water (Fietsen door het Water), C-Mine ex-coal site, La Biomista. Family/cultural axis.
Tongeren (BE, ~15 km): Belgium's oldest city, Gallo-Roman Museum, Sunday antique market (largest in Benelux), basilica. Historic-half-day candidates.
Hasselt (BE, ~25 km): Jenever Museum, fashion district, Japanese Garden, Z33 contemporary art. Belgian Limburg capital — what's worth the drive?
Valkenburg + Dutch hill country (NL, ~20–30 km): Valkenburg castle ruins, marl caves, Thermae 2000, Drielandenpunt (Vaals 3-country point), Margraten cemetery. Best Dutch-side picks within 30 km.
