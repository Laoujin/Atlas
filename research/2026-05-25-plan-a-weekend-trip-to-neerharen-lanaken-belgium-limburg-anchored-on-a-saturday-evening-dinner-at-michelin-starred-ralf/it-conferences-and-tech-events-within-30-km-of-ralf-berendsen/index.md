## The six headline conferences

### [BSides Limburg 2026][c6] — Corda Campus, Hasselt — 12-13 Mar

Community cybersecurity conference (workshop day Thu 12 Mar, conference day Fri 13 Mar) [[7]](https://www.bsides-limburg.be/). The BSides format: speakers and topics rejected from mainstream cons, plus a strong local infosec community vibe [[8]](https://cybersecuritycoalition.be/event/bsides-limburg-2026/). **Best weekend-pairing candidate**: Fri-night conference close → 25 min drive south to Ralf Berendsen for the Sat dinner. Venue is ~5 min walk from Kiewit station, easy E313/E314 access [[26]](https://www.cordacampus.com/).

### [ICT&health World Conference 2026][c1] — MECC Maastricht — 27-29 Jan

3-day digital-health conference focused on how digital innovation is changing healthcare delivery, co-organised with LIOF, Brightlands Heerlen and Maastricht, and 8 ecosystem partners [[27]](https://www.brightlands.com/en/event/icthealth-world-conference-2026). 7 km from the restaurant — closest venue of the year [[3]](https://nl.distance.to/Lanaken/Maastricht).

### [IEEE WCCI 2026][c10] — MECC Maastricht — 21-26 Jun

The flagship AI/computational intelligence event of 2026 in the region. Three IEEE-CIS conferences bundle into one week: IJCNN (neural networks), FUZZ-IEEE, and CEC (evolutionary computation) [[10]](https://attend.ieee.org/wcci-2026/). ~1500 scientists; topics include LLM explainability, neural networks, evolutionary and fuzzy systems [[11]](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/news/maastricht-will-host-2026-world-conference-computational-intelligence). Hybrid format — in-person sessions during local daytime, online sessions overnight to cover global time zones [[25]](https://www.mecc.nl/en/events/wcci-2026/). Co-chaired by Anna Wilbik (Maastricht) and Christian Wagner (Nottingham) [[10]](https://attend.ieee.org/wcci-2026/).

### [BNAIC/BeNeLearn 2026][c15] — Maastricht — 21-23 Oct

38th Benelux Conference on AI + 35th Belgian/Dutch Conference on ML — the reference regional AI/ML conference [[12]](https://ict-research.nl/event/bnaic-2026/). Organised by Maastricht University's Institute of Data Science. Smaller and more regional than WCCI; better fit if you want Benelux-flavoured AI research rather than the global IEEE deluge.

### [Big Science Business Forum 2026][c16] — MECC Maastricht — 27-30 Oct

4th edition (after Trieste, Copenhagen, Granada). 1000+ representatives from scientific institutions (CERN, ESA-type orgs) and European tech companies. Procurement and technology-transfer flavour rather than pure dev/IT. Main conference days 28-29 Oct, surrounding programme on 27 and 30 [[14]](https://www.mecc.nl/en/leading-big-science-business-forum-event-at-mecc-maastricht-in-2026/). Early-bird €440 until 1 June 2026 [[13]](https://www.bsbf2026.org/).

### [FTI Festival 2026 — Limburg leg][c14] — Corda Campus Hasselt + Thor Park Genk — 16 Oct – 15 Nov

Month-long Flanders Technology & Innovation festival across 8 Flemish cities; Hasselt + Genk anchor the Limburg programme [[18]](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/en/2026/03/11/date-set-for-2026-flanders-technology-and-innovation-festival/). Tech safaris for entrepreneurs, STEM tours for kids, tech talks (including Frank De Winne), and a light festival; closes 13 Nov with the **opening of Corda Arena** (architects also did The Sphere in Las Vegas; 4000 seats) [[19]](https://www.cordacampus.com/nieuws/fti-festival/). Less a single conference, more a calendar of ~100 activities — pick individual events rather than commit to a "ticket".

## What's *not* in range

- **Aachen** (DE) — 33 km, just outside. RWTH Aachen and its conferences (e.g., Aachen Colloquium Automobile and Engine Technology) do not qualify under a strict 30 km radius [[4]](https://nl.distance.to/Lanaken).
- **Liège** — borderline ~30 km from Lanaken; some sources put it at 31-32 km by road. Treat as out-of-radius for safety [[4]](https://nl.distance.to/Lanaken).
- **Eindhoven, Leuven, Brussels, Antwerp** — all >50 km, well outside.
