§ 05Before you book

Five notes worth carrying

⚠ KubeCon Europe is not here

Frequent confusion: KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2026 is in Amsterdam (Mar 23 – 26). Cloud Native Denmark is the local community-grade substitute — different scale.

September → book hotels early

TechBBQ (late Aug), Nordic Fintech Week, Future Product Days, and GOTO all crowd into a 5-week window. Hotels go fast; the airport gets busy.

Language

Default to English at the international names. Driving IT and some IDA tracks run partly in Danish — check the program before buying.

The Michelin pairing

GOTO and Nordic Fintech Week share TAP1 in Carlsberg City — walking distance to several starred restaurants in Vesterbro and Frederiksberg. NDC and Codegarden at Øksnehallen sit inside Kødbyen, the city's restaurant district.

Workshop pricing benchmark

NDC 2-day workshops list at 8,900 DKK (~€1,195). Full-conference passes for the big internationally-attended events typically run €800–€2,000 depending on early-bird timing.

Confirm dates close to the trip

The full Copenhagen developer-events calendar (conferences + meetups) is maintained at dev.events/EU/DK/Copenhagen. Useful for confirming dates close to your visit.
