Quartier № 01

Refshaleøen

if dinner is → Alchemist
Reffen
The island afternoon. Industrial harbourfront with a chef's-island weight to it.

  • Copenhagen Contemporary — art halls
  • La Banchina — harbour-bath kiosk
Quartier № 02

Østerbro / Langelinie

if dinner is → Geranium or Koan
Calm Copenhagen — leafy avenues, the long harbour walk, Royal Yacht at anchor.
  • Juno the Bakery — cardamom bun
  • Langelinie promenade — walk past the Royal Yacht
  • Skip the Little Mermaid stop on the way

Gentofte (S-train)

if dinner is → Jordnær
