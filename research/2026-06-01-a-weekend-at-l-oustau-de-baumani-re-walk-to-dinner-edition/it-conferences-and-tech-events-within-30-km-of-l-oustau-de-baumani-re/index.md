> **TL;DR.** Within a hard 30 km radius of L'Oustau de Baumanière [[1]](https://www.baumaniere.com/en/infos-contact/) the IT-conference scene is thin. The only recurring **pure-IT** events are CIP-PACA's monthly *Incontournable CIPMed* evenings at Novotel Avignon Nord (decision-maker afterworks: 9 Mar, 13 Apr, 18 May, 15 Jun 2026 [[6]](https://www.cip-paca.org/evenements)) and the weekly research seminars at Avignon Université's LIA (Agroparc) [[9]](https://lia.univ-avignon.fr/home/). Pair them with **art‑tech crossovers** at LUMA Arles and the Rencontres d'Arles, plus AgriTech at MED'Agri (Avignon, 13–15 Oct 2026 [[22]](https://paca.chambres-agriculture.fr/toutes-les-actualites/detail-de-lactualite/medagri-2026-le-rendez-vous-incontournable-de-lagriculture-mediterraneenne-a-avignon)). For a real dev/cloud/AI conference, plan a 90 km drive to Marseille (e.g. **WAX**, 1 Oct 2026 [[25]](https://www.waxconf.fr/)).

## Ongoing communities and venues to keep tabs on

[**CIP-PACA — Club Informatique Provence Méditerranée**](https://www.cip-paca.org/) [[6]](https://www.cip-paca.org/evenements). The de-facto regional gathering for CIOs and IT partners — 50+ years old, runs *Incontournable CIPMed* evenings split by *territoire* (84 = Vaucluse/Avignon, 13 = Bouches-du-Rhône, 06 = Côte d'Azur), plus a *Soirée Festive* on 26 Jun 2026. Talks are practitioner retex, not vendor pitches; Avignon-area evenings consistently land at Novotel Avignon Nord. The 18 May 2026 session features the City of Avignon's CIO on sovereign telecom infrastructure with adista [[8]](https://www.adista.fr/evenements/afterwork-club-informatique-provence-a-avignon-le-18-mai/).

[**Laboratoire Informatique d'Avignon (LIA)**](https://lia.univ-avignon.fr/home/) [[9]](https://lia.univ-avignon.fr/home/). ~90 people, two teams: **CORNET** (networks, optimization, decision under uncertainty) and **SLG** (speech and language). Seminars are open, often in English, free to attend on the Agroparc campus. If you're in Provence in March or April 2026, there is a near-weekly speech/NLP or networks talk to drop in on.

[**La French Tech Grande Provence**](https://lafrenchtech-grandeprovence.fr/) [[14]](https://lafrenchtech-grandeprovence.fr/). Re-labelled French Tech Community for 2026–2028; covers Vaucluse + Pays d'Arles. Monthly afterworks rotate venues — the AI-themed afterwork on 21 May 2026 was at Gare Numérique Carpentras (~55 km, outside) but other 2026 editions land at Créativa Avignon, which **is** inside the 30 km perimeter.

[**Créativa — Pépinière d'Entrepreneurs du Grand Avignon**](https://pepiniere-creativa.com/) [[15]](https://pepiniere-creativa.com/). Largest startup incubator in the Sud region, on the Agroparc technopôle. Hosts thematic breakfasts and workshops (innovation, fundraising, growth hacking) open to non-residents on application.

## Art × technology — the regional speciality

Within 30 km, the *richest* tech-adjacent programming is curatorial/art-side, not engineering-side:

- [**LUMA Arles**](https://luma.org/en/arles/whats-on) [[18]](https://luma.org/en/arles/whats-on/symposium-e.a.t). The Frank-Gehry tower's 2026 cycle opens 1 May with [Camille Henrot's *In the Veins*][c7], a moving-image work on invisible circulations; alongside an ongoing **Experiments in Art and Technology (E.A.T.)** strand inherited from 2025's *Sensing the Future*. Symposia and screenings throughout the year, generally free with registration.
- [**ENSP Arles**](https://www.ensp-arles.fr/) [[16]](https://www.ensp-arles.fr/les-formations/bourses-post-master-intelligence-artificielle-2025-2026/). France's only national photography school runs a 2025–2026 **Post-Master Intelligence Artificielle** bursary; the in-house workshop runs 16–20 March 2026 at ENSP. The school's Fablab teaches generative-AI image production and post-production.
- [**Festival d'Avignon 2026 (80th edition)**](https://festival-avignon.com/en) [[23]](https://festival-avignon.com/fr/edition-2026/programmation/par-date). Three weeks in July. The *Spectacle Vivant, Scènes Numériques* programme is a professional strand on digital/augmented/virtual stagecraft, plus performances using AI-generated voices (e.g. Forced Entertainment's *Everything Must Go*).
- [**Les Rencontres de la Photographie d'Arles 2026**](https://www.rencontres-arles.com/en) [[20]](https://www.frequence-sud.fr/art-39056-les_rencontres_de_la_photographie_2026_arles). 6 Jul → 4 Oct 2026, theme "Des mondes à relire" [[21]](https://arles.fr/actualites/les-rencontres-2026-un-nouveau-regard-sur-le-monde/). Generative-AI photography surfaces through the ENSP strand rather than as a headline theme.

## What's *not* here

A check against [dev.events' France calendar][d1] [[27]](https://dev.events/EU/FR) confirms there is **no DevFest, no Agile Tour, no JS/Python/Go conference and no hackathon-format event scheduled in Avignon, Arles or the Alpilles in 2026**. The closest such events cluster in Marseille (~90 km), Lyon (~250 km) and Paris. If a developer-conference itinerary is the goal, the Alpilles are a beautiful base but not a destination — anchor the trip on dinner at Baumanière and treat the regional tech calendar as a bonus, not the reason to come.
