The hotel that contains the restaurant. 53 rooms split across five buildings — L'Oustau, Le Manoir, Flora, Carita, and La Maison de Famille (12-person private villa, since 2023). Three pools, spa, tennis court. A private door on the estate opens onto a footpath to the medieval village above — for hotel guests only. Complimentary shuttle runs between the estate and the village (20–25 min on foot otherwise).

4 rooms above the restaurant at 4 Rue Porte Mages, inside the car-free medieval village. Panoramic terrace overlooking the Val d'Enfer. Walk to L'Oustau is downhill; the return after a long tasting menu is uphill in the dark — the cliff paths and approach roads are not strongly lit.

17th-century farmhouse at 555 Route des Oliviers (D27a), partly carved into the Alpilles rock. 22 rooms (19 original + 3 terrace rooms added 2024), troglodyte restaurant with fireplace, heated pool, EV charging. Sits on the east side of the village — 5 min to the village gate, then cross through (or take the road detour) to descend into the Val d'Enfer to reach L'Oustau.
⚠ Open late March to mid-November only

100-hectare estate on the western side of Les Baux — 18-hole golf course, spa, large outdoor pool. Hosts its own Michelin-starred restaurant: Aupiho (Lieven Van Aken, 1★), making it a two-Michelin-meal weekend if you pair Friday dinner here with Saturday at L'Oustau. ~1.6 km from the village centre, partly along the road to L'Oustau. At the outer edge of plausible walking distance — plan a 5-minute taxi or hotel transfer for dinner night.

Practical notes · Saturday-evening dinner

  • Book the L'Oustau table before you chase a room — reservations typically need weeks to months ahead. Don't let lodging logistics precede a confirmed dinner slot. [15]
  • Sleeping at Baumanière: the walk from table to room is the whole point — no designated driver needed. [1]
  • Sleeping at Reine-Jeanne or Mas d'Aigret: plan the return climb uphill in the dark. The cliff paths and approach roads are poorly lit — bring a phone-light.
  • Sleeping at Manville or further: a 5-minute taxi or hotel transfer is the practical answer for dinner night, regardless of map distance.
  • All top picks reduce service or close in winter — confirm dates before booking outside the late-March to early-November window. [2][7]
  • Unconfirmed: does a Baumanière room reservation accelerate a table booking? The property is a Relais & Châteaux estate where table and bed share an operator — worth asking directly when you call.
