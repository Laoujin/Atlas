## The lay of the land

L'Oustau de Baumanière (3★ Michelin, chef Glenn Viel) [[15]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/provence-alpes-cote-dazur/les-baux-de-provence/restaurant/l-oustau-de-baumaniere) sits at the **Mas de Baumanière** on Route du Val d'Enfer / Route d'Arles, at the foot of the cliffs *below* the medieval clifftop village of Les Baux [[14]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/restaurants/loustau-de-baumaniere/). A private door on the Baumanière estate opens onto a footpath that climbs to the village in about 5 minutes — but that path is for hotel guests only [[3]](https://www.audleytravel.com/us/france/accommodation/baumaniere-les-baux-de-provence). Anything else "walkable" means using the public road (D27 / Route d'Arles) or descending from the village car parks. The village itself is car-free above the gate, so even Les Baux-village hotels mean a short on-foot descent to the restaurant.

## The options

### Baumanière — the only zero-walk option
The 3★ restaurant is one of two restaurants on the estate; the 53 rooms are split across five buildings — L'Oustau, Le Manoir, Flora, Carita, and (since 2023) the 12-person private villa La Maison de Famille [[1]](https://www.baumaniere.com/en/accommodation/). Relais & Châteaux 5★, with three pools, spa, and tennis court [[2]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/hebergement/baumaniere/). 2026 rates start at **€325** for a classic room and reach **€1,500** for a Suite Prestige Luxe [[2]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/hebergement/baumaniere/). Two closures matter for shoulder-season planning: hotel **25 Jan – 5 Feb 2026**, restaurant **26 Jan – 6 Mar 2026** [[2]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/hebergement/baumaniere/). TripAdvisor 4.3 / 5 from 1,963 reviews; recent guests describe a **20–25-minute walk** to the village proper plus a complimentary hotel shuttle [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1819081-d219937-Reviews-Baumaniere_Les_Baux_de_Provence-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_C.html).

### La Riboto de Taven — the cheaper neighbour
3-room luxury B&B in the **Vallon de la Fontaine** at the foot of the same cliffs, family-run by Christine and Philippe; two rooms are troglodyte (carved into rock) [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1819081-d310356-Reviews-Taven_Residences-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html). Ranked #1 B&B in Les Baux, **4.9 / 5** (69 TripAdvisor reviews) — the highest-rated property in the village by some margin [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1819081-d310356-Reviews-Taven_Residences-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html). The site previously held a Michelin star as a restaurant; the family now runs a small table d'hôtes on the grounds. ⚠ Only 3 rooms — book months ahead. ⚠ Verify with the property that they are open for your dates — there has been historic chatter on TripAdvisor about the owners eventually retiring, but the listing remains active and reviewed [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1819081-d310356-Reviews-Taven_Residences-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html).

### Hostellerie de la Reine-Jeanne — budget in the village
4 rooms above the restaurant at 4 Rue Porte Mages, inside the medieval village; family-run, with a panoramic terrace over the Val d'Enfer [[9]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1819081-d219938-Reviews-Hostellerie_de_la_Reine_Jeanne-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_Co.html). TripAdvisor 4.0 / 5 across 123 reviews — solid value but two recurring caveats: **no air-conditioning**, and rooms sit directly above the restaurant so light sleepers may struggle on busy evenings [[9]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1819081-d219938-Reviews-Hostellerie_de_la_Reine_Jeanne-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_Co.html). Walk to L'Oustau is **down out of the village** (uphill on the return — relevant after a 3★ tasting menu).

### Le Mas d'Aigret — 4★ with the view, but east-side approach
17th-century farmhouse partly carved into rock, 22 rooms total (19 + 3 newer PMR/terrace rooms from 2024), troglodyte restaurant with fireplace, heated pool, EV charging [[6]](https://www.masdaigret.com/en) [[7]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/hebergement/mas-daigret/). Address **555 Route des Oliviers (D27a)** puts it on the *east* side of the village — 5 min on foot to the village gate, but to reach L'Oustau you cross the village (or walk around it on the road) then descend into the Val d'Enfer. TripAdvisor 4.6 / 5 (745 reviews) [[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1819081-d455258-Reviews-Le_Mas_d_Aigret-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html). 2025 published rates ran **€125 – €385**; 2026 not yet posted but the hotel only opens late March to mid-November [[7]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/hebergement/mas-daigret/).

### Domaine de Manville — luxury alternative at the edge of walkable
5★ resort on a 100-hectare estate with an 18-hole golf course, spa, large outdoor pool, and a **1-Michelin-star** restaurant — *Aupiho*, by Belgian chef Lieven Van Aken [[12]](https://www.domainedemanville.fr/en). 30 rooms plus 9 villas [[12]](https://www.domainedemanville.fr/en). ~1 mile / 1.6 km from the centre of Les Baux — a 12-minute stroll to the castle [[13]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1819081-d5895678-Reviews-Domaine_De_Manville-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html), more like 25–30 min on foot to L'Oustau. 4.6 / 5 across 1,619 TripAdvisor reviews [[13]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1819081-d5895678-Reviews-Domaine_De_Manville-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html). → Consider this if you'd rather sleep at a self-contained resort and **drive (or taxi) the 5 min** to L'Oustau on the dinner night.

### ⚠ Hotel Benvengudo — Route d'Arles address, but verdict: drive
Address 1800 Route d'Arles (D78F) [[10]](https://benvengudo.com/en/) — the *same* road that fronts Baumanière — and on a map ~1.4 km away. But the road has no proper pedestrian footpath in this stretch and recent guests are explicit: "would not stay here again without our own transportation" [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1819081-d219939-Reviews-Hotel_Benvengudo-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html). It's a lovely 4★ country house with 28 rooms in 4 hectares of garden, 4.5 / 5 on TripAdvisor [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1819081-d219939-Reviews-Hotel_Benvengudo-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html) — just don't book it on the assumption you'll walk to L'Oustau for dinner.

## Practical notes for a Saturday-evening L'Oustau dinner

- Reservations at L'Oustau de Baumanière (3★, Glenn Viel) [[15]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/provence-alpes-cote-dazur/les-baux-de-provence/restaurant/l-oustau-de-baumaniere) typically need to be made weeks to months ahead — book lodging *after* you have a dinner slot, since closeness is only useful if you're actually dining there.
- If sleeping at Baumanière: the on-site walk back to your room after a long tasting menu is the whole point — you do not need a designated driver [[1]](https://www.baumaniere.com/en/accommodation/).
- If sleeping in the village (Reine-Jeanne, Mas d'Aigret): plan the **return** walk uphill back into the village in the dark; bring a phone-light, the cliff paths and approach roads are not strongly lit.
- If sleeping at Manville or anywhere outside the village: a **5-min taxi or hotel transfer** is the practical answer for the dinner night, regardless of map distance.
- All three top picks (Baumanière, La Riboto, Reine-Jeanne) close or reduce service in winter — confirm dates before booking outside the late-March to early-November window [[2]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/hebergement/baumaniere/) [[7]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/hebergement/mas-daigret/).
