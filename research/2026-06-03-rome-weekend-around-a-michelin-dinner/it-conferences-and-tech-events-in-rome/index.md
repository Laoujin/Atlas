> **TL;DR** Rome's tech calendar runs in two windows. **March–June**: developer-focused events, heavy on free community days — Codemotion Roma (Mar 31, from €159), Cyber 4.0 Forum (Jun 3–4, free), Azure Day (Jun 19, free). **October**: the flagship stretch — [Cybertech Europe](https://italy.cybertechconference.com/) (Oct 13–14, La Nuvola), a Data Science & Cloud Summit (Oct 19–20), and [Maker Faire Rome](https://makerfairerome.eu/en/blog/maker-faire-rome-2026-opens-its-global-call/) (Oct 23–25, Gazometro) arrive back-to-back, ideal for a single autumn trip. Cybersecurity is the deepest track year-round: four distinct events from May through October.

## Upcoming Events

### Forum Cyber 4.0 — Jun 3–4 (starting today)

The 4th edition of [Forum Cyber 4.0](https://www.cyber40.it/eventi/) [[9]](https://www.cyber40.it/eventi/) opens today at Luiss Guido Carli University's Aula Chiesa. Two days of panels bringing together cybersecurity experts, institutions, and companies to examine emerging threats and policy direction. **Free attendance.**

### Azure Day Roma — Jun 19

[Azure Day Roma 2026](https://azureday.it/azure-day-2026) [[11]](https://azureday.it/azure-day-2026) is a free hybrid day at Microsoft Italia HQ (Via Avignone 10), organised by [Community DotNetCode](https://sessionize.com/azure-day-roma-2026/) [[12]](https://sessionize.com/azure-day-roma-2026/). Focus: intelligent agents and cloud architecture for architects, DevOps, and developers. Sponsors: Microsoft (Platinum), Relatech, Key2 Next. [Register free on Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.it/e/registrazione-azure-day-roma-2026-1985671434714) [[13]](https://www.eventbrite.it/e/registrazione-azure-day-roma-2026-1985671434714); lunch included.

### NGRome + JSRome — Jun 17–19 (postponed ⚠)

[NGRome](https://ngrome.io/home) [[10]](https://ngrome.io/home) is Italy's largest Angular conference — 7 editions since 2019, historically 600+ attendees from 15+ countries. The 2026 edition expanded to include a dedicated JSRome day for JavaScript talks, making it a three-day event. **Original June 17–19 dates postponed; new dates not yet announced.** Monitor [ngrome.io](https://ngrome.io/home) for updates.

### Cybertech Europe — Oct 13–14

[Cybertech Europe 2026](https://italy.cybertechconference.com/) [[14]](https://italy.cybertechconference.com/) at La Nuvola Convention Center (Viale Asia 41, EUR district) is Europe's premier cybersecurity gathering: government officials, CISOs, and executives from across the continent [[15]](https://www.sagemarketing.io/events/cybertech-europe-2026/). Format: two-day conference + exhibition, with both established vendors and a dedicated startup pavilion.

### Global Summit on Data Science & Cloud Computing — Oct 19–20

Two days of data science and cloud computing in Rome [[16]](https://dev.events/EU/IT/Rome/tech). Venue not yet confirmed; check dev.events for updates. Positioned between Cybertech and Maker Faire, it completes the autumn cluster.

### Maker Faire Rome — Oct 23–25

[Maker Faire Rome — The European Edition](https://makerfairerome.eu/en/blog/maker-faire-rome-2026-opens-its-global-call/) [[17]](https://makerfairerome.eu/en/blog/maker-faire-rome-2026-opens-its-global-call/) runs its 14th edition at Gazometro Ostiense, a cavernous former gas works in southern Rome [[18]](https://makezine.com/article/maker-news/calling-all-prototypes-to-maker-faire-rome-2026/). Scope: robotics, AI, sustainability, creative technology, and public science outreach — the broadest audience of any event on this list. The Global Call for Makers deadline was June 15.

## Earlier in 2026

### Rome .NET Conference — Feb 20

[Rome .NET Conference](https://dotnetconf.it/) [[1]](https://dotnetconf.it/) was a free hybrid day at Microsoft Italia HQ covering .NET 10, C# 14, ASP.NET, Azure, and DevOps [[2]](https://sessionize.com/rome-net-conference-2026/), organised by DotNetCode Community. Expected to recur annually; watch dotnetconf.it.

### Rome AI & Tech Week / Codemotion Roma — Mar 28–Apr 1

Co-organised by [Maker Faire Rome](https://makerfairerome.eu/eventi/ai-tech-week-2026/) [[3]](https://makerfairerome.eu/eventi/ai-tech-week-2026/) and [Codemotion](https://community.codemotion.com/codemotion-italy/meetups/ai-tech-week-roma) [[19]](https://community.codemotion.com/codemotion-italy/meetups/ai-tech-week-roma), the five-day event drew 3,000+ participants and 250+ tech leaders [[6]](https://www.innovacamera.it/area_letturaNotizia/633650/pagsistema.html):

| Day       | Format                           | Venue                                   |
| --------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Mar 28–29 | Hackathon "Coding for Good"      | ROAD Gazometro, Via del Commercio 9/11  |
| Mar 30    | Tech Lead Summit (CIOs, CTOs)    | ROAD Gazometro                          |
| Mar 31    | [Codemotion Roma Conference][d1] | Palazzo dei Congressi, Piazza Kennedy 1 |
| Apr 1     | Builders Lab (hands-on training) | Rome                                    |

[d1]: https://sessionize.com/codemotion-roma-2026/

Conference tracks [[5]](https://sessionize.com/codemotion-roma-2026/): AI/ML (advanced), Backend & Software Architectures, Cloud & DevOps, Data Engineering, Cybersecurity. Conference tickets from €159; the [hackathon](https://conferences.codemotion.com/rome-ai-tech-week/hackathon/) [[4]](https://conferences.codemotion.com/rome-ai-tech-week/hackathon/) was free with team matching available. Notable speakers: Adriaan Engelbrecht (Anthropic) and Enrica Maria Porcari (CERN) [[19]](https://community.codemotion.com/codemotion-italy/meetups/ai-tech-week-roma).

### RomeCup 2026 — Apr 28–30

[RomeCup](https://www.romecup.org/) [[7]](https://www.romecup.org/) is Fondazione Mondo Digitale's annual festival of robotics and responsible AI, held across Sapienza University (Marco Polo Building) and the Campidoglio. Theme: "WHAT'S NEXT? – Intelligences and talents in dialogue." Features 19 international robotics competitions (Rescue Line, Soccer, Explorer categories), hackathons, teacher training on AI, and a Research Award ceremony. Free public event.

### Red Hot Cyber Conference — May 18–19

[Red Hot Cyber Conference 2026](https://www.redhotcyber.com/post/red-hot-cyber-conference-2026-la-quinta-edizione-a-roma-18-e-19-maggio/) [[8]](https://www.redhotcyber.com/post/red-hot-cyber-conference-2026-la-quinta-edizione-a-roma-18-e-19-maggio/) was the 5th edition at Teatro Italia (Via Bari 18, 800 seats, 2 km from Termini). Day 1: ethical hacking workshops + CTF competition launch. Day 2: full conference with institutional panels, CTF awards at 18:00. Partner: Accenture Italia (3rd consecutive year).
