From the planning desk

Via Fava 18 · Orta San Giulio (NO) · 28 800 · Italia

Cari viaggiatori,

If you only have half a day, leave the car behind and walk: Villa Crespi → Sacro Monte → Piazza Motta → boat to Isola San Giulio. All under 1 km from the door, free entrance, about three hours.

For a full day, drive 23 minutes to Stresa and ferry-hop the three Borromean Islands. Or take the SP41 Armeno road up to Mottarone — the cable car has been closed since 2021 and is not coming back this season.

Two warnings on the back of this packet: Parco Pallavicino closes Friday 22 May for the Giro d'Italia, and the late-May lake is 15 °C — wetsuit weather. Tutto il resto è meraviglia.

Menu del Lago

Three things to ask for, within the Cusio circle
Tapulone BORGOMANERO

Slow-cooked stew of minced donkey meat with lard, onion, garlic, red wine, cabbage, bay, cloves and rosemary — served with polenta. The culinary symbol of Borgomanero. [76]

Miacce PIEMONTE

Paper-thin waffle-like discs made since the 15th century in long-handled irons over fire. Batter of milk, flour and eggs; round in Piedmont, paired with soft cheese or jam. [79]

Gorgonzola DOP NOVARA · VCO

Legally producible only in a defined set of provinces — including Novara and Verbano-Cusio-Ossola, putting the cheese's heartland directly around Lake Orta. [80]

Programma del Weekend

2 days · 2 lakes · 1 michelin night
SAB · 10:00
walk: Villa Crespi → Sacro Monte → Piazza Motta → boat to Isola San Giulio. Way of Silence loop. [free chapels]
SAB · 13:00
light lunch on the island — trattoria, then back to Piazza Motta for an espresso.
SAB · 14:00
choose one: Stresa + 1 island ferry (€16.90 day pass) OR Mottarone via Armeno (free road, 30 min winding).
SAB · 20:00
dinner at Villa Crespi — the anchor. Walk back uphill to wherever you're staying.
DOM · 09:00
drive to Stresa → Borromean ferry-hop · Isola Bella first, lunch on Pescatori, optional Isola Madre.
DOM · 15:00
finale — choose lake. Maggiore: Villa Taranto rhododendrons (€15). Orta: Madonna del Sasso cliff hike.
DOM · 18:00
if weekend = 30–31 May → swing through Cantine Aperte in Ghemme/Gattinara on the way back.
P.S. — Lake Maggiore activities cluster east of Mottarone; Lake Orta activities cluster west of it. Pick one lake per half-day. Late May = wetsuit weather for swimming (15 °C); pleasant for kayak / SUP.
