> **TL;DR.** Within 30 km of Villa Crespi, the only **upcoming** tech event in 2026 is **TEDxVerbania on Saturday 21 November** in Pallanza [[11]](https://www.ted.com/tedx/events/69414). The two real IT conferences in the radius — **Ambrosetti's Technology Forum** in Stresa [[6]](https://www.ambrosetti.eu/en/innotech-hub/technology-forum/) and **AUSED's CIO Leaders Summit** in Baveno [[10]](https://aused.org/grande-evento/cio-leaders-summit/) — both happen in spring and **already ran** this year (and both are invite-only anyway). For an open, walk-in tech conference you have to drive: Milan and Cernobbio are 75–85 km out, outside the radius.

### What each one actually is

- **Technology Forum (Ambrosetti / TEHA Group)** — the 15th edition wrapped on 22 May 2026, a week before this research. Two-day flagship for innovation strategy; 2026 agenda was agentic AI, AI Giga Factories, deep tech, nuclear innovation, future factories and European digital sovereignty [[8]](https://tg24.sky.it/tecnologia/2026/05/19/technology-forum-2026-stresa-programma-eventi-ospiti). Listed under "TLC, Digital e Cybersicurezza" and "Impresa" on the Italian institutional events portal [[9]](https://www.takethedate.it/Eventi/52505-technology-forum-2026.html). Access is reserved to InnoTech Hub Service subscribers [[7]](https://innotechhub.ambrosetti.eu/it/incontri/view/15908) — paying clients of Ambrosetti, not a public registration.
- **CIO Leaders Summit (AUSED)** — closed-door, by-invitation gathering of the CIOs of major Italian groups; ~80–100 CIOs plus supporting ICT vendors. Not streamed [[10]](https://aused.org/grande-evento/cio-leaders-summit/). Held annually at Grand Hotel Dino, Baveno.
- **TEDxVerbania** — the only **open-to-public, ticketed** tech-adjacent event in the radius this year. 2025 edition ran 19 Dec at Centro Eventi Il Maggiore; the 2026 edition moves to Saturday 21 November at Corso Zanitello 10 in Pallanza (Villa Giulia area). Theme not yet announced; speaker call still open as of late May [[11]](https://www.ted.com/tedx/events/69414).
- **Tecnologia e comunità oggi** — included for completeness, but it's a single 1-hour community talk (media-education expert Andrea Pozzi on relationships in the digital age) inside Borgomanero's "Fare Comunità" cultural programme, **not** an IT conference [[12]](https://www.visitborgomanero.it/tecnologia-e-comunita-oggi/). Already past.

## Why so thin

The Stresa / Baveno / Verbania strip is one of Italy's best-equipped congress destinations on paper — the Grand Hotel Dino's conference centre holds up to 1,200 delegates across 36 rooms [[13]](https://www.cvent.com/venues/baveno/hotel/grand-hotel-dino-lago-maggiore/venue-483d7f00-d871-4697-82af-5f0dc9406d3b), and Stresa's Palazzo dei Congressi auditorium seats 713 [[14]](https://thedmccollective.com/italy-event-venues/lake-maggiore/stresa-convention-centre-lake-maggiore/), with Verbania's Centro Eventi Il Maggiore as a third option [[15]](https://www.ilmaggioreverbania.it/). But the venues are used **as bookable lake-side stages by national organisers** (Ambrosetti, AUSED, medical congresses, finance off-sites) rather than as the home of a recurring local IT-conference circuit. There is no local Linux Day, WordCamp, PyCon, DevFest or Codemotion node in VCO or Novara province in 2026 — those cluster in Milan and Turin.

The lake summer calendar from June through early September is given over to the **Stresa Festival** (classical music and jazz, 16 Jul – 9 Sep 2026) [[16]](https://www.stresafestival.eu/stresa-festival-2026/) and to cultural / food events, not to tech.

## Bottom line for the weekend traveller

If the trip is fixed around dinner at Villa Crespi and you want to bolt on a real IT conference inside the 30 km radius, **plan for late November and aim at TEDxVerbania**. Otherwise treat Lake Orta as the off-site you go to *after* a conference in Milan or Cernobbio, not the conference itself.
