### Why Motor Valley Fest is the standout *right now*

You're reading this on May 28 — opening day. The Thursday-night keynote at **Teatro Storchi** is *"Global Trends, New Frontiers and Human Responsibility"* [[3]](https://tg24.sky.it/motori/eventi/2026/05/28/motor-valley-fest-2026-modena), an unusually serious framing for a motorsport festival. Friday's program is the tech-heavy day: tracks on software-defined vehicles, autonomous driving, connected mobility, space economy and virtual racing [[1]](https://www.motorvalley.it/motorvalleyfest/en/). Confirmed presence from Ferrari, Lamborghini, Maserati, Ducati, Pagani, Dallara and Accenture [[2]](https://www.motorvalley.it/en/news/motor-valley-fest-2026-four-days-of-innovation-culture-and-passion-for-motors-in-modena/). It is the 8th edition [[4]](https://www.quattroruote.it/news/eventi/2026/04/27/motor_valley_fest.html) and the closest Modena gets to a global tech-and-industry crossover event.

### The October double-bill is the better fit if you have flexibility

DevFest Modena (Oct 3-4) and Smart Life Festival (Oct 15-18) are explicitly co-themed around *"Intelligences"* — natural, artificial, human [[8]](https://emiliaromagnaturismo.it/en/events/modena-smart-life). DevFest is the warm-up: a community-organized two-day developer conference at Fondazione San Carlo with AI/Cloud/Web/WIT tracks, 600-800 attendees, 30-min sessions [[6]](https://sessionize.com/devfest-modena-2026). Smart Life is then four free days of public-facing talks, installations and panels across multiple city venues [[7]](https://www.smartlifefestival.it/) — much broader audience, low signal density for a developer but high signal for design / ethics / society topics. Sandwich them with a weekend in between for the Michelin dinner.

## Venues to know

The Modena tech scene clusters around three buildings. Useful for orienting a walking-distance hotel:

- **[Tecnopolo di Modena][16]** — UNIMORE research park; home of the new **UniMORE AI Center** (inaugurated March 13, 2026; 100+ AI researchers, AImageLab + ELLIS Modena + AIRI; *de facto* AI hub of Emilia-Romagna [[16]][16]). Hosts academic conferences like IRCDL [[17]](https://www.ingmo.unimore.it/it/tutti-gli-eventi/ircdl-2026). Industrial-zone location, not walking distance from the centro storico.
- **[Laboratorio Aperto Modena][18]** (ex-AEM power plant, restored) — coworking + 545 sqm event gallery, the city's innovation hub. Hosts Smart Life Festival sessions, Indie SaaS Day, GDG Cloud meetups, Talk Together IT-community sessions [[18]][18] [[19]][19].
- **Fondazione San Carlo** — historic theater and conference building; DevFest's home venue [[6]](https://sessionize.com/devfest-modena-2026), also a Smart Life Festival venue [[7]](https://www.smartlifefestival.it/).
- **Teatro Storchi** — Motor Valley Fest opening keynote venue [[3]](https://tg24.sky.it/motori/eventi/2026/05/28/motor-valley-fest-2026-modena).

## Practical notes

- **Most events are in Italian**, slides often English. Smart Life Festival in particular is largely Italian-language. DevFest historically has a mix.
- **Pair smartly with the Michelin dinner.** Motor Valley Fest is Thu-Sun; the Friday tech day pairs naturally with a Saturday-night reservation in Modena. The October arc gives more weekend choices but both DevFest (Sat) and Smart Life Festival opening night (Thu Oct 15) work as anchor points.
- **Reggio Emilia is 25 minutes by train** — worth noting if a meetup or restaurant pulls you there for a side trip [[21]](https://www.meetup.com/reggio-emilia-full-stack/).
- **Linux Day Oct 24** is a separate weekend from Smart Life — only worth it if open-source is your specific interest.
