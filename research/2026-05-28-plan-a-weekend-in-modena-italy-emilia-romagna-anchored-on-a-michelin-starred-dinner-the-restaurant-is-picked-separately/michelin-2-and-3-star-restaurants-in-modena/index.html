Decision: Modena's only 2★-or-3★ Michelin restaurant is Osteria Francescana (3★, Massimo Bottura, Via Stella 22)[1]. The 2-star tier is empty in the Modena city and province — there are no 2★ restaurants here in the 2026 Guida[2].

Practical: Anchor the weekend on Francescana — €350 tasting + €240 pairing, 11 courses, ~3 hours; book the moment reservations open[4][3]. If Francescana is full, the closest 2★ is San Domenico in Imola, ~1h by car[6][8]. For the second dinner, stay in Modena's 1★ tier — Al Gatto Verde, Cavallino in Maranello, or L'Erba del Re — all genuinely strong[11][13][16].

The 3★ — book this if you can

★★★ Osteria Francescana

Via Stella 22, 41121 Modena · Massimo Bottura

Three stars since 2012; twice World's 50 Best #1 (2016, 2018)[1]. Bottura's house in Modena's historic centre — small dining room, deeply theatrical food rooted in Emilia (mortadella, parmigiano, balsamic, tortellini) but pulled through contemporary art references.

Menu (2026)
"Ho Fame! Ho Fame!" — 11 courses, €350; wine pairing €240 (7 wines)[4][5]
À la carte
Available; tables of 5+ must take the tasting menu[3]
Booking
Online only via the restaurant's site; €300/person card hold 4 days before; cancel free up to 96 h prior, then full charge[3]
Duration
~3 hours; max 10 per booking; minimum age 12[3]
Dress
Smart casual — no jacket-and-tie requirement[3]

⚠ How to actually get in: tables open ~1 month ahead and disappear in minutes. Set a calendar reminder for the day reservations open for your target month and refresh at the top of the hour.

The 2★ — there are none in Modena. Nearest options:

If a 2-star dinner is a hard constraint and Modena city is just your base, two restaurants in Emilia-Romagna are reachable on a day-trip.

★★ San Domenico — Imola

Via Sacchi 1, 40026 Imola (BO) · Massimiliano Mascia · ~1 h drive (96 km) from Modena[8]

Italy's longest-running 2★: first star 1975, second 1977, held continuously since[7]. Cuisine is classic Italian by way of haute-French technique — the antithesis of Francescana's conceptual register. If you want a "great old-school grand restaurant" night to complement Francescana's avant-garde one, this is the move[6].

Signature
Uovo in raviolo "San Domenico" — soft yolk inside a single raviolo, tartufo, butter (the dish that defined a generation of Italian fine dining)[7]
Approach
Mascia keeps the kitchen deliberately off-trend — flavour and guest satisfaction over modernism[6]
Logistics
Easier to combine with a Bologna leg (30 min by train from Bologna[19]) than as a same-day there-and-back from Modena

★★ Magnolia — Longiano

Relais Villa Margherita, Longiano (FC) · Alberto Faccani · ~1 h 45 drive (~140 km) from Modena[9]

Romagna-hills 2★ in an 18th-c. villa — Faccani's "bucolic avant-garde" mixes career-favourite dishes with experimental newer plates[10]. Relocated from Cesenatico to Villa Margherita in 2017; veranda dining room with sweeping views of the hills.

Best for
Pairing with a stay in Romagna (Forlì-Cesena), not as a Modena day-trip — the drive is real
Cuisine
Local Romagnolo produce, fish-leaning, with a more technical / playful hand than San Domenico
→ Honest take: if your weekend is Modena-anchored, a 1h-each-way drive to Imola for San Domenico is the only 2★ that's actually weekend-compatible. Magnolia is a weekend in itself.

The 1★ tier in Modena — the realistic "second dinner"

If you're in Modena for two nights and Francescana takes one of them, the 1★ tier here is unusually deep. All three options below are run by or trained inside Bottura's Francescana Family ecosystem or share that lineage.

★ + 🌿 Al Gatto Verde — Casa Maria Luigia

Stradello Bonaghino 56, San Damaso (Modena) · Jessica Rosval · ~15 min from Modena centre[12]

Inside Bottura & Lara Gilmore's country-house hotel, Rosval cooks entirely over live fire — wood-fired, ember-cooked Emilian produce. Holds a 2026 Michelin star and a Green Star for sustainability[11].

Vibe
Pastoral, garden-to-fire, much looser than Francescana — explicit counterpoint, not a runner-up
Booking
Through Casa Maria Luigia's reservation system; non-refundable rate required; min age 15[12]
Open
Tue–Sun from noon; closed Sun & Mon nights[12]

→ The pick if you already have Francescana booked and want a thematically different second meal still inside the Bottura universe.

★ Cavallino — Maranello

Via Abetone Inferiore 1, 41053 Maranello (MO) · Riccardo Forapani & Virginia Cattaneo · ~25 min from Modena[15]

Newly starred in the 2026 Guida (awarded Nov 2025)[13]. The farmhouse opposite the Ferrari factory where Enzo Ferrari took lunch every day — reopened 2021 under Bottura's direction with his protégé Forapani in the kitchen[14]. "Slow food and fast cars" is the literal pitch[20].

Menus
"Welcome to Emilia" from €65 (+ €38 pairing); longer "Gran Turismo / Supercars" developed with the Ferrari Styling Centre[15]
Hours
Mon–Sat 12:30–14:30 & 19:30–21:45; Sun lunch only 12:30–15:00[15]

→ The pick if you're combining the weekend with a Ferrari Museum / Maranello morning. The "Welcome to Emilia" menu at €65 is the cheapest Bottura-touched Michelin meal you can book.

★ L'Erba del Re — Modena

Via Castel Maraldo 45, 41121 Modena · Luca Marchini · in the centre, walkable from Francescana[17]

The non-Bottura Modena 1★. Marchini opened in 2003 and has held the star since 2008 — 9 tables in a period palazzo near one of Modena's oldest churches, with a 700+ label cellar[17]. Cuisine described by the Guide as clean and balanced, rooted in Emilian tradition with experimental flourishes[16].

Booking
Tel 059 218188 / booking@lerbadelre.it[17]

→ The pick if you want a Modena-centre starred dinner that is not in the Francescana orbit — a different chef, different palate.
