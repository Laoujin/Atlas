§ V · Heuristics for picking Four rules surfaced by the calendar

The rules the grid makes obvious.

i.
Weekend overlap beats prestige.
PyData (Sat–Sun) beats London Tech Week (Mon–Fri) on this single axis, even though LTW is ~10× larger [1][2].
ii.
Free fringe > paid expo.
If you don't need badges or 1:1 meetings, the LTW fringe is the cheapest way to brush against the Microsoft / AWS / Anthropic crowd [15][21].
iii.
Bookend the dinner.
A Saturday Michelin booking pairs cleanly with PyData Saturday afternoon (Convene Sancroft is EC4M, walkable to most central restaurants) or a Sunday brunch + Big Data LDN Monday opener.
iv.
Venue distance is real.
ExCeL (Hardware Pioneers, Infosecurity) is in the Royal Docks — 40–50 min on the Elizabeth line from central. Olympia (LTW expo, Big Data LDN) is W14, ~25 min. Tobacco Dock (AI Summit) is Wapping, 15–20 min. Plan the dinner table accordingly.
