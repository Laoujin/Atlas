> **TL;DR — Pick the weekend.**
> If you want a tech anchor that overlaps Saturday & Sunday, lock in **Jun 5-7 for [PyData London](https://pydata.org/london2026)** at Convene Sancroft, St Paul's — tutorials Friday, talks Sat-Sun [[1]](https://pydata.org/london2026). It is the only major weekend-spanning tech conference in the 2026 run. Otherwise the **Jun 8-12 [London Tech Week](https://londontechweek.com/) megacluster** (Olympia + Tobacco Dock + ExCeL + ICC) is the gravitational centre everything else orbits [[2]](https://londontechweek.com/) — a weekend that brackets it (Jun 6-7 in, Jun 13-14 out) buys side-events and city buzz without the £2,699 VIP ticket [[7]](https://london.theaisummit.com/passes-pricing/). Skip both and the next anchor weekends are **Sep 23-24** ([Big Data LDN](https://www.bigdataldn.com/), free) [[10]](https://www.bigdataldn.com/) and **Oct 23 & 26** ([React Advanced](https://reactadvanced.com/) hybrid) [[11]](https://reactadvanced.com/).

**Reading the cluster.** Microsoft and AWS are LTW headline partners; Deloitte, Accenture, HSBC, Salesforce and ServiceNow round out the top tier [[21]](https://thequantuminsider.com/2025/12/25/london-tech-week-2026-registration-opens/). ## Decision: which weekend to anchor on

→ **Want a working weekend in the topic?** Jun 5-7, PyData London. Sat & Sun are full programme days; tutorials Friday for early arrivals [[1]](https://pydata.org/london2026).
→ **Want the tech-buzz weekend but no badge?** Jun 6-7 in, then leave Monday before LTW; or Jun 13-14 to ride the post-Tech-Week dinner-and-drinks tail. Fringe events run through Friday Jun 12 [[15]](https://londontechweek.com/fringe-events-2026).
→ **Want a free, weekday-but-skip-able expo to wander?** Big Data LDN, Sep 23-24, Olympia — free entry, 15 theatres, Snowflake/Databricks/Google Cloud booths [[10]](https://www.bigdataldn.com/). Pair with a Sep 26-27 weekend.
→ **Want a frontend-flavoured October weekend?** React Advanced, Oct 23 & 26 at [The Brewery](https://reactadvanced.com/) — day-1 in-person Friday, day-2 online Monday. Saturday Oct 24 stays free for the Michelin booking [[11]](https://reactadvanced.com/).
→ **Avoid:** mid-May 2026 if the dinner needs early-week capacity — DevOpsCon London ran May 11-15 and is past [[9]](https://devopscon.io/london/), but the late-May 2027 edition is the same window.

## Heuristics for picking

- **Weekend overlap matters more than prestige** for a leisure-anchored trip. PyData (Sat-Sun) beats London Tech Week (Mon-Fri) on this single axis, even though LTW is ~10× larger [[1]](https://pydata.org/london2026) [[2]](https://londontechweek.com/).
- **Free fringe > paid expo** if you don't need badges or 1:1 meetings. The LTW fringe is the cheapest way to brush against the Microsoft/AWS/Anthropic crowd [[15]](https://londontechweek.com/fringe-events-2026) [[21]](https://thequantuminsider.com/2025/12/25/london-tech-week-2026-registration-opens/).
- **Bookend the dinner.** A Saturday Michelin booking pairs cleanly with PyData Saturday afternoon (Convene Sancroft is in EC4M, walkable to most central restaurants) or a Sunday brunch + Big Data LDN Monday-Tuesday opener.
- **Venue distance is real.** ExCeL (Hardware Pioneers, Infosecurity) is in the Royal Docks — 40-50 min on the Elizabeth line from central. Olympia (LTW main expo, Big Data LDN) is W14, ~25 min from the City. Tobacco Dock (AI Summit) is Wapping, 15-20 min. ⚠ Plan the dinner table accordingly.
