> **Decision** — Within 30 km of Reale: **zero open IT conferences or tech meetups** in 2026. The only tech-adjacent thing in town is Eni-Joule's closed **MEAL** food-tech accelerator at Niko Romito's Academy (Demo Week 5-7 May 2026) [[5]](https://www.eni.com/joule/en-IT/media/2026/05/meal-demo-week-co-development-process-four-finalist-startups.html). For a real IT-conference fix, drive ~110 km / 1h46 north to **L'Aquila** [[14]](https://it.distanze-chilometriche.himmera.com/distanza-castel_di_sangro-l_aquila-carte_stradale_km_auto-95205.html) — Italian Capital of Culture 2026, with the L'Aquila Software Tech Forum (16 May) [[9]](https://uan.univaq.it/2026/05/06/allaquila-il-software-tech-forum-ricerca-e-industria-a-confronto-sulle-sfide-del-digitale/) and the FAIEMA AI conference (9-11 Sep) [[10]](https://www.faiema.org/) being the highest-value targets.

## The 30 km zone

Castel di Sangro sits in a remote Apennine valley. Its closest neighbours — Roccaraso, Pescocostanzo, Rivisondoli, San Pietro Avellana, Alfedena and Montenero Val Cocchiara — are small ski/mountain towns [[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castel_di_Sangro). The 30 km radius reaches the Molise border to the south, brushes Isernia at the very edge (30 km, 35 min drive) [[2]](https://it.distanze-chilometriche.himmera.com/distanza-isernia-castel_di_sangro-carte_stradale_km_auto-103212.html), and **stops short of every Abruzzese city**: Sulmona sits at 44 km / 45 min [[3]](https://www.percorsokm.it/it-it/percorso-da-Castel_di_Sangro-a-Sulmona), L'Aquila at 110 km [[14]](https://it.distanze-chilometriche.himmera.com/distanza-castel_di_sangro-l_aquila-carte_stradale_km_auto-95205.html), Pescara further still.

Castel di Sangro's own 2026 calendar — the "Muovi l'estate 2026" programme — is wholly sports: football, futsal, swimming, basketball, fencing, tennis, padel [[4]](https://www.laquilablog.it/castel-di-sangro-rilancia-la-stagione-sportiva-dal-1-maggio-al-via-muovi-lestate-2026/). No tech track, no developer meetup, no industry expo.

**MEAL** is the only thread tying Castel di Sangro to "tech" in 2026. It's a "Lab to Market" accelerator run jointly by [Joule](https://www.eni.com/joule/en-IT/) (Eni's accelerator), [Enilive](https://www.enilive.com/) and the [Accademia Niko Romito](https://www.accademianikoromito.com/), in which startups co-develop food-retail products in the Academy's laboratories [[6]](https://www.agrodolce.it/eni-enilive-e-accademia-niko-romito-lanciano-meal-il-primo-programma-lab-to-market-rivolto-a-startup-e-pmi). Demo Week (5-7 May 2026) was closed-door work sessions with four finalists — [Meala Foodtech](https://www.mealafoodtech.com/), Onima, [Summ Ingredients](https://www.summingredients.com/) and [Foreverland Food](https://foreverland.it/) [[5]](https://www.eni.com/joule/en-IT/media/2026/05/meal-demo-week-co-development-process-four-finalist-startups.html). **Not a conference you attend** — but worth knowing: the Academy sits ~500 m from Reale, so on the Saturday window the food-tech R&D activity is literally next door.

## What's *not* happening (so you don't go looking)

- **Sulmona** (44 km, just outside the radius): no IT/digital conferences in 2026; local calendar is concerts, food festivals, comedy.
- **Roccaraso / Pescocostanzo / Rivisondoli** (≤15 km, inside the radius): zero tech listings. These are ski resorts and FIS-cup hosts; calendars are winter races + summer hiking.
- **Isernia** (30 km, right on the edge): no tech-conference listings for 2026.
- **"Pescara tech conferences"** as listed on dev.events: **online-only** masterclasses accessible from Pescara, not physical events anywhere in Abruzzo [[7]](https://dev.events/EU/IT/Pescara/tech).
- **National-tier Italian tech events** — [WMF Bologna](https://www.wemakefuture.it/) 24-26 June [[13]](https://www.wemakefuture.it/), Cybertech Europe Rome, SPS Italia Parma — are all 300+ km away.

The honest takeaway: Reale's location is a remote-mountain feature, not a bug — but it does mean "IT conference within 30 km" returns the empty set. The closest *open* tech event with a credible 2026 date is **110 km away**.
