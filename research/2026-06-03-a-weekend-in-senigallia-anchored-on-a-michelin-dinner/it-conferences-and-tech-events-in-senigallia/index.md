> **TL;DR** Senigallia has no dedicated annual IT conference. The best tech-relevant fixture is [Fosforo](https://fosforo.me/it) — a 4-day science festival every late May with AI talks, 220+ free events, and ~15,000 visitors [[5]](https://fosforo.me/it)[[3]](https://www.senigallianotizie.it/1327661525/fosforo-2026-chiude-con-il-pubblico-delle-grandi-edizioni). Beyond that, the scene is civic digital-literacy talks and a regional dev network ([DevMarche](https://dev.marche.it)) with occasional meetups within 30 km [[10]](https://dev.marche.it/). No vendor expos, hackathons, or developer conferences are scheduled in Senigallia as of mid-2026.

## Fosforo — La Festa della Scienza

[Fosforo](https://fosforo.me/it) is Senigallia's flagship annual event: a science communication festival now in its 16th edition. It is not an IT conference, but technology and AI are woven throughout the programme each year [[2]](https://www.ansa.it/marche/notizie/2026/05/21/scienza-al-via-fosforo-2026-a-senigallia-che-diventa-capitale-della-divulgazione_5a94d26d-6818-4325-89c9-13a079cd9166.html).

**2026 edition (21–24 May):** 222 free events, 65+ speakers, 15,000 visitors across four days [[3]](https://www.senigallianotizie.it/1327661525/fosforo-2026-chiude-con-il-pubblico-delle-grandi-edizioni). The main AI session was *"The AI Era"* — a conference-spectacle by festival director Mattia Crivellini on generative AI and geopolitics — at Teatro La Fenice Grande, 22 May at 21:30 [[4]](https://www.quisenigallia.it/2026/05/22/la-sedicesima-edizione-di-fosforo-accende-senigallia/). All evening theatre sessions require free advance registration via fosforo.me.

The festival itself demonstrates applied AI: the 2026 website was built in weeks with ~120,000 lines of AI-generated code [[6]](https://www.vocemisena.it/scienza-fosforo-2026-intelligenza-artificiale-pannolini-lavabili-scoperta-fotografia-spazio-intervista-mattia-crivellini/).

| Venue                       | Format                      | Sessions                               |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Vallato della Rocca Roveresca | Outdoor main + green stage | Demonstrations, shows, live experiments |
| Teatro La Fenice            | Evening theatre             | Featured talks including *The AI Era*  |
| Rotonda a Mare              | Outdoor / experiential      | VR demos, interactive stands           |
| Biblioteca Antonelliana     | Indoor labs                 | School groups & family workshops       |

[Organised][5] by [Associazione Culturale NEXT ETS](https://fosforo.me/it) (founded 2011). **Next edition: 19–23 May 2027** [[3]](https://www.senigallianotizie.it/1327661525/fosforo-2026-chiude-con-il-pubblico-delle-grandi-edizioni).

[5]: https://fosforo.me/it

**Fit for IT visitors:** strong if you want a broad science+tech audience, free entry, and genuine AI content. Not useful for B2B networking, vendor demos, or developer-only discussion.

## Digital Day Senigallia 2026

A civic one-day event on **12 May 2026**, organised by FdI council candidate Giulia Marinelli [[1]](https://www.viveresenigallia.it/2026/05/06/digital-day-senigallia-2026-il-12-maggio-una-giornata-aperta-a-tutti-su-intelligenza-artificiale-innovazione-e-cybersicurezza/76790). Two back-to-back sessions:

- **15:00 — Digital Lab**, Palazzetto Baviera (Piazza del Duca): free hands-on AI demos with local young entrepreneurs.
- **18:00 — Cybersecurity conference**, Senbhotel (Viale Bonopera 32): keynote by Edgardo Fantazzini (CEO, Fortgale) on protecting data and operational continuity; audience of SME owners, professionals, and public administrators.

Attendees included Mayor Massimo Olivetti, MEP Carlo Ciccioli, and other institutional figures [[1]](https://www.viveresenigallia.it/2026/05/06/digital-day-senigallia-2026-il-12-maggio-una-giornata-aperta-a-tutti-su-intelligenza-artificiale-innovazione-e-cybersicurezza/76790). No evidence this is an established annual event — it appears to be a one-off political initiative.

## Tech it Easy / A.G.O.R.À.

A cycle of **three free seminars on generative AI** launched by the Comune di Senigallia as part of its A.G.O.R.À. project, which is transforming the Biblioteca Antonelliana into an open innovation hub for young residents [[7]](https://www.senigallianotizie.it/1327622456/tech-it-easy-al-via-a-senigallia-un-ciclo-di-incontri-sullai-generativa).

| Session | Date               | Topic                                                    |
| ------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| #1      | 22 Feb 2025, 11:00 | AI from fiction to daily reality — neural nets to GenAI  |
| #2–3    | Not published      | Further generative AI topics (same 3-session series)     |

Venue: Sala Conferenze, Biblioteca Antonelliana, Senigallia. Led by Mattia Crivellini (Fosforo director). Targeted ages 18–35. Free; registration via agora@comune.senigallia.an.it [[7]](https://www.senigallianotizie.it/1327622456/tech-it-easy-al-via-a-senigallia-un-ciclo-di-incontri-sullai-generativa).

## "Come Sopravvivere alla Transizione Digitale" Conference

Held **21 February 2025** at Auditorium San Rocco, Senigallia. Joint initiative of Comune di Senigallia, Università Politecnica delle Marche (UNIVPM), and Questura di Ancona [[8]](https://www.viveresenigallia.it/2025/02/24/un-successo-la-conferenza-sulla-transizione-digitale/28037).

Topics: digital rights and hate speech (Prof. Avv. Marta Cerioni, UNIVPM), police cybercrime enforcement, online fraud, and digital domicile legislation. Part of a broader regional tour covering **47 municipalities** in the Marche [[9]](https://www.senigallianotizie.it/1327622779/avviato-a-senigallia-il-progetto-come-sopravvivere-alla-transizione-digitale).

## DevMarche (Regional Developer Network)

[DevMarche](https://dev.marche.it/) is the network of software-development communities across the Marche region [[10]](https://dev.marche.it/). Events are held regionally — primarily around Ancona — but Senigallia is within 20–40 km of most venues.

Recent events (2025): AI careers, LLM integration with MCP, frontend trends, gaming, AI agent observability [[11]](https://dev.marche.it/eventi/). The **Summer AI Afternoon** (10 Jul 2025) ran at Croce Rossa in Marotta (~10 km north of Senigallia), with post-event dinner at Acquapazza in Senigallia [[12]](https://www.meetup.com/it-IT/devromagna/events/308666578/)[[15]](https://www.eventbrite.it/e/summer-ai-afternoon-tickets-1428458029419). Speakers have included open-source contributors such as Nicola Iarocci [[13]](https://nicolaiarocci.com/im-speaking-at-devmarche-summer-ai-afternoon/).

**How to follow:** Telegram group (primary community channel), LinkedIn, newsletter at dev.marche.it. To propose a talk: open an issue on [DevMarche/eventi](https://github.com/DevMarche/eventi) ⭐ 33 [[14]](https://github.com/DevMarche/eventi).
