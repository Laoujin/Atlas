Senigallia is unusual among Italian beach towns: 13 km of fine sand classified Blue Flag since 1997 [[1]](https://www.feelsenigallia.it/en/sea-and-beach/), and five Michelin stars split across two restaurants in the same stretch of coastline [[2]](https://www.senigallianotizie.it/1327644248/guida-michelin-2026-senigallia-conferma-le-sue-5-stelle-ma-perde-un-bib-gourmand). The beach day happens regardless of which restaurant you book — but the restaurant choice shapes the rest of the weekend more than it first appears.

**Restaurant choice drives the weekend's shape.** [Uliassi](https://uliassi.com/) (3★, €260–280pp) sits beachfront in central Senigallia, walkable from any city hotel, closed Monday–Tuesday [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g194914-d1100666-Reviews-Uliassi-Senigallia_Province_of_Ancona_Marche.html). [Madonnina del Pescatore](https://www.morenocedroni.it/en/madonnina-del-pescatore/) (2★, ~€230pp) is 7 km south in Marzocca — a taxi or short car ride — closed Wednesday–Thursday [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g194914-d1011774-Reviews-Madonnina_del_Pescatore-Senigallia_Province_of_Ancona_Marche.html). The styles diverge as much as the logistics: Uliassi is precision-anchored to the Adriatic; Madonnina ranges globally through Cedroni's fermentation lab and spice-route tasting menus [[5]](https://www.gastromondiale.com/madonnina-del-pescatore-senigallia/). A Saturday-night reservation fits either; a Friday–Sunday trip can fit both.

**The Giacomelli thread.** The most coherent cross-cutting sequence the research surfaced runs through Mario Giacomelli, the photographer born in Senigallia in 1925. MUSINF holds his complete donated anthology — the *Scanno* series, *Io non ho mani che mi accarezzino il viso* — along with the legacy of the Misa Photographic School he helped found [[6]](https://www.feelsenigallia.it/attrazioni/museo-arte-moderna-informazione-fotografia/). Uliassi's Lab 2026 menu is dedicated to Giacomelli's centenary, with colour-coded courses (Beige, Bianco, Verde Primavera, Nero, Giallo) that respond to his photographic palette [[7]](https://www.scattidigusto.it/ristorante-uliassi-a-senigallia). Spend the afternoon with the photographs — currently at Palazzo del Duca while MUSINF is under renovation [[8]](https://www.comune.senigallia.an.it/vivere-il-comune/luoghi/museo-darte-moderna-dellinformazione-e-della-fotografia/) — then the evening in a tasting menu that refracts them. This pairing is not advertised anywhere; it emerged from reading both children together.

**Day trips require a car.** All the options within 30 km — Corinaldo + Mondavio (~20 min), Numana/Conero riviera (~38 min), Gradara (~36 min) — need a car [[9]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Senigallia/Corinaldo)[[10]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Senigallia/Numana). Jesi (wine capital, ~22 km) is the one reachable by train in ~32 minutes [[11]](https://www.thetrainline.com/en/train-times/senigallia-to-jesi). Without a car, the weekend is beach + old town + museums — a full programme, not a compromise.

**Timing and caveats.** The Summer Jamboree (31 Jul–9 Aug 2026) brings ~400,000 visitors and books the Rotonda out for ten nights running [[12]](https://www.summerjamboree.com/en/summer-jamboree-2026/)[[13]](https://www.adriaeco.eu/2015/08/10/oltre-400-000-presenze-a-senigallia-per-il-summer-jamboree/). Late May–June and early September deliver beach weather without the crowds. On the food side: moscioli, the wild Conero mussels and a Slow Food Presidium since 2004, were under a de facto fishing ban in 2025 after a documented population collapse [[14]](https://www.cibotoday.it/storie/estinzione-moscioli-slow-food-moreno-cedroni.html) — don't plan around them appearing at either starred restaurant.

**The IT-conferences angle** produced no relevant findings for a weekend leisure trip. There is no annual IT conference in Senigallia; [Fosforo](https://fosforo.me/it), the science festival held in late May, is the only fixture with AI content [[15]](https://fosforo.me/it). It could be a reason to time a visit to late May, but contributes nothing to the dinner-centred itinerary otherwise.

The sharper open question: Uliassi's Lab 2026 is the Giacomelli centenary edition — a dated concept that rotates. Confirm directly when booking whether this menu is still running on your visit date, or whether Lab 2027 has replaced it.
