> **TL;DR.** One real anchor in this radius: **[KIKK Festival](https://kikkfestival.be/)** 21–25 October 2026 in Namur [[1]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/kikk-festival-celebrating-digital-world-namur) — Wallonia's flagship digital-culture event with ~50 speakers across AI, dataviz, design and XR. Outside KIKK week, the area is venue-rich but conference-thin: year-round programming at **[Le Pavillon](https://www.le-pavillon.be/en/events)** [[5]](https://www.le-pavillon.be/en/events) and **[TRAKK](https://www.trakk.be/)** [[6]](https://www.trakk.be/) in Namur, and Charleroi's **[A6K](https://www.a6k.be/)** innovation hub [[8]](https://www.a6k.be/) sitting right at the 30 km edge. If you want a developer-track conference (Devoxx, Java, AWS user groups), nothing fits — those all happen in Antwerp/Brussels [[15]](https://dev.events/EU/BE/Namur)[[16]](https://devoxx.be/).

## The geography

L'Eau Vive [[9]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/namur/arbre/restaurant/l-eau-vive) is in Arbre, a hamlet of Profondeville, about **12.5 km south of Namur** by road [[11]](https://www.marinareservation.com/distance/calculator/Profondeville/Namur). A 30 km circle from there covers Namur city centre, Dinant (~20 km south), Gembloux (~25 km north), Andenne (~22 km east), and reaches **just barely to Charleroi** at ~30 km west [[10]](https://be.toponavi.com/57061-28578). Brussels, Liège, Louvain-la-Neuve and Antwerp all fall outside the radius.

**KIKK is the one to plan around** [[1]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/kikk-festival-celebrating-digital-world-namur)[[2]](https://kikkfestival.be/). Five days, ~50 speakers on AI, dataviz, creativity, design, XR, music tech and branding; conferences for pros plus the city-wide "KIKK In Town" art trail and Le Market for prototypes. The Conference Pass covers the talks; the Full Pass adds exhibitions, workshops and parties. Topics lean creative-tech (artists, researchers, designers) rather than enterprise IT — if you want a JVM/cloud/DevOps track, this isn't it.

**Data Forum 2026** is sector-specific (public-sector open data, AI in administration, ministerial keynotes) and is happening **this Thursday** — only worth it if open data / govtech is your beat [[3]](https://www.eventbrite.be/e/digital-wallonia-data-forum-2026-focus-secteur-public-tickets-1987071780185).

**La Semaine Numérique** is broad digital-literacy programming aimed at families, seniors, and the general public, themed *"Aux rythmes de l'humain"* — useful for community-tech outreach, less so for a working engineer [[4]](https://lasemainenumerique.be/).

## What I checked and didn't find

- **Developer-conference listings (dev.events) show zero physical conferences in Namur for 2026–2027** [[15]](https://dev.events/EU/BE/Namur). The Java/PHP/JS/cloud conference circuit doesn't pass through the radius.
- **No GDG Namur, no AWS user group Namur, no Microsoft user group Namur.** GDG Mons and GDG Brussels are the nearest community chapters; AWS User Group Belgium meets in Brussels.
- **Predatory "conference alert" listings** for Namur (ICDLT, ICDMM, ICDS-SGES, ICETCS in June/July 2026) are virtual events using Namur as a nominal address and are not credible local conferences — ignore them.
- **Digitalize Namur** — the recurring digital-transformation trade show at Namur Expo (4 editions through Jan 2025) has been discontinued; no 2026 edition.

## Practical pairing with the L'Eau Vive Saturday

[L'Eau Vive](https://www.eau-vive.be/en) [[9]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/namur/arbre/restaurant/l-eau-vive) is closed Tuesday and Wednesday, and for Saturday lunch — Saturday **dinner** is the canonical slot. The KIKK 2026 dates (Wed–Sun, **21–25 Oct**) put Saturday **24 October** inside the festival, which is the obvious anchor: Saturday is a programmed conference day with the art trail running through the city centre. Book L'Eau Vive well ahead — Saturday evenings during KIKK weekend will be tight.
