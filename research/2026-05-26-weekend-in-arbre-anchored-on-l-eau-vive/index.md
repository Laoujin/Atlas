The dinner sets every other constraint. [L'Eau Vive](https://www.eau-vive.be/en) is a two-Michelin-star table in a 17th-century mill on the Burnot brook at Route de Floreffe 37, 5170 Rivière (Profondeville) — the Michelin entry says "Arbre", the postal section is Rivière, and the village of Arbre proper sits 2.5 km up on the plateau [[1]](https://www.eau-vive.be/en)[[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/namur/arbre/restaurant/l-eau-vive). It is closed Tuesday and Wednesday and for Saturday lunch, so Saturday **dinner** is the canonical anchor [[1]](https://www.eau-vive.be/en). Book several weeks ahead by phone — and if you intend to sleep within walking distance, book the bed in the same call.

**The walking-distance question has one answer.** The only door reachable on foot from the dining room is [Le Cube](http://www.eau-vive.be/en/le-cube), Pierre Résimont's own 150 m² two-bedroom cottage in the restaurant garden, €300/night for two, €350 for four, breakfast €20 pp (no breakfast Tue/Wed — irrelevant for a weekend) [[3]](http://www.eau-vive.be/en/le-cube)[[4]](https://www.eau-vive.be/hebergement/le-cube). If it is gone, the next-best move is [Espace Médissey](http://www.eau-vive.be/medissey) in Bois-de-Villers — 3 km away, also run by the Résimonts (named for their two children Mehdi & Issey), pool and hot tub, and the restaurant team can usually arrange a post-dinner transfer; confirm at booking [[5]](https://www.beperfect.be/leau-vive-pierre-resimont-30-ans-detoiles-au-compteur/)[[6]](http://www.eau-vive.be/medissey). Past those two, *every* option requires a taxi — [Allo Taxi Namurois](https://taxinamurois.be/transfert-vers-villes-proches/profondeville) and [Hepp'Taxi](https://www.hepptaxis.be/) are the 24/7 local operators; pre-book the 23:00+ return [[7]](https://taxinamurois.be/transfert-vers-villes-proches/profondeville).

**Pick lodging by the story you want.** The character-lodging map and the day-trip map overlap heavily: [La Maison du Meunier](https://maisondumeunier.be/fr/) in Crupet (16 km) and [L'Échappée Belle](https://www.lechappeebelle.be/en/lechappee-belle-en/) in Falaën (15 km) put you to bed inside the same *Plus Beaux Villages de Wallonie* you would otherwise drive to on Sunday morning — Crupet for the devil-tableau Grotte Saint-Antoine and the 13th-century Carondelet keep, Falaën five minutes from its castle ruin [[8]](https://maisondumeunier.be/fr/)[[9]](https://beauxvillages.be/en/decouvertes/grotte-saint-antoine-de-padoue-crupet/)[[10]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/echappee-belle.html). [River Lodge](https://www.riverlodge.be/en/) in Maredret (14 km) is a working watermill with a Titanic-cabin themed suite five minutes from [Maredsous Abbey](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/maredsous-abbey-cheese-and-beer-character) and its Saint-Joseph cheese-and-beer centre [[11]](https://www.riverlodge.be/en/)[[12]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/maredsous-abbey-cheese-and-beer-character). Sleep in Namur old town at [Les Tanneurs](https://www.tanneurs.com/en/index.php) (15 connected 17th-century tannery houses, ~15 km) and the [Citadelle de Namur](https://citadelle.namur.be/en/prices-and-timetables) is at your door for Sunday morning [[13]](https://www.tanneurs.com/en/index.php)[[14]](https://citadelle.namur.be/en/prices-and-timetables). All of this trades the in-garden seam between dinner and bed for a €25–30 taxi each way [[15]](https://taxi-profondeville-be.taxihowmuch.fr/).

**Time the weekend to KIKK if you can.** The only IT/tech event of any size in the radius is [KIKK Festival](https://kikkfestival.be/) — 21–25 October 2026, ~50 speakers on AI, dataviz, design and XR across Namur venues; Saturday 24 October is a programmed conference day with the "KIKK In Town" art trail running through the city [[16]](https://kikkfestival.be/)[[17]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/kikk-festival-celebrating-digital-world-namur). That is the one Saturday of 2026 where the dinner reservation will be hardest to land — call as early as you can. Outside that week, nothing else in the developer-conference circuit hits the 30 km circle (Devoxx is Antwerp, ~130 km) [[18]](https://devoxx.be/).

**One gap to flag.** The dinner mechanics that should feed every sub-topic — tasting-menu vs à-la-carte pricing, wine-pairing cost, meal length, dress code, exact reservation lead time, whether the table books with a Le Cube stay — were *not* directly verified in this run. Treat the cottage and the restaurant as separate bookings until L'Eau Vive confirms a package by phone (+32 81 41 11 51, [email contact](https://www.eau-vive.be/en)) [[1]](https://www.eau-vive.be/en).
