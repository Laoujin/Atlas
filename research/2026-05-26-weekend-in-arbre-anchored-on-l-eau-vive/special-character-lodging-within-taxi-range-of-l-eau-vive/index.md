> **Decision.** Book the chef's own **Le Cube** (in L'Eau Vive's garden, sleeps 6) or **Espace Médissey** (3 km, pool, full gourmet packages with the restaurant) if you want the dinner-and-bed seam to vanish[[1]](https://www.eau-vive.be/en)[[2]](https://www.beperfect.be/leau-vive-pierre-resimont-30-ans-detoiles-au-compteur/). For an actual *hotel* with riverside character, **Hôtel Vedette** in Profondeville (~4 km) is the local favourite, four-star, 6 rooms[[3]](https://www.hotelvedette.be/fr/). If you'd rather sleep in Namur old town and taxi south, **Les Tanneurs** (15 connected 17th-century tannery houses) is the high-character pick[[4]](https://www.tanneurs.com/en/index.php). Themed-room novelty seekers: **River Lodge** in Maredret (Titanic cabin, trapper's hut, in an old watermill)[[5]](https://www.riverlodge.be/en/).

## Where L'Eau Vive sits

Route de Floreffe 37, 5170 Rivière (Profondeville) — the section is **Arbre**, on the west bank of the Meuse south of Namur[[6]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/namur/arbre/restaurant/l-eau-vive)[[1]](https://www.eau-vive.be/en). Pierre Résimont's two-Michelin-star table is in a 17th-century mill on a small river, with a Gault&Millau score of 17.5/20[[6]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/namur/arbre/restaurant/l-eau-vive)[[7]](https://www.gaultmillau.be/en/restaurants/leau-vive-profondeville-arbre). Namur centre is ~13 km / 12–17 min north; a Namur ↔ Profondeville taxi runs ~€25–30 standard tariff, so anything within ~25 km of Arbre is a sensible after-dinner ride[[8]](https://taxi-profondeville-be.taxihowmuch.fr/).

## The chef's own beds (walk or 5-minute taxi)

### [Le Cube](https://www.eau-vive.be/en) — in the garden, sleeps 6 ⚠ whole-house
Modern cube-shaped guesthouse Pierre Résimont built next to L'Eau Vive, in the restaurant garden along the stream[[2]](https://www.beperfect.be/leau-vive-pierre-resimont-30-ans-detoiles-au-compteur/). 150 m², two bedrooms with showers, living room with sofa-bed, fully equipped kitchen, terrace with BBQ — rented as a whole house (no shared rooms), and you don't have to dine at L'Eau Vive to stay[[2]](https://www.beperfect.be/leau-vive-pierre-resimont-30-ans-detoiles-au-compteur/). Best for a family or two couples; quote pricing through L'Eau Vive directly[[1]](https://www.eau-vive.be/en).

### [Espace Médissey](http://www.eau-vive.be/medissey) — chef-run B&B with pool, 3 km
Six rooms in three tiers — *Standard* (Cocooning & Cosy), *Luxe* (Détente & Volupté, ~€150, jacuzzi with mood-lit ambiance), *Suite* (Bel Espace)[[10]](http://www.eau-vive.be/medissey)[[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g2316146-d2295097-Reviews-Espace_Medissey-Bois_De_Villers_Profondeville_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html). Heated outdoor pool, fitness room, garden, kitchen workshop[[12]](https://www.hotelbusiness.be/bb-espace-medissey-a-profondeville/?lang=fr). Sold in formules — *room+breakfast*, *détente*, *découverte*, or *gourmande* (2 nights + dinner at L'Eau Vive)[[12]](https://www.hotelbusiness.be/bb-espace-medissey-a-profondeville/?lang=fr). 5 minutes by taxi or by the restaurant's shuttle on request. Tripadvisor reviewer headline: "Pour une bonne nuit après le restaurant l'Eau Vive"[[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g2316146-d2295097-Reviews-Espace_Medissey-Bois_De_Villers_Profondeville_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html).

→ If you want zero logistics between dinner and bed, these two beat everything else on the list. Independent of food, **Médissey's modern aesthetic isn't to everyone's taste** — if "character" for you means old stone and beams, skip down.

## Real hotels with riverside or old-town character

### [Hôtel Vedette](https://www.hotelvedette.be/fr/) — Profondeville centre, 4 km
19th-century manor at Chaussée de Namur 51, on the Meuse between Namur and Dinant; bought in 2018 by locals Nathalie and Tom and renovated keeping the columns, grand staircase and mouldings[[13]](https://www.augoutdemma.be/108861-hotel-vedette-profondeville-namur). Four-star, six rooms/suites, gastro restaurant *LÖCTAVE* on-site, breakfast €20[[3]](https://www.hotelvedette.be/fr/)[[14]](https://hotel-vedette.visit-wallonia.com/en/). Ranks #1 of 1 hotel in Profondeville on Tripadvisor (the only hotel, but the rating is genuine — 4/5)[[14]](https://hotel-vedette.visit-wallonia.com/en/). One review flag: ⚠ road noise on the Chaussée side.

### [Les Tanneurs de Namur](https://www.tanneurs.com/en/index.php) — Namur old town, ~15 km
Fifteen interconnected 17th-century houses on rue des Tanneries, the former tanners' quarter, restored into one 4-star hotel: 37 rooms, *all different*, exposed beams, blue-stone walls, brick vaults[[4]](https://www.tanneurs.com/en/index.php). Two restaurants — gastronomic *L'Espièglerie* (since 1988) and brasserie *Le Grill des Tanneurs*[[4]](https://www.tanneurs.com/en/index.php). Rates from ~€90 standard, ~€200 for a duplex luxury suite with sauna[[15]](https://www.expedia.com/Namur-Hotels-Les-Tanneurs-De-Namur.h9853263.Hotel-Information). ⚠ Many staircases, height differences between the old buildings — accessibility-limited.

### [Château de Namur](https://www.chateaudenamur.com/) — atop the citadel, ~17 km
Hôtel + restaurant of the Namur hotel school, set in a park on the citadelle, 4-star, ~29 rooms across Club / Comfort / Executive / Junior Suite tiers, panoramic terrace[[16]](https://www.chateaudenamur.com/). Eco-certified *Clef Verte*, free parking, cable-car down to the old town in 7 min[[16]](https://www.chateaudenamur.com/). Caveat: rooms are described in reviews as "slightly dated" — character comes from the setting and the apprentice service, not from edgy design[[17]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g188663-d263861-Reviews-Chateau_De_Namur-Namur_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html).

## Idiosyncratic B&Bs (book one room — these are tiny)

### [Le Manoir Ivoire](http://www.manoir-ivoire.com/pages/12_1.html) — Wépion, ~9 km
1903 manor on the Meuse at Chaussée de Dinant 642, four double rooms named after literary figures: Madame Bovary, Marie-Antoinette, Mrs Dalloway, Suite Dorian Gray — each decorated in the style of its namesake[[18]](https://visitwallonia.be/fr-be/content/maison-dhotes-a-wepion-le-manoir-ivoire)[[19]](http://www.manoir-ivoire.be/pages/13_1.html). English-cottage-meets-French-elegance, shared boudoir, breakfast with champagne available[[18]](https://visitwallonia.be/fr-be/content/maison-dhotes-a-wepion-le-manoir-ivoire). From ~€125 on the property's tariff page, ~$138 via Kayak[[20]](http://www.manoir-ivoire.com/pages/21_1.html).

### [Orivage](https://orivage.be/) — Annevoie-Rouillon, ~10 km
Two-room *slow-travel* maison d'hôtes directly on the Meuse, run by Jacques. Both rooms (*rive droite* / *rive gauche*) face the river, with TV, Wi-Fi, queen-size bed, separate entrance[[21]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g671590-d6622339-Reviews-Orivage-Annevoie_Rouillon_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html). #1 of 1 B&B in Annevoie-Rouillon on Tripadvisor with a 5-star average; reviewers single out Jacques and the breakfast veranda[[21]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g671590-d6622339-Reviews-Orivage-Annevoie_Rouillon_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html). Three-course dinner on request[[21]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g671590-d6622339-Reviews-Orivage-Annevoie_Rouillon_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html).

### [La Maison du Meunier](https://maisondumeunier.be/fr/) — Crupet, ~16 km
Master house built in 1866 by miller Henri Purnode, opened as a guesthouse in 2012; three rooms (Hortensia, Vigne, Glycine — the last a family duplex)[[22]](https://maisondumeunier.be/fr/). Crupet has been a *Plus Beau Village de Wallonie* since 1996, and the house is next to the 12th-century church of Saint-Martin[[22]](https://maisondumeunier.be/fr/). *Wallonie Destination Qualité* label since 2018[[22]](https://maisondumeunier.be/fr/). Pool, free Wi-Fi[[22]](https://maisondumeunier.be/fr/).

### [L'Échappée Belle](https://www.lechappeebelle.be/en/lechappee-belle-en/) — Falaën, ~15 km
Restored house, four rooms, in the Molignée valley a 5-minute walk from the ruin of Falaën castle[[23]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/echappee-belle.html). Wellness focus: hot tub, sun deck, in-house spa packages, table d'hôtes (French)[[23]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/echappee-belle.html). Avg ~$159, range $167–168 on booking aggregators[[24]](https://www.momondo.com/hotels/falaen/B-B-L-Echappee-Belle.mhd384829.ksp).

### [River Lodge — Hôtel Insolite](https://www.riverlodge.be/en/) — Maredret (Anhée), ~14 km
Ten themed suites in an old watermill whose two wheels still produce heat and electricity, 5 min from Maredsous Abbey[[25]](https://www.riverlodge.be/en/the-hotel/)[[5]](https://www.riverlodge.be/en/). Themes include mountain chalet, trapper's hut, **Titanic cabin**[[5]](https://www.riverlodge.be/en/). Breakfast and the *Festin du Moulin* dinner are delivered to the room (each room has a microwave)[[26]](https://www.letsgomylove.com/en/place/river-lodge/). From ~€205 per night[[26]](https://www.letsgomylove.com/en/place/river-lodge/). 4-star, family-run by Rodolphe and Amandine.

## How to read the picks

- **Want the dinner-to-bed line to dissolve:** Le Cube > Médissey > Hôtel Vedette. Médissey actively packages with the restaurant, and a taxi from L'Eau Vive is single-digit euros[[12]](https://www.hotelbusiness.be/bb-espace-medissey-a-profondeville/?lang=fr).
- **Want a *hotel* with old-stone character:** Les Tanneurs in Namur. Pay the ~€25–30 taxi each way — the building itself is the reason to come[[4]](https://www.tanneurs.com/en/index.php)[[8]](https://taxi-profondeville-be.taxihowmuch.fr/).
- **Want a one-of-one B&B and don't care about hotel-grade service:** Le Manoir Ivoire (period drama feel) or Orivage (river-bank, slow travel) — both have ≤4 rooms so book early[[19]](http://www.manoir-ivoire.be/pages/13_1.html)[[21]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g671590-d6622339-Reviews-Orivage-Annevoie_Rouillon_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html).
- **Travelling with kids or as a group of four:** Le Cube (whole-house, 150 m²) or La Maison du Meunier's *Glycine* family duplex in the most-beautiful-village setting[[2]](https://www.beperfect.be/leau-vive-pierre-resimont-30-ans-detoiles-au-compteur/)[[22]](https://maisondumeunier.be/fr/).
- **Want a story to tell at the next dinner party:** River Lodge's Titanic cabin in a working watermill[[5]](https://www.riverlodge.be/en/).
