Les Tanneurs de Namur

Rue des Tanneries · former tanners' quarter
4★ Hotel · 37 rooms 15 interlinked houses 17th-century 2 restaurants Stairs · low access

Fifteen interconnected 17th-century houses on rue des Tanneries — the former tanners' quarter — restored into a single 4-star hotel: 37 rooms, all different, exposed beams, blue-stone walls, brick vaults[4]. Two restaurants on premises: gastronomic L'Espièglerie (since 1988) and brasserie Le Grill des Tanneurs. Rates from ~€90 at standard and up to ~€200 for the duplex luxury suite with sauna[15]. Caveat: many staircases and floor-level changes between the old buildings make this a hard pick for limited mobility.

A few things the front desk would mention.

Distances above are road kilometres from Arbre, baselined against the Profondeville↔Namur leg (~12.5 km, 12 min by car)[9]. A Namur–Profondeville taxi runs about €25–30 standard tariff; a 10-km ride is closer to €20[8]. Pre-book the after-dinner return — the dining room runs late.

Two beds package directly with the restaurant: Le Cube in the garden, and Espace Médissey three kilometres up the road. Everything else is its own booking and its own taxi.

Character notes: for old-stone-and-beam character, Les Tanneurs is the clearest pick[4]. For a one-of-one B&B and you can live without hotel-grade service, Le Manoir Ivoire (period-drama) or Orivage (river bank, two rooms only) — both with ≤4 keys, so book early.
