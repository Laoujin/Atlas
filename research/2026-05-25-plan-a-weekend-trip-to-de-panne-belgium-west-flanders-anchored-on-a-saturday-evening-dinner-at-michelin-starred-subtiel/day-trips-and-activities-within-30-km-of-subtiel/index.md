> **TL;DR — pick your day.**
> **Coastal classic before dinner:** Westhoek dunes walk [[1]](https://depanne.com/en/westhoek.php) → UNESCO horseback shrimp fishermen on Oostduinkerke beach (free, ~45 min, 40 dates Apr–Oct) [[5]](https://www.navigomuseum.be/en/when-do-horseback-fishers-go-out-sea) → coffee on Veurne's Grote Markt [[32]](https://www.veurne.be/en/sint-walburga-church).
> **Family Saturday:** Plopsaland Belgium (4 new attractions/shows for 2026) [[67]](https://mopawa.com/plopsaland-2026-new-attractions-shows-expansion-plans/) + Plopsaqua slide park next door [[69]](https://www.plopsa.com/en/plopsaqua-de-panne) — both ~5 min by car from the French border / North Sea in Adinkerke [[70]](https://www.parcs-aquatiques.com/plopsaqua-lapanne/).
> **WWI day:** Nieuwpoort's King Albert I monument + Yser Panorama [[22]](https://www.hiflanders.be/en/experiences/westfront-nieuwpoort) → Diksmuide IJzertoren + Trench of Death [[36]](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/belgium/diksmuide/attractions/ijzertoren/a/poi-sig/1261967/1325720)[[18]](https://dodengang.be/en/plan-your-visit/opening-hours). Ypres (37 km) [[28]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ieper/De-Panne) sits just outside the radius but is the natural Last Post anchor [[24]](https://lastpost.be/ceremony/).
> **Cross-border Sunday:** Dunkirk Dynamo museum + Musée Portuaire combined ticket €10 [[47]](https://museemaritimeportuaire.com/horaires) → Bergues belfry climb (24 km, 26 min) [[44]](https://www.rome2rio.com/fr/s/La-Panne/Bergues)[[51]](https://www.ot-hautsdeflandre.fr/en/what-to-do-what-to-see/exists/belfry-bergues) → Bray-Dunes wild beach [[54]](https://www.evancy.com/bray-dunes-tourist-guide-beaches).
> **Move car-free:** Kusttram from De Panne terminus reaches every Belgian coast town on this page in 10–20 min headways [[14]](https://www.delijn.be/en/content/kusttram/).

## WWI heritage

The 30 km radius hits the **Yser front** (where the Belgian army held the line 1914–18 by flooding the polders). Ypres itself is **~37 km / 39 min** [[28]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Ieper/De-Panne) — just outside the brief. Tyne Cot lies a further 9 km NE of Ypres [[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyne_Cot), placing it well beyond the 30 km radius. Both stay below as borderline picks.

## Getting around (without renting a car)

- The **Kusttram** is the world's longest metre-gauge tram line (67 km, 67/68 stops, De Panne → Knokke-Heist in 2 h 21 min end-to-end) [[82]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coast_Tram) and runs every 10, 15 or 20 minutes during the day [[14]](https://www.delijn.be/en/content/kusttram/) — check De Lijn for current cadence.
- **Train** from De Panne reaches Veurne in 7 minutes [[41]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/De-Panne/Veurne); Diksmuide and Ieper run via the same line.
- **Cross-border France** is car-only unless you cycle: there is no through tram or train from De Panne to Dunkirk.
