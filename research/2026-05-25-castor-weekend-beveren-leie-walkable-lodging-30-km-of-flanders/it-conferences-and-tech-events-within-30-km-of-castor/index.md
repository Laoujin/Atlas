> **TL;DR — Kortrijk Xpo and Howest do all the lifting.** Inside a strict 30 km radius from Castor (Kortrijkseweg 164, 8791 Beveren-Leie [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/beveren-leie/restaurant/castor) [[20]](https://cas-tor.be/en)), the IT-event calendar is essentially **Kortrijk** (~13 km). Kortrijk Xpo runs four named trade shows in 2026 — Indumation (Feb), Machineering NextGen (Mar), Sustainable Solutions (Sep), ABISS (Oct) [[4]](https://www.kortrijkxpo.com/en/calendar/) — and Howest Kortrijk Weide adds Research Day (Mar), Tech Connect (Jun) and the **UNWRAP + FTI + WONDER + Mutation** city-festival cluster in late Oct–early Nov. **Reading the calendar:** the Feb–Mar–Sep–Oct cadence at Kortrijk Xpo is the industrial-tech backbone — automation, manufacturing, energy, IIoT. Howest's two dates are smaller and more academic. The late-Oct/early-Nov window collapses four events into one trip — UNWRAP for paid deep tracks, FTI/WONDER/Mutation as free-to-mixed city programming overlapping the same weeks [[10]](https://www.ftifestival.be/programma/kortrijk).

## Recurring community / meetups

Software-developer community life within 30 km is thin and Kortrijk-centric. Expect to plan around individual sessions, not annual conferences.

- **[Belgian Java User Group (BJUG)][m1]** — visits Kortrijk roughly once or twice a year at [Sweet Mustard][m1] (Nijverheidskaai 3, 8500 Kortrijk); a January 21 2026 evening session is confirmed there, with most other 2026 dates in Gent [[14]][m1]. Free, JVM-focused, ~25–30 attendees.
- **[K-Tech Meetup][m2]** — 786-member Kortrijk-Technology group covering IoT, cloud, AI, robotics, automation and blockchain; format is Lean Coffee evening discussion (Wednesdays, 19:00) at Nijverheidskaai 3; cadence is irregular and 2026 dates were not posted at time of writing [[15]][m2].
- **[DigiPinguïns — Kortrijk FOSS/Linux Meetup][m3]** — small (~99 members), occasional Linux/FOSS evenings in Kortrijk; bring-your-laptop format [[16]][m3].
- **[CoderDojo Waregem][m4]** — monthly Saturday 09:30–12:15 coding sessions for kids 7–17 at Bibliotheek Waregem (Boekenplein 1) on 17 Jan, 21 Feb, 14 Mar, 18 Apr, **23 May (10-year anniversary)** and 20 Jun 2026 [[17]][m4]. Free, advance registration via Eventbrite. Closest tech-community venue to Castor (~4 km).
- **[Hangar K][m5]** — the Kortrijk co-creation hub for start-ups and scale-ups; hosts the **SPARK!** pitch nights and curates much of the FTI Kortrijk programme. Worth checking the [Hangar K calendar][m5] for one-off Tech Talks during any visit window [[18]][m5].

## Just outside the 30 km radius — worth noting

If your trip can stretch a bit, **Ghent** (~36 km from Beveren-Leie [[2]](https://nl.distance.to/Gent/Beveren)) is where the larger Belgian dev-community calendar lives — most BJUG meetups, Fronteers BE chapters, Software Crafting & Testing Belgium events, and the FTI Gent Summit at the Wintercircus all sit there. Bruges (~42 km) hosts the other West-Flemish FTI 2026 leg. Brussels (FOSDEM, AI-focused conferences) and Antwerp (Devoxx) are clearly out of radius (>85 km).
