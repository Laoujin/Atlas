## The shape of the weekend

[Castor](https://cas-tor.be) closes Sunday, Monday and Tuesday [[1]](https://cas-tor.be/en/contact), which collapses the calendar to one option: arrive Saturday, sit the table Saturday evening, depart Sunday. Saturday afternoon has to end early enough to dress and arrive on time; **Sunday is the day-trip day** because Castor is dark.

## What the dinner anchor actually demands — and the gap

The parent brief treated Castor's **tasting-menu pricing, wine-pairing cost, meal length, dress code, reservation lead time, and whether the table books with a room** as a verified constraint feeding every sub-topic. **None of the four children verified these directly from Castor.** The Michelin listing confirms two stars [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/west-vlaanderen/beveren-leie/restaurant/castor) and Castor's own contact page gives address and closure days [[1]](https://cas-tor.be/en/contact), but menu prices, pairing cost, sitting duration, dress code and lead time were not pulled. **Resolve this directly with the restaurant before booking lodging** — Castor's contact page is the single source of truth [[1]](https://cas-tor.be/en/contact). Treat the rest of this plan as conditional on that call.

## Lodging: one bed walks, everything else taxis

Reading the constraint strictly — "walking distance, no driving after dinner" — there is **exactly one fit**: [Casa Liebaard](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/en/a/pXu3GojGAs9G/casa-liebaard) in Desselgem, 2.1 km / ~28 min on foot via Liebaardstraat–Schoolstraat–Kortrijkseweg [[3]](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/en/a/pXu3GojGAs9G/casa-liebaard). Single ground-floor apartment, books out for Castor weekends — reserve as early as you confirm the table. The [Cotton Suites](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/en/a/np4Elu73Qapf/the-cotton-suites-bb) (3.3 km) and [La Maison des Papillons](https://lamaisondespapillons.be/) (4.0 km) are walkable in principle but not after a wine-paired sitting at 21:00.

If you'll accept a 5-minute taxi as still honouring "no driving" — [Taxi Waregem City](https://www.taxiwaregemcity.be/) runs 24/7 at +32 470 41 75 40 [[4]](https://www.taxiwaregemcity.be/) — the trip's character changes. **[Kasteel Ter Wallen](https://www.terwallen.com/en)** in Izegem (12 km, ~€20–25 taxi) is a genuine 1906 neo-Flemish Renaissance castle on four hectares with six rooms [[5]](https://www.terwallen.com/en/history) — the only property in range that justifies "stay in a castle" as a literal description. In central Kortrijk, [Hotel Messeyne](https://www.hotelmesseyne.be/en) (28 rooms, classified monument, 8 km) [[6]](https://www.hotelmesseyne.be/en) and [B&B Broel 4](https://www.broel.be/en/) (four rooms in the listed 'Tack' brewery directly on the Lys) [[7]](https://www.broel.be/en/) anchor a walkable city base for Sunday. **Book the return taxi when you book the table** — Beveren-Leie has no rank.

## Lodging villages → Sunday day-trips

Each lodging village earns its own afternoon programme inside the 30 km circle:

- **Desselgem / Beveren-Leie itself** — the [Leie towpath](https://www.visitwestvlaanderen.be/en/flax-lys-cycle-route) runs past Casa Liebaard's door; the 42.2 km Flax-Lys loop passes Beveren-Leie's Zavelput welcome site [[8]](https://www.visitwestvlaanderen.be/en/flax-lys-cycle-route). Rent at [Velo Vainqueur](https://www.velovainqueur.be/) in Beveren-Leie itself [[9]](https://www.velovainqueur.be/).
- **Waregem** (Hotel T / ParkHotel / Au Paradis, ~3 km) — Saturday-morning market 07:30–13:00 [[10]](https://www.waregem.be/twee-wekelijkse-openbare-markten), [Cascata](https://www.cascata.be/) private spa, [BE-Part](https://www.be-part.be/en/visit/our-locations/be-part-waregem) contemporary art (exhibition days only).
- **Kortrijk** (Broel 4 / Messeyne, 8 km) — UNESCO [Begijnhof](https://www.visitkortrijk.be/en/beguinage-and-experience-center) [[11]](https://www.visitkortrijk.be/en/beguinage-and-experience-center), Belfry, Buda Island, Sint-Maartenskerk tower. Kortrijk's market is **Monday only** — don't plan Saturday around it [[12]](https://www.kortrijk.be/onderneeminkortrijk/markten-in-kortrijk).
- **Vichte** (Villami) — [Brouwerij Verhaeghe](https://www.brouwerijverhaeghe.be/duchesse), home of Duchesse de Bourgogne, daily tours by reservation €10 [[13]](https://www.petitfute.co.uk/v70211-vichte-8570/c650-produits-gourmands-vins/c1117-vins-alcools/c714-biere-brasserie/c1352-brasserie-artisanale-et-micro-brasserie/1394958-verhaeghe.html).
- **Izegem** (Kasteel Ter Wallen) — slot Oudenaarde (22 km) and the Koppenberg / Oude Kwaremont / Paterberg cobbled climbs on the route back [[14]](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-belgium/flanders/kwaremont-koppenberg-taaienberg-tour-of-flander-blue-route/).
- **Deerlijk / Wielsbeke / Zulte** — the Wielsbeke ([De Pethaan](https://depethaan.be/)) and Zulte ([Hoeve La Cascina](https://en.planetofhotels.com/belgium/zulte/bb-hoeve-la-cascina)) options weren't reconsidered as their own day-trip anchors by the activities pass; treat them as accommodation only and plan the day around Kortrijk or Oudenaarde.

## Weekend-availability traps

Two of the closest cellar-door visits are weekday-only or thinly scheduled: **[Brouwerij De Brabandere](https://www.brouwerijdebrabandere.be/en/visits)** (Bavikhove, 8 km) requires a 15-person minimum and runs weekdays only [[15]](https://www.brouwerijdebrabandere.be/en/visits); the **[Roger Raveelmuseum](https://rogerraveelmuseum.be/plan-je-bezoek)** in Machelen-aan-de-Leie is closed for renovation until 28 June 2026 [[16]](https://rogerraveelmuseum.be/plan-je-bezoek). **[Brouwerij Omer Vander Ghinste](https://omer.be/en/visit)** (Bellegem, 17 km) is the weekend-friendly brewery anchor — Mon–Sun, €20, ~2 hours, advance booking required [[17]](https://omer.be/en/visit).

## If the trip is hybrid business/leisure

The tech-event calendar inside the 30 km circle is dominated by Kortrijk Xpo and Howest. Indumation (Feb), Machineering NextGen (Mar), Sustainable Solutions (Sep), ABISS (Oct) and the **UNWRAP + FTI + WONDER + Mutation** city-festival cluster in late October are the calendar tentpoles [[18]](https://www.kortrijkxpo.com/en/calendar/); the late-Oct window in particular collapses four events into one trip and pairs naturally with a Castor Saturday.

**The open question for the booker:** does "walking distance, no driving" exclude a four-minute taxi? If yes, Casa Liebaard is the entire shortlist. If no, Kasteel Ter Wallen and central-Kortrijk heritage rooms are materially better experiences and the trip's character changes from "logistics-first" to "weekend-with-a-castle".
