A castle after Castor.

There are no B&Bs in Beveren-Leie itself, only an 8–12 km arc of villages, brewery conversions and one genuine 1906 castle in the woods. We drove it. Here is what is worth the fare.

Field report · Castor, Kortrijkseweg 164, 8791 Beveren-Leie[1] — 18 sources, 4–20 km radius
The Verdict.What we'd book

Book Kasteel Ter Wallen in Izegem if you want the trip itself to be the story — a literal castle, six rooms, four hectares of forest[5]. Pick B&B Broel 4 or Hotel Messeyne if you'd rather wake up in walkable Kortrijk. Skip Hotel T, ParkHotel, and Blue Woods — comfortable, but they don't pay back the dinner.

I. The Headline Pick · 12 km
Izegem · 1906 · Neo-Flemish Renaissance

Kasteel Ter Wallen

A genuine castle — not a "castle hotel."
"The stately castle in neo-Flemish Renaissance style dates from 1906 … built by Joseph Van Naemen on the site where the farmstead De Rode Poort used to be."[5]

Designed by Walter Vercoutere of Bureau Technique Vercoutere in Ghent, commissioned by textile entrepreneur Joseph Van Naemen[5]. Six rooms inside, four hectares of forest outside, a swimming pool with a view of both[4].

Rates from ~€204 a night[6] — the high end of the local market, and the only option in range that sells "stay in a castle" as a literal description rather than a marketing line.

II. Heritage Kortrijk · 8 km · The Pair

Same distance, same heritage register — different scale.

Kortrijk · Groeningestraat 17

Hotel Messeyne

Twenty-eight rooms inside a classified monument. The signature is a spiral stone staircase and bare-beam guestrooms; downstairs an art-deco bar geared, with a straight face, to "cigar and whisky aficionados."[9]

Five minutes' walk from the Grote Markt[8]. Full operation — gym, hammam, gourmet restaurant on site — if that matters.

28 rooms Spiral staircase Hammam Art-deco bar
Kortrijk · Broelkaai 4 · waterfront

B&B Broel 4

Four rooms inside the listed Tack city brewery, directly on the river Lys with a sun terrace over the water. Each room is individually decorated and named for its colour — Gold Superior, Black Deluxe, Green Garden, Blue Night.[7]

Small, owner-run, the kind of place where sleeping in the building's history is the point.

III. Also Considered · 4–20 km

The closer beds and the wildcards.

Deerlijk · 4 km

La Maison des Papillons

Rated 5/5 on TripAdvisor. Spa, a year-round outdoor pool, hot tub, outdoor fireplace, garden.[14] Hospitality-driven, not heritage-architecture.

~5 rooms Min taxi
Wielsbeke · ~5 km

De Pethaan

"A nearly dilapidated cottage transformed into a jewel."[15] Three double rooms, rebuilt by PETer, HAns and ANneleen. Family-run and not on Castor's official list — worth a look if the headline picks book up.

3 rooms Family-run
Vichte · 9.6 km

Villami

A renovated villa with themed rooms (Boho / Oriental / Vichte), salon with fireplace and a seasonal outdoor pool.[11][12] Aesthetic-led rather than heritage-led — Castor calls it "stylish between two rivers."[3]

~5 rooms Themed
Zulte · 20 km

Hoeve La Cascina

The distance outlier — a rural farm conversion with a serious wellness fit-out: private sauna, hot tub, hammam, massages.[13] Taxi-feasible (≈€35–45 each way) only if the spa is the point.

~4 rooms Wellness
IV. Editor's Note · What to Skip
Skip.Don't pay the premium

Hotel T in Waregem (7 km) is repeatedly described as "boutique" and "design-led," but its calling card is a meeting venue with multifunctional event spaces — rooms are styled, not historic.[10]

The same goes for ParkHotel Waregem and Blue Woods (Deerlijk, 5 km), described on Castor's own list as a "modern hotel close to the E17 exit."[3] Fine if you only need a bed. They don't pay back the trip's premise.

Beveren-Leie has no taxi rank.

Logistics · 24/7

Two operators serve the corridor around the clock and will collect from Castor's door: Taxi Waregem City[17] and Waregemse Taxi[18].

Book the return for 22:30–23:00 when you book the table — Castor's last seating runs late and Saturday-night demand spikes.
