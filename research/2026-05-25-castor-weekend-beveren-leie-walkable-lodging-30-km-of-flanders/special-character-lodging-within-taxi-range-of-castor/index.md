> **Decision.** For a Saturday night after Castor (Kortrijkseweg 164, 8791 Beveren-Leie, two Michelin stars in the 2026 Guide[[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/west-vlaanderen/beveren-leie/restaurant/castor)[[2]](https://cas-tor.be/en)): book **[Kasteel Ter Wallen](https://www.terwallen.com/en)** in Izegem (12 km, actual 1906 castle, 6 rooms, from ~€204)[[4]](https://www.terwallen.com/en)[[5]](https://www.terwallen.com/en/history)[[6]](https://www.trip.com/hotels/izegem-hotel-detail-47891056/bb-ter-wallen/) if you want the trip-anchoring landmark. Pick **[B&B Broel 4](https://www.broel.be/en/)** (Kortrijk, 8 km, listed brewery on the Lys)[[7]](https://www.broel.be/en/) or **[Hotel Messeyne](https://www.hotelmesseyne.be/en)** (Kortrijk, 8 km, classified monument with spiral stone staircase)[[8]](https://www.hotelmesseyne.be/en)[[9]](https://www.authentichotels.com/en/hotels/hotel-messeyne) if you'd rather wake up in a walkable city centre. Skip the Hotel T / ParkHotel / Blue Woods tier — comfortable but generic. Taxi back is fine: Waregem and Beveren-Leie operators run 24/7.[[17]](https://www.taxiwaregemcity.be/)[[18]](https://www.waregemsetaxi.be/taxi-beveren-leie/)

## Geography

Castor sits on the Kortrijkseweg in Beveren-Leie, postcode 8791 — administratively part of Waregem.[[2]](https://cas-tor.be/en) **There are no B&Bs inside Beveren-Leie itself**; bedandbreakfast.eu's village page is just an index of stays in the neighbouring municipalities (Waregem, Deerlijk, Wielsbeke, Anzegem).[[16]](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/nl/l/2802034/beveren-leie) Castor's own "stay the night" page lists nine recommendations across a 4–20 km arc and is the authoritative distance reference used below.[[3]](https://cas-tor.be/en/stay-the-night)

## What you're actually choosing between

### [Kasteel Ter Wallen](https://www.terwallen.com/en) — the headline pick

A genuine castle, not a "castle hotel". Built 1906 by textile entrepreneur Joseph Van Naemen on the site of the medieval De Rode Poort farmstead, designed by Walter Vercoutere of Bureau Technique Vercoutere in Ghent.[[5]](https://www.terwallen.com/en/history) Six rooms, four hectares of forest, swimming pool with a view, jacuzzi.[[4]](https://www.terwallen.com/en) Rates from ~€204 per night — high end of the local market but the only option here that sells "stay in a castle" as a literal description.[[6]](https://www.trip.com/hotels/izegem-hotel-detail-47891056/bb-ter-wallen/) 12 km to Castor → ~€20–25 taxi each way.

### [Hotel Messeyne](https://www.hotelmesseyne.be/en) vs. [B&B Broel 4](https://www.broel.be/en/) — heritage Kortrijk

Both are 8 km from Castor and both occupy heritage buildings in central Kortrijk. They differ in scale, not in character density.

- **[Messeyne](https://www.hotelmesseyne.be/en)** — 28 rooms in a classified monument; the spiral stone staircase and bare-beam guestrooms are the architectural signature, with an art-deco bar geared to "cigar and whisky aficionados".[[9]](https://www.authentichotels.com/en/hotels/hotel-messeyne) Address: Groeningestraat 17, five minutes from Grote Markt.[[8]](https://www.hotelmesseyne.be/en) Choose this if you want a full hotel operation (gym, hammam, gourmet restaurant on site).
- **[Broel 4](https://www.broel.be/en/)** — 4 rooms inside the listed 'Tack' brewery on Broelkaai, directly on the Lys with a waterfront sun terrace. Each room individually decorated and named for its colour.[[7]](https://www.broel.be/en/) Choose this if you want a small, owner-run feel with a stronger sense of "you're sleeping in the building's history".

### Closer but less unique — [La Maison des Papillons](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3247613-d23498655-Reviews-La_Maison_Des_Papillons-Deerlijk_West_Flanders_Province.html) and [De Pethaan](https://depethaan.be/)

If you want to minimise the taxi (and the post-dinner travel time), **[La Maison des Papillons](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3247613-d23498655-Reviews-La_Maison_Des_Papillons-Deerlijk_West_Flanders_Province.html)** in Deerlijk is 4 km from Castor, 5/5 on TripAdvisor, with spa, year-round outdoor pool, hot tub and outdoor fireplace.[[14]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3247613-d23498655-Reviews-La_Maison_Des_Papillons-Deerlijk_West_Flanders_Province.html) The character is hospitality-driven rather than architectural — a well-run modern B&B, not a heritage building.

**[De Pethaan](https://depethaan.be/)** in Wielsbeke is the under-the-radar option: a derelict farm cottage rebuilt by PETer, HAns and ANneleen, three double rooms, family-run, geographically the closest after La Maison des Papillons but not on Castor's official list.[[15]](https://depethaan.be/) Worth a look if those two book up.

### Wildcards

- **[Villami](https://villami.be/)** (Vichte, 9.6 km) — themed rooms (Boho, Oriental, Vichte), salon with fireplace, seasonal pool. Aesthetic-led rather than heritage-led but Castor calls it "stylish between two rivers".[[3]](https://cas-tor.be/en/stay-the-night)[[11]](https://villami.be/)
- **[Hoeve La Cascina](https://en.planetofhotels.com/belgium/zulte/bb-hoeve-la-cascina)** (Zulte, 20 km) — the outlier on distance. Rural farm conversion with a serious wellness fit-out (sauna, hot tub, hammam, massages).[[13]](https://en.planetofhotels.com/belgium/zulte/bb-hoeve-la-cascina) 20 km is still taxi-feasible (≈€35–45 each way) but only worth it for the spa angle.

## What to skip

[Hotel T](https://www.hotel-t.be/) (Waregem, 7 km) is repeatedly described as "boutique" and "design-led" but its calling-card is a meeting venue with multifunctional event spaces, not heritage — rooms are styled, not historic.[[10]](https://www.hotel-t.be/) The same applies to ParkHotel Waregem and Blue Woods (Deerlijk, 5 km, "modern hotel close to the E17 exit"[[3]](https://cas-tor.be/en/stay-the-night)) — fine if you only need a bed, but they don't pay back the trip's premise.

## Taxi logistics

Two operators serve the corridor 24/7, both will pick up from Beveren-Leie and the restaurant directly: [Taxi Waregem City](https://www.taxiwaregemcity.be/)[[17]](https://www.taxiwaregemcity.be/) and [Waregemse Taxi](https://www.waregemsetaxi.be/taxi-beveren-leie/)[[18]](https://www.waregemsetaxi.be/taxi-beveren-leie/). Book the return for ~22:30–23:00 when you book the table — Castor's last seating runs late and Saturday-night demand spikes. From Castor: Deerlijk ~4 km, Waregem ~7 km, Kortrijk ~8 km, Izegem ~12 km, Zulte ~20 km.[[3]](https://cas-tor.be/en/stay-the-night)
