Before you book lodging

Call Castor first [1]

The full research treated tasting-menu price, pairing cost, sitting length, dress code and lead time as inputs to every other choice — but none of these were verified directly from the restaurant. Resolve before lodging is booked.

  • Menu format & price per cover (and wine-pairing supplement)
  • Expected sitting length — when is the dress unbuttoning, when does the taxi turn up
  • Dress code — collared / jacket / "smart casual"
  • Reservation lead time — weeks vs months out

Castor open ★★ Day-trip day Castor closed [1]

Castor closes Sunday, Monday and Tuesday [1], which collapses a weekend to one option: arrive Saturday, sit the table that evening, depart Sunday after a day-trip. Saturday afternoon has to end early enough to dress and arrive on time; Sunday carries the day-trip because Castor is dark.

The 30 km circle from Castor catches Kortrijk's UNESCO core to the south, Oudenaarde and the Tour-of-Flanders cobbles to the east, brewery cellars to the south and west, and Deinze / Ooidonk to the north-east. Beveren-Leie itself sits on the Leie towpath.

Weekend availability traps

Three things that look open and aren't

  • Brouwerij De Brabandere — weekdays only, 15-person minimum. Don't plan a Sunday cellar tour here. [37]
  • Roger Raveelmuseum — closed for renovation through 28 June 2026. [38]
  • Kortrijk's weekly market — Monday only. Plan Saturday around Waregem instead. [26]
