The weekend writes itself once you accept a simple geometry: Valence's historic core is genuinely compact — everything fits in a two-hour walk — so the dinner anchors an evening rather than an entire day. The two remaining chunks of time go to the vineyards directly across the Rhône and, if you have a third day, one farther arc into the Drôme.

**The restaurant question is settled.** [Restaurant Pic](https://www.restaurant-pic.com/) (3 stars, Anne-Sophie Pic) is Valence's only multi-star Michelin table — there are no 2-star restaurants in the city [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/fr/fr/auvergne-rhone-alpes/valence/restaurants). Two 1-star alternatives exist in the centre for a lighter-budget dinner: Flaveurs and L'Épicurien. Pic's tasting menus run €280–€380 per person before wine [[2]](https://www.restaurant-pic.com/menus/); book months ahead. The address (285 avenue Victor Hugo) is a 15-minute walk south of Champ de Mars [[3]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/hotel/pic/), so it slots cleanly at the end of the city day.

**Day 1 — city loop, then dinner.** The half-day walking route covers Champ de Mars and the 1890 [Kiosque Peynet](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiosque_Peynet) [[4]](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiosque_Peynet), [Parc Jouvet](https://www.valence-romans-tourisme.com/en/catalog/detail/le-parc-jouvet-150032/) [[5]](https://www.valence-romans-tourisme.com/en/catalog/detail/le-parc-jouvet-150032/), the medieval côtes of Vieux Valence, the [Maison des Têtes](https://www.valence-romans-tourisme.com/en/sinspirer/must-sees/maison-des-tetes/) (free courtyard) [[6]](https://www.valence-romans-tourisme.com/en/sinspirer/must-sees/maison-des-tetes/), and the [Musée de Valence – Art et Archéologie](https://www.museedevalence.fr/) [[7]](https://www.museedevalence.fr/) in the former bishops' palace. Hard constraint: the museum is **closed Monday and Tuesday** — plan accordingly.

**Day 2 — Hermitage.** [Tain-l'Hermitage](https://www.ardeche-hermitage.com/en/focus-on/tain-lhermitage-top-6-visit-ideas/) is the obvious full day: 10 minutes by TER with ~27 services daily [[8]](https://www.thetrainline.com/en/train-times/tain-lhermitage-tournon-to-valence-ville), no car needed. Walk the terraced granite hillside to Chapelle Saint-Christophe for the valley panorama [[9]](https://www.ardeche-hermitage.com/en/focus-on/hiking-on-the-hermitage-hillside/), taste at Cave de Tain or M. Chapoutier (book ahead), then cross the suspension bridge to Tournon for lunch. If you arrive Saturday morning with spare time, [Château de Crussol](https://en.ardeche-guide.com/discover/top-10-explore/castle-crussol/) — the ruined fortress staring back at Valence from the Ardèche bank — takes a brisk morning and is only 10 minutes away [[10]](https://en.ardeche-guide.com/discover/top-10-explore/castle-crussol/).

**Optional third slot.** Crest (~29 min [[11]](https://distanceenvoiture.fr/fr/itineraire/Valence/Crest)) for the tallest medieval keep in France; Grignan (~59 min [[12]](https://www.l-itineraire.com/de_valence-26_a_grignan)) for the Sévigné château with a full 2026 festival programme; Romans-sur-Isère (~25 min [[13]](https://www.valence-romans-tourisme.com/en/sinspirer/must-sees/shoes-museum/)) for the Musée international de la Chaussure. Pick one depending on whether you want fortress, literary château, or artisan market.

**One June caveat.** Drôme lavandin only starts blooming mid-June [[14]](https://routes-lavande.com/en/the-flowering-of-the-lavender/) — fields around Grignan will be green on a first-weekend-of-June trip. Don't route south chasing lavender.

**IT conferences angle.** The IT conferences child found no overlap with this trip's scope. Mardinnov #24 (today, 2 June) and Décrypt'innov #5 (4 June) are both free community networking events [[15]](https://www.ftalps.com/territoires/valence-romans/accueil-valence-romans/), not tourist draws. For major developer conferences, Lyon is 35 minutes north by TGV.

The open question this run leaves: whether [La Brasserie Pic](https://www.picgroupebrasserie.com/) at the same address warrants its own reservation strategy for a lower-commitment lunch the day after the tasting menu — the activities and restaurant children don't address walk-in availability or same-address dining sequencing.
