Decision

Pivot the anchor. La Durée in Izegem permanently closed in June 2025; the Michelin Guide 2026 confirmed the loss of both its stars[1][2], and the chef was Angelo Rosseel — not Vincent Florizoone[5]. The strongest in-radius replacement is Boury 3* in Roeselare (~10 km from Izegem) — the only 3*** within the 30 km arc[66] — open Wed–Sat[72]; book Boury's own guest rooms (closing August 2026) so the table genuinely walks to bed[70]. If Boury is full or you want to stay in Izegem proper, base at Parkhotel Izegem (197 m from the old La Durée address)[20] and reach Boury by a 15-min, ~€35 taxi[34].

1. The premise has changed: what's true in May 2026

The original brief assumed a Michelin-starred La Durée in Izegem run by Vincent Florizoone. Two independent facts contradict that:

  • La Durée Izegem is closed. La Libre's 2026-05-04 Michelin recap states "Le restaurant La Durée, à Izegem, avait également deux étoiles mais a fermé ses portes"[1]. Horeca Magazine lists it among the two-star losses in the 2026 Belux Guide[2]. Focus-WTV reported chef Angelo Rosseel's farewell — regular service through March 2025, reduced service April–June 2025, then the Leenstraat 28 building sold[3]. The official site advertises only a Bruges pop-up at Café Partagé, Smedestraat 15, open April–June, Tuesday–Friday dinner — Saturday is not served, 4-course menu €110[4].
  • Vincent Florizoone never ran La Durée. La Durée was always Angelo Rosseel's restaurant[5]. Florizoone is a different West-Flemish chef, historically tied to Grand Cabaret in Nieuwpoort (now reported closed) and later Brasserie Albert at Thermae Palace Oostende[6].
For historical reference only — what a Saturday at La Durée Izegem used to look like: single 6-course tasting, 18:00 seating that ran to ~01:30 (≈5 h), smart-casual, average all-in spend €255, champagne €26/glass, recent bills €660 for two, no on-site rooms[7][8][9].

2. The new anchor: Boury 3*, Roeselare

Boury sits at Rumbeeksesteenweg 300, 8800 Roeselare, ~9.6 km road from Leenstraat 28 (≈15 min by car)[74]. It is the only 3*** Michelin restaurant within the 30 km arc[66] and is open Wed–Sat for both lunch and dinner[72][71].

6. Day-trips and activities within 30 km of Izegem

Saturday afternoon needs to end by ~17:30 to dress and travel to a 19:00 Boury seating. The plan below leaves Saturday evening clear and uses Sunday for the bigger excursion.

Inside Izegem itself (no taxi needed)

Eperon d'Or

National shoe & brushes museum · restored 1930s factory

Open Tue–Sun 13:30–17:30 (10:00 in school holidays); closed Mondays and 2nd/3rd weeks of January[35]. The reason Izegem is on a museum-goer's map.

Roeselare cluster (~5 km — pair with Boury day)

KOERS Museum of Cycle Racing

Roeselare

Mon–Sat 10:00–17:00 year-round; also Sundays 10:00–17:00 March–September; closed Sundays Oct–Feb[37].

Brouwerij Rodenbach

Roeselare · guided tours only

Individual tours Wed–Sun at 14:00 year-round (Classic / Grand Cru / Vintage blend-your-own); renewed visitor experience launched Oct 2024[38]. Sunday-afternoon natural slot.

Sint-Michielskerk

Roeselare

Late-Gothic, 65 m tower, 49 carillon bells rung manually by a 100-year-old guild, the largest organ in West Flanders; 256-step tower climb by appointment[54].

Provinciedomein Sterrebos

Rumbeke (Roeselare) · 27 ha

Star-pattern park around Rumbeke Castle, radial lanes, 200-year-old plane trees[42]. Walking distance from Boury — natural pre-dinner walk.

Kortrijk cluster (~12–20 km)

Texture

Flax & River Lys museum · 1912 depot, Noordstraat 28

Three themed rooms in an authentic flax warehouse[36].

Begijnhof Sint-Elisabeth (UNESCO)

Kortrijk

Founded 1238 · 41 17th-century houses · free, 09:00–21:00 · new experience centre in the Sint-Annaroom[44].

Abby Kortrijk

Modern art (former 1302 Battle museum, reopened April 2025)

Three parts of the former abbey — dormitorium and two chapels — host the modern-art collection[46].

Provinciedomein De Gavers

Harelbeke · ~20 km south

Lake loop, kayaks, whisper boats, climbing wall, bird-hides, arboretum — strong all-weather family card[43].

Edge of arc — Ieper & Diksmuide (≈35–40 km, stretch)

In Flanders Fields Museum

Grote Markt 34, Ieper · ~39 km via A19[41]

Daily 10:00–17:00, last tickets 1 h before close, adult ~€12[39].

IJzertoren / Museum aan de IJzer

Diksmuide

Apr–Sep daily 09:00–18:00 (weekends from 10:00); Oct–Mar 09:00–17:00, closed Wed outside school holidays[45].

Bellewaerde Aquapark

Near Ieper · indoor

Fully indoor water park (since July 2019): slides, sauna, lazy river — the rainy-Saturday backup[52].

Green walks within the arc

Bulskampveld (Beernem)

West Flanders' largest forest area; 9.3 km signposted loop from neo-Gothic Bulskampveld Castle with a visitors' centre[49].

Beisbroek-Tillegem

144 ha of Kasteelbossen on Bruges' green west edge; moated Kasteel Tillegem; a unique Planet Trail through the forest[50].

Vloethemveld (Zedelgem)

365 ha forest/heath/ponds — and the site of the largest British WWII PoW camp in Belgium with surviving barracks and prisoner art[51].

Bruges & the Damme cycle (stretch — ~38–45 km)

Bruges is ~38 km / 28 min from Izegem[40] — just past the strict 30 km arc but the obvious cultural splurge. Pair with the flat, traffic-free 7 km canal ride to Damme along the Damse Vaart[47]. Tielt to the northeast offers the UNESCO Belfort-Halle ensemble for a short market-town stop[53]. For self-guided cycling, use the West-Flanders fietsknooppunten node network — Izegem is on the grid[48].
