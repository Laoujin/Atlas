> **TL;DR** Penang's "Silicon Valley of the East" identity anchors a dense 2026 tech event calendar. **July is peak season**: [PDX2026](https://penangdigitalisation.com/) (Jul 15–16, ~10k attendees) and the [EMAX/SMAX/PMAX electronics cluster](https://emaxasia.com/) (Jul 22–24) both land at the Setia SPICE Convention Centre within the same week. IEEE academic conferences run quarterly. The grassroots developer community is modest — a few active Meetup groups — not a KL-scale scene.

**PDX2026** [[7]](https://penangdigitalisation.com/) — Penang's flagship AI/digitalisation event; targets 10,000 delegates and 170 exhibitors [[8]](https://www.buletinmutiara.com/penang-to-host-second-major-digital-conference-with-pdx2026/) from Dell Technologies, Microsoft Malaysia, and KPMG. Themed "Leading Organisations Through AI, Innovation & the Digital Economy." HRDC-claimable for Malaysian firms.

**EMAX cluster** [[9]](https://emaxasia.com/) — five co-located shows (EMAX, [SMAX](https://smaxasia.com/) [[10]](https://smaxasia.com/), PMAX, ASFAX, CirTech); targets chip makers, equipment suppliers, and PCB manufacturers across Southeast Asia. Free entry for trade professionals; pre-register on the official sites.

**TENSYMP 2026** [[5]](https://ieeemy.org/tensymp2026/) — the IEEE Region 10 (Asia-Pacific) flagship annual symposium; HRDF-claimable for Malaysian attendees; covers six tracks from AI to nanotechnology.

**AIM Asia CXO Forum** [[4]](https://www.eventbrite.sg/e/advanced-innovation-manufacturing-asia-cxo-forum-2026-penang-tickets-1989199582499) — half-day executive panel at KOMTAR's sky-level floor; guest of honour is Penang's Deputy Chief Minister; panellists from AMD, Micron, Inari, Dell, and UWC Berhad.

**IPEE 2026** [[6]](https://ipee.net/) — hosted by Universiti Sains Malaysia; IEEE Industry Applications Society-sponsored; seven research tracks including virtual power plants and AI for energy systems; papers submitted to IEEE Xplore and recommended for IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications (IF 4.7).

**AIM Asia Week** [[11]](https://advancedmanufacturingasia.com/) — 3rd annual edition; SGD 788 (≈ MYR 2,700) VIP pass; HRDC-approved for Malaysian firms; two-day format mixing keynotes, workshops, and a product showcase.

## Community & Recurring Meetups

Penang's grassroots developer scene is small relative to KL — a few stable Meetup groups rather than a dense event calendar:

- **[Penang Startup Founder 101](https://www.meetup.com/penang-startup-founder-101/)** [[12]](https://www.meetup.com/penang-startup-founder-101/) — 1,160 members; regular free events on early-stage startup building; co-runs global webinars with Founder Institute.
- **[Penang WordPress Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/penang-wordpress-meetup-group/)** [[13]](https://www.meetup.com/penang-wordpress-meetup-group/) — 888 members; local developers and designers sharing WordPress knowledge.
- **[AWS Student Builder Group at USM](https://www.meetup.com/find/my--penang/networking/)** [[14]](https://www.meetup.com/find/my--penang/networking/) — 603 members; student-led cloud/AWS learning group at Universiti Sains Malaysia.

No dedicated fintech or Web3/blockchain events in Penang in 2026 — that scene is concentrated in Kuala Lumpur (Malaysia Blockchain Week).
