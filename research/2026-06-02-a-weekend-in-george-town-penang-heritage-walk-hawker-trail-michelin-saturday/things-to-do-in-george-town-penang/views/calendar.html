Hawker Lunch
Penang Road: CKT → Cendol
Bib Gourmand Inside Joo Hooi Cafe on Penang Road — Jin Kor CKT stall, charcoal wok, celebrity-photographed. [17] Then walk two doors to Penang Road Famous Teochew Chendul (since 1936) for iced coconut-milk cendol with gula melaka. [27]
13:30
Dessert
Cendol at Lebuh Keng Kwee
Shaved ice, pandan jelly noodles, kidney beans, gula melaka — a one-minute walk from Jin Kor, standing or inside Joo Hooi. The two stalls back-to-back form a classic Penang Road double. [27]
14:30
Heritage Afternoon
Mansion → Clan House → Street of Harmony
Three stops on a single 20-minute walk through the UNESCO core:
Pinang Peranakan Mansion — 29 Church St, ~1,000 Nyonya antiques, 09:30–17:00 RM20 [7]
Leong San Tong Khoo Kongsi — 18 Cannon Square, the city's most ornate clan house RM10 [4]
Street of Harmony — Kapitan Keling Mosque (1916 Mughal Revival) + Goddess of Mercy Temple (1800) + Sri Mahamariamman (1833) — all free [11]
16:45
Waterfront
Chew Jetty at golden hour
Most-visited clan jetty — free, 09:00–21:00. Stilt houses over the Straits, 15–20 min end to end. TripAdvisor 3.7/5: commercialised but the water light reads well. [5] [6] For quieter atmosphere, Lee / Tan / Yeoh jetties are a few minutes further.
17:45
Transition
Return to hotel & change
Au Jardin dress code: collared shirt, long trousers, covered footwear (gentlemen). [32]  ·  Auntie Gaik Lean's: none enforced. Au Jardin first seating is 17:15 — if booked there, you are already running late; aim for 15-min Grab from Chew Jetty.

Day 2  ·  Penang Hill & the summit

Penang Hill is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve (designated 2021, 12,481 ha). [42] Buy the express ticket (RM80 vs RM40 regular) on weekends — regular queue can exceed 90 minutes. [40] Note: the much-anticipated Penang Hill Cable Car was 28% complete in April 2026, targeting December 2026 — not open for a June trip. [47]
