The Two Restaurants

Au Jardin

★ 1 Michelin Star · since 2023
Address: The Warehouse @ Hin Bus Depot, 125 Jalan Timah, George Town[3]
Hours: Thu–Sun · Lunch 11:30am–2pm · Dinner 5:15pm–10pm[3]
Price: Lunch RM 388++ (4 courses) · Dinner RM 568++ (8 courses)[4]
Cuisine: French-Malaysian natural cuisine (La Cuisine Naturelle)[3]
Chef: Su Kim Hock (trained at Michelin-starred restaurants in UK & Taiwan)[4]
fine dining tasting menu seasonal vegetable-forward

Signature dishes

  • Cognac & Hay Aged Duck — pre-order required, RM 80 deposit/person[5]
  • Lacto-fermented bread (thosai-inspired house sourdough)[4]
  • Sardine Parfait with squid ink & anchovy sablé[12]
  • Semporna Sea Harvest (abalone, sea cucumber, vin jaune)[4]
  • Lacto Fermented Highland Pineberries with guava sorbet & kantan[4]
What to know 18 seats in a restored warehouse inside an arts space; menu rotates monthly; 85% Malaysian ingredients, 65% from Penang.[4] Smart dress enforced (collared shirt, long pants, covered shoes). Wine and mocktail pairings available.

Booking Via restaurant-aujardin.com up to 3 months in advance; 48h cancellation fee applies. Hay-aged duck pre-order ≥2 days before; dietary needs require 72h notice.[13] TripAdvisor: 4.5/5 (46 reviews).[6]

Auntie Gaik Lean's Old School Eatery

★ 1 Michelin Star · since 2023
Address: 1 Lebuh Bishop (Bishop Street), George Town[8]
Hours: Wed–Sun · 12pm–2:30pm · 6pm–9:30pm · Closed Mon–Tue[8]
Price: À la carte, ~RM 50–100/head[8]
Cuisine: Peranakan (Nyonya)[7]
Chef: Beh Gaik Lean (cooking Peranakan family recipes since age 12)[9]
peranakan à la carte heritage family recipes

Signature dishes

  • Pie Tee — crispy top-hat shells, RM 18[8]
  • Curry Kapitan — chicken in handmade coconut milk & spices, RM 48–58[8]
  • Gulai Tumis — 8-spice curry paste with fresh saffron[7]
  • Nasi Ulam — jasmine rice with kaffir lime & lemongrass[9]
  • Kiam Chye Ark — preserved vegetable & duck soup[9]
  • Nyonya Beef Rendang, RM 88[8]
What to know Two heritage shophouses packed with 1960s memorabilia — vintage radios, porcelain, ceiling fans. Homey rather than formal; service is earnest, not polished.[10] Dishes arrive as they're ready (no particular order); no menu customisation.

Booking Groups of 4+ require reservation + RM 200 nett deposit. Groups of 3 or fewer: walk-in only, first-come first-served. Book 1–2 weeks out; demand is high.[8] TripAdvisor: 4.2/5 (325 reviews).[10]
