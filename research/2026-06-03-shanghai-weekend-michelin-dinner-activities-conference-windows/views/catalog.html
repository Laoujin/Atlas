Practical notes
  • Venue key: SNIEC = Shanghai New International Expo Centre (Pudong, Metro Line 7 Huamu Rd); SICC = Shanghai International Convention Center (Pudong, near Century Ave).
  • September developer block: MCPCon (Sep 6–7) + KubeCon+PyTorch (Sep 8–9) are four consecutive days at the same venue — one trip, the full open-source + agentic AI stack.
  • WAIC access: Exhibition floor is typically free or nominally priced; specialist summits are separately ticketed. Check worldaic.com.cn for 2026 pass tiers.
  • KubeCon support: Linux Foundation offers travel scholarships, visa letter requests, and childcare for international attendees.[32]
  • Language: MWC Shanghai runs bilingually (EN/ZH). WAIC is primarily Chinese with English interpretation at major keynote sessions.
Payments Bind Visa/Mastercard to Alipay and WeChat Pay before departure. QR is near-universal; cash is backup.
Visa-free transit 240-hour (10-day) transit for 55+ nationalities via Shanghai with an onward ticket to a third country.
Ride-hailing DiDi has a full English interface and accepts foreign Visa/Mastercard. No Chinese SIM needed.
Airport transfer Pudong Maglev → Longyang Rd (Line 2) in ~8 min at 300 km/h. ¥40 with boarding pass.
Metro Cheap and extensive. Pay by Alipay/WeChat QR or buy a rechargeable Jiaotong card at any station.
