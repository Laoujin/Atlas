> **TL;DR** June is London's peak tech month: [Infosecurity Europe](https://www.infosecurityeurope.com/en-gb.html) (free, 2–4 Jun at ExCeL), [London Tech Week](https://londontechweek.com/) (8–12 Jun, Olympia), and [AI World Congress](https://aiconference.london/) (23–24 Jun, £495) all overlap. For free access at any time of year, also look at Tech Show London (March, ExCeL) and AWS Summit London (April, ExCeL). Developer-focused premium conferences — QCon, Devoxx UK, DevOpsCon — cluster March–May at £650–£2,700. Autumn brings three Gartner summits at ExCeL covering security, apps, and infrastructure.

## Now Happening: 2–7 June 2026

**[Infosecurity Europe](https://www.infosecurityeurope.com/en-gb.html)** (2–4 Jun, ExCeL London) — Europe's leading cybersecurity event [[2]](https://www.infosecurityeurope.com/en-gb.html): 13,000+ professionals, 380+ exhibitors, 250 speakers. 2026 themes: agentic AI in security, post-quantum cryptography, ransomware economics. Standard passes are £49+VAT (were free before 5 May). SANS Institute technical workshops cost extra (£75–£125+VAT). Nearest station: Custom House (Elizabeth line / DLR).

**[AI Native DevCon](https://tessl.io/blog/ai-native-devcon26-the-london-conference-for-developers-building-with-ai/)** (1–2 Jun, The Brewery, Barbican) — Two-day in-person conference for senior engineers and platform leads running AI agents in production [[3]](https://tessl.io/blog/ai-native-devcon26-the-london-conference-for-developers-building-with-ai/). Four tracks: context engineering, agent orchestration, org enablement, agent security. Speakers include Ryan Lopopolo (OpenAI), Dana Lawson (Netlify CTO), Dave Farley. Discount code `R30` cuts 30% off registration.

**[LDX3 London](https://leaddev.com/leaddev-london/)** (2–3 Jun, InterContinental O2) — LeadDev, StaffPlus, and LeadingEng merged into one festival for engineering leadership [[17]](https://leaddev.com/leaddev-london/). 2,500+ attendees, three main stages, Director+ track, workshops. Riverside venue near the O2 Arena (North Greenwich tube, Jubilee line).

**[London's Calling](https://www.londonscalling.net/)** (5 Jun, The Brewery, EC1Y 4SA) — Europe's largest community-led Salesforce event, 11th edition [[5]](https://www.londonscalling.net/). Multi-track across dev, admin, architecture, marketing, and career growth. Community-run; full day including meals.

**[PyData London](https://pydata.org/london2026/about)** (5–7 Jun, Convene Sancroft, St. Paul's) — Annual gathering for Python data practitioners [[4]](https://pydata.org/london2026/about). Tutorials Friday, talks Saturday–Sunday. Organised by NumFOCUS. Nearest tube: St. Paul's (Central line).

## London Tech Week (8–12 June)

[London Tech Week](https://londontechweek.com/) is Europe's largest tech festival [[1]](https://londontechweek.com/), drawing 8,000+ companies and 2,000+ senior decision-makers to Olympia London. The Expo and Content Stages run 8–10 June; Fringe Events (meetups, side conferences, roundtables) extend across the city through 12 June. 2026 theme: "Shaping the Future of Business Through Technology." Headline partners: Microsoft, AWS, Deloitte, Accenture, Salesforce. Notable speakers: Aravind Srinivas (Perplexity CEO), Ivan Zhao (Notion Co-Founder), Liz Kendall (UK Secretary of State for Science). Multiple pass tiers; details and pricing at the official site.

## Late June: AI World Congress (23–24 June)

[AI World Congress 2026](https://aiconference.london/) at Kensington Conference and Events Centre [[16]](https://aiconference.london/) bills itself as Europe's premier AI summit for senior decision-makers. 100+ speakers drawn from Anthropic, Microsoft, IBM, and leading research institutions; tracks on autonomous systems, neural networks, and enterprise AI deployment. Tickets from £495; VIP packages (£1,550) include Speakers' Lounge access and a Congress Gala Dinner.

## Practical Notes

- **ExCeL London** (Custom House, Elizabeth line / DLR) hosts Tech Show London, AWS Summit, Infosecurity Europe, and the two autumn Gartner summits — all in the same Royal Docks campus.
- **QEII Centre, Westminster** (St. James's Park tube) is London's other major conference hub, used by QCon and NDC London.
- **The Brewery** (Barbican, EC1Y) co-hosts AI Native DevCon (June 1–2) and London's Calling (June 5) in the same week.
- UK Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) required for most nationalities since January 2025; check eligibility before booking.
