The Larrabetzu weekend is anchored by a single family's compound: [Azurmendi](https://azurmendi.restaurant/en/) (3-star), [Eneko](https://eneko.restaurant/en/) (1-star), and [Gorka Izagirre](https://www.gorkaizagirre.com/en/) txakoli winery all share the same Barrio Legina hillside address above Bilbao Airport. The structure builds itself — a morning winery tasting (€30 p.p.) [[1]](https://www.gorkaizagirre.com/en/enoturismo/), the dinner of your choice, then one or two days ranging out along the Basque coast and countryside.

**The fork is the dinner.** Azurmendi is a five-stage theatrical journey — wicker-basket picnic outdoors, snacks in the kitchen corridor, appetisers in a greenhouse, then a seated meal in a glass dining room overlooking the valley — for €315 (Adarrak) or €350 (Gran Menú), plus €150–300 wine pairing [[2]](https://azurmendi.restaurant/en/menus-2/). It holds a Michelin Green Star and was named World's Most Sustainable Restaurant in 2014 and 2018 [[3]](https://inhabitat.com/leed-gold-certified-azurmendi-crowned-worlds-most-sustainable-restaurant/), housed in a LEED Gold bioclimatic building with geothermal energy and an in-house germplasm bank of 400+ local seed varieties [[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eneko_Azurmendi). Eneko, one floor below, delivers the same seasonal Basque cooking in a live open-kitchen format for ~€96 (Sutan menu) [[5]](https://eneko.restaurant/en/menu-2/) — accessible in price, still Michelin. The surrounding weekend plan is identical regardless of which you book; the gap is purely what you want from the experience and what you're prepared to spend.

Larrabetzu village itself is a half-day: a neoclassical old town rebuilt after an 1830 fire, a 15th-century Gothic church in the Goikolexea neighbourhood with Bizkaia's second-ranked Hispano-Flemish gilded altarpiece [[6]](https://www.guiarepsol.com/es/fichas/monumento/iglesia-de-san-emeterio-y-san-celedonio-19178/), and a municipal frontón [[7]](https://www.larrabetzu.eus/es-ES/Servicios/Deporte/Paginas/Fronton.aspx). The ~30–45 km radius is where the weekend fills out. Bilbao (~20 min) leads with the Guggenheim (adult €15, closed most Mondays) [[8]](https://www.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/en/hours-and-admission) and the Casco Viejo pintxos zone. Gernika (~25 min) has the Peace Museum's 3D recreation of the 1937 bombing (€6.50, free Sat afternoons) [[9]](https://www.museodelapaz.eus/en/museum/visit-information/) and the Oak of Gernika. The coast branches to San Juan de Gaztelugatxe (~40 min; free timed ticket required, daily cap 1,462) [[10]](https://public.tiketa.eus/gaztelugatxe/reservation-regulations/?lang=en) and Mundaka–Bermeo (~35 min) for the left-hand wave and working fishing harbour [[11]](https://tourism.euskadi.eus/en/blog/essential-things-to-do-in-the-urdaibai-biosphere-reserve/webtur00-contfichapost/en/). None of these require a car in themselves — but Azurmendi sits beside an expressway with no walkable transit, so the dinner leg needs a taxi (~€25–35 from central Bilbao) or self-drive [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/FAQ-g1605350-d1599938-Azurmendi.html).

One research angle — IT conferences near Larrabetzu — was pursued in case the trip could be combined with a work trip. The village has no conference infrastructure, but Zamudio Technology Park (Spain's first, 1985) is only 5 km away [[13]](https://parke.eus/en/campus/zamudio-derio/). Two Bilbao-area events pair well logistically with an Azurmendi or Eneko dinner: **Euskal Encounter** (LAN party, BEC Barakaldo, Jul 23–26 2026) [[14]](https://ee26.euskalencounter.org/es/index.html) and **DOK Summit** (AI/DevOps/cybersecurity, Bilbao, Nov 23–24 2026) [[15]](https://doksummit.com/dok-2026-bilbao-conectar-innovacion-tecnologia-personas/). Both are ~25 km from Larrabetzu — an easy post-conference taxi ride.

Weather is permissive: both restaurants operate Friday–Saturday dinners year-round, so there is no forced window [[16]](https://azurmendi.restaurant/en/). June–mid-September is the driest stretch [[17]](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/spain/bilbao), but the real constraint is Azurmendi availability — it seats fewer guests and books out faster than Eneko. Once that reservation is confirmed, the rest snaps into place.
