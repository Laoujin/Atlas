> **Decision — five best-fit "dos" for a Ghent couple, first visit, relaxed-to-active:**
> 1. **Southern Ridges + Henderson Waves** — Singapore's signature canopy-and-bridge walk, 10 km of mostly elevated boardwalk, zero permit, free, half-day [[1]](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/southern-ridges-singapore/)[[2]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/docs/default-source/parks-docs/shared/hortpark-southern-ridges-diy-walk.pdf).
> 2. **Pulau Ubin cycle to Chek Jawa** — rent a bike at the jetty (~S$8–15/€5.5–10) and pedal the island's last-kampong jungle to the mangrove boardwalk; the standout offbeat full-day [[3]](https://www.littledayout.com/a-noobs-guide-to-cycling-pulau-ubin-bike-rental-trails/)[[4]](https://www.jasminealley.com/pulau-ubin/)[[5]](https://pulau-ubin.nparks.gov.sg/chek-jawa-wetlands/).
> 3. **MacRitchie TreeTop Walk** — rainforest loop to a 250 m suspension bridge in the canopy; moderate, half-to-full-day, no guide [[6]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/central-catchment-nature-reserve/activities/hiking-nature-walk)[[8]](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/macritchie-treetop-walk/).
> 4. **Mangrove kayak at Pulau Ubin** — 4 h guided beginner paddle through calm mangrove channels, the best on-water adventure within the no-extreme brief [[11]](https://www.adventures.network/mangrovekayaking).
> 5. **Swim off Lazarus Island** — ferry to St John's, walk the causeway, white-sand bay; bring your own water [[9]](https://www.sassymamasg.com/st-johns-lazarus-island/)[[10]](https://islandcruise.com.sg/ferry-schedule/).
>
> **Timing flag:** do the jungle, cycling and island legs in **Feb–Apr** (driest); **Jun–Oct** carries transboundary-haze risk and **Nov–Jan** is the wet NE-monsoon [[41]](https://www.highlightstravel.com/singapore/best-time-to-visit)[[42]](https://www.weather.gov.sg/weather-forecast-monsoon-update/)[[43]](https://blog.onevasco.com/worst-time-to-visit/singapore).

All prices SGD with rough EUR (1 EUR ≈ 1.45 SGD). Difficulty is for a fit-but-not-athletic couple. None of the picks below need permits or vertical/expedition skill; the one hard-excluded item (AJ Hackett bungee) is flagged where it appears.

## Jungle hikes & canopy walks

**[Southern Ridges](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/southern-ridges-singapore/)** links five parks over ~10 km, the centrepiece being the wave-form **Henderson Waves**, Singapore's highest pedestrian bridge at 36 m [[1]](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/southern-ridges-singapore/). NParks rates the full DIY route 3–5 h; it is free, open 24 h and needs no permit, so a half-day with a HarbourFront-MRT start is ideal [[2]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/docs/default-source/parks-docs/shared/hortpark-southern-ridges-diy-walk.pdf). Mostly shaded boardwalk — comfortable even outside the dry months. Tack on the **Mount Faber cable car** down (SkyPass S$35/~€24 round-trip; Mount Faber line 26–30 min) for a scenic finish [[52]](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/singapore-cable-car/)[[53]](https://mountfaberleisure.com/attraction/singapore-cable-car/).

**[MacRitchie TreeTop Walk](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/central-catchment-nature-reserve/activities/hiking-nature-walk)** is the rainforest highlight: a loop through Central Catchment to a 250 m free-standing suspension bridge ~25 m up, one-way only [[6]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/central-catchment-nature-reserve/activities/hiking-nature-walk)[[8]](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/macritchie-treetop-walk/). Reckon a moderate 7–11 km loop, ~3 h, depending on whether you start from MacRitchie or the shorter Venus Drive side [[7]](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/singapore/central/macritchie-reservoir-and-treetop-walk-loop). ⚠ The bridge is **closed Mondays** [[8]](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/macritchie-treetop-walk/). Expect macaques — don't carry visible food.

**[Bukit Timah](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/bukit-timah-nature-reserve/activities/hiking-and-nature-walks)** is Singapore's highest hill (163 m). Short but punchy: the red trail is easy (1.2 km), green/yellow are "moderate to difficult" with steep final steps; a round trip is ~3 km and doable in under an hour at pace [[28]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/bukit-timah-nature-reserve/activities/hiking-and-nature-walks). A good early-morning leg-stretch in the dry season.

Easy mangrove-and-coast strolls, both free, both under an hour: **[Labrador / Berlayer Creek](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/labrador-nature-reserve/activities/natural-coastal-walk)** — a 0.9 km mangrove boardwalk plus the 300 m Bukit Chermin coastal boardwalk, flat and stroller-easy [[44]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/labrador-nature-reserve/activities/natural-coastal-walk)[[45]](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/berlayer-creek-boardwalk/); and **[Sungei Buloh](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/sungei-buloh-wetland-reserve)** in the NW — flat boardwalks over mudflats with crocodiles, otters and monitor lizards, free entry, free guided tours, best 7–9 am and in the Sep–Mar migratory season [[26]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/sungei-buloh-wetland-reserve)[[27]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/sungei-buloh-wetland-reserve/activities/nature-walks-tours).

## On the water: kayak, SUP, bumboat

**[Mangrove kayak at Pulau Ubin](https://www.adventures.network/mangrovekayaking)** is the pick for accessible adventure: a 4 h guided beginner trip crossing a short open stretch then threading calm mangrove channels for kingfishers, eagles and herons; tandem kayaks, all gear provided, min age 7, covered shoes required [[11]](https://www.adventures.network/mangrovekayaking). Operators also bundle it with the easy Puaka Hill walk; book via Klook/Seek Sophie [[12]](https://www.klook.com/en-SG/activity/115-nature-kayaking-singapore/).

Closer to base, **[PAssion Wave @ Marina Bay](https://www.onepa.gov.sg/cc/passion-wave-marina-bay)** rents kayaks, pedal boats and SUP on the sheltered Marina Reservoir — note SUP needs valid certification, and it is **closed Mondays** [[31]](https://www.onepa.gov.sg/cc/passion-wave-marina-bay). On Sentosa, **[Ola Beach Club](https://watersports.olabeachclub.com/)** at Siloso offers walk-up single/double kayaks and SUP (age 7+, basic swimming needed) plus jet-ski/banana-boat add-ons [[54]](https://watersports.olabeachclub.com/)[[55]](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/ola-beach-club/).

The low-effort water classic: a **[Singapore River bumboat](https://rivercruise.com.sg/river-cruise/)** from Clarke Quay — ~40 min past the quays, Merlion and Marina Bay Sands, departures every 15 min, 9 am–11 pm, ~S$28/€19 adult [[20]](https://rivercruise.com.sg/river-cruise/)[[21]](https://www.singapore-tickets.com/singapore-river-cruises/timings/). Touristy but a genuinely good orientation on day one.

**[East Coast Park](https://www.littledayout.com/east-coast-park-bicycle-rental/)** is the easy crowd-pleaser: a flat 11 km coastal connector with rental chains like **[GoCycling](https://gocycling.sg/singapore/)** at ~S$10–12/€7–8 per hour [[13]](https://www.littledayout.com/east-coast-park-bicycle-rental/)[[14]](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/bicycle-rental-singapore/)[[15]](https://gocycling.sg/singapore/). For something quieter, **[Coney Island](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/coney-island-park)** pairs a 2.5 km connector with a 3.6 km walking route through mangrove and casuarina, 80+ bird species [[46]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/coney-island-park)[[47]](https://thehoneycombers.com/singapore/guide-to-coney-island-singapore/). The **[Round Island Route](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/know-our-pcn/rir/)** is the headline 150 km park-connector loop (110–160 km depending on the western detour) — a stretch goal, not a casual ride [[16]](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/know-our-pcn/rir/)[[17]](https://sportsadvice.decathlon.sg/the-ultimate-guide-to-round-island-cycling-route-in-singapore). For walkers, the **[Coast-to-Coast Trail](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/our-pcn/c2c/)** runs 36 km across the island, 8–12 h end-to-end — do a single segment unless you want the full-day challenge [[18]](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/our-pcn/c2c/)[[19]](https://trevallog.com/singapore-coast-to-coast-trail/).

## Islands & swims

**[St John's + Lazarus](https://www.sassymamasg.com/st-johns-lazarus-island/)** is the standout offbeat swim: ferry from Marina South Pier (~S$15–18/€10–12 return), land at St John's, then walk the causeway ~15 min to **Lazarus Beach** — white sand, calm clear bay [[9]](https://www.sassymamasg.com/st-johns-lazarus-island/)[[10]](https://islandcruise.com.sg/ferry-schedule/). ⚠ No shops or toilets on Lazarus — bring water and carry trash out. Go on a weekend when ferries run more often.

For snorkel/dive-light, the **[Sisters' Islands Marine Park](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/sisters-islands-marine-park/activities/hiking--nature-walks-or-tours-at-big-sister's-island)** has 250+ hard-coral species and two marked dive trails (6 m and 15 m) [[24]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/sisters-islands-marine-park/activities/hiking--nature-walks-or-tours-at-big-sister's-island)[[25]](https://www.smartsinga.com/guide-to-sisters-islands-singapore/). ⚠ Diving here is gated — Advanced cert, 20+ logged dives, NParks-approved operators only — so for a first visit the accessible route is the **free monthly NParks intertidal walk** (45 spots, released first Sunday of the month, books out in minutes) [[48]](https://www.littledayout.com/free-family-fun-at-sisters-island-marine-park-intertidal-guided-walk-by-nparks/). The equivalent at Pulau Ubin is the **[Chek Jawa](https://pulau-ubin.nparks.gov.sg/chek-jawa-wetlands-tour/)** guided intertidal/boardwalk tour (~S$60/€41, 1 h, max 15) [[49]](https://pulau-ubin.nparks.gov.sg/chek-jawa-wetlands-tour/).

True dive-light: **[Pulau Hantu](https://www.orpheusdive.com/singapore-diving-pulau-hantu-day-trip/)** is Singapore's home reef, ~45 min by boat from West Coast Pier, rich in nudibranchs and soft coral — but ⚠ visibility is only 0.5–5 m, so manage expectations; go with a charter/PADI operator [[56]](https://www.orpheusdive.com/singapore-diving-pulau-hantu-day-trip/)[[57]](https://travel.padi.com/dive-trip/singapore/pulau-hantu-dive-trip-13981/).

**[Pulau Ubin](https://www.jasminealley.com/pulau-ubin/)** is the single best activity day: bumboat from Changi Point (S$4/€2.8, +S$2 with own bike; leaves once 12 passengers board), rent a bike at the jetty (S$8–15/€5.5–10/day; take a mountain bike for the rougher tracks), ride to **Chek Jawa Wetlands** for the mangrove boardwalk and Jejawi Tower — note bikes aren't allowed on the boardwalk itself [[4]](https://www.jasminealley.com/pulau-ubin/)[[3]](https://www.littledayout.com/a-noobs-guide-to-cycling-pulau-ubin-bike-rental-trails/)[[5]](https://pulau-ubin.nparks.gov.sg/chek-jawa-wetlands/). Add the mangrove kayak above to make it a packed full day.

**[Chestnut Nature Park](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/chestnut-nature-park/activities/mountain-biking)** is Singapore's only dedicated MTB venue: an 8.2 km trail graded easy to "extremely difficult" — stick to the **Northern loop** (novice-friendly) and the pump track; the Southern loop is advanced [[39]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/chestnut-nature-park/activities/mountain-biking)[[40]](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/chestnut-nature-park/).

For an overseas hop, **[Bintan](https://www.brf.com.sg/)** is 60–70 min by ferry from Tanah Merah; same-day getaways run ~S$87/€60 and Lagoi Beach offers kayak, SUP, snorkelling and mangrove boat tours [[34]](https://www.brf.com.sg/)[[35]](https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/blog/featured/day-trip-bintan). **[Batam](https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/blog/featured/day-trip-batam)** is the cheaper, closer (~60 min) alternative for a beach-and-watersports day [[36]](https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/blog/featured/day-trip-batam).

## City-edge "dos" you participate in

- **[Night Safari](https://www.mandai.com/en/night-safari.html)** at Mandai — ride the open tram with live commentary through nocturnal habitats, then walk the 4 forest trails (900+ animals) and catch the keeper-led Creatures of the Night show; the walking trails beat the tram for engagement [[22]](https://www.mandai.com/en/night-safari.html)[[23]](https://www.sassymamasg.com/play-night-safari-singapore-review-guide-kids-tram-shows/).
- **[OCBC Skyway / Supertree Observatory](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/ocbc-skyway.html)** at Gardens by the Bay — walk the aerial skyway between the Supertrees (tourist ~S$14/€10), best at the evening light show [[29]](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/ocbc-skyway.html)[[30]](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/supertree-observatory.html).
- **[Jewel Changi Canopy Park](https://www.jewelchangiairport.com/en/attractions/canopy-park.html)** — the L5 play-garden with bouncing nets, slides and the **Canopy Bridge**, a 23 m-high walk with a glass-bottomed, fog-wreathed centre; an easy, fun layover-day filler [[37]](https://www.jewelchangiairport.com/en/attractions/canopy-park.html)[[38]](https://www.jewelchangiairport.com/en/attractions/Mastercard-canopy-bridge.html).
- **Sentosa active picks:** the **[Skyline Luge](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/skyline-luge-singapore/)** (gravity carts, 4 trails, night-luge option) [[32]](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/skyline-luge-singapore/), the free 350 m **[Sensoryscape](https://www.sassymamasg.com/play-sentosa-for-kids-guide-attractions-beach-staycation/)** walkway [[33]](https://www.sassymamasg.com/play-sentosa-for-kids-guide-attractions-beach-staycation/), and the **[MegaZip](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/mega-adventure-park/)** — a 450 m zipline from Imbiah Hill to Siloso Beach at up to 60 km/h, an accessible thrill within the brief [[50]](https://sg.megaadventure.com/)[[51]](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/mega-adventure-park/). ⚠ The adjacent **SkyPark AJ Hackett bungee/giant-swing** is the kind of extreme/vertical activity outside your no-extreme line — skip it.

## Timing & heat notes (applies to every outdoor "do")

- **Best window:** Feb–Apr is driest and best for hikes, cycling and island swims [[41]](https://www.highlightstravel.com/singapore/best-time-to-visit).
- **Wet:** Nov–Jan is the NE monsoon, the wettest stretch (~19 rain-days/month) — keep cycling/island plans flexible [[42]](https://www.weather.gov.sg/weather-forecast-monsoon-update/).
- **Haze:** Jun–Oct carries transboundary-haze risk (PSI can spike); check readings before jungle hikes or long rides, and have an indoor fallback (Jewel, Night Safari, river cruise) [[43]](https://blog.onevasco.com/worst-time-to-visit/singapore).
- **Heat year-round:** start jungle/cycling legs at dawn, carry water (some islands have none), and favour the shaded boardwalk routes (Southern Ridges, Sungei Buloh, Labrador) on hotter days [[2]](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/docs/default-source/parks-docs/shared/hortpark-southern-ridges-diy-walk.pdf)[[9]](https://www.sassymamasg.com/st-johns-lazarus-island/).
