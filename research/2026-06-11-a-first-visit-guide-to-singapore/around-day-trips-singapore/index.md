> **Decision.** EU passports get 30 days visa-free; just file the free SG Arrival Card online within 3 days of arrival [[10]](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore) [[11]](https://worldtraveldocs.com/entry-requirements/singapore.html). From Changi, take the **MRT** (~€1–1.50, 35 min) unless you land late or are loaded with bags, then **Grab** (~€13–24) [[2]](https://www.airporttransferportal.com/airport-guides/sin/cost-to-city). Singapore itself is small and walkable — lean on MRT + the odd Grab, ~€4–8/day [[3]](https://publictransportguide.com/singapore-public-transport/). The **two day-trips worth your time**: **Pulau Ubin** (offbeat kampong/cycling, half-day, ~€6) [[12]](https://www.jasminealley.com/pulau-ubin/) and **Johor Bahru** for cheap food + Legoland (full day, bus ~€5 return) [[20]](https://tripcabinet.com/blog/johor-bahru-from-singapore-day-trip). A **Bintan beach day** is the splurge-worthy third if you want sand [[9]](https://bintanvoyage.com/ferry-to-bintan-guide/). Prices in EUR at ~1 S$ = €0.67 [[25]](https://www.exchange-rates.org/converter/sgd-eur).

## Getting IN & OUT

EU citizens enter visa-free for up to 30 days; passport must be valid 6+ months with an onward ticket [[11]](https://worldtraveldocs.com/entry-requirements/singapore.html). The **SG Arrival Card (SGAC)** is mandatory but free, submitted online within 3 days (incl. arrival day) — it is not a visa [[10]](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore). Singapore is the standalone leg here, but it chains naturally to Malaysia (Johor Bahru, by land) and Indonesia (Batam/Bintan, by ferry).

*Bus time is mostly immigration queues at the Causeway, not distance. The KTM Shuttle Tebrau (Woodlands → JB Sentral) skips the road jam but sells out — book ahead; the Woodlands counter is cashless (KTM Wallet only) [[5]](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/ktm-shuttle-guide/). Cross-border buses (CW2, 160, 170, 950) run frequently for S$1.28–1.38 (~€1) each way [[6]](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/public-bus-to-jb/). The faster **RTS Link** rail (Woodlands North → Bukit Chagar) is due to open around Jan 2027 at S$5–7 — likely just after your trip [[7]](https://www.you.co/sg/blog/train-to-jb/).

Batam ferries leave HarbourFront or Tanah Merah, S$58–80 return plus a S$10 departure fee, four operators, 60+ daily sailings [[8]](https://www.instarem.com/blog/singapore-to-batam-ferry-guide-ticket-price-schedule/). Bintan runs only from Tanah Merah; Bintan Resort Ferries drops you at Lagoi Bay near the beaches [[9]](https://bintanvoyage.com/ferry-to-bintan-guide/).

Taxi from Changi is metered S$25–45 plus a S$6–8 airport surcharge and a 50% midnight surcharge (00:00–05:59) [[1]](https://www.changiairport.com/en/at-changi/transport-and-directions/leaving-the-airport.html); Grab shows one all-in price with no airport surcharge [[2]](https://www.airporttransferportal.com/airport-guides/sin/cost-to-city).

## Getting AROUND locally

The MRT covers every neighbourhood on your list (Marina Bay, Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam, Tiong Bahru) and reaches HarbourFront for Sentosa. **No card needed**: tap any contactless Visa/Mastercard or phone via SimplyGo; adult fares run S$1.28–2.57 (~€0.85–1.70) per ride after the Dec 2025 fare revision [[3]](https://publictransportguide.com/singapore-public-transport/). For heavy MRT/bus days the **Singapore Tourist Pass** gives unlimited rides — S$17 / 24 / 29 for 1 / 2 / 3 days (~€11 / 16 / 19), though it excludes the Sentosa Express [[4]](https://thesingaporetouristpass.com.sg/type-of-passes/). Most couples won't beat plain contactless; budget **~€4–8/day** on transit, more if you favour Grab.

Singapore is genuinely walkable, with 730+ km of cycling paths and a 360 km Park Connector Network linking most of the island [[23]](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/getting_around/active_mobility/walking_cycling_infrastructure/cycling.html). Tourist bike rental (e.g. GoCycling) runs ~S$10/hr or S$10–20/day (~€7–14) — handy for East Coast Park or Pulau Ubin [[24]](https://www.homejourney.sg/blog/singapore-cycling-routes-tourists-prices-hours-2026-202602221401).

**Pulau Ubin** — the best offbeat half-day: a 15-min bumboat from Changi Point (S$4/~€2.70 each way, cash only, leaves once 9–12 passengers board) drops you on a 1960s-kampong island for cycling and the Chek Jawa wetlands [[12]](https://www.jasminealley.com/pulau-ubin/) [[13]](https://pulau-ubin.nparks.gov.sg/visitubin/).

**Sentosa** — touristy but easy: walk the **free** Sentosa Boardwalk from VivoCity, or pay the S$4 (~€2.70) Sentosa Express monorail in (rides within and back to the mainland are free) [[19]](https://sg.trip.com/guide/transport/sentosa-express.html). The scenic alternative is the cable car, round-trip S$35 adult / S$25 child (~€24 / €17), running 08:45–22:00 [[18]](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/singapore-cable-car/).

**Southern islands** — St John's, Lazarus and Kusu on one round-trip ferry from Marina South Pier, S$15 adult (~€10); Lazarus has the quiet swimming beach [[14]](https://www.klook.com/en-SG/activity/41168-island-hopping-pass-st-john-kusu-sisters/). ⚠ In 2026 St John's main jetty is closed for repairs (re-routed to Lazarus) and Big Sisters' weekday trips are suspended [[15]](https://islandcruise.com.sg/ferry-schedule/).

**Southern Ridges** — a free 10 km hill walk (4 parks) over the 36 m Henderson Waves bridge, both ends on the MRT [[16]](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/southern-ridges-singapore/). ⚠ Sections are closed for works until **July 2026**, so during your June trip do Henderson Waves alone rather than the full ridge [[17]](https://www.tripadvisor.com.sg/Attraction_Review-g294265-d1523862-Reviews-The_Southern_Ridges-Singapore.html).

**Johor Bahru** — the value full-day: cross by bus (~€5 return) for dim sum, Korean BBQ and City Square mall at a fraction of Singapore prices [[20]](https://tripcabinet.com/blog/johor-bahru-from-singapore-day-trip). For families, [Legoland Malaysia][lego] added a MINILAND Singapore in 2026; tickets ~MYR 199 adult (~€38) [[21]](https://littlebigreddot.com/jb-weekend-getaway-2026-miniland-legoland-new-attractions/) [[22]](https://www.klook.com/en-SG/activity/7713-legoland-admission-ticket-johor-bahru/).

[lego]: https://www.klook.com/en-SG/activity/7713-legoland-admission-ticket-johor-bahru/

**Bintan** — the only true beach in the orbit: a same-day Bintan Resort Ferries getaway from Tanah Merah to Lagoi Bay runs ~S$87 (~€58), ~70 min, best in the March–October dry season — go if you want sand, skip if you're tight on days [[9]](https://bintanvoyage.com/ferry-to-bintan-guide/). Batam is closer and cheaper but more city than beach [[8]](https://www.instarem.com/blog/singapore-to-batam-ferry-guide-ticket-price-schedule/).
