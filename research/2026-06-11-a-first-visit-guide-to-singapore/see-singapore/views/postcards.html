SINGAPORE

A rack of the icons & heritage worth posting home · first-visit guide

5can't skip

Five you cannot skip: Gardens by the Bay + the free Garden Rhapsody, MBS SkyPark for the skyline, the free Merlion & Jewel Rain Vortex, the ethnic-quarter heritage walk, and the UNESCO Botanic Gardens.

Most quarters, parks, temples, viewpoints and light shows are free — the paid list is short. Everything is walkable or MRT-linked; only Pulau Ubin and the southern islands need a boat.

Postage rate: S$1 ≈ €0.69 · best weather Feb–Apr & Jun–Sep; wet Nov–Jan [48] · walk the outdoor sights before 10am or after 5pm [38]

Merlion Park

The 8.6 m lion-fish (1972), spouting 24/7 and free, framed against MBS. Come before 9am or after 7pm for thin crowds and the good light. [9][10]

📮 no ticket ✓ Must-do
0€free Changi40 m

Jewel Rain Vortex

The world's tallest indoor waterfall — 40 m, completely free to watch, with a nightly 20:00 light show. Reason enough to arrive at Changi early. [11][12]

📮 no ticket ✓ Must-do
0€free Civicest. 1887

Raffles Hotel & Civic core

Walk the 1887 colonial landmark's lobby and arcades, then loop City Hall, the old Supreme Court, Esplanade and St Andrew's neo-Gothic cathedral on one free stroll. [23][24]

📮 no ticket · cathedral 9–16:00 ⚠ If time

Singapore Botanic Gardens

Singapore's only UNESCO World Heritage Site — the first tropical botanic garden ever listed (2015). [15] Free, open 05:00–24:00; the one paid corner is the National Orchid Garden — 60,000+ orchids, the world's largest tropical display. Go early for cool air and birdsong. [14][17]

📮 gardens free · 10-min MRT from Orchard ✓ Must-do

Chinatown temples

Three temples of three faiths in a few blocks: the five-storey Buddha Tooth Relic, the 1827 Hindu Sri Mariamman, and Thian Hock Keng — the oldest Chinese temple, built 1839–42 with no nails. [25][27]

📮 free · cover shoulders & knees ✓ Must-do
0€free Little Indiasensory

Little India

The most sensory quarter — garland stalls, the Tekka Centre wet market, the candy-coloured Tan Teng Niah villa [29], and the sculpture-laden Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple to Kali. [30]

📮 free · temple ~08:00–12:00 & 16:00–21:00 ✓ Must-do
0€free Kampong GlamMalay-Arab

Kampong Glam

The Malay-Arab quarter: the gold-domed Sultan Mosque (free robes lent; avoid Fri 11:00–14:00), textile-lined Arab Street, and the mural-and-boutique alley Haji Lane. Best photos around 8am. [31][32]

📮 free · dress modestly ✓ Must-do
0€free Tiong Bahruest. 1936

Tiong Bahru

Singapore's first public-housing estate (1936–41): low-rise Art Deco blocks with rounded "streamline" corners, plus three nostalgic Yip Yew Chong murals. The quiet, café-dense outlier of the four quarters. [33]

📮 free · self-guided walk ⚠ If time

Haw Par Villa

A gloriously kitsch 1937 park of 1,000+ statues and dioramas of Chinese mythology (free); the paid Hell's Museum houses the lurid Ten Courts of Hell. Park partially closed for works from Dec 2025 — Museum stays open. [34][35]

📮 park free · walk-up ⚠ If time
0€free Sthn Ridges10 km

Southern Ridges

A 10 km elevated park trail; the wave-form Henderson Waves (36 m, Singapore's highest pedestrian bridge) and Mount Faber give free panoramas of city, harbour and islands. Ride the cable car down to Sentosa. [36][37]

📮 no ticket · go early or after 5pm ⚠ If time
0€free Centralcanopy

MacRitchie TreeTop Walk

A free 250 m suspension bridge at jungle-canopy level (long-tailed macaques en route). Open Tue–Sun, closed Mondays — go before 10am to beat the heat. [38][39]

📮 no ticket · closed Mon ⚠ If time
€2.80each way NE islandbumboat

Pulau Ubin

Singapore's last surviving kampong — rent a bike and ride dirt tracks past wooden houses to the Chek Jawa wetlands boardwalk (best at low tide). 10-min bumboat from Changi Point; departs once 12 passengers board. [40][42]

📮 on-demand boat · boardwalks 07:00–19:00 ⚠ If time
€12ferry rt S islandsquiet sand

Southern Islands

For an empty beach: ferry from Marina South Pier to St John's, then walk the causeway to Lazarus Island's quiet sand; Kusu adds a temple and tortoise sanctuary. [47]

📮 book ahead on weekends ⚠ If time
0€most sights Sentosatouristy

Sentosa (the free bits)

Skip the paid theme parks for the free ones: the Fort Siloso Skywalk (WWII coastal fort, lift up), Palawan Beach's suspension bridge to the "southernmost point of continental Asia," and a free beach shuttle tram. [44][43]
