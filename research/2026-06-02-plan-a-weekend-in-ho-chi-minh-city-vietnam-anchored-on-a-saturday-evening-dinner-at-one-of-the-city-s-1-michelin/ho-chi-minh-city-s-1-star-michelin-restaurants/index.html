Decision

Iconic HCMC pick, best energy: Ănăn Saigon — Vietnam's first Michelin star, vibrant D1 location, $100–145/person.

Most intimate/special occasion: CieL — only 15 seats, Young Chef Award winner, French-Vietnamese villa in Thao Dien, ~$120/person.

Best views + hotel luxury: Akuna — Sam Aisbett's Modern Australian-Vietnamese on Le Méridien's 9th floor overlooking the Saigon River, ~$165/person.

Grand Cantonese, largest group options: Long Trieu — classic dim sum and seafood at The Reverie Saigon, from ~$85/person.

Narrative-driven progressive Vietnamese: Coco Dining — newest star, storytelling tasting menu, District 3.

All five are open Saturday evening. [1]

The 2025 MICHELIN Guide Vietnam [1] awards one star to five restaurants in Ho Chi Minh City — two of them newly recognised. [2]

Booking tips for a Saturday evening
  • CieL: 15 seats — book at least 2–3 weeks ahead. Email or online form. [6]
  • Ănăn Saigon: 2 weeks minimum; email hello@anansaigon.com with preferred seating (5:30 or 8:30 PM) and tasting menu choice. [3]
  • Akuna: Online via akunarestaurant.com; book 1–2 weeks ahead. [5]
  • Coco Dining & Long Trieu: Email/phone reservations recommended; somewhat easier to book than CieL or Ănăn. [4] [7]
  • Getting to CieL: Thu Duc / Thao Dien is ~20 min taxi from D1 or accessible via Metro Line 1 (An Phu station). [8]
