L'ATELIER DU CODE — A.D. 2026

Menu du Workshop

Vibe-coded apps a non-developer can ship in one sitting
❦ ✦ ❦
Prix fixe·5 courses·2 to 3 hours
A note from the kitchen

For a 2–3 hour service we cook to a strict shape — single user, one screen, no auth, localStorage or nothing. The "rectangle of stuff on a page" school [1][86].

From that shape we serve five reliable courses, each portioned by technical depth. A guest who insists on a marketplace, payments, or a native App Store deploy will be quietly steered to the Allergens panel — and a single-user variant.

— with thanks to Stanford UIT, Duke Co-Lab, and the 65labs kitchen in Singapore [4]

Course I

Hors-d'œuvre

Calculators & personal trackers. One input form, one output panel, one optional localStorage list — the safest first plate.
✦ ✦ ✦
Tip Splitter ●●●●●NOVICE

Bill × tip % ÷ people, with 15 / 18 / 20 / 25 % preset buttons. Mobile-first, no persistence [13].

Pomodoro Flow ●●●●●EASY

Three timer modes, browser notification, themed accents. Ann Jose's first-person build is the reference plate [22][12].

Resist the temptation to add accounts or sync — the "last 10 %" trap is real [6].
Habit Tracker ●●●●●EASY

Named habit, emoji icon, a 30-day streak grid. Stays in localStorage; never asks for an account [71].

BMI Buddy ●●●●●NOVICE

Height + weight → BMI + category. A worked plate from the Lovable kitchen [16]; AllCalculator serves the same dish another way [15].

Mortgage Calculator ●●●●●NOVICE

Principal + rate + term → monthly payment. Pairs neatly with Handy Calc Lab, a bundled GST / EMI / Interest set [24].

Event Countdown ●●●●●EASY

Birthday, wedding, holiday — multi-event list with countdown clocks. localStorage carries the bookings [18].

Meeting Cost Calculator ●●●●●EASY

Attendees × rate × duration → dollars wasted. A Taskade staff favourite [14].

Expense Splitter ●●●●●INTERMEDIATE

Group expenses → minimum-transfer settlement. A Devpost first-prompt build in Bolt [23].

Push the high edge of the timebox. Serve only to a confident guest.
Course II

Amuse-bouche

Decision toys, generators & word games. Higher fun-per-prompt — guests iterate on what comes out, not what gets stored.
✦ ✦ ✦
Random Restaurant Picker ●●●●●NOVICE

One button — one restaurant from your list. v0's community sample, copied liberally [19].

Whee'Lunch ●●●●●NOVICE

Spin-the-wheel for the midday meal. Its sibling WheelWise picks names, winners, yes/no [20][21].

Roblox Name Generator ●●●●●NOVICE

Stylised random usernames, mobile-first. Same skeleton as My Poké Creator — a randomChoice(array) in disguise [28][30].

Meowmoire ●●●●●EASY

Upload a cat photo, receive a shareable meme. Sentimental and silly in equal measure [29].

Mixels.ai ●●●●●INTERMEDIATE

A pixel-art generator and editor — one canvas, a paint bucket, an AI sketcher. A Lovable-shipped piece [31].

Mighty Drums ●●●●●INTERMEDIATE

A step-sequencer drum machine with MIDI export. For the guest who arrives with a musician's appetite [32].

Gradient Generator ●●●●●EASY

Pick HSL stops, watch the gradient render, copy the CSS. Lovable's own step-by-step [43]. Pair with ToolsFlow for HEX/RGB/HSL conversion [42].

Wordle, à la maison ●●●●●EASY

Five-letter daily word, the familiar colour feedback. A non-coder documented the whole build in 30 minutes on v0.dev — well under one workshop block [35].

QuizGenius ●●●●●INTERMEDIATE

Make a quiz, or take one. The AI plates the questions; the guest grades themselves [33].

Course III

Plat principal

Fetch-and-render with one no-key API. Promote the guest from "stuff stored in my browser" to "I called a real internet thing" — without key juggling.
✦ ✦ ✦
Open-Meteo Weather Card ●●●●●EASY · HOUSE PICK

The strongest single starting point. No API key, no sign-up, no credit card; 10K free daily calls [44]. A March 2026 walkthrough exists for the layman cook [45].

PokéAPI Pokédex ●●●●●EASY

100 req/IP/min, no auth. James Q Quick's vanilla-JS Pokédex is the canonical short tutorial [46][47].

Frankfurter Currency Converter ●●●●●EASY

200 currencies, 55 central banks, no key. A quiet workhorse on the line [48].

Random-Dog Button ●●●●●NOVICE

One endpoint, one random image. A DEV walkthrough exists for the absolute beginner [49][50].

ISS Live Tracker ●●●●●INTERMEDIATE

Latitude, longitude, next overhead pass-times. 101 Computing's recipe pairs it with a Leaflet map [51][52].

NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day ●●●●●EASY

DEMO_KEY works out of the box. Hardik Kawale's step-by-step is the kitchen reference [53][54].

Address on a Map ●●●●●INTERMEDIATE

Leaflet + OpenStreetMap + Nominatim geocode. No key, no card — Derick Rethans shows the move [55][56].

Dad-Joke Card ●●●●●NOVICE

Plain GET, JSON via Accept header. Wes Bos uses this as the very first fetch lesson [57][58].

Open Trivia Quiz ●●●●●EASY

Categorised Q & A, no auth. Pair with Advice Slip, JokeAPI or Chuck Norris for one-line-card riffs [61][59][60].

TheMealDB Recipe Finder ●●●●●EASY

Random meal + search by ingredient. Tuts+ walks the whole build [62][63].

Word Definition ●●●●●NOVICE

GET-only /api/v2/entries/en/<word>. Pair with Wikipedia REST for a topic-card variant [64][65].

Next SpaceX Launch ●●●●●EASY

/latest and /next mission JSON. Mixed Analytics's 2026 list is the larder for the rest of this course [66][67].

Maître d's tip

Serve these stories at the start of service — before the guest invents something. The single biggest scope-down lever is "build the thing you genuinely need this week"; the cook knows when it is enough [85].

Course V

Mignardises

Micro-games, under 200 lines of state. Single-player or hot-seat — never real-time multiplayer.
✦ ✦ ✦
Falling-Objects Catcher ●●●●●EASY · HOUSE PICK

A paddle catches falling treats. Canonised by the imagi × Lovable Hour of AI; the prompt that works is concretely "create a game where a cat catches falling treats and earns points" [36].

Pong (Retro Paddle Battle) ●●●●●INTERMEDIATE

Two paddles, one ball, score to ten. Hot-seat only — keep the dish single-machine [26].

Hangman (Hangin' Man) ●●●●●EASY

Five-minute word timer, alphabet keyboard. A spin-off classic [27].

Asteroids · CandyClicker · Anxiety Balloon Pop ●●●●●INTERMEDIATE

A small flight of arcade pieces from the madewithlovable showcase [25].

DOOMscroll · Piece Together · Latin Learner ●●●●●INTERMEDIATE

From a Hacker News thread of impressive vibe-coded fun: a doomscrolling browser game, an animated jigsaw, an Anki-lite Latin flashcard set [38].

For the trivia-quiz variant, drop to ~20 hand-picked questions; Cursor-built AI-quiz games scaled to seven hours at 100 questions [37].

Dishes the kitchen will not serve inside a 2–3 hour service. When the guest insists, we offer the substitution in rust.

  • M
    Marketplace (Uber / Airbnb for X)
    Three user types × three UIs × auth × payments × admin. Sharetribe's 60-hour post-mortem is the cautionary plate. Swap → directory page with seed data and a "request" form[87].
  • $
    Payments & subscriptions (Stripe, Patreon)
    Idempotency keys, webhook signatures, refunds, disputes. AI consistently skips all four [88]. Swap → a tip-jar QR linking to an existing payment URL[86].
  • ↻
    Real-time multiplayer
    Websockets, state sync, latency tuning. "Kills the vibe" by pulling beginners into DevOps [91]. Swap → single-player or hot-seat on one device.
  • 📱
    Native iOS / Android (App Store)
    A first-hand memoir: "30 % prompting, 70 % debugging various errors that show up due to the AI constantly losing context"[90]. Swap → a PWA via Lovable or Bolt — same URL, still installable.
  • 🔑
    OAuth login (Google / GitHub)
    Over 60 % of vibe-coded security issues arrive with the login screen — RLS, exposed keys, IDOR, client-side bypass [10][92]. Swap → a local pseudonym field saved in localStorage.
  • ✉
    Twilio SMS / SendGrid email
    Even Twilio's own vibe-coding post admits the setup (account, API keys, appsettings.json, terminal comfort) is itself a beginner barrier [93]. Swap → a "copy this text" button or mailto: link.
  • 🧠
    ML training / RAG / vector DB
    Embedding pipelines, eval, infra. Out of scope for two hours [2]. Swap → one LLM call per click via the builder's built-in.
  • …
    "A productivity app for everyone"
    No audience, no shape [89]. Swap → a one-role utility for one named person.
From the sommelier

Five picks to default to

  1. Tip / split calculator
    For a mixed-audience adult workshop. Single screen, instant satisfaction, mobile-first feels real [13].
  2. Habit tracker with emoji + 30-day grid
    For participants who want something they will actually use. localStorage only [71].
  3. Open-Meteo weather card for "my city"
    For the one-API milestone. No key, deploys cleanly [44][45].
  4. Falling-objects catcher
    For kids, classrooms, anyone who wants a game. Class-tested as the Hour-of-AI canonical [36].
  5. A scratch-an-itch the guest names in the first 10 minutes
    Overrides everything above. Patil and Roose both picked things that mattered to them; that motivation carried them through [75][83].
Final rule of thumb

If the guest cannot describe the idea as "one screen with [list of widgets] and a button that does X", it is not workshop-scale. Help them shrink it before any prompt is typed [7][12].
