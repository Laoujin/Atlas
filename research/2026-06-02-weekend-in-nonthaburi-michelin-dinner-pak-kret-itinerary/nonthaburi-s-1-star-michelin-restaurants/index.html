Decision: Both restaurants are in Pak Kret, Nonthaburi — ~35 min from central Bangkok by Grab. Pick AKKEE for a bold tasting-counter evening: chef Sittikorn "Ou" Chantop revives lost Eastern-coast Thai recipes, paired with craft beers, set menu ฿2,150, open until 11 PM on Saturdays.[1] Pick Suan Thip for refined Royal Thai in a serene riverside garden: moderate prices, no alcohol, closes 9 PM.[3]

Chef Sittikorn "Ou" Chantop — 2025 Michelin Young Chef Award winner (first Thai man to receive it),[6] revives antique recipes using ingredients sourced exclusively from Thailand's eastern coast: Gulf seafood, wild-foraged herbs, hand-made fish sauce.[2] The set menu cannot be adapted for seafood allergies or vegetarian/vegan diets; à la carte accommodates.[2] Request Thai craft beer pairings.[1]

Getting there

Both restaurants are in Pak Kret district, roughly 5 km apart, ~35–40 min from central Bangkok.[7]
  • Grab/taxi — easiest option; use the address above for navigation
  • MRT Purple Line — interchange at Tao Poon (Blue Line) → Pak Kret area; short taxi/songthaew onward
  • Chao Phraya Express Boat — alight N30 (Nonthaburi) or N33 (Pak Kret) for a scenic river approach to Suan Thip
