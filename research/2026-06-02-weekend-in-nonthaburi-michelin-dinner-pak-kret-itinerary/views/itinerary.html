(or 10:00)
Chatuchak Weekend Market SAT/SUN ONLY
MRT Purple Line → Tao Poon → Blue Line to Mo Chit / Chatuchak Park. One of Asia's largest markets: 8,000+ stalls, 200,000 daily visitors on weekends. Opens 09:00 Sat/Sun — go before noon before the heat becomes the priority.
or
09:30
Rattanakosin — Grand Palace & temples
MRT Blue Line to Sanam Chai. Grand Palace + Wat Pho in a single morning. Wat Arun across the river. Transport: Grab ฿180–250 or MRT — NOT the Orange Flag boat (does not run weekends).
bonus
Floating markets SAT/SUN
Khlong Lat Mayom or Taling Chan (~30 km, west-bank Thonburi). Sat/Sun and holidays only. Pair with Rattanakosin — both are west-side Bangkok, single Grab run.
14:00+
Return / depart
MRT Purple Line from Tao Poon back to Nonthaburi Civic Center, or ride-share onward. Airport link from Mo Chit via ARL if needed.
Pick Suan Thip if —

You want the rarest thing central Bangkok can't offer: a Michelin-starred meal in a riverside garden that's been starred since 2019. The khao chae in April aside, the full experience lives outdoors at dusk. A calm, early night. No alcohol means the focus stays on the food.

Pick AKKEE if —

You want a chef's counter built around one obsession — Eastern-coast Thai ingredients, seafood, insects. Sittikorn Chantop won the 2025 Young Chef Award for a reason. The tasting menu rotates with the catch. And you plan to extend the night — JODD Fairs or Yaowarat after makes this a full Bangkok evening.

⊠ Optional overlay — tech event 25–27 Nov 2026 only
DigiTech ASEAN + AI Connect runs at IMPACT Exhibition Centre, Pak Kret, Nonthaburi — November 25–27. This is the only date in the 2026 calendar where a major IT event and a Pak Kret Michelin dinner are within a 10-minute ride of each other. Every other major conference (Techsauce at QSNCC, True Digital Park, KMUTT) sits in central Bangkok — pairing one with a Pak Kret dinner means a full cross-city round-trip. In November, the geography collapses.

Transport quick-ref

⊠ Orange Flag Express
N30 → Rattanakosin. Does not run Saturdays or Sundays. Weekdays only. Plan MRT or Grab instead.
MRT Purple + Blue
Nonthaburi Civic Center → Tao Poon (interchange) → Mo Chit (Chatuchak) or Sanam Chai (Rattanakosin). Weekend-reliable.
Grab · Pak Kret → Bangkok
฿250–350 late night. Both Michelin restaurants within Grab distance. ~30 min to Ratchada / Yaowarat.
Koh Kret ferry
Cross-river boat from Pak Kret Pier (near Wat Sanam Neua). ฿2–5. Short crossing, runs Sat/Sun.
