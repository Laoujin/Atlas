### Highlights

**[Techsauce Global Summit](https://summit.techsauce.co/)** is SE Asia's largest annual startup gathering — 130+ sessions across 10+ stages, 200+ tech exhibitors, 300+ speakers, 1-on-1 business matching [[1]](https://summit.techsauce.co/). Three-day pass required; draws investors, founders, and corporate digital teams. QSNCC is directly on BTS Asok / MRT Sukhumvit interchange.

**[IAIT 2026](https://www.iait-conf.org/2026/)** is the IEEE CIS-co-organised academic conference at KMUTT's Knowledge Exchange Center, Krung Thon Buri Road. Nine research tracks: LLMs, trustworthy AI, blockchain, data governance, sustainable computing [[2]](https://www.iait-conf.org/2026/). Registration: 6,500 THB (attendee-only); 15,000–18,500 THB (author/full). Accessible via BTS Wongwian Yai then taxi.

**[Microsoft Build //localhost:Bangkok](https://developer.microsoft.com/en-us/reactor/events/27200/)** is a free half-day Microsoft Reactor event for developers building AI on Azure — hands-on labs, Foundry and GitHub Copilot demos, developer networking [[3]](https://developer.microsoft.com/en-us/reactor/events/27200/). Advance registration required; capacity limited.

**[CYSEC Thailand 2026](https://thailand.cysecglobal.com/)** is CYSEC Global's SE Asia debut, targeting 150+ cybersecurity decision-makers [[4]](https://thailand.cysecglobal.com/). Theme: "Thailand Cyber Shield." Covers cloud security, AI-driven threat defence, zero-trust, and critical infrastructure. CPD-approved. Venue is the Royal Orchid Sheraton on the Chao Phraya riverside (BTS Saphan Taksin area).

**[DigiTech ASEAN + AI Connect](https://digitechasean.com/)** (Nonthaburi) is the region's dedicated digital business expo: 400 exhibitors, 12,000+ buyers, 10,000 sqm, co-hosted by Thailand's Ministry of Digital Economy & Society [[5]](https://digitechasean.com/overview/) [[6]](https://www.impact.co.th/en/visitors/event-calendar/exhibition-trade/digitech-asean-thailand-ai-connect-2026). AI Connect is a co-located track for AI business applications. Exhibition floor entry is typically free; certain conference sessions require a paid pass.

**[Test Automation Summit Bangkok](https://www.testingmind.com/event/test-automation-summit-bangkok/)** (Jun 11) is an Testingmind-organised full-day conference with 10+ speakers, keynotes and panel discussions. Theme: "Human-Centric Quality: AI-Driven, Sustainable, and Secure Testing." ISTQB Conference Network–recognised; team coaching sessions available [[12]](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/test-automation-summit-bangkok-2026-tickets-1963785950642).

**[Bangkok Tech Mixer](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/bangkok-tech-mixer-and-social-tech-ai-data-it-tickets-1987407602639)** is the easiest no-commitment option — free entry, no RSVP, every Friday 7pm at 8855 Restaurant & Bar (upstairs), Sukhumvit Soi 8 [[7]](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/bangkok-tech-mixer-and-social-tech-ai-data-it-tickets-1987407602639). Mix of developers, data scientists, product managers, founders, and career changers; Discord + WhatsApp communities available for pre-event attendee lookup.

**[AI Tinkerers Bangkok](https://bangkok.aitinkerers.org/)** is part of a 220-city global network with 103,000+ members [[8]](https://www.createwith.com/cities/bangkok). Monthly sessions feature live code demos from local AI builders — hands-on focus, not a lecture series.

**[Global AI Bangkok](https://www.meetup.com/global-ai-thailand/)** hosts monthly free evening events; past editions include AgentCamp Bangkok and Season of AI series. 791 members, 4.6★ rating [[17]](https://www.meetup.com/global-ai-thailand/).

**[GDG Bangkok](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-bangkok/)** (Google Developer Groups) runs free Google Cloud and AI workshops periodically [[9]](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-bangkok/). March 2026 "Build with AI Bangkok" was a full-day conference at Club Siam Glowfish, Siam Patumwan House.

**IMPACT** (47/569-576 Popular 3 Rd, Pakkred, Nonthaburi 11120) is Asia's largest convention campus at 140,000 sqm usable indoor space, open since 1999 [[10]](https://www.meetings-conventions.com/Meeting-Event-Venues/Nonthaburi-Thailand/Convention-Center/IMPACT-Exhibition-Center-p53227310). It is physically in Nonthaburi despite common "Bangkok" marketing. Best access: MRT Chaengwattana (Purple Line) → free shuttle, or expressway from Mo Chit / Don Mueang.

**[True Digital Park](https://www.truedigitalpark.com/en)** in South Sukhumvit is SE Asia's largest tech and startup campus (230,000 sqm), with 6,000+ innovation partners and 1,600+ international events hosted [[11]](https://technode.global/2026/03/09/true-digital-park-2026-is-the-year-for-thai-tech-ecosystem-to-find-its-true-rhythm-qa/) [[18]](https://www.truedigitalpark.com/en). Main informal meetup and community workshop venue; co-working, accelerators, and incubators all on-campus.
