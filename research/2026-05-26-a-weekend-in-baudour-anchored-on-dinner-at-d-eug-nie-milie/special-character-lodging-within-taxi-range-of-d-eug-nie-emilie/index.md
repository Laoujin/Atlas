> **Decision** — Closest to the table: **Cense de Lalouette** — an 18th-c Spanish post-relay farm in the same Saint-Ghislain commune, ~5-min taxi after dinner [[1]](https://www.visitmons.be/fr/les-hotels/cense-de-lalouette-218688). Most theatrical: **Martin's Dream Hotel** in central Mons, built inside a former neo-Gothic chapel/convent [[2]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/martin-s-dream-hotel-mons-sleep-church). Best small-group take-over: **Mons Dragon House** — five rooms each by a different local designer [[3]](https://www.monsdragonhouse.be/en). Bucket-list splurge: **Pairi Daiza Resort** — lodges and underwater rooms inside the zoo [[4]](https://www.pairidaiza.eu/en/pairi-daiza-resort/).

## The anchor

[d'Eugénie à Émilie](https://eugenie-emilie.be/) sits at 1 Place de la Résistance, 7331 Baudour [[5]](https://www.visitmons.be/fr/restaurants/deugenie-a-emilie-590762) — a village inside the Saint-Ghislain commune, ~8 km west of Mons. The Fernez family kitchen keeps its **two Michelin stars** in the 2026 guide [[6]](https://www.lavenir.net/lifestyle/2026/05/04/guide-michelin-2026-voici-les-restaurants-etoiles-en-wallonie-et-bruxelles-par-province-455VUTFCVZE5PJYGHKUE7TM7L4/). ⚠ The dining room was closed for renovation with a reopening targeted for late February 2026 — confirm your dinner date before booking lodging [[7]](https://www.gaultmillau.be/en/restaurants/deugenie-a-emilie-baudour).

A taxi from Mons centre to Baudour runs ~€35–€45 and ~13 min [[8]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Mons/Baudour); local taxi service operates in Baudour itself [[9]](https://www.taxiproxi.be/tarif-taxi-ville-baudour). Everything below is inside that radius.

## The shortlist

### [Cense de Lalouette](https://www.censedelalouette.be/) — Tertre (Saint-Ghislain)

An 18th-century farm built by the Spanish as a post-relay station, fully renovated [[1]](https://www.visitmons.be/fr/les-hotels/cense-de-lalouette-218688). Six independent apartment-gîtes accommodate 2–6 people each, with equipped kitchens, modern bathrooms and weekly housekeeping [[10]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/gite-de-la-tour-de-la-cense-de-lalouette.html). 188 Rue de Tournai, 7333 Saint-Ghislain [[11]](https://www.meetinhainaut.be/offres/cense-de-lalouette-saint-ghislain-fr-3336991/) — same commune as the restaurant, → the shortest taxi home of any option here. Period courtyard with old stone paving and a pond. The property also hosts weddings and events; ask about quiet evenings when booking.

### [Martin's Dream Hotel](https://www.martinshotels.com/en/page/martins-dream-hotel/serenity-and-originality-martins-dream-hotel-mons.11057.html) — Mons centre

4★, 62 rooms, opened under the Martin's Hotels banner in 2019 [[13]](https://www.martinshotels.com/en/page/martins-dream-hotel/serenity-and-originality-martins-dream-hotel-mons.11057.html). The building is a 19th-c neo-Gothic former chapel that earlier housed the Sisters of Belian Abbey [[2]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/martin-s-dream-hotel-mons-sleep-church). Rooms are themed on Belgian flavours, the Carnival of Binche, and the five continents; the Spa Martin's Alterego runs Cinq Mondes treatments with hammam, sauna and jacuzzi [[12]](https://www.martinshotels.com/en/page/martins-dream-hotel/.11212.html). Le Bistro Martin dines you under the chapel vaulting. 17 Rue de la Grande Triperie, Mons 7000.

### [Mons Dragon House](https://www.monsdragonhouse.be/en) — Mons centre

Five rooms, each by a different local Mons designer, themed on the city's folklore: Belfry, Golden Chariot, Doudou, Dragon, Canonesses [[3]](https://www.monsdragonhouse.be/en). House sleeps up to 9 — works as a family or small-group take-over. Reviewers single out the Doudou room ("warm, light, bright and spacious") and the panoramic Belfry view; the house is 1 minute from the Grand-Place [[14]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g188647-d14196539-Reviews-Mons_Dragon_House-Mons_Hainaut_Province_Wallonia.html). Rue de la Grande Triperie 7 — two doors from Martin's Dream.

### [Hôtel Saint-James](https://www.hotelstjames.be/) — Mons centre

3★ in an 18th-c Mons residence; 21 rooms arranged around an interior courtyard, less than 10 minutes on foot from the Grand-Place [[15]](https://www.visitmons.be/en/the-hotels/hotel-saint-james-474192). Quieter and smaller than Martin's Dream, lower price band — the practical Mons option without the chapel theatrics.

### [Hôtel Infotel](https://www.hotelinfotel.be/) — Mons centre

18th-c mansion 60 m south of the Grand-Place, rooms and suites around a private courtyard [[16]](https://www.tripadvisor.in/Hotel_Review-g188647-d271707-Reviews-Infotel-Mons_Hainaut_Province_Wallonia.html). ⚠ Tripadvisor reviews are mixed on cleanliness and ageing rooms in the older wing — ask explicitly for a refurbished room or skip.

### L'Écureuil — Frameries

A 2-room family B&B in Frameries, 10 min from Mons and 400 m from the SPARK'OH science park. Ranked #1 specialty lodging in Frameries on Tripadvisor with notably warm reviews of host Fabian [[17]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g2437918-d21409129-Reviews-Chambre_D_Hotes_L_Ecureuil-Frameries_Hainaut_Province_Wallonia.html). The hosts arrange airport, station and bus pickups on 72-h notice — handy for a no-car weekend.

### Les Peupliers — Erbisœul (Jurbise)

Three bedrooms in a fully renovated farmhouse surrounded by fields, 8 km from Mons centre and 12 km from Pairi Daiza [[18]](https://gitesdewallonie.be/fr/les-peupliers). Heated outdoor pool (27 °C, May–Sept), sauna for a small fee, and the kind of copious homemade-product breakfast that gets called out by name in reviews. Best fit if you want countryside and you're stacking the dinner with a Pairi Daiza day.

### [Pairi Daiza Resort](https://www.pairidaiza.eu/en/pairi-daiza-resort/) — Brugelette

The most singular option in the region — 209 themed lodge accommodations plus the new **Edenya** wing adding 88 rooms in 2026, including underwater hotel rooms [[4]](https://www.pairidaiza.eu/en/pairi-daiza-resort/). The Walrus House offers 6 rooms with underwater views into the walrus habitat [[19]](https://www.pairidaiza.eu/en/pairi-daiza-resort/walrus-house/). Books out fast: the resort logged 4,000 stays in 24 hours during a recent release, with weekend nights and underwater rooms going first [[20]](https://pairi-daiza.prezly.com/4k). ⚠ Set expectations on the views — one Tripadvisor reviewer paid €800 for a polar-bear room and saw a pump rather than a bear [[21]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1104709-d18894544-Reviews-Pairi_Daiza_Resort-Brugelette_Hainaut_Province_Wallonia.html). Stacking dinner at d'Eugénie with a Sunday at Pairi Daiza is the obvious play.

## How to pick

- **Want zero hassle after dinner?** → Cense de Lalouette. Same commune, ~5-min taxi, full apartment.
- **Want a Mons weekend with the most photogenic lodging?** → Martin's Dream Hotel. Sleep in the chapel, walk to the Grand-Place.
- **Travelling as 4–9 people?** → Mons Dragon House. Five themed rooms, full take-over.
- **Budget-conscious or solo traveller?** → Hôtel Saint-James (Mons) or L'Écureuil (Frameries).
- **Combining the dinner with Pairi Daiza?** → Pairi Daiza Resort if you book months ahead; otherwise Les Peupliers (12 km from both).
