Bed down after the two-star.

Eight character properties within taxi distance of the table at d'Eugénie à Émilie — an 18th-century Spanish post-relay farm in the same commune, a converted neo-Gothic chapel in Mons, designer-themed rooms, a renovated farmhouse with a heated pool, and underwater rooms inside a zoo. One bed each itinerary, ranked for how you actually weekend.
