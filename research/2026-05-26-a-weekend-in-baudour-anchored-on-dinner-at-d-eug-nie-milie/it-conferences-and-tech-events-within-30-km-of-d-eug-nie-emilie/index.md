> **Decision.** If you're anchoring a weekend on dinner at [d'Eugénie à Emilie][m1] in Baudour [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/hainaut/baudour/restaurant/d-eugenie-a-emilie), pin the trip to one of two December flagships in Mons (~10 km away): [**DevDay 2026**](https://www.devday.be/) on **3 Dec** (Wallonia's biggest dev conf, 600+ attendees) [[4]](https://www.devday.be/), or [**DevFest Belgium 2026**](https://devfest-belgium.be/) on **27 Nov** (GDG Mons, AI/Cloud/DevOps/Web) [[5]](https://dev.events/EU/BE/Mons)[[6]](https://devfest-belgium.be/). Off-flagship weekends, the recurring layer is [Le CLICK](https://le-click.be/) in Initialis Park — Forum Innovation & Cloud (March) [[8]](https://www.eventbrite.fr/e/billets-forum-innovation-cloud-2026-1977499324713), Girls in ICT Day (April) [[10]](https://technocite.be/girls-in-ict-day-2026/) — plus GDG Mons monthly meetups [[7]](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-mons/).

## The 30 km bubble

Baudour sits in Saint-Ghislain commune, west of Mons [[2]](https://www.visitmons.be/fr/restaurants/deugenie-a-emilie-590762). The disk pulls in: **Mons centre (~10 km)**, **Hornu (~3 km)**, **Saint-Ghislain (~3 km)**, **Frameries / Dour / Beloeil**, plus the Belgian border into **Valenciennes** (~30 km, on the edge) [[3]](https://www.lowcostroutes.com/en/cities-near-Baudour,_Belgium)[[19]](https://www.rome2rio.com/fr/s/Valenciennes/Baudour). Tournai is **just outside** at ~35 km straight-line [[18]](http://fr.distances-routieres.himmera.com/distance-tournai-baudour-voiture_km_itineraire-105015.html). The tech density is heavily concentrated in **Mons** — UMONS's Faculté Polytechnique, the Initialis science park, and the Microsoft Innovation Center anchor an unusually high IT footprint for a city of ~95k.

## Where the action actually happens (venues, ~10 km from Baudour)

- **[Le CLICK](https://le-click.be/)** — 1,500 m² innovation venue inside Mons's Initialis science park: a LAB (machine park), modular conference room, ideation space, coworking, and the de-facto home of Forum Innovation & Cloud and Girls in ICT Day [[9]](https://le-click.be/)[[10]](https://technocite.be/girls-in-ict-day-2026/). Walking distance to MIC.
- **MIC Belgique (Microsoft Innovation Center)** — Boulevard Initialis 1, Mons. Accelerator for tech startups; runs thematic Wednesday workshops, hackathons ("Hack in the Woods"), and hosted DevFest Mons 2023 [[12]](https://www.linkedin.com/company/micbelgique).
- **[TechnocITé](https://technocite.be/)** — Walloon ICT competence centre. Sites in **Mons** (~10 km) and **Hornu** (~3 km — closer to Baudour than Mons centre). Runs Girls4Cyber (3-month cyber programme starting Oct 2026), continuous ICT training, public events at Le CLICK [[10]](https://technocite.be/girls-in-ict-day-2026/)[[11]](https://technocite.be/).
- **[UMONS](https://web.umons.ac.be/en/)** — Faculté Polytechnique runs research conferences and colloquia on its agenda page; check before any weekend you're in town [[21]](https://web.umons.ac.be/en/agenda-events/).
- **[CETIC](https://www.cetic.be/?lang=en)** — applied ICT-research institute. HQ in Charleroi (outside the radius) but maintains a satellite research group at UMONS Mons; produces hybrid events and academic-industry workshops [[20]](https://www.cetic.be/?lang=en).

## On the edge (Valenciennes, ~30 km, across the FR border)

- **[Serre Numérique](https://serre-numerique.fr/)** — 17,000 m² ecosystem for video games, animation, design, VR, serious games, 3D printing; houses Rubika and a business space. Tram-served from Valenciennes centre. Hosts the IA Day during Festival "IA avec Nous" each June and recurring Rubika open houses [[14]](https://serre-numerique.fr/)[[15]](https://numerique360.banquedesterritoires.fr/evenements/locaux/festival-ia-avec-nous-2026/)[[16]](https://www.valenciennes.fr/agenda/rubika-journee-portes-ouvertes/).

## Just outside the bubble (worth knowing)

- **[Eurometropolitan e-Campus](https://ee-campus.be/)** — in Froyennes near Tournai, ~35 km from Baudour (just over the 30 km line). Wallonie Picarde digital training campus; host of the **W'happy Digital Day** (60+ exhibitors, 20 conferences, 15 workshops in past editions). No confirmed 2026 date in public sources yet; check directly [[17]](https://ee-campus.be/).
- **DevFest Belgium 2026 venue caveat** — `dev.events` and `conference-hall.io` list the event as Mons-region for 27 Nov, but the official site shows "à définir" (TBD) for the date and venue as of May 2026. Confirm before booking [[5]](https://dev.events/EU/BE/Mons)[[6]](https://devfest-belgium.be/).

## Bottom line

Two weekends a year (late Nov, early Dec) can be built **with conviction** around DevDay or DevFest Belgium plus the Saturday-night Michelin two-star at d'Eugénie à Emilie [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/hainaut/baudour/restaurant/d-eugenie-a-emilie). Outside those, Mons offers a steady drip of one-day events at Le CLICK and GDG Mons meetups — fine if the dinner is the anchor and the tech event is the side; less compelling as the primary draw. For deeper programming (a multi-day conference, exhibitor floor), the Belgian options closer to ~50 km in Brussels or Ghent outrank anything inside the strict 30 km bubble.
