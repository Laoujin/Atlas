## The two anchor institutions

**[Stellwerk18](https://www.stellwerk18.de/)** — Digitales Gründerzentrum Südostoberbayern, Eduard-Rüber-Straße 7, 83022 Rosenheim [[13]](https://www.bayern-innovativ.de/en/kompetenzen/competencies-technology-transfer/stellwerk-18-digitale-wirtschaft-suedostoberbayern/). Runs most of the public-facing startup / digital events in the radius, jointly with TH Rosenheim and the IHK [[6]](https://www.stellwerk18.de/events/gruendungsfestival-2026-traunstein/). Worth following their `/veranstaltungen` page if you might return.

**[ROSIK e.V.](https://www.rosik.com/)** — the regional IT-companies industry network, 230+ members, founded 1999, closely partnered with TH Rosenheim's computer science faculty [[12]](https://www.rosik.com/). Runs occasional Stammtisch evenings (e.g. ISO 27001 practice for IT service providers). Schedule beyond the June AI breakfast wasn't posted as of late May [[11]](https://www.stellwerk18.de/veranstaltungen/) — monitor `rosik.com/termine` mid-summer for autumn dates.

**[TH Rosenheim Campus Chiemgau](https://www.th-rosenheim.de/die-hochschule/standorte/campus-chiemgau-in-traunstein)** — Stadtplatz 32, 83278 Traunstein. Digitalization-focused satellite campus; hosts the Sundowner, Gründungsfestival, "KI im Fokus" and the InnovateTheAlps hackathon when it runs in November [[10]](https://www.th-rosenheim.de/die-hochschule/standorte/campus-chiemgau-in-traunstein/events-am-campus-chiemgau).

## Honest assessment

The Chiemgau-around-ES:SENZ is not a tech destination. It's a regional startup + applied-automation pocket leaning on one university campus (TH Rosenheim) and one incubator (Stellwerk18). Programming is concentrated in spring (Feb–Apr) and one autumn conference (PPC Camp, Oct 29) [[9]](https://www.ppc-camp.de/). If you want to combine ES:SENZ with a tech event in 2026, your realistic options are:

- **Time the trip for 29 October 2026** → dinner Friday + PPC Camp at KuKo on the Thursday [[9]](https://www.ppc-camp.de/).
- **Mid-June 2026** → easy combo with Stellwerk18 STARTUP FESTIVAL or the Sundowner (both 17 June) if you're already booked [[10]](https://www.th-rosenheim.de/die-hochschule/standorte/campus-chiemgau-in-traunstein/events-am-campus-chiemgau) [[11]](https://www.stellwerk18.de/veranstaltungen/), but neither is a substantial conference.
- **Otherwise** → expand the radius. Munich (~85 km) and Salzburg (~50 km) carry the actual regional IT conference calendar; the 30 km circle around Grassau will not.
