TL;DR. ES:SENZ sits at Das Achental in Grassau, dead-centre of a 30 km circle that overlaps the southeast Chiemsee shore and the Chiemgau Alps. Use the weekend like this:

· Headline day: the Chiemsee boat circuit — Übersee–Fraueninsel–Herreninsel with Schloss Herrenchiemsee. From Übersee/Feldwies (7 km from Grassau) the pier runs only 25 May–22 Sep, four sailings/day[15]; otherwise drive 12 km to Prien/Stock for year-round ferries[25].

· Half-day mountain: Kampenwand (Aschau, 1,669 m, €28–32 RT)[1] or Hochfelln (Bergen, 1,674 m, €30 RT, 15-min gondola)[6]. Both no-hike-required panoramas.

· Rainy-day: Chiemgau Thermen Bad Endorf (iodine thermal, 09:00–22:00, €31–39)[59] or Museum Salz & Moor at Klaushäusl literally between Rottau and Grassau (≤5 km, €4)[77].

· Don't bother: Rauschberg cable car (closed indefinitely for rebuild)[8]; Königssee and Berchtesgaden Salzbergwerk (~60 km, both outside the 30 km radius — feasible but eat the whole day)[91][38].

The Chiemsee day (headline option)

This is the trip everyone in Grassau does at least once, because the lake is on your doorstep and Ludwig II's Versailles is on an island in it.

Getting on the water

Two embarkation points matter from Grassau. Übersee/Feldwies is the closest (~7 km), but the pier runs only 25 May – 22 September, with four daily sailings at 10:00, 11:55, 14:15, 15:50 — Fraueninsel in ~20 min, Herreninsel +10 min[15][16]. Übersee round-trip to Fraueninsel is €12.60 adult / €6.30 child[16]. Prien/Stock (~12 km) is the year-round hub: Prien→Herreninsel €11.50 RT, Inseltour West (both islands) €13.40 RT[14][25]. Bundle the boat with the Chiemsee-Bahn, the world's oldest continuously operated steam tram[48] (since 1887, 1.8 km Prien station→Stock harbour, 8 min, mid-May to mid-September, departures :15 from Prien and :50 from Stock)[19][20].

Herreninsel — Schloss Herrenchiemsee

From the pier it is a 15–20 min walk through woods to the palace, or take a horse-drawn carriage[24]. The New Palace — Ludwig II's unfinished Versailles homage — opens daily 09:00–18:00 (April–24 Oct, last tour ~17:00) and 10:00–16:45 in winter; admission is by guided tour only at €11 / €10 reduced[17][18]. The "Herrenchiemsee Island" combo at €14 (regular) bundles the New Palace, the Ludwig II Museum, the Augustinian Monastery and the 18 May–18 Oct special exhibition; under-18s free[18]. Don't skip the Altes Schloss — the former Augustinian canonry houses the museum on the 1948 Verfassungskonvent that drafted Germany's Basic Law, plus a Johann Baptist Zimmermann-painted library[26].

Fraueninsel — Abtei Frauenwörth

15.5 ha, car-free, ~300 residents[21]. Frauenwörth abbey, founded by Duke Tassilo III in the late 8th century (the abbey itself dates its founding to 772; Wikipedia says 782), is the oldest continuously operating nunnery in Germany; the three-aisled Romanesque minster (built c.1100 on 9th-century Carolingian foundations) has eleven altars and early-Romanesque frescoes[27]. The Carolingian Torhalle dates from c. 850; two ~1000-year-old lime trees (Tassilolinde, Marienlinde) shade the cloister[21]. 45-min guided tours by a nun €6/person, weekday late mornings by appointment[28]. Provisioning: nuns sell marzipan and Chiemseer Klosterlikör; island fishermen smoke fresh Renken in their huts[22].

Swim it instead

Strandbad Übersee in Feldwieser Bay claims southern Germany's longest contiguous sandy beach — 5 km of shallow entry, lifeguard, café June–Sept 09:00–20:00, paid entry with Kurkarte discount[23][53]. A 40,000 m² lido with volleyball and table-tennis[53].

Castles, history, pilgrimages

Schloss Hohenaschau

Aschau · ~14 km · €7 adult / €5 child / €15 family

Guided tour only, May–Oct. Slots: Tue/Thu/Sun/holidays 13:30 & 15:00; Wed/Fri 10:00 & 11:30. 15–20 min uphill walk to the castle gate[29]. Pair with the Falknerei: 45-min raptor flight shows at 11:00 and 15:00 through 18 Oct 2026, €10/5 (family €28), cash only, weather-dependent[30][44].

Holzknechtmuseum Ruhpolding

Ruhpolding · ~27 km · €9 adult / €20 family

Bezirk-Oberbayern open-air museum on Chiemgau woodcutter life. 30,000 m² with 3 indoor exhibition levels & thematic outdoor trail. Open Easter–October, Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00. Fully accessible, bilingual DE/EN, dogs free[31][32][52].

Maria Eck

Siegsdorf · ~20 km · 882 m

Most-visited pilgrimage church in the Archdiocese of Munich-Freising. 1626 Franciscan monastery with adjoining Gasthof — combine with a hike from the Eckerwirt parking area rather than visiting standalone[33].

Maria Klobenstein

Schleching/Kössen border · in the Tiroler-Ache gorge

Loreto Chapel (1701) and Mariahilf Chapel (1731–33) structurally joined since 1890, built around a venerated split rock and spring. Sits directly on the Bavaria–Tyrol border[34].

Salinenpark Traunstein

Traunstein · ~18 km · free

Opened 2019 for the city's 400th salt-works anniversary; freely accessible open-air museum with reconstructed Reiffenstuel pump-wheel, a working 19th-c. Reichenbach water-column engine, and ~140-species apothecary garden[35]. Inside the Ferdinandi- and Albertistock, the Soleleitung exhibition lets you take a virtual flight along "the oldest pipeline in the world", with the baroque Salinenkapelle St. Rupert & Maximilian (1630–31) attached[36]. Costumed Salzmaier tours recount life around 1800[88].

Burg Marquartstein

Marquartstein · ~5 km · exterior/hike only

1075 castle on a rocky spur above the Tiroler Ache, the historic core of the Achental. Richard Strauss married soprano Pauline de Ahna in the castle church on 10 Sept 1894. Privately owned — appreciate from circular trails (e.g. from Freiweidach)[37].

Pfarrkirche St. Georg Ruhpolding

Ruhpolding · ~27 km · free

High-Rococo jewel begun 1738, consecrated 1754. Left-side pulpit unmatched in Bavaria. Shelters the c.1220/30 Ruhpoldinger Madonna, one of the oldest Marienfiguren in the archdiocese[51].

Lakes for swimming

Reifinger See

In Grassau · 540 m · 8 m deep · free

20,000 m² butterfly-shaped lake with non-swimmer zone, beach volleyball, changing rooms, barrier-free access. Open 08:00–21:00, free parking, no lifeguard, dogs banned May–Sept[67][68].

Wössner See

Unterwössen · ~10 km · free

Natural bathing lake with sun terrace, water slide, children's play area, Seestüberl. Swim at own risk[69].

Taubensee

Above Schleching · alpine, hike-in

3.6 ha, 40 m deep, reaches ~24 °C in summer. Reachable only on foot via Kroatensteig. The Bavaria–Tyrol border runs through the lake[70].

Weitsee · Mittersee · Lödensee

Reit im Winkl/Ruhpolding · ~25 km

Three-lake group in a nature reserve. Drinking-water-quality swimming[71].

SUP

HEIUKI at Julius-Exter-Promenade 15, Übersee — contactless self-service rental, daily May–October. Suggested routes: 3 km beginner tour to Feldwieser Bay, 6 km advanced loop around Krautinsel[72]. Additional SUP hubs at Bernau-Felden, Seebruck and Chieming Strandbad[73].

Rainy-day picks

Museum Salz & Moor (Klaushäusl)

Between Rottau and Grassau · ≤5 km · €4 adult / €7 family

Open 1 May–19 Oct, Tue–Sun 11:00–17:00[77]. Covers the world's first pipeline (Bad Reichenhall→Traunstein, 1619) and high-moor ecology. Strongest rainy-day fit because of distance[78].

Freizeitpark Ruhpolding

Ruhpolding · ~27 km

Bills itself as Bavaria's most weather-proof amusement park. Dragon ride, indoor crystal mine, Biathlon-Duell, animatronic fairy-tale houses[89].

PRIENAVERA Erlebnisbad

Prien am Chiemsee · ~12 km

Five indoor pools, 62 m daylight tube slide, sauna world — strong rainy-day choice with kids on the lake's northwest shore[93].

Alpine coaster & gondola joyrides

Chiemgau Coaster

Ruhpolding · ~27 km · €6 single / €33 hour-flatrate

1,100 m alpine coaster with two loops 7 m above a hiking trail. Daily 10:00–18:00 June–early Nov[74][75].

Kampenwandbahn (no-hike option)

Aschau · ~14 km

Ride to 1,461 m for the panorama, café at the top, walk back the same way[81].

Hocheck Oberaudorf borderline

~32 km · just outside 30 km radius

1,000 m alpine coaster with a 360° super-loop. Worth the extra 2 km if you've exhausted Ruhpolding[92].
