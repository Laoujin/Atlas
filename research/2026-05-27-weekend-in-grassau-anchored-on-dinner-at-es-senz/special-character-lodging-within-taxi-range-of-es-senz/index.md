## The anchor

ES:SENZ sits inside Das Achental at Mietenkamer Straße 65, 83224 Grassau, in the Chiemgau corridor between Lake Chiemsee and the Kampenwand[[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/bayern/grassau/restaurant/es-senz)[[2]](https://www.das-achental.com/en/es-senz.html). Edip Sigl's kitchen earned its third Michelin star in March 2024 — currently Bavaria's only three-star[[3]](https://www.traunsteiner-tagblatt.de/region/landkreis-traunstein/grassau_artikel,-drei-michelinsterne-fuer-edip-sigl-_arid,860489.html). Dinner service runs Wed–Sat 18:30–23:00[[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/bayern/grassau/restaurant/es-senz), so "taxi range" here means **a property where a 22:30 departure won't ruin the evening** — call it ≤20 minutes on the meter.

## The picks, in detail

### 1. Residenz Heinz Winkler — the heritage default

The building anchored medieval Aschau as the village's *Hotel Post*; the brasserie *Tafern* still references the early-15th-century tavern on the same footprint[[5]](https://www.residenz-heinz-winkler.de/en/). Heinz Winkler converted it into a 5-star boutique in 1989, ran a 3-Michelin-star restaurant for decades, then died in October 2022 — the two stars went in 2023 and the kitchen clawed one back in 2024 under son Alexander[[6]](https://www.abendzeitung-muenchen.de/promis/nach-heinz-winklers-tod-residenz-in-aschau-verliert-beide-michelin-sterne-art-891477). 32 rooms with balconies, an interior pool/Laconium spa, and a mountain terrace facing the Kampenwand[[5]](https://www.residenz-heinz-winkler.de/en/). ⚠ The hotel itself is the kitchen's home turf — if you came specifically for ES:SENZ, picking a competing Michelin star one valley over may or may not appeal.

### 2. Hotel Bonnschlössl — actual castle, intimate scale

Built 1477 as a farmhouse, converted to a small *Schloss* in the early 20th century by the Bavarian stage actor Ferdinand Bonn (who played Hamlet at Vienna's Burgtheater and later King Ludwig II in silent films)[[7]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/de/bonnschloessl.html). After serving as a children's home after WWII, the Stolz family bought it in 1965 and reopened it in July 2023 following a deep refurbishment[[7]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/de/bonnschloessl.html). Now ten apartments (2–5 person) at Ferdinand-Bonn-Straße 2 with steam bath, Finnish sauna and a manicured park[[8]](https://www.ferienwohnungen-bonnschloessl-chiemsee.de/). ⚠ Apartments not hotel rooms — booking platforms often show multi-night minimums; check direct for short weekends.

### 3. Inselhotel zur Linde — the only car-free option

A 600-year-old *Gasthaus* at the high point of Fraueninsel, framed by Benedictine convent buildings — historically the regular table of the *Chiemseemaler* painters who broke from 19th-century historicism[[12]](http://www.linde-frauenchiemsee.de/impressum.html). The catch: the scheduled Gstadt ferry stops running ~19:00. **The fix:** Chiemsee Schifffahrt's *Nachttaxi* runs on demand between Gstadt and the islands until midnight — phone **+49 170 2053542** about 30 minutes before you want to leave the mainland after dinner[[13]](https://www.chiemsee-schifffahrt.de/de/sonderfahrt/nachttaxifahrten). Pre-dinner plan: taxi Grassau → Gstadt (~20 min), Nachttaxi to the island. Pick this if the *island* itself is the point of the weekend.

### 4. Farmhouse 1604 — the curated stone house

The smallest property in this list: a listed fisherman's stone house in Seethal whose foundation stone was laid in 1604, refurbished by the former owners of nearby Chiemgauhof into four large self-catering apartments (95–230 m²) with open kitchens, balconies, an outdoor sauna, an Airstream, and a Farmhouse e-boat for the lake[[9]](https://farmhouse1604.com/). Übersee is ~4.8 km / 9 min taxi from Grassau[[11]](https://www.rome2rio.com/de/s/%C3%9Cbersee-Chiemgau/Grassau). Right pick if you'd rather have a private kitchen than a front desk, and if "character" to you means *patina* not gilt.

### 5. Chiemgauhof Lakeside Retreat — the modernist counter-pick

Same village as Farmhouse 1604, opposite aesthetic. 28 suites of Matteo Thun minimalism directly on the lake with private beach and pier, member of The Leading Hotels of the World[[10]](https://www.chiemgauhof.com/en/). Stylistically the closest fit to ES:SENZ's own modern-Bavarian register — *contemporary* character rather than historic, but distinctly designed and very much not generic.

### 6. Das Achental — zero-friction in-building

If your priority is walking 90 seconds from the dining room to your bed: Das Achental is the building that *contains* ES:SENZ. Michelin's hotel listing calls it "a perfect representative of modern-rustic Bavarian hospitality" with alpine-chic rooms and a full spa & golf course[[17]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/hotels-stays/Grassau/das-achental-resort-13234). Character is contemporary alpine, not historic — the trade-off is honest: lowest logistics, lowest narrative.

## Practical notes for a Saturday ES:SENZ booking

- **Taxi back.** Order the return at the restaurant before sitting down; the Chiemgau region has dedicated taxi services around the lake and Grassau itself is small enough that 23:00 returns are routine.
- **Fraueninsel logistics.** If staying on the island, leave the island around 17:30, taxi Gstadt → Grassau (~20 min), then Nachttaxi back from Gstadt after dinner — and pre-confirm the night-taxi line is staffed that weekend[[13]](https://www.chiemsee-schifffahrt.de/de/sonderfahrt/nachttaxifahrten).
- **Two-night minimums.** Both Bonnschlössl[[8]](https://www.ferienwohnungen-bonnschloessl-chiemsee.de/) and Farmhouse 1604[[9]](https://farmhouse1604.com/) operate as apartments rather than hotel rooms — check minimum-stay rules before locking in a one-night Saturday plan.
- **ES:SENZ closes in waves.** The kitchen is dark for several multi-week blocks in 2026 (2–11 Jan, 16–22 Feb, 7–12 Apr, 1–7 Jun, 31 Aug–13 Sep, 2–15 Nov)[[2]](https://www.das-achental.com/en/es-senz.html) — verify against your hotel dates.
