one castle, one island, one resort downstairs.

· TELEGRAM ·
FOR THE SATURDAY BOOKING
STOP · READ TWICE · STOP
  1. Taxi back. Order the return at the restaurant before you sit down. Grassau is small enough that 23:00 returns are routine. STOP
  2. Fraueninsel logistics. If sleeping on the island, leave by ~17:30 — taxi Gstadt→Grassau (~20 min), then Nachttaxi back from Gstadt after dinner. Pre-confirm the night line is staffed that weekend. STOP
  3. Two-night minimums. Bonnschlössl and Farmhouse 1604 both operate as apartments — check minimum-stay rules before locking in a one-night Saturday. STOP
  4. ES:SENZ runs Wed–Sat 18:30–23:00. Phone Das Achental to lock the table before the room.[1] STOP · END.
