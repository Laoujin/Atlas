> **Decision.** Book [Das Achental](https://www.das-achental.com/en/)'s **es:senz Dine & Sleep** package (from €515 pp/night) — the restaurant is literally in your hotel [[1]](https://www.das-achental.com/en/es-senz.html)[[2]](https://www.das-achental.com/en/). If the resort is full or out of budget, the only properties you can truly *walk* to after a wine-paired tasting menu sit in Mietenkam village (5–15 min): [Ferienwohnungen Moritz](https://www.ferienwohnungen-moritz.de/), [Gästehaus Hochgern](https://www.chiemsee-alpenland.de/chiemsee/ukv/house/Grassau-Mietenkam-Ferienwohnung-Gaestehaus-Hochgern-DEU00000061619833644), or [Ferienwohnung Kreuz](https://www.chiemsee-alpenland.de/chiemsee/ukv/house/Grassau-Mietenkam-Ferienwohnung-Ferienwohnung-Kreuz-DEU00000060350063389). Anything in Grassau town centre (Sperrer, Kleinrachlhof) is ~1.5–2 km along an unlit country road — book a [Taxi Chiemgau](http://www.taxi-chiemgau.de/) instead [[3]](http://www.taxi-chiemgau.de/).

## Where ES:SENZ actually is

ES:SENZ is the in-house three-Michelin-star restaurant of Das Achental Resort, Mietenkamer Straße 65, 83224 Grassau [[1]](https://www.das-achental.com/en/es-senz.html)[[4]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/bayern/grassau/restaurant/es-senz). The resort sits on the southern edge of the Mietenkam district, reached by a 900 m turnoff from Bahnhofstrasse, ~1 km south of Grassau town centre [[5]](https://www.das-achental.com/en/getting-here.html)[[6]](https://www.hrs.com/en/hotel/181). The "walking distance" question therefore breaks into three rings:

- **Ring 0 — same building.** Das Achental itself. Zero metres from your room to the dining-room door.
- **Ring 1 — Mietenkam village.** A small cluster of guesthouses and apartments around the Mietenkam church, north of the resort. Roughly 5–15 min on foot via Mietenkamer Straße [[7]](https://www.chiemsee-alpenland.de/chiemsee/ukv/house/Grassau-Mietenkam-Ferienwohnung-Gaestehaus-Hochgern-DEU00000061619833644).
- **Ring 2 — Grassau town centre.** Hotels and pensions ~1.5–2 km away along Bahnhofstrasse [[5]](https://www.das-achental.com/en/getting-here.html)[[8]](https://www.grassau.info/hotel-gasthof-sperrer-gmbh-co-kg). Walkable in daylight; **not** something you want to do at 23:00 after a wine pairing.

## Why "stay on-site" is the dominant pick

1. **Wine pairings make driving a non-option.** ES:SENZ is a three-star tasting-menu room with paired wines [[13]](https://lesgrandestablesdumonde.com/en/restaurant/es-senz/); the resort solves the "how do we get home" problem by being the home.
2. **The Dine & Sleep package is the only meaningful price discount.** €515 pp for room + tasting menu undercuts the sum of a room rate (~€448 [[12]](https://www.readytotrip.com/hotels/Germany/Bavaria/Chiemsee/Grassau/das-achental-resort/)) and an à-la-carte tasting menu booked separately.
3. **2,000 m² spa, infinity pool, multiple other restaurants on the property** [[2]](https://www.das-achental.com/en/) — turns a single-dinner trip into a full weekend without ever needing to leave.
4. **Mietenkam is residential and small.** The "village alternatives" are vacation apartments, not hotels — they suit a longer self-catering stay, less so a one-night Michelin run [[14]](https://www.ferienwohnungen-moritz.de/)[[7]](https://www.chiemsee-alpenland.de/chiemsee/ukv/house/Grassau-Mietenkam-Ferienwohnung-Gaestehaus-Hochgern-DEU00000061619833644).

## When village alternatives make sense

- Das Achental is sold out for your date (the resort has 179 rooms total — finite supply on a Saturday with a three-star booking pattern [[15]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1056778-d260107-Reviews-Das_Achental-Grassau_Upper_Bavaria_Bavaria.html)).
- You're staying multiple nights and only one of them is the ES:SENZ dinner — a Mietenkam Ferienwohnung at ~€100–€200/night beats two nights of resort pricing.
- You want a self-catering kitchen for the non-ES:SENZ meals.

Pick **Ferienwohnungen Moritz** or **Ferienwohnung Kreuz** in that case — both list direct phone contact (08641/3921 and +49 8641 697960 respectively) [[14]](https://www.ferienwohnungen-moritz.de/)[[10]](https://www.chiemsee-alpenland.de/chiemsee/ukv/house/Grassau-Mietenkam-Ferienwohnung-Ferienwohnung-Kreuz-DEU00000060350063389). Mietenkam has limited online booking; expect to email or call.

## If you're staying in Grassau centre

You're in taxi territory. [Taxi Chiemgau](http://www.taxi-chiemgau.de/) runs 24/7 around the Chiemsee — book the return leg in advance because the village taxi pool is small and a Saturday-night Michelin slot won't have a 22:30 walk-up available [[3]](http://www.taxi-chiemgau.de/). Budget €15–€25 each way from Marktstraße to Mietenkamer Straße 65.

## Heads-up on dates

ES:SENZ has multiple closed weeks across 2026 — relevant to this trip's window: **01.06–07.06** [[1]](https://www.das-achental.com/en/es-senz.html). If your "Saturday evening" lands on **6 June 2026**, the kitchen is dark; you'll want **30 May 2026** instead. Confirm before locking lodging — the resort itself stays open, but the entire premise of this folder is the dinner.

Chef [Edip Sigl](https://www.das-achental.com/en/our-head-chef-edip-sigl.html) is the culinary director — useful name to drop when booking the tasting menu directly through the resort [[16]](https://www.das-achental.com/en/our-head-chef-edip-sigl.html).
