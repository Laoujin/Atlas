Risks, limitations, and adoption barriers of AI-assisted code review (2026)

1. Quality/accuracy limitations: false positives, noise, hallucinated review comments, low precision, the shallow-context ceiling, and what AI reviewers systematically miss (architectural/logic/security bugs).
2. Security and confidentiality risks: prompt injection via PRs/issues, data exfiltration, code/IP leakage to vendors, model-training-on-your-code, supply-chain and autonomous-fix risks.
3. Human-factor adoption barriers: alert/review fatigue, automation bias and over-reliance, rubber-stamping, deskilling of junior engineers, trust erosion, developer pushback.
4. Organizational, economic, and governance barriers: cost/ROI uncertainty, integration friction, vendor lock-in, enterprise procurement, regulated-industry constraints, policy/governance gaps.
5. Legal, compliance, and accountability risks: liability for AI-missed or AI-introduced bugs, IP/licensing of AI-suggested code, EU AI Act and regulatory exposure, audit trails, accountability for the merge decision.
6. The evidence gap: vendor benchmark inflation vs independent studies, the productivity paradox (measured slowdowns), reproducibility, and what is genuinely unknown about whether AI review helps.
