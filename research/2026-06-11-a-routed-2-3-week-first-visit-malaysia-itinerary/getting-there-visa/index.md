> **Decision.** Fly into **Kuala Lumpur (KLIA)** — not Penang/Langkawi. It's the cheapest and best-connected entry, and it's the natural head of your West-Coast chain (KL → Malacca → Penang → Cameron → Langkawi) [[8]](https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-amsterdam-to-federal-territory-of-kuala-lumpur.html). As Belgians you get **90 days visa-free**, but you MUST file the **free MDAC** within the **3-day window before arrival** [[1]](https://www.imi.gov.my/index.php/en/pengumuman/malaysia-digital-arrival-card-mdac/)[[3]](https://www.hinterlandtravel.com/belgium/destinations/malaysia). Carry your **passport on every domestic flight into Sabah/Sarawak** — Borneo runs its own immigration and re-stamps you on arrival [[5]](https://snapbackpacker.com/entering-sabah-sarawak-in-2026-documents-procedures-dos-donts/).

## Flights: Ghent → KLIA

Ghent has no airport; rail to **BRU** (~1h) or **AMS** (~2h, direct IC). No carrier flies Europe→KL nonstop except **KLM AMS–KUL** (~12h25); everything else is one-stop via a Gulf/hub airport, ~15–18h door-of-plane [[8]](https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-amsterdam-to-federal-territory-of-kuala-lumpur.html)[[7]](https://www.kayak.com/flight-routes/Brussels-Bruxelles-National-BRU/Kuala-Lumpur-Intl-KUL).

|-----------------------|-------------------|--------------|-------------------------------|-------|
| Qatar Airways         | Doha (DOH)        | ~15–17h      | ~€680–760 [[7]][k]            | Best one-stop product; frequent BRU service |

Fares are shoulder-season round-trip estimates; peak (Jul–Aug, Dec) runs €200–400 higher. Book a **Thursday** departure where flexible — typically the cheapest day [[7]](https://www.kayak.com/flight-routes/Brussels-Bruxelles-National-BRU/Kuala-Lumpur-Intl-KUL).

**Why KLIA, not Penang/Langkawi direct:** no European one-stop reaches Penang — you'd buy a second connection and skip KL + Malacca [[8]](https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-amsterdam-to-federal-territory-of-kuala-lumpur.html). Reach the upper west coast as cheap internal hops instead: AirAsia/Batik/MAS **KUL→Penang or →Langkawi** run ~€20–50 pp. Plan the homebound long-haul out of **KL** at the end of the Borneo leg.

## Entry for a Belgian passport

| Requirement        | Detail |
|--------------------|--------|
| Visa               | None — **90 days visa-free** (EU national), per 180-day rolling window [[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_policy_of_Malaysia)[[3]](https://www.hinterlandtravel.com/belgium/destinations/malaysia) |
| Passport validity  | Valid **≥6 months** beyond intended stay [[3]](https://www.hinterlandtravel.com/belgium/destinations/malaysia) |
| Onward ticket      | Proof of onward/return travel required at check-in/immigration [[3]](https://www.hinterlandtravel.com/belgium/destinations/malaysia) |
| **MDAC**           | Compulsory digital arrival card; **free**; file **only within 72h before arrival** at the official portal (earlier submissions are rejected) [[1]](https://www.imi.gov.my/index.php/en/pengumuman/malaysia-digital-arrival-card-mdac/)[[2]](https://blog.wego.com/mdac/) |

**MDAC mechanics.** One form **per traveller** (so two for the couple) at [imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main](https://imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main); you get a QR/confirmation to show at the counter. It's required even to use the autogate [[2]](https://blog.wego.com/mdac/). Ignore the many lookalike sites that charge a "fee" — the official one costs nothing [[1]](https://www.imi.gov.my/index.php/en/pengumuman/malaysia-digital-arrival-card-mdac/).

## Borneo (Sabah + Sarawak) re-clearance

Sabah and Sarawak run **separate immigration from Peninsular Malaysia**: even on a domestic flight (e.g. KUL→Kota Kinabalu, or into Kuching) you clear immigration and get a fresh visit-pass stamp on arrival [[5]](https://snapbackpacker.com/entering-sabah-sarawak-in-2026-documents-procedures-dos-donts/)[[6]](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/international-travel/International-Travel-Country-Information-Pages/Malaysia.html). Practical effect for you:

- **Carry your physical passport on every internal flight** to/from Borneo — a copy won't clear the counter [[6]](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/international-travel/International-Travel-Country-Information-Pages/Malaysia.html).
- No new MDAC and no fee for these internal moves; your 90-day visa-free entitlement still covers the whole trip [[5]](https://snapbackpacker.com/entering-sabah-sarawak-in-2026-documents-procedures-dos-donts/).
- Each state issues its own pass, so **don't lose the stamps** — keep a couple of passport pages free.
