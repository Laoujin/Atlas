> **Decision.** For a Saturday three-Michelin-star dinner [[17]](https://guide.michelin.com/gb/en/lancashire/aughton/restaurant/moor-hall) at [Moor Hall](https://moorhall.com/) [[2]](https://moorhall.com/), the only genuine walking-distance lodging is **Moor Hall itself** — 14 rooms from £270 B&B [[4]](https://www.visitlancashire.com/places-to-stay/moor-hall-restaurant-with-rooms-p856170). It is also the only on-property option: the restaurant sits on a 5-acre rural plot on Prescot Road [[1]](https://moorhall.com/moor-hall/about/), and the next-closest hotels — [West Tower](https://www.west-tower.com/) (1.4 mi) and [Birches Brow](https://birchesbrow.co.uk/) (1.6 mi) — are along unlit country lanes [[9]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g3213860-d12388578-Moor_Hall_Restaurant_With_Rooms-Aughton_Ormskirk_Lancashire_England.html). Treat them as a £4–5 taxi ride [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3213860-d10823614-Reviews-Birches_Brow_Boutique_Guestrooms-Aughton_Ormskirk_Lancashire_England.html), not a walk back from dinner.

## What "walking distance" actually means here

Moor Hall sits at Prescot Road, Aughton, L39 6RT — a Grade II*-listed manor on a five-acre plot near (but not inside) Aughton village [[1]](https://moorhall.com/moor-hall/about/). The location is rural: Prescot Road, Formby Lane and Mill Lane are narrow lanes with limited pavement and no streetlights in stretches. After a long tasting menu finishing late, "walkable in daylight" and "walkable back from dinner" are different questions — and only Moor Hall passes the second test.

The nearest railway station, Town Green (Merseyrail Northern Line to Liverpool / Ormskirk) [[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_Green_railway_station), is a 15–20-minute walk from the restaurant [[8]](https://www.hot-dinners.com/Features/Travel/moor-hall-restaurant-hotel-review-aughton-lancashire-mark-birchall) — useful for arrivals, but not as a lodging anchor.

## On-property: Moor Hall

[m1]: https://moorhall.com/rooms

The Garden Rooms are prefabricated [Koto](https://koto.co.uk/) cabins opened July 2024, named after botanicals (Melilot, Sea Buckthorn, Woodruff, Sweet Cicely, Oxalis, Borage, Anise Hyssop) [[5]](https://koto.co.uk/koto-at-moor-hall) [[6]](https://moorhall.com/news/sneak-peak-new-garden-rooms). A *Country & Town House* reviewer staying in Borage praised the muted Scandi finish, outdoor hot tub, and UK-made Pelegrims toiletries [[7]](https://www.countryandtownhouse.com/food-and-drink/reviews/moor-hall-lancashire-review/). Rates are bed & breakfast inclusive [[8]](https://www.hot-dinners.com/Features/Travel/moor-hall-restaurant-hotel-review-aughton-lancashire-mark-birchall); the eight-course dinner is booked separately (~£235 in 2025 reviews, verify current price when booking).

⚠ **Closures:** the rooms close 1–17 Jan and 27 Jul–16 Aug each year [[4]](https://www.visitlancashire.com/places-to-stay/moor-hall-restaurant-with-rooms-p856170) — check before locking a Saturday.

**Pick:** Gatehouse (Elderberry/Medlar) is the value entry; Garden Rooms are the destination experience if the budget allows. Main House sits between, with lake views and period detail.

**Birches Brow** is the strongest pairing with a Moor Hall dinner. Guests report £4.50 taxi fares to the restaurant and the owner (Steve) has driven guests over personally [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3213860-d10823614-Reviews-Birches_Brow_Boutique_Guestrooms-Aughton_Ormskirk_Lancashire_England.html) — effectively a hosted alternative to a Moor Hall room at roughly a third of the Garden Room price. Breakfast is delivered to the room as a basket [[10]](https://birchesbrow.co.uk/). Tripadvisor ranks it #2 of 2 B&Bs in Aughton, 4.5/5 from 126 reviews [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3213860-d10823614-Reviews-Birches_Brow_Boutique_Guestrooms-Aughton_Ormskirk_Lancashire_England.html).

**West Tower** is closer but functions primarily as a wedding venue — Saturday-night availability is the constraint, not price [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1071013-d1009140-Reviews-West_Tower-Ormskirk_Lancashire_England.html).

**Miller & Carter / Innkeeper's Lodge** is the budget fallback — adequate chain rooms above a steakhouse, useful only if the other three are booked or you want to walk to Town Green station the next morning [[13]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/gb/innkeeper-s-lodge-ormskirk-aughton.html) [[14]](https://discoverormskirk.com/discover/the-innkeepers-collection-aughton/).

## Booking sequence

1. Book Moor Hall dinner first — three-Michelin Saturdays sell out weeks ahead [[17]](https://guide.michelin.com/gb/en/lancashire/aughton/restaurant/moor-hall).
2. On the dinner confirmation page, check rooms — bundling avoids a taxi, an extra check-in, and the 1.4-mile-after-tasting-menu problem [[8]](https://www.hot-dinners.com/Features/Travel/moor-hall-restaurant-hotel-review-aughton-lancashire-mark-birchall).
3. If Moor Hall rooms are full or out of budget, take **Birches Brow** [[10]](https://birchesbrow.co.uk/) and pre-arrange the £4.50 taxi return [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3213860-d10823614-Reviews-Birches_Brow_Boutique_Guestrooms-Aughton_Ormskirk_Lancashire_England.html). Book a return cab when you book the room — Aughton has limited late-night taxi supply.
4. Use the next morning for the canal walk that starts a mile from Moor Hall (8.5-mile Liverpool–Leeds Canal circular) or drive 25 min to Formby Beach / Crosby for Gormley's *Another Place* [[16]](https://moorhall.com/news/nearby-moor-hall-lancashire).
