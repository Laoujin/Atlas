The weekend pivots on one fact: Moor Hall is a rural manor on a five-acre plot off Prescot Road, Aughton L39 6RT [[1]](https://moorhall.com/moor-hall/about/), and only its own 14 bedrooms — Gatehouse from £275, Main House £300–£425, Koto Garden Rooms £450–£500 B&B [[2]](https://moorhall.com/rooms) — are a true walk from the table. The "next-closest" hotels (West Tower 1.4 mi, Birches Brow 1.6 mi) are along unlit country lanes; treat them as a £4.50 taxi, not a stroll [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3213860-d10823614-Reviews-Birches_Brow_Boutique_Guestrooms-Aughton_Ormskirk_Lancashire_England.html). The two lodging angles agree on the ranking and disagree only on framing: book Moor Hall first; if it's full or out of budget, Birches Brow is the closest non-Moor-Hall option, [Briars Hall](https://briarshallhotel.co.uk/) (1745 manor, 4.3 mi, from ~£99 [[4]](https://www.kayak.co.uk/Ormskirk-Hotels-Briars-Hall-Hotel.610751.ksp)) is the sleeper value, and [The Secret Garden Glamping](https://thesecretgardenglamping.uk/) (4.5 mi) is the wild card if you'd rather walk out of a hot-tub yurt at breakfast [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/HotelsNear-g3213860-d12388578-Moor_Hall_Restaurant_With_Rooms-Aughton_Ormskirk_Lancashire_England.html).

Two hard date constraints fall out of the lodging research and govern everything else: **Moor Hall's rooms close 1–17 January and 27 July–16 August** [[6]](https://www.visitlancashire.com/places-to-stay/moor-hall-restaurant-with-rooms-p856170) — pick a Saturday outside those windows — and Saturday tasting-menu seats sell out weeks ahead [[7]](https://guide.michelin.com/gb/en/lancashire/aughton/restaurant/moor-hall), so the booking sequence is **dinner → on-property room → fallback cab + B&B**, in that order.

Every village surfaced by the lodging children also reappears as a day-trip anchor in the activities ledger: **Burscough** (Briars Hall, The Farm Burscough) is also home to [WWT Martin Mere](https://www.wwt.org.uk/wetland-centres/martin-mere/) (~10 min, £17.10 adv [[8]](https://www.wwt.org.uk/wetland-centres/martin-mere/plan-your-visit/tickets)), Windmill Animal Farm and the canal-side [Burscough Wharf](https://burscough-wharf.co.uk/food/) food cluster [[9]](https://burscough-wharf.co.uk/food/); **Upholland** (Holland Hall) sits next to [Beacon Country Park](https://www.westlancs.gov.uk/leisure-recreation/parks-and-countryside/parks-and-countryside-sites/beacon-country-park-up-holland.aspx) [[10]](https://www.westlancs.gov.uk/leisure-recreation/parks-and-countryside/parks-and-countryside-sites/beacon-country-park-up-holland.aspx); **Southport** (The Vincent) is a full Year-of-Culture half-day [[11]](https://eyeonsouthport.co.uk/southport-unveils-ambitious-cultural-events-for-2026) with two flags — the Pier is shut for *all* of 2026 [[12]](https://www.sefton.gov.uk/mysefton-news/latest-news/southport-pier-repair-works-set-to-begin-in-early-2026-council-announces/) and the Marine Lake Events Centre is under construction [[13]](https://www.placenorthwest.co.uk/vinci-to-deliver-73m-marine-lake-events-centre/); and **Ormskirk** itself delivers a Saturday market 08:00–16:30 minutes from the restaurant [[14]](https://www.westlancs.gov.uk/leisure-recreation/visit-west-lancashire/ormskirk-market.aspx). The headline day-trip pick, regardless of where you sleep, is [Rufford Old Hall](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/liverpool-lancashire/rufford-old-hall) (Tudor, ~15 min N, house 11:00–16:30 [[15]](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/liverpool-lancashire/rufford-old-hall)) — because Speke Hall's *house* is closed on Saturdays in 2026 [[16]](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/liverpool-lancashire/speke-hall).

The 30 km radius is also the line that excludes most North-West tech. **Manchester Tech Week and DTX (29–30 April 2026) sit ~44 km / 56 km out** [[17]](https://www.distance.to/Ormskirk/Manchester) and cannot be combined with this weekend. Inside the circle, Liverpool carries the calendar: the **LCR Innovation Investment Fortnight runs 1–12 June 2026** [[18]](https://www.liverpoolcityregion-ca.gov.uk/news/liverpool-city-region-gears-up-for-innovation-investment-fortnight) and the **LCR AI Summit lands 23 October at ACC Liverpool** [[19]](https://www.liverpoolcityregion-ca.gov.uk/ai-summit) — if you can move the Saturday booking to either of those weeks, the weekend doubles as a conference trip.

**Open gaps after four sub-topics.** None of the children pinned down the *current* tasting-menu price, wine-pairing cost, dress code, or whether dinner books bundled with a room — the walking-distance child cited a 2025 figure of ~£235 for eight courses [[20]](https://www.hot-dinners.com/Features/Travel/moor-hall-restaurant-hotel-review-aughton-lancashire-mark-birchall) and noted that the dinner-confirmation page exposes rooms, but the live numbers and the bundled-table mechanic need a direct call to Moor Hall before booking.
