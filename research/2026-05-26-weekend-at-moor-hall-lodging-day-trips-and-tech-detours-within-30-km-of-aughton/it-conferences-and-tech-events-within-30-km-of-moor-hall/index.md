## What "within 30 km" means here

Moor Hall is on Prescot Road, Aughton, L39 6RT — between Ormskirk and Liverpool's northern edge [[1]](https://moorhall.com/). A 30 km circle from there comfortably covers Liverpool city centre, Southport, Preston, Wigan, St Helens, Birkenhead and Ormskirk itself. Manchester city centre is **outside** — Ormskirk → Manchester is 44 km as the crow flies and 56 km by road [[2]](https://www.distance.to/Ormskirk/Manchester).

## ⚠ Manchester Tech Week sits *outside* the 30 km circle

[Manchester Tech Week](https://www.dtxevents.io/manchester-tech-week) (27 April – 1 May) with **DTX Manchester** at Manchester Central on 29–30 April 2026 is the biggest North-West tech event of the year [[18]](https://www.dtxevents.io/manchester-tech-week), but Manchester Central sits **~44 km straight-line / 56 km by road** from Moor Hall [[2]](https://www.distance.to/Ormskirk/Manchester) — outside the stated 30 km cap. Worth a separate trip; don't expect to combine it with a Moor Hall weekend without ~1 h transit each way.

## Why this list is short

- The strict 30 km cap excludes **Manchester** (44 km), **Chester** (~50 km) and **Bolton city centre** (~32 km) — three of the four biggest North-West tech venues outside Liverpool [[2]](https://www.distance.to/Ormskirk/Manchester).
- Liverpool's tech-conference calendar clusters around two anchors — June's **Innovation Investment Fortnight** [[7]](https://www.liverpoolcityregion-ca.gov.uk/news/liverpool-city-region-gears-up-for-innovation-investment-fortnight) and October's **AI Summit week** [[11]](https://www.liverpoolcityregion-ca.gov.uk/ai-summit) — plus FinTech North in March [[3]](https://www.fintechnorth.uk/events/fintech-north-liverpool-2026/). Outside those, the scene is community-meetup-led, not large-conference-led.
- Preston (BCS branch, ~25 km) and Edge Hill University (Ormskirk, ~5 km) [[17]](https://www.edgehill.ac.uk/events/) are the only secondary nodes inside the radius — neither hosts a regular large IT conference in 2026.
