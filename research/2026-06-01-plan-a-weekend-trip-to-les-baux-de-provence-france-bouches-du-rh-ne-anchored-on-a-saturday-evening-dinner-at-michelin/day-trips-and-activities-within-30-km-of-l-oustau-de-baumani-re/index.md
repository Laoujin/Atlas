## On foot from the hotel — Les Baux cluster

Baumanière sits in the Vallon de la Fontaine directly below the village. Carrières des Lumières is ~200 m away (under 5 min on foot); the quarry sits less than 10 min from the village core [[9]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g1819081-d2651179-Reviews-Carrieres_des_Lumieres-Les_Baux_de_Provence_Bouches_du_Rhone_Provence_Alpes_Cote.html). The whole triangle — quarry, fortress, village streets — is a comfortable half-day with no driving.

The 2026 program at [Carrières des Lumières](https://www.carrieres-lumieres.com/en) is **"Picasso, l'art en mouvement"** (40-min long programme, 450+ images covering painting, drawing, engraving, sculpture, ceramics, collage) paired with a 14-min short **"Frida Kahlo, en plein cœur,"** opening 13 February 2026 [[2]](https://madeinmarseille.net/made-in-sud/194388-en-2026-picasso-et-frida-kahlo-sexposent-aux-carrieres-des-lumieres-des-baux-de-provence/). The quarry was repurposed in 1975 by Albert Plécy as *Cathédrale d'Images*; Culturespaces took over in 2008 and reopened it under the present name in 2012 [[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Pl%C3%A9cy).

[Château des Baux](https://chateau-baux-provence.com/) holds the largest collection of full-scale reconstructed medieval siege engines in Europe — trebuchet, ballista, couillard, battering ram — with live firing demos using real projectiles in season (typical slots 11:00, 15:30, 17:30) [[5]](https://www.bargaintraveleurope.com/08/France_Chateau_Des_Baux_Provence.htm).

Inside the village, two pieces of heritage reward a detour off the main drag: the hexagonal Renaissance **Pavillon de la Reine Jeanne** (~1568–1581) in the Vallon de la Fontaine, which Mistral copied for his own tomb in Maillane [[7]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/activites/patrimoine/pavillon-de-la-reine-jeanne/), and the partly rock-cut **Église Saint-Vincent** with Max Ingrand stained glass donated by Prince Rainier III of Monaco [[8]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/activites/patrimoine/). Baumanière guests skip the paid lots entirely [[80]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/prepare-you-visit/getting-here/parking-rates/).

## Saint-Rémy-de-Provence (12 km / 20 min) — go on Wednesday

The headline pilgrimage is the cloister of [Saint-Paul-de-Mausole](https://www.saintpauldemausole.fr/), where Van Gogh voluntarily admitted himself on 8 May 1889 and stayed until 16 May 1890 [[12]](https://www.saintpauldemausole.fr/). In those 53 weeks he produced 143 oil paintings and 100+ drawings, including *The Starry Night*, *Irises*, *Olive Orchard* and *Wheat Field with Cypresses* [[13]](https://www.avignon-et-provence.com/en/monuments/saint-paul-de-mausole-monastery). The site is still a working psychiatric institution; visitors see the Romanesque cloister, chapel, Roman promenades, and a reconstructed Van Gogh bedroom [[12]](https://www.saintpauldemausole.fr/).

About 1 km further south, CMN-managed [Glanum](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/places/archaeological-site-of-glanum-saint-remy-de-provence-en-2863185/) exposes a Greco-Roman city (capital of the Glaniques, abandoned ~270 AD) with villas, forum, baths and mosaics [[15]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/things-to-do/culture-and-heritage/places/archaeological-site-of-glanum-saint-remy-de-provence-en-2863185/). Just outside the paid enclosure, **Les Antiques** — the Mausoleum of the Julii (30–20 BC) and France's oldest triumphal arch — stand on an open esplanade, free, classified monuments since 1840 [[16]](https://www.alpillesenprovence.com/en/fiches/les-antiques/).

The **Wednesday Provençal market** (8:00–13:00) sprawls from Place de la République through the historic centre with hundreds of stalls — herbs, olives, oils, produce, honey, lavender [[17]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/things-to-do/events/all-events/weekly-provencal-market-of-saint-remy-de-provence-saint-remy-de-provence-en-2867828/). A smaller Saturday repeat runs the same hours [[18]](https://snippetsofparis.com/saint-remy-de-provence/). On Wednesdays, arrive before 9:00 or take the free 15-min shuttle from the **Petite Crau** car park, 9:00–13:00 [[21]](https://keithvansickle.com/secrets-of-st-remy-parking/) [[67]](https://www.alpillesenprovence.com/en/fiches/weekly-provencal-market-of-saint-remy-de-provence/). The pedestrianised old town threads past **Nostradamus's 1503 birthplace** at 6 rue Hoche, his fountain, and a zodiac fresco on Boulevard Mirabeau [[19]](https://www.alpillesenprovence.com/en/history-heritage/a-land-of-art-and-culture/nostradamus-in-saint-remy-de-provence/). **Musée Estrine** in an 18th-century *hôtel particulier* houses the Van Gogh Interpretation Centre — interpretive panels and a French-language film, ⚠ no original Van Goghs [[20]](https://www.nomads-travel-guide.com/places/musee-estrine/).

## Arles (20 km / 30 min) — go on Saturday

Eight Arles monuments are UNESCO-listed (1981) [[23]](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/164/); the city has the densest Roman remains in France after Rome itself. The **Arènes** (c. 90 AD, ~21,000 seats) is the headline — still hosts bullfights and concerts, and in 2026 runs daily 9:00–19:00 May–Sept at €11 [[24]](https://audiala.com/en/france/arles/arles-amphitheatre).

Buy the [Pass Liberté](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/things-to-do/shopping-arts-craft/all-crafts-and-shopping-activities/pass-liberte-arles-en-3577659/) (€13–15, valid 1 month): any 4 monuments of choice (Amphitheatre, Théâtre Antique, Cloître Saint-Trophime, Cryptoportiques, Alyscamps, Thermes de Constantin) + Musée Réattu + one more museum, plus a reduced Fondation Van Gogh rate [[26]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/things-to-do/shopping-arts-craft/all-crafts-and-shopping-activities/pass-liberte-arles-en-3577659/).

## Outdoors in the Alpilles

The signature hike is the **Mont Gaussier / Rocher des Deux Trous loop** from Saint-Paul-de-Mausole — ~8 km, 3 h, 422 m gain on partly cairned GR6, with metal ladders near the 306 m summit [[36]](https://www.trailexplorer.eu/en/france/provence-alpes-cote-d-azur/bouches-du-rhone/bouches-du-rhone-walks/1260-mont-gaussier.html). The Rocher des Deux Trous is the rock-with-two-holes Van Gogh painted in *Mountains of Saint-Rémy*. [AllTrails' shorter variant](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/france/bouches-du-rhone/rocher-des-deux-trous-depuis-saint-remy-de-provence) is 4.4 mi / 849 ft, moderate, 2–2.5 h, linking Mont Gaussier + the Rock + Lac du Peirou [[37]](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/france/bouches-du-rhone/rocher-des-deux-trous-depuis-saint-remy-de-provence).

The official [Alpilles climbing topo](https://escaladealpilles.com/) lists 1,792 routes across 13 sites in 7 communes (Aureille, Eyguières, Mouriès, Orgon, Paradou, Saint-Rémy, Tarascon) [[42]](https://escaladealpilles.com/).

⚠ **From 1 June to 30 September the prefecture closes Alpilles trails by daily decree** — published at 18:00 for the next day, green = open, red = closed, on the [official map](https://www.risque-prevention-incendie.fr/bouches-du-rhone/) [[40]](https://www.parc-alpilles.fr/actualite/acces-aux-massifs-forestiers/) [[41]](https://www.risque-prevention-incendie.fr/bouches-du-rhone/). Check it before any walk in summer.

⚠ **Mistral** — strong cold NW wind averaging 50 km/h, gusting to 100 km/h, often lasting several days [[53]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mistral_(wind)). Provençal hiking guidance is to skip ridge routes during mistral episodes — risk of being blown off-balance plus airborne debris [[52]](https://en.vaison-ventoux-provence.com/things-to-see-and-do-in-vaison-ventoux-provence/outdoor-activities/vaison-ventoux-provence-on-foot/safety-rules-to-know-when-hiking/).

**Lavender:** peaks late June through mid-July; the famous Valensole plateau is outside 30 km [[50]](https://travelfrancebucketlist.com/provence-lavender-season/), but working parcels exist on the Saint-Rémy plain itself — e.g. [Château de la Gabelle](https://chateaudelagabelle.fr/lavande-saint-remy-de-provence/), organic [[51]](https://chateaudelagabelle.fr/lavande-saint-remy-de-provence/).

## Gastronomy — AOP olive oil + AOC wine + Provençal markets

### Olive oil mills (AOP Vallée des Baux-de-Provence)

## Practical logistics

- **Best windows:** April–May and September–October — light, heat, crowds all in the right place [[22]](https://provencelovers.fr/en/things-to-do-village-saint-remy-de-provence-visit/) [[11]](https://www.the-travel-bunny.com/visit-les-baux-de-provence-guide/).
- **Drive times from Les Baux:** Saint-Rémy 20 min · Arles 30 min · Avignon 40 min · Tarascon 20 min · Fontvieille 15 min [[82]](https://www.france-justforyou.com/blog/3-days-in-provence-arles-les-baux-de-provence-st-remy-de-provence) [[77]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/prepare-you-visit/getting-here/).
- **Parking in Les Baux:** ⚠ no free spaces; paid lots €5/first hour, 8:00–19:00 year-round, €35 illegal fine [[80]](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/en/prepare-you-visit/getting-here/parking-rates/) — moot if staying at Baumanière.
- **Saint-Rémy market shuttle:** free from Petite Crau, every 15 min, 9:00–13:00 Wed [[21]](https://keithvansickle.com/secrets-of-st-remy-parking/).
- **Arles parking:** covered Parc du Centre (500 spaces, rue Émile Fassin, 24/7); Lamartine fills on Saturdays [[34]](https://community.ricksteves.com/travel-forum/france/parking-in-arles).
- **Sunday/Monday closures:** most non-food shops shut Sunday; bakeries close 13:00; many sights close Monday (Tarascon château [[71]](https://en.infotourisme.net/monument/tarascon/6674/chateau-du-roi-rene), Montmajour in winter [[74]](https://www.abbaye-montmajour.fr/en/visit/practical-information)); Sunday-open businesses often close Monday instead [[81]](https://www.frenchentree.com/living-in-france/local-life/opening-hours-in-france-shops-services-restaurants/).
- **Summer fire-closure:** check the Bouches-du-Rhône prefecture map every evening at 18:00 for next-day Alpilles access between 1 June and 30 September [[40]](https://www.parc-alpilles.fr/actualite/acces-aux-massifs-forestiers/) [[41]](https://www.risque-prevention-incendie.fr/bouches-du-rhone/).
- **Combined tickets:** Pass Baux (€21) for château + Carrières → use it [[1]](https://www.carrieres-lumieres.com/en/plan-visit/opening-times-rates); Pass Liberté (€13–15) for Arles → use it if you'll visit 4+ monuments [[26]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/things-to-do/shopping-arts-craft/all-crafts-and-shopping-activities/pass-liberte-arles-en-3577659/).
