> **TL;DR** The most compelling pairing is **CoTer Numérique** (June 23-24, public-sector IT congress, 1 300+ attendees) immediately followed by the free **Fête de l'Innovation** (June 25, startup pitches + networking). For cybersecurity specialists, **HS'R CTF** runs 5-6 June at URCA. Community meetups are sparse — **Sacrée Tech** is the main recurring developer series, active but infrequent.

## CoTer Numérique — 35th edition

**June 23-24, 2026 · Centre de Congrès de Reims** [[1]](https://coter-numerique.org/congres-2026/index.html)

France's main congress for IT directors and CISOs of *collectivités territoriales* (local governments). The 35th edition's theme is digital sovereignty: AI in public services, NIS2 compliance, cloud strategy, and data governance. [[2]](https://www.veillemag.com/23-et-24-juin-2026-Souverainete-numerique-les-collectivites-en-premiere-ligne-au-Congres-CoTer-Numerique-Reims_a7466.html)

- **Scale:** 120+ workshops, plenaries, and round tables; ~1 300 participants in 2025 [[3]](https://www.axess.fr/blog/actualites-axess/congres-coter-numerique-2026-reims)
- **Exhibitors:** open-source stack vendors (Nextcloud, GLPI, Zimbra), cybersecurity firms, cloud providers [[4]](https://lotexpo.com/events/coter-numerique)
- **Registration:** [congres.coter-numerique.org](https://congres.coter-numerique.org/) (member portal; pricing not public)
- **Fit for visitors:** highly specialised toward French public-sector IT — relevant if you work in that domain or want to prospect the sector; less relevant for private-sector developers

A **preparatory day (JET)** is scheduled June 22, making the full run June 22-24 for active participants. [[1]](https://coter-numerique.org/congres-2026/index.html)

## Fête de l'Innovation — 2026 edition

**June 25, 2026 · 16:00-23:00 · Villa Douce, 9 Bd de la Paix, Reims** [[5]](https://lafrenchtechest.fr/event/fete-de-linnovation-2026/)

Free evening event co-organised by [Innovact](https://innovact.com/), [PUI InnoRem](https://innovact.com/actualites/appel-a-projets-innovants-2026-starter-class-innovact-reims/), and [French Tech Est](https://lafrenchtechest.fr/). Programme: [[6]](https://my.weezevent.com/fete-de-linnovation-2026-1)

- 16:00 — Grand Pitch: startup pitches to a live audience
- 18:00 — Innovation Village opens (startups + research labs)
- 20:30 — Cocktail networking

Target audience is broad: startups, researchers, companies, students. [[7]](https://www.reims-legend-r.fr/a-la-une/une/la-fete-de-linnovation-une-soiree-pour-celebrer-la-creativite-et-les-talents) Well-suited to close a CoTer Numérique weekend — different vibe (open and convivial vs. professional congress).

Registration via Weezevent; past editions were free with mandatory RSVP.

## HS'R by HackSecu'R — CTF overnight

**June 5-6, 2026 · 21:00→9:00 · URCA, campus Moulin de la Housse** [[8]](https://www.hacksecureims.eu/)

8th edition of Reims' student-run cybersecurity competition, co-sponsored by DGSE and Root-Me. [[8]](https://www.hacksecureims.eu/) Format: day-sessions with company talks and exhibitor stands, then an overnight Jeopardy-style CTF. AI tools are banned for 2026 (stated explicitly on the website). Organised by the HackSecu'R student association and URCA's Master Networks & Telecom. [[9]](https://www.univ-reims.fr/actualites/actualites/hacksecu-reims,22307,36999.html?args=Vv5vipvzwG6LZfHmbaFMT5kDOmlrPOEThn6ZeqUuR7oQeWQsZ*71SIDH5u3rMtE3XdEz272YMBQDZLkVAYVfFA)

Primarily student-oriented but open to external participants; good entry point for the local security community.

## TERRITORIALIS 2026

**October 1-2, 2026 · Centre de Congrès de Reims** [[10]](https://www.territorialis.fr/)

Annual congress of the SNDGCT (national union of territorial directors-general). Theme: *"Avoir confiance & agir ensemble"*. Scale: ~1 700 participants, 70 exhibitors, 20 conferences/workshops in 2025. [[10]](https://www.territorialis.fr/) Digital transformation and e-government topics run throughout, but the audience is general territorial management, not IT specialists. Useful context if your work intersects public administration.

## Community: Sacrée Tech meetups

[Sacrée Tech](https://www.meetup.com/fr-FR/SacreeTech/) is Reims' main developer community, gathering local tech groups for talks and networking. Events are held at rotating venues — the ESGI Reims campus and [Ikoula's datacenter](https://www.ikoula.com/en/company/infrastructures) (34 rue du Pont Assy) have both hosted editions. [[11]](https://www.agendadulibre.org/events/32245) [[12]](https://www.agendadulibre.org/events/27008)

Recent edition topics (March 2025): interactive mapping with Leaflet/MongoDB, and a talk questioning "technical debt" as a concept. Earlier editions included datacenter tours + AI/UI component talks. Frequency is irregular (roughly quarterly). Contact: hello@sacreetech.fr.

No dedicated 2026 event dates were announced at time of research (June 2026); check their [Meetup page](https://www.meetup.com/fr-FR/SacreeTech/) or [Agenda du Libre](https://www.agendadulibre.org/groups/reims) for updates.

## Practical notes for a weekend visit

- **Best weekend hook:** June 23-25 stacks CoTer Numérique (Tue-Wed) + Fête de l'Innovation (Thu evening). Three evenings in Reims with a meaningful tech programme.
- **CoTer Numérique access:** registration is through the coTer member portal; non-members may need to contact the organisers directly. Budget travel for a congress-level paid event.
- **Fête de l'Innovation:** fully open; register via [Weezevent](https://my.weezevent.com/fete-de-linnovation-2026-1). Best solo access point for an IT visitor.
- **HS'R:** requires being present late-night into early morning; primarily a student event, but company stands during the day are accessible.
- **Outside June/October:** the tech event calendar is thin. Sacrée Tech is the only recurring option, but dates are not pre-announced far in advance.
