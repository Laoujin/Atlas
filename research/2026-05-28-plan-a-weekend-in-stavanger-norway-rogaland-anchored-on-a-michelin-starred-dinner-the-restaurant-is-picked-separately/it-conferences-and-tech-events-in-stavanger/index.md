Pick [ONS](https://www.ons.no/) (Mon–Thu, Aug 24–27) if your interest is AI/digital in energy — by far the largest event of the year, with a real Digital Transformation track [[2]](https://www.ons.no/) [[3]](https://www.ons.no/programme/technical-session). Skip the rest of 2026's headline tech events: ERF, Energy Norway and Nordic Edge Expo all wrapped up before late May. Weekend-pairing notes at the bottom.

### The three to actually plan around

**[Hello Stavanger](https://www.hellostavanger.no/)** is the natural pick for an IT-conference visit. Launched October 2024 as the first software conference in Stavanger [[14]](https://2024.hellostavanger.no/venue), now in its third edition. The 2026 CFP scope spans Agile, Architecture, Coding, DevOps, AI/LLM, Security, Testing, UX — CFP closed May 3, 2026 [[5]](https://sessionize.com/hellostavanger-2026/). Tou Scene is a converted brewery in Stavanger East, ~10 minutes' walk from the city centre [[14]](https://2024.hellostavanger.no/venue).

**[ONS](https://www.ons.no/)** is a different animal — a 4-day energy mega-event, not a "tech conference" in the developer sense, but the **Digital Transformation** track (Theme 4) covers AI-driven operational excellence and breaking data barriers between operators and suppliers, with subsurface AI/ML and digitalisation strands across Themes 1–3 [[3]](https://www.ons.no/programme/technical-session). The Strategic Conference frames "AI in action – building operational brilliance" as one of its 2026 challenges; the overall theme is "Courage" [[2]](https://www.ons.no/). Scale: 72k+ visitors, 1,100+ exhibitors, 1,150+ speakers, 102 countries [[2]](https://www.ons.no/). ⚠ Hotel pricing during ONS is brutal — book months out or stay outside the city.

**[HealthTech Festival – Stavanger](https://www.norwayhealthtech.com/events/the-healthtech-festival-2026-stavanger)** is a single morning (9:30–13:30) at HelseCampus Stavanger / Innovasjonsparken; relevant if your interest is robotics/automation in health, or the dual-use angle (preparedness, defence) [[4]](https://www.norwayhealthtech.com/events/the-healthtech-festival-2026-stavanger). One session inside a longer trip, not the anchor.

## Already happened in 2026 — recurs annually, watch for 2027

All three wrapped up before this research's date (2026-05-28):

- **[European Robotics Forum 2026](https://erf2026.eu/)** — Mar 23–27, Stavanger Forum. 1,000+ delegates, theme *"Robotics for the blue economy and growth in space"*, heavy on AI governance, field robotics, underwater tech [[6]](https://erf2026.eu/). ERF rotates host cities — the next edition will be elsewhere in Europe.
- **[Energy Norway 2026](https://www.uis.no/en/research/events/energy-norway-2026)** — Apr 28–29 at the University of Stavanger (Tjodhallen, Kjølv Egeland's house). NCS2030's annual subsurface-energy + digitalisation conference, more academic than ONS [[7]](https://www.uis.no/en/research/events/energy-norway-2026).
- **[Nordic Edge Expo 2026](https://nordicedge.org/events/nordic-edge-expo-4.-6.-may-2026)** — May 4–6 at Fiskepiren. Cross-sectoral smart-cities / regenerative-society expo, with a smart-city cluster, agritech cluster, and the EDIH Oceanopolis digital innovation hub on the floor [[8]](https://nordicedge.org/events/nordic-edge-expo-4.-6.-may-2026). Annual; returns early May 2027.

## Pairing a conference with a weekend

All three remaining 2026 conferences sit mid-week (Mon–Thu or Wed–Thu). Three workable shapes:

- **Hello Stavanger long weekend** — fly in Tue Oct 20, conference Wed–Thu, stay through Sat–Sun Oct 24–25. Best fit for a "tech + culture" trip; Tou Scene is in walking distance of restaurants and waterfront [[14]](https://2024.hellostavanger.no/venue).
- **ONS bookend** — either Fri–Sun Aug 21–23 *before* (lower hotel pressure, attend Mon onward) or Fri–Sun Aug 28–30 *after*. Avoid arriving mid-event; rooms inside the city are saturated by 72k visitors ⚠ [[2]](https://www.ons.no/).
- **HealthTech morning** — book a normal Thu–Sun, attend the half-day Sep 10, dinner that evening, weekend in town [[4]](https://www.norwayhealthtech.com/events/the-healthtech-festival-2026-stavanger).

⚠ Gotcha: the EIC Summit (Jun 3–4) appears on Stavanger Region's events calendar but actually takes place in Brussels (Tour & Taxis), not Stavanger — the regional partners syndicate the listing without flagging the venue [[15]](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-summit-2026_en). It is not a Stavanger trip.
