## Mega-events

**[Consensus Hong Kong](https://consensus-hongkong.coindesk.com/)** (Feb 10–12) is the flagship Web3/crypto event in Asia, drawing 11,000+ attendees from 122 countries [[2]](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/BLSH/consensus-hong-kong-2026-concludes-with-11-000-attendees-driving-the-ux8zmn8jr6rt.html) and recognized by the HK government as a city mega-event [[3]](https://www.hongkong-fintech.hk/en/events/events-2026/consensus-hong-kong-2026/). Four dedicated summits run in parallel: Open Money, AI & Robotics, Global Bitcoin, and Asia Macro. 400+ side events extend the week across the city.

**[LEAP East 2026](https://leapeast.com/)** (Jul 8–10) is the APAC edition of Saudi Arabia's LEAP conference, making its Hong Kong debut at HKCEC. 200+ speakers, 400+ exhibitors, and 250+ investors [[5]](https://leapeast.com/) converge across a main stage, DeepFest (AI/robotics), and startup/investor programming. Microsoft, Oracle, AWS, Google Cloud, and Huawei are among confirmed exhibitor names.

**[FinTech Week x StartmeupHK](https://www.fintechweek.hk/)** (Nov 2–6) is the result of merging Hong Kong FinTech Week with StartmeupHK into a single 45,000-person event spanning HKCEC and multiple city venues [[6]](https://www.fintechweek.hk/). Theme: *Fintech Nexus: Bridging the World, Building the Future*. 120+ economies represented; tracks cover AI, Web3, insurtech, greentech, and healthtech.

**[Bitcoin Asia 2026](https://asia.b.tc/)** (Aug 27–28) returns to HKCEC, expecting 15,000+ attendees and 200+ speakers [[8]](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/bitcoin-asia-2026-hong-kong-tickets-1977520078789). Organized by BTC Inc., it covers institutional Bitcoin, Layer-2s, ordinals, and regulatory developments. GA passes from $48.

## AI & Emerging Tech

- **[Embodied AI + AGIBOT Summit](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/the-first-hong-kong-embodied-ai-industry-summit-and-agibot-partner-conference-2026-hong-kong-opens-302770944.html)** (May 13, Ritz-Carlton): inaugural industry summit for physical AI/robotics; AGIBOT announced its international R&D HQ will be located in Hong Kong [[14]](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/the-first-hong-kong-embodied-ai-industry-summit-and-agibot-partner-conference-2026-hong-kong-opens-302770944.html). Invitee-only.
- **[Tech Fest Summit](https://techfest.hk/)** (May 21, HKCEC): one-day, 2,000+ attendees; tracks on Agentic AI, Cloud Transformation, and Cybersecurity [[4]](https://techfest.hk/). Free standard tickets; $100 USD for workshop access.
- **[AI+ Power 2026](https://www.aipluspower.com/)** (Jun 4–5, HKCEC): expo + conference combining vendor showcases with keynotes on AI in finance, retail, healthcare, and logistics [[9]](https://www.aipluspower.com/).
- **[WIC Asia-Pacific Summit](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-04-13/2026-World-Internet-Conference-Asia-Pacific-Summit-kicks-off-in-HKSAR-1MiWrD9kzwA/p.html)** (Apr 13): government-convened; themed *Digital and Intelligent Empowerment for Innovative Development* [[18]](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-04-13/2026-World-Internet-Conference-Asia-Pacific-Summit-kicks-off-in-HKSAR-1MiWrD9kzwA/p.html).

## Cybersecurity

- **[PCPD AI + Cybersecurity Summit](https://www.pcpd.org.hk/ai_cybersecurity_summit/en/index.html)** (Mar 31, HKCEC): half-day enterprise-focused session from Hong Kong's Privacy Commissioner [[19]](https://www.pcpd.org.hk/ai_cybersecurity_summit/en/index.html).
- **[DiCyFor & AI Security Summit](https://www.dicyfor.com/hongkong2026)** (Jul 21, InterContinental Grand Stanford, Kowloon): 250+ delegates, 74% director-level or above; covers zero trust, security mesh, and AI governance [[13]](https://www.dicyfor.com/hongkong2026). Speakers from CitiBank, AIA, and government agencies.

## Developer & Academic

- **[HKOSCon 2026](https://www.eventbrite.hk/e/hong-kong-open-source-conference-2026-tickets-1987799450667)** (Jun 6, HKIIT Tsing Yi): largest annual open source event in HK; 30 sessions on Agentic AI, DevOps, IoT, and Linux [[10]](https://www.eventbrite.hk/e/hong-kong-open-source-conference-2026-tickets-1987799450667). Free with limited seats.
- **[CityUHK FinTech & AI Conference](https://cityuhkfintech.com/)** (Jun 14–15, CityUHK): academic; keynotes by Prof. Allan Timmermann (UC San Diego) and Prof. Markus Pelger (Stanford) [[11]](https://cityuhkfintech.com/). No submission fee; lodging covered for accepted presenters.
- **[FutureCIO Conference HK](https://cxociety.com/events/4th-annual-future-cio-conference-hong-kong/)** (Oct 14): 120+ CIOs and senior IT leaders; theme *Forging Future Advantage* covering AI industrialization and data sovereignty [[15]](https://cxociety.com/events/4th-annual-future-cio-conference-hong-kong/). Early-bird from USD $1,795; free passes for qualifying CIOs.
- **[MarTech Summit HK](https://themartechsummit.com/hongkong)** (Jul 7, Hopewell Hotel): 120-person intimate summit for CMOs and marketing directors on AI automation and cross-border commerce [[12]](https://themartechsummit.com/hongkong).

## Awards

**[HKICT Awards 2026](https://www.hkictawards.hk/)** — 20th anniversary edition. Eight categories (FinTech, Smart Business, ICT Startup, Digital Entertainment, Smart Living, Smart Mobility, Smart People, Student Innovation) [[16]](https://www.hkictawards.hk/). Applications open April 28 – July 20 [[17]](https://www.digitalpolicy.gov.hk/en/our_work/digital_infrastructure/industry_development/industry_support/ict_awards/); awards gala November 19.

## Practical Notes

- **Venue**: HKCEC (Wan Chai harbourfront) hosts virtually every flagship event — book accommodation near Wan Chai/Admiralty well in advance.
- **Cost range**: free (HKOSCon, Tech Fest standard) → $48 GA (Bitcoin Asia) → $1,795+ (FutureCIO). Consensus and FinTech Week passes are separately priced; check official sites.
- **RISE 2026**: returning to Asia World Expo (Lantau Island, 5 min from airport) [[20]](https://riseconf.com/); dates not yet announced as of June 2026 — monitor riseconf.com.
