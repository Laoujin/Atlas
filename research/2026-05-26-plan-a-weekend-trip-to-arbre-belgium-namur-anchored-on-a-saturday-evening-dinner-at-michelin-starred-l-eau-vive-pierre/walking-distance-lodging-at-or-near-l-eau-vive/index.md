> **Decision:** Book **[Le Cube](http://www.eau-vive.be/en/le-cube)**, [L'Eau Vive](https://www.eau-vive.be/en)'s own 150 m² cottage in the restaurant garden by the Burnot brook — it is the only lodging within true walking distance of the dining room [[1]](https://www.eau-vive.be/en) [[2]](http://www.eau-vive.be/en/le-cube). €300/night for 2, €350 for 4 [[3]](https://www.eau-vive.be/hebergement/le-cube). Every other option sits 3–4 km away across hills or down the Meuse; expect to call a taxi after dinner [[12]](https://taxinamurois.be/transfert-vers-villes-proches/profondeville).

## Why "walking distance" is essentially one option

L'Eau Vive is at Route de Floreffe 37, 5170 Rivière (Profondeville) — riverside, on the right bank of the Meuse just south of the Floreffe bridge [[1]](https://www.eau-vive.be/en). The Michelin Guide labels the entry "Arbre" [[4]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/namur/arbre/restaurant/l-eau-vive), but the actual postal section is Rivière; the village of *Arbre* sits up on the plateau ~2.5 km to the south. Bois-de-Villers and Profondeville town are each 3–4 km away by road.

After a Pierre Résimont tasting menu with wine pairing, the only door you can reach on foot without a designated driver or taxi is **Le Cube**, a few metres across the garden.

## Le Cube — the one true walk-back option

Two-bedroom contemporary cottage that Pierre Résimont built in the restaurant's own garden, 150 m² across two levels, glazed entirely on the river-facing side [[2]](http://www.eau-vive.be/en/le-cube) [[11]](https://www.beperfect.be/leau-vive-pierre-resimont-30-ans-detoiles-au-compteur/).

### Why Medissey is the obvious #2

[Espace Medissey](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g2316146-d2295097-Reviews-Espace_Medissey-Bois_De_Villers_Profondeville_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html) is run by Anne and Pierre Résimont themselves — built in 2008, named after their two children Mehdi and Issey [[11]](https://www.beperfect.be/leau-vive-pierre-resimont-30-ans-detoiles-au-compteur/). It is wired into the restaurant's operations: pool, hot tub and fitness room mean a slow Sunday morning after the tasting menu, and the team can usually arrange a post-dinner transfer the 4 km from Rivière. Confirm the lift at booking. The price gap vs Le Cube is small (~€260 vs €300) but you give up the in-garden walk and gain a pool [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g2316146-d2295097-Reviews-Espace_Medissey-Bois_De_Villers_Profondeville_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html).

### Taxi from Profondeville

Two operators cover the area 24/7: [Allo Taxi Namurois](https://taxinamurois.be/transfert-vers-villes-proches/profondeville) [[12]](https://taxinamurois.be/transfert-vers-villes-proches/profondeville) and [Hepp'Taxi](https://www.hepptaxis.be/) [[13]](https://www.hepptaxis.be/). A short hop within Profondeville is typically a fixed-tariff trip; book ahead for a 23:00+ return.

## What to skip

- **"Hotel L'Eau Vive" in Vresse-sur-Semois.** Same name, completely different establishment ~80 km south in the Semois valley — not related to the Résimont restaurant.
- **Anything in Wépion or Namur city.** Plenty of accommodation, but 10–20 km from the table and you'll spend the evening watching the meter.
- **Walking from the Arbre village B&Bs after dinner.** The route climbs out of the Meuse valley on a road with no pavement; not realistic in the dark with wine on board.
