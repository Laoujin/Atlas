> **TL;DR** — Knokke-Heist has zero IT conferences of its own [[12]](https://knokkeheist.com/en/events.php). Inside 30 km, the anchor is Howest in Bruges (~17 km): free Tuesday-evening **Tech&Meet** talks year-round [[4]](https://sites.google.com/howest.be/howest-techmeet) plus a busy 2026 calendar of masterclasses and the **Tech BBQ** on 25 June [[8]](https://app.payit.be/tech-bbq26). The flagship 2026 academic event in the radius is **EUSIPCO 2026** at Concertgebouw Brugge, 31 Aug – 4 Sep [[1]](https://eusipco2026.org/). The big regional fairs (Connect IT Antwerp, Tech Connect Kortrijk) are all 60+ km away [[10]](https://www.howest.be/en/news/tech-connect-2026-talks-showcase)[[11]](https://www.connect-it26.be/en).

## The geography problem

Cuines 33 sits in Knokke-Heist on the Belgian North Sea coast. The 30 km circle covers:

| Place               | Road km from Knokke | Tech scene?                              |
|---------------------|--------------------:|------------------------------------------|
| Knokke-Heist        |                   0 | None — beach resort, no IT venues [[12]](https://knokkeheist.com/en/events.php) |
| Zeebrugge           |                  ~5 | Port logistics, no public tech events    |
| Sluis / Breskens (NL) |               ~5–15 | Border villages, no tech scene           |
| **Bruges**          |                 ~17 | **Howest campus + EUSIPCO 2026 venue**   |
| **Oostende**        |                 ~25 | **InnovOcean Campus** [[9]](https://www.innovoceancampus.be/en/) |
| Kortrijk            |                 ~60 | Outside radius (Howest's Tech Connect, ABISS Summit) [[10]](https://www.howest.be/en/news/tech-connect-2026-talks-showcase) |
| Gent / Antwerp      |              50–95+ | Outside radius (Connect IT, Devoxx)       |

Everything substantive is in **Bruges**, with **Oostende** as a secondary node. Both are reachable from Knokke by car (~20–30 min) or train (Knokke → Brugge ~16 min, Knokke → Oostende ~50 min via Brugge).

## Recurring communities (no calendar — watch the feed)

[**Building Bruges**](https://www.meetup.com/bruges-software-development-meetup-group/) — 575-member software-dev meetup organised by [Peter Morlion](https://petermorlion.com/) [[3]](https://www.meetup.com/bruges-software-development-meetup-group/). Recent past topics: refactoring workshop (Dec 2025, 17 attendees), "Software is a meal best served with different architectural spices" (Sep 2025, 35 attendees), "15 years of #devops" (Feb 2025, 29 attendees). No date currently on the calendar — irregular cadence, 4.3★ rating across 88 reviews. Subscribe and hope a session lands on your weekend.

[**Howest Tech&Meet**](https://sites.google.com/howest.be/howest-techmeet) — the most predictable tech-content drop in the radius: weekly during semesters, Tuesday evenings, free, registration via [howesttech.odoo.com](https://howesttech.odoo.com/) [[4]](https://sites.google.com/howest.be/howest-techmeet). Past topics have ranged from event sourcing & CQRS to NATO cyber-defence experience reports. If your Knokke weekend brackets a Tuesday in term, this is the easy pick.

## Flagship: EUSIPCO 2026

The 34th [European Signal Processing Conference](https://eusipco2026.org/) — EURASIP's flagship — lands at Bruges's [Concertgebouw](https://www.concertgebouw.be/) from **31 August to 4 September 2026**, organised by KU Leuven STADIUS [[1]](https://eusipco2026.org/). It's the only IEEE/EURASIP-scale conference inside the 30 km circle in 2026. Programme covers signal processing across audio, radar, multi-modal sensing, robust learning, sparse Bayesian methods — full week of tutorials, keynotes, posters, satellite workshops [[1]](https://eusipco2026.org/)[[2]](https://cmsworkshops.com/EUSIPCO2026/papers.php). Camera-ready deadline was 2 June 2026; registration via the conference site [[2]](https://cmsworkshops.com/EUSIPCO2026/papers.php). If you're already booked into Cuines 33 over that week, you can walk between the restaurant in Knokke and the venue via a 16-minute train.

## Bottom line

Don't plan a Knokke weekend *around* a tech event — the radius doesn't produce flagship-grade content most weeks. Do **piggy-back**: if your dinner at Cuines 33 falls in a week when EUSIPCO is running [[1]](https://eusipco2026.org/), a Howest Tech&Meet is scheduled [[4]](https://sites.google.com/howest.be/howest-techmeet), or the Tech BBQ is on [[8]](https://app.payit.be/tech-bbq26), a side-trip to Bruges fits cleanly into the weekend. Outside those windows, the scene is quiet enough that a Building Bruges drop-in [[3]](https://www.meetup.com/bruges-software-development-meetup-group/) — when one lands — is the only real lever.
