Chef Edwin Menue. One block south of Maurice Lippensplein, parallel to Lippenslaan, on the historic Knokkedijk. [5]
Five hotels are within fifteen minutes on foot. The first four you'd happily walk home from after a long tasting; the fifth is borderline. Beyond that, take a taxi. Distances measured great-circle from the restaurant; minutes assume an unhurried 5 km/h after dinner.

Hotel Prins Boudewijn ★★★

LIPPENSLAAN 35 — OPP. MARKT

The closest door. Family-run, fifty rooms, rooftop terrace; the parallel street, then a left turn, and you are on Smedenstraat. Same square as the SNCB platform — useful if you arrive by train. No private parking; the town-hall garage takes the car for €25 a day.

Manoir du Dragon ★★★★

ALBERTLAAN 73 — BY ROYAL ZOUTE GOLF

A 1927 villa turned seventeen-room boutique. Garden, balconies, spa-bath rooms; the Royal Zoute course over the hedge. Free private parking — the discreet answer if you drove. No on-site dining, which matters not at all on this errand.

La Réserve Resort ★★★★★ S

ELIZABETLAAN 160 — ON ZEGEMEER

The only Five-Stars-Superior on the Belgian coast since 2024. Indoor pool, hammam, sauna, fitness, restaurant; lake views opposite the (closed-for-renovation) casino. A flat thirteen-minute walk through Albertplein. Pick this when the weekend is a spa break that happens to include dinner.

Hotel Britannia ★★★★

ELIZABETLAAN 85 — ANGLO-NORMAN VILLA

A 1928 Anglo-Normandic villa, fully renovated in 2022; in the MICHELIN Guide for hotels. Thirty-six rooms, 9.1/10 on Booking across 575 reviews, location rated 9.2. On-site parking €30 a day (reserve ahead). Picks this band on character-per-euro.

Hotel Adagio ★★★

VAN BUNNENLAAN 12 — 50 M TO BEACH

Three-star family hotel, wellness centre, free private parking — and a beach that begins fifty metres from the door. Eighteen minutes back from a long tasting is doable, not joyful. The trade is a sea-view morning. Take it only if the morning matters more than the walk.
