## The walk

Cuines 33 sits at **Smedenstraat 33** in central Knokke [[1]](https://www.yelp.com/biz/cuines-33-knokke-heist) — a curved street running from Maurice Lippensplein (the train and tram square) inland toward Kragendijk, on the historic Knokkedijk along which the village grew [[5]](https://streets.openalfa.be/streets/smedenstraat-knokke-knokke-heist). OpenStreetMap places it at 51.3394 N, 3.2881 E in the Stationsbuurt of Het Zoute [[4]](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=Smedenstraat+33+Knokke-Heist&format=json), one block south of and parallel to **Lippenslaan**, Knokke's main shopping spine. The restaurant won its **second MICHELIN star** in the 2026 Belgium & Luxembourg guide; chef Edwin Menue's counter-only room seats 16 [[2]](https://www.michelin.com/en/publications/products-and-services/cuines-33-and-the-jane-are-rewarded-with-two-michelin-stars-in-the-2026-belgium-and-luxembourg-michelin-guide)[[3]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/knokke/restaurant/cuines-33). Translation: a long tasting menu with pairings, then a walk home through quiet residential streets. Choose lodging accordingly.

## Notes per option

### Hotel Prins Boudewijn — closest

Lippenslaan 35-37 sits ~360 m north of Smedenstraat 33 on the parallel main shopping street; you're at the restaurant door in about five minutes [[9]](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=Lippenslaan+35+Knokke-Heist&format=json). Family-run, 50 rooms, 3-star, buffet breakfast ~€20, small rooftop terrace [[6]](https://www.hotelprinsboudewijn.be/en/p/info)[[8]](https://www.hrs.com/en/hotel/39808). Opposite the **market square** and a few minutes from Knokke train/tram station at Maurice Lippensplein 25 — useful if arriving by SNCB rather than car [[7]](https://www.hotelprinsboudewijn.be/en/)[[25]](https://www.myknokke-heist.be/en/useful-addresses/station-knokke). ⚠ No private parking; budget for the town-hall garage. Pick this if you want the shortest possible after-dinner walk and don't need spa amenities.

### Manoir du Dragon — boutique-luxe

Albertlaan 73, ~690 m south-east [[12]](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=Albertlaan+73+Knokke-Heist&format=json) — a 1927 villa converted into a 17-room small luxury hotel adjacent to **Royal Zoute golf course** [[10]](http://www.manoirdudragon.be/index.asp?taal=en)[[11]](https://hotel-manoir-du-dragon-knokke.booked.net/). All rooms air-conditioned with balcony, spa-bath bathrooms, minibar; free private parking and free WiFi; Belgian-specialities breakfast buffet [[11]](https://hotel-manoir-du-dragon-knokke.booked.net/). No on-site restaurant or bar — but you're walking to dinner, so moot. Pick this for a quieter, garden-villa stay with parking and golf at the back.

### La Réserve Resort — the only 5★ superior

Elizabetlaan 160, by Zegemeer lake opposite Grand Casino Knokke [[17]](https://la-reserve.be/en). In 2024 it became the **first hotel on the Belgian coast** with the Tourism Flanders "Five Stars Superior" label [[18]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/la-reserve-resort.html). Indoor pool, hammam, sauna, fitness centre, restaurant and bar [[18]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/la-reserve-resort.html). About 1 km from Cuines 33 [[19]](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=Elizabetlaan+160+Knokke-Heist&format=json) — flat walk along Elizabetlaan or through Albertplein. Pick this if you want pre-dinner spa time and a proper resort wrapper around the weekend.

### Hotel Britannia — Anglo-Norman villa

Elizabetlaan 85, ~1.07 km [[16]](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=Elizabetlaan+85+Knokke-Heist&format=json) — a 1928 Anglo-Normandic villa fully renovated in 2022, in the **MICHELIN Guide** for hotels [[13]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/hotels-stays/knokke-heist/hotel-britannia-12583)[[14]](https://hotelbritannia.be/the-hotel/). 36 rooms, on-site private parking €30/day (reservation required) [[14]](https://hotelbritannia.be/the-hotel/). Booking.com rating 9.1/10 across 575 reviews, location 9.2 [[15]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/britannia.html). Restaurant *La Terrasse du Zoute* is actually housed at sister Hotel Lugano, so dining on-site is a five-minute detour. Pick this if you want character architecture and best-in-class reviews for the price band.

### Hotel Adagio — borderline

Van Bunnenlaan 12, ~1.43 km [[21]](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=Van+Bunnenlaan+12+Knokke-Heist&format=json) — a 3-star family hotel 50 m from the beach with wellness centre and free private parking [[20]](https://www.hoteladagio.be/en/). The 18-minute walk back from a long tasting menu is doable but not joyful. Worth it if a sea-view morning matters more than door-to-door proximity. Otherwise skip.

## How to choose

- **Train arrival, minimise logistics:** Prins Boudewijn — same square as the SNCB station, parallel street to Cuines 33.
- **Driving, want car off your hands:** Manoir du Dragon (free private) or Adagio (free private; longer walk).
- **Treat the weekend as a spa break too:** La Réserve.
- **Maximise hotel character / reviews per euro:** Britannia.

If you can split nights, La Réserve for Friday (spa, pool, lakeside) → Manoir du Dragon for Saturday (shorter walk home, garden breakfast) is the dense version.
