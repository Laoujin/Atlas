Cuines 33 — Two MICHELIN Stars, 2026

Promoted to two stars in the 2026 Belgium & Luxembourg guide [1], the restaurant sits ≈160 m from Knokke station [2]. "Taxi range" below means a one-way ride ≤ ~17 minutes — covering all of Het Zoute, Albertstrand, Duinbergen, plus Cadzand-Bad and Sluis just over the Dutch border [18].

— The Editor's Three —

Most distinctive Het Zoute villa, grandest reborn landmark, and the design-led splurge across the border.

Villa Pick

Hotel Britannia

A 1928 Anglo-Norman manor, fully renovated in 2022 by the second-generation family owners. The default for a villa-feel stay without five-star pricing. [3][4]

The Knokke landmark on the casino boulevard reopened in 2023 after architect Glenn Sestig restored it to a 1930s register: light-filled interiors in eucalyptus and onyx marble, an Art Deco bar with crystal sconces and red velvet banquettes [10]. Thirty-seven rooms, spa, indoor pool, La Rigue restaurant [9][19]. The most architecturally serious stay on the Belgian side, and under five minutes by taxi to Cuines 33.

"Gem of Anglo-Norman coastal architecture" — a 1928 villa by architect Buylaert, white-beige-brown façade with gold accents, completely renovated in 2022 by the second-generation family owners [4]. Twenty-six spacious rooms in English-style décor; a library lounge anchors the public spaces, 100 m from the beach [3][19]. The default pick for travellers who want a villa-feel without five-star pricing.

Studio Piet Boon designed both the interiors and the in-room furniture — minimalist neutral palette, floor-to-ceiling windows, Auping boxspring beds, private balcony or terrace in every room [14][15]. Heated indoor pool, saunas. Comfort rooms from €155, suites from €275, tower-suite sea view from €395 [14]. The defining stay for travellers who care about contemporary architecture and accept a 17-minute taxi to dinner [18].
