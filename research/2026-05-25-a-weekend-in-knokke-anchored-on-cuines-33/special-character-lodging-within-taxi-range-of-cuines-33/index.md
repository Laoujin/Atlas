> **Decision** — Most distinctive Het Zoute villa: [Hotel Britannia](https://hotelbritannia.be/home-en/), a 1928 Anglo-Norman manor fully renovated in 2022, 5 min by taxi [[3]](https://hotelbritannia.be/home-en/) [[4]](https://www.letsgomylove.com/en/place/hotel-britannia/). Grand 1930s landmark, just reborn: [La Réserve](https://la-reserve.be/en), 5★ superior reopened 2023 after a Glenn Sestig overhaul [[9]](https://la-reserve.be/en) [[10]](https://guide.michelin.com/ee/en/hotels-stays/knokke-heist/hotel-la-reserve-10240). Design-led splurge: cross the NL border to [Strandhotel Cadzand](https://www.strandhotel.eu/en/), interiors by Studio Piet Boon, 17 min taxi [[14]](https://www.strandhotel.eu/en/) [[15]](https://pietboon.com/corporate-projects/strandhotel-cadzand/) [[18]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/cadzand_bad-4506-zeeland-_-netherlands-to-knokke_heist-8300-bruges-flemish_region-belgium).

[Cuines 33](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/knokke/restaurant/cuines-33) is at Smedenstraat 33 in central Knokke (≈160 m from Knokke station), promoted to two MICHELIN Stars in the 2026 Belgium & Luxembourg guide [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/cuines-33-and-the-jane-awarded-two-stars-in-the-michelin-guide-belgium-and-luxembourg-2026) [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/knokke/restaurant/cuines-33). "Taxi range" below means ≤ ~17 min one-way: that covers all of Het Zoute, Albertstrand, Duinbergen, plus the dune hotels of Cadzand-Bad and Sluis just over the Dutch border [[18]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/cadzand_bad-4506-zeeland-_-netherlands-to-knokke_heist-8300-bruges-flemish_region-belgium).

## Knokke side — Het Zoute & Albertstrand

### [La Réserve][lr-off] — 5★ superior, opposite the Knokke casino (Albertstrand)
The Knokke landmark on the casino boulevard reopened in 2023 after architect [Glenn Sestig](https://guide.michelin.com/ee/en/hotels-stays/knokke-heist/hotel-la-reserve-10240) restored it to a 1930s register: light-filled interiors in eucalyptus and onyx marble, an Art Deco bar with crystal sconces and red velvet banquettes [[10]](https://guide.michelin.com/ee/en/hotels-stays/knokke-heist/hotel-la-reserve-10240). 37 rooms, spa, indoor pool, La Rigue restaurant [[9]](https://la-reserve.be/en) [[19]](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-knokke-heist/). Pick this if you want the most architecturally serious stay on the Belgian side and a sub-5-minute taxi to Cuines 33.

### [Hotel Britannia][br-off] — 4★, Elizabetlaan 85 (Het Zoute)
"Gem of Anglo-Norman coastal architecture" — a 1928 villa by architect Buylaert, white-beige-brown facade with gold accents, completely renovated in 2022 by the second-generation family owners [[4]](https://www.letsgomylove.com/en/place/hotel-britannia/). 26 spacious rooms with English-style décor; a library lounge anchors the public spaces, 100 m from the beach [[3]](https://hotelbritannia.be/home-en/) [[19]](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-knokke-heist/). The default pick for travellers who want a villa-feel without 5★ pricing.

### [Hotel Pavillon du Zoute][pv-off] — 4★, Bronlaan 4 (Het Zoute)
Sister-aged to Britannia: a 1923 Anglo-Norman villa with a south-facing English garden, two lounges, an indoor pool and a restaurant on-site [[7]](https://pavillonduzoute.be/en/) [[8]](https://hotel-pavillon-du-zoute.belgiancoast.net/en/). 39 rooms makes it the largest Norman-villa option; reviews split between "tasty restaurant, beautiful garden" and ⚠ inconsistent room-condition complaints [[8]](https://hotel-pavillon-du-zoute.belgiancoast.net/en/).

### [Manoir du Dragon][md-off] — 4★, Albertlaan 73 (Het Zoute)
Smallest of the four-star villas — just 16 individually decorated rooms, several with private terraces or fireplaces [[6]](https://www.hrs.com/en/hotel/106352). Sits directly next to the Royal Zoute golf course. Rated 9.2/10 across 103 reviews with very high marks for cleanliness (9.6) and comfort (9.4) [[5]](https://hotelmanoirdudragon.com-hotel.com/en/). Pick this if you want intimate manor-house energy over hotel-scale amenity.

### [Auberge St. Pol][sp-off] — 3★ superior, Bronlaan 23 (Het Zoute)
A painted-brick villa "in peaceful surroundings", recently re-done in a modern eco-design [[11]](https://hotelstpol.be/?lang=en). Polo paintings and bird art give it a quirky, owner-curated feel; superior and deluxe rooms get private terraces or balconies, breakfast buffet is exceptional, secure e-bike storage and chargers on-site [[20]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/hotelstpol.html). 8.9/10 on Booking — the best value-for-character pick in Het Zoute [[20]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/hotelstpol.html).

### [Lebeau 19][lb-off] — 3★, Zoutelaan 175 (Het Zoute)
26-room contemporary boutique under the Livingroom group; calming neutral interiors, on-site bar/brasserie, garden terrace, free private parking, walkable to Zwin Nature Reserve and four golf courses [[12]](https://www.lebeaubylivingroom.be/en/) [[13]](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-knokke-heist/lebeau-hotel.html). The most "design-modern" option that's still on the Belgian side.

## Dutch side — Cadzand-Bad & Sluis (the architectural payoff)

The drive Knokke ↔ Cadzand-Bad is ~14 km / ~17 min by car [[18]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/cadzand_bad-4506-zeeland-_-netherlands-to-knokke_heist-8300-bruges-flemish_region-belgium) — easily inside taxi range, and the design ambition jumps a level once you cross the border.

### [Strandhotel Cadzand][sc-off] — 4★, dune-front
Studio Piet Boon designed both the interiors and the in-room furniture — minimalist neutral palette, floor-to-ceiling windows, Auping boxspring beds, private balcony or terrace in every room [[14]](https://www.strandhotel.eu/en/) [[15]](https://pietboon.com/corporate-projects/strandhotel-cadzand/). Heated indoor pool, saunas. Comfort rooms from €155, suites from €275, tower-suite sea view from €395 [[14]](https://www.strandhotel.eu/en/). The defining stay for travellers who care about contemporary architecture.

### [Hotel Noordzee][nz-off] — 4★, Noordzeestraat 2, on the dunes
Individually designed rooms in a New England-inspired maritime palette (white, blue, sand); guests consistently highlight the "personality" of staff and the spa landscape — two saunas, steam bath, pool [[16]](https://www.hotelnoordzee.com/en/). Pick this over Strandhotel if you prefer warm/maritime over minimalist/Piet Boon.

### [De Lindenhoeve][ld-off] — boutique B&B + restaurant, Sluis (NL)
A converted family farmhouse in walled-town Sluis, transformed into 8 themed rooms plus a separate "lodge", with French/seafood restaurant on-site honouring "Tante Germaine" [[17]](https://delindenhoeve.nl/en/boutique-hotel/). 5-min walk to Sluis centre; ~12–15 min taxi to Cuines 33. The non-coastal alternative — pick this if you want hinterland character over a sea view.

## How to choose

- **Closest to Cuines 33, biggest design statement:** [La Réserve][lr-off] [[10]][10].
- **Most "Belgian-coast vintage villa" feeling:** [Britannia][br-off] [[4]][4] or [Pavillon du Zoute][pv-off] [[8]][8] — Britannia for the 2022 refresh, Pavillon for the garden and pool.
- **Smallest, most personal:** [Manoir du Dragon][md-off] (16 rooms) [[6]][6].
- **Best value with real character:** [Auberge St. Pol][sp-off] (€114, 8.9/10) [[20]][20].
- **You came for architecture, not just dinner:** [Strandhotel Cadzand][sc-off] [[15]][15] — accept the 17-min taxi each way [[18]][18].
- **Hinterland / non-beach pick:** [De Lindenhoeve][ld-off] in Sluis [[17]][17].
