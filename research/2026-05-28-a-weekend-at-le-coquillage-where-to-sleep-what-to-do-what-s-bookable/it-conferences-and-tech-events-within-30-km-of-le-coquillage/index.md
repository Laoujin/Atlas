## The April–May academic cluster

**[CPS-IoT Week 2026](https://cps-iot-week2026.inria.fr/)** — 19th edition, 11–14 May at the Palais des Congrès (1 Quai Duguay-Trouin, 35400 Saint-Malo) [[5]](https://cps-iot-week2026.inria.fr/venue.php). The premier annual gathering on cyber-physical systems and IoT: three flagship conferences (HSCC/ICCPS, SenSys, RTAS) plus workshops ENSsys, DESTION, CAADCPS, RAGE, FMSys, TADAM, FACCT all sharing one venue [[6]](https://cps-iot-week2026.inria.fr/events.php). HSCC/ICCPS added an *AI-enabled Autonomy* track in 2026 [[8]](https://hscc.acm.org/2026/). SenSys 2026 is the first edition under its new mandate as the unified conference for *Embedded Artificial Intelligence and Sensing Systems* — folding in the former IPSN and IoTDI communities [[9]](https://sensys.acm.org/2026/). RTAS focuses on time-critical systems across two tracks: Systems & Applications and Applied Methodologies & Foundations [[7]](https://2026.rtas.org/).

**[PQCrypto 2026](https://pqcrypto2026.irisa.fr/)** — 17th International Conference on Post-Quantum Cryptography, 14–16 April in Saint-Malo, organised by IRISA with INRIA and IRMAR [[10]](https://pqcrypto2026.irisa.fr/). Topic scope: code-, hash-, isogeny-, lattice-, multivariate- and MPC-in-the-head constructions, plus quantum cryptanalysis and side-channel attacks. 27 full papers from 66 submissions, published as Springer LNCS 16491/16492 [[11]](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-032-22695-2).

**Rencontres Stratégiques des Trois Océans** — inaugural edition 9–10 April, organised by Vates and focused on European digital sovereignty for ~100 defence and tech decision-makers [[12]](https://www.7jours.fr/actualites/defense-souverainete-et-numerique-au-coeur-dun-evenement-a-saint-malo/). Closed-door / invitation level rather than open registration.

## Industry: September

**[Digital Benchmark Saint-Malo 2026](https://digital-benchmark-2026.ebg.net/)** — 28–29 September, themed *L'entreprise 2030: Agentique, Souveraine, Décarbonée* (agentic AI, technological sovereignty, post-petroleum economy) [[13]](https://digital-benchmark-2026.ebg.net/). Organised by EBG (Elenbi Group), 660-member French digital think tank. ~300 CMOs and CDOs from non-competing brands, 25 keynotes, 30 workshops, 50+ vendor meetings. All-inclusive (transport, lodging, meals) — invitation-driven format rather than public ticket sales.

## Year-round anchors

**[Le Poool x La French Tech Rennes-St-Malo](https://lepoool.tech/)** — innovation hub spanning Rennes and Saint-Malo, runs the recurring *Digital Tech Conference*, *Matinales*, *Market Pitch* and *Startup on the Beach* programs alongside seven cohorts of its Incubation program [[16]](https://lepoool.tech/). The Saint-Malo coworking site sits near the train station / Grande Passerelle and is supported by Saint-Malo Agglomération [[17]](https://www.stmalo-agglomeration.fr/vie-economique/soutiens-a-linnovation-et-a-la-recherche/le-poool/). Easiest year-round option for evening tech meet-ups; their public agenda is the place to check for specific dates.

**[Flag'Malo CTF](https://www.flagmalo.fr/)** — annual capture-the-flag organised by IUT Saint-Malo's Réseaux & Télécoms students [[15]](https://www.flagmalo.fr/). Self-described as the largest CTF in Brittany; main edition typically December at the IUT campus. The 2026 IUT open house on 7 February also ran a mini-CTF for prospective students.

**[Forum des Métiers du Numérique](https://iut-stmalo.univ-rennes.fr/forum-des-metiers-du-numerique)** — annual at the IUT (held 21 March in 2026). Career-fair / mini-conference format with sessions on cybersecurity, cloud roles and network project management, plus participating employers including Orange, NXO, LACROIX, COMCYBER, Stelogy, Blue [[14]](https://iut-stmalo.univ-rennes.fr/forum-des-metiers-du-numerique). Free, public, Saturday format — the only Saturday tech event on the local calendar.

## Trip-planning notes

- **Saturday-evening dinner means the IT calendar is mostly already a miss.** Every major 2026 conference here is mid-week; Forum des Métiers and Flag'Malo are the only Saturday-shaped events.
- **April–May is the dense window.** If a future trip is open-dated, anchoring the Saturday dinner around mid-May lines it up with CPS-IoT Week (badge or sponsor halls aside, ambient activity at the Palais des Congrès and surrounding hotels is at its peak).
- **September window** (Digital Benchmark) is more closed-format / executive — less drop-in friendly.
- **Dinan is exactly on the 30 km boundary** by road [[3]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/saint_meloir_des_ondes-35350-ille_et_vilaine-brittany-france-to-dinan-22100-cotes_d_armor-brittany-france); as the crow flies it's well under, but no IT events surface there in 2026 either way.
