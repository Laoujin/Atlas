Cancale

Port · huîtres · GR34 · maison Roellinger

RF · Postes 4km CANCALE
VIA D155
Cancale 28•V•26 8 min
Cher voisin du Coquillage,

The marché aux huîtres is open daily 9 h–7 pm, 8 producers shucking on the spot — a dozen creuses runs €6–10[14]. Eat them on the seawall.

Pastries at Grain de Vanille (closed mardi), spices at Épices Roellinger. Then the GR34 to Pointe du Grouin — ~7 km, ~3 h[15].

Saint-Malo

Intra-muros · ramparts · corsaires · piscine de marée

RF · Postes 10km SAINT-MALO
VIA D155
St-Malo 28•V•26 15 min
Mon cher Coquillage,

Park P5 Esplanade Saint-Vincent or P1 Duguay-Trouin and walk the ramparts — 2 km loop, ~1 h, free; start at Porte Saint-Thomas[1].

Attention aux marées: Fort National only when the flag flies — windows posted daily[2]. The walk to Grand Bé spans a >12 m tidal range — easy to misjudge[10].

Wet weather? Grand Aquarium, 6 km south, bus 1.

Bons baisers de

Dinard

Belle Époque · Cannes du Nord · Plage de l'Écluse

RF · Postes 15km DINARD
VIA NAVETTE
Dinard 28•V•26 10 min mer
Mon cher convive,

Skip the car — take the Compagnie Corsaire sea bus from Saint-Malo's Gare maritime de la Bourse. 10-min crossing, €6.30 / €8.90 retour, runs 28 mars–3 juillet[31].

You land at the Promenade du Clair de Lune — palms, mimosas, agapanthus, ~15 min to the casino[43]. Plage de l'Écluse has those blue-and-white tents since 1890[35]. Pack a wetsuit — sea is 12–18°C[41].

NB: Villa Eugénie museum closed all 2026 — reopens April 2027 as inclusive housing[38].

Bons baisers de

Dol-de-Bretagne

Cathédrale · menhir · panorama du Mont-Dol

RF · Postes 16km DOL
VIA N176
Dol-de-Bne 28•V•26 20 min
Cher visiteur,

The Cathédrale Saint-Samson — founded VIe siècle, rebuilt after the fire of 1203, with the oldest monumental stained glass in Brittany still in situ (choir window, 1280–1290)[47]. Next door, the Cathédraloscope interprets it all (€8.50, audio guide included)[48].

2 km south on the D795: the Menhir du Champ-Dolent, 9.3 m of pink granite, ~100 tonnes — Brittany's second-tallest standing stone, said to have risen between two warring brothers[46].

Climb the 65 m of Mont-Dol for the panorama over the bay[56].

Dinan

Médiéval · remparts · maisons à colombages · Rance

RF · Postes 30km DINAN
VIA N176
Dinan 28•V•26 29 min
Mon cher convive,

Dinan is encircled by nearly 3 km of ramparts — Brittany's longest — with a 14e-siècle château over the Rance and more than 130 maisons à colombages[54].

The heart is Place des Merciers, still lined with timber-framed houses on ground-floor wooden porches. Walk down beyond the walls to the old port on the river[55].

30.8 km · 29 min via N176/D76[53] — pair it with Combourg for a full day.

Combourg

Château de Chateaubriand · Mémoires d'Outre-Tombe

RF · Postes 34km COMBOURG
VIA D73
Combourg 28•V•26 37 min
À mon cher correspondant,

The Château de Combourg reopens 1er avril 2026. Tickets €13.70 adulte, €10.70 réduit, €5.70 jeune (5–12); park alone €4.70. The interior tour is guided and lasts 45 minutes[50].

This is where the young François-René de Chateaubriand spent his childhood — the seat he immortalised in Mémoires d'Outre-Tombe[51].

33.8 km · 37 min via D73/D76 — just outside the strict 30 km circle, but a small detour pairs it with Dinan[52].

Abbaye · marée d'équinoxe · « la merveille »

RF · Postes 45km MT-ST-MICHEL
VIA N176
M.S.M. 28•V•26 47 min
Mon cher ami,

Beyond the 30 km brief — but skipping it on a weekend here would be perverse[24]. Park La Caserne in Beauvoir (~4 000 places, 2,5 km out)[27]; mi-saison 2026 a light vehicle pays €16 (3 h) / €19 (6 h) / €22 (24 h), first 30 min free[26].

The free Passeur shuttle runs 7 h 30 to minuit, every ~12 min, ~12-min ride vs 40–50 min walk[70].

Abbey ticket €16 (1 avr–30 sept), gratuit < 18 ans et UE 18–25[25]. Open 9 h–19 h mai–août; closed exceptionally 1 juin 2026[71].
