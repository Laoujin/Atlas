> **TL;DR — pick by the time you have.** A half-day → Saint-Malo's ramparts walk + Cancale's oyster market at the foot of Pointe des Crolles. A full day → add the GR34 to Pointe du Grouin or the 10-min sea-bus across the Rance to Dinard. A weekend → fold in Mont-Saint-Michel (45 km, just outside the radius but unmissable [[24]](https://www.l-itineraire.com/de_mont-saint-michel_a_saint-meloir-des-ondes)) and the Dol/Combourg/Dinan inland triangle. Bad weather → Grand Aquarium, Cathédraloscope, Demeure de Corsaire.

Le Coquillage sits in [Saint-Méloir-des-Ondes](https://www.maisons-de-bricourt.com/fr/places), 4 km from Cancale and 10 km from the Saint-Malo walls. Hugo Roellinger has run its kitchen since 2014 and won the third Michelin star in the 2025 guide [[19]](https://guide.michelin.com/fr/fr/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/un-inspecteur-raconte-le-coquillage-la-perle-rare-de-cancale-trois-etoiles-michelin) [[20]](https://www.roellinger-bricourt.com/en/page/famille-roellinger). The 30-km circle around the restaurant is one of the densest tourist landscapes in France.

## Cancale (4 km) — the closest stop, and the one with the most Roellinger

Cancale's port-side **Marché aux Huîtres** runs daily year-round at the foot of Pointe des Crolles, with eight independent oyster farmers shucking on the spot. Spring/summer hours 9 am–7 pm (8 pm July–August) [[13]](https://www.marcheauxhuitres-cancale.com/en/informations-en). A dozen huîtres creuses costs ~6–10 € — caliber 3 typically 6–8 €; flat oysters (Belon) and rarities like Tsarskaya cost more [[14]](https://www.cancale-tourisme.fr/marche-aux-huitres-de-cancale/). Eat them on the seawall facing the bay.

The Roellinger family runs several stops in town independent of Le Coquillage itself:

- **[Grain de Vanille](https://www.maisons-de-bricourt.com/fr/page/grain-de-vanille)** — pastry/tea-room at 12 place de la Victoire; 9 h–18 h 30 continuous, closed Tuesdays; Galettes Cancalaises, kouign-amann, spiced chocolates [[21]](https://www.maisons-de-bricourt.com/fr/page/grain-de-vanille).
- **[Épices Roellinger](https://www.epices-roellinger.com/en/places)** — flagship at 1 rue Duguesclin inside the Maison du Voyageur, the family's spice-blending lab; sister shops in Saint-Malo (12 rue Saint-Vincent) and Paris [[22]](https://www.epices-roellinger.com/en/places).
- **[Le Bistrot de Cancale](https://www.maisons-de-bricourt.com/fr/places)** — second restaurant above Plage de Port-Mer, opened June 2022 [[23]](https://www.maisons-de-bricourt.com/fr/places).
- **La Cuisine Corsaire École** — cooking school on Place Saint-Méen [[23]](https://www.maisons-de-bricourt.com/fr/places).

For walkers, **[GR34 Cancale → Pointe du Grouin](https://www.cancale-tourisme.fr/randonnee-pointe-du-grouin/)** is ~7 km one-way along clifftop paths, ~3 hours [[15]](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/france/ille-et-vilaine/gr-34-cancale-pointe-du-grouin). The Pointe itself looks out over the Île des Landes seabird reserve (~8 ha, France's oldest biological reserve, closed to landings) [[16]](https://www.cancale-tourisme.fr/randonnee-pointe-du-grouin/) [[18]](https://www.nhu.bzh/ile-des-landes-cancale-oiseaux/); on a clear day you see Mont-Saint-Michel from the cliff. To see the island from the water, [Compagnie Corsaire](https://compagniecorsaire.com/destination/croisiere-bateau-cancale/) runs a 2 h 30 commented cruise from Saint-Malo (10:30) or Dinard (10:45), no disembarkation — €37 adult, €24.40 child 3–15 [[17]](https://compagniecorsaire.com/destination/croisiere-bateau-cancale/).

## Saint-Malo intra-muros (10 km) — corsair ramparts and a tidal pool

The signature activity is the **ramparts walk**: free, ~2 km loop, ~1 hour. The tourist office recommends starting at Porte Saint-Thomas behind Place Chateaubriand; staircases at every gate let you drop in or out [[1]](https://www.saint-malo-tourisme.co.uk/our-8-preserved-treasures/saint-malo-le-bijou-corsaire/tour-the-ramparts-of-saint-malo/). Park outside the walls — **Esplanade Saint-Vincent (P5)** is underground at the main gate; **Duguay-Trouin (P1)** is open-air on the quay near Plage du Sillon; **Rocabey** is free with a 15–20 min walk [[12]](https://www.st-malo.com/tourisme/parking-saint-malo/).

## Dinard (15 km) — half a day of Belle Époque, easiest by sea bus

Skip the car: take the **[Compagnie Corsaire sea bus](https://compagniecorsaire.com/en/destination/saint-malo-dinard/)** from Saint-Malo's Gare maritime de la Bourse — 10-minute crossing, daily from 28 March to 3 July 2026, €6.30 one-way / €8.90 return (adult); €4.50 / €6.30 (child) [[31]](https://compagniecorsaire.com/en/destination/saint-malo-dinard/) [[32]](https://compagniecorsaire.com/en/infos-pratiques/horaires-et-tarifs/). It lands at the foot of the **Promenade du Clair de Lune**, a cliff-hugging pedestrian path lined with palms, mimosas and agapanthus, with direct views to Saint-Malo's ramparts; ~15 min to walk to the casino end [[33]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g187099-d5493585-Reviews-Prom_du_Clair_de_Lune-Dinard_Ille_et_Vilaine_Brittany.html) [[43]](https://monsieur-de-france.com/en/visit-dinard-what-to-see).

A few steps on is **[Plage de l'Écluse](https://www.dinardemeraudetourisme.com/en/explore/8-emerald-paradises/discover-dinard/)**, the southwest-facing golden crescent with the blue-and-white striped tents that have been a fixture since the 1890s, plus a seawater pool [[35]](https://portsmouthtostmalo.co.uk/day-trips-from-st-malo/dinard-day-trip/). The bronze **Alfred Hitchcock with birds on his shoulder** stands on the embankment by the casino, a nod to the Dinard British Film Festival, founded 1989 [[34]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g187099-d17338407-Reviews-Statue_d_Alfred_Hitchcock-Dinard_Ille_et_Vilaine_Brittany.html) [[36]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinard).

The tourist office runs walks among nearly 400 protected villas — **Villa les Roches Brunes** is the standout [[37]](https://www.dinardemeraudetourisme.com/en/explore/8-emerald-paradises/discover-dinard/).

⚠ **Villa Eugénie / Musée du Site Balnéaire is closed in 2026.** The city signed a 40-year lease with the Diadème association on 27 November 2025 to convert the building into inclusive housing; works start in 2026, with an April 2027 reopening as housing + solidarity restaurant — not a museum [[38]](https://www.lemoniteur.fr/bretagne/ille-et-vilaine/ille-et-vilaine-rehabilitation-de-la-villa-eugenie-pour-la-creation-de-logements-inclusifs-a-dinard.3LQIF3XTIRHRLEAYTLF5WNBPG4.html) [[39]](https://www.ville-dinard.fr/actualites/une-nouvelle-vie-pour-la-villa-eugenie/).

⚠ Spring water at Plage de l'Écluse feels cool in May–June — pack a wetsuit if you plan to swim [[41]](https://www.worldbeachguide.com/france/plage-de-lecluse-dinard-water-temp.htm).

Further west, **Saint-Lunaire** and **Saint-Briac-sur-Mer** are quieter family resorts with turquoise water; Saint-Lunaire's centre was shaped by Haitian businessman Sylla Laraque, making it one of the first seaside resorts on the Côte d'Émeraude [[40]](https://www.ille-et-vilaine-tourism.com/discover-ille-et-vilaine/the-loveliest-places/saint-briac-and-saint-lunaire/).

## Mont-Saint-Michel (~45 km — outside the radius, included anyway)

Mont-Saint-Michel is ~45 km / ~47 min by road from Saint-Méloir-des-Ondes via N176 then D4 [[24]](https://www.l-itineraire.com/de_mont-saint-michel_a_saint-meloir-des-ondes) — beyond the 30-km brief, but skipping it on a weekend here would be perverse.

**Parking & shuttle.** Park at La Caserne in Beauvoir (~4,000 spaces, 2.5 km from the rock) [[27]](https://saint-michel-tickets.org/parking-mont-saint-michel/). Mid-season (Mar–Jun) 2026 a light vehicle pays €16 (3 h) / €19 (6 h) / €22 (24 h); first 30 min free [[26]](https://montsaintmichel.gouv.fr/acceder-au-mont-saint-michel/horaires-et-tarifs). The free **Le Passeur** shuttle runs daily 07:30 to midnight at ~12-minute headways [[68]](https://visit-mont-saint-michel.com/blogs/mont-saint-michel-visit-guide/mont-saint-michel-shuttle); the official Normandy tourism page confirms 07:30–23:00 service plus an on-demand line at +33 (0)2 14 13 20 15 outside hours, with the shuttle dropping passengers ~350 m before the village entrance [[69]](https://www.ot-montsaintmichel.com/en/discover/visit-the-mont-saint-michel/access-the-mont-saint-michel/by-bus-and-coach/). End-to-end transit is ~12 minutes by shuttle vs. a 40–50 minute walk on the causeway [[70]](https://mont-saint-michel-tours.com/parking-shuttle-services-at-mont-saint-michel/).

**Abbey ticket.** €16 in 2026 from 1 April–30 September; €13 off-season. Free for under-18s and EU residents 18–25 [[25]](https://www.abbaye-mont-saint-michel.fr/en/visit/practical-information). Open daily 09:00–19:00 from 1 May to 31 August 2026 with last admission one hour before closing; exceptionally closed Monday 1 June 2026 [[71]](https://visit-mont-saint-michel.com/blogs/mont-saint-michel-visit-guide/opening-hours-of-mont-saint-michel-abbey).

**Tide drama in 2026.** A coefficient near 110 is needed for the sea to fully encircle the Mount [[30]](https://en.normandie-tourisme.fr/unmissable-sites/the-mont-saint-michel/high-tides-mont-saint-michel/). The year's peak is **18 April, coefficient 105 (HW 08:35)**, bracketed by 17 April (101), 19 April (104), 20 April (99) [[28]](https://mont-saint-michel-tours.com/mont-saint-michel-tide-schedule/). May peaks 17–18 May at coefficient 99 — second-best window of the year, with the 18 May 08:51 morning tide the cycle's highest [[72]](https://www.lebalcondelabaie.com/grandes-marees-baie-du-mont-saint-michel/) [[73]](https://www.sport-evasion-fr.com/grandes-marees-mont-saint-michel-2026). June tops out at 95 on the 16th [[29]](https://montsaintmicheltides.com/june-2026-tides). If you want the "island again" spectacle, 17–19 April is the year's only window near the threshold; otherwise expect a high tide that almost-but-doesn't-quite reach the causeway.

**GR34 vs Vélomaritime — don't confuse them.** The **GR34 'Sentier des Douaniers'** is a marked walker-only coastal footpath using clifftop paths, rock-cut stairs and beach passages — impassable by bike on many sections [[66]](https://www.ille-et-vilaine-tourisme.bzh/experiences/le-chemin-des-douaniers-une-experience-memorable/). The **Vélomaritime (EuroVelo 4 / V2)** is the parallel cycle route [[59]](https://en.francevelotourisme.com/cycling-destinations/brittany-by-bike/from-mont-st-michel-to-st-malo-by-bike).

## A weekend, shaped

> **Saturday before dinner at Le Coquillage:** Cancale oyster market for lunch → Grain de Vanille for coffee → walk a section of GR34 toward Pointe du Grouin → back to dress for the 19:30 service.
>
> **Sunday:** Saint-Malo ramparts in the morning → lunch intra-muros → 10-min sea bus to Dinard → Promenade du Clair de Lune → ferry back. *Or:* drive to Mont-Saint-Michel for a half-day (check the tide coefficient — if it's a 17–19 April or 17–18 May window, go; otherwise the abbey is the draw, not the flood).
>
> **Rainy alternates:** Grand Aquarium (6 km south of Saint-Malo) → Cathédraloscope in Dol → Demeure de Corsaire guided tour.

## What's *not* in the 30-km circle

Cap Fréhel and Fort la Latte, the iconic pink-sandstone cliffs of the western Côte d'Émeraude, sit ~45 km from Saint-Malo — outside the radius, and a separate day-trip rather than something to slot into this weekend [[42]](http://fr.distances-routieres.himmera.com/distance-saint_malo-cap_frehel-voiture_km_itineraire-128143.html).
