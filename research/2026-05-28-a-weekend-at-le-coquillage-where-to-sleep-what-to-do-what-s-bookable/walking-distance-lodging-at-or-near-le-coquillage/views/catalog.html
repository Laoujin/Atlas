I.

Inside the kilometre — ranked by proximity

Six options · 0 m to ~1 km · €50 – €450
Editor's pick · same building as dinner

Château Richeux

Relais & Châteaux · 11 rooms + 2 apartments · 1920s villa
0 m · same building €280 – €450 / night Sea-view supp. +€80–100 ★ #1 of 12 in Cancale Breakfast €32 extra

Spice-named rooms on the upper floors of the same 1920s villa where Hugo Roellinger cooks; the bay opens out below the bedroom windows and dinner is a staircase away.[10] Tripadvisor reviewers consistently call out the Cardamome and Gingembre rooms — the sea-view line item is the one to pay for.[15]

Pick when you want zero logistics on dinner night and the full Roellinger experience — sleeping in the villa is the point.

La Ferme du Vent

6 stand-alone "kled" gîtes · same gardens · off-grid
400 – 500 m · ~6 min €275 night 1 · €200 after Pkg incl. dinner €590 No wifi / no TV by design

Wood-and-stone kleds scattered through the same Château gardens, with access to the Celtic-style Bains Celtiques spa in the old farmhouse.[2][16] The €590 "kled + dinner at Le Coquillage" package is the cleanest way to combine the two on a single bill.[11]

03

Gîte Le Petit Buot

30 m² gîte for 2 · self-catering · pool access
300 – 500 m · ~6 min ~€53 / night (€371 / wk) Weekly rentals only

A 30 m² ground-floor cottage with a 140 cm bed and a shared pool, in Le Buot itself — under ten minutes' walk to dinner. The catch is the rhythm: rentals are by the week, so this is a multi-night base rather than a one-night room.[14]

La Petite Bergère

Chambre d'hôtes · doubles + duplex + apt · sea-view garden
~1 km · ~12 min from €85 · breakfast incl. ★ 9.1 couples (Booking) Check-in 17h – 19h

The most polished of the Les Nielles B&Bs — a sea-view garden, doubles starting at €85, a 9.1 Booking score on couple stays.[4][12] Call before you commit — the check-in window is narrow and there is no 24h front desk.

La Pastourelle

Chambre d'hôtes · Logis "3 Suns" · table d'hôtes possible
~1 km · ~12 min ~€70 – €100 · breakfast incl. Logis 3 Suns

A family-run Logis on the D155 in Les Nielles, run by the Ravenoux family — quieter than La Petite Bergère, with the option of a table d'hôtes evening if you are not at Le Coquillage that night.[13]

II.

Inside the search results — outside the walk

Often surfaced as "near Le Coquillage" — both need a taxi after dinner

Les Rimains

Roellinger sister · 5 km · cliff-top in Cancale port
Four sea-facing rooms at 62 rue des Rimains.[17] Book it for a Cancale-port morning — and bake in a ~10-minute taxi back from dinner.[15]

Maison Tirel-Guérin / Domaine du Limonay

4-star · ~6 km inland · at the train station
A few minutes' walk from the La Gouesnière–Cancale–St Méloir des Ondes station, so it's the right pick if you are arriving by train. Not walkable back from dinner.[18]

Book in this order.

  1. Confirm the table at Le Coquillage first — Tue–Sat lunch and dinner, closed Sun + Mon. Phone +33 2 99 89 64 76.[8]
  2. The same number books Château Richeux and Ferme du Vent — one switchboard for the Maisons de Bricourt group.[1]
  3. If you take the Château, ask for Cardamome or Gingembre — reviewers say the sea-view supplement is the line item worth paying.[10]
  4. If you take a Les Nielles B&B, agree the check-in time on the phone before you commit — these are individuals, not a 24h desk.[4]
