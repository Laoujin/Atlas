Practical notes
  • Venue key: SNIEC = Shanghai New International Expo Centre (Pudong, Metro Line 7 Huamu Rd); NECC = National Exhibition and Convention Center (Hongqiao, Line 2/17); SICC = Shanghai International Convention Center (Pudong riverside, near Century Ave).
  • September developer block: MCPCon (Sep 6–7) + KubeCon+PyTorch (Sep 8–9) are four back-to-back days at the same venue — one trip covers agentic AI protocols, cloud-native infrastructure, and ML frameworks.
  • WAIC access: The exhibition floor is typically free or nominally priced; specialist summits and industry forums are ticketed separately. Check worldaic.com.cn for 2026 pass tiers.[11]
  • Language: MWC Shanghai and Tech Week run bilingually (EN/ZH). WAIC and GDPS are primarily Chinese with English interpretation at major keynote sessions.
  • KubeCon support: Linux Foundation offers travel scholarships, visa letter requests, and childcare — useful for international attendees.[14]
