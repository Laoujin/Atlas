> **TL;DR** La Rochelle punches above its size for tech events. The main fixed dates are **IA-NA** (AI congress, early June, 3rd edition in 2026) and **EBG Digital Benchmark** (MarTech/data, October, recurring since at least 2023). For the rest of the year, [Technopole ATLAS](https://technopole-atlas.fr) and [Village by CA CMDS](https://cmds.levillagebyca.com) run a steady cadence of workshops and pitch events. There is no GDG or DevFest chapter in La Rochelle — the nearest active developer community hub is Bordeaux.

## Ecosystem & Recurring Events

### Technopole ATLAS workshops

[Technopole ATLAS](https://technopole-atlas.fr) [[8]](https://technopole-atlas.fr/agenda) is La Rochelle's main innovation hub (12 Avenue Albert Einstein, Minimes). It runs:

- **AI/growth workshops** — monthly themed sessions (e.g. "Growth & IA : De la théorie à la pratique")
- **IP & software protection workshops** — focus on AI and startup legal frameworks
- **Pitch&Invest** — pitch competitions at Village by CA La Rochelle
- **European funding workshops** — cascade funding for startups/SMEs
- **VR demonstrations** — demos at Tribord Sailing Lab (most recent: June 4, 2026)

All listed on the [ATLAS agenda](https://technopole-atlas.fr/agenda).

---

### Village by CA CMDS

[Village by CA CMDS](https://cmds.levillagebyca.com) [[9]](https://cmds.levillagebyca.com/) (Charente-Maritime Deux-Sèvres) is a startup accelerator co-located with Technopole ATLAS at 12 Avenue Albert Einstein. It runs workshops, networking afterworks, and the Pitch&Invest sessions. Its 400 m² auditorium is used for many of the ecosystem events above.

---

### Startup Weekend La Rochelle

A 49-hour entrepreneurship sprint [[10]](https://etudiant.opencampusinnov.fr/evenements-et-serious-games/startup-weekend-la-rochelle/) hosted at La Rochelle Université's Technoforum. Participants pitch ideas, form teams, and build MVPs over a weekend. Backed by the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region via [OpenCampusInnov](https://etudiant.opencampusinnov.fr/). Specific 2026 dates not yet published — check the [OpenCampusInnov events page](https://etudiant.opencampusinnov.fr/evenements-et-serious-games/startup-weekend-la-rochelle/) for updates.

[IUT La Rochelle's Informatique department](https://www.iut-larochelle.fr/actualites/journees-ia-2026-info/) [[11]](https://www.iut-larochelle.fr/actualites/journees-ia-2026-info/) ran a national AI pedagogy day on March 20, 2026 for IUT educators (topics: AI evaluation, AI agents in teaching). ## Tech Ecosystem Context

La Rochelle is part of [French Tech Atlantic Valley](https://www.agglo-larochelle.fr/-/bienvenue-a-french-tech-atlantic-valley-) [[14]](https://www.agglo-larochelle.fr/-/bienvenue-a-french-tech-atlantic-valley-) (renamed French Tech Poitou-Charentes in 2023), covering Angoulême, La Rochelle, Niort, and Poitiers. The local startup ecosystem ranks #627 globally [[15]](https://www.startupblink.com/startup-ecosystem/la-rochelle-fr) with ~16 startups and $5.3M in total funding — compact but growing (5.6% in 2025). For major developer conferences (DevFest, BDX I/O, Devoxx), the nearest hubs are **Nantes** (120 km) and **Bordeaux** (180 km).
