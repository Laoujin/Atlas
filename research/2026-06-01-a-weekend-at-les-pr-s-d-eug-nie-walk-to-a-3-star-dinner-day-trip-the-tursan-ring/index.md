The weekend hangs on one table. **Les Prés d'Eugénie** has held three Michelin stars since 1977 [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/hotels-stays/eugenie-les-bains/les-pres-deugenie-7394), and the estate's gravity is so total that the village (~500 inhabitants [[2]](https://www.guide-des-landes.com/en/tourism/discover/towns-and-villages/eugenie-les-bains-95/eugenie-les-bains-1216.html)) reads as Michel Guérard's campus with a restaurant inside it rather than the reverse. Every other choice — bed, daytime activity, even whether to bundle a tech-conference excuse — falls out of that anchor.

**Sleep on the estate.** The two lodging surveys converge on the same picks. The walkable hotel inventory is tiny — TripAdvisor lists five properties for the whole village [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Hotels-g950951-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine-Hotels.html) — and the three Guérard buildings (the 1863 Maison, the 18th-c **Couvent des Herbes** annex from €330, the rustic Logis de la Ferme aux Grives) plus the 3-star **Maison Rose** annex from €160 [[4]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/rooms-suites/) [[5]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/la-maison-rose/) all sit between 0 and 150 m of the dining-room door and share one set of gardens, heated pool and spa [[6]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/). Splurge pick: Couvent des Herbes (eight keys total, books out months ahead). Value pick: Maison Rose, roughly a third the main-house rate on the same campus. Independent fallback if every Guérard key is gone: **Hôtel Le Relais des Champs**, €80–90, 300 m back through the park [[7]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/le-relais-des-champs.html). The character-lodging survey adds taxi-range alternatives — Château de Lahitte (Vergoignan, ~12 km), Lassaubatju's working Armagnac distillery in Hontanx, Le Jardin sur l'Eau in Aire — but flags the friction: local taxis are not on-demand, and after-dinner pickups need ≥12 h advance booking [[8]](https://www.guide-des-landes.com/en/tourism/information/transports/taxis/aire-sur-l-adour-333/aire-taxi-2725.html). Champagne arithmetic at midnight is a poor design.

**The day-trip ring overlaps the lodging ring almost completely.** Of the six villages the lodging surveys surface within taxi range — Aire-sur-l'Adour, Vergoignan, Ségos, Hontanx, Betbezer-d'Armagnac, Saint-Sever — five reappear as distinct day-trip calls in the activities map. The strongest pairings: Aire-sur-l'Adour (Le Jardin sur l'Eau lodging, 9 km) doubles as the UNESCO **Sainte-Quitterie crypt** with its 4th-c. white-marble sarcophagus, free 45-min guided tours 1 Jul–14 Sep [[9]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/Sites-et-musees/eglise-sainte-quitterie/848); Hontanx is the Lassaubatju Armagnac sleep + on-estate tasting from €88 [[10]](https://www.armagnac-lassaubatju.com/hebergement/les-chambres-dhotes/); Saint-Sever is the **UNESCO Benedictine abbey-church** with the rare seven-staggered-apses choir and the Beatus facsimile (Treasure Room open through 31 Dec 2026) [[11]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/abbaye-benedictine-et-son-abbatiale-pcuaqu040010r9f0/) [[12]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/salle-du-tresor-de-labbaye-et-beatus-de-saint-sever-pcuaqu040fs0002k/). Betbezer-d'Armagnac (Domaine de Paguy, ~22 km from Eugénie [[13]](https://www.domaine-de-paguy.com/)) sits inside the lodging ring but adjoins Labastide-d'Armagnac's Bas-Armagnac écomusée at 43 km — outside the 30 km activity brief unless stretched.

**Two timing rails define the Saturday.** First, the Aire market runs Tuesday and Saturday 8:00–12:30 [[14]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/Agenda/marche-hebdomadaire-du-mardi/436) — pair it with the Sainte-Quitterie crypt for a one-call morning before the long evening at the table. Second, **most Landes museums close Sundays and/or Mondays** (Samadet faïence closes Mon; Hagetmau's Romanesque crypt closes both Sun and Mon) [[15]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/crypte-de-saint-girons-joyau-du-12eme-siecle-pcuaqu04001oc14w/); Saint-Sever's abbey-church is the rare site open daily 8:00–19:00 year-round [[16]](https://www.landes-chalosse.com/en/for-lovers-of-history-and-heritage/abbaye-benedictine-et-son-abbatiale-1034822), which makes it the obvious Sunday-morning departure stop. **Château de Bachen** — the Guérards' own Tursan vineyard 8 km away — is reservation-only Mon–Fri at 10:00 or 15:00 [[17]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/chateau-de-bachen-vineyard/), so it slots in only when the weekend stretches into the working week (`reservation@lespreseugenie.com`).

**The IT angle is a deliberate negative result.** The 30 km radius holds no DevFest, GDG, vendor user group or Saturday tech format; the only IT calendar is at the CCI Landes in Mont-de-Marsan (Mars@Hack CTF early March, Rencontres de la Cybersécurité late January) — weekday, and all already past for 2026 [[18]](https://www.landes.cci.fr/presse/marshack-2026-cybersecurite/). Bundling tech means waiting for Mars@Hack 2027 or stretching south to **UTNT in Pau** (Tue–Wed 7–8 Oct 2026) and attaching the Saturday dinner [[19]](https://netceed.com/nl/events/universite-de-la-transition-numerique-des-territoires-2026-pau-france/).

One question this run leaves open: for travellers willing to swap the walking-back-from-dinner serenity for a working Armagnac estate as their base, does **Maison Lassaubatju** — distillery sleep + on-estate tasting + Château de Bachen on a working day + Saint-Sever on Sunday — upgrade the weekend's Gascony fingerprint enough to justify the taxi friction? The trade is real, and which way it tips is personal.
