Hôtel · Réception

Eugénie-les-Bains

The walkable keys — every bed within strolling distance of the 3-star table
✦ ✦ ✦
Concierge's recommendation

Eugénie is essentially one village of ~500 people built around the Guérard estate, so every key on this board is a sub-10-minute walk to the Michelin 3-star dining room at Place de l'Impératrice.

Take the Couvent des Herbes key if the bill doesn't matter (8 hooks, books out months ahead). Take La Maison Rose if it does — same campus, same gardens, a third the rate. If the Guérard board is empty, walk one street over to Le Relais des Champs — €80 a night, 400 m through the park, and the front desk lends bicycles.

House Rules

Tiny inventory
~140–150 rooms across all walkable lodging. Saturday at the 3-star is the busiest dinner of the week — lock lodging the day you confirm the table.
B&Bs out of range
Nearest chambre d'hôte (Céline Paton, Route de Santon) is 2 km away — you'd be driving home from dinner.
Tourist tax
€5.76 per person per day at Les Prés d'Eugénie; comparable at the other 3★ houses.
The estate is one campus
Guests of Maison Rose, Couvent des Herbes and Logis des Grives all share the heated pool, gardens, and (with reservation) the thermal spa.
