> **Decision.** Eugénie-les-Bains is essentially one village of ~500 people built around the [Guérard](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/) estate, so every hotel below is a sub-10-min walk to the Michelin 3-star dining room at Place de l'Impératrice [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g950951-d263869-Reviews-Les_Pres_D_eugenie-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine.html). Best splurge: a room in **Le Couvent des Herbes** on the estate itself — 18th-c convent, ~0 m to the table, from €330 [[2]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/rooms-suites/). Best value still inside the Guérard universe: **La Maison Rose** (3★, same domain) from €160 [[4]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/la-maison-rose/). Best independent fallback if Guérard properties are full: **Hôtel Le Relais des Champs**, 300 m behind the thermes, €80–90 [[10]](https://en.montdemarsan-tourisme.com/offers/le-relais-des-champs-eugenie-les-bains-en-2955415/)[[11]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/le-relais-des-champs.html).

## What's actually walkable

## On-domain Guérard lodging (where to stay if you can swing it)

The estate has 45 rooms scattered across "5 historic buildings" sitting "a few dozen metres from each other" [[1]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/)[[16]](https://www.virtuoso.com/travel/luxury-hotels/11250530/les-pres-deugenie-maison-guerard), all sharing the same gardens, spa, pool, three restaurants, and key card. From any of them you stroll → dinner → bed without unlocking a car.

### [Maison Guérard](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/) — the main house

The 1863 manor at Place de l'Impératrice with 33 of the estate's rooms [[1]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/). Tiers run Classic/Superior 30–36 m² from €270, De Luxe 50–80 m² with terrace/balcony from €500, Suites 50–61 m² from €550, Royal Suites 80 m² from €700, Imperial Suites 120–150 m² from €900 [[2]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/rooms-suites/). Decorated in antiques and period paintings; many rooms have balconies or terraces over the gardens [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g950951-d263869-Reviews-Les_Pres_D_eugenie-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine.html). #1 on TripAdvisor with the Travelers' Choice Best of the Best 2025 badge [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g950951-d263869-Reviews-Les_Pres_D_eugenie-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine.html). Critique threads mention worn furnishings in some rooms and steep breakfast surcharges (€10 add-on for eggs noted) [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g950951-d263869-Reviews-Les_Pres_D_eugenie-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine.html).

### [Le Couvent des Herbes](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/) — 18th-c convent annex

Eight rooms and suites inside a former convent, sheltered by ancient cedars, some opening onto the curé's vegetable garden [[2]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/rooms-suites/). The most atmospheric stay on the estate; pale fruitwood floors, polished age-tiles, embroidered-linen drapes. Deluxe Rooms from €330, Convent Suites (50 m²) from €450, "Le Temps des Cerises" (with fireplace, terrace, marble bath) from €550 [[2]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/rooms-suites/). ⚠ Eight keys total — book early, especially Saturday nights.

### [Les Logis de la Ferme aux Grives](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/) — rustic farmhouse suites

Across the river in the vegetable-garden enclosure: 4 suites of 40–60 m² plus Deluxe rooms, with terracotta tiles, aged beams, Arudy-stone bathtubs and canopy beds [[6]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/dining/la-ferme-aux-grives/). Suites from €450, Deluxe from €330 [[2]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/rooms-suites/). La Ferme aux Grives the bistro (Guérard's casual sister to the 3-star) sits in the same building; useful for the Friday or Sunday meal you're not eating at the gastronomic [[6]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/dining/la-ferme-aux-grives/).

### [La Maison Rose](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/la-maison-rose/) — Guérard's 3-star sister

The cheapest way to stay on a Guérard key. Originally the spa/thermal-cure hotel; 24 classic-and-comfort rooms 15–21 m², 8 superior rooms 21–25 m², 4 junior suites 28–30 m² [[5]](https://www.chainethermale.fr/eugenie-les-bains/se-loger/hotel-restaurant-la-maison-rose). Set in a meadow with a heated pool, fireplaced lounges, a tearoom [[5]](https://www.chainethermale.fr/eugenie-les-bains/se-loger/hotel-restaurant-la-maison-rose). From €160 / €170 / €180 across the three tiers [[4]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/la-maison-rose/) → roughly **a third of the main-house rate** for the same village, same gardens, same evening walk.

## Independent walking-distance options

### [Hôtel Le Relais des Champs](https://en.montdemarsan-tourisme.com/offers/le-relais-des-champs-eugenie-les-bains-en-2955415/) — the value pick

3-star family hotel at 339 Route Nicolas, 300 m behind the thermes toward Classun [[10]](https://en.montdemarsan-tourisme.com/offers/le-relais-des-champs-eugenie-les-bains-en-2955415/). 26 rooms + 4 ground-floor suites (two adapted for wheelchair access); pool, gardens, bicycle rental, on-site restaurant with €19.50 daily menu [[11]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/le-relais-des-champs.html)[[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.fr/Hotel_Review-g950951-d3140340-Reviews-Hotel_Le_Relais_des_Champs-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine.html). Standard rooms €80–90, suites €122 [[11]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/le-relais-des-champs.html). Booking score 8.6 with a 9.2/10 location rating over 216 reviews [[11]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/fr/le-relais-des-champs.html). Reviewers consistently use the words "well-maintained" and "clean" — the boring-in-a-good-way option [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.fr/Hotel_Review-g950951-d3140340-Reviews-Hotel_Le_Relais_des_Champs-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine.html).

### [Le Clos Nicolas](https://www.hotel-clos-nicolas.fr/hotel/) — riverside 3★

20 rooms + 6 suites at 400 m from the thermes and village centre, set on the Bahus river [[8]](https://www.hotel-clos-nicolas.fr/hotel/). Air-conditioned, outdoor + indoor pools advertised, on-site restaurant. 4.3/5 across 61 TripAdvisor reviews [[9]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g950951-d3383048-Reviews-Le_Clos_Nicolas-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine.html). ⚠ Recent reviewer complaints: indoor pool tarped/awaiting plumber, gym equipment non-functional, "establishment outdated and deserves significant renovation" [[9]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g950951-d3383048-Reviews-Le_Clos_Nicolas-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine.html). Pick Relais des Champs over this one at the same price point.

### [Chez Léonie](https://www.chez-leonie.fr/) — the cheapest walkable bed

2-star family hotel at 113 rue René Vielle, directly opposite the thermes; founded in 1956 by Léonie Baqué and still family-run [[13]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/Ou-dormir/Hotels/chez-leonie/9)[[17]](https://www.chez-leonie.fr/). 11 rooms total (8 upstairs, 2 ground-floor with terraces — one wheelchair-accessible — plus a small apartment) [[13]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/Ou-dormir/Hotels/chez-leonie/9). Double €65–72, breakfast €7–10 [[13]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/Ou-dormir/Hotels/chez-leonie/9). 4.9/5 on a small TripAdvisor sample of 8 reviews — high but n is low [[14]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g950951-d15191908-Reviews-Chez_Leonie-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine.html). Basic but clean and warm; use it if you're spending the entire weekend budget on dinner.

### [Camping des Sources](https://www.guide-des-landes.com/en/tourism/accommodation/camping/campsites-mobile-homes/eugenie-les-bains-95/camping-des-sources-734.html) — 4★ chalets, edge of walking range

900 m from the thermal baths along the Bahus river, so 1–1.2 km to Place de l'Impératrice — doable but it's the upper bound of "walking distance after Champagne." 125 pitches + 43 air-conditioned chalets and mobile homes; open Feb–Dec [[15]](https://www.guide-des-landes.com/en/tourism/accommodation/camping/campsites-mobile-homes/eugenie-les-bains-95/camping-des-sources-734.html). Only worth it for a chalet stay with kids; ⚠ TripAdvisor flags a strict cancellation policy [[18]](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Hotels-g950951-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine-Hotels.html).

## Practical notes

- **Tiny inventory, single anchor.** Across all walkable lodging the village holds roughly 140–150 rooms total. Saturday at Les Prés d'Eugénie is the busiest dinner of the week; lock lodging the same day you confirm the table [[18]](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Hotels-g950951-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine-Hotels.html).
- **Chambres d'hôtes are mostly outside walking range.** The nearest listed B&B, Céline Paton, is at 49 Route de Santon — 2 km from the thermes [[18]](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Hotels-g950951-Eugenie_Les_Bains_Landes_Nouvelle_Aquitaine-Hotels.html). Pretty, but you'd be driving home from dinner.
- **Tourist tax.** Les Prés d'Eugénie charges €5.76/person/day on top of the room rate; comparable taxes apply at the 3★ hotels.
- **The "stay where you ate" upgrade.** Guérard properties share grounds — guests of Maison Rose, Couvent des Herbes, and Logis des Grives all use the same heated pool, gardens, and (with reservation) thermal spa as Maison Guérard guests [[1]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/). If the 3-star dinner is the anchor, paying for an on-domain room — even the cheapest one at La Maison Rose — turns the whole estate into your campus for the night.
