> **TL;DR — pick one per half-day.** Closest hit (8 km): walk the 1318 bastide grid of **Geaune** then taste at the Cave des Vignerons du Tursan [[6]](https://www.guide-des-landes.com/en/experiences/culture-and-heritage/article-bastide-geaune-184.html) [[23]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/Se-regaler/Nos-producteurs/la-cave-des-vignerons-tursan/881). Biggest-name half-day (14 km): Aire-sur-l'Adour's UNESCO **Sainte-Quitterie crypt** with the 4th-c. white-marble sarcophagus, free 45-min guided tour July–mid-September [[2]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/eglise-sainte-quitterie-pcuaqu04001oxgtr/) [[4]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/Sites-et-musees/eglise-sainte-quitterie/848). Full-day pick (26 km): the **Saint-Sever** Benedictine abbey-church — rare seven-staggered-apses choir, 77 capitals, UNESCO Compostela [[11]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/abbaye-benedictine-et-son-abbatiale-pcuaqu040010r9f0/) [[57]](https://www.chemins-compostelle.com/composante-du-bien/abbaye-saint-sever/). Vineyard pilgrimage (insider): **Château de Bachen**, Michel Guérard's own estate, by-appointment-only, Mon–Fri 10:00/15:00 [[22]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/chateau-de-bachen-vineyard/). Skip Pau (≥55 min) unless it's a Sunday or Monday when most Landes museums close [[74]](https://chateau-pau.fr/informations-pratiques).

## Bastides and old-stone walks

**[Geaune](https://www.guide-des-landes.com/en/experiences/culture-and-heritage/article-bastide-geaune-184.html)** — 1318 English bastide founded by Edward II's Genoese seneschal Antonio de Pessagne; 25-block grid, arcaded square, Augustinian tower, 15th-c. bell tower; walkable in an hour [[6]](https://www.guide-des-landes.com/en/experiences/culture-and-heritage/article-bastide-geaune-184.html). The Cave des Vignerons du Tursan has its seat here [[7]](https://www.landes-chalosse.com/geaune-du-vin-reconnu-et-une-bastide-historique-en-chalosse), with cooperative cellars open April–October [[42]](https://www.landes-chalosse.com/en/explore/unmissable-attractions/tursan-vineyards-and-potteries).

**[Pimbo](https://bastidesaquitaine.org/departement-40/pimbo/)** — the oldest bastide of the Landes, paréage 1268 between the abbot of Pimbo and a seneschal for English King Henry III [[8]](https://bastidesaquitaine.org/departement-40/pimbo/). The 12th-c. **collégiale Saint-Barthélemy** (foundation attributed to Charlemagne, 778) is three-naved, three-apsed Romanesque with a richly carved geometric portal, classified Monument Historique in 1998; free, open village, no ticketed hours — Sunday/Monday safe [[9]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/collegiale-de-pimbo-pcuaqu04001mjqab/) [[62]](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coll%C3%A9giale_Saint-Barth%C3%A9lemy_de_Pimbo) [[73]](https://www.guide-des-landes.com/en/experiences/culture-and-heritage/article-pimbo-fortified-town-landes-189.html). Climb to the **Jardins du Curé** for a Pyrenees panorama [[9]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/collegiale-de-pimbo-pcuaqu04001mjqab/).

**[Aire-sur-l'Adour](https://www.aire-sur-adour.fr/culture-et-traditions/histoire/eglise-sainte-quitterie/)** — the cathedral town (14 km, 17 min via D11 [[1]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/eugenie_les_bains-40320-landes-nouvelle_aquitaine-france-to-aire_sur_l_adour-40800-landes-nouvelle_aquitaine-france) [[66]](https://www.planderoute.net/de-aire-sur-adour-a-eugenie-les-bains-40.htm)) is anchored by **Sainte-Quitterie** — first listed monument of the Landes (1840), Gothic nave on an 11th–12th-c. chancel, restored crypt (2018) with a 4th-c. white-marble sarcophagus carved with Lazarus, Daniel and Baptism reliefs plus a miraculous fountain; UNESCO Compostela [[2]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/eglise-sainte-quitterie-pcuaqu04001oxgtr/) [[3]](https://www.aire-sur-adour.fr/culture-et-traditions/histoire/eglise-sainte-quitterie/) [[47]](https://re7-tourisme-aire-eugenie.ayaline.com/Je-suis-un-pelerin/Mon-etape-a-Aire-sur-l-Adour). Free 45-min guided tours run 1 Jul–14 Sep — Mon 16:00/17:00, Tue–Fri 10:00/11:00/16:00/17:00, Sat 10:00/11:00/15:00/16:00 — off-season by appointment [[4]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/Sites-et-musees/eglise-sainte-quitterie/848).

**[Grenade-sur-l'Adour](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/bastide-de-grenade-sur-ladour-pcuaqu040fs0009j/)** — 1322 bastide with a vast arcaded central square and 14th–15th-c. houses; the tourist office hands out a six-panel circuit booklet [[16]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/bastide-de-grenade-sur-ladour-pcuaqu040fs0009j/) [[18]](https://www.l-itineraire.com/de_eugenie-les-bains_a_grenade-sur-l-adour). The Gothic 15th-c. Saint-Pierre-et-Saint-Paul gained its twin-tower façade in 1833 [[17]](https://www.routard.com/fr/guide/france/aquitaine-bordelais-landes/landes/grenade-sur-l-adour).

**[Hagetmau](https://www.hagetmau.fr/Votre-ville/Patrimoine/La-crypte-de-Saint-Girons)** — at the 25 km edge. The 12th-c. **crypte de Saint-Girons**, all that survives of the abbey above town, holds four marble columns and 14 historiated Romanesque capitals classified Monument Historique; descend by two lateral wall-staircases; pamphlets in four languages [[14]](https://www.hagetmau.fr/Votre-ville/Patrimoine/La-crypte-de-Saint-Girons) [[15]](https://www.landes-chalosse.com/en/for-lovers-of-history-and-heritage/crypte-de-saint-girons-joyau-du-xiie-siecle-1091247). Open mid-June to mid-September, 14:30–18:30, closed Sundays, €3–4 (free under 10); outside that window only by Tourism Office appointment [[64]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/crypte-de-saint-girons-joyau-du-12eme-siecle-pcuaqu04001oc14w/) [[72]](https://www.crypte-st-girons.fr/en_GB/).

**[Labastide-d'Armagnac](https://www.tourisme-landesdarmagnac.fr/decouvrir/bastides-et-vieilles-pierres/les-bastides/labastide-darmagnac)** ⚠ at ~43 km it overshoots the brief by 13 km [[21]](https://www.calculerdistance.fr/eugenie-les-bains-et-labastide-darmagnac), but pairs the founded-1291 Place Royale (said to have inspired Henri IV's Place des Vosges) with the Bas-Armagnac écomusée — a viable full-day if the brief is flexible [[19]](https://www.tourisme-landesdarmagnac.fr/decouvrir/bastides-et-vieilles-pierres/les-bastides/labastide-darmagnac).

The pitch on each:

- **Château de Bachen** — Michel and Christine Guérard's own estate since 1983, first vintage 1987, late-18th-c. château over the Adour valley; flagship cuvées are the white *Baron de Bachen* (Sauvignon/Manseng/Baroque), red *Barocco*, rosé *Rosa la Rose*; book via `reservation@lespreseugenie.com` [[22]](https://lespresdeugenie.com/en/chateau-de-bachen-vineyard/).
- **Cave des Vignerons de Tursan** — 1958 cooperative grouping ~60 growers over 500 ha, AOP Tursan red/rosé/white plus IGP Landes; bottles €4.60–€7.65, curated wine boxes from €35.40 [[24]](https://www.tursan.fr/). Closer than the 15-minute drive suggests at ~7.7 km as the crow flies [[25]](https://www.verymap.net/distances/3016442-3019324/geaune/eugenie-les-bains/).
- **Vignoble Dulucq** — small family estate on a south-facing clay-limestone slope dotted with wild orchids, year-round by appointment with free tastings of dry, sweet and rosé Tursan [[26]](https://www.landes-chalosse.com/en/the-wines-of-tursan-and-armagnac/vignoble-dulucq-chateau-de-perchade-1044073).
- **Domaine d'Ognoas** — département-owned, 565 ha forest/vines/meadows, 7 centuries of history, the oldest functioning still in Gascogne (1804, Monument Historique); €2–€5 tastings cover vintage bas-armagnacs + Floc de Gascogne, on-site escape game and Terra Aventura geocaching [[29]](https://www.landes-chalosse.com/en/the-wines-of-tursan-and-armagnac/domaine-departemental-d-ognoas-4400391) [[30]](https://www.domaine-ognoas.com/).
- **Château Garreau / Écomusée de l'Armagnac** — 82-hectare estate, 17th-c. Musée du Vigneron, 10+ alembics, underground cellar, pond/botanical trails, on-site escape game; *Vigneron* visit €6, *Prince* visit €12, family €14 (2 adults + 3 children), Écomusée admission €4.50; 2025 Œnotourism Trophy winner [[27]](https://www.chateau-garreau.fr/fr/content/6-visite-degustation) [[28]](https://www.tourisme-landesdarmagnac.fr/voir-faire/sites-visiter/582419-ecomusee-de-larmagnac) [[71]](https://www.ecomusee-armagnac.fr/en_GB/).

## Outdoor — walks, ride, paddle, putt

**Hiking from the village door.** The tourist office maps three signposted loops out of Eugénie-les-Bains: **Blue 3.3 km**, **Green 6.6 km**, **Red 7.2 km**; plus the longer **Circuit de la Grave** (8 km) and **Circuit de la vallée du Bahus** (15.1 km) departing from the former station [[46]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/A-voir-a-faire/Pique-nique-et-Rando).

**Camino energy.** Aire-sur-l'Adour is a GR65 / Via Podiensis stage — 26 km from Nogaro, 17 km onward to Miramont-Sensacq, passing Sainte-Quitterie [[47]](https://re7-tourisme-aire-eugenie.ayaline.com/Je-suis-un-pelerin/Mon-etape-a-Aire-sur-l-Adour). Saint-Sever sits on the Voie de Vézelay (GR®654) [[57]](https://www.chemins-compostelle.com/composante-du-bien/abbaye-saint-sever/).

**Cycling.** The **[Scandibérique / EuroVelo 3](https://www.scandiberique.fr/)** is a 1,776 km waymarked route inaugurated in 2018, Maubeuge → Roncevaux [[48]](https://www.scandiberique.fr/). ⚠ Aire-sur-l'Adour is **not** on the marked main line — the official route runs Mont-de-Marsan → Tartas → Dax; a CycloTransEurope variant via Saint-Sever loops through Chalosse on a rustic former-railway greenway [[49]](https://eurovelo3.fr/eurovelo3/des-landes-au-pays-basque/mont-de-marsan-saint-sever/). That greenway is the **[Voie Verte de la Chalosse](https://www.landes-chalosse.com/en/to-see-and-do/for-nature-lovers/la-voie-verte-the-greenway)** — 34 km on the old Saint-Sever–Dax railway via Montfort, Montaut, Mugron [[50]](https://www.landes-chalosse.com/en/to-see-and-do/for-nature-lovers/la-voie-verte-the-greenway). ⚠ Surface is stabilised gravel, not paved — fine for hybrids/MTB, poor for road bikes; recommended start at Hinx or Saint-Sever (Augreilh); central Hauriet section closes during pigeon-hunting season [[51]](https://www.af3v.org/les-voies-vertes/voies/347-voie-verte-de-la-chalosse/).

**Paddle.** **[Canoë Kayak Aturin](https://ckaturin.wixsite.com/canoeairesuradour/location)** on the Adour at Aire-sur-l'Adour: kayak €15 half-day / €25 day; double canoe €30 / €45; SUP €10/h / €15/2h; season June → early September; 7 km / 2.5 h loop or descents up to 14 km; daily July–August, by appointment otherwise; swim-ready required [[52]](https://ckaturin.wixsite.com/canoeairesuradour/location) [[53]](https://www.landes-holidays.com/activite/canoe-kayak-aturin-ascaqu04001oxgvp/).

**Golf.** **[Les Greens d'Eugénie / Golf du Tursan](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/les-greens-eugenie-golf-du-tursan/920)** at Bahus-Soubiran — 2 km from the village; 9 holes, par 35, 2,786 m, slope 133/126; Jean Larrouy 1996; vineyard-lined fairways, a stream crossing; 12-station practice (6 covered), putting green, lessons, clubhouse with panoramic terrace; open daily 9:00–18:30 year-round [[54]](https://lecoingolf.fr/parcours-de-golf/golf-du-tursan-les-greens-deugenie/) [[55]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/les-greens-eugenie-golf-du-tursan/920).

**Horseback.** **[Domaine de Benquet](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/domaine-benquet/947)** at Le Houga (8 km past Aire, ~25 km from Eugénie, just into the Gers) — Cheval Qualité France-certified; pony baptisms from age 3, trail rides, vaulting, lessons; open daily [[56]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/domaine-benquet/947).

**The two essential cultural calls:**

- **Saint-Sever Abbey** is the regional heavy-hitter: founded c. late 10th c. by Duke William Sanche of Gascony, UNESCO-listed for Compostela, the rare seven-staggered-apses choir, 77 capitals, Romanesque tympanum [[11]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/abbaye-benedictine-et-son-abbatiale-pcuaqu040010r9f0/) [[57]](https://www.chemins-compostelle.com/composante-du-bien/abbaye-saint-sever/). Its 1028–1072 scriptorium produced the **Saint-Sever Beatus** — the only illustrated Beatus made north of the Pyrenees, whose Great Flood figure inspired Picasso's Guernica; original at the BnF, facsimile in the cloister Treasure Room (open through 31 Dec 2026) [[12]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/salle-du-tresor-de-labbaye-et-beatus-de-saint-sever-pcuaqu040fs0002k/) [[59]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Sever_Beatus). Free entry to the abbey-church; guided tours €3–5, or rent a tablet from the Tourist Office [[58]](https://www.landes-chalosse.com/en/for-lovers-of-history-and-heritage/abbaye-benedictine-et-son-abbatiale-1034822). The town's **Musée d'art et d'histoire du Cap de Gascogne** in the Jacobins convent (€4/€3, free under-10s) covers the Gallo-Roman Gleyzia villa and the Beatus [[63]](https://www.musee-saintsever.fr/).
- **Samadet faïence museum** preserves the 1732 royal manufactory legacy with 300+ decorated pieces and contemporary ceramics in the adjoining Maison de la Céramique; faïence-making demos run the second Sunday of each month, 15:30–16:30; €4.50 adult / €3.50 reduced; 14:00–18:00, 15 Feb–15 Nov, **closed Mondays** [[43]](https://www.landes.fr/musee-faience-samadet) [[60]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/musee-departemental-de-la-faience-et-des-arts-de-la-table-pcuaqu04001mjqd3/).

**Niche but punchy:** the **[Chapelle Notre-Dame des Cyclistes](https://www.notredamedescyclistes.net/)** at Labastide-d'Armagnac is the national sanctuary of cycling (consecrated 1959) with 700+ donated jerseys including Poulidor, Merckx, Ocaña, Virenque and Jeannie Longo [[20]](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapelle_Notre-Dame-des-Cyclistes) [[61]](https://www.notredamedescyclistes.net/). ⚠ ~34 km out and open **only** 1 May–15 Oct, Tue–Sun 15:00–18:00 [[70]](https://www.tourisme-landesdarmagnac.fr/voir-faire/sites-visiter/582420-chapelle-notre-dame-des-cyclistes).

## Sunday / Monday closure map

Rural Landes museums skew closed on Sundays and/or Mondays — the Samadet museum and Hagetmau crypt both close Mondays, the crypt also closes Sundays [[60]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/musee-departemental-de-la-faience-et-des-arts-de-la-table-pcuaqu04001mjqd3/) [[64]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/patrimoine-culturel/crypte-de-saint-girons-joyau-du-12eme-siecle-pcuaqu04001oc14w/). Saturday is the strongest day inside 30 km — markets fire, halles open, and most cellars/écomusées run. On a closed-museum Sunday or Monday, the **[Château de Pau](https://chateau-pau.fr/informations-pratiques)** (Henri IV's birthplace) is open daily 9:30–11:45 / 14:00–17:00 (closed only 25 Dec, 1 Jan, 1 May) — but at ~69 km / 55 min via A65 it falls outside the strict 30 km cordon [[65]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/Venir-chez-nous) [[69]](https://www.planderoute.net/de-pau-64-a-eugenie-les-bains-40.htm) [[74]](https://chateau-pau.fr/informations-pratiques). Pimbo (open village, free) and the Eugénie hiking loops always work [[73]](https://www.guide-des-landes.com/en/experiences/culture-and-heritage/article-pimbo-fortified-town-landes-189.html) [[46]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/A-voir-a-faire/Pique-nique-et-Rando).

## Practical numbers

Driving times confirmed from multiple sources: Aire 13.5 km / 17 min [[66]](https://www.planderoute.net/de-aire-sur-adour-a-eugenie-les-bains-40.htm), Geaune 8 km / 9 min [[5]](https://www.viamichelin.fr/itineraires/resultats/eugenie_les_bains-40320-landes-nouvelle_aquitaine-france-to-geaune-40320-landes-nouvelle_aquitaine-france) [[25]](https://www.verymap.net/distances/3016442-3019324/geaune/eugenie-les-bains/) [[10]](http://fr.distances-routieres.himmera.com/distance-eugenie_les_bains-geaune-voiture_km_itineraire-102457.html), Grenade 14 km / 16 min [[18]](https://www.l-itineraire.com/de_eugenie-les-bains_a_grenade-sur-l-adour), Saint-Sever 26 km / 29 min [[13]](https://www.l-itineraire.com/de_saint-sever_a_eugenie-les-bains), Hagetmau 25.5 km / 34 min [[67]](https://www.l-itineraire.com/de_hagetmau_a_eugenie-les-bains), Samadet via Hagetmau (~10 km Hagetmau–Samadet) [[68]](https://www.calculerdistance.fr/hagetmau-et-samadet), Arthez-d'Armagnac 24 km [[31]](https://www.viaouest.com/calcul-distance-france.php?v1=8940), Labastide-d'Armagnac 43 km / 47 min [[21]](https://www.calculerdistance.fr/eugenie-les-bains-et-labastide-darmagnac). Eugénie's tourist office consolidates the radius: Aire ~15 km, Grenade ~15 km, Mont-de-Marsan ~26 km, Pau ~58 km, with A65 fastest into Aire [[65]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/Venir-chez-nous).

**Family stops.** No zoo within 30 km — Zoo de Labenne is on the Atlantic coast, well outside the radius. Substitutes: a 900 m² mini-farm at Aire-sur-l'Adour with ~70 animals including rare ducks and Vietnamese pigs, and **La Forge animée** at Castelnau-Tursan [[75]](https://www.campings-landes-vacances.com/fr/visite-de-fermes-dans-les-landes-notre-top-10). Ferme Parlariou's afternoon tour (gavage + tasting, €6/€3) is the most kid-friendly producer visit [[40]](https://ferme-parlariou.fr/la-ferme/) [[41]](https://www.tourismelandes.com/degustation/ferme-parlariou-degaqu040017pcga/).

**Transport.** A car is effectively required: there is no marked Scandibérique passing through Aire [[49]](https://eurovelo3.fr/eurovelo3/des-landes-au-pays-basque/mont-de-marsan-saint-sever/), and rural Tursan/Chalosse stops sit off the regional train network — the A65 autoroute and D11/D944/D2 D-roads carry everything [[65]](https://www.tourisme-aire-eugenie.fr/Venir-chez-nous). Accessibility is not uniformly documented; the Hagetmau crypt has stair-only access via two lateral wall-staircases [[15]](https://www.landes-chalosse.com/en/for-lovers-of-history-and-heritage/crypte-de-saint-girons-joyau-du-xiie-siecle-1091247), and most Romanesque crypts in the region are similar — confirm with the relevant tourist office before booking if step-free access matters.
