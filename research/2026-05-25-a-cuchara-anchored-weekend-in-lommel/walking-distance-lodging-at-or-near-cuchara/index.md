> **TL;DR / Decision.** For a Saturday at [Cuchara](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/) **(2 MICHELIN Stars, 2026)**[[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/be-limburg/lommel/restaurant/cuchara) book **[Hotel Corbie Lommel](https://www.corbielommel.be/)** — Hertog Janplein 68, ~150 m from the restaurant door, and the hotel's own car park is literally Cuchara's recommended overflow parking[[1]](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/)[[4]](https://www.corbielommel.be/). If you want something smaller and quieter, **[Mi Casa Naranja](https://mi-casa-naranja.be/)** at Molsekiezel 24 is a 3-room boutique B&B, ~4 min walk to Cuchara, 4.9★ on TripAdvisor[[7]](https://mi-casa-naranja.be/)[[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641789-d25154526-Reviews-Mi_Casa_Naranja-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html). Cuchara also recommends **'t Wilgenhuys** (~2 km out, ⚠ taxi after dinner) and **Ars Vivendi** (~10 min drive, ✗ not walkable)[[1]](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/) — listed below for completeness.

## Why this is a short list

Cuchara sits at **Lepelstraat 3, 3920 Lommel** — a pedestrian-only street one block off the central Hertog Janplein, in a rear building of an old family house with a long black corridor as the entrance[[3]](https://www.visitlommel.be/content/detail/166/cuchara). The restaurant itself explicitly recommends three properties: Corbiehotel, B&B 't Wilgenhuys and B&B Ars Vivendi — only Corbie is tagged "walking distance" on Cuchara's own page[[1]](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/).

A genuine "walk back after the tasting menu" radius around Lepelstraat 3 is roughly **600 m / 10 min** on flat, lit streets. Mi Casa Naranja makes the cut on that criterion; Wilgenhuys (≈2 km, ≈25 min) is borderline and partly residential; Ars Vivendi (≈7 km in the Lommel-Werkplaatsen hamlet) doesn't[[12]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/nl/overnachten/t-wilgenhuys)[[13]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641789-d12683331-Reviews-B_B_Ars_Vivendi-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html). The town has no abundance of in-centre lodging — Visit Lommel's directory lists basically the two Corbie locations, a handful of B&Bs, and outlying apartments[[16]](https://www.visitlommel.be/plan-je-bezoek/overnachten) — so the table below is essentially exhaustive for "walk back to bed".

### [Hotel Corbie Lommel](https://www.corbielommel.be/) — the default pick

Hertog Janplein 68, 35 rooms and studios in a design-hotel format, sitting directly on the city-hall square[[4]](https://www.corbielommel.be/). Expedia clocks it at **0.2 km from Lommel centre** and 8.0/10 across 549 reviews[[6]](https://www.expedia.com/Lommel-Hotels-Corbie-Lommel.h3748406.Hotel-Information); TripAdvisor (smaller sample) is more lukewarm at 3.4★ but explicitly calls out "within walking distance of … Cuchara"[[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641789-d1550779-Reviews-Corbie_Hotel_Lommel-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html). Consistent praise across both: helpful multilingual staff, strong breakfast (fresh OJ, varied buffet), free parking on the square. Consistent gripes: thin walls, smallish rooms in need of a refresh, no in-house dinner restaurant. Standard rooms run **$196-$205** in recent reviews[[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641789-d1550779-Reviews-Corbie_Hotel_Lommel-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html). ⚠ Book the **"City" location** — there is a separate **Corbie Lommel Ring** on the ring road that is *not* walking distance to Cuchara[[16]](https://www.visitlommel.be/plan-je-bezoek/overnachten).

### [Mi Casa Naranja](https://mi-casa-naranja.be/) — the boutique pick

Molsekiezel 24, three luxury rooms tied to the Spanish restaurant Mi Media Naranja[[7]](https://mi-casa-naranja.be/). TripAdvisor scores **4.9★** across 8 reviews and pegs Cuchara at "approximately 4 minutes away"[[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641789-d25154526-Reviews-Mi_Casa_Naranja-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html). Pricing is friendly: **€110/night for 1 night, €100 for 2-3 nights, €90 for 4+**; the larger Sombrero room with kitchen and terrace is €120[[7]](https://mi-casa-naranja.be/). One asterisk: breakfast is **not included** — €20 extra if you want it[[7]](https://mi-casa-naranja.be/). Pet-friendly with cookies and blankets for the dog, free street parking, Nespresso/fridge/TV in every room. Aggregator listings show entry rates from €79[[9]](https://www.lodging-world.com/be/hotels/b&b-mi-casa-naranja-lommel-molsekiezel-24-in-lommel-99802629). Best fit for a couple who wants quiet and character over hotel amenities, and is OK with breakfasting at a café on the square.

### [B&B 't Wilgenhuys](https://www.wilgenhuys.be/) — Cuchara's reco, but plan a taxi

Jacob Smitsdreef 2, on the western edge of town next to the Bosland forest. Visit Limburg puts the centre **2 km away**[[12]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/nl/overnachten/t-wilgenhuys); the B&B's own site frames it as "only a few minutes from the centre"[[10]](https://www.wilgenhuys.be/) — which is true by bike or car, not on foot after a 4-hour tasting menu in November. 4 rooms, 4.9★ across 13 TripAdvisor reviews, Travellers' Choice award, $128-$148/night[[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641789-d21275488-Reviews-B_b_t_Wilgenhuys-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html). Breakfast in-room or on the terrace. **Use Taxi Flament (+32 475 86 53 10)** — listed by Cuchara itself[[1]](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/) — for the post-dinner return.

### [B&B Ars Vivendi](https://bb-arsvivendi-lommel.be/) — strictly not walkable

Werkplaatsen 47, three rooms in a 1904 engineer's villa inside the historic Lommel-Werkplaatsen workers' settlement, run by Ann and Guido since 2014[[14]](https://bb-arsvivendi-lommel.be/). Universally praised on TripAdvisor but the **10-minute drive** from centre puts it squarely in taxi-only territory for a Cuchara evening[[13]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641789-d12683331-Reviews-B_B_Ars_Vivendi-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html). Worth it if the rest of the weekend involves Lommel Sahara / Bosland and you have a car; not the smart pick if the dinner is the anchor.

### [Hotel Lommelhof](https://www.zenhotels.com/hotel/belgium/lommel/mid7464926/hotel_lommelhof/) — backup

Molsekiezel 95, 29 rooms, two-star, mid-7s scores; further down Molsekiezel than Mi Casa, so ~750 m to Cuchara. On-site restaurant and free breakfast included, ~US$177/night[[15]](https://www.zenhotels.com/hotel/belgium/lommel/mid7464926/hotel_lommelhof/). Pick this only if Corbie and Mi Casa are sold out — Mi Casa is on the same street, closer, better-reviewed, and cheaper.

## Practical notes for the Saturday-dinner scenario

- **Parking:** Lepelstraat is pedestrian-only. Cuchara directs guests to **Adelbergpark 1** first, **Hertog Janplein** if full[[1]](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/) — i.e. the Hotel Corbie square. If you've booked Corbie, you're already parked.
- **Taxi:** Cuchara lists **Taxi Flament +32 (0) 475 86 53 10**[[1]](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/). Useful for Wilgenhuys/Ars Vivendi (or anywhere off-centre after wine pairings).
- **Restaurant timing:** Cuchara serves Tue-Sat evenings only; dinner arrival is **18:30 or 19:00**[[1]](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/) — long enough for a 22:30-23:30 finish, so a walking-distance bed matters.
- **Tourist tax:** As of **1 January 2026**, Lommel charges a municipal tourist tax on room-based accommodation — surfaces as a small per-night line on your bill.
