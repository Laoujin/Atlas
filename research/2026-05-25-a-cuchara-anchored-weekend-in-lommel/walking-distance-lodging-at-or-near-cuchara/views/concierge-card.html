The Concierge's Verdict

Book Hotel Corbie if you want the door of your room to be one hundred and fifty metres from the door of the restaurant — and the hotel car park to be Cuchara's overflow parking. [1][4]

Book Mi Casa Naranja if you would rather trade twenty rooms for three, walk four minutes through quiet streets, and breakfast somewhere else. [7][8]

Everything else on this card — yes, even the two beds Cuchara itself recommends — is a taxi after the wine pairings.

Notes from the Front Desk

Parking

Lepelstraat is pedestrian. Navigate to Adelbergpark 1 first; Hertog Janplein (the Corbie square) if full. [1]

Taxi

Taxi Flament · +32 (0) 475 86 53 10 — Cuchara's own listed driver. Book on arrival for the 23:00–23:30 return. [1]

Service window

Cuchara serves Tuesday–Saturday evenings only. Arrivals 18:30 or 19:00; expect a 22:30–23:30 finish on a full tasting menu. [1]

Tourist tax

As of 1 January 2026, Lommel adds a small per-night municipal tourist tax to room-based stays — it surfaces as a line on the bill, not in the headline rate.

Two Corbies

Visit Lommel lists Corbie Lommel City and Corbie Lommel Ring as separate properties. Only City is walkable; the Ring sits on the bypass. Confirm before booking. [16]

The rear building

The dining room moved into the rear of the family house in 2015 — the long black corridor at Lepelstraat 3 is the way in, not a service door. [3]
