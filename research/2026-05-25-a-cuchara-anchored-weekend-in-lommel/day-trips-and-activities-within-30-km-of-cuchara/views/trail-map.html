Cycling Through the Trees

A free double-circle bridge at Kiefhoekstraat 16, Hechtel-Eksel — climbs in two looping ramps to 10 m, riding 700 m through the pine canopy. Opened June 2019. Sunrise to sunset. Travel blogger verdict: "utterly wonderful" [13].

Sahara Red Loop
▲
11.7 km sand · 25 km view ⌖ House of Nature

The full desert experience — dunes, lakes, heather and pine. Ends at the 30 m Reus van Bosland, 144 steps, 25 km panorama. Follow red triangle markers [14].

Sahara Children's Trail
●
3.1 km play forest ⚠ sand · no strollers

Willow-branch play elements and the natural-play forest. Kids only — strollers don't survive the sand [28].

In den Brand
⌂
2 · 4.4 · 5.5 km 3 loops ⌖ Hechtel-Eksel

Largest sandpit in Flanders, plus a 2-ha fenced off-leash dog park with separate small/large zones [19].

Pijnven Orange Loop
●
6.6 km easy ⌖ Pijnven · treehouse

Easy pine-forest stroll with a treehouse and access to Butterfly Valley — pairs naturally with a Cycling Through the Trees ride [20].

Kattenbos Heath
♣
9.1 km heather · deer · badger ⌖ Leyssensmolen

Purple heathland and pine forest, prime Aug–Sep for bloom. A 25 May visit catches young growth but no purple sea [17].

Hageven · De Plateaux
↔
1.38 → 9.69 km 8 loops ⌖ De Wulp, Neerpelt

600 ha cross-border reserve. The 1.38 km Hubert Lehaenpad is the wheelchair/stroller path; dogs welcome on lead [18].

SHEET · 06

Heritage Stops

abbey · mine · church · cemetery
21 KM · NE · ABBEY

Achelse Kluis

HAMONT-ACHEL · BE/NL BORDER

1.5-hour guided abbey tours Fri–Sun (Apr–Sep) at 11:00 & 14:00, €15 incl. 2-beer tasting. The monastery proper is off-limits; the brasserie pours draft 5° Blond & Bruin you cannot get anywhere else [39].

26 KM · SW · MINE

be-MINE Beringen

BERINGEN · FORMER COLLIERY

Flanders' largest preserved colliery — mining museum + underground simulation, plus Todi diving, Alpamayo climbing and Adventure Mountain in one site [44]. Daily 10–17; tours at 11/13/15.

⛪
SINT-PIETER 1388

Sint-Pietersbandenkerk

LOMMEL · IN-TOWN

1388 medieval tower, 32 Gustaaf Ladon stained-glass windows, 63-bell carillon, the oldest extant Clerinx organ in Belgium. Free, sunrise–sunset [51].

✝
KATTENBOS · 39 105

German War Cemetery

LOMMEL · KATTENBOS

Largest WWII cemetery in Western Europe — 16 ha, 39,105 graves including 6,400 unidentified soldiers and roughly 1,200 child soldiers. Free, daylight [49].

⊕
LEYSSENS · 1797

Leyssensmolen

LOMMEL · KATTENBOS

1797 wooden grain mill — restored 1988, grinding again. Open free 1st Sunday and 3rd Saturday of each month, 13:00–17:00 [22].

🍺
MARTENS 1621

Bocholter Brouwerijmuseum

BOCHOLT · 25 KM E

Europe's largest brewery museum at 4,000 m². ⚠ Individual visits only Jul–Aug Wed/Fri 13:30, reservation required, €10 [50].

13 KM · N · DESIGN

't Spectrum

BERGEIJK (NL) · 1941

Founded 1941; under Martin Visser from 1954 — he hired Friso Kramer, Constant Nieuwenhuys and Benno Premsela. The BR 02 sofa-bed is the icon [73].

19 KM · NE · MOTORSPORT

Eurocircuit Valkenswaard

VALKENSWAARD (NL)

Only permanent off-road race centre in NL — 1033 m, 60 % paved. The 2026 Belgian Rallycross R4 lands 20–21 June, not the 25 May weekend [79].

3 KM · W · DUNES

Lommelse Sahara

LOMMEL · SPEELPLEINSTRAAT

Free dune reserve at Speelpleinstraat 201 — off-leash dog meadow, willow-branch play forest and the Reus tower at the high point [15].

The Pour & Plate

stamps for tasting menus, draft pours, weekly market

Cuchara

Jan Tournier's two-Michelin-star kitchen [77], 17.5/20 G&M 2026 — the anchor. Late May overlaps Limburg white-asparagus peak (mid-Apr → 24 Jun) [65].

LEPELSTRAAT 3 · TUE–SAT · 18:30 / 19:00

Achelse Kluis Brasserie

Closed Mondays. Apr–Nov: Tue–Thu 10–20, Fri–Sun 10–22 (kitchen shuts an hour earlier). The 5° Bruin & Blond are draft-only here — bottled product elsewhere is the 8° [55].

DE KLUIS 1 · HAMONT-ACHEL · 21 KM

Bocholter Brouwerij Museum

Europe's largest brewery museum — 4,000 m² of industrial archaeology ending in a tasting room [57]. ⚠ Individual visits Jul–Aug only.

BOCHOLT · 25 KM E · €10 ADULT

Hertog Janplein Market

Lommel weekly market — Wed 13:30–17:30, two blocks from Cuchara. The right stop for local cheese and charcuterie before driving home [63].

Suggested Weekend

anchored on the Saturday-evening table
FRI · 16:00
Sahara + Tower

Check in, walk the Sahara red loop and climb the Reus van Bosland at golden hour [14].

SAT · 10:00
Ride the Bridge

The 38 km Through-the-Trees loop from junction 272, or the gentler 16.2 km Butterfly Valley Komoot loop. Lunch at Achelse Kluis brasserie with the draft 5° Blond [55].

SAT · 19:00
Cuchara

The anchor. Tasting menu finishes around 22:30–23:30 — late-May means Limburg white asparagus is on the table [65]. Walk back if you booked Corbie or Mi Casa Naranja; Taxi Flament otherwise.

SUN · MORNING
Pick One Branch

(a) be-MINE Beringen underground + climbing/diving. (b) Hageven-De Plateaux cross-border walk + lunch in Pelt. (c) Push the rim — half-day in Eindhoven for Van Abbe + Philips [82].
