> **TL;DR / Decision.** Inside 30 km of [Cuchara](https://guide.michelin.com/en/be-limburg/lommel/restaurant/cuchara) (Lepelstraat 3, Lommel) [[78]](https://www.gaultmillau.be/en/restaurants/cuchara-lommel) — Jan Tournier's two-Michelin-star kitchen [[76]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/be-limburg/lommel/restaurant/cuchara) [[77]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/cuines-33-and-the-jane-awarded-two-stars-in-the-michelin-guide-belgium-and-luxembourg-2026) — you have one world-class outdoor act: **[Cycling Through the Trees](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/cycling-through-the-trees)** at Pijnven, 12 km away [[1]](https://www.visitlommel.be/en/content/detail/7/cycling-through-the-trees). Plus the **[Lommelse Sahara](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/hiking-sahara)** dunes + Reus van Bosland tower [[15]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/hiking-sahara) [[27]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g641789-d6560030-Reviews-Uitkijktoren_Lommelse_Sahara-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html), and the **[Achelse Kluis](https://achelsekluis.be/en/guided-tours/)** abbey-café on the Dutch border [[39]](https://achelsekluis.be/en/guided-tours/). Cross-border: a quick 13 km hop to **[Bergeijk](https://www.spectrumdesign.nl/en/about-spectrum/)** for the 't Spectrum design heritage [[85]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Lommel/Bergeijk) [[73]](https://www.spectrumdesign.nl/en/about-spectrum/), or 19 km to **[Valkenswaard](https://www.valkenswaard.nl/eurocircuit)** [[84]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Lommel/Valkenswaard). Honest skip-list: **Cycling Through Water** (Bokrijk, 44 km [[5]](https://routiq.com/nl/route/5244686/lommel-bokrijk-(fietsen-door-het-water))), **Hoge Kempen** (56 km [[25]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Lommel/Maasmechelen)), **Hertog Jan Arcen** brewery (80 km [[81]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Lommel/Arcen)), **Adventure Parc Warredal** (Maaseik 45 km [[88]](http://nl.afstand-meten.himmera.com/afstand_tussen-maaseik-lommel-meten_routeplanner_wegenkaart_km-234889.html)) and **Beekse Bergen safari** (54 km [[75]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Lommel/Safaripark-Beekse-Bergen)). Eindhoven sits at the threshold — 30 km road to the southern edge, 47 km to the city centre [[82]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Lommel/Eindhoven) [[83]](https://wanderlog.com/drive/between/14964/9985/lommel-to-eindhoven-drive).

## Cycling — the headline act

Cuchara sits inside **Nationaal Park Bosland**, the largest forest in Flanders, and at the centre of the **Limburg fietsroutenetwerk** — 1,800+ km of signposted routes connected by numbered *knooppunten* on eye-level blue signs, a system invented locally by mine engineer Hugo Bollen after the coal pits closed in the 1980s [[7]](https://www.fietsroute.org/fietsroutenetwerken.html). One travel blogger's verdict on the bridge: "utterly wonderful" [[13]](https://notesfromcamelidcountry.net/2024/05/29/cycling-through-the-trees-in-bosland-national-park/).

**Cycling Through the Trees** is the one you came for: a free double-circle bridge at Kiefhoekstraat 16, Hechtel-Eksel, climbing 3-4% over 700 m to 10 m height through the pine canopy, opened June 2019, accessible to bikes, walkers and wheelchairs, sunrise to sunset [[2]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/cycling-through-the-trees) [[26]](https://www.nationaalparkbosland.be/ontdek/fietsen/fietsen-door-de-bomen/83/). Free parking on site.

### Other riding worth doing

- **Sahara child-friendly loop** — 16.5 km paved with 15 m elevation gain, nodes 224-230-231-232-260-224 from Parking Soeverein, passing the [House of Nature](https://www.visitlommel.be/en/content/detail/129/child-friendly-bicycle-route-around-the-sahara) sculpture (7.5 m × 50 m), the Bocholt-Herentals canal pedestrian bridge, Blauwe Kei and the desert/blue lake [[8]](https://www.visitlommel.be/en/content/detail/129/child-friendly-bicycle-route-around-the-sahara).
- **Mountainbikenetwerk Noord-Limburg** — 270 km of signposted MTB across Bosland with five combinable Lommel loops (Kattenbos, Sahara, Heuvelse Heide) [[9]](https://www.nationaalparkbosland.be/ontdek/mountainbiken/5/).
- **[BoslandBike 2026](https://www.boslandbike.be/)** — gravel/MTB festival Sun 26 April 2026 at Forestique (Witherendreef 1, Pelt). 100 km gravel/MTB €39.50, 10/25/50 km family routes €5 [[10]](https://www.boslandbike.be/). ⚠ For a 25 May visit the 2026 edition has already passed — flag for the 2027 calendar.

## Walking — Sahara and the rest of Bosland

The **Lommelse Sahara** is the anchor: free entry at Speelpleinstraat 201, with an off-leash dog meadow, a willow-branch play forest, and the 30 m **Reus van Bosland** observation tower (144 steps, 25 km panorama, free, 24/7) [[15]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/hiking-sahara) [[27]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g641789-d6560030-Reviews-Uitkijktoren_Lommelse_Sahara-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html). The tower is ranked #1 of 24 things to do in Lommel on TripAdvisor (4.6/5, 253 reviews) [[27]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g641789-d6560030-Reviews-Uitkijktoren_Lommelse_Sahara-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html). Bosland's *wandelnetwerk* runs 70%+ on quiet sand tracks [[16]](https://www.nationaalparkbosland.be/ontdek/wandelen/4/).

The 1797 **[Leyssensmolen](https://www.visitlommel.be/en/content/detail/132/leyssens-mill)** wooden grain mill at the Kattenbos trailhead grinds again after a 1988 restoration; open free 1st Sunday and 3rd Saturday of each month, 13:00-17:00 [[22]](https://www.visitlommel.be/en/content/detail/132/leyssens-mill). For peak heather, target mid-August to mid-September [[17]](https://www.visitlommel.be/en/4-times-enjoying-the-blooming-heath) — a 25 May visit catches young growth but no bloom. Quirky add: **Klankenbos** in Pelt is an interactive sound-art forest with sculptures by European artists [[23]](https://www.thecrazytourist.com/15-best-things-to-do-in-lommel-belgium/).

The **Achelse Kluis** is the cultural centrepiece of the radius. The abbey lost its Trappist designation in January 2021 when the last two monks moved to Westmalle; Belgian entrepreneur Jan Torman later bought the abbey and beer continues to be produced — just not labelled Trappist [[56]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achel_Brewery). The monastery itself is closed, but the café-brasserie pours the abbey beers in a beautifully restored complex on the Dutch border [[40]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/de-achelse-kluis-hidden-abbey-treasure-gates-nature-park-de-groote-heide) [[74]](https://www.gettingstamped.com/visiting-achel-trappist-brewery/). **be-MINE Beringen** sits right at the 26 km mark [[45]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Lommel/Beringen-Belgium) and stacks a mining museum + underground simulation with the Todi diving centre, Alpamayo climbing wall and Adventure Mountain in one site [[43]](https://be-mine.be/en/experience/).

Beer pilgrimages:

- **Achelse Kluis brasserie** — closed Mondays; summer (1 Apr → 30 Nov) Tue-Thu 10:00-20:00, Fri-Sun 10:00-22:00, kitchen shuts an hour earlier [[54]](https://achelsekluis.be/en/brasserie/). The **5° Bruin** and **5° Blond** are *draft-only* here — the bottled product elsewhere is the 8° [[55]](https://www.beertourism.com/blogs/breweries/brouwerij-der-sint-benedictusabdij-de-achelse-kluis). That makes the café a genuine "you can only drink this here" pour.
- **Bocholter Brouwerijmuseum** (Brouwerij Martens) — Europe's largest brewery museum at 4,000 m², ends in a tasting room [[57]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/bocholter-brouwerij-museum). ⚠ Individual visits only July-August Wed/Fri 13:30, reservation required [[50]](https://www.bocholterbrouwerijmuseum.be/).

⚠ Note on **Hertog Jan Brouwerij in Arcen (NL)**: often suggested as a Limburg beer day-trip, but it's 79.7 km by road from Lommel — well outside the 30 km radius [[81]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Lommel/Arcen). Don't anchor a day around it from Cuchara.

Seasonal: late May falls in **peak Limburg white asparagus** ("white gold", mid-April → 24 June), so farm shops and seasonal menus on both sides of the border feature it [[65]](https://www.hartvanlimburg.nl/en/asparagus). The **Lommel weekly market** sets up Wed 13:30-17:30 on Hertog Janplein — two blocks from Cuchara — and is the right place for local cheese and charcuterie before driving home [[63]](https://www.lommel.be/hertog-janplein-wekelijkse-markt).

**Eindhoven, honestly.** Rome2Rio puts the road distance at 30.2 km to where the route first enters the urban area [[82]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Lommel/Eindhoven); Wanderlog returns 47 km for a centre-to-centre drive [[83]](https://wanderlog.com/drive/between/14964/9985/lommel-to-eindhoven-drive). So Eindhoven city centre is *just* over a strict 30 km circle. If you're willing to stretch slightly, the cluster is strong: **[Van Abbemuseum](https://vanabbemuseum.nl/en/visit/opening-hours-and-admission/)** (modern/contemporary, Tue-Sun 11-17, €16, free Tue 15-17, under-13 free [[46]](https://vanabbemuseum.nl/en/visit/opening-hours-and-admission/)), the **[Philips Museum](https://www.philips.nl/en/a-w/philips-museum/visit/practical-information.html)** on Gerard Philips' 1891 light-bulb workshop [[66]](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/the-netherlands/eindhoven/attractions/philips-museum/a/poi-sig/1596765/1003896) (Tue-Sun 11-17, €12.50/€6, free Tue 15:30 [[67]](https://dutchreview.com/traveling/cities/eindhoven/things-to-do-in-eindhoven/)), the reopened **[Evoluon — Next Nature Museum](https://nextnature.org/en/museum/expo/retrofuture)** with the immersive RetroFuture exhibition across ten tunnels [[48]](https://nextnature.org/en/museum/expo/retrofuture) (Wed-Sun 11-17, €16/€8 [[67]](https://dutchreview.com/traveling/cities/eindhoven/things-to-do-in-eindhoven/)), and the **[Strijp-S](https://www.thisiseindhoven.com/en/see-and-do/dynamic-districts/strijp-s)** repurposed Philips factory zone — design studios, MU Hybrid Art House, Area 51 skatepark, food trucks [[68]](https://www.thisiseindhoven.com/en/see-and-do/dynamic-districts/strijp-s).

## A suggested 30 km weekend pivot (anchored on a Saturday-evening Cuchara dinner)

- **Friday afternoon arrival** — check in, walk the **Sahara** + climb **Reus van Bosland** before dinner [[14]](https://www.visitflanders.com/en/flandersfinesthikingroutes/hiking-bosland-national-park-sahara-walk) [[27]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g641789-d6560030-Reviews-Uitkijktoren_Lommelse_Sahara-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html).
- **Saturday daytime** — ride the **38 km Cycling Through Trees loop from junction 272** [[3]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/route/cycling-through-trees-bosland-national-park), or rent bikes and take the gentler 16.2 km Komoot Butterfly Valley loop [[11]](https://www.komoot.com/guide/661816/cycling-around-hechtel-eksel). Lunch at the **Achelse Kluis brasserie** with a draft 5° Blond [[54]](https://achelsekluis.be/en/brasserie/) [[55]](https://www.beertourism.com/blogs/breweries/brouwerij-der-sint-benedictusabdij-de-achelse-kluis).
- **Saturday evening** — Cuchara [[76]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/be-limburg/lommel/restaurant/cuchara).
- **Sunday** — pick one: (a) **be-MINE Beringen** for the underground simulation + climbing/diving [[44]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/be-mine); (b) **Hageven-De Plateaux** cross-border walk + lunch in Pelt [[18]](https://www.natuurpunt.be/natuurgebieden/hageven-de-plateaux); (c) push the radius and do an **Eindhoven** half-day (Van Abbe + Philips, walkable in the centre) [[46]](https://vanabbemuseum.nl/en/visit/opening-hours-and-admission/) [[47]](https://www.philips.nl/en/a-w/philips-museum/visit/practical-information.html). Asparagus on every menu in late May [[65]](https://www.hartvanlimburg.nl/en/asparagus).
