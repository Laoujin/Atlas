> **Decision:**
> - **Want owner-curated art and the most distinctive house — pick [B&B Ars Vivendi](https://bb-arsvivendi-lommel.be/)** [[3]](https://bb-arsvivendi-lommel.be/) [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641789-d12683331-Reviews-B_B_Ars_Vivendi-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html).
> - **Want the freshest rooms and a polished "boutique hotel" feel — pick [Eikenhoef](https://www.eikenhoef.be/)** [[7]](https://www.eikenhoef.be/) [[9]](https://www.booking.com/reviews/be/hotel/eikenhoef.html).
> - **Want forest on the doorstep with the Lommelse Sahara out the back door — pick [B&B 't Wilgenhuys](https://www.wilgenhuys.be/)** [[5]](https://www.wilgenhuys.be/) [[6]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/39-t-wilgenhuys.html).
>
> All three are 2–5 km from Cuchara, well inside a single taxi hop. Cuchara itself partners with Taxi Flament (+32 475 86 53 10) — quote the restaurant when you book [[1]](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/). Lommel taxi rates are ~€4.90 base + €1.90/km, so any of these is well under €25 one-way [[17]](https://www.gobytaxi.com/europe/belgium/limburg/lommel).

## Cuchara, the anchor

Two-Michelin-star Cuchara sits at **Lepelstraat 3, 3920 Lommel** in a pedestrian street in the town centre; chef Jan Tournier opened it in his grandparents' house in 2009 (first star 2014, second 2019) [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/be-limburg/lommel/restaurant/cuchara). The restaurant's own contact page names exactly three lodging partners — **Corbiehotel** (walkable, in the town centre), **B&B 't Wilgenhuys** and **B&B Ars Vivendi** [[1]](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/). That list is a useful baseline: each is genuinely close, and Cuchara has worked with them. **Eikenhoef** isn't on it (it only opened summer 2023, after the list was last updated [[7]](https://www.eikenhoef.be/)) but belongs in the conversation.

## The picks in detail

### [B&B Ars Vivendi](https://bb-arsvivendi-lommel.be/) — the character pick
Ann and Guido have run a three-room B&B since 2014 inside their 1904 engineer's villa at **Werkplaatsen 47** — the property sits within the **Lommel-Werkplaatsen garden city**, a heritage-listed neighbourhood of ~75 workers' and managers' houses laid out around the former zinc factory between 1904 and 1914 [[16]](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lommel-Werkplaatsen) [[3]](https://bb-arsvivendi-lommel.be/). All rooms are renovated with rainfall-shower en-suites; breakfast in the garden under century-old beeches and chestnuts when weather allows [[3]](https://bb-arsvivendi-lommel.be/). TripAdvisor ranks it #1 specialty lodging in Lommel at 5.0/5; one reviewer's line — "From the moment you turn into the drive you know this won't be an ordinary weekend away" — captures the appeal: it's a personal house with the owners' art and antique collections, not a hotel [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641789-d12683331-Reviews-B_B_Ars_Vivendi-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html). Caveat: only three rooms, so book early.

### [Eikenhoef](https://www.eikenhoef.be/) — the boutique pick
Opened **summer 2023** at **Knapenhoef 42**, in coniferous forest on cycling-junction 264/265, run hands-on by its young owners [[7]](https://www.eikenhoef.be/) [[8]](https://www.eikenhoef.be/praktisch). 4–7 rooms (sources differ — small either way) — doubles, family and quadruple rooms with private bathrooms, air-conditioning, balconies and garden views [[10]](https://eikenhoef.belgiumhotel24.com/en/). 9.4/10 across 275 Booking reviews, with consistent praise for breakfast, spacious modern rooms and the hosts; ⚠ one reviewer flagged the "Luxury Boutique" naming as overstated for what are still relatively simple rooms — calibrate expectations against the price tag [[9]](https://www.booking.com/reviews/be/hotel/eikenhoef.html). Bike storage with charging on site, free bikes for guests [[8]](https://www.eikenhoef.be/praktisch). Visit Lommel formally categorises it as a boutique hotel [[18]](https://www.visitlommel.be/content/detail/374/boutique-hotel-eikenhoef).

### [B&B 't Wilgenhuys](https://www.wilgenhuys.be/) — the Sahara pick
At **Jacob Smitsdreef 2**, ~2 km north of Cuchara and **25 metres from the Lommelse Sahara** cycling network, with a 9.6 Booking score [[5]](https://www.wilgenhuys.be/) [[6]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/39-t-wilgenhuys.html). Four rooms total: three ground-floor doubles, plus one larger upstairs suite with its own balcony, fridge and coffee corner that sleeps up to four [[5]](https://www.wilgenhuys.be/). The owners describe the interior as "a lively mix of vintage, Scandinavian, urban and classical elements" — modern B&B aesthetic rather than heritage-villa character [[5]](https://www.wilgenhuys.be/). Petanque court and built-in trampoline in the garden [[6]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/39-t-wilgenhuys.html). Best fit if you're combining Cuchara with hiking/cycling the Sahara dunes on Sunday morning.

### [Corbie Hotel Lommel](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641789-d1550779-Reviews-Corbie_Hotel_Lommel-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html) — the no-taxi option
The only **walkable** option — central, named directly by Cuchara as a partner [[1]](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/). 8.0/10 across 549 TripAdvisor reviews; reviewers consistently praise breakfast and multilingual staff, but call out small dated rooms, occasional maintenance issues and street noise [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g641789-d1550779-Reviews-Corbie_Hotel_Lommel-Lommel_Limburg_Province.html). Choose this only if walking home after the tasting menu matters more than character — otherwise the B&Bs win on every other axis and a return taxi is €13–€24.

## The nearby-but-different options

### [B&B Hoeven Het Gehucht](https://hetgehucht.be/) — the working-farm pick
Working hobby/children's farm with vegetable and fruit cultivation in a 1957 farmhouse at **Kortestraat 1, 3900 Pelt (Lindelhoeven)**, run by Rudy and Tania since 2006 — ~10 km from Cuchara, well inside taxi range [[12]](https://hetgehucht.be/). Sits on the edge of Bosland national park. Different proposition: this is hospitality combined with a working farm, not a romantic-weekend setup — pick it if you've got kids in tow.

### [Natuur-like Glamping Bosland](https://glamping-bosland.be/) — the wildcard
Bell tents on wooden platforms next to a pond inside Bosland national park, at Kievitsdriesen (Lindelhoeven, Pelt), ~10 km from Cuchara [[13]](https://glamping-bosland.be/). 8.6 on Booking; tents sleep up to three, **compost toilet, no running water in the tent, gas-fire cooking** — proper glamping, not luxury safari tent [[14]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/natuur-like.html). ⚠ Only sane in summer/dry weather, and the contrast with a two-star tasting menu the same evening is the whole point of considering it.

## Honourable mention out of taxi range

[B&B Huis Spaas](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/b-amp-b-huis-spaas.html) in Sint-Huibrechts-Lille (Pelt) is a 9.3-rated recently renovated B&B in a historic building [[15]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/b-amp-b-huis-spaas.html), but it's ~12 km from Cuchara so the taxi cost climbs toward €30 each way — fine, but at that point the three core Lommel B&Bs are cheaper, closer and at least as characterful. Larger Limburg destinations like La Butte aux Bois (Lanaken) are well outside taxi range from Lommel and not realistic for a Cuchara-anchored stay.

## Booking logistics

- Book the room first, then book Cuchara, then ring **Taxi Flament +32 475 86 53 10** the day-of and say you're going to Cuchara — they know the drill [[1]](https://cuchara.be/restaurant/).
- Lommel taxis: ~€4.90 base + €1.90/km; 5 km is ~€13, 10 km is ~€24 [[17]](https://www.gobytaxi.com/europe/belgium/limburg/lommel). Budget €25–€30 round-trip from any of the listed stays.
- The three small B&Bs all have 3–4 rooms each and Cuchara is a destination restaurant — same booking-pressure problem. For a Saturday in season, lock the room as soon as you have the dinner reservation, not after.
