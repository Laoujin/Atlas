Six properties — a 1904 engineer's villa, a Sahara-edge B&B, a forest-fringe boutique opened in 2023, a 1957 working farm, a glamping tent on a pond, and one polite mention — every one of them a short ride from Cuchara's 22:30 cab call.
The Anchor · Lepelstraat 3, 3920 Lommel

Cuchara, two stars.

Jan Tournier opened Cuchara in 2009 in his grandparents' Lommel house — first Michelin star in 2014, second in 2019 [2]. The restaurant's own contact page names exactly three lodging partners. This lookbook adds three more.

The character pick · Werkplaatsen 47

Ars Vivendi

A 1904 engineer's villa, three rooms, owners' art
anno 1904 heritage garden city owner-curated art three rooms

Ann and Guido have run this three-room B&B since 2014 inside their authentic engineer's villa, set within the Lommel-Werkplaatsen garden city — ~75 listed workers' and managers' houses laid out between 1904 and 1914 around the former zinc factory [16]. Rooms are renovated with rainfall-shower en-suites; breakfast in the garden under century-old beeches when weather allows [3]. "From the moment you turn into the drive you know this won't be an ordinary weekend away."[4]

The boutique pick · Knapenhoef 42

Eikenhoef

Newest rooms in the radius; coniferous forest on the doorstep
est. 2023 luxury boutique cycling junction 264/265 bike storage + charging

Opened summer 2023 between cycling junctions 264 and 265, run hands-on by its young owners [7]. Four to seven rooms (sources vary — small either way), with private bathrooms, air-conditioning, balconies and garden views [10]. 9.4/10 across 275 Booking reviews — consistent praise for breakfast, spacious modern rooms and the hosts [9]. Visit Lommel categorises it as a boutique hotel [18]. ⚠ One reviewer flagged the "Luxury Boutique" naming as overstated — calibrate against the price tag.

The Sahara pick · Jacob Smitsdreef 2

't Wilgenhuys

Vintage-Scandi B&B, 25 metres from the dunes
vintage / scandi sahara edge four rooms petanque + trampoline

~2 km north of Cuchara and 25 metres from the Lommelse Sahara cycling network [5]. Four rooms total: three ground-floor doubles, plus one upstairs suite with balcony, fridge and coffee corner that sleeps up to four [6]. The owners describe the interior as "a lively mix of vintage, Scandinavian, urban and classical elements"[5] — modern B&B aesthetic rather than heritage-villa character. 9.6 on Booking. Petanque court and a built-in trampoline in the garden.

The no-taxi option · Lommel town centre

Corbie Hotel

Central, walkable, named directly by Cuchara as a partner
central business walkable 50 rooms dated

The only walkable option in this lookbook — central, named directly by Cuchara as a partner [1]. 8.0/10 across 549 TripAdvisor reviews; reviewers consistently praise breakfast and multilingual staff, but flag small, dated rooms, occasional maintenance issues and street noise [11]. Choose this only if walking home after the tasting menu matters more than character — otherwise the B&Bs win on every other axis and a return taxi runs €13–€24.

The working-farm pick · Kortestraat 1, Pelt

Hoeven Het Gehucht

A 1957 hobby-and-children's farm on the Bosland edge
anno 1957 working farm vegetable + fruit cultivation bosland edge

A hobby- and children's farm with vegetable and fruit cultivation in a 1957 farmhouse at Kortestraat 1, 3900 Pelt (Lindelhoeven), run by Rudy and Tania Mertens-Van Leuven since 2006 [12]. ~10 km from Cuchara — well inside taxi range. Sits on the edge of Bosland national park, which connects to the activities map in the day-trips survey. A different proposition altogether: this is hospitality wrapped around a working farm, not a romantic-weekend setup. Best for travelling with kids.

The wildcard · Kievitsdriesen, Pelt

Natuur-like

glamping national park compost toilet gas-fire cooking summer only

Bell tents on wooden platforms next to a pond inside Bosland national park, ~10 km from Cuchara [13]. 8.6 on Booking; tents sleep up to three, compost toilet, no running water in the tent, gas-fire cooking — proper glamping, not luxury safari tent [14]. ⚠ Only sane in summer and dry weather. The contrast with a two-star tasting menu the same evening is the whole point of considering it.

Beyond the taxi belt

An honourable mention.

Outside the one-taxi-hop radius, but worth a footnote.
~12 km · taxi creeps toward €30 each way

B&B Huis Spaas · Sint-Huibrechts-Lille

A recently renovated B&B in a historic building, 9.3/10 for couples on Booking [15]. At ~12 km from Cuchara, taxi rises into the €30-each-way bracket — at which point the three core Lommel B&Bs are cheaper, closer and at least as characterful. La Butte aux Bois (Lanaken) is well outside taxi range and not realistic for a Cuchara-anchored stay.

The mechanics

How to actually book this.

i.
First

Book the room. The three core B&Bs each have 3–4 rooms — same booking pressure as Cuchara itself [3].

ii.
Then

Book Cuchara. Tue–Sat service; arrivals at 18:30 or 19:00. For a Saturday in season, lock both the room and the table as soon as you have either.

iii.
Day of

Ring Taxi Flament. Say you're going to Cuchara — they know the drill. Round-trip from any listed stay sits between €18 and €48 [17].
