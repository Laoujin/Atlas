The whole weekend pivots on one fixed point: a 19:30–20:00 seating, Thu–Sun only, at [Les Morainières](https://www.les-morainieres.com/fr/) — promoted to three Michelin stars in March 2026 [[1]](https://www.tendancehotellerie.fr/articles-breves/communique-de-presse/25437-article/les-morainieres-a-jongieux-savoie-decroche-trois-etoiles-dans-la-nouvelle-selection-du-guide-michelin-france-monaco-2026), with a €240 tasting menu plus wine pairings on a hillside reached by an unlit vine-terraced road [[2]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1926328-d1903848-Reviews-Restaurant_Les_Morainieres-Jongieux_Savoie_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html). Book the table *first*, weeks ahead; every other decision below follows from the date you get.

**The lodging children disagree, and the disagreement is the point.** Under the literal walking-distance constraint, the winners are [Domaine Carrel & Senger](https://domainecarreletsenger.com/) (1.42 km / 19 min, 4.9/5 Tripadvisor, working winery since 1830 [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1926328-d33419203-Reviews-Domaine_Carrel_Senger-Jongieux_Savoie_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html)) and the budget [Les Chambres du Cru](https://edmondjacquin.com/chambre-dhotes/) at €80–€85 [[4]](https://www.gites-de-france-savoie.com/Hebergements/chambre-d-hotes/les-chambres-du-cru-73G140600.html). Under the *spirit* of "no driving after dinner", the cleanest pick is the chef's own [Maison des Morainières](https://www.les-morainieres.com/fr/chambres.html) — 4.6 km away but with a free door-to-door shuttle for every service and a packaged Séjour Découverte (room + tasting menu + wine + breakfast for €880/two) [[5]](https://www.les-morainieres.com/fr/offres-speciales/sejour-decouverte.html). Both children converge on [Château de la Mar](https://chateaudelamar.fr/) as the strongest hybrid — 1.6 km walk, 17th-c. château with spa, and its on-site bistro [La Table 1625](https://chateaudelamar.fr/restaurant-la-table-1625/) is run by Arnoult himself at €35 menus [[6]](https://chateaudelamar.fr/restaurant-la-table-1625/), turning Friday night into a chef warm-up before Saturday's main event. Even the walking-distance child notes most diners ask for the shuttle on the return anyway [[2]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1926328-d1903848-Reviews-Restaurant_Les_Morainieres-Jongieux_Savoie_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html).

**The 30 km bubble is small enough that lodging villages double as day-trip villages.** Chanaz appears as both a possible base ([Le Doux Nid](https://le-doux-nid.com/en/) in an 18th-c. coaching inn) *and* a Friday-arrival destination — Bateaucanal cruises run daily 4 Apr–25 Oct 2026 [[7]](https://www.bateaucanal.com/nos-croisieres/) and the [Moulin de Chanaz](https://www.moulindechanaz.com/) walnut-oil mill is Historic-Monument-listed [[8]](https://www.moulindechanaz.com/). Yenne similarly: a winter weekend rules out [Logis Le Fer à Cheval](https://www.hotel-restaurant-leferacheval.com/) (open Apr–Oct only [[9]](https://www.cybevasion.fr/logis-hotel-du-fer-a-cheval-yenne-h2668803.html)) but keeps the Thursday market and Notre-Dame-de-l'Assomption [[10]](https://www.savoie-mont-blanc.com/en/visits-and-cultural-sites/listed-church-in-yenne-118970/).

**Time-of-year blockers stack up.** The Musée Faure in Aix-les-Bains is closed for all of 2026 [[11]](https://www.aixlesbains-rivieradesalpes.com/en/patrimoine-culturel/faure-museum-closed-aix-les-bains/); Mont Granier opens 1 May–30 Nov only [[12]](https://www.chartreuse-tourisme.com/en/offers/le-mont-granier-hike-entremont-le-vieux-en-2777360/); the Hautecombe Royal Apartments tour runs Wed–Sun [[13]](https://hautecombe.chemin-neuf.fr/). The single date-magnet within the bubble is the **3rd Festival du Cru Jongieux on 18–19 July 2026**, with food pairings by Arnoult himself [[14]](https://magazine-exquis.com/festival-cru-jongieux-2026-accords-mets-vins/) — if a date can be moved, that weekend doubles the food angle for €80–€90.

**The tech-events angle came back empty, honestly.** Inside 30 km, 2026 offers one free 90-min USMB cybersecurity lecture on Tue 21 April [[15]](https://www.grandchambery.fr/tous-les-evenements/amphis-pour-tous-et-pour-toutes-cybersecurite-comment-les-mathematiques-veillent-sur-vos-donnees) and the SOPHIA PV workshop the same week (already full [[16]](https://www.ines-solaire.org/en/agenda/sophia-pv-reliability-workshop-2026/)) — neither on a Saturday. The realistic stack is DevFest Lyon, Friday 4 December 2026, ~1 h drive from Jongieux [[17]](https://devfest.gdglyon.com/), with Saturday at Les Morainières and a Sunday return through the Bugey.

Château de la Mar: +33 4 79 96 09 84. Carrel & Senger: +33 4 79 44 00 20. Chambres du Cru: Gîtes de France Savoie 04 58 14 12 12. Lead time: assume 6–10 weeks for the dinner now that the third star has landed [[1]](https://www.tendancehotellerie.fr/articles-breves/communique-de-presse/25437-article/les-morainieres-a-jongieux-savoie-decroche-trois-etoiles-dans-la-nouvelle-selection-du-guide-michelin-france-monaco-2026); lodging follows once the date is confirmed.

The unresolved call: does the literal "walk back" promise matter more than a door-to-door shuttle that achieves the same no-driving outcome? If it does, Carrel & Senger; if not, the chef's Maison des Morainières with the Séjour Découverte is the single-button trip.
