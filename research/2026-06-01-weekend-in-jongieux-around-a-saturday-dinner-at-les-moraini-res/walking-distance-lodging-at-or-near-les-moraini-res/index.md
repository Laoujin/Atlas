> **Decision.** Nothing is on the restaurant's doorstep — [Les Morainières](https://www.les-morainieres.com/fr/) is perched alone in the Marestel vines at 1400 route de Marestel [[1]](https://www.les-morainieres.com/fr/situation-et-acces.html). Four village B&Bs put you 1.4–1.6 km (19–22 min) away on foot through the vineyards [[23]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/5.7970003,45.7331699;5.7982989,45.7449480?overview=false)[[24]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/5.8022380,45.7328880;5.7982989,45.7449480?overview=false)[[25]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/5.8014880,45.7317470;5.7982989,45.7449480?overview=false)[[26]](https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/foot/5.7986270,45.7329630;5.7982989,45.7449480?overview=false). Pick **[Château de la Mar](https://chateaudelamar.fr/)** if you want the splurge experience under one roof (own bistro La Table 1625 by chef Arnoult, spa, 9.1/10 on Booking [[14]](https://www.booking.com/reviews/fr/hotel/chac-teau-de-la-mar.html)); **[Domaine Carrel & Senger](https://domainecarreletsenger.com/)** at 1.42 km is the shortest stroll and the highest-rated (4.9/5 [[17]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1926328-d33419203-Reviews-Domaine_Carrel_Senger-Jongieux_Savoie_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html)); **[Les Chambres du Cru](https://edmondjacquin.com/chambre-dhotes/)** at €80 is the budget bet [[18]](https://www.gites-de-france-savoie.com/Hebergements/chambre-d-hotes/les-chambres-du-cru-73G140600.html). If walking isn't the point, book the chef's own **[Maison des Morainières](https://www.les-morainieres.com/fr/chambres.html)** in Saint-Pierre-de-Curtille (4.6 km, free shuttle) — the *Séjour Découverte* package bundles dinner + wine + room + breakfast for €880/two [[6]](https://www.les-morainieres.com/fr/offres-speciales/sejour-decouverte.html).

## What "walking distance" actually means here

Les Morainières moved up to three Michelin stars in March 2026 [[2]](https://www.tendancehotellerie.fr/articles-breves/communique-de-presse/25437-article/les-morainieres-a-jongieux-savoie-decroche-trois-etoiles-dans-la-nouvelle-selection-du-guide-michelin-france-monaco-2026). It is a single building on a vine-terraced hillside above the Rhône — no other lodging shares the road. The Michelin Guide entry itself notes the official guestrooms are "a few kilometres from the restaurant" [[3]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/auvergne-rhone-alpes/jongieux/restaurant/les-morainieres); one Tripadvisor diner griped the room is 6 km away and they wished it were posted up front [[28]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1926328-d1903848-Reviews-Restaurant_Les_Morainieres-Jongieux_Savoie_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html).

The four village options below are clustered in the lower hamlets (Aimavigne and the Jongieux-le-Haut side along route des Vignobles) and connect to the restaurant by the small *route de Marestel* network. Elevations are nearly identical — restaurant 373 m, lodgings 372–403 m — so the "perched" feel is about commanding views over the Rhône, not a brutal climb [[27]](https://api.open-elevation.com/api/v1/lookup?locations=45.7449480,5.7982989|45.7331699,5.7970003|45.7328880,5.8022380|45.7317470,5.8014880|45.7329630,5.7986270). The Marestel Promenade hiking circuit confirms how walkable the area is: 5.1 km loop from the mairie, very-easy grade [[8]](https://www.aixlesbains-rivieradesalpes.com/en/equipement/marestel-promenade-jongieux/).

Caveats before you decide to actually walk it Saturday night:

- The restaurant's evening service window is tight, **19:30–20:00 seating, Thu–Sun only** [[28]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1926328-d1903848-Reviews-Restaurant_Les_Morainieres-Jongieux_Savoie_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html). Don't gamble with a 20-minute uphill walk if the weather turns.
- Rural roads, no streetlights — bring a torch for the post-dinner descent.
- The €240 tasting menu plus wine pairings [[28]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1926328-d1903848-Reviews-Restaurant_Les_Morainieres-Jongieux_Savoie_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html) lands heavy. Many guests will prefer the chef's shuttle on the way back even from a B&B in walking range — ask when you confirm dinner.

## Per-option notes

### Château de la Mar — the splurge, with a chef tie-in

13th-century estate at 391 route de la Charvaz in the Aimavigne hamlet, recently re-renovated [[10]](https://www.pagesjaunes.fr/pros/51855046)[[9]](https://fr.mappy.com/poi/670d8b57c34ece3763193cd2)[[14]](https://www.booking.com/reviews/fr/hotel/chac-teau-de-la-mar.html). One room and four suites, all with private bath. Spa, heated outdoor pool, sauna; Tesla chargers; HV3-certified 6 ha vineyard with cellar tastings [[11]](https://chateaudelamar.fr/). Critically, the on-site bistro **La Table 1625** is operated *in collaboration with Les Morainières* under Michaël Arnoult — same chef, second-tier price (lunch and dinner menus at €35) [[12]](https://chateaudelamar.fr/restaurant-la-table-1625/). Useful if you want a Friday-night warm-up before the Saturday gastronomic dinner. Mixed 2026 reviews flag occasional check-in / breakfast lapses; the property otherwise scores 9.1/10 [[13]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1926328-d4789786-Reviews-Chateau_de_La_Mar-Jongieux_Savoie_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html)[[14]](https://www.booking.com/reviews/fr/hotel/chac-teau-de-la-mar.html). Reservation: +33 4 79 96 09 84.

### Domaine Carrel & Senger — shortest walk, best ratings

Working winery in operation since 1830, at 3387 route des Vignobles [[15]](https://fr.mappy.com/poi/69237d3118a73055a68a26e1)[[16]](https://domainecarreletsenger.com/). Four ensuite rooms above the cellar with AC, soundproofing, queen beds, parquet. Buffet breakfast 08:00–10:00, free parking, rooftop terrace. Wine-tasting on site for €15 [[16]](https://domainecarreletsenger.com/). Tripadvisor 4.9/5 over 36 reviews [[17]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1926328-d33419203-Reviews-Domaine_Carrel_Senger-Jongieux_Savoie_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html). Prices aren't listed publicly — call +33 4 79 44 00 20.

### Les Chambres du Cru — best value, working winegrowers

Three rooms (Pinot, Mondeuse, Gamay) above the Jacquin family's cellar at 3552 route des Vignobles in Jongieux-le-Haut [[18]](https://www.gites-de-france-savoie.com/Hebergements/chambre-d-hotes/les-chambres-du-cru-73G140600.html)[[19]](https://fr.mappy.com/poi/683caf5e563a7a3ea51ab861). €80–€85/night for two including breakfast, Gîtes de France 4.34/5. Gets you the same 22-minute vineyard walk to dinner as Château de la Mar at a quarter the price. Booking via Gîtes de France Savoie (04 58 14 12 12) or owner site [edmondjacquin.com](https://edmondjacquin.com/chambre-dhotes/).

### Les Balcons de la Charve — only if you're a group

Whole-house 4★ gîte at 2906 route des Vignobles, 148 m² over two floors, four ensuite bedrooms, sleeps up to 12 [[20]](https://lesbalconsdelacharve.fr/)[[29]](https://www.gites-de-france-savoie.com/Hebergements/gite/les-balcons-de-la-charve-73G140103.html). Mid-week from €1,100, week from €1,650 in 2026 [[21]](https://www.pays-lac-aiguebelette.com/hebergement-locatif/les-balcons-de-la-charve-jongieux/). Not rented by the room. Makes economic sense only if you're buying out for friends/family.

### La Maison des Morainières — not walkable, but it's the chef's house

68 route du Grand Bois, Saint-Pierre-de-Curtille — **4.6 km by road** from the restaurant [[4]](https://www.les-morainieres.com/fr/chambres.html)[[22]](https://www.charme-traditions.com/fr/chambres-d-hotes/102067/jongieux/1). The only marked footpath connection (Cirkwi via GR65) is a 12 km, 3h30 hike — *not* a "walk to dinner" [[7]](https://www.cirkwi.com/fr/circuit/245902-de-saint-pierre-de-curtille-a-jongieux). What the Arnoults offer instead is a **complimentary navette** to and from the restaurant for every service [[4]](https://www.les-morainieres.com/fr/chambres.html). Six plant-named rooms (35–45 m²), pool, garden; published 2024 rate was €250–€320/night [[5]](https://www.aixlesbains-rivieradesalpes.com/en/hebergement-locatif/la-maison-des-morainieres-saint-pierre-de-curtille/). The packaged **Séjour Découverte** (€880 for two) bundles a Découverte tasting menu with wine pairings, one night, and breakfast — cleanest single-button option for the trip [[6]](https://www.les-morainieres.com/fr/offres-speciales/sejour-decouverte.html). Note the package does not include shuttle (it's complimentary anyway for in-house guests).

## How to choose

- ⚠ Reserve the restaurant *first*. Three-star + small dining room + tight 19:30–20:00 seating window means most weekends sell out weeks ahead [[28]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1926328-d1903848-Reviews-Restaurant_Les_Morainieres-Jongieux_Savoie_Auvergne_Rhone_Alpes.html). Confirm the date before you book any of the lodgings below.
- **Want to genuinely walk to dinner →** Domaine Carrel & Senger (shortest, best rated) or Les Chambres du Cru (cheapest).
- **Want a château weekend, with the same chef's bistro as a Friday warm-up →** Château de la Mar.
- **Want the easiest pre-packaged 3-star experience, walk be damned →** Maison des Morainières with the *Séjour Découverte* and shuttle.
- **Traveling as a group of 6+ →** Les Balcons de la Charve for the buy-out.
