Where to walk back from a three-star dinner.

Four village B&Bs sit 1.4–1.6 km on foot from Les Morainières through the Marestel vines. The chef's own guesthouse is 4.6 km away — with a shuttle.

Concierge's note —

Nothing is on the doorstep. Les Morainières stands alone at 1400 route de Marestel[1]. For the literal walk: Domaine Carrel & Senger — 1.42 km, 19 min, 4.9/5[17]. For the splurge with a chef tie-in: Château de la Mar, 1.6 km, with Arnoult's own bistro La Table 1625 on site[12]. For one-button bliss: the chef's Maison des Morainières, 4.6 km with shuttle, packaged at €880/two[6].

Maison des Morainières — off the postcard, but in the package.

The chef's own guesthouse in Saint-Pierre-de-Curtille: six plant-named rooms (35–45 m²), pool, garden, €250–€320/night[5]. The only on-foot connection is a 12 km / 3h30 GR65 hike[7] — not a walk to dinner. What you get instead is a complimentary navette door-to-door for every service[4].

Séjour Découverte — €880 / 2 pers · menu + wine + room + breakfast[6]
P.S. — service window Dinner is 19:30–20:00 only, Thu–Sun.[28] Don't gamble a 22-min uphill walk if the weather turns at 18:00.
P.P.S. — bring a torch Rural roads, no streetlights on the route de Marestel. Phone-torches die fast at €240 plus pairings.[28]
P.P.P.S. — the climb is a lie It looks perched. It isn't, much. Restaurant 373 m, lodgings 372–403 m — essentially rolling.[27]

If… then book

  • If you want to literally walk back through the vines → Domaine Carrel & Senger (shortest, 4.9/5)[17] or Les Chambres du Cru (€80, same 22-min walk)[18]
  • If you want a château weekend with a Friday warm-up → Château de la Mar, then La Table 1625 (same chef, €35) Friday night[12]
  • If you want the cleanest pre-packaged 3-star experience, walk be damned → Maison des Morainières + the Séjour Découverte €880 bundle + free shuttle[6]
  • If you're travelling as a group of six or more → Les Balcons de la Charve — whole-house gîte, sleeps 12, mid-week €1,100+[21]
