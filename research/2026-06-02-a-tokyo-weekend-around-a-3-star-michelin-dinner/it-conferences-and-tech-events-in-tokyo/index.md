> **TL;DR** Two annual peaks: a **summer surge (June–July)** stacking [Code with Claude](https://gadgetbond.com/code-with-claude-2026-anthropic-developer-conference/) [[7]](https://gadgetbond.com/code-with-claude-2026-anthropic-developer-conference/), [AWS Summit Japan](https://aws.amazon.com/jp/summits/japan/) [[8]](https://aws.amazon.com/jp/summits/japan/), [KubeCon Japan](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-japan/) [[12]](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-japan/), and [Google Cloud Next Tokyo](https://www.googlecloudevents.com/next-tokyo/) [[13]](https://www.googlecloudevents.com/next-tokyo/) within seven weeks; and an **autumn run (Sept–Nov)** with [DroidKaigi](https://2026.droidkaigi.jp/en/) [[14]](https://2026.droidkaigi.jp/en/), [iOSDC Japan](https://iosdc.jp/2026/) [[16]](https://iosdc.jp/2026/), [ETHGlobal Tokyo](https://ethglobal.com/events/tokyo) [[17]](https://ethglobal.com/events/tokyo), and [CODE BLUE](https://codeblue.jp) [[21]](https://codeblue.jp). Enterprise visitors anchor on [Japan IT Week](https://japan-it.jp/spring) [[3]](https://japan-it.jp/spring) (April/October). Makuhari Messe (AWS, Japan IT Week Autumn) and Yokohama (KubeCon) are ~30 min from Shinjuku by train.

## Event Profiles

### Trade Shows & Expos

**[Japan IT Week](https://japan-it.jp/spring)** — Japan's largest IT exhibition, twice yearly. Spring 2026 (Apr 8–10, Tokyo Big Sight) drew 51,370 visitors across seven co-located shows covering software dev, IoT, cybersecurity, AI/DX, and e-commerce [[3]](https://japan-it.jp/spring). Autumn edition (Oct 21–23) moves to Makuhari Messe [[19]](https://japan-it.jp/autumn). Free admission both editions.

**[NexTech Week Tokyo](https://nextech-week.jp)** — B2B trade show at Tokyo Big Sight running parallel to Japan IT Week (Spring: Apr 15–17; Autumn: Nov 11–13 at Makuhari Messe). Skews more forward-looking than Japan IT Week: AI, quantum computing, humanoid robotics, and blockchain [[5]](https://nextech-week.jp).

**[SusHi Tech Tokyo](https://sushitech-startup.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/en/)** — Tokyo Metropolitan Government-backed startup event. 2026 edition (Apr 27–29) drew 60,000 participants, 700+ startups, and ~10,000 business meetings at Tokyo Big Sight [[6]](https://sushitech-startup.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/en/). Strong international presence; 2027 edition expected May 20–22.

### Cloud & Infrastructure

**[AWS Summit Japan](https://aws.amazon.com/jp/summits/japan/)** — June 25–26 at Makuhari Messe. Free registration. 260+ sessions, keynotes, expo floor, and a Startup Zone. 2026 theme: agentic AI transforming enterprise operations [[8]](https://aws.amazon.com/jp/summits/japan/). Largest cloud vendor event in Japan by session count.

**[Google Cloud Next Tokyo](https://www.googlecloudevents.com/next-tokyo/)** — July 30–31 at Tokyo Big Sight. Free daytime Open Stage sessions (no reservation needed). Nine breakout categories anchored on Gemini AI. Dev Night evening event for developers; Learning & Certification hub on-site [[13]](https://www.googlecloudevents.com/next-tokyo/).

**[KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Japan](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-japan/)** — July 28–30 in Yokohama. CNCF's flagship Asia Pacific event. Strong English-language program. Co-located ArgoCon, KeycloakCon, and Japan Community Day. Scholarship and travel-fund applications available [[12]](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-japan/).

**[SRE NEXT](https://sre-next.dev/2026/)** — July 10–11, TOC Ariake, Tokyo. Community-run SRE conference themed "Inclusive SRE." Hybrid (in-person + stream). Primarily Japanese; international attendees should expect limited English sessions [[10]](https://sre-next.dev/2026/).

### Developer Conferences

**[Code with Claude](https://gadgetbond.com/code-with-claude-2026-anthropic-developer-conference/)** — June 10, Tokyo. Anthropic's three-city developer conference, final stop after San Francisco (May 6) and London (May 19). Three tracks: Research, Claude Platform, and Claude Code. Free entry by lottery (distributed April 2026); global livestream available. June 11 spin-off "Code with Claude: Extended" for indie devs and founders [[7]](https://gadgetbond.com/code-with-claude-2026-anthropic-developer-conference/).

**[DroidKaigi 2026](https://2026.droidkaigi.jp/en/)** — Sep 1–3, Bellesalle Shibuya Garden. Android developer conference by engineers, for engineers. Bilingual sessions; one of the few Tokyo dev conferences with meaningful English-language programming [[14]](https://2026.droidkaigi.jp/en/).

**[iOSDC Japan 2026](https://iosdc.jp/2026/)** — Sep 11–13, Tokyo. iOS and Swift community conference. Hybrid offline/online. Primarily Japanese-language; best suited for Japanese-speaking iOS developers [[16]](https://iosdc.jp/2026/).

**[ETHGlobal Tokyo](https://ethglobal.com/events/tokyo)** — Sep 25–27. 48-hour Ethereum hackathon with workshops, mentors, and prizes. Open to Web2 developers entering the Ethereum ecosystem [[17]](https://ethglobal.com/events/tokyo).

### AI, Web3 & Emerging Tech

**[Tokyo AI Week / TS26](https://tokyo.waytoagi.com/)** — April 6–8 at Happo-En (past; watch for 2027 dates). 80+ AI speakers, 500+ attendees, co-located with [TEAMZ Summit](https://www.teamz.co.jp/en) (Asia's largest Web3 & AI conference, 10,625 attendees in 2026) [[1]](https://tokyo.waytoagi.com/) [[2]](https://www.teamz.co.jp/en). TEAMZ general admission from ¥31,550; 58 side events across Tokyo Web3/AI Week (Apr 4–10).

**MCP Dev Summit / AGNTCon + MCPCon Japan** — Sep 10–11, Tokyo. New for 2026, back-to-back events focused on the Model Context Protocol and agentic AI patterns — the clearest Tokyo signal for the current agentic AI wave [[15]](https://dev.events/AS/JP/Tokyo/tech).

### Security

**[CODE BLUE 2026](https://codeblue.jp)** — Conference Nov 17–18 at Versailles Takadanobaba (trainings Nov 11–15 at Versailles Shinjuku). International cybersecurity research conference with simultaneous Japanese-English interpretation. Youth Track for presenters under 25. CFP open [[21]](https://codeblue.jp). One of Japan's top security research gatherings alongside SECCON.

### Testing & Open Source

**[Tokyo Test Fest](https://tokyotestfest.com)** — Nov 12–13, Osaki Bright Core Hall. Community-run QA and software testing conference [[20]](https://tokyotestfest.com).

**[Open Source Summit Japan](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/open-source-summit-japan/)** — Dec 7–9, Tokyo. Linux Foundation event covering Linux, open-source tooling, and community governance [[22]](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/open-source-summit-japan/).
