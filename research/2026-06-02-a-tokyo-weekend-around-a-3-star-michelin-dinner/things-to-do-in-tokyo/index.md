> **Decision.** Sat AM: Tsukiji outer market 08:00 [[25]](https://getlostinjapan.com/breakfast-at-tsukiji-market/) → Senso-ji 09:30 [[5]](https://matcha-jp.com/en/3759). Sat PM: Meiji Shrine + Harajuku/Omotesando walk into Shibuya [[21]](https://tanuki-tabi-travel.com/blog/shibuya-harajuku-guide), Shibuya Sky sunset slot booked 28 days ahead [[54]](https://shibuyaskytickets.com/). Sat dinner: Michelin anchor. Sun AM: teamLab Borderless or Planets (book ≥ 2 weeks ahead) [[53]](https://www.adamandlinds.com/blog/teamlab-planets-tokyo-tickets-guide/). Sun PM: a contemplative half-day — Hamarikyu + tea at Nakajima [[38]](https://tokyotourists.com/hamarikyu-garden/), or Yanaka old town [[76]](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3068.html). Skip Imperial Palace East Gardens (closed Mon/Fri) [[2]](https://www.kunaicho.go.jp/e-event/higashigyoen02.html) and Tsukiji on Sunday [[25]](https://getlostinjapan.com/breakfast-at-tsukiji-market/). For a Tokyo day-trip swap, Kamakura is the cheapest lift [[70]](https://japannook.com/en/articles/tokyo-day-trips-guide); Hakone is the highest reward [[71]](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e2358_008.html).

Skip-or-swap notes:
- The Imperial Palace East Gardens close every Monday and Friday and the Saturday/Sunday window is your only weekend shot [[2]](https://www.kunaicho.go.jp/e-event/higashigyoen02.html); if your Saturday is otherwise booked and Sunday is busy, drop this one.
- Skytree vs Shibuya Sky cover the same proposition — pick one. Skytree wins for raw altitude; Shibuya Sky wins for the Scramble money shot at sunset [[7]](https://www.magical-trip.com/media/the-icons-of-shibuya-scramble-crossing-and-hachiko-statue/).
- The free [Tokyo Metropolitan Government](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3011.html) 45F observatories give a Shinjuku-side skyline view at zero yen — a budget hedge if Shibuya Sky is sold out [[16]](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3011.html).

Roppongi has a "complicated reputation" — the densest foreigner nightlife in Tokyo but also the area most associated with tourist scams, overpriced cover charges and aggressive touts [[24]](https://tokyointernationalmeetup.com/2026/04/08/best-neighborhoods-tokyo-foreigners/). ## Food beyond the Michelin anchor

[Tsukiji outer market](https://getlostinjapan.com/breakfast-at-tsukiji-market/) opens around 05:00 and most stalls shutter by 14:00; closed Sundays and select Wednesdays, so plan it for Saturday morning [[25]](https://getlostinjapan.com/breakfast-at-tsukiji-market/). The outer-market stalls remain even though the wholesale tuna auction moved to Toyosu. The signature stops:

| Stop                                                | What                                  | Notes                                                              |
|-----------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| [Yamachou vs Shouro][t1]                            | tamagoyaki (egg)                      | Yamachou sweet/caramelised; Shouro savoury/dashi [[t1]]            |
| [Maguroya Kurogin][t2]                              | tuna cuts                             | Long line moves fast [[t2]]                                        |
| [Kitsuneya][t2]                                     | horumon-ni (offal stew)               | For the adventurous [[t2]]                                         |

[t1]: https://www.svadore.com/best-street-food-stalls-tsukiji-outer-market/
[t2]: https://foodtourintokyo.com/tsukiji-outer-market-guide/

**Ramen.** The 2026 short list per Tabelog rankings: [Ichiran](https://iamafoodblog.com/tokyo-ramen/), [Nakiryu](https://iamafoodblog.com/tokyo-ramen/) (Michelin Bib), [Kipposhi](https://iamafoodblog.com/tokyo-ramen/), and the breakout [Chuka Soba Kamofuku](https://iamafoodblog.com/tokyo-ramen/) (opened May 2024, Tabelog 3.93) [[28]](https://iamafoodblog.com/tokyo-ramen/). Important correction on Tsuta — the first Michelin-starred ramen shop: it closed Sep 2022 after chef Yuki Onishi's death and reopened Feb 2023 in **Yoyogi-Uehara**, not the old Sugamo address [[29]](https://japantoday.com/category/features/food/first-michelin-starred-ramen-shop-japanese-soba-noodles-tsuta-to-reopen-in-tokyo).

**Yakitori / izakaya yokocho.** [Omoide Yokocho][o1] on Shinjuku's west side packs ~80 counter-only joints; skewers ¥100-200, a full meal ¥2,000-3,000 [[o1]]. [Ebisu Yokocho][o2] runs 21 retro stalls open 17:00-04:00 with cross-stall food delivery [[o2]].

[o1]: https://selfguidejapan.com/blog/omoide-yokocho-guide
[o2]: https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/451/index.html

**Depachika.** [Isetan Shinjuku B1](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/shopping/best-depachika-food-halls-in-tokyo) is consensus best — in-store sake/whisky sommeliers, ornate bento, wagashi, outposts of [Pierre Hermé](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/shopping/best-depachika-food-halls-in-tokyo) and [Jean-Paul Hévin](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/shopping/best-depachika-food-halls-in-tokyo) [[32]](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/shopping/best-depachika-food-halls-in-tokyo). Treat it as a takeaway bento source on your way to a park picnic.

**Themed cafes.** Quality is wildly uneven. Apply a five-point welfare filter for animal cafes (lighting, no tethering, optional handling, vet statement, ≤ 2 guests per active animal). [Akiba Fukurou](https://japannook.com/en/articles/japan-themed-cafes-guide) in Akihabara meets all five; many Harajuku/Asakusa owl cafes do not [[33]](https://japannook.com/en/articles/japan-themed-cafes-guide). ⚠ Skip the Harajuku/Asakusa owl-cafe touts.

## Weekend scheduling gotchas

A short list of date-dependent closures and booking windows that wreck weekend plans if missed.

- **Imperial Palace East Gardens — closed Mon and Fri** [[2]](https://www.kunaicho.go.jp/e-event/higashigyoen02.html). A Saturday-anchored weekend is fine; a Friday arrival is not.
- **Tsukiji outer market — closed Sundays and select Wednesdays** [[25]](https://getlostinjapan.com/breakfast-at-tsukiji-market/). Always Saturday morning.
- **Shinjuku Gyoen — closed Mondays** [[36]](https://www.env.go.jp/garden/shinjukugyoen/english/index.html). Pick Hamarikyu or Rikugien for a Monday extension.
- **Sumo at Ryogoku — only Jan 11-25, May 10-24, Sep 13-27 in 2026** [[39]](https://ryogokukokugikan.com/ryogoku-kokugikan-schedule-2026). Outside those windows, only morning stable practice exists, and most stables only admit guests with an interpreter.
- **Shibuya Sky sunset slots — release exactly 28 days before at 00:00 JST and disappear within minutes** [[54]](https://shibuyaskytickets.com/). Set a calendar alarm.
- **teamLab Borderless / Planets — book ≥ 2 weeks ahead for weekends** [[53]](https://www.adamandlinds.com/blog/teamlab-planets-tokyo-tickets-guide/).
- **Ghibli Museum — tickets release 10:00 JST on the 10th for the following month** [[65]](https://www.ghibli-museum.jp/en/tickets/). If you're booking in the same month, you are too late.
- **Pokémon Cafe TOKYO — closed for renovation Mar 23-Jun 16, 2026; reopens Wed Jun 17** [[63]](https://www.pokemon-cafe.jp/en/cafe/news/260511_3392.html). Reservations release 31 days out at 18:00 JST [[64]](https://www.pokemon-cafe.jp/en/cafe/reservation.html).
- **Ginza Chuo Dori pedestrian zone — Sat/Sun afternoons only (12:00-18:00; until 17:00 Oct-Mar)** [[14]](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3005.html). Time the walk accordingly.
- **Akihabara Chuo Dori pedestrian zone — Sundays only, 13:00-18:00** [[15]](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3003.html).
- **Tokyo City View Sky Deck — closed since Apr 2024; 2026 has partial indoor closures** [[55]](https://tcv.roppongihills.com/en/news/2024/04/7228/). Choose Shibuya Sky or Skytree instead for an outdoor option.
- **Cherry blossom in 2026 — open Mar 19, peak around Mar 27-29, several days earlier than average** [[49]](https://tokyocheapo.com/living/tokyo-cherry-blossom-forecast/).
