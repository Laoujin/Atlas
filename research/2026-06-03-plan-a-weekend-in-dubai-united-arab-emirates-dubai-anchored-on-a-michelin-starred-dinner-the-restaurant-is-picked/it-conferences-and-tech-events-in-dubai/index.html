Events in Detail

GITEX Global 2026

Dec 8–11 · Dubai Exhibition Centre, Expo City · [1]

The world's largest tech expo in its 46th year — now relocated to Expo City's 2.2M sq ft venue for its "boldest reinvention."[16] 200,000+ attendees, 6,800+ exhibitors, 400+ government entities from 180+ countries.[17]

Sub-events: Expand North Star (Dec 8–10; 2,000+ startups, 1,200+ investors with $1.1T+ AUM)[7] · GITEX Cyber Valley · GITEX Quantum Expo · GITEX Move (mobility). New debut: TechCation — citywide tech+leisure experiences.[15]

Tickets: Visitor AED 580 (~$160) · Conference AED 1,600 · Student AED 50 · VIP up to AED 10,000 (prices incl. 5% VAT)[11]

GISEC Global 2026

Sep 16–18 · Dubai · [3]

Arab world's leading cybersecurity expo, now in its 15th edition. 25,000+ infosec professionals from 180+ countries, 750+ exhibiting brands, 300+ hours of conference content.[3] Focus areas: AI-powered threats, deepfakes, ransomware, zero-trust architectures, and live red-team demos.

Audience: CISOs, security engineers, threat analysts, government security leads

AWS Summit Dubai — Free

Jun 10 · Dubai World Trade Centre · [6]

Free one-day cloud event. Covers agentic AI, serverless, cloud modernisation, and AI infrastructure. Interactive workshops and direct Q&A with AWS engineers. Best value in Dubai's tech calendar — no ticket cost, solid technical depth for developers and architects.

Tickets: Free — register at aws.amazon.com/events/summits/dubai

Dubai FinTech Summit 2026

Nov 2–3 · Madinat Jumeirah · [5]

4th edition, organised by DIFC. Convenes C-suite finance leaders, regulators, and fintech founders around blockchain, digital banking, and AI-powered finance. Tied to Dubai's D33 goal of reaching the top-4 global financial hubs by 2033.[18]

Sub-events: DFS Dialogues (exclusive C-suite), FinTech World Cup, FinTech Launchpad.

Audience: CFOs, fintech founders, regulators, investors

Gartner CIO & IT Executive Conference

Oct 5–7 · Grand Hyatt Dubai · [9]

Gartner's regional IT leadership conference. Delivers Gartner research, technology trend briefings, and strategy sessions directly to CIOs and senior IT executives.[8] Smaller and more curated than GITEX — better for executive decision-makers than practitioners.

Audience: CIOs, IT VPs, enterprise technology leaders · Pricing: Gartner member rates apply
