> **TL;DR** Cassis hosts **no public IT conferences**. It's a corporate-seminar venue town — the Oustau Calendal congress centre handles team off-sites up to 300 people. Developer events are in the neighbouring cities: **[KCD Provence](https://cloudnative-provence.fr/en/) on 10 Dec 2026** in Aix-en-Provence (600+ expected, Kubernetes / cloud-native) [[1]](https://cloudnative-provence.fr/en/) and **[Agile Tour Aix-Marseille](https://atmrs.esprit-agile.com/)** every autumn (~270 attendees). [[8]](https://atmrs.esprit-agile.com/) Both are under 45 minutes by car from Cassis.

## Cassis as a conference venue, not a conference city

Cassis (~8,800 residents) is built for tourism and wine, not tech summits. The tourism office markets it for MICE — Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, Events — with 11 registered venues and a formal congress-request desk. [[3]](https://www.ot-cassis.com/en/congresses-seminars.html) [[4]](https://www.aleou.fr/ville/lieux-congres-seminaires/cassis) None of those venues hosts a recurring public IT conference; the use case is private: a squad of engineers converging for a sprint review, architecture off-site, or leadership retreat where a cliff-top terrace doubles as a whiteboard backdrop.

## Practical tips for the Cassis-based attendee

- **By car**: Cassis → Aix-en-Provence ~40 min (A50 → A51); Cassis → Marseille ~25 min (A50). Conference parking at both destinations is typically free or cheap.
- **By train**: Gare de Cassis is on the Marseille–Toulon TER line; Marseille Saint-Charles in ~25 min (hourly). Marseille → Gardanne (Agile Tour venue) by train in ~20 min.
- **Pricing window**: Cassis hotel rates drop sharply after October, making a KCD Provence trip (10 Dec) economical to combine with a Provence weekend.
