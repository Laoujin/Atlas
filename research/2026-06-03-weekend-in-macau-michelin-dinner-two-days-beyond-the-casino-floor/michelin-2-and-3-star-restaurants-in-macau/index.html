Decision: Jade Dragon for the finest Cantonese in Macau; Robuchon au Dôme for French gastronomy in an unmatched setting with Asia's largest wine list (18 straight 3-star years).[1] Among 2-stars: Chef Tam's Seasons is the prestige pick (No.7 Asia's 50 Best);[8] Feng Wei Ju offers the best value (≈ MOP 300/pax, Sichuan-Hunan, lowest of the eight); The Huaiyang Garden is the only Huaiyang Michelin table in all of Hong Kong and Macau.[13]

18th Michelin Guide Hong Kong & Macau, released 19 March 2026. 8 Macau restaurants at 2-star or above out of 59 total.[2] Prices per person, estimated in MOP (1 MOP ≈ 1 HKD ≈ USD 0.12).[16] Cotai restaurants require a 15–20 min taxi from the Peninsula ferry terminals.
