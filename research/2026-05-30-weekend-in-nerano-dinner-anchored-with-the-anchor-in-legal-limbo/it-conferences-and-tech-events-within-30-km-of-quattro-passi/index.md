> **TL;DR.** Within 30 km of Quattro Passi (Nerano) is essentially Sorrento Peninsula + Naples city centre [[1]](https://www.distancefromto.net/between/Naples/Sorrento). The peninsula itself hosts **one** major IT-adjacent event per year — **ICSO 2026** (space optics, 12–16 Oct at Hilton Sorrento Palace, 10-min walk from Sorrento centre) [[2]](https://atpi.eventsair.com/icso-2026/)[[3]](https://www.spaceagenda.com/event/icso-2026/). For everything else — IEEE conferences, GDG DevFest, hackathons, meetups — you'll drive 60–90 min into Naples, where the calendar clusters at Federico II, Città della Scienza (Bagnoli), and Stazione Marittima. If your weekend lands on **17 Oct**, you can do Quattro Passi Saturday evening and Napoli DevFest the same day [[11]](https://www.napolidevfest.it/).

## Geographic scope

Nerano sits at the tip of the Sorrento Peninsula. Naples city centre is ~26 km from Sorrento as the crow flies (Sorrento → Nerano adds another 5 km of coastal road) [[1]](https://www.distancefromto.net/between/Naples/Sorrento). That puts the 30-km circle from Quattro Passi at the *edge* of central Naples — west Naples (Bagnoli, Pozzuoli) and the deep periphery (Caserta, Salerno/Fisciano, Aversa) fall outside. Road time from Nerano is the real constraint: 75–90 min to central Naples through SS145 and the A3.

## Recurring community meetups (Naples)

Naples has a small but real Italian-language dev community. None of these meet on the Sorrento Peninsula.

- [GDG Napoli](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-napoli/) — 1,750 members, runs the annual Napoli DevFest plus irregular meetups and hackathons; flagship 2025 was 11 Oct, 2026 is 17 Oct [[12]](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-napoli/).
- [DevDay Napoli](https://www.meetup.com/devdays/) — 817 members, monthly developer talks, venue rotates around Naples [[13]](https://www.meetup.com/devdays/).
- [AWS User Group Napoli](https://www.meetup.com/it-IT/aws-user-group-napoli/) — 434 members; largely dormant for in-person (last regular meetup Apr 2024). They co-hosted the synchronous **AWS Community GameDay Europe** on 17 Mar 2026, but at VMEngine in Pozzuoli (~33 km, just outside) [[14]](https://www.meetup.com/it-IT/aws-user-group-napoli/).
- [PyCampania](https://www.meetup.com/cities/it/na/napoli/tech/) — 167 members, Python-focused, irregular [[21]](https://www.meetup.com/cities/it/na/napoli/tech/).
- [GDG Campania](https://www.meetup.com/it-it/gdgcampania/) — regional GDG covering Naples & Salerno; mostly the same crowd as GDG Napoli [[22]](https://www.meetup.com/it-it/gdgcampania/).

Realistic advice: check the meetup pages a week before your weekend. The community runs on bursts of activity around the universities (Federico II, Parthenope, Vanvitelli), not on a fixed calendar.

## How to use this page

Pick your weekend on the calendar above. If it lands on a Saturday with no IT event in the area, that's normal — the Sorrento Peninsula is a tourism economy, not a tech hub. Sorrento's one shot at a marquee tech weekend in 2026 is **ICSO, 12–16 October** (Quattro Passi is bookable that Saturday — the 17th) [[3]](https://www.spaceagenda.com/event/icso-2026/). The next-best overlap is the **17 Oct Saturday**, when Napoli DevFest runs at Città della Scienza and you can be back in Nerano for an 8pm tasting menu [[11]](https://www.napolidevfest.it/).
