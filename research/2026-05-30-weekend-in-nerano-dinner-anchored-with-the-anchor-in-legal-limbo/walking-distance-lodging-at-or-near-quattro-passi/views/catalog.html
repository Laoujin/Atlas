Before you book

Three checks the canonical research surfaced — translate from "looks bookable on Booking" to "will actually be there in October."

  1. Restaurant status

    Re-check info@ristorantequattropassi.it closer to your dates; the legal situation is fluid pending the October 2026 merits hearing[2].

  2. Relais bookability

    The Relais is part of the seized complex[3] but still shows 2026 availability[14]. Get written confirmation that your dates will be honoured before paying a non-refundable rate.

  3. Shoes for Marina stays

    The village-to-beach grade is unforgiving after a long tasting menu — even Villarena reviewers (~100 m above the beach) flag the climb[6]. Pack accordingly or budget a taxi back up.
