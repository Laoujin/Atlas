> **TL;DR — Sleep in Nerano village.** The two properties on the same address block as Quattro Passi let you walk back from dinner in 1-5 minutes on flat ground: **Quattro Passi Relais** (the restaurant's own 7-room adults-only guesthouse, 9.3/10 [[4]](https://quattro-passi-relais-guest-house.hotels-in-campania.com/en/)) is the same complex; **Villarena Relais** (apartments, 4.8/5 [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1472375-d508153-Reviews-Villarena_Relais-Nerano_Massa_Lubrense_Province_of_Naples_Campania.html)) is metres away. Beach-level alternatives at Marina del Cantone (Hotel La Certosa, Taverna del Capitano) work only if you accept a 20-30 min uphill walk back from a late dinner [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1472375-d508153-Reviews-Villarena_Relais-Nerano_Massa_Lubrense_Province_of_Naples_Campania.html)[[13]](https://www.italyheaven.co.uk/campania/marina-del-cantone/).
>
> ⚠ **Trip-blocker for 2026:** Quattro Passi will **not reopen** for the 2026 season. The whole tourist-accommodation complex was seized in January 2026 over building-permit issues [[3]](https://en.cronachedellacampania.it/2026/01/sequestrato-ristorante-quattro-passi-di-nerano/) and the Mellino family confirmed closure on 18 May 2026; the merits hearing is October 2026 [[2]](https://www.italiaatavola.net/attualita-mercato/2026/5/18/quattro-passi-salta-stagione-2026-niente-riapertura-ristorante-tre-stelle/119254/). The **Relais accommodation** still shows 2026 availability on Booking [[14]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/it/quattro-passi-relais.html), but get written confirmation before paying. Rebook your Saturday-night dinner (Taverna del Capitano below is a same-bay Michelin-1★ fallback) before booking lodging around it.

## Geography in one paragraph

Quattro Passi sits at **Via Amerigo Vespucci 13N** in **Nerano village** [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/campania/nerano_7770444/restaurant/quattro-passi), on a low ridge above the cove of Marina del Cantone. Via Vespucci runs from the village down to the beach; the footpath from the marina car park back up to the village is an "easy" 20-30 min climb [[13]](https://www.italyheaven.co.uk/campania/marina-del-cantone/), and reviewers consistently call out the return leg as "steep, challenging" [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1472375-d508153-Reviews-Villarena_Relais-Nerano_Massa_Lubrense_Province_of_Naples_Campania.html). The split that matters: numbers 13M/13/25 on Via Vespucci form a cluster at the top, around Quattro Passi; everything else is down at the marina.

## Picks by traveller

- **Best splurge, dinner-anchored** — [Quattro Passi Relais](https://www.ristorantequattropassi.it/en/quattro-passi). Next door, adults-only, pool, 9.3/10 [[4]](https://quattro-passi-relais-guest-house.hotels-in-campania.com/en/). The premium is real (~$600+/night [[5]](https://www.kayak.com/Nerano-Hotels-Quattro-Passi-Relais.831098.ksp)) but you walk out of dinner straight into bed. ⚠ Same legal status as the restaurant — confirm bookings in writing.
- **Best mid-range, in the village** — [Villarena Relais](https://www.villarena.it/). Kitchen apartments, same street as QP, $122–$374/night [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1472375-d508153-Reviews-Villarena_Relais-Nerano_Massa_Lubrense_Province_of_Naples_Campania.html); Travelers' Choice 2025. The "in Nerano, not at the beach" address means quick walks to QP and back from any village restaurant.
- **Best for beach mornings, no car** — [Hotel La Certosa](https://www.hotelcertosa.com/en/). Straight onto the sand, monastery setting, 4.4/5 [[9]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotels-g1488739-Marina_del_Cantone_Massa_Lubrense_Province_of_Naples_Campania-Hotels.html), → trade-off is the 20-25 min uphill walk after dinner [[13]](https://www.italyheaven.co.uk/campania/marina-del-cantone/). Buses to Sorrento/Positano/Amalfi leave 50 m from the door [[10]](https://www.hotelcertosa.com/en/).
- **Plan B if QP stays closed** — [Taverna del Capitano](https://www.booking.com/hotel/it/locanda-del-capitano.en-gb.html). Sleep above the kitchen of a 1-Michelin-star restaurant on the same bay [[11]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/it/locanda-del-capitano.en-gb.html), Booking 9.3/10. Solves dinner and lodging in one move.

## Verify before booking

- **Restaurant status** — Re-check `info@ristorantequattropassi.it` or the official site closer to your date; the legal situation is fluid pending the October 2026 hearing [[2]](https://www.italiaatavola.net/attualita-mercato/2026/5/18/quattro-passi-salta-stagione-2026-niente-riapertura-ristorante-tre-stelle/119254/).
- **Relais bookability** — The Relais is part of the seized complex [[3]](https://en.cronachedellacampania.it/2026/01/sequestrato-ristorante-quattro-passi-di-nerano/) but still shows 2026 availability [[14]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/it/quattro-passi-relais.html). Get written confirmation that your dates will be honoured before paying a non-refundable rate.
- **Shoes for Marina del Cantone stays** — The village-to-beach grade is unforgiving after a long tasting menu; Villarena reviewers (only ~100 m above the beach proper) routinely flag the climb [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1472375-d508153-Reviews-Villarena_Relais-Nerano_Massa_Lubrense_Province_of_Naples_Campania.html). Pack accordingly or budget a taxi back up.
