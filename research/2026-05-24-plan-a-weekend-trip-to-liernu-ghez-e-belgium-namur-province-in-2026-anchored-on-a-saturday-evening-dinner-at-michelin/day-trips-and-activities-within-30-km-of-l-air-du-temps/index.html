Decision

Stay on-site. L'Air du Temps runs an 11-room B&B in the same farmstead — Tripadvisor's #1 in Éghezée — and that one booking removes the only real friction (driving home after a 2-star tasting menu).[77][78]

Default Saturday: morning in Gembloux (12 min), afternoon at the Citadel of Namur + cable car (13 min back), dinner at 19:30, sleep in-house.[82][79][45]

Foodie Saturday: Domaine du Chenoy 10h30 organic-vineyard tour → Floreffe Abbey tour + abbey-beer-and-cheese tasting → dinner.[59][14]

Active Saturday: bike the Meuse RAVeL Namur→Wépion→Annevoie (28 km flat) with rental from Pro Velo Namur; shower, dinner.[26][36]

With kids: Walibi Belgium (20 min drive, new Mecalodon coaster in 2026) or the cheaper Parc Reine Fabiola on the Citadel.[41][43]

The anchor

L'Air du Temps is at Rue de la Croix Monet 2, 5310 Liernu (Éghezée), a renovated white farmstead built around a square courtyard in the Hesbaye fields between Namur and Gembloux. Two MICHELIN Stars + a Green Star in the 2026 guide.[75][74] Closed Mon & Tue, €€€€, reserve well ahead at +32 81 81 30 48.[76] Around it sit 5 ha of land, half cultivated, growing ~400 species that supply ~90% of the kitchen's plant produce[72] — so every nearby producer visit ends up being thematically linked.

Drive times from Liernu (the only metric that matters)

Liernu itself has no scheduled bus — only a TEC Flexitec on-demand stop, "Liernu-Corbeau".[84] Plan around a car or a taxi; the TEC line 822 to Namur exists but runs every 4 hours.[79]

Sunday morning, before 11:00 checkout

Breakfast at L'Air du Temps runs 08:30–10:00 and is included in the room rate (€25/person if booked separately).[77] Then pick one:

Gros Chêne de Liernu walk

on foot 7 km · 2h

The village's millennium-old oak — 14 m trunk circumference, classified Walloon heritage — is the start of a 7 km signed loop in the Hesbaye fields. Zero drive.[56][38][39]

Brunch at Rosalie, Gembloux

13 min drive

Sunday brunch, local/organic produce, on the way out of Wallonia.[87]

Le Brunchist, Namur

13 min drive

Dedicated brunch and specialty coffee in town if you want to stroll the Sambre after.[88]

Namur Saturday market

Saturdays only

If you arrive Saturday morning instead: Marché du Bord de l'Eau on Bd Frère Orban, beside the Sambre under the Citadelle.[64] Gembloux's market is Friday, not Saturday.[65]

Namur city — the dense afternoon

The whole old-town hit-list sits inside a 15-minute walk:

Citadel of Namur

10:00–18:00 daily, 1 Apr–27 Sep

One of the longest underground passage networks in Europe; 90-min guided tour covers 495 m of restored 17th-c. galleries with 3D animation, constant 12–14 °C — book day before.[3][4] 4-activity Citadelle Pass €36 (or €18 for 3 visits).[45][5]

Téléphérique

€8 return · 4 min

From Place Maurice Servais up to Terra Nova, peaking 103 m above the Sambre–Meuse confluence. Weekends 10–18h.[1][2][85]

Musée Félicien Rops

€5 · Tue–Sun 10–18h

Provocative 19th-c. engraver; Rue Fumal 12, last entry 17h, closed 3rd weekend of September.[6][51]

TreM.a

€3 · Tue–Sun 10–18h

Provincial Museum of Ancient Arts, Rue de Fer 24, holds the medieval Treasure of Oignies — easily a one-hour stop.[7]

Walloon Parliament

Free, select Saturdays

Free guided tours (~90 min) at 9h30, 11h, 14h, 15h30 on select Saturdays — first 2026 date Sat 27 Jun; group booking required ≥2 weeks ahead.[8]

Cathédrale Saint-Aubin

Free

Italianate neoclassical, 1767; Van Dyck and Jordaens canvases; on Place Saint-Aubain, where the Fêtes de Wallonie Stilt Joust takes place.[9]

Le Grognon

Free, always open

Pig-snout spit at the Sambre–Meuse confluence — medieval heart of Namur, landscaped as the Confluence esplanade.[11][10]

MusAfrica

Wed–Sat 10–17h

Renamed Musée africain — 10-min walk from Namur station, plus the first Sunday of each month.[50]

Quirky local — within Eghezée itself

The Gros Chêne de Liernu — >1,000 years old, 14 m trunk circumference, classified Walloon heritage — stands in the same village as the restaurant. There's a Brotherhood whose 47th Chapter is on 30 May 2026.[56] The signed 7 km loop "La balade sur les terres du Gros Chêne" starts there.[38]

Explicitly out of range — skip these

Plenty of "near Namur" lists pull these in; they don't fit a 30 km radius from Liernu.

  • Domaine de Chevetogne — 600-ha family domain, but ~45 km south.[53]
  • Pairi Daiza — ~85 km from Namur (1 h 2 min).[54]
  • Euro Space Center (Transinne, Luxembourg Province) — well outside the radius.[55]

Practical notes

  • Weather: May 9–19 °C, June 12–20 °C, ~11 rainy days/month. Pack a waterproof for any garden visit.[86]
  • L'Air du Temps rooms: 11 doubles, ~€210 avg incl. breakfast, vacate by 11:00; breakfast 08:30–10:00.[77]
  • Transit reality: Liernu has no scheduled bus, only an on-demand Flexitec stop. Hire a car — the 4-hourly TEC line 822 to Namur won't fit a dinner schedule.[84][79]
  • Citadel underground: reserve the day before — slots cap and the site explicitly refuses walk-ins.[3]
  • Floreffe beer ≠ Floreffe Abbey: the abbey serves it, but Lefebvre brews it under licence in Quenast since 1983; same caveat for Maredsous (Duvel-Moortgat).[62][63]
