## Academic Conferences

### WSSE 2026 — 8th World Symposium on Software Engineering

**Oct 16–18 · Nara Women's University · ACM proceedings**

Organised by the Science and Engineering Institute and co-sponsored by Nara Women's University, Hiroshima University, Kindai University, and several international institutions. [[5]](https://wsse.org/) Accepted, registered, and presented papers appear in the ACM Conference Proceedings, indexed by Ei Compendex and Scopus. [[6]](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/the-8th-world-symposium-on-software-engineering-wsse-2026-tickets-1984176154290)

Key themes: software architecture and design, software quality and reliability, software testing, AI and intelligent systems, HCI, and data management. [[7]](https://easychair.org/cfp/WSSE2026)

A dedicated special session titled **"AI-Enabled New Paradigms in Software Engineering"** (chair: Hongping Gan) runs alongside the main programme. Select top papers are invited to a *Software* journal special issue on reliability, security, and quality assurance. [[7]](https://easychair.org/cfp/WSSE2026)

**Deadlines (as of June 2026):**

| Milestone            | Date           |
| -------------------- | -------------- |
| Paper submission     | June 25, 2026  |
| Acceptance notice    | July 20, 2026  |
| Registration         | August 5, 2026 |
| Special session CFP  | August 30, 2026|

Submissions via EasyChair at `easychair.org/conferences/?conf=wsse2026`. Contact: `wsse@sciei.org`. [[5]](https://wsse.org/)

### NLPIR 2026 — 10th International Conference on NLP & Information Retrieval

**Dec 11–13 · Nara Women's University · Springer LNNS**

The tenth edition of NLPIR, a recurring conference at the intersection of natural language processing and information retrieval. [[9]](https://www.aiexpertmagazine.com/nlpir-2026-10th-international-conference-on-natural-language-processing-and-information-retrieval-everything-you-need-to-know/) Accepted papers appear in Springer's *Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems* series, indexed by Ei Compendex, Scopus, and INSPEC. [[10]](https://www.allconfs.org/wap/index_en.asp?id=69843)

Topics include: large language models and transformer architectures, machine translation, sentiment analysis, information extraction, question answering, recommender systems, and ethical AI frameworks. The conference also features peer-reviewed poster sessions and workshops. [[9]](https://www.aiexpertmagazine.com/nlpir-2026-10th-international-conference-on-natural-language-processing-and-information-retrieval-everything-you-need-to-know/)

A featured tutorial on **"Brain-inspired Computing"** is led by professors from FernUniversität in Hagen, Germany. [[11]](https://www.zmeeting.org/submission/NLPIR2026)

**Deadlines:**

| Milestone            | Date              |
| -------------------- | ----------------- |
| Paper submission     | June 30, 2026     |
| Acceptance notice    | July 30, 2026     |
| Registration         | August 20, 2026   |

Minimum paper length: 10 pages (single column, English). Papers over 15 pages incur a USD 50/page surcharge. Official website: [nlpir.net](https://www.nlpir.net/). [[8]](https://www.nlpir.net/)

### ICDCN 2026 — 27th International Conference on Distributed Computing and Networking *(Past)*

**Jan 6–9, 2026 · Nara Kasugano International Forum IRAKA · ACM**

Already concluded. The 27th edition drew researchers to a selective programme of 23 papers in six technical sessions. [[3]](https://dblp.org/db/conf/icdcn/icdcn2026.html) Keynote speakers were Robert Tarjan (Princeton, Turing Award winner), Seth Gilbert (NUS), and Kaoru Ota (Tohoku/Muroran). [[1]](https://sites.google.com/view/icdcn2026/) Proceedings are archived in the ACM ICPS. [[2]](https://dl.acm.org/doi/proceedings/10.1145/3772290) The Nara Kasugano International Forum IRAKA — a dedicated venue with a Noh-theatre-style auditorium and simultaneous interpretation for up to four languages — hosted the event. [[4]](https://x.com/icdcn_conf/status/1917174997216354360)

## Community Events

### CoderDojo Nara / Ikoma

[CoderDojo奈良/生駒/平群](https://coderdojo-nara-ikoma.connpass.com/) runs free programming sessions on Saturdays at two Nara-area locations: [[12]](https://coderdojo-nara-ikoma.connpass.com/)

- **Ikoma (生駒)** — Ikoma Citizens' Activity Center ララポート, roughly monthly
- **Nara City (大和西大寺)** — Fushimi Community Center, roughly monthly

Sessions welcome children and adults, with participants bringing their own laptops. The club is part of the global CoderDojo non-profit network. The next listed event is **CoderDojo Ikoma #96** on June 6, 2026. [[13]](https://coderdojo-nara-ikoma.connpass.com/event/388530/) Registration via connpass; seats are limited (21 spots per session).

⚠ Sessions are primarily in Japanese.

## Nara as a Tech Research Location

[NAIST (Nara Institute of Science and Technology)](https://www.naist.jp/en/), located in Ikoma, is a graduate-only national university with schools in Information Science, Biological Sciences, and Material Sciences. [[14]](https://www.naist.jp/en/) [[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nara_Institute_of_Science_and_Technology) It is not primarily a conference-hosting institution for the public, but it is the research anchor behind Nara's presence on the academic circuit — both WSSE and NLPIR involve NAIST-affiliated researchers and use Nara Women's University as their venue.

## Practical Notes

- Both WSSE and NLPIR submission deadlines fall in **late June 2026** — if you are an academic, this is the active submission window.
- Neither conference has published registration fees as of June 2026; contact organisers directly.
- The Nara Kasugano International Forum IRAKA (used by ICDCN) can seat up to ~500 and is a 15-minute walk from Kintetsu Nara Station.
- Nara Women's University (used by WSSE and NLPIR) is a 10-minute walk from Kintetsu Nara Station.
