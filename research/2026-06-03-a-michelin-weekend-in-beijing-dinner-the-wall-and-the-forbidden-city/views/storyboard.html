Day 1 — The Wall
01 EXT · Wide · Establishing 09:00–14:00

Mutianyu Great Wall

60–70% fewer visitors than Badaling. Cable car ascent, 4+ watchtowers along a restored Ming-dynasty section. Plan 3–4 hours on the wall.

¥40 entrance · ¥200 cable car + toboggan combo · 65 km northeast · 90 min each way[11]
02 EXT · Action · Close 13:30
⟶
1,580 m · 40 km/h

The Toboggan Descent

1,580-meter metal slide, 260-meter vertical drop. Fast lane peaks at 40 km/h. A kinetic close to the wall morning — children’s slow lane also available.

Included in cable car + toboggan combo · No separate booking required[12]
03 INT · Transit · Interlude 15:00–18:30
➜
Chaoyang · 90 min

Return to Chaoyang

90-minute private car back to central Beijing. Hotel, refresh, dress. You arrive precisely in the district where six of Beijing’s eight starred restaurants cluster.

No public bus from Mutianyu — private car or day-tour transfer recommended for the return
04 INT · Closeup · The Anchor 19:30
★★

Lamdre

Asia’s 50 Best #17 · Highest Climber 2026 (+33 places) · Seasonality-Driven Plant-Based

Booking Instant on Resy · English interface · No Mandarin or hotel concierge needed[4]
Location Chaoyang · 5-min DiDi from most city hotels
Price Tasting menu approx. ¥988 / person
Why Only Beijing entry in Asia’s 50 Best 2026 · First top-20 Beijing finish ever · Jumped 33 places in a single year[3]
Alternate Picks
Xin Rong Ji ★★★ — Maximum prestige. Phone-only, Mandarin, hotel concierge essential. 28-day-old duck must be pre-ordered at booking. Weeks of lead time.[2]
King’s Joy ★★ — Pair with a Dongcheng day. Courtyard beside Lama Temple. Vegetarian tasting menus ¥999–3,999. Select menu at booking.[5]
Blackswan ★★ — French contemporary inside Luohong Art Museum. Harmony menu ¥1,680 · Freedom ¥1,080. Near Capital Airport (Shunyi) — logical only if PEK is your arrival/departure.[14]
Day 2 — The City
05 EXT · Dawn · Wide 05:30
天安门

Tiananmen Square

Flag-raising ceremony at sunrise. Free admission, but a passport-linked WeChat reservation is required before 22:00 the evening before. Qianmen South Gate for faster security.

⚑ Book by 22:00 the night before · WeChat mini-program · Choose time window (flag-raising / morning / afternoon) · Passport required[10]
06 EXT/INT · Sequence 09:00–13:00

The Forbidden City

40,000 daily cap — the hard constraint that everything else is scheduled around. 3–4 hours for the main meridian axis. Add the Treasure Gallery for an extra ¥10.

⚑ Tickets: 7 days ahead · 20:00 BJT release · ¥60 peak / ¥40 off-peak · Closed Mondays · Original passport required[9]
07 EXT · Panorama · Wide 13:00

Jingshan Pavilion

45-meter artificial hill directly north of the palace. Wanchun Pavilion gives a full rooftop-level view of the Forbidden City’s entire courtyard sequence. The closing wide shot.

10-min walk from Forbidden City north gate · UNESCO Central Axis 2024 · Best light: late afternoon[13]

INSTANT
Lamdre ★★
