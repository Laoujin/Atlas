## Upcoming Events (June 2026 onward)

### BCS 2026 — [Beijing Cyber Security Conference](https://bcs.qianxin.com/en)

Annual international cybersecurity summit hosted by [QI-ANXIN](https://bcs.qianxin.com/en), co-sponsored by China Electronics Corporation and the Internet Society of China [[1]](https://bcs.qianxin.com/en). The 2024 edition (Jun 5-6) ran the theme "AI ∞ Cybersecurity" with 2 summits, 21 forums, ~500 expert speakers, and 2,000+ participating organizations. Exact 2026 dates not yet published — monitor bcs.qianxin.com directly.

**Audience:** Enterprise security practitioners, government cybersecurity staff, product security engineers.

### GDEC 2026 — [Global Digital Economy Conference](https://www.gdec.net.cn/?lang=en)

Opening ceremony July 2 at the Beijing National Convention Center [[2]](https://www.gdec.net.cn/?lang=en). Sixth annual edition themed "Building a Digital-Friendly City — Inclusive Intelligence, Borderless Connectivity" [[3]](https://www.facebook.com/BeijingFocus/posts/the-2026-global-digital-economy-conference-gdec-digital-economy-cooperation-and-/1340106478147535/). Structure: one opening ceremony + six main forums + ~50 thematic breakouts ("1+6+N"). Main forums cover AI integration, data elements, digital security, digital finance, digital health, and digital governance. International satellite event ran in Geneva (Mar 31) ahead of the main Beijing gathering.

**Audience:** Digital economy executives, government policy teams, AI product and data teams.

### Community Over Code Asia 2026

The [Apache Software Foundation](https://asia.communityovercode.org/)'s Asia flagship (formerly ApacheCon Asia), August 7-9 at the Zhongguancun National Innovation Demonstration Zone Conference Center [[4]](https://asia.communityovercode.org/). Nineteen technical tracks: AI, cloud-native, big data, streaming, FinTech, IoT, messaging, search, security, web frameworks, and open-source governance [[5]](https://medium.com/@ApacheDolphinScheduler/see-you-in-beijing-this-august-cfp-for-community-over-code-asia-2026-is-now-open-791d66ed5578). Presentations accepted in English or Chinese; vendor-neutral environment.

**Audience:** Apache committers and contributors, open-source engineering leads, cloud-native practitioners.

### ISC.AI 2026 — Internet Security Conference

[360 Security](https://isc.360.com/index.html)'s annual AI-security summit, historically held at the Beijing National Convention Center in August [[7]](https://isc.360.com/index.html). Fourteenth edition in 2026. The 2025 edition (13th) was themed "All In Agent", focusing on AI agent security [[6]](https://www.facebook.com/beijingdaily19521001/posts/according-to-the-13th-internet-security-and-artificial-intelligence-conference-i/787676510450275/). Cumulative 200,000+ past attendees from 30+ countries. Dates TBC — watch isc.360.com.

**Audience:** Security researchers, red/blue team practitioners, AI security engineers.

### 2nd World Humanoid Robot Games

August 22-26 at the National Speed Skating Oval ("Ice Ribbon") [[8]](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-04-22/Beijing-to-host-2nd-World-Humanoid-Robot-Games-in-August-1MxQtTFEhBm/index.html). Over 30 competitive events across nine disciplines: athletics, football, gymnastics, weightlifting, martial arts, street dance, sport dance, tug-of-war, and pitch-pot [[9]](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202604/1359427.shtml). Not a conference, but the world's leading live showcase of embodied-AI hardware from Chinese and international robotics labs.

**Audience:** Robotics engineers, embodied-AI researchers, hardware investors.

### COSCon 2026 — China Open Source Conference

[Kaiyuanshe](https://opensource.org/blog/top-50-open-source-conferences-in-2026)'s annual open-source community conference [[10]](https://opensource.org/blog/top-50-open-source-conferences-in-2026), December 2026 (exact date TBC; the 2025 edition ran Dec 6-7 at Park Plaza Beijing Science Park). The 2025 edition drew 1,500+ attendees including international guests from OSI, OpenChain, and OpenUK [[11]](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202512/25/WS694d0ab6a310d6866eb3075a.html) — the largest international participation to date.

**Audience:** Open-source developers, community organizers, maintainers, students.

## Already Past in 2026 (Annual — Returns 2027)

### Zhongguancun Forum

Beijing's flagship government-backed science-and-tech summit, founded 2007. The 2026 edition ran March 25-29 [[12]](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/latest/news/202603/t20260319_4561745.html), organized by the Ministry of Science and Technology, NDRC, and MIIT [[13]](https://english.news.cn/20260318/529f35375ad54d4690f1f8b97b676b14/c.html). Theme: "Full Integration Between Technological and Industrial Innovation". 1,000+ guests from 100+ countries, 100+ events including plenary sessions, the ZGC International Technology Trade Fair, and an innovation competition [[14]](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202603/1357181.shtml). Frontier focus areas: 6G, brain-computer interfaces, cell and gene therapy.

### International Sci-Fi & Future Tech Expo Beijing

March 27-29 at Shougang Park (Shijingshan) [[15]](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/latest/news/202603/t20260308_4551646.html), running concurrently with ZGC Forum and the 10th China Science Fiction Convention. 10,000 sqm; 100+ Chinese and international brands showcasing future-tech hardware and the sci-fi industry chain [[16]](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-03-28/Beijing-sci-fi-expo-opens-to-public-1LSvUlI5Nfy/p.html).

### CICNA 2026 — China Intelligent Cloud-Network & AI-Native Summit

April 15-16, Four Points by Sheraton Beijing Haidian Hotel [[17]](https://www.infoexevents.com.cn/chinasdn/). Fifteenth edition; evolved from the China SDN/NFV Conference. Theme: "From Cloud-Native to AI-Native". Topics: autonomous "Observe-Reason-Act" networks, AI chips, 5G-Advanced/6G, 800G-1.2T optics, NaaS monetization. Lead sponsors: Huawei, VIAVI, ZTE.

### InfoComm China 2026

April 15-17, China National Convention Center (CNCC), Olympic Green [[18]](https://www.infocomm-china.com/press-releases/20th-beijing-infocomm-china-2026-takes-place-next-week-top-technology-line-up). 20th anniversary edition; 400+ brands (BARCO, Hisense, Christie), 24,000+ professional visitors [[19]](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/20th-beijing-infocomm-china-2026-takes-place-next-week-top-technology-line-up-302735552.html). Asia's leading Pro AV trade show, with an AI Tech Application Zone; conference sessions aligned to China's 15th Five-Year Plan under the theme "Beyond Connectivity, Strategic Leap 2030" [[22]](https://www.bignewsnetwork.com/news/279084911/beijing-infocomm-china-2026-conference-lineup-presents-future-of-ai-in-tech-aligning-with-the-nation-next-strategic-five-year-plan).

### 28th China Beijing International High-Tech Expo (CHITEC)

May 8-10, CNCC [[20]](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/the-28th-china-beijing-international-high-tech-expo-grand-opening-soon-302759048.html). Theme: "Technology Leads the Way, Innovation Shares the Future". Six exhibition zones: IT, healthcare, green/low-carbon, intelligent manufacturing, digital economy, and regional innovation. 50,000 sqm, 800+ exhibitors [[21]](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/whatson/events/exhibition/202601/t20260130_4478731.html). Highlights: immersive AI ecosystem zone, L4 RoboBus demos, 4D Gaussian Splatting, autonomous driverless tractors.

## Practical Notes

**Key venues.** [CNCC](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/beijinginfo/sci/event/) (China National Convention Center, Olympic Green) is in Chaoyang District — subway Line 8, Olympic Green station. The Zhongguancun Innovation Center is in Haidian District (Beijing's tech corridor) on Line 4.

**Language.** GDEC, InfoComm China, CHITEC, and ISC.AI run bilingual Chinese/English programs. Community Over Code Asia explicitly accepts English submissions and presentations. ZGC Forum has simultaneous interpretation for major plenary sessions.

**Not in Beijing.** Three major China tech events are sometimes confused with Beijing events: [KubeCon + CloudNativeCon China 2026](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-china/) is in Shanghai (Sep 8-9) [[23]](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-china/); WAIC 2026 is in Shanghai (July); [GAITC 2026](https://gaitc.caai.cn/en) (Global AI Technology Conference) is in Hangzhou (May 23-24) [[24]](https://www.caai.cn/site/content/7764.html). All are reachable from Beijing by high-speed rail in 1-5 hours.
