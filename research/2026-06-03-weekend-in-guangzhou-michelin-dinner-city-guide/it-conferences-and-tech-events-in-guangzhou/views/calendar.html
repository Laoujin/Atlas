⚠ Nearby · 30 min by CRH CITE 2026 — April 9–11 — Shenzhen

The 14th China Information Technology Expo, organised by China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology — the highest-profile national IT expo of the year. Coverage: new-generation IT, AI computing and data centres, advanced semiconductors, intelligent terminals, wearables, robotics. Worth adding as a same-trip day when visiting Guangzhou in April; the Canton Fair opens April 15, creating a natural two-event window. [21] [22]

1,800+ exhibitors 150,000 visitors 110,000 sqm Guangzhou South → Shenzhen North: ~30 min

Chinese primary. VR/AR Expo, AICE, and Canton Fair have English-language tracks.
Pre-registration
Required for most expos. Trade-visitor status unlocks full floor and forum access.

Late Oct – early Mar: avoids Canton Fair hotel-rate spikes (Apr 15–May 5 doubles rates citywide).
