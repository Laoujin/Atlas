Getting around

Metro AED 3–8 along Airport → Downtown → Marina. Nol card AED 25 (incl. AED 19 credit). Careem/Uber for Palm Jumeirah, beaches, late nights.

📅

Booking windows

FZN: 1st of month, 10am local · Trèsind: weeks ahead · Burj Khalifa: 7–10 days · Museum of the Future: mandatory pre-book.

💰

June upside

Low-season rates on flights and hotels. Thinner crowds — no competition for prime Burj or Museum slots. Global Village closed until October.
