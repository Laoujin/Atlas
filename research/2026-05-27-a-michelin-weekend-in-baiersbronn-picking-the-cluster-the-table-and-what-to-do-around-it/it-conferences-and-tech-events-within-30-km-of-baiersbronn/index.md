> **TL;DR.** Baiersbronn is too rural for tech conferences. Within 30 km the only recurring program is the IHK Nordschwarzwald **Innovation Breakfast** AI series at the [IHK-Bildungszentrum Nagold](https://www.ihk.de/nordschwarzwald/) [[2]](https://www.ihk.de/nordschwarzwald/innovationn/innovation/innovation-veranstaltungen-und-termine/inno-breakfast-social-media-trends-2026-6825748), plus single-day events at [DHBW Campus Horb][8a] (22.30 km Luftlinie [[1]](https://www.luftlinie.org/Horb-am-necker/Baiersbronn)) [[8]](https://www.iket-horb.de/2026/01/30/horber-werkzeugtag-2026/). For a real international IT conference you have to cross the line: **[WFCS 2026](https://www.hs-offenburg.de/wfcs2026/en/welcome)** (IEEE Factory Communication Systems) in Offenburg, 21–24 Apr 2026 — 31.91 km Luftlinie [[15]](https://www.luftlinie.org/Offenburg/Baiersbronn) — is the nearest serious option [[12]](https://www.hs-offenburg.de/wfcs2026/en/welcome).

## What "within 30 km" actually covers

Baiersbronn sits at 48.50° N, 8.37° E. The 30 km Luftlinie circle includes Freudenstadt, Alpirsbach, Bad Wildbad, Horb am Neckar (22.30 km [[1]](https://www.luftlinie.org/Horb-am-necker/Baiersbronn)) and Nagold. Offenburg falls just outside at 31.91 km Luftlinie [[15]](https://www.luftlinie.org/Offenburg/Baiersbronn); Bad Herrenalb, Calw and Pforzheim are further again. Driving distances are 1.5–2× the straight line because of the forest topography — the road from Baiersbronn to Offenburg via Freudenstadt/Hausach runs closer to 50 km.

**Reality check on the Werkzeugtag.** It's a Kunststoff-/Werkzeugbau tagung, not classic IT — but the 22nd edition explicitly added "industrielle Automation" to its scope, which is where it touches manufacturing IT/IIoT [[8]](https://www.iket-horb.de/2026/01/30/horber-werkzeugtag-2026/). Next edition: 28 Jan 2027 [[9]](https://www.iket-horb.de/kunststoff-polymer-veranstaltungen/fachtagung-horber-werkzeugtag/).

**Reality check on the Digital Hub.** The funded project ended at the close of 2025; the partner network (Wirtschaftsförderung Nordschwarzwald + DHBW + IHK) keeps the formats running into 2026, but ad-hoc rather than on a published calendar [[11]](https://nordschwarzwald.de/der-digital-hub-nordschwarzwald-geht-die-digitalisierung-bleibt/). The Horb location's regular event calendar shows no 2026 dates as of May 2026.

**No dedicated developer/Meetup scene.** Freudenstadt, Baiersbronn, Bad Wildbad and Alpirsbach return no IT or developer meetups for 2026 — the towns have conference infrastructure (Kurhaus, Forum König-Karls-Bad) but no tech community using it. Use the closest big cities (Karlsruhe, Stuttgart, Strasbourg) for that.

## Picking one if you're already in Baiersbronn

- **You want a real research conference.** WFCS 2026 in Offenburg [[12]](https://www.hs-offenburg.de/wfcs2026/en/welcome), 21–24 Apr — TSN, industrial wireless, JCAS, edge IIoT and AGV/UAV real-time comms [[13]](https://www.hs-offenburg.de/wfcs2026/en/call-and-dates). ~80 attendees from twelve countries [[23]](https://www.stadtanzeiger-ortenau.de/offenburg-stadt/c-lokales/wirtschaftlicher-erfolg-von-morgen_a122678); IEEE-quality but small enough to talk to authors. First time in Offenburg [[14]](https://www.hs-offenburg.de/en/research-and-technology-transfer/iaf-services-for-researchers/crt-events/article/wfcs-findet-erstmals-in-offenburg-statt). Registration closes 13 Apr [[12]](https://www.hs-offenburg.de/wfcs2026/en/welcome).
- **You want regulatory/policy.** 112. Datenschutzkonferenz, Bad Herrenalb, 24–26 Nov 2026 [[17]](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/ueberblick/datenschutz-konferenz-ki-und-digitalisierung-bestimmen-agenda-2026/68489473). LfDI Baden-Württemberg chairs the year, AI Act implementation is the headline theme [[16]](https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de/lfdi-vorsitz-dsk-2026-pm/). Closed authority meeting — not an open conference — but the run-up produces public position papers worth tracking.
- **You want hands-on.** Black Forest Hackathon Smart Automation, 16–18 Oct 2026, Offenburg [[18]](https://www.blackforesthackathon.de/) — 48-hour weekend format. The Pentest (12–14 Jun) and Smart Health (4–6 Dec) editions are in Freiburg, too far for a Baiersbronn base.
- **You want SME-style local content.** IHK Nagold's Innovation Breakfast cycle [[3]](https://www.ihk.de/nordschwarzwald/innovationn/innovation/innovation-veranstaltungen-und-termine/inno-breakfast-ki-einfach-erklaert-reihe-2026--6930908) — ~2 hour morning sessions on practical AI use, free to attend (the KI-Führerschein on 9 Jul is the paid exception at 250 € [[6]](https://www.ihk.de/nordschwarzwald/system/veranstaltungssuche/vstdetail-tibros/5872700/121?terminId=26454)). Walk-in level, not deep technical.
- **You're at a university.** DHBW Stuttgart's Horb campus runs the dual Informatik bachelor with ~200 partner companies [[24]](https://www.dhbw-stuttgart.de/horb/) — the May Schnupperstudium [[22]](https://www.dhbw-stuttgart.de/horb/campus-horb/veranstaltungen/) is the only campus event tagged "Informatik" in 2026.

## What doesn't exist

- No DevOps / cloud / SaaS conferences in the 30 km circle in 2026.
- No security-track conferences (CCC-style or otherwise) in the circle — closest is HS Pforzheim's KI-Hackathon and the BfH Pentest weekend in Freiburg.
- No regular tech meetups in Freudenstadt or Baiersbronn — none registered on dev.events or Meetup.com for 2026.
- The Käpsele festival (Freiburg) [[19]](https://www.innovation-festival.de/en) and any Karlsruhe/Stuttgart events fall well outside both 30 km and a reasonable day-trip from a Michelin-restaurant weekend.
