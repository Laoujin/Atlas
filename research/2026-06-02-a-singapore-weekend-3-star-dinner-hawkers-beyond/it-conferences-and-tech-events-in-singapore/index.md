## Upcoming Events

### SuperAI — Jun 10–11

Part of [Singapore AI Week](https://www.superai.com/) (Jun 8–14) [[7]](https://www.superai.com/). 10,000+ attendees, 150+ speakers from OpenAI, Nvidia, Anthropic, Meta, Apple, and DeepMind; early-bird passes at $499 USD [[7]](https://www.superai.com/). Two competitions run alongside: **NEXT Hackathon** ($130k+ prize pool, 200 builders) and **Genesis Startup Competition** ($850k prize pool, backed by OpenAI and Microsoft for Startups) [[7]](https://www.superai.com/).

### PyCon Singapore 2026 — Jun 19–21

At the [Lifelong Learning Institute](https://pycon.sg/) near Paya Lebar MRT [[8]](https://pycon.sg/). Keynotes from Google, NUS, Python Software Foundation, and Hugging Face; sessions span Python, ML/AI, and open source; hackathon included [[8]](https://pycon.sg/). Community pricing (not yet announced).

### Tech Week Singapore — Sep 29–30

Five co-located events at Marina Bay Sands [[9]](https://www.singaporetechnologyweek.com/): [Cloud & AI Infrastructure Asia](https://www.singaporetechnologyweek.com/cloud-ai-infrastructure), DevOps Live!, [Cyber Security World Asia](https://www.singaporetechnologyweek.com/cyber-security-world), Data Centre World Asia, and Big Data & AI World Asia. Targets cloud architects, DevOps engineers, security professionals, and data centre leaders across APAC. Call for speakers currently open [[9]](https://www.singaporetechnologyweek.com/).

### TOKEN2049 — Oct 7–8

[TOKEN2049](https://www.token2049.com/singapore) bills itself as the world's largest crypto conference [[10]](https://www.token2049.com/singapore). 25,000+ attendees from 7,000+ companies across 160 countries; 300+ speakers including Vitalik Buterin; 500+ exhibitors across all five floors of Marina Bay Sands [[10]](https://www.token2049.com/singapore). Side events: **TOKEN2049 Origins** hackathon (160 developers, $50k prize pool) and **Nexus** startup competition. Closing party Oct 9 at MBS SkyPark.

### SWITCH 2026 — Oct 27–29

[Singapore Week of Innovation and Technology](https://www.switchsg.org/) — **free** attendee passes [[11]](https://www.switchsg.org/). 400+ exhibitors, 30 global pavilions. Flagship activity: **SLINGSHOT** deep-tech pitching competition with S$2M+ in grant prizes — **application deadline Jun 30, 2026** [[11]](https://www.switchsg.org/). Stages cover AI, robotics, and quantum (SWITCH Beyond), market expansion (SWITCH Global), and startup–industry matchmaking (SWITCH Converge).

### Singapore FinTech Festival — Nov 18–20

[SFF](https://www.fintechfestival.sg/) at Singapore EXPO (Halls 1–6) + Sands Expo — the world's largest fintech festival by attendance [[12]](https://www.fintechfestival.sg/). 70,000+ participants from 142 countries; 600+ exhibitors; 41 international pavilions [[12]](https://www.fintechfestival.sg/). 2026 focus themes: New Payments Settlement Layer, Intelligent Financial Systems, New Encryption Architecture. Early-bird SGD 750 (deadline Jul 31) [[12]](https://www.fintechfestival.sg/). Includes Global FinTech Hackcelerator and Investor Hours programme.

### STACK Conference 2026 — H2 (date TBD)

[GovTech Singapore's](https://www.developer.tech.gov.sg/communities/events/conferences/stack-conference-2026/overview) biennial developer conference and the largest government-led tech event in Singapore [[13]](https://www.developer.tech.gov.sg/communities/events/conferences/stack-conference-2026/overview). STACK 2024 drew 1,900+ in-person attendees [[13]](https://www.developer.tech.gov.sg/communities/events/conferences/stack-conference-2026/overview). 2026 tracks: AI autonomy beyond chatbots, data-enabled UX, intent-driven development/engineering velocity, cloud sustainability & resilience, and digital safety & inclusivity. Speaker proposals currently open; date not yet announced.
