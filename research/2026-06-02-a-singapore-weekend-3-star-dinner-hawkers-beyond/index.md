The first thing this run surfaced is a hard constraint: **Zén closed for "renewal" on June 2, 2026 — no reopen date announced.** [[1]](https://restaurantzen.com) The three-restaurant brief collapsed to a two-way decision the day research started.

**The Odette vs Les Amis fork is a Saturday scheduling question as much as a cuisine one.** Odette's last seating is 8:15 PM [[2]](https://www.odetterestaurant.com); Les Amis's is 9:30 PM [[3]](https://www.lesamis.com.sg). That 75-minute gap defines two structurally different days:

- *Odette arc* — cultural-neighbourhood walk (Kampong Glam or Chinatown) until 5 PM → one cocktail at Jigger & Pony (Asia's #3, World's #9) [[4]](things-to-do-in-singapore/) → National Gallery by 7:45 PM. The dinner is the closing act; nothing after.
- *Les Amis arc* — afternoon at Gardens by the Bay or MacRitchie → Maxwell hawker lunch (Tian Tian, Bib Gourmand) [[4]](things-to-do-in-singapore/) → Atlas aperitivo (Art Deco lobby, 1,300+ gin labels) [[4]](things-to-do-in-singapore/) → Orchard by 8:30 PM → dinner runs past midnight with the wine list to explore.

The cuisine gap is real but narrow. Odette (Julien Royer) is French-Asian: kampot pepper pigeon and a chilli-crab bun bring Singapore into the plate [[2]](https://www.odetterestaurant.com); the dining room was renovated December 2025. Les Amis (Sébastien Lepinoy, 17 years under Joël Robuchon) is classical French [[3]](https://www.lesamis.com.sg) with 1,800+ labels in one of Asia's deepest French wine cellars — and the 9:30 PM last seating means the wine program has time to breathe. At SGD 498++ vs SGD 565++ the price difference is minor against the overall trip cost.

**Saturday and Sunday split cleanly around the dinner.** The things-to-do research [[4]](things-to-do-in-singapore/) maps directly onto the two days: Saturday is urban (hawker lunch, cultural stroll, bar pre-game, dinner); Sunday morning is nature — Singapore Botanic Gardens (UNESCO, free beyond the orchid garden) or the MacRitchie TreeTop Walk (free, 250 m suspension bridge). Sentosa works as a full Sunday if the group includes kids: the Singapore Oceanarium reopened July 2025 at 3× its former size [[4]](things-to-do-in-singapore/) and Universal Studios added Minion Land in February 2025 [[4]](things-to-do-in-singapore/).

**The tech-events calendar adds a trip-timing angle the original brief didn't request — but it matters.** SuperAI runs June 10–11 [[5]](https://www.superai.com), one week after the nearest Saturday (June 7). A five-day stay covering June 6–7 (dinner weekend) through June 10–11 (SuperAI, 10,000+ attendees, OpenAI/Nvidia/Anthropic speakers) turns a weekend trip into a justified work trip. TOKEN2049 (October 7–8, 25,000+ crypto attendees) [[6]](https://www.token2049.com/singapore) and SWITCH (October 27–29, free admission, S$2M+ deep-tech grants) [[7]](https://www.switchsg.org) offer the same stacking logic in Q4.

The cross-cutting question the children don't resolve: **is the priority the dinner itself, or the hours around it?** That answer determines Odette vs Les Amis more cleanly than any comparison of cuisine styles.
