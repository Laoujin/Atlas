> **TL;DR.** Two days, anchored on the Michelin dinner you've already booked. Day 1 inside the Ring: Stephansdom at 09:00 [[9]](https://www.findingtheuniverse.com/a-2-day-vienna-itinerary/), Hofburg/Sisi (€20 day ticket) [[2]](https://www.sisimuseum-hofburg.at/en/tickets/tickets-prices), a long Melange at **Café Sperl** or **Café Bräunerhof** [[37]](https://www.wien.info/en/dine-drink/coffeehouses/top-traditional-coffee-houses-in-vienna-361666) [[42]](https://jewishviennesefood.com/vienna-bucket-list/vienna-20-plus-1-iconic-coffee-houses-kaffeehaus-guide/) — **not Café Central**, which closed in March 2026 for a 150th-anniversary renovation and reopens autumn 2026 anyway [[34]](https://thechillreport.com/en/2026/03/16/cafe-central-in-vienna-closed-reopening-in-fall-2026/). Day 2: Schönbrunn at opening (timed-slot mandatory) [[1]](https://www.schoenbrunn.at/en/tickets-and-prices/all-tickets-tours), then tram D to **Upper Belvedere** for Klimt's *The Kiss* [[4]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/museums/belvedere-sites/kiss/). If one museum slot: **Kunsthistorisches** (Bruegel, Vermeer, the Saliera) on Thursday late-night when the Bruegel room empties [[12]](https://www.khm.at/en/visit) [[15]](https://www.wien-tickets.com/kunsthistorisches-museum/opening-hours-timings/). Evening that isn't the Michelin: €13-€18 standing-room at the **Staatsoper** (the season runs until 30 June 2026) [[45]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/opera/cheap-tickets/) [[48]](https://www.wiener-staatsoper.at/en/season-25-26/), or the free outdoor LED broadcast on Karajan-Platz [[49]](https://www.wiener-staatsoper.at/ihr-besuch/service/oper-live-am-platz/refDate/2017-01-01/). Refuse every powdered-wig "Mozart concert" tout on Stephansplatz [[54]](https://tabiji.ai/scams/vienna/).

Hard rules drawn from the official sites and recent itineraries:

- **Schönbrunn requires a fixed time slot** booked on imperialtickets.com — slots fill hours ahead, so arrive at opening [[1]](https://www.schoenbrunn.at/en/tickets-and-prices/all-tickets-tours) [[9]](https://www.findingtheuniverse.com/a-2-day-vienna-itinerary/). The Grand Tour is the consensus pick over the Imperial Tour — same rooms up to the Great Gallery, but only the Grand continues into the significant private apartments [[8]](https://community.ricksteves.com/travel-forum/austria/schonbrunn-imperial-vs-grand-tour) [[95]](https://skiptheline.tickets/austria/vienna/schonbrunn-palace/) [[96]](https://www.bigworldsmallpockets.com/schonbrunn-palace-tickets/). If slots are gone, the Baroque gardens, Neptune Fountain and the walk up to the Gloriette arch are free year-round — only the Gloriette terrace, Maze, Privy Garden and Orangery cost extra [[11]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/schonbrunn/gloriette/).
- **The Hofburg Silver Collection has been closed since 1 April 2023** and is still shuttered in 2026 — drop it from any older guide you read [[2]](https://www.sisimuseum-hofburg.at/en/tickets/tickets-prices).
- **Klimt's *The Kiss* is at the Upper Belvedere, 1st floor, Gallery 10**, anchoring the world's largest single Klimt collection (24 paintings) — Lower Belvedere holds rotating temporaries and is skippable on a tight weekend [[4]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/museums/belvedere-sites/kiss/) [[31]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/footsteps/gustav-klimt/). Both buildings require timed slots [[3]](https://www.belvedere.at/en/visit).
- **Stephansdom**: nave free; South Tower is a 343-step climb (no lift) for €6 with the best panoramic view; North Tower has a lift, shows the Pummerin bell and the famous roof tiles for €5; the 25-30 min catacombs tour is €8 and reveals Rudolf IV's tomb plus urns of Habsburg organs in the Ducal Chamber [[5]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/south-tower-stephansdom/) [[6]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/stefansdom/) [[7]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/stephansdom-catacombs/).
- **Spanish Riding School Morning Exercise** (training-only, in the Baroque Winter Riding School inside the Hofburg, weekday mornings ~10:00-12:00) is the cheaper drop-in if you want Lipizzaners without booking a full performance [[10]](https://www.srs.at/en/tickets/morning-exercise).

## If you have one museum slot

**Kunsthistorisches.** Nothing else competes with the world's largest Bruegel concentration, Vermeer's *The Art of Painting* (owned by the Austrian Republic, on permanent display) [[13]](https://www.khm.at/en/artworks/the-art-of-painting-2574), and the Kunstkammer's ~2,200 Habsburg curiosities anchored by Cellini's gold *Saliera* salt cellar [[14]](https://www.khm.at/en/exhibitions/kunstkammer). Go Thursday — late opening until 21:00 is when the Bruegel room finally empties [[15]](https://www.wien-tickets.com/kunsthistorisches-museum/opening-hours-timings/).

Notes worth knowing before you queue:

- **Albertina** at €19.90 mixes Dürer/Monet graphic holdings with a permanent Monet-to-Picasso show; a Picasso-Bacon retrospective opens September 2026 [[16]](https://www.albertina.at/en/visit/tickets/) [[17]](https://www.albertina.at/en/exhibitions/monet-to-picasso/).

It owns the Schiele collection Rudolf and Elisabeth Leopold assembled over 50 years, plus Klimt's *Death and Life*, *Am Attersee*, Kokoschka and a Wiener Werkstätte floor [[20]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/museums/leopold-museum/) [[21]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g190454-d2199859-Reviews-Leopold_Museum-Vienna.html). It is the strongest single venue in the MuseumsQuartier complex [[28]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sightseeing/vienna-museums/mq/).
- **MAK** is in Heinrich von Ferstel's 1871 neo-Renaissance building and permanently displays Klimt's nine surviving full-scale Stoclet Frieze cartoons [[24]](https://www.mak.at/en/program/exhibitions/gustav_klimt_expectation_and_fulfillment) [[25]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/museums/mak/); standard adult €15.50 [[99]](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/mak-austrian-museum-of-applied-arts-contemporary-art-vienna-5283/tickets-prices), open Tue 10-21 then Wed-Sun 10-18, closed Mon [[98]](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/mak-austrian-museum-of-applied-arts-contemporary-art-vienna-5283/opening-hours).
- **Weltmuseum** holds Montezuma's quetzal-feather *Penacho* and Cook voyage objects, but reviewers want 3-4 hours including the medieval armoury and ancient-instruments collection upstairs [[26]](https://www.weltmuseumwien.at/en/information/) [[27]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/museums/khm/weltmuseum/) — too much for a weekend. €16 adult, ticket also admits the KHM Imperial Armoury and Historic Musical Instruments collection [[97]](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/weltmuseum-wien-vienna-5326/tickets-prices).
- **Freud Museum** at Berggasse 19 is atmospheric rather than object-rich after a recent renovation; an AR reconstruction stands in for the missing famous couch [[22]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/museums/sigmund-freud/) [[23]](https://saltertonartsreview.com/2026/04/sigmund-freud-museum-vienna/).
- **Wien Museum** Karlsplatz reopened December 2023 after a 4-year rebuild with free permanent admission — a first for Vienna [[30]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sightseeing/vienna-museums/wien-museum/). Tue-Fri 9-18, Thu late to 21, Sat-Sun 10-18 [[100]](https://www.wien.info/en/art-culture/museums-exhibitions/museum-karlsplatz-352826); special exhibitions €8-€12, free first Sunday of the month [[101]](https://whichmuseum.com/museum/wien-museum-karlsplatz-vienna-5358/tickets-prices).
- The **MQ Libelle rooftop** on top of the Leopold is free and open March-October — coffee with a Vienna view that doesn't cost anything [[29]](https://www.wien.info/en/art-culture/museums-exhibitions/top-museums/mq).

## Coffee houses: which are still real

The Viennese coffee house was inscribed on Austria's UNESCO intangible-heritage list in October 2011, defined by the line "time and space are consumed, but only the coffee is on the bill" [[32]](https://www.unesco.at/en/culture/intangible-cultural-heritage/national-inventory/news-1/article/viennese-coffee-house-culture) [[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viennese_coffee_house_culture). Etiquette: a single coffee buys you hours at the table, staff leave you alone until you flag them, and a glass of tap water arrives with every order and is refilled on long stays [[35]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/eatingdrinking/coffee-house-experience/) [[39]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/eatingdrinking/food/coffee-glossary/).

**The 2026 change you need to know:** Café Central closed 16 March 2026 for a comprehensive renovation in its 150th-anniversary year and reopens autumn 2026; a pop-up called DECENTRAL operates at Palais Harrach on the Freyung in the meantime [[34]](https://thechillreport.com/en/2026/03/16/cafe-central-in-vienna-closed-reopening-in-fall-2026/). Forum locals call it a tourist trap "spoiled by influencers" with long queues even when open [[36]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g190454-i147-k14191864-Cafe_central_or_other_coffee_houses-Vienna.html) — the closure is no loss.

What to order — never just "a coffee" [[39]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/eatingdrinking/food/coffee-glossary/):

- **Melange** — espresso topped with steamed milk and a little foam (less milk than a cappuccino) [[38]](https://www.oesterreichinstitut.com/en/austrian-coffee-types-what-are-melange-einspaenner-co/).
- **Einspänner** — black coffee under a generous dome of whipped cream, served in a glass; named after one-horse carriages whose drivers favoured it [[38]](https://www.oesterreichinstitut.com/en/austrian-coffee-types-what-are-melange-einspaenner-co/).
- **Kapuziner** — black coffee with a dash of milk; named after the Capuchin friars' brown robes [[38]](https://www.oesterreichinstitut.com/en/austrian-coffee-types-what-are-melange-einspaenner-co/).

The **Sacher-Demel feud** is real: Franz Sacher invented the cake in 1832 at 16, for Prince Metternich; his grandson sold rights to Demel during financial trouble, and the legal fight ended 1963 — Hotel Sacher won the "Original Sacher-Torte" name with two sponge layers; Demel kept its single-layer recipe sold as "Demel's Sachertorte" [[40]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachertorte) [[41]](https://www.tasteatlas.com/the-century-long-cake-war-demel-vs-sacher-and-the-fight-for-viennas-sweet-crown). Try one or both; the queue is part of the deal.

## Music: the Stehplatz move

The Wiener Staatsoper 2025/26 season runs 3 September 2025 to 30 June 2026 [[48]](https://www.wiener-staatsoper.at/en/season-25-26/), so a late-May 2026 weekend lands firmly in season. The signature visitor move is standing-room (*Stehplatz*).

| ------------------------------ | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Staatsoper][so]               | €13-€18       | Parterre €18 / Galerie €15 / Balkon €13; surtitle screen each spot |
| [Musikverein Goldener Saal][mv]| 300 places    | Tickets ~€25-€150; Vienna Philharmonic subs have ~13-yr waitlist |
| [Konzerthaus][kh]              | varies        | Home of Wiener Symphoniker; ~750 events Sept-June              |
| [Volksoper][vo]                | (seated only) | Vienna's only operetta house; English surtitles on selected shows |

[so]: https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/opera/cheap-tickets/
[mv]: https://www.musikverein.at/
[kh]: https://konzerthaus.at/en
[vo]: https://www.volksoper.at/premieres/season-2025-2026.en.html

How Stehplatz actually works in 2026:

1. **Online from 10:00 on the day of the show**, max 2 per transaction [[46]](https://www.moonhoneytravel.com/get-stehplatz-tickets-vienna-state-opera/). Or in person at the dedicated Stehplatz-Kasse on the Operngasse side, which opens **80 minutes before curtain** [[45]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/opera/cheap-tickets/).
2. **The veteran tactic**: arrive ~2 hours before showtime, then tie a scarf, T-shirt or necktie to the rail to claim your standing spot before exploring the foyers [[47]](https://www.whereverwriter.com/standing-room-tickets-vienna-opera-house/).
3. **Sold out or shoes hurting?** *Oper live am Platz* broadcasts 80+ performances per season free on a 50 m² LED wall on Herbert-von-Karajan-Platz during April-June and September, with 180 chairs [[49]](https://www.wiener-staatsoper.at/ihr-besuch/service/oper-live-am-platz/refDate/2017-01-01/).

**Musikverein** Goldener Saal seats 1,744 with 300 standing places and is the home of the Vienna Philharmonic; tickets range €25-€150, box office Mon-Fri 9-19 / Sat 9-13 [[50]](https://www.musikverein.at/). Philharmonic subscriptions have a ~13-year waiting list — chase returned tickets, Konzerthaus dates, Theater an der Wien appearances, or the annual free Schönbrunn open-air gala [[51]](https://vienna-unwrapped.com/vienna-philharmonic-orchestra-get-concert-tickets/). **Konzerthaus** at Lothringerstraße 20 stages ~750 events Sept-June across three halls [[52]](https://konzerthaus.at/en). **Volksoper** at Währingerstraße 78 (U6) is the only operetta house, with English surtitles on selected shows [[53]](https://www.volksoper.at/premieres/season-2025-2026.en.html).

> ⚠ **Hard avoid: the powdered-wig "Mozart concert" touts.** Costumed sellers in wigs and red frock coats work Stephansplatz, Graben, Kärntner Strasse, Opernring and the Hofburg, selling €55-€85 tickets to commission-rigged student-ensemble concerts in much smaller venues than the brochure shows [[54]](https://tabiji.ai/scams/vienna/). Tourist complaints confirm the pattern — promised "Mozart & Strauss with ballet and orchestra in Palais Palffy" turns out to be a dilapidated venue with no room for ballet or orchestra [[55]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g190454-d4356378-r735503333-Vienna_Walzer_Concerts-Vienna.html). If you genuinely want a Strauss/Mozart greatest-hits night, book the legitimate [Wiener Hofburg Orchester](https://www.hofburgorchester.at/en/strauss-mozart-concerts/) direct (Hofburg Zeremoniensaal + Konzerthaus Mozart-Saal, Tue/Thu/Sat May to mid-October) [[56]](https://www.hofburgorchester.at/en/strauss-mozart-concerts/).

## Eating around the Michelin anchor

Your Michelin dinner is one slot. The weekend needs five other meals.

### Naschmarkt as a Saturday-morning anchor

[Naschmarkt](https://www.wien.info/en/dine-drink/markets/naschmarkt-353536) has ~130 stalls Mon-Sat from 6am, food vendors to 11pm, with the **Saturday flea-market 6:30am-2pm** extension worth timing the weekend around [[57]](https://www.wien.info/en/dine-drink/markets/naschmarkt-353536). Locals shop the southern *Linke Wienzeile* side — less touristy, better value [[58]](https://www.myguidevienna.com/travel-articles/naschmarkt-wien). Standout names: **Urbanek** (cheese, charcuterie, a glass of wine at the counter), **Umar** (fish), **Neni** (Israeli-Oriental), **Tewa** (organic plates) [[57]](https://www.wien.info/en/dine-drink/markets/naschmarkt-353536) [[58]](https://www.myguidevienna.com/travel-articles/naschmarkt-wien).

### Würstelstand — quick fillers between sights

Vienna's sausage-stand culture was inscribed on UNESCO's intangible-heritage list in 2024 [[60]](https://www.wien.info/en/dine-drink/viennese-cuisine/hot-dog-stands-348128).

| [Zum Scharfen René][sr]            | Mon-Fri 11am-4am; **closed weekends** | Chili-loaded sausages; award-winning curry sauce  |
| [Würstelstand Leo][wl]             | Mon-Sat 10am-1am; Sun 12-mid.  | Vienna's oldest (1928); outside the tourist zone     |

[bz]: https://bitzinger.wien/en/wuerstelstand/
[sr]: https://viennawurstelstand.com/guide/the-11-best-vienna-wurstelstande-you-have-to-experience/
[wl]: https://wuerstelstandleo.at/en/

Bitzinger has run the Albertinaplatz stand since 1999 — Sepp Bitzinger develops each sausage himself and the original Käsekrainer is the house signature [[59]](https://bitzinger.wien/en/wuerstelstand/). Leo on Döblinger Gürtel, founded 1928 by Leopold Mlynek, is the oldest in town; the "BIG MAMA" Käsekrainer platter feeds up to four [[61]](https://wuerstelstandleo.at/en/). Hours matter — Zum scharfen René is closed weekends [[62]](https://viennawurstelstand.com/guide/the-11-best-vienna-wurstelstande-you-have-to-experience/).

### Beisl — the traditional sit-down

For a casual lunch or non-Michelin dinner, [Figlmüller](https://www.figlmueller.at/en/) (Wollzeile original or Bäckerstraße sister) plates the 30 cm pounded-veal schnitzel (~250g, overhangs the plate) and claims to have invented the dish in 1905 [[63]](https://www.figlmueller.at/en/). [Plachutta Wollzeile](https://www.plachutta.at/en/menu-wollzeile/) is the Tafelspitz address — boiled-beef cut served in a copper pot with marrow bone, root vegetables, bouillon, apple-horseradish and chive sauce [[64]](https://www.plachutta.at/en/menu-wollzeile/). Vienna Tourism's broader Beisl shortlist adds Beim Czaak (1st), Glacis Beisl at the MuseumsQuartier (7th), Steman (6th), Gasthaus Wild (3rd), Ubl (4th) and Café Anzengruber (4th) [[65]](https://www.wien.info/en/dine-drink/viennese-cuisine/beisl-340946).

### Pastry side-quests

Aida's pink-interior mid-century Konditorei is reliable and cheap; Heiner is the conservative classic for Kardinalschnitte [[69]](https://jewishviennesefood.com/best-of-jewish-viennese-food/pastry-shops-viennas-10-very-best-must-visit-patisseries-and-confectioneries-konditorei/). Café Landtmann is widely cited as the best Kaiserschmarrn in the city [[70]](https://community.ricksteves.com/travel-forum/austria/deserts-in-vienna).

## Heuriger: the Sunday-afternoon move

A *Heuriger* is a wine tavern serving the producer's own current-vintage wine. **Skip Grinzing** unless you actively want tour-bus territory. Go to **Nussdorf** for restaurant-grade (Mayer am Pfarrplatz — Beethoven's old house, in business as a Heuriger since 1683, award-winning estate wines) [[66]](https://www.pfarrplatz.at/en/) or cross the Danube to **Stammersdorf** (21st district) for the most tourist-free pours [[67]](https://viennatrips.com/blog/heurigen-vienna-wine-taverns). Mauer is a middle path.

Order **Gemischter Satz** — Vienna's signature DAC wine, a field-blend of grapes co-planted and co-fermented [[68]](https://www.myguidevienna.com/vienna-wine-heuriger-guide). **Sturm** (partially-fermented grape must) appears only September-October during harvest, so it's not on the menu for late-May/June. **Tram D** runs from the Ring directly to Nussdorf [[68]](https://www.myguidevienna.com/vienna-wine-heuriger-guide).

## Prater: the evening / morning-walk option

Two Praters, not one. **Tourists** mean the Wurstelprater funfair around the [Wiener Riesenrad](https://www.wienerriesenrad.com/en/private-luxury-cabins/candle-light-dinner) — 65 m tall, 1897, world's oldest surviving Ferris wheel of its type, daily 09:00-23:45, €14.50 [[85]](https://www.wien.info/en/see-do/prater/giant-ferris-wheel-346994). **Viennese** mean the **Green Prater** — a ~6 km² recreational area nearly twice the size of Central Park, anchored by the 4.5 km Hauptallee chestnut-tree avenue, with skate park, sports meadows, and Tram 1 from Schwedenplatz dropping you at the door [[88]](https://www.austria.info/en-gb/locations/prater/) [[89]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/prater/) [[90]](https://www.wien.info/en/see-do/prater).

For a Vienna weekend, two ways to use it:

- **Evening Riesenrad spin + sausage** — wheel ride (~12-20 min at 2.7 km/h) then [Bitzinger's Prater Würstelstand](https://prater.at/en/foodanddrinks/bitzinger/) at the wheel's foot for Käsekrainer or Bosna; the stand runs 10:00-01:00 Mon-Wed/Sun and 10:00-04:00 Thu-Sat [[91]](https://prater.at/en/foodanddrinks/bitzinger/). U1/U2 to Praterstern, 5-min walk under the railway arches; budget ~2 hours including a Schweizerhaus beer [[92]](https://thingstodoinvienna.com/landmark/prater-and-riesenrad/). The thrill add-on is Platform No. 9 — a glass-bottomed, wall-less platform attachment open since May 2022 [[94]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/riesenrad/).
- **Morning walk on the Hauptallee** — the locals' Prater. Combine with a Danube Canal-to-Prater walk up Praterstraße through Leopoldstadt past Café Balthasar and Supersense at Dogenhof [[93]](https://viennawurstelstand.com/location/guide-to-leopoldstadt-praterstrasse/).

The candlelight-dinner-in-a-cabin upsell exists — 90 min, 3 courses + sparkling wine + pairing, ~€507 for two, book 6-8 weeks ahead [[87]](https://www.wienerriesenrad.com/en/private-luxury-cabins/candle-light-dinner) — but it's a one-off gimmick that competes with the Michelin anchor, not complements it. Skip it. Main-season Wurstelprater rides typically run 11:00-23:00 (some until midnight or later weekends) March 15-October 31 [[86]](https://www.aohostels.com/en/traveltips/vienna-prater-at-night/).

## Getting in, getting around

**Airport → city:** the [S7](https://www.wien.info/en/travel-info/arrival-departure/airport-to-center/express-train-s7-345644) from VIE to Wien Mitte/Landstrasse takes 20-22 min and costs **€5.40** (€2.20 airport-to-boundary + €3.20 within Vienna) [[72]](https://www.wien.info/en/travel-info/arrival-departure/airport-to-center/express-train-s7-345644). The [CAT](https://nomadepicureans.com/europe/austria/vienna-airport-transfer-2026cat/) is **€14.90** nonstop in 16 min — six extra minutes for triple the price; S7 wins unless you need the in-city airline check-in [[73]](https://nomadepicureans.com/europe/austria/vienna-airport-transfer-2026cat/).

## Late May / early June 2026 — what's actually on

- **Wiener Festwochen 2026** runs **28 May - 22 June** (75th anniversary, theme "Republic of Gods", 1000+ venues including Volkstheater, MAK and MuseumsQuartier) [[78]](https://www.festwochen.at/home) — your weekend lands inside this.
- **Vienna Pride Regenbogenparade** is **13 June 2026**; the Pride Village is a single-day event the same day due to budget cuts [[79]](https://www.meinbezirk.at/wien/c-lokales/diese-events-darfst-du-2026-in-wien-nicht-verpassen_a7879708).
- **Donauinselfest** is **3-5 July 2026** — a late-May/early-June weekend misses it [[80]](https://www.wien.info/en/now-on/events/danube-island-festival-2025-1118592).
- **Weather:** June is Vienna's wettest month — 74mm of rain across 14 days, afternoon highs ~23 °C, lows ~12 °C [[81]](https://www.holiday-weather.com/vienna/averages/june/). Pack a compact umbrella; do not skip the umbrella for a fancy-dinner weekend.

## Two-day worked example

**Day 1 (inside the Ring):** Stephansdom at 09:00 (nave free; pick South Tower OR catacombs, not both) → walk 10 min to Hofburg, Sisi+Imperial Apartments with audio guide (60-90 min) → long Melange and Kaiserschmarrn at Café Sperl or Bräunerhof → afternoon at the **Kunsthistorisches** (open until 21:00 on Thursday) → free *Oper live am Platz* on Karajan-Platz if an opera is on, or your Michelin dinner [[9]](https://www.findingtheuniverse.com/a-2-day-vienna-itinerary/) [[2]](https://www.sisimuseum-hofburg.at/en/tickets/tickets-prices) [[12]](https://www.khm.at/en/visit) [[49]](https://www.wiener-staatsoper.at/ihr-besuch/service/oper-live-am-platz/refDate/2017-01-01/).

**Day 2 (outside the Ring):** Schönbrunn at opening, Grand Tour booked online, ~75 min inside + gardens to the Gloriette → quick Bitzinger Würstel at Albertinaplatz on the way back → tram D from Karlsplatz to **Upper Belvedere** for *The Kiss* → Heuriger at Mayer am Pfarrplatz in Nussdorf (Gemischter Satz, board with cold cuts), or Beisl dinner at Plachutta for Tafelspitz if the Michelin slot was Day 1 [[1]](https://www.schoenbrunn.at/en/tickets-and-prices/all-tickets-tours) [[4]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/museums/belvedere-sites/kiss/) [[66]](https://www.pfarrplatz.at/en/) [[64]](https://www.plachutta.at/en/menu-wollzeile/).

**Saturday-morning option (if a 3rd day):** Naschmarkt + the Saturday flea-market 6:30am-2pm; counter snack at Urbanek [[57]](https://www.wien.info/en/dine-drink/markets/naschmarkt-353536) [[58]](https://www.myguidevienna.com/travel-articles/naschmarkt-wien).
