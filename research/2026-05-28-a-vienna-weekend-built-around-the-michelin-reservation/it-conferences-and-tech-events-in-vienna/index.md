**TEDAI is the priciest ticket on the list** — Standard Pass €1,500 ex-VAT, Executive Track €2,500 [[13]](https://tedai-vienna.ted.com/). OWASP and Cloud Native Days are community-priced; techConference is built around Microsoft partner training packages [[9]](https://www.cancom.at/en/events/2026/techconference-2026). RubyConf Austria, ThreatModCon and uxcon sit in the mid-hundreds-of-euros band typical for two-day specialist conferences.

⚠ **Not in Vienna in 2026:** WeAreDevelopers World Congress moved to Berlin (Jul 8-10) — 15,000+ attendees, 500+ speakers [[17]](https://www.wearedevelopers.com/world-congress). 4GAMECHANGERS Festival is on a creative break, 2026 dates TBA [[28]](https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/4gamechangers/). European Forum Alpbach is in Alpbach (the village), not Vienna — don't confuse the two.

## Where things happen

Three venues account for most of the action:

- **[weXelerate](https://wexelerate.com/)** — 9,000m² hub at Praterstraße 1, 1020 (Jean Nouvel building, 5 min from Schwedenplatz). Hosts Google Cloud Meetup, Future Forward, frequent AI/startup events [[27]](https://wexelerate.com/).
- **Austria Center Vienna (ACV)** — the city's big international congress centre; OWASP AppSec EU lives here [[4]](https://owasp.glueup.com/event/owasp-global-appsec-eu-2026-vienna-austria-162243/).
- **Messe Wien Exhibition Congress Centre** — Microsoft techConference and other large IT trade events [[9]](https://www.cancom.at/en/events/2026/techconference-2026).

For the showpiece events, the Hofburg (TEDAI) [[13]](https://tedai-vienna.ted.com/) and Das MuTh (RubyConf) [[3]](https://10times.com/e1h1-k1d5-h3gs-d-rubyconf-austria) both put you inside historic Vienna — the venue choice is part of the value, not just a hall.
