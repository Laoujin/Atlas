ii.

Where it happens

three venues carry most of the year

weXelerate

Praterstraße 1 · 1020 · 5 min from Schwedenplatz

9,000m² hub in the Jean Nouvel building. Hosts Google Cloud Meetup, Future Forward, frequent AI/startup events [27].

Austria Center Vienna (ACV)

22nd district · large international congress centre

The city's big international congress hall — OWASP Global AppSec EU lives here [4].

Messe Wien & WKÖ

Messe Wien: 2nd district · WKÖ: Wiedner Hauptstr. 63, 1045

Messe Wien hosts Microsoft techConference and other large IT trade events [9]; WKÖ hosts AAIC [12].

For the showpiece events, the Hofburg (TEDAI [13]) and Das MuTh (RubyConf [3]) put you inside historic Vienna — the venue choice is part of the value, not just a hall.

⚠ Not in Vienna in 2026

Vienna's long-time flagship moved to Berlin for 2026 (Jul 8–10). 15,000+ attendees, 500+ speakers across 20+ stages [17].

4GAMECHANGERS Festival

ProSiebenSat.1 PULS 4's annual is on a creative break — 2026 dates listed as TBA [28].

European Forum Alpbach

Held in Alpbach the village (Tyrol), not Vienna. Easy to confuse the two — don't book the wrong train.
