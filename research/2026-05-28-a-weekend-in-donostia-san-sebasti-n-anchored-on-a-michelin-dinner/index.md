The trip plans backwards from the dinner, and the restaurant pick is the most-binding constraint — not just because all five carry a [2–3 month lead time for May–October bookings](https://www.eatingeurope.com/blog/michelin-star-restaurants-san-sebastian/), but because each of the five stars sits in a different micro-geography that decides the rest of the day. **Akelarre** is on top of Monte Igueldo; you ride the [1912 funicular](https://www.monteigueldo.es/organize-your-visit) up there at sunset anyway, so the dinner doubles as the panorama. **Arzak** is in Gros, walkable from a [Zurriola surf lesson at Pukas](https://eskola.pukassurf.com/pages/pukas-surf-eskola-san-sebastian-servicios-y-precios) and a stop at [Bar Bergara](https://pinchosbergara.es/pinchos-san-sebastian.html) on the way. **Amelia** is inside [Hotel Villa Favorita directly on La Concha](https://hotelvillafavorita.com/en/) — pair with [La Perla thalasso](https://la-perla.net/en/) and a Monte Urgull loop. **Mugaritz** in Errenteria sits on the [E20 bus line via Pasaia](https://ekialdebus.eus/es/linea-e20-hondarribia-errenteria-pasai_antxo-donostia/), so the [Camino del Faro de la Plata cliff path to Pasai Donibane](https://turismovasco.com/en/gipuzkoa/what-to-see-in-gipuzkoa/silver-lighthouse-ulia-pasaia-route/) becomes the natural afternoon. **Berasategui** in Lasarte-Oria is the outlier with no walking pairing — [a 15–20 min taxi each way](https://www.yelp.com/biz/martin-berasategui-lasarte-oria) and that's it; book it for the technique, not the day plan.

The closures map is the second filter, and it's strict enough to invalidate a naive itinerary. Avoid Mondays: most pintxo bars and [San Telmo Museoa close](https://doeatbetterexperience.com/blog/pintxos-san-sebastian-guide-parte-vieja/); [Chillida-Leku is closed Tue–Wed](https://www.tiqets.com/en/chillida-leku-tickets-l180856/); [Berasategui serves Wed–Sun only](https://guide.michelin.com/en/pais-vasco/lasarte-oria/restaurant/martin-berasategui); and [Mugaritz runs May 1 – October 25 only](https://www.infobae.com/espana/2026/05/22/andoni-luis-aduriz-estrena-nueva-temporada-en-mugaritz-nuestra-cocina-no-tiene-que-estar-buena-tiene-que-tener-sentido/). Late May — the date of this run — is also Donostia's tactical shoulder: [the official txotx sidrería season ended May 3](https://discoverdonosti.com/cider-houses-in-astigarraga/) so Astigarraga needs a year-round house like [Petritegi (€44.50)](https://www.petritegi.com/en/booking-menus), and the [Santa Clara motora is on reduced service until 1 June](https://motorasdelaisla.com/en/fares/) so paddle yourself across with [AloKayak](https://www.alokayak.com/en/kayak-canoe-sup-in-donostia/) instead.

If the dates are flexible, the IT-conference layer is a real lever rather than a footnote. [EuskalHack Security Congress IX, 19–20 June at Olarain](https://securitycongress.euskalhack.org/index_es.html), is the next pairable event and falls inside Mugaritz's open window — a clean Friday-talks + Saturday-dinner stack with the venue on Paseo de Ondarreta, walkable from the bay. [Zinemaldia & Technology, 18–26 September during the 74th Film Festival](https://tourism.euskadi.eus/en/agenda/san-sebastian-international-film-festival/webtur00-evento/en/), inverts the booking order: lodging vanishes and prices spike during the festival, so the Michelin slot has to be chosen around the festival window, not vice versa. [Donostia Week-INN in November](https://weekinn.fomentosansebastian.eus/es/) is the only autumn anchor with city-wide programming, but it lands after Mugaritz closes — drop to the three-star shortlist plus Amelia.

What this run does not decide for you is which restaurant to book. Pick that first — date, neighborhood, and at least one afternoon's category fall out of it automatically.
