PICK 01 · HIGH
Fri–Sat · 19–20 June 2026 · 3 weeks out

EuskalHack Security Congress IX

Ninth edition of the Basque ethical-hacking association’s flagship con at Colegio Mayor Olarain — a residence-hall venue on Paseo de Ondarreta, walkable from Ondarreta beach. Programme spans reverse engineering, vulnerability development, mobile attacks, APTs and AI-in-cybersecurity, with an open-space block Friday evening running into Saturday morning. [3] Capacity ~200 — small enough that buying late means risking sold-out. This is the highest-signal event to pair with a Michelin dinner.

PICK 02 · MED
Fri–Sat · 18–26 September 2026

Zinemaldia & Technology

Industry track of the 74th San Sebastián Film Festival, co-organised with Vicomtech and Tecnalia. Centrepiece is the Zinemaldia Startup Challenge — €10,000 each for Spanish and European audiovisual-tech startups (AI, blockchain, streaming, VFX). [16] Caveat: hotel prices spike, book months ahead.

PICK 03A · MED
mid-November 2026 · TBC · city-wide

Donostia Week-INN

Annual innovation week run by Fomento de San Sebastián. 2025 ran Nov 15–20 across Kursaal, Anoeta, EKINN and Hotel María Cristina, with AI, cybersecurity, quantum and talent as core threads. [8] DIPC and other Basque research centres participate. [9] Format is 20+ smaller sessions — good for sampling, less good for a single deep dive. Most events free.

PICK 03B · LOW (niche)
Thu–Sat · 19–21 November 2026 · TBC

Berrilan Basque Tech Summit IV

Fourth edition at Anoeta (Real Sociedad’s stadium complex). Verticals are construction-tech and smart-city: BIM, GIS + digital twins, industrialised construction, asset management, sustainable urbanism. Gold sponsors include Hexagon Voyansi, Saltoki, Allplan. [7] Niche unless built-environment is the angle, but it’s the largest pure-industry tech summit on the calendar and often overlaps Week-INN.

13 MAY · KURSAAL
ApplAI 2026
Year’s biggest applied-AI day for Basque industry. Pilar Manchón (Google Research), Anna Schlegel, Javier Del Ser, Nerea Aranguren. 34 sponsoring entities. Free, full-day. Track for 2027. [1]
25–26 MAR · FICOBA
MUBIL Mobility Expo 5
Southern Europe’s lead sustainable-mobility expo: 110 exhibitors, France as strategic partner, autonomous-vehicle and electric-bus demos. Ficoba is in Irun (~20 km east), not Donostia proper. [4]
11 APR · CEMEI
Debug′Em All — Cider House Ed.
Niche analytics-engineering meetup, free entry with optional €50 cider-house dinner. Travelling community con — returns to a different city each year. [10]

Between Conferences

ongoing local scene

Tech Connect Donostia

monthly · evenings · english

487 members, 4.8/5 across 40 prior events, organiser Luis S. at Drop, Larramendi Kalea 3 [12]. Easiest weekend-of drop-in: relaxed format, English by default.

Saturdays.AI Donostia

saturdays · 9am–2pm · cycles

ML/DL course cycles, 7–14 sessions, €179–€249, Demo Day at Impact Hub Donostia in Tabakalera [13]. WEF-recognised [14].

EuskalHack Association

year-round · online + olarain

First Basque ethical-hacking non-profit. Runs the IX congress [2], plus year-round community programming online and at Olarain.

Impact Hub Donostia

daily coworking · tabakalera

Hosts most non-affiliated community tech events from the 3rd floor of Tabakalera — the old tobacco factory turned culture centre, worth visiting in its own right [19].

Venues to recognise

①
Kursaal Congress Centre
Rafael Moneo’s two cubic glass “stranded rocks”; 20,000 m², 1,806-seat auditorium, 300+ events / 600,000 visitors a year [20]. Default venue for the biggest events.
②
Colegio Mayor Olarain
University residence on Paseo de Ondarreta; intimate; EuskalHack’s home [18].
③
Palacio Miramar
Queen María Cristina’s former summer palace overlooking La Concha; the more academic conferences (statistical learning, materials science) [18].
④
Ficoba (Irun)
Large exhibition halls 20 km east; MUBIL’s home; requires a transfer.
⑤
UPV/EHU — Escuela de Ingeniería de Gipuzkoa
Engineering campus; ISC′2026 and other academic-industrial events [18].
⑥
Anoeta
Real Sociedad’s stadium complex with conference facilities; Berrilan Summit’s home.
⑦
Impact Hub Donostia (Tabakalera)
Community-event base in the old tobacco factory turned culture centre.

Common misses

Codemotion, DevOps Spain, JSConf, etc. — these cluster in Madrid and Barcelona, not Donostia.
Cyber Intelligence Europe 2026 — Brussels, not San Sebastián, despite Basque speaker representation.
Donostia Tech Week — historic city-led brand (2017–2019 era); domain unreachable as of this run; programming appears folded into Donostia Week-INN.
Bilbao events (BIG Conference, Euskal Encounter, DOK) — an hour away by car/bus; pairable, but not in Donostia.

Kursaal · the default stage

ApplAI, Week-INN headline sessions and the Zinemaldia industry programme all land here [20].
