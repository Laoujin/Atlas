## Happening this week (May 27-29)

**[ISC'2026 — 24th Industrial Simulation Conference](https://systemdynamics.org/event/24th-annual-industrial-simulation-conference-isc2026/)** is on the UPV/EHU engineering campus right now [[5]](https://systemdynamics.org/event/24th-annual-industrial-simulation-conference-isc2026/). EUROSIS bills it as Europe's premier annual industrial-simulation conference, with this edition emphasising the integration of AI and agent-based modelling into industrial decision-support [[6]](https://www.eurosis.org/conf/isc/2026/index.html). ~150 attendees — academic in flavour, not a casual drop-in. If the trip is already booked for this weekend, the Friday closing day is the realistic window to attend.

## The four events worth planning around

### [EuskalHack Security Congress IX](https://securitycongress.euskalhack.org/index_es.html) — Jun 19-20

Ninth edition of the Basque ethical-hacking association's flagship con [[2]](https://securitycongress.euskalhack.org/index_es.html). Two days at Colegio Mayor Olarain (a residence-hall venue on Paseo de Ondarreta, walkable from Ondarreta beach). Programme spans reverse engineering, vulnerability development, mobile attacks, APTs, and AI-in-cybersecurity, with an open-space block on Friday evening running into Saturday morning [[3]](https://10times.com/esc-san-sebasti-n). Tickets €85 early-bird (now closed), €125 standard, €190 late (Jun 1-15), reduced rates for under-25 and unemployed [[2]](https://securitycongress.euskalhack.org/index_es.html). Capacity ~200 — small enough that buying late means risking sold-out. **This is the highest-signal event to pair with a Michelin dinner**: the cybersec crowd in Euskadi is dense, the venue is in the heart of the bay, and it's the next thing happening.

### [Zinemaldia & Technology](https://www.sansebastianfestival.com/industria/zinemaldia_and_technology/1/22407/in/t_8021) — Sep 18-26 (during the Film Festival)

Industry track of the 74th San Sebastián International Film Festival (18-26 September 2026) [[17]](https://tourism.euskadi.eus/en/agenda/san-sebastian-international-film-festival/webtur00-evento/en/), positioning the festival as a reference space for tech-in-audiovisual [[15]](https://www.sansebastianfestival.com/industria/zinemaldia_and_technology/1/22407/in/t_8021). Co-organised with Tecnalia, [Vicomtech](https://www.vicomtech.org/en) (the local visual-computing + AI applied-research centre) and ALÍA (Audiovisual Industrial Alliance) [[16]](https://www.vicomtech.org/en/news/detail/558_the-sixth-edition-of-zinemaldia-startup-challenge-is-now-open-aimed-at-spanish-and-european-entrepreneurs-and-start-ups). The centrepiece is the **Zinemaldia Startup Challenge** — €10,000 each for the winning Spanish and European audiovisual-tech startups (AI, blockchain, streaming, VFX/SFX) [[16]](https://www.vicomtech.org/en/news/detail/558_the-sixth-edition-of-zinemaldia-startup-challenge-is-now-open-aimed-at-spanish-and-european-entrepreneurs-and-start-ups). Caveat: the festival drives hotel prices up sharply and books out months ahead — plan the Michelin reservation around the festival, not vice versa.

### [Donostia Week-INN](https://weekinn.fomentosansebastian.eus/es/) — November (city-wide)

Annual innovation week run by **Fomento de San Sebastián** (the city's economic-development agency); 2025 ran Nov 15-20 across Kursaal, Anoeta, EKINN and Hotel María Cristina, with AI, cybersecurity, quantum and talent as the core threads [[8]](https://weekinn.fomentosansebastian.eus/es/). DIPC and other Basque research centres participate [[9]](https://dipc.ehu.eus/en/science-society/donostia-week-inn). Format is 20+ smaller sessions rather than one big floor — good for sampling, less good for a single deep dive. Most events are free; 2026 dates not yet published as of late May 2026.

### [Berrilan Basque Tech Summit IV](https://basquetechsummit.com/) — late November (TBC)

Fourth edition at **Anoeta** (Real Sociedad's stadium complex) with co-organisers Berrilan, Real Sociedad and BUTIC [[7]](https://basquetechsummit.com/). Verticals are construction-tech and smart-city: BIM, GIS + digital twins, industrialised construction, asset management, sustainable urbanism. Gold sponsors include Hexagon Voyansi, Saltoki, Allplan; silver: ACCA, MagiCAD, Dalux [[7]](https://basquetechsummit.com/). Niche unless built environment is the angle — but it's the largest pure-industry tech summit on the Donostia calendar, and it often overlaps Week-INN.

## What you already missed (Jan-May 2026)

- **[ApplAI 2026][m3]** — 13 May at Kursaal, the year's biggest applied-AI day for Basque industry: Pilar Manchón (Google Research), Anna Schlegel, Javier Del Ser (Tecnalia/EHU), Nerea Aranguren (Mondragon Corp), 34 sponsoring entities [[1]](https://www.deia.eus/ciencia-y-tecnologia/2026/04/25/applai-2026-sera-congreso-ia-10984690.html). Free, full-day, business-implementation angle (not research/theory). Worth tracking for 2027.
- **[MUBIL Mobility Expo 2026][m1]** — 25-26 March at Ficoba, Southern Europe's lead sustainable-mobility expo: 110 exhibitors, France as strategic partner, autonomous-vehicle and electric-bus demos, data/AI/digital-platform programme threads [[4]](https://www.mubilexpo.eus/en/mubil-mobility-expo-2026-is-strengthening-its-european-presence-with-110-exhibiting-companies-and-france-as-a-strategic-partner/). Note: Ficoba is in Irun (~20 km east), not Donostia proper.
- **[Debug'Em All Meetup — Cider House Ed.][m2]** — 11 April, niche analytics-engineering meetup, free entry with optional €50 cider-house dinner [[10]](https://www.eventbrite.es/e/debugem-all-meetup-san-sebastian-2026-cider-house-ed-tickets-1984541271365). Travelling community con, returns to a different city each year.

## Venues to recognise

- **[Kursaal Congress Centre](https://www.kursaal.eus/en/)** — Rafael Moneo's two cubic glass "stranded rocks" at the Urumea river mouth; 20,000 m², 1,806-seat auditorium, hosts 300+ events / 600,000 visitors a year [[20]](https://www.kursaal.eus/en/). Default venue for the biggest events (ApplAI, Week-INN headline sessions, Zinemaldia industry programme).
- **Colegio Mayor Olarain** — university residence on Paseo de Ondarreta, intimate venue, EuskalHack home.
- **Palacio Miramar** — Queen María Cristina's former summer palace overlooking La Concha; runs the more academic conferences (statistical learning, materials science) per the Convention Bureau agenda [[18]](https://conventionbureau.sansebastianturismoa.eus/en/conference-agenda/).
- **Ficoba (Irun)** — large exhibition halls, MUBIL home, requires a transfer.
- **UPV/EHU Escuela de Ingeniería de Gipuzkoa** — engineering school campus, hosts ISC'2026 and other academic-industrial events.
- **Anoeta** — Real Sociedad's stadium complex with conference facilities, hosts Berrilan Summit.
- **Impact Hub Donostia (Tabakalera)** — community-event base; the old tobacco factory turned culture centre is itself worth visiting.

## What is NOT happening in Donostia (common misses)

- **Codemotion, DevOps Spain, JSConf, etc.** — these cluster in Madrid and Barcelona, not Donostia.
- **Cyber Intelligence Europe 2026** — Brussels, not San Sebastián, despite Basque speaker representation.
- **Donostia Tech Week** — historic city-led brand (2017-2019 era); the donostiatechweek.eus site is unreachable as of this run, and its programming appears to have been folded into Donostia Week-INN.
- **Bilbao events (BIG Conference, Euskal Encounter, DOK)** are an hour away by car/bus — pairable, but not in Donostia.
