> **TL;DR** Megève has no dedicated public IT/tech conference of its own. The village's real value for tech professionals is as a **luxury corporate retreat venue**: 15+ seminar spaces, a 2,000 m² congress hall, and year-round MICE services [[2]](https://www.megeve-tourisme.fr/en/sejour-professionnel/) [[3]](https://www.megeve-tourisme.fr/en/decouvrir-megeve/les-infrastructures/espace-congres-du-palais/). The nearest recurring public tech events are [Mountain Planet](https://www.mountain-planet.com/en/) in Grenoble (~1.5 h, biennial) and [Black Alps](https://blackalps.ch/) near Lausanne (~1.5 h, annual).

## Megève as a Corporate Tech Retreat Destination

Megève actively markets itself to tech companies for off-sites, all-hands, and leadership retreats — not public industry conferences. The town offers [[2]](https://www.megeve-tourisme.fr/en/sejour-professionnel/):

- **32+ equipped seminar rooms** across the village, ranging from intimate boardrooms to large plenary halls
- Dedicated MICE coordination via `reservation-groupes@megeve.fr`
- Year-round calendar (both ski season and summer)
- High-end team-building (Evolution 2, Origyne·s) and top-tier catering

Accessibility: 1 h from Geneva Airport, 2.5 h from Lyon, TGV connection via Sallanches [[5]](https://www.seminairesbusiness.com/en/seminar-megeve).

### Palais de Megève — main congress venue

The [Espace Congrès du Palais](https://www.megeve-tourisme.fr/en/decouvrir-megeve/les-infrastructures/espace-congres-du-palais/) is the largest dedicated congress facility [[3]](https://www.megeve-tourisme.fr/en/decouvrir-megeve/les-infrastructures/espace-congres-du-palais/):

| Adjoining auditorium    |              ~142  | 260 m²; concerts, screenings, keynotes     |
| Total modular floor     |          ~2,000 m² | Ground floor, direct lobby access          |

## Only Public Professional Event: Megève Leadership

The sole recurring public professional event is [Megève Leadership](https://www.megeveleadership.com/), launched in 2026 [[1]](https://www.megeveleadership.com/):

- **First edition:** January 16–18, 2026 (past) — sold out
- **Second edition:** March 8–10, 2027
- **Format:** Conferences, roundtables, film screening at the Palais de Megève
- **Theme:** Humanistic leadership inspired by mountain values (*esprit de cordée*)
- **Speakers 2026:** Pascal Sancho (mountain rescue commander), Sophie Lavaud (alpinist, 14 × 8,000 m peaks), Yoann Offredo (professional cyclist), Claudia Riegler (Olympic skier/moderator)

⚠ No IT/digital/tech content — this is a leadership and entrepreneurship event, not a technology conference.

### French Tech in the Alps ecosystem

[French Tech in the Alps](https://www.ftalps.com/en/) covers Annecy (45 min from Megève), Grenoble, Chambéry, and Genevois Français [[10]](https://www.ftalps.com/en/). The Annecy basin alone has 100+ startups. Meetups, startup cafés, and pitch events run year-round — mostly in Annecy/Grenoble, not Megève itself.
