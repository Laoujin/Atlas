Cartmel village core: priory, racecourse, gin distillery, sticky toffee pudding, village bakery, micro-things to do on foot before/after dinner at L'Enclume.
Lake Windermere experience within 30 km: Bowness, Ambleside, Windermere cruises and steamers, Blackwell Arts & Crafts House, Brockhole visitor centre, water-based activities.
Coniston Water and Hawkshead area: Brantwood (Ruskin's home), Steam Yacht Gondola, Coniston village, Hill Top (Beatrix Potter), Hawkshead village, Tarn Hows.
Historic houses and gardens within 30 km: Holker Hall, Sizergh Castle, Levens Hall topiary garden, Cartmel Priory itself, Muncaster — which are worth the visit and which to skip.
Walks and hikes within 30 km: Hampsfell Hospice walk from Grange-over-Sands, Cartmel village circular, Whitbarrow Scar, Arnside Knott, Coniston Old Man, Gummer's How — graded by effort and view payoff.
Morecambe Bay and Arnside-Silverdale AONB: Grange-over-Sands promenade, cross-bay walks with a guide, RSPB Leighton Moss, Arnside village.
