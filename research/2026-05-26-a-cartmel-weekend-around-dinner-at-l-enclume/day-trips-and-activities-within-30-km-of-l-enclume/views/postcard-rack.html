★ THE VERDICT

One half-day in the village.
One full-day in the radius.

Pick one half-day in Cartmel before Saturday dinner — the Priory, Unsworth's Yard and the Hampsfell Hospice walk from Grange[1][7].

For the full day, choose mood — the Coniston literary loop (Brantwood + Hill Top + Tarn Hows)[27] or the Windermere boat day (Freedom of the Lake + Blackwell) [15][20].

RETURN TO SENDER Skip Muncaster Castle — 1h 15+ each way, off the 30 km radius[48]
