> **Decision.** Book a [room with L'Enclume itself](https://www.lenclume.co.uk/our-rooms) first — the rate guarantees your Saturday table and breakfast at Rogan & Co [[2]](https://www.lenclume.co.uk/our-rooms), and every room is a ≤5-minute village walk [[3]](https://www.visitlakedistrict.com/accommodation/our-rooms-by-lenclume-p1839711). If they're sold out, the [Cavendish Arms](https://thecavendisharms.co.uk/) is on the same street as the restaurant [[8]](https://www.coolplaces.co.uk/hotels/uk/england/north-west-england/cumbria/cartmel/10681-the-cavendish-arms), or [Kings Arms](https://kingsarmscartmel.pub/) is 60 paces across the square [[10]](https://kingsarmscartmel.pub/). For more comfort at a 15-min walk: [Aynsome Manor](https://aynsomemanor.com/) (5-star Georgian, complimentary chauffeur) [[14]](https://aynsomemanor.com/) or [Cartmel Old Grammar](https://cartmeloldgrammar.co.uk/) (boutique, by the racecourse) [[12]](https://cartmeloldgrammar.co.uk/).

L'Enclume sits on Cavendish Street in the centre of Cartmel village [[18]](https://www.visitlakedistrict.com/food-and-drink/lenclume-p1211551). Cartmel is small — three intersecting streets and a market square — so "walking distance" here is genuinely 2-15 minutes on foot, not a euphemism.

## The one to book first

### [L'Enclume — Our Rooms](https://www.lenclume.co.uk/our-rooms) — 16 rooms scattered across Cartmel

Sixteen individually designed bedrooms in four categories (Suites, Junior Suites, Superior, Double), clustered in village cottages and a townhouse overlooking the square [[2]](https://www.lenclume.co.uk/our-rooms) [[5]](https://finest.co.uk/finest-directory/stay-at-lenclume/) [[6]](https://www.thehotelguru.com/hotel/lenclume-cumbria). One reviewer's "Elderberry" room was upstairs from the main office with a river/arch-bridge view [[4]](https://www.straysleepeat.com/blog/lenclume-cartmel). Rates from around $174/night; Nespresso + Bose in every room [[5]](https://finest.co.uk/finest-directory/stay-at-lenclume/) [[6]](https://www.thehotelguru.com/hotel/lenclume-cumbria).

What makes this the default pick:
- **A Saturday-night table is locked in with the room** — booking the room *is* how you secure dinner [[1]](https://www.lenclume.co.uk/stay) [[2]](https://www.lenclume.co.uk/our-rooms).
- Breakfast next morning at Rogan & Co (Simon Rogan's neighbouring Michelin-starred bistro) is included [[1]](https://www.lenclume.co.uk/stay).
- The **DB&B Package** (Tue-Fri only, ⚠ *not* Saturdays) bundles the 15-course tasting menu, sparkling wine on arrival, room, and breakfast — worth knowing if your trip can flex to mid-week [[4]](https://www.straysleepeat.com/blog/lenclume-cartmel).
- ⚠ Caveat: rooms run Tuesday-Saturday only, and Saturday bookings fill 6+ months out for the same reason the dinner does [[1]](https://www.lenclume.co.uk/stay).

## If L'Enclume's rooms are full — three village-centre options

### [The Cavendish Arms](https://thecavendisharms.co.uk/) — same street as the restaurant

A 500-year-old Grade II listed coaching inn on Cavendish Street, "a stone's throw" from L'Enclume's front door [[9]](https://stayinapub.co.uk/the-cavendish-arms-cartmel-grange-over-sands-cumbria/) and TripAdvisor pins it at 0.0 mi away [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/HotelsNear-g499539-d270004-L_Enclume-Cartmel_Grange_over_Sands_Lake_District_Cumbria_England.html). Ten rooms ranging from the quirky Cavendish Loft through superior and classic doubles down to small doubles; £75-£125/night including breakfast [[8]](https://www.coolplaces.co.uk/hotels/uk/england/north-west-england/cumbria/cartmel/10681-the-cavendish-arms). Pub dining downstairs (not Michelin-level but a respectable seasonal British menu), open fires, beer garden. The fact this is the *closest* non-L'Enclume bed and still in two-figure territory most nights makes it the obvious fallback.

### [The Kings Arms](https://kingsarmscartmel.pub/) — on the market square

Six rooms, each named after a Romantic poet (Coleridge, Blake, Shelley, Byron, Keats, Wordsworth), in a 300-year-old building overlooking Cartmel's medieval square at the front and the Priory at the back [[10]](https://kingsarmscartmel.pub/). It's a gastro pub with live music — louder than a B&B, quieter than a city — and roughly 60 paces from L'Enclume. Better than the Cavendish if you want a square view; worse if you want quiet on a Saturday.

### [The Royal Oak](https://royaloakcartmel.co.uk/) — also on the Square, just around the corner

A "small number" of rooms above a traditional flagstone-and-beam pub; full cooked breakfast included [[11]](https://www.thelakedistrict.org/food-drink/pubs/the-royal-oak-inn-cartmel/). Cuisine downstairs leans pub-classic plus stone-baked pizza — useful as a Friday-night warm-up before Saturday at L'Enclume. ⚠ The building's age means tall guests will duck for ceiling beams and Wi-Fi can be patchy [[11]](https://www.thelakedistrict.org/food-drink/pubs/the-royal-oak-inn-cartmel/).

## Slightly further, materially nicer

### [Cartmel Old Grammar](https://cartmeloldgrammar.co.uk/) — 0.3 mi / 6-min walk

Twelve rooms in a Georgian boutique country house pressed up against Cartmel Racecourse [[12]](https://cartmeloldgrammar.co.uk/) [[13]](https://www.expedia.co.uk/Grange-Over-Sands-Hotels-Cartmel-Old-Grammar.h113627672.Hotel-Information). Cooked-to-order breakfast, two lounges with log fires, on-site Refectory Restaurant using Lake District ingredients [[12]](https://cartmeloldgrammar.co.uk/) [[13]](https://www.expedia.co.uk/Grange-Over-Sands-Hotels-Cartmel-Old-Grammar.h113627672.Hotel-Information). Closer to "boutique hotel" than "pub with rooms" — pick this if you want quieter and more polished than the village-centre inns but a still-trivial walk to dinner.

### [Aynsome Manor](https://aynsomemanor.com/) — 0.7 mi / 15-min walk through meadow

5-star Georgian manor B&B in open meadowland three-quarters of a mile from the village [[14]](https://aynsomemanor.com/) [[15]](https://aynsome-manor.hotels-lake-district.com/en/). Locally-sourced "Cartmel breakfast", evening turndown, dog-friendly, and — relevant for a 15-course dinner you don't want to walk back from — a **complimentary chauffeur service** for the local area [[14]](https://aynsomemanor.com/). This is the strongest pick if you'd rather not walk Cartmel Lane in the dark after wine pairings; you sleep in the countryside and Aynsome drives you to and from L'Enclume's door.

### [Uplands Inn](https://www.uplandscartmel.co.uk/) — 0.9 mi / 15-30 min walk

Eight bedrooms in a family-run independent inn set in two acres of gardens between Cartmel and Grange-over-Sands [[16]](https://www.uplandscartmel.co.uk/). On-site restaurant, real-ale bar with Cask Marque accreditation, views over Morecambe Bay [[16]](https://www.uplandscartmel.co.uk/). Walk to L'Enclume is genuinely 15-30 minutes depending on pace and which direction round the lane — fine in daylight, less appealing post-tasting-menu in the rain [[17]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/gb/uplands-cumbria.en-gb.html). Take a taxi back.

## Beyond walking distance — worth a sentence

[Heft, Inn + Restaurant](https://guide.michelin.com/gb/en/cumbria/newton-in-cartmel/restaurant/heft) in High Newton is a Michelin-starred 17th-century inn with five rooms, run by Kevin Tickle (eight years a chef under Rogan at L'Enclume before opening Heft) [[19]](https://guide.michelin.com/gb/en/cumbria/newton-in-cartmel/restaurant/heft) [[20]](https://kentsbankholiday.co.uk/heft-inn-a-michelin-starred-gem/). It's a ~5-mile drive — not walkable, but a relevant alternative if "Michelin star with your bed" is the actual brief and L'Enclume's own rooms are gone.

## Practical notes for a Saturday-evening L'Enclume booking

- **Book the room and the dinner together via L'Enclume** whenever possible — it's the only way to *guarantee* the Saturday table without separately competing for restaurant-only seats [[1]](https://www.lenclume.co.uk/stay) [[2]](https://www.lenclume.co.uk/our-rooms).
- **The DB&B Package doesn't apply on Saturdays** (Tue-Fri only) [[4]](https://www.straysleepeat.com/blog/lenclume-cartmel), so a Saturday stay is room + dinner billed separately.
- Cartmel has no rail station; nearest are **Cark-in-Cartmel** (2 mi) and **Grange-over-Sands** (3 mi) [[18]](https://www.visitlakedistrict.com/food-and-drink/lenclume-p1211551). Most lodgings will arrange a taxi; Aynsome Manor includes a chauffeur outright [[14]](https://aynsomemanor.com/).
- Every option above includes breakfast — L'Enclume's room rate uniquely sends you to a separate Michelin-starred restaurant (Rogan & Co) for it [[1]](https://www.lenclume.co.uk/stay).
