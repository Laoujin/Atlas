Sheet 1 of 1 · Rhine valley · 30 km radius from Memories

Field briefing
Late-May 2026

Anything above ~1,800 m is a coin flip. The valley floor, the Rhine vineyards and Heidi-Alp are wide open; Pizol's 5-Lake hike is snow-locked until mid-July and the full lift chain doesn't run until 27 June [11] [10].

Trace the thermal water from source (Tamina Gorge) to soak (Tamina Therme). Walk vineyards into Heididorf. Cross the Rhine to Vaduz for museums + 15:00 wine tasting at Hofkellerei. Skip Bad RagARTz — next edition is 2027 [68].

Wellness · trace the thermal water 2 pins · valley floor

1 Open Tamina
Therme

Tamina Therme

Public spa · 8,600 m² · 36.5 °C

⛰510 m ◷in-village CHF67/74 · 85/92

Six pools, coed (16+) sauna world with Kelo, Nero and Latvian Pirts [3]. Resort guests free [2]. ⚠ Annual closure 8–12 June [1].

2 Open · 2 May

Tamina Gorge + Old Bath Pfäfers

The source · 450 m secured path

⛰600 m →4 km CHF5 flat

750 m slot canyon, 70 m deep, 8,000 L/min spring at the grotto [7]. PostBus 453 "Schluchtenbus" ~15 min from station [6]. Walk the gorge first, soak second — turns a spa day into a story.

Hiking · sorted by altitude band 7 trails · 425 → 2,844 m

H1 Open Heidiweg
vineyard loop

Heidiweg loop

Easy · valley floor + vineyard

↔6.5 km · 1h30 ⛰595 m △minimal

From Maienfeld station through vineyards to Heididorf, Heidihof viewpoint and Heidi's fountain — signposted red "Heidiweg" [12]. The walk up is the real attraction [43].

H2 Open Heidi
Adventure Trail

Heidi Adventure Trail → Heidi-Alp

Easy–moderate · food at the alp

↔one-way ~1h45 ⛰1,111 m △+450 m

12 themed stations from Heididorf up to Heidialp Ochsenberg, the "real" grandfather's alp [13] [38].

H3 Open Swiss Fine
Wine Trail

Swiss Fine Wine Trail

Easy · cellar stops · station to station

↔7.3 km · ~2 h ⛰560–650 m △+138 m

Malans → Jenins → Maienfeld through Pinot Noir vineyards with multiple cellar stops [50].

H4 Open

Walensee N-shore → Quinten

Moderate · ferry return · fig trees

↔~10 km · 3.5–4 h ⛰425 m △+500 m

Forested cliff path Weesen → Betlis → Seerenbachfälle [16]. Quinten is foot- or ferry-access only, Mediterranean microclimate [17].

H5 Caution · wire-rope

Fürstensteig (LI)

Moderate, exposed · ridge alpine

↔5.9 km · 2h45 ⛰1,929 m △+478 m

Wire-rope-secured exposed sections; sure-footedness and head for heights required [28]. Crosses the snowline — check conditions. Take LIEmobil to Gaflei/Kurhaus.

H6 Closed · snow

Pizol 5-Lake hike

Medium · snow-locked until mid-July

↔11.4 km · 4h30 ⛰2,844 m △+553 m / −911 m

Full 5-lift chain only from 27 June 2026 [10]; the lakes are typically snow-free only from mid-July [11]. Bad Ragaz gondola alone runs daily from Ascension Day 14 May.

H7 Skip · snow risk Falknis & Alvier

Falknis (2,562 m) · Alvier (2,343 m)

T4-T3 · sure-footed · exposed

↔5–7 h · full day ⛰2,343 / 2,562 m △huge

Both well above the late-May snowline. Falknis = Swiss-LI border T4 [18]; Alvier is an exposed rocky ridge full-day [19]. Postpone to summer.

Gantenbein · Fläsch

Cult · no tours, no tastings

Explicitly does not offer tours or tastings; not accepting new customers [47]. Listed so you don't waste a drive.

V5 14 GM points

Alter Torkel · Jenins · destination dinner

"Fine-Wineing" · the wine determines the dish

⛰580 m ◷Mon–Sat L+D · Sun L ★14 Gault Millau · >1,500 archive

21 open Graubünden wines by the glass; archive includes Gantenbein, Donatsch, Obrecht, Adank [51]. Chef David Esser, sommelier Jennifer List, since 2020 under Julia and Oliver Friedrich [52]. The natural counterpoint to Memories: same region, same producers, different format.

Liechtenstein · across the Rhine 5 pins · 50 min from station

L1 Interior closed

Schloss Vaduz

Private residence of the Princely Family

⛰630 m →~21 km · ~50 min PT ★grounds only

Interior not open to the public — open to visitors only on August 15 (National Day) [20]. Grounds via a 15-minute footpath behind the Landesmuseum for valley views.

L2 Tue–Sun Städtle
museum cluster

Landesmuseum + Schatzkammer + Kunstmuseum + PostMuseum

4 museums in 100 m on Städtle

⛰460 m ◷10–17 (Wed +Kunst free) CHF10–15 / free

Schatzkammer holds Fabergé eggs, Apollo 11/17 moon rocks, Rudolf II's perpetual calendar [22]. Kunstmuseum free Wed [23]; PostMuseum free daily [29].

L3 Mon–Sat 15:00

Hofkellerei des Fürsten

Walk-in · champagne + 6 wines · 60–90 min

⛰460 m ◷Mon–Sat 15:00 ★no booking under 6

Champagne reception, vineyard + cellar tour, six wines [24]. The Herawingert vineyard is 4 ha with SW exposure and the same Föhn microclimate as Bündner Herrschaft [25].

L4 App-guided Liechtenstein-Weg
75 km · 5 stages

Liechtenstein-Weg

All 11 municipalities · LIstory app narration

↔75 km · 5 stages ⛰~450–800 m △stage 2/3 = Vaduz

Stage 2/3 passes Schalun Castle ruins, Vaduz Castle and the Court Winery [32]. Useful as a serious half-day walk that also covers the sightseeing.

Center

Liechtenstein Center

⛰460 m ◷daily CHF3 passport stamp

Souvenir passport stamp CHF 3 [26]. Start point for the museum cluster — first stop off bus 11.

Historic towns & museums 4 pins · seasonal Apr–Oct

M1 Open Chur
old town

Chur old town

Switzerland's oldest · 13,000-year history

⛰595 m →~22 km · 25 min train ★Arcas + 2 museums

Traffic-free old town around Arcas square — painted façades, monthly flea market, Saturday farmers' market [58]. Anchors: Bündner Kunstmuseum and Rätisches Museum.

M2 Tue–Sun · Apr–Oct Werdenberg

Werdenberg Castle

Switzerland's smallest town · ~800 yrs old

⛰490 m →~18 km ◷1 Apr – 31 Oct

Two streets of medieval wooden houses below an ~800-year-old castle founded c. 1230 by Count Rudolf I of Montfort [62] [63]. Half-day combined with Buchs.

M3 Tue–Sun · Apr–Oct Sargans
Castle

Sargans Castle

12C structure · 6 floors · 1983 Museum of the Year

⛰490 m →~8 km · 6 min train ◷10–17

Houses the Sarganserland Museum (founded 1904, six floors of the tower; named European Museum of the Year 1983) [65]. Easy 10-min detour from the train interchange.

M4 Open Pfäfers
Abbey

Pfäfers Abbey

Founded 731 · Baroque rebuild 1694

⛰820 m →5 km ★valley-bottom: Old Bath

Founded 731 from Reichenau as Monasterium Fabariense; rebuilt in Baroque style after a 1665 fire, new abbey church dedicated 1694 [66]. Above the village; the valley-bottom companion is the Old Bath.

Off the map this weekend

Closed · annual maintenance

Plan around the full closure if the weekend slides forward — bath, sauna and pools all dark [1].

Snow · until mid-July

Pizol 5-Lake hike

Full lift chain only from 27 June; lakes typically snow-free only mid-July [10] [11].

Between weekends

Flumserberg 26–29 May

Wrong year · next edition 2027

Bad RagARTz sculpture triennial

10th edition runs 1 May–31 October 2027, not 2026 [68]. Note for a return trip.

Interior closed · private residence

Vaduz Castle interior

Private residence of the Princely Family — open inside only on August 15 (National Day) [20]. Grounds + footpath only.

Closed to public

Gantenbein · Fläsch

No tours, no tastings, not accepting new customers [47]. Don't waste the drive.

Misnamed train

"Heidi Express" ≠ Maienfeld shuttle

The Heidi Express is the Landquart–Tirano scenic train (~5 h) [44]. The Heididorf shuttle is the weekend Heidibus [36].
