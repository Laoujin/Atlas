## Orientation

Memories sits inside the [Grand Resort Bad Ragaz](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/spa-wellness/wellness-area/tamina-therme) on the Rhine valley floor, at the foot of the Pizol massif. A 30 km radius pulls in three countries (Switzerland, Liechtenstein, briefly Austria's Vorarlberg edge) and a complete vertical stack: spa-town valley (500 m), wine villages of the Bündner Herrschaft (550–700 m), Heidi-Alp (1,111 m), Pizol summits (2,800+ m). The valley itself runs north–south along the Rhine, which makes east–west day-trip dispersal short and predictable.

## Wellness: trace the thermal water to its source

This is the local signature: the same 36.5 °C spring that feeds the resort's thermal pools surges out of solid rock at 8,000 L/min, 4 km up the Tamina valley [[7]](https://www.swissactivities.com/en-ch/tamina-gorge/). Doing this loop in order — gorge first, soak second — turns a generic spa day into a story.

### 1. Tamina Gorge + Old Bath Pfäfers (the source)

The [Tamina Gorge](https://en.heidiland.com/region/taminaschlucht.html) is a 750 m slot canyon, 70 m deep, walkable via a 450 m secured path that ends at the grotto where the spring gushes out at 8,000 L/min [[7]](https://www.swissactivities.com/en-ch/tamina-gorge/). 2026 season is 2 May–25 October, CHF 5 flat admission for all ages, daily 10:00–17:15 through 30 August (10:00–16:15 thereafter) [[5]](https://en.heidiland.com/region/taminaschlucht.html). At the gorge mouth sits the **Old Bath Pfäfers** (1704–1718), Switzerland's oldest preserved Baroque bath, now a bath-and-monastery museum with a Paracelsus memorial, neo-Gothic bathing chapel and on-site restaurant [[8]](https://ostschweiz.ch/en/map/detail/old-bath-pfafers-7b3b71e1-93fa-4968-9910-7f97c6099834.html). Benedictine monks discovered the spring in 1242; early guests were lowered 70 m into the gorge in baskets [[4]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/spa-wellness/wellness-area/thermal-water).

**Access:** PostBus 453 "Schluchtenbus" from Bad Ragaz station, ~15 min, adult CHF 6 one-way / CHF 11 return, hourly [[6]](https://www.postauto.ch/en/leisure-offers/excursion-tips/mystical-tamina-gorge). Private vehicle access is restricted, so the PostBus is the practical way in.

### 2. Tamina Therme (the destination)

The public [Tamina Therme](https://www.taminatherme.ch/en/opening-hours-prices) is an 8,600 m² thermal complex with six indoor/outdoor pools, current channels, neck showers, underwater massage, plus a coed (16+) sauna world featuring a Nero sauna, a Kelo sauna with infusion ceremonies and a Latvian Pirts [[2]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/spa-wellness/wellness-area/tamina-therme) [[3]](https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/experiences/tamina-spa/). 2026 pricing: bath-only CHF 67 weekday / CHF 74 weekend; bath + sauna CHF 85 weekday / CHF 92 weekend; hours Mon–Thu 08:00–22:00, Fri 08:00–23:00, weekends 08:00–22:00; ⚠ full closure 8–12 June for annual maintenance [[1]](https://www.taminatherme.ch/en/opening-hours-prices). Grand Resort hotel guests enter free during their stay [[2]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/spa-wellness/wellness-area/tamina-therme).

## Liechtenstein day trip (Vaduz)

The country is a 50-minute door-to-Vaduz hop via train+bus (Bad Ragaz → Sargans → LIEmobil bus 11 → Vaduz Post) [[30]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Bad-Ragaz/Vaduz) [[31]](https://liemobil.li/en/timetable/). Swiss francs work everywhere; the Swiss Travel Pass covers LIEmobil [[31]](https://liemobil.li/en/timetable/). Start at the **Liechtenstein Center** (Städtle 39), where the souvenir passport stamp costs CHF 3 [[26]](https://en.tourismus.li/map/poi/liechtenstein-center-84f7a125-1058-41b2-8b95-00219f83026c.html).

**Vaduz Castle:** ⚠ private residence of the Princely Family; interior not open to the public [[20]](https://en.tourismus.li/map/poi/schloss-vaduz-8fb7e6cf-99b5-4ffb-b600-9e4c66398e1f.html). The grounds are reachable via a 15-minute footpath behind the Landesmuseum for Rhine-valley views.

**Hofkellerei tasting (the wine alternative to Bündner Herrschaft):** the [Hofkellerei des Fürsten von Liechtenstein](https://www.hofkellerei.at/en/startseite.html) runs walk-in tastings **Mon–Sat at 15:00** — champagne reception, vineyard and cellar tour, six wines, 60–90 min; groups under 6 don't need to book [[24]](https://en.tourismus.li/map/poi/degustation-in-der-hofkellerei-66aebd64-1eb0-4e08-b7a7-d016075ecce4.html). The Herawingert vineyard is 4 ha with SW exposure and the same Föhn microclimate as Bündner Herrschaft [[25]](https://www.hofkellerei.at/en/startseite.html).

Stage 2/3 through Vaduz passes Schalun Castle ruins, the castle and the Court Winery — useful if you want a serious half-day walk that also covers the sightseeing.

## Wine: the Bündner Herrschaft

Five to fifteen km from the resort, the four villages of **Fläsch, Maienfeld, Jenins and Malans** form Switzerland's premier Pinot Noir address [[39]](https://en.heidiland.com/region/buendner-herrschaft/winter.html). Across the wider Graubünden canton, Pinot Noir (Blauburgunder) accounts for 287 ha — 68 % of all plantings [[45]](https://www.swisswine.com/en/swiss-wine-regions/german-speaking-switzerland-wine-region/graubuenden-en); inside the Bündner Herrschaft itself, nearly 80 % of the vineyard area is planted with red grapes, and Decanter calls it "Burgundy with altitude" [[53]](https://www.decanter.com/learn/burgundy-with-altitude-inside-switzerlands-graubunden/). The Föhn, locally nicknamed *traubenkocher* ("grape cooker"), funnels warm dry air through the San Bernardino corridor → accelerates ripening and suppresses mildew (a 2014 late-season foehn lifted Pinot Noir Brix from 20.8° to 23.5°) [[46]](https://artisanswiss.com/2024/01/22/foehn-the-alpine-wind/) [[53]](https://www.decanter.com/learn/burgundy-with-altitude-inside-switzerlands-graubunden/).

**Late-May reality:** vines are budding, not harvesting — cellars are open, but you're tasting last year's release in a quiet village, not standing in a working harvest. April–November, Maienfeld vintners rotate weekend open-cellar tastings in their *Torkels* [[56]](https://www.graubuenden.ch/en/attractions/wine-experience-bundner-herrschaft).

### Wine walk and dinner

- **Swiss Fine Wine Trail** (Bündner Herrschaft): 7.3 km / ~2 h easy, Malans → Jenins → Maienfeld, station to station, cellar stops en route [[50]](https://www.outdooractive.com/en/route/hiking-route/heidiland/swiss-fine-wine-trail-buendner-herrschaft/105346237/).
- **Backpack wine tasting** (Graubünden Tourism, self-guided): from CHF 75 per person [[55]](https://www.graubuenden.ch/en/booking/offers/backpack-wine-tasting-the-bundner-herrschaft).

## Heidi: pilgrimage or skip?

[Heididorf](https://www.heididorf.ch/) sits in Maienfeld, ~10 min by car from the resort [[42]](https://www.newlyswissed.com/heidi-village-heididorf-maienfeld/). It is built around an authentic 300-year-old Heidi-Haus plus an alp-cottage replica, a school in the town hall, a goat barn, a Spyri exhibit and Switzerland's smallest post office [[35]](https://www.heididorf.ch/en/welcome/heidis-village/).

**The numbers:** 2026 season 13 March – 1 November, daily 10:00–17:00, last entry 15:45 [[34]](https://www.heididorf.ch/en/info/opening-hours/); adult CHF 14.90, child (5–14) CHF 6.90, under-4 free, 10+ adult group CHF 12.50 [[33]](https://www.heididorf.ch/en/info/tickets-info/); ~150,000 visitors/year [[40]](https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/buendner-herrschaft/).

**Honest take.** TripAdvisor reviews split between "magical" and complaints about steep walks, animal welfare and commercialisation [[41]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g659909-d1907336-Reviews-Heididorf-Maienfeld_Canton_of_Graubunden_Swiss_Alps.html). Two independent blogger verdicts converge: 2–3 hours max, best as a literary nostalgia stop, and the **vineyard walk up from Maienfeld station is the real attraction** [[42]](https://www.newlyswissed.com/heidi-village-heididorf-maienfeld/) [[43]](https://switzerlanding.com/heidi-village/). Bundle it with Bündner Herrschaft tastings and it earns its slot; treat it as a stand-alone destination and it disappoints.

**The genuine upgrade:** continue past the village on the **Heidi Adventure Trail** to **Heidialp Ochsenberg** at 1,111 m — the "real" grandfather's alp hut from the story, ~1h45 above Heididorf via 12 themed stations, with food and drink at the alp [[13]](https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-se/experiences/route/in-the-footsteps-of-heidi/) [[37]](https://www.heidialp-ochsenberg.ch/en/directions/) [[38]](https://www.graubuenden.ch/en/tours/hike-from-heididorf-to-heidialp).

**Practical:**
- Walk up via the [Heidiweg](https://www.heididorf.ch/en/experience/heidi-trail/) (6.5 km loop, 1h30, signposted red) [[12]](https://www.heididorf.ch/en/experience/heidi-trail/), or take the **Heidibus** PostBus shuttle from Maienfeld station Sat/Sun/holidays end-April to mid-October [[36]](https://www.postauto.ch/en/leisure-offers/excursion-tips/heidibus).
- ⚠ The "Heidi Express" by name is the Landquart–Tirano scenic train, **not** a Maienfeld shuttle [[44]](https://swissinfo.ch/eng/biggest-trip-ever-for-21st-century-heidi/1968778). Don't book it expecting a 10-min ride.

## Historic towns

### [Chur](https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/attractions/chur-old-town-0) (25 min by train)

Switzerland's oldest continuously settled city, with more than 13,000 years of habitation [[57]](https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/attractions/chur-old-town-0) and a 25-minute train ride from Bad Ragaz [[61]](https://www.winalist.com/blog/switzerland/german-switzerland/bad-ragaz-things-to-do). The traffic-free old town centres on the **Arcas square** (painted façades, café tables, monthly flea market and Saturday farmers' market) [[58]](https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/experiences/old-town-chur/). Two anchor museums:
- **Bündner Kunstmuseum** on Postplatz, 5 min from the station, post-2014–2016 expansion makes it one of Switzerland's most attractive [[59]](https://kunstmuseum.gr.ch/en/besuch/besucherinfo/Seiten/Home.aspx).
- **Rätisches Museum** in a 1675 Baroque patrician house, prehistory to present across Graubünden [[60]](https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/experiences/rhaetian-museum/).

### Werdenberg (Switzerland's smallest town)

Two streets of medieval wooden houses below an ~800-year-old castle founded c. 1230 by Count Rudolf I of Montfort [[62]](https://www.dieschweizerschloesser.ch/en/our-castles/werdenberg-castle) [[63]](https://www.electrummagazine.com/2012/01/the-rhine-castles-of-werdenberg-vaduz-and-schattenburg/). Two museums (castle museum + Schlangenhaus in the town below) run seasonally **1 April–31 October** [[62]](https://www.dieschweizerschloesser.ch/en/our-castles/werdenberg-castle). Half-day trip; combine with Buchs.

### Sargans Castle

12th-century structure (Hugo expanded it before 1228), houses the **Sarganserland Museum** (founded 1904, six floors of the tower; named **European Museum of the Year 1983**) — Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00, April–October [[64]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sargans_Castle) [[65]](https://www.wonderfulmuseums.com/museum/schloss-sargans-museum/). Easy 10-min detour from the train interchange.

### Pfäfers Abbey

Founded **731** from Reichenau as *Monasterium Fabariense*; rebuilt in Baroque style after a 1665 fire, with the new abbey church dedicated 1694 [[66]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pf%C3%A4fers_Abbey). Sits above the village of Pfäfers (the same direction as the gorge). The current spa-museum complex of [Altes Bad Pfäfers](https://www.pfaefers.ch/sehenswuerdigkeiten) is its valley-bottom companion [[67]](https://www.pfaefers.ch/sehenswuerdigkeiten) [[8]](https://ostschweiz.ch/en/map/detail/old-bath-pfafers-7b3b71e1-93fa-4968-9910-7f97c6099834.html).

### ⚠ Bad RagARTz: not this year

The [Bad RagARTz](https://badragartz.ch/en) Swiss Triennial of Sculpture transforms Bad Ragaz's spa-town parks with ~400 sculptures and pulls up to a million visitors, with a Festival of Small Sculptures at Altes Bad Pfäfers [[69]](https://badragartz.ch/en/ueber-bad-ragartz). The 10th edition runs **1 May – 31 October 2027** — not 2026 [[68]](https://badragartz.ch/en). Note for a return trip, not this one.
