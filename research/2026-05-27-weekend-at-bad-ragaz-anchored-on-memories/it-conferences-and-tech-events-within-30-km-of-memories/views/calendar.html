Technopark · Vaduz

Liechtenstein

10–14 km
Vaduz · Schaan · Triesen
  • Technopark Liechtenstein — Schaanerstrasse 27, Vaduz. Year-round programme of startup lectures, workshops, pitches; topics span pitching, growth hacking, agile dev, exit strategy. Also hosts the annual Cyber Security Workshop.
  • digital-liechtenstein.li — the standortinitiative behind Digital Summit, the Cyber Workshop, and a recurring Cybersecurity Roundtable series.
  • Vaduzer-Saal — flagship conference hall, host of Digital Summit.
  • Kunstmuseum Vaduz — Digitaltag venue, museum-format AI evening.
RHEINTAL · APPLIED-TECH CORRIDOR · OST + UNIVERSITY OF LIECHTENSTEIN

St. Galler Rheintal

5–17 km
Sargans · Buchs · Werdenberg
  • RhySearch — applied-tech research centre. Owns the 2026 triple of OCLA (Mar), Technologietag (Jun), PreXcon (Nov) — optical coatings, laser tech, precision manufacturing.
  • Hightech Campus Buchs — co-located with OST and the University of Liechtenstein.
  • Industrial / applied-tech, not software. If you're looking for dev-conference content, this cluster won't carry it — but for hardware, optics, manufacturing, it punches well above its size.
AREON Eventhalle · Chur

Graubünden

3–21 km
Chur · Maienfeld
  • FHGR — Fachhochschule Graubünden. Hosts the Service Innovation Lab (SIL), Infotag (Oct), and serves as the recurring venue for KI-Meetup. AI/SE, Mobile Robotics, Computational & Data Science programmes.
  • KI-Meetup Graubünden — applied-AI quarterly, free, alternates between ibW Chur and FHGR SIL. Cadence suggests Nr. 8 lands August/September.
  • UX Meetup Chur — 166 members, bi-monthly-ish, interdisciplinary UX/usability. Past venues: FHGR Standort B, Werkstatt LABOR, KostBar Sennhof. No 2026 dates listed; watch the page.
  • AREON Eventhalle — new (Sep 2026), capacity 6,500. Wirtschaftsforum 9 Oct is one of its first headline events; expect more in 2027+.

What this corridor doesn't have

GAPS
No regular dev conference
Closest is START Hack in St. Gallen (~70 km, outside radius). For mainline software conferences, you're driving to Zürich (~110 km).
No cybersecurity conference at scale
The Cyber Workshop and Roundtable are practitioner-level, not full conferences. Swiss Cyber Security Days sit in Bern.
AREON opens Sep 2026
New AREON Eventhalle in Chur (capacity 6,500). Wirtschaftsforum 9 Oct is among the first headline events. Tech-conference inventory may grow from 2027.
