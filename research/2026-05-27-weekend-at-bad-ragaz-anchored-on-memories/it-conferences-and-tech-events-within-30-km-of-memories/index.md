> **Decision.** If you want to pair a Memories weekend with an IT event in the same 30 km, target **late March** (Digital Summit in Vaduz [[1]](https://digitalsummit.li/) — the region's flagship, 450+ attendees [[2]](https://www.vaduz.li/veranstaltungen/digital-summit-2026-globale-technologie-trifft-europaische-souveranitat)) or **November** (Digitaltag Vaduz [[6]](https://www.digitaltag.li/) + PreXcon precision-tech convention in Buchs [[8]](https://www.rhysearch.ch/en/news/events.html)). June carries the recurring AI/dev scene (KI-Meetup Graubünden [[5]](https://eventfrog.ch/en/p/science-and-technology/ki-meetup-graubuenden-nr-7-7447191608016009720.html), RhySearch Technologietag [[8]](https://www.rhysearch.ch/en/news/events.html)). St. Gallen tech events (START Hack, SwissHacks) and Davos sit outside the 30 km radius.

## The 30 km radius

[Memories](https://www.resortragaz.ch/de/restaurants/memories) sits in the [Grand Resort Bad Ragaz](https://www.resortragaz.ch). Within ~30 km by road that pulls in three tech clusters along the Rhine valley [[13]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Vaduz/Chur):

| Cluster      | Cities                                  | Distance from Bad Ragaz | Tech anchor                          |
|--------------|-----------------------------------------|-------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Liechtenstein| Vaduz, Schaan, Triesen                  | 10–14 km                | Digital Summit, Technopark, Hilti HQ |
| St. Galler Rheintal | Sargans, Buchs, Werdenberg       | 5–17 km                 | RhySearch, OST, Hightech Campus      |
| Graubünden capital  | Chur, Maienfeld                  | 3–21 km                 | FHGR, KI-Meetup, ibW                 |

[13]: https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Vaduz/Chur

St. Gallen (~70 km) and Davos (~40 km) are outside the radius and excluded.

## Flagship: Digital Summit 2026

The single event that puts the region on the international tech map. Held 24 March 2026, 13:30, at [Vaduzer-Saal](https://digitalsummit.li/) in Vaduz [[1]](https://digitalsummit.li/). Ninth edition, organised by [digital-liechtenstein.li](https://digital-liechtenstein.li/) with the Liechtenstein government. 450+ decision-makers from business, science and policy [[2]](https://www.vaduz.li/veranstaltungen/digital-summit-2026-globale-technologie-trifft-europaische-souveranitat). 2026 theme: "Global Technology Meets European Sovereignty".

Speaker lineup that punches above the country's weight class:

- Marc Stampfli, [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com)
- Prof. Marco Hutter, [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch) Robotics
- Viktoria de La Rochefoucauld, [Palantir Technologies](https://www.palantir.com)
- Karsten Neugebauer (G2K Group, acquired by [ServiceNow](https://www.servicenow.com) 2023)
- Cornelia Diethelm, [Centre for Digital Responsibility](https://www.digitalresponsibility.ch)
- Hubert Büchel (Liechtenstein Economic Minister), Fabian Schmid (IT Office head) [[2]](https://www.vaduz.li/veranstaltungen/digital-summit-2026-globale-technologie-trifft-europaische-souveranitat)

Half-day format ending in a networking apéro [[1]](https://digitalsummit.li/). If you can only attend one tech event in the radius all year, this is it.

## Recurring scenes worth knowing

**[KI-Meetup Graubünden](https://eventfrog.ch/en/p/science-and-technology/ki-meetup-graubuenden-nr-7-7447191608016009720.html)** — applied-AI quarterly, free, alternating between ibW Chur and FHGR's Service Innovation Lab. Format: keynote + interactive discussion + networking. Organised by Markenkern AG with GRdigital and Code Crush GmbH [[5]](https://eventfrog.ch/en/p/science-and-technology/ki-meetup-graubuenden-nr-7-7447191608016009720.html). Nr. 7 ran 20 May 2026; Nr. 8 not yet announced but the cadence suggests August/September [[4]](https://www.chur.graubuenden.ch/en/overview-events/ki-meetup-graubunden-nr-6).

**[UX Meetup Chur](https://www.meetup.com/ux-meetup-chur/)** — 166 members, irregular cadence (roughly bi-monthly), interdisciplinary UX/usability/human-centred design. Past venues: FHGR Standort B, Werkstatt LABOR, KostBar Sennhof [[11]](https://www.meetup.com/ux-meetup-chur/). No 2026 dates currently listed; watch the page.

**[Technopark Liechtenstein](https://www.technopark-liechtenstein.li/)** — Schaanerstrasse 27, Vaduz. Year-round programme of startup lectures, workshops, pitches, discussion rounds; topics span pitching, growth hacking, agile dev, exit strategy [[10]](https://www.technopark-liechtenstein.li/). Also the host venue for the annual Cyber Security Workshop [[3]](https://digital-liechtenstein.li/cyber-security-workshop-praxisnahe-strategien-fuer-kmu-und-enterprises/) and a regular [Cybersecurity Roundtable](https://digital-liechtenstein.li/cybersecurity-konferenz-2025-digitale-resilienz-im-fokus/) series [[12]](https://digital-liechtenstein.li/cybersecurity-konferenz-2025-digitale-resilienz-im-fokus/).

**[RhySearch](https://www.rhysearch.ch) / [Hightech Campus Buchs](https://www.hightechcampusbuchs.ch)** — industrial/applied-tech rather than software. The 2026 triple of OCLA (March), Technologietag (June), PreXcon (November) covers optical coatings, laser tech, precision manufacturing [[7]](https://www.rhysearch.ch/en/news/events/events-detail/ocla-2026.html)[[8]](https://www.rhysearch.ch/en/news/events.html). Co-located with OST – Ostschweizer Fachhochschule and the University of Liechtenstein.

## What this region *doesn't* have

- No regular software developer conference in the radius — for that, the closest is **[START Hack](https://www.startglobal.org/start-hack)** in St. Gallen (~70 km, outside radius) or anything in Zürich (~110 km).
- No cybersecurity conference at scale — the cyber workshop and roundtable [[3]](https://digital-liechtenstein.li/cyber-security-workshop-praxisnahe-strategien-fuer-kmu-und-enterprises/)[[12]](https://digital-liechtenstein.li/cybersecurity-konferenz-2025-digitale-resilienz-im-fokus/) are practitioner-level, not full conferences. **[Swiss Cyber Security Days](https://scsd.ch/en)** are in Bern (out of radius).
- The new **[AREON Eventhalle](https://areonchur.ch/)** in Chur opens September 2026 (capacity 6,500) — Wirtschaftsforum Südostschweiz on 9 October [[14]](https://www.moneycab.com/graubuenden/hochkaraetige-gaeste-am-wirtschaftsforum-suedostschweiz-2026/) is one of its first headline events, so the tech-conference inventory here may grow from 2027 onward.

## Pairing with a Memories weekend

Memories serves Saturday evenings; most tech events in this region land Wednesday–Friday daytime. The clean pairings:

- **March (best)**: Digital Summit Tue 24 → drive 20 min to Bad Ragaz, two-night stay, Memories Sat 28. Adds OCLA in Buchs the following Thursday if you stay longer.
- **June**: RhySearch Technologietag Tue 9 → Memories Sat 13.
- **November (densest)**: PreXcon Tue 3, Digitaltag Vaduz Fri 20 — pick one and pair it with that Saturday at Memories.
