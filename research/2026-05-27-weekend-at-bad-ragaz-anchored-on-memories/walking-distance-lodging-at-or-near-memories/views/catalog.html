Where to sleep after Memories.

Sven Wassmer's three-star kitchen serves a single seating from 19:00 — pairings push past 22:30, the last train to Zürich leaves before midnight, and taxis in the village thin out fast on a Saturday. The realistic options collapse to two: sleep on the resort, or sleep within a five-minute walk of it.

The pick

Sleep on the resort.

Memories sits inside the Grand Hotel Quellenhof [1] — you walk down a hallway to your room. If the resort's CHF 700+/night rates are off the table, the Sorell Hotel Tamina is the only credible 4★ alternative, three minutes on foot [2]. Three-star self-check-in options — Krone, Ochsen, Esos Quelle — are all under 300 m from the Quellenhof front door [3].
Why walking-distance matters

Memories serves Wed–Sat, single seating from 19:00; pairings push the meal well past 22:30 [4]. The last train from Bad Ragaz station toward Zürich leaves before midnight, and village taxis are scarce on a Saturday. Either you sleep on the resort, or you sleep within a five-minute walk of it. Memories reopens 5 February 2026 after winter break, with Wed–Sat 19:00–21:30 service through year-end.

Practical notes · for the Memories evening

Book the resort hotels direct.

Not via OTAs. The inclusive thermal-spa, transfer, and Tamina Therme package only applies on direct rates [4].

Memories — 5 Feb 2026 reopening.

Wed–Sat service window, 19:00–21:30 single seating. The Hof Ragaz ground-floor renovation runs Feb–Dec 2026; verve by sven, Zollstube, gladys, and Memories all remain open [4].

Dress code is smart.

Easier from an in-house room than a five-minute village walk in winter weather. Add another reason to favour the on-campus picks if it's still cold in late February or early March.

3★ self-check-in: collect the code.

Krone and Ochsen are automated check-in. Get the access code before you leave for dinner — front desks may not be staffed when you walk back at 23:00.
