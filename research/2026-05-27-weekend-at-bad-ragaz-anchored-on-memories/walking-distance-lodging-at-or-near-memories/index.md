> **Decision:** Sleep on the resort. Sven Wassmer Memories sits inside the [Grand Hotel Quellenhof][q-site] [[1]](https://www.badragaz.ch/restaurants/153827) — you walk down a hallway to your room. If the resort's rates (CHF 700+/night) are off the table, the [Sorell Hotel Tamina][st-site] is the only credible 4★ alternative, 3 minutes on foot [[2]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g198797-d277429-Reviews-Sorell_Hotel_Tamina_Garni-Bad_Ragaz_Canton_of_St_Gallen.html). 3★ self-check-in options ([Krone][kr-site], [Ochsen][och-site], [Esos Quelle][eq-site]) are all <300 m from the Quellenhof front door [[3]](https://www.kayak.com/Bad-Ragaz-Hotels-Grand-Resort-Bad-Ragaz.48200.ksp).

## Why walking-distance matters

Memories serves Wed–Sat, single seating from 19:00; pairings push the meal well past 22:30 [[4]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/rooms-suites/inclusive-services-2026). The last train from Bad Ragaz station toward Zürich leaves before midnight, and taxis in the village are scarce on a Saturday. The realistic options are: sleep on the resort, or sleep within a 5-minute walk of it.

## On-property (Grand Resort Bad Ragaz campus)

Four hotels share the campus that houses Memories. All four are operating in 2026; only the **ground floor** of the Hof Ragaz is under renovation Feb–Dec 2026 (rooms unaffected; the Olives d'Or, Namun and Hof Bar venues are closed, but verve by sven, Zollstube, gladys and Memories remain open) [[4]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/rooms-suites/inclusive-services-2026).

What you get either way: complimentary transfer from Bad Ragaz train station, access to the private Thermal Spa and the public Tamina Therme, the 550 m² Family Spa, daily yoga/aqua classes [[4]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/rooms-suites/inclusive-services-2026). Tripadvisor #1 of 7 in Bad Ragaz, 4.7/5 from 1,162 reviews; value rated 4.4 — guests do flag the premium [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g198797-d2070866-Reviews-Grand_Resort_Bad_Ragaz-Bad_Ragaz_Canton_of_St_Gallen.html).

**Pick the Palais** if you want the quietest, most distinctive room — 14 keys, baroque envelope, restored 1976, member of Relais & Châteaux [[6]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/rooms-suites/palais-bad-ragaz). **Pick the Quellenhof** if proximity to Memories is the priority and you want the contemporary look [[8]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/rooms-suites/grand-hotel-quellenhof). **Pick the Hof Ragaz** for the lowest on-property entry price in 2026 (ground-floor works tend to push rates down), accepting the construction caveat.

## Practical notes for the Memories evening

- **Book the resort hotels via the resort, not OTAs** — the inclusive thermal + transfer package is only on direct rates [[4]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/rooms-suites/inclusive-services-2026).
- **Dress code at Memories is smart** — easier from an in-house room than a 5-minute walk in winter.
- **Memories opens 5 Feb 2026 after winter break**, Wed–Sat 19:00–21:30 service window [[4]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/rooms-suites/inclusive-services-2026).
- The off-property 3★ hotels mostly use **self check-in** (Ochsen, Krone) — collect the code before you leave for dinner.
