> **Decision.** Want to walk home from [Memories](https://memories.ch/en/) [[1]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/cuisine/memories): **[Boutique Hotel Ochsen](https://www.ochsenragaz.ch/)** in Bad Ragaz centre [[3]](https://www.ochsenragaz.ch/). Want a second Michelin meal the next day and a short taxi back: **[Park Hotel Sonnenhof](https://www.sonnenhof.li/en/)** in Vaduz, Relais & Châteaux, 19 km / 13 min [[7]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/hotel/park-hotel-sonnenhof/) [[14]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Bad-Ragaz/Vaduz). Want full Bündner Herrschaft wine-village immersion: **[Hotel Weiss Kreuz](https://www.weisskreuzmalans.ch/)** in Malans, four rooms, on-site Michelin restaurant [[9]](https://www.weisskreuzmalans.ch/hotel/) [[10]](https://www.gaultmillau.ch/restaurants/hotel-weiss-kreuz-331423). Want the novelty story: a **[converted 8,000-litre wine barrel](https://schlaf-fass.ch/schlaf-faesser/maienfeld)** above Heidi's village [[11]](https://schlaf-fass.ch/schlaf-faesser/maienfeld).

Memories sits inside the Grand Resort Bad Ragaz at Bernhard-Simon-Strasse 14 [[1]](https://www.resortragaz.ch/en/cuisine/memories), holds three MICHELIN stars [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/sankt-gallen-region/bad-ragaz/restaurant/memories), and closes its kitchen around 21:30 — so "taxi range" here means the cluster reachable in 5–25 minutes after dinner: Bad Ragaz itself, the Bündner Herrschaft wine villages, and Vaduz.

## The options in detail

### [Boutique Hotel Ochsen](https://www.ochsenragaz.ch/) — Bad Ragaz centre
The cult-revival pick. Reopened after a full renovation: 17 rooms across six categories, "vintage and modern" pairings, attached winery, in-house art gallery and bar [[3]](https://www.ochsenragaz.ch/). Address: Bartholoméplatz 4, central Bad Ragaz — a ~10-minute stroll from Memories, no taxi required. ⚠ Self-check-in only; some Tripadvisor guests report street and bell-tower noise — request a courtyard-facing room.

### [Hotel Bellevue](https://shop.heidiland.com/Heidi/ukv/house/TDS00020012267045436?lang=en) — Bad Ragaz centre
"Grand old house" boutique with cosy nooks and stylish accessories; the Heidiland tourism office literally calls it the "perfect boutique hotel" [[4]](https://shop.heidiland.com/Heidi/ukv/house/TDS00020012267045436?lang=en). Tripadvisor consensus: bohemian atmosphere, terraces for an evening glass, and not overpriced for the area — a contrast to the Grand Resort tier [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g198797-d21174111-Reviews-Hotel_Bellevue-Bad_Ragaz_Canton_of_St_Gallen.html).

### [Hotel Schloss Ragaz](https://www.hotelschlossragaz.ch/en/hotel-en/) — Bad Ragaz, parkland edge
A genuine castle, built around 1890 as the summer residence of Conrad Sonderegger, a Swiss engineer who worked on the Panama Canal [[6]](https://www.hotelschlossragaz.ch/en/hotel-en/). Three sleeping options under one estate: antique-furnished castle rooms, garden pavilions, and glamping lodges — the last is the unusual angle [[6]](https://www.hotelschlossragaz.ch/en/hotel-en/). Adjacent to the golf course and Tamina Therme, still a walk or 3-min taxi to Memories.

### [Park Hotel Sonnenhof](https://www.sonnenhof.li/en/) — Vaduz, Liechtenstein
The serious Michelin-on-Michelin play. Relais & Châteaux member since 1979, 29 rooms in the 4★-superior tier, perched above Vaduz with views to the castle and the Rhine valley [[7]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/hotel/park-hotel-sonnenhof/) [[8]](https://www.sonnenhof.li/en/). The in-house **Marée** ("Eagle's Nest"), run by owner-chef Hubertus Real, carries a Michelin star and 16 GaultMillau points [[17]](https://alpen-erleben.com/en/liechtenstein/find-host/hotels/vaduz/hotel-vaduz-park-hotel-sonnenhof-relais-chateaux/panoramarestaurant-maree). 19.3 km / 13 min from Bad Ragaz — a 20-ish-minute taxi back from Memories, and you've crossed into another country [[14]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Bad-Ragaz/Vaduz).

### [Hotel Weiss Kreuz](https://www.weisskreuzmalans.ch/) — Malans (Bündner Herrschaft)
The smallest, most idiosyncratic option: **four** double rooms above a Michelin-starred restaurant on the village square of Malans, a Bündner Herrschaft wine village [[9]](https://www.weisskreuzmalans.ch/hotel/) [[10]](https://www.gaultmillau.ch/restaurants/hotel-weiss-kreuz-331423). CHF 220 weekday, CHF 260 weekend, breakfast and Nespresso machine included [[9]](https://www.weisskreuzmalans.ch/hotel/). About 13 km / a 20-min taxi from Memories [[16]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Bad-Ragaz/Malans).

### [Schlaf-Fass Maienfeld](https://schlaf-fass.ch/schlaf-faesser/maienfeld) — converted wine barrel
The novelty entry, if your Saturday-night brain wants a story to tell on Monday. Two 8,000-litre oak barrels on the Hermann family farm, at the foot of Falknis, a few minutes from Heidi Village, Heidi Fountain and Heidi Alp [[11]](https://schlaf-fass.ch/schlaf-faesser/maienfeld) [[12]](https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/accommodations/schlaf-fass-riesling-sylvaner-1/). Sleeps up to four. ⚠ No shower; WC and sink are 40 m away in an annex — not a fit if you want a serious après-dinner unwind [[11]](https://schlaf-fass.ch/schlaf-faesser/maienfeld).

### [Berghotel Gaffia](https://pizol.com/en/catering-establishments/mountain-hotel-gaffia/) — Pizol, 1,750 m (summer only)
Worth a mention if you want to bookend the Memories night with an alpine morning. Nine cozy double/family rooms in a mountain hut at 1,750 m, served by the Pizol cable car ~4 km / 5 min from Bad Ragaz to the base station [[13]](https://pizol.com/en/catering-establishments/mountain-hotel-gaffia/) [[15]](https://www.skiresort.info/ski-resort/pizol-bad-ragaz-wangs/arrival-car/). → not a late-night taxi destination; arrive the day before, walk the 5-Lakes trail, taxi down for dinner.

## Picking between them

- **Path of least friction:** **Ochsen** or **Bellevue** — walk home from Memories, no late-night taxi negotiation.
- **Treat the lodging as another course:** **Sonnenhof** in Vaduz — Marée is a credible Sunday lunch in its own right; the 13-min drive is a feature, not a cost.
- **Wine-village weekend, not a spa-town weekend:** **Weiss Kreuz** in Malans — four rooms, the same building as a starred restaurant, in the heart of Bündner Herrschaft.
- **Story over comfort:** **Schlaf-Fass** — accept the 40-m midnight walk to the WC.
- **History buff:** **Schloss Ragaz** — actual 1890 castle, antique rooms, parkland.
