The note on the back of the rack

Memories sits inside the Grand Resort, 3 ★ MICHELIN, last seating at 19:00, kitchen closes ~21:30 [2]. Six honest alternatives to the resort, in order of how far you'll taxi home:

  • Walk home — Boutique Hotel Ochsen, ~700 m straight up Bartholoméplatz [3]
  • Second Michelin tomorrow — Park Hotel Sonnenhof in Vaduz, 13 min, Marée on the same property [7][14]
  • Wine-village weekend — Hotel Weiss Kreuz in Malans, four rooms over another starred kitchen [10]
  • A story to tell on Monday — Schlaf-Fass, an 8,000-litre wine barrel above Heidi's village [11]

Hotel Schloss Ragaz history

A real castle. Built ~1890 by Conrad Sonderegger, a Swiss engineer who worked on the Panama Canal.[6]

Three lodging tiers under one estate — antique castle rooms, garden pavilions, glamping lodges. 3-min taxi back from dinner.

Park Hotel Sonnenhof Marée ★

The Michelin-on-Michelin play — perched above town with castle views, Marée (1 star, 16 GaultMillau) in the Eagle's Nest, Hubertus Real cooking.[7][17]

Treat the lodging as another course — Sunday lunch at Marée is its own reason. The 13-min taxi from Memories crosses a national border.[14]

Hotel Weiss Kreuz ★ + 16 GM

Four double rooms above a Michelin-starred restaurant on the village square of Malans — Bündner Herrschaft's heart.[9][10]

The smallest, most idiosyncratic option. If you want the weekend to be wine village, not spa town, this is the answer.

Schlaf-Fass Maienfeld novelty

An actual converted 8,000-litre wine barrel on the Hermann family farm at the foot of Falknis — minutes from Heidi Village, Heidi Fountain, Heidi Alp.[11][12]

Bring the right brain for a 40-metre midnight walk to the WC. Story over comfort — accept the trade or don't.

Berghotel Gaffia

Bookend the Memories night with a mountain morning at 1,750 m.

Nine cozy double and family rooms in an alpine hut, served by the Pizol cable car ~4 km from Bad Ragaz to the base.[13][15]

→ arrive the day before, walk the 5-Lakes trail, taxi down for dinner. Not a late-night taxi destination.

A note to the booker

Which postcard do you want to send home?

  • Path of least friction — Ochsen or Bellevue. Walk home. No taxi.
  • Lodging as another course — Sonnenhof in Vaduz. Marée is a credible Sunday lunch in its own right; the 13-min drive is a feature.
  • Wine-village weekend — Weiss Kreuz. Four rooms, in the same building as a starred kitchen, in the heart of Bündner Herrschaft.
  • Story over comfort — Schlaf-Fass. Accept the 40-m midnight walk to the WC.
  • History buff — Schloss Ragaz. An actual 1890 castle, antique rooms, parkland.
