IV.

The Calendar Trap

Belgian retail rhythm
Sun ≠ Mon

Sunday — museum day

  • OpenKMSKA, MAS, Plantin-Moretus, Het Anker, Mechelen Skywalk, Lier Beguinage.[17]
  • ShutAlmost all shops; many restaurants do reduced hours.[16]
  • Best forThe morning museum, the long Sunday lunch, the brewery tour.

Monday — shops day

  • OpenDIVA (closed Wed instead), shops, cafés, parks.
  • ShutKMSKA, MAS, Plantin-Moretus, FOMU, Red Star Line, MoMu and most other Antwerp/Mechelen museums.
  • Best forAntwerp's Meir + Kammenstraat, a Het Zuid wander, the chocolatiers.
2026 trapsThe Rubens House main building is closed for restoration through ~2030 (only the garden and Rubens Experience are open). MoMu Fashion Museum is shut from May 2026 through March 2027 — verify dates before you build a Sunday around either.[11][12]
