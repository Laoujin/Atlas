> **TL;DR.** Within 30 km of [Nuance](https://guide.michelin.com/en/antwerpen/duffel/restaurant/nuance) [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/antwerpen/duffel/restaurant/nuance) (Duffel — 15 km from Antwerp, 7 km from Mechelen, *Brussels at 53 km is out* [[2]](https://www.distancefromto.net/distance-from-duffel-be-to-brussels-be)) ten major IT conferences run in 2026. Most end Thursday or Friday — the natural Saturday-Nuance bundles are **Devoxx Belgium (5-9 Oct)** [[9]](https://devoxx.be/), **DDD Europe + BC TechDays (8-12 Jun)** [[6]](https://2026.dddeurope.com/) [[7]](https://www.bctechdays.com/event), **SmashingConf (12-15 Oct)** [[11]](https://smashingconf.com/antwerp-2026/) and **CloudBrew (10-11 Dec)** [[13]](https://www.cloudbrew.be/). The only event already on a Saturday is **dataMinds Saturday (21 Feb, free)** [[14]](https://datamindssaturday.be/) — same-day bundle with dinner.

## Saturday-dinner pairing logic

Nuance dinner is the anchor. Conferences in Belgium run on weekdays — to bundle them, target the Saturday *after* a multi-day event so you stay one extra night.

| Saturday dinner   | Pair with                                          | Reasoning                                                |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sat 21 Feb**    | [dataMinds Saturday][e1] (same day)                | Conference 08:45-18:00 in Mechelen → 19:00 dinner Duffel |
| **Sat 16 May**    | [Techorama][e3] (ends Wed 13 May)                  | Stay through weekend in Antwerp                          |
| **Sat 13 Jun**    | [DDD Europe][e4] / [BC TechDays][e6] (both end Fri 12 Jun) | Densest week — three confs converge                |
| **Sat 26 Sep**    | [BruCON][e7] (ends Fri 25 Sep)                     | After hacker-con wind-down in Mechelen                   |
| **Sat 10 Oct**    | [Devoxx][e8] (ends Fri 9 Oct)                      | The largest, most international option                   |
| **Sat 17 Oct**    | [SmashingConf][e10] / [dataMinds Connect][e9] (end 14-15 Oct) | Two confs, one weekend                         |
| **Sat 7 Nov**     | [AutomationSTAR][e11] (ends Thu 5 Nov)             | Long weekend buffer                                      |
| **Sat 12 Dec**    | [CloudBrew][e12] (ends Fri 11 Dec)                 | Year-closer                                              |

## Recurring communities (no fixed 2026 schedule but worth tracking)

Smaller free events, mostly evening sessions — useful filler if your weekend wraps a weekday meetup.

- **[OWASP Belgium chapter][m1]** — quarterly free meetups; 2026 sessions confirmed on Karel de Grote Hogeschool Groenplaats campus (Antwerp). Pentest themes, named speakers (Pentesterlabs, Toreon) [[17]][m1].
- **[VISUG][m2]** — Belgian Visual Studio User Group, evening .NET/Microsoft talks; **20-year anniversary in 2026** with a VISUG XL full-day [[18]][m2].
- **[Belgian Kotlin User Group][m3]** — confirmed Antwerp meetup **Thu 4 Jun 2026, 18:30** [[19]][m3].
- **[Fronteers BE][m4]** — front-end community; meetup at **Axxes Antwerp, Thu 21 May 2026, 18:00** [[20]][m4].
- **[Antwerp School of AI][m5]** — regular AI learning sessions, beginner-friendly [[21]][m5].
- **[Belgian Java User Group][m6]** — BeJUG, founded by Devoxx organiser Stephan Janssen; free JVM evening sessions year-round [[22]][m6].
- **[Open Garage][m7]** (Borsbeek, adjacent to Antwerp) — weekly Thursday electronics/maker night via Hackeragenda BE [[23]][m7].

## Notes

- Lamot Mechelen is the single most-used venue in the 30 km ring — three majors per year (BruCON, dataMinds Connect, CloudBrew) plus regular smaller events. Worth knowing if you book a Mechelen hotel: walk from Lamot → train → Duffel is faster than driving back to a hotel in Antwerp.
- Brussels (53 km) and Ghent (70 km) carry their own conference scenes (EuroBSDCon, EuroSTAR, NG-BE) but fall outside the 30 km brief — not listed.
- The user-group landscape has thinned: Full Stack Antwerp wound down after 8 years; AntwerpJUG is marked inactive on Meetup. BeJUG and VISUG remain the active country-wide JVM/.NET communities [[22]][m6] [[18]][m2].
