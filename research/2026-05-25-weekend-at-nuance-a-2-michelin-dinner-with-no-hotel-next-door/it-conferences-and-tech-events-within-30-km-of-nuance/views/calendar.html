★ Same-day bundle

Saturday 21 February

dataMinds Saturday runs 08:45–18:00 at Thomas More De Vest in Mechelen — free, one day, Microsoft Data Platform community talks. The only conference on this list that is a Saturday.

Walk to Mechelen station, hop one stop to Duffel, taxi to Nuance for 19:00. Same-day pairing, no second night.

★★ Densest week of the year

Saturday 13 June

DDD Europe (Queen Elisabeth Hall) and BC TechDays (Kinepolis) both end Friday 12 June. Three days of overlap, two venues 4 km apart, ~1,500+ combined delegates.

Stay Friday night in Antwerp, Saturday is yours — KMSKA, the Plantin-Moretus, then 25 min by car to Nuance.
