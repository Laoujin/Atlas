Sunday = museums.

Belgian museums mostly open Sunday, closed Monday. Spend Sunday in Mechelen or at the KMSKA in Antwerp. [103]

Monday = shops & cafés.

Belgian retail closes Sunday and reopens Monday 10–18. Save the brown café crawl, the DIVA diamond museum (the only big one open Mon) and the brewery walk-ins for the Monday after. [102]

Planning notes

Don't plan around these — they bite first-timers.[103]

  • Rubens House main building — closed for renovation through ~2030. Garden & Rubens Experience still open.
  • MoMu Fashion Museum — full closure 4 May 2026 → end of March 2027.
  • Kazerne Dossin closes Wednesdays + Jewish holidays.
  • DIVA closes Wednesdays — but is open both Sunday and Monday, the only big one.
  • Hof van Busleyden is Wed–Sun only — closed Mon AND Tue.
  • Antwerp Cathedral on Sunday is tourist-open 13:00–17:00 only. Mornings = mass.
  • Last train Duffel ⇄ Mechelen runs late; the Duffel station S1 spine runs 2×/h.[84]
  • From the station, the F1 fietssnelweg goes through Duffel to Antwerp — Blue-bike at €1 / 30 min for members.[92]
