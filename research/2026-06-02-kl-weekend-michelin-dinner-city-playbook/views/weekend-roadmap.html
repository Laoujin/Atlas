Step 3 — Before you go

Practical notes

Dress code
Smart-casual minimum at every Michelin restaurant. Molina and DC by Darren Chin attract a formally dressed room. Practical fix: linen or tropical-weight fabric — meets the floor without melting in 32°C midday heat. [1]
Weather — June
Tropical monsoon. Routine afternoon thunderstorms 3–5pm. Morning windows stay clear. Plan outdoor activity before noon. Restaurants and malls run aggressive A/C — bring a thin layer for the dinner.
Booking window
Book 4–6 weeks in advance. Dewakan and Beta KL fill fastest on Saturdays. Dewakan requires a credit card guarantee; Beta KL holds RM300/pp. [2] [4]
Getting around
Grab is the default. MRT/KTM for Batu Caves (35 min, MYR 2.30). Day trips beyond ~30 km — Melaka, Genting, Sky Mirror — don't slot into a Friday–Sunday window. They need a Thursday arrival.
