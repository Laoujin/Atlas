Decision — If you can secure a table at El Celler de Can Roca (3★, Girona city, book the 1st of the month for a date 11 months out), do it: it is Spain's only 3-star and rated 99/100 by La Liste in 2026.[3] If El Celler is full, three 2-star alternatives are within 50 minutes: Bo.TiC (closest, creative Catalan in a historic carpentry), Les Cols (volcanic terroir + Pritzker Prize architecture), or Miramar (seafront, sleepable, Mediterranean sea-cooking).

The Restaurants

⭐⭐⭐ El Celler de Can Roca

📍 Carrer de Can Sunyer 48, Girona 📞 +34 972 222 157 ⏰ Tue–Sat, lunch 12:30–13:30 / dinner 19:00–20:30

The only 3-Michelin-star restaurant in Girona province[1] and twice ranked #1 in the World's 50 Best (2013, 2015).[11] 2026 is its 40th anniversary year.[3]

Three brothers run distinct domains: Joan leads the savoury kitchen, Josep oversees an 80,000-bottle wine cellar, and Jordi handles desserts — they describe themselves as "an equilateral triangle that encompasses the three sides of gastronomy."[15] Each service deploys 60 staff.[15]

Menu: A single Festival Menu — appetizers + 12 courses + 3 desserts. Smart casual dress; no flip-flops.[15]

Booking: book exactly 11 months out Reservations open on the 1st of each month at midnight (local time) for dates 11 months later — e.g. 1 July opens July of next year. Tables for 2–12 guests. No prepayment; credit card required. Book at cellercanroca.com or call Tue–Sat 10am–6pm.[2]

Price: €315/person (Festival Menu) + optional wine pairing €155.[2]
Summer closure 2026: 16 Aug – 1 Sep.

⭐⭐ Bo.TiC 24 km / 28 min from Girona

📍 Av. Costa Brava 6, 17121 Corçà 📞 +34 972 630 869 ⏰ Lunch 12:45–4:45 / Dinner 7:45–11:45

Set inside a 110-year-old restored carriage carpentry (renovated 2017) in a quiet Baix Empordà village.[5] Chef Albert Sastregener and director Cristina Torrent have run the kitchen since 2007 with a team of 20 professionals.[5]

Two minimalist dining rooms: one with a chef's table overlooking the open kitchen, one in the original stone-walled carpentry space.[6] Imaginative techniques rooted in Catalan tradition; some vegetables and Japanese microgreens supplied by zero-miles grower Hidenori Futami.[6] Wine list: 400+ references.[4]

⚠ No public transport — car or taxi from Girona.[17]

Price: Degustación menu €190 · Chef's menu €250 (per person, excl. drinks).[4]
Book at bo-tic.myrestoo.net or call directly.

⭐⭐ 🍃 Green Star Les Cols 51 km / 43 min from Girona

📍 Ctra de la Canya S/N, 17800 Olot (La Garrotxa) 📞 +34 972 269 209

Chef Fina Puigdevall and her daughter Martina Puigvert have shaped this restaurant for over 30 years.[12] The philosophy: seasonal produce from the volcanic Garrotxa landscape — buckwheat, local potatoes, freshwater fish, farm eggs — described as "austere and humble but intuitive and authentic."[9]

The restaurant occupies a converted 13th-century farmhouse, expanded by RCR Arquitectes (2017 Pritzker Prize, based in Olot) with signature glass pavilions: 3 m-tall, 30 cm-thick glass slabs forming zigzagging vegetal corridors and a sky-framing overnight wing of 5 rooms.[10] The space is considered among the world's most architecturally distinctive dining rooms.

Holds a Michelin Green Star for sustainability alongside its two gastronomy stars.[1]

Price: €180 single tasting menu (VAT incl.) · wine pairing +€65 · cheese course +€25.[9]
Reservations: lescols.com or email lescols@lescols.com.

⭐⭐ Miramar 63 km / 49 min from Girona

📍 Passeig Marítim 7, 17490 Llançà 📞 +34 972 380 132 ⏰ Wed–Sat lunch + dinner / Sun lunch / closed Mon–Tue

Founded in 1939 on the seafront promenade of Llançà (Alt Empordà). Chef Paco Pérez centres his cuisine on the Mar d'Amunt — the local stretch of Mediterranean — with avant-garde techniques applied to hyperlocal seafood, seaweed, and Empordà produce.[16]

Two tasting menus: MAR (a marine journey through local and Galician waters) and Memory, Territory & Culture (a shorter Empordà-focused selection).[7] À la carte also available (individual dishes €40–€120).[8]

Stay the night: 5 seafront suites allow a full evening without the drive back — the only 2-star in the province with overnight accommodation.[7] Girona is also reachable by train (~55 min, hourly).[13]

Price: Tasting menus ~€225 est. (contact restaurant for current rate).[8]
Book at restaurantmiramar.com or call +34 972 380 132.
