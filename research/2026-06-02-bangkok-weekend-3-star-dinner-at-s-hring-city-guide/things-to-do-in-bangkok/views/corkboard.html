🇹🇭 Bangkok Weekend

temples · street food · rooftops · markets · massage · river
Anchor plan: Riverside hotel (walk to 3★ dinner) → Sat AM Wat Pho + Wat Arun → Or Tor Kor lunch → Health Land massage → 3★ dinner → nightcap at BKK Social Club. Sun: Chatuchak before 10:30 → Mahanakhon sunset → Yaowarat crawl. [6]
