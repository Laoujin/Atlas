## Major Multi-Track Events

### Techsauce Global Summit — Aug 26–28
[Queen Sirikit National Convention Center (QSNCC)](https://www.qsncc.com/en/), Bangkok [[1]](https://summit.techsauce.co/). Southeast Asia's largest private-sector tech summit: 20,000+ attendees, 400+ speakers, 10+ stages, 130+ sessions, 30+ workshops, 200+ tech exhibitors, and curated 1-on-1 business matching [[1]](https://summit.techsauce.co/). Topics span AI, fintech, sustainability, and venture investment. Tickets via summit.techsauce.co (2026 pricing TBA at time of writing).

### Money20/20 Asia — Apr 21–23 (past)
[QSNCC](https://www.qsncc.com/en/), Bangkok [[2]](https://asia.money2020.com/attend). Asia's flagship fintech summit: autonomous AI agents, continuous identity verification, programmable compliance, cross-border payments, ASEAN tokenisation strategies [[2]](https://asia.money2020.com/attend). Audience: banks, fintechs, payment networks, regulators. 2027 edition scheduled Apr 27–29 [[15]](https://asia.money2020.com/).

### DigiTech ASEAN Thailand + AI Connect — Nov 25–27
Hall 7–8, [IMPACT Exhibition & Convention Centre](https://www.impact.co.th/en/), Bangkok [[3]](https://digitechasean.com/overview/). 10,000 sqm trade expo co-organized by Thailand's Ministry of Digital Economy & Society and Ministry of Higher Education, Science, Research and Innovation: ~400 exhibitors, ~12,000 attendees [[3]](https://digitechasean.com/overview/). AI Connect 2026 (AI For Business) runs simultaneously. Visitor registration: digitechasean.com.

## Developer & Open-Source Events

### FOSSASIA Summit — Mar 8–10 (past)
[True Digital Park West](https://www.truedigitalpark.com/en), Bangkok [[4]](https://www.techtalkthai.com/fossasia-summit-2026-returns-to-bangkok-focusing-on-ai-postgresql-cybersecurity-and-devops-8-10-march-2026/). Largest open-source event in Southeast Asia: Mar 8 free community day, Mar 9–10 main summit with 200+ international speakers [[4]](https://www.techtalkthai.com/fossasia-summit-2026-returns-to-bangkok-focusing-on-ai-postgresql-cybersecurity-and-devops-8-10-march-2026/). Tracks: AI/Data, PGDay Bangkok, Cloud/DevOps, Cybersecurity/Privacy, Web/Mobile, Open Hardware. Annual event; 2027 edition expected Mar 2027.

### Microsoft Build //localhost:Bangkok — Jun 14 & Jun 21
Two related sessions in the Microsoft Build //localhost:Asia series [[14]](https://dev.events/AS/TH/Bangkok/tech):

- **Jun 14** (Global AI Community): [SCBX NEXT TECH](https://www.scbx.com/), 4th Floor, Siam Paragon — Azure AI workshops, hands-on labs [[5]](https://globalai.community/events/build-localhost/4n0bissa/)
- **Jun 21** (Microsoft Reactor official): 4th Floor, 991/1 Rama I Rd, Pathum Wan — Azure AI, Microsoft Foundry, GitHub Copilot; conducted in Thai; register with Microsoft Account at the link above [[6]](https://developer.microsoft.com/en-us/reactor/events/27200/)

Space is limited for both; early registration recommended [[6]](https://developer.microsoft.com/en-us/reactor/events/27200/).

## Research Conferences

### IAIT2026 — Jun 17–19
Knowledge Exchange Center (KX), 10th Floor, 110/1 Krung Thon Buri Road, Bangkok ([King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi](https://www.kmutt.ac.th/)) [[7]](https://www.iait-conf.org/2026/). Theme: *Trustworthy AI and Cybersecurity: Foundations for a Resilient Digital Future*. 9 tracks including LLMs, cybersecurity architecture, blockchain, digital governance, cloud/edge/IoT, data ethics, and sustainable computing. Keynotes from IIT Delhi and NASA JPL [[7]](https://www.iait-conf.org/2026/).

| Ticket type                           | Fee        |
|---------------------------------------|------------|
| Full registration (international)     | 18,500 THB |
| Full registration (Thai affiliation)  | 15,000 THB |
| Attendee (non-author)                 | 6,500 THB  |

Papers published in ACM ICPS (open access) [[7]](https://www.iait-conf.org/2026/).

### ICTIS 2026 — Apr 9–11 (past)
Hybrid — physical Bangkok Apr 10, online Apr 9–11. Focus: intelligent agents, distributed computing, collaborative technologies [[8]](https://ictisthailand.com/). Springer-published proceedings. Phase 2 held in New York May 22–23 [[8]](https://ictisthailand.com/).

## Cybersecurity Events

### CYSEC Thailand 2026 — Jul 22
[Royal Orchid Sheraton Hotel & Towers](https://www.royalorchidsheraton.com/), Bangkok [[9]](https://thailand.cysecglobal.com/). CYSEC Global's inaugural Thailand edition. Theme: *Thailand Cyber Shield: Advancing the Nation's Digital Security*. Targets 150+ cybersecurity decision-makers. Topics: cloud security, threat intelligence, AI-driven defence, critical infrastructure resilience, public-private partnerships [[9]](https://thailand.cysecglobal.com/). CPD-approved. Register at thailand.cysecglobal.com.

### Cybersec Asia × Thailand International Cyber Week
[QSNCC](https://www.qsncc.com/en/), Bangkok [[16]](https://cybersec-asia.net/). Annual cross-sector summit for CISOs and government stakeholders. 2027 edition: Jan 20–21 [[16]](https://cybersec-asia.net/).
