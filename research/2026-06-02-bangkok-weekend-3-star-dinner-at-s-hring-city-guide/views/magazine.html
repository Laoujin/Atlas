The Weekend Brief
Friday — Arrive
Evening
Pick your base. Silom/Sathorn (Banyan Tree) is 5 min from BTS Chong Nonsi — Sühring at walking distance. Riverside adds 15–20 min taxi each way, but puts you on the Chao Phraya.[3]
Night
Drinks at BKK Social Club (Four Seasons riverside, Asia's 50 Best Bars #19[30]) or The Bamboo Bar at Mandarin Oriental.[20]
Saturday — The Anchor Day
08:00
Wat Pho (300 THB, open 8:00 — Reclining Buddha, mother-of-pearl feet[9]). Cross-river to Wat Arun (200 THB, first terrace only in 2026[10]). Beat tour buses.
11:00
Or Tor Kor Market — Ministry of Agriculture-run food hall, adjacent to Chatuchak. Get there before 10 AM: mango sticky rice and curries sell out.[34]
13:30
Thai massage. Wat Pho school ~520 THB/60 min[11] or Health Land Sathorn (~650–700 THB/2h). Pre-dinner prep.
17:30
Sühring ★★★ — No. 10 Yen Akat Soi 3, Chong Nonsi. The reason for the trip.
Late
Nightcap. Penthouse Bar + Grill (Park Hyatt, live jazz Wed–Sun) or BKK Social Club.
Sunday — The City
08:30
Chatuchak Weekend Market (15,000+ stalls, weekends only 09:00–18:00[12]). Arrive before 10:30 — ~200,000 visitors each weekend.[13]
17:30
Mahanakhon SkyWalk — 314 m, glass-floor tray, closes 19:00. Not a nightcap — a sunset stop.
19:30
Yaowarat street-food crawl — T&K Seafood → Nai Mong Hoi Tod → Pa Tong Go Savoey. (Jay Fai needs its own evening — 2–4h waits.[26])
Skip on a 2-day trip
  • Ayutthaya — 9-hour day trip; only fits a 3+ day stay[14]
  • Damnoen Saduak — widely flagged "busloads buying overpriced souvenirs"
  • Lebua Sky Bar — iconic view, $50+ cocktails, crowded; one-and-done photo stop[23]
  • Asiatique — only worth it if shopping is the priority
  • Grand Palace — 500 THB, polarising; Wat Pho + Wat Arun deliver more per baht[33]

Live Entertainment

Rajadamnern Stadium — the original 1945 Muay Thai venue, with weekly fight cards. Tickets 1,600 THB (2nd class) to 4,500 THB (VIP Lounge Wings). Book online — marquee fights sell out.[24]
