> **Decision:** Book [Jardines Villaverde](https://jardinesvillaverde.com/) — it shares the Plaza del Sol with [Cenador de Amós](https://cenadordeamos.com/en/) [[4]](https://jardinesvillaverde.com/en/activities/) and is the only "open the gate, you're there" option. If full, fall back to [Posada Río Cubas](https://www.posadariocubas.com/web/en/) (≈0.9 km, ~12 min walk) or [Hostería El Laurel](https://www.booking.com/hotel/es/hosteraa-el-laurel.en-gb.html) in Hoz de Anero (≈1.0 km) [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g5562707-d1752010-Cenador_de_Amos-Villaverde_de_Pontones_Cantabria.html). Beyond ~1.5 km after a 3-star tasting menu and wine pairings, take a taxi.

## Why this is a short list

Villaverde de Pontones is a hamlet on the eastern edge of the Bay of Santander. [Cenador de Amós](https://cenadordeamos.com/en/) is set in an 18th-century stone mansion at Plaza del Sol, s/n [[1]](https://cenadordeamos.com/en/) and holds three MICHELIN stars in the 2026 guide [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/cantabria/villaverde-de-pontones/restaurant/cenador-de-amos). The settlement is small enough that Tripadvisor lists only **two** specialty-lodging properties inside Villaverde itself; the rest of the "near" list spills into Cubas, Hoz de Anero, Rivas, and Hoznayo [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g5562707-d1752010-Cenador_de_Amos-Villaverde_de_Pontones_Cantabria.html).

## The pick: Jardines Villaverde

Same address as the restaurant (Calle del Sol s/n, 39793 Villaverde de Pontones) [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g5562707-d25562422-Reviews-Jardines_Villaverde-Villaverde_de_Pontones_Cantabria.html). Its own website volunteers that it sits next to the three-Michelin-star [Cenador de Amós](https://cenadordeamos.com/en/) [[4]](https://jardinesvillaverde.com/en/activities/), and a June 2025 diner review on Tripadvisor confirms staying there to avoid the car: *"We also stayed in apartments that are right next door, Los Jardines de Villaverde, so we avoided taking the car"* [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g5562707-d1752010-Reviews-Cenador_de_Amos-Villaverde_de_Pontones_Cantabria.html).

It is an **aparthotel**, not a hotel — rooms come with full kitchens, balconies, and access to a saltwater pool, hot tub, and gardens; Booking.com guest scores: 9.6 cleanliness, 9.8 location, 9.8 value across 471 reviews [[5]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/es/jardines-villaverde.html). Standard rooms run roughly $124–$277/night depending on date and unit [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g5562707-d25562422-Reviews-Jardines_Villaverde-Villaverde_de_Pontones_Cantabria.html). ⚠ Book early — there are only ~10 units and one obvious reason every Cenador diner wants this address.

## Fallbacks if Jardines Villaverde is sold out

- **[Posada Río Cubas](https://www.posadariocubas.com/web/en/)** — ≈0.9 km on the Cubas valley side; small rural posada with a 9.0 recent-guest score, garden, fireplace lounge, homemade breakfast; "Q" tourist-quality award and Michelin Guide listed [[9]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g644321-d1902978-Reviews-Posada_Rio_Cubas-Somo_Cantabria.html). Closest "character" option and the right vibe if you want the rural-Cantabria experience over a pool deck.
- **[Hostería El Laurel](https://www.booking.com/hotel/es/hosteraa-el-laurel.en-gb.html)** — ≈1.0 km, technically in Hoz de Anero; 8 rooms, balconies over a garden, tennis court; cheap (~$41/night) and the only sub-€100 walking-distance option [[10]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g2561253-d2646344-Reviews-Hosteria_El_Laurel-Hoz_de_Anero_Cantabria.html)[[11]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/es/hosteraa-el-laurel.en-gb.html). ⚠ 4.5★ but only ~60 reviews; expect rural-inn basic, not boutique.
- **[El Mirador de Rivas](https://www.hosteriaelmiradorderivas.com/)** — ≈1.4 km, on the ridge in Agüero/Marina de Cudeyo; 7-room rural posada from ~€64 with views over the Bay of Santander and toward the Picos de Europa, dinner by request [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g8524752-d8021617-Reviews-El_Mirador_de_Rivas-Aguero_Cantabria.html). At the upper edge of what counts as walkable post-tasting-menu; check the route in daylight before committing.

## What's *not* walking distance

Properties in the same Tripadvisor list at 1.6–1.7 km (Palacio de los Acevedo, Posada Termino, Hotel Spa Villa Pasiega, Hotel Los Pasiegos, Apartamentos La Albarca) sit in Hoznayo, the next village over the rise [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g5562707-d1752010-Cenador_de_Amos-Villaverde_de_Pontones_Cantabria.html). They're a ~5-minute drive but a 20+ minute uphill walk on unlit rural road — taxi territory after dinner. Santander itself is ~15 min by car [[5]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/es/jardines-villaverde.html) and the obvious choice if you want urban dining and beaches around the meal, but it's not walking distance to the restaurant.

## Practical notes

- **Reserve in this order:** dinner first (Cenador de Amós books out months ahead), lodging second once the date is locked.
- **Taxis:** rural Cantabria has thin late-night cab availability; arrange the return ride at the start of dinner, not the end.
- **Walking the lane:** Plaza del Sol is unlit village street — bring a phone torch if walking the ~1 km options after a long tasting menu.
