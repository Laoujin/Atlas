The dinner-anchor overlay

Saturdays that already line up.

The geometry of the trip is set by one constraint: do not drive after dinner. So the calendar gets read backwards — find an event that brackets a Saturday, book the dinner that night, sleep in Santander, drive 20 min for the tasting menu. Three weekends already qualify in 2026.

▣ SAT 13 JUN

Dinner → Sun open of ACEDE

ACEDE opens Sunday 14 Jun at UC [3]. Dinner Saturday, hotel in Santander, walk into the congress Sunday morning.
▣ SAT 04 JUL

TAEE close → dinner

TAEE wraps Friday 3 Jul at ETSIIT [4]. Stay one extra night, Saturday at Cenador, drive home Sunday.
▣ SAT ≈ 3 OCT

C-Meet weekend (if confirmed)

C-Meet historically lands early October at Escenario Santander [6]. If the 5th-edition dates land Thu–Fri, the Saturday after pairs cleanly.

Not on the calendar — outside the 30-km circle

The big Spanish IT crowd-pullers — Spring I/O, South Summit, DES — sit in Barcelona, Madrid and Málaga respectively. No FOSDEM/KubeCon/PyData-scale event lands inside the radius in 2026. Torrelavega, Reinosa and Bilbao all fall outside the circle [1].

Borderline. IC2 (construction innovation, 1 June at Palacio de la Magdalena) is on the PINN agenda but skews AEC industry, not software [12].
