> **TL;DR.** A 30 km circle around Cenador de Amós is essentially Santander's metro area [[1]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/villaverde_de_pontones-39793-cantabria-cantabria-spain-to-santander-39001-cantabria-cantabria-spain), and the IT-conference scene there is thin but not empty. **The two anchors for 2026 are [ACEDE](https://www.congresoacede.org/en/) (14–16 Jun, AI-in-business academic congress at the University of Cantabria) [[3]](https://www.congresoacede.org/en/) and [TAEE](https://www.upv.es/entidades/dieo/2025/10/14/xvii-congreso-internacional-taee-2026/) (1–3 Jul, electronics-teaching conference at ETSIIT-UC) [[4]](https://www.upv.es/entidades/dieo/2025/10/14/xvii-congreso-internacional-taee-2026/).** Outside those, expect smaller fare: the **[Caña Digital](https://ceoecantabria.es/ceoe-cepyme-cantabria-y-el-cluster-tera-lanzan-una-nueva-edicion-de-la-cana-digital-2/)** monthly tech-talk series (free, evenings, runs to 17 Dec) [[5]](https://ceoecantabria.es/ceoe-cepyme-cantabria-y-el-cluster-tera-lanzan-una-nueva-edicion-de-la-cana-digital-2/), **CTFs and free training at the [C3 cybersecurity centre](https://centrocibercantabria.es/eventos/categoria/ctf/2026-05/)** in PCTCAN [[7]](https://centrocibercantabria.es/eventos/categoria/ctf/2026-05/)[[8]](https://www.cantabria.es/web/comunicados/w/sodercan-refuerza-el-programa-formativo-del-centro-de-ciberseguridad-para-pymes-y-aut%C3%B3nomos), and **[C-Meet](https://c-meet.es/)** at [Escenario Santander](https://escenariosantander.com/) (5th edition expected autumn, 2026 dates not yet posted; the 2025 edition ran 2–3 Oct with 500+ companies) [[6]](https://c-meet.es/). If your trip date is fixed by the dinner, pair it with whichever event lines up — the calendar is sparse enough that any match is opportunistic, not abundant.

## The 30 km circle, in practical terms

Cenador de Amós sits in Villaverde de Pontones (Ribamontán al Monte), ~10.6 km in a straight line and ~23 km by road from central Santander [[1]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/villaverde_de_pontones-39793-cantabria-cantabria-spain-to-santander-39001-cantabria-cantabria-spain)[[2]](https://www.distanciasentreciudades.com/distancia-villaverde-de-pontones-a-santander). A 30 km radius therefore covers:

- All of Santander city, including [PCTCAN](https://www.pctcan.es/en/) (the science-tech park) [[13]](https://www.pctcan.es/en/), the [University of Cantabria](https://web.unican.es/en/Pages/default.aspx) campuses (Las Llamas, Avda. Castros, ETSIIT) and [Palacio de Exposiciones y Congresos](https://palaciodeexposicionesycongresos.es/en) in El Sardinero.
- The bay-side ring: Astillero, Camargo, Maliaño (Parayas airport).
- East along the A-8 to Solares and Liérganes.
- North-east to Santoña / Laredo (around the edge of the radius).

Nothing in Torrelavega, Reinosa or Bilbao falls inside the circle.

## Picking one for your trip

- **Saturday-dinner anchor + tech for Friday or Sunday.** TAEE 2026 ends Friday 3 July [[4]](https://www.upv.es/entidades/dieo/2025/10/14/xvii-congreso-internacional-taee-2026/) and ACEDE 2026 opens Sunday 14 June [[3]](https://www.congresoacede.org/en/) — both bookend a Saturday at Cenador de Amós cleanly. Stay at a Santander hotel, drive ~20 min for dinner.
- **Casual evening tech-fix.** The Caña Digital talks at Cervezas Smach are 18:30–20:00 free entry with networking [[5]](https://ceoecantabria.es/ceoe-cepyme-cantabria-y-el-cluster-tera-lanzan-una-nueva-edicion-de-la-cana-digital-2/) — pair a Thursday session with a Saturday-dinner weekend.
- **Hands-on security.** C3 runs free training at Torre Xtela (PCTCAN) and a monthly Capture-The-Flag competition [[7]](https://centrocibercantabria.es/eventos/categoria/ctf/2026-05/)[[8]](https://www.cantabria.es/web/comunicados/w/sodercan-refuerza-el-programa-formativo-del-centro-de-ciberseguridad-para-pymes-y-aut%C3%B3nomos) — check the calendar before booking, dates rotate.
- **Bigger Cantabrian tech crowd.** C-Meet is the only event in the region that draws 500+ companies and tier-1 vendor keynotes [[6]](https://c-meet.es/); the autumn slot historically hits early October, so a late-Sept / early-Oct dinner could line up if the 5th edition is announced soon.
- **Local dev community.** GDG Santander runs Google-tech meetups (Flutter, Android, web) but the calendar is sparse — only one upcoming event listed at time of writing [[9]](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-santander/).

## What's *not* here

- No FOSDEM/KubeCon/PyData-scale event lands within 30 km of Cenador de Amós in 2026. The big Spanish IT crowd-pullers (Spring I/O, South Summit, DES) are in Barcelona, Madrid and Málaga respectively.
- Past listings on the Cantabria PINN agenda for May 2026 (CantabRobots + Math at Palacio de Exposiciones, 14 May, 1,500+ schoolkids) [[11]](https://educantabria.es/web/cited/cantabrobots) and the CERN International Quantum Computer Master Class at Facultad de Ciencias UC on 14 April [[10]](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1667605/) are already past relative to today (30 May 2026) but signal what re-runs annually.
- IC2 (construction-innovation, 1 June at Palacio de la Magdalena) is a borderline-tech sector event noted on the [PINN agenda][pinncan] but skewed to AEC industry rather than software/IT [[12]](https://pinncan.cantabria.es/agenda).

## Ecosystem context (why the calendar is sparse)

Cantabria's tech ecosystem is real but small. It is organised around three nodes inside the 30 km radius: the **[Clúster TERA](https://clustertera.es/en/)** ICT industry association (co-organiser of Caña Digital and of the *Green Valley Cantabria Tech* branding) [[14]](https://clustertera.es/en/), the **[PCTCAN](https://www.pctcan.es/en/)** science-tech park hosting 82 companies and ~4,800 employees including the C3 cybersecurity centre [[13]](https://www.pctcan.es/en/)[[8]](https://www.cantabria.es/web/comunicados/w/sodercan-refuerza-el-programa-formativo-del-centro-de-ciberseguridad-para-pymes-y-aut%C3%B3nomos), and **[CISE](https://www.cise.es/programas-para-emprendedores/startinnova/)** for entrepreneurship programming [[15]](https://www.cise.es/programas-para-emprendedores/startinnova/). When new conferences are announced, they almost always land at the University of Cantabria, Palacio de Exposiciones, or Escenario Santander — bookmark those three plus the [PINN agenda][pinncan] for last-minute additions [[12]](https://pinncan.cantabria.es/agenda).
