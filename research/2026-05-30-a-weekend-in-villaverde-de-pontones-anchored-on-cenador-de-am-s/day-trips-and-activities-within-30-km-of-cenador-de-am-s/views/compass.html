A 30 km radius
around Cenador de Amós

Anchor: Villaverde de Pontones. North-east the dunes and surf-reserve beaches; west the Bay, Santander city, and the reclaimed iron mine that turned wildlife park; south the Pasiego village of Liérganes and its four-century-old spa. Everything inside a 30-minute drive.

The four outings the radius is built around

  1. Cabárceno + Peña Cabarga. Half-day wildlife park on a reclaimed iron mine; the new glass-floor mirador opened March 2026. [31][32][34]
  2. Santander city. Centro Botín + the Magdalena peninsula + a pintxo crawl on the port. [15]
  3. Santoña. Anchoa-factory tasting and the Monte Buciero loop — but the Faro del Caballo staircase is closed all summer 2026. [76]
  4. The bay itself. Cross by Los Reginas ferry to Somo for surfing or a Pedreña port lunch. [75]
⚠ Notice to navigators · summer 2026

Faro del Caballo staircase — closed all summer

The famous 763-step descent on Monte Buciero is barred for rockfall stabilisation: loose blocks ~20 m above the steps. The wider 9.5 km Monte Buciero loop (~3 h, moderate) stays open and is the substitute. [76] [77]

Liérganes

Nicknamed the "Santillana pasiega" — cobbled centre of 17th–18th-c. stone casonas, anchored by the 16th-c. Renaissance Puente Mayor, the Hombre Pez statue and the cannon-decorated Casa de los Cañones. The twin hills above are locally las tetas de Liérganes. [38] [46]

Spa · 4 centuries old

Balneario de Liérganes

Four-century-old thermal house using sulphurated mineral-medicinal waters from the Santa Fuente spring (declared of public utility 1869). 3-ha park of centenary trees. Birthplace of the hombre pez legend. [39] [40]

Practical · before you go

CarA rental is essential. Drive times above are off-peak; in August add 20–30% for coastal traffic.
Best seasonMay–June and September: mild, less crowded, Cabárceno medium tariff applies, surf is mellower for beginners.
Bay crossingLos Reginas ferry runs 365 days/yr, every ~30 min, €4 OW / €6.50 RT — cheapest scenic crossing on the bay.
ReservationsCenador books months ahead. Anchoa tours (5Ñ, Avelina, Codesa) want a few days notice. Pedreña needs handicap up front.
ClosureThe 763-step Faro del Caballo descent on Monte Buciero is closed all summer 2026 — rockfall stabilisation.
Cantabrian ciderFor Cantabrian (not Asturian) cider, pair cordero a la estaca at El Molino de la Hoz with Sidra Somarroza.
