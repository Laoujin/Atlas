The Friday-vs-Saturday call

the open question of the brief
  • Anchor first. De Librije reservations go months out — pick a weekend window only after the table is locked [12].
  • If flexible, Thursday arrival bolts a Spoorzone tech event onto the front half and aligns the museum with its free-Friday slot.
  • If May: 29–31 May sits inside the Unlimited opening, no Wine Festival.
  • If June: 5–7 June overlaps the Wine Festival at Grote Kerkplein on 5 June [7].
  • Open: how far ahead does the Friday seating actually fill vs. Saturday — and is the Friday cheaper, or just easier?
