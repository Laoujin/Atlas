> **TL;DR** — Zwolle hosts no flagship IT conference. Plan around two recurring meetups (DevPulse, quarterly, free, Sep 3 & Dec 3 2026 [[2]](https://www.meetup.com/dev-pulse/); DevSessions, monthly-ish, free [[1]](https://www.meetup.com/nl-nl/webdevzwolle/)) and a busy two weeks in early-mid June at the Spoorzone / Perron038 hub on Hanzelaan 95B (Demodag Jun 2, Additive Manufacturing Jun 5, Innovation Day Jun 11, Boost Summit Jun 17) [[5]](https://www.spoorzonezwolle.nl/evenementen). For a national-scale tech conference, look to Amsterdam (DEVWorld, DevOpsDays) [[12]](https://devworldconference.com/).

Note the cluster: four knowledge events at Hanzelaan 95B between Jun 2 and Jun 17 [[5]](https://www.spoorzonezwolle.nl/evenementen), anchored by the paid Innovation Day festival [[6]](https://innovationday.protozwolle.nl/spoorzone-zwolle/). If a weekend in Zwolle straddles one of those Thursdays, it's easy to bolt a half-day of tech onto the trip.

## The three places it actually happens

- **Perron038 — Hanzelaan 95B, Spoorzone.** Open innovation centre with five themed labs: 3D metal printing, AI, vision/optics, robotics, data-driven manufacturing [[4]](https://www.perron038.nl/). Operated by [Tembo](https://www.tembo.eu/our-companies/highlights-of-the-tembo-companies/perron038); houses the manufacturing-adjacent knowledge events above.
- **De Makersfabriek — Ceintuurbaan 15.** The former Philips factory, now home to [ZWINC](https://zwinc.nl/) (Zwolle Incubator): flex workspace €195/month, a €2,750 innovation budget, and the Ketelhuis hall where DevPulse meets [[8]](https://zwinc.nl/). Manufacturing + IT startup focus, partnered with Windesheim [[13]](https://www.windesheim.nl/bedrijven-en-organisaties/samenwerkingen/zwinc).
- **Windesheim University — Campus 2.** Hosts Winnovation (HBO-ICT showcase) twice a year and a small calendar of HBO-ICT company contact days [[7]](https://www.windesheim.nl/agenda/2026/januari/winnovation).

[Digital District Zwolle](https://www.digitaldistrictzwolle.nl/) is the umbrella network linking these — 100+ regional IT organisations, running Digital Impulse, IT Café, and C-level Executive Meetings [[10]](https://www.digitaldistrictzwolle.nl/). [Regio Zwolle IT Platform](https://www.regiozwolleitplatform.nl/home) is the directory of jobs/events/orgs, live since 2019 [[11]](https://www.regiozwolleitplatform.nl/home).

## Reality check

There is no Zwolle equivalent of DEVWorld, GOTO, or DevOpsDays — those live in Amsterdam, Utrecht, and Rotterdam, which together host the overwhelming majority of Dutch IT conferences [[12]](https://devworldconference.com/). Zwolle's strength is the recurring meetup and the Spoorzone knowledge-event cluster — community-scale, not conference-scale. Plan accordingly: if you want a big-tent IT conference in your weekend, ride the train to Amsterdam (75 min); if you want a free evening of talks with dinner included, time your trip to a DevPulse or DevSessions Thursday.
