Hanzelaan 95B · Spoorzone

Open innovation centre — five themed labs: 3D metal printing, AI, vision/optics, robotics, data-driven manufacturing. Houses the four June knowledge events. [4]

devpulse

De Makersfabriek

Ceintuurbaan 15 · ex-Philips factory

Home of ZWINC (Zwolle Incubator) — flex workspace €195/mo, €2,750 innovation budget, partnered with Windesheim. DevPulse meets in the Ketelhuis hall. [8]

winnovation

Windesheim · Campus 2

Building X · HBO-ICT showcase

Winnovation twice yearly — Jan 15 (past) and Jun 11. 3rd/4th-year students present Business / Dev / Games / Security / Smart projects. Free; small HBO-ICT contact-day calendar alongside. [7]

No DEVWorld in Zwolle.

The conference-scale stuff — DEVWorld, GOTO, DevOpsDays — lives in Amsterdam, Utrecht, Rotterdam. Zwolle's strength is community-scale: the recurring meetup and the Spoorzone knowledge-event cluster. [12] If you want a big-tent IT conference in your weekend, ride the train; if you want a free evening of talks with dinner included, time the trip to a DevPulse Thursday.
