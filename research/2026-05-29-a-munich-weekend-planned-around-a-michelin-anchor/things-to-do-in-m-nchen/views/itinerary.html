Plan rule · 01

Anchor the Michelin dinner on Saturday night. None of Munich's six top-tier rooms open Sunday, JAN also skips Saturday — five of the six (Tohru, Tantris, KOMU, Alois, Atelier) serve Saturday [★]. Saturday-evening lock makes Friday a walking arrival day and Sunday a short morning + departure.

Friday · Arrival
Land, drop bags, walk the Altstadt loop. End in a beer hall, not a kitchen — Saturday gets the kitchen.

Base · Lehel or Altstadt
Check in, then walk
Lehel is airier and quieter, Museum Mile on Prinzregentenstr. [4]. Altstadt puts Marienplatz at your door [1].
16:30sight
Sight · 15 min
Marienplatz + Glockenspiel
Time the 17:00 chime (extra show Mar–Oct) [9]. Watch one verse, move on — the wait is famously a tax some travellers reject [10].
17:30sleeper hit
Sight · 15 min · free
Asamkirche
10 min south of Marienplatz on Sendlinger Str. — a private rococo chapel by the Asam brothers, tiny and over-the-top [24].
18:15graze
Food · Mon–Sat only
Viktualienmarkt
Leberkäs-Semmel at the Metzgerzeile butcher row, Brezn, Obatzda [59]. The market biergarten in the centre rotates all 6 city breweries every 5–6 weeks [50].
19:30stroll
Sight · 20 min · free
Hofgarten + Residenz facade
Renaissance pocket-park next to the Residenz — a breather, not a destination [26]. Skip the museum interior tonight; book a Saturday morning if you want it (€10, combo €15) [11].
20:30first beer
Beer hall · daily 10–24
Augustiner Stammhaus
The 1328 brewery's city tavern, Jugendstil rooms, hidden back biergarten. Locals' pick over Hofbräuhaus — though the latter is "must do at least once" [54] [55].
22:30nightcap
Beer hall · optional
Schneider Bräuhaus
Tap 7, Aventinus, the full Schneider wheat range — more local-feeling than Hofbräuhaus, at Tal 7 near Marienplatz [56].
Walking: ~4 km · all Altstadt Eat light — kitchen's tomorrow
30
Saturday · Anchor day
Front-load museums + Englischer Garten, then home to change. Evening belongs to the kitchen.
09:00breakfast
Food · daily
Rischart at Marienplatz
Marienplatz 18, reportedly Germany's most-visited bakery branch. Sourdough, Brezn, breakfast platters [60].
10:00main museum
Art · 2–3 h · Tue–Sun
Lenbachhaus
World's largest Blue Rider holding — 90 Kandinskys plus Marc, Münter, Klee from the 1957 Münter bequest [33]. Sat: 10–18 [34]. The single most distinctive art museum in town [44].
12:30bonus
Art · 1.5 h · optional
Museum Brandhorst
Next door — 170+ Cy Twombly works including the Lepanto cycle on the whole upper floor [35]. Skip and head straight to the Eisbach if Lenbachhaus filled you up.
14:00lunch
Food · cash only
Café Frischhut · Schmalznudel
Prälat-Zistl-Str. 8 — lard-fried Auszogne, cult Munich snack near Viktualienmarkt. Cash only [58].
15:00sight
Sight · 15 min · free
Eisbach surfers
Bridge by Haus der Kunst on Prinzregentenstr. — the world's most consistent city-centre river-surf wave, reopened May 2026 after a year of closure [16]. Watching from the bridge is free and unrestricted.
15:30stroll
Park + half-Maß · 90 min
Englischer Garten → Chinesischer Turm
Walk north from the Eisbach. 7,000-seat biergarten with rotating brass bands, Steckerlfisch and Hendl [48]. One small Maß only — the kitchen tonight is serious.

31
Sunday · Short morning + out
Most museums shut Mon, but Viktualienmarkt is shut Sun. Pick a high vantage and a Sunday biergarten lunch.
09:30tradition
Food · before noon
Weisswurst breakfast
"White sausages don't hear the 12 o'clock chime" — a pre-refrigeration rule still widely honoured [57]. Take them at Schneider or Augustiner Stammhaus.
11:30opens late
Sight · 30 min · €7.50
Frauenkirche south tower
89 steps + lift to a 360° deck; Alps visible on a clear day [18]. Sun opens 11:30, last ascent 16:30 [19].
13:00lunch
Biergarten · Sun OK
Augustiner-Keller or Hirschgarten
Augustiner-Keller (Arnulfstr. 52) — Munich's oldest, 5,000 seats, Edelstoff straight from the wooden barrel [45]. Hirschgarten is bigger (8,000, the world's largest), fewer tourists, a deer paddock alongside [47].

Local code

Biergarten like a local

BYO
In the self-service section you may bring your own food but must buy drinks on site — a 200-year-old Bavarian law, revised 1999 [52].
✕
Tablecloths mark the waitered area where outside food is forbidden. Read the table before you sit [52].
🍺
Locals' default order is Augustiner Edelstoff at any Augustiner garden [61].
💰
Expect a small refundable mug deposit on the Maß.
