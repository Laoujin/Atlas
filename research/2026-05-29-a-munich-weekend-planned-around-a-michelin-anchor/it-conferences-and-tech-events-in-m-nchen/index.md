## Picking by trip shape

- **One Michelin dinner + one conference day:** Cloud Native Summit (Mon Jun 29) or apidays (Wed Jul 8) — both one-day-pass-able [[8]](https://www.cnsmunich.com/) [[7]](https://www.apidays.global/events/munich).
- **Weekend-only, no vacation days:** hackaTUM (Nov 27–29) or EDTH (Jun 26–28) [[14]](https://hack.tum.de/) [[15]](https://luma.com/edth-anniversary-munich).
- **Combined with Oktoberfest:** Bits & Pretzels Sep 28–30 ends on Wiesn day 3 — extend through the weekend [[4]](https://www.bitsandpretzels.com/).
- **Pure networking, no agenda lock-in:** any AI Tinkerers month [[16]](https://munich.aitinkerers.org/).
- **Avoid these weeks unless that's the trip:** automatica (Jun 22–25) and electronica (Nov 10–13) — hotels spike across the city [[12]](https://automatica-munich.com/en/) [[13]](https://electronica.de/en/trade-fair/).
