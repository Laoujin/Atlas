In the margin
Monthly fixtures

For a weekend trip that lands on a Mon/Tue/Wed evening, the meetup circuit is the path of least resistance — free, drop-in, no badge.

↓ what runs every month ↓

  • monthly
    AI Tinkerers Munichcode-only

    5-min lightning demos · running code only · no slides allowed · ~3hr · hosted at CDTM. Filters for builders rather than the keynote circuit [16].

  • ~monthly
    Munich Datageeks

    Talks + networking at Giesinger Bräu Werk2. Slides, YouTube content, free RSVP [18].

  • irregular
    Munich Kubernetes / Cloud Native / Platform Eng

    Talks · rotating sponsors. The local arm of the dev-conference scene between W-JAX, DevOpsCon and Cloud Native Summit cycles [19].

  • annual
    Forum UnternehmerTUMinvite-only

    May 21 at Munich Urban Colab · ~600 hand-picked changemakers and international visionaries · Europe's Leading Startup Hub flagship [17].

Picking by trip shape

1 Michelin + 1 day
Cloud Native Summit (Mon Jun 29) or apidays (Wed Jul 8) — both one-day-pass-able [8][7].
weekend only
hackaTUM (Nov 27–29) or EDTH (Jun 26–28) — no vacation days burnt [14][15].
w/ Oktoberfest
Bits & Pretzels Sep 28–30 ends on Wiesn day 3 — extend through the weekend [4].
pure networking
Any AI Tinkerers month. Zero lock-in, free RSVP, builders only [16].
⚠ avoid
automatica (Jun 22–25) and electronica (Nov 10–13) unless that is the trip — hotels spike across the city [12][13].
⚠ not in 2026
