> **TL;DR:** Arrive by **ETS train to Butterworth + the RM2 (€0.45) RapidFerry** into George Town [[3]](https://malaysiatrains.com/kuala-lumpur-to-penang-by-train/) [[14]](https://onpenang.com/butterworth-to-penang-island-ferry/), or a 4–5h coach from the Cameron Highlands (~€8) [[1]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Cameron-Highlands/Penang) [[2]](https://www.busonlineticket.com/booking/cameron-highlands-to-penang-bus-tickets); leave for Langkawi by a **20-min flight from ~€13** — the safe choice, since the direct ferry is intermittently revived and unreliable [[5]](https://onpenang.com/penang-to-langkawi/) [[8]](https://www.skyscanner.com.my/routes/pen/lgk/penang-to-langkawi.html). On the ground, use Grab and the **free CAT shuttle** [[10]](https://onpenang.com/george-town-free-cat-bus/). The one unmissable day-trip: **[Penang Hill](https://www.penanghill.gov.my/) funicular + Kek Lok Si temple at Air Itam** [[11]](https://www.penanghill.gov.my/index.php/en/tickets) [[12]](https://onpenang.com/kek-lok-si-temple/).

Currency: **€1 ≈ MYR 4.6** (1 MYR ≈ €0.217, June 2026) [[17]](https://www.foreignexchange.org.uk/fx-rates/conversion/1/MYR/EUR). All EUR below are approximate. Penang Island has no airport-rail link and no metro — the rhythm is *intercity by train/coach/flight, local by bus + Grab*.

## 1) Getting in & out

George Town sits on Penang Island; the mainland rail/bus hub is **Butterworth (Penang Sentral)**, linked by a 10–15 min passenger ferry. Penang International (PEN) is in the island's southeast.

| Hop | Mode | Time | Approx EUR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cameron Highlands → Penang | Direct coach (Sg. Nibong / Penang Sentral) [[1]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Cameron-Highlands/Penang) [[2]](https://www.busonlineticket.com/booking/cameron-highlands-to-penang-bus-tickets) | 4.5–5h | €7–9 |
| KL (KL Sentral) → Butterworth | [KTMB](https://www.ktmb.com.my/) ETS train (Gold/Platinum/Express) [[3]](https://malaysiatrains.com/kuala-lumpur-to-penang-by-train/) [[4]](https://ets-tickets.com/kl-sentral-to-butterworth/) | 3h35–4h20 | €17–24 |
| KL (TBS) → Penang | Intercity coach [[20]](https://www.redbus.my/bus-tickets/kuala-lumpur-to-penang) [[23]](https://www.geckoroutes.com/malaysia/penang-to-kuala-lumpur/) | 5–6h | €7–13 |
| KL / elsewhere → PEN airport | Flight ([AirAsia](https://www.airasia.com/), MAS, Firefly) [[8]](https://www.skyscanner.com.my/routes/pen/lgk/penang-to-langkawi.html) | ~1h | from €13 |
| Butterworth ↔ George Town | [RapidFerry](https://onpenang.com/butterworth-to-penang-island-ferry/) (cashless) [[14]](https://onpenang.com/butterworth-to-penang-island-ferry/) | 10–15 min | €0.45 (RM2) |
| Penang → Langkawi | Flight (recommended) [[5]](https://onpenang.com/penang-to-langkawi/) [[8]](https://www.skyscanner.com.my/routes/pen/lgk/penang-to-langkawi.html) | 20 min flight | €13–27 |
| Penang → Langkawi | Direct ferry (⚠ status unstable) [[6]](https://www.visitpenang.com/destinations/ferry-schedules) [[7]](https://www.busonlineticket.com/ferry/super-fast-ferry-ventures-sdn-bhd/) | 2h30–2h45 | €13–16 |
| Penang → Langkawi | Via mainland (Kuala Kedah/Kuala Perlis ferry) [[5]](https://onpenang.com/penang-to-langkawi/) | ~5h door-to-door | €10–15 |

**Arriving from the Cameron Highlands.** Direct coaches (e.g. CS Travel, Unititi Express) run from Tanah Rata to Sungai Nibong or Penang Sentral, ~4.5–5h, tickets from RM32 (€7) [[1]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Cameron-Highlands/Penang) [[2]](https://www.busonlineticket.com/booking/cameron-highlands-to-penang-bus-tickets). *Touristy, easy.*

**Arriving from KL.** The scenic choice is the [KTMB](https://www.ktmb.com.my/) **ETS train** to Butterworth: Express ~3h35, Gold ~4h20; Gold fares from ~RM79 (€17), Platinum/Express ~RM98–112 (€21–24) under the new Flexi-Fare (book early for the discount) [[3]](https://malaysiatrains.com/kuala-lumpur-to-penang-by-train/) [[4]](https://ets-tickets.com/kl-sentral-to-butterworth/). At Butterworth, walk to the ferry for the 10–15 min crossing to George Town's [Raja Tun Uda terminal](https://onpenang.com/butterworth-to-penang-island-ferry/), RM2 (€0.45), every 20–30 min, ~06:30–23:00 [[14]](https://onpenang.com/butterworth-to-penang-island-ferry/). Coaches from TBS are cheaper (RM32–61, €7–13) but slower at 5–6h [[20]](https://www.redbus.my/bus-tickets/kuala-lumpur-to-penang) [[23]](https://www.geckoroutes.com/malaysia/penang-to-kuala-lumpur/).

**From PEN airport into George Town.** [Rapid Penang](https://onpenang.com/bus-guide/) bus **401E / 401 / 102** runs to town, RM2.70 (€0.60), ~1–1.5h, every 23–35 min, 05:30–23:00; Grab ~RM30 (€6.50); metered taxi RM40–60 (€9–13), with a 50% surcharge 00:00–06:00 [[22]](https://penanglrt.com/penang_airport/public_transport.htm). *Bus = budget, Grab = sweet spot for a couple.*

**Leaving for Langkawi.** Fly: AirAsia/MAS/Firefly, 5–6 flights daily, 20 min in the air, fares from ~RM60 one-way (€13) up to ~RM126 (€27) [[5]](https://onpenang.com/penang-to-langkawi/) [[8]](https://www.skyscanner.com.my/routes/pen/lgk/penang-to-langkawi.html). The **direct ferry is the contested item**: dedicated guide *onpenang* says the pre-2020 service never restarted [[5]](https://onpenang.com/penang-to-langkawi/), yet *VisitPenang* and booking platforms list operators (Langkawi Ferry Services, [Super Fast Ferry Ventures](https://www.busonlineticket.com/ferry/super-fast-ferry-ventures-sdn-bhd/)) sailing ~2h30–2h45 from Swettenham Pier for RM60–75 (€13–16) [[6]](https://www.visitpenang.com/destinations/ferry-schedules) [[7]](https://www.busonlineticket.com/ferry/super-fast-ferry-ventures-sdn-bhd/). Treat the ferry as a sometimes-revived novelty; **for a couple on dates, fly.** The reliable surface alternative is via the mainland — train/bus to Kuala Kedah or Kuala Perlis, then a 1h15–1h45 ferry (RM35–45, €8–10), ~5h total [[5]](https://onpenang.com/penang-to-langkawi/).

For a couple, the practical pairing is **CAT + walking inside the old town, Grab for everything else.** Buses are excellent value for the coastal runs (101/102 to Batu Ferringhi and the National Park) but slow. Scooters suit the confident; George Town's narrow one-ways and trishaws keep speeds low [[18]](https://www.motorrentalpenang.my/). Trishaws themselves are now a tourist experience (negotiate per ride), not real transport.

## 3) The day-trip orbit from George Town

| Day-trip | Where | Half/full | How | Approx EUR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Penang Hill + Kek Lok Si** | Air Itam (centre-west) — *touristy* | Full | Bus 204 / 201 / 502 (~50 min, RM2) or Grab; funicular up [[11]](https://www.penanghill.gov.my/index.php/en/tickets) [[12]](https://onpenang.com/kek-lok-si-temple/) | €7–13 (funicular + Habitat optional) |
| **Penang National Park** | Teluk Bahang (NW tip) — *offbeat-ish* | Full | Bus 101/102 to Teluk Bahang (~1h, RM2.70); hike or boat to Monkey Beach [[13]](https://onpenang.com/penang-national-park/) | €11 entry + ~€22 boat |
| **Balik Pulau** | Rural west — *offbeat* | Full | Grab/scooter/car; guided cycling tours [[16]](https://asianitinerary.com/balik-pulau-cycling-tour-penang/) | €20–40 tour |
| **Batu Ferringhi** | North coast — *touristy* | Half/full | Bus 101/102 from Chulia St (~45 min, RM2.70) [[15]](https://www.travel-penang-malaysia.com/bus-to-batu-ferringhi.html) | €0.60 each way |
| **Butterworth/mainland** | Across the strait — *offbeat* | Half | RapidFerry RM2 [[14]](https://onpenang.com/butterworth-to-penang-island-ferry/) | €0.45 |

**Penang Hill + Kek Lok Si (Air Itam).** The signature full day. Bus 204 reaches the funicular lower station; 203/204 also serve Kek Lok Si, ~30–50 min from town, RM2 (€0.45) [[12]](https://onpenang.com/kek-lok-si-temple/). The [funicular](https://www.penanghill.gov.my/) is RM30 (€6.50) return for foreigners — book online to skip the queue [[11]](https://www.penanghill.gov.my/index.php/en/tickets). Up top, **[The Habitat](https://onpenang.com/the-habitat-penang-hill/)** canopy walk (RM60 / €13) is the recommended add-on to escape the funicular-summit crowds; go early or near sunset, weekdays [[21]](https://onpenang.com/the-habitat-penang-hill/). [Kek Lok Si](https://onpenang.com/kek-lok-si-temple/), Malaysia's largest Chinese temple, sits 1.5 km from the lower station — pair the two. *Touristy but genuinely worth it.*

**Penang National Park (Teluk Bahang).** Bus 101/102 from Weld Quay/KOMTAR to the park gate, ~1h, RM2.70 (€0.60); Grab RM10–50 [[13]](https://onpenang.com/penang-national-park/). Entry RM50 for non-Malaysians (€11) [[13]](https://onpenang.com/penang-national-park/). To **Monkey Beach (Teluk Duyung)**: hike ~3.5 km (~2h) or charter a return boat ~RM100 (€22) from the jetty [[13]](https://onpenang.com/penang-national-park/). A nature half-to-full day; pack water.

**Balik Pulau (rural west).** Penang's green hinterland — paddy fields, fishing kampungs, nutmeg and durian farms. Best done as a **guided countryside cycling tour** (~2h riding, light breakfast) or a durian-tasting visit (Musang King, Black Thorn) at farms like Bao Sheng/Audi [[16]](https://asianitinerary.com/balik-pulau-cycling-tour-penang/). Reach it by Grab, scooter or car (no quick bus). Famous for asam laksa. *The offbeat pick for a couple who've "done" George Town.*

**Batu Ferringhi (north coast).** Penang's main beach strip, ~11–17 km NW. Bus 101 from Chulia Street (opposite the 7-Eleven), every 10–20 min, ~45 min, RM2.70 (€0.60); last bus back ~23:20 [[15]](https://www.travel-penang-malaysia.com/bus-to-batu-ferringhi.html). The sand is decent rather than spectacular; the draw is the **night market**, beachfront dinners and watersports (parasailing) [[15]](https://www.travel-penang-malaysia.com/bus-to-batu-ferringhi.html). Half-day, or stay for sunset and the market. *Touristy.*

**Mainland hop.** The cheapest "trip" is the RM2 RapidFerry itself — ride across to Butterworth/Penang Sentral for the harbour views and the working-mainland contrast, then ride back [[14]](https://onpenang.com/butterworth-to-penang-island-ferry/). *Offbeat, half-day at most.*
