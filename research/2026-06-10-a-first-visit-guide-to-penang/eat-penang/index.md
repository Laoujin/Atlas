> **TL;DR:** Penang is Malaysia's hawker capital — eat at the stalls, not the star list. For the one iconic plate, queue at [Siam Road Char Koay Teow](https://guide.michelin.com/my/en/pulau-pinang/my-george-town/restaurant/siam-road-char-koay-teow) (smoky, charcoal-wok, Michelin Bib, ~€2) [[10]](https://guide.michelin.com/my/en/pulau-pinang/my-george-town/restaurant/siam-road-char-koay-teow)[[12]](https://eatbook.sg/best-penang-char-kway-teow/). For the assam-laksa pilgrimage, the stalls at the foot of Kek Lok Si in Air Itam [[13]](https://smartdory.com/2026/04/air-itam-laksa-penang/). For one-stop grazing at sunset, [Gurney Drive Hawker Centre](https://penangtime.com/review/gurney-drive-hawker-centre/) [[41]](https://penangtime.com/review/gurney-drive-hawker-centre/). For a sit-down splurge, the one-Michelin-star Nyonya kitchen [Auntie Gaik Lean's](https://guide.michelin.com/en/pulau-pinang/my-george-town/restaurant/auntie-gaik-lean-s-old-school-eatery) (book ahead) [[37]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/pulau-pinang/my-george-town/restaurant/auntie-gaik-lean-s-old-school-eatery)[[38]](https://danielfooddiary.com/2025/05/03/auntiegaiklean/). Go **Dec–Feb** (driest); avoid the May–Oct wet bursts [[44]](https://my.trip.com/guide/info/best-time-to-visit-penang.html)[[45]](https://onpenang.com/best-time-to-visit/).

Prices in this guide use **€1 ≈ MYR 4.7** (mid-market, mid-2026) [[47]](https://wise.com/gb/currency-converter/eur-to-myr-rate). At hawker level a full plate is RM 5–15 (≈ €1–3) [[53]](https://jumppy.com.sg/penang-hawker-centre-guide/) — you can eat your way through a dozen legends for the price of one Western meal. **Bring cash**; many stalls take e-wallet but few take cards [[24]](https://eatbook.sg/chulia-street-night-market/). Penang is celebrated as Malaysia's, arguably Asia's, street-food heartland [[5]](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/travel/article/best-places-to-eat-in-george-town-malaysia-food-guide).

## When to come (and the durian flag)

Best window: **mid-November to mid-February** — driest skies, fewer storms, less brutal heat than April–August [[44]](https://my.trip.com/guide/info/best-time-to-visit-penang.html). The wetter monsoon stretch is roughly **May–October**, with heavier rain usually in short bursts rather than all-day washouts [[45]](https://onpenang.com/best-time-to-visit/). If you love (or want to brave) **durian**, the main Penang season peaks **June–August** — that is also wet season, so it's a trade-off; prices and variety are best at peak [[46]](https://durianpenang.com/calendar). For a relaxed couple, the Dec–Feb dry window wins for walking-and-eating George Town.

## The hawker centres & night markets (graze here)

These are the "many stalls, one table" hubs — order from several carts, note your table number, pay on delivery.

- **[Gurney Drive Hawker Centre](https://penangtime.com/review/gurney-drive-hawker-centre/)** — Gurney Drive (Pulau Tikus edge), seafront, ~100 stalls; the tourist classic for one-stop tasting (curry mee, assam laksa, ais kacang, cendol, rojak, CKT). Go **~7–8pm at sunset**; packed on weekends, arrive early/late for a table [[41]](https://penangtime.com/review/gurney-drive-hawker-centre/)[[1]](https://www.timetravelturtle.com/malaysia/best-food-in-penang/). `touristy`
- **New Lane / Lorong Baru** — George Town, off Macalister; the locals' evening street, vehicle-free during hours; CKT, oh chien, loh bak, satay, curry mee. **4–10/11pm, closed Wed** [[52]](https://www.springtomorrow.com/new-lane-hawker-centre-penang/)[[53]](https://jumppy.com.sg/penang-hawker-centre-guide/). `touristy↔local`
- **Chulia Street Night Hawker** — George Town heritage core; push-cart stalls ~6pm–12am. Hunt the ~50-yr **Mother & Son Wantan Mee**, lok lok skewers, ham chee peng, the viral 80-sen apom aunty [[24]](https://eatbook.sg/chulia-street-night-market/)[[25]](https://thesmartlocal.my/chulia-street-night-hawker/). `touristy`
- **Kimberley Street night stalls** — George Town; loh bak, popiah, koay chap, duck-egg CKT after dark [[1]](https://www.timetravelturtle.com/malaysia/best-food-in-penang/). `offbeat`
- **Pulau Tikus Market** — two faces: **6:30–11am** breakfast (dim sum, curry mee, prawn mee, kuih, kopi) and a popular dinner food-court at night; RM 6–10 plates [[58]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g660694-d5990546-Reviews-or10-Pulau_Tikus_Markets-Penang_Island_Penang.html)[[53]](https://jumppy.com.sg/penang-hawker-centre-guide/). `local`
- **Air Itam morning market** — beneath Kek Lok Si; assam laksa, Nyonya curry mee, char koay kak; combine with Penang Hill / Kek Lok Si day-trip [[13]](https://smartdory.com/2026/04/air-itam-laksa-penang/)[[31]](https://penangfoodie.com/best-penang-curry-mee/). `day-trip`
- **New World Park Food City** — George Town; a tidy, partly-covered hub good if you're short on time, home to a 50-year Hokkien-mee master [[19]](https://smartdory.com/2026/02/penang-hokkien-mee-new-world-park/)[[3]](https://thebackpackingfamily.com/what-are-the-best-penang-hawkers/). `touristy`
- **Batu Ferringhi Night Market / Long Beach food court** — beach belt; 7–11pm nightly. More mixed/international (satay, seafood, pad thai, BBQ wings) — convenient if sleeping beachside, less "legend" density than George Town [[50]](https://foreverbreak.com/travel/malaysia/penang/batu-ferringhi-night-market/)[[51]](https://en.tripadvisor.com.hk/Restaurant_Review-g635527-d1772228-Reviews-or2565-Long_Beach-Batu_Ferringhi_Penang_Island_Penang.html). `beach belt`

## Historic kopitiams & institutions

The old coffee-shop ritual: charcoal-toasted bread, thick kaya, soft eggs, robusta kopi.

- **[Toh Soon Café](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g298303-d3706666-Reviews-Toh_Soon_Cafe-George_Town_Penang_Island_Penang.html)** — Lebuh Campbell back-alley; the quintessential old-Penang kopitiam, homemade kaya + bold robusta, charcoal toast. 8am–5pm [[26]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g298303-d3706666-Reviews-Toh_Soon_Cafe-George_Town_Penang_Island_Penang.html). `touristy`
- **Kheng Pin Café** — Jln Penang; classic kopitiam shell housing famous loh bak, CKT, prawn mee, wonton mee, porridge stalls. **Closed Mon** [[27]](https://www.yelp.com/biz/kheng-pin-cafe-george-town-3)[[2]](https://www.willflyforfood.net/penang-food-guide-15-must-eat-restaurants-street-food-stalls-in-penang-malaysia/). `touristy`
- **Tai Tong Restaurant** — Chinatown shophouse; trolley dim sum pushed by elderly aunties from breakfast; queues form ~7:30am; ~RM 30 ≈ €6.40 pp [[49]](https://www.showmemalaysia.com/best-dim-sum-spots-penang/). `touristy`
- **First Famous Federal** (Lorong Susu) — pi pa duck & roast meats, lunch only, **closed Mon**; an old roast-meat name [[2]](https://www.willflyforfood.net/penang-food-guide-15-must-eat-restaurants-street-food-stalls-in-penang-malaysia/). `offbeat`

## The Michelin thread (one strand, not the story)

The **Michelin Guide Kuala Lumpur & Penang 2026** lists **74 Penang establishments**, of which **33 hold a Bib Gourmand** (great value) [[8]](https://www.buletinmutiara.com/penang-continues-to-impress-in-michelin-guide-2026/). Crucially for Penang, the Bibs are mostly *hawker stalls* — so the "Michelin list" and the "street-food list" heavily overlap.

**Penang's two one-stars (2026):**

- **[Auntie Gaik Lean's Old School Eatery](https://guide.michelin.com/en/pulau-pinang/my-george-town/restaurant/auntie-gaik-lean-s-old-school-eatery)** — George Town; authentic, labour-intensive **Nyonya/Peranakan** (hand-cut nasi ulam with up to 10 herbs); held its star, reservations essential [[37]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/pulau-pinang/my-george-town/restaurant/auntie-gaik-lean-s-old-school-eatery)[[38]](https://danielfooddiary.com/2025/05/03/auntiegaiklean/). `touristy / book ahead`
- **Au Jardin** — George Town; contemporary fine dining, retained its star in 2026 [[8]](https://www.buletinmutiara.com/penang-continues-to-impress-in-michelin-guide-2026/). `splurge`

**New for 2026:** three Penang stalls joined the **Bib Gourmand** — **Awesome Char Koay Teow**, **Bee Hwa Café**, and **Sifu Nyonya Cuisine** — the last part of a Peranakan-cuisine spotlight this year [[8]](https://www.buletinmutiara.com/penang-continues-to-impress-in-michelin-guide-2026/)[[6]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/malaysia-bib-gourmand-2026)[[9]](https://www.malaymail.com/news/eat-drink/2025/11/11/michelin-guide-kuala-lumpur-and-penang-2026-bib-gourmand-list-has-five-new-entrants-three-dropped/198038). Several new **MICHELIN Selected** entries broaden the fine-dining field too — Blacklinen (barbecue), Du-An (Malaysian heritage), Mémoire (tasting menu), Peninsula House (contemporary), Sood by Chef Ton (Thai) [[7]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/malaysia-full-selection-2026)[[8]](https://www.buletinmutiara.com/penang-continues-to-impress-in-michelin-guide-2026/).

**Bib-honoured hawker names worth queuing for:** Siam Road CKT [[10]](https://guide.michelin.com/my/en/pulau-pinang/my-george-town/restaurant/siam-road-char-koay-teow), Penang Road Famous Jin Kor CKT (inside Joo Hooi food court) [[11]](https://guide.michelin.com/my/en/pulau-pinang/my-george-town/restaurant/penang-road-famous-jin-kor-char-kuey-teow), Ah Leng CKT [[6]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/malaysia-bib-gourmand-2026), 888 Hokkien Mee [[20]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/pulau-pinang/my-george-town/restaurant/888-hokkien-mee-lebuh-presgrave), and the storied **Penang Road Famous Laksa** [[57]](https://guide.michelin.com/my/en/article/dining-out/behind-the-bib-penang-road-famous-laksa).

## Sit-down Nyonya & a heritage-café splurge

- **[Kebaya Dining Room](https://kebaya.com.my/)** at Seven Terraces — George Town; refined Straits-Chinese/Indochinese set menus inside a restored 19th-c. terrace with chandeliers and live music; one of Penang's signature fine-dining Nyonya rooms [[35]](https://kebaya.com.my/)[[36]](https://www.georgetownheritage.com/best-nyonya-restaurants-penang/). `splurge / book ahead`
- **ChinaHouse** — Beach St; three knocked-through heritage shophouses (café, restaurant, bar, gallery) famous for a counter of ~30 cakes daily; 9am–1am; great for a long, air-con afternoon [[56]](https://thesmartlocal.my/bakeries-in-penang/). `touristy`
- **Mugshot Café** — Chulia St; bagels, homemade yoghurt and coffee on one of Penang's oldest roads — a Western-leaning break [[56]](https://thesmartlocal.my/bakeries-in-penang/). `offbeat`

## Unusual locations (the "where" is the story)

- **Penang Hill — [David Brown's Restaurant & Tea Terraces](https://penanghillco.com.my/)**: Penang's highest restaurant, garden tea-terrace with sweeping George Town views; daily 11am–10pm. Take the funicular up; come for the setting + afternoon tea more than hawker authenticity [[39]](https://penanghillco.com.my/)[[40]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g660694-d2061893-Reviews-David_Brown_s_Restaurant_and_Tea_Terraces-Penang_Island_Penang.html). `day-trip / scenic splurge`
- **Chew Jetty (clan jetties)**: a stilt village over the water; a small food court at the entrance plus walkway stalls — laksa, CKT, cendol, kuih, grilled seafood, durian ice-cream, sugarcane juice. Daytime; go early for more open stalls [[42]](https://malaysiafact.com/penang-jetty-chew-guide-travel-tips-2026/)[[43]](https://onpenang.com/clan-jetties-of-penang/). `touristy`
- **Air Itam, foot of Kek Lok Si**: eat assam laksa with the temple/market backdrop, tied into the Penang Hill + Kek Lok Si day-trip — note the legendary corner stall runs **weekends only** [[13]](https://smartdory.com/2026/04/air-itam-laksa-penang/)[[14]](https://www.springtomorrow.com/air-itam-laksa-penang/). `day-trip`
- **Balik Pulau (rural west)**: nutmeg-tinged country laksa (**Kim Laksa**, since the 1940s), durian farms (e.g. **Bao Sheng**, hilltop stay), old kopitiam breakfasts — a green half-day escape from George Town [[33]](https://www.lexissuitespenang.com/media/blogs/must-try-famous-food-in-balik-pulau)[[34]](https://www.penang-insider.com/balik-pulau-travel-guide/). `day-trip / offbeat`

## Practical notes

- **Closing days bite.** Many legends shut one weekday (Deen Maju Fri; Kheng Pin / Pitt St koay teow Mon; New Lane Wed; Air Itam laksa weekday-variable). Check before trekking [[17]](https://penangfoodie.com/top-10-best-nasi-kandar-in-penang/)[[2]](https://www.willflyforfood.net/penang-food-guide-15-must-eat-restaurants-street-food-stalls-in-penang-malaysia/)[[52]](https://www.springtomorrow.com/new-lane-hawker-centre-penang/).
- **Timing is the dish.** CKT and oh chien are evening foods; assam laksa, curry mee, koay teow th'ng and dim sum are morning-to-early-afternoon — many close by ~1–3pm [[1]](https://www.timetravelturtle.com/malaysia/best-food-in-penang/)[[31]](https://penangfoodie.com/best-penang-curry-mee/).
- **Cash + small notes.** Hawkers prefer cash/e-wallet; cards rare [[24]](https://eatbook.sg/chulia-street-night-market/)[[50]](https://foreverbreak.com/travel/malaysia/penang/batu-ferringhi-night-market/).
- **Heat & queues.** Eat early/late to dodge midday heat and weekend crowds at Siam Road, Gurney and the cendol stall [[12]](https://eatbook.sg/best-penang-char-kway-teow/)[[41]](https://penangtime.com/review/gurney-drive-hawker-centre/).
