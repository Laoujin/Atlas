> **TL;DR:** Penang is one walkable heritage island and you plan it from George Town. Build the trip around five things: **eating the hawker legends** — smoky charcoal char koay teow at [Siam Road](https://guide.michelin.com/my/en/pulau-pinang/my-george-town/restaurant/siam-road-char-koay-teow) [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/my/en/pulau-pinang/my-george-town/restaurant/siam-road-char-koay-teow), [Air Itam assam laksa](https://smartdory.com/2026/04/air-itam-laksa-penang/) at the foot of Kek Lok Si [[2]](https://smartdory.com/2026/04/air-itam-laksa-penang/), a sunset graze at [Gurney Drive](https://penangtime.com/review/gurney-drive-hawker-centre/) [[3]](https://penangtime.com/review/gurney-drive-hawker-centre/); **walking the UNESCO core** — the 2008-inscribed heritage zone [[4]](https://whc.unesco.org/en/canopy/georgetown-2/) with its [street art](https://www.timetravelturtle.com/malaysia/penang-street-art/) [[5]](https://www.timetravelturtle.com/malaysia/penang-street-art/), [Khoo Kongsi](https://www.khookongsi.com.my/) [[6]](https://www.khookongsi.com.my/) and the stilt-village [Chew Jetty](https://onpenang.com/clan-jetties-of-penang/) [[7]](https://onpenang.com/clan-jetties-of-penang/); **Penang Hill** by [funicular](https://www.penanghill.gov.my/index.php/en/tickets) plus [The Habitat](https://thehabitat.my) canopy walk [[8]](https://www.penanghill.gov.my/index.php/en/tickets)[[9]](https://thehabitat.my); **[Kek Lok Si](https://www.travel-penang-malaysia.com/kek-lok-si-temple.html)**, Malaysia's largest temple [[10]](https://www.travel-penang-malaysia.com/kek-lok-si-temple.html); and **sleeping inside the heritage** — a restored shophouse or the indigo [Blue Mansion](https://www.cheongfatttzemansion.com/rooms/) [[11]](https://www.cheongfatttzemansion.com/rooms/). Come **December–February** for the driest skies [[12]](https://my.trip.com/guide/info/best-time-to-visit-penang.html).

Penang is a single small island you plan as one base. Sleep in **George Town** — the UNESCO heritage core — and everything else (Penang Hill, the national park, Balik Pulau, the beaches) folds in as a half- or full-day trip. The exhaustive lists live in the seven axis pages below; this page pulls the highlights up and wires them into a ~3-night order.

## Best season & quick flags

- **When to go:** the dry, clear window is **mid-November to mid-February** (highs ~31 °C), with December–February the sweet spot for walking-and-eating George Town [[12]](https://my.trip.com/guide/info/best-time-to-visit-penang.html). That's also peak season — book flights and heritage hotels weeks ahead. The wetter stretch is roughly **May–October**, but rain mostly arrives in short bursts, not all-day washouts [[13]](https://onpenang.com/best-time-to-visit/). One trade-off: **durian** peaks **June–August**, which overlaps the wet season — go mid-year if the fruit is the point, the dry window otherwise [[14]](https://durianpenang.com/calendar).
- **Money:** ~**€1 ≈ RM4.7** in 2026 [[15]](https://wise.com/gb/currency-converter/eur-to-myr-rate). Food is the bargain — a hawker plate is €1–3 — and a heritage hotel is the splurge. A comfortable couple's daily budget runs roughly **€70–130** excluding big-ticket experiences. **Carry cash** for hawkers; many stalls take e-wallet but few take cards.
- **Getting in:** from KL, the scenic option is the **KTMB ETS train** to Butterworth (~3h35–4h20, €17–24) then the ferry across [[18]](https://malaysiatrains.com/kuala-lumpur-to-penang-by-train/); from the **Cameron Highlands** a direct coach runs ~4.5–5h (~€7) [[17]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Cameron-Highlands/Penang). The mainland rail/bus hub is **Butterworth (Penang Sentral)**, linked to George Town by the **RapidFerry** — 10–15 min, RM2 (≈€0.45), cashless [[16]](https://onpenang.com/butterworth-to-penang-island-ferry/).
- **Getting around:** George Town is walkable; the **free CAT shuttle** loops the heritage sights every 10–15 min [[19]](https://onpenang.com/george-town-free-cat-bus/), and Grab fills the gaps. There's no metro and no airport rail.
- **Etiquette:** cover shoulders and knees for temples and mosques (robes are lent at Kapitan Keling); treat the clan jetties as the lived-in homes they are.

## A suggested ~3-night plan

Three nights fits Penang as a single base: enough for the George Town icons, a hawker crawl, the Penang Hill–Kek Lok Si morning and one nature day-trip. Base yourself for **character** in the UNESCO core — a restored shophouse, the indigo [Blue Mansion](https://www.cheongfatttzemansion.com/rooms/) [[11]](https://www.cheongfatttzemansion.com/rooms/), or the seafront colonial E&O (full Sleep page).

- **Arrival evening.** Ferry in from Butterworth (or coach from the Cameron Highlands), drop bags in George Town, and graze **[Gurney Drive Hawker Centre](https://penangtime.com/review/gurney-drive-hawker-centre/)** at sunset — char koay teow, rojak, cendol, one plate at a time [[3]](https://penangtime.com/review/gurney-drive-hawker-centre/).
- **Day 1 — the UNESCO core on foot.** Walk the **[street art](https://www.timetravelturtle.com/malaysia/penang-street-art/)** lanes and iron caricatures [[5]](https://www.timetravelturtle.com/malaysia/penang-street-art/), the gilded **[Khoo Kongsi](https://www.khookongsi.com.my/)** clan house [[6]](https://www.khookongsi.com.my/) and the **[Pinang Peranakan Mansion](https://www.travel-penang-malaysia.com/pinang-peranakan-mansion.html)** (the single best museum, ~€4–5) [[20]](https://www.travel-penang-malaysia.com/pinang-peranakan-mansion.html), then the stilt-village **[Chew Jetty](https://onpenang.com/clan-jetties-of-penang/)** [[7]](https://onpenang.com/clan-jetties-of-penang/). Book a **[Blue Mansion](https://www.cheongfatttzemansion.com/thebluemansion/discover/daily-tours/)** guided tour for the architecture [[29]](https://www.cheongfatttzemansion.com/thebluemansion/discover/daily-tours/). Dinner: the assam-laksa-and-cendol corner on Penang Road, or 24-hour nasi kandar.
- **Day 2 — Air Itam: hill + temple.** Early **[Penang Hill funicular](https://www.penanghill.gov.my/index.php/en/tickets)** [[8]](https://www.penanghill.gov.my/index.php/en/tickets) and the paved **[The Habitat](https://thehabitat.my)** canopy walk before the haze and crowds [[9]](https://thehabitat.my), then down to **[Kek Lok Si](https://www.travel-penang-malaysia.com/kek-lok-si-temple.html)**, Malaysia's largest temple [[10]](https://www.travel-penang-malaysia.com/kek-lok-si-temple.html), with the famous **[Air Itam assam laksa](https://smartdory.com/2026/04/air-itam-laksa-penang/)** at the foot of the steps [[2]](https://smartdory.com/2026/04/air-itam-laksa-penang/).
- **Day 3 — pick your texture.** Either a nature day-trip — the **[Penang National Park](https://capturetheatlas.com/penang-national-park/)** jungle trek to Monkey/Turtle Beach (moderate, ~1.5h each way, no guide) [[21]](https://capturetheatlas.com/penang-national-park/) or **[Balik Pulau](http://explorebalikpulau.com.my/)** countryside cycling through durian orchards [[22]](http://explorebalikpulau.com.my/) — or go offbeat to the **[Snake Temple](https://www.malaysia-traveller.com/penang-snake-temple.html)** of live pit vipers [[23]](https://www.malaysia-traveller.com/penang-snake-temple.html) and the haunted war tunnels.
- **Onward.** For **Langkawi**, fly (20 min, from ~€13) — the direct ferry has not reliably restarted since 2020 [[24]](https://onpenang.com/penang-to-langkawi/).

## Footer — cautions & changes

- **The Habitat's Curtis Crest Tree Top Walk** is under maintenance from **13 May 2026**, with some areas temporarily closed — the rest of The Habitat (Langur Way canopy walk) stays open [[25]](https://www.excursionmania.com/ttd/3083/the-habitat-penang-hill-blg-3083). The **Penang National Park's own 250m canopy walkway** is closed long-term — don't plan around it [[21]](https://capturetheatlas.com/penang-national-park/).
- **Penang State Museum** (Farquhar St) has been in a RM20m restoration (slated to reopen end-2025) — confirm before going [[26]](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2025/05/19/penang-state-museum-and-art-gallery-set-to-reopen-by-year-end).
- **Blue Mansion tours** (11:00 & 15:30, max 24) sell out — book ahead [[29]](https://www.cheongfatttzemansion.com/thebluemansion/discover/daily-tours/).
- **Festivals that reshape a trip:** **Thaipusam (Feb 2, 2026)** brings the silver-chariot procession and big crowds to the Waterfall Temple [[27]](https://www.odynovotours.com/malaysia/thaipusam-festival.html); the **George Town Festival (Aug 1–9, 2026)** is the arts peak, 80% free [[28]](https://www.buletinmutiara.com/george-town-festival-2026-to-feature-over-40-events-including-seven-ticketed-shows/). **Durian season (Jun–Aug)** overlaps the wetter months [[14]](https://durianpenang.com/calendar).
- **Responsible travel:** dress modestly at temples and mosques, don't handle the vipers at the Snake Temple [[23]](https://www.malaysia-traveller.com/penang-snake-temple.html), and treat the clan jetties as living homes, not a photo set. George Town's core is small and busy in peak season — walk early, eat local.
