The booking cascade determines everything else. The French Laundry's reservations open at midnight 60 days before service on [exploretock.com](https://www.exploretock.com/thefrenchlaundry) [[1]](https://www.exploretock.com/thefrenchlaundry) and fill within minutes; that slot is the weekend's foundation, not a detail to arrange later. Once secured: lock the hotel the same day (Yountville's walkable properties track the same demand curve); book wineries 2–4 weeks out; [[2]](https://jessupcellars.com/napa-valley-wine-tasting-cost/) consider any conference attendance last.

**Two lodging angles, one answer.** The walking-distance and character studies cover the same Yountville core — the overlap is the finding, not a flaw. Both converge on five walkable properties: [Napa Valley Railway Inn](https://www.napavalleyrailwayinn.com/) ($245) [[3]](https://www.napavalleyrailwayinn.com/) for maximum quirk (literal steam-era railcars on Washington St.); [Maison Fleurie](https://www.maisonfleurienapa.com/) ($228, breakfast and wine hour included) [[4]](https://www.kayak.com/Yountville-Hotels-Maison-Fleurie-A-Four-Sisters-Inn.67820.ksp) as Napa Valley's oldest hotel in an 1873 stone manor 0.2 miles away; [Lavender Inn](https://www.lavendernapa.com/) ($319, Michelin Guide listed, 0.1 miles) [[5]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/hotels-stays/napa-sonoma-valleys/lavender-a-four-sisters-inn-13991) in a National Historic Register 1850s farmhouse; [North Block Hotel](https://northblockyountville.com/) ($342, Michelin Two Keys) [[6]](https://northblockyountville.com/) with the highest guest ratings in town; and [Bardessono](https://www.bardessono.com/) ($450+, LEED Platinum) [[7]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/us/bardessono.html) with in-room massage tables and plunge pool villas 0.3 miles out. Saturday weekend premiums run 1.5–2× all these floors.

The divergence begins at taxi range. [Sttupa Estate](https://sttupaestate.com/) ($1,250+, five poet-named suites on a Howard Backen hillside above Silverado Trail, rebranded from Poetry Inn in March 2026) [[8]](https://sttupaestate.com/experience/our-story/) [[9]](https://wineindustryadvisor.com/2026/03/04/poetry-inn-begins-a-new-chapter-as-sttupa-estate-napa-valley/) is the only property outside Yountville whose intimate, literary character genuinely matches the occasion — 5 minutes by taxi. [Auberge du Soleil](https://auberge.com/auberge-du-soleil/) ($2,010+, 33 Provençal acres, Michelin-starred restaurant, 10 min taxi) [[10]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g32997-d111930-Reviews-Auberge_du_Soleil-Rutherford_Napa_Valley_California.html) and [Carneros Resort](https://www.carnerosresort.com/) ($512+, corrugated-metal cottage village, 20 min south) [[11]](https://www.carnerosresort.com/location/) round out the taxi-range tier, though Carneros' distance compounds transport costs on a driving-intensive day.

**Saturday daytime: pace around the dinner.** One mid-morning activity is the right load before a multi-course evening that runs 3–3.5 hours. Best no-car options: a dawn hot-air balloon launch from Yountville (~$300, meets at 6525 Washington St) [[12]](https://nvaloft.com/balloon-flight-options/) ends well before noon; [Domaine Chandon](https://www.chandon.com/en-us/visit-us/wineryexperiences.html) in town accepts walk-ins from $36. [[13]](https://www.chandon.com/en-us/visit-us/wineryexperiences.html) A 20-minute drive south reaches Oxbow Public Market in Napa for a casual morning. [[14]](https://oxbowpublicmarket.com/) Calistoga mud baths ($105–170 at Indian Springs) [[15]](https://www.indianspringscalistoga.com/spa-treatments/mud-mineral-baths) are at the 30 km edge (~28 min) and better suited to Friday arrival rather than the day of dinner.

**Villages as day-trip destinations.** Every town surfaced by the lodging studies is also a candidate for the day before dinner. Rutherford (~8 min) holds [Quintessa](https://www.quintessa.com/the-experience/) (organic estate, $175–300) [[16]](https://www.quintessa.com/the-experience/) and HALL cave tastings, plus Auberge du Soleil as a taxi-range base. St. Helena (~14 min) anchors the historic [Model Bakery](https://www.sthelena.com/listing/model-bakery/) (since 1908) [[17]](https://www.sthelena.com/listing/model-bakery/) and [CIA at Greystone](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g33000-d102977-Reviews-The_Culinary_Institute_of_America_at_Greystone-St_Helena_Napa_Valley_California.html). [[18]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g33000-d102977-Reviews-The_Culinary_Institute_of_America_at_Greystone-St_Helena_Napa_Valley_California.html) Carneros (~25 min south) offers [di Rosa](https://www.dirosaart.org/visit/) contemporary art (217 acres, $25, Fri–Sun) [[19]](https://www.dirosaart.org/visit/) and [Domaine Carneros](https://www.domainecarneros.com/visit) sparkling. Calistoga holds [Castello di Amorosa](https://castellodiamorosa.com/) (14th-century-style castle, $60–115) [[20]](https://castellodiamorosa.com/tour-tasting2/) and [Old Faithful Geyser](https://www.calistogaspa.com/things-to-do/old-faithful-geyser) ($15). [[21]](https://www.calistogaspa.com/things-to-do/old-faithful-geyser) Sonoma is just outside the 30 km radius by road and was not researched. Rideshare is unreliable north of St. Helena and sparse after dark; [[22]](https://www.napavalley.com/blog/napa-valley-without-a-car/) a hired driver ($60–90/hr) [[23]](https://accessnapavalley.com/napa-valley-transportation-options/) or the car-free [Vine Trail](https://www.traillink.com/trail/napa-valley-vine-trail/) between Yountville and Napa covers the daytime gaps.

**Conferences: secondary.** The three qualifying 2026 tech events — PV ModuleTech USA (Jun 16–17, Napa Valley Marriott, ~10 km), [[24]](https://www.pvtechconferences.com/pv-moduletech-usa/) Operating Partners Forum Napa (Jun 23–25, Silverado Resort, ~11 km), [[25]](https://live.peievents.com/operating-partners-forum-napa) and Summit Napa Valley (Oct 12–14, Meritage Resort, ~16 km) [[26]](https://summit.co/event/napa26) — are executive-tier gatherings at resort venues, none in Yountville. Pure IT conferences are essentially absent from the radius. The practical order is still TFL first; if a conference window overlaps, layer it in.

The unresolved tension: Yountville's walkable hotels eliminate all post-dinner logistics, but Sttupa Estate or Auberge du Soleil offer an experience better-matched to the occasion at the cost of one taxi ride. Which constraint matters more on a once-or-twice-in-a-lifetime night?
