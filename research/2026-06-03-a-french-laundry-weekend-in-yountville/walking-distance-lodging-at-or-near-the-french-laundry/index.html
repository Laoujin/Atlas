Top picks in detail

Lavender A Four Sisters Inn

2020 Webber Ave · ~2 min walk
from ~$499 / night[5]

9-room Provençal B&B roughly 500 ft behind the restaurant — stumbling distance after the cheese course. Rooms have fireplaces and private patios; rate includes full breakfast and afternoon wine & hors d'oeuvres. Free bikes.

Closest to FL Breakfast incl. Afternoon wine Free bikes 4.5/5 TA

Vintage House (Estate Yountville)

6451 Washington St · ~2 min walk
from ~$370 / night[13]

The north wing of the 22-acre Estate Yountville, described as "modern farmhouse sanctuaries with soaring ceilings." Guests access all estate amenities: 3 pools, spa, 3 restaurants, and complimentary e-bikes for vineyard excursions.[12]

Steps to FL 3 pools Full spa E-bikes incl. 3 restaurants

North Block Hotel

6757 Washington St · ~3 min walk
from ~$427 / night[14]

The highest-rated hotel in Yountville (4.8/5 TA; 9.9/10 KAYAK) with only 20 rooms around a Mediterranean courtyard.[10] Earned Michelin Keys 2024–2025. Heated pool, full spa, on-site restaurant with seasonal California cuisine. Rate includes gourmet breakfast and free bike rentals.

Michelin Key ★ 4.8/5 TA 20 rooms only Breakfast incl. Pool + spa

Bardessono Hotel & Spa

6526 Yount St · ~6 min walk
from ~$568 / night[7]

Eco-certified LEED Platinum resort with a rooftop pool, full spa offering in-room treatments, yoga, and an organic restaurant. Rooms have gas fireplaces and large spa bathrooms.[9] Booking.com prices run $568–$2,966 depending on season; destination fee ~$68/night additional.

LEED Platinum Rooftop pool In-room spa 4.8/5 TA Organic restaurant

Napa Valley Railway Inn

6523 Washington St · ~6 min walk
from ~$245 / night[3]

The most affordable option on Washington St itself — 8 rooms inside restored 100-year-old railcars. Quirky but beloved; guests cite the attached Mini Model Bakery & Cafe as a morning highlight. No breakfast included, but no hidden fees either. Self check-in.

Lowest rate Railcar novelty Free parking Bakery on-site

Maison Fleurie

6529 Yount St · ~5 min walk
from ~$228 / night[8]

The most affordable B&B in Yountville, in an 1873 stone farmhouse on Yount St. 13 rooms, pool, hot tub, daily breakfast, and afternoon wine — the same Four Sisters formula as Lavender but at a lower price point. Weekends and summer weekends can hit $400–$560.[8]

Best B&B value Breakfast incl. Afternoon wine Pool + hot tub Historic 1873

Napa Valley Lodge — the breakfast-included mid-range pick

Napa Valley Lodge (2230 Madison St, 0.39 km) is the best conventional hotel in the mid-price band: 55 rooms, heated pool, hot tub, champagne breakfast included, rates from ~$277.[11] Guests report walking to The French Laundry from here without issue — about a 5-minute stroll north on Madison to Washington.[11] TripAdvisor 4.7/5 across 200+ reviews.

Booking notes

  • Book early. French Laundry reservations open 60 days out and fill in minutes; hotels around it sell out similarly. Lock in lodging the same day you secure the dinner.
  • Weekend premiums are steep. All rates above are low-season/weekday floors. Expect Saturday-night rates to be 1.5–2× higher at Lavender, North Block, and Bardessono.
  • Ask about the destination fee. Bardessono charges ~$68/night extra.[7] Confirm any resort fee before booking all properties.
  • No car needed after dinner. Every property above is ≤ 12 min on foot from 6640 Washington St, on flat paved sidewalks — a genuine advantage after a 3-hour tasting menu with wine pairings.
