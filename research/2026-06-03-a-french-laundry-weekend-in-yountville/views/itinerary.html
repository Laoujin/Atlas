Before You Arrive — The Booking Cascade
60 Days Out

Book The French Laundry

Reservations open at midnight PST, exactly 60 days before the service date, on exploretock.com. [1] Tables fill within minutes. Set an alarm. This slot is the weekend’s foundation — everything else follows from here.

Same Day

Lock the Hotel

Yountville’s walkable properties track the same demand curve as the restaurant. Book immediately after securing your TFL reservation. Saturday-night premiums run 1.5–2× weekday floors.

The Weekend
Friday arrival day
Afternoon
Indian Springs · Calistoga

Calistoga Mud Baths

Indian Springs Calistoga — $105–170 per treatment [15]
28 km north (∼28 min). Schedule on arrival day rather than Saturday: better to arrive to dinner relaxed than to scramble from the mud baths.

Evening

Check In · Settle

Light dinner in Yountville or an easy stroll. Save your appetite for tomorrow evening.

Saturday the main event
Dawn — Option A
Napa Valley Aloft · Yountville

Hot-air Balloon

Napa Valley Aloft — $300/person [12]
Meet 30 min before sunrise at 6525 Washington St (walking distance from Yountville hotels). Total commitment ~3–4 hours; ends well before noon, leaving time to pace before the evening. Book in advance — weather cancellations are possible.

Morning — Option B

Domaine Chandon or Oxbow Market

Domaine Chandon is within Yountville itself — walk-ins welcome from $36, 10am–4pm [13]
Oxbow Public Market in Napa is 20 min south — artisan food stalls, no reservation needed [14]

Afternoon

Rest · Prepare

One mid-morning activity is the right load before a multi-course evening. Light lunch, a walk through Yountville, and leave well-rested.

Saturday Dinner

The French Laundry

6640 Washington St · Yountville — exploretock.com [1]
Dressy. Multi-course tasting menu. Plan 3–3.5 hours.
Walk home. No car, no logistics. This is why you booked a walkable hotel.
