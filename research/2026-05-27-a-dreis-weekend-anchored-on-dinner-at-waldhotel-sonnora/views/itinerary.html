Route A · South
If the weather is fine and the company is wine-loving
Wine & Medieval

Bernkastel marktplatz · Riesling cellars · castles on the Mosel.

Saturday Late morning in Bernkastel-Kues — the 1416 Spitzhäuschen on the Marktplatz, lunch under half-timber. Afternoon: a tasting at Markus Molitor's Klosterberg vinothek in Zeltingen-Rachtig (no appointment, 10:00-17:00 daily) [24]. Home to dress for dinner.
Sunday Manderscheid's twin castles on the Lieser river — the Oberburg free, the Niederburg open daily 11:00-17:00 April-November [19]. Optionally bend east to Traben-Trarbach for the Bruno Möhring Jugendstil and the Underworld cellar tour [18].
Route B · North
If the boots are laced and the legs are willing
Volcanic & Active

Crater lakes · forest hikes · Germany's only Rheinland-Pfalz via ferrata.

Saturday Drive the 25 km up to Daun and walk the Dronke Tower loop above Weinfelder Maar — a 5.3 km easy ramble of ~1h32, then climb the 43 steps of the Dronketurm for the panorama of all three Daun Maare [22]. Return for dinner — the route is short enough to allow it.
Sunday The Burgenklettersteig Manderscheid: 4.6 km of via ferrata, three stages A-D, a 60 m rope bridge — the only via ferrata in Rheinland-Pfalz [20]. Equipment rents at the Kurhaus Tourist-Information. Cycling alternative: the asphalted Maare-Mosel-Radweg, ~58 km on a former railway line down to Bernkastel [23].

Les Villages
Where to spend the daytime hours — every option a worthy half-day on its own.
22 km · 19 min
Mosel · Route A

Bernkastel-Kues

Medieval marktplatz around the 1416 Spitzhäuschen [45]; the yellow Burg Landshut Express climbs hourly to the ruin above [16].

235 m above
Castle · 13th c.

Burg Landshut

Built on a late-Roman Princastellum, destroyed by fire 1692, barrier-free circular path and an on-site restaurant [15].

15 km · 18 min
Eifel · Route B

Manderscheid

Twin medieval ruins facing each other across the Lieser. Oberburg free year-round; Niederburg daily 11:00-17:00 April-November [19].

Daun & the Maare
Vulkaneifel · 25 km

Daun

Three water-filled crater lakes — Gemündener, Weinfelder, Schalkenmehrener. The 11 km MaareGlück trail or the 5.3 km Dronke Tower loop [22].

31 km · 26 min
Mosel · Jugendstil

Traben-Trarbach

Around 1900 the second-largest wine-trading town in Europe after Bordeaux [18]. Möhring's Brückentor above; vast cellars below as the Underworld.

18 km · 16 min
Castle hotel · Mosel

Schloss Lieser

Neo-Renaissance + Art Nouveau, Kaiser Wilhelm II slept here 1906/1911/1913; today a 49-room Marriott Autograph hotel on the riverbank [7].
