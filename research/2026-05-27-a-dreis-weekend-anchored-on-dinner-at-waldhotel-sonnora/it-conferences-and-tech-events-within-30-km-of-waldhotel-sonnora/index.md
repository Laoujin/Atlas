> **TL;DR.** The 30 km circle around Waldhotel Sonnora is rural Eifel — no startup hub, no recurring developer conference. Almost everything IT/tech-flavoured is anchored at the University and Hochschule of Trier (25 km straight-line south [[1]](https://de.entfernung.himmera.com/entfernungen_stadten-trier-deutschland.html)). The flagship picks for 2026 are **CiE 2026** (theoretical CS, July 27-31) [[4]](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-cie-2026) and the **WRO Deutschlandfinale** (robotics, June 19-20) [[7]](https://www.trier.de/aktuelles/nachrichten/16974.Deutschland-Finale-der-World-Robot-Olympiad-2026-in-Trier.html). Outside Trier the offering is thin: a few SMB AI workshops in Daun/Nerdlen [[9]](https://wirtschaft.eifel.info/2026/03/02/ki-praxisnah-und-datenschutzkonform-einsetzen/) and online sessions from the Bernkastel-Wittlich Wirtschaftsförderung [[11]](https://www.bernkastel-wittlich.de/pressemitteilungen/2026/februar-2026/neue-online-workshopreihe-vermittelt-grundlagen-im-online-marketing-und-den-einsatz-von-kuenstlicher-intelligenz/).

## Geography — what's actually in the 30 km circle

Waldhotel Sonnora sits in 54518 Dreis, between Wittlich and Bernkastel-Kues [[2]](https://www.eifel.info/en/gastro/waldhotel-sonnora). Distances from Dreis:

| Bernkastel-Kues      | ~15 km       | ~20 min  | Tourism + Mosel                                           |

| Daun / Nerdlen       | ~25 km       | ~30 min  | TGZ Daun (Konrad-Zuse-Str.) [[9]](https://wirtschaft.eifel.info/2026/03/02/ki-praxisnah-und-datenschutzkonform-einsetzen/) |

Reference point for the radius: Wittlich-to-Trier alone is 31 km straight-line [[3]](https://www.luftlinie.org/Trier/Wittlich); Dreis sits closer to Trier than Wittlich does, so Trier University events fit inside the 30 km rule.

## What's worth planning a weekend around

- **CiE 2026 + Grammar Systems 2026 (Jul 27 – Jul 31).** The only multi-day academic IT conference inside the radius. Theoretical CS, computability, complexity, formal languages. Full registration €200 until June 30, €300 after; students €150/€200 [[4]](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-cie-2026). Pair the Sat-30-Aug-1 evening with Sonnora and the weekend bridges the workshop's closing.
- **WRO Deutschlandfinale (Jun 19-20, Fri-Sat).** Not a developer conf, but the country's largest school robotics event — 150 teams, ~2,500 visitors, MINT booths [[7]](https://www.trier.de/aktuelles/nachrichten/16974.Deutschland-Finale-der-World-Robot-Olympiad-2026-in-Trier.html). Saturday at the SWT-Arena → 40-min drive to Sonnora for dinner is realistic.
- **Informatikcamp (Jun 29 – Jul 3).** Aimed at high-school students, not professionals — relevant only if traveling with a teenager [[6]](https://www.hochschule-trier.de/hochschule/aktuelles/terminkalender/termin-detail/informatikcamp-2026).
- **vitis live (Jul 1-2).** Closest event geographically (Piesport, ~10 km) but it's a viticulture technology show, not IT — included for completeness because the workflow asks "tech events" [[14]](https://en.bernkastel.de/event/vitis-live-grosse-internationale-tec/event.html).

## What's missing — and why

- **No recurring developer meetups / user groups** found in Trier (no active Java/Python/.NET Stammtisch turned up). The student-led "Trier Tech Talk Conference" last ran in 2018 with no 2026 edition announced [[17]](https://5vier.de/die-zweite-trier-tech-talk-conference-an-der-uni-192111.html).
- **No hackathon or cybersecurity conference** inside the radius for 2026.
- **No vendor / commercial IT trade show.** The regional Wirtschaftskreis Bernkastel-Wittlich calendar is dominated by company tours and a career fair (TAKE OFF, Sept 13-14 at Eventum Wittlich) — useful for the local labour market, not for developers [[15]](https://wirtschaftskreis.de/events).

## Disambiguation note

There are two "Dreis" in Rheinland-Pfalz: **54518 Dreis** (Bernkastel-Wittlich, Sonnora) and **54552 Dreis-Brück** (Vulkaneifel, near Daun). One distance source quoted "Dreis → Daun, 7.8 km road" — that refers to Dreis-**Brück**, not the Sonnora location, which is closer to ~25 km from Daun [[18]](https://entfernung-von.de/daun/nerdlen.htm). Worth checking when searching for local events.
