The walkable-lodging question — the trip's hardest constraint — was only partially answered: the dedicated walking-distance child failed (`run.sh exit 1`), but the taxi-range sub-topic incidentally resolves it. Dreis has exactly one hotel, and that hotel *is* Waldhotel Sonnora itself, rated #1 of 1 in the village on Tripadvisor [[1]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g20304189-d1812361-Reviews-Waldhotel_Sonnora-Dreis_Rhineland_Palatinate.html). The dining room and the 20 rooms above it share one tiny inventory and one phone call — Tripadvisor reviewers and the property's own positioning both warn that "rooms exist mostly to serve the restaurant's guests" [[2]](https://www.travelsforstars.com/blog/2024/12/05/waldhotel-sonnora-dreis), and the gourmet breakfast is part of the three-star ritual [[3]](https://hotel-sonnora.de/en/hotel-en/). Standard renovated doubles run ~€268, the Heritage Suite €315, the 90 m² Forest Suite €595 [[4]](https://hotel-sonnora.de/en/hotel-en/forest-suite/). Lead time should be measured in months, not weeks. Book table and room in the same call.

If Sonnora is full, the constraint **breaks**: there is no walkable second-best, and the next options are 16–26 minutes by taxi — Schloss Lieser (castle, on the Mosel), Weinromantikhotel Richtershof (working winery, Mülheim), or the 1903 Bruno Möhring Art Nouveau Bellevue in Traben-Trarbach (€164+) [[5]](https://www.bellevue-hotel.de/en/rooms-suites/standard-doppelzimmer). All require the taxi to be pre-booked the day before, since public transport balloons to 90+ minutes and the last bus dies early [[6]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Dreis-Germany/Traben-Trarbach).

Daytime is the easy part. Dreis sits at the unusual collision of the Middle Mosel and the volcanic Vulkaneifel, and the 30 km arc covers four medieval old towns, six VDP Riesling estates, three crater lakes, Germany's only RLP via ferrata and a Roman villa with intact mosaics — most stops 15–35 minutes from the hotel [[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreis) [[8]](https://burgenklettersteig.de/). Every village surfaced as a lodging option doubles as a worthy day-trip destination — Lieser (castle + VDP estate), Mülheim (vitis live tech show 1–2 July, ~10 km) [[9]](https://en.bernkastel.de/event/vitis-live-grosse-internationale-tec/event.html), Traben-Trarbach (Jugendstil + Underworld cellar tours), Bernkastel-Kues (Marktplatz + Burg Landshut), Eisenschmitt (Salmtal forest hikes). Pick the **wine-and-medieval** shape (Bernkastel + Molitor tasting on Saturday; Manderscheid castles on Sunday) or the **volcanic-and-active** shape (Dauner Maare loop Saturday; Burgenklettersteig or Maare-Mosel-Radweg Sunday). Markus Molitor's daily 10:00–17:00 vinothek in Zeltingen-Rachtig is the only winery that works without an appointment [[10]](https://markusmolitor.com/en/opening-hours); Dr. Loosen, Schloss Lieser, Prüm, Fritz Haag and Selbach-Oster all require advance contact, and most close Sunday [[11]](https://en.visitmosel.de/wine-cuisine/gastro/dr-loosen-winery).

Tech-conference timing is sparse but specific: only **CiE 2026** (theoretical CS, 27–31 July at Uni Trier, ~25 km, €200 early-bird) [[12]](https://www.uni-trier.de/en/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-iv/faecher/informatikwissenschaften/professuren/theoretische-informatik/research/conferences-and-workshops/translate-to-englisch-cie-2026) and the **WRO Deutschlandfinale** (19–20 June, SWT-Arena Trier) [[13]](https://www.trier.de/aktuelles/nachrichten/16974.Deutschland-Finale-der-World-Robot-Olympiad-2026-in-Trier.html) bridge naturally into a Saturday-night Sonnora dinner. Other 2026 date traps: Wittlich's Säubrennerkirmes (3rd weekend August, 100k visitors blocking the town centre) [[14]](https://www.kirmes-in-deutschland.de/saeubrennerkirmes-in-wittlich.html); Vulkaneifel-Therme closed 15–28 June [[15]](https://www.vulkaneifeltherme.de/); Mythos Mosel (29–31 May) and the Middle Mosel Wine Festival (3–7 September) as the two big regional pull-dates if you want the wine festival overlay [[16]](https://www.bonvinitas.com/en/ev/may-29-31-2026-mythos-mosel-discover-top-wines-also-from-many-young-talents-along-the-moselle).

What still isn't researched in this run: the dinner mechanics themselves — tasting-menu vs à-la-carte pricing under Clemens Rambichler, wine-pairing cost, the actual meal length to use as the Saturday time anchor, dress code, the live reservation lead time, and whether the room booking forces the dinner reservation (or vice-versa). The walking-distance child failed before producing those numbers; the [Michelin guide entry](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/rheinland-pfalz/dreis/restaurant/waldhotel-sonnora) and the hotel's reservations line are the next call to make before any of the above gets locked in.
