> **TL;DR.** Book a [Brasserie Bristol residence](https://brasseriebristol.be/verblijf) (Zeedijk 290–291, ~3 min walk) for same-promenade convenience and sea view [[5]](https://brasseriebristol.be/verblijf)[[6]](https://residentiebristol.be/ligging). If those are full, pick [Hotel St Yves](https://booking.westtoer.be/hotel/st-yves-hotel-knokke-heist?lang=en) for an 8-room dike hotel ~6 min away [[7]](https://booking.westtoer.be/hotel/st-yves-hotel-knokke-heist?lang=en), or [Residentie De Laurier](https://www.residentie-delaurier.be/en) (Vlamingstraat 45, 0.5 km) for themed suites at the highest guest rating in the cluster [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.be/HotelsNear-g230021-d741974-Bartholomeus_Heist_Aan_Zee-Knokke_Heist_West_Flanders_Province.html)[[16]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g230021-d1835731-Reviews-De_Laurier-Knokke_Heist_West_Flanders_Province.html).

[Bartholomeus](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/heist/restaurant/bartholomeus) sits at Zeedijk-Heist 267 on the seafront promenade, two Michelin stars, 18 seats at the kitchen counter, Wed–Sat only with a single 19:00–19:15 dinner seating [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/west-vlaanderen/heist/restaurant/bartholomeus)[[2]](https://www.strandverblijf.be/nl/eten-en-drinken/118/bartholomeus)[[3]](https://www.restaurantbartholomeus.be/). The 6-course tasting usually breaks up around 22:30–23:00, so a short, well-lit walk back to the room is the actual constraint — not a 1 km radius on a map.

## How to choose

- **Want to walk out the door onto the same dike as Bartholomeus** → [Brasserie Bristol residences](https://brasseriebristol.be/verblijf) (Zeedijk 290–291). Apartments rather than hotel service, but unbeatable proximity and a sea-view balcony for the morning after [[5]](https://brasseriebristol.be/verblijf)[[6]](https://residentiebristol.be/ligging).
- **Want a small, traditional hotel with a sea-view room and breakfast included** → [Hotel St Yves](https://booking.westtoer.be/hotel/st-yves-hotel-knokke-heist?lang=en), 8 rooms, also on the Zeedijk, ~500 m east of Bartholomeus [[7]](https://booking.westtoer.be/hotel/st-yves-hotel-knokke-heist?lang=en).
- **Want the highest-rated boutique room within 10 minutes** → [Residentie De Laurier](https://www.residentie-delaurier.be/en) (8.8/10), themed suites with private parking [[8]](https://www.residentie-delaurier.be/en)[[16]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g230021-d1835731-Reviews-De_Laurier-Knokke_Heist_West_Flanders_Province.html).
- **Budget pick** → [B&B Charlatan](https://www.charlatans.be/), €95–120/night with breakfast, 3 rooms only — book early [[11]](https://www.charlatans.be/).
- **Couple-only weekend with a pool** → [Maison Seki](https://en.maisonseki.com/home); rated 9.4 on Booking and #5 specialty lodging in Knokke-Heist [[9]](https://en.maisonseki.com/home)[[15]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g230021-d17603018-Reviews-Maison_Seki-Knokke_Heist_West_Flanders_Province.html). ⚠ Agoda lists it as adults-only — confirm at booking [[10]](https://www.agoda.com/maison-seki/hotel/knokke-heist-be.html).
- **Travelling with family** → [Hotel du Soleil](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g230021-d631477-Reviews-Hotel_Du_Soleil-Knokke_Heist_West_Flanders_Province.html), 27 rooms, family-oriented, pet-friendly, kitchenettes, 100 m from beach [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g230021-d631477-Reviews-Hotel_Du_Soleil-Knokke_Heist_West_Flanders_Province.html).
- **Want a recently renovated boutique villa and don't mind a 15-min walk** → [Villa Bonnie](https://www.villabonnie.be/en/) in Duinbergen, 14 rooms, 2024 refit, 400 m to the seawall then a short walk along the dike to the restaurant [[13]](https://www.villabonnie.be/en/).

## Practical notes for a Saturday-evening sitting

- Bartholomeus only does Wed–Sat and runs one seating per service (19:00 sharp) — a 10-minute walk back at 23:00 in winter is fine on the lit Zeedijk, but anything inland (Walstraat, Patriottenstraat, Oud-Strijdersstraat) means a few minutes through quiet residential streets [[3]](https://www.restaurantbartholomeus.be/).
- The restaurant is car-free on the dike side — driving guests will need on-street parking or a hotel with private parking. De Laurier, Villa Bonnie, and Hotel du Soleil all list parking; Brasserie Bristol residences and St Yves do not advertise it on their pages [[7]](https://booking.westtoer.be/hotel/st-yves-hotel-knokke-heist?lang=en)[[8]](https://www.residentie-delaurier.be/en)[[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g230021-d631477-Reviews-Hotel_Du_Soleil-Knokke_Heist_West_Flanders_Province.html)[[13]](https://www.villabonnie.be/en/).
- Arriving by train? Heist station is 485 m / 7 min from the dike, so a no-car weekend works for every option in the shortlist except Villa Bonnie (which is closer to Duinbergen station) [[14]](https://moovitapp.com/index/en/public_transit-Heist_aan_Zee-Belgium-site_223096381-1682).
