Things to know before the 19:00 seating.

⏱

The walk back at 23:00

The Zeedijk is well-lit at night. Inland streets (Walstraat, Patriottenstraat, Oud-Strijdersstraat) are quiet but residential — a 10-min walk back is fine for any of these picks[3].

🅿

Parking

The dike is car-free. De Laurier, Villa Bonnie, and Hotel du Soleil list private parking. Bristol and St Yves do not advertise it[8][13].

🚆

No-car weekend

Heist station is 485 m / 7-min walk from the Zeedijk — works for every option except Villa Bonnie, which is closer to Duinbergen station[14].

★★

The seating

Wed–Sat only; one seating per service at 19:00 sharp; 18 seats at the kitchen counter; 6-course tasting wraps around 22:30–23:00[1][3].
