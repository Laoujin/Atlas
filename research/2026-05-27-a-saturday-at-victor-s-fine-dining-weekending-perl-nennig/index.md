## The booking-anchor

One constraint ties every other decision: [Victor's Fine Dining runs Thursday–Sunday dinner and Saturday–Sunday lunch](https://www.victors-fine-dining.de/en/restaurant), with creative-pause closures 18 May–7 Jun and 21 Sep–4 Oct 2026. The [restaurant FAQ confirms they will book your Schloss Berg room alongside the dinner reservation](https://www.victors-fine-dining.de/en/faq) — making the simplest weekend a single phone call. Secure the date first; if your Saturday falls inside a closure window, none of the planning below applies.

## Where the lodging angles converge

Both surveys reach the same dominant answer from opposite directions. The [walking-distance angle](walking-distance-lodging-at-or-near-victor-s-fine-dining-by-christian-bau/) finds only one zero-walk option: the [on-property Schloss Berg 5★ Superior](https://www.victors.de/en/hotels/victor-s-residenz-hotel-schloss-berg), with [Hotel Zur Traube (~11 min on foot, from €99/night)](https://traube-nennig.de/en/hotel-restaurant-zur-traube-en/) as the realistic fallback. The [character survey](special-character-lodging-within-taxi-range-of-victor-s-fine-dining-by-christian-bau/) lists Schloss Berg first too, but flags [Tripadvisor reviews noting dated furnishings and no central A/C](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1185063-d1751985-Reviews-Victor_s_Residenz_Hotel_Schloss_Berg-Nennig_Saarland.html). The cleaner sidestep: [Hôtel Château Schengen](https://www.chateauschengen.lu/hotel/), 2 km across the river in Luxembourg (reopened June 2024), reachable by taxi with no border formality — or [Domaine la Forêt in Remich (~5 km)](https://foret.lu/en/hotel-spa-wellness-restaurant/) if a 700 m² spa and second gastronomic table complete the weekend.

## Activities radius mirrors the lodging map

Every village surfaced by either lodging angle is simultaneously a day-trip destination, so there is no friction between hotel choice and sightseeing. The [Roman Villa Nennig mosaic (~800 m walk, 160 m² of in-situ tesserae)](https://www.visitsaarland.co.uk/Media/Attractions/Roman-Villa-Nennig) and [Mahlknopf tumulus](https://www.saarschleifenland.de/en/poi/detail/tumulus-perl-nennig-958bb6a0ba) need no car; [Villa Borg's reconstructed villa rustica with its Taverne serving Roman-recipe dishes](https://www.villa-borg.de/) is 9.7 km. Saturday afternoon before dinner: [Saarschleife Cloef viewpoint + Treetop Walk in Mettlach (19 km)](https://treetop-walks.com/saarschleife/en/) or the VDP winery cluster around Ayl/Ockfen (~25 km). Sunday recovery: [Mondorf Domaine Thermal, open daily, 18 km into Luxembourg](https://www.mondorf.lu/en/timetable). The [European Museum Schengen — reopened June 2025 with the restored MS Princesse Marie-Astrid](https://www.visitschengen.lu/en/european-museum/opening-hours/) is a 3-minute bus ride, exploiting [Luxembourg's free public transport](https://luxembourg.public.lu/en/living/mobility/public-transport.html).

## The Luxembourg thread runs through all four angles

A pattern invisible in any individual child: Luxembourg is the connective tissue of the entire expedition. Every lodging alternative outside Nennig village is either in Luxembourg or crosses to it effortlessly. And all 2026 tech events in scope are in Luxembourg City or Belval at ~26 km — there are no IT events in Perl, Saarburg, or Trier. [Nexus Luxembourg (Jun 10–11, LuxExpo Kirchberg, ~10,000 attendees, €410 last-call)](https://nexusluxembourg.com/) is the largest hook for a mid-June pairing with Victor's. An October cluster — [Open Source Conference LU (Oct 7)](https://conference.opensource.lu/), [WomenHack Luxembourg (Oct 8)](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/womenhack-luxembourg-employer-ticket-october-8-2026-tickets-1980042388084), [Luxembourg Venture Days (Oct 14–15)](https://www.venture-days.lu/) — stacks three events across two weekends, and the Victor's creative-pause reopening on 5 Oct makes the timing clean.

## Tensions to settle before booking

Trier sits at [44–48 km from Perl](https://www.entfernungvon.com/de/entfernung-von-trier-nach-perl-deutschland/EntfernungHistorie/30291.aspx) — outside the 30 km rule but holding the densest Roman inventory in the region and opening [Hotel Villa Hügel's 1914 Jugendstil rooms](https://hotel-villa-huegel.de/en/), at the cost of a €100+ late-night taxi back through the Moselle valley. Without that stretch, the entire weekend fits inside a 20 km triangle: Nennig–Schengen–Mettlach.

The sharpest unresolved question: with Saturday lunch *also* on the menu, does Christian Bau's kitchen accept a same-day double booking — lunch and dinner back-to-back on the same Saturday — or must you choose?
