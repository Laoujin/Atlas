Event Details

Nexus Luxembourg — Jun 10–11, 2026

LuxExpo The Box · 10 Circuit de la Foire Internationale, L-1347 Kirchberg · ~26 km from Victor's

Europe's flagship AI and technology summit, now in its third edition. 13,500 m² across 5 stages; 10,000+ attendees from 50+ countries. Keynote speakers include Luxembourg PM Luc Frieden, OVHcloud founder Octave Klaba, and leaders from NVIDIA and UNESCO. Four zones: Intelligence Forum (applied AI, cybersecurity, autonomous systems), Fintech Sphere (digital finance, regulated innovation), Launchpad Arena (250 startups compete for a €100k prize), and Luxembourg Makes It Happen (EU policy and national innovation). Tickets at last-call price €410; B2B matchmaking app included.[4][5]

Open Source Conference Luxembourg — Oct 7, 2026

Terres Rouges · 14 Porte de France, L-4360 Belval · ~32 km from Victor's · 5 min walk from Belval-Université station

Annual community conference; 2026 theme: "Leading by example in Digital Sovereignty." Four tracks: Public Sector OSPOs, AI (open democratised tooling vs. big-tech dependency), Cybersecurity (Luxembourg-developed open source tools), EdTech & Academia. Free entry; call for papers opens annually. Organised with the Luxembourg House of Cybersecurity and CLUSIL.[7][8]

Luxembourg Venture Days — Oct 14–15, 2026

LuxExpo The Box · Kirchberg · ~26 km from Victor's

Curated dealflow event co-organised by Luxinnovation and the Luxembourg Private Equity and Venture Capital Association (LPEA). Features Fit 4 Start accelerator pitches, investor-startup matchmaking, and scaleup showcases. Attendance by invitation or application. [10]

BSides Luxembourg — annually in May (2026 edition: May 6–8, past)

Terres Rouges · Belval · ~32 km from Victor's

Community-run, non-profit cybersecurity conference. Red/blue team workshops, technical talks, CTF-style networking. Part of the global Security BSides network; returns each spring. Plan for a May 2027 visit if cybersecurity is the angle.[6]

Getting There

Victor's Fine Dining sits metres from the German-Luxembourg border in Perl-Nennig.[1] For events at LuxExpo The Box (Kirchberg) or central Luxembourg City, take the A13/E25 motorway; no tolls, ~26 km, ~24 min.[3] Belval (Terres Rouges, for BSides and Open Source Conference LU) adds ~6 km south via the A13 and N31; ~32 km, ~30 min. Public transit (regional bus from Nennig) is possible but adds roughly one hour each way; a hire car or taxi makes the evening reservation at Victor's practical.

Outside 30 km: Trier (CiE 2026, Jul 27–31, academic CS) is 36 km straight-line / 44 km by road — too far to combine with a Victor's dinner without an overnight. Saarbrücken and Metz events exceed 60 km. No dedicated IT conferences found in Thionville, Merzig, or Saarlouis for 2026.
