> **TL;DR — Decision.** Stay at **[Victor's Residenz-Hotel Schloss Berg][s1]** — the restaurant is inside the same complex, the dining FAQ recommends it and will book it for you, and it's the only property at zero walking distance [[2]](https://www.victors-fine-dining.de/en/faq). If it's sold out or over-budget, **[Hotel Zur Traube][s2]** (Bübinger Str. 22) is the realistic fallback at ~11 min on foot, €99–244/night [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1185063-d3399983-Reviews-Hotel_Zur_Traube-Nennig_Saarland.html)[[9]](https://www.preiswert-uebernachten.de/perl-nennig/hotel-zur-traube/5201). For self-catered stays, **[Wieser Gästehaus][s3]** in the Wies district covers the 4–5 star Ferienwohnung niche [[11]](http://www.wieser-gaestehaus.de/).

## Geography

The restaurant sits at **Schloßstraße 27–29, 66706 Perl-Nennig** [[1]](https://www.victors-fine-dining.de/en/contact). Nennig is a small Mosel-river village; the village core is compact enough that anywhere on Bübinger Straße or Wieser Straße is reachable from the castle in under 15 minutes on foot [[10]](https://onlinestreet.de/strassen/B%C3%BCbinger+Stra%C3%9Fe.Perl.529847.html). Anything in Perl town centre (the next municipality south) is 4–5 km — taxi territory, not walking.

## Detail

### [Victor's Residenz-Hotel Schloss Berg][r1] — pick this if it's available

Two-building 5★ Superior complex: a Roman-style villa (most of the 87 rooms) and a Renaissance castle (where Christian Bau cooks). The restaurant FAQ states plainly that they will book your room for you [[2]](https://www.victors-fine-dining.de/en/faq), which is the easy path: one reservation covers dinner + bed and avoids any post-pairing walk. Room types span Classic / Superior / Deluxe / Junior, Classic, Imperial, Roman suites in the villa, and Princess / Prince / Queen Junior / Empress Classic in the castle [[3]](https://www.victors.de/en/hotels/victor-s-residenz-hotel-schloss-berg/rooms1). Spa with indoor pool, sauna, steam bath, hot tub, gym; second restaurant Bacchus and beer-garden style Landgasthaus on-site [[5]](https://victor-s-residenz-hotel-schloss-berg-nennig.hotel-mix.de/). Listed in the Michelin Guide hotels section [[6]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/hotels-stays/nennig/victors-residenz-hotel-schloss-berg-9737) and on the Saarschleifenland tourism board [[17]](https://www.saarschleifenland.de/en/poi/detail/victors-residenz-hotel-schloss-berg-fdc61676bc). ⚠ Note creative-break closures of the fine-dining room are scheduled May 18 – Jun 7, 2026 and Sep 21 – Oct 4, 2026 — check dates before booking.

### [Hotel Zur Traube][r2] — best fallback

3-star inn at **Bübinger Straße 22**, 21 rooms / 42 beds, owner Carlo Boesen [[8]](https://traube-nennig.de/en/hotel-restaurant-zur-traube-en/). Premium 42 m² doubles in *Landhaus-Stil* with kitchenettes and balconies; standard singles from €99, doubles from €129; high-season standard rate range $179–$244 per Tripadvisor data [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1185063-d3399983-Reviews-Hotel_Zur_Traube-Nennig_Saarland.html)[[9]](https://www.preiswert-uebernachten.de/perl-nennig/hotel-zur-traube/5201). Tripadvisor places the walk at ~11 min [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1185063-d3399983-Reviews-Hotel_Zur_Traube-Nennig_Saarland.html); breakfast and the regional-cuisine restaurant get generally positive notes, with one recurring complaint about slow front-desk handling. Phone +49 6866 349.

### [Wieser Gästehaus][r3] — for a self-catered weekend

Small family-run guesthouse at **Wieser Str. 14** in Nennig's historic Wies district, converted from an old farm barn into 4- and 5-star Ferienwohnungen [[11]](http://www.wieser-gaestehaus.de/)[[12]](https://web2.cylex.de/firma-home/wieser-gaestehaus-10829161.html). The 2017 Verkehrsverein gastgeber list quoted €50/night for a 2-person apartment plus €10 per extra adult [[13]](https://s09faffb97f56d094.jimcontent.com/download/version/1545332571/module/12189391326/name/GVPerl_2017.pdf) — rates almost certainly higher in 2026, contact for current pricing. Phone 06866 274. Right scale if you want a private kitchen for the morning after.

### [Gästeappartements Lisa / KMK Monteure][r4] — budget floor, mention only

At **Bübinger Straße 13**, 3 apartments holding 4–7 guests each with kitchenette, washer/dryer, TV-Sat, DSL [[14]](https://www.pension-lisa.de/nennig/). Pricing is per-person and contractor-oriented (from €16.75/night/person for 8-person, multi-month blocks plus VAT) [[15]](https://www.preiswert-uebernachten.de/hotel-pensionen/perl-nennig/3865). Walking distance to the restaurant is fine, but the property is positioned for tradespeople on long stays — not the right ambience for a Michelin Saturday. Listed here only because it's the budget reference point in the village.

## What's *not* walking distance

Hotel Sonnenhof, Hotel Hammes, Haus & Hof Guest House, and Hotel Perler Hof all sit in Perl town centre, ~4–5 km south of the restaurant [[15]](https://www.preiswert-uebernachten.de/hotel-pensionen/perl-nennig/3865). Good options on their own merits but require a car or taxi after dinner — Tripadvisor's own guidance for the Schloss Berg area is to plan for a taxi rather than walk with luggage [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1185063-d3399983-Reviews-Hotel_Zur_Traube-Nennig_Saarland.html). Across the Mosel in Remich (Luxembourg) is also non-walking — the nearest bridge crossings add real distance.

## Booking sequence

1. Book the dinner at Victor's Fine Dining first [[16]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/saarland/perl/restaurant/victor-s-fine-dining-by-christian-bau).
2. Ask the restaurant to book a Schloss Berg room with the reservation [[2]](https://www.victors-fine-dining.de/en/faq). Or book directly via [the hotel's own site](https://www.victors.de/en/hotels/victor-s-residenz-hotel-schloss-berg) or [Booking.com][bk] [[18]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/de/victor-s-residenz-schloss-berg.html).
3. If Schloss Berg is full, contact Hotel Zur Traube at +49 6866 349 [[8]](https://traube-nennig.de/en/hotel-restaurant-zur-traube-en/), then Wieser Gästehaus at 06866 274 [[12]](https://web2.cylex.de/firma-home/wieser-gaestehaus-10829161.html).
