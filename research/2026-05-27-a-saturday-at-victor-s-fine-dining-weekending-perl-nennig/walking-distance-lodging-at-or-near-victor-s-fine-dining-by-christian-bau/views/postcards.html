How to book it

In this order · dinner first, always
  1. Book dinner first at Victor's Fine Dining — the rest depends on the date.
  2. Ask the restaurant to book a Schloss Berg room with the reservation — or go direct via the hotel's site or Booking.com.
  3. If Schloss Berg is sold out: ring Hotel Zur Traube at +49 6866 349.
  4. Self-catering instead? Wieser Gästehaus on 06866 274.

What's not walking distance

Hotel Sonnenhof, Hotel Hammes, Haus & Hof Guest House, and Hotel Perler Hof all sit in Perl town centre, ~4–5 km south — car or taxi after dinner. Tripadvisor itself recommends a taxi rather than walking with luggage. Remich across the Mosel adds bridge distance — also not on foot.
