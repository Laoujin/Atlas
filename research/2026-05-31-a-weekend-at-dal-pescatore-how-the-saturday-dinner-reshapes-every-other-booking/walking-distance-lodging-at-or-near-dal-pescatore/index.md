> **Decision.** Runate has no inn. The closest bed is **[Agriturismo L'Airone](http://lnx.laironeagriturismo.com/ns/)** ("Hotel Airone" on Tripadvisor) at ~1.6 km — a ~20-min walk on a quiet country road that's fine in daylight but unlit at night [[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g21085013-d2100659-Reviews-Hotel_Airone-Castelfranco_d_Oglio_Province_of_Cremona_Lombardy.html). For an evening that ends late after a tasting menu, the sensible booking is **[9 Muse B&B](https://beb.it/9muse/en/)** (3 km): co-owner Alberto used to cook at Dal Pescatore and runs a free drop-off / pick-up service so you skip the drive-after-wine problem [[15]](https://beb.it/9muse/en/) [[16]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g1238427-d1234929-r127489241-9_Muse_B_B-Canneto_sull_Oglio_Province_of_Mantua_Lombardy.html).

## Why there is no walking-distance hotel

[Dal Pescatore](https://www.dalpescatore.com/en/) sits at Via Runate 15, in the hamlet of Runate — a *frazione* of Canneto sull'Oglio (MN) with about 31 residents [[3]](https://mapcarta.com/30283814). Three Michelin stars since 1996 [[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dal_Pescatore); a Relais & Châteaux member, but listed as a restaurant only — no on-site rooms or partner-hotel page on the official site [[1]](https://www.dalpescatore.com/en/) [[5]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/restaurant/restaurant-dal-pescatore-santini/). The adjacent Cascina Runate is the restaurant's own 17th-c. farmstead — it supplies vegetables, meat and herbs to the kitchen and does not offer lodging [[6]](https://www.lombardiabeniculturali.it/architetture/schede/MN360-00191/). The aggregator-curated "B&Bs near Dal Pescatore" index flags this directly: the closest registered property is 2 km away and "not walkable" [[7]](https://www.bed-and-breakfast.it/en/near/dal-pescatore-famiglia-santini-canneto-sull-oglio).

## Which to pick

- **You actually want to walk it.** Book Agriturismo L'Airone — 1.6 km on a quiet country road past the Oglio. Fine in daylight; after a 9-course tasting menu it's a 20-min walk in the dark with no streetlights, so bring a torch and decide on the day [[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g21085013-d2100659-Reviews-Hotel_Airone-Castelfranco_d_Oglio_Province_of_Cremona_Lombardy.html). 14 rooms, $134–203/night, bikes available [[9]](http://lnx.laironeagriturismo.com/ns/).
- **You want the easiest evening.** Book 9 Muse — free shuttle to the door, owners who know the kitchen team, four small rust-restored rooms. Multiple Tripadvisor reviewers say this is the right pick specifically for diners at Dal Pescatore [[16]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g1238427-d1234929-r127489241-9_Muse_B_B-Canneto_sull_Oglio_Province_of_Mantua_Lombardy.html). Confirm the shuttle on booking — it's offered, not a published amenity.
- **You want a "real hotel" with parking and pool.** Hotel Margot in Canneto town centre, 23 rooms, 4-min drive. Weak Tripadvisor sample (3 reviews, 2.7/5) — manage expectations [[13]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1238427-d1439374-Reviews-Hotel_Margot-Canneto_sull_Oglio_Province_of_Mantua_Lombardy.html).
- **You want a destination stay, not just a bed.** Palazzo Quaranta in Isola Dovarese — 18th-c. residence, hotel + restaurant in its own right; reviewers regularly book it as the Dal Pescatore base [[18]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g1985440-d1896799-r177990527-Palazzo_Quaranta-Isola_Dovarese_Province_of_Cremona_Lombardy.html). 6 km drive; ask about shuttle when booking.
- **You'd rather base in a city.** Cremona is ~30 km / ~30 min; budget €40–45 each way by taxi [[19]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g1238427-d799437-r321901638-Dal_Pescatore-Canneto_sull_Oglio_Province_of_Mantua_Lombardy.html). Mantua is similar.

## Booking notes

- Italy's drink-drive limit is 0.5 g/L — a Dal Pescatore wine pairing puts you over it. Plan a shuttle, taxi, or designated driver, not a self-drive return.
- All the B&Bs above have 4–14 rooms — for Saturday dinner slots, book lodging the same week you confirm the restaurant.
- Dal Pescatore closes Monday and Tuesday and pauses in parts of August and January [[4]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/lombardia/runate/restaurant/dal-pescatore) — confirm the restaurant calendar before locking lodging.
