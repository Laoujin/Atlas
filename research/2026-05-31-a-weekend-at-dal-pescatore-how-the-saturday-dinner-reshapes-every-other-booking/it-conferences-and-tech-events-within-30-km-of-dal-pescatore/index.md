> **Decision.** Within a strict 30 km radius of Dal Pescatore there are **no general-purpose IT/developer conferences in 2026** — the area is rural Lombardy, between Cremona [[2]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Canneto-sull-Oglio/Cremona) (31 km, on the boundary) and Mantova [[3]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Canneto-sull-Oglio/Mantua) (46 km straight-line, 39 km road, just outside). The realistic options are **Politecnico di Milano's Cremona campus** (occasional STEM/cyber talks, summer camp) [[10]](https://www.cremonaoggi.it/2026/05/21/cremona-summer-camp-al-politecnico-dal-30-giugno-al-10-luglio/) and **CremonaFiere** (Cremona Musica in October has a 3D-printing-in-lutherie strand; agritech expo in November) [[15]](https://acousticguitarvillage.net/en/2026/03/lacoustic-guitar-village-a-cremona-musica-2026-presenta-il-programma-delle-masterclass-di-liuteria-il-concorso-per-emergenti-new-sounds-of-acoustic-music-cremona-acoustic-blues-meetin/)[[17]](https://fierezootecnichecr.it/?lang=en). For a real IT conference, stretch the radius to Milan (~120 km).

## The geography problem

Dal Pescatore is in the hamlet of Runate, municipality of Canneto sull'Oglio, province of Mantova [[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dal_Pescatore). It sits in flat agricultural country between Cremona and Mantova [[4]](https://en.db-city.com/Italy--Lombardy--Mantova--Canneto-sull'Oglio). What that means for a 30 km radius:

| City        | Straight-line | Road    | In radius?         | Citation |

| Mantova     | 46 km         | 39 km   | ✗ just outside     | [[3]][1] |
| Brescia     | ~50+ km       | ~55 km  | ✗ outside          | —        |

Within the small towns inside 30 km — Asola, Casalmaggiore, Viadana, Bozzolo, Piadena, Sabbioneta, Castel Goffredo — there is **no tech conference activity**. The cluster is rural; tech infrastructure here is industrial (Industry 4.0 via Confindustria networks), not conference-driven [[21]](https://www.dihlombardia.com/network/confindustria-mantova).

**[Cremona Summer Camp](https://www.polo-cremona.polimi.it/en/)** — free, intensive 9-hour courses across five paths (Automation, AI, Sustainability, "Facciamo un'App" Flutter dev). Limited to 3rd/4th-year high schoolers, max 20 per course, registration by 14 Jun [[10]](https://www.cremonaoggi.it/2026/05/21/cremona-summer-camp-al-politecnico-dal-30-giugno-al-10-luglio/). Not useful for IT professionals; useful if you have a teenager travelling with you.

**[Cremona Musica](https://cremonamusica.com/en/)** — the largest international fair for high-quality string instruments [[14]](https://cremonamusica.com/en/), running Fri-Sat-Sun [[16]](https://www.tradefairdates.com/Cremona-Musica-M1476/Cremona.html). Tech angle: the Acoustic Guitar Village strand runs masterclasses on professional fretwork and 3D printing applied to lutherie [[15]](https://acousticguitarvillage.net/en/2026/03/lacoustic-guitar-village-a-cremona-musica-2026-presenta-il-programma-delle-masterclass-di-liuteria-il-concorso-per-emergenti-new-sounds-of-acoustic-music-cremona-acoustic-blues-meetin/). Niche, but the most credibly "tech-adjacent" event in radius all year.

**[Cremona International Livestock Exhibition](https://fierezootecnichecr.it/?lang=en)** — Italy's and Europe's #1 agro-zootechnology trade fair [[17]](https://fierezootecnichecr.it/?lang=en). Heavy on farm automation, sensor systems, dairy-tech — real industrial tech, just not "IT conference" in the developer sense.

## Year-round / network organisations

These don't run conferences but explain why standalone events are rare here — the local tech motion is B2B-association-driven.

- **[innexHUB](https://www.innexhub.it/)** — Lombardy's Industry 4.0 Digital Innovation Hub, founded 2017 jointly by the business and craft associations of Brescia, Cremona and Mantova [[20]](https://www.innexhub.it/). Operates through member events for SMEs, not public conferences.
- **[Confindustria Mantova](https://www.dihlombardia.com/network/confindustria-mantova)** — territorial antenna of DIH Lombardia, serving 480+ companies on Industry 4.0 and Transition 5.0 themes [[21]](https://www.dihlombardia.com/network/confindustria-mantova).
- **[Startup Weekend Mantova Community](https://www.meetup.com/startup-weekend-mantova-community/)** — monthly meetups for founders/devs around the annual Techstars weekend [[6]](https://www.meetup.com/startup-weekend-mantova-community/).

## What if you really want a developer/IT conference?

The closest real options require a drive well beyond 30 km. For context: dev.events lists **no physical tech conferences in Brescia or Parma for 2026** — only online events accessible from those cities [[22]](https://dev.events/EU/IT/Brescia/tech)[[23]](https://dev.events/EU/IT/Parma/it). The community-curated [awesome-italy-events](https://github.com/ildoc/awesome-italy-events) ⭐ 149 (May 2026) list confirms the Mantova/Cremona corridor has no recurring tech events on its radar [[24]](https://github.com/ildoc/awesome-italy-events).
