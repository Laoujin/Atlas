> **TL;DR — for the Sat 30 / Sun 31 May 2026 weekend anchored on dinner at Agnes:**
> - **Saturday afternoon (compact, on foot from the village):** walk the [8 km Bruegel route](https://www.visit-dilbeek.be/bruegelwandeling) at Sint-Anna-Pede, ~10 min from Agnes [[33]](https://www.visit-dilbeek.be/bruegelwandeling) — it lands you back fresh for dinner.
> - **Sunday daytime (one strong pick):** **Pajottenland beer-and-castle loop** — [3 Fonteinen](https://www.3fonteinen.be/en/lambikodroom/) tasting room opens Sun 13:00 (free tour 14:00) [[27]](https://www.3fonteinen.be/en/lambikodroom/) + [Gaasbeek Castle](https://www.kasteelvangaasbeek.be) (10–18, family Arnoldus exhibit) [[23]](https://www.kasteelvangaasbeek.be/en/opening-hours)[[24]](https://www.kasteelvangaasbeek.be/en/present-1). Alternatives: Brussels Grand Place + a museum, or Leuven historic core.
> - **Active locally that weekend:** [Kunstenfestivaldesarts](https://kfda.be/en/festivals/2026/programme/) closes Sat 30 May across 24 Brussels venues [[79]](https://kfda.be/en/festivals/2026/programme/); [Brussels Street Photography Festival](https://www.bspfestival.org/) finale 28-31 May (Koudelka award ceremony Sat 30) [[83]](https://www.bspfestival.org/); [Les Nuits Botanique](https://botanique.be/en/projects/les-nuits-botanique/les-nuits-2026) Sat 30 May (Ibeyi + Nubya Garcia) [[84]](https://botanique.be/en/projects/les-nuits-botanique/les-nuits-2026); the new [Bodegems-h-ART](https://www.dilbeek.be/nl/uit-in-dilbeek/bodegems-h-art-kunstwandeling) art walk is on *in your village* (runs 10 May – 1 Oct 2026) [[82]](https://www.dilbeek.be/nl/uit-in-dilbeek/bodegems-h-art-kunstwandeling). **⚠ Avoid driving into central Brussels on Sun 31 May** — the 20 km door Brussel race starts 10:00 at Jubelpark with road closures [[85]](https://www.20kmdebruxelles.be/nl/).
> - **Weather backup (rain):** [AfricaMuseum](https://www.africamuseum.be) Tervuren (~15 km, closed Mon) or [Atomium](https://atomium.be) + [Mini-Europe](https://www.minieurope.com) at Heysel — both open Sun [[69]](https://www.africamuseum.be/en/visit/opening_hours)[[59]](https://atomium.be/atomium-price)[[57]](https://www.minieurope.com/en/opening-hours/).

Sint-Martens-Bodegem is a sub-village of Dilbeek in Flemish Brabant, ~15 km west of Brussels-Central — its own SNCB station sits on the S4/S10 regional lines, with hourly direct trains reaching Brussels-Central in ~28 minutes [[1]](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/station-information/vlaams-brabant/sint-martens-bodegem)[[2]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sint-Martens-Bodegem-Station/Brussels-Central-Station)[[3]](https://citymapper.com/brussels/train/stations/sint-martens-bodegem). A 30 km radius from Agnes therefore covers all of Brussels, the entire Pajottenland, the Sonian Forest, Hallerbos, Tervuren, and just clips Mechelen, Leuven, and the bigger family parks at Planckendael and Walibi [[77]](https://www.distancecalculator.net/from-dilbeek-to-leuven).

## In the village & on foot from Agnes

The standout: you're inside the literal landscape Pieter Bruegel the Elder painted. The [Bruegelwandeling](https://www.visit-dilbeek.be/bruegelwandeling) is a signposted 8 km loop (~3 h) starting at Sint-Anna-Pede's church — the building immortalised in *The Blind Leading the Blind* — passing 11 weatherproof reproductions of Bruegel paintings through the Pedevallei, including *Parable of the Blind* and *Peasant Wedding* [[33]](https://www.visit-dilbeek.be/bruegelwandeling)[[44]](https://www.visitvlaamsbrabant.be/producten/wandelen/bruegelwandeling/). Dilbeek presents the whole valley as an open-air "Bruegel's Eye" landscape museum [[34]](https://www.dilbeek.be/en/bruegels-eye-reconstructing-landscape), and the route crosses under the 520 m 1932 viaduct locally known as *The 17 Bridges* [[35]](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/en/2019/03/06/take-a-walk-through-the-landscape-that-inspired-breugel/). Dilbeek itself adds [Groot-Bijgaarden Castle](https://www.kasteelgrootbijgaarden.be) (spring Floralia bulb show), De Viron Castle and its public park, and the green Pede Valley hiking area [[21]](https://www.tiqets.com/en/dilbeek-attractions-c60829/tickets-for-groot-bijgarden-castle-floralia-brussels-entry-p1109373/)[[22]](https://www.whatdo.in/belgium/dilbeek).

On now in the village: **[Bodegems-h-ART](https://www.dilbeek.be/nl/uit-in-dilbeek/bodegems-h-art-kunstwandeling)**, a free open-air art walk starting at *Huisje Mostinckx* in Sint-Martens-Bodegem, running 10 May – 1 October 2026 — walkable straight from Agnes [[82]](https://www.dilbeek.be/nl/uit-in-dilbeek/bodegems-h-art-kunstwandeling). The nearby [Wolfsputten reserve](https://www.visit-dilbeek.be/wolfsputtenwandeling) (the 90-ha Dilbeek nature area usually meant when locals say "Wolvenberg") offers 4.6 km or 8.6 km loops from the Westrand cultural-centre car park, ending at a suspended platform with sightlines back to the Atomium and the Koekelberg Basilica [[49]](https://www.visit-dilbeek.be/wolfsputtenwandeling).

## Pajottenland — unique to this region

The rural belt south-west of Brussels (Dilbeek, Gooik, Lennik, Galmaarden, Pepingen, Roosdaal, Bever, Halle) is famous for spontaneous-fermentation beer, working watermills and Bruegel landscapes [[38]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pajottenland)[[39]](https://www.pajot-zenne.be/projecten/landschap-erfgoed/water-en-wind/7257). Three flagship visits sit inside the 30 km bound:

### Castle of Gaasbeek

[Kasteel van Gaasbeek][gb1] in Lennik is open Tue–Sun [gb2] 10:00–18:00 from 1 April to 15 November 2026 (closing 17:00 from October; museum garden 1 May–31 Oct; closed Mon, also closed 18–25 June for setup) [[23]](https://www.kasteelvangaasbeek.be/en/opening-hours). For the target weekend the live programme is the family-friendly *Attic of Arnoldus the Mouse* (April–May); from 26 June a Clara Spilliaert solo opens [[24]](https://www.kasteelvangaasbeek.be/en/present-1). Visit Flanders pitches Gaasbeek as the gateway between Brussels and Pajottenland's hills [[25]](https://www.visitflanders.com/en/flemish-destinations/gaasbeek).

### Industrial heritage: Herisem Paper Mill

[Papiermolen Herisem](https://en.herisem.be) in Alsemberg opens to individuals Sundays 14:00–17:00 (closed mid-July to early August and 1 Dec–29 Feb); the ~1h30 guided tour includes a handmade-paper demo. Steam-engine demonstrations run only on the first Sunday of June and third Sunday of November — so **Sun 7 June** is the next chance if your party can be flexible [[36]](https://en.herisem.be/bezoek)[[37]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g1947954-d10119610-Reviews-Paper_Mill_Herisem-Alsemberg_Flemish_Brabant_Province.html).

## Brussels (~15 km, hourly direct train)

Brussels is the obvious half-day pick — the hourly SNCB direct from Sint-Martens-Bodegem takes ~28 minutes door-to-door [[2]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Sint-Martens-Bodegem-Station/Brussels-Central-Station), so a car only helps for the outskirts (Tervuren, Heysel). The Visit Brussels short-list is the UNESCO-listed Grand Place, Manneken Pis, the Royal Galleries of Saint Hubert, and the 102 m Atomium from Expo '58 [[5]](https://www.visit.brussels/en/visitors/what-to-do/must-see-attractions)[[4]](https://www.itimaker.com/blog/grand-place-brussels-guide)[[6]](https://www.latroupe.com/en/city-stories/brussels-3-days/). **⚠ Royal Palace of Brussels was closed for renovation in 2025 and is only expected to reopen for visitors in summer 2026** [[7]](https://www.secretflying.com/guides/brussels/things-to-do/) — don't bank on it for this weekend.

The Brussels Card / federal-museums free-Sunday programme: many Brussels museums are free on the **first Sunday of each month** — relevant 2026 dates are 3 May and 7 June [[76]](https://www.brusselsmuseums.be/en/agenda/free-entrance-first-sunday-of-the-month). **Sun 31 May is NOT a free Sunday** — buy ahead. Horta is also free on the first Sunday [[70]](https://www.hortamuseum.be/en/reservation-en); the Royal Fine Arts give Wednesday-afternoon free entry [[68]](https://fine-arts-museum.be/en/visit/planning-my-visit/buy-a-ticket/collections); under-18s are free essentially everywhere [[68]](https://fine-arts-museum.be/en/visit/planning-my-visit/buy-a-ticket/collections)[[71]](https://www.mim.be/en/admission-fees).

Pair them as a half-day if travelling with kids. **⚠ The Royal Greenhouses of Laeken closed on 10 May 2026** — three weekends before your trip — and won't reopen this year [[63]](https://www.koninklijke-serres-royales.be/en/tickets)[[81]](https://www.koninklijke-serres-royales.be/en/).

## Nature & outdoors

Late May is *not* peak Hallerbos bluebell season — the carpet bloomed mid-April through early May 2026 [[41]](https://www.hallerbos.be/en/bloom-bluebells/flowering-season-2026/) — and the festival weekend shuttles ended 1 May [[40]](https://www.hallerbos.be/en/visitors-info/bluebell-festival-2026/). But the beech canopy is excellent year-round, and the forest itself is the easiest "big nature" hit at ~20 km south.
