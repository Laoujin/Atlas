> **TL;DR.** Agnes sits ~10 km west of Brussels [[2]](https://places-in-the-world.com/belgium/sint-martens-bodegem/2786595), so the 30 km circle catches almost every major Brussels venue (ULB Solbosch, VUB Etterbeek, Brussels Expo, BeCentral) plus Mechelen at the edge. The anchor dates for 2026 are **FOSDEM (31 Jan – 1 Feb)** [[3]](https://fosdem.org/2026/), **Microsoft AI Tour (26 Mar)** [[7]](https://www.reply.com/en/newsroom/events/microsoft-ai-tour-2026-brussels), **Cybersec Europe (20–21 May)** [[9]](https://www.cyberseceurope.com/artikelen/cybersec-europe-on-20-and-21-may-2026-first-speakers-announced), **EuroBSDCon (9–13 Sep)** [[11]](https://2026.eurobsdcon.org/), and **Apply AI Summit (17 Nov)** [[18]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/apply-ai-summit). Leuven, Antwerp and Ghent fall outside the 30 km ring.

## Geography first

[Agnes](https://agnes.restaurant/en/) is in Sint-Martens-Bodegem (Dilbeek), Flemish Brabant, Processiestraat 3, 1700 [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/vlaams-brabant/sint-martens-bodegem/restaurant/agnes-1207215). At ~9.6 km from Brussels-Centre as the crow flies [[2]](https://places-in-the-world.com/belgium/sint-martens-bodegem/2786595), the 30 km radius covers:

| Place                      | Approx. distance from Agnes | In radius?  |
|----------------------------|-----------------------------|-------------|
| Brussels (Centre / ULB / VUB) | 10–17 km                  | ✓           |
| Brussels Expo (Heysel)     | ~13 km                      | ✓           |
| Aalst                      | ~15 km                      | ✓           |
| Vilvoorde                  | ~17 km                      | ✓           |
| Halle                      | ~17 km                      | ✓           |
| Mechelen (BruCON venue)    | ~22 km crow / ~30 km road   | ✓ (edge)    |

| Ghent                      | ~50 km                      | ✗           |
| Antwerp (Devoxx)           | ~50 km                      | ✗           |

Leuven is the painful miss — KU Leuven, imec, and PyData Belgium [[15]](https://www.meetup.com/pydata-belgium/) all sit just outside the strict 30 km circle. Antwerp Devoxx (5–9 Oct 2026) is well outside.

## Pick by interest

**Open source / Linux / FOSS.** Block out the **last week of January 2026** — FOSDEM is the gravitational centre of European open source [[3]](https://fosdem.org/2026/), and EU Open Source Week wraps a policy summit, the OSS Awards, and dozens of fringe meetups around it [[5]](https://opensourceweek.eu/)[[20]](https://commons.ngi.eu/event/eu-open-source-week-2026/). Both ULB Solbosch and the surrounding fringe venues are 15–20 min by car from Agnes.

**BSD / systems / infra.** [**EuroBSDCon 2026**](https://2026.eurobsdcon.org/) (9–13 Sep) — the European BSD conference rotates each year; this is its Brussels turn [[11]](https://2026.eurobsdcon.org/). Sponsors include Apple, ARM, OPNsense.

**Cybersecurity.**
- **Industry expo:** [Cybersec Europe](https://www.cyberseceurope.com/) (20–21 May, Brussels Expo) — biggest Benelux floor, free badge [[9]](https://www.cyberseceurope.com/artikelen/cybersec-europe-on-20-and-21-may-2026-first-speakers-announced).
- **Hacker / community:** [BruCON](https://www.brucon.org/) (24–25 Sep, Mechelen) — at the edge of the radius, but the canonical Belgian infosec con [[13]](https://www.brucon.org/conference).
- **Policy / government:** [Cyber Intelligence Europe](https://intelligence-sec.com/events/cyber-intelligence-europe-2026/) (22–23 Apr, Hilton Brussels Grand Place) — CCB Belgium speakers, EU policy angle [[8]](https://cybersecurity-centre.europa.eu/events/cyber-intelligence-europe-22-23-april-2026-brussels-belgium-2026-04-22_en).
- **AppSec local:** [OWASP Belgium chapter](https://owasp.org/www-chapter-belgium/) meets quarterly; one 2026 meeting at BeCentral / Proximus-ADA Brussels is in radius (the 2 Jun KU Leuven one isn't) [[14]](https://owasp.org/www-chapter-belgium/).

**AI / ML.**
- **Vendor day:** [Microsoft AI Tour Brussels](https://www.reply.com/en/newsroom/events/microsoft-ai-tour-2026-brussels) (26 Mar, Brussels Expo) — free, Azure/Copilot focus [[7]](https://www.reply.com/en/newsroom/events/microsoft-ai-tour-2026-brussels).
- **EU / governance:** [AI Summit Brussels](https://aisummitbrussels.com/) (26 May, VUB U-Residence) — EU AI Act compliance, governance, engineering [[10]](https://aisummitbrussels.com/). Same week as Cybersec Europe — feasible double-header.
- **EC flagship:** [Apply AI Summit](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/apply-ai-summit) (17 Nov, The EGG) — European Commission convenes 900 industry stakeholders on AI adoption [[18]](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/apply-ai-summit).

**Java / JVM.** No single big conference inside the ring in 2026 (Devoxx is Antwerp, ~50 km out). Fall back on meetups: [BeJUG](https://bejug.github.io/) hosts free evening sessions across Belgium [[16]](https://www.meetup.com/belgian-java-user-group/), and [BruJUG](https://www.meetup.com/brujug/) is the Brussels-local chapter [[19]](https://www.meetup.com/brujug/).

**Python / data.** No big Brussels conference; [PyData Belgium](https://www.meetup.com/pydata-belgium/) meets quarterly but recent venues are Dataminded / Dataroots in Leuven [[15]](https://www.meetup.com/pydata-belgium/) — just outside the ring. If a future PyData lands at a Brussels venue it'd qualify.

## What's outside the ring (worth the extra drive)

- **Devoxx Belgium** — 5–9 Oct 2026, Kinepolis Antwerpen. Biggest JVM conference in Europe, ~50 km from Agnes.
- **Leuven.AI Conference / KU Leuven / imec events** — Leuven is ~36 km out. DATE 2026 (imec) and Neurotech Leaders Forum (23–24 Jun, imec Leuven) are the standouts.
- **OWASP BeNeLux Days** — co-organized by OWASP Belgium, rotates location each year [[14]](https://owasp.org/www-chapter-belgium/).

## Notes on the ring

The 30 km radius from Sint-Martens-Bodegem is a tight constraint: it includes essentially all of metro Brussels and reaches Mechelen at the edge, but **deliberately excludes Leuven, Ghent, and Antwerp**. If the constraint is "drivable for a same-day return after a 19:00 dinner at Agnes", the radius effectively becomes Brussels-only — Mechelen is 30 min by car, the others are 45+ min.
