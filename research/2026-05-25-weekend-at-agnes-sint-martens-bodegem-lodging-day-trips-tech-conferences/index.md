**Verify the dinner before you book anything else.** Agnes's own site lists Saturday as a closed day, with one designated Sunday per month as the only weekend service [[1]](https://agnes.restaurant/en/). The whole expedition presupposes a Saturday evening that may not exist on the public calendar — phone +32 2 880 74 31 before reserving lodging. If the only Saturday options are private-event nights or the once-a-month Sunday slot, the rest of this plan still works; only the day-anchor moves.

**Lodging splits into three tiers, and the choice constrains the rest of the weekend.** Inside the village there are exactly three walkable B&Bs [[2]](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/en/l/2786595/sint-martens-bodegem): chef Thomas Locus's own **Louis 1924** (8 rooms, €550 dinner-and-stay bundle for two, shuttle to the restaurant [[3]](https://agnes.restaurant/en/overnight-stay/)[[4]](https://brasseriejulie.be/over-ons/)); the four-room **De Onsemhoeve** (4.9/5 TripAdvisor, ~1 km walk, Michelin Guide listed [[5]](https://www.onsemhoeve.be/)[[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g6735910-d3331492-Reviews-B_B_Onsemhoeve-Sint_Martens_Bodegem_Flemish_Brabant_Province.html)); and the same **B&B Bodegem** that the character-lodging angle frames as the "walk-back wellness" option but that sits at 2.6/5 with cleanliness complaints [[7]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g6735910-d11461249-Reviews-B_B_Bodegem-Sint_Martens_Bodegem_Flemish_Brabant_Province.html). The two lodging angles agree: pick Louis 1924 for the package, Onsemhoeve for the shortest walk, B&B Bodegem only as a fallback. Outside the village, **Waer Waters** (Groot-Bijgaarden, ~6 km) is the strongest hotel-grade taxi-back option [[8]](https://www.waerwaters.com/en/hotel-eng), and **Villa Servais** in Halle (~17 km) is the architectural set-piece [[9]](https://www.villaservais.be/?lang=en).

**Lodging villages double as Sunday day-trip anchors.** Picking up the activities child's brief to reconsider every village surfaced: Gaasbeek (Trionfo, Het Verblijf, De Windheer) is at the gate of [Kasteel van Gaasbeek](https://www.kasteelvangaasbeek.be/en/opening-hours) — open Tue–Sun with a family Arnoldus-the-Mouse show through May 2026 [[10]](https://www.kasteelvangaasbeek.be/en/present-1). Beersel and Lembeek host **3 Fonteinen** and **Boon**, the lambic stops [[11]](https://www.3fonteinen.be/en/lambikodroom/)[[12]](https://boon.be/en). Halle (Villa Servais) is 200 m from a station that puts Brussels 15 min away [[9]](https://www.villaservais.be/?lang=en). A Sunday loop of 3 Fonteinen at 13:00 → Gaasbeek by 14:30 → back for departure is the cleanest single-day move. **Avoid driving into Brussels on Sun 31 May 2026** — the 20 km door Brussel race starts 10:00 at Jubelpark with city-wide closures [[13]](https://www.20kmdebruxelles.be/nl/).

**Calendar collision.** The activities child treats 30–31 May 2026 as the target weekend; the tech child shows that **Cybersec Europe (20–21 May)** and **AI Summit Brussels (26 May)** sit the week *before*, not on it [[14]](https://www.cyberseceurope.com/artikelen/cybersec-europe-on-20-and-21-may-2026-first-speakers-announced)[[15]](https://aisummitbrussels.com/). For a conference-anchored variant, the FOSDEM weekend (31 Jan – 1 Feb 2026) is the obvious pivot [[16]](https://fosdem.org/2026/) — same village, same lodging shortlist, different season.

The strongest single recommendation, contingent on Saturday actually being open: **Louis 1924's €550 dinner-and-stay package** [[3]](https://agnes.restaurant/en/overnight-stay/), with the chef's own bike/Vespa rental for a Sunday Pajottenland loop [[17]](https://www.charmio.com/en/bed-breakfast/bb-louis-1924-sint-martens-bodegem). It collapses the dinner reservation, the walk-back problem, and the Sunday-activity logistics into a single booking call.
