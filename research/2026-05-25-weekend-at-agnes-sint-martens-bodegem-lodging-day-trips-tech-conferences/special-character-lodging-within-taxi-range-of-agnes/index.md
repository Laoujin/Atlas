## The anchor

[Agnes by Thomas Locus](https://agnes.restaurant/en/) sits at Processiestraat 3, 1700 Sint-Martens-Bodegem — a historic village inside Dilbeek municipality, Flemish Brabant [[2]](https://agnes.restaurant/en/) [[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sint-Martens-Bodegem). It earned one MICHELIN star in the 2026 Belgium guide and occupies a restored former presbytery, modern French, €€€ [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/vlaams-brabant/sint-martens-bodegem/restaurant/agnes-1207215) [[21]](https://www.gaultmillau.be/en/restaurants/agnes-dilbeek-sint-martens-bodegem) [[22]](https://www.visit-dilbeek.be/agnes). The village is on the western edge of Brussels, surrounded by the rolling Pajottenland — the area's lodging is small, character-led B&Bs in restored farmhouses, equestrian estates and castle parks rather than chain hotels.

Working cap for "taxi range": **≤ 20 km / ≤ 25 min by car at night** — comfortable for the post-dinner ride home (Belgian night taxis run ~€2/km plus base, so a 20 km hop is roughly €45 one-way).

## Pick-by-pick

### Walk-back option — [B&B Bodegem](https://www.bbbodegem.be/) ⚠

Adults-only wellness B&B in the same village as Agnes (Bruine-Lieveheerstraat 7, ~1 km north of Processiestraat). Indoor pool, sauna, hot tub; 3 rooms; ~$120/night [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g6735910-d11461249-Reviews-B_B_Bodegem-Sint_Martens_Bodegem_Flemish_Brabant_Province.html) [[6]](http://wellness.bbbodegem.be/). The catch: TripAdvisor rating sits at **2.6/5** with multiple guests flagging sheet/cleanliness issues, while Bedandbreakfast.eu shows a milder 7.9/10 [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g6735910-d11461249-Reviews-B_B_Bodegem-Sint_Martens_Bodegem_Flemish_Brabant_Province.html) [[5]](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/en/a/HU7rJcF5JlBU/bb-bodegem). Pick only if walking home from Agnes outweighs the review risk — and inspect the room on arrival.

### Best wellness + hotel-grade rooms — [Waer Waters spa Hotel](https://www.waerwaters.com/en/hotel-eng)

Rodenberg 21, Groot-Bijgaarden (Dilbeek) — about 6 km / 10-min taxi. 4★, adults-only (21+), 27 rooms with heated floors and Smart TVs, six pools, 12 000 m² wellness garden, two restaurants [[7]](https://www.waerwaters.com/en/hotel-eng) [[8]](https://www.expedia.ie/Dilbeek-Hotels-Waer-Waters-Spa-Hotel.h46279590.Hotel-Information). The strongest pure-hotel option in taxi range; → book ahead, walk-ins routinely turned away at peak.

### Architectural set-piece — [Villa Servais](https://www.villaservais.be/?lang=en)

Servaislaan 8, 1500 Halle (~17 km / 22 min). Neo-Palladian villa designed in 1847 by Jean-Pierre Cluysenaar for cellist Adrien François Servais; bas-reliefs by Cyprien Godebski (1864); empty 1981–2016, then restored room-by-room by Maud and Geert de Poorter [[9]](https://www.villaservais.be/?lang=en) [[10]](https://www.visithalle.be/en/villa-servais) [[11]](https://brusselsmorning.com/villa-servais-geert-de-poorter-revives-19th-century-cellists-legacy-in-halle/60884/). Eight rooms; the first-floor Ernest Van Dijck library looks over the Halle Basilica [[9]](https://www.villaservais.be/?lang=en). 200 m from Halle station — Sunday-morning train to Brussels if you're combining cities.

### Castle stay — [B&B T'Rest at Kasteel Ter Rijst](https://t-rest.be/nl/)

Terrest 2, Heikruis (Pepingen) — the outer edge of taxi range at ~20 km. B&B + bistro inside a neoclassical castle in an English landscape park with reflecting ponds; the venue hosted four weddings for the Belgian TV show "Blind Getrouwd" [[13]](https://t-rest.be/nl/) [[14]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/t-39-rest.html) [[15]](https://www.reisroutes.be/blog/pajottenland-zennevallei/hotels-overnachten-pajottenland-verblijven/). Cheapest "wow" stay on the list (€85+).

### Farmhouse character (Pajottenland triplet)

Three near-identical-in-spirit options 7–10 km south, in the rolling hop-and-hedgerow Pajottenland:

- **[Het Verblijf](https://www.hetverblijf.be/en)** — restored 200-year-old Brabant three-sided farm, walking distance to Gaasbeek Castle [[15]](https://www.reisroutes.be/blog/pajottenland-zennevallei/hotels-overnachten-pajottenland-verblijven/) [[17]](https://www.hetverblijf.be/en).
- **[De Windheer](https://www.dewindheer.be/homeuk)** — 5 rooms (including a suite and a wheelchair-accessible room), draft horse "Prins" out back, ~1 km from Gaasbeek Castle [[18]](https://www.dewindheer.be/homeuk) [[20]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g11205989-d13834011-Reviews-Bed_Breakfast_De_Windheer-Gaasbeek_Lennik_Flemish_Brabant_Province.html).
- **[De Woestijn](https://www.bnbdewoestijn.be/en)** — 3 deluxe rooms in a renovated farmhouse with a heated outdoor pool, regional-product breakfast [[15]](https://www.reisroutes.be/blog/pajottenland-zennevallei/hotels-overnachten-pajottenland-verblijven/) [[19]](https://www.bnbdewoestijn.be/en).

### Horse people — [B&B St-Hubert](https://www.bbsthubert.be/nl/)

Working Friesian-horse manège at Rosweg 68B, Sint-Martens-Lennik (~10 km). 5 rooms named after the resident horses (Mien, Jack, Roos, Julius, Zafiera); outdoor pool, hot tub; from €110 [[12]](https://www.bbsthubert.be/nl/). Pick if a stable view at sunrise is your idea of character.

### Castle-park quiet — [Trionfo](https://www.trionfo.be)

Postweg 3, Lennik (Gaasbeek) — at the gate of Gaasbeek Castle's domain, named for the 1813 triumphal arch Marquis Arconati built as a Napoleon tribute [[16]](https://www.trionfo.be). Outdoor enthusiast / business-stay framing, terrace + e-charging, no pets. Lower-key alternative to Het Verblijf / De Windheer if you want the castle-park location without farmhouse interiors.

## Booking notes

- **Adults-only restrictions** apply at B&B Bodegem and Waer Waters — relevant if you're travelling with kids [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g6735910-d11461249-Reviews-B_B_Bodegem-Sint_Martens_Bodegem_Flemish_Brabant_Province.html) [[8]](https://www.expedia.ie/Dilbeek-Hotels-Waer-Waters-Spa-Hotel.h46279590.Hotel-Information).
- **Pre-book a return taxi** — most of these B&Bs are rural; do not rely on hailing one in Gaasbeek or Heikruis at 23:00.
- **Train alternative** — Villa Servais is 200 m from Halle station, so a Brussels-bound Sunday-morning extension is easy [[9]](https://www.villaservais.be/?lang=en).
