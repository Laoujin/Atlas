## Anchor: where Le Calandre actually is

Le Calandre sits at Via Liguria 1, Sarmeola di Rubano (PD) — three Michelin stars in the 2026 *Italia* guide [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/veneto/rubano/restaurant/le-calandre), run by the Alajmo family and a Relais & Châteaux table [[2]](https://www.relaischateaux.com/us/restaurant/ristorante-le-calandre/). Sarmeola is ~6 km west of Padova's historic centre on the Padova–Vicenza axis — a short taxi to anything in the metro area [[14]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Rubano/Padova). Padova has 24/7 night taxi dispatch via TaxiPadova [[16]](https://taxipadova.it/en/services/night-taxi/), so a late return after a tasting-menu finish is straightforward.

"Taxi range" here means **anywhere within ~20 min, €25 each way** — i.e. Padova old town, Selvazzano/Mestrino to the west, and the Euganean Hills spa towns to the south.

## Picks by character

### Relais Villa Giulia — the closest *villa* stay
[Relais Villa Giulia](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1574991-d2509160-Reviews-Relais_Villa_Giulia-Mestrino_Province_of_Padua_Veneto.html) in Mestrino was built out of "two ancient barns adjacent to a Venetian villa of the '600" — 12 rooms plus two apartments, soundproofed, individually furnished [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1574991-d2509160-Reviews-Relais_Villa_Giulia-Mestrino_Province_of_Padua_Veneto.html). 4.8/5 on TripAdvisor, Travelers' Choice, #1 of 3 small hotels in Mestrino [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1574991-d2509160-Reviews-Relais_Villa_Giulia-Mestrino_Province_of_Padua_Veneto.html). Mestrino sits ~5 km west of Le Calandre on the same Padova–Vicenza axis — *closer to the restaurant than most Padova hotels*, with countryside quiet instead of city noise.

### Belludi 37 — palazzo boutique facing the Basilica
[Belludi 37](https://www.collezionebelludi.it/en/struttura/belludi-37/) — now the flagship of the small Collezione Belludi group [[7]](https://www.collezionebelludi.it/en/struttura/belludi-37/) — is a 16-room boutique between Prato della Valle and the Basilica of St. Anthony [[6]](https://www.oyster.com/veneto/hotels/belludi-37/). Mid-century-inspired furniture, vivid purple rugs, ebony-stained oak floors, coffered ceilings; arrival prosecco and Venetian biscuits, ricotta pie at breakfast [[6]](https://www.oyster.com/veneto/hotels/belludi-37/). Couples score it 9.7 for location; rates from ~€205 [[6]](https://www.oyster.com/veneto/hotels/belludi-37/). ⚠ No elevator [[6]](https://www.oyster.com/veneto/hotels/belludi-37/).

### Majestic Hotel Toscanelli — 1500s bones in the medieval Ghetto
[Majestic Toscanelli](https://www.toscanelli.com/en/) is built into a 1500s structure in the Ghetto, the oldest quarter of Padova's old town, 100 m from Piazza delle Erbe [[8]](https://www.toscanelli.com/en/). The architecture and uneven room shapes carry the character — rooms are individually furnished rather than a unified design statement. ⚠ Sits in a ZTL limited-traffic zone — pre-register the plate if arriving by car [[8]](https://www.toscanelli.com/en/).

### Bellavista Terme — Relais & Châteaux + thermal mud
[Hotel Bellavista Terme](https://www.bellavistaterme.com/en/spa-montegrotto/relais-chateaux-venice) in Montegrotto Terme is one of just four Relais & Châteaux properties in Veneto [[10]](https://www.bellavistaterme.com/en/spa-montegrotto/relais-chateaux-venice). Three thermal pools, fango (thermal-mud) treatments, Euganean Hills setting [[10]](https://www.bellavistaterme.com/en/spa-montegrotto/relais-chateaux-venice) — turns dinner into a weekend, not just a meal. 7.3/10 across 591 verified Booking reviews; staff 8.3, value-for-money the weakest at 6.8 [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g194832-d578468-Reviews-Bellavista_Terme_Resort_Spa-Montegrotto_Terme_Province_of_Padua_Veneto.html). ~15 km south of Sarmeola, ~20 min by taxi.

### Hotel Maccaroni — Alajmo's adjacent property
[Hotel Maccaroni](https://www.italiancollection.com/it/hotel-maccaroni.html) is the Alajmo family's own 34-room hotel "just steps from" Le Calandre, with the Il Calandrino bakery on-site [[3]](https://www.italiancollection.com/it/hotel-maccaroni.html). Modern, funky design — character here is *operational*, not architectural: you skip the taxi entirely. TripAdvisor still lists the same address as *Hotel Le Calandre* (the property's older name), with 34 rooms and basic-but-clean reviews [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1028608-d6614826-Reviews-Hotel_le_Calandre-Rubano_Province_of_Padua_Veneto.html). ⚠ Reviewers note the décor reads dark and breakfast is basic [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1028608-d6614826-Reviews-Hotel_le_Calandre-Rubano_Province_of_Padua_Veneto.html).

### Hotel El Rustego — Rubano's restored manor villa
[Hotel El Rustego](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1028608-d609262-Reviews-Hotel_El_Rustego-Rubano_Province_of_Padua_Veneto.html) at Via A. Rossi 16, Rubano, was created from the restoration of a 19th-century manor villa; rustic architecture, internal restaurant, ~2 km from Le Calandre on the Padova–Vicenza road [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1028608-d609262-Reviews-Hotel_El_Rustego-Rubano_Province_of_Padua_Veneto.html). Less polished than Villa Giulia — trades polish for proximity and a *villa-feel* address inside Rubano itself.

### Hotel Donatello — Basilica view at a lower price point
[Hotel Donatello](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g187867-d237363-Reviews-Hotel_Donatello-Padua_Province_of_Padua_Veneto.html) is a 19th-century building directly across from the Basilica of St. Anthony, 44 rooms, some with Basilica views from the window [[13]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g187867-d237363-Reviews-Hotel_Donatello-Padua_Province_of_Padua_Veneto.html). Character comes from the view and the address, not the room interiors — bathrooms read dated [[13]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g187867-d237363-Reviews-Hotel_Donatello-Padua_Province_of_Padua_Veneto.html). Good fit if you want to put the budget into the tasting menu.

### Methis Hotel & Spa — modern design + spa
[Methis](https://www.methishotel.it/en/hotel-methis) is a 4-star design hotel on the Naviglio canal at the edge of Padova's historic centre [[9]](https://www.methishotel.it/en/hotel-methis). Floors themed by the four elements — earth on the ground floor up to air on the fourth — with an in-house spa, sauna, and Turkish bath [[9]](https://www.methishotel.it/en/hotel-methis). Less *historical* character, more *designed* character — fits if you'd rather have a spa than a palazzo.

## Verdict by trip-shape

- **"Make the weekend a destination":** Bellavista Terme — Relais & Châteaux, thermal spa, dinner is the centrepiece, not the whole trip.
- **"I want the villa fantasy":** Relais Villa Giulia — closest restored Venetian villa, near-zero taxi.
- **"Walk into Padova in the morning, taxi 12 min to dinner":** Belludi 37 (palazzo, near Basilica) or Majestic Toscanelli (1500s, near Piazza delle Erbe).
- **"Don't make me think about transport":** Hotel Maccaroni — Alajmo property, walk to dinner.
- **"Character on a budget":** Hotel Donatello — 19th-c address on St Anthony's Basilica, lower rates than the boutiques.
