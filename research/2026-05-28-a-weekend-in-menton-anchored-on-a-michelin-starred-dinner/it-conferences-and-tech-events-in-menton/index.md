> **TL;DR.** Menton is a tech-conference desert — its Palais de l'Europe books medical and scientific congresses (JAS, PILM, ICQC), not IT [[1]](https://www.menton-congres.com/). But it's a 10-minute train ride from Monaco and ~35 minutes from Nice, so you can base a weekend in Menton and step out for [Ready For IT](https://www.ready-for-it.com/en) in Monaco (2–4 Jun) [[5]](https://www.grimaldiforum.com/en/events-schedule-monaco/ready-for-it-7), [WAIB Summit](https://www.waibsummit.com/) (9–10 Jun) [[7]](https://www.waibsummit.com/), [WAICF](https://www.worldaicannes.com/) in Cannes (12–13 Feb) [[9]](https://www.worldaicannes.com/), [Riviera DEV](https://www.rivieradev.fr/) in Sophia Antipolis (6–8 Jul) [[12]](https://www.rivieradev.fr/) or the ETSI conferences year-round [[14]](https://www.etsi.org/events/2645-etsi-security-conference-oct2026/).

## What's actually in Menton

Almost nothing tech. The town's main convention venue is the [Palais de l'Europe](https://www.menton-congres.com/), a 3,000 m² Belle Époque hall that books mostly medical and scientific congresses — quantum chemistry (ICQC), lymphoma imaging (PILM), hospital architecture (JAS), and similar — none of which are IT or developer-focused [[1]](https://www.menton-congres.com/). The flagship recurring event there in 2026 is the [Journées de l'Architecture en Santé](https://www.ja-sante.fr/) (12–14 October), aimed at architects and hospital engineers [[2]](https://www.ja-sante.fr/) [[3]](https://www.architecture-hospitaliere.fr/blog/2025/10/03/les-journees-de-larchitecture-en-sante%E2%80%89-cap-sur-menton-pour-une-7e-edition-tres-attendue/). [Sciences Po Menton](https://www.sciencespo.fr/college/en/campus-life/campus/menton/)'s campus does run public conferences, but it specialises in Middle East / Mediterranean politics — useful if that's your beat, irrelevant if you want IT [[4]](https://www.sciencespo.fr/college/en/campus-life/campus/menton/).

If you came to Menton expecting a software event, you've come to the wrong town. If you came to Menton for the gardens, the lemons, or [Mirazur](https://www.mirazur.fr/), you can still bolt on a Riviera tech day-trip without moving hotels.

## The tech events reachable from a Menton base (2026)

## How to pick by visit window

- **Going in February.** Anchor on WAICF in Cannes (12–13 Feb) [[9]](https://www.worldaicannes.com/) or the ETSI AI & Data Conference in Sophia Antipolis the same week (9–11 Feb) [[15]](https://www.etsi.org/events/2591-etsi-ai-data-conference-2026/). Generalist AI crowd → WAICF; standards/telco/infra crowd → ETSI.
- **Going in April.** Three back-to-back: ETSI UCAAT (14–16) [[16]](https://www.etsi.org/events/2540-2026-04-12th-ucaat-user-conference-on-advanced-automated-testing/), WAiFF (21–22) [[10]](https://en.palaisdesfestivals.com/offers/world-artificial-intelligence-film-festival-waiff-cannes-en-3686586/), and CUG (26–30) [[11]](https://cug.org/cug-2026/) — pick by interest, not timing.
- **Going in June.** Two days apart in Monaco: Ready For IT (2–4) [[5]](https://www.grimaldiforum.com/en/events-schedule-monaco/ready-for-it-7) for an enterprise-IT decision-maker week, WAIB Summit (9–10) [[7]](https://www.waibsummit.com/) for Web3/AI/digital-assets. Either is a same-day round-trip from Menton via the 11-minute coastal train [[18]](https://www.thetrainline.com/en-us/train-times/monaco-monte-carlo-to-menton).
- **Going in early July.** Azur Tech Summer (2 Jul) [[13]](https://www.telecom-valley.fr/azurtechsummer/) and Riviera DEV (6–8 Jul) [[12]](https://www.rivieradev.fr/) are both at SKEMA Sophia Antipolis — bundle them into one Sophia mini-trip with a hotel in Antibes, since the bus connection from Nice eats time twice a day [[21]](https://moovitapp.com/index/en/public_transit-Sophia_Antipolis-Nice-site_197815420-3260).
- **Going in October.** ETSI Security (19–22) [[14]](https://www.etsi.org/events/2645-etsi-security-conference-oct2026/) and IASP World Conf (27–30) [[17]](https://www.iasp.ws/activities/news/iasp-s-world%E2%80%AFconference%E2%80%AF2026%E2%80%AFwill-be-in-sophia-antipolis-in-france-) bracket the month; JAS at the Palais de l'Europe (12–14) [[2]](https://www.ja-sante.fr/) is the only Menton-native option but is a healthcare-architecture audience, not IT.
- **Going at any other time.** Nothing major lines up — fall back to the year-round options below or accept that this trip isn't a conference trip.

## Year-round / lower-commitment options

- **[French Tech Côte d'Azur](https://www.frenchtechcotedazur.fr/evenement/)** publishes a rolling calendar of pitches, meetups and community evenings across Nice and Sophia [[23]](https://www.frenchtechcotedazur.fr/evenement/). Best single page to check before any visit.
- **[Telecom Valley](https://www.telecom-valley.fr/contenu-sophiaconf/)** runs SophiaConf (open-source, ~1,000 attendees, summer) plus year-round "Tech Events" workshops at SophiaTech [[24]](https://www.telecom-valley.fr/contenu-sophiaconf/).
- **[French Riviera Software Craftsmanship Community](https://www.meetup.com/fr-FR/Riviera-SCC/)** on Meetup runs evening sessions on TDD, DDD, clean code — small, technical, in Nice/Sophia [[22]](https://www.meetup.com/fr-FR/Riviera-SCC/).
- **Monaco's standalone AI conference circuit** (2025 saw an AI Summit at the Principality with global speakers) tends to be announced 2–3 months ahead; check [Invest in Côte d'Azur](https://www.investincotedazur.com/) for new editions [[25]](https://www.investincotedazur.com/en/team-nice-cote-dazur-showcases-ai-ecosystem-at-ai-business-tour-and-waicf-2026/).

## One caveat on "based in Menton"

The coastal rail line is fast but it's a single line — last-train-back times tightening around 22:30–23:30 east of Nice will cut into Cannes/Sophia evening receptions. For events that run into the evening (Riviera DEV after-parties, WAIB Summit's Yacht Club dinners), expect to either taxi back (~€80 from Monaco, €150+ from Cannes) or sleep one night at the venue city. WAIB's evening events at Yacht Club de Monaco are invite-only family-office dinners [[8]](https://monacolife.net/waib-summit-returns-to-monte-carlo-for-web3-and-ai-conference/); if you're not on the list, the 11-minute train back to Menton is the right call.
