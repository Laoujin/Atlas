Three sub-topics, one consistent shape: **Menton is base camp, not the activity zone.** The single 3★ in town ([Mirazur](https://www.mirazur.fr/en/), Mauro Colagreco) sets the only fixed point in the weekend; the rest is logistics around it. There is no 2★ anywhere inside the commune — the closest sits a 14-minute drive west at [Hostellerie Jérôme in La Turbie](https://www.hostellerie-jerome.com/) [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/gb/en/provence-alpes-cote-dazur/la-turbie/restaurant/hostellerie-jerome) — and no IT conference uses Menton's [Palais de l'Europe](https://www.menton-congres.com/), which books medical and architectural congresses instead [[2]](https://www.menton-congres.com/).

**The coastal TER line is the through-thread.** Monaco is 10–11 minutes away with 17 trains a day [[3]](https://www.sncf-connect.com/en-en/train/timetables/monaco/menton); Ventimiglia 9 minutes east [[4]](https://www.thetrainline.com/en-us/train-times/menton-to-ventimiglia); Nice ~35 minutes [[5]](https://www.thetrainline.com/en-us/train-times/nice-to-menton); Cannes ~1h09 [[6]](https://www.thetrainline.com/en-us/train-times/cannes-to-menton). That same line connects every interesting layer of the trip — the [Le Corbusier coastal path at Roquebrune-Cap-Martin](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/things-to-do/routes-and-walks/hiking-in-roquebrune-cap-martin-the-cap-martin-coastal-path-roquebrune-cap-martin-en-4346612/) [[7]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/things-to-do/routes-and-walks/hiking-in-roquebrune-cap-martin-the-cap-martin-coastal-path-roquebrune-cap-martin-en-4346612/), the [reopened Jardin Exotique de Monaco](https://www.monaco-tribune.com/en/2026/03/in-pictures-prince-albert-ii-princess-charlene-and-princess-caroline-reopen-monacos-exotic-garden-after-six-years/) [[8]](https://www.monaco-tribune.com/en/2026/03/in-pictures-prince-albert-ii-princess-charlene-and-princess-caroline-reopen-monacos-exotic-garden-after-six-years/), and every tech event reachable from base camp ([Ready For IT](https://www.grimaldiforum.com/en/events-schedule-monaco/ready-for-it-7) 2–4 Jun in Monaco [[9]](https://www.grimaldiforum.com/en/events-schedule-monaco/ready-for-it-7); [WAIB Summit](https://www.waibsummit.com/) 9–10 Jun [[10]](https://www.waibsummit.com/); [WAICF](https://www.worldaicannes.com/) Cannes in February [[11]](https://www.worldaicannes.com/)). The single-line constraint shows up as a hard edge though: last-train-back times east of Nice tighten around 22:30–23:30, so Cannes evening receptions or WAIB yacht-club dinners force either a taxi (~€80–€150) or a one-night displacement.

**Booking discipline is the dominant operational risk.** Mirazur's Universe Menu is €450 at both lunch and dinner with a €450/person no-show fee inside 15 days [[12]](https://www.mirazur.fr/reserver.php?LNG=EN/); the booking system opens future months in batches months ahead [[13]](https://www.mirazur.fr/en/). Three of the six "Jardins Remarquables" are visitable only by phone or online appointment ([Maria Serena](https://www.menton-riviera-merveilles.co.uk/offers/maria-serena-garden-menton-en-3051210/) [[14]](https://www.menton-riviera-merveilles.co.uk/offers/maria-serena-garden-menton-en-3051210/), [Fontana Rosa](https://www.messortiesculture.com/visites-guidees/office-de-tourisme-de-la-ville-de-menton/jardin-fontana-rosa) [[15]](https://www.messortiesculture.com/visites-guidees/office-de-tourisme-de-la-ville-de-menton/jardin-fontana-rosa), [Les Colombières](https://www.lescolombieres.com/) [[16]](https://www.lescolombieres.com/)) and a fourth (Clos du Peyronnet) is Fridays-in-September-only [[17]](https://waterfieldgarden.fr/en/visit/). Plan the dinner first, then back-fill gardens around it — not the other way round.

**Two time-sensitive realities to absorb.** The headline [Musée Jean Cocteau Severin Wunderman is shut](https://www.lejournaldesarts.fr/patrimoine/le-musee-cocteau-ne-rouvrira-pas-avant-plusieurs-annees-177461) — seven years on from Storm Adrian, the mayor's own line is no reopening before the end of the decade [[18]](https://www.lejournaldesarts.fr/patrimoine/le-musee-cocteau-ne-rouvrira-pas-avant-plusieurs-annees-177461), so Cocteau in Menton means [Le Bastion](https://www.museecocteaumenton.fr/) [[19]](https://www.museecocteaumenton.fr/), the Salle des Mariages and the Wunderman rotation at the Palais de l'Europe (closing 27 Aug 2026) [[20]](https://www.menton.fr/exposition-les-chefs-d-oeuvre-du-musee-jean-cocteau-collection-severin.html), not the headline museum. And the [tech calendar is windowed](https://www.frenchtechcotedazur.fr/evenement/): genuine Riviera tech weekends only land in Feb, Apr, Jun, Jul and Oct [[21]](https://www.frenchtechcotedazur.fr/evenement/) — outside those, the conference-bolted version of this weekend doesn't exist and the brief collapses to gardens + dinner.

The sharpest open question: which side of the dinner you put the day-trip on. Mirazur's hillside-and-Italian-border view is the more singular at lunch; the Universe Menu is the same €450 either way [[22]](https://mirazur.bonkdo.com/en/cadeau-gastronomie-table-etoilee-5924), so the trade is daylight-over-the-garden versus a free Saturday for [Sainte-Agnès](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/things-to-do/events/all-events/visit-to-fort-sainte-agnes-maginot-line-military-structure-sainte-agnes-en-5813787/) or the Roquebrune coastal path [[23]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/things-to-do/events/all-events/visit-to-fort-sainte-agnes-maginot-line-military-structure-sainte-agnes-en-5813787/).
