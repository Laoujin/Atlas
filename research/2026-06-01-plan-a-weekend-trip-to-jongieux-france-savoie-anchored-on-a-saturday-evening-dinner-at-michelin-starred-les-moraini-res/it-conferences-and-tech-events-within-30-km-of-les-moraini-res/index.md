> **TL;DR.** Don't plan the trip around a conference. The 30 km bubble around Jongieux contains essentially zero proper IT/tech conferences in 2026 [[7]](https://dev.events/EU/FR/Chambery/it). The two scheduled fixtures both fall in **the same week of April 21-24, 2026**: a free 90-min USMB cybersecurity lecture in Chambéry on Tue April 21 [[8]](https://www.grandchambery.fr/tous-les-evenements/amphis-pour-tous-et-pour-toutes-cybersecurite-comment-les-mathematiques-veillent-sur-vos-donnees), and the SOPHIA PV reliability workshop at INES/Savoie Technolac on April 23-24 (registrations already closed) [[10]](https://www.ines-solaire.org/en/agenda/sophia-pv-reliability-workshop-2026/). The three Chambéry dev-meetup groups (GDG Alps 361 members [[13]](https://www.meetup.com/Google-Developer-Group-Alps/), Developers In The Alps 127 [[14]](https://www.meetup.com/Developers-In-The-Alps/), Dev Tech Chambéry 50 [[15]](https://www.meetup.com/Dev-Tech-Chambery/)) fire irregularly and don't keep a public 2026 calendar. For a real conference, plan a separate trip to Lyon (DevFest, Dec 4 2026 [[19]](https://devfest.gdglyon.com/)) or Paris (Devoxx, Apr 22-24 2026 [[18]](https://www.devoxx.fr/en/)).

The bubble effectively means **Chambéry + Aix-les-Bains + Savoie Technolac**. Annecy is just outside, so anything at LAPP, IMERIR, or in the Haute-Savoie French Tech hub is excluded by the 30 km rule — flagged below for completeness.

## Scheduled 2026 events in the bubble

### 1. USMB cybersecurity lecture — Tue 21 Apr 2026, Chambéry

The only IT-flavoured public event with a published 2026 date inside the radius. Part of [Université Savoie Mont Blanc](https://www.univ-smb.fr/)'s free "Amphis pour Tous" series, which mixes alpine-climate, AI, and pure-math talks across the year [[9]](https://nosalpes.eu/fr/2025/12/29/universite-de-savoie-dans-les-conferences-amphis-2026-entre-climat-alpin-ia-mathematiques/).

- **Title:** *Cybersécurité : comment les mathématiques veillent sur vos données* [[8]](https://www.grandchambery.fr/tous-les-evenements/amphis-pour-tous-et-pour-toutes-cybersecurite-comment-les-mathematiques-veillent-sur-vos-donnees)
- **When:** Tuesday April 21 2026, 18:00–19:30 (90 min)
- **Where:** Présidence USMB, 27 rue Marcoz, 73000 Chambéry (25 km from Les Morainières)
- **Format:** Single talk, two USMB speakers (one from the Électronique-Télécoms-Réseaux dept, one from LAMA maths lab). Free, public, no registration mentioned. Recorded and posted to the USMB YouTube channel afterward [[9]](https://nosalpes.eu/fr/2025/12/29/universite-de-savoie-dans-les-conferences-amphis-2026-entre-climat-alpin-ia-mathematiques/).
- **Vibe:** general-public outreach, elliptic-curve crypto framed for non-specialists. Not a developer conference. Worth it if you're already in town and want a 90-min cultural anchor — not worth planning a weekend around.

### 2. SOPHIA PV Reliability Workshop — Thu-Fri 23-24 Apr 2026, Le Bourget-du-Lac

A research workshop on photovoltaic-module reliability, run inside the [INES CEA](https://www.ines-solaire.org/) campus on Savoie Technolac [[10]](https://www.ines-solaire.org/en/agenda/sophia-pv-reliability-workshop-2026/).

- **When:** April 23-24 2026, two days
- **Where:** INES CEA, 50 avenue Lac Léman, Bâtiment Lynx 3, Technopôle Savoie Technolac, 73375 Le Bourget-du-Lac (15 km from Les Morainières)
- **Status:** ⚠ **Registrations closed — fully booked** as of the page's last update [[10]](https://www.ines-solaire.org/en/agenda/sophia-pv-reliability-workshop-2026/).
- **Vibe:** solar-tech R&D for PV-module reliability specialists. Energy-tech adjacent, not IT — closer in spirit to a materials-science workshop than a dev conference. The Saturday slot (April 25) is therefore free.
- INES's other 2026 dates run mostly outside the bubble (OPEN ENERGIES at Chassieu near Lyon, Intersolar Munich, Saint-Priest storage day, etc.) [[12]](https://www.ines-solaire.org/agenda/).

### 3. INES at ASDER Open-Doors Day — Fri 24 Apr 2026, Chambéry

The same week, INES Formation & Évaluation runs a public-facing presence at ASDER's open-doors day [[11]](https://www.ines-solaire.org/en/agenda/ines-formation-evaluation-participe-a-la-journee-portes-ouvertes-de-lasder-a-chambery).

- **When:** Friday April 24 2026, 11:00–17:00
- **Where:** Maison des Énergies, 124 rue du Bon Vent, 73000 Chambéry (25 km)
- **Format:** Open doors, drop-in, free admission. Energy training + solar career info.
- **Vibe:** career fair / public outreach, not a conference. Useful only if you're stacking with the SOPHIA workshop or the USMB lecture.

### 5. French Tech in the Alps - Chambéry (organization, not a public event series)

The [Chambéry hub](https://www.ftalps.com/territoires/chambery/accueil-chambery/) of French Tech Alpes is one of five territorial hubs (Annecy, Chambéry, Genevois Français, Grenoble, Valence-Romans) supporting 700+ startups across the alpine ridge [[16]](https://www.ftalps.com/territoires/chambery/accueil-chambery/). They run pitch days, financing breakfasts, and the annual French Tech Summit (held in **Annecy**, out of range). Their public Chambéry events page still shows the 2025 programme as of June 2026 — no 2026 calendar published [[17]](https://www.ftalps.com/evenements-chambery/). Newsletter or LinkedIn is the only feed.

## Practical alignment with a Saturday-anchored weekend

The anchor is a **Saturday evening** dinner at Les Morainières. None of the in-bubble fixtures fall on a Saturday in 2026:

- The USMB lecture is a **Tuesday** evening [[8]](https://www.grandchambery.fr/tous-les-evenements/amphis-pour-tous-et-pour-toutes-cybersecurite-comment-les-mathematiques-veillent-sur-vos-donnees).
- SOPHIA is **Thu-Fri** [[10]](https://www.ines-solaire.org/en/agenda/sophia-pv-reliability-workshop-2026/) and full anyway.
- The ASDER open-doors is **Friday** [[11]](https://www.ines-solaire.org/en/agenda/ines-formation-evaluation-participe-a-la-journee-portes-ouvertes-de-lasder-a-chambery).

If you want to stack a tech-event with the dinner: **the week of April 20-25 2026 is the only window with anything pre-scheduled** — arrive Tuesday, catch the USMB lecture, optional ASDER drop-in Friday, then Saturday at Les Morainières. Otherwise treat the bubble as event-free and plan the trip purely on the wine + food angle. The serious-conference detour is DevFest Lyon on **Friday 4 December 2026** — drive ~1 h to Lyon Friday, dinner at Jongieux Saturday, drive home Sunday [[19]](https://devfest.gdglyon.com/).
