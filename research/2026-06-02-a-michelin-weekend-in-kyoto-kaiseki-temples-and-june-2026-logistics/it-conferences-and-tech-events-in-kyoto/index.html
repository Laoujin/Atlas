TL;DR — Kyoto's two flagship tech events are IVS2026 (July 1–3, Japan's largest startup conference, 13,000+ attendees) and BitSummit PUNCH (May 22–24, Japan's largest indie game festival, free public entry).[4][1] For visitors outside those windows, the Kyoto Tech Meetup runs English-friendly morning sessions every week.[12]

Flagship Events

Jul 1–3, 2026

IVS2026

Kyoto Miyakomesse · Hotel Okura Kyoto · ROHM Theatre Kyoto
Startup / Venture
Japan's largest startup conference, now in its 32nd edition. 13,000+ registered participants from ~70 countries.[5] Theme: "Japan is Back."[6]
  • IVS CORE — invite-only, ~1,000 C-suite decision-makers at Hotel Okura, Jul 1–2.[8]
  • LAUNCHPAD — pitch contest finals Jul 3 at ROHM Theatre; winner eligible for ¥10M Startup Kyoto International Award.[7]
  • Startup Market — ~300 startups exhibiting at Miyakomesse.
Tickets: ¥4,980 (student) – ¥39,800 (general).[4]
May 22–24, 2026

BitSummit PUNCH

Miyako Messe (Kyoto International Exhibition Hall)
Indie Gaming Free (public days)
Japan's largest indie game festival, held in Kyoto every year since 2013.[1] Attracted 58,000+ visitors in 2025.[3] Theme: "High Impact."[2]
  • May 22 — Business Day (developers, publishers, media, sponsors).
  • May 23–24 — Public days; free admission.
Attendees: gaming enthusiasts, indie devs, Sony/Nintendo/Valve representatives, VGM composers.

Innovation Infrastructure

Kyoto Research Park (KRP), Japan's first privately operated urban research park (est. 1989), hosts 510 companies and runs ~266 events per year.[15] Its HVC KYOTO startup acceleration programme has backed 194 startups and facilitated ¥76.5 billion in funding as of March 2025, earning the METI Minister's Award at the Japan Open Innovation Prize.[16] KRP does not publish a public event calendar, but its network is a common route into Kyoto's startup community.

Outside Kyoto (Kansai context): The 4th World Congress of AI Industry (WCAI-2026) runs Jun 16–18 in Osaka — a 30-min shinkansen ride — covering AI research and industry matchmaking.[18]
