Upcoming Highlights

Riviera DEV 2026 [1]

Jul 6–8 · SKEMA Business School, Sophia Antipolis · ~700 attendees
The flagship local developer conference. Day 1 (Jul 6): intensive 3-hour deep dives and hands-on coding labs. Days 2–3: keynotes, 50-min tech talks, 25-min quick talks, and signature "We're not just coders" workshops. Quarkus dedicated track. Evening beach dinner (Jul 6) and cocktail buffet with live music (Jul 7) included in registration. Tickets via Billetweb.[2]

Azur Tech Summer [7]

Jul 2 · SKEMA Business School, Sophia Antipolis · €100 day / €50 eve
4th edition. One day of keynotes, panels, and roundtables for tech executives and IT directors across all verticals (healthcare, tourism, industry). AI & data, cybersecurity, sustainable digital, organisational transformation, e-tourism. Evening aperitif from 5:30 PM. Organised by Telecom Valley; members get promo codes.

ETSI Security Conference 2026 [4]

Oct 19–22 · ETSI HQ, Valbonne – Sophia Antipolis · Free
20th edition. 4-day programme for government, standards bodies, academia, and industry. Topics: quantum-safe cryptography, AI security, 5G→6G, consumer device regulation, fraud and standards convergence. Attendees typically from 40+ countries. Submission deadline was May 15; registration open.

SophI.A Summit [9]

~Nov 2026 (date TBA) · Sophia Antipolis area · Annual
Annual international AI conference by Université Côte d'Azur / 3IA Côte d'Azur. 8th edition (Nov 2025) was at Mouratoglou Hotel & Resort, Biot. Covers AI in biology, healthcare, mobility, and smart systems. 2026 date listed as "TBA" — watch univ-cotedazur.eu.

Just Past (Annual — Worth Tracking for 2027)

WAICF — World AI Cannes Festival [6]

Feb 12–13, 2026 (past) · Palais des Festivals, Cannes · ~40 min from Nice
Europe's largest AI festival: 10 k+ professional visitors, 320 speakers, 220 exhibitors. Not in Nice, but the local tech community attends en masse — Team Nice Côte d'Azur ran an AI Business Tour for 50+ international decision-makers the day before.[15] Annual; next edition February 2027.

ETSI AI & Data Conference [5]

Feb 9–11, 2026 (past) · ETSI HQ, Sophia Antipolis · Annual
3rd edition. Focused on the intersection of AI standardisation and data governance: EU AI Act, Data Act, agentic AI, data spaces. Speakers from OECD, IDSA, telecom standards bodies. Free. Annually in February; proposals for 2027 typically due in autumn.
