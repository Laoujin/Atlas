1. Picos de Europa highlights within 30 km of Arriondas: Covadonga Sanctuary, Lakes of Covadonga (Enol and Ercina), Bulnes funicular — access, shuttle bus rules, hiking, what to see and do.
2. Sella river canoe descent and other adventure activities (kayaking, rafting, canyoning, horseback) launching from Arriondas.
3. Cangas de Onís and the cultural/prehistoric belt: Roman bridge with Victory Cross, Tito Bustillo cave in Ribadesella (UNESCO), Iglesia de Santa Cruz.
4. Coastal day-trips and hidden beaches within 30 km: Ribadesella, Playa de Gulpiyuri, Playa de San Antolín, Cuevas del Mar, Playa de Vega.
5. Scenic drives and viewpoints: Mirador del Fitu, Mirador de la Reina, Mirador del Sueve, the AS-260 corkscrew up to the Lakes.
6. Asturian gastronomy beyond Casa Marcial: cider houses (sidrerías) in Nava and around Arriondas, Cabrales blue-cheese cave in Tielve/Arenas de Cabrales, regional food markets and tastings.
