> **Decision:** With one full day, pair the **Sella canoe descent** (morning, 3–4 h) with the **Lakes of Covadonga** via the mandatory shuttle (afternoon). With half a day, drive the **Mirador del Fitu** loop and lunch in Cangas de Onís. In bad weather, swap to the **Tito Bustillo cave** in Ribadesella plus the **Covadonga sanctuary**. The single biggest planning gotcha: from 1 June to 18 October 2026 **private cars are banned all day** on the road to the Lakes [[1]](https://en.asturias.com/covadonga-lakes-plan/) — book the shuttle in Cangas de Onís.

Casa Marcial sits in La Salgar, ~5 km from Arriondas, so all distances below are anchored to Arriondas. The 30 km radius covers the western Picos de Europa, the Sella valley, and the coast from Ribadesella to Cuevas del Mar. A few must-do items (Bulnes funicular, Cabrales cheese cave, Nava cider museum) sit **just outside** the radius — they're flagged inline so you can decide.

## The Sella canoe descent — the headline activity

Arriondas brands itself the *capital de la piragua*; the canoe descent down the Sella to Ribadesella is the default reason most visitors come. Two routes, one ritual.

| ---------------------------------------- | ------ | --------- | ----- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Mini Sella → Toraño bridge][20]         |  8 km  | 1.5–2 h   | €35   | €25         | Canoe, paddle, life vest, dry barrel, return van           |
| [Full descent → Llovio/Omedina][20]      | 14 km  | 3.5–4 h   | €35   | €25         | Same + hot showers; must exit river by 18:00               |
| [International Descent day (8 Aug 2026)][21] | 14 km  | —         | €45   | —           | Race-day rental surcharge                                  |

Standard retail across operators converges at **€35 adult / €25 child** [[10]](https://www.aipolaventura.com/)[[11]](https://fronteraverde.com/actividades/descenso-rio-sella-en-canoa/). Groups slash that: [TurAventura](https://www.turaventura.com/en/prices/) charges €25/person for 1–10 paddlers and drops to €17–€18 for groups of 40+ [[12]](https://www.turaventura.com/en/prices/). Minimum age is 5, minimum height 1.15 m, swimming ability is legally required [[10]](https://www.aipolaventura.com/). Picnic adds ~€5 (Asturian) or €7 (vegetarian/GF). Pickup vans run continuously from 14:00 at the take-out [[11]](https://fronteraverde.com/actividades/descenso-rio-sella-en-canoa/).

The **88th [Descenso Internacional del Sella][22]** falls on **Saturday 8 August 2026, 12:00 cannon-start from the Arriondas bridge** — a *Fiesta de Interés Turístico Internacional* with the *Tren Fluvial* spectator train running alongside the river, regional anthem at the start line, prize ceremony 17:00 in Ribadesella and a *fabada* + *arroz con leche* meal at Campos de la Oba [[13]](https://www.descensodelsella.es/descenso-internacional-sella-2026/)[[14]](https://www.k2aventura.com/blog/descenso-internacional-del-sella-2026/). Watch from the riverbanks; rental canoes that day are €45 [[11]](https://fronteraverde.com/actividades/descenso-rio-sella-en-canoa/).

## Lakes of Covadonga + Covadonga Sanctuary — read the shuttle rules first

The two lakes (Enol and Ercina) sit at ~29 km from Arriondas [[8]](https://www.distanciasentre.com/distancia-entre-arriondas-y-lagos-de-covadonga-parque-nacional-de-los-picos-de-europa/DistanciaHistoria/524128.aspx). **The decisive 2026 logistics fact:**

> ⚠ The CO-4 road to the Lakes is **closed to private cars 00:00–24:00 every day from 1 June to 18 October 2026**, plus Easter (Mar 28 – Apr 12), every weekend in May, May 1, and the bridge days Oct 24–25, Oct 31 and Nov 1–2 [[1]](https://en.asturias.com/covadonga-lakes-plan/). Only reduced-mobility passes and refuge guests drive up.

**The Plan Lagos shuttle:** park at Cangas de Onís (P1, 419 spaces), El Repelao (P4) or El Peregrino (P5) for €2/day; ALSA buses every 30 min, €9 round-trip adult / €3.50 child, first bus 8:00, last ascent ~17:45 in summer [[1]](https://en.asturias.com/covadonga-lakes-plan/).

**What to do once you're up:** the **[PR-PNPE 2 Lakes loop][29]** from the Buferrera car park — 5 km / 2.5 h (or 3 km short version), only ~50 m of climb, past two viewpoints, the old [Buferrera mines][30], Lake Ercina and Lake Enol [[2]](https://www.miteco.gob.es/en/parques-nacionales-oapn/red-parques-nacionales/parques-nacionales/picos-europa/guia-visitante/ruta-lagos-covadonga.html)[[3]](https://www.turismoasturias.es/en/senderismo/rutas/familias/ruta-de-los-lagos-de-covadonga). Low difficulty, fits in the shuttle window.

[29]: https://www.miteco.gob.es/en/parques-nacionales-oapn/red-parques-nacionales/parques-nacionales/picos-europa/guia-visitante/ruta-lagos-covadonga.html
[30]: https://www.turismoasturias.es/en/senderismo/rutas/familias/ruta-de-los-lagos-de-covadonga

**Combine with the [Sanctuary of Covadonga][31]** (~17 km, free) on the way down or up: the basilica, the Santa Cueva chapel above a waterfall holding the tombs of Pelayo and other Asturian royalty, the Pelayo monument and a small museum [[28]](https://www.moonhoneytravel.com/sanctuary-of-covadonga-spain/). Open 365 days; basilica roughly 09:00–13:30 and 15:30–19:00 outside Mass [[4]](https://santuariodecovadonga.es/horarios/)[[5]](https://xn--cangasdeons-xcb.com/como-visitar-la-basilica-de-covadonga/).

[31]: https://santuariodecovadonga.es/horarios/

**The [Mirador de la Reina][33] is on the same road**, 910 m altitude, ~8 km up the CO-4 from the basilica [[49]](https://cangas-de-onis.vivirasturias.com/entorno-natural/i/58718486/mirador-la-reina). On regulated days the shuttle **does not stop there** — your options are: take a taxi from Cangas (~€10/adult round trip, will halt on request), cycle the CO-4 (warning: 12–14% sustained grades), or visit outside regulated periods [[48]](https://www.kevmrc.com/el-mirador-de-la-reina-asturias-spain)[[51]](https://bikerfriendly.es/negocio/mirador-de-la-reina/).

## Cangas de Onís — Roman bridge, dolmen, Sunday market

7 km from Arriondas, this is the natural lunch + history stop on the way to or from Covadonga. The headline sights:

- **[The "Roman" bridge][34]** is actually a 14th-century medieval span built under Alfonso XI of Castile across the Sella; the replica Victory Cross was hung from the central arch in 1939 after the Virgin of Covadonga returned from Paris [[25]](https://en.asturias.com/Roman-bridge-of-Cangas-de-On%C3%ADs/).
- **[Iglesia de la Santa Cruz][35]** — 8th-century pre-Romanesque royal foundation consecrated 27 October 737 by King Favila that once held Pelayo's *Cruz de la Victoria*; rebuilt in 1632 and again in 1950 after Civil War destruction [[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iglesia_de_la_Santa_Cruz_(Cangas_de_On%C3%ADs)).
- **[The Santa Cruz dolmen][36]** sits inside the church crypt — engraved Neolithic orthostats. Tickets via the Cangas tourist office, €2, ~10–15 min visit, closed Mondays/Tuesdays, last entry 13:00, guided slots Easter and summer [[27]](https://yacimientos.asturias.es/dolmen-de-santa-cruz).
- **[Sunday market][37]** every week ~09:30–13:30 around the Iglesia de Santa María: artisan Cabrales, Gamoneu and Beyos cheeses, *fabes*, honey, cured meats — a 200-year tradition and one of the most important markets in the Picos region [[60]](https://www.turismocangasdeonis.com/mercado-de-cangas-de-onis.html).

**Ribadesella town itself** is worth the stop — old-town and beach atmospheres on either side of the Sella estuary, with cider bars and seafood restaurants [[35]](https://www.kevmrc.com/ribadesella-asturias-spain). Park at the eastern end of Naves off the A-8, then walk 400 m [[32]](https://www.inspain.org/en/asturias/llanes/beaches/gulpiyuri/).

## Tito Bustillo cave — the rainy-day pick, book ahead

The [Tito Bustillo cave][45] in Ribadesella is on the UNESCO *Cave Art of Northern Spain* list and one of the rare painted caves still open to the public. 2026 window: **4 March – 30 October, Wed–Sun, first tour 10:15, last 17:00, closed Mondays/Tuesdays and 8–9 August**, 15 per slot, 150/day cap [[21]](https://www.centrotitobustillo.com/en/visita-la-cueva-de-tito-bustillo).

- **Tickets:** €4.14 general, €2.12 reduced, **free on Wednesdays** [[22]](https://en.asturias.com/tito-bustillo-cave/). Tickets for visits from 1 July go on sale **1 April 2026** — expect them to sell out for peak weekends [[22]](https://en.asturias.com/tito-bustillo-cave/)[[29]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g660742-d3728534-Reviews-Cueva_de_Tito_Bustillo-Ribadesella_Asturias.html).
- **Language:** guided tours run only in Spanish; English audio guides and a QR-code English tour are provided on the path [[23]](https://chchcamino.net/tito-bustillo-caves-ribadesella/).
- **Arrive 30 minutes early** at the [Centro de Arte Rupestre][46] reception or you lose your slot [[24]](https://www.centrotitobustillo.com/en/visita-centro-arte-rupestre).
- **Backup:** if the cave is sold out, the on-site Centro de Arte Rupestre museum is bilingual and tells most of the story [[23]](https://chchcamino.net/tito-bustillo-caves-ribadesella/)[[24]](https://www.centrotitobustillo.com/en/visita-centro-arte-rupestre).

## Mirador del Fitu + Pico Pienzu — the easiest panorama in Asturias

[Mirador del Fitu][50] sits at 597 m on the AS-260 between Arriondas and Colunga, ~11 km from each [[44]](https://en.asturias.com/mirador-del-fitu/). The 1927 concrete watchtower hands you a 360° sweep over the Sueve, Ponga, Picos de Europa and the Cantabrian coast — Galicia to Vizcaya on clear days [[44]](https://en.asturias.com/mirador-del-fitu/). Free, 24/7, with a small lot at the platform and a larger gravel overflow ~100 m further up near the telecom antennas; closes only in snow [[45]](https://www.sellavirtual.com/en/mirador-del-fitu-panoramic-views-of-the-picos-de-europa-and-the-asturian-coast/).

[50]: https://en.asturias.com/mirador-del-fitu/

Stronger legs: the **PR-AS 71 trail to [Pico Pienzu][51]** (1,159 m, highest in the Sueve — this is what older guidebooks sometimes call the "Mirador del Sueve") starts at the Fitu car park — 11.73 km out-and-back, ~700 m gain, 3–5 h, graded *Dura* [[46]](https://www.turismoasturias.es/en/descubre/naturaleza/rutas/senderismo/ruta-al-pico-pienzu). The summit lays Gijón, Ribadesella, Colunga and Villaviciosa out below with the Picos behind [[50]](https://www.asturiasvacations.com/blog-asturias-vacations/mirador-del-fitu-amp-picu-pienzu-asturias-most-spectacular-360-views). Up on the pastures: dark herds of **Asturcón**, a four-foot ancient pony, still roam free [[47]](https://en.asturias.com/the-sierra-del-sueve/).

[51]: https://www.turismoasturias.es/en/descubre/naturaleza/rutas/senderismo/ruta-al-pico-pienzu

## Asturian food and cider — within the radius and just outside

In the radius:

- **[Cangas de Onís Sunday market][37]** — covered above, the best in-radius pick for Cabrales / Gamoneu / Beyos cheese and *fabes* [[60]](https://www.turismocangasdeonis.com/mercado-de-cangas-de-onis.html).
- **The cider-pour ritual (*escanciado*)** — natural cider is poured from above the head so the stream hits the rim of the glass, aerating to release CO₂ and aromas; the resulting *culín* (~100 ml) is drunk in one go before the foam dies [[56]](https://www.sidraturismoasturias.es/nuestra-sidra/escanciado/)[[62]](https://www.turismoasturias.es/en/-/blogs/guia-para-disfrutar-de-la-sidra-asturiana). ## The Bulnes funicular — outside the radius but worth flagging

[Bulnes][70] sits ~44 km / 48 min from Arriondas [[9]](https://www.calcularruta.com/de-arriondas-a-poncebos.html), past Arenas de Cabrales, so it falls **outside the 30 km brief** — but it's the single most famous half-day from this area, so:

- **Funicular** runs every 30 min, ~7 minutes to climb 400 m over 2,227 m of track [[6]](https://blog.telecable.es/funicular-bulnes/).
- **Price:** €17.61 one-way / €22.16 round-trip adult, €4.32/€6.71 children 4–12 [[6]](https://blog.telecable.es/funicular-bulnes/).
- **What's up there:** Bulnes splits into Bulnes de Abajo and Bulnes de Arriba (El Castillo, 600 m higher); the upper village is where you get the cleanest view of the [Naranjo de Bulnes / Picu Urriellu (2,519 m)][71] [[7]](https://en.asturias.com/bulnes-funicular/).
- **Free alternative:** the Canal del Texu footpath from Poncebos walks you up the same canyon [[7]](https://en.asturias.com/bulnes-funicular/).

## Two suggested day-shapes anchored to a Saturday Casa Marcial dinner

**Active full day (Sat):** 09:30 Sella canoe descent → 14:00 lunch in Cangas de Onís → 16:00 Mirador del Fitu drive → 21:00 dinner Casa Marcial. Skip the Lakes shuttle (won't fit; do Sunday instead).

**Cultural / scenic full day (Sat):** 10:30 shuttle from Cangas to the Lakes → PR-PNPE 2 loop → 14:30 down to Covadonga sanctuary → 16:30 Cangas de Onís bridge + dolmen → 21:00 Casa Marcial.

**Sunday recovery:** Cangas Sunday market → drive to Ribadesella → Tito Bustillo cave (book in advance) → Santa Marina beach → Cuevas del Mar on the way back.
