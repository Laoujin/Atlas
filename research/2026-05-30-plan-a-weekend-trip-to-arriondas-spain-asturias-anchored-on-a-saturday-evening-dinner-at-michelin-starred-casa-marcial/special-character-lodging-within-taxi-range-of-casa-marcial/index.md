> **Decision:** Book **[Narbasu](https://www.narbasu.com/en/)** if dates allow — Casa Marcial's own sister-property, a 14th-c. palace 20 min away with a Michelin Green-Star restaurant on-site [[3]](https://casamarcial.es/en/manzano/) [[5]](https://guide.michelin.com/gb/en/principado-de-asturias/cereceda/restaurant/narbasu). If sold out: **[Puebloastur](https://www.puebloastur.com/en)** is the closest serious sleep (~3 km from the dining room) and the only 5★ Gran Lujo in the region [[7]](https://www.puebloastur.com/en) [[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1079283-d10070323-Reviews-Puebloastur_Eco_Resort_Wellness_Spa-Cofino_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html). **[Hotel Casona del Sella](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g608995-d578140-Reviews-Hotel_Casona_Del_Sella-Arriondas_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html)** is the value pick — a 14-room 1923 Indiano in central Arriondas at €90–€130/night [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g608995-d578140-Reviews-Hotel_Casona_Del_Sella-Arriondas_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html).

[Casa Marcial](https://casamarcial.es/en/) sits in the hamlet of La Salgar, ~3 km outside Arriondas (Parres) [[1]](https://www.oadguides.com/restaurant/casa-marcial). It has no rooms of its own and points diners at Narbasu [[2]](https://casamarcial.es/en/casa/). Every pick below is within a ~25-minute taxi; an Arriondas↔Cereceda hop is roughly 9 min and €20–€25 [[6]](https://www.rome2rio.com/es/s/Arriondas/Cereceda-Asturias-Espa%C3%B1a).

## The picks, with reasons

### Narbasu — the obvious choice
Esther and Nacho Manzano's own 23-room hotel inside the 14th-c. Palacio de Rubianes at Cereceda (Piloña), ~13 km east of La Salgar [[3]](https://casamarcial.es/en/manzano/) [[4]](https://www.narbasu.com/en/hotel/). The on-site restaurant holds a Michelin Green Star — the sustainable-Asturian sister concept to Casa Marcial [[5]](https://guide.michelin.com/gb/en/principado-de-asturias/cereceda/restaurant/narbasu). Breakfast is sourdough, Tineo butter, Caserinos yogurt, cured meats [[4]](https://www.narbasu.com/en/hotel/). **Gotcha:** the property runs Thursday–Sunday, so a Sat-night Casa Marcial dinner aligns but a Sunday-night extension may not [[4]](https://www.narbasu.com/en/hotel/).

### Puebloastur Eco-Resort & Spa — closest + most luxurious
The nearest serious hotel to the restaurant — ~1.8 mi per TripAdvisor's "closest hotels" list [[15]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g608995-d1001616-Casa_Marcial-Arriondas_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html). Awarded **Gran Lujo** in 2025, Asturias's highest hospitality distinction [[7]](https://www.puebloastur.com/en). 28–30 designer rooms, full Nature Spa (sauna, hammam, heated outdoor pool), a 9-metre Salvador Dalí sculpture on the grounds [[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1079283-d10070323-Reviews-Puebloastur_Eco_Resort_Wellness_Spa-Cofino_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html). Rates are heavy — roughly **€470–€770** standard double — and the property is set in pasture, so the day plan is the spa rather than the village [[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1079283-d10070323-Reviews-Puebloastur_Eco_Resort_Wellness_Spa-Cofino_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html).

### Palacio de Cutre — the stone palace
A palace with wall, tower and chapel in La Goleta (Piloña), with seasonal Asturian cooking on-site [[9]](https://palaciodecutre.com/en/). Booking guests average **9.1/10**, with Staff at 9.6 and Comfort at 9.3 [[10]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/es/palacio-de-cutre.html). Two flagged caveats: GPS struggles to find the access road, and there's **no A/C** — fine for a May–June trip, less so in August [[10]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/es/palacio-de-cutre.html). ~20 km / 20 min east of La Salgar; a real taxi but worth the historic shell.

### Hotel Aultre Naray — quietest character
An 1873 casona in Peruyes (Cangas de Onís), restored in 1995 with original limestone portico, chestnut beams, and iron-handrail staircase preserved [[11]](https://www.ruraliberica.com/info/index.asp?Id=5115). Paco Terán-designed interiors, fireside lounges, candles-and-books vibe [[11]](https://www.ruraliberica.com/info/index.asp?Id=5115). Closer to Cangas de Onís than Arriondas — good if your weekend also takes in Covadonga and the Lagos.

### Hotel Casona del Sella — value, in the heart of Arriondas
1923 Indiano-style mansion (built by returning Spanish-Cuban emigrants) on Plaza Venancio Pando, restored 2001; 14 individually-decorated rooms with antique furnishings and hardwood floors; terrace over the Sella [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g608995-d578140-Reviews-Hotel_Casona_Del_Sella-Arriondas_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html). 83/100 walkability — the only pick where you can roll out the door for a *sidra* nightcap [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g608995-d578140-Reviews-Hotel_Casona_Del_Sella-Arriondas_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html). TripAdvisor 3.6★ reflects "thin walls, prioritises charm over polish"; €87–€129/night, by far the cheapest character option [[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g608995-d578140-Reviews-Hotel_Casona_Del_Sella-Arriondas_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html).

### Hotel El Babú — coastal-rural alternative
Tiny 18th-c. Asturian manor on the N-632 (km 17) between Ribadesella and Colunga, 7 rooms, on the Camino del Norte, renewable-energy throughout, Asturian-cheese-and-cured-meat breakfast [[14]](https://www.elbabu.com/). Playa de la Espasa 1.5 km, Mirador del Fito viewpoint 7 km [[14]](https://www.elbabu.com/). ~22 km from Casa Marcial — pair with beach mornings.

### La Raposera — sea-view Indiano (couples only)
6-room 1917 Indiano on a 5,000 m² Caravia farm, rehabilitated 2014, with both Cantabrian-Sea and Sierra del Sueve views, glass-walled breakfast room, fireplace lounge [[13]](https://en.asturias.com/hotel-rural-la-raposera-asturias/). No on-site restaurant — village dining is a 5-min walk; adults/teens 14+ only, no pets [[13]](https://en.asturias.com/hotel-rural-la-raposera-asturias/). 5.0/5 on TripAdvisor — among the highest-rated rural sleeps near Casa Marcial [[15]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g608995-d1001616-Casa_Marcial-Arriondas_Parres_Municipality_Asturias.html).

### Backup: Hotel El Quintanal
Renovated 2008 Asturian manor in Bode, 2 km from Arriondas, 11 rooms (two Junior Suites with hydromassage) [[17]](https://hotelquintanal.com/). Less heritage character than the above — feels more "country B&B" than "boutique" — but a workable fallback if everything else is booked.

## How to pick

| If you prioritise…                                  | Pick                       |
|-----------------------------------------------------|----------------------------|
| The Manzano family experience end-to-end            | Narbasu                    |
| Closest sleep + spa + 5★ luxury                     | Puebloastur                |
| Stone-walls historic                                | Palacio de Cutre           |
| Quiet, designer-restored, intimate                  | Aultre Naray               |
| Walkable to town, cheapest character pick           | Casona del Sella           |
| Coastal base — beach mornings, dinner inland        | El Babú or La Raposera     |

## Logistics

- **Taxi pre-book the return.** La Salgar is a hamlet with no rank — get the driver's number on the way in or have the restaurant call one [[1]](https://www.oadguides.com/restaurant/casa-marcial) [[6]](https://www.rome2rio.com/es/s/Arriondas/Cereceda-Asturias-Espa%C3%B1a). One diner wrote up using Ribadesella as a coastal base ~30 min away, which works but adds a longer taxi than any pick above [[16]](https://www.travelsforstars.com/blog/2024/12/06/casa-marcial-arriondas).
- **Book in this order:** Casa Marcial dinner first (months ahead) → hotel after the dining date confirms [[2]](https://casamarcial.es/en/casa/).
- **Sunday note:** Narbasu operates Thursday–Sunday only [[4]](https://www.narbasu.com/en/hotel/) — fine for a Sat-night dinner with a Sun-morning checkout, less so for a Sun-night extension.
