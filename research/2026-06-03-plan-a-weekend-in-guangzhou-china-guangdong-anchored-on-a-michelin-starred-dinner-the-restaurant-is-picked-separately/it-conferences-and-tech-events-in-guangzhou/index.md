> **TL;DR** Guangzhou's 2026 tech calendar is trade-expo-heavy, peaking March–June at the Canton Fair Complex. Best standalone bets for IT professionals: **VR/AR/XR Expo** (May 10–12) for immersive tech depth, or **Asia AI Conference** (June 27–29) for AI industry breadth. Smart-manufacturing teams: **GIT/SPS** (March 4–6) or **EAI Show** (October 21–23) for embodied robotics. Add a 30-min train ride to Shenzhen for the national CITE IT mega-expo in April — it's worth it. ## Major Expos

### Guangzhou Industrial Technology Fair (GIT) + SPS — March 4–6

Co-located at **Canton Fair Complex Area B**, the [Guangzhou Industrial Technology Fair](https://gitfair.hk.messefrankfurt.com/guangzhou/en/) and [SPS Smart Production Solutions Guangzhou](https://spsguangzhou.hk.messefrankfurt.com/) run over three days. GIT 2026 debuts an **International Robot and Intelligent Equipment Zone** covering industrial robots, service robots, specialised robotics, and industrial AI [[1]](https://gitfair.hk.messefrankfurt.com/guangzhou/en/press/press-releases/2026/GIT26_PR1.html). Scale: 500+ exhibitors, 30,000 sqm [[2]](https://asiamanufacturingnewstoday.com/2025/11/china-import-and-export-fair-complex-guangzhou-4-6-march-2026/). Technical sessions, business matchmaking, and industry forums run alongside the floor [[3]](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/02/14/sps-guangzhou-2026-driving-smart-manufacturing-in-the-greater-bay-area/). Regional context: Guangdong Province's intelligent robotics sector generated RMB 99.2 billion in 2024, making this a genuine supply-chain hub event, not just a showcase [[1]](https://gitfair.hk.messefrankfurt.com/guangzhou/en/press/press-releases/2026/GIT26_PR1.html).

→ Best for: automation engineers, robotics procurement, industrial IoT professionals.

### 139th Canton Fair — April 15 – May 5 (3 phases)

The world's largest trade fair [[4]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-139th-canton-fair-to-open-on-april-15-with-expanded-scale-and-stronger-innovation-focus-302741285.html) spans three phases at the **Canton Fair Complex** [[6]](https://cief.cantonfair.org.cn/en/cfintro/cfintro.html). **Phase 1 (Apr 15–19)** is the relevant window for IT professionals: it covers Electronics & Appliances, Industrial Automation, New Energy Vehicles, and Smart Mobility. Four new zones debut — Smart Wearables, Display Technologies, Consumer Drones, and Agricultural Drones (~670 companies, 1,300+ booths) [[5]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/a-look-at-the-highlights-from-phase-1-of-the-139th-canton-fair-302741317.html). A dedicated Service Robots Zone displays AI/humanoid robot advances. Over 60% of the fair's 32,000+ exhibitors have adopted AI, 5G, or industrial internet technologies [[4]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-139th-canton-fair-to-open-on-april-15-with-expanded-scale-and-stronger-innovation-focus-302741285.html). The Canton Fair app now integrates 14 AI application scenarios for buyer matching [[4]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-139th-canton-fair-to-open-on-april-15-with-expanded-scale-and-stronger-innovation-focus-302741285.html). Scale: 1.55M sqm, 75,700 booths, 4.65M products [[4]](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-139th-canton-fair-to-open-on-april-15-with-expanded-scale-and-stronger-innovation-focus-302741285.html).

→ Best for: hardware sourcing, emerging consumer tech, supply-chain intelligence.

### World VR/AR/XR/MR Industry & Smart Glasses Expo — May 10–12

The [10th edition](https://www.vrarfair.com/index.php?lang=en) at the **Canton Fair Complex** is 40% larger than its predecessor — 50,000 sqm, 800+ exhibitors from 30+ countries [[8]](https://www.xrom.in/post/world-vr-ar-xr-mr-expo-2026-guangzhou-hosts-global-xr-industry-feast). Named participants include Meta, Pico, Huawei, and Thunderbird Innovation alongside ~100 specialised startups [[8]](https://www.xrom.in/post/world-vr-ar-xr-mr-expo-2026-guangzhou-hosts-global-xr-industry-feast). Scope: XR headsets and optics, smart glasses (hearing assistance, sports recording, translation), motion capture, metaverse platforms, digital twins, spatial computing for medicine and education [[10]](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/world-vrarxrmr-industry-smart-glasses-expo-2026-tickets-1981978178084). A "Trade Bridge" matchmaking program targets ~20 billion RMB in commercial deals [[8]](https://www.xrom.in/post/world-vr-ar-xr-mr-expo-2026-guangzhou-hosts-global-xr-industry-feast). Attendance: ~25,000 trade visitors [[7]](https://10times.com/vr-ar). China's XR market is projected at 350 billion RMB by end of 2026 [[8]](https://www.xrom.in/post/world-vr-ar-xr-mr-expo-2026-guangzhou-hosts-global-xr-industry-feast).

→ Best for: XR/VR/AR hardware developers, metaverse product teams, enterprise spatial computing.

### PAGC 2026 — Global Product & Growth Expo + Global AI Growth Conference — May 27–28

Held at **Canton Fair Complex Area C (Halls 14.2–15.2)**, PAGC 2026 is in its 6th edition and now co-branded as the 1st Global AI Growth Conference (AGC) [[13]](https://www.morketing.com/detail/28241). Theme: "AI for Growth × Monetisation" [[11]](https://www.yfchuhai.com/html/pagc2026/en/). Scale: 200 exhibitors, 20,000 visitors, ~20,000 sqm [[12]](https://www.prnasia.com/lightnews/lightnews-1-67-94283.shtml). Programming: 1 AI global growth summit, 8 thematic forums, 3 networking sessions, 100+ international speakers [[13]](https://www.morketing.com/detail/28241). Coverage: AI applications, game globalisation, short-drama platforms, fintech, e-commerce, ad tech, and payment solutions — oriented toward Chinese tech companies expanding internationally [[11]](https://www.yfchuhai.com/html/pagc2026/en/).

→ Best for: app/game publishers, ad-tech teams, founders scaling China-made AI products globally.

### Asia AI Conference + Asia Robot Conference (AICE + ARCE) — June 27–29

Held at **Guangzhou International Procurement Center**, [AICE 2026](https://www.cnaiplus.com/a/latest/8882656.html) pairs an AI industry exhibition with 30+ concurrent forums [[14]](https://www.cnaiplus.com/a/latest/8882656.html). Scale: 4 hectares, ~490 exhibitors, 20 countries/regions, ~60,000 visitors [[14]](https://www.cnaiplus.com/a/latest/8882656.html). Technology coverage spans foundational AI (chips, algorithms, sensors, cloud computing), integrated applications (smart manufacturing, smart cities, autonomous vehicles), and emerging platforms (VR/AR, metaverse) [[14]](https://www.cnaiplus.com/a/latest/8882656.html). The co-located [ARCE 2026](https://www.cnaiplus.com/a/robot/8907936.html) (Asia Robot Conference and Exhibition) adds dedicated robotics industry coverage [[15]](https://www.cnaiplus.com/a/robot/8907936.html). Booth pricing: standard 3×3 m at 16,800 RMB; open space at 1,500 RMB/m² (36 m² minimum) [[14]](https://www.cnaiplus.com/a/latest/8882656.html).

→ Best for: AI engineers, ML practitioners, robotics investors, enterprise AI buyers.

### EAI Show 2026 — GBA Embodied Intelligent Robot Expo — October 21–23

Held at **Guangzhou Aerotropolis Expo Center** (airport area), the [GBA International Embodied Intelligent Robot Industry Conference and Exhibition](https://gba.eirobotshow.com/en/) is South China's dedicated showcase for embodied AI [[16]](https://gba.eirobotshow.com/en/overview/). Scale: 30,000 sqm, 200+ exhibitors, 30,000 professional visitors, 20+ purchasing groups [[16]](https://gba.eirobotshow.com/en/overview/). The event covers the full supply chain — raw materials, components, simulation, testing, through to finished humanoid robots — under the theme "Innovation-Driven Smart Manufacturing for the Future" [[16]](https://gba.eirobotshow.com/en/overview/). International partners include the International Federation of Robotics, European Robotics Association, and Japan Robot Association [[17]](https://www.tradefairdates.com/EAI-Show-GBA-International-Embodied-Intelligent-Robot-Industry-Conference-and-Exhibition-M5740/Guangzhou.html).

→ Best for: robotics engineers, embodied AI researchers, hardware investors.

## Nearby: CITE 2026 in Shenzhen (April 9–11)

⚠ Not Guangzhou — but 30 minutes by high-speed rail. [CITE 2026](https://www.citexpo.org/en) (14th China Information Technology Expo) is organised by China's **Ministry of Industry and Information Technology** and the Shenzhen municipal government, making it the highest-profile national IT expo of the year [[21]](https://www.citexpo.org/en). Scale: 1,800+ exhibitors, 150,000 visitors, 110,000 sqm [[22]](https://10times.com/china-electronics-fair). Coverage: new-generation information technology, AI computing and data centres, advanced semiconductors, intelligent terminals, wearables, and robotics. Worth adding as a same-trip day if visiting Guangzhou in April; the Canton Fair also starts April 15, creating a natural two-event window.
