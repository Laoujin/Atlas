> **TL;DR.** Cenador de Amós sits in Villaverde de Pontones, 15–25 minutes by car from the four highest-value outings in the 30 km radius: **(1)** [Parque de la Naturaleza de Cabárceno](https://parquedecabarceno.com/) for the half-day wildlife park on a reclaimed iron-mine landscape [[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabarceno_Natural_Park); **(2)** Santander city for [Centro Botín](https://www.centrobotin.org/en/) + the [Magdalena Peninsula](https://turismo.santander.es/en/tren-turistico-de-la-magdalena) + a pintxo crawl on the port [[15]](https://audiala.com/en/spain/santander/botin-centre); **(3)** [Santoña](https://www.anchoasdesantona.net/que-ver-en-santona/) for an anchoa-factory tasting and the Monte Buciero loop — but the famous Faro del Caballo staircase is **closed all summer 2026** for rockfall stabilisation [[76]](https://www.radionervion.com/2026/05/20/cierre-total-de-la-bajada-al-faro-del-caballo-en-santona-este-verano-por-riesgo-de-desprendimientos/); **(4)** the bay itself — cross by [Los Reginas](https://www.losreginas.com/) ferry to Somo for surfing or a Pedreña port lunch [[75]](https://www.losreginas.com/paseos-en-barco-por-la-bahia-de-santander-con-escala-en-pedrena-y-somo/horarios-y-tarifas-viaje-pedrena-somo/). Add the [Liérganes](https://www.miruta.es/que-ver-en-lierganes/) Pasiego village for a slow afternoon. Santillana del Mar is ~10 km past the radius but worth the stretch.

## Lay of the land

Villaverde de Pontones is in the **Trasmiera** comarca on the eastern arm of the Bay of Santander. The 30 km radius stacks heavyweight sights in three directions, all reachable inside 30 minutes by car:

- **West, into the bay (10–25 km):** [Pedreña](https://blog.exclusivegolftravel.com/the-legend-of-pedrena), Los Reginas bay ferry, Santander city, Cabárceno, Peña Cabarga.
- **North-east, along the coast (5–25 km):** Somo, Loredo, Langre, Galizano beaches; Santoña and the Asón marshes; Laredo.
- **South, into the mountains (10–20 km):** the Miera valley with [Liérganes](https://www.miruta.es/que-ver-en-lierganes/) and the Pasiego foothills.

Boundary calls: **Santillana del Mar** sits at ~40 km west [[50]](https://spainroam.com/destinations/santillana-del-mar/) (just outside the radius — included as a borderline pick), and **Playa de Los Locos in Suances** at ~31 km [[29]](https://www.surfandplan.com/en/playas/los-locos/). **Bilbao** and **Castro Urdiales** are well outside.

A rental car is essential. Public transport into Santander runs, but inland villages need wheels.

## Cabárceno + Peña Cabarga — the highest-density half-day

[Cabárceno](https://parquedecabarceno.com/) sits 17 km south of Santander on a 750-ha reclaimed open-pit iron mine, opened 1989 [[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabarceno_Natural_Park). Over 100 species live in vast semi-free-range enclosures: Cantabrian brown bears have 35 ha to themselves and the African elephant herd is the largest in captivity outside Africa, with gorillas, tigers, lions and jaguars plus birds-of-prey and sea-lion shows [[33]](https://www.ladieswhattravel.com/cabarceno-nature-park-cantabria-santander/). Plan **4½–5 hours minimum** to do it justice [[33]](https://www.ladieswhattravel.com/cabarceno-nature-park-cantabria-santander/). Open daily from 09:30, closing 17:00–20:00 by season; closed only 24, 25, 31 Dec and 1 Jan [[31]](https://parquedecabarceno.com/planifica-tu-visita/tarifas/).

Directly above the park, [Peña Cabarga](https://cantabriaspain.co.uk/nature-2/pena-cabarga-viewpoint-an-immersive-experience-just-20-minutes-from-santander/) (600 m) is topped by the 38.31 m **Monumento al Indiano**. The paved summit road is drivable; go at sunset for the bay panorama.

## Santander city

A 20–25-minute drive west [[15]](https://audiala.com/en/spain/santander/botin-centre). The compact waterfront walks in half a day; add Magdalena and Sardinero for a full day.

- **[Centro Botín](https://www.centrobotin.org/en/)** — Renzo Piano's barnacle-on-stilts art centre on the bay. **2026 programme:** *ITINERARIOS XXX* and *Point and Counterpoint* until 19 Apr; *Yuko Mohri: Entanglements* 28 Mar – 6 Sep [[2]](https://www.centrobotin.org/en/whats-on/exhibitions/). Admission €9 online (€10 door), €5 concession, free under 18 [[3]](https://ideasparaviajar.com/precio-entrada-centro-botin). Hours: Tue–Sun 10:00–21:00 June–Sep; split 10:00–14:00 / 16:00–20:00 the rest of the year [[1]](https://www.centrobotin.org/en/).
- **[Magdalena Peninsula](https://turismo.santander.es/en/tren-turistico-de-la-magdalena)** — free public park crowned by Alfonso XIII's 1909–13 summer palace; interior visits mostly weekends [[5]](https://www.palaciomagdalena.com/en/visits). A tourist train circles the headland year-round, 10:00–19:00 in summer / 10:00–14:00 + 15:30–18:00 in winter, every 15–20 minutes [[4]](https://turismo.santander.es/en/tren-turistico-de-la-magdalena). The free **mini-zoo** of seals, sea lions and penguins feeds at ~13:00 [[6]](https://www.minube.net/place/mini-zoo-of-santander-a32364).
- **[El Sardinero](https://turismo.santander.es/en/what-to-do/travelling-around-the-city/el-sardinero)** — 1,300 m city beach split into First (smart) and Second (popular) by the 1925 Jardines de Piquío [[7]](https://turismo.santander.es/en/what-to-do/travelling-around-the-city/el-sardinero), home to the *Bola del Mundo* limestone sphere and the *Mesa Zodiacal* zodiac stone [[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sardinero). The Belle Époque [Gran Casino](https://www.expedia.co.uk/Gran-Casino-Del-Sardinero-El-Sardinero.d553248621532487532.Attraction) (1916) overlooks First Beach [[9]](https://www.expedia.co.uk/Gran-Casino-Del-Sardinero-El-Sardinero.d553248621532487532.Attraction).
- **Mercado de la Esperanza** — 19th-c. cast-iron food hall behind the town hall, fish below / meat-veg-cheese above [[11]](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/spain/cantabria-asturias-and-galicia/santander/attractions/mercado-la-esperanza/a/poi-sig/1266800/360749). Mon–Sat 08:00–14:00; Thu/Fri also 17:00–19:30 [[12]](https://pamelacahill.com/2013/09/29/mercado-esperanza-market-santander/).
- **Catedral de Santander** — €1 entry, two-level structure; Mon–Fri 10:00–13:00 / 16:00–19:30, Sat mornings + 16:30–20:00, Sun 8:00–14:00 / 17:00–20:00 [[10]](https://audiala.com/en/spain/santander/santander-cathedral).
- **[Funicular del Río de la Pila](https://www.santander.info/funicular-rio-pila)** — free, council-run, 78 m climb up to Paseo del General Dávila for a bay panorama; runs 06:00 to midnight, bicycles allowed [[13]](https://www.santander.info/funicular-rio-pila) [[14]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g187484-d7007467-Reviews-Funicular_del_Rio_de_la_Pila-Santander_Cantabria.html).

## Heritage towns

### [Santoña](https://www.anchoasdesantona.net/que-ver-en-santona/) — ~22 km east

Spain's anchovy capital. Three defensive fortifications climb Monte Buciero in a layered coastal defence: the 17th-c. **Fuerte de San Carlos** at the foot of the mountain (the oldest, traced to a 1688 proposal by *santoñés* Juan de Maeda), the V-shaped **Fuerte de San Martín** with two superimposed casemated levels behind it, and the smaller **Fuerte del Mazo** (renamed **Fuerte de Napoleón** after the French rebuilt it during the Peninsular War) high above on the north flank — the latter preserves the only watch-turret (*garita*) of its era in Cantabria [[85]](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuertes_de_Santo%C3%B1a). The Santoña Marshes Natural Reserve at the Asón mouth is one of the north coast's best year-round birding spots [[43]](https://www.anchoasdesantona.net/que-ver-en-santona/).

The headline hike is the **Faro del Caballo**: 763 stairs originally built by 19th-c. prison inmates and rebuilt in 2013, dropping to the lighthouse that operated 1863–1993 [[44]](https://www.fascinatingspain.com/articulo/what-to-see-in-cantabria/caballo-lighthouse-700-steps-cantabrian-marvel/20210804170025067161.html). ⚠ **Status:** the staircase is **closed throughout summer 2026** for rockfall stabilisation — rocks ~20 m above the steps are not securely seated [[76]](https://www.radionervion.com/2026/05/20/cierre-total-de-la-bajada-al-faro-del-caballo-en-santona-este-verano-por-riesgo-de-desprendimientos/). The wider 9.5 km loop on Monte Buciero (Faro del Caballo–Faro del Pescador, ~3 h, moderate) remains open and is the substitute hike [[77]](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/spain/cantabria/santona-faro-del-caballo-faro-del-pescador). Pair with an anchoa visit (see Food experiences).

### [Laredo](https://www.spain.info/en/destination/laredo/) — ~30 km east

The medieval **Puebla Vieja** has been a Historic-Artistic Site since 1970, with surviving wall remnants and noble *rúas* climbing to the Gothic **Santa María de la Asunción**, which holds a Flemish-style Bethlehem altarpiece ranked among Spain's most important [[41]](https://www.spain.info/en/destination/laredo/) [[42]](https://turismodecantabria.com/municipios/laredo/). **Playa Salvé** below town is Cantabria's longest beach: a shell-shaped 4,250 m sweep developing inside a wide bay *"al abrigo de la protección del Monte Buciero"* — sheltered, in other words, by the same massif that holds the Santoña forts opposite [[86]](https://www.laredoturismo.es/conoce/laredo/el-litoral/playa-salve/). The modern marina holds 850+ moorings [[42]](https://turismodecantabria.com/municipios/laredo/).

### [Liérganes](https://www.miruta.es/que-ver-en-lierganes/) — ~15 km south-east

Nicknamed the "*Santillana pasiega*", Liérganes is one of *Los Pueblos Más Bonitos de España* and the Miera valley's most-visited village. The cobbled centre is a Conjunto Histórico-Artístico of 17th–18th-c. stone *casonas* [[38]](https://www.hola.com/viajes/20250712843753/pueblos-mas-bonitos-valles-pasiegos-cantabria/), anchored by the 16th-c. Renaissance **Puente Mayor** (often mis-called "Roman") over the Miera, the **Hombre Pez** statue, and the cannon-decorated **Casa de los Cañones** [[46]](https://www.miruta.es/que-ver-en-lierganes/). The twin hills above the village are known locally as *las tetas de Liérganes* [[38]](https://www.hola.com/viajes/20250712843753/pueblos-mas-bonitos-valles-pasiegos-cantabria/). The four-century-old [Balneario de Liérganes](https://www.balneariosrelaistermal.com/es/balneario-lierganes/) uses sulphurated mineral-medicinal waters from the Santa Fuente spring (declared of public utility 1869), sits in a 3-ha park of centenary trees and runs March–December — lodging from ~€48 B&B [[39]](https://www.balneariosrelaistermal.com/es/balneario-lierganes/) [[40]](https://www.elplural.com/oxigena2/planes/pueblo-cantabria-balneario-digno-reyes-donde-nace-misterio-hombre-pez-v_332085102).

### Pedreña — ~8 km west

Seve Ballesteros's birthplace. Seve debuted as a caddie at [Real Golf de Pedreña](https://www.realgolfdepedrena.com/club/tarifas/) aged 9 (his father worked there as a gardener), and learned to play hitting balls on the beach with a discarded 3-iron [[47]](https://blog.exclusivegolftravel.com/the-legend-of-pedrena). A welcome statue stands at the village entrance, and a small park holds a faux-Swilcan Bridge with a bronze of Seve's iconic fist-pump from the 1984 Open [[49]](https://leisureandpleasure.eu/inicio/en/severiano-ballesteros-a-legend-of-golf-born-in-pedrena-marina-de-cudeyo/). The Los Reginas ferry stops here (see Outdoor activities).

### Santillana del Mar — ~40 km west (borderline)

Just over the 30 km bound, but worth the extra 15 min. A traffic-free medieval town anchored by the 12th-c. Romanesque **Colegiata de Santa Juliana** with celebrated carved cloister capitals [[50]](https://spainroam.com/destinations/santillana-del-mar/), paired with the **Altamira Museum's** replica neocave (the original UNESCO cave is closed to general visitors) [[51]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santillana_del_Mar).

### Hiking

- **Monte Buciero loop** (Santoña) — 9.5 km / ~3 h, moderate; the cleanest substitute for the closed Faro del Caballo descent [[77]](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/spain/cantabria/santona-faro-del-caballo-faro-del-pescador).
- **[Costa Quebrada PR-S 3](https://www.picuco.com/en-es/regions/costa-quebrada-cantabria/)** (Liencres–Covachos) — flagship coast walk through vertical Cretaceous flysch ridges, sea stacks and dunes; the popular Canallave–Madero–Somocuevas circuit is a 6 km / 1 h 36 min loop [[80]](https://www.picuco.com/en-es/regions/costa-quebrada-cantabria/) [[37]](https://www.alltrails.com/parks/spain/cantabria/parque-natural-de-las-dunas-de-liencres).
- **Peña Cabarga** (Pico Llen, 569 m) — 7.2-mile out-and-back paved climb with ~600 m gain, panoramic; ⚠ shared with vehicles, so look for the side paths [[82]](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/spain/cantabria/cima-ca01-pena-cabarga).
- **GR-74 Corredor Oriental** — long-distance trail crossing the Pasiego valleys (Selaya, Vega de Pas, San Pedro del Romeral); pick a single stage for a day-walk through karst, beech forest and the scattered *cabañas pasiegas* — stone-built cattle huts with ground-floor stable and upper hayloft, ~10,000 of which were built across the Pas valleys in the 18th–19th centuries and still define the settlement pattern [[81]](https://www.vallespasiegos.eu/en/patrimonio/gr-74-corredor-oriental/) [[87]](https://www.vallespasiegos.eu/en/patrimonio/cabana-pasiega/).

### Bay ferry, kayak, SUP, ride and bike

- **[Los Reginas](https://www.losreginas.com/)** runs the Santander–Pedreña–Somo ferry **365 days a year**, every ~30 min, **€4 one-way / €6.50 return** — the cheapest scenic crossing on the bay and the painless way to combine a Santander morning with a Somo beach afternoon [[75]](https://www.losreginas.com/paseos-en-barco-por-la-bahia-de-santander-con-escala-en-pedrena-y-somo/horarios-y-tarifas-viaje-pedrena-somo/).
- **[Stand Up Santander](https://www.standupsantander.com/EXCURSIONES/)** runs guided kayak and SUP tours from Playa de los Peligros to La Magdalena, Mouro and La Torre islets — **€30 pp, 1.5–2 h**, kit and insurance included [[78]](https://www.standupsantander.com/EXCURSIONES/).
- **[Plea Loredo](https://www.plea.es/en/horse-riding-lessons)** runs horseback lessons and beach rides on Somo–Loredo sands [[83]](https://www.plea.es/en/horse-riding-lessons).
- **Vías Verdes** (rail-trail greenways): the flat Santander–Astillero route and Miera-valley connections toward Liérganes are easy family-friendly cycling [[84]](https://cantabriaspain.co.uk/nature-2/cantabria-by-bike-cycling-routes-for-all-levels/).

## Food experiences

Cenador de Amós (Jesús Sánchez, two Michelin stars) is the dinner anchor. The rest of the trip's eating slots are well served by lower-key food experiences inside the 30 km radius — anchovy producers, pintxo bars, sobaos bakeries, port-front fish lunches.

### Pintxos and casual food in Santander

- **[La Bodega del Riojano](https://bodegadelriojano.com/)** — historic cellar with the *Museo Redondo* of 60+ hand-painted wine-barrel tops; Michelin guide mention for authenticity [[60]](https://bodegadelriojano.com/).
- **[Casa Lita](https://carta.menu/restaurants/santander-1/casa-lita)** — port-front; the staple pintxo crawl bar at ~€2.70 per pincho; monthly special sells out [[61]](https://carta.menu/restaurants/santander-1/casa-lita).
- **[La Caseta de Bombas](https://www.guiarepsol.com/es/fichas/solete/la-caseta-de-bombas-332590/)** — 1908 building on the Gamazo dock, buys daily at the Santander fish auction without intermediaries; better as a sit-down seafood lunch than as a pintxo stop [[62]](https://www.guiarepsol.com/es/fichas/solete/la-caseta-de-bombas-332590/).
- **Mercado de la Esperanza** — see Santander city section. Best mid-morning [[12]](https://pamelacahill.com/2013/09/29/mercado-esperanza-market-santander/).

### Sobaos and quesadas pasiegas

The Pasiego valleys make **sobaos** (butter-rich sponge cakes) and **quesadas** (a curd-set "cheesecake" — recognised among Spain's seven gastronomic wonders [[70]](https://www.anchoasdeluxe.com/en/293-quesada)).

- **[Sobaos Joselín](https://www.sobaosjoselin.com/museo/)** (Selaya, ~30 km south) — 40-min museum visit with free tasting, plus a 2-h hands-on bake-your-own workshop (bookable on 942 590 268) [[63]](https://www.sobaosjoselin.com/museo/).
- **[Sobaos Etelvina Sañudo](https://sobaosetelvina.com/)** (Vega de Pas) — IGP-certified sobaos and quesadas, 100% butter, no preservatives; village shop on Calle El Cruce 3 [[64]](https://sobaosetelvina.com/).

### Cider and port lunches

- **[Sidrería El Molino de la Hoz](https://www.facebook.com/ElMolinodelaHoz/)** (Penagos, ~15 km) — *cordero a la estaca* paired with local [Sidra Somarroza](https://sidrasomarroza.es/en/homepage/), the Cantabrian (not Asturian) cider [[65]](https://www.facebook.com/ElMolinodelaHoz/) [[66]](https://sidrasomarroza.es/en/homepage/). Cantabria's cider tradition is thinner than Asturias's — this is the closest authentic option.
- **[Sidrería El Pegollu](https://sidreriaelpegollu.com/)** (Santander) — Asturian-style *chigre* (cachopo, fabada, *escanciado* cider) inside the Centro Asturiano, near Plaza de Numancia [[67]](https://sidreriaelpegollu.com/).
- **[Asador Itxaski](https://asadoritxaski.com/)** (Pedreña port) — grilled fish and seafood with bay views, own fish-market supply [[68]](https://asadoritxaski.com/). Combine with the Los Reginas ferry across.
- **[Restaurante Marina](https://restaurantguru.com/Pedrena)** (Pedreña port) — more rustic harbour-front alternative, *arroz con nécoras* the standout [[69]](https://restaurantguru.com/Pedrena).

## Practical notes

- **Car** essential. Drive times above are off-peak; in August add 20–30 % for coastal traffic.
- **Best season.** May–June and September are sweet spots — mild, less crowded, the Cabárceno medium-season tariff applies, surf is mellower for beginners. Summer (July–August) is high season at Cabárceno (€45 adult) and packed at every beach but the cliff-access ones (Langre, Mataleñas).
- **Reservations.** Cenador de Amós books out months ahead. Anchoa tours (5Ñ, Avelina, Codesa) need at least a few days notice. Pedreña golf needs handicap certificate up front.
- **Faro del Caballo:** closed all summer 2026 [[76]](https://www.radionervion.com/2026/05/20/cierre-total-de-la-bajada-al-faro-del-caballo-en-santona-este-verano-por-riesgo-de-desprendimientos/). Substitute the wider Monte Buciero loop.
- **Family with young kids:** Cabárceno + Sardinero + Magdalena tourist train + mini-zoo will fill three full days without repeating.
- **Foodie weekend:** Santoña anchoa tour + Mercado de la Esperanza + Pasiego sobaos workshop + Casa Lita pintxos + Cenador on Saturday night.
- **Outdoors weekend:** surf morning in Somo + Liérganes balneario afternoon + Monte Buciero loop + Costa Quebrada PR-S 3 + Cenador on Saturday night.
