> **TL;DR** Valence hosts no large-scale international IT conferences. The local tech scene is driven by [French Tech Alpes Valence-Romans](https://www.ftalps.com/territoires/valence-romans/accueil-valence-romans/) [[1]](https://www.ftalps.com/territoires/valence-romans/accueil-valence-romans/) — a grassroots community running biannual startup pitches, open-innovation roundtables, and occasional industry AI days. **June 2026 is an unusually good window**: Mardinnov #24 is tonight (Jun 2) and Décrypt'innov #5 falls on Jun 4. For major multi-track developer conferences, Lyon is 65 km north (35 min by TGV).

## Recurring Community Events

**[Mardinnov](https://mardinnov.com/)** [[7]](https://mardinnov.com/) — The flagship local event. Twice yearly (typically June and December), five Valence-Romans startups pitch for 5 min each, followed by a networking buffet. Organised by [Le Moulin Digital](https://lemoulindigital.fr/) [[10]](https://lemoulindigital.fr/), Valence Romans Agglomération, Digital League, and the Drôme Department. Register free via Eventbrite. 24 editions as of June 2026.

**Pitch Presque Parfait** [[4]](https://www.ftalps.com/pitch-presque-parfait-30/) — Recurring startup pitch rehearsal series (30+ editions as of Mar 2026). Held alongside Kokorico and independently; targets founders preparing investor presentations.

**Décrypt'innov** [[1]](https://www.ftalps.com/territoires/valence-romans/accueil-valence-romans/) — Quarterly lunch roundtable (~90 min) linking corporate open-innovation challenges with local startups and service providers. Organised by Valence Romans Innov.

**Kokorico** [[3]](https://www.ftalps.com/kokorico-2026/) — Annual French Tech ecosystem gathering (typically Q1). Combines ecosystem updates, startup highlights, and partner announcements. 2026 focused on French Tech re-labelling strategy, AI, cybersecurity, and angel investment.

## Tech Infrastructure

[Le Moulin Digital](https://lemoulindigital.fr/) [[10]](https://lemoulindigital.fr/) anchors the scene at Parc Rovaltain (adjacent to Valence TGV station), open Mon–Fri 9–18h — coworking, home base for French Tech Alpes, and the main event venue [[1]](https://www.ftalps.com/territoires/valence-romans/accueil-valence-romans/). [Campus Numérique in the Alps](https://le-campus-numerique.fr/campus-valence/) [[8]](https://le-campus-numerique.fr/campus-valence/) runs three IT developer training diploma programmes at the same Rovaltain site. The complete community events calendar is on [OpenAgenda — French Tech Alpes Valence-Romans](https://openagenda.com/fr/french-tech-alpes-valence-romans) [[9]](https://openagenda.com/fr/french-tech-alpes-valence-romans).

## Context

Valence (pop. ~65 k) punches above its weight for grassroots startup activity but has no equivalent to a multi-day international developer conference. No active developer community meetups were found on Meetup.com for the city specifically. The nearest dense conference calendar is Lyon — 35 min by TGV, worth checking alongside any Valence weekend trip.
