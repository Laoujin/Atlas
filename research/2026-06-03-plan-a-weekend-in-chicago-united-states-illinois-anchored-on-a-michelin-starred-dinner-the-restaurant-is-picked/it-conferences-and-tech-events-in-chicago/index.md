> **TL;DR** Chicago's tech calendar is packed through year-end. **June is the hottest month**: PG DATA (Jun 4-5, $50), SANS cybersecurity (Jun 8-13), TechCon 365 Microsoft (Jun 15-19), Automate robotics expo (Jun 22-25), and Chicago AI Week (Jun 24-26) all land within weeks. Fall brings [DjangoCon US](https://2026.djangocon.us/) (Aug), [ElixirConf](https://elixirconf.com/) (Sep), and the massive [IMTS](https://www.imts.com/) manufacturing tech show (Sep 14-19, 90k attendees). Most summer/fall events still have open registration as of June 3. [[1]](https://dev.events/NA/US/IL/Chicago/tech) [[2]](https://xl.net/blog/top-tech-conferences-chicago/)

## Upcoming Events — June 2026

The next three weeks are the densest stretch of the year.

### PG DATA 2026 — Jun 4–5

[PG DATA 2026](https://2026.pg-data.org/) is a brand-new PostgreSQL-focused conference at **Loyola University's Water Tower Campus**, organized by Prairie Postgres NFP [[3]](https://2026.pg-data.org/) [[20]](https://prairiepostgres.org/pg-data-2026-is-coming-to-chicago-at-june/). It targets database developers and DBAs across the Midwest.

- **Tickets:** $50 general · $20 students (includes half-day training) [[3]](https://2026.pg-data.org/)
- **Audience:** PostgreSQL users, DBAs, open-source database devs
- **Tone:** Community-first, non-commercial

### SANS Chicago 2026 — Jun 8–13

[SANS Chicago 2026](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-training-events/chicago-2026) delivers 16 hands-on cybersecurity courses at the **Embassy Suites by Hilton Chicago Downtown Magnificent Mile** [[4]](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-training-events/chicago-2026). Courses span fundamentals (SEC401) through cloud security (SEC540) and a new AI/LLM security course (SEC545).

- **Tickets:** ~$8,260–$8,780 per course (in-person + virtual options)
- **Hotel:** $309/night block rate through May 18 (may be gone — check directly)
- **Bonus:** NetWars tournament + SANS@Night talks on AI security
- **Audience:** Practitioners, SOC analysts, pen testers, security leaders

### TechCon 365 Chicago — Jun 15–19

[TechCon 365 Chicago 2026](https://techcon365.com/Chicago/) is a 5-day Microsoft 365, Power Platform, and Copilot/AI conference at **McCormick Place South** [[5]](https://techcon365.com/Chicago/) [[6]](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/communitynewsdesk/event-guide---microsoft-at-techcon-chicago-2026/4517561). Mon–Tue are workshops; Wed–Fri are breakout sessions. Microsoft participates directly.

- **Tickets:** $850–$2,995 (5-day pass was $200 off before May 29)
- **Topics:** Teams, Fabric, Power BI, Copilot, SharePoint, Azure OpenAI, Purview
- **Audience:** Microsoft 365 admins, power users, developers, business analysts

### Automate 2026 — Jun 22–25

[Automate 2026](https://www.automateshow.com/) is the largest automation show in the Americas, at **McCormick Place** [[7]](https://www.automateshow.com/). Over 1,000 exhibitors and 200 speakers cover robotics, AI, machine vision, and motion control.

- **Tickets:** Free floor registration · Paid conference sessions (140+ sessions, 20+ topic tracks)
- **Scale:** 50,000+ expected attendees
- **Audience:** Automation engineers, manufacturing leaders, robotics developers

### Accelerate Chicago 2026 — Jun 22–24

[Accelerate Chicago 2026](https://gotochgo.com/accelerate-chicago-2026) is a software development conference themed "Craft software with confidence in 2026," covering AI tooling, agile methodologies, and cloud technologies [[8]](https://gotochgo.com/accelerate-chicago-2026).

- **Tickets:** From $845
- **Audience:** Software engineers, engineering managers, tech leads

### Chicago AI Week 2026 — Jun 24–26

[Chicago AI Week 2026](https://chicagoaiweek.com/) is a three-track summit (Women in AI Day, Responsible AI Day, AI Industry Day) powered by AI 2030, 1871, and the Chicago AI Council [[9]](https://chicagoaiweek.com/). 100+ sessions, 300 speakers, focused on governance, enterprise adoption, and applied AI in healthcare, finance, and public sector.

- **Tickets:** $499 (gov/non-profit) · $999 (standard) · $1,999 (VIP) [[10]](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/chicago-ai-week-tickets-1981473535685)
- **Audience:** AI practitioners, executives, policy leaders, researchers

## Summer — July & August 2026

### TechChicago Week — Jul 20–26

[TechChicago Week 2026](https://gotechchicago.com/week/) is an umbrella of events under the theme "Solving Grand Challenges," organized by nonprofit **P33** [[11]](https://gotechchicago.com/week/). Anchor events include the Global Quantum Forum (Jul 22-23), Chicago Sports Tech Week (Jul 20-23), and Chicago Climate Week (Jul 18-25). Registration opens individually per event; the full calendar launched in June 2026.

- **Tickets:** Free to paid, varies by sub-event
- **Audience:** Broad — founders, researchers, policymakers, civic tech

### DjangoCon US 2026 — Aug 24–28

[DjangoCon US 2026](https://2026.djangocon.us/) is at the **voco Chicago Downtown** (350 W Wolf Point Plaza, River North) [[12]](https://2026.djangocon.us/). Talks run Aug 24-26; open-source contribution sprints on Aug 27-28.

- **Audience:** Django/Python developers, web engineers, open-source contributors
- **Vibe:** Community-run, affordable, beginner-friendly

## Fall 2026

### ElixirConf US 2026 — Sep 10–11

[ElixirConf US 2026](https://elixirconf.com/) is Chicago's premier Elixir and Phoenix conference, hybrid format with two tracks [[13]](https://elixirconf.com/). Early bird tickets open June 25.

- **Tickets:** $249 virtual · $579 in-person training day
- **Audience:** Elixir, Erlang, Phoenix developers

### IMTS 2026 — Sep 14–19

[IMTS 2026](https://www.imts.com/) (International Manufacturing Technology Show) is the largest manufacturing technology trade show in the Western Hemisphere, at **McCormick Place** [[14]](https://www.imts.com/) [[15]](https://www.imts.com/show/abouttheshow.cfm). Held biennially.

- **Scale:** 90,000 visitors · 2,000+ exhibitors · 1.2M sq ft of exhibits · 3,000+ conference registrants
- **Focus:** Additive manufacturing, robotics, CNC, AI in manufacturing, digital twins
- **Tickets:** Floor access free with conference ticket; conference registration opened Feb 2026

### Leaders In AI Summit Chicago — Sep 15–16

A curated executive-format AI summit featuring small-room workshops on enterprise AI strategy and governance [[16]](https://epochal-intelligence.com/Illinois-Events.html).

- **Audience:** C-suite, AI leaders, enterprise decision-makers

### ElixirConf / Umbraco US Festival — Sep 30–Oct 1

[Umbraco US Festival 2026](https://dev.events/NA/US/IL/Chicago/tech) brings the .NET CMS community to Chicago for a focused two-day event [[1]](https://dev.events/NA/US/IL/Chicago/tech).

## By Interest

**Developer / open source:** PG DATA (Jun, $50) → DjangoCon US (Aug, community-priced) → ElixirConf (Sep, $249 virtual). All three are practitioner-run with minimal vendor noise.

**Cybersecurity:** SANS Chicago (Jun 8-13, deep practitioner training) is the most intensive. FutureCon (past) and the Cybersecurity Summit (past) covered the executive/strategic side.

**AI & data:** Chicago AI Week (Jun 24-26) for governance and applied AI. Leaders In AI Summit (Sep) for executive workshops. Applied AI for Distributors (Jun 23-25) for supply chain/distribution verticals [[16]](https://epochal-intelligence.com/Illinois-Events.html).

**Enterprise Microsoft:** TechCon 365 (Jun 15-19) is the clear pick — independent, practitioner-led, covers the full M365/Copilot stack [[5]](https://techcon365.com/Chicago/).

**Industrial / manufacturing tech:** Automate (Jun, free) for robotics/automation. IMTS (Sep, free floor) for the full manufacturing technology spectrum at scale [[14]](https://www.imts.com/).
