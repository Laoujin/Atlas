> **TL;DR:** Eat Langkawi by *where*, not by star count. Chase the rotating nightly **pasar malam** (a different village every day of the week) for satay, char kway teow and cucur udang at RM1–10 [[5]](https://mytrip.my/langkawi-night-markets-schedule-timings-locations-travel-guide/). For story, book a longtail to the **floating fish-farm kitchen "Hole in the Wall"** deep in the Kilim mangroves [[7]](https://www.mangrovetourslangkawi.com/post/floating-restaurant-fish-breeding-farm). For a feast, do **Chinese-Muslim open-air seafood in Kuah** — a family of four eats for ~RM120 *with beer* because the island is duty-free [[11]](https://www.langkawi-insight.com/langkawi_000138.htm)[[26]](https://gotripzi.com/destinations/langkawi-my/cost). Local must-eats: **Laksa Kedah/Utara**, **nasi dagang**, **ikan bakar** and **gulai nangka** [[1]](https://www.airasia.com/blog/malaysia/langkawi/must-try-langkawi-food/)[[4]](https://www.visitkedah.com.my/about-kedah/food-in-kedah.html). Come **Nov–Mar (dry season)** and your beach dinners stay dry [[32]](https://www.wanderlustchloe.com/best-time-to-visit-langkawi/).

Prices in MYR (RM) with EUR at the **June 2026 rate ≈ 1 EUR : 4.6 RM** (RM10 ≈ €2.20) [[30]](https://www.foreignexchange.org.uk/fx-rates/conversion/1/MYR/EUR). Every spot below is tagged with its **area** and a **touristy ↔ offbeat** flag.

## Before you book a table: timing & the duty-free angle

- **Season.** Dry, sunny window is **Nov–Mar** (hot, ~33°C, minimal rain) — best for beachfront and night-market eating [[32]](https://www.wanderlustchloe.com/best-time-to-visit-langkawi/). The wet **monsoon runs Sep to mid-Nov** with daily downpours; Apr and Nov are the shoulder months that dodge the worst [[33]](https://www.agatetravel.com/malaysia/langkawi/weather.html). Open-air kitchens and pasar malam are the first casualties of a rainy evening — plan a covered fallback.
- **Duty-free = a drinking-and-feasting island.** Langkawi is a tax-free island; **beer drops to ~RM5.97 (€1.30)** and spirits run a third-to-a-sixth of mainland prices [[26]](https://gotripzi.com/destinations/langkawi-my/cost)[[25]](https://theperpetualsaturday.com/duty-free-shops-in-langkawi/). This is *why* the Kuah Chinese-style seafood houses serve cold beer with chilli crab so cheaply, and why a self-catered sunset of supermarket beer + night-market satay is a legitimate Langkawi ritual. Duty-free shops cluster in **Kuah town** (~20 outlets) and along **Pantai Cenang** [[27]](https://www.langkawi-insight.com/langkawi_0000bf.htm).
- **Cash.** Most stalls and night markets are **cash-only** [[5]](https://mytrip.my/langkawi-night-markets-schedule-timings-locations-travel-guide/).

## Langkawi & Kedah specialties — what to actually order

Kedah palates lean spicy, sour and seafood-heavy, leaning on tamarind and sambal [[4]](https://www.visitkedah.com.my/about-kedah/food-in-kedah.html). Order these before any pizza:

| Dish | What it is | Where / price | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Laksa Kedah / Laksa Utara** | Rice noodles in a thick sour mackerel-sardine broth with *daun kesum*, torch ginger, lemongrass | [Laksa Power](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g298283-d1144527-Reviews-Nasi_Kandar_Tomato-Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html) (Pantai Serama, near airport) RM5–8; cendol on the side while you wait for sunset [[2]](https://my.trip.com/guide/food/what-to-eat-in-langkawi.html)[[29]](https://tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g298283-d1144527-Reviews-Nasi_Kandar_Tomato-Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html) | offbeat |
| **Nasi Dagang** | Coconut-milk rice with fish curry (Langkawi version is lighter, herbal); pair with *gulai ikan tongkol* | Nasi Dagang Pak Malau, Jalan Makam Mahsuri — paddy-field views, RM8–12 [[28]](https://www.trip.com/moments/poi-nasi-dagang-pak-malau-31396478/)[[2]](https://my.trip.com/guide/food/what-to-eat-in-langkawi.html) | offbeat |
| **Ikan bakar** | Whole fish in banana leaf, charcoal-grilled with sambal | Hassan Ikan Bakar (Pantai Cenang area); Medan Selera Nelayan, Bukit Malut — RM20–40 [[15]](https://mytrip.my/top-10-seafood-restaurants-in-langkawi/)[[2]](https://my.trip.com/guide/food/what-to-eat-in-langkawi.html) | offbeat |
| **Gulai nangka** | Young-jackfruit curry, a Kedah village delicacy | Nasi campur stalls, Padang Matsirat & Ayer Hangat [[4]](https://www.visitkedah.com.my/about-kedah/food-in-kedah.html)[[3]](https://langkawimangrovetrip.com/blog/best-makan-langkawi/) | offbeat |
| **Langkawi satay** | Island twist: fish & squid skewers, not just chicken/beef | Night-market stalls, RM0.80–1.20/stick [[1]](https://www.airasia.com/blog/malaysia/langkawi/must-try-langkawi-food/)[[2]](https://my.trip.com/guide/food/what-to-eat-in-langkawi.html) | offbeat |
| **Cucur udang** | Crispy prawn fritters | Kuah Market (Wed/Sat); Padang Matsirat (Sun) RM4–6 [[2]](https://my.trip.com/guide/food/what-to-eat-in-langkawi.html) | offbeat |
| **Nasi lemak** | Coconut rice, sambal, anchovies, peanuts — the breakfast | Dangau Langkawi, RM5–10 [[1]](https://www.airasia.com/blog/malaysia/langkawi/must-try-langkawi-food/) | touristy-ok |
| **Cendol / ais kepal** | Shaved-ice desserts; cendol sells out fast | Roadside island-wide; Ais Kepal Tok Janggut (Kuah) RM4 [[31]](https://sillysuitcase.com/langkawi-local-food/)[[2]](https://my.trip.com/guide/food/what-to-eat-in-langkawi.html) | offbeat |
| **Coconut ice cream** | Served in the shell, from island-harvested coconuts | Cenang beach stalls, Kuah night market [[1]](https://www.airasia.com/blog/malaysia/langkawi/must-try-langkawi-food/) | touristy |

Breakfast like a local: **roti canai + teh tarik** at any Kuah mamak (RM3–5), or *nasi campur* where "the tables are already full at 11am" [[3]](https://langkawimangrovetrip.com/blog/best-makan-langkawi/)[[1]](https://www.airasia.com/blog/malaysia/langkawi/must-try-langkawi-food/).

## The rotating night markets (pasar malam) — a different village each night

The single best-value, most local eating on the island. Markets rotate by village, ~**6–10 PM**, **cash only**; sweet spot is **7–9 PM** [[5]](https://mytrip.my/langkawi-night-markets-schedule-timings-locations-travel-guide/). Schedule corroborated across two guides [[5]](https://mytrip.my/langkawi-night-markets-schedule-timings-locations-travel-guide/)[[6]](https://www.theislanddrum.com/langkawi-things-to-see-places-to-eat/langkawi-places-to-eat/langkawi-night-market-schedule/):

| Day | Location | Area | Note | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Ulu Melaka (near Makam Mahsuri) | Central | Malay snacks, grilled seafood | offbeat |
| **Tue** | Kedawang | Near Pantai Cenang | Fried chicken, tropical fruit | offbeat |
| **Wed** | Kuah Town (near jetty) | Kuah | **Biggest** market, widest spread | touristy-ish |
| **Thu** | Bohor / Temoyong | Near Pantai Cenang | Most convenient for beach-strip stayers; char kway teow, rojak, grilled seafood | touristy-ish |
| **Fri** | Ayer Hangat (Hot Springs) | NE, near Tanjung Rhu | Seafood, satay, grilled meats | offbeat |
| **Sat** | Kuah Town | Kuah | Weekend crowd | touristy-ish |
| **Sun** | Padang Matsirat | Near airport | Local snacks & crafts | offbeat |

Budget ~RM15–30/person at Kuah, RM20–40 at Pantai Cenang [[2]](https://my.trip.com/guide/food/what-to-eat-in-langkawi.html). Bring a reusable container — these markets drown in styrofoam [[6]](https://www.theislanddrum.com/langkawi-things-to-see-places-to-eat/langkawi-places-to-eat/langkawi-night-market-schedule/).

## Eat-here-for-the-story: unusual locations

- **Hole in the Wall — the floating fish-farm kitchen.** *Kilim mangroves (east). Offbeat-but-popular.* Langkawi's only floating restaurant, resting on a raft of drums on the Kilim River, ~7 min by boat from Kilim Jetty inside the Geoforest Park; run by owner Rahmad since 2003 [[8]](https://www.langkawi-insight.com/langkawi_00001d.htm)[[10]](https://malaysia-asia.my/hole-in-the-wall-floating-restaurant-and-fish-farm-in-langkawi/). It doubles as a working fish farm — sea bass, moray eels, stingrays you can hand-feed — and cooks prawn, crab, snapper, grouper and squid Malay- or Thai-style straight from the pens [[7]](https://www.mangrovetourslangkawi.com/post/floating-restaurant-fish-breeding-farm). Usually bundled into Kilim mangrove tours; also sold as a "floating restaurant + snorkelling" day trip [[9]](https://www.getyourguide.com/kuah-l206030/langkawi-floating-restaurant-snorkeling-trip-t793121/). This is *the* signature where-am-I-eating moment.
- **The Cliff.** *Pantai Cenang. Touristy (the famous one).* Built out on a rocky headland over the Andaman Sea — the most dramatic sunset address on the beach strip; fusion Asian/continental, artful plating [[14]](https://www.discover-langkawi.com/restaurants-pantai-cenang). Book the sunset slot.
- **The Gulai House (The Datai).** *Datai Bay (NW jungle). Touristy-luxe.* Award-winning Malay-Indian curry house in a traditional *kampung* stilt house set in ancient rainforest — floor seating, lantern light, the soundtrack is forest wildlife; 60 seats, dinner 18:30–23:00 [[16]](https://www.thedatai.com/dining/the-gulai-house/)[[17]](https://www.thedatai.com/dining/). The jungle-dining splurge.
- **Kapal Layar.** *Pantai Kok. Touristy.* Boat-shaped restaurant with an open-air terrace over the water — butter prawns, salted-egg squid, group feasts at sunset [[15]](https://mytrip.my/top-10-seafood-restaurants-in-langkawi/).
- **Kelapa Grill.** *Tanjung Rhu (NE quiet beach). Touristy-luxe.* Resort beachfront grill — lobster, seafood paella on one of the island's calmest sands [[15]](https://mytrip.my/top-10-seafood-restaurants-in-langkawi/).

## Seafood + beer: the Kuah open-air feast (best value on the island)

The duty-free island's defining meal — Chinese-style (often Chinese-Muslim, no pork) open-air seafood with cold beer, riverside in Kuah.

- **[Wonderland Food Store](https://www.langkawi-insight.com/langkawi_000138.htm).** *Kuah. Touristy-but-deserved.* The longest-running and best-reviewed of Kuah's seafood houses, open-air beside a river; softshell crab with salted egg, butter prawns, steamed grouper. **~RM120 for four, including beer**; daily 18:00–23:00 [[11]](https://www.langkawi-insight.com/langkawi_000138.htm)[[12]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g298283-d948684-Reviews-Wonderland_Food_Store-Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html). Listed by Lonely Planet [[13]](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/malaysia/peninsular-malaysia-west-coast/pulau-langkawi/restaurants/wonderland-food-store/a/poi-eat/1327834/357005).
- **Langkawi Fish Farm Restaurant.** *Kuah. Offbeat.* Fresh-from-the-tank seafood and Nyonya steamed grouper on an over-water terrace [[15]](https://mytrip.my/top-10-seafood-restaurants-in-langkawi/).
- **Weng Fung.** *Kuah. Offbeat.* Old-school signature chilli crab and grouper [[15]](https://mytrip.my/top-10-seafood-restaurants-in-langkawi/).
- **Orkid Ria.** *Pantai Cenang. Touristy.* Founded by "Mother Loh," now seats 250 and fills every night — live aquarium, grouper and lobster; 11:00–15:00 & 18:00–23:00 [[14]](https://www.discover-langkawi.com/restaurants-pantai-cenang).
- **Happy Happy Cenang.** *Pantai Cenang. Touristy.* Affordable live-seafood, garlic lobster, salted-egg crab, lazy-susan family tables [[14]](https://www.discover-langkawi.com/restaurants-pantai-cenang)[[15]](https://mytrip.my/top-10-seafood-restaurants-in-langkawi/).

## Special-occasion & legendary tables

- **[fatCUPID](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g2053325-d2661653-Reviews-FatCUPID-Pantai_Tengah_Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html).** *Pantai Tengah. Offbeat.* Tucked down a quiet lane in the grounds of La Pari-Pari; run by three cousins cooking their grandmother's **Nyonya** recipes plus Aussie touches — a cult breakfast/lunch and intimate dinner spot [[24]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g2053325-d2661653-Reviews-FatCUPID-Pantai_Tengah_Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html).
- **[Unkaizan](https://www.langkawi-insight.com/langkawi_0000f4.htm).** *Pantai Tengah (up a hill, hidden). Offbeat-legendary.* Chef Katsuji Takabayashi's hillside Japanese house — sashimi, charcoal grills; voted Malaysian Tatler's best restaurant in Malaysia six years running from 2005 [[23]](https://www.langkawi-insight.com/langkawi_0000f4.htm)[[22]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g2053325-d948680-Reviews-Unkaizan-Pantai_Tengah_Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html).
- **The Datai's dining cluster.** *Datai Bay. Touristy-luxe.* Beyond the Gulai House: The Pavilion (open-air Thai), The Dining Room (fine dining) and a beach club over the water; the resort's **2026 "Chef Series"** flies in Michelin-starred chefs through the year for ticketed dinners [[17]](https://www.thedatai.com/dining/)[[18]](https://www.thedatai.com/events-and-occasions/the-chef-series/).

## Cooking classes & food tours

- **Four Seasons — Lesung Batu Cooking School.** *Tanjung Rhu (NE). Luxe.* Hands-on Malay cookery at the luxury resort [[19]](https://www.fourseasons.com/langkawi/dining/culinary-experiences/culinary-activities/lesung-batu-cooking-school/).
- **The Datai — The Dapur.** *Datai Bay. Luxe.* Garden-to-table class: forage ingredients with a Malay/Thai/Indian chef, cook, then lunch with a paired wine [[17]](https://www.thedatai.com/dining/).
- **Casa del Mar.** *Pantai Cenang. Mid.* Step-by-step 3-course Malay class with the resort chef [[20]](https://casadelmar-langkawi.com/activity/cooking-class).
- **Food tours / floating lunch.** Guided evening street-food crawls "skip the tourist restaurants" with a private driver; a popular alternative is the **Floating Lunch — local cook-and-grill** of fresh seafood off a pontoon [[38]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g298283-Activities-c36-Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html)[[21]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g298283-Activities-c36-t203-Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html). The Kuah Wed/Sat night market is itself the cheapest "food tour" — real street food and fruit you won't recognise [[5]](https://mytrip.my/langkawi-night-markets-schedule-timings-locations-travel-guide/).

## A first-timer's 3-meal day (sample)

- **Morning:** roti canai + teh tarik at a Kuah mamak, or nasi lemak [[1]](https://www.airasia.com/blog/malaysia/langkawi/must-try-langkawi-food/)[[3]](https://langkawimangrovetrip.com/blog/best-makan-langkawi/).
- **Lunch in the mangroves:** longtail to **Hole in the Wall** for tank-fresh crab [[7]](https://www.mangrovetourslangkawi.com/post/floating-restaurant-fish-breeding-farm).
- **Sunset + dinner:** **Laksa Power** cendol at golden hour, then whichever **pasar malam** is on that night for satay and cucur udang — or push the boat out at **Wonderland** in Kuah with duty-free-cheap beer [[29]](https://tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g298283-d1144527-Reviews-Nasi_Kandar_Tomato-Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html)[[5]](https://mytrip.my/langkawi-night-markets-schedule-timings-locations-travel-guide/)[[11]](https://www.langkawi-insight.com/langkawi_000138.htm).

Cross-checks on the dish canon and halal/local picks: KKday and Wahdah corroborate laksa, ikan bakar, char kway teow and cucur udang as the local short-list [[34]](https://www.kkday.com/en-sg/blog/32894/asia-malaysia-best-food-in-langkawi)[[35]](https://www.wahdah.my/blog/top-15-halal-restaurants-eateries-in-langkawi); general beachside-café and where-to-eat framing per Foreverbreak and Maisinggah [[37]](https://foreverbreak.com/travel/malaysia/langkawi/restaurants/)[[39]](https://www.maisinggah.com/tempat-makan-menarik-di-langkawi/); street-food staples (cendol, pisang goreng, satay) per TripAdvisor's street-food list [[36]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurants-g298283-c10686-Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html). Nasi Kandar Tomato (Pantai Cenang, daily 12:00–23:30) is the late-night curry-rice standby [[29]](https://tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g298283-d1144527-Reviews-Nasi_Kandar_Tomato-Langkawi_Langkawi_District_Kedah.html).
