> **Decision.** Only one property is a comfortable walk back from a wine-paired dinner: **[Casa Liebaard](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/en/a/pXu3GojGAs9G/casa-liebaard)** in Desselgem, 2.1 km / ~28 min on foot [[5]](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/en/a/pXu3GojGAs9G/casa-liebaard) [[19]](https://router.project-osrm.org/). Beyond it, **[The Cotton Suites](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/en/a/np4Elu73Qapf/the-cotton-suites-bb)** (3.3 km) and **[La Maison des Papillons](https://lamaisondespapillons.be/)** (4.0 km) are 40–55 min strolls — fine if the digestif walk is part of the plan, otherwise a 5-minute taxi. Anything in Waregem proper ([Hotel T](https://www.hotel-t.be/), [ParkHotel](https://www.parkhotelwaregem.be/), [Au Paradis](https://au-paradis.be/)) is 6.7–7.8 km — book the cab on the way in [[20]](https://www.taxiwaregemcity.be/).

## Why the choice is so narrow

[Castor](https://cas-tor.be) is a two-Michelin-star restaurant [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/west-vlaanderen/beveren-leie/restaurant/castor) on the N43 at Kortrijkseweg 164, 8791 Beveren-Leie [[1]](https://cas-tor.be/en/contact). Beveren-Leie itself is a residential strip-village absorbed into the municipality of Waregem; **there are zero B&Bs or hotels inside postal code 8791** — every "near Beveren-Leie" listing on the Belgian B&B index starts in a neighbouring town [[4]](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/nl/l/2802034/beveren-leie). Castor's own *Stay the night* page bears this out: its shortest recommendation is 4 km away in Deerlijk [[3]](https://cas-tor.be/en/stay-the-night). The bedandbreakfast.eu directory finds one option closer — in Desselgem, the next village east, 1.3 km from Castor as the crow flies [[4]](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/nl/l/2802034/beveren-leie).

## The picks

### 1. Casa Liebaard — the only true walk back &nbsp; *(2.1 km, ~28 min)*

A 90 m² ground-floor self-catering apartment on Liebaardstraat in Desselgem: two bedrooms, equipped kitchen, walk-in shower, private terrace, free parking and EV charging. 9.5/10 across 114 reviews on bedandbreakfast.eu, 9.3 on Booking; rates from ~US$102/night [[5]](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/en/a/pXu3GojGAs9G/casa-liebaard) [[6]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/sfeervolle-accommodatie.nl.html). **No breakfast** — it's a self-catering let, not a B&B; the walk-back route follows the Liebaardstraat → Schoolstraat → Kortrijkseweg axis. Single ground-floor unit, so book early — Castor weekends fill it.

### 2. The Cotton Suites — long walk or 5-min cab &nbsp; *(3.3 km, ~44 min)*

A 5-bedroom boutique B&B at Kortrijkse Heerweg 5a in Deerlijk with **indoor pool, fitness room, bike rental**, à-la-carte breakfast including warm dishes and champagne. 8.2/10 across 48 reviews on bedandbreakfast.eu and a perfect-but-single 5.0 on Tripadvisor; nightly rates $143–217 [[15]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3247613-d33466719-Reviews-The_Cotton_Suites_B_b-Deerlijk_West_Flanders_Province.html) [[16]](https://www.bedandbreakfast.eu/en/a/np4Elu73Qapf/the-cotton-suites-bb). The 3.3 km route is rural and quiet; doable after a light tasting menu, less so after the full pairing.

### 3. La Maison des Papillons — pool-and-garden B&B &nbsp; *(4.0 km, ~53 min)*

The closest property on Castor's own list [[3]](https://cas-tor.be/en/stay-the-night). Just two rooms at Desselgemse Steenweg 26 in Deerlijk, with **outdoor pool, jacuzzi**, garden, air-conditioning, blackout curtains [[10]](https://lamaisondespapillons.be/) [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3247613-d23498655-Reviews-La_Maison_Des_Papillons-Deerlijk_West_Flanders_Province.html). Pricier ($189–197) and the review base is thin (4 reviews, all 5★). 53 min on foot is past most diners' tolerance after a 9pm sitting — treat it as taxi-distance.

### 4. Blue Woods Hotel — best hotel option &nbsp; *(5.1 km, taxi)*

Four-star, 18 soundproofed rooms, **heated outdoor pool (Apr–Oct), sauna, Sky Lounge, 80-space underground parking, complimentary breakfast** [[12]](https://www.bluewoodshotel.be/nl/) [[13]](https://www.bluewoodshotel.be/en/). 8.6/10 on Booking across 388 reviews — the only property here with a large, statistically meaningful sample [[14]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/blue-woods.html). Rates from ~$164. At 5.1 km / 68 min walking it's a non-starter on foot after dinner; 5-minute drive.

### 5. Waregem-centre options — only if you want a town base

[Au Paradis Guesthouse](https://au-paradis.be/) (Zeswegenstraat 115) [[7]](https://au-paradis.be/contact) is a single deluxe apartment for up to 4 with sauna and **a 7-minute walk to Waregem station** [[8]](https://au-paradis.be/); $100–127, but only 3 Tripadvisor reviews to date [[9]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1081544-d1493310-Reviews-Au_Paradis_Guesthouse-Waregem_West_Flanders_Province.html). [Hotel T](https://www.hotel-t.be/) (Stationsstraat 154) [[17]](https://www.hotel-t.be/) and [ParkHotel Waregem](https://www.parkhotelwaregem.be/) (Zuiderlaan 31) [[18]](https://www.parkhotelwaregem.be/) sit in town. All three are 6.7–7.8 km from Castor — taxi territory either direction.

## Logistics

- **Taxi.** [Taxi Waregem City](https://www.taxiwaregemcity.be/) operates 24/7 at +32 470 41 75 40 [[20]](https://www.taxiwaregemcity.be/). Book the return on the way in — Beveren-Leie has no rank.
- **Parking.** Castor has two car parks: in front, and behind via the Stuivenbergstraat intersection [[1]](https://cas-tor.be/en/contact). Every option above has free parking, so driving + designated driver also works.
- **Closed days.** Castor closes Sun/Mon/Tue [[1]](https://cas-tor.be/en/contact) — book lodging on a Wed–Sat night.

## Source-credibility note

The two "perfect" ratings in the table (La Maison des Papillons 5.0/5, Au Paradis 4.3/5) are based on **3–4 reviews each** — treat them as anecdotal. Casa Liebaard (114 reviews) and Blue Woods (388 reviews) are the only properties here with large enough samples for the score to be load-bearing.
