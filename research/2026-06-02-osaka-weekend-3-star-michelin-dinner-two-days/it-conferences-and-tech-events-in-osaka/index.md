### Google I/O Extended 2026 Osaka — Jul 18

[GDG Greater Kwansai](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-greater-kwansai/)'s local event sharing and discussing Google I/O announcements. [[1]](https://gdgkwansai.connpass.com/event/391029/) Sessions span AI, Cloud, Android, Web, and Flutter. 13:30–18:00 at QUINTBRIDGE (Higashinodacho, Osaka); networking 16:30–17:30. Separate paid social afterward. Register on connpass; ~56 of 100 in-person spots taken as of early June.

### Developers Summit 2026 KANSAI — Aug 21

Shoeisha/CodeZine's annual flagship developer conference in Kansai, running since 2012. [[2]](https://event.shoeisha.jp/devsumi/20260821) Theme: **"Re:Developer — Engineer Redefinition"** — how AI advances reshape developer roles, team structures, career paths, and security practices. Venue: Umeda South Hall, 11F, Osaka Umeda Twin Towers South. Free; **registration opens early July**. In-person only; no recording or photography. 18 sessions across 2 tracks, curated by a 9-person regional content committee.

### Global Startup EXPO 2026 (GSE2026) — Oct 5–7

METI/JETRO/NEDO flagship deep-tech event, successor to the 2025 Expo-era edition. [[3]](https://global-startup-expo.com/) [[5]](https://www.meti.go.jp/english/press/2026/0203_002.html) Focus sectors: bio/healthcare, AI & robotics, fusion energy, quantum, semiconductors/comms, and space. Primary venues: Knowledge Capital Congress Convention Center (Grand Front Osaka North Bldg, B2F) and Congress Square Grand Green Osaka (South Bldg, 4F) — both within 5 min of JR Osaka Station. Side events in Kyoto and Hyogo.

### Japan IT Week & Japan DX Week [Osaka] — Nov 18–20

Western Japan's largest IT/DX trade show. [[4]](https://www.japan-it.jp/osaka/en-gb.html) 10:00–17:00 daily at INTEX Osaka (Suminoe-ku). 13 sub-expos across two umbrella shows:

**Japan IT Week:** Software Dev & Engineering Support · Embedded & Edge AI · IoT Systems Dev · Information Security · AI Infrastructure & Data Center · IT Staffing & Talent Development

**Japan DX Week:** AI & Business Automation · Office-work DX · Data-Driven Management · Deskless Work DX · B2B E-Commerce DX · Sales DX · Digital Marketing

## Recurring Community Events

### [OKTech](https://oktech.jp/) — Monthly, English-friendly
Kansai's largest English-speaking tech meetup, 2,000+ members. [[9]](https://oktech.jp/) Monthly Saturday sessions, typically 17:00–19:30. **Upcoming:** Jun 20 (R3 Institute/gusuku Ashibinaa OSAKA) · Jul 18 (Cybozu Osaka, 35F) · Aug 29 (R3 Institute). Topics rotate across web dev, AI/ML, QA, software architecture. Free; join via [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/) or Discord.

### [Lambda Kansai](https://lambda-kansai.connpass.com/) — Functional Programming
Regular reading groups (currently *Functional Programming in Lean*) and occasional in-person sessions. [[10]](https://lambda-kansai.connpass.com/) Primarily online; Osaka-based community.
