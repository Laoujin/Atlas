Venue — COEX

  • 513 Yeongdong-daero, Gangnam-gu [15]
  • Subway: Samseong (Line 2), exit 5 or 6 — 3 min walk
  • Most trade shows: pre-register for free / discounted entry; walk-in rates are 4–5× higher

Peak window

Aug 11–21 packs four events into 11 days: OSS Korea + MCP Dev Summit (Aug 11–14) then AI Summit Seoul (Aug 19–21). Best single stretch for a developer-focused visit. UbuCon on Aug 29 extends the run if you can stay. [13]

Language & clusters

  • Trade floors: primarily Korean
  • Linux Foundation & AI Summit: English
  • Dev Korea: explicitly English-first [4]
  • Startup scene: Seongsu-dong [14]
  • GDG Seoul runs free monthly workshops [16]
