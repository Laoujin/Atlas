> **TL;DR** Seoul's tech event calendar peaks in June and August, almost entirely at [COEX](https://www.coexcenter.com/) in Gangnam. For practitioners: **Open Source Summit Korea + MCP Dev Summit** (Aug 11–14) is the highest-signal developer week, followed by **AI Summit Seoul** (Aug 19–21). For industry scale: **Smart Tech Korea** (Jun 10–12) is the year's largest umbrella expo with 500+ exhibitors. **Korea Blockchain Week** (Sep 29–Oct 1) at Walkerhill is the region's crypto flagship. Community meetups (Dev Korea, GDG Seoul) run year-round and are free.

## Major Trade Expos

**[AI Expo Korea 2026](https://www.aiexpo.co.kr/en/v1.php?s=31)** [[1]](https://www.aiexpo.co.kr/en/v1.php?s=31) — May 6–8 at COEX Hall A. Korea's dedicated AI exhibition; pre-registration before March 31 was free, on-site entry is KRW 20,000. English-language access is limited at individual booths, but the show floor is navigable.

**[Smart Tech Korea (STK) 2026](https://en.smarttechkorea.com/Exhibition)** [[2]](https://en.smarttechkorea.com/Exhibition) [[3]](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/designing-the-future-with-ai--smart-tech-korea-2026-brings-together-six-shows-shaping-the-next-era-of-smart-connected-industries-302703670.html) — Jun 10–12. The 15th edition runs six co-located shows: AI & Big Data Show (Hall A, 500 exhibitors / 2,000 booths), Robot Tech Show, Smart Tech Show, Secu Tech Show, Retail & Logis Tech Show, and AI Factory Expo. Theme: "Designing the Future with AI." Largest single-venue tech event in Seoul by exhibitor count.

**[Metaverse Expo (MVEX) 2026](https://metavexpo.com/eng/)** [[5]](https://metavexpo.com/eng/) — Jun 17–19 at COEX. ~500 exhibitors, ~50,000 expected visitors. Covers XR/VR/AR, digital twins, spatial computing, and AI integration across manufacturing, healthcare, and education.

**[IMID Korea Display Exhibition](https://www.cantonfair.net/updated-information/imid-korea-display-industry-exhibition-2026-seoul-will-be-on-2026-07-22)** [[6]](https://www.cantonfair.net/updated-information/imid-korea-display-industry-exhibition-2026-seoul-will-be-on-2026-07-22) — Jul 22–24 at COEX, Gangnam. Korea's premier display industry show — OLED, micro-LED, and automotive display tech. Industry-facing; relevant for hardware and semiconductor engineers.

**[AI Summit Seoul & Expo](https://www.aisummitseoul.com/)** [[9]](https://www.aisummitseoul.com/) — Aug 19–21 at COEX Grand Ballroom + Exhibition Hall B. 9th edition; organized by DMK Global and COEX. Focus on Agentic AI, enterprise LLM deployment, RAG, and AI+Robotics. Early bird registration open; contact lucy@dmkglobal.co.kr.

## Developer Conferences

**[Open Source Summit Korea](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/open-source-summit-korea/)** [[7]](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/open-source-summit-korea/) — Aug 11–12. Linux Foundation flagship in Seoul. Two days of keynotes and breakout sessions across cloud-native, open source tooling, and community. Travel grants and scholarships available. Gold sponsors include Red Hat and OpenSearch.

**[MCP Dev Summit Seoul](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/mcp-dev-summit-seoul/)** [[8]](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/mcp-dev-summit-seoul/) — Aug 13–14, co-located with Open Source Summit Korea. Agentic AI Foundation × Linux Foundation event focused on Model Context Protocol (MCP), multi-agent orchestration, enterprise integration, and production security. Seoul's consumer tech and gaming industry makes it a natural fit for large-scale agentic infrastructure talks.

Running Aug 11–14 back-to-back across both events is the most efficient use of time for engineers working in AI infrastructure.

**[UbuCon Korea × MiniDebConf Korea 2026](https://discourse.ubuntu.com/t/ubucon-korea-x-minidebconf-korea-2026/81652)** [[10]](https://discourse.ubuntu.com/t/ubucon-korea-x-minidebconf-korea-2026/81652) [[13]](https://dev.events/AS/KR/Seoul) — Aug 29 at Microsoft Korea 13F. Joint Ubuntu + Debian community conference. Smaller, free, community-run. Exact schedule TBD (CFP closed June 4, 2026).

## Community Meetups

**[Dev Korea](https://dev-korea.com/events)** [[4]](https://dev-korea.com/events) — English-language tech meetup series. Dev Korea #9 on June 17 (antifragile software). Free, held ~monthly. Past editions partnered with Supabase, ClickHouse, PostHog. Best option for visiting developers looking to network with Seoul's English-speaking tech community.

**[GDG Seoul](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-seoul/)** [[16]](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-seoul/) — Google Developer Group chapter, runs local workshops and study jams. Ran "Build with AI Seoul 2026 with Google DeepMind" in May. Check the GDG page for upcoming events; cadence is roughly monthly.

## Practical Notes

- **COEX** (513 Yeongdong-daero, Gangnam-gu) [[15]](https://www.coexcenter.com/events/ai-expo-korea-2026/) is the epicenter — subway: Samseong Station (Line 2), exit 5 or 6. Three-minute walk to all halls.
- Most trade shows require **pre-registration** for free or reduced entry; walk-in rates are 4–5× higher.
- **Language:** trade show floors are primarily Korean; major developer conferences (Linux Foundation, AI Summit) run in English. Dev Korea is explicitly English-first.
- **Tech clusters nearby:** Gangnam/COEX for enterprise tech; Seongsu-dong for startup scene [[14]](https://www.nucamp.co/blog/top-10-tech-coworking-spaces-and-incubators-in-south-korea-in-2026); Pangyo Techno Valley (~30 min south, home to Naver, Kakao) for deep-tech.
- **Peak window:** mid-August (Aug 11–21) packs Open Source Summit, MCP Dev Summit, and AI Summit Seoul into 11 days — the highest-density stretch for a developer-focused visit.
