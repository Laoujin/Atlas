> **Decision.** For pure Afrobeats/R&B, the best in-reach picks are **[Couleur Café](https://www.couleurcafe.be/en/)** in Brussels (26–28 Jun, ~35 min from Ghent, the strongest genre fit) [[29]](https://www.couleurcafe.be/en/) and **[Daniel Caesar](https://thatgrapejuice.net/2026/05/daniel-caesar-expands-son-of-spergy-tour-with-new-uk-european-dates/)** at Brussels Forest National (8 Sep) [[13]](https://thatgrapejuice.net/2026/05/daniel-caesar-expands-son-of-spergy-tour-with-new-uk-european-dates/), with **[Omah Lay](https://www.livenation.be/event/omah-lay-clarity-of-mind-tour-brussels-tickets-edp1666899)** (Forest National, 6 Nov) and **[Asake](https://www.songkick.com/artists/10243489-asake/calendar)** (Paris/London, Oct) the headline Afrobeats arena shows [[55]](https://www.livenation.be/event/omah-lay-clarity-of-mind-tour-brussels-tickets-edp1666899)[[2]](https://www.songkick.com/artists/10243489-asake/calendar). For big-room pop, July is a stadium glut — **[The Weeknd](https://www.theweeknd.com/tour/)** (Lille/Paris/Amsterdam/Frankfurt/London) [[19]](https://www.theweeknd.com/tour/) and **[SZA](https://www.joe.co.uk/music/kendrick-lamar-sza-tour-london-paris-dates-tickets-475831)** with Kendrick Lamar (Cologne/Amsterdam/Paris/London) [[9]](https://www.joe.co.uk/music/kendrick-lamar-sza-tour-london-paris-dates-tickets-475831) — but both are resale-only at this point. Note the UK's marquee Afrobeats festival, **Wireless London, is cancelled** [[31]](https://localpage.uk/blog/wireless-festival-uk-2026-dates-lineup-ticket-guide), and genre-perfect **Afro Nation is in Portugal** — a flight, not a drive [[27]](https://www.rap-up.com/article/afro-nation-portugal-2026-lineup-tyla-gunna-mariah-the-scientist).

Scope: confirmed shows from **9 Jun 2026 onward**, in cities reachable from Gent-Sint-Pieters in 4–6h (Brussels, Antwerp, Lille, the Dutch Randstad, Paris, London via Eurostar, and the German Rhine). All eleven target cities fall comfortably inside the band — see [Getting there](#getting-there).

## Don't bother refreshing these

- **Afro Nation Portugal** (3–5 Jul, Portimão) — genre-perfect (Wizkid, Tyla, Burna Boy, Asake) but in the Algarve: a flight, outside surface reach [[27]](https://www.rap-up.com/article/afro-nation-portugal-2026-lineup-tyla-gunna-mariah-the-scientist)[[28]](https://www.afronation.com/).
- **Wireless London** (Finsbury Park, 10–12 Jul) — **cancelled** after the Home Office blocked headliner Ye [[31]](https://localpage.uk/blog/wireless-festival-uk-2026-dates-lineup-ticket-guide).
- **Rolling Loud Europe** — no 2026 dates, venue or line-up announced; its Rotterdam Ahoy slot (the former WOO HAH!) is unconfirmed [[32]](https://www.rollingloud.com/europe)[[39]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WOO_HAH!).
- **We Are FSTVL** (Essex) — returns summer 2026 but is house/techno, no Afro/R&B focus and nothing announced [[38]](https://www.wearefstvl.com/line-up/). *(Encore Amsterdam is a monthly club night, not a festival [[40]](https://encoreamsterdam.nl/).)*
- **No 2026 regional dates:** Chris Brown & Usher (The R&B Tour is North America-only) [[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breezy_Bowl_XX); PARTYNEXTDOOR (Drake run was 2025) [[18]](https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/drake-partynextdoor-uk-europe-tour-dates-tickets-1235987958/); Cleo Sol & Brent Faiyaz (off tour) [[16]](https://www.songkick.com/artists/2262208-cleo-sol); Dua Lipa (no 2026 tour) [[25]](https://www.songkick.com/artists/8310783-dua-lipa); Olivia Rodrigo (Amsterdam/London/Paris are spring **2027**) [[24]](https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/olivia-rodrigo-adds-shows-unraveled-world-tour-dates-1236241301/); Charli XCX (North America + Reading/Leeds only) [[26]](https://consequence.net/2026/06/charli-xcx-music-fashion-film-tour/).
