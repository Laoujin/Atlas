The call

One spare day → Hydra on the early hydrofoil. 07:30 out, 17:00 or 18:30 back, 7–8 hours ashore on a car-free island for €60–120 all-in [24]. If you want antiquity instead of swimming, Delphi — 8–10 hours organised, €33–84 with entry included [25].

Half a day only → Cape Sounion at sunset, not at midday [2].

Two entries on this board are traps: Meteora in a day — the direct train is suspended until late 2026/mid-2027 [4] — and the one-day three-island cruise, 11 hours bought for 4.5 hours ashore [6].

The two traps, in full

Meteora as a day trip

9 h transit~3 h on site€54+ each way€5 cash per monastery

The direct Athens–Kalambaka train is suspended until late 2026/mid-2027; what remains is a 4 h 30 express coach [4]. The Meteora monasteries then work against you: Great Meteoron 09:30–15:00, Holy Trinity 10:00–16:00, each closed on a different weekday, each cash-only at the gate [5].

The guide who mapped the route is blunt about it: I personally wouldn't do it… it's a LONG day! [4]

The one-day three-island cruise

6.5 h afloat4.5 h ashore, in 3 pieces€110–130

07:00 to 18:00 for one hour at Poros, an hour and a half at Hydra and two hours at Aegina [6]. Travellers who booked it in the Rick Steves forum: It appears you'll spend more time on a boat than actual time on each island, plus non-stop smoking on the open rear deck where the views are [7].

The hydrofoil to one island costs less and buys 6–7 hours ashore instead of 1.5 [6].

The heat overrides the ranking

43.5 °CGreece's first 2026 heatwave peaked at 43.5 °C on 22 July, and fires reached Psatha near Athens on 2 August before being contained by 4 August [22]. Forecasters expect August 2026 to run hotter than July [23].

On extreme-heat days the Culture Ministry shuts the Acropolis and other archaeological sites from roughly midday to 17:00 [23] — a ruins day trip can arrive to a closed gate. Two consequences:

  • Front-load any archaeology to 08:00–12:00. Delphi opens 08:00 in summer [3]; Mycenae and Epidaurus open 08:00 [13].
  • Trips whose payoff is water or altitude — Hydra, Aegina, the Riviera, Delphi at 600 m — beat trips whose payoff is a bare exposed field: Corinth, Mycenae, Marathon.

When the board says stay

Kaisariani, €3, 08:30–16:00

Bus 224 from Syntagma to the last stop, then a 40-minute uphill walk; 1–2 hours inside walled, shaded cypress grounds. Closed Tuesdays [15].

Or move the trip to the tail

Nafplio, Mycenae and Epidaurus stop being a 10–12 hour day the moment they stop being a day trip [13] — see the mainland tail and the island cluster angles below.
