Piraeus · Rafina · Lavrio

Three ports, two vessel classes, one wind. The board below is the whole decision: which quay, which hull, how much of your week each crossing eats — and the four legs where the aeroplane finally wins.

Routes and fares [2]
Fare trap · the airport zoneThe airport metro is a separate zone starting at Doukissis Plakentias: €9 single, €16 return. A normal €1.20 city ticket will not open the barrier. [24]
Fare trap · the €40 flat fareThe airport taxi flat fare covers central Athens only. Piraeus sits outside the zone, so the meter runs: €50–58 by day. The X96 alternative runs 24 h from between Exits 4 and 5, every 30–40 min. [11] [12]

If it cancels EU Regulation 1177/2010 gives you a choice of “reimbursement of your ticket within seven days, or re-routing to your final destination under comparable conditions at the earliest opportunity, at no extra cost.” [9] Weather is an extraordinary circumstance, so the operator owes you the fare or a seat — not your hotel, not your missed flight [9]. Refunds are not automatic: request one and keep the cancellation notice. Operators notify by email or SMS [7].

Booking & boarding

05 / 07

Ticket sequence

−3 moBook. Two to three months ahead for a July/August crossing; three to four if you need a cabin or a car slot. Cabins vanish first, and ferry prices do not surge like airfares — early booking costs nothing [8].
−48 hBlue Star web check-in opens [15].
−2 hWeb check-in closes. You get a QR e-ticket shown with ID or passport — no printing [15].
−1 hLast moment to collect paper at the port office with your reservation code, if you skipped web check-in [14]. Smaller operators and third-party bookings are less forgiving — verify per operator, not per booking site.
Sold out?Blue Star deck seats rarely go. SeaJets seats, cabins and vehicle spaces routinely do in July–August [7].

Vehicle supplement

€30–74

Taking a rental car across needs the rental company's permission in writing, and standard insurance may lapse while the car is at sea [16] [17].

Unauthorised crossings void all protection [17]. Island one-way drop-offs are almost never permitted — rent fresh on each island [16].
