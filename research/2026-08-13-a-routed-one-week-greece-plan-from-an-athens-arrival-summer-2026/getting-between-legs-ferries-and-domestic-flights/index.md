> **Decision.** Ferry by default: Piraeus for anything except Mykonos/Tinos/Andros, Rafina for those three because it is 30 minutes from ATH by KTEL bus at €4–5.50 [[13]](https://myartemida.gr/en/article/direct-bus-from-athens-airport-to-rafina-and-artemida---timetable-prices--stops/). Fly only when the crossing exceeds ~7 hours — Crete (7–13 h) and Rhodes (12–22 h) [[4]](https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/ferries/greece/piraeus). And never put the return leg on the day of your international flight home: a weather cancellation makes you a no-show and nobody pays [[9]](https://greece-trips.com/travel-guide/when-a-ferry-is-cancelled/).

Two fare traps. The airport metro is a separate zone starting at Doukissis Plakentias — €9 single / €16 return, and a normal €1.20 city ticket will not open the barrier [[24]](https://publictransportguide.com/athens-metro-ticket-prices-2026/). The €40 taxi flat fare covers central Athens only; Piraeus is outside the zone, so the meter runs and you pay €50–58 by day [[11]](https://taxitoathens.com/athens-airport-to-piraeus-port/). The X96 runs 24 h from arrivals between Exits 4 and 5, every 30–40 min [[12]](https://www.athensairportbus.com/en/timetable/x96airporttopiraeusport.html); the Rafina KTEL leaves from Exits 2–3, roughly hourly, first 04:30 and last 23:00, cash only [[13]](https://myartemida.gr/en/article/direct-bus-from-athens-airport-to-rafina-and-artemida---timetable-prices--stops/).

## Piraeus gates and buffer

Piraeus has 12 gates (E1–E12) spread around a working harbour, and your gate is printed on the ticket [[25]](https://www.athenstourism.org/getting-around-athens/athens-to-greek-islands-ferry-guide/). Blue Star's own port map shows the pattern: E1 for Santorini/Crete/Dodecanese sailings, E6 and E7 for Cyclades departures (E6 foot passengers, E7 passengers plus vehicles), E9 for Saronic high-speeds to Aegina, Hydra and Spetses [[3]](https://www.bluestarferries.com/en-gb/piraeus-port-map). Assignments shift in summer — confirm against the electronic boards on the day.

Buffer: 60–90 minutes at the gate [[25]](https://www.athenstourism.org/getting-around-athens/athens-to-greek-islands-ferry-guide/), and 90 min–2 h door-to-departure from central Athens in July/August because ferries do not wait [[11]](https://taxitoathens.com/athens-airport-to-piraeus-port/). Coming off an international flight, allow ≥4 h to a Rafina sailing and ≥5 h to Piraeus [[1]](https://ferrygogo.com/athens-ferry-ports-compared-piraeus-rafina-or-lavrio/).

## Meltemi, cancellations and what happens to the booking

The meltemi is a dry north wind blowing June–September, peaking July–August at 7–8 Beaufort and occasionally over 120 km/h; it strengthens mid-morning, eases by sunset, and runs in spells of up to a week [[10]](https://www.davestravelpages.com/what-are-the-meltemi-winds-in-greece/). Mykonos, Tinos and Andros take the worst of it [[10]](https://www.davestravelpages.com/what-are-the-meltemi-winds-in-greece/). Practical hedge: book morning departures and large ships.

When a sailing is cancelled, EU Regulation 1177/2010 gives you a choice of "reimbursement of your ticket within seven days, or re-routing to your final destination under comparable conditions at the earliest opportunity, at no extra cost" [[9]](https://greece-trips.com/travel-guide/when-a-ferry-is-cancelled/). Weather counts as an extraordinary circumstance, so the operator owes you the fare or a seat — not your hotel, not your missed flight [[9]](https://greece-trips.com/travel-guide/when-a-ferry-is-cancelled/). Refunds are not automatic; request one and keep the cancellation notice. Operators notify by email/SMS [[7]](https://santorinidave.com/greek-ferries). → Sleep in Athens the night before you fly home.

## Booking and tickets

Book 2–3 months ahead for a July/August crossing, 3–4 months if you need a cabin or a car slot — cabins vanish first, and prices do not surge like airfares, so early booking costs nothing [[8]](https://www.go-ferry.com/trends-and-insights/greek-ferries). Blue Star deck seats rarely sell out; SeaJets seats, cabins and vehicle spaces routinely do in July–August [[7]](https://santorinidave.com/greek-ferries).

**Tickets.** Blue Star web check-in opens 48 h and closes 2 h before departure, issuing a QR e-ticket you show with ID or passport — no printing needed [[15]](https://www.bluestarferries.com/en-gb/online-booking-web-check-in). If you skipped web check-in, collect paper at the port office with your reservation code up to 1 h before departure [[14]](https://ferries-greece.com/ferry-companies/blue-star-ferries/ticket-collection/). Smaller operators and third-party bookings are less forgiving — verify per operator, not per booking site.

**Rental cars.** Ferry travel needs the rental company's permission in writing; fees run ~€30–74 and standard insurance may lapse while the car is at sea [[16]](https://santorinidave.com/rent-car-greece) [[17]](https://koscarhire.com/taking-rental-cars-ferry/). Unauthorised crossings void all protection [[17]](https://koscarhire.com/taking-rental-cars-ferry/). Island one-way drop-offs are almost never permitted — rent fresh on each island instead [[16]](https://santorinidave.com/rent-car-greece).

## Flying instead

ATH–JTR is about 50 minutes in the air on Aegean, Sky Express, Volotea or Ryanair [[18]](https://blog.wego.com/athens-to-santorini/). Aegean one-ways start at €50 (August 2026 calendar €50–106) [[19]](https://flights.aegeanair.com/en/flights-from-athens-to-santorini); Sky Express from €44, with August dates €52–99 [[20]](https://www.skyexpress.gr/en/flights-from-athens-to-santorini). That is the same money as a SeaJets seat [[18]](https://blog.wego.com/athens-to-santorini/) — the argument is time, not price.

Door-to-door, though, the 50-minute flight carries 90+ minutes of security and bag claim [[25]](https://www.athenstourism.org/getting-around-athens/athens-to-greek-islands-ferry-guide/), plus the ATH transfer, plus the island end: JTR wants 1 h before a carry-on-only departure and 2 h+ with checked bags, and about 40 taxis serve the whole island at €30–45 to Fira or Oia [[22]](https://santorinidave.com/santorini-airport). Net: ~4 h door-to-door versus ~8 h by fast ferry from central Athens.

## Rule of thumb

- **Fast ferry to the near Cyclades (Paros, Naxos, Mykonos):** half a day. Leave the hotel at 07:00, be swimming by 14:00.
- **Any Piraeus→Santorini sailing:** a whole day, conventional or high-speed — 8 h at sea plus 2 h of buffer and transfers [[18]](https://blog.wego.com/athens-to-santorini/) [[11]](https://taxitoathens.com/athens-airport-to-piraeus-port/).
- **Flight to Santorini/Mykonos:** ~4 h door-to-door — half a day, saved only if you are already near ATH.
- **Crete or Rhodes:** fly, or accept an overnight ferry as the transfer *and* the hotel night [[4]](https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/ferries/greece/piraeus).

On a 7-night trip, budget one full day per island change. Three islands means three lost days — two is the honest maximum.
