Logistics

Getting around

  • Hola Barcelona 48h €18.70 / 72h €27.30 — includes airport L9 Sud, metro, bus, tram[28]
  • T-casual 10-trip €13 — cheaper/ride if you walk; needs separate €11.80 airport ticket
  • Aerobús: ~35 min airport→Plaça Catalunya, €7.75 one-way
  • All conference venues: metro 20–35 min from Eixample

Costs to know

  • Tourist tax doubled Apr 2026 — €12/night (5★), €8.40 (4★), capped 7 nights[27]
  • Park Güell: up ~80% to €18; not reflected in most travel guides[3]
  • Wine pairings: €140–180/person above menu price — budget separately[22]
  • Sagrada Família basic €26; tower add €10
  • Sat afternoon free: MNAC 15:00+, MACBA 16:00+ (both walk-in)

Safety & tips

  • Pickpockets, not violence. Hotspots: Las Ramblas, La Boqueria, Gothic Quarter, Barceloneta, metro L3/L4. Teams work by distraction[2]
  • Front crossbody bag; nothing in back pockets
  • Bunkers del Carmel closes at 19:30 — strictly enforced
  • Magic Fountain: Jun 3–Sep 27 only, Wed–Sun
  • Enigma closed weekends — plan accordingly
