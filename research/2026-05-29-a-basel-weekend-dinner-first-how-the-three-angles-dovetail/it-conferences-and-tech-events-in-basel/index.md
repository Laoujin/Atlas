### Why BaselOne is the one to pencil in for software people

BaselOne is the only **vendor-independent, pure software-development** conference in the city — committee-curated talks in English and German at the Markthalle (2 min from Basel SBB), organised by [Karakun](https://karakun.com/), [ERNI Schweiz](https://www.betterask.erni/), the Kanton Basel-Stadt IT department and [Baloise](https://www.baloise.com/) [[1]](https://baselone.org/en/baselone-home/)[[18]](https://baselone.org/en/about-us/). The 2025 edition drew 310+ attendees from IT, pharma, insurance, banking and retail across CH/DE/FR — a tight, multi-industry crowd rather than a pure-FAANG audience [[1]](https://baselone.org/en/baselone-home/). CfP for 2026 closed 30 April via Sessionize [[2]](https://sessionize.com/baselone-2026/).

### The Messe Basel life-sciences cluster

Four of the seven 2026 events are life-sciences-flavoured and run out of Messe Basel / Messeplatz — together they're the reason Basel reads as a "biotech IT" hub more than a generic developer hub. All four have explicit AI/data tracks aimed at engineers, not just biologists:

- **health.tech** (Mar) — joint venture of Bits & Pretzels and MCH Group; the 2025 premiere pulled ~6,000 participants [[6]](https://www.mch-group.com/en/media/news/2026/successful-premiere-of-the-healthtech-global-summit-in-basel-attracts-around-6000-participants).
- **Swiss Biotech Day** (May) — flagship of the Swiss Biotech Association; 2026 hit 3,500+ from 58 countries with explicit AI/data sessions [[7]](https://swissbiotechday.ch/).
- **Future Labs Live** (May) — Terrapinn-organised; tracks on "Automated, Integrated, Interoperable Discovery", "Data Management, Standards & Analytics" and "AI/ML" [[4]](https://www.terrapinn.com/conference/future-labs-live/index.stm).
- **BioTechX Europe** (Oct) — also Terrapinn; explicit tagline "Data. AI. Discovery. Healthcare." with 100+ biotech startups on the floor [[8]](https://www.terrapinn.com/conference/biotechx/index.stm).

If your day job is ML engineering for pharma, all four are realistically on your radar; if you ship React, only one is (BaselOne).

## What Basel isn't

Three honest gaps a visitor should know:

1. **No major dedicated cybersecurity conference.** Switzerland's flagship is the [Global Cyber Conference](https://globalcyberconference.com/) in **Zurich** (1–2 Sep 2026), not Basel — plan a day-trip.
2. **No big general-purpose web/JS conference.** BaselOne covers software development broadly; for JavaScript-specific tracks, the Swiss anchor is [ZurichJS](https://zurichjs.com/) (11 Sep 2026 at Technopark Zurich), 1h by train.
3. **The "Basel" listings on aggregator sites are misleading.** [dev.events](https://dev.events/EU/CH/Basel) and similar directories show "online events accessible from Basel" rather than physical Basel conferences — most of their listings are not in the city. Cross-check before booking travel.
