If you fly in for one thing

BaselOne is the pin.

Vendor-independent, committee-curated, EN/DE talks at the Markthalle (2 min from Basel SBB). The 2025 edition pulled 310+ attendees from IT, pharma, insurance, banking and retail across CH/DE/FR — multi-industry, not pure-FAANG [1]. Organised by Karakun, ERNI Schweiz, IT-S Kanton Basel-Stadt and Helvetia Baloise [18].

CfP closed 30 Apr 2026 via Sessionize [2].

Cross-angle collision

The 26 Sept deadline.

If you're piggybacking a tech trip onto a Michelin dinner, the BaselOne weekend (mid-Oct) cannot include Stucki — Stucki's last service before its 13-month renovation is 26 September. For a conference-driven trip, Cheval Blanc or Roots are the candidates.

▲ What Basel isn't

Three gaps a visitor should know.

  • CYBER:No major dedicated cybersecurity conference. Switzerland's flagship is the Global Cyber Conference in Zurich (1–2 Sep 2026) — plan a day-trip.
  • JS:No big general-purpose web/JS conference. BaselOne covers software broadly; for JavaScript-specific tracks, the Swiss anchor is ZurichJS (11 Sep 2026 at Technopark Zurich), 1h by train.
  • AGG:Aggregator listings mislead. dev.events Basel and similar directories show "online events accessible from Basel" rather than physical Basel conferences. Cross-check before booking travel.
