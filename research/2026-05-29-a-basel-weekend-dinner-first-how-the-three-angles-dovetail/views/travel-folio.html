Sun The forced shape
All three top-starred houses — Cheval Blanc[1], Roots[2], Stucki[3] — are closed Sundays and Mondays. That isn't preference, it's arithmetic: the anchor cannot be Sunday night. Friday or Saturday holds the dinner; Sunday is daytime-only.
Calendar gates · 2026

Three deadlines decide whether your dates work at all.

Treat these as hard filters before picking a date. Two are blackouts, one is the once-in-a-generation Bruderholz renovation cutoff. Outside the green band, an angle drops off the table.

26 September 2026 · hard cutoff

Stucki takes its last booking.

Tanja Grandits closes the Bruderholz house for a 13-month renovation. The interim "Tanja am Rhy" only opens 1 Feb 2027; Bruderholz reopens Nov 2027.[6]

1 – 24 August 2026 · staff blackout

Cheval Blanc is dark.

An August weekend silently removes Switzerland's only 3★ option in town. Closed 20–26 Dec too.[1]

Every Sun + Mon · all year

All three houses closed.

A constant in the Basel fine-dining calendar — Sunday becomes a museum-and-day-trip day, by force.[1][2][3]

The anchor · three Michelin houses

Choosing the restaurant pre-decides the daytime.

Switzerland holds only four 3★ houses; Basel has one of them.[18] The other two top-starred Basel restaurants are 2★. The dress code, price, and vibe of the dinner pre-decide whether the afternoon is a calm museum or a Rhine float.

Tech overlay · mostly non-overlap

Conferences run mid-week. They extend the weekend, they don't compete with it.

Basel's tech calendar is bimodal — life-sciences mega-events at Messe (health.tech Mar, Swiss Biotech Day + Future Labs Live May, BioTechX Oct) and one pure-developer flagship, BaselOne (14–15 Oct, Markthalle Basel).[11][12]

The genuine collision: a BaselOne piggyback in October cannot include Stucki — last service is 26 September.[6] For a conference-driven trip post-September, Cheval Blanc or Roots are the only candidates.
