> **TL;DR.** Maison Colette stands on De Trannoyplein, ~1 min on foot from the Norbertine [Tongerlo Abbey](https://www.tongerlo.org/tongerlo/english/) and its full-scale Leonardo *Last Supper* — start there [[1]](https://www.tongerlo.org/tongerlo/english/). Pick **one** half-day from: UNESCO [Lier](https://zimmertoren.be/?lang=en) (begijnhof + Zimmertoren, the strongest cultural day) [[40]](https://zimmertoren.be/?lang=en)[[41]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g680681-d3734496-Reviews-Begijnhof_Lier-Lier_Antwerp_Province.html); [Averbode Bos en Heide](https://www.natuurpunt.be/natuurgebieden/averbode-bos-en-heide) + abbey café (the strongest outdoor) [[23]](https://www.natuurpunt.be/natuurgebieden/averbode-bos-en-heide)[[26]](https://www.abdijaverbode.be/); [Bobbejaanland](https://www.bobbejaanland.be/openingsuren-en-prijzen) (the strongest family day) [[58]](https://www.bobbejaanland.be/openingsuren-en-prijzen). Coffee/dessert before dinner: ⚠ [Café Trappisten](https://www.hours.be/cafe-trappisten/malle/1) at Westmalle sits at the radius edge but is worth the drive [[75]](https://www.hours.be/cafe-trappisten/malle/1). ⚠ Time the day so you're back to change for dinner — most Maison Colette services seat from ~19:00.

## The ten-minute radius (on foot from the restaurant)

Maison Colette is at De Trannoyplein 17, 2260 Westerlo — the abbey gate sits on the same square.

### [Abdij van Tongerlo](https://www.tongerlo.org/tongerlo/english/) — Norbertine, c.1130

Founded c.1130 by canons from Antwerp's Sint-Michielsabdij, dissolved 1796, restored 1838–40 [[2]](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdij_van_Tongerlo). The reason to visit: the only full-scale 16th-century canvas copy of Leonardo's *Last Supper* (~8 × 4 m, painted 1507–08 in Milan, bought in 1545 by Abbot Streyters for 450 Rhenish florins) [[3]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/hangt-er-een-echte-da-vinci-in-westerlo)[[9]](https://www.tongerlo.org/tongerlo/nederlands/). Recent multispectral analysis (Isbouts) attributes the sfumato and St. John's face directly to Leonardo, with Giampietrino, Solario and d'Oggiono identified as workshop collaborators [[3]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/hangt-er-een-echte-da-vinci-in-westerlo). The dedicated Da Vinci Museum closed 1 Nov 2021 for restoration; since summer 2025 the canvas hangs back in the abbey church, with a full-size photo reproduction in the Tiendschuur (tithe barn) [[4]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/abdij-van-tongerlo).

- **Hours / cost:** Mon–Sat 08:00–11:00 & 13:00–17:00, Sun 08:00–10:00 & 13:00–17:00. Free. Push-button 5-min light in the side aisle [[1]](https://www.tongerlo.org/tongerlo/english/).
- **Guided tour:** every first Sunday April–October at 14:00 from the gate, €6 (under-12 free); groups of 25 are €70 for 90 min (courtyard + tithe barn + church; book 3 weeks ahead via Toerisme Westerlo) [[5]](https://www.tongerlo.org/2016/05/25/guided-tours/).
- **Abbey shop "De Oude Linden":** Tongerlo Blond/Bruin/Tripel, honey, jam, liqueurs. ⚠ Unlike Averbode, you cannot taste the beer on site — it is brewed under licence by Brouwerij Haacht in Boortmeerbeek since 1990 (the abbey's own kettles were seized by the WWI occupier) [[6]](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tongerlo_(bier))[[84]](https://www.tongerlo.org/2023/05/18/abdijproducten/).
- **Right next door:** [Torenhof Brasserie](https://www.feestzalen-restaurant.be/brasserie-restaurant/) opposite the abbey gate — Flemish-Belgian seasonal, child menu, adjacent bakery + park ice-cream café [[8]](https://www.feestzalen-restaurant.be/brasserie-restaurant/).

### [Kasteel de Merode](https://www.kasteel-westerlo.be/en/castle/history) — "Oud Kasteel"

The moated castle of the de Merode family, still privately inhabited and **normally closed to the public** [[10]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/wat-doen/bezienswaardigheden/kastelen/kasteel-prins-de-merode). Three open windows: **Kasteelfeesten** on the first weekend of July (interior halls, knight's hall, dining room, chapel; €8, free under-14) [[13]](https://nnieuws.be/artikel/kasteelfeesten-van-westerlo-op-6-en-7-juli); **Kerstmagie**, a costumed-character December evening tour [[14]](https://www.kasteel-westerlo.be/en/events/kerstmagie); and the biennial **Historalia** summer productions on the domain [[10]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/wat-doen/bezienswaardigheden/kastelen/kasteel-prins-de-merode). When shut: walk between De Merode junctions 12 and 13 along the Grote Nete for views of castle + mirror pond [[18]](https://www.landschapsparkdemerode.be/onze-troeven/kasteel-van-prins-de-merode). The 12 ha English park around the castle and the 60 ha French formal park across the Nete (laid out c.1710 by Jean Philippe Eugène de Merode after a Versailles trip) make a stylistic combination called unique in Belgium [[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castle_of_Westerlo)[[12]](https://www.kasteel-westerlo.be/en/castle/history).

### Westerlo village extras

- [Kasteel Jeanne de Merode](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/wat-doen/bezienswaardigheden/kastelen/kasteel-jeanne-de-merode-1) — neo-Gothic 1909–11 "New Castle" / town hall since 1972; freely walkable during municipal hours and exhibitions [[15]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/wat-doen/bezienswaardigheden/kastelen/kasteel-jeanne-de-merode-1).
- **Sint-Lambertuskerk** — 1416 choir, 16th-c. nave, 1760 tower, de Merode burial chapel, protected monument 1976 [[19]](https://inventaris.onroerenderfgoed.be/erfgoedobjecten/41236).
- **Grote Markt** — elongated triangular square. ~100-stall market every **Wednesday morning** — ⚠ not weekends [[20]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/wat-doen/bezienswaardigheden/mooie-plekjes/grote-markt)[[82]](https://www.westerlo.be/nl/wekelijkse-marktdag-op-woensdag).
- ~31 cafés and 34 brasseries in the municipality; on the Markt itself Alice Gastronomia (nr 26B) and Hotel Geerts (nr 50) [[85]](https://www.toerismewesterlo.be/drank-en-eetgelegenheden).

## Outdoor: heath, abbey, knooppunten

The walking and cycling network here — **wandelnetwerk de Merode** — is signposted across 700 km / 3 provinces and described by the official tourism board as "the most popular walking area in Flanders" [[16]](https://www.bezoekdemerode.be/aanbod/ontdek-wandelnetwerk-de-merode). For cycling, the **Kempen fietsknooppunten** offer 2,000+ km, with a refreshed provincial map rolling out end of March 2026 [[35]](https://www.kempen.be/fietsnetwerk-en-fietsplanner). Komoot lists a "Top 10 Bike Rides around Westerlo" guide as a starting point [[37]](https://www.komoot.com/guide/659895/cycling-around-westerlo). Three major cycle networks (Kempen, Hageland, Limburgse Kempen) cross the De Merode estate, with the dedicated MTB network 231 km long (123 km of in-estate trails) [[22]](https://www.provincieantwerpen.be/aanbod/dvt/fietsen-en-wandelen/fietsen/15-keer-op-de-fiets/landschapspark_demerode-.html).

**Averbode Bos en Heide** is the standout. Natuurpunt manages 593 ha within a 1,500+ ha cross-province complex bought in 2004 from the de Merode family [[24]](https://www.bezoekdemerode.be/onze-troeven/averbode-bos-en-heide). EU LIFE habitat restoration (LIFE06 NAT/B/000081, 2006–12) converted Corsican-pine monoculture into 58 ha heath, 10+ ha open dunes, 21 ha Nardus grassland; nightjar has returned as a breeding bird [[25]](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/life/publicWebsite/project/LIFE06-NAT-B-000081/habitat-restoration-in-averbode-bos-en-heide). The 1134 Premonstratensian [Abdij Averbode](https://www.abdijaverbode.be/) (55 canons in residence) is freely visitable mornings till 11:30 and afternoons from 14:00, with **Het Moment** café-shop housing an on-site micro-brewery (draught only — bottles are contract-brewed by Huyghe in Melle) [[71]](https://www.averbodebeer.be/en/visit-our-brewery)[[72]](https://averbodemoment.be/en/het-moment/abbey-shop/).

⚠ Just **outside** the 30 km radius: [Abdij Postel](https://www.visitmol.be/en/postel-walk) (~35–40 km, five waymarked 3–12 km walks) [[38]](https://www.visitmol.be/en/postel-walk) and Hageven–De Plateaux (~55 km, 600 ha heath/fen) [[39]](https://www.gemeentepelt.be/natuurreservaat-hageven---plateaux). Treat both as separate excursions.

**Lier wins** because everything sits in a 15-minute walking loop: [Zimmertoren](https://zimmertoren.be/?lang=en) (Louis Zimmer's Centenary Clock, Astronomical Studio) → 1258 Begijnhof (11 streets, 162 houses, UNESCO 1998) → Sint-Gummaruskerk → a spiced *Liers Vlaaike* (300 years old, EU-recognised regional product) [[40]](https://zimmertoren.be/?lang=en)[[41]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g680681-d3734496-Reviews-Begijnhof_Lier-Lier_Antwerp_Province.html)[[42]](https://www.visitlier.be/en/eat-and-drink/regional-products/lier-vlaaike)[[57]](https://www.takeyourbackpack.com/backpacking-in-belgium/visit-lier/). ⚠ The Stedelijk Museum Wuyts-Van Campen & Baron Caroly has been **closed since Feb 2018** — don't plan it [[43]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stedelijk_Museum_Wuyts-Van_Campen_en_Baron_Caroly).

Diest's pull: the Groot Begijnhof (founded 1253) is among the largest worldwide; Sint-Sulpitiuskerk (1320–1537) holds Philip William of Orange-Nassau's tomb; the mid-19th-c. citadel (1845–53) is one of the few European citadels whose defence system survives intact [[44]](https://www.flemishmastersinsitu.com/nl/locaties/sint-sulpitiuskerk-diest)[[45]](https://www.toerismevlaamsbrabant.be/producten/bezoeken/bezienswaardigheden/begijnhof-diest/)[[46]](https://travel.bhushavali.com/2021/06/things-to-do-in-diest-travel-guide.html). In-walls beer: Loterbol brewery, open first Saturday of the month [[47]](https://itsabrewtifulworld.com/2025/04/21/brewtiful-diest/).

## Quick decision tree

- **One cultural day:** Tongerlo Abbey in the morning → Lier (Zimmertoren + Begijnhof + Vlaaike) in the afternoon → dinner.
- **Outdoor day:** Averbode Bos en Heide red loop (11.2 km, ~170 min) → lunch at Het Moment → De Beeltjens or Kasteel-pond stroll back in Westerlo before dinner.
- **Family day:** Bobbejaanland whole day (Lichtaart) → quick shower at the hotel → dinner. Indoor backup: Plopsa Hasselt or Sunparks Aquafun.
- **Slow beer-themed day:** Het Moment Averbode (taste the abbey-only draught) → Café Trappisten Malle for Extra/Dubbel/Tripel + cheese → dinner.
- **Begijnhof tour:** Diest in the morning (UNESCO + Sulpitiuskerk + citadel) → Tongerlo Abbey late afternoon → dinner.

## Outside the 30 km ring (do not plan as day-trip)
