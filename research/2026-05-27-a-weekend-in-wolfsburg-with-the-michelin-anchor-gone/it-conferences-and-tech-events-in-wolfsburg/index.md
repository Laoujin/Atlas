> **TL;DR.** Wolfsburg's tech calendar in 2026 has exactly two heavyweights — both automotive: **Circularity Days** at the MobileLifeCampus (20–21 May) [[3]](https://www.ist.fraunhofer.de/en/events-trade-fairs/2026/circularity-days-2026.html) and **IZB**, Europe's leading automotive supplier fair, at Allerpark (27–29 Oct, ~40 k visitors, 800+ exhibitors) [[1]](https://www.izb-online.com/en/)[[2]](https://www.izb-online.com/en/izb/news/news-overview/news/izb-2026-postponed-new-dates-confirmed.html). Outside those, **there is no standalone IT/cloud/cyber conference of Berlin-or-Munich scale** — the pulse is community-format: monthly phaeno science talks [[10]](https://www.phaeno.de/en/events/science/), quarterly 42 Wolfsburg open days [[13]](https://42wolfsburg.de/faq/), the #WolfsburgDigital Stammtisch [[11]](https://wolfsburgdigital.org/), and Volkswagen-internal events like the AI Campus and ISMC.

**Notes on the marquee fairs:**

- **IZB** is biennial and is what brings the city's hotels to capacity — book months ahead if you'll be there 27–29 Oct 2026. The 2026 edition adds a Cross Industry Area, opening the floor beyond traditional automotive suppliers [[1]](https://www.izb-online.com/en/).
- **Circularity Days** descends from the older Werkstoffsymposium and the Future Automotive Production Conference, now merged under the OHLF research-campus umbrella [[4]](https://circularity-days.com/about/)[[8]](https://open-hybrid-labfactory.de/news/detail/zusammenschluss-zweier-fachkongresse-geballtes-know-how-in-wolfsburg-1). The 2026 programme has an evening at the Autostadt ZeitHaus and an optional Salzgitter AG steel-mill excursion [[4]](https://circularity-days.com/about/).
- **WE & AI Campus** and **ISMC** are run by Volkswagen Group rather than public organisers — they're worth knowing about because they fill the MobileLifeCampus / Autostadt and can affect venue availability for outside hires [[5]](https://www.handelsblatt.com/adv/presseportal/autostadt-gmbh-we-und-ai-campus-ki-kongress-am-21-und-22-november-in-der-autostadt/30056558.html)[[20]](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/volkswagen-group-international-security-management-conference-maack-csu1e).

## Recurring meetups and community pulse

For anything happening on a typical week (not fair week), the scene is small and Volkswagen-shaped:

- **[phaeno Science Talks](https://www.phaeno.de/en/events/science/)** — one Thursday per month, 18:30, science theatre at the Zaha Hadid-designed phaeno. Free, hybrid (in-person + YouTube), one-hour. 2026 line-up: 28 May (3D-printing & robotics), 25 Jun (microfluidics), 30 Jul (zoonosis), 27 Aug (medical diagnostics), Sep–Oct AI lecture series [[10]](https://www.phaeno.de/en/events/science/). ⚠ The closest thing to a publicly-bookable, regular tech talk in the city.
- **[#WolfsburgDigital Stammtisch](https://wolfsburgdigital.org/)** — informal meetup format jointly run by the City of Wolfsburg and Volkswagen AG since 2016, rotates between Schiller40, the Markthalle, and the Autostadt [[11]](https://wolfsburgdigital.org/). Not on a fixed cadence — check the WolfsburgDigital LinkedIn / Facebook for the next date.
- **[42 Wolfsburg](https://42wolfsburg.de/) Open Days & Piscine** — quarterly open days; the 2026 Piscine immersion sessions run 6–31 July and 21 September – 16 October [[13]](https://42wolfsburg.de/faq/). The campus itself (Learning Spaceship) is open 24/7 to students [[12]](https://42wolfsburg.de/).
- **Local tech meetups (Meetup.com)** — the Wolfsburg-area listing is thin compared to Hamburg/Berlin: 42 Wolfsburg Coding School (~616 members), WOB Online | Developer & Clean Code (~23), Artificial Intelligence Meetup Wolfsburg, plus Braunschweig-area groups (eXXchange IT BS ~103, orclapex Oracle APEX BS) [[14]](https://www.meetup.com/cities/de/wolfsburg/tech/).
- **[Schiller40](https://www.wolfsburg.de/en/rathaus/stadtverwaltung/35-referat-digitalisierung-und-wirtschaft/schiller40-coworking-space)** events — Germany's first municipal coworking space (opened 2012) [[19]](https://hitech.itubs.de/besuch-im-schiller40-coworking-space-in-wolfsburg/); hosts the **Mobility Startup Day Niedersachsen** and assorted founder / freelancer evenings [[18]](https://www.wolfsburg.de/en/rathaus/stadtverwaltung/35-referat-digitalisierung-und-wirtschaft/schiller40-coworking-space)[[19]](https://hitech.itubs.de/besuch-im-schiller40-coworking-space-in-wolfsburg/).

## Reality check — what Wolfsburg is *not*

- **Not a generalist IT-conference city.** No Wolfsburg edition of cybersec / cloud / web-dev fixtures: no Heise dev:next, no CCC chapter conference, no enterprise cloud summit. Closest equivalents sit in Berlin, Hamburg or Hannover.
- **Gravity is automotive software & circular materials.** That's the topic stack you'll see at IZB and Circularity Days [[1]](https://www.izb-online.com/en/)[[3]](https://www.ist.fraunhofer.de/en/events-trade-fairs/2026/circularity-days-2026.html): SDV (software-defined vehicle), AI in production, battery + recycling, supplier sourcing. Helpful if your interests overlap; thin if you're after, say, frontend or fintech.

## If your visit is the May 2026 weekend (post-Circularity Days)

The trip you're planning lands ~one week after Circularity Days (Wed–Thu, 20–21 May) [[3]](https://www.ist.fraunhofer.de/en/events-trade-fairs/2026/circularity-days-2026.html) — so the city will not be in fair-mode. The one publicly-bookable tech event on that exact week is the **phaeno Science Talk on Thu 28 May 2026** at 18:30: *Sustainable Construction — 3D Printing and Robotics*, free, hybrid [[10]](https://www.phaeno.de/en/events/science/). If your dinner is Friday or Saturday, this fits as a Thursday-evening warm-up at the Hadid building five minutes from Autostadt.

For October weekends, **avoid 24–31 Oct 2026 unless you have IZB business** — hotel pricing and traffic during IZB week (27–29 Oct) will be the year's worst [[1]](https://www.izb-online.com/en/)[[2]](https://www.izb-online.com/en/izb/news/news-overview/news/izb-2026-postponed-new-dates-confirmed.html).
