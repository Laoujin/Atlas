> **Decision.** As of May 2026, Wolfsburg has **zero** Michelin 2★ or 3★ restaurants. The city's only 3★, [Aqua](https://www.restaurant-aqua.com/en.html) at the Ritz-Carlton, served its final guests on **21 March 2026** [[1]](https://www.visitchef.com/en/news/2815-end-of-an-era-sven-elverfeld-to-close-three-star-restaurant-aqua)[[2]](https://frontrowsociety.com/fine-cuisine/restaurants/the-end-of-an-era-the-3-star-restaurant-aqua-bids-farewell-after-26-years/), and the concept will not be continued [[3]](https://www.restaurant-ranking.com/news-magazine/details/15785-the-aqua-in-wolfsburgs-autostadt-closes/). Your three live anchors for a starred dinner are: stay in Wolfsburg and downgrade to a **1★** ([La Fontaine](https://www.gevrekandginger.com/en/la-fontaine-a-fine-alternative-in-wolfburg) is the safer pick); or ride the ICE 31 min west to Hannover for a **2★** ([Jante](https://www.jante-restaurant.de/en/) or [Votum](https://vo-tum.de/en/)) [[16]](https://www.thetrainline.com/en/train-times/wolfsburg-hbf-to-hannover-hbf).

## What happened to Aqua

Aqua opened in 2000 inside The Ritz-Carlton at Wolfsburg's Autostadt [[4]](https://www.restaurant-aqua.com/en.html) and under Sven Elverfeld earned its first Michelin star in 2002, second in 2006, third in 2009 — held uninterrupted for 15+ years [[17]](https://www.magazin-kueche.de/news/aktuelles/restaurant-aqua-schliesst-im-maerz-2026-sven-elverfeld-beendet-drei-sterne-aera-in-wolfsburg/). Elverfeld closed it by choice, at peak; an 8-course tribute menu ran Wed–Sat until 20 March 2026, with an invited farewell dinner on 21 March 2026 [[1]](https://www.visitchef.com/en/news/2815-end-of-an-era-sven-elverfeld-to-close-three-star-restaurant-aqua)[[2]](https://frontrowsociety.com/fine-cuisine/restaurants/the-end-of-an-era-the-3-star-restaurant-aqua-bids-farewell-after-26-years/). The Ritz-Carlton will "realign its gastronomy" — no successor announced and no timeline [[3]](https://www.restaurant-ranking.com/news-magazine/details/15785-the-aqua-in-wolfsburgs-autostadt-closes/). The Michelin Guide page for Aqua remains live but is historical at this point [[5]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/niedersachsen/wolfsburg/restaurants).

## Nearby 2★ alternatives (Hannover, 31 min by ICE)

Niedersachsen's only two 2★ restaurants are both in Hannover [[15]](https://www.niedersachsen-tourism.com/interests/culinary-experience-in-lower-saxony/gourmets). Wolfsburg Hbf → Hannover Hbf is ~71 km, fastest ICE 31 min, ~55 trains/day [[16]](https://www.thetrainline.com/en/train-times/wolfsburg-hbf-to-hannover-hbf) — a workable evening-out from a Wolfsburg base, though the safer call is to sleep in Hannover that night.

Jante earned its second star in 2020 and the Michelin Service Award in 2023 [[10]](https://guide.michelin.com/de/de/article/features/neuigkeit-aus-hannover-das-jante-hat-jetzt-2-sterne)[[9]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/niedersachsen/hannover/restaurant/jante); it leans precise, ingredient-led, no theatrics — pricing is notably gentler than Votum [[11]](https://hannover-living.de/en/location/jante/). Votum's set is more "choose-your-own-adventure" — diners pick course order, pairing tier (normal / premium / non-alcoholic), upgrades — inside an architecturally striking room attached to the state parliament [[12]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/niedersachsen/hannover/restaurant/votum)[[13]](https://www.visit-hannover.com/Event-Highlights,-Kultur-Freizeit/Essen-Trinken/Michelin/VOTUM)[[14]](https://vo-tum.de/en/).

## How to decide

- **Want a 2★ experience, fixed weekend in Wolfsburg:** book Jante or Votum, take the ~19:00 ICE west, sleep in Hannover (or last ICE back is late — but cutting it fine after a 6-course is a bad trade).
- **Want to stay in Wolfsburg, accept 1★:** book La Fontaine far in advance; 7–8 tables and Wed–Sat only [[6]](https://www.gevrekandginger.com/en/la-fontaine-a-fine-alternative-in-wolfburg).
- **Wanted Aqua specifically:** that ship sailed in March; there is no equivalent successor in the city right now [[3]](https://www.restaurant-ranking.com/news-magazine/details/15785-the-aqua-in-wolfsburgs-autostadt-closes/). The closest 3★ in Germany are in Hamburg ([Haerlin](https://restaurant-haerlin.de/en/home.html), ~2 hr by ICE) or Berlin (~1 hr 40 by ICE) — outside the scope of a Wolfsburg-anchored weekend.
