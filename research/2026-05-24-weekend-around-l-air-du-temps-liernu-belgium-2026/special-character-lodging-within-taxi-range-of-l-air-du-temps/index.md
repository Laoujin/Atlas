> **Decision.** For walk-to-dinner with zero taxi, book one of L'Air du Temps's own 11 farmstead rooms[[1]](https://qualitylodgings.com/en/lair-du-temps) or [Les Ruines du Château](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/welcome/) — two suites inside actual castle ruins, 500 m away[[2]](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/welcome/). For a real private château, [Château Bayard](https://chateaubayard.be/en/) — five 17th-century rooms ~5 km off[[4]](https://chateaubayard.be/en/bedrooms). For Namur old-town energy on Sunday, [Les Tanneurs](https://www.tanneurs.com/en/index.php) (15 connected 17th-century tannery houses)[[5]](https://www.tanneurs.com/en/index.php) or [The Royal Snail](https://theroyalsnail.com/en/) (design boutique with pool and spa)[[6]](https://theroyalsnail.com/en/). All within €40 each-way taxi[[7]](https://www.taxiproxi.be/tarif-taxi-departement-namur).

## The anchor and the taxi-range budget

L'Air du Temps is at Rue de la Croix Monet 2, 5310 Liernu (Éghezée), in farmland off the E411 — about 30 min from Brussels and ~22 km north of Namur city[[8]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/namur/liernu/restaurant/l-air-du-temps371956). The chef Sang Hoon Degeimbre moved the restaurant to this restored white farmstead in 2013; the same square courtyard houses the dining room, 5-hectare vegetable garden, and the guest rooms[[9]](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Air_du_temps_(restaurant)).

Namur-area taxis run ~€11 for 5 km, ~€20 for 10 km, ~€38 for 20 km[[7]](https://www.taxiproxi.be/tarif-taxi-departement-namur). That makes anything in Éghezée commune a single-digit ride, Gembloux ~€25, and downtown Namur ~€38 each way. Maredsous Abbey (45 km, ~€85 each way) is the cliff edge of "taxi range" and is excluded below[[13]](https://tourisme-maredsous.be/accommodations/?lang=en).

## Picking among the close-in three (Éghezée commune)

The choice within 5 km of the restaurant is philosophical, not practical:

- **[L'Air du Temps](https://airdutemps.be/en/rooms-en/) (on-site, 11 rooms).** Eliminates the only friction of a tasting menu — no taxi at midnight. Rooms exude "contemporary class" with views over the 5-ha vegetable garden that drives the menu[[1]](https://qualitylodgings.com/en/lair-du-temps)[[9]](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Air_du_temps_(restaurant)). At ~€210 incl. breakfast it undercuts most adjacent-rooms-at-two-star packages elsewhere in Europe.
- **[Les Ruines du Château](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/welcome/) (2 suites).** The most theatrical pick — literal restored ruins of the medieval Château d'Aische-en-Refail, with the in-room kit dialled up: La Charmante has an outdoor jacuzzi on the terrace by the pond; L'Envoûtante puts a combined infrared/steam sauna under the castle tower itself[[3]](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/our-rooms/). 500 m to the restaurant means a 5-minute country walk back[[2]](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/welcome/). ⚠ Only 2 keys total — book months ahead for a Saturday.
- **[Château Bayard](https://chateaubayard.be/en/) (5 rooms).** A genuine private 17th-century family château ~5 km away, with park, pond, hundred-year-old trees, preserved fireplaces and alcove stuccoes[[4]](https://chateaubayard.be/en/bedrooms). At ~€190 it's the cheapest of the three close-ins. ⚠ Booking is email-only (`chambre-chateau-bayard@gmail.com`); check-in is from 16:00, key-handover at 11:00 the next day[[10]](https://www.letsgomylove.com/en/place/chateau-bayard/).

## Picking among the Namur city options

A ~€38 each-way taxi (~€76 round-trip) is the trade for staying in Namur and tacking on Sunday brunch + the citadel:

- **[Les Tanneurs](https://www.tanneurs.com/en/index.php)** — the historical-fabric pick. 15 connected 17th-century tannery buildings preserved as one rambling 4-star with blue stone, brick vaults and stone arches, near the Sambre–Meuse confluence and the train station[[5]](https://www.tanneurs.com/en/index.php).
- **[The Royal Snail](https://theroyalsnail.com/en/)** — the spa-and-design pick. Five room categories €135–€239, heated outdoor pool, full spa, jacuzzi, La Table du Royal Snail restaurant, vertical garden[[6]](https://theroyalsnail.com/en/). Furthest from "old" but most resort-like.
- **[Château de Namur](https://www.chateaudenamur.com/)** — the view-and-quiet pick. 4-star on the citadel grounds with cable-car access to old town and free parking (which the in-town boutiques cannot beat)[[12]](https://www.chateaudenamur.com/).

## What got cut

- **Maredsous Abbey hotel** (~45 km) — beautiful, but breaks the taxi budget at ~€85+ each way[[13]](https://tourisme-maredsous.be/accommodations/?lang=en).
- **[Château de Petit-Leez](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/chateau-de-petit-leez)** (Gembloux, ~12 km) — gorgeous Mosan château-farm with a contemporary-art park, but no overnight rooms; visit it during the day on Sunday instead[[14]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/chateau-de-petit-leez).
- Generic Booking.com listings around Sombreffe / Fernelmont — present, but none with the historical-architecture signature of the seven above.
