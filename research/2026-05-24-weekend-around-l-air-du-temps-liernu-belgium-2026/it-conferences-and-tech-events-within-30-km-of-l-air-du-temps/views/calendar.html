The Saturday-pairs, up close

Two events anchor a Saturday inside the 30 km circle. A third — FOSDEM — sits 15 km past the line but covers the right Saturday and is the country's biggest open-source weekend.

Wed–Sun 21–25 Oct Sat 24 ★

KIKK Festival (15th ed.)

NAMUR · ~20 KM · DIGITAL-CULTURE FESTIVAL

Five days of pro conferences, a ~60-installation digital-art trail through central Namur (KIKK in Town), a designers' market on Place d'Armes and kids' workshops on gaming, sound tech and robotics [8][9]. Sat 24 Oct: KIKK by afternoon, 30-minute drive to Liernu for dinner.

kikkfestival.be visitwallonia
Fri–Sun 27–29 Mar Sat 28 ★

CSLabs Hackathon — "Hack the Narrative"

UNAMUR FAC. D'INFO · ~20 KM · 48 H HACK

48-hour hackathon at the UNamur Faculty of Computer Science on Rue Grandgagnage [12]. Theme: digital-literacy through Black-Mirror-style fiction. Free with €20 refundable deposit; non-coders welcome [13]. Sat 28 March is the full hack day — meals on-site, the dinner is a deliberate escape.

hackathon.cslabs.be unamur.be
FOSDEM
~45 KM · BRUSSELS
Sat–Sun 31 Jan – 1 Feb Out of radius · Sat covers

FOSDEM 2026

ULB SOLBOSCH · ~45 KM · FREE · ~8 000 ATTENDEES

Largest BE open-source event of the year. Free, no registration. ~45 km / 50 min drive from Liernu [5] — the strongest argument for stretching the circle. The Saturday session 31 Jan pairs cleanly with the same-evening dinner [15].

fosdem.org rome2rio

~850 ATTENDEES

Mind & Market Forum LLN

AULA MAGNA, OTTIGNIES-LLN · ~22 KM · STARTUP FORUM

Invitation-only workshops in the morning; public access from 12:45 with pitches and AI demos in the afternoon. ~850 attendees, 100 startups, 80 partners [10]. Flag only if the trip can flex mid-week.

mindandmarket.com

EU AI Week @ TRAIL

TRUSTED AI LABS, LLN · ~22 KM · 3-HOUR SESSION

Three-hour slot at TRusted AI Labs ground floor, Place du Levant, Louvain-la-Neuve. Talks, project showcases, networking with AI researchers and industry leaders [11]. Useful only if you're already in the area.

digitalwallonia.be

NAMUR · ~20 KM · ACADEMIC CONFERENCE

International Conference on Software Engineering for Cybersecurity in Namur on 23 January [14]. Confirm registration is open before planning around it — the listing is from an aggregator, not the conference's own page.

Year-round venues to check closer to the date

The in-radius cadence is driven by three hubs whose public agendas churn faster than annual conferences — where ad-hoc tech evenings, AI demos and meetups appear.

TRAKK

CENTRAL NAMUR

"+Tech" evenings, FabLab open days, KIKK-adjacent workshops — 3 000 m² creative-tech hub jointly run with UNamur and KIKK [16].

TRAIL @ UCLouvain

LOUVAIN-LA-NEUVE

AI labs that hosted the EU AI Week slot above — recurring talks and project showcases [11].

WSL incubator

GEMBLOUX / LLN / NAMUR

Tech-entrepreneur incubator across all three in-radius cities — startup pitch nights, investor demos [17].

UNamur NaDI / PReCISE

NAMUR

Multidisciplinary digital research institute (big data, AI, security) — public seminars and PhD defences [18].

A6K / E6K Charleroi

~33 KM · OUT BUT CLOSE

Regional tech & industry hub; the E6K training-campus extension lands through fall 2026 [19]. Check their calendar near the trip dates.

Devoxx Belgium

5–9 OCT · ANTWERP · ~85 KM

National flagship dev conference but ~85 km away [20] — different trip.

Straight-line vs road. "Within 30 km" is measured straight-line from Liernu; actual driving distance is 5–10 km longer through the rural road network east of Gembloux [1].

KIKK window. Some sources gave conflicting KIKK 2026 windows (22–25 vs 21–25 Oct); the 21–25 range matches KIKK's usual Wed–Sun cadence [8].

ICSECS. The listing is from an aggregator [14], not the conference's own page — confirm registration before planning around it.

Restaurant. Dinner anchor is the 2-star L'Air du Temps in Liernu (Éghezée, Namur province) [2].
