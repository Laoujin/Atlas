> **Decision.** For a Saturday-dinner trip to [L'Air du Temps](https://guide.michelin.com/en/namur/liernu/restaurant/l-air-du-temps371956) [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/namur/liernu/restaurant/l-air-du-temps371956), only two in-radius 2026 events span a Saturday: **[KIKK Festival](https://kikkfestival.be/) 21–25 Oct** in central Namur [[8]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/kikk-festival-celebrating-digital-world-namur) (the obvious pairing — digital-arts festival, ~20 km, runs Wed–Sun) and the **CSLabs hackathon 27–29 Mar** at UNamur [[12]](https://www.unamur.be/fr/agenda/hackathon-leducation-aux-enjeux-du-numerique-en-utilisant-des-elements-de-fiction). The other three confirmed in-radius events fall on weekdays. If a single weekday off-site visit is acceptable, the AI talks at TRAIL in Louvain-la-Neuve (19 Mar) or the Mind & Market startup forum in LLN (14 Apr) are both ~22 km away. **[FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org/2026/) (31 Jan–1 Feb)** in Brussels [[15]](https://fosdem.org/2026/) sits just outside the radius at ~45 km / 50 min drive [[5]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Brussels/Liernu) — the biggest open-source event in the country and the strongest "stretch the circle" candidate.

### KIKK Festival — the headline pairing

Fifteenth edition. Five days of conferences for digital-sector professionals, a ~60-installation digital-art trail through central Namur (KIKK in Town), a designers' market on Place d'Armes, and family/kids workshops on gaming, sound tech and robotics [[8]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/kikk-festival-celebrating-digital-world-namur)[[9]](https://www.namurtourisme.be/en/evenements/kikk/). Saturday 24 Oct is the natural pivot day — KIKK by afternoon, 30-minute drive to Liernu for dinner. Official site: [kikkfestival.be][e5b] [[7]](https://kikkfestival.be/).

[e5b]: https://kikkfestival.be/

### CSLabs Hackathon — the budget option

48-hour student hackathon at the [UNamur Faculty of Computer Science][e3] on Rue Grandgagnage [[12]](https://www.unamur.be/fr/agenda/hackathon-leducation-aux-enjeux-du-numerique-en-utilisant-des-elements-de-fiction). Theme: "Hack the Narrative" — digital-literacy projects framed around fiction (Black-Mirror-style scenarios). Free entry with a €20 refundable deposit; open to non-coders (design, storytelling, electronics welcome) [[13]](https://hackathon.cslabs.be/). Saturday 28 March is the full hack day; meals on-site, so the Saturday dinner is a deliberate escape.

### Mind & Market LLN — only if you can shift the dinner

Tuesday 14 April, 08:30 onwards at the Aula Magna in Ottignies-LLN. Hybrid format — invitation-only workshops in the morning, public access from 12:45, pitches and AI demos in the afternoon. ~850 attendees, 100 startups, 80 partners [[10]](https://www.mindandmarket.com/les-evenements-forums/forum-mind-and-market-louvain-la-neuve-14-avril-2026/). Doesn't anchor a Saturday — flag it only if the trip can flex to mid-week.

### EU AI Week @ TRAIL — short slot, focused audience

Thursday 19 March, 15:00–18:00, at the [TRusted AI Labs (TRAIL)][e2] ground floor, Place du Levant, Louvain-la-Neuve. Talks, project showcases, networking, AI researchers + industry [[11]](https://www.digitalwallonia.be/en/events/eu-ai-week-2026/). Three-hour slot, weekday — useful only if you're already in the area.

## Just outside the circle, worth a mention

- **FOSDEM 2026** — Sat 31 Jan & Sun 1 Feb, ULB Solbosch, Brussels [[15]](https://fosdem.org/2026/). Free, no registration, ~8000 attendees, biggest BE open-source event. ~45 km / 50 min drive from Liernu [[5]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Brussels/Liernu) — the strongest argument for stretching the radius, and the Saturday session 31 Jan pairs cleanly with the same-evening dinner.
- **A6K / E6K Charleroi** — ~33 km from Liernu [[6]](https://be.toponavi.com/238-36393). Regional tech & industry hub; the E6K training-campus extension lands through fall 2026 [[19]](https://www.a6k.be/). Check their calendar near the trip dates.
- **Devoxx Belgium** — Mon 5–Fri 9 Oct, Kinepolis Antwerp [[20]](https://devoxx.be/). National flagship dev conference but ~85 km away — different trip.

## Caveats

- "Within 30 km" is measured straight-line from Liernu; actual driving distance is 5–10 km longer through the rural road network east of Gembloux.
- Some date sources gave conflicting KIKK 2026 windows (22–25 vs 21–25 Oct); the 21–25 range is the one published by VisitWallonia and matches KIKK's usual Wed–Sun cadence [[8]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/kikk-festival-celebrating-digital-world-namur).
- The ICSECS listing comes from an aggregator [[14]](https://allconferencealert.net/eventdetails.php?id=100111000), not the conference's own page — confirm registration is open before planning around it.
