> **Decision** — Book **L'Air du Temps SETTING** (on-site, 11 rooms) if you want the Séjour package and zero post-dinner walk [[3]](https://airdutemps.be/les-chambres/). If full or you want more character, **Les Ruines du Château** at ~500 m offers a jacuzzi-or-sauna suite for €170–200 [[6]](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/practically/) [[8]](https://www.rtbf.be/article/une-nuit-au-chateau-du-cote-d-eghezee-grace-aux-chambres-d-hote-les-ruines-du-chateau-11229035). **B&B La Chambre d'à Côté** (~1.8 km in Aische-en-Refail) is the budget pick — boutique, ~€140–150, free bikes [[10]](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-aische-en-refail/bb-la-chambre-da-cote.html) [[12]](https://www.kayak.com/Aische-en-Refail-Hotels-B-B-La-Chambre-d-a-Cote.456685.ksp). Beyond ~2 km, you're driving — Manoir de Thorembais (5.78 km) is the next nearest hotel [[13]](https://www.belvicci.com/restaurant/belgium/liernu/lair-du-temps).

L'Air du Temps sits at rue de la Croix Monet 2, Liernu (commune of Eghezée, Namur province) — a renovated white farmstead with a 5-hectare permaculture garden, 2 Michelin stars + green star in the 2026 Guide [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/namur/liernu/restaurant/l-air-du-temps371956) [[2]](https://qualitylodgings.com/en/lair-du-temps). The village is tiny rural Wallonia: walking-distance lodging is a short list.

## [L'Air du Temps SETTING](https://airdutemps.be/les-chambres/) — the on-site option

11 rooms across two buildings: rooms 1-5 in the original restaurant block (external stairs, garden views), rooms 6-11 in the converted barn (internal staircase, added 2018). **No lift** — flag if mobility matters [[2]](https://qualitylodgings.com/en/lair-du-temps) [[3]](https://airdutemps.be/les-chambres/).

Two stay packages, both per-person, include 1 night + 2 breakfasts + the tasting menu (wines extra) [[3]](https://airdutemps.be/les-chambres/):

- **Séjour Signature — €640** (not available Fri/Sat evening — relevant for your Saturday-dinner anchor)
- **Séjour Suprématie — €730**
- Room upgrades: Moyenne +€20, Supérieure +€70, Suite +€90

→ If you're eating Saturday night, the Signature package excludes that slot — book the **Suprématie** package or split: room-only at ~€210 double + breakfast [[4]](https://www.belvicci.com/hotel/belgium/liernu/lair-du-temps-setting) and pay the menu (~€270 all-in [[4]](https://www.belvicci.com/hotel/belgium/liernu/lair-du-temps-setting), or ~€300 per Belvicci's average [[13]](https://www.belvicci.com/restaurant/belgium/liernu/lair-du-temps)) separately.

Tripadvisor: 4.6/5, #1 of 3 B&Bs in Eghezée; sleep quality 5.0/5; recent gripes: no A/C in rooftop rooms (summer), no 24h reception, tasting-menu-only restaurant [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1756478-d4994192-Reviews-L_Air_du_Temps-Eghezee_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html).

## [Les Ruines du Château](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/welcome/) — castle-ruins suites, ~500 m

Two suites built into the ruins of the old Château d'Aische-en-Refail [[7]](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/our-rooms/):

- **La Charmante** — €200, outdoor jacuzzi on a terrace by the pond, bath + shower [[7]](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/our-rooms/) [[8]](https://www.rtbf.be/article/une-nuit-au-chateau-du-cote-d-eghezee-grace-aux-chambres-d-hote-les-ruines-du-chateau-11229035)
- **L'Envoûtante** — €170, combined infrared + steam sauna under the castle tower [[7]](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/our-rooms/) [[8]](https://www.rtbf.be/article/une-nuit-au-chateau-du-cote-d-eghezee-grace-aux-chambres-d-hote-les-ruines-du-chateau-11229035)
- Connected via underground corridor, bookable together [[7]](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/our-rooms/)

All-inclusive of breakfast (delivered on wooden boards), Sofitel bedding, smart TV with Netflix, mini-fridge; evening aperitif service on request [[9]](https://www.letsgomylove.com/en/place/les-ruines-du-chateau/). 4.8/5 from 30 reviews [[9]](https://www.letsgomylove.com/en/place/les-ruines-du-chateau/). Won **Horecatel Hospitality Awards 2025** public favourite, RTBF "C'est du Belge" exceptional accommodation 2024 [[8]](https://www.rtbf.be/article/une-nuit-au-chateau-du-cote-d-eghezee-grace-aux-chambres-d-hote-les-ruines-du-chateau-11229035). Check-in 17:00, check-out 11:00, free parking, no dogs, 48h cancellation [[6]](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/practically/) [[9]](https://www.letsgomylove.com/en/place/les-ruines-du-chateau/).

Best for a couple weekend with the dinner as the centrepiece — pre-dinner sauna, post-dinner short walk back, jacuzzi nightcap.

## [B&B La Chambre d'à Côté](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-aische-en-refail/bb-la-chambre-da-cote.html) — boutique B&B, ~1.8 km

Place d'Aische 10/12, in the centre of Aische-en-Refail [[10]](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-aische-en-refail/bb-la-chambre-da-cote.html). 2-3 rooms (50 m² One-Bedroom Suite with queen, 55 m² Loft Suite with twins, 30 m² Double with garden view) [[10]](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-aische-en-refail/bb-la-chambre-da-cote.html). From ~$151/night, seasonal $113-$159 [[12]](https://www.kayak.com/Aische-en-Refail-Hotels-B-B-La-Chambre-d-a-Cote.456685.ksp). **Free bikes** [[10]](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-aische-en-refail/bb-la-chambre-da-cote.html) — usable for the trip to L'Air du Temps (~5 min ride vs ~20 min walk).

Guest rating 9.5/10 from 65 reviews on myboutiquehotel; 9.4 on Booking.com [[10]](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-aische-en-refail/bb-la-chambre-da-cote.html) [[11]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/la-chambre-d-a-ca-ta-c.html). Highly rated for breakfast and host [[11]](https://www.booking.com/hotel/be/la-chambre-d-a-ca-ta-c.html). Best for budget-conscious diners or those wanting separation from the restaurant complex.

## What's beyond walking distance

Belvicci's hotel index for L'Air du Temps lists only two other nearby hotels, both car-only [[13]](https://www.belvicci.com/restaurant/belgium/liernu/lair-du-temps):

- **Manoir de Thorembais** — 5.78 km
- **Koru** — 9.86 km

For a Saturday-night Michelin dinner with paired wines, plan a taxi or designated driver if you book anything outside the 500 m radius and don't want to walk in the dark on rural lanes.
