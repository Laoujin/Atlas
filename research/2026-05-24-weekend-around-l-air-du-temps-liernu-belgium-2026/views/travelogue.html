04 · The Three Walking-Distance Rooms

Sixteen keys in the gravitational well.

The pairings rule out the car. These are the only beds inside a 2 km walk of the dining room. Demand outstrips supply by miles — book the table and the room in the same conversation.

★ The default

L'Air du Temps · The Farmstead

0 m · 11 keys

Restored white farmstead with garden views overlooking the 5-hectare potager [49]. ~€210 double + breakfast is unusually gentle at this Michelin tier [6]. Catch: the Séjour Signature pack excludes Fri/Sat evening — for the Saturday tasting you need Suprématie at €730 pp [3].

€210 / €730 pkg 11 keys No A/C upstairs
★★ The romance

Les Ruines du Château

500 m · 2 keys

Two suites built into the actual ruins of the 13th-century Château d'Aische-en-Refail [9]: La Charmante with an outdoor jacuzzi over a pond, L'Envoûtante with infrared sauna under the castle tower [10]. €170–€200 all-in; Horecatel Awards 2025 public's favourite [11].

€170–€200 2 keys Jacuzzi / sauna
★★★ The boutique

B&B La Chambre d'à Côté

1.8 km · 2–3 keys

Place d'Aische 10/12, Aische-en-Refail — small, photo-quiet, with free bike rentals. 9.5/10 across 65 reviews [12]. Half an hour on foot to the dining room — bring proper shoes for the gravel last 200 m.

06 · The Tech-Pair Saturday
24
Oct The Pivot Day

One Saturday in 2026 turns this trip into a tech conference excuse.

The KIKK Festival runs Wed 21 → Sun 25 October in central Namur — digital art trail of ~60 installations, professional conferences, a market, kids' workshops on gaming, sound and robotics [41]. Saturday the 24th is the pivot day — a 30-minute drive to Liernu lands you in time for 19:30.

Mar 27–29 — Hack the Narrative, CSLabs hackathon at UNamur (the only other Saturday-spanning tech event inside the radius) [44]
Jan 31 – Feb 1 — FOSDEM, Brussels — ~45 km, just outside the circle, the one "stretch" date
Caveat — by 24 Oct the citadel téléphérique is closed (27 Sep cutoff [20]); the underground tour stays open and Annevoie runs to 11 Nov.
07 · Before You Book

Five phone calls, in order.

The first call decides everything else. The price the menu, the lead time, the room availability, the dress code — confirm them at the source before you commit to anything downstream.

① The Anchor

L'Air du Temps
+32 81 81 30 48

Ask: tasting-menu price + pairings, meal length, dress code, whether the table books with the on-site room (it does, under Suprématie). Closed Mon/Tue.

② The Castle Suites

Les Ruines du Château
Online · 2 keys total

Two suites only — book months ahead for any Saturday between April and October. Check-in 17:00, check-out 11:00.

③ The Family Château

Château Bayard
chambre-chateau-bayard@gmail.com

Email only. Five rooms, three "prestige". ~€190 + €15 breakfast. Lead time: allow a week for response.

④ The Citadel Tour

Underground galleries
Online · cut-off day before

90-minute tour, ~12–14 °C in the tunnels. Bring a jumper even in August. Closes for the season 27 September.

⑤ The Vineyard

Domaine du Chenoy
Sat only · Apr–Oct

Reserve a Saturday slot in advance. Organic-only Walloon vineyard ~15 min from Liernu — fits cleanly with a Floreffe afternoon.

⑥ The Plan B

Namur boutique (taxi range)
~€38 each way · 20 km

If the three walking-distance rooms are full: Royal Snail, Les Tanneurs, or Château de Namur on the citadel. Taxi the dinner; never drive.
