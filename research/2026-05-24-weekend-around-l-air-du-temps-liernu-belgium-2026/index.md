The dinner's gravity collapses every other choice. A Saturday-night tasting at [L'Air du Temps](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/namur/liernu/restaurant/l-air-du-temps371956) (2 MICHELIN Stars + Green Star in the 2026 guide, +32 81 81 30 48, closed Mon/Tue [[1]](https://en.resto.be/restaurant/eghezee/5310-eghezee/3123-lair-du-temps/)) forbids both driving home and missing the morning's start time — and Liernu has no scheduled bus, only an on-demand TEC Flexitec stop [[2]](https://www.eghezee.be/votre-commune/services-communaux/mobilite/tec-bus/bus-amelioration-de-la-ligne-71). That single fact prunes the walking-distance lodging list to **three** (~16 rooms total: 11 on-site, 2 at [Les Ruines du Château](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/welcome/), 2–3 at [B&B La Chambre d'à Côté](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-aische-en-refail/bb-la-chambre-da-cote.html) [[3]](https://airdutemps.be/les-chambres/) [[4]](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/our-rooms/) [[5]](https://www.myboutiquehotel.com/en/boutique-hotels-aische-en-refail/bb-la-chambre-da-cote.html)), shapes day-trip selection (every itinerary in the activities child finishes with a 13- to 20-minute drive back for 19:30 [[6]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Liernu/Namur)), and reduces the 2026 IT-events calendar to **two** Saturday-spanning options inside the 30 km radius [[7]](https://www.unamur.be/fr/agenda/hackathon-leducation-aux-enjeux-du-numerique-en-utilisant-des-elements-de-fiction) [[8]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/kikk-festival-celebrating-digital-world-namur).

The on-site farmstead room is the cross-cutting recommendation — both lodging sub-topics put it first, and the activities sub-topic adopts it in its decision block; ~€210 double + breakfast undercuts most adjacent-rooms packages at this Michelin tier in Europe [[9]](https://www.belvicci.com/hotel/belgium/liernu/lair-du-temps-setting). Whichever room you target, the binding constraint is **lead time**: the on-site Séjour Signature package explicitly excludes Fri/Sat evening, forcing the pricier €730 Suprématie if you want the room bundled with the Saturday-night menu [[3]](https://airdutemps.be/les-chambres/); the castle-ruins suites are 2 keys total [[4]](https://lesruinesduchateau.be/en/our-rooms/); [Château Bayard](https://chateaubayard.be/en/) is email-only at `chambre-chateau-bayard@gmail.com` [[10]](https://www.letsgomylove.com/en/place/chateau-bayard/).

The Liernu/Aische-en-Refail villages surfaced by the lodging sub-topics fold cleanly into the activities radius: the [Gros Chêne de Liernu](https://www.lavenir.net/actu/discover/2026/05/22/le-gros-chene-de-liernu-est-millenaire-la-meilleure-facon-de-le-conserver-et-de-lui-foutre-la-paix-HSEMG7WWTRBUBLBZ4JDK5VOZL4/) (millennium-old oak, 7 km signed loop) is a zero-drive Sunday morning, and the 30 May 2026 Brotherhood Chapter is a literal village event [[11]](https://www.lavenir.net/actu/discover/2026/05/22/le-gros-chene-de-liernu-est-millenaire-la-meilleure-facon-de-le-conserver-et-de-lui-foutre-la-paix-HSEMG7WWTRBUBLBZ4JDK5VOZL4/). Gembloux (13 min), Namur (13 min), and Louvain-la-Neuve (~22 km) all sit inside; Brussels at ~45 km is the one "stretch the circle" line, redeemed only by FOSDEM 31 Jan–1 Feb 2026 [[12]](https://fosdem.org/2026/).

Two natural weekend shapes emerge. The **gastronomy default** works any open Saturday: morning at Gembloux or [Domaine du Chenoy](https://www.domaine-du-chenoy.com/fr/visites-et-degustations-451) (Sat-only organic-vineyard tours, Apr–Oct [[13]](https://www.domaine-du-chenoy.com/fr/visites-et-degustations-451)), afternoon on the [Citadel of Namur](https://citadelle.namur.be/fr/tarifs-et-horaires) underground galleries (book the day before [[14]](https://citadelle.namur.be/en/guided-underground-tour)), 19:30 dinner, on-site room. The **tech-pair Saturday** is 24 October 2026 — KIKK Festival's pivot day in central Namur, 30 minutes to the dinner [[8]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/kikk-festival-celebrating-digital-world-namur). Caveat for that date: the Citadel cable car closes 27 September [[15]](https://citadelle.namur.be/en/cable-car), so the underground tour stays but the téléphérique doesn't — and Annevoie's water gardens still run to 11 November [[16]](https://www.annevoie.be/fr/infos-pratiques/).

What none of the children fully nailed down is the dinner mechanics the brief asked to treat as a *verified* constraint: tasting-menu price, wine-pairing cost, meal length, dress code, and whether the table books automatically with a room are surfaced only second-hand (~€270–300 menu average via Belvicci [[17]](https://www.belvicci.com/restaurant/belgium/liernu/lair-du-temps), the €730 bundled package as a proxy). Before booking anything else, call +32 81 81 30 48 and get a primary-source menu sheet — then back-solve the room and the afternoon around the real curtain time.
