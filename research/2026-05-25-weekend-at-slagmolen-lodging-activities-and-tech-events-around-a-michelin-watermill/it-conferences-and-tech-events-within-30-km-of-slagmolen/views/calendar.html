Flagship dates · pin these first

Five events anchor the year
12–13 Mar · Hasselt
Thu 12 – Fri 13 March · Cybersecurity

BSides Limburg 2026

Venue Corda Campus / PXL-NeXT City Hasselt Distance ~26 km Scale Regional

The one local IT conference everyone in scope should know. Workshops Thursday; talks Friday in the new PXL-NeXT building inside Corda Campus. Community-driven infosec, framed by the Cyber Security Coalition as a deep-discussion complement to the bigger Belgian security cons.

cites [2] [3] [4]
WCCI 21–26 Jun · Maastricht
Sun 21 – Fri 26 June · AI / CI research

IEEE WCCI 2026

Venue MECC ~1500 attendees Distance ~25 km

Three IEEE conferences (IJCNN, FUZZ-IEEE, IEEE CEC) bundled into one congress. The biggest AI/CI event ever staged this close to Limburg. New Industry Day component bridging academia and applied teams.

cites [5] [6]
16 Oct – 15 Nov
Fri 16 Oct – Sun 15 Nov · Tech festival

FTI Festival 2026

Hub Hasselt + Genk +100 events +1000 innovations

Flanders Technology & Innovation: 30-day festival across 8 cities. Hasselt & Genk are paired as the Limburg pillar — tech safaris, Smart Manufacturing Conference, hackathon, STEM tours, public light festival.

cites [8] [9] [10]
BNAIC 21–23 Oct · Maastricht
Wed 21 – Fri 23 October · Benelux AI/ML

BNAIC/BeNeLearn 2026

Venue Maastricht Univ. Edition 38th Distance ~25 km

38th Benelux AI conference co-hosted with the 35th Belgian/Dutch ML conference. Smaller and more academic than WCCI, but the reference event for the regional AI/ML community.

cites [7]
12 Nov · Genk
Thu 12 November · Smart factories

SMAFACC 2026

Venue Thor Park City Genk Distance ~10 km

Smart factories, digitisation, automation — sits inside the FTI Festival window and is the most production-engineering-flavoured day of the year. The Corda Arena opening follows the day after.

Geography · the 30 km arc

How "within 30 km of Slagmolen" plays out

Slagmolen sits in Opglabbeek (Oudsbergen), in the Belgian Limburg "Bosbeek Valley". The 30 km circle catches Genk, Hasselt, Maaseik, Maasmechelen — and crosses the border to Sittard and Maastricht. Eindhoven, Antwerp, Liège and Aachen all fall outside.

  • ~10 kmThor Park / EnergyVilleGenk · energy + smart manufacturing
  • ~26 kmCorda Campus / PXL-NeXTHasselt · BSides, FTI hub
  • ~25 kmMECC / Maastricht Univ.Maastricht NL · WCCI, BNAIC
  • ~30 km ⚠Brightlands Smart Services CampusHeerlen NL · borderline
Do not plan around these

Outside the 30 km radius

Belgium's major dev conferences cluster in Antwerp, Mechelen and Brussels — all >70 km from Slagmolen. Listed so you don't accidentally try to staple them onto a Slagmolen weekend.

Devoxx Belgium
5–9 Oct · Antwerp · ~75 km
Java & JVM, the big one — out of scope.
DDD Europe
8–12 Jun · Antwerp · ~75 km
Domain-driven design conference, overlaps WCCI week.
BruCON 0x12
21–25 Sep · Mechelen · ~100 km
Belgium's flagship security con.
CloudBrew
10–11 Dec · Mechelen · ~100 km
Azure / cloud, Belgian winter staple.

Source: dev.events/EU/BE. Eindhoven's tech scene sits at ~50 km — also outside, but inside an hour's drive if you stretch the brief.
