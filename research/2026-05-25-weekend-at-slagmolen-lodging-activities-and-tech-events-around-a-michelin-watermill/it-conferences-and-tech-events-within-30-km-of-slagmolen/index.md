## Event notes

### BSides Limburg — the one local IT conference everyone in scope should know

Two-day format: workshops on Thursday 12 March, conference talks on Friday 13 March, both at Corda Campus, Hasselt [[2]](https://www.bsides-limburg.be/). Conference day is hosted in the new PXL-NeXT building inside the campus [[4]](https://10times.com/e13h-kp7r-hk2s-2-bsides-limburg). Vibe is community-driven infosec: the [Cyber Security Coalition](https://cybersecuritycoalition.be/event/bsides-limburg-2026/) frames it as a deep-discussion complement to the bigger Belgian security cons [[3]](https://cybersecuritycoalition.be/event/bsides-limburg-2026/). ~25 km drive from Slagmolen — closest "real" IT conference in the radius.

### IEEE WCCI 2026 — the biggest AI/CI event ever staged this close to Limburg

Three IEEE conferences (IJCNN, FUZZ-IEEE, IEEE CEC) bundled into one congress at MECC Maastricht, 21-26 June [[5]](https://attend.ieee.org/wcci-2026/). Roughly 1500 researchers expected; a new Industry Day component is being added to bridge academia and applied teams [[6]](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/news/maastricht-will-host-2026-world-conference-computational-intelligence). Dual format — in-person daytime + online evening sessions for non-EU time zones [[5]](https://attend.ieee.org/wcci-2026/). At ~25 km this is genuinely commutable.

### BNAIC/BeNeLearn 2026 — the Benelux AI venue

38th edition of the [Benelux AI conference](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/bnaic2026), co-hosted with the 35th Belgian/Dutch ML conference, 21-23 October in Maastricht [[7]](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/bnaic2026). Smaller and more academic than WCCI but the reference event if your work touches the regional AI/ML community.

### FTI Festival — month-long tech-and-innovation showcase, with Limburg as a real hub

Government-backed Flanders Technology & Innovation festival, 16 October to 15 November 2026 [[9]](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/en/2026/03/11/date-set-for-2026-flanders-technology-and-innovation-festival/). 8 cities; Hasselt and Genk are paired as the Limburg pillar, anchored on Corda Campus and Thor Park [[10]](https://www.cordacampus.com/nieuws/fti-festival/). Programme includes tech safaris through innovation hubs, a [Smart Manufacturing Conference](https://www.ftifestival.be/), a student/entrepreneur hackathon, STEM tours and the public-facing light festival [[8]](https://www.ftifestival.be/) [[10]](https://www.cordacampus.com/nieuws/fti-festival/). Programme culminates in the opening of the new Corda Arena on 13 November [[10]](https://www.cordacampus.com/nieuws/fti-festival/).

## What's *outside* the 30 km radius (don't plan around these)

Belgium's major dev conferences cluster in Antwerp, Mechelen and Brussels — all >70 km from Slagmolen [[22]](https://dev.events/EU/BE):

- **Devoxx Belgium** (Antwerp, 5-9 Oct) — ~75 km
- **DDD Europe** (Antwerp, 8-12 Jun) — ~75 km
- **BruCON 0x12** (Mechelen, 21-25 Sep) — ~100 km
- **CloudBrew** (Mechelen, 10-11 Dec) — ~100 km

Eindhoven's tech scene (e.g. High Tech Campus events) sits at ~50 km — outside the 30 km filter but inside an hour's drive if you stretch the radius.
