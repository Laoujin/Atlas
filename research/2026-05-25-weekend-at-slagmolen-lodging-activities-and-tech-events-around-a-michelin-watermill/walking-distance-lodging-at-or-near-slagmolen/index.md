> **TL;DR.** Book one of [De Slagmolen](https://slagmolen.be/en/)'s **four on-site villa rooms** — they sit beside the watermill dining room and prices include breakfast [[1]](https://slagmolen.be/en/overnight/). The only genuine walking-distance fallback is **Bed and Beyond** at Vinkenkantstraat 33, a 1.6 km / ~20-min walk away [[2]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g3478302-d809326-Slagmolen-Opglabbeek_Limburg_Province.html)[[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g3478302-d3489379-Reviews-Bed_and_Beyond-Opglabbeek_Limburg_Province.html). Everything else is ≥3.7 km — taxi or car only after a wine pairing.

## Why the shortlist is so short

Slagmolen sits at Molenweg 177 in a wooded patch of the Bosbeek valley, between the hamlets of **Louwel** and **Dorne** [[4]](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slagmolen_%28Opglabbeek%29). It is genuinely rural: no village street, no streetlamps, no late buses. TripAdvisor's "10 closest hotels" list confirms that only **one** lodging besides Slagmolen's own rooms lies within 2 km; the next-nearest is 3.7 km out [[2]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g3478302-d809326-Slagmolen-Opglabbeek_Limburg_Province.html). The "walk after dinner" set is therefore exactly two options.

## The two walking-distance options

Slagmolen runs an adjacent villa with **4 luxe rooms** [[5]](https://slagmolen.be/en/), described as chic Scandi-design guestrooms a few steps from the restaurant [[6]](https://www.thebicestercollection.com/maasmechelen-village/nl/lokale-attracties/de-slagmolen). The Michelin Guide specifically flags them: *"The establishment boasts four deluxe rooms to prolong the evening in style."* [[7]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/be-limburg/opglabbeek/restaurant/slagmolen).

**Plan the ride before dinner.** Rural Limburg has no reliable late-night ride-hailing. Uber operates in Opglabbeek but driver availability is thin after dark [[11]](https://www.uber.com/global/en/r/cities/opglabbeek-limbourg-be/) — book a local cab in advance (Taxi ML +32 475 88 88 80 or similar) when you confirm the table. Many guests pair the dinner with [Hotel Mardaga](https://hotelmardaga.be/en/) because it sits in the centre of As across from the trail network for the next day's cycling [[10]](https://hotelmardaga.be/en/).

## Decision

- **You want zero post-dinner logistics and can book by phone →** Slagmolen's own villa rooms. Same parcel as the dining room.
- **Slagmolen's rooms are sold out, you prefer online booking, or you want a sauna →** Bed and Beyond, then walk in daylight or taxi back at night.
- **Both full →** book Hotel Mardaga (4.3 km) and a return cab when you confirm the table; it is the cleanest taxi-distance fallback.
