**One phone call collapses the whole weekend.** Both lodging sub-topics converge independently on the same first move: call Slagmolen on **+32 89 85 48 88** and ask for one of the four villa rooms beside the watermill. The Michelin guide describes the rooms as built precisely so diners "prolong the evening in style" [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/be-limburg/opglabbeek/restaurant/slagmolen); Slagmolen takes the booking by phone only, with no online form and no email [[2]](https://slagmolen.be/en/overnight/). Treat that single call as the trip's load-bearing constraint — the table almost certainly books in the same conversation.

**The walking-radius answer is essentially binary.** TripAdvisor's nearest-hotels list shows exactly one non-Slagmolen lodging inside 2 km — Bed and Beyond at 1.6 km, a designer-host B&B on dark rural roads [[3]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g3478302-d809326-Slagmolen-Opglabbeek_Limburg_Province.html)[[4]](https://www.bedandbeyond.be). The next-nearest property is 3.7 km out. So the decision tree is short: villa room, B&B, or pre-book a taxi when you confirm the table — Uber coverage in Opglabbeek is real but thin after dark [[5]](https://www.uber.com/global/en/r/cities/opglabbeek-limbourg-be/).

**Lodging villages map cleanly onto the activity map**, with two villages worth surfacing that the activities sub-topic under-weights. **Dilsen-Stokkem** — home of [Hostellerie Vivendum](https://www.restaurant-vivendum.com/hotel)'s 1736 chaplain's residence — sits 5 km east and runs its own one-Michelin-star kitchen, making it the strongest *Sunday-lunch* play after the Saturday tasting [[6]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/be-limburg/dilsen/restaurant/hostellerie-vivendum). **As** — where [Hotel Mardaga](https://hotelmardaga.be/en/) and the André Dumont-derrick Station As gateway both sit — pairs the taxi-distance fallback bed with the morning's Hoge Kempen entry point [[7]](https://www.nationaalparkhogekempen.be/en/gateway/as-railway-station-as). Genk (C-Mine, Carbon Hotel), Maaseik (Markt, Aldeneyck winery), Maasmechelen (Terhills, Elaisa) and Lanaken (Pietersheim castle gateway) all double as both bed and day-trip [[8]](https://c-mine.be/en/event/c-mine-expedition)[[9]](https://wijndomein-aldeneyck.be/en/)[[10]](https://www.elaisawellness.com/en/)[[11]](https://www.nationaalparkhogekempen.be/en/gateway/pietersheim-lanaken).

**For a Saturday dinner pinned to a working week of conferences**, BSides Limburg (Thu–Fri 12–13 Mar) and BNAIC/BeNeLearn (Wed–Fri 21–23 Oct) both end on a Friday at venues inside the 30 km arc — Corda Campus Hasselt and MECC Maastricht respectively [[12]](https://www.bsides-limburg.be/)[[13]](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/bnaic2026). The natural extension is a Saturday at Slagmolen, then a Sunday on the *Fietsen door de Heide* bridge before the drive home [[14]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/cycling-through-the-heathland).

**The brief asked for dinner mechanics — pricing, meal length, wine-pairing cost, dress code, table lead time — and none of the four children pinned those down beyond the room-booking process.** That gap is real; close it on the phone call you were going to make anyway.
