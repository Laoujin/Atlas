Decision

Book the four rooms above the watermill itself if available — €245–325, zero taxi, dinner-and-bed in one address[2]. They are the obvious move and they sell out.

If full, taxi 5 km east to Hostellerie Vivendum — a 1736 chaplain's-residence with five rooms above its own 1★ kitchen[7][8]. For a designer B&B in Slagmolen's own village, Bed and Beyond; for a woodland cabin with a hot tub, Warredal.

Slagmolen sits at Molenweg 177, 3660 Opglabbeek — a watermill in the Bosbeek valley holding two Michelin stars[1]. "Taxi range" here means anything inside ~25 km / 25 minutes by road — Genk, Bree, Maaseik, Dilsen-Stokkem all qualify[23]. The eight stays below were picked for character rather than count of stars; routine 3★ business hotels are excluded on purpose.

Sleep where you ate

De Slagmolen — four rooms + Scandi villa

Molenweg 177, Opglabbeek · 0 km · €245–325/night

Four luxury rooms over the restaurant plus chic Scandi-design guestrooms in the adjacent villa, conceived precisely so diners do not have to negotiate alcohol limits or a long drive home[2][3]. Rooms 1 & 3 are €245, rooms 2 & 4 (duplex) €275 — rising to €295 / €325 from 1 July. Phone booking only: +32 89 85 48 88[2]. Includes breakfast and minibar.

Hostellerie Vivendum — 1736 chaplain's residence

Vissersstraat 2, Dilsen-Stokkem · ~5 km · €180–245+

A chaplain's residence dating to 1736, converted into a one-Michelin-star hotel-restaurant whose terrace the guide singles out as one of Belgium's finest[7][8]. Five rooms (incl. one suite and one duplex loft); the catch is that rooms are sold only paired with dinner — so this is a "Sunday-night-after-Slagmolen" play, not a Saturday-night bed-only[7]. ViaMichelin clocks the Opglabbeek → Dilsen drive at ~5 km — a 7-minute taxi[9].

Design-led, intimate

Bed and Beyond — designer's showroom B&B

Vinkenkantstraat 33, Oudsbergen · ~3 km · 4 rooms

Run by Wendy and Tom — an interior designer — who built the four rooms "as a showroom so people can experience the designs," each with its own look[4][5]. Indoor plunge pool, sauna and sanarium are bookable as private wellness[6]. Tripadvisor: #1 of 1 B&Bs in Opglabbeek, 5/5[5]. Same postcode as Slagmolen → ~5-minute cab.

Carbon Hotel — coal-black design hotel

Europalaan 38, Genk · ~15 km · Michelin Guide listed

Sooty-black palette throughout, in tribute to Genk's coal-mining past — minimalist rooms with rain showers, a fifth-floor Carbon Sense City Spa (hamam, treatments) and Restaurant Gusto on-site[19][20]. Two minutes from Genk station, so feasible by train next morning rather than a return taxi[19].

Hotel de Maretak — small inn, private wellness

Oud-Dilsen, near the Oude Maas · ~7 km · 6 rooms

Six rooms with B&B charm but hotel standards, attached private wellness (sauna, jacuzzi, outdoor pool) and a refined restaurant accessible only to hotel guests — so a useful Sunday-lunch fallback if you over-indexed on Slagmolen the night before[10][11].

In the woods

Warredal — cabins & Hobbit House in Hoge Kempen

Ketelstraat 77, Neeroeteren (Maaseik) · ~15 km · from ~€115/night

Around 37 cosy cabins, a Hobbit House and wellness lodges in woodland on the edge of Hoge Kempen National Park, with private saunas and hot tubs available; the park's stated principle is back-to-nature and there is no wifi[16][17]. Pricing from ~€115/night for a classic cabin; treetrunks-with-hot-tub list at €822 / 3 nights[18]. ⚠ Best as a 2–3 night base, not a single Saturday — the park is the point.

Landhuisjes Bij De Wouterbron — bungalows in a nature reserve

Opglabbeek · ~5 km · self-catering bungalows · 8.9/10

Secluded self-catering bungalows inside a nature reserve in Opglabbeek itself, with a seasonal outdoor swimming pond and walking/cycling trails on the doorstep[12]. Listed as official accommodation by Visit Limburg[13]. Each unit has its own bathroom, terrace and fully equipped kitchen — pitched at couples/groups who want privacy over hotel service.

Historic estate

Kasteel Wurfeld — castle hotel on 2.5 ha

Kapelweg 60, Maaseik · ~15 km · 32 rooms

A small castle hotel ranked #1 of 2 hotels in Maaseik, with 32 atmospheric rooms in landscaped grounds and the Wintergarden restaurant on-site[14][15]. ⚠ Tripadvisor average is a moderate 4.1/5 over 313 reviews and Jan–Feb 2025 stays flagged musty odours and inconsistent food — the building delivers, the operation is uneven[15].

Hotel Stiemerheide — English-cottage golf hotel

Wiemesmeerstraat 105, Genk · ~15 km · 69 rooms

Late-80s build in the style of an English country cottage, 69 rooms, set on the 18th hole of Spiegelven Golf Club at the gateway of Hoge Kempen National Park[21]. Restaurant Moonstone under chef Koen Somers carries a Michelin Bib Gourmand — a useful Sunday-lunch option if you do not want to repeat fine dining[22]. The scale (69 rooms) makes this the least "boutique" of the set — included because the building's character genuinely is distinctive.

What we deliberately left out

La Butte aux Bois in Lanaken (5-star, 2★ La Source) and Hotel Van Eyck on Maaseik's central square are both excellent, but Lanaken sits ~30 km south and Van Eyck is a conventional 4★ on a market square — neither fits "character + taxi range" the way the eight above do. Anywhere ≥25 km starts to push a one-way cab past €50 and the value of staying near Slagmolen evaporates[23].
