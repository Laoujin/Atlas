> **TL;DR / Decision.** Slagmolen sits inside Belgium's only national park, so a 30 km arc covers an unusually dense slice of Flanders. Pick one and book the rest around dinner:
> – **Nature day:** Mechelse Heide + the 294 m *Fietsen door de Heide* heath-bridge [[2]](https://www.nationaalparkhogekempen.be/en/gateway/mechelse-heide-maasmechelen)[[11]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/cycling-through-the-heathland).
> – **Culture day:** Bokrijk open-air museum + C-Mine Expeditie in Genk [[27]](https://bokrijk.be/en/your-visit/information-on-tickets/opening-hours/)[[28]](https://c-mine.be/en/event/c-mine-expedition).
> – **Family day:** Plopsa Indoor Hasselt then Kelchterhoef lake [[41]](https://www.plopsa.com/en/plopsa-indoor-hasselt/tickets)[[44]](https://visit.houthalen-helchteren.be/voor-gezinnen/recreatiedomeinen/domein-kelchterhoef/lekker-genieten-aan-de-plas).
> – **Soft day before dinner:** Maasmechelen Village outlet + Elaisa wellness at Terhills [[60]](https://www.thebicestercollection.com/maasmechelen-village/en/opening-hours/)[[77]](https://www.elaisawellness.com/tarieven-vanaf-05-01-2026/).

## Where you are

Slagmolen is on the Bosbeek in Opglabbeek (Oudsbergen), in eastern Belgian Limburg. The mill itself is a 1511-mentioned undershot oil watermill, restored in 1685 and protected since 1995 — the restaurant is the period building, not a recreation [[39]](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slagmolen_(Opglabbeek)). A 30 km arc reaches every Hoge Kempen gateway, all three Limburg cycle-through bridges, Maaseik in the north, Hasselt on the western edge and Lanaken on the southern. Things that *just* miss the radius: Lommelse Sahara (~44 km) [[59]](http://belgium.eudistance.com/distance/24108935-24105684) and Thermae 2000 in Dutch Valkenburg (~40-45 km) [[84]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/valkenburg_aan_de_geul-6301-limburg-_-netherlands-to-maasmechelen-3630-tongeren-flemish_region-belgium).

**Wildlife.** Year-round grazers — Norwegian Fjord horses, Konik horses, Angus cattle, donkeys — maintain landscape variation in the park itself; along the Maas in adjacent Rivierpark Maasvallei you see almost exclusively Galloways and Koniks creating mosaic open grassland [[13]](https://www.nationaalparkhogekempen.be/nl/natuur/beheer-voor-meer-en-betere-natuur).

**When to come for the purple.** Calluna heather blooms mid-August to mid-September; that's the postcard window for Mechelse Heide [[14]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/hoge-kempen-national-park).

**On the doorstep.** [Duinengordel](https://www.duinengordel.be/) in Oudsbergen is a ~3,000 ha nature area, part of Hoge Kempen since 2020, with 82 km of marked trails across Oudsberg, Donderslag and Solterheide — no entry fee [[56]](https://www.duinengordel.be/). It contains the 12 ha Joekelbos, the country's largest adventure-style play forest, stroller-accessible at Speeltuinstraat [[57]](https://www.oudsbergen.be/duinengordel).

## 2. The three "Fietsen door…" bridges

Limburg built three signature elevated cycle structures in the last decade, all reachable inside 30 km via the province's numbered-node bike network. They are the single most-photographed cycling thing in Flanders.

| Bridge                       | Where                                  | Node | What it is                                                                                  | Opened   | Source |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------ |
| **Fietsen door het Water**   | Bokrijk, Genk (Bokrijklaan 1)          | 91   | >200 m, 3 m wide dyke path through a De Wijers pond; water at eye level on both sides       | Apr 2016 | [b1][b1], [b2][b2] |
| **Fietsen door de Bomen**    | Pijnven / Bosland, Hechtel-Eksel       | 272  | 700 m double helix, 100 m diameter, climbs to 10 m above the pine canopy; wheelchair-OK    | Jun 2019 | [b3][b3], [b4][b4] |
| **Fietsen door de Heide**    | Mechelse Heide, Maasmechelen           | 550-551 | 4 km route across 700 ha of heath, anchored by a ~300 m wooden panoramic bridge          | Jul 2021 | [b5][b5], [b6][b6] |

**How to ride them.** Bikes (city, family, e-bike, tandem, mobility scooter) come from [Fietsparadijs Limburg](https://fietsparadijslimburg.be/afhaalpunten) hubs at Bokrijk, Station As, Kattevennen, Hasselt, Houthalen-Helchteren, Lieteberg-Zutendaal and Pietersheim-Lanaken; 2026 rental runs daily 10:00-18:00 from 4 April to 8 November [[21]](https://fietsparadijslimburg.be/afhaalpunten).

**Suggested loops.**
- From Bokrijk Castle parking, an accessible **6.3 km loop from junction 243** drops you onto the *Fietsen door het Water* span at node 91 [[24]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/bicycle-rental-bokrijk-genk).
- From the Pijnven parking, Bosland publishes **34 km and 62 km loops** that take in the *Fietsen door de Bomen* helix [[23]](https://www.nationaalparkbosland.be/ontdek/fietsen/fietsen-door-de-bomen-suggestielussen/94/).
- The *Fietsen door de Heide* bridge has **no parking on-site** — enter through any Hoge Kempen gateway (Terhills, Lieteberg, Station As, Kattevennen, Pietersheim) [[11]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/cycling-through-the-heathland). It drew ~15,000 cyclists in its first opening week so go early at weekends [[20]](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fietsen_door_de_Heide).
- One-day greedy combo: Fietsknooppunt.be publishes a stitched route that does *Fietsen door het Water + Fietsen door de Heide* in a single loop [[22]](https://www.fietsknooppunt.be/nl-be/fietsen/routes/r1015/fietsen-door-het-water-en-fietsen-door-de-heide).

## 5. Food, drink, shopping

### Outlet & city shopping

[Maasmechelen Village](https://www.thebicestercollection.com/maasmechelen-village/en/) is the Bicester Collection's only Belgian outlet — **>100 designer boutiques** with up to 60% off [[61]](https://www.thebicestercollection.com/maasmechelen-village/en/brands/), open **every day including Sunday 10:00-19:00** (closed only 25 Dec and 1 Jan), with 1,600+ free parking spaces [[60]](https://www.thebicestercollection.com/maasmechelen-village/en/opening-hours/)[[62]](https://www.thebicestercollection.com/maasmechelen-village/en/company/faq). For a small Belgian outlet centre the Sunday opening is a non-trivial weekend hook.

### Jenever

Hasselt has been the centre of the Belgian jenever industry since the 17th century [[63]](https://www.jenevermuseum.be/en/jenever); *Hasseltse jenever* carries protected geographical status. If your visit overlaps **17-18 October 2026** you'll hit the **Jeneverfeesten** — the Borrelmanneke fountain on the Maastrichterstraat then dispenses jenever instead of water [[64]](https://jeneverfeesten.hasselt.be/nl).

### Wine

[Wijndomein Aldeneyck](https://wijndomein-aldeneyck.be/en/) in Maaseik (~20 km) farms 10 ha on the south-east-facing Meuse slopes — the biggest serious winery in range [[65]](https://wijndomein-aldeneyck.be/en/). It has repeatedly been crowned **Best Belgian Wine** and won the **Gault-Millau Belgian Wine Award 2023** for Riesling 2019 (white) and Charles Pinot Noir 2018 (red) [[67]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/vineyard-aldeneyck). Open tastings Fri-Sat 10:00-18:00, reservation recommended [[66]](https://wijndomein-aldeneyck.be/en/wijndegustatie/). Smaller alternative on the radius edge: [Wijngoed Pietershof](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/vineyard-pietershof), whose Chardonnay was once named Best Belgian Wine [[68]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/vineyard-pietershof).

For quiet outdoor decompression there's no need to pay: a Mechelse Heide loop in late August or the 2.5 km Beestige Multimovopad at Kelchterhoef both deliver a long afternoon for nothing [[45]](https://visit.houthalen-helchteren.be/te-doen/voor-gezinnen/recreatiedomeinen/domein-kelchterhoef)[[14]](https://www.visitlimburg.be/en/what-to-do/hoge-kempen-national-park).
