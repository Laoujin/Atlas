> **Decision.** Want logistics to disappear after a 3-star tasting? Sleep on-site at the Hôtel & Spa du Castellet — same building as the restaurant [[1]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/plan-your-stay/restaurants/all-restaurants/the-table-du-castellet-le-castellet-en-4874085/). Want Provençal-village character at a fraction of the rate? [Maison Bérard](https://hotel-berard.com/en/) in La Cadière-d'Azur, ~10 min by taxi [[5]](https://hotel-berard.com/en/). Want a chambres d'hôtes where hosts pour breakfast? [Bastide de Fontvieille](https://www.bastidedefontvieille.com/en/) in Saint-Cyr-sur-Mer [[13]](https://www.bastidedefontvieille.com/en/). Want a Ducasse-restored 18th-c. abbey as the destination itself? [Hostellerie de l'Abbaye de la Celle](https://www.abbaye-celle.com/en/) [[7]](https://www.abbaye-celle.com/en/) — but at 49 km it stretches the meaning of "taxi range" [[8]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/La-Celle-Var/Le-Castellet).

## Where La Table sits

La Table du Castellet is inside the Hôtel & Spa du Castellet at 3001 Route des Hauts du Camp, 83330 Le Castellet [[1]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/plan-your-stay/restaurants/all-restaurants/the-table-du-castellet-le-castellet-en-4874085/) — three Michelin stars since March 2024 under chef Fabien Ferré, retained in the 2026 guide [[2]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/provence-alpes-cote-dazur/le-castellet/restaurant/la-table-du-castellet). The restaurant is *in* the hotel, so the shortest stay is no taxi at all; everything else is graded by how long the trip back takes after dinner ends.

## The properties

### [Hôtel & Spa du Castellet](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/) — on site

43 rooms and suites in ochre-toned, Tuscan-influenced buildings spread across a 12-hectare umbrella-pine park at 430 m elevation [[3]](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/). Beyond La Table, the property runs Le San Felice (wood-fired grill), L'Arèn Bar, a 700 m² spa, and an on-site golf course [[3]](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/). Relais & Châteaux + Les Clefs d'Or [[3]](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/). Indicative rates run $303–$939/night per TripAdvisor's 2026 ranges; 4.6/5 from 926 reviews, with mixed signals — service and grounds get strong marks, some reviewers flag dated décor [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1596637-d279305-Reviews-Hotel_et_Spa_du_Castellet-Le_Castellet_Var_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html). Picks itself if you want the meal-to-bed distance to be a corridor.

### [Maison Bérard](https://hotel-berard.com/en/) — La Cadière-d'Azur (~10 min taxi)

32 rooms across four village buildings — Maison historique, Les Peintres, Le Couvent, La Bastide — with pool and bistro on the back side [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g635827-d279297-Reviews-Hostellerie_Berard-La_Cadiere_d_Azur_Var_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html). Family-run, Teritoria-affiliated, with the Riva restaurant (Provence + Italy) and a 500 m² AromaSpa [[5]](https://hotel-berard.com/en/). ⚠ Reviews are mixed on rooms — quality varies meaningfully by which of the four buildings you're booked into [[6]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g635827-d279297-Reviews-Hostellerie_Berard-La_Cadiere_d_Azur_Var_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html). Best when you target the renovated La Bastide rooms specifically.

### [La Maison du Castellet](https://www.la-maison-du-castellet.fr/en) — Bandol-vineyard side, Le Plan (a few min)

A small residence with five independent guest rooms opening onto private terraces, plus pool, sauna, gym, run by hosts Nafissa and Sébastien [[12]](https://www.la-maison-du-castellet.fr/en). Right scale if you want the medieval village close at hand without committing to a 5★ rate. ⚠ Five rooms only — books out far ahead during Paul Ricard Grand Prix weeks.

### [Bastide de Fontvieille](https://www.bastidedefontvieille.com/en/) — Saint-Cyr-sur-Mer (~15 min)

Four suites in an 18th-century former wine estate lined with cypress, plane, olive and fruit trees, 3 km from the sea [[13]](https://www.bastidedefontvieille.com/en/). Rooms open onto the garden; breakfast is local-discovery-of-the-day from hosts Stéphanie and Éric [[13]](https://www.bastidedefontvieille.com/en/). #1 of 6 B&Bs in Saint-Cyr per TripAdvisor with 5/5 [[13]](https://www.bastidedefontvieille.com/en/). The right answer if "special character" means hosts who know the village, not a 5★ logo.

### [Île Rousse Thalazur](https://www.bandoltourisme.fr/en/hotels-a/hotel-ile-rousse-thalazur/) — Bandol, Renécros Bay (~15 min taxi, $30–40)

5★, 55 rooms + 12 suites overlooking Renécros Bay, infinity pool heated year-round with sea water, two private beaches [[9]](https://www.bandoltourisme.fr/en/hotels-a/hotel-ile-rousse-thalazur/). The thalasso draw is real — 100 m² marine zone, indoor seawater pool at 32 °C, hammam, hydromassage, jacuzzi [[9]](https://www.bandoltourisme.fr/en/hotels-a/hotel-ile-rousse-thalazur/). Gourmet dining is Les Oliviers under chef Martin Féragus; lighter at Bistrot Lumière and La Goélette on the sand [[9]](https://www.bandoltourisme.fr/en/hotels-a/hotel-ile-rousse-thalazur/). Bandol-to-Castellet is the cheapest, fastest taxi pair on this list [[14]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Bandol-Station/Le-Castellet).

### [Les Roches Blanches](https://www.roches-blanches-cassis.com/en/) — Cassis (~21 min taxi, $70–90)

An 1887 mansion converted to a hotel in the early 1920s, perched between Cassis village and the Calanques with Cap Canaille views; past guests include Churchill and Piaf [[10]](https://www.roches-blanches-cassis.com/en/). Sisley spa, Michelin 2 Keys 2025, four dining venues: gastro Les Belles Canailles, Loup Bar (Nikkei), Rocco (Italian), plus brunch [[10]](https://www.roches-blanches-cassis.com/en/). Round-trip taxi to La Table runs ~$140–180 [[11]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Cassis/Le-Castellet) — you're paying for the view, not the shortest commute. Best if Cassis + Calanques are part of the weekend plan anyway.

### [Grand Hôtel des Bains](https://www.provenceweb.fr/e/var/sanary/hotels.htm) — Sanary-sur-Mer (~25 min)

1890 Belle-Époque property in a 3,000 m² park, 2 minutes' walk from the nearest beach, recently renovated [[17]](https://www.provenceweb.fr/e/var/sanary/hotels.htm). Lower star-rating than the Bandol/Cassis 5★ peers, but the period architecture and the Sanary port — Sunday market, Provençal harbor town — carry more character than chain options. Right call if you want Sanary's Sunday morning as part of the weekend, not just dinner.

### [Hostellerie de l'Abbaye de la Celle](https://www.abbaye-celle.com/en/) — La Celle (~49 min ⚠)

Alain Ducasse's 1999 conversion of an 18th-c. inn beside the Abbey of La Celle; ten refined rooms on 2.5 ha of vineyards, olive groves, and a vegetable garden that supplies the restaurant under chef Nicolas Pierantoni [[7]](https://www.abbaye-celle.com/en/). The catch: 48.9 km, 49-minute drive [[8]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/La-Celle-Var/Le-Castellet) — a round-trip taxi after a 3★ dinner crosses €200 easily. Treat this as a destination in itself, paired with a *lunch* booking at La Table (Friday–Sunday) rather than Saturday dinner.

## Taxi logistics — the actual constraint

- **Le Castellet has no permanent taxi stand at the hillside village** — call a Var VTC company in advance for both the pickup and the return [[15]](https://reserver-un-vtc-dans-le-var.fr/en/taxi-le-castellet/).
- **24/7 service operates out of Bandol** — Taxi Bandol Gérald advertises drop-and-return runs for nightlife [[16]](https://taxi-bandol.fr/), which is the service shape a late dinner needs.
- **Indicative fares** (Rome2Rio, 2026): Bandol ↔ Le Castellet 10.3 km / 15 min / $30–40 [[14]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Bandol-Station/Le-Castellet); Cassis ↔ Le Castellet 25.6 km / 21 min / $70–90 [[11]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Cassis/Le-Castellet); La Celle ↔ Le Castellet 48.9 km / 49 min [[8]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/La-Celle-Var/Le-Castellet), figure ≈€100 each way at night.
- **Book the return when you book the pickup.** La Table's dinner service runs late; you don't want to be hunting for a VTC at 23:30 on a hill.

## Picks by use case

- **Apex weekend, no compromise:** [Hôtel & Spa du Castellet](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/) — same address as the restaurant [[1]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/plan-your-stay/restaurants/all-restaurants/the-table-du-castellet-le-castellet-en-4874085/), full spa and golf as the Sunday [[3]](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/).
- **Best character-to-spend ratio:** [Maison Bérard](https://hotel-berard.com/en/) in La Cadière-d'Azur, 10 min away, book La Bastide rooms specifically [[5]](https://hotel-berard.com/en/).
- **Chambres d'hôtes with hosts who know the area:** [Bastide de Fontvieille](https://www.bastidedefontvieille.com/en/) [[13]](https://www.bastidedefontvieille.com/en/).
- **Sea-view 5★ + Sunday spa:** [Île Rousse Thalazur](https://www.bandoltourisme.fr/en/hotels-a/hotel-ile-rousse-thalazur/) on Renécros Bay [[9]](https://www.bandoltourisme.fr/en/hotels-a/hotel-ile-rousse-thalazur/) — cheapest taxi pair to La Table [[14]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Bandol-Station/Le-Castellet).
- **Cinematic seaview, Cassis + Calanques weekend:** [Les Roches Blanches](https://www.roches-blanches-cassis.com/en/) [[10]](https://www.roches-blanches-cassis.com/en/).
- **Trade taxi time for a one-of-one stay:** [Hostellerie de l'Abbaye de la Celle](https://www.abbaye-celle.com/en/) [[7]](https://www.abbaye-celle.com/en/) — but book a Castellet *lunch*, not Saturday dinner [[8]](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/La-Celle-Var/Le-Castellet).
