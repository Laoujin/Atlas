Hôtel & Spa du Castellet

Le Castellet · 3001 Route des Hauts du Camp · 430 m

⌂ walk · 0 min ★ ★ ★ ★ ★ 43 rooms / suites Relais & Châteaux 700 m² spa

Tuscan-ochre estate, 12-ha umbrella-pine park. The restaurant is in the building; the shortest possible commute home is a corridor [1].

Beyond La Table, the property runs Le San Felice (wood-fired grill), L'Arèn Bar, the 700 m² spa, and an on-site golf course [3]. Indicative rates run $303–$939/night per TripAdvisor; 4.6/5 from 926 reviews, with mixed signals — service and grounds get strong marks, some flag dated décor [4].

Best forZero logistics + full Sunday spa.
Provenance · Bandol-AOC vineyards along the route de Bandol (Wikimedia Commons). The property sits on the same vineyard side, in Le Plan du Castellet.

La Maison du Castellet

Le Plan du Castellet · independent residence

⛟ taxi · ~5 min 5 rooms Pool · sauna · gym Independent hosts

Five independent guest rooms, each with private terrace. Run by hosts Nafissa and Sébastien [12].

A small residence on the Bandol-vineyard side of the commune, with pool, sauna and gym — the right scale if you want the medieval village close at hand without committing to a 5★ rate. ⚠ Five rooms only — books out far ahead during Paul Ricard Grand Prix weeks.

Family-run, Provençal-restored, four village buildings. Maison historique, Les Peintres, Le Couvent, La Bastide — plus the Riva restaurant (Provence + Italy) [5].

Bastide de Fontvieille

Saint-Cyr-sur-Mer · 18th-c. wine estate

⛟ taxi · 15 min 4 suites Chambres d'hôtes 3,200 m² garden 5/5 · #1 B&B

18th-c. former wine estate, cypress / plane / olive / fruit trees, 3 km from the sea. Hosts Stéphanie and Éric; breakfast is local-discovery-of-the-day [13].

Four suites in the garden bastide, ranked #1 of 6 B&Bs in Saint-Cyr on TripAdvisor with 5/5. The right answer if "special character" means hosts who know the village, not a 5★ logo.

⛟ taxi · 15 min $30–40 each way ★ ★ ★ ★ ★ 55 rooms + 12 suites Thalasso · 100 m²

Year-round-heated seawater infinity pool. Two private beaches. The cheapest, fastest taxi pair to La Table on this list [14].

100 m² marine zone, indoor seawater pool at 32 °C, hammam, hydromassage, jacuzzi [9]. Gourmet dining at Les Oliviers (chef Martin Féragus); lighter at Bistrot Lumière and La Goélette on the sand.

Les Roches Blanches

Cassis · Cap Canaille view · Michelin 2 Keys 2025

⛟ taxi · 21 min $70–90 each way ★ ★ ★ ★ ★ ~45 rooms Sisley Spa · 4 venues

1887 mansion, converted to a hotel in the early 1920s. Past guests include Churchill and Piaf [10]. Perched between Cassis village and the Calanques.

Four dining venues: gastro Les Belles Canailles, Loup Bar (Nikkei), Rocco (Italian), plus brunch. Round-trip taxi to La Table runs ~$140–180 [11] — you're paying for the view, not the shortest commute.

Grand Hôtel des Bains

Sanary-sur-Mer · 1890 Belle-Époque

⛟ taxi · 25 min est. 1890 3,000 m² park 2-min walk to beach

1890 Belle-Époque property, recently renovated, in a 3,000 m² park. A 2-minute walk to the nearest beach [17].

Lower star-rating than the Bandol/Cassis 5★ peers, but the period architecture and the Sanary port — Sunday market, Provençal harbor town — carry more character than chain options. Right call if you want Sanary's Sunday morning as part of the weekend, not just dinner.

Best forPeriod seaside + harbor-town Sunday.

~49 min taxi · La Celle, inland Var ⚠

Hostellerie de l'Abbaye de la Celle

La Celle · 18th-c. inn beside the Abbey of La Celle

⛟ taxi · 49 min ⚠ ~€100 each way ★ ★ ★ ★ ★ 10 rooms Ducasse · 1999

Alain Ducasse's 1999 conversion of an 18th-c. inn beside the abbey. Ten refined rooms on 2.5 ha of vineyards, olive groves, and a vegetable garden that supplies the restaurant under chef Nicolas Pierantoni [7].

⚠ The catch: 48.9 km, 49-minute drive [8] — round-trip taxi after a 3★ dinner crosses €200 easily. Treat this as a destination in itself, paired with a lunch booking at La Table (Friday–Sunday) rather than Saturday dinner.

Best forSide-trip pilgrimage, not stumble home.
The actual constraint

Taxi logistics.

Le Castellet is a hillside village; there's no walk-up stand. Every property on this catalog, except the on-site one, depends on a pre-booked VTC for the return.

Local rule № 1

No taxi stand on the hill

Le Castellet has no permanent taxi rank at the village. Call a Var VTC company in advance for both the pickup and the return [15].

Local rule № 2

24/7 service runs out of Bandol

Taxi Bandol Gérald advertises drop-and-return runs for nightlife [16] — exactly the service shape a late tasting menu needs.
