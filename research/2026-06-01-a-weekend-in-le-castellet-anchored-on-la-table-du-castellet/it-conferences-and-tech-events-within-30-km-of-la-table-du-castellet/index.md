> **TL;DR.** Inside a 30 km radius of La Table du Castellet, **Toulon (~23 km) is the only real tech hub** [[1]](https://www.viamichelin.com/routes/results/toulon-83000-var-provence_alpes_cote_d_azur-france-to-le_castellet-83330-var-provence_alpes_cote_d_azur-france) — driven by [French Tech Toulon](https://www.frenchtechtoulon.fr/) monthly meetups [[27]](https://www.frenchtechtoulon.fr/les-meetups), [ISEN Méditerranée](https://isen-mediterranee.fr/) hackathons [[10]](https://isen-mediterranee.fr/en/evenements/actinspace-2026/), and [Pôle Mer Méditerranée](https://polemermediterranee.com/) defence/maritime-AI events [[16]](https://polemermediterranee.com/evenements/journee-mer-et-defense-2026/). La Ciotat (~19 km) [[2]](https://www.rome2rio.com/fr/s/La-Ciotat/Le-Castellet) and the Bandol/Sanary/Six-Fours coast have business clusters but no IT conferences of their own. **For a Saturday-dinner trip, the only weekend-shaped events are Var Gaming Festival (late Jan / early Feb) and ActInSpace (24h hackathon, same weekend)** [[7]](https://www.info83.fr/var-gaming-festival-2026-un-week-end-dedie-au-jeu-video-et-a-la-creativite-numerique-a-toulon/)[[10]](https://isen-mediterranee.fr/en/evenements/actinspace-2026/). Everything else is a weekday evening meetup.

| Aubagne               | ~32 (⚠ just outside)    | Some startup activity, outside the strict radius                              |

Hyères, Marseille and Aix-en-Provence sit beyond 30 km and are excluded.

## The ecosystem in one screen

- **[TVT Innovation](https://www.tvt.fr/)** — Métropole Toulon Provence Méditerranée's economic-development agency. Operates Chalucet (coworking + FabLab + accelerator) and runs ~500-member innovation community [[24]](https://www.tvt.fr/). Most weekday meetups land here.
- **[French Tech Toulon](https://www.frenchtechtoulon.fr/)** — national label renewed in 2025; monthly meetups, AI focus across the 2026 cycle [[6]](https://mesinfos.fr/83000-toulon/la-french-tech-toulon-fait-sa-rentree-2026-sous-le-signe-de-l-ouverture-239853.html).
- **[ISEN Méditerranée](https://isen-mediterranee.fr/)** — engineering school anchoring the student hackathons (ActInSpace, Global Game Jam, Nuit du Code) and the Rencontre Recherche & Entreprises [[10]](https://isen-mediterranee.fr/en/evenements/actinspace-2026/)[[11]](https://isen-mediterranee.fr/rencontre-recherche-entreprise-2026/)[[12]](https://isen-mediterranee.fr/en/hackathons-2026/).
- **[Pôle Mer Méditerranée](https://polemermediterranee.com/)** — maritime competitiveness cluster (one of TVT's three business units). Runs the AI & Maritime rendez-vous and the headline Journée Mer & Défense [[16]](https://polemermediterranee.com/evenements/journee-mer-et-defense-2026/)[[17]](https://polemermediterranee.com/evenements/rendez-vous-mer-donnees-rencontre-marche-ia-maritime/).
- **[Université de Toulon](https://www.univ-tln.fr/)** — runs the academic conferences (digital sovereignty, AI ethics, media & AI), plus Ingémédia at Champ de Mars [[13]](https://www.univ-tln.fr/Conference-L-informatisation-du-monde-un-progres-pour-l-humanite.html)[[14]](https://presseagence.fr/toulon-medias-lintelligence-artificielle-au-coeur-dun-debat-public-a-luniversite/).
