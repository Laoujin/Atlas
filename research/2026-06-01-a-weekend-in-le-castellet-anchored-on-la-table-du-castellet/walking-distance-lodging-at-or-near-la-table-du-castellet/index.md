> **Decision:** Book the [Hôtel & Spa du Castellet](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/) — La Table du Castellet is on the ground floor of the same building, no transfer needed [[1]](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/la-table-du-castellet.html). The only other hotel within walking distance is the 3★ [Grand Prix Hôtel](https://grandprixhotel.fr/en/) (~700 m east on Route des Hauts du Camp [[9]](https://www.kayak.com/Le-Castellet-Hotels-Grand-Prix-Hotel.315989.ksp)), useful as a budget fallback but on an unlit rural road ⚠ — plan to walk back in daylight or take a 5-minute taxi after dinner [[7]](https://grandprixhotel.fr/en/access.html). The perched medieval village of Le Castellet is several km away on a separate hill [[12]](https://www.france-voyage.com/tourism/castellet-784.htm) — not walkable.

## What "walking distance" actually looks like here

La Table du Castellet sits inside the [Hôtel & Spa du Castellet](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/) at 3001 Route des Hauts du Camp [[1]](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/la-table-du-castellet.html). The whole site — hotel, restaurant, 700 m² spa, 6-hole golf course, 12-hectare park of umbrella pines — is on the **Camp du Castellet plateau** at 430 m altitude, roughly 1 km north of Circuit Paul Ricard [[2]](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/) [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g1596637-d7015106-Circuit_Paul_Ricard-Le_Castellet_Var_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html).

It is an isolated plateau. Aside from the circuit, an airfield, and the [Grand Prix Hôtel](https://grandprixhotel.fr/en/) ~700 m east at 3100 Route des Hauts du Camp [[7]](https://grandprixhotel.fr/en/access.html) [[9]](https://www.kayak.com/Le-Castellet-Hotels-Grand-Prix-Hotel.315989.ksp), there is **nothing else within 1 km on foot**. The famous perched stone village of Le Castellet — the postcard image — is on a separate hill several kilometres south [[12]](https://www.france-voyage.com/tourism/castellet-784.htm); reachable by car or taxi only.

### Hôtel & Spa du Castellet — the obvious pick

5-star Relais & Châteaux on a 12-hectare park, 43 rooms each with a private terrace [[2]](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/) [[3]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/plan-your-stay/accommodation/all-accommodation/hotels/hotel-spa-du-castellet-in-the-var-le-castellet-en-2867884/). The spa was named "Best Luxury Spa in Europe" in 2019 [[3]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/plan-your-stay/accommodation/all-accommodation/hotels/hotel-spa-du-castellet-in-the-var-le-castellet-en-2867884/). Ranked #1 of 2 hotels in Le Castellet on Tripadvisor at 4.5/5 [[5]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1596637-d279305-Reviews-Hotel_et_Spa_du_Castellet-Le_Castellet_Var_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html); momondo: 9.2/10 "Excellent" across 262 reviews [[6]](https://www.momondo.com/hotels/le-castellet/Hotel-et-Spa-du-Castellet.mhd25952.ksp). Common gripes: dated decor in some rooms; breakfast not included [[6]](https://www.momondo.com/hotels/le-castellet/Hotel-et-Spa-du-Castellet.mhd25952.ksp).

For a Saturday-evening dinner that anchors the whole weekend, this is the no-brainer. You walk down to dinner; you walk back up. No taxi, no after-wine driving, no parking math. The Michelin Guide also lists it as a recommended hotel stay [[11]](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/provence-alpes-cote-dazur/le-castellet/restaurant/la-table-du-castellet).

### Grand Prix Hôtel — the budget walkable option

3-star Best Western at 3100 Route des Hauts du Camp [[7]](https://grandprixhotel.fr/en/access.html), about 0.45 mi (~720 m) east of the Hôtel du Castellet along the same road [[9]](https://www.kayak.com/Le-Castellet-Hotels-Grand-Prix-Hotel.315989.ksp). 117 rooms, 4.3/5 on Tripadvisor [[4]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsNear-g1596637-d7015106-Circuit_Paul_Ricard-Le_Castellet_Var_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html) [[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1596637-d1599602-Reviews-Grand_Prix_Hotel_Restaurant_3-Le_Castellet_Var_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html). Outdoor pool, fitness room, on-site Italian restaurant La Squadra Trattoria, soundproof rooms — built for the motorsport clientele next door [[8]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1596637-d1599602-Reviews-Grand_Prix_Hotel_Restaurant_3-Le_Castellet_Var_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur.html).

**Walk-back caveats after a 3-star tasting menu:**

- Route des Hauts du Camp is a country road across a rural plateau — no street lighting once you leave hotel grounds [[7]](https://grandprixhotel.fr/en/access.html). ⚠
- 10 minutes on foot in daylight is fine; after dinner in May–June you'll likely finish around 22:30–23:00 in the dark.
- Either walk earlier (a Friday/Sunday lunch is daylight; Wed–Sat dinner is not [[1]](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/la-table-du-castellet.html)), bring a phone flashlight and sensible shoes, or pre-book a 5-minute taxi from the Hôtel du Castellet front desk before you sit down.
- Worth it when the spread between the two hotels is large — Grand Prix from ~$143/night vs Hôtel du Castellet from ~$309–347 [[6]](https://www.momondo.com/hotels/le-castellet/Hotel-et-Spa-du-Castellet.mhd25952.ksp) [[9]](https://www.kayak.com/Le-Castellet-Hotels-Grand-Prix-Hotel.315989.ksp) — i.e. more than 2×. On Formula 1 / motorsport weekends both surge; the spread compresses and the convenience gap matters more.

## Everything else is a taxi

The plateau ends at the perimeter of the circuit. Stay anywhere else and you're driving — there is no sidewalk network between the plateau and the surrounding villages.

| Off-plateau option                              | Approx. distance to La Table | Why not walking                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Medieval village Le Castellet (B&Bs, restaurants) | ~6 km on D26 [[12]][c12]      | Separate perched hill, downhill country road         |
| Le Beausset, Le Camp-du-Castellet hamlet        | 4–7 km                        | Country roads, no pedestrian infrastructure          |
| Bandol, Sanary-sur-Mer (coast)                  | 15–25 km                      | ~20–30 min by car; coastal route                     |

[c12]: https://www.france-voyage.com/tourism/castellet-784.htm

A taxi from Marseille Provence airport to Circuit Paul Ricard runs ~€130 daytime [[13]](https://www.taxienprovence.com/details-taxi+aeroport+marseille+provence+tarif+pour+circuit+paul+ricard+castellet-199.html); local short hops from a village B&B are naturally far less, but the logistics of finding a return car at 23:00 on an unlit rural plateau is the real cost. If you stay off-plateau, **pre-book the return ride before you sit down to dinner**.

## Booking notes

- La Table du Castellet opens **Wed–Sat for dinner, Fri–Sun for lunch** [[1]](https://www.hotelducastellet.net/en/la-table-du-castellet.html) [[10]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/plan-your-stay/restaurants/all-restaurants/the-table-du-castellet-le-castellet-en-4874085/). A Saturday-evening anchor is in scope; book the restaurant first, then the room around it.
- The hotel closes **Dec 1 – Feb 28** every year [[3]](https://provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/en/plan-your-stay/accommodation/all-accommodation/hotels/hotel-spa-du-castellet-in-the-var-le-castellet-en-2867884/) — La Table closes with it. Confirm dates before booking.
- Both on-plateau hotels book out months ahead on F1 / motorsport weekends at Circuit Paul Ricard — and price accordingly.
