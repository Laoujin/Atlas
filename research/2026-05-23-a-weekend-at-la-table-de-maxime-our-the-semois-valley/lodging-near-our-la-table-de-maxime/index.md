> **Decision.** Book one of the **6 rooms above La Table de Maxime itself** (€240–260 [[1]](https://www.maximecollard.be/en/la-table-de-maxime/)) — it's the only way to wobble three metres from a 2-Michelin-star tasting menu [[7]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/be-luxembourg/our/restaurant/la-table-de-maxime) to bed. If those are full (likely — only six exist), the next-best on-site picks are **La Fabrique du Pré Maho** (tree-stilt eco-build 200 m away [[2]](https://www.maximecollard.be/en/la-fabrique-du-pre-maho/)) or budget **Les Terrasses de l'Our** in the same village from €150 [[5]](https://www.lesterrassesdelour.be/). True fallback when the hamlet is sold out: **Le Moulin de Daverdisse**, 4★ with pool ~11 km away [[9]](https://www.daverdisse.com/).

## Context

Our is a 30-house granite hamlet inside the Paliseul commune, on the river Our in the Belgian Ardennes [[17]](https://qualitylodgings.com/en/la-table-de-maxime-hotel-restaurant). Chef Maxime Collard — Gault&Millau Chef of the Year 2024 and two Michelin stars in the 2026 guide [[7]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/be-luxembourg/our/restaurant/la-table-de-maxime) [[8]](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/luxembourg/2026/05/04/michelin-2026-la-table-de-maxime-garde-ses-2-etoiles-et-7-restaurants-conservent-la-leur-mais-un-bib-gourmand-en-moins-en-province-de-luxembourg-ICNM5DCL2NBPFL5P7ML7IMH57U/) — runs a small portfolio of four lodgings within walking or cycling distance of the restaurant [[16]](https://www.maximecollard.be/en/). Sleeping in one of his rooms removes the only awkward question about dinner at La Table de Maxime: how to get home after the wine pairing.

## Reasoning per tier

**Tier 1 is the point of the trip.** The whole logic of staying near La Table de Maxime is to drink the wine pairing without thinking about the road home. The hamlet only has ~24 rooms total across all four properties [[1]](https://www.maximecollard.be/en/la-table-de-maxime/) [[2]](https://www.maximecollard.be/en/la-fabrique-du-pre-maho/) [[5]](https://www.lesterrassesdelour.be/), and Tripadvisor ranks La Table de Maxime #1 Best Value among Paliseul hotels (4.6/5) [[15]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotels-g1081533-Paliseul_Luxembourg_Province_The_Ardennes_Wallonia-Hotels.html). Book 3–6 months out, especially for Friday/Saturday.

**Tier 2 — Les Jardins de Maxime** in Beth is for guests who want the Collard hospitality at the same price point as the manor but with extra quiet and bike access; the walk back from dinner is 18 minutes through fields, or arrange a lift [[3]](https://www.maximecollard.be/en/les-jardins-de-maxime/).

**Tier 3 — when Our is full.** Pick **Le Moulin de Daverdisse** if you want a separate spa-style anchor for the weekend (pool, hammam, 6 ha of forest, on the Lesse) and don't mind a 15-minute taxi to dinner [[9]](https://www.daverdisse.com/) [[18]](https://visitwallonia.com/en-gb/content/le-moulin-de-daverdisse-perfect-hotel-relax-ardenne). **Le Fournil in Redu** trades luxury for the book-village vibe at half the price [[10]](https://hotel-restaurant-redu.be/en/le-fournil-en/). The Paliseul-village options (Le Gastronome, Le Passage, Auberge la Hutte Lurette) are practical bases with restaurants of their own — useful for night two of the trip when you don't want to drive 4 km to dinner [[13]](https://www.le-gastronome.be/en/hotel-paliseul/) [[14]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1081533-d2470802-Reviews-Le_Passage_B_B-Paliseul_Luxembourg_Province_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html) [[11]](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g1081533-d645302-Reviews-Auberge_La_Hutte_Lurette-Paliseul_Luxembourg_Province_The_Ardennes_Wallonia.html).

## Practical

- **All Collard properties: no dogs.** [[1]](https://www.maximecollard.be/en/la-table-de-maxime/) [[3]](https://www.maximecollard.be/en/les-jardins-de-maxime/) La Fabrique du Pré Maho is the exception — €10/night supplement [[2]](https://www.maximecollard.be/en/la-fabrique-du-pre-maho/). Hostellerie l'Authentique (in Paliseul, not tabled above) does take pets if you must travel with one.
- **Closures.** La Table de Maxime closes 21 Dec – 8 Jan plus other weeks scattered through the year [[1]](https://www.maximecollard.be/en/la-table-de-maxime/) — confirm the restaurant is open *before* booking the room, or you're paying €240 to stay next to a closed kitchen.
- **Half-board exists.** Les Terrasses de l'Our packages room + 3-course menu at €250–330 for two [[5]](https://www.lesterrassesdelour.be/); Le Gastronome at €290 [[13]](https://www.le-gastronome.be/en/hotel-paliseul/). At La Table de Maxime itself there is no "menu + room" combined rate — the tasting menu and the room are billed separately.
- **No elevators** at Les Terrasses de l'Our [[5]](https://www.lesterrassesdelour.be/) or La Roche des Lutons [[4]](https://www.maximecollard.be/la-roche-des-lutons/) — flag if mobility matters.
- **Phone, not form.** Maxime Collard's properties take bookings by phone (+32 61 23 95 10) rather than instant-book OTAs [[1]](https://www.maximecollard.be/en/la-table-de-maxime/) — write the call into the trip plan, don't expect to grab a room at midnight on Booking.com.
