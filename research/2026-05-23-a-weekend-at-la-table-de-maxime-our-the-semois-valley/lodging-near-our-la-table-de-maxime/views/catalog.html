Editor's pick · The booking call

Book one of the six rooms directly above La Table de Maxime — it's the only way to wobble three metres from tasting menu to bed.

€240–260 a night, breakfast included [1]. If those are full — likely, only six exist — the next-best on-site picks are La Fabrique du Pré Maho (tree-stilt eco-build, 200 m away [2]) or budget Les Terrasses de l'Our in the same hamlet from €150 [5]. True fallback when Our is sold out: Le Moulin de Daverdisse, four-star with pool, eleven kilometres away [9].

La Table de Maxime

"The three-metre commute from the wine pairing to bed."

Six rooms only. Renovated granite manor, oak beams, breakfast included. Comfort double €240, superior €260, single rooms €15 less [1]. Chef Maxime Collard — Gault&Millau Chef of the Year 2024, two Michelin stars retained in the 2026 guide [7][8]. Tripadvisor ranks the hotel #1 Best Value in Paliseul at 4.6/5 [15]. No dogs. Bookings by phone only.

Les Terrasses de l'Our

The cheapest way to sleep inside the hamlet — fourteen contemporary rooms €150–210, sauna €20, half-board packages €250–330 for two [5]. Collard transferred the property to the Rouard family from Wanlin on 3 October 2024 after running it for nine years [6]. No elevator — flag if mobility matters.

Before you book

Six things to ask on the same phone call
☎
Phone, not form

All Maxime Collard properties take bookings by phone — not by OTA, not by web form.
+32 61 23 95 10
Write the call into the trip plan; don't expect to grab a room at midnight on Booking.com [1].

✕
No dogs (mostly)

All Collard rooms refuse dogs [1] [3]. The single exception is La Fabrique du Pré Maho at €10/night supplement [2].

▤
Half-board exists

Les Terrasses de l'Our packages room + 3-course menu at €250–330 for two [5]; Le Gastronome at €290 [13]. At La Table itself the tasting menu and room are billed separately.

⊠
Closures

La Table de Maxime closes 21 Dec – 8 Jan plus other weeks scattered through the year [1]. Confirm the restaurant is open before booking the room — or you're paying €240 to stay next to a closed kitchen.

↥
No elevators

No lifts at Les Terrasses de l'Our [5] or La Roche des Lutons [4]. Flag mobility on the booking call.

◷
Three to six months

The hamlet has roughly twenty-four rooms total across all four Collard properties [1] [2] [5]. Book three to six months out — especially Friday and Saturday.

◉
Our, in one line

Roughly thirty granite houses on the river Our, inside the Paliseul commune, Belgian Ardennes [17]. Twenty-four lodging rooms total. One Michelin kitchen.

⌖
Tier-3 wants a car

Paliseul village, Daverdisse, Redu and Maissin all need a car or a 10–15 minute taxi to dinner. Build the round trip and a designated driver into the plan, or commit to the Tier 1 hamlet.
