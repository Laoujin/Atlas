## Event Highlights

### AWS Summit Los Angeles — Jun 10 [[1]](https://aws.amazon.com/events/summits/los-angeles/)
Free, one-day event at the LA Convention Center. 145+ sessions covering cloud, agentic AI, serverless, and industry-specific solutions. Keynote 11 AM–12 PM; networking reception 5–6 PM. Badge pickup available June 9. Registration at [hub.awsevents.com](https://aws.amazon.com/events/summits/los-angeles/).

### SIGGRAPH 2026 — Jul 19–23 [[2]](https://s2026.siggraph.org/)
The marquee LA tech event of the year. The world's premier conference on computer graphics and interactive techniques, now deeply AI-integrated. Tracks span Production & Animation, Research & Education, Gaming & Interactive, and New Technologies. Exhibition runs July 21–23. Keynotes include Lanny Smoot (Disney Research). AlmaLinux Day [[4]](https://almalinux.org/almalinux-day-los-angeles-2026/) on July 18 is a free satellite event the day before at E-Central, aimed at VFX/rendering pipeline engineers. ⚠ The LA Convention Center is the anchor; hotels in the Fig+Flower corridor book out fast.

### Tech Week LA — Oct 12–18 [[8]](https://www.tech-week.com/)
A16z-presented decentralized conference — no single venue or ticket. Hundreds of independent events (hackathons, founder panels, VC office hours, community meetups) organized by startups and funds across the city. Major sponsors: a16z, IBM, Adobe, AWS, Fenwick, HSBC. To attend: browse the event calendar once it launches; each host controls their own guest list.

### API & AI Summit (Kong) — Sep 30–Oct 1 [[7]](https://konghq.com/events/conferences/api-ai-summit)
Targeted at engineers and architects building AI systems in production — API infra, microservices, LLM connectivity. Includes optional certification training Sep 29 (limited to 100 seats). Early bird ($249) closes June 10.

### Data Con LA — Aug 22 [[5]](https://www.dataconla.com/)
14th year at CSU Long Beach. Community-focused; explicitly anti-hype framing on AI's practical impact on data engineering and analyst workflows.

### Generative AI Summit LA — Aug 26 [[6]](https://world.aiacceleratorinstitute.com/location/losangeles)
~300 attendees at Conrad LA. Three tracks: agentic architectures, live GenAI builds, LLMOps optimization. Speakers from DeepSeek, Google DeepMind, NVIDIA, OpenAI, Meta. Early bird ends July 4.

## Planning Notes

- **Best single weekend for tech + culture:** Jul 18–19 — AlmaLinux Day (free, Friday) rolls directly into SIGGRAPH opening weekend (Saturday). The downtown convention cluster is walkable from DTLA dining and arts.
- **Cloud/infra professionals:** Jun 10 AWS Summit (free) or Sep 30–Oct 1 Kong API Summit are the tightest, most focused picks.
- **Startup/VC scene:** Oct 12–18 Tech Week spreads events across multiple neighborhoods; plan to pick 4–6 events and build restaurant reservations around them.
- **Cybersecurity:** No single mega-conference; FutureCon (Jan, past), TechEx (May, past), and the Cybersecurity Summit (Nov 2) cover exec/CISO audiences throughout the year.
- **SCaLE** [[3]](https://www.socallinuxexpo.org/) is in Pasadena (~30 min from DTLA), not central LA — budget travel time. At $20, it's the cheapest full-week tech conference on the list.
