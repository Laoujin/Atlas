PLANNING NOTES
HOW TO USE THIS CALENDAR
  • Best single weekend: Jul 18–19 — AlmaLinux Day (free, Fri) rolls into SIGGRAPH opening day (Sat). Downtown convention cluster is walkable from DTLA dining and arts.
  • Cloud / infra: Jun 10 AWS Summit (free, one day) or Sep 30–Oct 1 Kong API Summit are the tightest focused picks.
  • Startup / VC: Oct 12–18 Tech Week spreads events across multiple neighborhoods — plan 4–6 events and build restaurant reservations around them.
  • GenAI early bird: GenAI Summit LA early bird closes Jul 4. Kong early bird closes Jun 10.
  • SCaLE note: SCaLE 23x is in Pasadena (~30 min from DTLA), not central LA — budget travel time. At $20, it was the cheapest full-week conference on this list.
