A Michelin Weekend in Los Angeles

SHOT LIST · FIVE SCENES · FIVE NEIGHBORHOODS · FIVE RESTAURANTS
The load-bearing decision is the restaurant. Its neighborhood sets your geography for the day. LA has five restaurants holding 2+ Michelin stars — each anchoring a distinct pocket of the city. [2]
→ Pick your restaurant first. Then build the day around it.
SCENE 01 EXT./INT. HOLLYWOOD — DAY + NIGHT DINNER: PROVIDENCE ★★★
01-A AFTERNOON
EXT. GRIFFITH OBSERVATORY — GRIFFITH PARK
Griffith Observatory
LA's consensus standout free attraction. Building, grounds, and public telescopes always free; planetarium $10. Telescope viewing after sunset only. [22]
FREE
Park at Greek Theatre lot → DASH shuttle $0.50
🎭
01-B EVENING
EXT./INT. HOLLYWOOD BOWL — HIGHLAND AVE
Hollywood Bowl
"Forever Summer" 2026 season — $1 bench tickets for select shows (limit 4 per household). Or picnic on the grounds before the short ride to Providence. [20]
$1 bench · $31–$103 general
🌅
01-C MORNING
EXT. RUNYON CANYON / BRUSH CANYON — HOLLYWOOD HILLS
Hollywood Hills Hike
Runyon Canyon: 3.3 mi loop, skyline views, off-leash dogs, free. Or Brush Canyon to the Hollywood Sign: 4.7 mi, ~1,000 ft gain, fewer crowds.
FREE
Runyon: arrive before 8am weekends (street parking fills)
01-D · DINNER NIGHT
INT. PROVIDENCE — 5955 MELROSE AVE
Providence
★★★
West Coast seafood with French technique — Big Island abalone, Santa Barbara spot prawns. Michael Cimarusti. LA's first 2-star restaurant (2009); earned its third June 26, 2025. [1] A la carte available alongside tasting menus. [10]
$375 classic · $495 chef's tasting · +$45 A5 wagyu
Tue–Fri 5:45pm · Sat 5:30pm · closed Sun–Mon
Book: OpenTable · up to 2 months ahead
Cancel: 7-day notice or $375/person charge
▶ BOOKING: ACCESSIBLE — OpenTable · 2 months out
SCENE 02 EXT./INT. WEST HOLLYWOOD — DAY + NIGHT DINNER: SOMNI ★★★
🚶
02-A ALL DAY
EXT. WEST HOLLYWOOD CORE — SUNSET STRIP
The Walkable Mile
West Hollywood is the most walkable square mile in California — groceries, world-class gyms and dining within a 10-minute walk. [23] Sunset Strip, Santa Monica Blvd, Design District.
NO CAR NEEDED
Best weekend base when dining at Somni
🎸
02-B EVENING
INT. THE TROUBADOUR / THE ROXY — SUNSET STRIP
Live Music Before Dinner
Intimate shows at The Troubadour and The Roxy on Sunset. Comedy Store and Laugh Factory for stand-up. A 7:30pm Somni seating pairs with an early set.
$15–$60 typical ticket
02-C AFTERNOON
INT./EXT. GETTY CENTER — BRENTWOOD
Getty Center
Free admission; timed-entry reservation required. World-class collection, gardens, and panoramic city views. Open until 8pm Saturdays — natural pairing with Somni's 7:30pm counter seating. [19]
FREE · $25 parking ($15 after 3pm)
15 min ride from WeHo · closed Monday
02-D · DINNER NIGHT
INT. SOMNI — 9045 NEMO ST
Somni
★★★
20+ course El Bulli–lineage meal by Aitor Zabala. Shiso leaves puffed like balloons; caviar on dashi meringue. 14-seat counter, single seating 7:30pm. Reopened November 2024 in West Hollywood. [25] Wine pairing required. [15]
$600 tasting + $145 min pairing = $745+ all-in
Wed–Sun 7:30pm · 14 seats
Book: Resy · first weekday of month at 1pm
⚠ No dietary restrictions or allergies accommodated
▶ BOOKING: HARD — Monthly Resy drop · plan 1+ month out
SCENE 03 EXT./INT. ARTS DISTRICT, DTLA — DAY + NIGHT DINNER: HAYATO ★★
🖼
03-A MORNING
INT. THE BROAD — 221 S GRAND AVE, DTLA
The Broad
Free general admission; timed tickets recommended. Infinity Mirror Rooms by Yayoi Kusama. Contemporary collection — Basquiat, Koons, Cindy Sherman. [4] 10-minute ride to Hayato at ROW DTLA.
FREE · reserve timed ticket in advance
03-B AFTERNOON
INT. GRAND CENTRAL MARKET — 317 S BROADWAY
Grand Central Market
Pre-dinner reconnaissance. Standouts: Villa's Tacos (blue-corn quesatacos), Broad Street Oyster ($25 lobster rolls), For The Win smashburgers. [21]
FREE entry
Open daily · walk south from The Broad
🏭
03-C LATE AFTERNOON
EXT./INT. ROW DTLA — 1320 E 7TH ST
ROW DTLA Complex
Repurposed 1917 warehouse campus; galleries, boutiques, and coffee. Hayato sits at Suite 126 inside the complex — you're already on site. [14]
FREE to explore
Arts District walkway between sets
⛩
03-D · DINNER NIGHT
INT. HAYATO — SUITE 126, ROW DTLA
Hayato
★★
LA's most intimate fine dining: 7 seats around an open kaiseki counter. Brandon Hayato Go explains every course. Handcrafted ceramics from Japan. [7] Only chef-owned authentic kaiseki restaurant in LA.
$450/person · Wed–Sun 6:30–10:30pm · 7 seats, 1 seating
Book: Tock · monthly drop · max 1×/month per guest
⚠ Photo ID at door · no reservation transfers
▶ BOOKING: HARDEST — 7-seat monthly Tock drop · plan 1+ month out
SCENE 04 EXT./INT. SANTA MONICA — DAY + NIGHT DINNER: MÉLISSE ★★
🏖
04-A MORNING
EXT. SANTA MONICA BEACH + PIER — PCH
Santa Monica Beach
Classic family beach anchored by the 1909 pier (National Historic Landmark). Ferris wheel, carousel, aquarium. Walk south 1 mile to Venice boardwalk.
FREE entry · Pacific Park rides $8–$17
Unlimited wristband $50 · $30 kids under 7
🚴
04-B MORNING
EXT. THE STRAND BIKE PATH — COASTAL
The Strand (22-mile coastal path)
Flat paved coastal path from Will Rogers to Torrance, passing Santa Monica and Venice while hugging the Pacific the whole way. Rentals nearby.
FREE · bike rentals ~$15–25/hr
Or run the shorter Santa Monica section (4–6 mi)
🛍
04-C AFTERNOON
EXT. THIRD STREET PROMENADE — SANTA MONICA
Third St Promenade + Montana Ave
Walkability peaks along Wilshire and Third St Promenade — open-air shops, street performers, cafés. Montana Ave for local boutiques. Mélisse is on Wilshire; walk from the Promenade.
FREE to walk · 10 min walk to Mélisse
⚜
04-D · DINNER NIGHT
INT. MÉLISSE — 1104 WILSHIRE BLVD
Mélisse
★★
"Restaurant within a restaurant" — 14-seat back-of- house tasting room inside the Citrin bistro. Chef Josiah Citrin & Ken Takayama. 18-course chef-guided menu; California farms San Diego to Napa Valley. [5]
$399/person · 18 courses · 14 seats
Book: OpenTable · (310) 395-0881
Corkage: $50/bottle (first 2) · $75/bottle after
▶ BOOKING: ACCESSIBLE — OpenTable · most available of the five
SCENE 05 EXT./INT. CULVER CITY — DAY + NIGHT DINNER: VESPERTINE ★★
🎨
05-A AFTERNOON
EXT./INT. CULVER CITY ART CLUSTER
Culver City Galleries
Reinvented from MGM studio town into a walkable arts hub — galleries and restaurants dense enough to ditch the car. [24] Blum & Poe, Night Gallery, Anat Ebgi within 5 blocks.
FREE to browse
Walk from Vespertine · all within ~5 blocks
☕
05-B MORNING
INT. DESTROYER — 3578 HAYDEN AVE
Destroyer (Kahn's daytime café)
Jordan Kahn's casual daytime counterpart to Vespertine sits directly across the street. The same imagination; a fraction of the price. Natural morning anchor for a Vespertine dinner night.
~$15–25 · directly across from Vespertine
🌿
05-C MORNING
EXT. KENNETH HAHN STATE REC AREA — S LA
Kenneth Hahn Park
Native coastal sage scrub, fishing lake, lotus pond, ~5 miles of trails with sweeping LA basin views. 15 minutes from Culver City; uncrowded and very local.
FREE · 4100 S La Cienega Blvd
05-D · DINNER NIGHT
INT. VESPERTINE — 3599 HAYDEN AVE
Vespertine
★★
16 courses spread across multiple floors of "The Waffle" — a twisted-steel building by Eric Owen Moss. Music, sculpture, and scent woven into the meal. Primordial ingredients tracing to the Cambrian Period. [6]
$395/person · Tue–Sat 6–8:30pm · 16 courses
Book: OpenTable · typically carries open slots
Destroyer across the street for morning brunch
▶ BOOKING: ACCESSIBLE — OpenTable · available slots most weeks

⚠ PRODUCTION NOTES

  • Settle the reservation before booking flights. Hayato and Somni release monthly — missing the drop means a one-month wait. Providence and Mélisse book up to 2 months via OpenTable. Vespertine carries open slots most weeks.
  • La Brea Tar Pits closes July 7, 2026 for a two-year renovation — remove from any itinerary. [11]
  • Eaton Canyon and Temescal Canyon trails closed through 2027 from the January 2025 fires. Griffith Park, Getty Center, and the beach corridor are unaffected. [12]
  • Somni accommodates zero dietary restrictions — confirm this hard stop before booking. [8]
  • Tech angle: SIGGRAPH 2026 runs July 19–23 at LA Convention Center — Hayato in the Arts District is a 10-minute ride and shares the same walkable DTLA pocket. [13]
