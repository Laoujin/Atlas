> **Decision.** Within 30 km of Troisgros there is no full-format IT conference happening on a weekend in 2026. The only weekend-aligned tech event is **Fête de la Science / Village des Sciences Roanne** on **2-12 October 2026** [[21]](https://www.fetedelascience.fr/) [[22]](https://www.aggloroanne.fr/enfance-jeunesse-education/enseignement-superieur-et-recherche/le-village-des-sciences-346.html). For weekday options, the recurring **Cafés numériques** (Fridays 8:30) and **FabLab Roannais Agglomération** open sessions are the only consistent meet-ups [[17]](https://www.roannais-tourisme.com/espace-pro/accompagnement-professionnel/les-cafes-numeriques-de-roannais-agglomeration/) [[20]](https://www.fablabroannaisagglomeration.fr/nos-evenements-2481.html). All real conference-grade IT events sit in **Saint-Étienne (~83 km)** or **Lyon (~85 km)** — outside the 30 km radius but within an hour's drive [[5]](https://fr.distance.to/Roanne/Saint-%C3%89tienne).

## Why the radius is thin

Troisgros (Le Bois sans Feuilles) is in Ouches, ~8 km west of Roanne [[1]](https://guide.michelin.com/en/auvergne-rhone-alpes/ouches/restaurant/troisgros-le-bois-sans-feuilles) [[2]](https://www.jpmoser.com/troisgros.html). A 30 km circle around it covers Roanne, Riorges (Le Scarabée expo centre), Le Coteau, Mably, Villerest, Saint-Romain-la-Motte, Perreux, Renaison, Balbigny and out to Semur-en-Brionnais [[3]](https://en.db-city.com/France--Auvergne-Rh%C3%B4ne-Alpes--Loire--Roanne) [[4]](https://www.eterritoire.fr/territoires/france,auvergne-rhone-alpes,loire,roanne/cable,xrac-30) — Roanne is the only town with any tech infrastructure. Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes' actual tech mass sits in **Saint-Étienne and Lyon**: the [French Tech Saint-Étienne Lyon](https://www.lafrenchtech-stl.com/notre-ecosysteme/) Capitale formally covers Loire and Rhône, but its events and hubs are in those two cities, not Roanne [[26]](https://www.lafrenchtech-stl.com/notre-ecosysteme/). The historic local cluster, Numelink (Saint-Étienne, 2002), was absorbed into [Digital League](https://www.digital-league.org/) — which moved cluster activity further away from Roanne [[27]](https://www.digital-league.org/) [[28]](https://www.lemondeinformatique.fr/actualites/lire-clust-r-numerique-et-numelink-preparent-leur-fusion-65234.html).

The one local trade fair, **L'instant numérique**, last ran as its 7th edition in **May 2022** at Le Scarabée; no 8.0 has been announced as of mid-2026 [[6]](https://www.linstant-numerique.com) [[7]](https://www.aggloroanne.fr/actualites-277/linstant-numerique-70-10147.html). Treat it as dormant.

## Sparse-but-real local institutions

- **Le Scarabée** (Riorges, adjacent to Roanne) — the only conference-grade venue in radius; hosts L'instant numérique (when it runs), Forum de l'Entrepreneuriat, eco-durable, et al. [[8]](https://mesinfos.fr/42153-riorges/roannais-la-dixieme-edition-du-forum-de-l-entrepreneuriat-le-19-mars-2026-au-scarabee-239895.html)
- **[Maison du Numérique](https://garage404.com/garage-404-une-ecole-du-numerique-eclot-a-roanne/)** (Roanne, opened Apr 2026) — €1M restoration of an old château at Place Clémenceau by Garage 404; consolidates training, hosts Simplon cohorts, runs public workshops. New, so its public-event calendar is still forming [[11]](https://mesinfos.fr/42300-roanne/la-maison-du-numerique-de-roanne-ouvrira-au-printemps-place-clemenceau-238597.html) [[12]](https://garage404.com/) [[13]](https://www.simplon.co/campus/roanne).
- **[FabLab Roannais Agglomération](https://www.fablabroannaisagglomeration.fr/)** (7 Place des Minimes, Roanne) — 400 m² maker space, 3D printing / laser / electronics; runs the public workshop slots in the table above [[19]](https://www.aggloroanne.fr/ma-collectivite/numerique-et-innovation/espace-dinnovation-numerique-fablab-1808.html).
- **[Roanne'Tech](https://www.facebook.com/roannetech/)** — local association posting on Facebook, organises occasional conferences. No public 2026 calendar at time of search; check the FB page close to your trip date [[15]](https://www.facebook.com/roannetech/).
- **[Polytech Roanne](https://www.onisep.fr/ressources/structures-enseignement/auvergne-rhone-alpes/loire/ecole-polytechnique-universitaire-de-l-universite-lyon-1-site-de-roanne-universite-claude-bernard-lyon-1)** (Technopôle Diderot) and **[IUT de Roanne](https://iut-roanne.univ-st-etienne.fr/)** — engineering and tech-IUT presence, but their public events are JPO/open-day style (last in Jan/Feb 2026); no public industry conferences on calendar [[23]](https://iut-roanne.univ-st-etienne.fr/fr/tout-l-agenda/annee-2025-2026-2/venez-a-notre-rencontre.html) [[24]](https://www.onisep.fr/ressources/structures-enseignement/auvergne-rhone-alpes/loire/ecole-polytechnique-universitaire-de-l-universite-lyon-1-site-de-roanne-universite-claude-bernard-lyon-1).
- **[Espace Numérique de Roannais Agglomération](https://www.aggloroanne.fr/numerique-et-innovation/espace-public-numerique/espace-numerique-pole-de-ressources-numeriques-195.html)** (53 rue Albert Thomas) — public digital space, host of the Friday Cafés numériques [[16]](https://www.aggloroanne.fr/numerique-et-innovation/espace-public-numerique/espace-numerique-pole-de-ressources-numeriques-195.html) [[17]](https://www.roannais-tourisme.com/espace-pro/accompagnement-professionnel/les-cafes-numeriques-de-roannais-agglomeration/).

If a real conference is the reason to align the Troisgros weekend, anchor on SIDO and accept the 1h drive both ways.

## Practical alignment for the weekend

1. **Best fit for a Saturday-dinner-anchored weekend in 2026:** book the trip during **Fête de la Science (2-12 Oct 2026)** — Village des Sciences runs Saturday/Sunday in Roanne, ~10 minutes from Troisgros [[21]](https://www.fetedelascience.fr/) [[22]](https://www.aggloroanne.fr/enfance-jeunesse-education/enseignement-superieur-et-recherche/le-village-des-sciences-346.html).
2. **If trip date is fixed and not Oct:** the most likely tech touch-points are a Friday Cafés numériques (if arriving Friday morning) plus a FabLab open session if one falls in your window [[17]](https://www.roannais-tourisme.com/espace-pro/accompagnement-professionnel/les-cafes-numeriques-de-roannais-agglomeration/) [[20]](https://www.fablabroannaisagglomeration.fr/nos-evenements-2481.html). Maison du Numérique is worth a visit as a building/project even outside scheduled events [[11]](https://mesinfos.fr/42300-roanne/la-maison-du-numerique-de-roanne-ouvrira-au-printemps-place-clemenceau-238597.html).
3. **If a real IT conference is non-negotiable:** plan around **SIDO Lyon** (September) and treat Troisgros as the Saturday-night anchor of a Lyon-week trip [[29]](https://www.sido-lyon.com/en/).
4. **Watch list close to trip date:** Roanne'Tech Facebook for ad-hoc conference announcements, and `linstant-numerique.com` in case an 8.0 is announced [[6]](https://www.linstant-numerique.com) [[15]](https://www.facebook.com/roannetech/).
